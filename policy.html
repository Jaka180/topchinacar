Policy & Tariffs政策与关税

EU tariffs, US restrictions, homologation, subsidies — the regulatory forces shaping where Chinese cars can go.解读欧盟关税、美国限制、海外认证与补贴政策，追踪决定中国汽车能去哪里的监管力量。

Trade policy is now the single biggest variable in China auto globalization. EU anti-subsidy duties pushed Chinese brands toward European plants; US rules effectively closed one market while opening questions about Mexico; certification regimes decide launch timelines everywhere. This section tracks the rules and what automakers do about them.贸易政策已成为中国汽车全球化的最大变量。欧盟反补贴税把中国品牌推向欧洲建厂；美国规则实际关闭了一个市场；各国认证体系决定上市节奏。本栏目追踪规则本身，以及车企的应对。

EU: anti-subsidy duties and the price-undertaking talks

Since 30 October 2024 the EU has applied definitive anti-subsidy (countervailing) duties on Chinese-built EVs, on top of the standard 10% import duty: BYD +17.0%, Geely +18.8%, SAIC +35.3%, with other cooperating producers at about +21% — taking total tariffs as high as roughly 45%. Brussels and Beijing have since been negotiating a minimum-import-price undertaking as an alternative to the duties; on 12 January 2026 the European Commission issued a guidance document for submitting such offers, while the duties remained in place. In June 2026 the German business daily Handelsblatt reported the Commission was preparing countervailing duties on Chinese-made plug-in hybrids (targeting BYD, Chery and SAIC) to close the PHEV loophole, with a member-state vote still pending. On 12 October 2026 China's Ministry of Commerce said the two sides had reached an understanding on hybrids with an upward arrangement from the 2025 base (Eurostat data: EU imports of Chinese hybrids were 107,000 in 2024 and 302,000 in 2025), while the EU side would continue the company-level price-undertaking procedure and related reviews for BEVs; EU Trade Commissioner Šefčovič said on 9 October the understanding included halving hybrid (HEV/PHEV) exports to the EU, without specifying a baseline. In Q1 2026 Chinese exports to Europe reached 438,400 units (+84.7% year on year), with PHEVs up 152.4% to 106,000 units, according to Gasgoo's research institute. [European Commission] [CnEVPost] [MOFCOM] [CnEVPost] [Gasgoo]

United States: tariffs and the connected-vehicle ban

On 14 May 2024 the Office of the U.S. Trade Representative announced proposed Section 301 modifications. The increases on Chinese EVs, from 25% to 100%, and on lithium-ion EV batteries, to 25%, took effect on 27 September 2024. On 14 January 2025 the Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS) issued a final rule restricting the import and sale of connected vehicles and vehicle-connectivity-system (VCS) hardware and software with a nexus to China or Russia: sales of vehicles using covered software are prohibited from model year 2027, and imports of covered VCS hardware from model year 2030 (1 January 2029 for non-model-year components). The rule took effect on 17 March 2025. In June 2026 BIS granted Volvo Cars a specific authorization (26 May 2026) but declined Polestar, which said it would stop selling new vehicles in the US from model year 2027. [BIS] [USTR] [WSJ]

Mexico, Brazil, Turkey and Russia: the tariff map

Mexico applied a 50% duty on cars from non-FTA countries — mainly China — from 1 January 2026, after Congress approved the measure in December 2025 (published in the Official Gazette on 30 December 2025). Brazil is phasing out its EV tariff exemption: the BEV import duty rose to 25% in July 2025 and reaches the 35% Mercosur cap in July 2026, while CKD kits stay at 14% until end-2026 and rise to 35% in January 2027, and SKD kits rise to 35% from July 2026 — alongside a six-month, US$463 million zero-tariff quota for CKD and SKD kits (from 1 July 2026; fully-assembled vehicles are not covered by this quota). Turkey added a 40% duty on Chinese EVs in March 2023 (total about 50%). On 8 June 2024 it imposed a 40% additional tariff on conventional and hybrid passenger cars imported from China, at least $7,000 per vehicle, effective 7 July 2024. In July 2024 it exempted investment-incentive imports back to a 10% duty to pull in local production — BYD signed its Turkey plant agreement on 8 July 2024; on 8 October 2024 China requested WTO consultations over the duties and import licensing. Russia raised the scrappage (utilization) fee on imported passenger cars by 70–85% from 1 October 2024, with annual 10–20% increases planned through 2030. [Mexico News Daily] [Agência Brasil] [CGTN] [Securities Times] [China Daily] [Meduza]

China: export controls and the anti-involution push

In October 2025 the Ministry of Commerce and the General Administration of Customs announced export controls (Announcement No. 58, effective 8 November 2025) on: rechargeable lithium-ion batteries with a gravimetric energy density of 300 Wh/kg or more (cells and packs), and battery-making equipment on the list (winding machines, stacking machines, electrolyte-filling machines, hot presses, formation-and-grading systems, and grading cabinets), plus related production technology; lithium iron phosphate (LFP) cathode material with a compacted density of at least 2.5 g/cm³ and a specific capacity of at least 156 mAh/g; nickel-cobalt-manganese and nickel-cobalt-aluminium hydroxide precursors; lithium-rich manganese-based cathode material, and cathode-making equipment on the list (roller hearth kilns, high-speed mixers, sand mills and jet mills); and artificial graphite anode material (including anodes that mix artificial and natural graphite). Granulation equipment on the list is vertical and continuous granulation kettles of at least 5 m³. Graphitisation equipment on the list is box furnaces, Acheson furnaces, internal-series furnaces and continuous graphitisation furnaces. Coating equipment on the list is fusion-coating equipment larger than 300 L, spray dryers larger than 60 m³, and CVD rotary kilns with a barrel diameter larger than 0.5 m, plus the related process technology. Exports require a licence. Announcement No. 70 then suspended these measures from 7 November 2025 until 10 November 2026. On the domestic side, the December 2025 Central Economic Work Conference called for "deeply rectifying involution-style competition"; on 12 December 2025 the State Administration for Market Regulation published a draft compliance guide on auto-industry pricing, and the MIIT, NDRC and SAMR jointly urged an end to disorderly price wars. [MOFCOM] [证券时报网] [中国经济网]

China: trade-in subsidies

On 30 December 2025 eight ministries issued the 2026 auto trade-in subsidy rules (Document 商办消费函〔2025〕467号, effective 1 January 2026), switching from fixed amounts to a share of the new-car price: for a scrappage update, 12% of the new-car price and at most ¥20,000 when a qualifying old car is scrapped and a NEV passenger car is bought, or 10% of the price and at most ¥15,000 when a qualifying ICE passenger car is scrapped and an ICE passenger car of 2.0 litres or below is bought, and a replacement-update subsidy of 8% up to ¥15,000 for NEVs or 6% up to ¥13,000 for ICE vehicles of 2.0 litres or less. The 2026 Government Work Report allocated RMB 250 billion of ultra-long special government bonds to consumer-goods trade-ins. [税务总局政策法规库] [中国工业新闻网] [中国经济网]

欧盟：反补贴税与价格承诺谈判

自 2024 年 10 月 30 日起，欧盟在 10% 基准关税之上，对中国产纯电动车征收最终反补贴税：比亚迪 +17.0%、吉利 +18.8%、上汽 +35.3%，其他配合调查车企约 +21%，综合税率最高约 45%。此后布鲁塞尔与北京围绕「最低进口价」承诺（价格承诺）作为替代方案持续谈判；2026 年 1 月 12 日，欧盟委员会发布价格承诺申报指引，反补贴税继续有效。2026 年 6 月，德国《商报》报道欧盟正筹备对华产插混征收反补贴税（针对比亚迪、奇瑞、上汽）以堵住插混漏洞，尚待成员国表决。到 2026 年 10 月 12 日，中国商务部表示双方已就混动达成谅解，在 2025 年基础上作向上安排——欧盟统计局口径下，2024 年欧盟自中国进口混动 10.7 万辆、2025 年为 30.2 万辆；纯电方面，欧方将继续推进企业价格承诺程序及相关复审。欧方贸易委员 Šefčovič 于 10 月 9 日称，该谅解包含把混动（HEV、PHEV）对欧出口减半，但未说明基数。据盖世汽车研究院，2026 年一季度中国对欧汽车出口 43.84 万辆（同比 +84.7%），插混增长 152.4% 至 10.6 万辆。[欧盟委员会] [CnEVPost] [商务部] [CnEVPost] [盖世汽车]

美国：关税与联网汽车限制

2024 年 5 月 14 日，美国贸易代表办公室宣布 301 条款修改方案。对华电动车关税从 25% 提高到 100%、锂离子电动车电池关税提高到 25%，于 2024 年 9 月 27 日生效。2025 年 1 月 14 日，商务部工业与安全局（BIS）发布最终规则，限制进口与销售与中国或俄罗斯有关联的联网汽车及车辆连接系统（VCS）软硬件：软件禁令自 2027 车型年生效，硬件禁令自 2030 车型年生效（非车型年零部件为 2029 年 1 月 1 日），规则于 2025 年 3 月 17 日生效。2026 年 6 月，BIS 批准沃尔沃汽车特定授权（5 月 26 日），但拒绝极星（Polestar），后者宣布自 2027 车型年起停止在美国销售新车。[BIS] [USTR] [WSJ]

墨西哥、巴西、土耳其、俄罗斯：关税版图

墨西哥自 2026 年 1 月 1 日起对非自贸协定国家（主要是中国）整车征收 50% 关税（2025 年 12 月国会批准，12 月 30 日刊于官方公报）。巴西逐步取消电动车关税豁免：纯电整车进口关税 2025 年 7 月升至 25%，2026 年 7 月达到南方共同市场 35% 上限；CKD 组装件维持 14% 至 2026 年底、2027 年 1 月升至 35%，SKD 组装件自 2026 年 7 月起升至 35%，另设 6 个月、4.63 亿美元零关税配额（仅限 CKD/SKD 组装件，自 2026 年 7 月 1 日起算；整车不在此配额内）。土耳其 2023 年 3 月对中国电动车加征 40% 附加关税（合计约 50%）。2024 年 6 月 8 日，对自中国进口的传统燃油和混合动力乘用车加征 40% 附加关税，每辆最低 7000 美元，2024 年 7 月 7 日生效。2024 年 7 月又对符合投资激励的进口免征附加关税（恢复 10%）以吸引本地投资——比亚迪于 2024 年 7 月 8 日签署土耳其建厂协议；2024 年 10 月 8 日中国就土方关税与进口许可在世贸组织提出磋商请求。俄罗斯自 2024 年 10 月 1 日起将进口乘用车报废税（回收税）上调 70%–85%，并计划 2025–2030 年每年再上调 10%–20%。[Mexico News Daily] [Agência Brasil] [CGTN] [证券时报网] [中国日报] [Meduza]

中国：出口管制与反内卷

2025 年 10 月，商务部与海关总署发布 2025 年第 58 号公告，对重量能量密度 ≥300 Wh/kg 的可充放电锂离子电池（含电芯和电池组；电池设备为卷绕机、叠片机、注液机、热压机、化成分容系统、分容柜，以及相应生产技术）、压实密度 ≥2.5 g/cm³ 且克容量 ≥156 mAh/g 的磷酸铁锂正极材料、镍钴锰氢氧化物与镍钴铝氢氧化物前驱体、富锂锰基正极材料及正极制造设备（辊道窑、高速混料机、砂磨机、气流粉碎机），以及人造石墨负极材料（含人造石墨与天然石墨混合的负极材料）实施出口管制。列入管制的造粒设备为容积不低于 5 立方米的立式造粒釜和连续造粒釜；石墨化设备为箱体炉、艾奇逊炉、内串炉和连续石墨化炉；包覆设备为容积大于 300 升的融合包覆设备、容积大于 60 立方米的喷雾干燥设备，以及桶体直径大于 0.5 米的化学气相沉积（CVD）回转窑，另有相应工艺技术。上述管制自 2025 年 11 月 8 日起实施，出口需申请许可；第 70 号公告自 2025 年 11 月 7 日起暂停实施至 2026 年 11 月 10 日。国内方面，2025 年 12 月中央经济工作会议提出「深入整治内卷式竞争」；12 月 12 日市场监管总局发布《汽车行业价格行为合规指南（征求意见稿）》；工信部、发改委、市场监管总局联合召开新能源汽车行业企业座谈会，坚决抵制无序价格战。[商务部] [证券时报网] [中国经济网]

中国：以旧换新补贴

2025 年 12 月 30 日，商务部等 8 部门印发《2026 年汽车以旧换新补贴实施细则》（商办消费函〔2025〕467 号，2026 年 1 月 1 日起执行），由定额补贴改为按新车售价比例补贴：报废符合条件的旧车并购买新能源乘用车，按售价 12%、最高 2 万元；报废符合条件的燃油乘用车并购买 2.0 升及以下燃油乘用车，按售价 10%、最高 1.5 万元；置换更新新能源按售价 8% 且最高 1.5 万元，置换更新购买 2.0 升及以下燃油乘用车按售价 6% 且最高 1.3 万元。2026 年《政府工作报告》安排 2500 亿元超长期特别国债支持消费品以旧换新。[税务总局政策法规库] [中国工业新闻网] [中国经济网]
