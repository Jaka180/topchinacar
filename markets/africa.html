Chinese Cars in Africa非洲：中国汽车市场观察

South Africa: the anchor

Africa is primarily a pickup, SUV and commercial-vehicle story, and South Africa is the anchor market with the best data. Chery has become the standout: after acquiring Nissan's Rosslyn plant near Pretoria, it inaugurated the site in July 2026, with production now scheduled to begin in the second half of 2027 and the first vehicles expected to roll out in Q3 2027 (single-shift capacity of about 50,000 units a year; the 2027 output target is about 15,000 units during the third- and fourth-quarter ramp-up), and its group ranked #2 in South African industry deliveries in January–May 2026, behind only Toyota. GWM (including Haval and Tank) sold about 27,000 units in 2025, ranking around #6 by brand. Chinese brands collectively passed 19% of South Africa's passenger and light-commercial market in Q1 2026 (TransUnion) and about 40% of WesBank-financed new cars by July 2026.

Egypt, Nigeria, Morocco

Egypt is a fast-emerging volume market where Chery holds about 10% share (third overall) and MG ranks in the top five; MG (SAIC) is building a roughly $135 million plant in New October City targeted for late 2026, and Jetour has signed a roughly $123 million assembly deal with the Kasrawy Group. Nigeria is earlier-stage, with GAC and others evaluating assembly to position the country as an ECOWAS hub. Morocco is the manufacturing and battery play: leveraging free-trade access to the EU, it has drawn Chinese battery investment, including Gotion High-Tech's Kenitra gigafactory — Africa's first, with a first phase targeted for 2026.

The localisation turn

The through-line for 2025–2026 is a move from importing fully built vehicles toward local assembly. Chery's Rosslyn plant is the flagship: in mid-July 2026 Chery first announced the Tiggo 4 as its initial locally built model, and in October 2026 it confirmed the Omoda C4 hybrid would be the first Omoda built there — with petrol and EV variants to follow and South African production starting in Q3 2027; GWM is weighing sharing or acquiring a plant; and legacy manufacturers are lobbying for higher import tariffs on Chinese and Indian fully built units. Africa remains overwhelmingly ICE, with Chinese brands leading the small but growing EV and hybrid segment.

South Africa figures via NAAMSA, Chery International and local media (Reuters/CNBC Africa); other countries from local media and company releases.

南非：锚点市场

在非洲，中国汽车主要是皮卡、SUV 与商用车的故事，南非则是数据最完整的锚点市场。奇瑞已成为最大亮点：收购比勒陀利亚附近日产 Rosslyn 工厂后，于 2026 年 7 月启用该厂，生产现计划于 2027 年下半年开始、首批车辆预计 2027 年三季度下线（单班年产能约 5 万辆；2027 年第三、四季度爬坡期间的产量目标约 1.5 万辆），其集团 2026 年 1–5 月在南非交付量位居行业第二，仅次于丰田。长城（含哈弗、坦克）2025 年销量约 2.7 万辆，品牌排名约第 6。2026 年一季度中国品牌合计占南非乘用车与轻型商用车市场逾 19%（TransUnion），2026 年 7 月已占 WesBank 融资新车的约 40%。

埃及、尼日利亚、摩洛哥

埃及是快速崛起的走量市场，奇瑞约占 10% 份额（整体第三），MG 进入前五；上汽 MG 正在新十月城建设约 1.35 亿美元的工厂（目标 2026 年底），捷途已与 Kasrawy 集团签署约 1.23 亿美元的组装协议。尼日利亚仍处早期阶段，广汽等正评估建厂，以将该国打造为西非（ECOWAS）枢纽。摩洛哥则是制造与电池的重头戏：凭借对欧盟的自由贸易通道，吸引了中国电池投资，包括国轩高科在 Kenitra 的超级工厂——非洲首座，第一期目标 2026 年。

本地化转向

2025–2026 年的主线，是从整车进口转向本地组装。奇瑞 Rosslyn 工厂是旗舰项目：奇瑞 2026 年 7 月中曾宣布 Tiggo 4 为首款本地生产车型，2026 年 10 月又确认 Omoda C4 混动将成为该厂首款 Omoda 车型——燃油与纯电版本随后跟进，南非本地生产自 2027 年三季度开始；长城正考虑共用或收购工厂；传统车企正游说对中国和印度整车进口提高关税。非洲仍以燃油车为绝对主体，中国品牌在小而快速增长的电动与混动细分市场领先。

南非数据来自 NAAMSA、奇瑞国际及当地媒体（路透/CNBC Africa）；其他国家数据来自当地媒体与企业公告。
