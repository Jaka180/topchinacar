Thailand: the EV assembly hub

Southeast Asia is China's right-hand-drive EV workshop, and Thailand — the "Detroit of Southeast Asia" — is its centre. In January–July 2026, Chinese automakers accounted for 90.3% of the 126,439 battery-electric passenger cars registered (Department of Land Transport, compiled by JETRO). Kasikorn Research Center said on 14 August 2026 that more than 91% of battery-electric vehicles sold were Chinese brands. A same-basis share for BYD alone is not available. BYD's Rayong plant (around 150,000 units a year) has run since mid-2024, GWM upgraded a former GM plant in Rayong, and MG/SAIC also assembles locally. Under the EV3.5 framework updated in December 2025, manufacturers that imported EVs duty-free must now build locally — a 2-to-1 production ratio in 2026 rising to 3-to-1 in 2027, with exports counting 1.5x toward the quota. The squeeze has already claimed casualties: Neta's parent Hozon filed for bankruptcy in 2025, and Thailand's Finance Ministry is planning to sue its local unit to recover subsidies exceeding 2 billion baht (about $63.5 million) paid since 2022.

Indonesia and Malaysia

Indonesia is the other manufacturing pillar: BYD went from nothing to a top-four automaker with close to half the EV market, and its roughly $1 billion Subang plant (150,000 units a year) was inaugurated on 3 September 2026 and produced 24,877 vehicles between April and August 2026. CBU import incentives expired at end-2025, and from 2026 incentives apply only to locally produced EVs meeting a 40% local-content threshold that rises toward 60% and 80% over the decade. Malaysia is the region's largest overall market (over 820,000 vehicles in 2025); BYD's CKD project in Tanjung Malim was cancelled — the company confirmed in September 2026 it would not proceed — and BYD instead signed a local-assembly MoU with Sime Motors' Inokom plant in Kulim, Kedah; BYD registered 5,675 units in H1 2026 (#8). Chery was the best-selling Chinese brand in H1 2025 (led by the Jaecoo J7), and Geely backs the national brand Proton.

Vietnam and the wider region

Vietnam is the exception: domestic champion VinFast dominates with around 175,000 units in 2025, and Chinese brands remain small there against a strong home player. Regionally, Chinese-branded sales rose sharply through 2025, and ASEAN plants increasingly export onward — including to Europe. The strategic picture has hardened: after an incentive-fuelled boom, Thailand, Indonesia and Malaysia are all shifting from import perks to hard localisation requirements, separating well-capitalised builders like BYD from undercapitalised entrants.

Sources: Department of Land Transport via JETRO, Kasikorn Research Center, Federation of Thai Industries, local industry data (Carlist/The Edge), company releases, local media.

泰国：电动车组装枢纽

东南亚是中国车企的右舵电动车工坊，而有"东南亚底特律"之称的泰国是其中心。2026 年 1–7 月，泰国纯电乘用车登记 126,439 辆，其中中国车企合计占 90.3%（陆运局数据，JETRO 汇总）。开泰研究中心 2026 年 8 月 14 日称，售出的纯电车超过 91% 是中国品牌。比亚迪单独份额暂无数据。比亚迪罗勇（Rayong）工厂（年产能约 15 万辆）2024 年年中投产，长城升级了罗勇的前通用工厂，MG/上汽也在当地组装。在 2025 年 12 月更新的 EV3.5 框架下，享受整车进口优惠的车企必须本地补产——2026 年进口 1 辆补产 2 辆，2027 年升至 1 比 3，出口按 1.5 倍计入配额。压力已开始显现：哪吒母公司合众 2025 年破产，泰国财政部拟起诉其本地子公司，追回自 2022 年以来发放的超过 20 亿泰铢（约 6,350 万美元）补贴。

印尼与马来西亚

印尼是另一制造支柱：比亚迪从零起步跃升为前四大车企，占据当地电动车市场近半份额，其约 10 亿美元的 Subang 工厂（年产能 15 万辆）于 2026 年 9 月 3 日揭幕，4–8 月已生产 24,877 辆。整车进口激励 2025 年底到期不再延长，自 2026 年起激励仅适用于满足 40% 本地含量门槛（本十年内逐步升至 60%、80%）的本地生产电动车。马来西亚 2025 年以逾 82 万辆成为区域最大整体市场；比亚迪丹戎马林 CKD 项目已取消——公司 2026 年 9 月确认不再推进——改与 Sime Motors 旗下 Inokom（吉打州居林）签署本地组装谅解备忘录；2026 上半年比亚迪登记 5,675 辆（第 8）。奇瑞是 2025 上半年最畅销的中国品牌（由 Jaecoo J7 领衔），吉利扶持国民品牌 Proton。

越南与更广区域

越南是例外：本土冠军 VinFast 以 2025 年约 17.5 万辆占据主导，中国品牌在强势本土玩家面前体量尚小。区域层面，中国品牌销量在 2025 年大幅增长，东盟工厂正日益向外出口——包括欧洲。战略图景已趋明朗：在激励推动的爆发之后，泰国、印尼、马来西亚正从进口优惠转向强硬的本地化要求，把资金雄厚的建设者（如比亚迪）与资本不足的入局者区分开来。
