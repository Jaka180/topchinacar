BYD and Geely Automobile both reported higher quarterly profits, while SAIC Motor's Q3 profit fell sharply. On the technology side, Volkswagen Group China unveiled a locally developed technology matrix, and Dongfeng and battery maker CATL signed a new group-level agreement on ultra-fast charging and battery swapping.

Q3 Results: BYD and Geely Up, SAIC Down

BYD reported Q3 revenue of RMB 221.488 billion, up 13.59% year on year, and net profit attributable to shareholders of RMB 10.02 billion, up 28.08%. For the first nine months, revenue was RMB 566.304 billion, roughly flat, and net profit was RMB 22.345 billion, down 4.24%. The report did not separate domestic and overseas revenue or volumes. [21st Century Business Herald]

Geely Automobile Holdings, the Hong Kong-listed car unit of Geely, reported Q3 revenue of RMB 99.0 billion, up 10%, and core net profit attributable to shareholders of RMB 5.21 billion, up 32%; both were records for the period. Nine-month revenue was RMB 272.6 billion and core net profit RMB 14.9 billion, up 41%, with a gross margin of 18%, 1.5 percentage points higher than a year earlier. The report did not provide a domestic/overseas split. [Gasgoo]

SAIC Motor, the Shanghai state-owned group that is also the Chinese partner in SAIC Volkswagen and SAIC-GM, reported nine-month total operating revenue of RMB 455.221 billion, down 2.94%, and net profit attributable to shareholders of RMB 5.638 billion, down 30.4%. Its Q3 net profit was RMB 486 million, down 76.7%. The company attributed the decline mainly to fair-value changes in financial assets and foreign-exchange losses in overseas operations. SAIC sold 3.147 million vehicles in January–September, including 1.166 million overseas, up 52.4%; the report did not break out domestic sales. [The Paper]

Supplier Sunwoda also reported results: nine-month revenue rose 45.78% to RMB 63.462 billion. After a capital increase that brought in Xiaomi as a strategic shareholder, Sunwoda's stake in its EV-battery unit Sunwoda Power fell to 22.78% and the unit was deconsolidated, which the company expects to add RMB 3.2–4.5 billion to 2026 net profit. [Gasgoo]

Volkswagen China's VEGA Matrix

Volkswagen Group China unveiled VEGA in Hefei on October 28. Developed by its China team, it covers the CMP platform for mainstream A-segment cars, the CSP platform for full-size flagships, the CEA electrical/electronic architecture and driver assistance. The platforms support battery-electric, plug-in hybrid and range-extended powertrains, and 850V fast charging is planned for 2027. The L2++ HS8 system from CARIZON, Volkswagen's joint venture with Horizon Robotics, will be fitted to 7 new models from Volkswagen's three Chinese joint ventures in 2026. Volkswagen holds an L3 road-test permit in Hefei and targets L3 readiness in 2027. [Yicai]

For international readers, the key detail is the structure: Volkswagen's China car business runs through three separate joint-venture operating companies, SAIC Volkswagen, FAW-Volkswagen and Volkswagen Anhui. VEGA is presented as a shared technology base for models from these separate companies.

Batteries, Charging and Driver Assistance

Dongfeng Motor and CATL signed a strategic cooperation agreement in Wuhan on October 29, their third at group level. They will co-develop next-generation ultra-fast charging, with 10C-and-above products debuting on Dongfeng's Voyah, M-Hero and Yijing brands, and develop battery-swapping across passenger and commercial vehicles. No value or volume was disclosed. [21st Century Business Herald]

Zhuoyu, the former DJI Automotive, launched its commercial-vehicle driver-assistance brand Panxing in Zhuhai with seven truck and bus makers, including Shaanxi Automobile, Sinotruk and Dongfeng Commercial Vehicle. Shaanxi's Delong X5000 with the PDS 4 system entered mass production in June 2026, and Zhuoyu says its fuel-saving algorithm cut fuel use by 4.5%–7.5% in tests. [Gasgoo]

New Models and Policy

Xpeng launched the Mona L05 mid-size SUV in China at official prices of 144,800–176,800 yuan, in BEV and range-extended versions. Xpeng delivered 41,256 vehicles in September and has targeted more than 60,000 monthly deliveries in Q4. [CnEVPost] Changan's Avatr brand launched the 07L Max BEV at 229,900 yuan, with an 89.33 kWh CATL battery and Huawei Qiankun ADS 5, after cancelling its planned online launch event. [MyDrivers]

In Beijing, the municipal commerce bureau extended the application deadline for its 2026 car trade-in subsidy from November 30, 2026 to January 10, 2027, unless the annual budget runs out first. [The Beijing News] Separately, BYD chairman Wang Chuanfu said the company now has more than 1.02 million employees, including about 120,000 R&D engineers. [Gasgoo]

三季报：比亚迪、吉利增长，上汽承压

比亚迪第三季度营业收入2214.88亿元，同比增长13.59%；归母净利润100.2亿元，同比增长28.08%。前三季度营业收入5663.04亿元，与上年同期基本持平；归母净利润223.45亿元，同比下降4.24%。报道未披露国内与海外的收入或销量拆分。[21世纪经济报道]

港股上市的吉利汽车控股三季度收入990亿元，同比增长10%；核心归母净利润52.1亿元，同比增长32%，均为同期新高。前三季度收入2726亿元，核心归母净利润149亿元，同比增长41%；毛利率18%，同比提升1.5个百分点。报道未提供国内与海外的拆分。[盖世汽车]

上汽集团是上海的国有汽车集团，也是上汽大众、上汽通用的中方股东。前三季度营业总收入4552.21亿元，同比下降2.94%；归母净利润56.38亿元，同比下降30.4%；其中第三季度归母净利润4.86亿元，同比下降76.7%。公司将利润下滑主要归因于金融资产公允价值变动及海外业务汇兑损失。前三季度销量314.7万辆，其中海外销量116.6万辆，同比增长52.4%；报道未单列国内销量。[澎湃新闻]

零部件方面，欣旺达前三季度营收634.62亿元，同比增长45.78%。欣旺达动力增资后小米成为其战略股东，欣旺达持股降至22.78%，动力电池子公司不再并表，公司预计此举将增加2026年净利润32亿–45亿元。[盖世汽车]

大众中国发布VEGA技术矩阵

大众汽车集团（中国）10月28日在合肥发布由中国团队研发的技术矩阵VEGA，涵盖面向主流A级车的CMP平台、面向全尺寸旗舰的CSP平台、CEA电子电气架构以及智驾。平台支持纯电、插混和增程，850V快充计划于2027年推出。由大众与地平线合资的酷睿程开发的L2++智驾系统HS8，将在2026年搭载于大众三家在华合资企业的7款新车。大众已在合肥获得L3道路测试牌照，计划2027年具备L3能力。[第一财经]

需要说明的是，大众在华整车业务由上汽大众、一汽-大众和大众安徽三家合资企业分别经营，三者是独立的经营主体。VEGA被定位为统一的技术底座，将用于这些不同主体的车型。

电池、补能与智驾供应链

东风汽车与宁德时代10月29日在武汉签署战略合作协议，这是双方第三次集团级合作。双方将联合研发下一代超充技术，10C及以上超充产品将在岚图、猛士、奕境品牌首发；同时共同研发覆盖乘用车与商用车的换电体系。协议未披露金额或采购量。[21世纪经济报道]

卓驭（原大疆车载）在珠海发布商用车智驾品牌“卓驭磐行”，合作方包括陕汽、中国重汽、东风商用车等七家商用车企。搭载PDS 4的陕汽德龙X5000已于2026年6月量产；卓驭称其节油算法实测节油4.5%–7.5%。[盖世汽车]

新车与政策

小鹏MONA L05中型SUV在国内上市，提供纯电和增程版本，官方指导价14.48万–17.68万元。小鹏9月交付41,256辆，此前设定四季度单月交付超6万辆的目标。[CnEVPost]长安旗下阿维塔取消原定线上发布会后，上市07L Max纯电版，官方指导价22.99万元，搭载宁德时代89.33kWh电池和华为乾崑ADS 5。[快科技]

北京市商务局将2026年汽车以旧换新补贴申报截止时间由2026年11月30日延长至2027年1月10日，年度补贴资金使用完毕除外。[新京报]此外，比亚迪董事长王传福表示，公司员工总数超过102万人，其中研发工程师约12万人。[盖世汽车]
