Denza Enters India's Luxury EV Market as XPeng Broadens Its European Lineup腾势进入印度高端电动车市场，小鹏扩充欧洲产品阵容

Denza has launched the D9 in India at INR 9 million alongside the Z9 GT. XPeng has disclosed UK prices from £32,990 for the L03 and £64,990 for the G9L, while opening Czech L03 orders.腾势在印度推出D9与Z9 GT，D9展厅价为900万印度卢比。小鹏L03与G9L英国起价分别为32,990和64,990英镑，同时在捷克开启L03订购，扩大欧洲产品选择。

By TopChinaCar Editorial作者：TopChinaCar 编辑部 · Published November 14, 2026发布于 2026-11-14 · Compiled from the primary sources cited below基于文末所列一手信源编写

Denza's Indian launch and XPeng's latest European announcements put concrete product decisions at the center of China's automotive expansion abroad. Denza has introduced the D9 and Z9 GT in India, while XPeng has disclosed UK pricing for the L03 and G9L and opened L03 orders in Czechia. The common thread is the move from presenting vehicles to setting out local prices, sales arrangements and delivery expectations. These are useful markers of execution, although they do not yet establish how much demand the models will attract.

Denza adds products and a sales footprint in India

BYD's Denza launched the D9 luxury MPV and Z9 GT in India on November 13. The D9 is priced at INR 9 million ex-showroom, with a booking deposit of INR 1 million. Both models will be offered through official dealerships in Gurugram, Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Chennai and Hyderabad. The six-city footprint provides a concrete sales framework for the launch. [CarWale]

The editorial significance lies in that combination of products and distribution. A launch establishes what the brand intends to sell; an identified dealer footprint makes its route to customers more tangible. Neither the price announcement nor the number of cities should be treated as evidence of completed deliveries. For now, the development establishes an expansion of BYD's premium offering in India, with subsequent sales disclosures needed to judge its reception.

XPeng sets out different propositions for UK buyers

XPeng's G9L starts at £64,990 in the UK in both battery-electric and range-extender form, with first deliveries expected in February 2027. The rear-wheel-drive BEV offers 367 miles of WLTP range. The model has seven seats, while a six-seat configuration is available on a higher trim. Together, the pricing, seating options and delivery forecast make the flagship's local proposition more specific. [EV Powered]

The L03 provides a different entry point, starting at £32,990. Its entry BEV offers 276 miles of range, while the rear-wheel-drive Long Range version offers 323 miles. XPeng's UK managing director also confirmed a range extender with a 37.25 kWh battery, 133 miles of electric range and 631 miles of combined WLTP range. These figures refer to different versions and operating measures; the combined range must not be read as battery-only capability. [EV Powered]

Seen together, the announcements show XPeng broadening the choices within its UK range through different vehicle sizes and powertrains. That is a product strategy rather than a demonstrated commercial result. The delivery timetable and the eventual take-up of each version will be more revealing than the starting prices alone. In particular, the G9L's February 2027 forecast remains an expectation to track.

Czech orders connect the lineup to local distribution

At Prague's e-Salon, XPeng presented the L03 and G9L. Czech L03 BEV orders opened at CZK 859,900–1,139,900, while the range extender is priced at CZK 939,900. Local customer deliveries are expected in mid-November. The G9L is planned to enter the Czech market in 2027. XPeng currently has eight sales locations in the country and plans to add two. [CarNewsChina]

The Czech development adds a distribution dimension to the European product story. Offering a vehicle, opening orders and adding sales locations are connected steps, but each describes a different stage of expansion. The UK and Czech announcements also use different currencies and market specifications, so their list prices alone do not support a direct assessment of relative affordability. What they do show is the same product family being prepared for customers in separate markets.

What to Watch

The next useful evidence will concern delivery execution: whether Czech L03 handovers follow the expected mid-November timing and whether the UK G9L reaches customers in February 2027 as anticipated. Denza's Indian rollout will similarly be easier to assess once sales or delivery information supplements the launch and dealer arrangements. Across today's developments, the analytical question is how clearly announced products and channels translate into sustained local business.

腾势在印度上市、小鹏公布欧洲新车型销售安排，让今天的中国汽车出海动态集中在具体产品决策上。腾势正式推出D9和Z9 GT，小鹏则公布L03与G9L英国售价，并在捷克开启L03订购。几条消息的共同点，是车型展示进一步转化为当地价格、销售安排和交付预期。这些是观察执行进度的具体标志，但尚不能据此判断实际需求规模。

腾势在印度增加产品与销售覆盖

比亚迪旗下腾势11月13日在印度正式推出D9豪华MPV和Z9 GT。D9展厅价为900万印度卢比，订金为100万卢比。两款车将通过古尔冈、孟买、艾哈迈达巴德、班加罗尔、金奈和海得拉巴的官方经销商销售，六个城市的覆盖为此次上市提供了明确的销售框架。[CarWale]

从编辑观察看，值得关注的是产品与销售渠道一起出现。上市说明品牌准备销售什么，列出经销商覆盖城市则让通向客户的路径更加具体。不过，价格公布和城市数量都不能直接作为已经完成交付的证据。目前可以确认的是，比亚迪在印度增加了高端产品选择；市场接受程度仍需要后续销量披露来判断。

小鹏在英国提供不同产品选择

小鹏G9L英国纯电及增程版本均从64,990英镑起，首批交付预计在2027年2月。纯电后驱版WLTP续航为367英里。车型提供七座布局，高配版本可以选择六座。价格、座椅选择和交付预期放在一起，让这款旗舰车型在英国的产品安排变得更加清楚。[EV Powered]

L03则提供另一种选择，英国起价为32,990英镑。入门纯电版续航为276英里，后驱长续航版为323英里。小鹏英国负责人同时确认将推出增程版，配备37.25千瓦时电池，纯电续航133英里、WLTP综合续航631英里。这些数字对应不同版本及不同衡量范围，综合续航不能理解成仅依靠电池的行驶能力。[EV Powered]

两项发布放在一起看，显示小鹏正通过不同车型尺寸和动力形式增加英国市场的选择。这是产品策略层面的变化，商业效果仍需实际结果验证。交付时间安排和各版本后续接受程度，会比单看起售价更有说明力。其中，G9L预计2027年2月首批交付，是接下来可以持续核对的节点。

捷克订购将产品阵容与当地渠道衔接

在布拉格e-Salon展会上，小鹏展示了L03和G9L。L03纯电版在捷克开启订购，价格为859,900至1,139,900捷克克朗，增程版为939,900捷克克朗，预计11月中旬开始向当地客户交付。G9L计划2027年进入捷克市场。小鹏目前在当地有八个销售网点，并准备再增加两个。[CarNewsChina]

捷克消息为欧洲产品扩充增加了渠道层面的内容。提供车型、开启订购、增加销售网点互相衔接，但分别代表业务拓展的不同阶段。英国与捷克消息使用不同货币，也涉及各自的市场规格，因此仅凭标价不足以直接比较相对购买负担。能够确认的是，同一产品系列正在分别为不同市场的客户做好销售准备。

值得关注

下一步更有价值的证据将来自交付执行：捷克L03是否按预计在11月中旬开始交付，英国G9L是否在2027年2月如期到达客户手中。腾势印度业务也需要在上市和经销商安排之外增加销量或交付信息，才能更充分评估。贯穿今天几条新闻的分析问题是，已经公布的产品和渠道安排，将如何转化为持续的当地业务。
