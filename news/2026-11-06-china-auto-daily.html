Jaecoo 7 Tops UK Sales Again as BYD Passes 10,000 in Australia and Yangwang Heads to Abu DhabiJaecoo 7再登英国销量榜首，比亚迪澳洲月销破万，仰望将在阿布扎比首发

Chery's Jaecoo 7 was Britain's best-selling car for a second month with 3,412 registrations, BYD sold 10,025 cars in Australia in October, and Geely hit a record ninth place there.奇瑞旗下Jaecoo 7以3,412辆连续第二个月成为英国最畅销车型，比亚迪10月在澳大利亚售出10,025辆，吉利排名升至创纪录的第9。

By TopChinaCar Editorial作者：TopChinaCar 编辑部 · Published November 6, 2026发布于 2026-11-06 · Compiled from the primary sources cited below基于文末所列一手信源编写

October registration data from two of the world's most competitive right-hand-drive markets point the same way: Chinese brands are no longer niche challengers in mature markets. In the UK, Chery's Jaecoo 7 topped the model ranking for a second straight month, while SAIC's MG, BYD and Leapmotor all posted strong year-on-year gains. In Australia, BYD sold more than 10,000 cars for the second time and Geely climbed to its best-ever ranking. South Africa, meanwhile, recorded its biggest sales month ever, with Chery and Jetour among the brands that powered it.

Chery's Multi-Brand Push Pays Off in Britain

Preliminary SMMT data show the Jaecoo 7 registered 3,412 units in the UK in October 2026, holding the top spot among all models for a second month, with the Jaecoo 5 tenth on 2,171. At brand level, Jaecoo registered 6,089 cars (+113.6% year on year, a 4.0% share), Omoda 3,137 (+49.2%) and the Chery brand itself 3,707 (+193.5%) [SMMT].

The significance lies in the breadth of the result. Chery is not relying on one hit model: three separate brands under its umbrella are each growing quickly in the UK. That spreads risk and lets the group cover different price points and buyer profiles without a single nameplate carrying the whole load.

The same playbook is visible in South Africa. NAAMSA data compiled by Best Selling Cars Blog show the market hit a record 61,890 sales in October 2026. The Chery brand sold 2,754 (+24.6%) to rank seventh and Jetour 2,102 (+145.6%) to rank tenth, while the Chery Q, launched in September, was the country's best-selling EV with 438 units [Best Selling Cars Blog].

BYD: Volume in Australia, Growth in the UK, Luxury in the Gulf

According to Best Selling Cars Blog's tally of Australia's October 2026 sales, BYD sold 10,025 cars (+153.2%), ranking second among brands with a 9.3% share, its second month above 10,000. The Sealion 7 placed fifth with 2,524 sales (+88.2%) and the Shark 6 ute ninth with 1,843 (+72.2%) [Best Selling Cars Blog]. A ute in the top ten matters: it shows BYD competing in one of Australia's most important segments, not only with electric SUVs.

In the UK, BYD registered 6,065 cars in October (+73.3%, a 4.0% share), taking its Jan–Oct total to 74,470 (+90.5%, a 3.9% share) [SMMT].

At the top end, BYD released a teaser on November 5 confirming that Yangwang's Altior flagship sedan (Dingyao in China) will debut in Abu Dhabi on November 8, taking aim at the Rolls-Royce Ghost and Mercedes-Maybach S-Class. It carries a roof-mounted lidar for BYD's God's Eye driver assistance; earlier reports put its length at about 5,500 mm and its wheelbase at about 3,350 mm, though official specifications have not been released [CarNewsChina]. Choosing the Gulf for the debut signals where BYD sees early demand for an ultra-luxury Chinese car.

BYD's battery business is expanding abroad too. SNE Research data show BYD installed 45.9 GWh of EV batteries outside China in Jan–Sep 2026 (+66.8%), lifting its share to 10.8% from 8.0% a year earlier and ranking third [CnEVPost].

MG and Leapmotor Accelerate in the UK

SAIC's MG registered 7,837 cars in the UK in October (+85.1%, a 5.1% share), the highest of the Chinese brands covered today, with the MG HS eighth among models on 2,393. Its Jan–Oct total reached 86,767 (+24.6%, a 4.6% share). Leapmotor registered 1,773 cars (+107.1%, a 1.2% share) [SMMT]. Leapmotor's overseas business runs through Leapmotor International, its joint venture with Stellantis, which gives it access to an established dealer network.

Geely Reaches a Record Ranking in Australia

The Geely brand sold 4,365 cars in Australia in October (+520%), reaching a record ninth place, with the EX5 tenth among models on 1,789 (+421.6%). Its Zeekr brand sold 1,874 (+767.6%), ranking 18th [Best Selling Cars Blog]. Like Chery, Geely is winning with several brands at once rather than a single flagship.

What to Watch

The Yangwang Altior's Abu Dhabi debut on November 8 will show how BYD prices and positions its most expensive car outside China. Next, watch whether the UK momentum of Jaecoo, MG and BYD holds into year-end, and whether the official CAAM export data for October, due in mid-November, confirm the strength seen in these registration figures.

10月海外上牌数据陆续出炉，两个竞争最激烈的右舵市场传递出同一个信号：在成熟市场，中国品牌已经不再是边缘挑战者。在英国，奇瑞旗下Jaecoo 7连续第二个月登顶单一车型榜，上汽MG、比亚迪和零跑也都同比大增；在澳大利亚，比亚迪第二次月销破万，吉利排名创下历史新高。与此同时，南非10月车市创下单月历史纪录，奇瑞和捷途是重要推手。

奇瑞：多品牌打法在英国见效

据英国汽车制造商与贸易商协会（SMMT）初步数据，2026年10月Jaecoo 7在英国上牌3,412辆，连续第二个月位居单一车型榜首，Jaecoo 5以2,171辆排第10。品牌层面，Jaecoo当月上牌6,089辆（同比+113.6%，份额4.0%），Omoda上牌3,137辆（同比+49.2%），奇瑞品牌上牌3,707辆（同比+193.5%）[SMMT]。

这组数据的意义在于广度。奇瑞并不依赖单一爆款，旗下三个品牌在英国同时快速增长，既分散了风险，也能覆盖不同价位和人群，不必让某一款车型独自扛起销量。

同样的打法也出现在南非。据NAAMSA数据（Best Selling Cars Blog整理），南非2026年10月新车销量61,890辆，创单月历史新高。奇瑞品牌售出2,754辆（同比+24.6%）排第7，捷途售出2,102辆（同比+145.6%）排第10；9月上市的纯电车Chery Q以438辆成为当月南非最畅销纯电车[Best Selling Cars Blog]。

比亚迪：澳洲走量、英国增长、中东冲高端

据Best Selling Cars Blog整理的澳大利亚2026年10月销量，比亚迪当月售出10,025辆（同比+153.2%），在品牌榜排名第二、份额9.3%，这是其第二个销量过万的月份。Sealion 7售出2,524辆（同比+88.2%）排第5，Shark 6皮卡售出1,843辆（同比+72.2%）排第9[Best Selling Cars Blog]。皮卡进入车型前十很关键，说明比亚迪在澳大利亚最重要的细分市场之一也有了竞争力，而不只是靠纯电SUV。

在英国，比亚迪10月上牌6,065辆（同比+73.3%，份额4.0%），1–10月累计74,470辆（同比+90.5%，份额3.9%）[SMMT]。

高端方面，比亚迪11月5日发布预告视频，确认仰望旗舰轿车Altior（国内名鼎耀）将于11月8日在阿布扎比首发，对标劳斯莱斯古思特和迈巴赫S级。该车配备车顶激光雷达，搭载比亚迪天神之眼辅助驾驶；据此前报道，车长约5,500毫米、轴距约3,350毫米，官方尚未公布参数[CarNewsChina]。选择海湾地区首发，说明比亚迪看好当地对超豪华中国车的早期需求。

电池业务同样在海外扩张。据SNE Research数据，2026年1–9月比亚迪在中国以外市场的动力电池装机量为45.9GWh（同比+66.8%），份额由上年同期的8.0%升至10.8%，排名第三[CnEVPost]。

上汽MG与零跑：英国加速

上汽旗下MG 10月在英国上牌7,837辆（同比+85.1%，份额5.1%），在今天统计的中国品牌中最高，MG HS以2,393辆排在单一车型榜第8；1–10月累计86,767辆（同比+24.6%，份额4.6%）。零跑当月上牌1,773辆（同比+107.1%，份额1.2%）[SMMT]。零跑的海外业务由其与Stellantis的合资公司零跑国际负责，可以借助成熟的经销渠道。

吉利：澳大利亚排名创新高

吉利品牌10月在澳大利亚售出4,365辆（同比+520%），排名升至创纪录的第9，EX5售出1,789辆（同比+421.6%）排在车型榜第10；旗下极氪售出1,874辆（同比+767.6%），排第18[Best Selling Cars Blog]。和奇瑞一样，吉利也是靠多个品牌同时发力，而不是只押注一款旗舰。

值得关注

11月8日仰望Altior在阿布扎比首发，将显示比亚迪如何在海外为其最贵的车型定价和定位。接下来，还要看Jaecoo、MG和比亚迪在英国的势头能否延续到年底，以及中汽协预计11月中旬公布的10月出口数据，能否印证这些上牌数据所体现的强势。
