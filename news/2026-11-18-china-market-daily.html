China Updates Vehicle Approvals and Tax Relief Lists as CATL Secures a Casing Supply Deal工信部更新车型准入和税惠目录，宁德时代签署电池壳配套协议

China issued batch 412 of vehicle approvals and two tax relief lists. CATL’s casing agreements cover Suzhou and Quanzhou, and two new SUVs went on sale.工信部公布第412批车辆准入和两份税惠目录。宁德时代的电池壳配套协议涉及苏州和泉州，东风猛士与捷途也在国内推出新款SUV。

By TopChinaCar Editorial作者：TopChinaCar 编辑部 · Published November 18, 2026发布于 2026-11-18 · Compiled from the primary sources cited below基于文末所列一手信源编写

China’s industry ministry published new vehicle approvals and tax relief lists, while battery maker CATL signed supply agreements covering battery casing production in Suzhou and Quanzhou.

BMW told China’s Ministry of Commerce it intends to invest more in the country, expand local research and development, and increase supply-chain localization. Two Chinese brands, meanwhile, put new SUV offerings on the domestic market. Dongfeng M-Hero added a high-specification plug-in hybrid off-road trim, while Chery’s Jetour launched the X70M with three configurations. The BMW statement gives a direction for its China operations. The two launches give buyers specific products and official list prices.

New vehicle approvals and tax relief lists

On November 16, the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology released batch 412 of approved road motor vehicle manufacturers and products. The same announcement published batch 91 of the vehicle and vessel tax relief list and batch 36 of the new energy vehicle purchase tax relief list. The approvals document records the ministry’s licensing decisions. The two tax lists identify models eligible for relief under separate measures. [MIIT]

CATL names two sites for battery casing supply

Suzhou SLAC said on November 17 that its controlled subsidiary Fenyi Hejun had signed agreements with CATL covering investment in the production of battery casings and related components in Suzhou, Jiangsu, and Quanzhou, Fujian. Capacity at the two sites is to be prioritized for CATL. The agreements run through December 31, 2028, with execution subject to individual orders or procurement contracts. [Suzhou SLAC filing]

BMW puts local sourcing alongside R&D

Chinese Vice Minister of Commerce Ling Ji met Milan Nedeljković, chairman of BMW Group’s board of management, in Beijing on November 16. According to the ministry’s account of the meeting, Nedeljković said BMW would expand both investment and research and development in China and would increase supply-chain localization. The statement matters to suppliers because it explicitly puts local sourcing alongside the company’s plans for engineering and investment. [Ministry of Commerce]

The ministry’s readout gave neither an investment amount nor a timetable. Those details will determine what the announcement means for the suppliers and development teams involved. For now, the stated intention is the concrete development from the meeting. It applies to BMW Group’s China operations, and the readout leaves the scale and timing of implementation open.

Two SUV launches at different price points

Dongfeng’s M-Hero launched the M817 Ultimate in China on November 17 with a list price of RMB 519,900. The Ultimate adds a plug-in hybrid off-road configuration to the M817 range. Dedicated off-road tires and a suspension lift fitted at the factory distinguish the trim. These features define what Dongfeng is adding to the range and show where the new version differs in its hardware. [IT Home — M-Hero launch]

Jetour took a different approach with the new X70M, which also launched in China on November 17. The Chery brand offered three trims at list prices from RMB 65,900 to RMB 80,900. All three use a 1.5-liter turbocharged engine. The entry model comes with a manual gearbox, and the other two have dual-clutch transmissions. The gearbox choice is one of the distinctions Jetour uses to structure the new range, with the manual retained at the lowest list price. [IT Home — Jetour launch]

These prices describe two specific offerings in the domestic SUV market. One is the list price of a single off-road trim within the M817 range. The other is a range of list prices for three X70M trims. Both sets of figures are official list prices published for the China market at launch. Their published configurations show how the brands have structured their products, while the launch reports leave sales volumes and buyer uptake open.

What to Watch

For the casing agreements, individual orders or procurement contracts will show how the supply arrangements move into execution. For BMW, the next details to look for are the size and timing of any announced China investment and the scope of the local sourcing work. For M-Hero and Jetour, deliveries or sales broken out by version would show how the new offerings are received.

新一批车辆准入和税惠目录

11月16日，工信部公布第412批获许可的道路机动车辆生产企业及产品，并同步发布第91批车船税减免车型目录和第36批新能源汽车购置税减免车型目录。车辆准入公告记录工信部的许可结果，两份税惠目录分别列出适用各自税收措施的车型。[工信部]

宁德时代电池壳供应涉及两个基地

斯莱克11月17日公告称，控股子公司分宜和骏与宁德时代签署了配套供应协议，涉及江苏苏州、福建泉州的电池壳体等产品投资生产。两地相关产能优先供应宁德时代。协议有效期至2028年12月31日，具体执行以订单或采购合同为准。[斯莱克公告]

宝马将本地采购与在华研发并列

11月16日，商务部副部长凌激在北京会见宝马集团董事长聂科维。根据商务部发布的会晤通报，聂科维表示，宝马将扩大在华投资和研发，并进一步提高供应链本地化水平。对供应商而言，值得关注的是，宝马把本地采购与研发和投资同时列入在华业务计划。[商务部]

商务部通报没有披露投资金额或实施时间表。落实的规模和节奏，将决定这番表态对相关供应商和研发团队的具体影响。目前可以确认的是，宝马集团表达了扩大在华业务投入的意向，具体如何实施仍有待披露。

两款SUV覆盖不同的价格区间

11月17日，东风猛士M817 Ultimate在中国上市，官方指导价51.99万元。这款插电混动越野SUV为M817车系增加了一个越野取向的版本，配备专用越野轮胎，并在出厂时提高了底盘离地间隙。轮胎和底盘是这次新增版本在硬件上的主要看点，也说明了东风猛士如何在原有车系中增加一种选择。[IT之家·猛士上市报道]

同在11月17日，奇瑞旗下捷途推出全新X70M。该车三个配置的官方指导价为6.59万至8.09万元，均采用1.5T发动机。入门版配手动变速箱，其余两款使用双离合变速箱。变速箱是这三款配置之间的明确差别之一，捷途把手动挡保留在指导价最低的版本。[IT之家·捷途上市报道]

两家品牌公布的价格对应不同范围。51.99万元是M817车系中一个越野版本的官方指导价，6.59万至8.09万元则覆盖全新X70M的三个版本。两组数字均为中国市场上市时公布的官方指导价。配置和指导价反映了两家品牌如何安排各自的产品梯度，上市信息尚未给出各版本的销量或市场接受度。

值得关注

电池壳配套协议如何落实，可关注双方后续订单或采购合同。宝马后续如披露在华投资的规模、时间表和本地采购范围，将更容易判断本次表态如何落到具体项目。对东风猛士和捷途而言，分版本的交付量或销量可以进一步说明新车型的市场反馈。
