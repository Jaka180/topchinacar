BYD launched a second-generation Seal 07 with lower entry prices than the model it replaces, and a Jiemian investigation described falling retail prices and a shrinking dealer network at Volvo Cars in China. Zeekr, Jetour, Smart and XPeng added or ramped up new models, two foreign-brand joint ventures surfaced new China-built cars in regulatory filings, and lidar maker RoboSense started a fully automated production line.

Pricing: BYD Resets the Seal 07, Volvo Dealers Discount

BYD launched the second-generation Seal 07 sedan in China on November 5. The plug-in hybrid version comes in two trims at 139,900 to 149,900 yuan, an entry price 9,900 yuan below the outgoing DM-i model. The all-electric version's three trims cost 149,900 to 172,900 yuan, which BYD's pricing puts 20,000 yuan below the entry price of the EV launched in March 2026. The electric car is rated at 775 km on China's CLTC test cycle and uses BYD's second-generation Blade Battery with flash charging. The launch comes as the wider Seal family sold 46,834 units in October, down 19.32% year on year [CnEVPost].

Volvo Cars, which is controlled by Zhejiang Geely Holding, is under visible pressure in China. Jiemian reported on November 4 that several Beijing dealers were quoting on-the-road prices below 200,000 yuan for the two-wheel-drive XC70 plug-in hybrid, with the S90 down to about 230,000 yuan. According to the report, Volvo moved dealer sales assessments from annual to quarterly and then to monthly this year. It also said pre-sales at Volvo's Beijing Xidan Joy City flagship store had been cancelled and that the brand's Guangzhou dealers had been cut from 6 to 3. Volvo's Greater China deliveries were 20,300 in the third quarter, down 40.6% year on year, and the company plans China-only models built on Geely's technology platform from around mid-2027 [Jiemian]. These dealer quotations are a reporter's market survey, not an official price list.

New Models: Zeekr, Jetour, Smart and XPeng

Geely's premium EV brand Zeekr opened pre-sales for the 2027 Zeekr 7X on November 5 at 229,800 yuan, unchanged from the previous model. The update makes a 900-volt architecture standard and adds an 85-kWh battery option between the existing packs. The 7X is an important volume model for Zeekr: it delivered 86,806 units in January to September, or 30.10% of the brand's deliveries in that period [CnEVPost].

Chery's Jetour brand launched the T1 lineup on November 4 with petrol, all-wheel-drive and C-DM plug-in hybrid versions. Listed prices run from 113,900 to 159,900 yuan, with a promotional start of 89,900 yuan under limited-time trade-in conditions; the hybrid offers 150 km of CLTC electric range. Jetour said its boxy SUV family has passed 840,000 cumulative global sales [CarNewsChina].

Smart, the joint venture brand of Mercedes-Benz and Geely Auto, launched the #2 Premium in China at a limited-time 99,900 yuan; the car is built by a Geely-owned factory [CnEVPost]. XPeng, meanwhile, started deliveries of its Mona L05 SUV on November 4. Chairman He Xiaopeng set a first-month delivery target of 10,000 units, and the company said the model runs on a new dedicated production line [CnEVPost].

Joint Ventures in Regulatory Filings

China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (MIIT) publishes vehicle specifications before cars can be sold; a filing is not a launch. On November 4, MIIT published details of the Lincoln Aviator EX8, a plug-in hybrid SUV built in Chongqing by Changan Ford, the joint venture between Changan and Ford. It uses a Changan-developed 1.5-liter turbo engine, an LFP battery from the Changan-CATL joint venture and a roof-mounted lidar, on a 3,010 mm wheelbase. The report said it will debut at the 2026 Guangzhou Auto Show and launch in 2027 [CarNewsChina].

Filings also show that the next-generation BMW 3 Series to be made by BMW Brilliance, BMW's Chinese joint venture, will have a 2,970 mm wheelbase, 100 mm longer than the global car, with two 2.0-liter petrol versions. It is expected in 2027 [CarNewsChina].

Supply Chain: Lidar Automation and Charging Networks

RoboSense announced on November 4 that a fully automated digital lidar production line had started operating at its Honghualing base in Shenzhen, with annual capacity of 500,000 units per line and a 99% automation rate. The company calls it the world's first such line [Gasgoo].

Chery Green Energy, Chery Group's energy platform, signed a strategic agreement with Didi Charging to link charging networks, jointly build sites and pilot integrated solar-storage-charging stations in key cities. Investment and station numbers were not disclosed [Gasgoo].

What to Watch

  • Whether XPeng reaches its 10,000-unit first-month target for the Mona L05.
  • The Lincoln Aviator EX8's debut at the Guangzhou Auto Show, and Volvo's planned China-only models from around mid-2027.

新车：极氪、捷途、smart与小鹏

合资企业新车现身工信部申报

工信部申报公示是新车上市销售前的准入程序，申报不等于上市。工信部11月4日公示了长安福特在重庆生产的林肯Aviator EX8插电混动SUV。该车搭载长安自研1.5T发动机，磷酸铁锂电池由长安与宁德时代的合资公司供应，轴距3,010毫米，车顶配备激光雷达。报道称该车将在2026广州车展亮相、2027年上市 [CarNewsChina]。

申报信息还显示，宝马在华合资企业华晨宝马将生产的新一代宝马3系轴距比全球版加长100毫米至2,970毫米，提供两款2.0T汽油版本，预计2027年在国内上市 [CarNewsChina]。
