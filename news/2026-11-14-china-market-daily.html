CATL Sets Swap Network Target as Nissan and Chinese Brands Roll Out New SUVs宁德时代明确换电目标，日产与自主品牌推进SUV产品更新

CATL targets 6,000 passenger-car swap stations in 2027, while Dongfeng Nissan opens NX7 pre-sales and Aito, Chery and BYD advance SUV launches and updates in China.宁德时代提出2027年乘用车换电站达到6000座的目标；东风日产开启NX7预售，问界、奇瑞及比亚迪推进中国市场SUV上市、更新与上市时间安排。

By TopChinaCar Editorial作者：TopChinaCar 编辑部 · Published November 14, 2026发布于 2026-11-14 · Compiled from the primary sources cited below基于文末所列一手信源编写

China's latest vehicle announcements span battery swapping, plug-in hybrids and battery-electric SUVs. CATL outlined a network construction target, while Dongfeng Nissan and Chinese brands disclosed prices, equipment changes and launch dates. For international readers, the central distinction is between infrastructure plans, vehicles already launched and models still at the pre-sale stage. AI-generated.

Battery swapping: a network target and a compatible vehicle

CATL said on November 13 that it aims to bring its Choco passenger-car battery swap network to 6,000 stations across 297 Chinese cities in 2027. The planned composition is 5,000 urban sites and 1,000 highway sites. These figures describe a future construction target; they do not represent a completed network. Arcfox also launched a compatible Beta T1 swap version, and CATL introduced battery-health checks and repair and replacement services. Together, the announcements cover both network availability and the vehicles and services using it. [CnEVPost]

Joint-venture and domestic-brand product updates

Dongfeng Nissan, the joint-venture operating entity rather than the Dongfeng parent group, opened NX7 plug-in hybrid SUV pre-sales on November 13. Three trims are offered at RMB 129,900–149,900, ahead of an official launch scheduled for November 27. All use a 40.9-kWh CATL battery and quote 320 km of CLTC electric range. The commercial milestone is the opening of pre-sales: the announced prices should not be treated as final launch pricing. [CnEVPost]

Aito's updated M8, by contrast, has officially launched. Its ten variants span extended-range and battery-electric powertrains, five- and six-seat layouts, and list prices of RMB 379,800–469,800. All switch to an 800V architecture and include Huawei Qiankun ADS 5 driver assistance. The BEV versions quote up to 830 km of CLTC range. This is a product and equipment update across the lineup; the driver-assistance system remains described here as assistance. [CnEVPost]

Chery launched the six-seat 2027 Fulwin T11 extended-range SUV on November 12, according to reporting published the following day. Four trims retain official prices of RMB 189,900–249,900. Falcon 700 driver assistance, with LiDAR and urban navigation assistance, is standard on three trims and optional on the entry version. The concrete change is therefore additional assistance capability within an unchanged official price range. [CarNewsChina]

What to Watch

BYD opened third-generation Tang EV pre-sales on November 13 at RMB 225,000, RMB 240,000 and RMB 260,000. The five-seat SUV offers quoted CLTC ranges of 850 km for its two rear-wheel-drive versions and 770 km for its all-wheel-drive version. Pre-sales are the newly confirmed step; the item does not establish a completed official launch. [CarNewsChina]

Separately, Fang Cheng Bao confirmed November 19 for the Tai 9's official launch, with vehicle purchase agreement signing opening on launch day. Display vehicles had reached seven Chinese cities, including Shanghai, Beijing, Chengdu and Hangzhou. Final prices were not disclosed in that announcement. The next stated milestone is therefore the official launch and opening of purchase agreement signing, distinct from the earlier introduction of the model. [CnEVPost]

These announcements cover different commercial stages. The NX7 and Tang figures are pre-sale offers, the M8 and T11 updates are launches, and the Tai 9 has a specified future launch date. Keeping those stages separate makes the daily record useful for tracking execution without treating a price announcement or a network target as a sales result.

换电：网络目标与兼容车型同步推进

宁德时代11月13日公布，计划在2027年将巧克力乘用车换电网络扩展至6000座站点，覆盖297座中国城市，其中包括5000座城市站和1000座高速站。这组数字描述的是未来建设目标，并非已经建成的网络规模。同日，极狐推出采用巧克力换电电池的贝塔T1换电版，宁德时代还发布电池健康检测及维修更换服务。两项进展分别涉及网络可用性，以及接入网络的车型与配套服务；网络目标的提出本身不能代替实际建设进度。[CnEVPost]

合资与自主品牌推进产品更新

东风日产11月13日开启NX7插混SUV预售，三款配置的预售价为12.99万至14.99万元，正式上市安排在11月27日。全系采用40.9千瓦时宁德时代电池，公布的CLTC纯电续航为320公里。对国际读者而言，这条新闻的经营主体是东风日产这一合资企业，而非东风母集团全部业务。本次商业进展是开启预售，相关价格也应保留“预售”限定，不能直接视为最终上市价格。[CnEVPost]

问界更新版M8则已正式上市，十款配置涵盖增程和纯电两种动力、五座与六座布局，指导价为37.98万至46.98万元。全系转用800V架构，并标配华为乾崑ADS 5辅助驾驶系统，纯电版本公布的CLTC续航最高为830公里。此次新闻的核心是产品和配置在全系层面的更新；对于相关驾驶功能，本文仍沿用辅助驾驶表述，不将其等同于无需驾驶员负责的自动驾驶。[CnEVPost]

奇瑞在11月12日推出2027款风云T11六座增程SUV，相关报道于次日发布。四款配置的官方指导价仍为18.99万至24.99万元，猎鹰700辅助驾驶系统在三款配置上标配，入门款可选装，增加激光雷达和城区领航辅助功能。因此，这次可确认的产品变化是在官方价格区间不变的情况下增加辅助驾驶能力，不能把其他优惠价格混同为指导价调整。[CarNewsChina]

值得关注

比亚迪第三代唐纯电SUV于11月13日开启预售，三款配置分别为22.5万、24万及26万元。新车采用五座布局，两款后驱版公布的CLTC续航均为850公里，四驱版为770公里。本次新确认的阶段是预售启动，今日条目并未将其认定为已经完成正式上市。价格与续航需按具体配置对应，不能将不同版本的数据组合为同一款车型的指标。[CarNewsChina]

另一项明确的时间安排来自方程豹：钛9定于11月19日正式上市，上市当天开放购车协议签署。展车已进入包括上海、北京、成都及杭州在内的七座中国城市，本次公告尚未披露正式售价。下一项已公布的执行节点是正式上市与开放购车协议签署，与此前车型已亮相的事实应分开理解；本文不据此推算订单、销量或收入表现。[CnEVPost]

这些公告对应不同的商业阶段：NX7与唐公布的是预售信息，M8与T11更新对应车型上市，钛9则给出未来的上市日期。将阶段分别记录，才能跟踪实际执行，避免把价格公告或网络建设目标直接当成销售结果。
