Leapmotor Signs Opel SUV Development Deal as WeRide Expands in the Middle East零跑签约联合开发欧宝SUV，文远知行扩张中东车队

Leapmotor and Opel plan to build a jointly developed SUV in Spain, with sales expected in 2028. WeRide reported a Middle East fleet of about 600 vehicles.零跑与欧宝签署紧凑型SUV联合开发协议，新车拟在西班牙生产，预计2028年开售。文远知行披露中东车队已增至约600辆。

By TopChinaCar Editorial作者：TopChinaCar 编辑部 · Published November 21, 2026发布于 2026-11-21 · Compiled from the primary sources cited below基于文末所列一手信源编写

Leapmotor’s formal agreement to develop an Opel SUV for production in Spain gives the partnership a specific product and factory to track. The deal comes as the Chinese carmaker reports rapid export growth and autonomous-driving company WeRide discloses the size of its Middle East fleet. Together, the announcements show what to measure as Chinese automotive businesses expand abroad, from engineering and production milestones to service coverage and revenue.

Leapmotor’s Opel deal puts development and production on the same timetable

Leapmotor and Opel have signed a formal agreement to co-develop a compact SUV for Opel. Engineering teams in Rüsselsheim and Hangzhou are working on the vehicle, which will be built at Opel’s plant in Zaragoza, Spain. Sales are expected to start in 2028. Leapmotor’s B10 had already entered mass production in Zaragoza. The new agreement extends the relationship to a vehicle for Opel and gives readers specific engineering locations, a factory and an expected sales start to follow. [Stellantis Media]

In a separate filing, Leapmotor reported third-quarter exports of 63,278 vehicles, up 263.7% year on year and equivalent to 20.4% of its quarterly sales. Exports in January–October exceeded 180,000, about three times the company’s full-year 2025 volume. The quarterly figures show how quickly shipments are growing. Progress on the Opel vehicle will be visible through a different set of milestones, from development to production and the opening of sales. [HKEX]

WeRide’s Middle East growth shows up in fleet size and revenue

WeRide says its Middle East fleet has grown to about 600 vehicles. The company aims to cover the core urban areas of Abu Dhabi and Dubai by year-end. Fleet size is a measure of the vehicles available for deployment, while coverage determines where a rider can book and complete a trip. Tracking both gives a clearer picture of what the expansion could mean for potential passengers. [WeRide IR]

The company reported third-quarter overseas revenue of RMB 146.9 million, up 171.5% year on year and equal to 45.7% of total revenue. Its quarterly robotaxi revenue topped RMB 100 million for the first time, with overseas markets contributing more than 90%. The first set of figures describes WeRide’s overseas business across the company. The second describes where revenue for one service comes from, and gives readers a way to track the commercial side of deployments alongside fleet and coverage updates. [WeRide IR]

BYD’s hybrid team adapts to different markets

At a Gasgoo summit, BYD electrification platform development director Lu Guoxiang said the company’s hybrid team had shifted its focus overseas to address Europe’s Euro 6 and Euro 7 emissions requirements. He also pointed to the Song Pro in Brazil, where BYD deployed a flex-fuel powertrain capable of running on pure ethanol. The examples illustrate different local engineering requirements. European emissions standards shape development for that market, while fuel compatibility is a separate consideration for a model sold in Brazil. [Gasgoo]

What to Watch

In Zaragoza, the milestones to follow are how the jointly developed Opel SUV moves into production and whether sales start in 2028 as expected. In the Middle East, watch whether WeRide delivers its planned coverage in the core areas of Abu Dhabi and Dubai and how subsequent reports describe the revenue that overseas robotaxi operations generate. Both will give a more concrete view of how the companies turn stated plans into products and services available to customers.

零跑与欧宝签下联合开发协议，新车计划在西班牙生产。同一份日报里，零跑披露了出口增长，文远知行则披露了海外业务的收入和中东车队规模。把几项消息放在一起看，能看到中国汽车企业走出去之后，如何把产品开发、生产和运营落到当地。

零跑把联合开发和生产放在同一个项目里

零跑与欧宝已正式签署紧凑型 SUV 联合开发协议。德国吕塞尔斯海姆和中国杭州的项目团队共同推进研发，车辆将在西班牙萨拉戈萨的欧宝工厂生产，预计 2028 年开售。此前，零跑 B10 已在萨拉戈萨量产。新的协议把合作推进到为欧宝开发一款车，研发地点、生产工厂和开售预期都已经明确。 [Stellantis Media]

零跑同日披露，三季度出口 63,278 辆，同比增长 263.7%，占当季总销量的 20.4%；1–10 月累计出口超过 18 万辆，约为 2025 年全年的 3 倍。出口数字衡量从既有供应体系发往其他市场的规模。与欧宝的项目需要按开发、生产和开售节点来观察。把两个口径分开，才能在出口继续增长时，也看到本地生产项目到底推进到了哪一步。 [HKEX]

文远知行把中东车队做到了约 600 辆

文远知行披露，中东车队已增至约 600 辆，公司计划在年底前覆盖阿布扎比和迪拜的核心城区。对 Robotaxi 运营来说，车队规模回答的是有多少车可部署，覆盖范围回答的是乘客在哪里能叫到车、哪些行程能走完。把两个指标放在一起，才有办法判断增加的车能给当地乘客带来多少实际变化。 [WeRide IR]

公司第三季度海外收入为人民币 1.469 亿元，同比增长 171.5%，占总收入的 45.7%。同期 Robotaxi 收入首次超过人民币 1 亿元，其中海外市场贡献逾 90%。一组数字看的是全部海外业务占公司收入的比重，另一组数字看的是 Robotaxi 这项业务的收入从哪里来。两种口径放在一起，也解释了为什么在跟踪新市场时，除了看车队和覆盖，还要看公司后续披露的收入。 [WeRide IR]

比亚迪把混动研发重心转向海外

在盖世汽车主办的峰会上，比亚迪汽车新技术研究院电动化平台开发中心主任卢国祥说，混动团队已将工作重心转向海外，以应对欧洲 Euro 6 和 Euro 7 排放标准的要求。他同时举了巴西宋 Pro 的例子，这款车在当地采用可使用纯乙醇的灵活燃料动力系统。 这两处市场给研发团队提出了不同的问题。在欧洲，排放标准形成了明确的开发约束。在巴西，动力系统能否适应当地燃料，是产品落地时要解决的另一道题。对比亚迪而言，接下来值得看的是，研发重心的调整会通过哪些具体车型和动力配置体现出来。 [Gasgoo]

值得关注

在萨拉戈萨，接下来要看联合开发的欧宝 SUV 何时转入生产，能否如期在 2028 年开售。在中东，要看文远知行能否按计划覆盖阿布扎比和迪拜的核心城区，以及后续披露如何反映海外 Robotaxi 运营带来的收入。这些节点能帮助读者判断，已经宣布的计划如何变成当地客户可以购买的车型和使用的服务。
