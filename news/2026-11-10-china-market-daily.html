China Auto Retail Falls 27.1% as BMW Sets Launch Dates and Suppliers Advance Investment Plans中国乘用车零售降27.1%，宝马明确上市日程、供应商推进投资合作

Final CPCA figures put October passenger-car retail sales at 1.643 million. BMW sets China launch dates, WEY and Fang Cheng Bao open pre-sales or reservations, and suppliers disclose new plans.乘联分会正式数据将10月乘用车零售确定为164.3万辆。宝马明确中国上市日程，魏牌和方程豹推进预售或预订，汽车板投资及材料合作也有新披露。

China's latest auto briefing brings together a weaker domestic retail market, more specific product schedules and new supplier plans. Final October statistics provide a different measure from preliminary estimates and factory wholesale totals. The announcements also sit at different operating stages: a reservation opening, a delivery date, an investment plan and a regulatory notice each establish a different kind of progress. Domestic retail and factory wholesale tell different stories

The China Passenger Car Association (CPCA), which reports passenger-vehicle market statistics, released its final October monthly report on November 9. Domestic passenger-car retail sales were 1.643 million, down 27.1% year on year. Passenger new energy vehicle retail sales reached 1.109 million, down 13.7%, while their retail share was 67.5%. These final figures were below earlier preliminary estimates. They describe domestic passenger-car retail rather than all vehicles or factory sales including exports. [CnEVPost / CPCA]

Tesla illustrates the importance of that distinction. CnEVPost calculated October China retail sales of 8,817 vehicles from CPCA export data and the previously disclosed wholesale total. Shanghai exported 59,012 vehicles out of wholesale volume of 67,829. Xiaomi's newly disclosed figures likewise separate domestic retail of 50,123 from wholesale volume of 50,168; the destinations and uses of the difference were not disclosed. NIO's model breakdown shows another dimension of concentration: ES8 and ES9 deliveries together represented 85.25% of the main brand's October total, excluding Onvo and Firefly. [CnEVPost: Tesla] [CnEVPost: Xiaomi] [CnEVPost: NIO]

Product schedules become more concrete

BMW has set November 25 for the China launch of the Neue Klasse iX3, with customer deliveries and test drives beginning that day. The Neue Klasse i3 will launch and open firm orders at the Guangzhou Auto Show on November 27. Both will use nationwide uniform pricing. Dongfeng Nissan, Nissan's Chinese joint venture, separately confirmed November 13 for the NX7 plug-in hybrid SUV's debut and pre-sales, adding a precise date to the earlier schedule. Its announcement is a pre-sale timetable, not confirmation of a completed market launch. [CnEVPost: BMW] [CnEVPost: Dongfeng Nissan]

WEY opened second-generation Lanshan pre-sales with four variants, five- and six-seat layouts and a range of RMB 289,800–329,800. Fang Cheng Bao opened Tai 9 reservations without disclosing reservation pricing. The latter confirmed BYD's DMS hybrid platform, second-generation Blade Battery and flash charging, while specific charging performance remained undisclosed. Both developments expand the announced product offering, but neither reservation activity nor pre-sales establish completed customer deliveries. [Sina Auto] [CnEVPost: Fang Cheng Bao]

Supplier plans cover materials and a regulatory step

Valin Steel's automotive sheet subsidiary plans a third-phase project with estimated investment of RMB 3.147 billion including tax. Planned facilities include an 850,000-tonne annual JVD vacuum-coating line and a 1-million-tonne annual continuous annealing line. Separately, CATL disclosed cooperation with Chinalco covering new nonferrous materials, recycled metals and green-power and storage activities. Procurement value and supply volumes were not disclosed. These are investment and cooperation announcements, rather than evidence that the planned output or purchases have already occurred. [Yicai] [CATL]

China's State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) published a simplified merger-review notice for a new Tonly Technology–Yanfeng International joint venture. The notice period runs through November 18. The new fact is the regulatory public notice; the companies had already announced their cooperation, and the notice itself is not approval. [SAMR]

What to Watch

The next checks are whether the announced product dates lead to firm orders and deliveries, and whether supplier plans advance beyond their current disclosed stages. Market comparisons should continue to keep domestic retail, wholesale including exports and individual-brand delivery scope separate. 国内零售与工厂批发反映不同情况

发布乘用车市场统计的乘联分会于11月9日公布10月正式月报。国内乘用车零售为164.3万辆，同比下降27.1%；新能源乘用车零售110.9万辆，同比下降13.7%，零售渗透率为67.5%。正式值低于此前初步估计。这些数据反映国内乘用车零售，不是全部汽车销量，也不是包含出口的工厂销售。[CnEVPost / 乘联分会]

特斯拉的数据体现了区分口径的必要性。CnEVPost根据乘联分会出口数据和此前披露的批发总量，计算出特斯拉10月中国零售8,817辆；上海工厂批发67,829辆，其中出口59,012辆。小米新披露数据同样将国内零售50,123辆与批发50,168辆分开，差额车辆的目的地及用途未披露。蔚来的车型明细则反映另一种集中度：ES8与ES9合计占主品牌10月交付的85.25%，不包含乐道和萤火虫。[CnEVPost：特斯拉] [CnEVPost：小米] [CnEVPost：蔚来]

产品日程进一步具体化

宝马将新世代iX3的中国上市日期确定为11月25日，同日开启客户交付和试驾；新世代i3将于11月27日在广州车展上市并开放锁单，两款车型均采用全国统一定价。日产在中国的合资公司东风日产则明确，NX7插混SUV将于11月13日首发并开启预售，为此前公布的计划补充具体日期。这是预售时间表，不能视为已经完成正式上市。[CnEVPost：宝马] [CnEVPost：东风日产]

魏牌二代蓝山开启预售，推出四款车型，提供五座和六座布局，预售价28.98万至32.98万元。方程豹开启钛9预订，预订价格未披露；该车确认采用比亚迪DMS混动平台、第二代刀片电池及闪充技术，但具体充电性能尚未披露。这两项进展扩充了已公布的产品选择，不过预订和预售本身均不能证明已经完成客户交付。[新浪汽车] [CnEVPost：方程豹]

供应商计划涉及材料与监管进展

华菱钢铁汽车板子公司拟实施三期项目，总投资估算31.47亿元，包含税费。计划设施包括年产85万吨的JVD真空涂镀线和年产100万吨的连续退火线。宁德时代则披露与中铝集团合作，涵盖有色金属新材料、再生金属、绿电及储能等领域，未披露采购金额和供应量。这些分别是投资和合作公告，不能据此认定计划产量或采购已经实现。[第一财经] [宁德时代]

市场监管总局公示了通力科技与延锋国际新设合营企业的经营者集中简易案件，公示期至11月18日。此次新增事实是进入监管公示环节；双方此前已宣布合作，而公示本身不等于交易获批。[市场监管总局]
