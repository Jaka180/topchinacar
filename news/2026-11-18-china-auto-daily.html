Hongqi Weighs European Production as XPeng Eyes 100,000 Overseas Sales红旗评估欧洲生产方案，小鹏预计海外销量突破10万辆

Hongqi is evaluating European production partners as XPeng forecasts more than 100,000 overseas sales in 2026. Apollo Go says its fully driverless Dubai fleet has doubled.红旗正评估欧洲本地生产伙伴，小鹏预计2026年海外销量首次突破10万辆。百度同期披露，萝卜快跑迪拜全无人车队规模翻倍。

By TopChinaCar Editorial作者：TopChinaCar 编辑部 · Published November 18, 2026发布于 2026-11-18 · Compiled from the primary sources cited below基于文末所列一手信源编写

FAW’s Hongqi is weighing European manufacturing partners as XPeng forecasts its first year above 100,000 overseas sales. Those developments capture two sides of the same challenge: finding a way to build closer to customers and making sure there is a sales network capable of reaching them. The latest developments in the UAE and Australia show what that work can look like on the ground, from service coverage to hiring and local vehicle development.

Hongqi looks for a factory partner

FAW’s Hongqi has put local manufacturing on the agenda for Europe. Its international business chief told Reuters in Venice that Hongqi was assessing options including Stellantis and preferred to use an existing European automaker’s plant. Volkswagen China said it had held no talks with FAW on European production. The distinction matters because a potential partner, a signed manufacturing agreement and a plant ready to build vehicles are different milestones. [Reuters]

Hongqi already has 90 dealers across 27 European countries and regions and aims for more than 150 outlets by the end of 2027. The dealer target gives the European strategy a more concrete benchmark than the manufacturing discussions currently provide. It also shows how much execution lies outside the factory: Hongqi needs to expand the network that sells and supports the vehicles while it works out where to build them. [Reuters]

XPeng puts a number on overseas growth

XPeng expects overseas sales to exceed 100,000 vehicles for the first time in 2026, and it has set a fourth-quarter overseas sales target above 40,000. The quarterly figure gives readers an earlier point at which to gauge progress toward the full-year milestone. Both figures are forward-looking disclosures from the automaker’s earnings update. [XPeng]

The company expects its overseas sales network to exceed 1,000 stores in 2027 and sees that network supporting international sales at more than twice the 2026 level. That ties the forecast to a visible operating commitment. A larger network creates more opportunities for buyers to see a vehicle, ask about its features and arrange a purchase, while the sales figures will show whether the expanded reach translates into demand. [XPeng]

The UAE and Australia show how local operations take shape

The UAE offers another view of expansion. Baidu said Apollo Go had doubled the size of its fully driverless Dubai fleet and widened the commercial service area. Residents can request rides through Apollo Go and Uber. For a ride-hailing service, those are practical measures of availability: where the service operates, how many vehicles it can deploy, and how customers can book. [Baidu]

In Abu Dhabi, Geely opened its largest showroom in the Middle East with a dedicated hall for new-energy vehicles. The company told DubiCars it plans 20 product launches in the UAE in 2027, comprising eight combustion-engine and 12 new-energy products. The dedicated showroom space gives buyers a place to explore the electrified part of the range as that rollout proceeds. [DubiCars]

Australia illustrates two different stages of setting up local operations. Li Auto is recruiting a Melbourne-based head of channel and sales and has advertised product, software and aftersales roles covering Australia and New Zealand. GWM, meanwhile, plans at least 14 Australian launches in 2027 across new vehicles, powertrains and updates, and is seeking a Melbourne site for an R&D center focused on local tuning, accessories and technical training. [Carsales] [Carsales]

What to Watch

Watch whether Hongqi names a manufacturing partner and identifies a European production site. XPeng’s fourth-quarter overseas sales will provide the next measure of progress toward its full-year forecast. In both cases, subsequent disclosures can show how far today’s plans have moved toward operating milestones.

一汽红旗正在评估欧洲生产伙伴，小鹏预计今年海外销量首次突破10万辆。两条消息对应出海经营的两项工作：把生产布局向客户所在市场靠近，并建立能够触达客户的销售网络。阿联酋与澳大利亚的最新动态则展示了本地运营的具体环节，包括服务覆盖、招聘和车辆调校。

红旗寻找欧洲生产伙伴

一汽红旗正在评估欧洲本地生产方案。一汽国际业务负责人在威尼斯接受路透社采访时说，红旗考虑包括Stellantis在内的合作选项，倾向使用欧洲车企的现有工厂。大众中国表示，未与一汽就欧洲生产进行谈判。接下来值得关注的是，红旗会选择哪家合作伙伴，以及双方能否确定具体的生产安排。 [Reuters]

红旗已在欧洲27个国家和地区拥有90家经销商，计划在2027年底前增至150家以上。经销网点是衡量欧洲业务进展的一个具体指标。红旗需要一边扩大面向消费者的销售与服务网络，一边确定生产安排。 [Reuters]

小鹏明确海外销量预期

小鹏预计2026年海外销量将首次突破10万辆，并将第四季度海外销量目标定在4万辆以上。两项数字来自公司的业绩沟通，分别对应全年预期和季度目标。随着季度销量陆续披露，可以观察实际进度。 [XPeng]

小鹏还预计，2027年海外销售网络将超过1,000家门店，支撑国际销量较2026年翻逾一番。网点扩张和销量进展可以放在一起看。门店增加，消费者就有更多接触车辆、了解功能和安排购车的机会，实际销售则能反映覆盖范围扩大之后的市场需求。 [XPeng]

阿联酋与澳大利亚业务各有侧重

百度披露，萝卜快跑在迪拜扩大全无人商业运营范围，车队规模翻倍，乘客可通过萝卜快跑和Uber应用叫车。对于网约车服务，运营区域、可调度车辆和叫车入口都关系到乘客能否实际用上服务。 [Baidu]

在阿布扎比，吉利启用其中东最大展厅，设有独立的新能源车展区。吉利向DubiCars披露，2027年计划在阿联酋推出20款产品，包括8款燃油车和12款新能源车。随着产品陆续到店，独立展区将为当地消费者提供集中了解新能源车型的场所。 [DubiCars]

澳大利亚的两家公司处于不同的业务阶段。理想汽车在墨尔本招聘渠道与销售负责人，并招聘覆盖澳大利亚和新西兰的产品、软件与售后人员。长城计划2027年在澳大利亚至少推出14项新车型、动力总成及车型更新，同时在墨尔本为研发中心选址，拟开展本地调校、配件开发和技术培训。 [Carsales] [Carsales]

值得关注

接下来可关注红旗是否公布欧洲制造合作方与生产地点，以及小鹏第四季度海外销量能否支撑其全年预期。后续披露将帮助读者判断，本地制造和海外销售计划各自推进到了哪一步。
