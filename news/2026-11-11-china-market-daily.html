Domestic retail provides a common comparison

Rankings released by the China Passenger Car Association, or CPCA, on November 10 showed BYD selling 250,301 passenger NEVs at retail in China during October, for a 22.4% share. Geely Auto ranked second and Leapmotor retained third. SAIC-GM-Wuling rose to fifth. The relevant comparison is domestic passenger NEV retail: these figures should be kept separate from manufacturers' group sales and wholesale volumes that include exports. [CnEVPost / CPCA]

For international readers, the scope matters as much as the ordering. The ranking describes sales within a defined Chinese retail segment. It does not establish a ranking of total worldwide deliveries or combine every company's disclosures into a comparable measure. SAIC-GM-Wuling is identified as its own operating entity in this ranking, rather than being substituted with its parent group's name. Keeping those boundaries visible makes the newly published comparison useful without extending what it measures.

HELLA moves a steering sensor into production

FORVIA HELLA announced on November 10 that its latest angle sensor had entered series production in China, with deliveries to several Chinese EV manufacturers underway. Teams in Lippstadt and Shanghai jointly developed the component for steer-by-wire systems. The announcement did not disclose customer names, production volume or the investment involved. [FORVIA HELLA]

The operating milestone here is production and the start of deliveries. That is more specific than a technology introduction alone, but it does not provide a basis for estimating revenue or the scale of an individual customer program. The disclosed development arrangement also identifies the Shanghai team's involvement without implying that the announcement specifies a factory location.

Joint ventures update products and sales leadership

Lincoln launched an updated Z with five gasoline and full-hybrid trims, according to IT Home's November 10 report. Listed trim prices range from RMB 235,800 to RMB 299,800. A separate limited-time benefits offer starts at RMB 169,900 through November 30. The model receives styling and cockpit-interaction changes; the benefits offer should remain distinct from the listed trim-price range. [IT Home]

Dongfeng Honda also launched its updated CR-V Quanneng Rongyao edition that evening. IT Home reported current starting prices of RMB 137,900 for gasoline versions and RMB 156,900 for hybrids. The update adds a Fengshang gasoline AWD trim and supports cloud-based cockpit applications using Huawei Cloud technology. These current selling prices do not by themselves establish a permanent list-price reset. [IT Home]

Separately, Changan Mazda announced on November 9 that Liu Bin would immediately succeed Wu Xuxi as executive deputy general manager of its automotive sales division, Netcom reported. Wu was to receive another assignment, which the report did not identify. This is a position within the joint venture's sales operation, rather than a group-level appointment at Changan Automobile. [Netcom]

以国内零售口径观察厂商表现

据CnEVPost报道，乘联分会11月10日发布的厂商排名显示，比亚迪十月中国新能源乘用车零售为250,301辆，份额22.4%；吉利汽车位列第二，零跑保持第三，上汽通用五菱升至第五。这里的比较范围是国内新能源乘用车零售，不能直接与车企包含出口的集团销量或批发量混用。[CnEVPost / 乘联分会]

对于国际读者，统计范围与排名顺序同样重要。这份榜单描述的是中国零售市场内一个明确细分范围的表现，并不代表全球交付排名，也不能把各家公司自行披露的不同销量口径直接拼接成同一比较。上汽通用五菱在榜单中是单独的经营主体，不应被替换成上汽集团整体。保留这些边界，才能在使用新排名时不扩大其所衡量的范围。

海拉转向传感器进入量产交付

佛瑞亚海拉11月10日宣布，新一代角度传感器已在中国量产，并开始向多家中国电动车制造商交付。产品由德国利普施塔特与上海研发团队联合开发，用于线控转向系统。公告未披露具体客户名称、生产规模及投资金额。[佛瑞亚海拉]

这条消息的经营进展是进入量产并开始交付，比单纯的技术展示更明确。不过，现有披露仍不足以估算业务收入或某一客户项目的规模。联合研发安排确认了上海团队参与，也不等于公告已经说明具体量产工厂所在地。

合资企业更新产品与销售管理安排

据IT之家11月10日报道，新款林肯Z正式上市，提供五款燃油及强混车型，车型标价为235,800至299,800元。报道另列截至11月30日的限时权益起价169,900元。此次产品更新涉及外观与座舱交互，限时权益价需要与车型标价区间分别理解，不能把两者混成一个长期价格体系。[IT之家]

东风本田CR-V全能荣耀款也于当晚上市。IT之家所列现时燃油版起价137,900元，混动版起价156,900元。产品新增风尚四驱燃油版本，并支持华为云技术赋能的云车机应用。这些是报道所列的现时售价，单凭该信息不足以认定永久指导价已经调整。[IT之家]

组织方面，据网通社报道，长安马自达11月9日宣布，刘斌即日起接任汽车销售分公司执行副总经理，原负责人吴旭曦因工作调整将接受新的任命，报道未说明其新岗位。此次调整属于合资企业的销售经营安排，不能表述成长安汽车集团层面的管理岗位变化。[网通社]
