BYD's Yangwang Breaks 500 km/h in Abu Dhabi as 14 MEPs Seek EU Security Review of BYD仰望在阿布扎比突破500 km/h，14名欧洲议员要求欧盟评估比亚迪安全风险

Yangwang's U9 Xtreme hit 503.17 km/h on public roads in Abu Dhabi, while 14 MEPs asked Brussels to assess BYD's security risks. Xiaomi EV set up an International Business Division ahead of its 2027 entry into Europe.仰望 U9 Xtreme 在阿布扎比公共道路跑出 503.17 km/h，同时 14 名欧洲议会议员要求欧盟评估比亚迪安全风险；小米汽车成立国际业务部，为 2027 年进入欧洲做准备。

By TopChinaCar Editorial作者：TopChinaCar 编辑部 · Published November 7, 2026发布于 2026-11-07 · Compiled from the primary sources cited below基于文末所列一手信源编写

BYD had two very different days abroad. In the Gulf, its premium brand Yangwang put a production car past 500 km/h on public roads. In Brussels, a group of lawmakers asked the EU to look into whether BYD poses a security risk. Together they show where Chinese carmakers now stand overseas: a lot more ambitious, and watched a lot more closely. Elsewhere, Xiaomi EV built out the organization for its overseas push, FAW's Hongqi widened its right-hand-drive lineup, and two Southeast Asian stories, a Proton facelift and a Jaecoo wheel incident, showed both the opportunities and the scrutiny that come with volume.

BYD: A Speed Record in the Gulf

Yangwang says the U9 Xtreme reached 503.17 km/h on public roads in Abu Dhabi on November 1, beating the 496.22 km/h it set earlier at Germany's ATP proving ground. Only 30 units will be built worldwide, and its four motors produce a combined 2,220 kW. [CnEVPost]

The location matters as much as the number. Yangwang also plans to unveil its ultra-luxury sedan in the UAE, and the record gives BYD's top brand something to show buyers in the Gulf, a region where Chinese makers are trying to sell on prestige, not just price.

BYD: Security Questions in Brussels

On November 4, 14 MEPs led by French MEP François Kalfon sent a written question to the European Commission asking the EU cybersecurity agency to assess BYD's security risks and consider listing it as a high-risk supplier. They also asked whether Chinese cars would be kept out of public procurement and dual-use strategic infrastructure. The Commission usually has six weeks to reply. BYD said data from vehicles it sells in Europe cannot be accessed remotely from China and will not be shared with its entities or third parties in China. [Gasgoo]

A written question is not a regulation, and nothing changes for BYD today. But it shows that worries about connected-car data are moving from technical discussion into politics. For any Chinese brand growing quickly in Europe, storing data locally and being able to explain how it is governed is becoming part of the price of entry.

Xiaomi and Hongqi Build Their Overseas Machinery

Xiaomi EV has set up an International Business Division, according to Chinese media reports, with automotive vice president Yu Liguo as general manager reporting to Lei Jun and Lu Weibing. The unit covers overseas marketing, sales, dealer networks, delivery and logistics, and customer service. Xiaomi has said its global rollout begins in Europe in the second half of 2027, followed by right-hand-drive markets in the first half of 2028, and it has signed MoUs with 8 German dealer groups. [ChinaEVHome] Moving from a preparation team to a full division is the step that turns a timetable into a company.

FAW's Hongqi, meanwhile, showed right-hand-drive versions of the E-HS9 and EHS4 at the 16th China (Macau) International Auto Show; both will go on sale in Macau alongside the EHS5 and E-QM5. The RHD E-HS9 made its global debut in Singapore in May before entering Thailand and Hong Kong, and the RHD EHS4 is due in Europe in November. Hongqi's RHD strategy, announced in June, calls for 6 new RHD NEV models over the next three years. [Gasgoo]

Southeast Asia: New Metal and Quality Scrutiny

Geely-backed Proton revealed the facelifted 2027 S70 turbo with an RS suffix, topped by a Sportline variant. It is a twin of Geely's facelifted fourth-generation Emgrand, and its 1.5-litre turbo makes 181 PS and 290 Nm through a seven-speed wet dual-clutch. Pricing and launch date are still to come; the RS will be presented at a fashion showcase on November 10. [paultan.org] It is another example of Geely bringing its Chinese platforms to Southeast Asia through a local partner brand.

In Jakarta, a Chery Jaecoo J5 EV lost its front-right wheel on November 4 after hitting a curb and barrier. Jaecoo Indonesia's inspection concluded the collision caused the separation, not a product defect, and no independent investigation has been reported. The incident drew attention because it follows Chery's recall of 8,878 Omoda 5s in Malaysia over rear axles. The J5 EV posted 26,635 wholesale deliveries in Indonesia in January–September, making it the country's best-selling battery-electric passenger car. [CarNewsChina] The more cars a brand sells, the more attention every single incident gets, and how fast and how openly the brand responds starts to shape its reputation.

What to Watch

  • The Commission's reply to the MEPs. It usually has six weeks. The answer will show how far Brussels is willing to treat connected-car data from Chinese brands as a security issue.
  • Hongqi's EHS4 arrival in Europe this month, and whether Xiaomi's new division names markets and dealer partners beyond its first 8 German groups ahead of its 2027 launch.

比亚迪今天在海外的两条消息，方向完全不同。在海湾地区，旗下高端品牌仰望让一台量产车在公共道路上跑过了 500 km/h；在布鲁塞尔，一批欧洲议会议员要求欧盟评估比亚迪是否构成安全风险。两件事放在一起看，正好说明中国车企在海外的处境：雄心更大，被审视得也更严。另外，小米汽车为出海搭起了组织架构，一汽红旗扩充了右舵车型，东南亚则出现了一款宝腾改款车和一起捷酷掉轮事件，销量上来之后，机会和关注是一起来的。

比亚迪：在海湾创下速度纪录

仰望称，U9 Xtreme 于 11 月 1 日在阿布扎比的公共道路上跑出 503.17 km/h，刷新了它此前在德国 ATP 试验场创下的 496.22 km/h。这台车全球限量 30 台，四电机总峰值功率 2,220 kW。[CnEVPost]

地点和数字同样重要。仰望还计划在阿联酋发布超豪华轿车，这次纪录给了比亚迪最高端的品牌一个在海湾市场展示的机会。在这一地区，中国车企想靠品牌溢价而不只是价格来竞争。

比亚迪：在布鲁塞尔面对安全质询

11 月 4 日，以法国议员 Kalfon 为首的 14 名欧洲议会议员向欧委会提交书面质询，要求欧盟网络安全局评估比亚迪的安全风险，并考虑把它列为高风险供应商；他们还询问是否要限制中国汽车参与公共采购和军民两用的战略基础设施。欧委会通常有六周时间答复。比亚迪回应称，其在欧洲销售车辆的数据无法从中国远程访问，也不会提供给它在中国的实体或中国境内第三方。[盖世汽车]

书面质询不是法规，比亚迪眼下的经营不会因此改变。但它说明，围绕联网汽车数据的担忧正在从技术讨论走向政治议题。对在欧洲快速放量的中国品牌来说，数据本地存储、能说清楚数据怎么管理，正在变成进入市场的门槛之一。

小米与红旗：搭建出海的组织和产品

据中国媒体报道，小米汽车成立了国际业务部，由汽车部副总裁于立国兼任总经理，向雷军和卢伟冰汇报，负责海外市场营销、销售、经销商网络、交付物流和客服等。小米此前表示，全球化将在 2027 年下半年从欧洲开始，2028 年上半年进入右舵市场，并已和 8 家德国经销商集团签署谅解备忘录。[ChinaEVHome] 从筹备组变成正式的业务部门，意味着时间表开始落到具体的组织上。

一汽红旗则在第十六届中国（澳门）国际汽车博览会上展出右舵版 E-HS9 和 EHS4，两款车将与 EHS5、E-QM5 一同在澳门上市。右舵 E-HS9 今年 5 月已在新加坡全球首发，随后进入泰国和香港；右舵 EHS4 计划 11 月进入欧洲市场。按照红旗 6 月公布的右舵战略，未来三年将推出 6 款右舵新能源车型。[盖世汽车]

东南亚：新车与质量考验

吉利持股的宝腾发布了改款 2027 款 S70 涡轮版，并加上「RS」后缀，顶配为 Sportline。该车和吉利第四代帝豪改款车同源，1.5T 发动机输出 181 PS、290 Nm，匹配 7 速湿式双离合。价格和上市日期尚未公布，RS 将在 11 月 10 日的一场时装秀上亮相。[paultan.org] 这是吉利通过本地合作品牌把国内平台带到东南亚的又一个例子。

在雅加达，一辆奇瑞捷酷 J5 EV 于 11 月 4 日撞上路缘和隔离栏后右前轮脱落。捷酷印尼的检查结论是，轮子脱落由碰撞造成、不是产品缺陷，目前没有独立调查。此事之所以引人注意，是因为奇瑞此前因 Omoda 5 后桥问题在马来西亚召回了 8,878 辆。J5 EV 今年 1 至 9 月在印尼批发 26,635 辆，是当地最畅销的纯电乘用车。[CarNewsChina] 品牌卖得越多，单个事件得到的关注就越多，响应的速度和透明度会开始影响口碑。

值得关注

  • 欧委会对议员质询的答复。欧委会通常有六周时间答复，答复内容将显示布鲁塞尔在多大程度上把中国品牌的联网汽车数据当作安全问题。
  • 红旗 EHS4 本月进入欧洲，以及小米国际业务部在 2027 年上市前，除首批 8 家德国经销商集团之外，会不会公布更多市场和合作伙伴。
