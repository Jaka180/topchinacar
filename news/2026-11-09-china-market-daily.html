Today's Updates

BYD's Yangwang unveiled the Altior in Abu Dhabi and opened pre-orders for the four-seat Shanhe Dingyun limited edition. Chinese customers are required to place a RMB 300,000 deposit. A five-seat version will follow outside this initial batch, and the report's expected selling price is not final. [CnEVPost]

GWM released official rear images of the ORA 7 and confirmed coupe-style and wagon versions. Autohome also reported that display cars were reaching dealerships. These disclosures describe preparations ahead of launch, rather than a completed launch or delivery. [Autohome]
