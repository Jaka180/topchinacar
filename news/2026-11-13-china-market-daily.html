Denza and SAIC Volkswagen Expand China's EV Lineups as Jetour Sets TX Pre-Sales腾势与上汽大众扩充中国电动车产品线，捷途TX确定预售时间

Denza N8 and SAIC Volkswagen ID. ERA 5X join launches from GAC Aion and BAIC. Jetour sets November 16 pre-sales for the TX, with pricing still undisclosed.腾势N8、上汽大众ID. ERA 5X、埃安Ray 7及北汽星钽5X正式上市，扩充纯电与插混产品选择；捷途TX确定11月16日开启预售，价格尚未披露。

By TopChinaCar Editorial作者：TopChinaCar 编辑部 · Published November 13, 2026发布于 2026-11-13 · Compiled from the primary sources cited below基于文末所列一手信源编写

China's latest product updates span electric sedans, battery-electric SUVs and models offering both electric and plug-in hybrid powertrains. Denza, SAIC Volkswagen, GAC Aion and BAIC announced launches on November 12, while Jetour set a pre-sale date for its first battery-electric SUV. The developments broaden the products available or approaching sale in China, although a launch announcement and a pre-sale plan represent different stages of commercialization. AI-generated.

Electric additions from a joint venture and Aion

SAIC Volkswagen launched the ID. ERA 5X in four trims with suggested retail prices of RMB 119,900–159,900. The SUV is the first all-electric model in the ID. ERA family. The operating entity is SAIC Volkswagen, the joint venture, and the announcement concerns its own product range. Its higher trims offer lidar and urban navigation assistance. The vehicle combines the CMP platform with CEA electrical architecture co-developed by Volkswagen and XPeng, bringing that architecture into the newly launched SUV. [CnEVPost]

GAC Aion's Ray 7 adds an electric sedan to the day's launches. Its five trims have list prices of RMB 123,800–153,800. As the first model in Aion's Ray series, it establishes the opening product in that range. Huawei DriveONE electric drive is fitted throughout the lineup, while urban navigation assistance is offered on three lidar-equipped trims. That distinction matters when describing the product: the electric drive is common equipment, but the stated lidar-based assistance offering applies to selected versions. [CnEVPost]

Two powertrain options for family SUVs

Denza launched the five-seat N8 with both battery-electric and plug-in hybrid powertrains, extending its family SUV range. The BEV versions have list prices of RMB 269,900–329,900. The delivery schedule separates the two powertrains: BEVs are due to begin reaching buyers one week after launch, while PHEVs are scheduled to follow one to two months later. Those are announced delivery arrangements; the launch itself does not establish completed customer deliveries for either powertrain. [CnEVPost]

BAIC also introduced a model offering both BEV and PHEV versions. Beijing Off-road's Xingtan 5X, the first product in its Xingtan range, comes in seven trims at list prices of RMB 129,900–174,900. Selected versions use Huawei Qiankun ADS 5 Pro. Urban navigation assistance requires a paid subscription and is not standard across the lineup. The equipment and subscription distinction defines what the announced price range covers, without implying that every version provides the same assistance functions. [CnEVPost]

What to Watch

Jetour's TX remains at a different stage. The brand announced November 16 pre-sales during its Global Travel+ Conference, according to a November 12 report. Pricing was not disclosed. Its first battery-electric SUV offers two- and four-wheel-drive configurations, 550 km of CLTC range and Falcon 500 driver assistance. The range figure is stated on the CLTC test basis. The next announced commercial step is pre-sales, rather than a completed formal launch. Alongside that milestone, Denza's scheduled BEV and PHEV delivery starts provide the specific execution steps to follow from today's updates. [CarNewsChina]

Across these updates, the disclosed information concerns product availability, list prices, equipment and announced timing. It does not provide a common measure of customer demand. The launches therefore support a comparison of product offerings, without establishing a sales ranking among the participating brands.

合资公司与埃安增加纯电产品

上汽大众推出ID. ERA 5X，四款车型指导价为119,900至159,900元。这是ID. ERA系列首款纯电SUV，本次经营主体为上汽大众合资公司，产品更新对应其自身车型阵容。新车高配提供激光雷达和城区领航辅助驾驶，采用CMP平台及大众与小鹏共同开发的CEA电子电气架构，将该架构应用于这款新上市SUV。读者在理解这一动态时，应区分合资公司的具体产品发布与母集团层面的经营口径。[CnEVPost]

广汽埃安Ray 7则为当天的上市产品增加了一款纯电轿车。五款车型指导价为123,800至153,800元，作为Ray系列首款车型，它构成这一产品系列的起点。全系使用华为DriveONE电驱系统，三款配备激光雷达的版本提供城区领航辅助驾驶。这里需要明确配置适用范围：电驱系统为全系采用，所述激光雷达辅助驾驶配置则对应部分版本，不能将某一版本的功能直接视为全系标准配置。[CnEVPost]

家庭SUV提供两种动力选择

腾势五座N8上市，同时提供纯电与插混动力，进一步扩充家庭SUV产品线。纯电版指导价为269,900至329,900元。交付安排按动力类型区分：纯电版计划在上市一周后开始交付，插混版随后一至两个月交付。这些时间属于已经公布的交付安排，上市信息本身不能用来证明相应动力版本已经完成用户交付；产品发布与交付执行需要分别记录。[CnEVPost]

北汽同样推出了兼有纯电与插混版本的车型。北京越野星钽5X是星钽系列首款产品，七款车型指导价为129,900至174,900元。部分版本搭载华为乾崑ADS 5 Pro，城区领航辅助驾驶需要付费订阅，并非全系标配。车型价格范围、辅助驾驶硬件适用版本与功能订阅条件共同界定了此次发布的产品组合，不能据此认为每款车均提供相同的辅助驾驶功能。[CnEVPost]

值得关注

捷途TX仍处于不同阶段。据11月12日报道，捷途宣布将于11月16日在全球旅行+大会期间开启预售，价格尚未披露。这款品牌首款纯电SUV提供两驱和四驱配置，CLTC续航为550公里，搭载猎鹰500辅助驾驶系统。续航数字应保留CLTC测试口径，不能改写成其他测试标准或实际道路表现。下一项已经公布的商业节点是预售，而非已经完成正式上市。结合当天其他动态，腾势纯电版与插混版各自计划开始交付的时间，也是后续可核对的具体执行节点。[CarNewsChina]

这些动态披露的是产品推出、指导价、配置和计划时间，并未提供可以统一比较的客户需求指标。因此，本次上市信息适合用来观察产品组合的变化，不能据此排列相关品牌的销量名次。
