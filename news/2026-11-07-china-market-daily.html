SAIC Motor approved a driver-assistance joint venture in which its supplier Momenta will hold the majority stake, while the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (MIIT) opened a draft rule giving every electric-vehicle battery a digital identity. A large domestic recall at Chery, a higher localization figure from Tesla's Shanghai plant and a leadership change at Toyota's China region rounded out the day.

SAIC hands majority control of a driver-assistance venture to Momenta

SAIC Motor's board on November 5 approved a plan to set up a joint venture with Momenta and Yandian Technology, with registered capital of RMB 300 million, to strengthen R&D on advanced driver assistance. Momenta will contribute RMB 153 million for a 51% stake, SAIC RMB 75 million for 25% and Yandian Technology RMB 72 million for 24%. The venture's name has not been disclosed [Jiemian].

For international readers, the notable detail is the ownership structure: SAIC, a Shanghai state-owned carmaker, is taking a minority position in a venture with a software supplier, rather than keeping driver-assistance development fully in-house. The filing itself does not say which SAIC brands or models the venture will serve.

SAIC also featured in a second development story. The Audi Innovation and Technology Center (AITC), a dedicated R&D entity for the AUDI brand formed by Audi AG and SAIC Motor, opened in Shanghai on November 5 with a team of about 300. It is developing the second-generation ADP 2.0 digital platform, and four new models based on it will enter the Chinese market from 2028 [Gasgoo]. Separately, SAIC's IM Motors launched the all-electric LS8 on November 5 from RMB 239,800, below the earlier pre-sale price of RMB 249,800 [21st Century Business Herald].

Regulation: battery digital IDs and a Chery recall

On November 5, MIIT opened a draft of its interim rules on digital IDs for new-energy vehicle power batteries for public comment, with feedback due by December 6. The 20-article draft uses a 24-digit national-standard code as each battery's unique index, linking data from design and production through sales and recycling. It is proposed to take effect in 2027, with the exact date to be set. It is a consultation draft and is not yet in force [Gasgoo].

In China, recalls are filed with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR). According to a SAMR notice published on November 6, Chery will recall 53,882 Omoda-series and Tiggo 5x vehicles built between June 6, 2022 and December 31, 2023, starting November 27. Welds joining the rear-axle trailing arm and sleeve may be incompletely fused, which in extreme cases could affect rear-wheel control; Chery will replace defective-batch axles free of charge [CarNewsChina].

Foreign carmakers and their Chinese partners

Tesla's Shanghai plant is wholly owned by Tesla rather than a joint venture. In a press release issued during the China International Import Expo (CIIE), Tesla said parts localization there has risen to 98%, up from its long-standing description of "over 95%." Tesla has signed more than 400 local tier-1 suppliers in China, more than 60 of which have entered its global supply chain [CnEVPost].

Toyota announced on November 5 that Sukenaga Ota, head of Lexus sales promotion, will become chief of its China region, while current China chief Tatsuro Ueda becomes a Senior Fellow, effective January 2027. Ota previously served as a sales vice president at FAW Toyota. The change comes as Toyota's two Chinese joint ventures, FAW Toyota and GAC Toyota, move to coordinate their operations [Gasgoo].

FAW-Volkswagen, the joint venture between state-owned FAW and Volkswagen, signed a memorandum with Volkswagen Group during CIIE to import some components and complete Audi vehicles in 2027; volumes and value were not disclosed [Gasgoo]. Dongfeng Peugeot, the joint-venture brand of Dongfeng Motor and Stellantis, launched the 2027 Peugeot 408 with an official guide price of RMB 99,700 [21st Century Business Herald]. Nissan said its Dongfeng Nissan joint venture will open pre-sales for the NX7 plug-in hybrid SUV next week, and plans five more NEV models by summer 2027 [CnEVPost].

Domestic brands: launch calendar and partnerships

BYD's premium brand Denza will launch the new N8 battery-electric SUV on November 12, with up to 1,003 km of range [CnEVPost]. BYD also signed a strategic agreement with smartphone maker vivo covering phone-car connectivity, digital car keys and in-car fast charging [Jiemian]. Geely's Lynk & Co will debut the seven-seat 900L, a 5,280 mm-long plug-in hybrid SUV, on November 10 [CnEVPost].

What to Watch

Two dates are fixed: MIIT's comment period on the battery digital ID draft closes on December 6, and Chery's recall begins on November 27. Product launches from Lynk & Co on November 10 and Denza on November 12 are also scheduled. Details of the SAIC–Momenta venture, including its name, have not yet been disclosed.

上汽与Momenta设立智驾合资公司，Momenta控股

上汽集团董事会11月5日审议通过议案，拟与Momenta、延电科技共同设立合资公司，注册资本3亿元，用于加强高阶智能驾驶技术研发。Momenta拟认缴1.53亿元、持股51%，上汽集团认缴7500万元、持股25%，延电科技认缴7200万元、持股24%。合资公司名称未披露[界面新闻]。

值得国际读者注意的是股权结构：上汽在这家合资公司中持少数股权，由智驾供应商Momenta控股，而不是把智驾研发完全留在体系内部。目前披露的信息没有说明合资公司将服务上汽旗下哪些品牌或车型。

上汽还有另一项研发动态。奥迪与上汽集团共同组建的AUDI品牌专属研发实体奥迪创新技术中心（AITC）11月5日在上海开业，团队约300人，正在开发第二代智能数字平台ADP 2.0，基于该平台的四款新车将从2028年起进入中国市场[盖世汽车]。另外，上汽旗下智己汽车11月5日上市LS8纯电版，上市权益价23.98万元起，低于此前24.98万元的预售价[21世纪经济报道]。

监管：电池数字身份证与奇瑞召回

工信部11月5日就《新能源汽车动力电池数字身份证管理暂行规定（征求意见稿）》公开征求意见，反馈截止12月6日。征求意见稿共20条，以国标24位编码作为唯一索引，串联电池设计、生产、销售到回收利用等环节信息，拟于2027年施行，具体日期待定。该文件目前仍为征求意见稿，尚未生效[盖世汽车]。

在中国，汽车召回需向国家市场监督管理总局备案。据市场监管总局11月6日发布的召回信息，奇瑞将召回2022年6月6日至2023年12月31日生产的53,882辆欧萌达系列和瑞虎5x，召回自11月27日起实施。原因是后桥拖曳臂与套管焊接可能存在未熔合或未焊透，极端情况下可能影响后轮控制；奇瑞将对缺陷批次免费更换后桥[CarNewsChina]。

外资车企与中国合资伙伴

特斯拉上海工厂为特斯拉独资，而非合资企业。特斯拉在第九届进博会期间发布的新闻稿称，上海超级工厂零部件国产化率已提升至98%，此前其一贯表述为“超过95%”。特斯拉在华已签约超过400家本土一级供应商，其中超过60家进入其全球供应链体系[CnEVPost]。

丰田汽车11月5日宣布，雷克萨斯销售推进部部长太田资大将接任丰田中国本部长，原中国本部长上田达郎转任Senior Fellow，任命自2027年1月起生效。太田资大曾任一汽丰田销售副总经理。此次调整正值丰田在华两家合资公司一汽丰田与广汽丰田推进协同整合之际[盖世汽车]。

一汽与大众的合资公司一汽-大众在进博会期间与大众汽车集团签署备忘录，2027年将进口部分零部件及奥迪整车，进口数量和金额未披露[盖世汽车]。东风汽车与Stellantis的合资品牌东风标致上市2027款408，官方指导价9.97万元[21世纪经济报道]。日产表示，东风日产NX7插混SUV将于下周开启预售，并计划在2027年夏季前再推出五款新能源车型[CnEVPost]。

自主品牌：新车日程与合作

比亚迪旗下腾势新款N8纯电SUV定于11月12日上市，续航最高1,003公里[CnEVPost]。比亚迪还与手机厂商vivo签署战略合作协议，覆盖手车互联、数字车钥匙和车载快充等方向[界面新闻]。吉利旗下领克七座版900L将于11月10日首发，这款插混SUV车长5,280毫米[CnEVPost]。
