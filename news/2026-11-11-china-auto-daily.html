Changan Plans Brazil Expansion as BYD Adds a Third Shift and Li Auto Signs Asian Partners长安规划巴西扩张，比亚迪启动第三班，理想签约亚太伙伴

Changan plans eight new models in Brazil in 2027, while BYD adds about 2,000 workers with a third shift in Bahia. Li Auto's five new Asia-Pacific partnerships underline the work behind overseas launches.长安计划2027年在巴西推出八款新车，比亚迪巴伊亚工厂启动第三班并新增约2,000名员工。理想新签五个亚太市场伙伴，海外经营的产品、产能与渠道准备同步推进。

By TopChinaCar Editorial作者：TopChinaCar 编辑部 · Published November 11, 2026发布于 2026-11-11 · Compiled from the primary sources cited below基于文末所列一手信源编写

Chinese automakers' latest overseas developments put the practical work of expansion in focus: assembly capacity, operating shifts, distribution partners and vehicle certification. Changan is planning a broader Brazilian range and more local assembly, while BYD has started a third shift in Bahia. In Asia-Pacific, Li Auto has signed partners in five markets, GWM has unveiled a new Ora model in Thailand and Hongqi has cleared another Australian certification milestone. Together, the announcements suggest that the quality of execution deserves as much attention as the breadth of the expansion plans.

Brazil brings production and distribution together

Caoa Changan plans eight new models in Brazil in 2027, including the formal introduction of Deepal and Nevo, alongside doubled assembly capacity in Anápolis. Its network targets are 50 active sales outlets by end-2026 and 100 dealerships in 2027. The combination matters because a wider product range needs both production support and places to sell and service vehicles. These remain company targets: the announcement should be read as a programme of work rather than as completed capacity or a fully established network. [Quatro Rodas]

BYD's development is an operating step already taken. Its Bahia industrial complex officially started a third production shift on November 9, adding about 2,000 workers. The plant now operates with three teams, with the company stating a 40-hour working week for employees. A new shift provides a concrete marker of industrial activity, although it should not be converted into an unstated annual production figure. The useful distinction for observers is between the staffing and shift change reported here and the production results that will follow. [Nossa Metrópole]

BYD is also addressing connectivity through a partnership with TIM. The plan covers eSIM services for light vehicles in Brazil and dedicated 4G and 5G networks at Camaçari. Factory coverage is planned across 4.6 million square metres, with seven external connection points and 63 devices in workshops and operating areas. This adds a digital infrastructure dimension to the industrial story, connecting production systems, people and vehicles; the reported plan does not establish that every element has already been deployed. [Money Report]

Li Auto prepares partners and right-hand-drive products

Li Auto announced partnerships covering Thailand, Hong Kong, Singapore, the Philippines and Vietnam, putting five markets into launch preparations and taking its Asia-Pacific footprint to nine markets this year. Thailand will be operated by a subsidiary working with local dealers. The right-hand-drive Mega is planned for a December Thai motor-show debut, followed by a brand and model launch in Hong Kong. Pricing and specific delivery dates have not been disclosed. The significance is the pairing of distribution arrangements with a product suited to right-hand-drive markets; signed partnerships should still be distinguished from completed commercial launches. [CnEVPost]

GWM links a Thai debut to local manufacturing

GWM gave the Ora 5 Plus its global debut in Thailand on November 10, with BEV and HEV Hi2 hybrid versions built at Rayong. Official pricing and deliveries are planned for December 1 at Motor Expo. The BEV's stated range is 520 km under the NEDC standard. The local production link and choice of powertrains make the announcement relevant beyond the unveiling itself. For readers comparing products, the range figure needs to retain its test-cycle label, while the pricing announcement remains a separate milestone still to come. [iMoD]

Hongqi moves through Australia's approval process

Hongqi's EHS4 has secured Australian ADR certification after the flagship E-HS9. The brand will operate locally as HQ and plans customer deliveries from mid-January 2027. The EHS4 and EHS5 are expected within six months of the E-HS9 launch, but the EHS5 has not yet received ADR certification. That difference is important when assessing the readiness of the planned range: approval for one vehicle does not settle the status of another. The EHS4 milestone advances launch preparations without turning the entire product schedule into a completed rollout. [CarExpert]

What to Watch

The next useful checks concern delivery against these plans. In Brazil, watch how Changan's product, assembly and dealer targets progress alongside BYD's expanded operating shifts and planned connectivity infrastructure. In Asia-Pacific, the next pricing, launch and delivery announcements will help show how partnership agreements and vehicle preparations turn into customer-facing operations. Across these developments, the editorial test is consistent: track completed steps separately from targets, and assess factories, products and distribution as connected parts of overseas execution.

中国车企最新一轮海外动态，把扩张背后的具体工作推到台前：组装产能、生产班次、经销伙伴，以及车辆认证。长安规划在巴西增加车型并扩大本地组装，比亚迪巴伊亚工厂已经启动第三班；亚太方面，理想签约五个市场伙伴，长城在泰国首发欧拉新车，红旗则取得新的澳大利亚认证进展。这些消息放在一起，说明观察海外扩张时，落地执行与布局范围同样值得关注。

巴西：生产与销售网络同步准备

Caoa Changan计划2027年在巴西推出八款新车，正式引入Deepal和Nevo产品线，并将Anápolis组装产能翻倍。网络目标是2026年底拥有50家活跃销售网点，2027年达到100家经销商。更丰富的产品线需要生产支持，也需要销售和服务承接，因此这几项目标应该放在一起评估。不过，它们仍然是企业计划，不能直接理解成已经落地的产能和已建成的完整网络。[Quatro Rodas]

比亚迪的消息则是已经采取的运营动作。其巴伊亚工业园11月9日正式启动第三班生产，新增约2,000名员工，工厂以三个团队运行，公司表示员工每周工作40小时。新班次为观察工业活动提供了具体进度，但不能据此推算原文未披露的年产量。需要区分的是本次已经报道的人员与班次调整，以及之后才会体现的生产成果。[Nossa Metrópole]

连接服务也是比亚迪在巴西补充的一环。公司与TIM合作，计划为轻型汽车提供eSIM连接，并在Camaçari工厂部署专用4G和5G网络；工厂覆盖方案涉及460万平方米，包括七个外部连接点以及63台车间和运营区域设备。这为当地工业经营增加了数字基础设施维度，服务生产系统、人员和车辆之间的信息交换，但报道中的计划并不意味着所有部署均已完成。[Money Report]

理想：伙伴网络与右舵产品一起准备

理想宣布签约泰国、香港、新加坡、菲律宾及越南合作伙伴，五个市场进入上市筹备，年内亚太布局扩展至九个市场。泰国业务由理想子公司运营并配合当地经销商，右舵MEGA计划12月在泰国车展亮相，随后在香港推出品牌和车型，售价及具体交付日期尚未披露。其意义在于渠道安排与适应右舵市场的产品相互配合，但已经签约合作仍应与已经完成商业上市分别看待。[CnEVPost]

长城：泰国首发连接本地制造

长城11月10日在泰国全球首发欧拉5 PLUS，提供由罗勇工厂生产的纯电和HEV Hi2混动版本，计划12月1日在Motor Expo公布正式售价并开始交付。纯电版公布的续航为520公里，采用NEDC标准。本地生产与不同动力选择，使这次消息的意义超出单纯展示新车；消费者比较产品时，应保留续航数字的测试标准，而定价仍是接下来需要单独关注的节点。[iMoD]

红旗：澳大利亚准入准备继续推进

红旗EHS4在旗舰E-HS9之后通过澳大利亚ADR认证。红旗将在当地使用HQ品牌，计划2027年1月中旬开始客户交付；EHS4与EHS5拟在E-HS9上市后六个月内跟进，但EHS5尚未获得ADR认证。评估整个产品阵容的准备程度时，这个区别很重要：一款车获得批准，并不代表另一款车的状态也已确定。EHS4的认证推进了上市准备，但整个产品时间表仍有后续工作。[CarExpert]

值得关注

接下来最有用的观察，是计划怎样兑现。巴西方面，可继续跟进长安的产品、组装和经销网络目标，以及比亚迪增加班次后的经营表现与连接基础设施建设。亚太方面，之后的定价、上市及交付公告，将帮助判断合作协议和车型准备如何转为面向客户的业务。贯穿这些事件的观察方法是一致的：把已完成的动作与目标分开，再把工厂、产品和渠道作为海外经营中相互联系的环节来评估。
