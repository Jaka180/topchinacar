Mercedes-Benz, Subaru and Porsche File China Recalls as Geely and Fang Cheng Bao Expand Lineups奔驰、斯巴鲁与保时捷备案召回，吉利和方程豹扩充产品阵容

Subaru's recall covers 196,494 imported cars, while Mercedes-Benz separates immediate and later remedies. Geely adds RMB 91,800 Xingyuan editions and Fang Cheng Bao outlines production and product plans.斯巴鲁召回涉及196,494辆进口汽车，奔驰不同批次分期实施；吉利新增指导价91,800元的星愿特别版，方程豹披露生产交付进展与产品规划。

By TopChinaCar Editorial作者：TopChinaCar 编辑部 · Published November 8, 2026发布于 2026-11-08 · Compiled from the primary sources cited below基于文末所列一手信源编写

China's November 8 auto briefing brings together two operating priorities: remedies for vehicles already on the road and additions to future and current product lineups. Mercedes-Benz, Subaru and Porsche recall filings sit alongside Geely Galaxy's new Xingyuan editions and Fang Cheng Bao's production and planning update. The distinction between an announced action and its implementation date matters throughout this edition.

Mercedes-Benz separates immediate and deferred remedies

China's State Administration for Market Regulation, or SAMR, published the Mercedes-Benz filings on November 6. Two China-built CLA electric vehicles are recalled immediately because of a rear electric-drive module defect, with free module replacement. This is a separate action from the larger door-lock recall involving locally built and imported vehicles. Beijing Benz and Mercedes-Benz China are the named operating entities; the production and import scopes should remain distinct.

The door-lock action covers 129,819 China-built A-Class, GLA and GLB cars and 50,458 imported vehicles, starting February 19, 2027. Moisture can affect the driver-door microswitch. The filing says locking remains functional, but automatic parking-brake functions may be affected; the remedy is a free door-lock replacement. These figures describe affected vehicles, rather than current sales, and the later start date should not be confused with the November announcement. [SAMR]

Subaru and Porsche specify different repair paths

Subaru China will recall 196,494 imported Forester, Legacy, Outback, XV and BRZ cars from December 21, 2026. Wiring inside the steering spiral cable may wear and break, disabling the horn. Announced by SAMR on November 6 following a regulatory defect investigation, the action provides free inspections and replacement where parts made before the improvement are fitted. Its scope is imported vehicles in China, not domestic production. [SAMR]

Porsche China's November 6 action takes immediate effect for 18,274 Cayennes. A parking-assistance software deviation may prevent the rear-view camera image from displaying, and the remedy is a free software update following a defect investigation. A separate action covers one 911 for inspection of its central wheel-lock fastener, with replacement where needed, extending an earlier recall. The two remedies address different systems and should be read separately. [SAMR]

Product additions range from available editions to future plans

Geely Galaxy introduced two Star-series Xingyuan special editions in China at its November 6 global user conference. Each carries an official guide price of RMB 91,800 and adds Flyme Auto 2.0 cockpit software and G-ASD H3 driver assistance. The development expands the existing lineup. Temporary subsidized prices should be kept separate from the official guide price and do not establish an official repricing of the range. [CnEVPost]

Fang Cheng Bao's general manager confirmed the Tai 6 on November 7, without providing its launch date, positioning or technical details. The Formula SL is expected to debut in 2027, while the Shark pickup is expected to enter China's market in December. More immediately, the Formula S series' DiSus-C version rolled off the line that day. Deliveries were expected in mid-November, or late November at the latest after logistics. Production progress and future model expectations therefore represent different stages of execution. [CnEVPost]

What to Watch

The next stated milestones are Fang Cheng Bao's expected November deliveries and the December China-market arrival of the Shark pickup. For recalls, Subaru's December 21 start and Mercedes-Benz's February 19, 2027 door-lock action provide the implementation dates to follow. Today's archived items do not establish repair completion rates or delivery outcomes; those remain distinct from the announcements reported here.

奔驰召回分为即日与延期实施批次

国家市场监督管理总局11月6日公布奔驰相关召回信息。其中，2辆国产CLA纯电动车因后电驱模块隐患即日起召回，企业将免费更换模块。这与涉及更多国产及进口车辆的门锁召回属于不同事项。备案主体包括北京奔驰及梅赛德斯-奔驰中国，阅读数量时应保留国产与进口范围的区别，不能把不同经营主体的车辆口径混在一起。

门锁相关召回涉及129,819辆国产A级、GLA和GLB，以及50,458辆进口汽车，自2027年2月19日起实施。驾驶员侧门锁微动开关存在受潮风险；开关故障不影响门锁锁止，但可能影响驻车功能自动启用，补救措施为免费更换门锁。这里的车辆数量是召回覆盖范围，并非当前销量；11月公布备案，也不意味着这一批次已经开始实施。[国家市场监督管理总局]

斯巴鲁与保时捷对应不同维修路径

斯巴鲁中国将自2026年12月21日起召回196,494辆进口森林人、力狮、傲虎、XV及BRZ。转向螺旋电缆内部线路可能磨损断线，造成喇叭失效。市场监管总局11月6日公布的这项行动，在监管部门启动缺陷调查后开展；企业将免费检查，对装配改善前零件的车辆予以更换。其范围是进入中国市场的进口汽车，不能解读为中国境内生产数量。[国家市场监督管理总局]

保时捷中国11月6日公布的行动则即日起实施，涉及18,274辆Cayenne。泊车辅助软件偏差可能导致倒车影像无法显示，企业将在缺陷调查后开展的召回中免费升级软件。另外1辆911需要检查车轮中央锁止紧固装置，必要时更换零件，属于此前召回的扩大范围。软件升级与紧固装置检查针对不同系统，应分别理解其风险和补救措施。[国家市场监督管理总局]

产品扩充涵盖新增版本与未来规划

吉利银河11月6日在全球用户大会上于中国推出星愿Star系列两款特别版，官方指导价均为91,800元。新版本配备Flyme Auto 2.0座舱与G-ASD H3辅助驾驶系统，属于现有产品阵容的扩充。理解这次产品变化时，官方指导价与限时补贴价格应分开记录，不能依据临时补贴就认定整个车系发生官方重定价。[CnEVPost]

方程豹总经理11月7日确认钛6存在，但没有披露上市时间、产品定位或技术细节。方程SL预计2027年亮相，鲨鱼皮卡预计12月进入中国市场。更接近交付环节的进展是，方程S系列云辇-C版本当天已下线；考虑物流，预计11月中旬、最迟下旬开始交付。因此，车型规划与已经发生的下线进展处于不同执行阶段，不能把未来亮相预期直接写成已经上市或交付。[CnEVPost]
