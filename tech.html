Under The Hood

Deep dives into the technologies powering China's automotive lead: 800V platforms, LFP, semi-solid and solid-state batteries, megawatt flash charging, city NOA, smart cockpits and battery swapping.

01

800V High-Voltage Architecture

High-voltage (800V and above) platforms cut current for the same power, reducing charging time, heat and weight. They enable ultra-fast charging of 300 kW and above, and are the foundation for 5C charging and silicon-carbide (SiC) power electronics.

Companies

Xpeng

Representative models

Xpeng G9 (the company said it was China’s first mass-production 800V SiC platform)

Production timing

Xpeng G9 launched 21 September 2022, with deliveries from late October 2022.

Sources

[Xpeng, Aug 2022] [中证网, Sep 2022]

Semi-Solid & Solid-State Batteries

Semi-solid (oxide-route) cells — with a reduced liquid electrolyte — commercialised first, while fully solid-state (sulfide-route) cells remain the next step toward energy density beyond 400–500 Wh/kg.

Companies

WeLion, Qingtao, BYD

Representative models

NIO (WeLion 150 kWh pack), SAIC MG4 (Qingtao cells), BYD sulfide all-solid-state

Production timing

Semi-solid shipped at scale in 2025–2026; all-solid-state mass production is dated 2027 at the earliest, with BYD planning small-batch trials in roughly 1,000 vehicles around 2027.

Sources

[Battery-Tech, Oct 2026]

04

Sodium-Ion Batteries

Sodium-ion cells trade a little energy density for lower cost, better cold-weather performance and abundant raw materials. They are commercialising first in energy storage, entry-level EVs and heavy vehicles.

Companies

CATL, BYD, HiNa Battery, EVE

Representative models

CATL–Changan sodium-ion passenger car (announced 5 Feb 2026; the plan stated that day was a mid-2026 launch; whether it was on sale by October 2026: no confirmed data); HiNa–Tonly 676 kWh sodium-ion mining truck (delivered to Sanyou Mining, July 2026)

Production timing

CATL began shipping sodium-ion cells in 2026 and signed a 60 GWh, three-year sodium-ion supply deal with HyperStrong on 27 April 2026; no completed storage delivery had been independently reported by early October 2026.

Sources

[Battery-Tech, Oct 2026]

05

Megawatt Flash Charging

BYD’s Super e-Platform uses a 1,000V architecture. The company stated a peak charge rate of 1 MW (1,000 kW), about 2 km of range per second. On the Han L EV the flash-charge rate is 10C, stated as 5 minutes for 400 km. On the Tang L EV the rate is up to 8.4C, stated as 5 minutes for 370 km.

Companies

BYD (with Zeekr and Huawei also building ultra-fast charging networks)

Representative models

BYD Han L EV (10C; 5 minutes for 400 km), Tang L EV (up to 8.4C; 5 minutes for 370 km). Both went on sale on 9 April 2025.

Production timing

Announced 17 March 2025; Han L and Tang L launched on 9 April 2025; BYD had built 4,239 stations by 5 March 2026 and the 10,000th on 28 August 2026, with a year-end target of 20,000.

Sources

[央广网, Mar 2025] [Sina Finance, Apr 2025] [21jingji, Aug 2026]

Smart Cockpit

The AITO M5 was the first model to ship with Huawei’s HarmonyOS cockpit. The Xiaomi SU7’s car system runs HyperOS.

Production timing

08

Battery Swapping

A swap station replaces a depleted pack with a charged one. China Youth Online reported on 23 October 2026 that CATL Choco-Swap completed a swap in 99 seconds.

Companies

NIO, CATL (Choco-Swap), Geely (signed a charging-and-swap cooperation with NIO on 28 September 2026)

Representative models

NIO ET5 / ES6 / ET9, Onvo L60; CATL Choco-Swap for entry EVs

Production timing

NIO has run swap stations since 2018 (4,099 stations by end-August 2026, per the China Electric Vehicle Charging Infrastructure Promotion Alliance); CATL runs an open Choco-Swap (EVOGO) network for multiple brands.

Sources

[China Youth Online, Oct 2026] [site: NIO battery-swap bet]
