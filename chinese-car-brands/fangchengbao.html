Overview

Fang Cheng Bao (方程豹) is the professional personalised new-energy vehicle brand of BYD Company Limited (SZSE 002594 / HKEX 01211), positioned between the Denza premium brand and the Yangwang ultra-luxury brand.[3][6] BYD chairman and president Wang Chuanfu first announced a new personalised brand on 16 November 2022; on 9 June 2023 BYD named the internal 'F-brand' project Fang Cheng Bao, and on 16 August 2023 formally launched the brand in Shenzhen together with the DMO super-hybrid off-road platform and its first model, the Bao 5 hardcore SUV, alongside the '583' hardcore family (Bao 5, Bao 8 concept 'SUPER 8' and Bao 3).[1][6][7] The name combines 'Fangcheng' (equation) and 'Bao' (leopard); BYD's official gloss is to use the professional wisdom of exploring the digital world to solve the personalised transformation of cars and users' lives, with the brand spirit 'there is a solution to every unknown world' and the vision 'make a better life different'.[1][7] The line-up is now organised into three complementary series: the 'Bao' high-end off-road series, the 'Titanium' all-round urban series, and the 'Fangcheng' full-scenario multi-form sedan series added in 2026.[1]

Sales have grown quickly: 5,712 units in 2023 (the Bao 5's partial first year), 56,388 in 2024 and 234,637 in 2025 (up 316.1% year on year), with cumulative deliveries passing 500,000 on 5 August 2026.[11] In BYD's 2025 annual report, the chairman's statement recorded that Fang Cheng Bao's full-year sales rose 316% year on year.[3] As of the research cut-off (2026-10-29), media compilations of BYD's disclosed data put the brand's first three quarters of 2026 at 285,436 units, with 43,181 in September, a monthly record.[14] Fang Cheng Bao is a brand of BYD, is not separately listed, and does not publish standalone financial data; parent-level figures are covered in the BYD deep research.[3][4]

Current operations

Model line-up and positioning

As of the research cut-off (2026-10-29), the production line-up consists of two hardcore off-road plug-in hybrids — the Bao 5 (launched 2023-11-09, RMB 239,800-302,800 after the July 2024 price cut) and the Bao 8 flagship (launched 2024-11-12 at RMB 379,800-407,800) — and two urban 'Titanium' models, the Titanium 3 electric SUV (launched 2025-04-16 from RMB 133,800) and the Titanium 7 mid-to-large SUV (launched 2025-09-09 at RMB 179,800-219,800).[8][10][2][27][20] On 2026-09-16 the brand launched its first sedans, the Fangcheng S (RMB 189,900-229,900) and Fangcheng S GT shooting brake (RMB 219,900-239,900), entering the pure-electric sport-sedan market.[15][1] The official timeline records successive refreshes through 2025-2026: the Bao 5 intelligent-driving edition (February 2025), the Bao 8 drone edition (March 2025), the Titanium 3 flash-charging version (March 2026), the Titanium 7 EV flash-charging version (April 2026) and the Bao 8/Bao 5 flash-charging versions carrying DiSus-P Ultra (May 2026).[1] Within the originally planned '583' hardcore family, the Bao 3 appeared only as the 'SUPER 3' concept and did not become a production hardcore model; its small-SUV position was later taken by the urban Titanium 3.[9][1]

Core technologies

Fang Cheng Bao leans on BYD's vertically integrated technology system and adds external partners. Its signature technology is the DMO super-hybrid off-road platform, launched on the same day as the brand (August 2023); it combines BYD's new hybrid body-on-frame architecture with an off-road-specific hybrid architecture, integrates the Blade Battery with a high-strength steel frame as CTC battery-chassis integration, and is compatible with the DiSus-P intelligent hydraulic body control system.[6] Launch coverage says DMO carries the world's first longitudinal EHS electric-hybrid system, an off-road-specific rear-drive e-axle, and 1.5T/2.0T longitudinal Xiaoyun high-power engines. That 'world's first' wording is the launch claim, not a separately verified fact.[6] On the Bao 5, system output reaches 505 kW with 760 N·m of torque, 0-100 km/h in the 4-second range, 16 drive modes, a 'leopard turn' with a 3.4-metre turning radius, 7.8 L/100 km fuel consumption and over 1,200 km combined range.[8] The Bao 8 carries DMO (2.0T) with the full-function DiSus-P hydraulic suspension, 550 kW combined power, 760 N·m torque and 0-100 km/h in 4.8 s.[10] The urban 'Titanium' models use the EVO+ pure-electric platform (Titanium 3) and a 1.5T plug-in hybrid (Titanium 7), and generally carry God's Eye driving assist and DiSus-C intelligent damping control; the launch report calls the Bao 8 BYD and Huawei's first jointly created intelligent hardcore product and says it carries Huawei Qiankun ADS 3.0, without verifying a 'world's first' claim.[27][20][10] The 2026 Fangcheng S series is the first to carry the DiSus-M magnetorheological suspension, the second-generation Blade Battery and flash-charging, with up to 900 km CLTC range.[15]

Manufacturing and production

Fang Cheng Bao vehicles are built within BYD's production network. BYD has invested over RMB 30 billion in the Shenshan Special Cooperation Zone, rolling out phases one to four covering battery, motor, electronic-control and complete-vehicle production; the Titanium 7 is produced there alongside the Yangwang U9, Denza Z9, Han and Tang, and mid-to-high-end models such as the Titanium 7 are exported through the Xiaomo port under a 'port-behind-plant' layout.[17] The brand does not separately disclose factory capacity or the latest store count.

Domestic market position

Fang Cheng Bao targets the new-energy hardcore off-road and urban 'box' SUV segments. Full-year 2025 sales were 234,637 units (+316.1% year on year). Gasgoo's January-September 2026 year-to-date column shows 285,436 units and gives no year-on-year rate for that column. September sales were 43,181 units, +79.02% versus 24,121 a year earlier. In that month the Titanium 7 (18,273) and Titanium 7 EV (13,871) totalled 32,144 units, 74.4% of brand sales, and the Bao 5 plus Bao 8 were about 12.1%.[12][14] Media called the Bao 5 the best-selling new-energy hardcore off-road SUV of 2025 (59,836 units), and the Titanium 7 the brand's volume leader (86,259 units in 2025).[12] At group level, BYD's September 2026 production and sales bulletin reports NEV sales of 463,561 units in September and 3,131,576 for January-September, with no brand share. In an investor-relations briefing reported by Eastmoney (reprinting Southern Finance, 11 September 2026), BYD said combined first-half 2026 sales of Fang Cheng Bao, Denza and Yangwang rose 61.0% year on year, and their share of group passenger-vehicle sales rose to 12.8%. The bulletin and this report do not give a September or January-September brand share.[5][28]

Overseas business

Model-level export volumes, overseas brand names and specific shipment batches for Fang Cheng Bao are not in the company filings or A/B-grade reports checked for this page. BYD's September 2026 production and sales bulletin gives group NEV sales only — 463,561 units in September and 3,131,576 for January-September — plus group NEV exports of 180,700 units in September, with no Fang Cheng Bao split.[5] A Nanfang Plus report says mid-to-high-end models including the Titanium 7 leave through the Xiaomo port in the Shenshan zone, but it does not give model-level export counts.[17] On Gasgoo's September 2026 sales page, the Titanium 7 (18,273) and Titanium 7 EV (13,871) total 32,144 units, 74.4% of the brand's 43,181. That page has no export split, so the 32,144 units are September sales, not global deliveries.[14]

Founders and leadership

Brand ownership and governance

Fang Cheng Bao is not a separate listed company; it is a brand of BYD Company Limited, and its sales entity Shenzhen Fangchengbao Automobile Sales Co., Ltd. is 100% owned by BYD (that company was renamed from Shenzhen BYD Auto R&D Co., Ltd. in May 2023).[18][3] The actual controller of parent BYD is Wang Chuanfu, who announced the brand in November 2022 as the listed company's chairman and president.[3][6]

Current management

The brand's public general manager is Xiong Tianbo. When the brand was named in June 2023, China Times reported, on BYD's account, that Xiong Tianbo — formerly director of BYD's automobile-industry office and president of its sales research institute — took up the post of general manager of the Fang Cheng Bao brand sales division, overseeing product planning, channel sales and brand building; the report added that she joined BYD in 2003 and had been foreign-trade manager of BYD Auto Sales and senior manager of the commerce department of the export-trade business division, with extensive overseas experience in Europe, the Americas, Asia-Pacific, the Middle East and Africa.[24] Xinhua (November 2023) and China Fortune Network (April 2025) referred to her as 'general manager of Fang Cheng Bao'; IT Home (October 2026) used 'general manager of the BYD Group Fang Cheng Bao business division'.[8][27][26] These are public business roles only. Fang Cheng Bao has no separate board; its parent BYD's 2025 annual report confirms Wang Chuanfu as controlling shareholder, actual controller, chairman and president, with the company listed as A-shares (002594) and H-shares (01211).[3][4]

Competition

Our judgment: the competitive field. Fang Cheng Bao spans the new-energy hardcore off-road and urban 'box' SUV segments. Its most direct rival is Great Wall Motor's Tank series (Tank 300/400/500/700); the off-road segment was previously dominated by Great Wall's Tank series and the BAIC BJ series.[20] Other nearby players include the Deepal G318; the Bao 8, in the RMB 400,000 class, competes with the Li Auto L9 and AITO M9; and the 2026 Fangcheng S enters the RMB 200,000-300,000 sport-sedan market against the Xiaomi SU7 and Tesla Model 3.[15] This is an analytical selection of the field, not a sales ranking.

Our judgment: strengths. The clearest advantages are DMO technology and BYD's vertically integrated cost and channel base, which let the brand price the Titanium 7 at RMB 179,800-219,800 and reach 234,637 units in 2025, growth driven mainly by the Titanium series.[12][20] The Bao 5 was called the best-selling new-energy hardcore off-road SUV of 2025 by media, and the Bao 8 brings Huawei's driving assist into the segment.[12][10] On channels, the brand shifted from fully direct sales to a 'direct-sale plus dealer' model.[2]

Our judgment: weaknesses and uncertainties. Growth has come with a lower price mix: the full-year 2024 average transaction price was about RMB 332,000, while a 2 January 2026 report put the brand transaction price at RMB 234,000 on a 'to date' basis, listed separately from cumulative sales of 296,737 units. The two figures use different bases and cannot be subtracted directly, but combined with the Titanium series scaling up and the flagship Bao 8 selling only 23,286 units in 2025, our reading is that the higher-priced 'Bao' line has not yet become a second pillar.[25][12][13] Overseas, model-level exports and any use of the Denza name were not confirmed in the filings and A/B reports checked here, so Fang Cheng Bao's standalone recognition abroad cannot be assessed from those materials.[5]

Outlook

Company plans and recent events. On 16 September 2026 the Fangcheng S and Fangcheng S GT were launched, which the brand called the start of a new personalised sedan category.[1][15] On 22 October 2026, Xiong Tianbo, general manager of the BYD Group Fang Cheng Bao business division, said the Titanium 9 would go on sale after 15 November 2026 with show cars and test-drive cars arriving from late October, and that the SHARK pickup would launch and start deliveries between late November and early December 2026, after the Titanium 9.[26] On 16 October 2026 BYD became an official partner of APEC 2026, and Fang Cheng Bao models joined Denza and Yangwang vehicles as official designated cars.[16][17] These are disclosed plans and events; the new sedan line is still ramping up deliveries.

Our judgment. The trajectory depends on three questions: whether the Fangcheng S sedan line can rebuild a higher price band after a 2 January 2026 report put the brand transaction price at RMB 234,000 on a 'to date' basis; whether the 'Bao' hardcore line can scale beyond the Bao 5/Bao 8's current volume; and how the brand is named and how many vehicles it sells overseas, which the checked filings do not disclose.[12][5] The Titanium 7 quickly became the volume leader (86,259 units in 2025 and 32,144 in September 2026), showing that the 'family hard-style' urban SUV is the current growth engine.[12][14] This is our analysis, not a forecast of financial results.

Risks

  • Price-mix pressure. Growth is driven by the lower-priced Titanium series; a 2 January 2026 report put the brand transaction price at RMB 234,000 on a 'to date' basis and the flagship Bao 8 sold only 23,286 units in 2025, leaving the higher-priced line a small share.[12][13]
  • No separate financial disclosure. The brand publishes no brand-level revenue or profit, so its standalone economics cannot be assessed from public filings.[3][4]
  • Entrenched off-road incumbent. The hardcore off-road segment is led by Great Wall's Tank series, and new entrants are intensifying competition.[20]
  • Overseas brand identity. Model-level exports and overseas naming have not been officially disclosed in the filings checked here, so the brand's recognition abroad cannot yet be assessed.[5]
  • Fast product cycle. The 2024 Bao 5 price cut and the rapid 2025-2026 refresh cadence point to a fast cycle; the new sedan/supercar line is unproven at volume.[2][1]
  • Dependence on BYD and partners. The line-up depends on BYD's platforms, and the Bao 8's driving assist relies on Huawei's Qiankun ADS.[10]

概览

方程豹（Fang Cheng Bao）是比亚迪股份有限公司（深交所 002594、港交所 01211）旗下的专业个性化新能源汽车品牌，定位于腾势豪华品牌与仰望超豪华品牌之间。[3][6] 比亚迪董事长兼总裁王传福于 2022 年 11 月 16 日首次官宣一个极具专业性、个性化的全新品牌；2023 年 6 月 9 日，比亚迪将内部代号「F 品牌」正式定名方程豹；同年 8 月 16 日，方程豹在深圳举行品牌暨技术发布会，发布 DMO 超级混动越野平台与首款车型硬派 SUV 豹 5，并亮相由豹 5、豹 8 概念车「SUPER 8」与豹 3 构成的「583」硬派家族。[1][6][7] 「方程豹」由「方程」与「豹」组合而来，官方释义为「用探索数字世界的专业智慧和精神，求解汽车与用户生活的个性化变革」，品牌精神为「一切未知世界都有解」，品牌愿景为「让美好生活不一样」。[1][7] 产品线现分为三大互补系列：「豹」系列高端化越野车、「钛」系列全能化城市车，以及 2026 年新增的「方程」系列全场景多形态个性轿车。[1]

销量增长迅速：2023 年（豹 5 上市首年）交付 5,712 辆，2024 年 56,388 辆，2025 年 234,637 辆（同比 +316.1%），并于 2026 年 8 月 5 日品牌三周年之际累计交付突破 50 万辆。[11] 比亚迪 2025 年年度报告董事长致辞记录「方程豹全年销量同比增长 316%」。[3] 截至研究截止日（2026-10-29），媒体整理的比亚迪口径数据显示 2026 年前三季度方程豹累计销量 285,436 辆，其中 9 月单月 43,181 辆创品牌单月新高。[14] 方程豹是比亚迪旗下品牌、未单独上市，不披露品牌级独立财务数据，母公司层面财务数据见比亚迪（byd）深度研究。[3][4]

现状

车型谱系与定位

截至研究截止日（2026-10-29），方程豹的量产车型包括两款硬派插混越野车——豹 5（2023-11-09 上市，2024 年 7 月焕新后 23.98 万-30.28 万元）与旗舰豹 8（2024-11-12 上市，37.98 万-40.78 万元），以及两款城市「钛」系列车型——纯电 SUV 钛 3（2025-04-16 上市，13.38 万元起）与中大型 SUV 钛 7（2025-09-09 上市，17.98 万-21.98 万元）。[8][10][2][27][20] 2026 年 9 月 16 日，品牌首款轿车方程 S（18.99 万-22.99 万元）与猎装车方程 S GT（21.99 万-23.99 万元）上市，品牌由此进入纯电运动轿车市场。[15][1] 官方品牌历程还记录 2025-2026 年多轮年度改款：豹 5 智驾版（2025 年 2 月）、豹 8 无人机版（2025 年 3 月）、钛 3 闪充版（2026 年 3 月）、钛 7EV 闪充版（2026 年 4 月）与豹 8/豹 5 闪充版（2026 年 5 月）。[1] 品牌最初规划的「583」硬派家族中，豹 3 以概念车「SUPER 3」亮相后未以硬派量产车身份落地，小型 SUV 的位置后由城市定位的钛 3 承接。[9][1]

核心技术

方程豹依托比亚迪垂直整合的技术体系并引入外部合作。其标志性技术是与品牌同日（2023 年 8 月）发布的 DMO 超级混动越野平台，该平台融合比亚迪全新混动非承载式架构与越野专用混动架构，将刀片电池与高强度钢大车架结合为 CTC 电池底盘一体化技术，并兼容云辇-P 智能液压车身控制系统。[6] 发布会报道称 DMO 搭载全球首款纵置 EHS 电混系统、越野专用后驱电总成，以及 1.5T、2.0T 纵置骁云高功率发动机。这里的「全球首款」是发布口径，不是另行核实过的事实。[6] 以豹 5 为例，其系统最大功率 505kW、综合扭矩 760N·m，零百进入 4 秒级，具备 16 种驾驶模式与最小转弯半径 3.4 米的「豹式掉头」，亏电油耗 7.8L/100km、综合续航超 1200km。[8] 豹 8 则搭载 DMO（2.0T）与全功能版云辇-P 液压悬架，系统综合功率 550kW、最大扭矩 760N·m、零百 4.8 秒。[10] 城市「钛」系列使用 EVO+ 纯电平台（钛 3）与 1.5T 插混动力（钛 7），普遍搭载天神之眼智驾与云辇-C 智能阻尼车身控制；上市稿称豹 8 是比亚迪与华为首次联合开创的智能硬派产品，并写明搭载华为乾崑智驾 ADS 3.0，没有把「全球首搭」写成已核实事实。[27][20][10] 2026 年上市的方程 S 系列首搭云辇-M 智能磁流变悬架、第二代刀片电池与闪充技术，CLTC 最高续航 900km。[15]

生产与产能

方程豹车型在比亚迪生产体系中制造。比亚迪在深汕特别合作区总投资超 300 亿元，一至四期逐步铺开，覆盖电池、电机、电控、整车全产业链，方程豹钛 7 与仰望 U9、腾势 Z9、汉、唐等车型均在深汕基地生产；依托「前港后厂、港产联动」布局，钛 7 等中高端车型从深汕小漠港出海。[17] 品牌未单独披露工厂产能与门店最新总数。

国内市场地位

方程豹以「专业个性化」切入新能源硬派越野与城市「方盒子」两大细分市场。2025 年全年销量 234,637 辆、同比 +316.1%。盖世汽车表内 2026 年 1-9 月累计为 285,436 辆，这一列没有同比。9 月销量 43,181 辆，同比 +79.02%（去年同期 24,121 辆）。当月钛 7 为 18,273 辆、钛 7 EV 为 13,871 辆，合计 32,144 辆，占品牌当月销量 74.4%；豹 5 与豹 8 合计约 12.1%。[12][14] 媒体称豹 5 为 2025 年新能源硬派越野 SUV 销冠（59,836 辆），钛 7 为品牌销量主力（2025 年 86,259 辆）。[12] 集团层面，深交所 2026 年 9 月产销快报的新能源销量为 9 月 463,561 辆、1-9 月累计 3,131,576 辆，快报没有品牌占比。东方财富转南方财经 2026-09-11 的投资者关系活动报道称，方程豹、腾势与仰望 2026 年上半年合计销量同比增长 61.0%，占集团乘用车总销量比重提升至 12.8%。快报和这篇报道都没有给出 9 月或 1-9 月的品牌占比。[5][28]

海外业务

方程豹的分车型出口量、海外销售名称和具体发运批次，在已核对的公司公告与 A/B 级报道中未获官方披露。比亚迪 2026 年 9 月产销快报只列集团新能源销量：9 月 463,561 辆，1-9 月累计 3,131,576 辆，并注明 9 月出口新能源汽车合计 180,700 辆，没有方程豹品牌拆分。[5] 南方+报道深汕小漠港承担钛 7 等中高端车型出海，但没有分车型出口辆数。[17] 盖世汽车 2026 年 9 月销量页中，钛 7 为 18,273 辆、钛 7 EV 为 13,871 辆，合计 32,144 辆，占品牌当月 43,181 辆的 74.4%。该页没有出口拆分，这 32,144 辆是 9 月销量，不是全球交付。[14]

创始人与管理层

品牌归属与治理

方程豹并非单独上市公司，是比亚迪股份有限公司旗下品牌；其销售主体深圳方程豹汽车销售有限公司由比亚迪 100% 控股（该公司由深圳市比亚迪汽车研发有限公司于 2023 年 5 月更名而来）。[18][3] 母公司比亚迪的实际控制人为王传福，他于 2022 年 11 月以上市公司董事长兼总裁身份官宣该品牌。[3][6]

现任管理层

品牌公开的总经理为熊甜波。2023 年 6 月品牌定名之际，《华夏时报》援引比亚迪方面消息报道：原比亚迪股份有限公司汽车产业办公室主任、汽车销售研究院院长熊甜波出任比亚迪方程豹品牌销售事业部总经理，全面主导品牌产品规划、渠道销售、品牌建设；报道并称其 2003 年加入比亚迪，历任比亚迪汽车销售有限公司外贸部经理、汽车产业群出口贸易事业部商务部高级经理，在欧美、亚太、中东非等海外市场有丰富工作经验。[24] 新华网（2023 年 11 月）与中国财富网（2025 年 4 月）在报道中称其为「方程豹汽车总经理」，IT 之家（2026 年 10 月）则称其为「比亚迪集团方程豹事业部总经理」。[8][27][26] 以上均为公开职务与商业履历。方程豹不设独立董事会；其母公司比亚迪 2025 年年度报告确认王传福为控股股东、实际控制人并任董事长兼总裁，公司 A 股（002594）、H 股（01211）上市。[3][4]

竞争格局

我们的判断：竞争范围。方程豹横跨新能源硬派越野与城市「方盒子」两大细分市场，最直接的对手是长城汽车坦克系列（坦克 300/400/500/700）；越野市场此前主要由长城坦克系列与北京越野 BJ 系列主导。[20] 相近的还有深蓝 G318 等；豹 8 处于 40 万级市场，与理想 L9、问界 M9 等竞争；2026 年上市的方程 S 进入 20-30 万元运动轿车市场，与小米 SU7、特斯拉 Model 3 竞争。[15] 此为分析所选的竞争范围，不是销量排名。

我们的判断：优势。方程豹最突出的优势是 DMO 技术与比亚迪垂直整合的成本、渠道基础，使其能以 17.98 万-21.98 万元定价钛 7，并在 2025 年实现 234,637 辆销量，增长主要由钛系列带动。[12][20] 豹 5 被媒体称为 2025 年新能源硬派越野 SUV 销冠；豹 8 将华为乾崑智驾引入硬派越野，形成差异化。[12][10] 渠道上，品牌从全直营转向「直营+经销商」双模式。[2]

我们的判断：短板与不确定性。销量增长伴随价格带下移：2024 年全年成交均价约 33.2 万元，而 2026-01-02 报道按「截止目前」写出的品牌成交均价为 23.4 万元，与同文累计销量 296,737 辆分列，不是覆盖这批销量的累计成交均价。两种口径不同、不能直接相减，但结合钛系列放量与旗舰豹 8 2025 年销量仅 23,286 辆，我们的判断是高价位「豹」系列尚未成为第二支柱。[25][12][13] 海外方面，分车型出口和是否使用腾势品牌名，在已核对的公告与 A/B 级报道中没有得到确认，方程豹在海外的独立认知因此还无法评估。[5]

未来展望

公司公开规划与近期事件。2026 年 9 月 16 日，方程 S 与方程 S GT 正式上市，官方称「开创个性轿车新品类」。[1][15] 10 月 22 日，比亚迪集团方程豹事业部总经理熊甜波公布新车进度：钛 9 将于 11 月 15 日后正式上市，展车、试驾车 10 月底陆续到店；SHARK 鲨鱼皮卡在钛 9 发布后，计划 11 月底至 12 月初上市并同步交付。[26] 比亚迪于 2026 年 10 月 16 日成为 2026 年 APEC 会议官方合作伙伴，方程豹车型与腾势、仰望等一同成为官方指定用车。[16][17] 以上为披露的计划与已发生事件，全新轿车线尚在交付爬坡。

我们的判断。方程豹的走向取决于三点：一是方程 S 轿车线能否在 2026-01-02 报道的「截止目前」品牌成交均价 23.4 万元之后重建高价位产品；二是「豹」硬派系列能否在豹 5/豹 8 现有销量上继续放量；三是海外以什么名称销售、卖了多少，已核对的公告没有披露。[12][5] 钛 7 上市后迅速成为销量主力（2025 年 86,259 辆、2026 年 9 月 32,144 辆），显示「家用硬派」城市 SUV 是当前最重要的增长引擎。[12][14] 以上为我们的分析，不构成对财务结果的预测。

风险

  • 价格带下移。增长主要由价格较低的钛系列驱动，2026-01-02 报道按「截止目前」写出的品牌成交均价为 23.4 万元，旗舰豹 8 2025 年全年仅 23,286 辆，高价位产品占比有限。[12][13]
  • 无独立财务披露。品牌未披露营收与利润，无法从公开文件评估其独立盈利能力。[3][4]
  • 在位越野对手。硬派越野细分市场由长城坦克系列主导，新入局者加剧竞争。[20]
  • 海外品牌身份。分车型出口和海外销售名称在已核对的公告中未获官方披露，海外独立认知因此还无法评估。[5]
  • 产品切换快。2024 年豹 5 官降与 2025-2026 年高频焕新显示产品周期较快，全新轿车/跑车线尚未经受规模验证。[2][1]
  • 依赖比亚迪与合作伙伴。产品依赖比亚迪平台，豹 8 智驾依赖华为乾崑 ADS。[10]
