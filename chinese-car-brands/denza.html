Overview

Denza is the premium new-energy vehicle brand of BYD Company Limited (SZSE: 002594 / HKEX: 01211). Its predecessor was a joint venture between BYD and Daimler (parent of Mercedes-Benz) set up in 2010 as Shenzhen BYD Daimler New Technology Co., Ltd., one of China's first joint-venture brands focused on new-energy vehicles; after successive equity transfers it became wholly owned by BYD in September 2024, with BYD Auto Industry Co., Ltd. (a wholly owned subsidiary of BYD Company Limited) as the direct 100% shareholder.[1][10][29][6] Denza positions itself as a 'technology, safety, new-luxury' brand under the proposition 'a new realm, extraordinary momentum', with a line-up spanning MPV, SUV, sedan/GT and supercar; in BYD's brand matrix it sits above the mainstream Dynasty/Ocean series and below the Yangwang ultra-luxury brand.[1][14]

Denza's relaunch turned on the D9 MPV, launched in August 2022: it sold 9,803 units that year and then topped the China MPV annual sales chart for three straight years (2023-2025), with 2025 full-year retail of 103,460 units (CPCA retail figure).[13][40][22] By BYD's official figure, Denza sold 157,134 vehicles in 2025, up 24.7% year on year, with a brand average transaction price of about RMB 361,000.[9][14] Among BYD's three premium brands in 2025, Denza (157,134 units) trailed Fangchengbao (about 234,600 units) and led Yangwang (4,785 units).[39] From 2024 the brand accelerated its overseas push, entering Hong Kong and Southeast Asia, then Europe in 2025-2026.[1][20]

Before the 2022 relaunch, the joint-venture-era Denza lost money for years. China Economic Net reported in 2023 that cumulative losses exceeded RMB 4 billion over 2016-2021; an earlier 2019 report put cumulative losses over the previous five years at about RMB 3.5 billion - different periods and amounts.[28][27] Sales after the relaunch were not monotonically rising: 9,803 units in 2022, about 127,800 in 2023, about 126,000 in 2024 (a slight dip), and 157,134 in 2025.[22][26][9] As a wholly owned brand that is not separately listed, Denza publishes no independent financial statements; parent-level figures are in the BYD deep research.[4]

Current operations

Model line-up and positioning

As of the research cut-off (2026-10-29), the official Denza China website lists the D9 MPV family (including the four-seat Chuangling edition), the N8L and N9 large SUVs, the Z9 GT, a technology-luxury intelligent sedan, and the Z electric supercar; a new wide-body five-seat SUV, the new N8, opened reservations on 2026-10-26 with a launch planned for November 2026, at 5,150 mm long with a 3,075 mm wheelbase and pure-electric (CLTC 1,003 km) and plug-in hybrid (CLTC 435 km electric) versions.[2][8] The brand's stated positioning is 'technology, safety, new-luxury'; the flagship D9 and N9 anchor volume, and the Z9 GT became the second volume driver after its March 2026 renewal.[1][14] The official site shows more than 400 service outlets across 180-plus cities.[2]

Development phases

Denza's development falls roughly into three phases: the exploratory joint-venture era (2010-2021) with the Denza 300/400/500 and X, marked by weak sales and persistent losses; the relaunch and ramp-up phase (2022-2024) once BYD took control, opened by the D9; and the globalisation phase from September 2024, when it became wholly owned and entered Southeast Asia and Europe.[11][1]

Core technologies

Denza reuses BYD's technology stack. Its models carry the e3 (Yisanfang) three-motor intelligent-control platform, first released in August 2024, integrating three-motor independent drive with independent rear-wheel steering for features such as extreme turning, tank turn and crab walk; they also use the DiSus body-control system and the God's Eye high-level driving assist (newer models use God's Eye 5.0).[1][14] From 2026 new models carry BYD's second-generation Blade Battery and megawatt-class flash charging: the 2026 Z9 GT has a claimed 1,036 km pure-electric range, and the second-generation D9 carries the battery as standard with plug-in hybrid CLTC 401 km and pure-electric 800 km range, all with God's Eye 5.0.[7][30][33]

Positioning and price band

Denza targets the RMB 300,000-500,000 mainstream-luxury band. The brand's 2025 average transaction price was about RMB 361,000; the N9 launched at RMB 389,800-449,800 and the 2026 Z9 GT at RMB 269,800-369,800, competing with AITO, Li Auto and Zeekr in the RMB 250,000-500,000 range.[9][14]

Production and parent

Denza does not disclose separate production capacity; its vehicles are built in BYD plants - for example, the second-generation D9 rolled off the line as BYD's 16-millionth NEV at the Changsha plant on 2026-04-17.[33] Parent BYD sold 4,602,436 vehicles in 2025, including 1,049,601 passenger cars and pickups overseas (up 145% year on year), and spent RMB 63.44 billion on R&D in 2025; in March 2026 BYD released the second-generation Blade Battery and flash charging and announced a 'flash-charging China' plan of 20,000 stations nationwide by year-end.[9][33][4]

Overseas business

Denza expands abroad on BYD's network. On 2026-04-08 it held a European launch at the Paris Opera, unveiling the Z9 GT and D9; the Z9 GT opened presales in seven European countries from about EUR 115,000, and the company plans to cover more than 30 European countries with over 150 retail stores by end-2026.[20][17] In Southeast Asia the D9 topped luxury-MPV sales in Indonesia, Thailand and Malaysia for multiple months, and has entered Singapore and Brazil.[40]

Founders and leadership

Brand ownership and governance

Denza is not a separate listed company. It began as Shenzhen BYD Daimler New Technology Co., Ltd. (renamed Shenzhen Denza New Energy Vehicle Co., Ltd. in 2016), a 50:50 joint venture with Daimler. In December 2021 the two sides agreed to raise BYD's stake to 90% and lower Daimler's to 10%; the 40% transfer was completed in June 2022, with Mercedes-Benz Group's 2022 annual report naming BYD Automotive Industry as the counterparty.[6][10][11][29] On 2024-09-14 Mercedes-Benz transferred its remaining 10%, after which Denza became 100% directly held by BYD Auto Industry Co., Ltd., a wholly owned subsidiary of BYD Company Limited. BYD Company Limited's actual controller is Wang Chuanfu.[5]

Current management

The current Denza sales-division general manager is Li Hui, who took the role on 2025-07-03 under BYD's rotation system. He joined BYD in 2004 and previously served as assistant general manager of BYD Auto Finance and general manager of BYD's rail-transit business, and in 2024 became general manager of the Denza-Fangchengbao direct-sales business unit.[25][32] From late 2025 he has appeared in that role in Denza's official releases and media interviews, including a November 2025 capital-markets event and 2026 interviews on the overseas push and the Z supercar.[3][17]

Former leadership

Zhao Changjiang led Denza from late 2021, when he was assigned to take over and relaunch the brand, until July 2025. He joined BYD in 2009 from a frontline sales role, became the company's youngest sales-company general manager in 2017 at age 31, and took over Denza around 2021; during his tenure the D9 became China's annual MPV sales champion.[18][26][13] On 2025-10-24 he announced his departure from BYD, and on 2026-01-13 announced he was joining Zhijie (a Chery-Huawei brand) as executive director and executive vice president.[19][18] The above are public roles and business records only.

Competition

Our judgment: the competitive field. By Denza's MPV, full-size SUV and GT line-up, its main rivals include domestic premium new-energy models such as AITO (Seres/Huawei), Li Auto and Zeekr, plus the Voyah Dreamer MPV, and incumbent MPV and SUV models such as the Buick GL8 and Toyota Sienna/Granvia. This is an analytical selection, not a sales ranking.[14][21]

Our judgment: strengths. Denza's clearest advantage is the D9's entrenched position: it has been China's best-selling MPV for three straight years (2023-2025) with cumulative sales above 350,000 units, backed by BYD's vertically integrated powertrain and battery stack.[13] The brand also benefits from BYD's technology platforms (e3, DiSus, God's Eye, second-generation Blade Battery and flash charging) and a growing overseas network built on the parent's infrastructure.[1][17] With a brand average transaction price of about RMB 361,000, Denza is one of the few domestic new-energy brands competing in the premium band above RMB 300,000.[9]

Our judgment: weaknesses and uncertainties. Sales remain concentrated in the D9, and the brand has yet to build a second D9-scale hit; the Z9 GT is only now becoming a second volume driver in 2026.[21][15] The N7 and the original N8 sold modestly, and the brand went through management turnover in 2025 (the Zhao Changjiang-Li Hui handover).[19] In full-size SUVs media note the N9 faces AITO M9/M8; in MPVs the Voyah Dreamer and new entrants are eroding the D9's lead.[21][14]

Outlook

Company plans. Denza's stated goals include deepening the 'technology, safety, new-luxury' positioning and upgrading its overseas push from product export to brand-value export. Sales-division general manager Li Hui said in 2026 that Denza plans to enter dozens more European countries within two years and that the N9 will successively enter Europe and Asia-Pacific markets.[17] The company plans European coverage of more than 30 countries and over 150 retail stores by end-2026; BYD also plans 6,000 overseas flash-charging stations, 3,000 of them in Europe.[20] On the product side, the second-generation D9 and the flash-charging N9 launched in 2026, and Li Hui described 2026 as a product-filled year including the Z supercar and the wide five-seat N8 opening reservations in October 2026.[16][8] These are disclosed plans and targets, not completed results.

Our judgment. The second-generation D9, Z9 GT, N8L, the Z supercar and the wide five-seat N8 give Denza its broadest line-up to date. Whether growth is durable depends on whether the brand can sustain D9 demand, broaden the Z9/N9 franchises, and convert its European and Asia-Pacific expansion into volume amid import-policy uncertainty. This is our analysis, not a forecast of financial results.[15][20]

Risks

  • D9 concentration. The D9 still drives most of Denza's volume and is the only model with a durable franchise; the brand has not yet built a second D9-scale hit.[21][13]
  • No separate financial disclosure. Denza publishes no brand-level revenue or profit, so standalone profitability cannot be assessed from public filings.[4]
  • Management turnover. The brand's top sales executive changed in 2025 (Zhao Changjiang rotated out and later left BYD), adding execution uncertainty.[19]
  • Overseas risks. Denza's European push is at an early stage and its overseas prices are several times domestic levels (the Z9 GT opens at about EUR 115,000); import duties and local demand shifts could affect the plan to cover 30-plus countries.[20]
  • Competition. In full-size SUVs the N9 faces AITO M9/M8 and other rivals; in MPVs the Voyah Dreamer and new entrants are eroding the D9's lead.[21][14]
  • Product transition and price band. The second-generation D9's presale price (RMB 389,800-489,800) was markedly higher than the outgoing model, and the N7 and original N8 have been wound down, creating volume volatility risk during the transition.[31][15]

概览

腾势（Denza）是比亚迪股份有限公司（深交所 002594 / 港交所 01211）旗下的高端新能源汽车品牌。其前身为比亚迪与戴姆勒（Daimler，梅赛德斯-奔驰母公司）于 2010 年合资设立的深圳比亚迪戴姆勒新技术有限公司，是中国最早专注新能源的合资品牌之一；历经多次股权调整，2024 年 9 月起成为比亚迪全资品牌，直接股东为比亚迪汽车工业有限公司（比亚迪股份有限公司的全资子公司）。[1][10][29][6] 腾势自我定位为「科技·安全·新豪华」，品牌主张「启新境 势不凡」，产品覆盖 MPV、SUV、轿车/GT、超跑等品类；在比亚迪品牌矩阵中位于王朝/海洋系列之上、仰望超豪华品牌之下。[1][14]

腾势的复兴始于 2022 年 8 月上市的 D9 MPV：该车当年销售 9,803 辆，此后 2023-2025 连续三年蝉联中国 MPV 市场年度销量冠军，2025 年全年零售 103,460 辆（乘联会零售口径）。[13][40][22] 按比亚迪官方口径，腾势品牌 2025 年销售 157,134 辆，同比增长 24.7%，品牌成交均价约 36.1 万元。[9][14] 在比亚迪三大高端品牌中，2025 年腾势（157,134 辆）低于方程豹（约 23.46 万辆）、高于仰望（4,785 辆）。[39] 2024 年起品牌加速出海，先后进入中国香港与东南亚，并于 2025-2026 年进入欧洲。[1][20]

在 2022 年焕新之前，合资时期的腾势长期亏损：中国经济网 2023 年报道称 2016-2021 年累计亏损超 40 亿元；另有 2019 年报道称「近 5 年累计亏损约 35 亿元」，两者期间与口径不同。[28][27] 焕新后销量并非逐年单调上升：2022 年 9,803 辆、2023 年约 12.78 万辆、2024 年约 12.6 万辆（小幅回落）、2025 年 157,134 辆。[22][26][9] 腾势为全资品牌、未单独上市，不披露独立财务报表，母公司层面财务数据见比亚迪（byd）深度研究。[4]

现状

车型谱系与定位

截至研究截止日（2026-10-29），腾势中国官网在售车型包括 D9 MPV 家族（含四座创领版）、大型 SUV N8L 与 N9、猎装轿跑 Z9GT、科技豪华智能轿车，以及纯电超跑 Z；一款全新宽五座 SUV「新款腾势 N8」于 2026-10-26 开启预订、计划 2026 年 11 月上市，车长 5,150 mm、轴距 3,075 mm，提供纯电（CLTC 1,003 km）与插混（CLTC 纯电 435 km）两种动力。[2][8] 品牌定位「科技·安全·新豪华」，旗舰 D9 与 N9 构成销量基盘，Z9GT 于 2026 年 3 月换代后成为第二大走量车型。[1][14] 官网显示全国服务网点超 400 家、覆盖 180 多个城市。[2]

发展脉络

腾势发展大致分三个阶段：2010-2021 年与戴姆勒合资的探索期（腾势 300/400/500、腾势 X，销量低迷、持续亏损）；2022-2024 年比亚迪主导后的焕新爬坡期（以 D9 打开 MPV 市场）；2024 年 9 月成为全资品牌后的全球化阶段（进入东南亚与欧洲）。[11][1]

核心技术

腾势复用比亚迪技术栈。车型搭载 2024 年 8 月首发的「易三方」三电机整车智能控制平台，融合三电机独立驱动与后轮双电机独立转向，具备极致转向、圆规掉头、智能蟹行等功能；并应用云辇车身控制系统与「天神之眼」高阶智驾（新车型普遍搭载天神之眼 5.0）。[1][14] 自 2026 年起新车型搭载比亚迪第二代刀片电池与兆瓦级闪充：2026 款 Z9GT 纯电续航 1036 公里；第二代 D9 标配第二代刀片电池与闪充，插混版 CLTC 纯电续航 401 公里、纯电版 800 公里，全系标配天神之眼 5.0。[7][30][33]

品牌定位与价格带

腾势主攻 30 万-50 万元主流豪华价格带。2025 年品牌成交均价约 36.1 万元；N9 上市价 38.98 万-44.98 万元，Z9GT 2026 款 26.98 万-36.98 万元，在 25 万-50 万元区间与问界、理想、极氪等品牌竞争。[9][14]

生产与母公司

腾势未单独披露产能，产品依托比亚迪工厂生产，例如第二代 D9 于 2026-04-17 在长沙工厂作为比亚迪第 1600 万辆新能源汽车下线。[33] 母公司比亚迪 2025 年全年销售 4,602,436 辆，其中乘用车及皮卡海外销售 1,049,601 辆（同比 +145%），全年研发投入 634.41 亿元；2026 年 3 月比亚迪发布第二代刀片电池与闪充技术，并提出「闪充中国」战略、年底前全国建设 2 万座闪充站。[9][33][4]

海外业务

腾势依托比亚迪海外网络出海。2026-04-08 在巴黎歌剧院举行欧洲上市发布会，向欧洲推出 Z9GT 与 D9，Z9GT 在七个欧洲国家开启预售（约 11.5 万欧元起）；公司计划到 2026 年底覆盖欧洲 30 多个国家、建成超 150 家零售门店。[20][17] 在东南亚，D9 曾多月登顶印尼、泰国、马来西亚豪华 MPV 销量榜首，并进入新加坡、巴西等市场。[40]

创始人与管理层

品牌归属与治理

腾势并非单独上市公司。其前身深圳比亚迪戴姆勒新技术有限公司（2016 年更名深圳腾势新能源汽车有限公司）为与戴姆勒 50:50 的合资公司；2021 年 12 月双方约定将比亚迪持股提高至 90%、戴姆勒降至 10%，40% 股权转让于 2022 年 6 月完成，梅赛德斯-奔驰集团 2022 年年报披露交易对手为 BYD Automotive Industry。[6][10][11][29] 2024-09-14 梅赛德斯-奔驰转让剩余 10%，此后腾势由比亚迪汽车工业有限公司（比亚迪股份有限公司全资子公司）直接持股 100%。比亚迪股份有限公司实际控制人为王传福。[5]

现任管理层

现任腾势销售事业部总经理为李慧，2025-07-03 按比亚迪轮岗制度接任。其 2004 年加入比亚迪，历任比亚迪汽车金融有限公司总经理助理、比亚迪轨道业务总经理，2024 年出任腾势方程豹直营事业部总经理。[25][32] 自 2025 年末起，其以该职务出现在腾势官方发布与媒体访谈中，包括 2025 年 11 月资本市场活动及 2026 年关于品牌出海与 Z 超跑的多次采访。[3][17]

前任管理层

赵长江自 2021 年末受命接管并焕新腾势品牌，执掌至 2025 年 7 月。其 2009 年入职比亚迪，从一线销售岗位起步，2017 年 31 岁时成为比亚迪销售公司总经理，约 2021 年接管腾势；在其任内 D9 成为中国 MPV 市场年度销量冠军。[18][26][13] 2025-10-24 其官宣从比亚迪离职，2026-01-13 官宣加入智界（奇瑞与华为合作品牌），任执行董事兼执行副总裁。[19][18] 以上均为公开职务与商业履历。

竞争格局

我们的判断：竞争范围。按腾势的 MPV、全尺寸 SUV 与 GT 产品线，其主要对手包括问界（赛力斯/华为）、理想、极氪等国内高端新能源车型，以及岚图梦想家 MPV，另有别克 GL8、丰田赛那/格瑞维亚等在位 MPV 与 SUV 车型。此为分析所选的竞争范围，不是销量排名。[14][21]

我们的判断：优势。腾势最突出的优势是 D9 的既有地位：其已连续三年（2023-2025）成为中国 MPV 市场销量冠军，累计销量超 35 万辆，背后有比亚迪垂直整合的三电与电池体系支撑。[13] 品牌还受益于比亚迪的技术平台（易三方、云辇、天神之眼、第二代刀片电池、闪充），以及依托母公司海外基础设施建立的渠道网络。[1][17] 腾势 2025 年品牌成交均价约 36.1 万元，是少数在 30 万元以上豪华价位竞争的自主新能源品牌。[9]

我们的判断：短板与不确定性。销量仍高度集中于 D9，品牌尚未打造出第二款 D9 级爆款，Z9GT 直到 2026 年才逐渐成为第二走量车型。[21][15] N7 与老款 N8 销量有限，品牌还经历了管理层更替（2025 年赵长江与李慧的轮岗交接）。[19] 在全尺寸 SUV 领域，媒体指出 N9 面临问界 M9/M8 的竞争；在 MPV 领域，岚图梦想家等竞品正削弱 D9 的领先地位。[21][14]

未来展望

公司公开规划。腾势的公开目标包括深化「科技·安全·新豪华」定位，并将出海由产品出口升级为品牌价值出口。腾势销售事业部总经理李慧 2026 年表示，腾势计划两年内进入数十个欧洲国家，N9 将陆续进入欧洲及亚太市场。[17] 公司计划到 2026 年底覆盖欧洲 30 多个国家、建成超 150 家零售门店；比亚迪并规划全球 6000 座海外闪充站、其中欧洲 3000 座。[20] 产品方面，第二代 D9 与 N9 闪充版于 2026 年上市，李慧称 2026 年为产品大年，包括全新超跑 Z 及 2026 年 10 月开启预订的宽五座 N8。[16][8] 以上为披露的计划与目标，不是已实现的业绩。

我们的判断。第二代 D9、Z9GT、N8L、全新超跑 Z 与宽五座 N8 使腾势拥有迄今最广的产品线。但增长能否持续，取决于品牌能否稳住 D9 需求、做大 Z9/N9 车系，并在进口政策不确定的情况下把欧洲与亚太扩张转化为实际销量。以上为我们的分析，不构成对财务结果的预测。[15][20]

风险

  • D9 依赖。D9 仍是腾势销量的绝对主力，也是唯一具备稳定基本盘的车型；品牌尚未打造出第二款 D9 级爆款。[21][13]
  • 无单独财务披露。腾势未披露品牌层面营收与利润，无法从公开文件评估品牌独立盈利能力。[4]
  • 管理层更替。2025 年品牌销售负责人发生更替（赵长江轮岗离任、后离开比亚迪），带来执行层面的不确定性。[19]
  • 海外风险。腾势欧洲布局尚处早期，海外定价数倍于国内（Z9GT 欧洲预售约 11.5 万欧元起），进口关税与当地需求变化可能影响其覆盖 30 多个国家的计划。[20]
  • 竞争。全尺寸 SUV 领域 N9 面临问界 M9/M8 等竞品，MPV 领域岚图梦想家及新入局者正削弱 D9 的领先地位。[21][14]
  • 产品切换与价格带。第二代 D9 预售价（38.98 万-48.98 万元）较现款明显上调，N7、老款 N8 等车型已明显收缩，产品切换期存在销量波动风险。[31][15]
