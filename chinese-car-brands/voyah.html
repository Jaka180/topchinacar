Overview

Voyah (岚图) is the premium smart new-energy vehicle brand of Dongfeng Motor Corporation, one of China's three central automotive SOEs. Dongfeng released the brand in July 2020, and the operating entity Voyah Automotive Technology Co., Ltd. was established on 26 June 2021 before being converted into a joint-stock company and listed by introduction on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange under 07489.HK on 19 March 2026 [1][6]. The brand designs, develops, manufactures and sells new-energy passenger vehicles across the sedan, SUV and MPV segments, positioned in the high-end market at a price band of roughly RMB 200,000-500,000 [1]. It grew sales from 19,409 units in 2022 to 150,169 in 2025 and turned profitable in 2025 with revenue of RMB 34.865 billion and net profit of about RMB 1.02 billion [6]. Starting from Dongfeng's internal 'H division' in 2019, Voyah completed its sedan/SUV/MPV line-up within about five years and became the first central-SOE premium NEV brand to reach 300,000 cumulative units off the line [1]. This page is a brand study of Voyah itself; the parent group Dongfeng Motor Corporation is covered in its own research.

Current operations

Business structure

Voyah's revenue is dominated by passenger-vehicle sales, with a fast-growing mix of parts, after-market services and technical services. In H1 2026 total revenue was RMB 18.16 billion (up 42.4%), of which vehicle sales were RMB 16.47 billion (up 36.2%), after-market revenue RMB 1.19 billion (up 113.5%) and technical-service revenue RMB 457 million (up about 591.7%); overseas revenue was RMB 2.54 billion (up 51.5%), and gross margin fell to 17.7% from 21.4% a year earlier [4]. Almost all revenue is earned in China, where the Voyah Dream MPV contributes the largest share of sales; per the prospectus, the Dream sold 26,518 units (52.7% of total sales) in 2023, 46,687 (58.3%) in 2024 and 40,111 (60.15%) in the first seven months of 2025 [1][35]. Over 2022-2024, passenger-vehicle sales were 89.0%, 96.1% and 94.9% of revenue respectively, with parts sales of RMB 140 million, 330 million and 600 million and other income of RMB 530 million, 170 million and 390 million [15].

Technology and platforms

Voyah organises its self-developed capabilities into five technology bases: the platform architecture (including the ESSA native intelligent electric architecture and the centralised SOA electronic/electrical architecture, named the Tianyuan architecture), the Lanhai (岚海) powertrain, the Xiaoyao (逍遥) intelligent cockpit, the Kunpeng (鲲鹏) intelligent-driving system and the Voyah technology ecosystem [35][23]. The ESSA architecture is described by the company as the world's first native intelligent electric architecture compatible with pure-electric, plug-in-hybrid and range-extender powertrains, with about 90% component commonality [11]. The 800V Lanhai intelligent super-hybrid delivers 360-410 km of pure-electric range and over 1,400 km of combined range on mid-to-large hybrid models [11]. In intelligent driving Voyah runs a dual track of self-development and open cooperation: it develops its own Kunpeng ADAS, and cooperates with Huawei, whose Qiankun ADS 4 and Harmony cockpit 5 equip the Qiankun-series versions of models such as the 2026 Dream, while the Kunpeng series of the same model uses the self-developed Kunpeng ADAS 2.0 [36][18]. As of 31 December 2025 Voyah held 1,874 granted patents and 5,405 filed patent applications, with invention patents about 88% of the total [29]. The Tianyuan centralised E/E architecture was included in SASAC's recommended catalogue of central-SOE technology achievements and has been licensed to more than 10 domestic brands [27]. Toward L3 autonomy, by end-2025 Voyah had completed a cumulative 110,000 km of on-road testing and 900,000 km of simulation testing; the Taishan Ultra carries an 896-line lidar [29].

Manufacturing and capacity

Voyah has two plants - Yunfeng and Huangjin - in the Wuhan Economic & Technological Development Zone with combined annual capacity of about 450,000 units; the Huangjin plant's rooftop solar generates about 20 GWh of electricity a year [10][29]. Both plants operate industrial-4.0 digital production lines covering stamping, welding, painting and final assembly [25].

Domestic market position

As of 31 July 2025 Voyah's sales-and-service network covered 148 cities with 407 stores (192 self-operated and 215 partner-operated), and by 30 June 2026 this had grown to 509 stores across 170 cities [35][17]. Citing CIC data, media coverage of the listing noted that Voyah's 2024 gross margin ranked second among Chinese NEV makers and that the Dream was the No.2 high-end NEV MPV by 2024 sales [18].

Overseas business

Overseas sales grew from 5,647 units in 2023 to 8,419 in 2024 and 18,333 in 2025 [1]. Voyah formally entered the Saudi market with a launch event in Riyadh on 17 June 2026, and by end-June 2026 was present in 40 countries and regions with over 240 overseas outlets; it is deepening its position in Europe, building out the Middle East and preparing right-hand-drive models [33][10].

Founders and leadership

Founder

Voyah has no private founder. It is a brand created by Dongfeng Motor Corporation (a central SOE), prepared from 2019 through Dongfeng's internal 'H division' and formally released in July 2020; You Zheng, a Dongfeng group vice-president, served as its first chairman from 2019 until September 2025 [6][1].

Key management (as of 2026-10-29)

  • Lu Fang (卢放) - chairman, party secretary and executive director. He served as CEO from June 2021 to December 2025 and became chairman on 22 September 2025. He joined Dongfeng Motor Group in December 2018 as technical lead for the strategic-planning special project and held that role until May 2021. Before Dongfeng he worked at FAW-Volkswagen from 1998 (rising to section chief for passive safety), then at FAW Car as deputy director of the product department from April 2014 to July 2018 and subsequently project-management director of the Benteng development institute, and was technical director of Changchun FAW Fusheng Lear Automotive Seating Systems from July to December 2018. The prospectus biography describes him as aged 50. He earned a bachelor's degree in automotive and tractor engineering from Tongji University in July 1999, a master's degree in vehicle engineering and an MBA from Jilin University in December 2006, and a doctorate in vehicle engineering in June 2014; he was rated a professor-level senior engineer in January 2017, and also served as chief brand officer from March 2022 to December 2024 [12][6][9].
  • Jiang Tao (蒋焘) - executive director, general manager, chief executive officer and chief operating officer. He has been Voyah's COO since the company's establishment in June 2021, became an executive director on 22 September 2025 and CEO in December 2025 (media report 10 December). The prospectus biography describes him as aged 46. He earned a bachelor's degree in plastic-forming processes and equipment (mould design and manufacturing) from Hefei University of Technology in June 2001 and a master's degree in materials science and engineering from Tsinghua University in July 2004. Before Voyah he was a quality-assurance engineer at Dongfeng Nissan (2004-2010), a section chief for the welding and final-assembly shops at Guangzhou Fengshen Motor (2011-2016), director of the engineering-affairs (gongwu) department and then general manager of Dongfeng Motor Co., Ltd. Dalian Branch (2016-2019), and COO of the Voyah project preparatory group at Dongfeng Motor Group (2019-2021) [12][9].

Board and shareholding

The board has nine directors: executive directors Lu Fang and Jiang Tao; non-executive directors Liao Xianzhi, Yang Yanding, Hu Xiao and Qin Jie; and independent non-executive directors Yang Yong, Xin Dinghua and Fu Bingfeng [3]. Dongfeng Motor Corporation is the controlling shareholder: after the listing and privatisation it directly held about 58.45%, and together with its controlled entity Dongfeng Asset Management (about 3.67%) and the employee platform Wuhan Woya (about 7.35%) held and exercised about 69.47% of the votes [12]. As of 25 June 2026, Dongfeng Motor Corporation, Dongfeng Asset Management, Dongfeng Motor (Hong Kong) International Co., Limited and Wuhan Woya together held 2,438,479,300 domestic shares and 168,100,609 H shares, about 70.83% of the shares in issue [38]. Other pre-listing investors include the mixed-ownership reform fund (about 2.85%), BOC Financial Asset Investment (about 2.56%), ICBC Financial Asset Investment, Wuhan Economic Development Fund and ABC Financial Asset Investment [12].

Competition

Competitive position: our assessment

Our assessment: Voyah competes in China's RMB 200,000-500,000 premium NEV segment against NIO, Li Auto, Xpeng and Zeekr, as well as Huawei-backed brands (AITO and others) and premium JV EVs [1]. Its scale is still well below the leaders, with 150,169 units sold in 2025, and the company is highly dependent on one model - the Dream MPV, which contributed 60.15% of sales in the first seven months of 2025 [35][6].

Strengths and constraints: our assessment

Our assessment: Voyah's main strengths are the manufacturing, engineering and supply-chain resources of Dongfeng, a strong technology base (ESSA architecture, Tianyuan SOA architecture, 800V Lanhai hybrid, Kunpeng ADAS and Huawei cooperation), and profitability achieved in 2025 - a rare outcome among Chinese NEV start-ups. Its gross margin rose from 8.3% in 2022 to 20.9% in 2025 [1]. Citing CIC data, media coverage of the listing noted that Voyah's 2024 gross margin ranked second among Chinese NEV makers and that the Dream was the No.2 high-end NEV MPV by sales in 2024 (about 47,000 units) [18]. But the return to a net loss of RMB 389 million in H1 2026 on higher material costs and spending shows the profit is not yet stable, and dependence on the MPV segment concentrates its exposure to a small market - total sales in the first seven months of 2025 were 66,680 units, implying a monthly average below 10,000 [4][35]. These judgements are based on the sales, gross-margin and product-mix data cited above.

Outlook

Company plans

Voyah plans to launch one to three new models each year and to have six to nine models by the end of 2026, expanding beyond its MPV concentration; its 2026 plan includes the Taishan Ultra, Taishan X8, FE and Zhufeng models and L3-level assisted driving [35][13]. It targets expanding its domestic retail network toward 1,000 touchpoints covering over 200 cities [35][18]. Overseas, it will deepen Europe, build out the Middle East and enter right-hand-drive markets, with a right-hand-drive Dream due in H2 2026 [10]. In May 2026 Dongfeng and Stellantis signed a non-binding memorandum of understanding to explore a European joint venture to handle Voyah sales and distribution; this is an agreement to explore, not a completed transaction [33]. For H2 2026 the company said it would push the Zhuiguang S pure-electric SUV and the Dream 9 flagship MPV to market [17]. These are the company's stated plans, not achieved results.

Our analysis

Our assessment: reducing dependence on the Dream MPV is the key near-term test. New flagships (Taishan X8, Dream 9, Zhuiguang S) must ramp quickly to offset the MPV concentration, and the H1 2026 loss shows that higher raw-material costs and channel/overseas investment can quickly reverse profitability [16]. Success in Europe and right-hand-drive markets depends on tariff and certification conditions that are outside Voyah's control, so the pace of overseas volume is uncertain [10]. As a separately listed entity, Voyah can raise capital in Hong Kong, but its long-term funding and governance outcome still depends on Dongfeng's continued support and subsequent disclosures [6]. On the macro side, in H1 2026 domestic passenger-vehicle insurance registrations fell 19% year on year and NEV registrations fell 14%, with the above-RMB 200,000 premium segment among the few growing tracks [16]. These judgements rest on the product, financial and market data cited above.

Risks

  • Dependence on a single model: the Dream MPV contributed 60.15% of sales in the first seven months of 2025, concentrating revenue in a small, cyclical MPV segment [35].
  • Profit volatility: Voyah returned to a net loss of RMB 389 million in H1 2026, from a RMB 464 million profit a year earlier, on higher lithium-carbonate and memory-chip costs and increased R&D, sales and marketing spending [4].
  • Intense price competition: the premium NEV market is contested by NIO, Li Auto, Xpeng, Zeekr and Huawei-backed brands, and industry price cuts pressured the brand to lower FREE and Zhuiguang starting prices over time [26].
  • Overseas trade barriers: Chinese-built battery-electric vehicles face EU countervailing duties and other tariffs; Voyah's overseas expansion is therefore exposed to trade-policy and certification risk [13].
  • Reliance on government subsidies: Dazhong Daily's breakdown of the interim statements noted that in the first seven months of 2025, government grants related to revenue (RMB 603 million) and to assets (RMB 39 million) totalled RMB 642 million, a material contributor to the RMB 434 million profit for that period [22].
  • Controlling-shareholder dependence: Voyah relies on Dongfeng for manufacturing, engineering and financing, and as of 25 June 2026 Dongfeng Motor Corporation together with Dongfeng Asset Management, Dongfeng Motor (Hong Kong) International Co., Limited and Wuhan Woya held about 70.83% of the shares in issue; related-party and governance risks follow from this concentrated ownership [38].

概览

岚图（Voyah）是中国三大汽车央企之一——东风汽车集团有限公司的高端智慧新能源汽车品牌。东风于 2020 年 7 月发布该品牌，运营主体岚图汽车科技有限公司于 2021 年 6 月 26 日成立，其后完成股份制改造，并于 2026 年 3 月 19 日以介绍方式在香港联交所主板挂牌，股份代号 07489.HK[1][6]。品牌在中国设计、开发、制造及销售新能源汽车，覆盖轿车、SUV、MPV 三大细分市场，定位于高端领域，产品主要覆盖约 20 万元至 50 万元价格带[1]。其销量由 2022 年的 19,409 辆增至 2025 年的 150,169 辆，并于 2025 年扭亏为盈：营收 348.65 亿元、净利润约 10.2 亿元[6]。从 2019 年东风内部 h 事业部起步，岚图用约五年时间完成「轿车 + SUV + MPV」三大品类布局，并成为首个达成 30 万辆整车下线的央国企高端新能源汽车品牌[1]。本页为岚图这一品牌的研究，母集团东风汽车集团有限公司另有深度研究。

现状

业务结构

岚图收入以整车销售为主，汽车零部件、后市场与技术服务收入快速增长。2026 年上半年总营收 181.6 亿元（同比增长 42.4%），其中整车收入 164.7 亿元（同比增长 36.2%）、后市场收入约 11.9 亿元（同比增长 113.5%）、技术服务收入 4.57 亿元（同比增长约 591.7%），海外收入 25.4 亿元（同比增长 51.5%）；毛利率由上年同期 21.4% 降至 17.7%[4]。公司收入几乎全部来自中国，其中梦想家贡献最大份额：据招股书，梦想家 2023 年销售 26,518 辆（占总销量 52.7%）、2024 年 46,687 辆（占 58.3%）、2025 年前七个月 40,111 辆（占 60.15%）[1][35]。2022—2024 年乘用车销售收入占比分别为 89.0%、96.1%、94.9%；汽车零部件收入分别为 1.4 亿元、3.3 亿元、6.0 亿元；其他收入分别为 5.3 亿元、1.7 亿元、3.9 亿元[15]。

技术与平台

岚图将自研能力归纳为「五大技术底座」：平台架构（含 ESSA 原生智能电动架构与中央集中式 SOA 电子电气架构，后者命名「天元架构」）、岚海动力、逍遥智能座舱、鲲鹏智能驾驶与岚图技术生态[35][23]。ESSA 架构被公司称为全球首个兼容纯电、插混、增程多动力模式的原生智能电动架构，核心零部件通用化率约 90%[11]。800V 岚海智能超混在中大型混动车上实现 360–410 公里纯电续航、超 1400 公里综合续航[11]。智能化采取「自研 + 开放合作」双轨：自研鲲鹏智驾，并与华为合作——2026 款梦想家等车型的乾崑版搭载华为乾崑智驾 ADS 4 与鸿蒙座舱 5，同一车型的鲲鹏版则搭载自研鲲鹏智驾 2.0[36][18]。截至 2025 年 12 月 31 日，拥有已授权专利 1,874 项、申报专利 5,405 项，发明专利占比约 88%[29]。天元中央集中式电子电气架构入选国资委《中央企业科技创新成果推荐目录》，并已授权给超过 10 家自主品牌使用[27]。面向 L3，截至 2025 年底已完成累计 11 万公里实际道路测试以及 90 万公里模拟测试；泰山 Ultra 搭载 896 线激光雷达[29]。

制造与产能

岚图在武汉经济技术开发区拥有云峰、黄金两座工厂，合计年产能约 45 万辆；黄金工厂屋顶光伏年发电约 2000 万度[10][29]。两座工厂均为工业 4.0 数字工厂，覆盖冲压、焊装、涂装、总装等工序[25]。

国内市场地位

截至 2025 年 7 月 31 日，岚图销售及服务网络覆盖 148 个城市、407 家门店（192 家自营、215 家生态合作伙伴运营）；至 2026 年 6 月 30 日增至 170 城、509 家门店[35][17]。据媒体援引灼识咨询数据，2024 年岚图毛利率在国内新能源车企中排名第二，梦想家则以 2024 年销量居高端新能源 MPV 第二[18]。

海外业务

海外销量由 2023 年的 5,647 辆增至 2024 年 8,419 辆、2025 年 18,333 辆[1]。2026 年 6 月 17 日在沙特利雅得举行上市活动正式进入沙特市场，至 6 月底已进入 40 个国家和地区、建成 240 余家海外网点；海外深耕欧洲、布局中东，并筹备右舵车型[33][10]。

竞争格局

竞争地位：我们的判断

我们的判断：岚图所处的 20 万–50 万元高端新能源市场，主要对手包括蔚来、理想、小鹏、极氪，以及华为加持的问界等品牌和高端合资电动车[1]。其规模仍明显小于头部玩家，2025 年销量为 150,169 辆，且高度依赖单一车型——2025 年前七个月梦想家 MPV 销量占比达 60.15%[35][6]。

优势与约束：我们的判断

我们的判断：岚图的核心优势在于东风的制造、工程与供应链资源，扎实的技术底座（ESSA 架构、天元 SOA 架构、800V 岚海超混、鲲鹏智驾及华为合作），以及在 2025 年实现盈利——这在中国新能源初创车企中较为少见；毛利率由 2022 年的 8.3% 升至 2025 年的 20.9%[1]。据媒体援引灼识咨询数据，2024 年岚图毛利率在国内新能源车企中排名第二，梦想家则以约 4.7 万辆位居 2024 年高端新能源 MPV 销量第二[18]。但 2026 年上半年因原材料涨价与投入加大重新录得 3.89 亿元净亏损，显示其盈利尚不稳定；对 MPV 这一小细分市场的依赖，也使其暴露于狭窄市场中——2025 年前七个月总销量 66,680 辆、月均不足 1 万辆[4][35]。上述判断依据为前述销量、毛利率与产品结构数据。

未来展望

公司公开规划

岚图计划每年推出 1–3 款新车型，到 2026 年底拥有 6–9 款车型，以改善对单一车型的依赖；2026 年规划包括泰山 Ultra、泰山 X8、FE、珠峰等多款新车及 L3 级自动驾驶[35][13]。渠道方面，目标 2026 年前国内零售网络触点大幅拓展至 1000 家、覆盖超 200 城[35][18]。海外方面，将深耕欧洲、布局中东、进军右舵市场，2026 年下半年推出梦想家右舵版[10]。2026 年 5 月，东风与 Stellantis 集团签署非约束性谅解备忘录，探讨在欧洲设立合资企业负责岚图销售与分销——此为待商谈的意向，并非已完成交易[33]。针对 2026 年下半年，公司表示将推动追光 S 纯电 SUV、梦想家 9 旗舰 MPV 等新品落地[17]。以上为公司目标与意向表述，并非已实现结果。

我们的分析

我们的判断：降低对梦想家 MPV 的依赖是近期关键考验。泰山 X8、梦想家 9、追光 S 等新旗舰需快速上量以对冲 MPV 集中度；2026 年上半年亏损显示，原材料涨价与渠道、海外投入会快速扭转盈利[16]。欧洲与右舵市场的开拓受关税与认证等外部条件制约，海外放量节奏存在不确定性[10]。作为独立上市主体，岚图可在香港融资，但其长期资金与治理成效仍取决于东风的持续支持与后续披露[6]。宏观背景上，2026 年上半年国内乘用车上险量同比下滑 19%、新能源乘用车上险量同比下降 14%，20 万元以上高端市场是少数保持正增长的细分赛道[16]。上述判断依据为前述产品、财务与市场数据。

风险

  • 单一车型依赖：2025 年前七个月梦想家 MPV 销量占比达 60.15%，收入集中于一个规模较小、具周期性的 MPV 细分市场[35]。
  • 盈利波动：因碳酸锂、内存芯片涨价及研发、销售费用增加，2026 年上半年由上年同期盈利 4.64 亿元转为净亏损 3.89 亿元[4]。
  • 价格竞争激烈：高端新能源市场面临蔚来、理想、小鹏、极氪及华为加持品牌竞争，行业价格战使岚图 FREE、追光起售价随时间下调[26]。
  • 海外贸易壁垒：中国产电池电动车在欧盟等地面临反补贴税等关税，岚图海外拓展面临贸易政策与认证风险[13]。
  • 政府补贴依赖：据大众日报对中期报表的拆解，2025 年前七个月与收入相关的政府补助 6.03 亿元、与资产相关的政府补助 0.39 亿元，合计 6.42 亿元，是当期 4.34 亿元净利润的重要支撑[22]。
  • 对控股股东依赖：岚图在制造、工程与融资上依赖东风，且截至 2026 年 6 月 25 日东风公司、东风资管、东风汽车（香港）国际有限公司与武汉沃雅合计持股约 70.83%，股权高度集中带来关联交易与治理风险[38]。
