Overview

Changan Ford Automobile Co., Ltd. (长安福特汽车有限公司) is a passenger-vehicle joint venture between Chongqing Changan Automobile Co., Ltd. and Ford Motor Company, each side holding 50%, registered in Chongqing on 27 April 2001 as Ford's first passenger-car production base in China [6][3]. It now operates two production bases, in Chongqing and Hangzhou, making four Ford-badged models (Mondeo, Edge L, Explorer, Escape) and four Lincoln models (Corsair, Nautilus, Aviator, Lincoln Z) [4].

Once a profit pillar for Changan, its annual sales peaked at about 957,000 units in 2016, then declined: below 200,000 in 2019, a brief recovery to about 304,700 in 2021, about 247,000 in 2024, and 121,500 wholesale / 99,400 retail in 2025, its first year under the 100,000-unit mark [9][21]. Financially it swung from a RMB 2.06 billion loss in 2023 to a RMB 2.09 billion profit in 2024 on the back of exports and cost cuts, before net profit roughly halved to RMB 952 million in 2025 [7][1].

  4. 2006-03

    Mazda takes a stake and the company is renamed Changan Ford Mazda Automobile Co., Ltd., with shareholdings of Changan 50%, Ford 35% and Mazda 15%.[19]

  5. 2012-11-30

    Changan Ford Mazda is split into two joint ventures: Changan Ford Automobile Co., Ltd. (50:50, Chongqing, Ford business) and Changan Mazda Automobile Co., Ltd. (Nanjing, Mazda business).[19]

  6. 2013-06

    A new engine plant opens in Chongqing (investment of about USD 500 million, capacity of 400,000 units).[19]

  7. 2014-11

  8. 2015-03

    The Hangzhou fourth vehicle plant opens (investment of about USD 760 million), adding 250,000 units of annual capacity.[19]

  9. 2016

    Annual sales reach a record of about 957,000 units, the company's all-time peak.[9]

  10. 2019-06-05

    The State Administration for Market Regulation fines Changan Ford RMB 162.8 million for a vertical monopoly agreement that restricted dealers' minimum resale prices in Chongqing from 2013.[17]

  11. 2019

  12. 2021-03-15

    CCTV's 3-15 consumer-rights programme reports water-ingress rusting in the dry dual-clutch transmissions of some Fiesta, Focus and EcoSport models built before the end of August 2019; Changan Ford later extends the warranty to 10 years or 240,000 km and refunds earlier self-paid repair costs.[13]

  13. 2022

    According to Changjiang Times reporting, Changan Ford posts a net loss of RMB 2.449 billion; Changan's 2022 annual report is cited as saying it shut legacy production lines and booked asset impairments, and the company said it impaired Ford-related plants and booked accelerated depreciation after discontinuing the Focus.[10]

  14. 2023-08

    Changan Ford takes over the China operation of the Ford Mustang Mach-E electric SUV, including sales, after-sales and user operations.[11]

  15. 2023-09

  18. 2025-11-03

    Changan Ford's registration is changed: Zhu Huarong steps down as chairman and Zhao Fei takes over; directors Wang Jun and Wang Hui exit and Liu Hong, Zhang Xiaoyu, Wang Xiaofei and Chen Yuting join the board.[23]

    Changan Automobile lists its 40% stake in Changan Ford NEV Technology for transfer at a reserve price of RMB 154 million (disclosure period to 29 June 2026), after the subsidiary's core business had been suspended since 1 October 2025.[18]

Current operations

Brands and products

Changan Ford currently builds four Ford-badged models — Mondeo, Edge L, Explorer and Escape — and four Lincoln models — Corsair, Nautilus, Aviator and Lincoln Z — spanning mid-size sedans and large SUVs [4]. The line-up has shrunk sharply: the Focus, Escort and Kuga have all been discontinued, and the range was cut to four mainstream models; in 2025 the Mondeo alone accounted for about 47,000 units, roughly half of brand sales [8][21]. On the Lincoln side, the Corsair, Lincoln's first locally produced model, launched on 12 March 2020 with a manufacturer's suggested retail price from RMB246,800, followed by local production of the Nautilus, Aviator and Lincoln Z [5][4].

Production bases and capacity

The company now has two production bases, in Chongqing and Hangzhou, down from its peak [4]. According to Changjiang Times (reprinted by The Paper), at its peak Changan Ford ran five vehicle plants in Chongqing, Harbin and Hangzhou with total capacity of about 1.6 million units; in May 2023 the company told the newspaper that vehicle-plant capacity was then 670,000 units and that no capacity had been disposed of in 2022 [10]. During its expansion phase the company built the Chongqing No.2 plant (opened February 2012), an engine plant (opened June 2013, capacity 400,000 units), a transmission plant (opened June 2014, initial capacity 400,000 units), the Chongqing third vehicle plant (opened November 2014, adding 360,000 units) and the Hangzhou fourth plant (opened March 2015, adding 250,000 units), making Chongqing once Ford's largest production base outside Michigan [19].

Exports

Exports were a main driver of the 2024 return to profit [7]. The Ford Mondeo has been exported since 2019, with cumulative exports exceeding 100,000 units by 2025, sold in the Middle East under the Taurus name; the Hangzhou-built Lincoln Nautilus has been exported back to North America since 2024, with about 31,000 units sold in the US in 2025 [16][14]. Ford China's overall vehicle exports (including Jiangling Ford) rose from 74,000 in 2022 to 112,000 in 2023, 168,000 in 2024 and 184,000 in 2025 (up 9% year on year) [16]. Within that matrix, the Jiangling-built Territory is the largest single exported model (95,000 units in 2025, up 28%), named the 2024 Arab Car of the Year in the Middle East, while Changan Ford's Edge L has entered markets such as Mexico; Ford China's export map has expanded to more than 80 countries and regions [16].

Latest performance and electrification

Full-year 2025 net profit of RMB 952 million was 54.4% below 2024's RMB 2.09 billion [1]. For the first half of 2026, Changan Ford's revenue was RMB 13.627 billion, down 25% year on year, and its net profit was RMB 262 million, down 65.2% year on year, which Changan's 2026 interim report attributed mainly to lower sales [2][24]. The company's electrification push has largely stalled: the Mustang Mach-E, its only battery-electric model, sold just 35 units in January-July 2025, and the Changan Ford NEV subsidiary suspended its core business from 1 October 2025 [9][18].

Founders and leadership

Origin

As a 50:50 joint venture, Changan Ford has no individual founder; it was set up in 2001 by Changan Automobile and Ford Motor [6].

Current management

  • Zhao Fei (赵非) — chairman. He succeeded Zhu Huarong as Changan Ford chairman in a registration change reported on 3 November 2025 [23]. On 16 October 2026 he was elected chairman and legal representative of Chongqing Changan Automobile (no longer president), while continuing as deputy party secretary, director and general manager of China Changan Automobile Group; his bio lists him as chairman of Changan Ford [20][1]. Born in 1974, he previously served as assistant to the president and head of the strategy and planning department of Changan Automobile, vice president and executive vice president, and party secretary and executive vice president of Changan Ford, among other roles [20].
  • Ai Xiaoming (艾小明) — president, appointed by Ford China on 6 May 2024, reporting to the Changan Ford board [12][11]. Before Ford he spent more than 20 years in manufacturing and consumer electronics, including as CEO of Giant KONE Elevator, president and CEO of Whirlpool China, and a vice president of Schneider Electric China [11][12]. His predecessor He Xiaoqing retired on 1 June 2024 [12].
  • Lan Xiangwen (兰祥文) — party secretary and executive vice president (the Changan-side head), who succeeded Wang Xiaofei in early 2026 [21][8]. He previously handled procurement management and Hebei Changan operations and logistics, became general manager of Changan Kaicene in June 2024, and deputy chairman of Changan Kaicene in August 2025 [21]. Wang Xiaofei had taken the post from Yang Dayong in September 2025, also serving as president and product vice president of Changan Ford NEV [21].

Equity and governance

Changan Automobile and the Ford side each hold 50% (the registered Ford shareholders are Ford Asia Pacific Auto Holding and Ford Motor (China) Co., Ltd.), and Changan accounts for the venture under the equity method [23][1]. At end-2025 the carrying value of Changan's equity investment in Changan Ford was RMB 1.178 billion (RMB 712 million at end-2024), not written down to zero [1].

Competition

Our judgment. Changan Ford competes in China's passenger-car market against domestic brands such as BYD, Geely, Changan's own brands, Chery and Great Wall, and against joint-venture players including Volkswagen, Toyota and Honda. Its competitive base rests on the Ford and Lincoln brand franchises and on an export business (Mondeo to the Middle East, Lincoln Nautilus to North America) that supports profitability. Its weaknesses are a shrunken line-up concentrated on the Mondeo and a largely failed electrification push — the Mach-E sold only 35 units in 2025 [9][8]. Jiemian also noted that, chasing per-vehicle profit, the company dropped volume models such as the Focus, leaving a gap in the RMB100,000 price band and holding prices while other joint-venture brands competed on price, which kept eroding its market share [9].

Our judgment. The 2025 results — retail sales below 100,000 units, net profit halving to RMB 952 million, and a further 31% first-quarter 2026 sales fall — indicate the business is highly sensitive to the domestic price war and to its largely stalled NEV transition [8]. Unlike SAIC-GM, which renewed its joint-venture contract to 2047, Changan Ford had announced no comparable large-scale NEV relaunch as of the research date [22][9].

Outlook

Company plans. Ford's China strategy since 2023 has been to run a leaner, more focused, higher-return business with less investment — in practice, cutting models, plants and capacity while growing exports [7]. Exports are one of Ford China's three core strategies, reaching 184,000 units in 2025; in February 2025 Ford announced that Ford China president and CEO Wu Shengbo would additionally oversee Ford's International Markets Group, folding the China market into Ford's global strategic hub [16]. In August 2026 media cited Bloomberg that Ford would stop exporting the China-made Lincoln Nautilus to the US and expand US production from 2030; that month Changan Ford confirmed the 2027 Corsair Hybrid would be exported from China to North America from early 2027 [14][15]. In September 2025 Ford China also set up a sales-service company in Shanghai, a wholly owned subsidiary managing marketing and services for Ford-brand passenger vehicles and pickups in China [21].

Our judgment. Ending Nautilus exports to the US removes a meaningful chunk of export revenue over the medium term and exposes Hangzhou plant utilisation, which the Corsair Hybrid export only partly offsets; the 31% year-on-year first-quarter 2026 retail decline suggests the slide has not yet reversed [15][8]. Whether Changan Ford stabilises depends on fresh product and electrification investment from Ford and Changan, and on how its remaining Mondeo, Edge L and Explorer models fare in the price war [8].

Risks

  • Sales and product concentration. Retail sales fell below 100,000 units in 2025, with the Mondeo alone providing roughly half of brand sales, leaving the portfolio exposed to a single model [8][21].
  • Financial volatility. The company lost RMB 2.06 billion in 2023, earned RMB 2.09 billion in 2024, then saw net profit halve to RMB 952 million in 2025, and H1 2026 net profit fell a further 65.2% [7][2].
  • Electrification failure. The Mach-E sold only 35 units in 2025, the NEV subsidiary suspended its core business from October 2025, and Changan's 40% stake was listed for transfer [9][18].
  • Export and tariff exposure. The plan to stop exporting the China-made Nautilus to the US cuts export income; media cited Lincoln as saying China-built vehicles imported to the US face combined tariffs of about 52.5% [14][15].
  • Technology-route mismatch. NEV penetration in China has passed 50%, but Changan Ford's line-up remains dominated by fuel and hybrid models, with its battery-electric model having effectively exited, leaving the mix out of step with the market [9].
  • Reduced disclosure. Since June 2025 Changan has stopped disclosing Changan Ford sales separately in its monthly reports, lowering public visibility into the venture [8].

概览

长安福特汽车有限公司是重庆长安汽车股份有限公司与美国福特汽车公司各持股 50% 的中外合资乘用车企业，2001 年 4 月 27 日在重庆注册成立，是福特在中国的第一个轿车生产基地 [6][3]。公司现设重庆、杭州两大生产基地，生产福特品牌（蒙迪欧、锐界 L、探险者、锐际）与林肯品牌（冒险家、航海家、飞行家、林肯 Z）共八款车型 [4]。

长安福特曾是长安汽车的利润支柱，2016 年销量达约 95.7 万辆的历史峰值，此后逐年下滑：2019 年首次跌破 20 万辆，2021 年短暂回升至约 30.47 万辆，2024 年销量约 24.7 万辆，2025 年批发 12.15 万辆、零售 9.94 万辆，首次跌破 10 万辆 [9][21]。财务上，公司 2023 年亏损 20.6 亿元，2024 年因出口增加与固定费用节支扭亏为盈、实现净利润 20.9 亿元，2025 年净利润同比近乎腰斩至 9.52 亿元 [7][1]。

历史沿革

  1. 2001-04-27

    长安福特汽车有限公司成立，由长安汽车与福特汽车按 50:50 合资设立，注册于重庆，成立初期总投资额 9800 万美元，是福特在中国的第一个轿车生产基地。[3][6]

  2. 2003-01-18

    首辆福特嘉年华下线，2003 年 3 月进入批量生产。[19]

  3. 2005-09

    福特福克斯三厢版引入国产，成为走量车型。[19]

  4. 2006-03

    马自达参股，公司更名为长安福特马自达汽车有限公司，三方持股变为长安 50%、福特 35%、马自达 15%。[19]

  5. 2012-11-30

    长安福特马自达存续分立为两家合资公司：长安福特汽车有限公司（50:50，重庆，承接福特业务）与长安马自达汽车有限公司（南京，承接马自达业务）。[19]

  6. 2013-06

    投资约 5 亿美元的长安福特重庆新发动机工厂投产，产能 40 万台。[19]

  7. 2014-11

    投资约 6 亿美元的长安福特重庆第三整车工厂投产，年产能增加 36 万辆。[19]

  8. 2015-03

    投资约 7.6 亿美元的长安福特杭州第四整车工厂投产，年产能增加 25 万辆。[19]

  9. 2016

    年销量达到约 95.7 万辆的历史峰值。[9]

  10. 2019-06-05

    市场监管总局对长安福特实施纵向垄断协议（2013 年起在重庆限制经销商整车最低转售价格）作出处罚，罚款 1.628 亿元。[17]

  11. 2019

    年销量首次跌破 20 万辆，降至约 18.4 万辆。[9][10]

  12. 2021-03-15

    央视 3·15 晚会报道部分嘉年华、福克斯、翼搏车型（2019 年 8 月底以前生产）干式双离合变速器进水生锈问题；长安福特随后将相关变速器保修期延长至 10 年或 24 万公里，并退还此前自费维修费用。[13]

  13. 2022

    据长江商报报道，长安福特净亏损 24.49 亿元；该报引述长安汽车 2022 年年报称其为加速振兴关停落后产线、计提资产减值，公司表示福克斯停产后对福特相关工厂计提减值、生产线计提加速折旧。[10]

  14. 2023-08

    长安福特接手福特电马（Mustang Mach-E）在华运营业务，包括销售、售后及用户运维。[11]

  15. 2023-09

    长安福特新能源汽车科技有限公司成立（长安福特持股 60%、长安汽车持股 40%，注册资本 2.5 亿元），负责新能源乘用车及福特品牌车型销售与服务。[18]

  16. 2024-05-06

    福特中国宣布艾小明出任长安福特总裁，接替何晓庆（何晓庆于 2024 年 6 月 1 日退休）。[12][11]

  17. 2024

    长安福特扭亏为盈，实现营业收入 483.06 亿元、净利润 20.9 亿元（2023 年为亏损 20.6 亿元），原因系出口增加拉动与固定费用节支。[7][1]

  18. 2025-11-03

    长安福特完成工商变更：朱华荣卸任董事长、赵非接任；王俊、王辉退出董事席位，刘宏、张晓宇、王孝飞、陈玉婷等新增为董事。[23]

  19. 2025

    零售销量首次跌破 10 万辆（9.94 万辆）；自 2025 年 6 月起长安汽车在月度产销快报中不再单独披露长安福特销量。[9][8]

  20. 2026-06

    长安汽车以 1.54 亿元底价挂牌转让所持长安福特新能源 40% 股权（信息披露至 2026 年 6 月 29 日）；该子公司核心业务自 2025 年 10 月 1 日起已停摆。[18]

现状

品牌与产品

长安福特现生产四款福特品牌车型——蒙迪欧、锐界 L、探险者、锐际，以及四款林肯车型——冒险家、航海家、飞行家、林肯 Z，覆盖中型轿车与中大型 SUV [4]。产品线已大幅收缩：福克斯、福睿斯、翼虎等车型均已停产，主流车型精简至 4 款；2025 年蒙迪欧一款车型贡献约 4.7 万辆、接近品牌销量一半 [8][21]。林肯方面，2020 年 3 月 12 日首款国产车型冒险家上市，厂商建议零售价 24.68 万元起，此后陆续国产航海家、飞行家与林肯 Z [5][4]。

生产基地与产能

公司现设重庆、杭州两大生产基地，较巅峰时期缩减 [4]。据长江商报（澎湃新闻转载）报道，巅峰时长安福特在重庆、哈尔滨、杭州设有 5 家整车工厂、总产能约 160 万辆；2023 年 5 月公司向其表示当时整车工厂产能为 67 万辆，并称 2022 年未处置产能 [10]。在产能扩张期，公司先后建成重庆二工厂（2012 年 2 月投产）、发动机工厂（2013 年 6 月投产、产能 40 万台）、变速箱工厂（2014 年 6 月投产、初期产能 40 万台）、重庆第三整车工厂（2014 年 11 月投产、新增 36 万辆）与杭州第四整车工厂（2015 年 3 月投产、新增 25 万辆），重庆一度成为福特在密歇根州以外全球最大的生产基地 [19]。

出口

出口是 2024 年扭亏的主要拉动因素 [7]。福特蒙迪欧自 2019 年起出口，截至 2025 年累计出口突破 10 万辆，在中东以「金牛座」之名销售；杭州工厂生产的林肯航海家自 2024 年起返销北美，2025 年在美国销售约 3.1 万辆 [16][14]。福特中国（含江铃福特）整车出口由 2022 年的 7.4 万辆增至 2023 年的 11.2 万辆、2024 年的 16.8 万辆、2025 年的 18.4 万辆（同比增 9%）[16]。在该出口矩阵中，江铃福特生产的领睿是出口量最大的单一车型（2025 年出口 9.5 万辆、同比增 28%），在中东获 2024 阿拉伯年度车型奖项，长安福特的锐界 L 亦进入墨西哥等市场；福特中国出口版图已拓展至全球 80 多个国家和地区 [16]。

最新业绩与电动化

2025 年全年净利润 9.52 亿元，较 2024 年的 20.9 亿元下降 54.4% [1]。2026 年上半年，长安福特实现营业收入 136.27 亿元、同比减少 25%，净利润 2.62 亿元、同比减少 65.2%；长安汽车 2026 年半年度报告称其主要因销量下滑、利润同比减少 [2][24]。公司的电动化推进基本停滞：唯一纯电车型福特电马 2025 年 1–7 月仅售出 35 辆，长安福特新能源子公司核心业务自 2025 年 10 月 1 日起停摆 [9][18]。

竞争格局

我们的判断。长安福特在中国乘用车市场与比亚迪、吉利、长安自主品牌、奇瑞、长城等自主品牌，以及大众、丰田、本田等合资品牌竞争，竞争基础在于福特与林肯的品牌力，以及支撑盈利的出口业务（蒙迪欧出口中东、林肯航海家返销北美）；短板在于大幅收缩且高度集中于蒙迪欧的产品线，以及基本失败的电动化——福特电马 2025 年仅售出 35 辆 [9][8]。界面新闻还指出，为追求单车利润，公司砍掉福克斯等走量车型、在 10 万元价位区间留下产品空白，当其他合资品牌「以价换量」时仍坚守价格，导致市场份额被持续挤压 [9]。

我们的判断。2025 年零售跌破 10 万辆、净利润近乎腰斩至 9.52 亿元，2026 年一季度销量再降 31%，显示公司业绩对国内价格战与（基本停滞的）新能源转型节奏高度敏感 [8]。相比已续约至 2047 年的上汽通用，截至研究日长安福特未公布同量级的新能源重启计划 [22][9]。

未来展望

公司公开规划。福特 2023 年以来的在华战略是「投资更少、更精简、更专注、回报更高」，落实到操作层面即削减车型、工厂与产能并扩大出口 [7]。出口是福特中国三大核心战略之一，2025 年整车出口达 18.4 万辆；2025 年 2 月福特宣布福特中国总裁兼首席执行官吴胜波兼管福特汽车国际市场集团业务，将中国市场纳入福特全球战略中枢 [16]。2026 年 8 月，媒体援引彭博报道福特将停止向美国出口中国产林肯航海家、自 2030 年起扩大美国本土产能；同月长安福特确认，2027 款冒险家混动版将于 2027 年初起从中国出口北美 [14][15]。2025 年 9 月，福特中国还在上海设立福特销售服务公司，作为全资子公司全面负责福特品牌乘用车与皮卡车型在华营销与服务 [21]。

我们的判断。航海家停止返销北美将在中期内削弱一块可观的出口收入，并令杭州工厂产能承压，冒险家混动出口只能部分对冲；2026 年一季度零售同比下滑 31% 表明下滑趋势尚未扭转 [15][8]。长安福特能否企稳，取决于福特与长安是否追加产品与电动化投入，以及剩余蒙迪欧、锐界 L、探险者等车型在价格战中的表现 [8]。

风险

  • 销量与产品集中度。2025 年零售跌破 10 万辆，蒙迪欧一款车型贡献品牌近半销量，产品线对单一车型依赖过高 [8][21]。
  • 财务波动。公司 2023 年亏损 20.6 亿元、2024 年盈利 20.9 亿元、2025 年净利润近乎腰斩至 9.52 亿元，2026 年上半年净利润再降 65.2%，盈利波动大 [7][2]。
  • 电动化失利。福特电马 2025 年仅售出 35 辆，新能源子公司核心业务 2025 年 10 月起停摆，长安汽车所持 40% 股权已挂牌转让 [9][18]。
  • 出口与关税风险。停止向美国出口中国产航海家的计划将削减出口收入；媒体援引林肯方面称中国产车辆进口美国预计面临约 52.5% 的综合关税 [14][15]。
  • 技术路线与市场错位。中国新能源乘用车渗透率已过半，而长安福特在售主力仍为燃油与混动车型，纯电车型基本退出，产品组合与市场主流存在错位 [9]。
  • 信息披露减少。自 2025 年 6 月起长安汽车在月度产销快报中不再单独披露长安福特销量，合资公司经营的公开可见度下降 [8]。
