Overview

Tank is the hard-core off-road SUV brand of Great Wall Motor Company Limited (GWM). Its first model, the Tank 300, went on sale on 17 December 2020 under the WEY (Wei) name, and on 19 April 2021 Tank became an independent brand, GWM's fifth vehicle brand.[6][9] Tank positions itself as a professional off-road brand under the slogan 'tough as iron, tender as heart', spanning compact to full-size SUVs with petrol, diesel, Hi4-T and Hi4-Z powertrains.[1]

The brand grew out of a single breakout product. The Tank 300 took more than 10,000 orders within 20 days of opening reservations and delivered 6,018 units in its first month; WEY suspended new orders from 28 March 2021 to clear the backlog, after which GWM spun the line off into an independent brand.[10]

In 2025 Tank sold 234,442 vehicles, taking cumulative global sales above 830,000 units and remaining China's best-selling off-road SUV brand; overseas sales reached 58,140 vehicles and new-energy penetration was 54.58%.[1][2] Annual sales grew from 123,881 in 2022 to 162,539 in 2023, 232,195 in 2024 per the internal-control evaluation report (the December 2025 production-and-sales brief lists the prior-year cumulative as 231,001) and 234,442 in 2025; in the first nine months of 2026 the brand sold 142,796 vehicles, down 13.53% year on year.[8][5][4][17][16]

Official suggested retail prices currently range from RMB 199,800 (Tank 300) to RMB 508,000 (Tank 700 Hi4-Z), placing Tank among the higher-positioned brands in GWM's portfolio.[7] A Hubei Daily interview stated that one in every two off-road vehicles sold in China is a GWM product and that GWM's hard-core off-road SUV share exceeds 50%; a Guojin Securities note put Tank's own domestic hard-core off-road share at about 50%.[13][15]

History

  1. 2020-12-17

    The Tank 300 off-road version goes on sale at RMB 175,800-213,800 as WEY's first off-road SUV, powered by a 2.0T engine (167 kW / 387 N·m) with a ZF 8AT and featuring three differential locks, creep mode and tank turn; it becomes the first model of the Tank line.[9][6]

  2. 2021-03

    With strong demand (over 10,000 orders within 20 days of opening reservations and 6,018 units delivered in the first month), WEY suspends new Tank 300 orders from 28 March to clear a large backlog.[10]

  3. 2021-04-19

    On 19 April 2021 Tank is announced at the Shanghai auto show as an independent off-road SUV brand, Great Wall Motor's fifth vehicle brand (GWM official history). A CRI report dated 6 April 2021 instead says the brand became the fifth brand from 31 March 2021. Both accounts are recorded.[6][10]

  4. 2021

    Tank's official news on 7 January 2022 says cumulative deliveries for the full year 2021 reached 84,588 vehicles. The figure is deliveries, not sales.[21]

  5. 2022-03-18

    The Tank 500, the brand's second model and a mid-to-large SUV, is launched at RMB 335,000-395,000; it is the first GWM model with the self-developed 3.0T V6 (260 kW / 500 N·m) plus a longitudinal 9AT, with a 48V mild-hybrid system.[11]

  6. 2022-12-25

    The Tank 300 receives a five-star safety rating from ANCAP (Australasian New Car Assessment Program) in Australia. GWM's news release of this date reports the rating. The 24 October 2023 entry on the official history page is the 2023 overseas-investor co-creation conference, not this safety rating.[20]

  7. 2022

    Tank sells 123,881 vehicles for the year (+46.45% YoY), of which the Tank 300 accounts for 101,787; the brand posts seven consecutive months above 10,000 units and about a 67% share of China's hard-core off-road segment.[8]

  8. 2023-06-26

    The Tank 500 Hi4-T is launched at RMB 335,000, the brand's first Hi4-T plug-in-hybrid off-road model.[6]

  9. 2023-09-25

    The Tank 400 Hi4-T is launched, extending the line-up into the urban-mechanism off-road segment.[6]

  10. 2023

    Tank sells 162,539 vehicles for the year (+31.21% YoY).[5]

  11. 2024-02-26

    The Tank 700 Hi4-T, the brand's flagship, is launched on 26 February 2024. Xinhua reports a suggested retail price of RMB 428,000 for the Jizhi edition, RMB 468,000 for the Jijing edition and RMB 700,000 for the limited edition, with a 3.0T V6 plus P2 motor and 9HAT.[24]

  12. 2024-10

    Per the 2024 internal-control evaluation report, GWM unveils Hi4-Z, the universal off-road super electric-hybrid architecture, complementing the strong off-road Hi4-T. The official history page describes the architecture shown at the autumn technology event as a longitudinal dual-motor series-parallel system (纵置双电机混联).[4][6]

  13. 2024

    The 2024 internal-control evaluation report records Tank sales of 232,195 and a stated increase of 42.57% year on year. Dividing 232,195 by the 162,539 vehicles that same report uses for 2023 gives about 42.85%, which does not match the stated 42.57%. The December 2025 production-and-sales brief lists the prior-year Tank cumulative as 231,001. Both primary-source figures are shown. That year the Tank range successively entered Kazakhstan, Chile, Indonesia, South Africa and Mexico.[4][17]

  14. 2025-01-01

    The Tank 500 Hi4-Z goes on sale globally, the brand's first model built on the Hi4-Z universal off-road hybrid architecture.[6]

  15. 2025-08-16

    GWM's Brazilian plant is completed and starts production. The first vehicle off the line is a Haval H6 GT, which Brazil's president signs. The official history page does not call the plant a Latin America hub or say it creates conditions for local Tank production.[6]

  16. 2025-08-27

    The all-new Tank 500 is launched with upgraded intelligent driving assistance and a smart cockpit.[6][1]

  17. 2025-11-06

    The all-new Tank 400 is launched (per the GWM official history page; the 2025 annual report summarises the two launches as August and October, a discrepancy between primary sources).[6][1]

  18. 2025

    Tank sells 234,442 vehicles in 2025 (annual report), with cumulative global sales above 830,000, remaining China's best-selling off-road SUV brand; overseas sales reach 58,140 and new-energy penetration hits 54.58%. The Tank 300 diesel travels to Antarctica with the Xue Long 2 research vessel. Tank's Hi4 team also wins the T2.E production new-energy group title at the China Taklimakan Rally, and the Tank 300 ranks first among hard-core SUVs in the 2025 CACSI customer-satisfaction survey.[1][2][6]

  19. 2026-01-06

    GWM's official history page says the company announced the final name of its new platform, Guiyuan (归元). That page does not describe it as a native AI all-powertrain platform.[6]

  20. 2026-10-23

    The 2027 model-year Tank 500 is officially launched in four variants, with a guide price of RMB 335,000-375,000. The starting price of RMB 335,000 still holds.[22][23]

Current operations

Model line-up and pricing

As of the research cut-off date the Tank China website lists four model families: the Tank 300 (including the long-wheelbase Tank 300L in Hi4-T and Hi4-Z forms), Tank 400, Tank 500 and Tank 700. Official suggested retail starting prices on the website, accessed on 2026-10-29, are: Tank 300 from RMB 199,800; Tank 400 petrol from RMB 249,800; Tank 500 from RMB 335,000; and Tank 700 from RMB 428,000 (Hi4-Z RMB 508,000).[7] Plug-in-hybrid (Hi4-T/Hi4-Z) prices are for the whole vehicle including the traction battery; petrol and diesel versions do not carry a traction battery.[7]

Powertrains and technology platforms

Per the 2025 annual report, Tank's powertrain matrix spans 2.0T petrol, 2.4T diesel and 3.0T V6 fuel engines, plus the Hi4-T (strong off-road) and Hi4-Z (universal off-road) hybrid architectures; new-energy penetration reached 54.58% in 2025.[1] The 2024 internal-control evaluation report describes Hi4-T as the strong off-road super-hybrid architecture, while Hi4-Z, unveiled in October 2024, is the universal off-road super electric-hybrid architecture for extended range and mixed on-road/off-road use.[4] In 2025 the second-generation Hi4-T was upgraded with charging speed up 30%; the all-new Tank 500 and Tank 400 launched in 2025 added what the annual report calls 'industry-leading' intelligent driving assistance and smart cockpits.[1] Models sit on a body-on-frame platform; the self-developed 3.0T V6 + 9AT was GWM's first large-displacement passenger-car powertrain, while the founding Tank 300 pairs a 2.0T with three differential locks, creep mode and tank turn.[11][9] The technology roots trace to the RMB 20 billion, five-year 'Tank·WEY' off-road platform.[9] On 6 January 2026 GWM announced the final name of its new platform, 'Guiyuan' (归元).[6]

Brand strategy and category evolution

The 2025 annual report describes a 'four-all strategy' (all segments, all powertrains, all scenarios, globalisation). The all-new Tank 500 and Tank 400 are positioned as 'all-scenario intelligent luxury off-road SUVs' with comfort features (refrigerator, large screen and sofa-style seats), extending the brand from hard-core off-road toward 'off-road + new energy'; by year-end both models exceeded 6,000 monthly sales.[1] A Hubei Daily interview places the Tank 400 and Tank 500 in the RMB 280,000-400,000 mid-to-high-end band.[13]

Manufacturing bases

A Hubei Daily interview published on 22 January 2025, conducted in mid-December 2024, described three GWM off-road plants at that time: the Chongqing Yongchuan base built the Tank 300 and Haval models (annual capacity about 220,000 units); the Hubei Jingmen base built the Tank 400 and Tank 500 (about 80% of that plant's output; designed annual capacity 120,000, maximum 180,000, utilisation around 145%); and the Baoding base built the Tank 700 and part of the universal off-road range. These are the interview's figures, not a capacity disclosure as of 2026-10-29.[13] On 16 August 2025 GWM's Brazil plant was completed and began production; the first vehicle off the line was a Haval H6 GT.[6]

Overseas business

In 2025 Tank sold 58,140 vehicles overseas, with a sales network covering more than 30 countries and regions including Australia, Mexico, the Middle East, ASEAN and South Africa; GWM has set up officially certified 'Tank Union' owner clubs in Australia, South Africa and Russia.[1] Per the 2024 internal-control evaluation report, during 2024 the Tank range successively entered Kazakhstan, Chile, Indonesia, South Africa and Mexico.[4]

Motorsport and reputation

In the 2025 China Taklimakan Rally, GWM's Tank Hi4 team won the T2.E production new-energy group title; at the 2026 event GWM's fleet again took the T2.E new-energy manufacturers' cup as part of a sweep of the production groups.[6][1] In the 2025 CACSI customer-satisfaction survey the Tank 300 ranked first among hard-core SUVs.[6] The Tank 300 diesel travelled to Antarctica with the Xue Long 2 research vessel, becoming the first Chinese off-road vehicle to take part in Antarctic scientific expeditions; the all-new Tank 500 was chosen as the designated vehicle of CCTV News' 2026 Spring Festival Gala new-media coverage; and on 25 December 2022 the Tank 300 received a five-star ANCAP safety rating in Australia.[1][6][20]

Founders and leadership

Founder

Great Wall Motor founder Wei Jianjun serves as the company's chairman and actual controller, and Tank is the premium off-road brand launched under his leadership. In May 2023 Wei received the China Automotive Industry Rao Bin Award from the Society of Automotive Engineers of China, and on 12 May 2025 Brazil's president met Wei and said he looked forward to models such as the Tank 300 Hi4-T entering Brazil.[6] Further detail on the founder and the parent group is covered in the Great Wall Motor (gwm) deep research.

Brand ownership and governance

Tank is not a separately listed company but a brand operated within Great Wall Motor, whose actual controller is Wei Jianjun. Per the 2025 annual report, the group's brands include Haval, WEY, Tank, ORA and Great Wall Pickup. Tank does not separately disclose a board of directors; group-level governance is covered in the Great Wall Motor (gwm) deep research.[1]

Current brand CEO

According to media reports, Gu Yukun currently serves as Tank brand CEO. GWM has not issued a formal announcement of this appointment, and the reporting is based on his social-media title and public appearances.[12][19] Gu has worked in the off-road segment for years across product development, user outreach, communications and service; he previously served as a GWM brand operations director and product director for the Tank 600 and Tank 500. On 3 November 2023 Sina Auto reported that Gu, then Tank 500 product director, became Tank executive deputy general manager; the same report quotes a Weibo post that day by GWM chief growth officer Li Ruifeng. It was not an exchange filing.[14][19]

Former brand heads

On 3 November 2023 Sina Auto reported that Liu Yanzhao would from that day manage both WEY and Tank, and that Gu Yukun, then Tank 500 product director, became Tank executive deputy general manager. The report quotes a Weibo post the same day by GWM chief growth officer Li Ruifeng, who said Liu had been WEY's chief technology officer and would concurrently become WEY general manager. This is a same-day media report of that Weibo post, not an exchange filing.[14] A separate media report says Chang Yao was Tank brand CEO from May 2025 and was later succeeded by Gu Yukun.[12] The above covers public roles and business experience only.

Competition

Our judgment: competitive scope. Tank competes in China's hard-core and universal off-road SUV segment. The most direct rival is BYD's Fang Cheng Bao, which by its own reports sold 234,637 vehicles in 2025, up 316.11% year on year, slightly ahead of Tank's preliminary full-year monthly-brief figure of 232,713.[18][17] The comparison uses the monthly production-and-sales-brief figure (232,713); the audited 2025 annual report records Tank sales of 234,442, so the two primary-source figures differ and the annual-report figure is slightly higher than Fang Cheng Bao's reported 234,637.[1] This is our analytical peer selection, not a sales ranking.

Our judgment: strengths. Tank has a first-mover position and an installed base: cumulative global sales exceeded 830,000 by end-2025, and the 2025 annual report records it as China's best-selling off-road SUV brand, with a differentiated three-powertrain matrix (fuel, Hi4-T, Hi4-Z), 54.58% new-energy penetration and production spread across three dedicated off-road plants.[1][13] A roughly 50% domestic share of the hard-core off-road segment, plus the brand's rally and Antarctica credentials, further differentiate it.[15][1]

Our judgment: weaknesses and uncertainty. Annual volume in 2025 was essentially flat against 2024: the annual report's 234,442 compares with 232,195 in the 2024 internal-control evaluation report and with 231,001, the prior-year cumulative in the December 2025 production-and-sales brief. Volume then fell 10.62% in the first half of 2026 and 13.53% in the first nine months.[1][4][17][3][16] Fang Cheng Bao's December 2025 monthly sales hit a record 50,868 units, and its Titan 7 model reached 100,000 cumulative sales within about four months of launch, by mid-January 2026.[18]

Outlook

Company plans. The 2025 annual report states that GWM will strengthen Tank's off-road ecosystem barriers and consolidate its premium position, relying on the off-road, premium-MPV and new-energy tracks. Tank's disclosed directions include deepening globalisation, expanding Hi4-T/Hi4-Z deployment and pushing intelligent driving assistance across the line-up.[1] In a Hubei Daily interview published on 22 January 2025 (conducted in mid-December 2024), the Jingmen plant general manager said the Tank 400 and Tank 500 were planned to add mapless NOA. That was a plan stated in the interview, not an unfulfilled company plan as of 2026-10-29. The 2025 annual report says the all-new Tank 500 and Tank 400 launched in August and October 2025 are equipped with intelligent driving assistance.[13][1] These are disclosed directions and targets, not completed results.

Our judgment. The brand's growth now hinges on replacing the Tank 300's lost volume with higher-priced models (Tank 400/500/700 and the 2027 Tank 500) and on overseas expansion, where 2025 overseas sales were 58,140 vehicles, roughly one-quarter of the total.[1] The 2027 Tank 500 refresh and the intelligent-driving upgrades already added to the all-new Tank 500 and Tank 400 are steps toward closing the gap with new-energy rivals, but whether this restores volume depends on sustained demand and pricing discipline, which the 10.62% first-half and 13.53% nine-month declines do not yet support.[3][16][7]

Risks

  • Domestic competition. New-energy hard-core off-road competitors, led by BYD's Fang Cheng Bao, are pressuring Tank's fuel-vehicle base; Fang Cheng Bao's reported 2025 sales of 234,637 slightly exceeded Tank's preliminary full-year figure of 232,713.[18][17]
  • Volume decline. Tank sales fell 10.62% year on year in the first half of 2026 (92,653 units) and 13.53% in the first nine months (142,796 units).[3][16]
  • Dependence on the parent. Tank does not publish separate financials; its revenue, cost and profit sit within Great Wall Motor, so the brand's standalone economics cannot be assessed from public filings.[1]
  • Management transition. Media report that Chang Yao was Tank brand CEO from May 2025 and was later succeeded by Gu Yukun. The current appointment is not documented by a formal company announcement, which leaves execution continuity uncertain while volume is falling.[12]
  • Overseas risks. The parent's 2025 annual report identifies international geopolitical conflict and rising trade barriers as sources of uncertainty in overseas markets, which likewise affect Tank's globalisation.[1]

概览

坦克是长城汽车股份有限公司旗下的硬派越野 SUV 品牌。其首款车型坦克 300 于 2020 年 12 月 17 日在魏牌（WEY）旗下上市，2021 年 4 月 19 日坦克品牌独立运营，成为长城汽车第五大整车品牌。[6][9] 坦克定位专业越野品牌，以「铁汉柔情」为品牌主张，覆盖紧凑型至大型 SUV，布局汽油、柴油、Hi4-T、Hi4-Z 多种动力。[1]

品牌由一款「现象级」产品孵化而来。坦克 300 预售 20 天订单破万，上市首月交付 6,018 辆；因订单积压过多，魏牌自 2021 年 3 月 28 日起暂停接收新订单，长城汽车随后将这一产品线分拆为独立品牌。[10]

2025 年坦克销售 234,442 辆，全球累计销量超 83 万辆，蝉联中国越野 SUV 销量第一；海外销售 58,140 辆，新能源渗透率 54.58%。[1][2] 品牌年销量从 2022 年的 123,881 辆，增至 2023 年 162,539 辆、2024 年 232,195 辆（内部控制评价报告；2025 年 12 月产销快报「去年同期」为 231,001 辆）、2025 年 234,442 辆；2026 年前三季度销售 142,796 辆，同比下降 13.53%。[8][5][4][17][16]

在售车型官方建议零售价约 19.98 万元（坦克 300）至 50.8 万元（坦克 700 Hi4-Z），是长城汽车产品矩阵中定位较高的品牌之一。[7] 湖北日报专访称，中国市场每售出两辆越野车就有一辆来自长城，长城汽车硬派越野 SUV 市占率超过 50%；国金证券研报则将坦克自身的国内硬派越野市占率表述为约 50%。[13][15]

历史沿革

  1. 2020-12-17

    坦克 300 越野版上市，售价 17.58 万-21.38 万元，作为魏牌首款越野 SUV，搭载 2.0T（167 kW / 387 N·m）+ 采埃孚 8AT，配备三把锁、蠕行模式与坦克转弯，成为坦克产品线首款车型。[9][6]

  2. 2021-03

    因需求旺盛（预售 20 天订单破万、上市首月交付 6,018 辆），魏牌自 3 月 28 日起暂停接收坦克 300 新订单以消化积压。[10]

  3. 2021-04-19

    2021 年 4 月 19 日，坦克品牌在上海车展宣布独立发展，成为长城汽车第五大整车品牌（长城汽车官网发展历程）。国际在线 2021 年 4 月 6 日稿则写，自 2021 年 3 月 31 日起坦克成为第五大品牌。两说并列。[6][10]

  4. 2021

    坦克官网 2022 年 1 月 7 日新闻称，2021 年全年累计交付 84,588 辆。这是交付口径，不是销量。[21]

  5. 2022-03-18

    坦克品牌第二款车型、中大型 SUV 坦克 500 上市，售价 33.5 万-39.5 万元，首次搭载长城自研 3.0T V6（260 kW / 500 N·m）+ 纵置 9AT 动力总成，并配备 48V 轻混系统。[11]

  6. 2022-12-25

    坦克 300 获得澳大利亚新车安全评价组织（ANCAP）五星安全认证。长城汽车当日新闻《再取佳绩 坦克300荣膺澳大利亚ANCAP五星安全评价》报道了这一结果。官网发展历程中 2023 年 10 月 24 日的条目是「2023海外投资者共创会」，不是这次安全认证。[20]

  7. 2022

    坦克品牌全年销售 123,881 辆（同比 +46.45%），其中坦克 300 销售 101,787 辆；品牌连续七个月销量破万，硬派越野市场市占率约 67%。[8]

  8. 2023-06-26

    坦克 500 Hi4-T 上市（33.5 万元），为品牌首款 Hi4-T 插电混动越野车型。[6]

  9. 2023-09-25

    坦克 400 Hi4-T 上市，补齐都市机能越野产品线。[6]

  10. 2023

    坦克品牌全年销售 162,539 辆（同比 +31.21%）。[5]

  11. 2024-02-26

    坦克品牌旗舰车型坦克 700 Hi4-T 于 2024 年 2 月 26 日上市。据新华网，极致版建议零售价 42.8 万元，极境版 46.8 万元，限定版 70 万元，搭载 3.0T V6 + P2 电机 + 9HAT。[24]

  12. 2024-10

    据 2024 年内部控制评价报告，长城汽车发布泛越野超级电混架构 Hi4-Z，与强越野架构 Hi4-T 形成互补。官网发展历程则称秋季技术发布会上亮相的该架构为「纵置双电机混联」。[4][6]

  13. 2024

    2024 年内部控制评价报告写坦克品牌销售 232,195 辆、同比 +42.57%。以同报告采用的 2023 年 162,539 辆相除，增速约为 42.85%，与所列 +42.57% 不一致。2025 年 12 月产销快报「去年同期」累计为 231,001 辆。两个一手数字并列。当年坦克系列车型相继登陆哈萨克斯坦、智利、印尼、南非、墨西哥等市场。[4][17]

  14. 2025-01-01

    坦克 500 Hi4-Z 全球上市，为品牌首款 Hi4-Z 泛越野混动架构车型。[6]

  15. 2025-08-16

    长城汽车巴西工厂竣工投产，首台下线车型为哈弗 H6 GT，巴西总统在该车上签名。官网发展历程没有写「拉美市场核心枢纽」，也没有写为坦克等车型本土化生产创造条件。[6]

  16. 2025-08-27

    全新坦克 500 上市，升级智能辅助驾驶与智慧座舱。[6][1]

  17. 2025-11-06

    全新坦克 400 上市（据长城汽车官网发展历程为 11 月 6 日；2025 年年报将两款新车概述为「8 月和 10 月」，一手来源间存在差异）。[6][1]

  18. 2025

    2025 年坦克品牌销售 234,442 辆（年报），全球累计销量超 83 万辆，蝉联中国越野 SUV 销量第一；海外销售 58,140 辆，新能源渗透率达 54.58%。坦克 300 柴油版随「雪龙 2 号」赴南极。同年环塔拉力赛坦克 Hi4 车队斩获 T2.E 量产新能源组冠军，坦克 300 在中国燃油汽车用户满意度（CACSI）测评中获硬派 SUV 第一名。[1][2][6]

  19. 2026-01-06

    长城汽车官网发展历程写，公司官宣新平台最终命名「归元」。该页没有写公司称其为原生 AI 全动力汽车平台。[6]

  20. 2026-10-23

    2027 款坦克 500 正式上市，共四款车型，指导价 33.5 万-37.5 万元。起售价 33.5 万元仍然成立。[22][23]

现状

车型谱系与定价

截至研究截止日，坦克中国官网列示坦克 300（含长轴版坦克 300L 的 Hi4-T、Hi4-Z 版本）、坦克 400、坦克 500、坦克 700 四大车系。2026-10-29 访问官网的官方建议零售起售价为：坦克 300 19.98 万元起；坦克 400 汽油版 24.98 万元起；坦克 500 33.5 万元起；坦克 700 42.8 万元起（Hi4-Z 50.8 万元）。[7] 插电混动（Hi4-T/Hi4-Z）车型价格为含动力电池的整车价格，汽油、柴油版不含动力电池。[7]

动力与技术平台

据 2025 年年报，坦克动力矩阵覆盖 2.0T 汽油、2.4T 柴油、3.0T V6 燃油，以及 Hi4-T（强越野）与 Hi4-Z（泛越野）混动架构，2025 年新能源渗透率达 54.58%。[1] 据 2024 年内部控制评价报告，Hi4-T 为「越野超级混动架构」，2024 年 10 月发布的 Hi4-Z 为「泛越野超级电混架构」，面向超长续航、可城可野的泛越野需求。[4] 2025 年第二代 Hi4-T 焕新升级、充电速度提升 30%；2025 年上市的全新坦克 500、全新坦克 400 搭载了年报所称「行业领先」的智能辅助驾驶与智慧座舱。[1] 车型基于非承载式车身平台，自研 3.0T V6 + 9AT 是长城首款大排量乘用车动力总成；开山之作坦克 300 则以 2.0T 匹配三把锁、蠕行模式与坦克转弯。[11][9] 品牌技术根基可追溯至魏牌斥资 200 亿元、历时 5 年打造的「坦克·WEY」越野平台。[9] 2026 年 1 月 6 日，长城汽车官宣新平台最终命名「归元」。[6]

品牌战略与品类演进

2025 年年报提出「四全战略」（全车型、全动力、全场景、全球化）。全新坦克 500、全新坦克 400 以「全场景智能豪华越野 SUV」定位，配以冰箱、彩电、大沙发等舒适配置，带动品牌从硬派越野向「越野+新能源」拓展；年末两车月销量双双突破 6,000 辆。[1] 湖北日报专访将坦克 400、坦克 500 的售价区间定位在 28 万-40 万元、中高端。[13]

生产基地

湖北日报 2025 年 1 月 22 日专访（采访在 2024 年 12 月中旬）描述了当时的三家越野车基地：重庆永川基地生产坦克 300 与哈弗车型，年产能约 22 万辆；湖北荆门基地生产坦克 400、坦克 500，约占该基地产量八成，设计年产能 12 万辆、最大 18 万辆，产能利用率约 145%；保定基地生产坦克 700 及部分泛越野车型。以上是专访当时的口径，不是截至 2026-10-29 的产能披露。[13] 2025 年 8 月 16 日长城汽车巴西工厂竣工投产，首台下线车型为哈弗 H6 GT。[6]

海外业务

2025 年坦克海外销售 58,140 辆，销售网络覆盖澳大利亚、墨西哥、中东、东盟、南非等 30 多个国家和地区，并在澳洲、南非、俄罗斯建立官方认证的「坦克联盟」俱乐部。[1] 据 2024 年内部控制评价报告，2024 年坦克系列车型相继登陆哈萨克斯坦、智利、印尼、南非、墨西哥等市场。[4]

赛事与口碑

2025 年中国环塔国际拉力赛，长城坦克 Hi4 车队斩获 T2.E 量产新能源组冠军；2026 年环塔拉力赛，长城车队再度夺得 T2.E 新能源厂商杯冠军，实现量产组「大满贯」。[6][1] 2025 年中国燃油汽车用户满意度（CACSI）测评中，坦克 300 获硬派 SUV 第一名。[6] 坦克 300 柴油版随「雪龙 2 号」赴南极，成为首辆参与南极科考的中国越野车；全新坦克 500 获选央视新闻 2026 年春晚新媒体指定用车；2022 年 12 月 25 日坦克 300 在澳大利亚获 ANCAP 五星安全认证。[1][6][20]

创始人与管理层

创始人

长城汽车创始人魏建军现任公司董事长、实际控制人，坦克即其主导下长城汽车推出的高端越野品牌。2023 年 5 月，魏建军获中国汽车工程学会颁发的「中国汽车工业饶斌奖」；2025 年 5 月 12 日，巴西总统会见魏建军并表示期待坦克 300 Hi4-T 等车型登陆巴西。[6] 创始人及母公司更详细信息见长城汽车（gwm）深度研究。

品牌归属与治理

坦克并非单独上市公司，而是长城汽车旗下品牌，长城汽车实际控制人为魏建军。据 2025 年年报，集团旗下品牌包括哈弗、魏牌、坦克、欧拉、长城皮卡等。坦克不单独披露董事会，集团治理详见长城汽车（gwm）深度研究。[1]

现任品牌 CEO

据媒体报道，谷玉坤现任坦克品牌 CEO。长城汽车未就该任命发布正式公告，媒体报道依据其社交平台头衔及公开场合现身。[12][19] 谷玉坤深耕越野车领域多年，历经产品开发、用户触达、传播、服务等环节；此前曾任长城汽车品牌运营总监、坦克 600 与坦克 500 商品总监。2023 年 11 月 3 日，新浪汽车报道原坦克 500 商品总监谷玉坤出任坦克品牌执行副总经理，并引用了长城汽车 CGO 李瑞峰当天的微博。这不是交易所公告。[14][19]

前任品牌负责人

2023 年 11 月 3 日，新浪汽车报道：刘艳钊即日起同时负责魏牌和坦克，原坦克 500 商品总监谷玉坤出任坦克品牌执行副总经理。报道引用长城汽车 CGO 李瑞峰当天的微博：刘艳钊此前一直担任魏牌首席技术官，此次兼任魏牌总经理。这是对当日微博的媒体报道，不是交易所公告。[14] 另据媒体报道，常尧自 2025 年 5 月起任坦克品牌 CEO，其后由谷玉坤接任。[12] 以上均为公开职务与商业履历。

坦克为长城汽车旗下硬派越野 SUV 品牌，未单独披露品牌层面的财务数据（营收、毛利率、净利润、研发投入、现金流等）。因不存在品牌层面的净利润，也就无从区分「归属于母公司股东」或「归属于普通股股东」的口径。作为参照，母公司长城汽车 2025 年年报（中国会计准则，经德勤华永审计）披露营业总收入 2,228.24 亿元、归属于上市公司股东的净利润 98.65 亿元（同比下降 22.07%）。母公司财务数据见长城汽车（gwm）深度研究。本页不构成任何交易推荐。

竞争格局

我们的判断：竞争范围。坦克所处的赛道是中国硬派越野与泛越野 SUV 市场。最直接的对手是比亚迪方程豹：据其自身披露，2025 年方程豹销售 234,637 辆、同比增长 316.11%，以微弱优势高于坦克产销快报口径的全年 232,713 辆。[18][17] 这一对比采用的是产销快报口径（232,713）；而经审计的 2025 年年报记录坦克销量 234,442 辆，两个一手来源数字存在差异，年报口径略高于方程豹披露的 234,637 辆。[1] 此为分析所选的竞争范围，不是销量排名。

我们的判断：优势。坦克具备先发优势与存量用户基础：截至 2025 年末全球累计销量超 83 万辆，2025 年年报载明其为「中国越野 SUV 销量第一」，拥有燃油、Hi4-T、Hi4-Z 三动力差异化矩阵与 54.58% 的新能源渗透率，且由三家专业越野工厂分工生产。[1][13] 国内硬派越野约 50% 的市占率，加上环塔夺冠与南极科考的经历，进一步构成差异化背书。[15][1]

我们的判断：短板与不确定性。品牌年销量 2025 年相对 2024 年基本持平：年报 234,442 辆，对照 2024 年内部控制评价报告的 232,195 辆，以及 2025 年 12 月产销快报「去年同期」的 231,001 辆。2026 年上半年同比下滑 10.62%，前三季度下滑 13.53%。[1][4][17][3][16] 方程豹 2025 年 12 月单月销量达 50,868 辆的历史新高；其钛 7 车型上市约四个月、至 2026 年 1 月中旬累计销量已达 10 万辆。[18]

未来展望

公司公开规划。2025 年年报称，长城汽车将依托越野、高端 MPV、新能源三大优势赛道，强化坦克品牌越野生态壁垒、巩固高端市场。坦克披露的方向包括深化全球化、扩大 Hi4-T/Hi4-Z 布局，并在全系推进智能辅助驾驶。[1] 湖北日报 2025 年 1 月 22 日专访（采访在 2024 年 12 月中旬）中，荆门基地总经理称未来坦克 400、坦克 500 等车型计划推出无图 NOA。这是该次采访里的计划，不是截至 2026-10-29 仍未落地的现行规划。2025 年年报写明，2025 年 8 月和 10 月上市的全新坦克 500、全新坦克 400 已搭载智能辅助驾驶。[13][1] 以上为公司披露的方向，不是已实现的业绩。

我们的判断。品牌后续增长取决于能否以坦克 400/500/700 及 2027 款坦克 500 等高价位车型填补坦克 300 下滑的量，并扩大海外市场（2025 年海外销售 58,140 辆、约占四分之一）。[1] 2027 款坦克 500 改款及全新坦克 500、全新坦克 400 已搭载的智能辅助驾驶升级，是缩小与新能源对手智驾差距的举措，但能否恢复销量，取决于持续需求与价格纪律，2026 年上半年 10.62%、前三季度 13.53% 的下滑尚不足以支撑这一判断。[3][16][7]

风险

  • 国内竞争。以比亚迪方程豹为首的新能源硬派越野竞品正在挤压坦克的燃油基本盘；方程豹披露的 2025 年销量 234,637 辆，略高于坦克产销快报口径的全年 232,713 辆。[18][17]
  • 销量下滑。2026 年上半年坦克销售 92,653 辆、同比下降 10.62%，前三季度销售 142,796 辆、同比下降 13.53%。[3][16]
  • 依赖母公司。坦克未单独披露财务，其收入、成本、利润均并入长城汽车，无法从公开文件评估品牌独立盈利能力。[1]
  • 管理层交接。据媒体报道，常尧自 2025 年 5 月起任坦克品牌 CEO，其后由谷玉坤接任。现任任命未见公司正式公告，在销量下滑期间，交接带来执行连续性上的不确定性。[12]
  • 海外风险。母公司 2025 年年报将国际地缘冲突、贸易壁垒增多列为海外市场不确定性来源，同样影响坦克的全球化布局。[1]
