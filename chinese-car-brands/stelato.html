Overview

Stelato (享界, stylised STELATO) is the premium new-energy brand jointly created by Beijing New Energy Vehicle Co., Ltd. (北汽新能源) — the operating subsidiary of the listed BAIC BluePark New Energy Technology Co., Ltd. (Shanghai Stock Exchange 600733.SH) — and Huawei, under Huawei's Harmony Intelligent Mobility Alliance (HIMA) Smart Selection model. In this model BAIC is responsible for whole-vehicle engineering, manufacturing and quality, while Huawei contributes product definition, intelligent-driving and cockpit technology, marketing and the HIMA sales channel; Huawei holds no equity in the brand [8][1].

The brand debuted with the Stelato S9 executive sedan on 6 August 2024, priced from RMB 399,800 [6][25]. It has since grown into a four-segment line-up spanning the S9 sedan, the S9T estate (September 2025), the G9 large hardcore SUV (August 2026) and the V8 MPV (pre-sale since September 2026) [12][17].

The brand targets the RMB 300,000-and-above premium segment, where it competes with Mercedes-Benz, BMW and Audi. In 2025 it sold 42,700 vehicles and passed 10,000 monthly sales for the first time that December [1]; in H1 2026 it delivered 21,018 vehicles, up 119.58% year on year [2], and by September 2026 cumulative S9/S9T deliveries had passed 60,000 with nine consecutive months leading the RMB 300,000-plus new-energy sedan segment [16].

Current operations

Business and positioning

Stelato is the premium arm of BAIC BluePark's dual-brand strategy, complementing the mass-market Arcfox (极狐) brand. BAIC BluePark's 2025 annual report describes Stelato as a global luxury new-energy brand focused on the RMB 300,000-and-above market, aiming to reach the first tier of the premium segment by 2030, with a sedan/SUV/MPV product matrix [1]. On the sales channel, in mainland China Stelato vehicles are sold through Zhongyou Zhixing (中邮智行), a subsidiary of China General Technology Group, as the exclusive general distributor, which purchases the vehicles and sells them to end consumers through its store network; as a result, China General Technology Group replaced BAIC Group as BAIC BluePark's largest customer in 2025 [29]. In Q1 2026 Stelato models accounted for more than 30% of BAIC BluePark's volume and over 60% of its revenue [22]; for H1 2026 the volume share was about 21% while the revenue contribution remained above 60%, making the brand the core of the group's premiumisation drive [28].

Latest financial performance (parent)

Because Stelato does not report separately, its economics are visible only through parent BAIC BluePark. The parent's H1 2026 revenue was RMB11.597 billion (up 21.86% year on year) and its net loss attributable to owners of the parent was RMB1.938 billion, narrower than RMB2.308 billion a year earlier; consolidated gross margin turned positive at 4.21% [2][28]. By quarter, Q1 2026 revenue was RMB4.099 billion (up 8.65%) with an attributable net loss of RMB0.870 billion, and Q2 2026 revenue was RMB7.498 billion with an attributable net loss of RMB1.068 billion [26][28].

Technology

The brand's models are built on Huawei's technology stack: Huawei Qiankun ADS intelligent driving (S9 first deployed ADS 3.0, later upgraded through ADS 4/4.1 and ADS 5), the Tuling chassis platform, Huawei's 800V high-voltage and Whale battery platform, and the HarmonyOS cockpit with the ALPS healthy-cabin suite [6][7]. From March 2026 the S9/S9T carry an 896-line dual-beam lidar, described by Huawei as the highest-specification mass-produced lidar at the time [21]. The G9 and V8 are designed on an L3 architecture; the G9 was approved for a 120 km/h L3 road-test licence [12].

Manufacturing

Stelato models are produced at the Stelato Super Factory in Miyun District, Beijing. The factory has steel-aluminium hybrid-body flexible production with 100% automation of key processes, and BAIC New Energy planned to invest about RMB1.991 billion to upgrade it for BE22 3.0-platform models and digitalised production [1]. Media reported in 2026 that after the upgrade the plant can build sedan, estate, SUV and MPV bodies on shared lines, roughly doubling delivery capacity [22].

Channel

Sales run through Huawei's HIMA channel. As of end-2025 BAIC BluePark had over 900 national outlets, of which 17 were admitted to the Stelato dedicated network, with a plan to operate 40 Stelato dedicated outlets in 2026 [1].

Sales and users

Stelato monthly sales first passed 10,000 in December 2025 [1]. The S9/S9T have led the RMB 300,000-plus new-energy sedan segment for nine consecutive months as of September 2026, with an average transaction price of RMB349,800 — second in the luxury-sedan market [22][16].

Overseas

Stelato has not disclosed overseas sales. BAIC BluePark's overseas expansion so far centres on the Arcfox brand, which entered 13 countries in 2025 [1]; no separate Stelato export figure has been published.

Founders and leadership

Founders and group sponsors

Stelato has no private founder; it was jointly created by BAIC and Huawei. On the BAIC side, group chairman Zhang Jianyong has been the public face of the project, saying at the S9 launch that BAIC put all its resources into the model and announcing in November 2025 that BAIC would invest RMB 20 billion in the brand; Huawei's Yu Chengdong (Richard Yu, executive director and chairman of Huawei's Device BG) has co-presented the brand's launches [25][10][6].

Stelato business-unit management

  • Ma Lei (马磊) — president of the Stelato business unit (享界事业部总裁) since June 2025, concurrently a deputy general manager of BAIC BluePark. The BAIC BluePark 2025 annual report records that he has served as a company deputy general manager since November 2024 and concurrently took the Stelato business-unit president role in June 2025 [1].
  • Pang Xinfu (庞新福) — vice-president of the Stelato business unit since October 2025, concurrently a deputy general manager of BAIC BluePark (per the 2025 annual report) [1].
  • Guo Dongdong (郭东栋) — vice-president of the Stelato business unit and general manager of the Stelato Super Factory, who joined from the Beijing Benz manufacturing system in late March 2026 [22].

BAIC BluePark leadership

  • Liu Guanqiao (刘观桥) — chairman of BAIC BluePark since 20 March 2026. He was BAIC BluePark general manager from 18 March 2025, and stepped up to chairman after Zhang Guofu's resignation; his earlier career included sales roles at Beijing Hyundai and management roles at BAIC Group [23][24].
  • Song Jun (宋军) — general manager of BAIC BluePark since 20 March 2026. Previously he was employee director, executive deputy general manager and chief financial officer of BAIC BluePark, and resigned those roles to become general manager [23].
  • Zhang Guofu (张国富) — BAIC BluePark chairman from 18 March 2025 until his resignation effective 19 March 2026, when media reported he moved to BAIC Motor (北汽股份); at the Stelato G9 first-delivery event on 23 September 2026 he attended as a standing committee member and deputy general manager of BAIC Group, stating the Huawei partnership is unchanged [24][16].

Ownership and governance

Stelato is operated by BAIC BluePark's subsidiary BAIC New Energy; Huawei participates under the Smart Selection model and holds no equity. BAIC BluePark's controlling shareholder is BAIC Group, which directly held 23.14% at end-2025 (with related entities Beijing Automotive Co., BAIC (Guangzhou) and Bohai Automotive holding further stakes), and BAIC Group is a wholly state-owned enterprise supervised by the Beijing SASAC [8][1][27].

Competition

Stelato competes in China's RMB 300,000-and-above premium new-energy segment, the traditional stronghold of Mercedes-Benz, BMW and Audi. The S9/S9T are positioned against the Mercedes-Benz E-Class, BMW 5 Series and Audi A6L, and the G9 against large luxury SUVs such as the Yangwang U8 [7][14]. Within HIMA, Stelato coexists with AITO (Seres), Luxeed (Chery), Maextro (JAC) and Shangjie (SAIC), occupying the premium sedan/estate band [17].

Our judgment. Stelato's main strengths, based on reported figures, are the combination of Huawei's intelligent-driving and cockpit stack with BAIC's manufacturing, and a strong premium trade-in base — about 70% of refreshed S9 buyers and about 80% of first G9 owners came from Mercedes-Benz, BMW or other traditional luxury brands, suggesting the brand is taking share from incumbents rather than only from new-energy peers [22][15]. Its main weaknesses are a short track record, dependence on Huawei's channel and intelligent-driving brand, and single-segment concentration: monthly volume cooled in H2 2026 as the S9/S9T aged, before the G9 and V8 scaled up [17].

Outlook

Company plans. BAIC Group chairman Zhang Jianyong announced in November 2025 that BAIC would invest RMB 20 billion in building the Stelato brand with a full-category line-up, and media reported in September 2026 that BAIC's cumulative investment in the project had already exceeded RMB 20 billion, describing it as the group's top-priority project [10][16]. BAIC BluePark's annual report says the brand will keep iterating the flagship 9-series and launch an intelligent luxury all-scenario SUV and a premium family MPV, with the Stelato dedicated network planned to reach 40 outlets [1]; the G9 (SUV) has already launched and the V8 (MPV) is in pre-sale [12][18].

Our judgment. Whether Stelato sustains its momentum depends on three premises: converting the strong G9 launch orders and the V8 pre-sale into stable monthly volume; broadening beyond the S9/S9T sedan/estate pair, which showed a mid-2026 cooling, into the SUV and MPV segments; and keeping its Huawei-supplied intelligence current amid HIMA's restructuring, in which AITO was handed to Seres to lead in September 2026 while Huawei said it would focus resources on the other four brands [17]. The brand's profitability also cannot yet be assessed independently, since it does not disclose standalone financials and its parent BAIC BluePark is still loss-making [1].

Risks

  • Segment concentration and demand cooling. Stelato's volume is concentrated in the S9/S9T; media covering HIMA monthly deliveries reported the brand's monthly deliveries cooled to roughly 1,600 units in August 2026 before the G9 and V8 scaled up [17].
  • Dependence on Huawei's channel and model. Sales run through the HIMA channel and the brand relies on Huawei's intelligent-driving and cockpit supply; BAIC has stated the partnership is unchanged, but HIMA's restructuring in late 2026 (AITO handed to Seres) is an external variable for the other four brands [16][17].
  • No standalone financial disclosure. As a brand within BAIC BluePark, Stelato does not report separate financials, so its profitability cannot be assessed independently; BAIC BluePark posted an attributable net loss of RMB4.563 billion in 2025 and RMB1.938 billion in H1 2026 [1][2].
  • Intense premium-market competition. The RMB 300,000-plus segment faces price competition from traditional luxury brands cutting prices and from rival new-energy flagships [7].
  • Short track record. The brand has existed only since 2024, and its long-term brand equity and residual value remain unproven [6].

概览

享界（英文名 STELATO）是北汽集团旗下高端新能源品牌，由上市公司北汽蓝谷新能源科技股份有限公司（上交所 600733.SH）的子公司北京新能源汽车股份有限公司（北汽新能源）与华为以鸿蒙智行「智选车」模式联合打造。该模式下，北汽负责整车工程、制造与品质，华为深度参与产品定义、智驾与座舱技术、营销及鸿蒙智行销售渠道，但华为不持有品牌股权[8][1]。

品牌以 2024 年 8 月 6 日上市的行政旗舰轿车享界 S9（39.98 万元起）为起点，S9 定位中大型行政豪华轿车，直接对标奔驰 E 级、宝马 5 系、奥迪 A6L[6][25]，随后扩展为轿车（S9）、旅行车（S9T，2025 年 9 月）、大型硬派 SUV（G9，2026 年 8 月）与 MPV（V8，2026 年 9 月预售）四大品类[12][17]。

品牌瞄准 30 万元以上高端市场，与奔驰、宝马、奥迪同台竞争。2025 年全年销量 4.27 万辆、12 月单月首次破万[1]；2026 年上半年交付 2.10 万辆、同比增长 119.58%[2]；截至 2026 年 9 月，S9/S9T 累计交付突破 6 万台，连续 9 个月居 30 万元以上新能源轿车销量冠军[16]。

现状

业务与定位

享界是北汽蓝谷「极狐＋享界」双品牌战略中的高端一翼。北汽蓝谷 2025 年年报将享界定位为「全球豪华新能源汽车品牌」，聚焦 30 万元及以上高端市场，规划轿车、SUV、MPV 全品类矩阵，目标 2030 年跻身高端市场第一阵营[1]。销售渠道上，享界车型在中国大陆由通用技术集团旗下中邮智行作为独家总经销商，采购后通过门店网络向终端消费者销售；2025 年北汽蓝谷第一大客户因此由北汽集团变更为通用技术集团[29]。2026 年一季度，享界车型销量占比超 30%、营收占比超 60%[22]；2026 年上半年享界销量占比约 21%、营收贡献仍超 60%，成为北汽蓝谷高端化与利润提升的核心支撑[28]。

母公司最新财务（截至 as_of）

因享界不单独披露报表，其经营表现只能透过母公司北汽蓝谷观察。北汽蓝谷 2026 年上半年营业收入 115.97 亿元（同比 +21.86%），归母净利润亏损 19.38 亿元，较上年同期亏损 23.08 亿元收窄；综合毛利率转正至 4.21%[2][28]。分季度看，2026 年一季度营收 40.99 亿元（同比 +8.65%）、归母净亏损 8.70 亿元；二季度营收 74.98 亿元、归母净亏损 10.68 亿元[26][28]。

技术

品牌车型围绕华为技术矩阵构建：华为乾崑智驾 ADS（S9 首发 ADS 3.0，后迭代至 ADS 4/4.1/5）、途灵平台、华为 800V 高压与巨鲸电池平台、鸿蒙座舱及鸿蒙 ALPS 健康座舱[6][7]。以首款车 S9 为例，其采用 OneBox 设计、风阻系数 0.193（上市时称全球量产豪华轿车最低），四驱零百加速 3.9 秒，CLTC 续航最高 816 公里，全系标配 5 档自适应空气悬架与后排零重力座椅[6][25]。2026 年 3 月起 S9/S9T 搭载 896 线双光路激光雷达，华为称其为当时全球量产最高规格[21]。G9 与 V8 采用 L3 级自动驾驶架构设计，G9 已获批时速 120 公里的 L3 道路测试牌照[12]。

制造

享界车型在北京市密云区享界超级工厂生产。该工厂具备钢铝混合车身柔性生产能力、关键工序自动化率 100%，并部署 AI 视觉防错、智能耐久测试等数智化技术；北汽新能源拟投资约 19.91 亿元建设「享界超级工厂高端平台车型产业化及产线数智化提升项目」，以具备 BE22 3.0 高端平台车型生产能力[1]。媒体 2026 年报道，技改完成后工厂可实现轿车、旅行车、SUV、MPV 多品类共线生产，交付能力翻倍[22]。

渠道

销售通过华为鸿蒙智行渠道进行。截至 2025 年末，北汽蓝谷全国门店总数超 900 家，其中享界专网准入 17 家，2026 年计划运营享界专网 40 家[1]。

销量与用户

享界月销于 2025 年 12 月首次破万[1]；截至 2026 年 9 月，S9/S9T 已连续 9 个月蝉联 30 万元以上新能源轿车销冠，销售均价 34.98 万元、位列豪华轿车市场第二名[22][16]。

海外

享界未披露海外销量。北汽蓝谷的海外布局目前集中于极狐品牌，2025 年极狐已进入 13 个国家[1]；享界暂无单独出口数据公开。

股权与治理

享界由北汽蓝谷的子公司北汽新能源运营，华为以智选车模式参与、不持股。北汽蓝谷的控股股东为北汽集团，2025 年末直接持股 23.14%（另有北京汽车、北汽（广州）、渤海汽车等关联方持股），北汽集团为北京市国资委监管的国有独资企业[8][1][27]。

享界为北汽蓝谷旗下品牌（由子公司北汽新能源运营），并非单独上市或单独披露报表的公司，不发布独立财务报表，因此品牌层面无单独营收、毛利率、净利润或研发投入数据可列示，本表留空；母公司财务见北汽集团与北汽蓝谷深度研究。以下参考数据均为北汽蓝谷合并报表口径（中国企业会计准则 CAS），净利润口径为「归属于母公司股东的净利润」（归母净利润）；北汽蓝谷无优先股，故等同于归属于母公司普通股股东的净利润。2025 财年：营业收入 279.40 亿元（同比 +92.53%）、归母净利润亏损 45.63 亿元、经营活动现金流净额 19.34 亿元、资产负债率 88.47%。2026 年上半年：营业收入 115.97 亿元（同比 +21.86%）、归母净利润亏损 19.38 亿元；其中 2026 年一季度营业收入 40.99 亿元、归母净利润亏损 8.70 亿元。本页仅呈现公开披露信息，不构成任何交易推荐。

竞争格局

享界在中国 30 万元以上高端新能源市场参与竞争，这一区间长期是奔驰、宝马、奥迪的腹地。S9/S9T 对标奔驰 E 级、宝马 5 系、奥迪 A6L，G9 对标仰望 U8 等大型豪华 SUV[7][14]。在鸿蒙智行体系内部，享界与问界（赛力斯）、智界（奇瑞）、尊界（江淮）、尚界（上汽）并存，占据高端轿车/旅行车区间；2026 年 9 月问界改由赛力斯主导产品与渠道后，华为表示资源将更多投向智界、享界、尊界、尚界[17]。

我们的判断。享界的主要优势（依据已披露数据）在于把华为智驾与座舱全栈技术与北汽制造结合，并拥有较高的豪华品牌增换购比例——新款 S9 用户约 70%、G9 首批车主约 80% 来自奔驰、宝马等传统豪华品牌，说明其更多在抢夺存量豪华车市场而非仅竞争新能源增量[22][15]。主要短板是品牌历史短、对华为渠道与智驾品牌的依赖度高，以及单一细分市场集中：2026 年下半年在 S9/S9T 步入周期中段、G9/V8 尚未放量时，月交付一度回落[17]。

未来展望

公司公开规划。北汽集团董事长张建勇 2025 年 11 月宣布，北汽将投入 200 亿元资金、布局全品类产品打造享界品牌；媒体 2026 年 9 月报道，北汽在享界项目上的累计投入已超 200 亿元，系集团倾资源重点推进的一号工程[10][16]。北汽蓝谷年报称，享界将持续迭代旗舰 9 系，并推出智能豪华全场景 SUV 与高端家用 MPV，享界专网计划运营 40 家[1]；其中 SUV（G9）已上市、MPV（V8）已开启预售[12][18]。北汽方面称，享界在成立两年多的时间内即完成轿车、旅行车、SUV、MPV 四大品类布局，后续 V8 将继续沿用北汽制造加华为乾崑智驾、鸿蒙座舱的深度联合开发模式[16]。

我们的判断。享界能否延续势头取决于三个前提：其一，把 G9 上市订单与 V8 预售转化为稳定的月度销量；其二，在 S9/S9T 轿旅组合于 2026 年中出现回落后，成功向 SUV、MPV 品类拓宽；其三，在鸿蒙智行体系调整（2026 年 9 月问界交由赛力斯主导、华为称聚焦其余四界）的背景下，保持华为智驾技术供应的领先性[17]。此外，享界不单独披露财务，母公司北汽蓝谷仍处亏损，其盈利能力尚无法独立评估[1]。

风险

  • 单一细分市场集中与需求回落。享界销量集中于 S9/S9T；媒体追踪鸿蒙智行月度交付指出，2026 年 8 月享界月交付一度回落至约 1600 辆，直至 G9、V8 放量前仍有波动[17]。
  • 对华为渠道与模式的依赖。销售依赖鸿蒙智行渠道与华为智驾/座舱供应；北汽表态合作保持不变，但 2026 年下半年鸿蒙智行体系调整（问界交由赛力斯主导）是其余四界的外部变量[16][17]。
  • 无单独财务披露。作为北汽蓝谷旗下品牌，享界不单独披露财务，盈利能力无法独立评估；北汽蓝谷 2025 年归母净亏损 45.63 亿元、2026 年上半年归母净亏损 19.38 亿元[1][2]。
  • 高端市场竞争激烈。30 万元以上区间面临传统豪华品牌降价与新势力旗舰的双重挤压[7]。
  • 品牌历史短。品牌自 2024 年才成立，长期品牌资产与保值率尚未得到验证[6]。
