Overview

Chery Automobile Co., Ltd. (HKEX: 9973.HK) is a passenger-vehicle manufacturer headquartered in Wuhu, Anhui, that designs, develops, manufactures and sells internal-combustion and new-energy passenger vehicles. Its predecessor was incorporated on 8 January 1997 [29][5]. The company operates five passenger-vehicle brands — Chery, Jetour, Exeed, iCAR and Luxeed [12] — and holds a 50% stake in the Chery Jaguar Land Rover joint venture [8]. It is one of China's largest passenger-vehicle exporters: in 2025 it exported about 1.294 million vehicles (up 33.2%); separately, the wider Chery group reported 23 consecutive years as the largest Chinese-brand passenger-vehicle exporter [1][33]. On 25 September 2025 it listed on the Main Board of the Hong Kong Stock Exchange at HK$30.75 per share, raising gross proceeds of HK$9,145 million [4]. For FY2025 it reported revenue of RMB300,287 million and profit attributable to owners of RMB19,019 million [1], and for H1 2026 revenue of RMB143,280 million and profit attributable to owners of RMB8,567 million [2].

Chery is a mixed-ownership enterprise with no actual controller; its single largest shareholder, Wuhu Investment Holding, is controlled by the Wuhu Municipal SASAC [3]. In 2025 the five brands Chery, Jetour, Exeed, iCAR and Luxeed sold 1,700,940, 622,590, 120,369, 96,989 and 90,493 vehicles respectively [14]. Under the broader Chery Holding group, total vehicle sales reached 2,806,393 units in 2025, and the group ranked No.233 on the Fortune Global 500 [14][6]; by March 2026 the group's cumulative vehicle exports exceeded 6 million [6].

Current operations

Business structure

Chery earns most of its revenue from passenger-vehicle sales. In FY2025 total revenue was RMB300,287 million, of which revenue from passenger-vehicle sales was RMB272,352 million (up 10.3%), revenue from automotive components and parts was RMB21,725 million, and other revenue was RMB6,210 million [1]. By customer location, revenue from China (including Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan) was RMB142,868 million and revenue from other countries and regions was RMB157,419 million, so overseas revenue exceeded domestic revenue for the first time, at 52.4% of the total [1]. In H1 2026 revenue was RMB143,280 million, of which overseas revenue was RMB98,968 million (up 51.0%, about 69.1% of the total) and new-energy revenue was RMB59,284 million (up 63.8%, 41.4% of the total) [2].

Group and business boundary

Chery Automobile is the listed passenger-vehicle entity. The wider Chery Holding group also covers commercial vehicles and other businesses: in 2025 the group sold 2,806,393 vehicles, of which Chery Automobile accounted for 2,631,381 [14]. After the shareholder 'sinking' completed on 20 January 2025, Chery Holding no longer holds any shares in Chery Automobile, which has no controlling or ultimate holding company; 'Chery group' is a business grouping rather than a legal parent-subsidiary relationship [1]. The Chery Holding group has more than 300 member companies including Chery Automobile, Chery Commercial Vehicle, Chery Auto Finance and Chery Technology, with business covering more than 130 countries and regions [6], and its commercial-vehicle operations span heavy, mini and light trucks and pickups [20]. Its finance arm, Chery Huishang Auto Finance, was China's first specialised auto-finance company jointly founded by a self-owned-brand automaker and a local bank [6]; its components arm Chery Technology holds more than 90 companies, of which three are listed [6].

Core technology and platforms

Chery's technology stack centres on five areas — the Mars architecture, Kunpeng powertrain, Lingxi intelligent cockpit, Falcon intelligent driving and the Galaxy ecosystem — with more than 400 core technology reserves [15]. Its Kunpeng powertrain architecture was released in April 2021 [5]. In April 2018 it launched its intelligentisation strategy brand 'Chery Lion', and in May 2021 its full-function hybrid DHT rolled off the line [6]. Research and development expenses were RMB11,444 million in FY2025, up from RMB9,243 million in FY2024 [1]. In 2025 invention-patent applications rose nearly 50% and grants rose over 300%, ranking first in Anhui province for 12 consecutive years [15]. The company runs multiple powertrain routes in parallel — hybrid, range-extended, battery-electric and hydrogen — and more than 90% of its newly launched models in 2025 were new-energy vehicles, covering a price band of roughly RMB100,000-400,000 [15]. On next-generation batteries, Chery has said a solid-liquid hybrid (semi-solid) battery would be carried first by the Exeed EX7 in Q4 2026, while full solid-state batteries target batch vehicle installation and road validation from 2027, first on the Exeed ES8 [25].

Production bases and capacity

The company relies on 12 main production bases, including three overseas bases, with sales and service networks covering Europe, South America, Africa and the Middle East [12]. Chery completed a vehicle plant in Brazil in August 2014 for local production [6], and in China it began construction of its Intelligent Connected Vehicle Plant No.1 in February 2021 and put its Qingdao super factory into production in November 2022 [6]. Its first European vehicle factory is the Barcelona plant operated through a joint venture with Spain's EBRO (EV Motors), which began producing its first model on 23 November 2024 [13]. The Chery Jaguar Land Rover joint venture runs a plant in Changshu with total annual capacity of 200,000 vehicles [8].

Domestic market position

In 2025 Chery sold 2,631,381 self-owned passenger vehicles on the listed-company basis, compared with 2,444,640 in 2024, up approximately 8% according to the annual sales announcement [35]. New-energy passenger-vehicle sales were 826,500 units (up 72.5%) [1]. The company has said that from November 2025 its new-energy wholesale volume ranked among the top three in China's new-energy industry [15]. Its 15-millionth vehicle rolled off the line in October 2024 [6]. By group measure, annual sales passed 1.23 million vehicles with revenue above RMB200 billion in 2022, exceeded 1.88 million in 2023, topped 2.6 million in 2024, and reached a record 2.8 million in 2025; group sales were 1.26 million in H1 2025 and over 1.35 million in H1 2026 [6]; its cumulative global vehicle users passed 10 million in December 2021 [6]; its 5-millionth vehicle rolled off the line in December 2015, and in September 2015 it ranked third among Chinese companies for 'best overseas image' [6].

Overseas business

Chery exported about 1.294 million vehicles in 2025 (up 33.2%), while the wider Chery group reported 23 consecutive years as the largest Chinese-brand passenger-vehicle exporter [1][33]. Overseas revenue was RMB157,419 million in 2025 (about 52.4% of the total) and RMB98,968 million in H1 2026 (about 69.1%) [1][2]. In Europe the company entered 16 countries including the UK and Italy, with full-year 2025 sales up more than 200% and right-hand-drive market sales up over 100%; its EBRO localisation project in Spain is proceeding as planned, and the company obtained China's first China-Europe carbon-footprint data mutual-recognition certificate [15][13].

New-energy business

In 2025 new-energy revenue was RMB98,023 million (up 66.3%), 32.6% of total revenue, and the new-energy gross margin improved to 8.8% [1]. More than 90% of models newly launched in 2025 were new-energy vehicles; the Fulwin A9L delivered over 10,000 units for four consecutive months and the Fulwin T11 entered the top three of the range-extended large-SUV segment in its first month, while new-energy export sales grew more than 500% [15]. New-energy products are spread across the Chery Fulwin, Exeed Sterra, Luxeed and iCAR series [12].

Financial position

As at 30 June 2026, cash and cash equivalents were RMB63,419 million (up 35.1% from end-2025), while bank loans and other borrowings fell to RMB10,004 million from RMB15,924 million [2]. H1 2026 net margin was 6.3%, and finance expenses fell to RMB549 million from RMB1,449 million (down 62.1%) [2]. The company's year-end liabilities-to-assets ratio declined to 80.4% in 2025 from 87.9% in 2024 [1].

Founders and leadership

Founder

Yin Tongyue (born November 1962) is the founder, executive director, chairman and president of Chery Automobile. He graduated from Hefei University of Technology with a bachelor's degree in automotive design in July 1984 and previously worked at FAW-Volkswagen as a workshop director and logistics section chief. He joined the founding of Chery in October 1996, has been an executive director since January 1997, chairman since February 2004, and president since December 2024 with overall responsibility for daily operations. He has been chairman of Chery Holding since October 2010 and chairman of Chery New Energy since April 2025, and was elected a deputy to the 14th National People's Congress in February 2023 [1]. His awards include the State Scientific and Technological Progress Award (second class, 2008) and the China Auto Industry Rao Bin Award (2024) [1].

Key management

  • Zhang Guozhong — executive director (since March 2024). He was executive vice president of Chery Automobile from December 2024 until 23 September 2026, when he was transferred to executive vice president of Chery Holding Group. He joined the group in July 1997 and has been a director of Chery Technology since September 2017 [1][24].
  • Zhang Guibing — executive vice president of Chery Automobile since 23 September 2026. He joined Chery in 1999 and has long led its overseas and international business, including as chairman of Chery Overseas Industrial Investment and general manager of Chery International [24].

Board and governance

According to the 2026 interim report, the board comprises: executive directors Yin Tongyue (chairman and president) and Zhang Guozhong; non-executive directors Wang Laichun, Li Jing, Wang Jinhua, Wang Xiaowei, Bao Siyu, Yin Xiangling and Hu Jingyuan; independent non-executive directors Shang Wenjiang, Yang Mianzhi, Ye Shengji, Lu Feng, Shi Qin (appointed 20 May 2026), Li Ruxiong and Yang Shanlin (retired 20 May 2026). The auditor is Ernst & Young, and the board has established committees covering risk control and audit, nomination and remuneration, and strategy and sustainable development [2].

Shareholders and control

Chery is a mixed-ownership enterprise with no actual controller: the prospectus states that, under the Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Transactions of State-owned Assets of Enterprises, the company is not a state-owned enterprise, a state-controlled enterprise or an enterprise actually controlled by the state as defined therein [3]. As at 30 June 2026, the main shareholders were Wuhu Investment Holding (about 19.93%), Luxshare Investment (Hong Kong) (about 15.85%) and the employee platform Ruichuang (about 10.84%); the three employee platforms Ruichuang, Hengrui and Zhenrui together held about 17.19% [2].

Competition

Main competitors

Our assessment: Chery competes in China's mass-market and export segments against BYD, Geely, Changan and Great Wall Motor, and in premium new energy through Luxeed against other Huawei-backed brands. In 2025 it sold 826,500 new-energy passenger vehicles (about 31.4% of its self-owned passenger-vehicle sales), and new-energy revenue was 32.6% of total revenue [1]. In 2025 the listed company exported about 1.294 million vehicles [1]. Separately, the wider Chery group reported cumulative exports of 5.85 million vehicles by end-2025 and 23 consecutive years as the largest Chinese-brand passenger-vehicle exporter; these group figures have a broader scope than the listed company’s annual exports [33].

Strengths — our assessment

  • Export scale: the listed company exported about 1.294 million vehicles in 2025 [1]; separately, the wider Chery group reported 23 consecutive years as the largest Chinese-brand passenger-vehicle exporter [33].
  • Scale and profitability improving together: FY2025 revenue reached RMB300,287 million and profit attributable to owners rose 34.6% to RMB19,019 million, equivalent to an attributable net margin of about 6.33% (RMB19,019 million / RMB300,287 million). Profit for the year, including non-controlling interests, was RMB19,507 million, equivalent to a net margin of about 6.5% [1].
  • Global manufacturing footprint: 12 main production bases including three overseas, with localisation advancing in Spain and elsewhere [12].
  • A five-brand portfolio spanning mass-market, premium, off-road, young-customer and intelligent-driving segments [12].

Weaknesses — our assessment

  • Relatively low gross margin: consolidated gross margin was 13.8% in 2025, and the new-energy gross margin of 8.8% was below the company's overall level [1].
  • Elevated leverage: the year-end liabilities-to-assets ratio was about 80.4% in 2025, down from 87.9% in 2024 but still high [1].
  • Small upmarket scale: in 2025 Exeed sold 120,369 vehicles (down about 15%) and Luxeed 90,493 (up 56%), both far below the core Chery brand [14].

Our assessment: Chery's core strengths are its export scale and localised manufacturing capability; its 2025 consolidated gross margin of 13.8% and new-energy gross margin of 8.8% are relatively moderate, and new-energy vehicles were about 31.4% of its sales, so if it cannot accelerate its domestic new-energy transition, growth and profit will remain more dependent on exports and its fuel-vehicle base [1].

Outlook

Company targets and plans

For 2026 the Chery group announced a sales target of 3.2 million vehicles (up about 14% from 2025) and plans to launch 17 key models, focusing on new-fuel, new-energy, new-overseas, new-business and new-technology tracks [19]. The company's FY2025 report emphasised brand-upmarket moves, with Exeed and Luxeed positioned to drive the high end; Exeed began blind bookings for its EX7 aimed at the RMB200,000 segment in March 2026 [16]. On solid-state batteries, Chery said a semi-solid (solid-liquid hybrid) battery would be carried first by the Exeed EX7 in Q4 2026, while full solid-state batteries would target batch vehicle-installation and road validation from 2027, first on the Exeed ES8 [25].

Our analysis

Our assessment: Chery's disclosed figures show a company whose growth and profit are increasingly carried by exports and, more recently, new energy. In H1 2026 overseas revenue reached about 69.1% of total revenue, so the balance between home-market transition and overseas expansion is a central variable [2]. Whether profitability keeps improving depends on at least three conditions — whether new-energy sales (826,500 units in 2025, up 72.5%) can keep scaling without eroding gross margin, whether the upmarket Exeed and Luxeed brands gain volume, and whether overseas expansion can continue in the face of European and other trade measures [1]. These conditions are not guaranteed, and none of this is a forecast of the share price.

Our assessment: the gap between the 2025 new-energy gross margin of 8.8% and the overall 13.8% margin makes product mix and pricing relevant to future profitability. Overseas growth also increases exposure to the international risks identified in the annual report [1]. If pricing or trade conditions deteriorate, margins and expansion could face pressure; a better product mix and effective localisation could mitigate it. These are conditional judgments.

Risks

  • Operating exposure: new-energy revenue accounted for 32.6% of FY2025 revenue; its gross margin was 8.8%, compared with 13.8% overall [1].
  • Financial exposure: year-end 2025 liabilities were about 80.4% of assets; operating cash inflow fell from RMB44,887 million in 2024 to RMB20,130 million in 2025 [1].
  • Overseas and currency risks: the annual report identifies geopolitical uncertainty, trade compliance, exchange-rate volatility and differing regulatory regimes as international operating risks [1].
  • Competition and technology risks: the annual report warns of intense competition, changing demand and technology, and potential delays in model development, launches, production or delivery [1].
  • Concentrated-shareholding risk: the final offer price announcement warns that highly concentrated shareholding could cause significant H-share price volatility even with small trading volumes [4].

概览

奇瑞汽车股份有限公司（港交所：9973.HK）总部位于安徽省芜湖市，是一家设计、开发、制造并销售燃油车与新能源乘用车的整车企业，前身于 1997 年 1 月 8 日注册成立 [29][5]。公司旗下拥有奇瑞、捷途、星途、iCAR、智界五大乘用车品牌 [12]，并持有奇瑞捷豹路虎合资公司 50% 股权 [8]。它是中国乘用车出口规模最大的自主品牌企业之一：2025 年出口约 129.4 万辆、同比增长 33.2%；另据集团披露，奇瑞集团连续 23 年位居中国品牌乘用车出口第一 [1][33]。2025 年 9 月 25 日，公司在香港联交所主板上市，发售价 30.75 港元，募资总额 91.45 亿港元 [4]。2025 财年营收 3,002.87 亿元、本公司拥有人应占利润 190.19 亿元 [1]；2026 年上半年营收 1,432.80 亿元、本公司拥有人应占利润 85.67 亿元 [2]。

奇瑞为混合所有制企业、无实际控制人，单一最大股东芜湖投资控股由芜湖市国资委控制 [3]。2025 年五大品牌奇瑞、捷途、星途、iCAR、智界销量分别为 170.09 万、62.26 万、12.04 万、9.70 万、9.05 万辆 [14]。更广口径的奇瑞控股集团 2025 年合计销售汽车 280.64 万辆，位列《财富》世界 500 强第 233 位 [14][6]；截至 2026 年 3 月，集团累计出口汽车突破 600 万辆 [6]。

现状

业务结构

奇瑞收入主要来自乘用车销售。2025 财年总收入 3,002.87 亿元，其中乘用车销售收入 2,723.52 亿元（同比增 10.3%）、汽车组件及零部件收入 217.25 亿元、其他收入 62.10 亿元 [1]。按客户所在地划分，来自中国（含港澳台）的收入为 1,428.68 亿元，来自其他国家和地区的收入为 1,574.19 亿元，海外营收首次超过国内、占总收入 52.4% [1]。2026 年上半年营收 1,432.80 亿元，其中海外收入 989.68 亿元（同比增 51.0%、约占 69.1%）、新能源收入 592.84 亿元（同比增 63.8%、占 41.4%）[2]。

集团与业务边界

奇瑞汽车是上市主体，业务集中于乘用车。更广口径的「奇瑞控股集团」还涵盖商用车等业务：2025 年集团销售汽车 280.64 万辆，其中奇瑞汽车 263.14 万辆 [14]。2025 年 1 月 20 日「股东下沉」完成后，奇瑞控股不再持有奇瑞汽车股份，奇瑞汽车无控股公司、亦无最终控股公司，「奇瑞集团」为业务称谓而非法律上的母子公司关系 [1]。奇瑞控股集团旗下拥有奇瑞汽车、奇瑞商用车、奇瑞汽金、奇瑞科技等 300 余家成员企业，业务覆盖全球 130 多个国家和地区 [6]；集团商用车板块覆盖重卡、微卡、轻卡、皮卡等品类 [20]。金融板块旗下奇瑞徽银汽车金融，是中国第一家由自主品牌车企与本土银行合资成立的专业汽车金融公司 [6]；零部件板块奇瑞科技旗下现有企业 90 余家，其中上市企业 3 家 [6]。

核心技术与平台

奇瑞技术栈以火星架构、鲲鹏动力、灵犀智舱、猎鹰智驾、银河生态五大领域为核心，已形成 400 余项核心技术储备 [15]。其鲲鹏动力架构于 2021 年 4 月发布 [5]。2018 年 4 月发布智能化战略品牌「奇瑞雄狮」，2021 年 5 月全功能混动构型 DHT 正式下线 [6]。2025 年研发开支 114.44 亿元（2024 年为 92.43 亿元）[1]；全年发明专利申请量同比增长近 50%、授权量增幅超 300%，连续 12 年位居安徽省发明专利排行榜第一 [15]。奇瑞坚持混动、增程、纯电、氢能多技术路线并举，2025 年上市新车中新能源占比超 90%，产品价格带覆盖约 10 万—40 万元 [15]。在下一代电池上，奇瑞规划固液混合电池于 2026 年第四季度由星途 EX7 率先搭载，全固态电池力争 2027 年启动批量装车与道路验证、由星途 ES8 首搭 [25]。

工厂与产能

公司依托全球 12 家主要生产基地（含 3 家海外基地），销售服务网络覆盖欧洲、南美洲、非洲、中东等地区 [12]。2014 年 8 月奇瑞巴西工厂落成并实现本地生产 [6]；在国内，2021 年 2 月奇瑞智能网联一工厂开工、2022 年 11 月青岛超级工厂落成投产 [6]。其欧洲首个整车工厂为与西班牙 EBRO（EV Motors）合资的巴塞罗那工厂，2024 年 11 月 23 日启动首款车型生产 [13]。奇瑞捷豹路虎合资公司在常熟设有工厂，总年产能 20 万辆 [8]。

国内市场地位

2025 年奇瑞自主乘用车销量为 2,631,381 辆（上市主体口径），2024 年为 2,444,640 辆，按年度销量公告口径同比增长约 8% [35]。其中新能源乘用车销量为 82.65 万辆（同比增 72.5%）[1]。公司表示，自 2025 年 11 月起其新能源批发销量稳居国内新能源行业前三 [15]；2024 年 10 月奇瑞全球第 1500 万辆汽车下线 [6]。从历年销量看（集团口径），2022 年销量突破 123 万辆、营收超 2,000 亿元，2023 年首次突破 188 万辆，2024 年首次突破 260 万辆，2025 年达 280 万辆创历史新高；2025 年上半年集团销量 126 万辆、2026 年上半年超 135 万辆 [6]；2021 年 12 月奇瑞全球汽车用户突破 1000 万 [6]；2015 年 12 月第 500 万辆汽车下线、2015 年 9 月获中国「最佳海外形象」企业第三位 [6]。

海外业务

2025 年奇瑞出口约 129.4 万辆（同比增 33.2%）；另据集团披露，奇瑞集团连续 23 年位居中国品牌乘用车出口第一 [1][33]。海外收入 2025 年为 1,574.19 亿元（约占总收入 52.4%）、2026 年上半年为 989.68 亿元（约占 69.1%）[1][2]。欧洲市场已进入英国、意大利等 16 个国家，2025 年全年销量同比增长超 200%、右舵市场销量同比增速超 100%；西班牙埃布罗（EBRO）本地化合作项目按计划推进，公司率先获得中国首张中欧碳足迹数据互认证书 [15][13]。

新能源业务

2025 年新能源业务收入 980.23 亿元（同比增 66.3%）、占总收入 32.6%，新能源毛利率提升至 8.8% [1]。2025 年全新上市车型中新能源占比超 90%，风云 A9L 连续 4 个月交付破万、风云 T11 上市首月即进入增程大型 SUV 市场前三，新能源出口销量同比增长超 500% [15]。新能源产品分布于奇瑞风云、星途星纪元、智界、iCAR 等多个品牌序列 [12]。

财务结构

截至 2026 年 6 月 30 日，现金及现金等价物 634.19 亿元（较 2025 年末增长 35.1%），银行贷款及其他借款由 159.24 亿元降至 100.04 亿元 [2]。2026 年上半年净利润率 6.3%，财务费用由 14.49 亿元降至 5.49 亿元（降幅 62.1%）[2]。公司期末资产负债率由 2024 年的 87.9% 降至 2025 年的 80.4% [1]。

股东与股权控制

奇瑞为混合所有制企业、无实际控制人：招股书明确，根据《企业国有资产交易监督管理办法》，公司并非其中界定的国有企业、国有控股企业或国有实际控制企业 [3]。截至 2026 年 6 月 30 日，主要股东为芜湖投资控股（约 19.93%）、立讯投资（香港）（约 15.85%）、员工持股平台瑞创（约 10.84%）；瑞创、衡瑞、振瑞三个员工平台合计持股约 17.19% [2]。

竞争格局

主要对手

我们的判断：奇瑞在中国大众市场与出口市场与比亚迪、吉利、长安、长城汽车竞争，在高端新能源领域通过智界与其他华为系品牌竞争。2025 年新能源乘用车销量 82.65 万辆（约占自主乘用车总销量的 31.4%）、新能源收入占公司总收入 32.6% [1]。2025 年上市主体出口约 129.4 万辆 [1]。另据奇瑞控股公告，更广口径的奇瑞集团截至 2025 年底累计出口 585 万辆，连续 23 年位居中国品牌乘用车出口第一；这两项集团数据与上市主体年度出口量的统计范围不同 [33]。

优势（我们的判断）

  • 出口规模：上市主体 2025 年出口约 129.4 万辆 [1]；另据集团公告，奇瑞集团连续 23 年位居中国品牌乘用车出口第一 [33]。
  • 规模与效益同步提升：2025 财年营收 3,002.87 亿元，本公司拥有人应占利润 190.19 亿元（同比增 34.6%），归母净利率约 6.33%（190.19 亿元 / 3,002.87 亿元）；包含少数股东损益的年内利润为 195.07 亿元，对应净利润率约 6.5% [1]。
  • 全球制造布局：12 家主要生产基地（含 3 家海外基地），并在西班牙等地推进本地化 [12]。
  • 五大品牌矩阵，覆盖大众、高端、越野、年轻化与智能驾驶等多元细分市场 [12]。

短板（我们的判断）

  • 毛利率偏低：2025 年综合毛利率 13.8%，其中新能源毛利率 8.8%、低于公司整体水平 [1]。
  • 杠杆偏高：2025 年末资产负债率约 80.4%，较 2024 年的 87.9% 下降，但仍处于较高水平 [1]。
  • 高端化规模尚小：2025 年星途销量 12.04 万辆（同比降约 15%）、智界 9.05 万辆（同比增 56%），均远低于奇瑞主品牌 [14]。

我们的判断：奇瑞的核心竞争力在于出口规模与本地化制造能力；其 2025 年综合毛利率 13.8%、新能源毛利率 8.8% 相对偏低，新能源销量占比约 31.4%，若国内新能源转型不能加速补齐，增长与盈利将更依赖海外与燃油车基本盘 [1]。

未来展望

公司目标与规划

面向 2026 年，奇瑞集团宣布销量目标为 320 万辆（较 2025 年增长约 14%），计划推出 17 款重点车型，聚焦「新燃油、新能源、新出海、新业务、新科技」五大航道 [19]。2025 年财报的展望强调「品牌向上」，星途与智界被寄予推动高端的厚望；2026 年 3 月星途瞄准 20 万级市场的 EX7 启动盲订 [16]。在固态电池上，奇瑞表示固液混合电池将于 2026 年第四季度由星途 EX7 率先搭载，全固态电池则力争 2027 年启动批量装车与道路验证、由星途 ES8 首搭 [25]。

我们的分析

我们的判断：奇瑞披露的数据显示，其增长与利润越来越依赖出口，近两年又叠加了新能源。2026 年上半年海外收入已占总收入约 69.1%，国内转型与海外扩张之间的平衡成为核心变量 [2]。盈利能力能否持续改善，取决于至少三个条件——新能源销量（2025 年 82.65 万辆、增 72.5%）能否在不侵蚀毛利率的前提下继续放量，星途、智界能否真正上量，以及海外扩张能否在欧洲等地贸易措施下持续推进 [1]。这些条件并非必然成立；以上不构成对股价的任何预测。

我们的判断：2025 年新能源毛利率 8.8% 与整体毛利率 13.8% 的差距，使产品结构与定价成为后续盈利的重要变量；海外业务增长也增加了公司对年报所列国际运营风险的敞口 [1]。若价格或贸易环境恶化，利润率与扩张可能承压；产品结构改善及有效本地化则可能缓解压力。以上为有条件的判断。

风险

  • 经营敞口：2025 财年新能源收入占总收入 32.6%，新能源毛利率为 8.8%，公司整体毛利率为 13.8% [1]。
  • 财务敞口：2025 年末资产负债率约 80.4%；经营现金流入净额从 2024 年的 448.87 亿元降至 2025 年的 201.30 亿元 [1]。
  • 海外与汇率风险：年报将地缘政治不确定性、贸易合规、汇率波动及不同监管制度列为国际运营风险 [1]。
  • 竞争与技术风险：年报提示行业竞争激烈、需求与技术变化，以及车型开发、上市、生产或交付延误的潜在风险 [1]。
  • 股权集中风险：最终发售价公告提示，股权高度集中可能导致即使少量 H 股交易也引发股价大幅波动 [4]。
