Overview

Huawei Harmony Intelligent Mobility Alliance (鸿蒙智行, HIMA) is the smart-car business through which Huawei, a private ICT company, works with automakers under its "Smart Selection" (智选车) model. Huawei announced the upgrade of its Smart Selection business to HIMA on 9 November 2023, and showed the alliance publicly at the 2023 Guangzhou Auto Show. [11] The business sits inside Huawei's terminal BG and is led by Huawei executive director Richard Yu (Yu Chengdong), chairman of the terminal BG and chairman of the product investment review committee. [1]

HIMA does not build cars itself. Under the Smart Selection model Huawei leads product definition, design, the intelligent-driving and cockpit technology, pricing, marketing and retail channels, while partner automakers own the brands and handle manufacturing and quality. Huawei has repeatedly said it does not make cars, positioning itself as an incremental-component supplier and ecosystem organiser. The five "Jie" brands are AITO/问界 (Seres), Luxeed/智界 (Chery), Stelato/享界 (BAIC), Maextro/尊界 (JAC) and Shangjie/尚界 (SAIC), together spanning roughly RMB 150,000 to over RMB 1 million. [3][42]

HIMA delivered 444,956 vehicles in 2024, 589,107 in 2025 (up 32%) and about 242,000 in the first half of 2026 (up 18.6%), and passed 1.56 million cumulative deliveries in September 2026. In September 2026 the AITO brand's operations returned to Seres-led management; Huawei said the other four brands remain Huawei-led, and Richard Yu said HIMA would focus on them. [10][9][34][16][46]

Current operations

Business model

Huawei groups its car business into three models: components supply, the HI (solutions) model, and the Smart Selection model, which is now called HIMA. Under HIMA, Huawei is deeply involved across product definition, design, sales and after-sales, while the partner automaker owns the brand, makes the car and takes manufacturing and quality responsibility. [11] Huawei holds no equity in the five "Jie" brands: Seres owns AITO after buying the brand's trademarks and design patents in 2024, and Chery holds 85% of Luxeed's operating company after its June 2026 registration change. [39][45] The related Yinwang company (the spun-off car BU, Huawei 80%) supplies the core intelligent components and the Qiankun ADS and HarmonyOS cockpit stacks to partners, and is the vehicle through which Huawei's smart-car technology is monetised. [13]

Technology

HIMA models are built around Huawei's technology suites: the Qiankun ADS intelligent-driving system (ADS 2.0 on the 2023 Luxeed S7, ADS 3.0 later, and ADS 4/5 on 2025-2026 models), the HarmonyOS cockpit, the Tuling chassis platform, the Huawei DriveONE 800V silicon-carbide powertrain, the Giant Whale battery platform and Huawei Galaxy communication. [21] The Maextro S800 was the first model to carry ADS 4, and 2026 models such as the Shangjie Z7/Z7T, the AITO M6, the Luxeed RX/V9 and the Maextro V800/V680 moved to ADS 4.1 or ADS 5. [19][35] Huawei's 2024 annual report states its Intelligent Automotive Solution business shipped over 23 million intelligent components in 2024, up nearly sevenfold, and that 15 cooperative models were on the market by end-2024. [12] The segment's revenue was RMB 26,353 million in 2024 (up 474.4%) and RMB 45,018 million in 2025 (up 72.1%), making it Huawei's fastest-growing segment. [29][43]

Sales trajectory

HIMA delivered 444,956 vehicles in 2024 (December record of 49,474), 589,107 in 2025 (up 32%, December record of 89,611). [10][9] In H1 2026 it delivered about 242,000 vehicles (up 18.6%); AITO remained the largest brand, with Seres's bulletin recording AITO wholesale sales of 160,770 in H1 (up 5.6%). [34][44] In September 2026 HIMA delivered 37,490 vehicles and cumulative deliveries passed 1.56 million; the cumulative total had passed 1.5 million by 16 August 2026. [16][17] Huawei said that as of March 2025 HIMA had ranked first among Chinese car brands by average transaction price for ten consecutive months. [12]

Channels and manufacturing

HIMA sells through Huawei's retail network; as of 22 April 2026 it had 1,835 sales stores and 946 service stores. [36] The partner automakers own the plants: Seres builds AITO at its Chongqing facilities, JAC builds Maextro at its dedicated Maextro super factory, and SAIC's Shangjie project settled in Lingang, Shanghai. [37][41]

Founders and leadership

Huawei leadership behind HIMA

HIMA has no separate management team; it is run within Huawei's terminal BG. Yu Chengdong (余承东), born 1969 with a master's degree from Tsinghua University, joined Huawei in 1993 and is now an executive director, chairman of the product investment review committee and chairman of the terminal BG; he previously served as terminal BG CEO and chairman of the Intelligent Automotive Solution BU, and is the public face of HIMA. He is also a vice-chairman of Yinwang. [1][25] He Gang (何刚), born 1973 with a master's degree from Xidian University, joined Huawei in 1998 and previously led the wireless GSM&UMTS product line, global wireless solutions sales and the terminal BG handset product line; he became terminal BG CEO in April 2024 and was promoted from alternate director to director on 23 October 2026 while continuing as terminal BG CEO. [2][32] Huawei's Smart Selection business department is headed by Wang Yanmin (汪严旻), who signed the SAIC cooperation agreement on Huawei's behalf on 21 February 2025. [42]

Yinwang leadership

The related car-BU entity, Yinwang Intelligent Technology, is chaired by Huawei rotating chairman Xu Zhijun (徐直军), with Yu Chengdong and Changan chairman Zhu Huarong (朱华荣) as vice-chairmen and Jin Yuzhi (靳玉志), a Huawei senior vice-president, as CEO (director and manager); Seres Group chairman Zhang Xinghai (张兴海) serves as a director. [25][31]

Governance

Huawei is a private, employee-owned company founded by Ren Zhengfei. As of October 2026 its board is chaired by Liang Hua (梁华), with Xu Zhijun, Meng Wanzhou and Wang Tao as vice-chairmen who rotate as rotating chairman (Xu Zhijun is on duty from 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027), and Zhang Ping'an, Yu Chengdong, Zheng Liangcai and Yang Chaobin as executive directors; He Gang became a director on 23 October 2026. [2][38] HIMA itself is not a company with shareholders: the brands are owned by the partner automakers, and Huawei's economic interest runs mainly through its 80% stake in Yinwang (Avatr and Seres Auto each hold 10%). Competition

Our judgment: the competitive field. HIMA competes through its five brands across China's premium new-energy market, mainly above RMB 200,000, against Tesla, Li Auto, NIO, XPeng, Zeekr, Avatr and the electrified line-ups of German premium brands; the lowest-priced Shangjie brand competes in the mainstream RMB 150,000-300,000 band against BYD's Song family, Xiaomi's SU7 and others. [5] This peer set is an analytical selection, not a ranking. In the first half of 2026 HIMA was the second-largest Chinese "new force" by deliveries (about 242,000), behind Leapmotor's 356,500 but ahead of Li Auto's 193,000, NIO's 191,000 and XPeng's 166,000. [34]

Our judgment: strengths. HIMA's main assets are Huawei's full intelligent stack (Qiankun ADS, HarmonyOS cockpit, Tuling chassis, 800V powertrain), a dense retail network of Huawei stores, and a proven ability to command premium pricing - Huawei reported HIMA ranked first among Chinese car brands by average transaction price for ten consecutive months as of March 2025. [12] The alliance has also shown it can extend upward, with the Maextro S800 passing 10,000 deliveries 202 days after launch and over 19,000 by August 2026 in the RMB one-million-plus segment. [19][37]

Our judgment: weaknesses. HIMA is not a carmaker: it owns no factories and, except through Yinwang, no equity in the brands it sells, so its success depends on maintaining partner cooperation. The alliance is also heavily concentrated in AITO, which is by far the largest brand (160,770 vehicles in H1 2026, against about 242,000 for the whole alliance). [44][34] In September 2026 AITO's operations returned to Seres-led management, which shows the model is rebalancing and could reduce Huawei's direct influence over its largest brand. [14] The above is our judgment based on the disclosed figures.

Outlook

Company targets and statements. Huawei has said HIMA would focus resources on four brands (Maextro, Stelato, Luxeed and Shangjie) after AITO returned to Seres-led operation in September 2026. [46] Media reported a 2026 HIMA delivery target of around one million vehicles, against which H1 2026 deliveries of about 242,000 represented a completion rate of roughly a quarter; Huawei executive Jin Yuzhi described a two-tier structure in which the "Jie" brands target the RMB 300,000-plus market and a separate set of "Jing" brands target below RMB 300,000. [18] HIMA's 2025 annual summary listed planned 2026 additions including a Shangjie coupe, the Luxeed V9 MPV, the AITO M6 and an extended M9, a Stelato off-road SUV and Maextro MPVs and SUVs. [33] For overseas, Seres announced at the 2026 Paris Motor Show a plan for AITO to enter Europe in autumn 2027. [27] These are disclosed plans and targets, not completed results.

Our analysis. HIMA's trajectory depends on several premises that are not guaranteed: whether the four non-AITO brands can scale after the AITO handover, whether the shared Huawei stack keeps differentiating the brands as rivals close the technology gap, and whether Huawei's "not building cars" commitment holds while it relies on partners for manufacturing. The main alternatives are a prolonged domestic price war, slower partner commitment, and overseas expansion in which, media report, Luxeed's export models and AITO's planned European models use non-Huawei intelligent-driving and cockpit solutions, diluting the alliance's core value proposition abroad. This is our judgment and does not predict any share price or financial result. [16][47]

Risks

  • No separate financial disclosure. HIMA is not an independent company and Huawei does not break out its results, so the alliance's standalone profitability cannot be assessed from public filings. [13]
  • Concentration in AITO. AITO is by far HIMA's largest brand (160,770 vehicles in H1 2026 against about 242,000 for the alliance); a slowdown in its product cycle, or friction after the September 2026 handover to Seres, would weigh on the whole alliance. [44]
  • Partner-cooperation risk. HIMA owns no manufacturing and little brand equity, so its model depends on sustained partner commitment; the AITO handover and media-reported overseas moves away from the Huawei stack illustrate how the relationship can shift. [14][47]
  • Price competition. The RMB 150,000-300,000 band, where Shangjie competes, is the most price-competitive segment in China, and the wider premium market has seen sustained discounting. [5]
  • Overseas and regulatory exposure. HIMA's China-built models face EU countervailing duties and other trade measures; announced overseas plans are not yet completed market entries. [27]
  • Technology differentiation. Huawei's advantage rests on its driving and cockpit stack; as rivals close the gap, the brands' premium pricing could come under pressure. [16]

概览

华为鸿蒙智行（HIMA）是华为这家民营 ICT 公司以「智选车」模式与车企合作的智能汽车业务。2023 年 11 月 9 日，华为宣布将智选车业务升级为「鸿蒙智行」，并在 2023 广州车展首次公开亮相。 [11] 该业务隶属华为终端 BG，由华为常务董事、产品投资评审委员会主任、终端 BG 董事长余承东主导。 [1]

鸿蒙智行本身不造车。智选车模式下，华为主导产品定义、设计、智驾与座舱技术、定价、营销与零售渠道，合作车企拥有品牌并负责制造与质量。华为多次强调「不造车」，定位为智能网联汽车的增量部件提供商与生态组织者。五个「界」字品牌分别为问界（赛力斯）、智界（奇瑞）、享界（北汽）、尊界（江淮）、尚界（上汽），共同覆盖约 15 万元至 100 万元以上市场。 [3][42]

鸿蒙智行 2024 年交付 444,956 辆，2025 年交付 589,107 辆（同比增长 32%），2026 年上半年交付约 24.2 万辆（同比增长 18.6%），2026 年 9 月累计交付突破 156 万辆。2026 年 9 月，问界品牌经营主导权交回赛力斯，华为表示其余四界仍由华为全流程主导，余承东称鸿蒙智行将聚焦这四个品牌。 [10][9][34][16][46]

现状

业务模式

华为将其汽车业务分为三种模式：零部件供应、解决方案（HI）模式，以及现称「鸿蒙智行」的智选车模式。智选车模式下华为深度参与产品定义、设计、销售与售后，合作车企拥有品牌、负责制造并承担制造与质量责任。 [11] 华为不持有五个「界」字品牌的股权：问界品牌由赛力斯于 2024 年收购商标及外观设计专利后归其所有，智界运营公司由奇瑞持股 85%（2026 年 6 月工商变更后）。 [39][45] 关联实体引望（剥离后的车 BU，华为持股 80%）向合作伙伴提供核心智能化部件与乾崑智驾、鸿蒙座舱等方案，是华为智能汽车技术的变现主体。 [13]

技术

鸿蒙智行车型围绕华为技术矩阵构建：乾崑智驾 ADS（2023 年智界 S7 首发 ADS 2.0，后续升级 ADS 3.0，2025-2026 年车型升级 ADS 4/5）、鸿蒙座舱、途灵底盘、华为 DriveONE 800V 碳化硅动力平台、巨鲸电池平台与华为星河通信。 [21] 尊界 S800 为首款搭载 ADS 4 的车型，尚界 Z7/Z7T、问界 M6、智界 RX/V9 及尊界 V800/V680 等 2026 年车型升级至 ADS 4.1 或 ADS 5。 [19][35] 华为 2024 年年报显示，其智能汽车解决方案业务 2024 年智能部件发货超 2300 万件、同比增长近 7 倍，截至 2024 年末已有 15 款合作车型上市。 [12] 该业务收入 2024 年为 263.53 亿元（同比增长 474.4%）、2025 年为 450.18 亿元（同比增长 72.1%），是华为增速最快的业务板块。 [29][43]

销量轨迹

鸿蒙智行 2024 年交付 444,956 辆（12 月 49,474 辆创新高），2025 年交付 589,107 辆（同比增长 32%、12 月 89,611 辆创新高）。 [10][9] 2026 年上半年交付约 24.2 万辆（同比增长 18.6%）；问界仍是最大品牌，赛力斯产销快报显示问界上半年销量 16.077 万辆（同比增长 5.6%）。 [34][44] 2026 年 9 月交付 37,490 辆、累计交付突破 156 万辆；此前已于 2026 年 8 月 16 日累计突破 150 万辆。 [16][17] 华为披露，截至 2025 年 3 月鸿蒙智行连续 10 个月位居中国汽车品牌成交均价第一。 [12]

渠道与制造

鸿蒙智行依托华为零售网络销售。截至 2026 年 4 月 22 日，其销售门店 1835 家、服务门店 946 家。 [36] 工厂由合作车企所有：赛力斯在重庆工厂生产问界，江淮在尊界专属超级工厂生产尊界，上汽尚界项目则落户上海临港。 [37][41]

鸿蒙智行无独立管理团队，由华为终端 BG 运营。余承东，1969 年出生，清华大学硕士，1993 年加入华为，现任华为常务董事、产品投资评审委员会主任、终端 BG 董事长；曾任终端 BG CEO、智能汽车解决方案 BU 董事长，是鸿蒙智行的公开代言人，并任引望副董事长。 [1][25] 何刚，1973 年出生，西安电子科技大学硕士，1998 年加入华为，历任无线 GSM&UMTS 产品线总裁、全球无线解决方案销售部部长、终端 BG 手机产品线总裁等职，2024 年 4 月接任终端 BG CEO，2026 年 10 月 23 日由候补董事升任华为董事、同时继续担任终端 BG CEO。 [2][32] 华为智选车业务部由汪严旻负责，他于 2025 年 2 月 21 日代表华为签署了上汽合作协议。 [42]

引望管理层

关联实体引望智能技术由华为轮值董事长徐直军任董事长，余承东与长安汽车董事长朱华荣任副董事长，华为高级副总裁靳玉志任 CEO（董事、经理），赛力斯集团董事长张兴海任董事。 [25][31]

治理

华为是由任正非创立的员工持股民营企业。截至 2026 年 10 月，董事会由梁华任董事长，徐直军、孟晚舟、汪涛任副董事长（三人轮值担任轮值董事长，其中徐直军自 2026 年 10 月 1 日至 2027 年 3 月 31 日当值），张平安、余承东、郑良材、杨超斌任常务董事；2026 年 10 月 23 日何刚出任董事。 [2][38] 鸿蒙智行本身并非有股东的独立公司：品牌由合作车企拥有，华为的经济利益主要通过持有 80% 股权的引望实现（阿维塔、赛力斯汽车各持 10%）。 [13]

鸿蒙智行（HIMA）是华为终端 BG 旗下的智选车业务联盟，并非独立公司，不发布单独财务报表，因此无鸿蒙智行层面的营收、毛利率、净利润、研发投入或现金流数据可列示（rows 为空）。华为本身为非上市民营企业，亦不单独披露鸿蒙智行口径的业绩。以下为关联实体的参考数据，口径已分别注明：其一，华为 2024 年年度报告（合并报表，国际财务报告准则 IFRS）显示，其智能汽车解决方案业务（车 BU）2024 年收入 263.53 亿元，同比增长 474.4%（2023 年为 45.88 亿元），并称该业务首次实现当年盈利；华为 2025 年年度报告显示，智能汽车解决方案业务 2025 年收入 450.18 亿元，同比增长 72.1%（2024 年为 261.58 亿元）。其二，车 BU 已剥离为引望智能技术有限公司（2025 年 3 月 31 日过户后华为持股 80%、阿维塔 10%、赛力斯汽车 10%）。据赛力斯 2024 年公告披露的引望经审计模拟财务报表，引望营业收入为 2022 年 20.98 亿元、2023 年 47 亿元、2024 年上半年 104.35 亿元；净利润口径为「归属于母公司股东的净利润」（归母净利润），分别为 2022 年 -75.87 亿元、2023 年 -55.97 亿元、2024 年上半年 22.31 亿元。上述数字描述的是关联实体引望与华为集团，而非鸿蒙智行本身。本页仅呈现公开披露信息，不构成任何交易推荐。

竞争格局

我们的判断：竞争范围。鸿蒙智行通过五个品牌在中国高端新能源市场（主要在 20 万元以上）与特斯拉、理想、蔚来、小鹏、极氪、阿维塔及德系豪华品牌的电动产品竞争；价位最低的尚界则在 15 万-30 万元主流区间与比亚迪宋家族、小米 SU7 等竞争。 [5] 该名单是分析所选的竞争范围，不是排名。2026 年上半年，鸿蒙智行以约 24.2 万辆交付位居中国新势力第二，低于零跑的 35.65 万辆，高于理想的 19.3 万辆、蔚来的 19.1 万辆与小鹏的 16.6 万辆。 [34]

我们的判断：优势。鸿蒙智行的主要资产是华为全栈智能化技术（乾崑智驾、鸿蒙座舱、途灵底盘、800V 动力）、密集的华为门店零售网络，以及已被验证的高端定价能力——华为披露截至 2025 年 3 月鸿蒙智行连续 10 个月位居中国汽车品牌成交均价第一。 [12] 联盟还展示了向上拓展的能力，尊界 S800 在百万元级市场上市 202 天交付破万、截至 2026 年 8 月累计交付突破 1.9 万辆。 [19][37]

我们的判断：短板。鸿蒙智行并非整车厂：它不拥有工厂，且除通过引望外不持有所售品牌的股权，成功依赖与合作伙伴关系的持续。联盟高度集中于问界——问界 2026 年上半年销量 16.08 万辆，而全系交付约 24.2 万辆，是其中规模最大的品牌。 [44][34] 2026 年 9 月问界经营交回赛力斯主导，显示模式正在再平衡，可能削弱华为对其最大品牌的直接影响。 [14] 以上为基于前述披露数据的判断。

未来展望

公司目标与表态。华为表示，2026 年 9 月问界回归赛力斯主导后，鸿蒙智行将聚焦尊界、享界、智界、尚界四个品牌。 [46] 媒体报道鸿蒙智行 2026 年交付目标约为 100 万辆，而 2026 年上半年交付约 24.2 万辆、完成率仅约四分之一；华为高管靳玉志描述「界」主打 30 万元以上、「境」主打 30 万元以下的双层结构。 [18] 鸿蒙智行 2025 年度总结列出的 2026 年新车计划包括：尚界首款轿跑、智界 V9 MPV、问界 M6 与加长版 M9、享界硬派越野 SUV，以及尊界 MPV 与 SUV。 [33] 海外方面，赛力斯在 2026 巴黎车展官宣问界计划 2027 年秋季进入欧洲。 [27] 以上为披露的计划与目标，不是已实现的结果。

我们的分析。鸿蒙智行的走向取决于若干并不必然成立的前提：问界交接后其余四个品牌能否放量、在对手缩小技术差距时共享的华为技术栈能否继续形成差异化，以及华为「不造车」承诺在依赖合作伙伴制造的前提下能否持续。主要变数包括国内价格战延续、合作伙伴投入放缓，以及海外扩张——据媒体报道，智界出口车型与问界计划登陆欧洲的车型将采用非华为的智驾与座舱方案，可能削弱联盟在海外的核心价值主张。以上为我们的判断，不构成任何股价或财务结果的预测。 [16][47]

风险

  • 无单独财务披露。鸿蒙智行并非独立公司，华为亦不单独披露其业绩，联盟自身的盈利能力无法从公开文件评估。 [13]
  • 集中于问界。问界是鸿蒙智行规模最大的品牌（2026 年上半年销量 16.08 万辆，全系交付约 24.2 万辆）；其车型周期放缓，或 2026 年 9 月交接赛力斯后出现摩擦，将拖累整个联盟。 [44]
  • 合作方风险。鸿蒙智行不拥有制造与大部分品牌股权，模式依赖合作伙伴的持续投入；问界交接与媒体报道的海外弃用华为方案，显示合作关系可能变化。 [14][47]
  • 价格竞争。尚界所处的 15 万-30 万元区间是中国价格竞争最激烈的市场，更广泛的高端市场亦持续降价。 [5]
  • 海外与监管风险。鸿蒙智行在中国生产的车型面临欧盟反补贴关税等贸易措施；已公布的海外计划尚未成为完成的落地。 [27]
  • 技术差异化。华为优势依赖智驾与座舱技术栈，随着对手追赶，各品牌的高端定价可能承压。 [16]
