Overview

Radar (RADAR; sold overseas as RIDDARA) is a new-energy pickup brand launched by Geely Holding Group on 12 July 2022, and Geely's first pure-electric pickup brand, positioned in mid-to-high-end new-energy pickups and outdoor-lifestyle use.[1][11] Its first model is the RD6, a pure-electric pickup built on Geely's SEA architecture and the brand's native new-energy pickup platform, M.A.P Radar Hanhai.[2] The brand was born as local pickup road-access restrictions were progressively lifted, pickups shifted toward passenger-oriented and premium positioning, and EV penetration rose, making the electric pickup a new market opportunity.[11]

The 15 May 2026 HKEX announcement states that Zhejiang Radar Automobile (Radar New Energy Vehicle (Zhejiang) Co., Ltd.) was ultimately wholly owned by Li Shufu and his associates, while Radar HK Limited and Radar Auto Limited (Radar BVI) were each indirectly 85%-owned by Li Shufu and his associates; the Thai distributor was held 99% by Radar HK and 1% by Radar BVI.[4] In May 2026, Geely Automobile Holdings Limited (0175.HK) announced the acquisition of Radar Auto (Shandong), Radar Auto Sales and Riddara Automobile (Thailand) for about RMB 218 million in total, after which the entities became wholly-owned subsidiaries of Geely Auto and the brand entered the listed company.[4][12]

Media citing official figures reported that Radar sold about 13,040 vehicles in full-year 2025.[22]

History

  1. 2022-07

    Geely Holding Group officially launched the RADAR brand (Chinese name 雷达), its first pure-electric pickup brand, on 12 July; the first model, the RD6, made its debut. Ling Shiquan was introduced as Radar CEO and Yi Han as Chief Growth Officer (CGO). The same launch introduced the outdoor-lifestyle merchandise sub-brand RADAR Weekend and partnerships with camping-gear brand Naturehike and National Geographic (Chinese edition).[1][11]

  2. 2022-08

    Radar announced its outdoor-ecosystem Duoyuan Benfu programme (多元奔赴计划) on its official site.[3]

  3. 2022-09

    The RD6 rolled off the line at the Zibo smart factory in Shandong and opened pre-sales from RMB 180,000.[2]

  4. 2022-11

    The Radar RD6 went on official sale in Hangzhou on 9 November, in four versions (Qihang, Chuangke, Yuexiang, Yueye) priced RMB 178,800-268,800. Geely Holding Group CEO Li Donghui said at the event that the group would preserve Radar's independent brand positioning, governance and development strategy while providing full support in R&D, smart manufacturing, quality management, channel services, supply-chain coordination and talent development.[10]

  5. 2023

    Per RIDDARA and media reports, Radar's new-energy pickup market share in China exceeded 60% in 2023, with a single-month share peaking at 84.2%.[6][17]

  6. 2024-04

    Radar launched the four-wheel-drive pure-electric pickup Radar Horizon (地平线) in Beijing on 23 April, priced RMB 181,800-221,800, alongside the 2024 RD6.[9]

  7. 2024-05

    RIDDARA's Thailand site said the brand established a subsidiary in Thailand in May 2024 as RIDDARA (Thailand) Co., Ltd.; Geely Auto's HKEX filings use the name Riddara Automobile (Thailand) Co., Ltd. (also Radar Thailand). Some media later reported that the subsidiary was established in July 2024.[8][5][22]

  8. 2024-10

    The RIDDARA RD6 was officially launched in Thailand on 29 October, becoming the first EV pickup sold in the Thai market, starting at THB 899,000; Thailand's Deputy Prime Minister Prasert Jantararuangtong attended.[6][17]

  9. 2024-12

    RIDDARA said Thailand bookings for the RD6 exceeded 1,000 units within two months of launch, including 523 at the Thailand International Motor Expo 2024.[7]

  10. 2025-05

    Radar launched two plug-in-hybrid pickups, the King Kong EM-P (金刚 EM-P) and Horizon EM-P (地平线 EM-P), on 28 May in Chengdu, priced from RMB 139,800, using Geely's NordThor EM-P super-hybrid system.[15]

  11. 2026-01

    Media reported Radar's Thailand subsidiary cut its 2026 sales target to 3,000 units, after 2025 sales of about 1,000 units fell far short of an initial 10,000-unit annual goal.[21]

  12. 2026-03

    At the March 2026 Bangkok International Motor Show, Radar received 2,569 orders for the King Kong EV, up 283% year-on-year, according to media reports.[22]

  13. 2026-04

    The 2026-model Radar King Kong EV (金刚 EV) was launched at the Beijing auto show on 24 April, priced from RMB 119,800 and the first pickup to carry Flyme Auto smart cockpit.[18]

  14. 2026-05-15

    Geely Automobile Holdings (0175.HK) announced the acquisition of Radar Auto (Shandong) for RMB 159 million, Radar Auto Sales for RMB 59 million, and Riddara Automobile (Thailand) for RMB 0.49 million, all from Geely Holding-related sellers, in a connected transaction.[4][12]

  15. 2026-05

    The acquisition completed in May 2026; the three entities became wholly-owned subsidiaries of Geely Auto and were consolidated as a combination under common control.[5]

  16. 2026-05

    Media reported that Radar would be folded into Geely Auto's China Star (中国星) brand under its pickup business line, at an early integration stage.[13]

Current operations

Business structure and product line-up

Radar focuses on a single new-energy pickup business and runs a dual pure-electric and plug-in-hybrid technology path, which the company describes as covering high-frequency urban use with pure-electric models and cross-city, cold-climate and under-served charging regions with hybrids.[15] The pure-electric line includes the two-wheel-drive RD6 (launched 2022), the four-wheel-drive pure-electric Horizon (地平线, launched April 2024) and the commercial-oriented King Kong EV (金刚 EV); the plug-in-hybrid line comprises the King Kong EM-P and Horizon EM-P launched in May 2025.[10][9][18] The company claims Radar is the only new-energy pickup brand in China running both pure-electric and plug-in-hybrid paths, and positions the brand as a mobile multi-functional electrified space that builds an outdoor ecosystem across user, industry, IoT, energy, outdoor and service ecosystems.[15][3]

After the May 2026 acquisition the three operating entities became wholly-owned subsidiaries of Geely Automobile Holdings (0175.HK); the acquisition announcement disclosed the targets' results, with the core entity Radar Auto (Shandong) turning from a 2024 after-tax profit of RMB 67.743 million to a 2025 after-tax loss of RMB 8.646 million, Radar Auto Sales posting a 2025 after-tax profit of RMB 12.325 million, and Riddara Automobile (Thailand) widening to a 2025 after-tax loss of RMB 10.697 million.[4]

Technology and platform

The brand draws on Geely's SEA architecture and its own native new-energy pickup platform M.A.P Radar Hanhai, which the company says supports pure-electric, plug-in-hybrid and range-extender powertrains.[2][16] The hybrid pickups use Geely's NordThor EM-P super-hybrid system (a 3DHT layout with a 1.5T engine and dual motors, 260 kW combined and 914 N·m peak torque), with a CLTC combined range above 1,000 km.[15] The 2026 King Kong EV is the first pickup to carry the Flyme Auto smart cockpit, with a 14.6-inch screen and L2 driving assistance.[18]

Model specifications

The RD6 has a company-stated 0-100 km/h time in the 6-second range and pure-electric range above 600 km, with a large storage space and high-power vehicle-to-load.[11] The four-wheel-drive Horizon uses a dual-motor drive of 315 kW and 594 N·m, with 0-100 km/h in 4.5 seconds, a top speed of 190 km/h, 815 mm wading depth, a 3.5-metre turning radius, 865 kg rated payload, 3,000 kg towing and up to 21 kW vehicle-to-load.[9] The 2026 King Kong EV carries a 280 kW / 485 N·m e-drive, over 1,300 kg design load, 910 kg rated payload, a 1,765 mm / 1,400 L cargo box and 55% full-load gradeability.[18] The King Kong EM-P and Horizon EM-P hybrids deliver 260 kW and 914 N·m combined, 0-100 km/h in 6.3 seconds unladen and over 1,000 km CLTC combined range; the King Kong EM-P 4WD standard-box version is priced RMB 139,800 (long box plus RMB 2,000), the Horizon EM-P all-round 4WD long box RMB 149,800 and the smart 4WD long box RMB 159,800, while the 2026 King Kong EV is split into Zhaocai King Kong 2WD and Dali King Kong 4WD series priced RMB 119,800-159,800.[15][18]

Factory and capacity

Production is at the Radar smart factory in Zichuan district, Zibo, Shandong, which the company describes as China's first native new-energy pickup plant and its global core production base; the RD6 began rolling off this line in September 2022 (media covering the 2026 acquisition also locate the plant in Zichuan, Zibo).[2][22] The company says the line uses intelligent production systems, with robots able to auto-identify and auto-assemble different models with 100% recognition, a 97% CNC rate for key equipment, over 90% overall equipment efficiency and 100% data collection across the production process.[2]

Market environment

Pickups remain a large, mostly fuel-powered segment in Thailand.[19] In Thailand, pickups have long accounted for over 40% of vehicle sales as the country's top-selling segment, but mostly fuel-powered.[17]

Domestic position and sales

Per CPCA Pickup Branch data compiled by media, Radar wholesaled 8,052 pickups in 2024 (+20%, ranked 10th among pickup makers). Media citing official figures reported full-year 2025 sales of about 13,040 vehicles.[19][22][19] Media citing official figures also reported about 13,040 vehicles in 2025.[22]

Overseas business

Overseas, Radar operates under the RIDDARA name. RIDDARA's Thailand site said it set up a subsidiary in Thailand in May 2024; some media later reported a July 2024 date. The RIDDARA RD6 launched there on 29 October 2024.[8][6][17] RIDDARA says it has already launched electric pickups in Eastern Europe, the Middle East, Central Asia, Central America and South America.[6] MarkLines, citing Thai media, reported Radar's 2025 Thailand sales at about 1,000 units and a 2026 target of 3,000 units. Media reported 2,569 orders for the King Kong EV at the March 2026 Bangkok International Motor Show, up 283% year on year.[21][22] The RIDDARA RD6 in Thailand is offered in 2WD and 4WD versions from THB 899,000, with a 6-year or 150,000 km vehicle warranty and an 8-year or 200,000 km motor-and-battery warranty.[7]

Founders and leadership

Brand ownership and governance

Radar is not a separately listed company. The 15 May 2026 HKEX announcement states that Zhejiang Radar Automobile was ultimately wholly owned by Li Shufu and his associates, while Radar HK and Radar BVI were each indirectly 85%-owned by Li Shufu and his associates; since May 2026 the operating entities have been wholly-owned subsidiaries of Geely Automobile Holdings (0175.HK).[4][5] The HKEX announcement states that the seller Zhejiang Radar Automobile was ultimately wholly owned by Li Shufu and his associates, while the sellers of the Thai entity, Radar HK and Radar BVI, were each indirectly 85%-owned by Li Shufu and his associates; all of them were connected persons of Geely Auto, so the acquisition was an exempted connected transaction.[4]

Brand CEO Ling Shiquan

Ling Shiquan was introduced as Radar CEO at the July 2022 launch. A March 2026 media profile still described him in that role, overseeing strategy and operations; later A-tier filings after the May 2026 acquisition do not restate the brand CEO title.[1][16] He holds the title of professor-level senior engineer and graduated with a master's degree in vehicle engineering from Harbin Institute of Technology in July 1997; the same profile said he was already disclosed as vice president of Geely Auto Group by 2018 and had served as CEO of Shandong Geely New Energy Commercial Vehicle.[16] The same profile said he had been responsible for the engineering of new and facelifted products across the Honda, Geely, Zeekr, Lynk & Co, Lotus, Volvo, Polestar, Smart and Radar brands.[16] Company materials also refer to him as Dr. Ling Shiquan.[2][6]

Other early executives

At the 2022 launch, Yi Han was introduced as Chief Growth Officer (CGO) and Chen Aiguo as manufacturing general manager.[1][2]

Reporting line after integration

A March 2026 media profile, written before the May 2026 acquisition, said Radar had been integrated into Geely Auto Group under CEO Gan Jiayue. Later A-tier filings do not restate that reporting line.[16] All of the above are public positions and business histories.

Competition

Radar's competitors fall into two groups: established fuel-pickup makers that still dominate in absolute volume, and new-energy pickup entrants including BYD. Per media compiled from CPCA data, Great Wall sold 177,100 pickups in 2024, with JAC, JMC, Changan, SAIC Maxus, Zhengzhou Nissan and Foton also in the top 10; Radar ranked 10th among pickup makers in 2024.[19] Media covering the 2026 acquisition noted that Great Wall, Changan and BYD have also entered the new-energy pickup segment, according to coverage of the May 2026 acquisition.[19]

In Thailand, Radar faces entrenched Japanese pickups - the Toyota Hilux, Isuzu D-Max and Mitsubishi Triton dominate - while Chinese brands sit in a third tier; MarkLines reported, citing Thai media, that Radar sold about 1,000 units in Thailand in 2025, far below its initial 10,000-unit annual goal.[21]

Our assessment: Radar's strengths are Geely's technology platforms (SEA architecture, NordThor EM-P hybrid) and supply-chain synergies, the payload and vehicle-to-load capability of its native electric-pickup platform, and the brand recognition built from entering the new-energy pickup segment early.[15][9] Its weaknesses are the small absolute scale (about 13,040 vehicles in 2025 and 8,052 in 2024), a young brand with a thinner sales and after-sales network than incumbent pickup makers, and an overseas business still at an early stage.[19][21]

Outlook

Company-stated goals and plans

On overseas expansion, MarkLines reported, citing Thai media, that Radar's Thailand subsidiary set a 2026 sales target of 3,000 units, after an initial goal of 10,000 units a year when it entered Thailand in late 2024.[21] At the Horizon launch in April 2024, the company said it would accelerate overseas expansion to Asia-Pacific, Central Asia, Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Africa, Australia-New Zealand and Central/South America.[9]

Integration into Geely Auto

Per Geely Auto's May 2026 announcement, the acquisition brings a new-energy pickup brand into its business and expands its product portfolio, and the directors expect integration to strengthen the group's position in the pickup segment and support export growth through the group's international distribution channels; the parties also entered a comprehensive-services procurement agreement with annual caps of about RMB 396.1 million, RMB 451.6 million and RMB 498.6 million for the three fiscal years to end-2028.[4][12] Media reported that Radar would be folded into Geely Auto's China Star (中国星) brand under its pickup business line.[13]

Our analysis

Our assessment: after consolidation into the listed Geely Auto, Radar should gain distribution, supply-chain and overseas synergies, but its room for independent development may narrow. New-energy pickups remain a small niche in China relative to the overall pickup market, and with BYD and others stepping up, Radar's scaling depends on product cadence, pickup road-access policy and the pace of overseas market entry. Media covering the acquisition cited CAAM figures that China pickup exports exceeded 300,000 units in 2025, with new-energy pickups seen as a new growth direction.[19]

Risks

Integration-period operating volatility: the acquisition announcement showed that in 2025 the core entity Radar Auto (Shandong) swung to an after-tax loss of RMB 8.646 million and Riddara Automobile (Thailand) widened to an after-tax loss of RMB 10.697 million, so the brand remains in an investment phase, and the pace of reaching profitability after consolidation is uncertain.[4]

Market size and competition: China's new-energy pickup segment remains small in absolute terms, and Radar faces pressure from both new-energy entrants such as BYD and incumbent fuel-pickup makers; its low sales base, including 8,052 wholesale units in 2024, limits resilience to volatility.[19]

Overseas markets: Southeast Asian pickup markets are long dominated by Japanese models such as the Toyota Hilux and Isuzu D-Max, with Chinese brands in a third tier; Radar's Thailand subsidiary acknowledged it had overestimated local EV-pickup demand.[21]

Dependence on the group: Radar's platform, technology, supply chain and channels rely heavily on the Geely group; after integration into the listed company, its independence and priority for resource allocation face uncertainty.[4][16]

Policy and technology path: pickup road-access deregulation is an important precondition for new-energy pickup demand; a slower policy pace would weigh on growth, and the dual pure-electric/plug-in-hybrid path adds R&D and channel investment pressure.[15]

Financial transparency: the brand does not publish standalone financials, so its profitability and per-vehicle economics cannot be assessed independently, and external observers can only see it through the parent's consolidated figures.[4][5]

概览

雷达（RADAR，海外以 RIDDARA 品牌销售）是吉利控股集团于 2022 年 7 月 12 日推出的新能源皮卡品牌，也是吉利旗下首个纯电皮卡品牌，定位中高端新能源皮卡与户外生活方式。[1][11] 首款车型为基于吉利 SEA 浩瀚架构与品牌原生新能源皮卡平台 M.A.P 雷达瀚海平台打造的纯电皮卡 RD6。[2] 品牌诞生背景是 2022 年前后国内皮卡进城限制持续松绑、皮卡向乘用化与高端化转型，以及新能源渗透率提升，电动皮卡被视为新的市场机会。[11]

现状

业务结构与产品矩阵

雷达聚焦单一的新能源皮卡业务，坚持「纯电 + 插混」双技术路线；官方称纯电面向城市高频出行、插混面向跨城长途、北方高寒及补能设施不完善地区。[15] 纯电产品线包括两驱纯电皮卡 RD6（2022 年上市）、四驱纯电超级皮卡地平线（2024 年 4 月上市）及面向商用场景的金刚 EV；插混产品线为 2025 年 5 月上市的金刚 EM-P 与地平线 EM-P。[10][9][18] 官方称雷达是中国唯一同时布局纯电与电混双技术路线的新能源皮卡品牌，品牌定位为「移动多元带电空间」，并致力于从用户生态、产业生态、物联生态、能源生态、户外生态、服务生态六大领域构建户外生态圈。[15][3]

2026 年 5 月收购完成后，三家运营主体成为吉利汽车控股（0175.HK）的全资附属公司；收购公告披露了标的公司经营情况：核心运营主体雷达汽车（山东）由 2024 年税后溢利 6,774.3 万元转为 2025 年税后亏损 864.6 万元，雷达汽车销售 2025 年税后利润 1,232.5 万元，Riddara Automobile (Thailand) 2025 年税后亏损扩大至 1,069.7 万元。[4]

技术与平台

品牌依托吉利的 SEA 浩瀚架构与自研原生新能源皮卡平台 M.A.P 雷达瀚海平台，官方称该平台支持纯电、插电及增程等多种动力形式。[2][16] 插混车型搭载吉利雷神 EM-P 超级电混系统（3DHT 构型、1.5T 发动机加双电机，综合最大功率 260 kW、峰值扭矩 914 N·m），CLTC 综合续航超 1,000 km。[15] 2026 款金刚 EV 为皮卡中首搭 Flyme Auto 智能座舱，配 14.6 英寸屏与 L2 级驾驶辅助。[18]

车型谱系与关键参数

RD6 是品牌首款车型，官方称其百公里加速 6 秒级、纯电续航超 600 公里，具备大储物空间与大功率全场景放电功能。[11] 四驱纯电地平线搭载双电驱系统，最大功率 315 kW、峰值扭矩 594 N·m，零百加速 4.5 秒，最高车速 190 km/h，最大涉水深度 815 mm，最小转弯半径 3.5 米，最大额定载重 865 kg、拖拽能力 3,000 kg，外放电功率最高 21 kW。[9] 2026 款金刚 EV 搭载最大功率 280 kW、峰值扭矩 485 N·m 的电驱系统，设计承载能力超 1,300 kg、额定载重 910 kg，货箱长 1,765 mm、容积 1,400 L，满载爬坡度达 55%。[18] 插混的金刚 EM-P 与地平线 EM-P 综合最大功率 260 kW、峰值扭矩 914 N·m，空载零百加速 6.3 秒，CLTC 综合续航超 1,000 km；其中金刚 EM-P 千里金刚四驱标箱售价 13.98 万元（长箱加 2,000 元），地平线 EM-P 全能版四驱长箱 14.98 万元、智能版四驱长箱 15.98 万元，2026 款金刚 EV 分「招财金刚」两驱与「大力金刚」四驱两大系列、售价区间 11.98 万-15.98 万元。[15][18]

工厂与产能

生产基地位于山东省淄博市淄川区的雷达智慧工厂，官方称其为国内首个原生新能源皮卡专业工厂、也是雷达全球核心生产基地；RD6 于 2022 年 9 月起在该厂下线。[2][22] 据官方介绍，该厂生产线采用智能生产信息化系统，机器人可 100% 自动识别不同车型并自动装配，关键装备数控化率 97%，设备综合利用率大于 90%，全生产工艺流程数据采集率 100%。[2]

市场环境

皮卡在泰国仍是以燃油车为主的大盘车型。[19] 泰国方面，皮卡长期占据该国汽车市场 40% 以上份额，是当地第一大热销车型，但以燃油车为主。[17]

国内市场地位与销量

据媒体整理的乘联分会口径数据，雷达 2024 年批发销售皮卡 8,052 辆（同比 +20%，皮卡厂商中排名第 10）。媒体援引官方公布数据称，2025 年全年销量约 13,040 辆。[19][22][19] 媒体援引官方公布数据亦称 2025 年全年约 13,040 辆。[22]

海外业务

海外以 RIDDARA 品牌运营。RIDDARA 泰国官网称 2024 年 5 月在泰国设立子公司，部分媒体另称成立于 2024 年 7 月；2024 年 10 月 29 日 RIDDARA RD6 在泰国正式上市。[8][6][17] RIDDARA 官网称，雷达已在东欧、中东、中亚、中美洲和南美洲市场推出电动皮卡。[6] 据 MarkLines 援引泰国当地媒体，雷达 2025 年在泰国销量约 1,000 辆，2026 年目标为 3,000 辆。媒体报道，2026 年 3 月曼谷国际车展雷达金刚 EV 获得 2,569 辆订单，同比增长 283%。[21][22] RIDDARA RD6 在泰国提供两驱与四驱版本，起售价 89.9 万泰铢，提供 6 年或 15 万公里整车质保、8 年或 20 万公里电机电池质保。[7]

品牌 CEO 凌世权

凌世权在 2022 年 7 月品牌发布会上以雷达汽车 CEO 身份亮相。2026 年 3 月的媒体人物稿仍如此称呼并称其负责品牌战略与运营；2026 年 5 月收购完成后的 A 级公告未再重述该职务。[1][16] 他拥有教授级高级工程师职称，1997 年 7 月硕士毕业于哈尔滨工业大学车辆工程专业；同一人物稿称，2018 年对外披露的职务已是吉利汽车集团副总裁，并曾任山东吉利新能源商用车有限公司 CEO。[16] 同一人物稿还称，他曾负责本田、吉利、极氪、领克、路特斯、沃尔沃、极星、Smart、雷达等品牌全新开发及改款产品的工程技术工作。[16] 公司官方材料亦称其为「凌世权博士」。[2][6]

竞争格局

雷达的竞争对手分两类：一是在绝对销量上仍占主导的传统燃油皮卡厂商，二是包括比亚迪在内的新能源皮卡新进入者。据媒体整理的乘联分会口径，长城 2024 年皮卡销量 17.71 万辆，江淮、江铃、长安、上汽大通、郑州日产、福田亦进入前十，雷达 2024 年在皮卡厂商中排名第 10。[19] 媒体在报道 2026 年收购时亦提到，长城、长安、比亚迪等企业均已布局新能源皮卡赛道（见收购报道）。[19]

在泰国，雷达面对根深蒂固的日系皮卡——丰田 Hilux、五十铃 D-Max、三菱 Triton 主导市场，中国品牌处于第三梯队；据 MarkLines 援引泰国当地媒体，雷达 2025 年在泰国销量约 1,000 辆，远低于最初 1 万辆/年的目标。[21]

我们的判断：雷达的优势在于吉利集团的技术平台（SEA 浩瀚架构、雷神 EM-P 电混）与供应链协同、原生纯电皮卡平台带来的承载与外放电能力，以及较早切入新能源皮卡赛道形成的品牌认知。[15][9] 短板在于绝对销量规模仍小（2025 年约 13,040 辆、2024 年 8,052 辆），品牌年轻、销售与售后网络不及传统皮卡厂商，海外业务尚处起步阶段。[19][21]

未来展望

公司公开的目标与规划

海外方面，据 MarkLines 援引泰国当地媒体报道，雷达泰国公司将 2026 年销量目标定为 3,000 辆，而 2024 年下半年进入泰国时的初始目标为年销 1 万辆。[21] 在 2024 年 4 月地平线上市时，公司表示将加速向亚太、中亚、东南亚、中东、非洲、澳新、中南美等市场拓展。[9]

并入吉利汽车

据吉利汽车 2026 年 5 月公告，收购将把新能源皮卡品牌纳入业务、扩充产品组合，董事预期整合可巩固集团在皮卡细分市场的地位，并借助集团国际分销渠道促进出口增长；双方并订立综合服务采购协议，截至 2028 年 12 月 31 日止三个财政年度的年度上限分别约 3.961 亿元、4.516 亿元、4.986 亿元。[4][12] 媒体报道，雷达品牌将并入吉利汽车旗下「中国星」品牌、隶属其皮卡业务线。[13]

我们的分析

我们的判断：并入吉利汽车上市公司后，雷达有望获得渠道、供应链与海外分销的协同支持，但品牌独立发展空间可能收窄。新能源皮卡在国内仍是相对整体皮卡市场的小众细分，叠加比亚迪等对手加码，雷达的规模扩张仍取决于产品投放节奏、皮卡路权政策与海外市场落地速度。媒体在报道此次收购时援引中汽协数据称，2025 年中国皮卡出口突破 30 万辆，新能源皮卡被视为行业新增长方向。[19]

风险

整合初期的经营波动：收购公告披露的标的公司数据显示，2025 年核心运营主体雷达汽车（山东）由盈转亏（税后亏损 864.6 万元）、Riddara Automobile (Thailand) 税后亏损扩大至 1,069.7 万元，品牌整体仍处投入期，并入上市公司后扭亏与降本增效的节奏存在不确定性。[4]

市场规模与竞争：中国新能源皮卡细分市场绝对规模仍小，且面临比亚迪等新能源车企与传统燃油皮卡厂商的双重挤压，雷达销量基数低（2024 年批发 8,052 辆），抗波动能力有限。[19]

海外市场：东南亚皮卡市场长期由丰田 Hilux、五十铃 D-Max 等日系车型主导，中国品牌处于第三梯队；雷达泰国公司承认此前高估了当地电动皮卡需求。[21]

依赖集团资源：雷达的平台、技术、供应链与渠道高度依赖吉利集团，并入上市公司后，其独立性与资源配置优先级存在不确定性。[4][16]

政策与技术路线：皮卡进城路权政策的松绑是新能源皮卡需求的重要前提，政策节奏放缓将影响增长；纯电与插混双路线也带来研发与渠道投入压力。[15]

财务透明度：品牌未单独披露财务数据，其盈利状况与单车成本难以独立评估，外部只能通过母公司合并口径观察。[4][5]
