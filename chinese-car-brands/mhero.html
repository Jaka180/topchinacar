Overview

M-Hero (Mengshi, 猛士) is the luxury electric (intelligent) off-road brand of Dongfeng Motor Corporation, a centrally administered state-owned enterprise. The brand was launched in Wuhan on 27 August 2022 alongside its 'M' logo system, the M-Terrain and M-Terrain S concept cars, and the M TECH intelligent off-road architecture [1]. Its brand proposition is 'fearless, conquer' (无畏·征服). The description of M-Hero as 'China's first luxury electric off-road brand' is the framing used by Dongfeng and official media, not an independent judgement [1][9]. The brand is operated through Mengshi Automotive Technology Co., Ltd. under Dongfeng Motor Group Company Limited, and spans three product lines — the 917 (extreme hardcore off-road), the M817 (luxury intelligent off-road) and the X series (family intelligent mobility) [13]. It has cooperated deeply with Huawei Qiankun on intelligent driving and cockpit systems since the M817 [6]. In 2025 the brand sold 10,228 vehicles, up 387% year-on-year [10]. The brand is not separately listed; its parent is covered in the Dongfeng Motor Group deep-dive [18].

Current operations

Business structure and product lines

M-Hero organises its lineup into three product sequences: the 917 series as the flagship for extreme hardcore off-roading, the M817 series for luxury intelligent off-roading, and the X series (starting with the X700) for family intelligent mobility, per Dongfeng's October 2026 release [13]. Production uses a Wuhan-Xiangyang dual-base arrangement: the Wuhan base focuses on core off-road technology, while the Xiangyang base handles scale smart manufacturing [13][15]. The Xiangyang base is the converted former Dongfeng Nissan Xiangyang plant, upgraded from a fuel-vehicle line into a mixed flexible new-energy line under the RMB 10.6 billion project, using a 'carmaker-led, Huawei-enabled' model and Huawei's IPD and IPMS processes; per Hubei Daily, three new models are planned within three years, the first targeting Q4 mass production across pure-electric and hybrid routes, all carrying Huawei's ADS and cockpit stack [23]. In the Xiangyang base's supply chain, 61% of components are procured within Hubei province, and the project has drawn 28 new-energy supply-chain companies to Xiangyang [14]. Huaxia Times separately says the vehicle programme is expected to drive more than RMB 4.5 billion in annual local industrial output [16].

Models and price range

The flagship 917's official guide-price range is RMB 637,700-1,098,000 (extended-range from RMB 637,700 and pure-electric from RMB 697,700 at its August 2023 launch), per Caijing magazine and the Wuhan ETDZ authority [3][17]. The M817 launched in August 2025 at RMB 319,900-349,900, with the 2026 facelift at RMB 299,900-399,900 [6][12]. The X700 pre-sold from RMB 249,800-289,800 in September 2026 and launched on 25 October 2026 at RMB 219,800-279,800, with a limited-time price from RMB 209,800 [14][21]. The M-HUNTER concept car was pre-sold from RMB 3.68 million [4].

Technology platform

The brand is built on the M TECH intelligent off-road architecture, comprising the MORA skateboard off-road platform, MEGA POWER drivetrain and M-ATS all-terrain intelligent system, unveiled together with the brand in August 2022 [1]. From the M817 onward, models carry Huawei Qiankun systems. The M817 that launched on 17 Aug 2025 applied Qiankun ADS 4 intelligent driving and the Hongmeng (HarmonyOS) cockpit 5 [6]. The all-new M817 on sale after its 27 Jun 2026 launch is described by the Wuhan ETDZ authority as the first off-roader to carry Huawei Qiankun ADS 5, with combined output of up to 715 kW, plus an 800V platform, 6C fast charging and 900 mm wading depth [28]; Xinhua the same day says it debuts the new-generation Huawei Qiankun ADS 5 and a new-generation Hongmeng cockpit off-road edition [29]. The X700 applies Huawei Qiankun intelligent driving and the HarmonySpace 6 cockpit with a 896-line dual-beam lidar, and rides on the Panshi (rock-solid) chassis 2.0 with dual-chamber air suspension, MRC dampers and a magic-carpet preview system [27].

Sales and market position

M-Hero sold 10,228 vehicles in 2025, up 387% year-on-year, with 3,002 units in December alone, according to the brand's annual sales report released on 1 January 2026; sales rose for four consecutive months from September 2025 [9][10]. Caijing reported that people familiar with the matter said Huawei set the M817 a sales task of completing 12,400 units in four and a half months, while Dongfeng Group's internal expectation was 15,000 units [17]. Huaxia Times cites CPCA data of 447,600 domestic 'boxy' crossover off-road vehicles sold in H1 2026 across 42 models, with over 70% of demand from urban family users [16].

Overseas business

In 2025 M-Hero exported over 1,000 vehicles and completed market-access certification for Europe, the CIS and the Gulf; the 917 and M817 have entered 47 countries and regions, and right-hand-drive development is under way [11]. Huaxia Times reports 2025 Middle East exports of 821 vehicles, up 45% year-on-year, with official showrooms in Saudi Arabia, Qatar, the UAE and Kuwait, and distribution outlets in Western Europe including Belgium [16]. On 15 December 2025, 400 units of the MHERO II (the overseas M817) were shipped from Shanghai to the Middle East [11].

Founders and leadership

Founding and early leadership

As a brand of a central SOE, M-Hero has no private founder. Cao Dongjie served as the first CEO (general manager) from the brand's establishment in 2022, appearing as M-Hero Technology CEO at the August 2022 brand launch and the August 2023 park opening; in September 2025 he was transferred to Dongfeng Honda as executive deputy general manager [8][2].

Current management

Wan Liangyu (万良渝) is CEO, appointed September 2025; he was previously M-Hero's deputy general manager and CMO (chief manufacturing officer) [8]. He attended the X700 production start on 10 October 2026 as M-Hero CEO [15], and at the X700 launch said the after-sales network would exceed 200 outlets in 2026 and 350 in 2027, covering Tibet and Xinjiang [21].

Ownership and governance

Mengshi Automotive Technology Co., Ltd. is a subsidiary of Dongfeng Motor Group Company Limited, whose H-share listing was withdrawn on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange with effect from 18 March 2026 under an absorption-merger privatisation; its ultimate parent is Dongfeng Motor Corporation, a centrally administered SOE [26]. A second entity, Mengshi Technology (Xiangyang) Co., Ltd., was established under the 17 September 2025 announcement with registered capital of RMB 8.47 billion: Dongfeng Motor Group about 41.9%, Dongfeng Motor Co., Ltd. about 10.9%, Xiangyang Holding 34% and Xianggao Investment 13.2%; per NBD, Dongfeng insiders said the new company runs in parallel with the existing Wuhan entity, with differentiated product positioning, jointly enlarging the M-Hero brand [7].

Competition

Our judgement. M-Hero competes in China's hardcore off-road segment against Great Wall Motor's Tank brand, BYD's Fangchengbao, BAIC's Beijing Off-road, Chery's Jetour and Toyota's Prado, among others. Its strengths are its military off-road heritage, Huawei Qiankun intelligent driving and Dongfeng's manufacturing scale; its weakness is small volume. In 2025 M-Hero sold 10,228 vehicles [9]. Huaxia Times, at the 12 Oct 2026 pre-sale stage (where it calls the model the M-Hero 700), said the vehicle was aimed at RMB 250,000-300,000 and described that band as a supply gap [16]. The official guide price set on 25 Oct 2026 is RMB 219,800-279,800, so the entry price is below RMB 250,000 [21].

Our judgement. The 917 and M817 were initially positioned above RMB 300,000, limiting their customer base; Caijing, citing third-party statistics rather than its own count or an official figure, reported the 917 sold 824 units in Aug-Dec 2023 and 2,101 in 2024 [17], which is why the M817 and X700 have progressively lowered prices into the RMB 200,000-400,000 band. Huaxia Times cites CPCA data of 447,600 boxy off-road vehicles in H1 2026 [16].

Outlook

Company plans. At the end of 2025 M-Hero launched its Dengfeng (summit) initiative, aiming to reform the organisation, technology and ecosystem toward becoming the leading luxury intelligent off-road brand, to invest RMB 12 billion in R&D, to deepen cooperation with Huawei Qiankun, and to open a first flagship brand experience centre with Huawei in 2026 [9]. The Hubei Daily release also cites the goal of a user-direct service system (10-minute response, one-hour solution, one-day closure) and the adoption of Huawei Qiankun's IPD and IPMS processes [9]. On the Chinese expansion of IPMS, China Business Journal uses 'integrated product marketing and sales' [8], while NBD uses 'integrated product marketing and service' [7]. The Hubei Daily sales article uses the abbreviation IPMS and does not give a Chinese expansion [9]. On after-sales, Wan Liangyu said the network would exceed 200 outlets in 2026 and 350 in 2027 [21]. Hubei Daily reports the Xiangyang base will launch three new models within three years, the first targeting Q4 mass production [23].

Our judgement. The X700's official price of RMB 219,800-279,800 marks M-Hero's first entry into the mainstream off-road band, shifting the brand from pure high-end positioning toward scale, with the goal of widening the user base and spreading R&D and manufacturing costs [21][16]. Overseas expansion, particularly the Middle East and Europe where local new-energy off-road supply is scarce, is the other growth lever; this judgement rests on the reported 2025 exports of over 1,000 units and market-access certifications [11]. Whether the X700 can sustain volume depends on mainstream off-road demand and competition in the same price band; this cannot be judged in advance.

Risks

  • Limited segment ceiling. Hardcore off-road is a niche market; Huaxia Times cites CPCA data of 447,600 'boxy' off-road vehicles in H1 2026 [16].
  • Price-band and brand positioning. M-Hero's move down to the RMB 200,000-400,000 band increases exposure to the most crowded mid-to-high off-road competition, where Tank and Fangchengbao are entrenched [16].
  • Dependence on Huawei. The M817 and X700 rely on Huawei's Qiankun stack for their main differentiators, deepening dependence on a single supplier [6].
  • Overseas trade barriers. The EU imposed definitive countervailing duties on battery-electric vehicles from China for five years from October 2024 [22]; M-Hero has entered Europe through Belgium and other Western European markets, so EU measures could affect pricing and margins for its pure-electric models [16].
  • Joint-venture governance and dual-line coordination. The Xiangyang joint venture is held by four shareholders and runs in parallel with the Wuhan entity with differentiated positioning, adding governance and product-coordination complexity [7].

概览

猛士（M-Hero）是东风汽车集团有限公司（国务院国资委监管的中央企业）旗下的豪华电动（智能）越野品牌，2022 年 8 月 27 日在武汉正式发布，同步发布品牌专属「M」标识体系、M-Terrain 与 M-Terrain S 两款概念车及猛士智能越野架构 M TECH[1]。品牌以「无畏·征服」为品牌主张；「中国首个豪华电动越野品牌」是东风及官方媒体的口径，非本研究判断[1][9]。品牌由东风汽车集团股份有限公司旗下猛士汽车科技公司运营，形成猛士 917（极致硬核越野）、猛士 M817（豪华智能越野）与 X 系列（家庭智慧出行）三大产品序列[13]，并自 M817 起与华为乾崑在智能驾驶、智能座舱等领域深度合作[6]。2025 年品牌销量 10,228 辆，同比增长 387%[10]。猛士未单独上市，母集团情况见「东风汽车集团」深度研究[18]。

现状

业务结构与产品序列

猛士将产品划分为三大序列：917 系列作为旗舰主打极致硬核越野，M817 系列主打豪华智能越野，X 系列（以 X700 为首）面向家庭智慧出行，据东风 2026 年 10 月披露[13]。生产采用武汉、襄阳「双基地」布局：武汉基地聚焦越野核心技术攻坚，襄阳基地承接规模化智能制造[13][15]。襄阳基地即改造后的原东风日产襄阳工厂，总投资 106 亿元，将燃油车产线升级为新能源混流柔性生产线，采用「车企主导、华为赋能」模式并引入华为 IPD 与 IPMS 体系；据湖北日报，未来三年内将推出 3 款全新车型，首款瞄准四季度量产、覆盖纯电与混动两种路线，全系搭载华为 ADS 与鸿蒙座舱[23]。襄阳基地的配套供应链中，湖北省内零部件采购占比 61%，并吸引 28 家新能源供应链企业落户襄阳[14]。华夏时报称，整车落地后预计带动当地产业链年产值超 45 亿元[16]。

产品与价格谱系

旗舰 917 的官方指导价区间为 63.77 万-109.8 万元（2023 年 8 月上市时增程版 63.77 万元起、纯电版 69.77 万元起），据《财经》与武汉经开区管委会[3][17]。M817 于 2025 年 8 月以 31.99 万-34.99 万元上市，2026 款改款售价 29.99 万-39.99 万元[6][12]。X700 于 2026 年 9 月预售 24.98 万-28.98 万元，10 月 25 日正式上市指导价 21.98 万-27.98 万元，限时焕新价 20.98 万元起[14][21]。M-HUNTER 概念车预售价 368 万元起[4]。

技术与平台

品牌基于猛士智能越野架构 M TECH 打造，该架构含猛士滑板越野平台 MORA、猛士动力 MEGA POWER 与猛士越野全地形智能解决方案 M-ATS，随品牌于 2022 年 8 月一同发布[1]。自 M817 起，车型搭载华为乾崑方案。2025 年 8 月 17 日上市的 M817，东风官网称其应用华为乾崑智驾 ADS 4 与鸿蒙座舱 5[6]。截至 2026 年 10 月 29 日在售的是 2026 年 6 月 27 日上市的全新 M817：武汉经开区管委会 2026 年 6 月 29 日稿写其首搭华为乾崑智驾 ADS 5，综合功率最高达 715kW，并写明 800V、6C 与 900mm 涉水[28]；新华网同日稿写其首发全新一代华为乾崑智驾 ADS 5 和新一代鸿蒙座舱越野版[29]。X700 应用华为乾崑智能驾驶与鸿蒙座舱 HarmonySpace 6，配备 896 线双光路激光雷达，并采用磐石底盘 2.0、双腔空气悬架、MRC 磁流变减振器与魔毯预瞄系统[27]。

销量与市场地位

猛士 2025 年销量 10,228 辆，同比增长 387%，其中 12 月单月 3,002 辆，据品牌 2026 年 1 月 1 日发布的年度销量快报，且自 2025 年 9 月起销量「四连涨」[9][10]。《财经》报道，知情人士称华为给 M817 立下「四个半月，完成 1.24 万台销售任务」，东风集团内部预期为 1.5 万台[17]。华夏时报援引乘联会数据称，2026 年上半年国内方盒子泛越野车型累计销量 44.76 万辆、在售产品 42 款，且 70% 以上购买需求来自城市家庭用户[16]。

海外业务

2025 年猛士整车出口量突破 1,000 辆，并完成欧洲、独联体、海湾等地区的市场准入认证；917 与 M817 已登陆 47 个国家和地区，右舵车型研发正在推进[11]。华夏时报报道，2025 年中东市场猛士系列出口 821 辆、同比增长 45%，已在沙特、卡塔尔、阿联酋、科威特设立官方展厅，并在比利时等西欧国家完成经销网点铺设[16]。2025 年 12 月 15 日，400 辆 MHERO II（猛士 M817 海外版）自上海港启运中东[11]。

创始人与管理层

创始人与早期管理层

猛士为中央企业旗下品牌，无民营意义上的单一创始人。曹东杰自 2022 年品牌创立起任首任 CEO（总经理），先后以猛士科技 CEO 身份出席 2022 年 8 月品牌发布与 2023 年 8 月园区开园；2025 年 9 月调任东风本田执行副总经理[8][2]。

现任核心管理层

万良渝任 CEO，2025 年 9 月上任，此前任猛士科技副总经理、CMO（首席制造官）[8]。2026 年 10 月 10 日以猛士汽车 CEO 身份出席 X700 量产下线仪式[15]，并在 X700 上市时表示 2026 年售后网络将突破 200 家、2027 年突破 350 家、实现对西藏新疆全覆盖[21]。

股权与治理

猛士汽车科技公司为东风汽车集团股份有限公司旗下子公司；该股份公司的 H 股上市地位已自 2026 年 3 月 18 日起撤销（吸收合并私有化），最终母公司为东风汽车集团有限公司（中央企业）[26]。另一主体猛士科技（襄阳）有限公司依据 2025 年 9 月 17 日公告设立，注册资本 84.7 亿元：东风汽车集团股份有限公司约 41.9%、东风汽车有限公司约 10.9%、襄阳控股 34%、襄高投资 13.2%；据每日经济新闻转述东风公司内部人员，新公司与现有武汉猛士科技双线并行、产品定位有所差异，共同做大猛士品牌[7]。

竞争格局

我们的判断：猛士所处的国内硬派越野赛道，主要竞争者包括长城坦克、比亚迪方程豹、北汽北京越野、奇瑞捷途以及丰田普拉多等。猛士的优势在于军工越野基因、华为乾崑智驾与东风的制造规模，短板是销量规模小。2025 年猛士销量 10,228 辆[9]。华夏时报在 2026 年 10 月 12 日预售阶段（文中称「猛士 700」）称该车定位于 25 万-30 万元，并认为这一价格带此前存在供给缺口[16]；2026 年 10 月 25 日正式指导价定为 21.98 万-27.98 万元，入门价低于 25 万元[21]。

我们的判断：917 与 M817 初期定价均在 30 万元以上，客群受限；《财经》援引第三方统计（非该刊自行统计，亦非官方口径）称，917 于 2023 年 8-12 月售出 824 辆、2024 年售出 2,101 辆[17]，这正是 M817 与 X700 相继把价格下探至 20 万-40 万元区间的原因。华夏时报援引乘联会数据称 2026 年上半年方盒子越野车 44.76 万辆[16]。

未来展望

公司公开规划。2025 年底猛士启动「登峰行动」，计划从组织、技术、生态三个层面进行系统性变革，目标直指「豪华智能越野第一品牌」，投入 120 亿的研发资金，深化与华为乾崑的战略协同，并计划 2026 年与华为乾崑联手打造首个品牌旗舰体验中心[9]。湖北日报还披露其打造「用户直联」敏捷服务体系（10 分钟响应、1 小时方案、1 天闭环）以及全面引入华为乾崑 IPD 与 IPMS 流程体系的目标[9]。IPMS 的中文展开有两种媒体表述：中国经营报作「集成产品营销与销售」[8]，每日经济新闻作「集成产品营销与服务」[7]。湖北日报销量稿只写 IPMS，没有中文展开[9]。售后方面，万良渝表示 2026 年售后网络将突破 200 家、2027 年突破 350 家[21]。湖北日报报道，襄阳基地未来三年内将推出 3 款全新车型，首款瞄准四季度量产[23]。

我们的判断。X700 以 21.98 万-27.98 万元的正式指导价首次切入主流越野区间，意味着品牌由纯高端定位转向规模上量，目标在于扩大用户基数、摊薄研发与制造成本[21][16]。海外（尤其是本地新能源越野供给稀缺的中东与欧洲）是另一条增长路径，这一判断依据媒体报道的 2025 年出口超 1,000 辆及市场准入认证[11]。X700 能否持续放量，取决于主流越野市场需求及同价位产品的竞争，尚无法预先判断。

风险

  • 细分市场天花板有限。硬派越野属小众市场，华夏时报援引乘联会数据称 2026 年上半年方盒子越野车 44.76 万辆[16]。
  • 价格带与品牌定位。猛士下探至 20 万-40 万元区间后，进入坦克、方程豹等已站稳的中高端越野竞争腹地[16]。
  • 对华为的依赖。M817 与 X700 的核心卖点高度依赖华为乾崑全栈方案，加深了对单一供应商的依赖[6]。
  • 海外贸易壁垒。欧盟自 2024 年 10 月起对原产中国的电池电动汽车征收五年期最终反补贴税[22]；猛士已通过比利时等西欧市场进入欧洲，其纯电车型的定价与利润可能受到影响[16]。
  • 合资治理与双线协同。猛士科技（襄阳）合资公司由四家股东共同持股、与武汉猛士科技双线并行、产品定位有差异，多主体运营增加了品牌治理与产品协同的复杂度[7]。
