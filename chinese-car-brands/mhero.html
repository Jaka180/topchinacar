M-Hero 猛士

Dongfeng's ultra-luxury off-road brand rooted in military vehicle heritage. The M-Hero 917 is a 4-motor 1,088 hp electric Defender-fighter.东风旗下超豪华越野品牌，源自军车血统。猛士 917 为四电机 1,088 马力电动硬派越野，对标卫士。

Founded创立2022
HQ总部Wuhan
Focus主攻Hardcore off-road
