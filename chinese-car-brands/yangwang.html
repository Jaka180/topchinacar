Overview

Yangwang is the ultra-luxury new-energy vehicle brand of BYD Company Limited, positioned at the top of BYD's brand portfolio and aimed at the million-yuan (RMB 1 million-plus) and above segment. On 5 January 2023, BYD held the 'Dare to Cross the Galaxy' brand and technology launch in Shenzhen, officially unveiling the Yangwang brand, the e4 (Yisanfang) four-motor platform, and its first two models — the U8 hardcore off-road SUV and the U9 electric supercar — with chairman and president Wang Chuanfu driving the U8 onto the stage.[4][6] Its slogan is 'surpassing the extreme, never seen before', and its products are built on the 'Yangwang architecture', which integrates six core technology modules — powertrain, chassis, battery, super body, intelligent driving and intelligent cockpit, with e4, DiSus and Blade Battery as its signature technologies.[1][3]

As of the research cut-off date (2026-10-29), the line-up spans the U7 flagship sedan, U8 hardcore off-road SUV, U8L administrative luxury SUV, U9 supercar and the limited-run U9 Xtreme hypercar, with the 2026 model year upgraded to BYD's second-generation Blade Battery and flash charging; a first ultra-luxury executive sedan, named Dingyao (YANGWANG ALTIOR) on 27 October 2026, is scheduled for a world debut in Dubai in early November 2026.[1][14][24] Within BYD's brand hierarchy, Yangwang sits above the Dynasty and Ocean series (mainstream), Denza (luxury) and Fangchengbao (personalised off-road), making it the group's highest-priced brand.[6]

In sales terms, Yangwang recorded cumulative sales of 2,001 units over November-December 2023, about 7,400 units in 2024 (U8 about 7,254), and 4,785 units in 2025 (U8 series 2,728, U7 1,953, U9 104, model split compiled by blogger Xiaodi Kuaibao); on 21 April 2025 it became the first Chinese million-yuan brand to pass 10,000 cumulative sales.[7][12][15][17][23] Yangwang is not separately listed and discloses no brand-level financial statements; BYD's consolidated figures are covered in the BYD deep research.[21][22]

History

  1. 2022-11-08

    BYD officially announces its premium brand on the evening of 8 November 2022 and names it 'Yangwang'; the 'Yangwang' trademark had already been registered, and the price range was expected to be RMB 800,000-1.5 million (reported by Securities Times on 9 November 2022).[29]

    Shenzhen Yangwang Automobile Sales Co., Ltd. is established (Securities Times reported on 9 November 2022 that it had recently been set up); its direct shareholder is BYD Auto Industry Co., Ltd., the legal representative is He Zhiqi and the registered capital is RMB 1 million.[29]

  4. 2023-09-20

    The Yangwang U8 Luxury Edition is launched at RMB 1.098 million.[5]

  5. 2023-11

    First deliveries of the U8 Luxury Edition begin in late October 2023, making November 2023 the first full delivery month.[5]

  6. 2023-12

    The U8 records cumulative sales of 2,001 units over November-December 2023 (1,593 in December).[7]

  7. 2024-02-25

    The Yangwang U9 pure-electric supercar is launched at RMB 1.68 million, with deliveries starting from mid-2024.[7][8]

  8. 2024-03

  9. 2024-04-25

    At the Beijing Auto Show, the U8 Off-road Master Edition is launched from RMB 1.098 million, the DiSus-Z (Yunnian-Z) suspension is released and debuts on the U7, and the U8, U9 and U7 appear together for the first time.[9]

  10. 2024-11

    The Yangwang Business Academy, a study organisation for Yangwang owners, is established, with BYD chairman and president Wang Chuanfu as its president.[2][28]

  11. 2025-03-27

Current operations

Model line-up and positioning

As of 2026-10-29, Yangwang's line-up comprises: the U8 hardcore off-road SUV (Luxury Edition launched 2023-09-20 at RMB 1.098 million; Off-road Master Edition 2024-04-25; 2026 model from RMB 1.008 million); the full-size administrative luxury U8L (Dingshi Edition launched 2025-09-12 from RMB 1.28 million; 2026 Dingshi from RMB 1.30 million; Dingcang four-seat edition launched 2026-08-05 at RMB 1.458 million); the U7 flagship sedan (launched 2025-03-27 from RMB 628,000, 2026 model from RMB 658,000); the U9 electric supercar (launched 2024-02-25 at RMB 1.68 million); and the 30-unit bespoke U9 Xtreme (over RMB 20 million).[5][7][11][12][14][30][19]

Core technologies

The Yangwang architecture integrates six core technology modules: powertrain, chassis, battery, super body, intelligent driving and intelligent cockpit.[3] The e4 platform uses four independently driven motors to control each wheel's torque, enabling tank turns, stable driving after a tyre blowout and emergency floating; the DiSus body-control system appears as DiSus-P on the U8/U8L, DiSus-X on the U9 and DiSus-Z — described as the world's first mass-produced electric suspension — on the U7.[5][9][11] The U7 also features 20-degree bi-directional rear-wheel steering and e4 agile steering with a 4.85 m turning radius, enabling parallel-shift parking.[28] The e4 concept traces back to BYD's ET electric concept car of about two decades earlier, later industrialised as the e4 platform.[6] From 2026 the range upgrades to the second-generation Blade Battery and flash charging, which BYD describes as '5 minutes charged, 9 minutes full'.[14] BYD also offered a flash-charging benefit: non-commercial owners who paid a deposit before 11 May 2026 get one year of free charging at BYD flash-charging stations after delivery.[14] The 2026 U8 lifts combined range 20.5% to 1,205 km with a 9C charge rate and adds a 3nm 5G cockpit chip and the God's Eye 5.0 system; the 2026 U8L adds full-scenario privacy safety, NOA blowout-stable stopping and automatic emergency-floating activation; the 2026 U7 debuts the DiSus-A+ intelligent air suspension.[14] The U8 Luxury Edition is a plug-in hybrid with 1,200 hp, 880 kW peak power, 8,700 Nm wheel-end torque, a 3.6-second 0-100 km/h, about 1,000 km CLTC combined range, 150 mm suspension travel and 30-minute emergency floating; the U9 has four motors and over 1,300 hp, with 2.36-second 0-100 km/h and a 309.19 km/h top speed.[5][8]

Manufacturing and capacity

Yangwang does not separately disclose its plants or capacity; its models are produced within BYD's manufacturing system, and no brand-level employee count is published.[21] BYD does not break out Yangwang in its segment reporting, so the brand's standalone economics cannot be assessed from public filings.[22]

Domestic market position

Yangwang was the first Chinese brand to sell new-energy vehicles into the million-yuan segment at meaningful scale: the U8 delivered about 7,254 units in 2024, ranking fourth among million-yuan SUVs, and cumulative sales passed 10,000 in April 2025.[17][23] The U8 sold 1,593 units in December 2023 and 1,652 in January 2024, staying above 1,500 for two consecutive months.[7] Volume then cooled: full-year 2025 sales were 4,785 units, while China EV DataTracker figures relayed by CarNewsChina put January-September 2026 at 3,251 units, up 38.9% year on year.[15][24]

Owner community and customer operations

Yangwang cultivates a high-net-worth owner community: the Yangwang Business Academy, set up in November 2024, provides a business-exchange platform for entrepreneur owners under the stated philosophy of 'sell one car, make one friend'; per Securities Times, over 80% of U8 owners are entrepreneurs, partners or executives.[2][28]

Overseas business

Yangwang's global rollout is at an early stage. The U8 Arabian Edition is sold in the UAE at AED 659,900 through the exclusive distributor Al-Futtaim Electric Mobility, with 1,180 hp, up to 800 km of range (WLTC) and about 112 km of pure-electric range (WLTC), plus a 6-year/150,000 km vehicle warranty and an 8-year unlimited-km battery warranty; it carries 38 smart sensors with ADAS and a 22-speaker Dynaudio system, retains 30-minute emergency floating (about 3 km/h) and on-the-spot turning, and its DiSus-P offers up to 150 mm of lift.[26] BYD Executive Vice President Stella Li announced in November 2025 that the brand would begin its global rollout in the Middle East (UAE) in early 2026, followed by Europe and the Americas.[18][27]

Founders and leadership

Brand ownership and governance

Yangwang is a brand of BYD Company Limited. Its operating entity, Shenzhen Yangwang Automobile Sales Co., Ltd., was established in November 2022 (Securities Times reported on 9 November 2022 that it had recently been set up); its direct shareholder is BYD Auto Industry Co., Ltd., the legal representative is He Zhiqi and the registered capital is RMB 1 million.[29] Yangwang is not separately listed; the actual controller of the parent BYD is Wang Chuanfu, who is also BYD's chairman and president.[21]

Brand founder

The Yangwang brand was created under BYD chairman and president Wang Chuanfu, who drove the U8 onto the stage at the January 2023 launch and serves as president of the Yangwang Business Academy, established in November 2024.[4][2][28]

Current management

The general manager of the Yangwang sales division is Hu Xiaoqing. CarNewsChina refers to her as 'Yangwang sales division general manager' in its October 2026 coverage of the Altior naming, and International Finance News described her as 'Yangwang automobile general manager' at the U8L launch in September 2025.[24][12] At group level, BYD Executive Vice President Stella Li announced Yangwang's global rollout plan in November 2025, and BYD brand and PR general manager Li Yunfei disclosed the U9 Xtreme price in April 2026.[18][19]

Competition

Our judgment: the competitive field. Yangwang's million-yuan-plus models compete with incumbent luxury and ultra-luxury nameplates — the Land Rover Range Rover and Defender, Mercedes-Benz GLS and G-Class, Maybach S-Class, Rolls-Royce Ghost and Porsche supercars — and, domestically, with other Chinese ultra-luxury efforts such as Hongqi's Golden Sunflower and Huawei/JAC's Maextro S800. This is an analytical selection of the competitive set, not a sales ranking.[17][24]

Our judgment: strengths. Yangwang is the first Chinese brand to sell new-energy vehicles into the million-yuan segment at meaningful scale: the U8 ranked fourth among million-yuan SUVs in 2024, and cumulative sales passed 10,000 in April 2025.[17][23] It is differentiated by BYD's vertically integrated e4, DiSus and Blade Battery stack, and the U9 Xtreme's 496.22 km/h top speed and 6:59.157 Nurburgring lap place it in the global top-tier supercar discussion.[31] Yangwang also cultivates a high-net-worth owner community — over 80% of U8 owners are entrepreneurs, partners or executives, per Securities Times — through its Business Academy and the limited-run U9 Xtreme, reinforcing scarcity.[2][28]

Our judgment: price and segment competition. In million-yuan SUVs, the U8/U8L face the Range Rover and Defender, Mercedes-Benz GLS and BMW X7, while domestic rivals such as Dongfeng's M-Hero 917 also target the luxury off-road market.[17] In the ultra-luxury executive sedan segment, the upcoming Dingyao is positioned against the Mercedes-Maybach S-Class and Rolls-Royce Ghost; media report a 'ding'-style front end, rear-hinged doors and a body length of 5,500 mm.[24][25]

Our judgment: weaknesses and uncertainties. Volume is small and volatile — 4,785 units in 2025 — and the U8 has cooled; the brand has yet to build a second U8-scale hit. Brand equity, channels and after-sales need time to accumulate, and the overseas rollout is just beginning. In the ultra-luxury segment, where buyers weight brand history and social recognition, Yangwang will struggle to displace incumbents on parameters and features alone in the near term.[15][17][18]

Outlook

Company plans. BYD has said Yangwang will begin its global rollout in the Middle East (UAE) in early 2026, then Europe and the Americas.[18][27] On product cadence, the 2026 U7/U8/U8L launched in March 2026, the U8L Dingcang four-seat edition followed in August 2026, and the first ultra-luxury executive sedan, Dingyao (YANGWANG ALTIOR), will make its world debut in Dubai in early November 2026.[14][30][24][25] These are disclosed plans and targets, not completed results.

Our judgment. Yangwang is best understood as a technology flagship that sets BYD's high-end credentials rather than a volume business; its near-term significance lies in whether the 2026 refreshed range can stabilise domestic sales and whether the UAE/Middle East launch, the U9 Xtreme and the Dingyao can establish the brand with ultra-luxury buyers. The UAE U8 is priced at AED 659,900 on the official site — above the base Range Rover's roughly AED 510,000 in that market, according to DrivenArabia — so whether brand value can support that premium is the core question for globalisation.[26][18] This is our analysis, not a forecast of financial results.[15]

Risks

  • Small and volatile volume. Full-year 2025 sales were 4,785 units, a year-on-year decline as the U8 cooled; the brand has yet to build a second U8-scale hit, though China EV DataTracker figures relayed by CarNewsChina put January-September 2026 at 3,251 units, up 38.9% year on year.[15][24]
  • No separate financial disclosure. Yangwang publishes no brand-level revenue or profit, so standalone economics cannot be assessed from public filings.[21]
  • Segment headwinds. China's million-yuan luxury market is under pressure: first-quarter 2025 sales of new cars priced above RMB 1 million were about 21,000 units, down 53% year on year, per data cited by Caijing.[17]
  • Overseas execution. The global rollout is at an early stage; the UAE is the first market and Europe and the Americas follow, leaving Yangwang exposed to import duties, local homologation and unfamiliar luxury consumers.[18]
  • Technology dependence and cadence. The range depends on BYD's platforms, and the pace of second-generation Blade Battery and flash-charging rollout will shape competitiveness.[14]
  • Pricing and generation-change risk. The 2026 U8's entry price fell from RMB 1.098 million to RMB 1.008 million, which could affect the brand's perceived premium.[5][14]

概览

仰望（Yangwang）是比亚迪股份有限公司旗下的超豪华新能源汽车品牌，位于比亚迪品牌矩阵最顶端，主攻百万元级及以上市场。2023 年 1 月 5 日，比亚迪在深圳举行「敢越星河」仰望品牌暨技术发布会，正式发布仰望品牌与易四方四电机平台，并同步亮相首批两款车型——百万级新能源硬派越野 U8 与百万级纯电性能超跑 U9，董事长兼总裁王传福亲自驾驶 U8 入场。[4][6] 品牌口号为「超越极致，前所未有」，产品以「仰望架构」六大核心技术模块为底座，涵盖动力系统、底盘系统、电池技术、超级车身技术、智能驾驶与智能座舱，其代表技术为易四方、云辇与刀片电池。[1][3]

截至研究截止日（2026-10-29），仰望已形成 U7（旗舰轿车）、U8（硬派越野 SUV）、U8L（行政豪华 SUV）、U9（纯电超跑）与限量高定超跑 U9 Xtreme 的产品矩阵，2026 款全系升级第二代刀片电池与闪充；首款超豪华行政轿车于 2026-10-27 定名「鼎曜」（YANGWANG ALTIOR），预计 2026 年 11 月初在迪拜全球首发。[1][14][24] 在比亚迪品牌体系中，仰望位于王朝网与海洋网（主流）、腾势（豪华）、方程豹（个性越野）之上，是集团价格带最高的品牌。[6]

销量方面，仰望 2023 年 11–12 月累计销量 2,001 辆，2024 年约 7,400 辆（其中 U8 约 7,254 辆），2025 年 4,785 辆（U8 系列 2,728 辆、U7 1,953 辆、U9 104 辆，分车型为博主「小迪快报」整理）；2025 年 4 月 21 日成为首个累计销量破万的国产百万级汽车品牌。[7][12][15][17][23] 仰望未单独上市、不披露独立财务数据，母公司比亚迪财务数据见比亚迪深度研究。[21][22]

现状

产品谱系与定位

截至 2026-10-29，仰望车型包括：硬派越野 U8（豪华版 2023-09-20 上市 109.8 万元，越野玩家版 2024-04-25 上市，2026 款 100.8 万元起）；全尺寸行政豪华 SUV U8L（鼎世版 2025-09-12 上市 128 万元起，2026 款鼎世版 130 万元起，鼎藏版四座 2026-08-05 上市 145.8 万元）；旗舰轿车 U7（2025-03-27 上市，五座 62.8 万/四座 70.8 万起，2026 款 65.8 万起）；纯电超跑 U9（2024-02-25 上市 168 万元）；以及全球限量 30 台的高定超跑 U9 Xtreme（单台超 2000 万元）。[5][7][11][12][14][30][19]

核心技术

仰望架构整合六大核心技术模块：动力系统、底盘系统、电池技术、超级车身技术、智能驾驶、智能座舱。[3] 易四方是四电机独立驱动平台，可毫秒级独立控制四轮扭矩，实现原地掉头、爆胎稳行、应急浮水等功能；云辇智能车身控制系统分云辇-P（U8/U8L）、云辇-X（U9）、云辇-Z（U7，全球首款量产电悬架）。[5][9][11] 其中 U7 配备双向 20 度后轮转向与易四方敏捷转向，转弯半径 4.85 米，可实现平行横移泊车。[28] 易四方技术的雏形可追溯至比亚迪约 20 年前发布的 ET 纯电动概念车，后以易四方平台形式量产上车。[6] 2026 年起全系升级第二代刀片电池与闪充，官方称「5 分钟充好、9 分钟充饱」。[14] 官方另推出闪充畅行礼遇：2026 年 5 月 11 日前支付 2026 款定金的非营运车主，交付后一年内在比亚迪闪充桩免费充电。[14] 2026 款 U8 综合续航提升 20.5% 至 1205 公里、充电倍率达 9C，并搭载 3nm 5G 车机芯片与天神之眼 5.0；2026 款 U8L 新增全场景隐私安全、NOA 爆胎稳行稳停与应急浮水自动开启；2026 款 U7 首次搭载云辇-A+ 智能空气车身控制系统。[14] 具体到车型，U8 豪华版为插电混动，拥有 1200 匹马力、880kW 最大功率、8700N·m 轮端扭矩，零百 3.6 秒，CLTC 综合续航约 1000 公里，150mm 悬架调节行程并具备 30 分钟应急浮水能力；U9 搭载四个电机、整车超 1300 匹马力，零百 2.36 秒、极速 309.19 km/h。[5][8]

工厂与产能

仰望未单独披露工厂、产能与员工数，其车型在比亚迪体系内工厂生产；比亚迪年报亦未单列仰望的财务分部。[21] 因此无法从公开文件评估品牌独立经营状况。[22]

国内市场地位

仰望是首个把国产新能源车以一定规模卖进百万级市场的品牌：U8 2024 年交付约 7,254 辆、位列百万级 SUV 销量第四，2025 年 4 月累计破万。[17][23] U8 在 2023 年 12 月与 2024 年 1 月分别销售 1,593 辆、1,652 辆，连续两个月突破 1,500 辆。[7] 随后销量回落：2025 年全年 4,785 辆；据 China EV DataTracker 数据（CarNewsChina 转引），2026 年 1–9 月交付 3,251 辆，同比增长 38.9%。[15][24]

用户与社群经营

仰望注重经营高净值用户社群：2024 年 11 月成立的「仰望商业研究院」为企业家车主提供商业交流平台，官方提出「卖一台车，交一个朋友」的经营理念；据证券时报报道，U8 超过 80% 的车主为企业家、合伙人或高管。[2][28]

海外业务

仰望的全球化刚起步。U8 Arabian Edition 在阿联酋由独家经销商 Al-Futtaim Electric Mobility 销售，官网标价 659,900 迪拉姆，拥有 1,180 匹马力、最高 800 公里续航（WLTC）、纯电约 112 公里（WLTC），并提供 6 年/15 万公里整车质保与 8 年不限里程电池质保；该车配备 38 个传感器与 ADAS 及 22 扬声器 Dynaudio 音响，保留 30 分钟应急浮水（约 3km/h）与原地掉头能力，云辇-P 可提供最高 150mm 升降。[26] 比亚迪执行副总裁李柯于 2025 年 11 月宣布，仰望将于 2026 年初从中东（阿联酋）开始全球布局，随后进入欧洲与美洲。[18][27]

创始人与管理层

品牌归属与治理

仰望为比亚迪股份有限公司旗下品牌。运营主体深圳仰望汽车销售有限公司于 2022 年 11 月成立（证券时报 2022-11-09 报道「近日已成立」），直接股东为比亚迪汽车工业有限公司，法定代表人何志奇，注册资本 100 万元。[29] 仰望未单独上市；母公司比亚迪实际控制人为王传福，其同时担任比亚迪董事长兼总裁。[21]

品牌创立者

仰望品牌由比亚迪股份有限公司董事长兼总裁王传福主导创立。在 2023 年 1 月的发布会上，王传福亲自驾驶仰望 U8 入场，并在 2024 年 11 月成立的仰望商业研究院担任院长。[4][2][28]

现任管理层

现任仰望销售事业部总经理为胡晓庆。CarNewsChina 在 2026 年 10 月报道鼎曜命名时称其为「仰望销售事业部总经理」，国际金融报在 2025 年 9 月 U8L 上市时称其为「仰望汽车总经理」。[24][12] 集团层面，比亚迪执行副总裁李柯于 2025 年 11 月宣布仰望全球布局计划，比亚迪集团品牌及公关处总经理李云飞于 2026 年 4 月披露 U9 Xtreme 售价。[18][19]

竞争格局

我们的判断：竞争范围。仰望的百万级以上车型主要面对路虎揽胜/卫士、奔驰 GLS/G 级、迈巴赫 S 级、劳斯莱斯古思特、保时捷等传统豪华与超豪华品牌，以及国内的红旗金葵花、尊界 S800 等。此为分析选定的竞争范围，不是销量排名。[17][24]

我们的判断：优势。仰望是首个把国产新能源车以一定规模卖进百万级市场的品牌：U8 2024 年位列百万级 SUV 销量第四，2025 年 4 月累计破万。[17][23] 其依托比亚迪垂直整合的易四方、云辇、刀片电池建立技术差异化，U9 Xtreme 更以 496.22 km/h 极速与 6:59.157 纽北圈速进入全球顶级超跑讨论。[31] 仰望还通过仰望商业研究院与 U9 Xtreme 限量发售经营高净值用户圈层——据证券时报报道，U8 超过 80% 的车主为企业家、合伙人或高管——强化品牌稀缺性。[2][28]

我们的判断：价格与细分竞争。在百万级 SUV 领域，U8/U8L 主要面对路虎揽胜/卫士、奔驰 GLS、宝马 X7 等在位车型，国产方面东风猛士 917 等也进入豪华越野市场。[17] 在超豪华行政轿车领域，仰望即将推出的鼎曜对标迈巴赫 S 级、劳斯莱斯古思特，媒体报道其采用「鼎」式前脸、对开式车门，车长 5,500mm。[24][25]

我们的判断：短板与不确定性。销量规模小且波动——2025 年全年 4,785 辆，U8 热度降温，品牌尚未打造出第二款 U8 级产品；品牌溢价、渠道与售后仍需时间积累，海外布局刚起步。在超豪华市场，用户对品牌历史与社交认同的看重，也意味着仰望难以仅靠参数与配置在短期内替代传统豪华品牌。[15][17][18]

未来展望

公司公开规划。比亚迪表示，仰望将于 2026 年初从中东（阿联酋）开始全球布局，随后进入欧洲与美洲。[18][27] 产品节奏上，2026 款 U7/U8/U8L 已于 2026 年 3 月上市，U8L 鼎藏版四座于 2026 年 8 月上市，首款超豪华行政轿车鼎曜（YANGWANG ALTIOR）将于 2026 年 11 月初在迪拜全球首发。[14][30][24][25] 以上为披露的计划与目标，不是已实现的业绩。

我们的判断。仰望更适合被理解为树立比亚迪高端技术形象的技术旗舰，而非走量业务；其短期看点在于 2026 款能否稳住国内销量，以及 UAE/中东上市、U9 Xtreme 与鼎曜能否在超豪华用户中建立品牌认知。U8 在阿联酋官网标价 659,900 迪拉姆，据 DrivenArabia 报道高于当地路虎揽胜基础版约 51 万迪拉姆的起价，能否以品牌价值支撑这一溢价，是其全球化的核心命题。[26][18] 以上为分析，不构成对财务结果的预测。[15]

风险

  • 销量规模小且波动。2025 年全年 4,785 辆、同比回落（U8 降温），尚未打造出第二款 U8 级产品；不过据 China EV DataTracker 数据（CarNewsChina 转引），2026 年 1–9 月交付 3,251 辆，同比增长 38.9%。[15][24]
  • 无独立财务披露。品牌未披露营收与利润，无法从公开文件评估品牌独立盈利。[21]
  • 细分市场承压。国内百万级豪车市场面临压力：据《财经》引用的数据，2025 年一季度成交价超 100 万元的新车销量约 2.1 万辆，同比下滑 53%。[17]
  • 海外执行。全球化刚起步，阿联酋为首站，欧洲、美洲在后，面临进口关税、本地化认证与陌生消费群体等风险。[18]
  • 技术依赖与节奏。全系依赖比亚迪平台，第二代刀片电池与闪充技术的量产节奏将影响竞争力。[14]
  • 价格与换代风险。2026 款 U8 起售价由 109.8 万元下调至 100.8 万元，换代与价格调整可能影响品牌溢价感知。[5][14]
