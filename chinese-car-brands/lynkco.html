Overview

Lynk & Co (领克) is a premium automotive brand incubated within the Geely–Volvo Cars partnership and owned by Geely Automobile Holdings (0175.HK). Unveiled in Berlin on 20 October 2016 with a 'born global, open and connected' positioning aimed at urban, younger users, it launched its first model, the 01 compact SUV, in China in November 2017 and now spans combustion, EM-P plug-in hybrid and battery-electric models. [39][2][4]

In February 2025 Zeekr completed the acquisition of 50% of Lynk & Co and subscribed about RMB367 million of new capital to reach 51%, forming Zeekr Group; after Zeekr's privatisation in December 2025, Lynk & Co became a wholly-owned subsidiary of Geely Automobile Holdings. In 2025 the brand sold 350,495 vehicles, up 22.8% year on year (the annual report rounds this to 23%), with new-energy vehicles reaching 65% of sales and cumulative sales since 2017 exceeding 1.68 million. Lynk & Co is not separately listed and does not disclose standalone financials. [9][2][1]

The brand shares Geely–Volvo modular architectures (CMA/CMA Evo, SPA/SPA Evo and SEA) and has built a 'trendy, sporty, personal' identity through motorsport and its user community. In September 2026 Geely Auto Group and Volvo Cars signed a final agreement making Volvo Cars the exclusive distributor of Lynk & Co in Europe from January 2027, a step the brand says will shift its European business to a more scalable retail model. [4][8][3]

Current operations

Business and product structure

Lynk & Co sits inside Zeekr Group alongside Zeekr. Under the division of labour stated in February 2025, Zeekr moves upmarket (above RMB 300,000) while Lynk & Co broadens its reach (around RMB 200,000), with small cars focusing on pure electric and mid-to-large cars on hybrid. As of October 2026 the official line-up comprises combustion models (01, 02 Hatchback, 03, 03+, 05+, 06 Relive, 09 MHEV), EM-P plug-in hybrids (07 EM-P, 07GT, 08 EM-P, 09 EM-P, 900, 10 EM-P) and battery-electric models (10, 10+, 20). The earlier Z10 and Z20 are no longer listed in the official battery-electric range, having been succeeded by the 10/10+ and the 20 respectively. [9][31]

Sales reached 285,441 units in 2024, up about 30% year on year, and 350,495 in 2025, up 22.8%; in 2025 new-energy sales rose 36% to 228,000 units, lifting the NEV share to 65%, and December 2025 set a monthly record of 33,751 units. In the first half of 2026 Lynk & Co sold about 144,000 units, down 6% year on year, a slower pace than Zeekr's roughly 178,000 units (+97%). According to the brand, its 2025 weighted-average transaction price exceeded RMB 200,000, with the EM-P family averaging RMB 254,200. [7][1][2][13][40]

Technology and platforms

Lynk & Co is built on Geely–Volvo modular architectures: CMA (01, 02, 03, 05) and CMA Evo (07, 08, 10 EM-P), Volvo's SPA (09) and SPA Evo (900), and Geely's SEA electric architecture (Z10, Z20, 10/10+, 20). Its electrification stack centres on the EM-P plug-in hybrid system, the cockpit on the Meizu-developed LYNK Flyme Auto, and assisted driving on the group's Qianli Haohan (千里浩瀚) system. The 2026 Lynk & Co 20 makes an 800V platform, 6C fast charging and a lidar standard across the range; the Lynk & Co 10 uses a 900V architecture, with the 95 kWh variant offering up to 816 km CLTC and the 77 kWh variant 701 km. [4][17][25][32]

Plants and channels

Lynk & Co models are built across Geely's plant network. The 01 was produced at the Taizhou Luqiao plant alongside Volvo models, while later models are made at Zhangjiakou, Yuyao and Chengdu. The Zhangjiakou plant, a Geely–Volvo flagship base, can produce up to 300,000 units a year and mixes combustion and battery-electric production (including the 03 and Volvo's EX30). The Chengdu plant was built specifically for the 06 to Volvo standards, with a planned annual capacity of 300,000 units. At end-2025 the brand served customers through 583 channel outlets in China. [4][33][20][2]

Overseas business

Overseas, the brand entered Europe in 2020 with a membership/subscription model and later stopped taking new subscriptions as it shifted to a retail model. In 2025 it exported more than 30,000 vehicles and by end-2025 had 195 overseas stores covering 50 countries/regions (112 in Europe across 25 markets; 83 outside Europe across 25 markets). By mid-2026 overseas sales had already exceeded the whole of 2025, and by July 2026 the brand covered 55 overseas countries/regions with 249 stores; according to Xinhua Finance it had more than 140 sales outlets in Europe as of August 2026. The 08 EM-P has been launched in 31 markets and, according to the brand, was Europe's first premium plug-in hybrid with a WLTP pure-electric range over 200 km. [22][2][3][30][1]

When the joint venture was formed in 2017, An Conghui, then president and CEO of Geely Auto Group, became its chairman, with Volvo Cars president and CEO Håkan Samuelsson, Li Donghui and Feng Qingfeng among the directors. At formation the Lynk & Co joint venture was held by Geely Auto (50%), Volvo Cars (30%) and Geely Holding (20%). Competition

Lynk & Co competes in China's mid-to-premium segment, spanning compact sedans and SUVs through to full-size SUVs. Its stated advantages are the Geely–Volvo architectures (CMA/SPA/SEA), a vertically integrated supply chain, and a distinctive design and motorsport identity built on successive WTCR/TCR titles. [25][8]

Our judgment: within its segments, the 03/07/08/09 compete against mainstream joint-venture models such as the Honda Accord and Toyota Camry and domestic new-energy rivals; the Z10, 10/10+ and 20 electric models face competitors such as the Xiaomi SU7, XPeng MONA and BYD Yuan Plus; and the 900 and 09 move into the large-SUV space contested by the Li Auto L-series and BYD Denza. Lynk & Co has genuine premium pricing power for a domestic brand — a 2025 weighted-average transaction price above RMB 200,000 and an EM-P average of RMB 254,200 — but its scale is moderate and, unlike Zeekr, it does not report standalone financials, so profitability cannot be assessed at brand level. First-half 2026 sales of about 144,000 units were down 6% year on year, a slower pace than Zeekr's +97%, suggesting pressure from the shift of its combustion base toward hybrids and from domestic price competition. Its motorsport titles and 'trendy, sporty' positioning give it differentiation among domestic marques, though that has not yet fully converted into volume growth. This is our assessment, based on the disclosed sales and pricing data. [1][13]

Outlook

Company plans. Within Zeekr Group, Lynk & Co is positioned to broaden its reach while Zeekr moves upmarket, with small cars focusing on pure electric and mid-to-large cars on hybrid. At the August 2026 interim results, the group said Lynk & Co would use the 07GT and the new Z20 (subsequently launched as the 20) to open up higher-end segments in the second half — a plan stated at that point, not a result. Under the 10 September 2026 agreement, Volvo Cars becomes the exclusive European distributor from January 2027, a shift to a more scalable retail model with no change to the ownership structure. At the outset of the 2025 integration Zeekr Group set a 2025 target of 390,000 units for Lynk & Co within a group target of 710,000; actual 2025 sales were about 350,000. These are stated plans and targets, not achieved results. [36][3][9]

Our analysis. Lynk & Co's 22.8% growth in 2025 was driven by new EM-P models (900 and 10 EM-P) rather than its legacy combustion base, and first-half 2026 volume slipped 6% year on year, showing that the combustion base is shrinking faster than the hybrid/BEV ramp is filling the gap. Sustained growth therefore depends on the continued ramp of the 07GT, 10/10+ and 20, on stabilising the combustion base through refreshes such as the third-generation 03, and on whether the Volvo European retail network converts into higher volume from 2027. The main alternatives are a prolonged domestic price war, EU countervailing duties on China-built battery-electric vehicles, the pace of Zeekr-integration synergies, and the concentration of growth in a few high-price models. This is our judgment and does not predict any share price. [13][25][12]

Risks

  • Slowing momentum. Lynk & Co's first-half 2026 sales of about 144,000 units were down 6% year on year, a slower pace than Zeekr, indicating pressure during the brand's transition. [13]
  • Price competition. The mid-to-premium segment is contested by joint-venture brands cutting prices and by domestic new-energy rivals, which can pressure transaction prices and margins. [16]
  • Tariff exposure. Lynk & Co exports battery-electric models; China-built BEVs face additional EU countervailing duties, raising the cost of its European exports. [12]
  • European-model transition. The subscription service stopped taking new customers in 2025; the shift to Volvo-distributed retail depends on the Volvo network for scale and carries execution risk. [2]
  • Integration execution. Folding Lynk & Co and Zeekr into one group requires product, R&D and channel coordination, and the group has said each brand's product count was cut by about 20%, which carries execution risk. [9]
  • Concentration on high-price models. 2025 growth was driven mainly by a few high-price EM-P models (900, 10 EM-P), raising dependence on individual nameplates. [1]
  • No standalone disclosure. Lynk & Co does not report standalone financials, so external observers cannot directly assess brand-level profitability. [2]

概览

领克（Lynk & Co）是吉利汽车控股有限公司（0175.HK）旗下、由吉利与沃尔沃汽车合作孵化的高端汽车品牌，2016 年 10 月 20 日在德国柏林发布，定位「生而全球、开放互联」，面向都市年轻用户。首款车型紧凑型 SUV 领克 01 于 2017 年 11 月在中国上市，此后逐步形成燃油、EM-P 插电混动与纯电覆盖的产品矩阵。 [39][2][4]

2025 年 2 月，极氪完成对领克 50% 股权的收购并认购约 3.67 亿元新增资本、最终持股 51%，组建极氪科技集团；2025 年 12 月极氪私有化完成后，领克成为吉利汽车控股的全资附属公司。2025 年领克销量 350,495 辆、同比增长 22.8%（年报四舍五入为 23%），新能源汽车占比升至 65%，自 2017 年以来累计销量超过 168 万辆；领克未单独上市，亦不单独披露财务数据。 [9][2][1]

品牌与沃尔沃共享 CMA/CMA Evo、SPA/SPA Evo 与 SEA 等模块化架构与动力、零部件体系，并以赛车运动与用户社群构建「潮流、运动、个性」标签。2026 年 9 月，吉利汽车集团与沃尔沃汽车签署最终协议，自 2027 年 1 月起沃尔沃将成为领克在欧洲的独家经销商，品牌称将借此向更具扩展性和规模化的欧洲零售模式转型。 [4][8][3]

现状

业务与产品结构

领克与极氪同属极氪科技集团。按集团 2025 年 2 月的表述，极氪「向上」主攻 30 万元以上、领克「向宽」覆盖 20 万元上下，小型车聚焦纯电、中大型车聚焦混动。截至 2026 年 10 月，官网在售序列为：燃油（01、02 Hatchback、03、03+、05+、06 Relive、09 MHEV）、EM-P 插电混动（07 EM-P、07GT、08 EM-P、09 EM-P、900、10 EM-P）与纯电（10、10+、20）。此前的 Z10、Z20 已不再列于官网纯电序列，分别由 10/10+ 与 20 接替。 [9][31]

销量方面，2024 年品牌全球销量 285,441 辆、同比增长约 30%；2025 年增至 350,495 辆、同比增长 22.8%，其中新能源汽车销量同比增长 36% 至 22.8 万辆、占比 65%，2025 年 12 月单月销量 33,751 台、创历史新高。2026 年上半年领克销量约 14.4 万辆、同比下降 6%，弱于集团内极氪约 17.8 万辆（同比增长 97%）。据品牌披露，2025 年其加权平均成交价突破 20 万元，EM-P 家族加权平均成交价达 25.42 万元。 [7][1][2][13][40]

技术与平台

领克基于吉利-沃尔沃模块化架构造车：CMA（01、02、03、05）与 CMA Evo（07、08、10 EM-P）、沃尔沃 SPA（09）与 SPA Evo（900）、吉利 SEA 浩瀚纯电架构（Z10、Z20、10/10+、20）。电动化以 EM-P 插电混动系统为核心，座舱采用与魅族合作开发的 LYNK Flyme Auto 系统，辅助驾驶接入集团千里浩瀚体系。2026 年上市的领克 20 将 800V 高压平台、6C 快充与激光雷达列为全系标配；领克 10 采用 900V 架构，95 度电版本 CLTC 最高续航 816 公里、77 度电版本 701 公里。 [4][17][25][32]

工厂与渠道

领克车型在吉利体系多座工厂生产：领克 01 早期在台州路桥工厂与沃尔沃车型共线生产，后续车型在张家口、余姚、成都等基地制造。张家口工厂是吉利-沃尔沃联合打造的标杆基地，年产能可达 30 万辆，实现燃油与纯电混线生产（含领克 03 与沃尔沃 EX30）。成都工厂系为领克 06 专门建设、按沃尔沃标准、规划年产能 30 万辆。截至 2025 年末，品牌在中国通过 583 家渠道网点服务客户。 [4][33][20][2]

海外业务

海外方面，领克 2020 年以会员制/订阅制进入欧洲，其后逐步转向零售模式并停止接受新订阅。2025 年出口超 3 万辆，截至 2025 年末拥有 195 家海外门店、覆盖 50 个国家/地区（欧洲 112 家/25 个市场，欧洲以外 83 家/25 个市场）。2026 年上半年海外销量已超越 2025 全年，截至 2026 年 7 月覆盖海外 55 个国家/地区、海外门店达 249 家；据新华财经报道，截至 2026 年 8 月领克在欧洲拥有超 140 个销售网点。据品牌披露，领克 08 EM-P 已进入 31 个市场，是欧洲首款 WLTP 工况纯电续航突破 200 公里的高端插电混动车型。 [22][2][3][30][1]

创始人与管理层

品牌渊源

领克诞生于吉利控股创始人李书福推动的吉利-沃尔沃合作框架。2017 年合资公司成立时，吉利汽车集团总裁兼 CEO 安聪慧出任领克合资公司董事长，董事会成员还包括沃尔沃汽车总裁兼 CEO 汉肯·塞缪尔森、李东辉、冯擎峰等。 [21]

股权与治理

成立之初，领克合资公司由吉利汽车持股 50%、沃尔沃汽车持股 30%、吉利控股持股 20%。2025 年整合并完成极氪私有化后，领克于 2025 年 12 月成为吉利汽车控股（0175.HK）的全资附属公司，吉利汽车控股由吉利控股控制；品牌层面没有在此持股链条之外的独立「实际控制人」认定。 [21][2][9]

竞争格局

领克在中国中高端市场参与竞争，产品覆盖紧凑型轿车、SUV 直至全尺寸 SUV。其公开优势在于吉利-沃尔沃架构（CMA/SPA/SEA）、垂直整合的供应链，以及依托连续多项 WTCR/TCR 冠军建立的鲜明设计与赛车运动标签。 [25][8]

我们的判断：在其细分市场中，03/07/08/09 面对本田雅阁、丰田凯美瑞等主流合资车型及本土新能源竞品，Z10、10/10+、20 等纯电车型面对小米 SU7、小鹏 MONA、比亚迪元 PLUS 等竞品，900 与 09 则进入理想 L 系列、比亚迪腾势等所处的大型 SUV 市场。作为本土品牌，领克具备真实的高端定价能力——2025 年加权平均成交价突破 20 万元、EM-P 家族达 25.42 万元；但其规模处于中等水平，且不单独披露财务数据，品牌层面的盈利能力无法单独评估。2026 年上半年领克销量约 14.4 万辆、同比下滑 6%，弱于集团内极氪（+97%），显示其面临燃油基盘向混动切换与国内价格竞争的双重压力。另一方面，凭借 WTCR/TCR 多项世界冠军与「潮流、运动」标签，领克在本土品牌中拥有一定品牌溢价与差异化基础，但该溢价尚未完全转化为规模增长。以上为我们的判断，依据为前述披露的销量与价格数据。 [1][13]

未来展望

公司公开规划。在极氪科技集团内，领克定位「向宽」拓展，小型车聚焦纯电、中大型车聚焦混动。2026 年 8 月中报时点，集团表示下半年领克将以 07GT、全新 Z20（后以「领克 20」名称上市）等新品开拓高端细分市场——此为当时计划表述，并非已实现结果。按 2026 年 9 月 10 日签署的协议，自 2027 年 1 月起沃尔沃成为领克在欧洲的独家经销商，领克称将借此向更具扩展性和规模化的零售模式转型，且不涉及股权变更。在 2025 年整合之初，极氪科技集团曾将领克 2025 年销量目标定为 39 万辆（集团 71 万辆），当年领克实际销量约 35 万辆。以上为公司目标与计划表述，并非已实现结果。 [36][3][9]

我们的分析。领克 2025 年 22.8% 的增长主要由 900、10 EM-P 等 EM-P 新品拉动，而非既有燃油基盘；2026 年上半年销量同比下滑 6%，显示燃油基盘收缩的速度快于混动/纯电新品放量填补的速度。持续增长因此取决于 07GT、10/10+、领克 20 等新品继续放量、以第三代 03 等改款稳住燃油基盘，以及 2027 年起沃尔沃欧洲零售网络能否转化为更高销量。主要变数在于国内价格战延续、欧盟对中国产纯电动车的反补贴关税、极氪整合协同兑现进度，以及增长集中于少数高价车型带来的波动风险。以上为我们的判断，不构成任何股价预测。 [13][25][12]

风险

  • 增长动能放缓。领克 2026 年上半年销量约 14.4 万辆、同比下滑 6%，弱于集团内极氪等增长品牌，品牌切换期面临压力。 [13]
  • 价格竞争。中高端市场面临合资品牌降价与本土新能源品牌的双重挤压，可能对成交价与利润形成压力。 [16]
  • 关税风险。领克出口纯电车型，中国产纯电动车在欧盟面临额外反补贴关税，推高其欧洲出口成本。 [12]
  • 欧洲模式切换。订阅制已于 2025 年停止接受新订阅，转向沃尔沃经销零售后，规模化依赖沃尔沃网络，存在执行与协同风险。 [2]
  • 整合执行风险。领克与极氪并入同一集团需在产品、研发、渠道协同，集团称两个品牌产品数量各削减约 20%，存在执行风险。 [9]
  • 高端车型集中度。2025 年增长主要由 900、10 EM-P 等少数高价车型拉动，品牌对个别车型的依赖度上升。 [1]
  • 无独立披露。领克不单独披露财务数据，外部观察者难以直接评估品牌层面的盈利或亏损。 [2]
