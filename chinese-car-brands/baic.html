BAIC 北汽

Beijing state-owned auto group with Beijing Auto, Arcfox and Foton, plus Beijing Benz and Beijing Hyundai joint ventures held through BAIC Motor. BAIC Group and Hong Kong-listed BAIC Motor (1958) are distinct entities.北京国有整车集团，覆盖北京汽车、极狐与福田，并通过北京汽车股份参与北京奔驰、北京现代等合资企业。北汽集团与香港上市的北京汽车股份（1958）是不同实体。

As an established manufacturing group, it ships complete knock-down and fully-built vehicles worldwide, with mature homologation experience across left- and right-hand-drive markets. For dealers, that typically means broader spare-parts availability and factory-backed export documentation.作为成熟的制造集团，其整车与 KD 件出口遍及全球，在左舵/右舵市场均有成熟的认证经验。对经销商而言，这通常意味着更完善的配件供应与厂家出口文件支持。
