Overview

Beijing Automotive Group Co., Ltd. (北汽集团 / BAIC Group) is a wholly state-owned automotive group supervised by the Beijing Municipal SASAC. It traces its origins to the Jinggangshan sedan produced by Beijing Automobile Works in 1958 and is headquartered in Shunyi District, Beijing. The group owns the own brands Beijing (BAIC), Arcfox (via BAIC Bluepark), Stelato (with Huawei) and Foton, together with the joint ventures Beijing Benz, Beijing Hyundai, Fujian Benz and Foton Daimler [5] [38]. Its businesses span whole-vehicle and component R&D and manufacturing, automotive services trade, and finance and investment, and it functions as the strategic-planning and capital-operation centre of Beijing's automotive industry [1].

BAIC Motor holds 51% of Beijing Benz and consolidates it [12]. In 2025 the group reported consolidated operating revenue of RMB281.49 billion and total net profit of RMB4.28 billion, of which only RMB74 million was attributable to owners of the parent, with the remainder accruing to minority shareholders such as Mercedes-Benz Group [2]. It sold 1.752 million vehicles and exported 308,000 in 2025, and is home to China's largest commercial-vehicle maker (Foton) as well as a major luxury-car producer (Beijing Benz) [5].

The group is controlled by the Beijing Municipal SASAC through its sole shareholder Beijing State-owned Capital Operation & Management Co., Ltd., and operates through three listed entities: BAIC Motor (01958.HK), BAIC Bluepark (600733.SH) and Foton Motor (600166.SH). Its current chairman is Zhang Jianyong (since March 2024) and general manager Chang Rui (since January 2026) [1] [7].

Current operations

Business structure

By segment in 2025, whole-vehicle manufacturing contributed RMB219.25 billion of operating revenue (gross margin 15.39%), components RMB37.89 billion (5.79%) and other businesses RMB24.35 billion (17.22%), for a total of RMB281.49 billion with a 14.26% comprehensive gross margin [1]. The group is organised around three listed entities: BAIC Motor (01958.HK), its passenger-car resource platform, sold 868,000 vehicles and reported revenue of RMB164.05 billion and attributable net profit of RMB123 million in 2025 [12]; BAIC Bluepark (600733.SH), the new-energy-vehicle maker, sold 209,600 vehicles with revenue of RMB27.94 billion and an attributable net loss of RMB4.563 billion [8]; and Foton Motor (600166.SH), the commercial-vehicle maker, sold 650,100 vehicles with revenue of RMB61.25 billion and attributable net profit of RMB1.364 billion [14].

In 2025 the group sold 1.752 million vehicles (+5.6% year on year), of which 1.07 million were own-brand vehicles, 61% of the total; new-energy-vehicle sales surpassed 390,000 (+95%). Among own-brand passenger vehicles, Arcfox sold 163,000 (+101%), Beijing off-road over 200,000 (+38%), and Stelato passed 10,000 monthly sales; Foton commercial-vehicle sales exceeded 650,000 [5]. In 2023, by comparison, BAIC sold 1.708 million vehicles (+17.6%), including 190,000 own-brand passenger vehicles (+69.1%) and 190,000 exports (+73.5%), with Foton already ranking No.1 in commercial vehicles [6].

Latest results (as of 29 October 2026)

For the first half of 2026, the three listed entities reported: BAIC Motor revenue of RMB57.726 billion (-29.94% year on year) and an attributable net loss of RMB1.594 billion, versus a profit of RMB360 million a year earlier [28]; BAIC Bluepark revenue of RMB11.597 billion (+21.86%) and an attributable net loss of RMB1.938 billion, narrowed from RMB2.308 billion a year earlier [29]; and Foton Motor revenue of RMB33.668 billion (+10.86%) with attributable net profit of RMB905 million (+16.54%) [30].

At group level, first-half 2026 volume was 795,000 vehicles, of which own-brand sales were 535,000 (+14.2%), NEV sales 181,000 (+22.6%) and exports 184,000 (+30.8%) [33]. By brand, Arcfox delivered 80,600 (+65.88%), Stelato 21,000 (+119.58%), and Beijing off-road 84,600 (of which 66,700 'boxy' models, +33.9%); Foton sold more than 360,000 commercial vehicles (+10.1%), including 59,000 NEVs and over 90,000 heavy trucks, with overseas volume of 109,000 (+38.9%) [33]. On the product side, Arcfox is completing its Alpha, Beta and Wendao series (the Beta S3 positioned as a battery-swap sedan and the Beta T1 as an entry pure-electric SUV); Beijing off-road launched the BJ40 EREV long-range and BJ30 Traveler versions, alongside the BIP user co-creation model and two new categories, the Beijing 81 and Beijing City Hunter [33]. Subsequently, the premium off-road SUV Taitan 700 went on sale on August 25, 2026 [37]. In H1 2026, Mercedes-Benz Cars sold 210,245 vehicles in China, down 28.3% year on year; within that total, locally produced vehicles from Beijing Benz accounted for 165,876 units, down 31.1%. The Beijing Benz comparison uses the locally produced-vehicle figure [42].

Core technology and platforms

In electrification the group self-develops a solid-liquid hybrid 'Aurora' battery and a third-generation 'Shenqing' powertrain; in intelligent driving it has laid out an L2-to-L4 roadmap, with L3 running routine pilot operations in Beijing and L4 robotaxis in commercial service. It also operates two proprietary safety/health systems ('Extreme Shield' body safety and 'Extreme Clean' cabin health). As of August 2026 it had filed about 42,000 patents and won the China Auto Industry Science and Technology Progress first prize seven times [34]. It has built battery production bases jointly with Huawei and CATL [34]. Arcfox is developing three product series — Alpha, Beta and Wendao — as reported by Xinhua on 12 July 2026 and confirmed in BAIC Group’s 9 October 2026 release [33] [44]. The facelifted Beta T1 launched on 16 June 2026 with an announced limited-time offer starting at RMB62,800; this is the launch offer for that facelift, not a current price for every T1 version [35].

Plants and overseas footprint

Foton operates 19 R&D centres worldwide and 32 overseas localised plants [14]. Beijing Benz marked its 20th anniversary in 2025 and began producing the all-electric CLA, the first MMA-platform model, moving towards cumulative whole-vehicle and engine output of '6 million + 6 million' [10] [12]; in the first half of 2026 it put the all-electric GLC into production [28]. Group exports exceed 300,000 units a year, reaching more than 130 countries and regions [34]. In H1 2026 Beijing Hyundai launched the new-energy brand IONIQ and its first model IONIQ V [28].

Founders and leadership

Founder

BAIC Group has no single founder in the private-company sense; its historical origin is the Jinggangshan sedan produced by Beijing Automobile Works in 1958 [38], and it is wholly owned by the Beijing Municipal SASAC through Beijing State-owned Capital Operation & Management Co., Ltd. [1].

Current core management

  • Zhang Jianyong — Party secretary and chairman since 27 March 2024, succeeding Jiang Deyi. He previously served as an accountant in the finance department of the China Electric Power Research Institute, finance manager of Beijing Automotive Industry Holding, head of BAIC Group's finance department and deputy CFO, deputy general manager of BAIC Group, and member and deputy director of the Beijing Municipal SASAC [19].
  • Chang Rui — deputy Party secretary, director and general manager since 30 January 2026. Previously deputy general manager of BAIC Group and, before that, Party secretary and chairman of Foton Motor (where he was also general manager) and chairman of Beijing Kawan New Energy Vehicle (卡文) [25].
  • Jiang Deyi — previous chairman, who stepped down in March 2024 at the statutory retirement age [19].

Their current titles as of October 2026 are confirmed by the Beijing municipal government [26]. At the July 2026 semi-annual work meeting, Zhang Jianyong delivered the keynote speech and Chang Rui delivered the operating report [33].

Competition

Our judgment.Among state-owned auto groups, BAIC's main peers are SAIC, GAC, Dongfeng, Changan and FAW; in commercial vehicles Foton competes with Sinotruk and Dongfeng; in luxury cars Beijing Benz competes with BMW Brilliance and FAW-Audi; and its own-brand NEVs compete with BYD, Geely and Changan. In luxury NEV sedans, the Stelato S9 competes with other Huawei Harmony Intelligent Mobility models from AITO (Seres), Luxeed (Chery) and Maextro (JAC) [20]. Our assessment of BAIC's strengths rests on three reported facts: Foton is China's largest commercial-vehicle maker [14]; Beijing Benz gives the group a foothold in the luxury segment [10]; and the Huawei partnership (Stelato) gives it access to advanced assisted-driving technology [20].

Our judgment.Its main weaknesses, based on 2025 results, are an over-reliance on Beijing Benz for profit — Mercedes-Benz Cars’ total China unit sales were 551,932 in 2025, down 19% [43], while Beijing Benz's revenue and after-tax profit fell 23.1% and 38.6%, respectively [10] — and a loss-making own-brand business: BAIC Bluepark has posted attributable losses every year since 2020, totalling RMB34.1 billion over six years [8]. Beijing Hyundai sold 210,000 units in 2025 [21]. At the passenger-car platform, BAIC Motor's attributable net profit fell 87.2% to RMB123 million in 2025, a third straight year of decline [12]. The above is our judgment, based on the disclosed financial data cited.

Outlook

Company targets. BAIC Group's 2024 Sustainability (ESG) Report, released on June 5, 2025, records that the group proposed its 'Three-Year Leap' plan in 2024, targeting 3 million vehicle sales by 2027 and aiming for over 2 million own-brand sales and over 1 million own-brand passenger-car sales [32] [41]. For 2026 it plans total sales of 2.2 million vehicles [5]. Foton's '15th Five-Year' (2026–2030) strategy, approved by its board on September 29, 2026 and disclosed on September 30, targets 1 million vehicle sales by 2030, with 500,000 NEV sales and 400,000 overseas sales [13] [40], and Beijing Hyundai's 'Zhiqi 2030' plan targets 20 new models over four to five years and 500,000 annual sales within five years, with two NEV models due in 2026 [21]. Foton opened 2026 with first-half sales of 360,400 vehicles (+10.1%) and attributable net profit of RMB905 million (+16.54%) [30].

Our judgment. These targets depend on three premises: that Beijing Benz stabilises after its 2025 decline, that own-brand sales keep growing without deepening losses, and that the NEV transition accelerates. The main alternative scenario is that continued price competition and a weak luxury market keep profits thin, which would make the 3-million-unit goal hard to reach while group attributable profit remains near zero. Two further risks weigh on the outlook: the group's own-brand NEV models have not yet reached scale (BAIC Bluepark's vehicle business still had a negative gross margin in 2025 [8]), and export growth is exposed to tariffs and policy changes in target markets (exports were 308,000 units in 2025 [5]). The above is our judgment, based on the disclosed financial data and the targets cited.

Risks

  • Declining Beijing Benz results. Beijing Benz's revenue fell 23.1% and after-tax profit 38.6% in 2025 [10]. In H1 2026, Mercedes-Benz Cars’ locally produced-vehicle sales in China (Beijing Benz) were 165,876 units, down 31.1% year on year [42].
  • Loss-making own-brand NEV business. BAIC Bluepark posted an attributable net loss of RMB4.563 billion in 2025 and has accumulated RMB34.1 billion in losses since 2020 [8].
  • Decline in consolidated gross margin. The group's comprehensive gross margin fell from 17.05% in 2024 to 14.26% in 2025 [1].
  • Profit attributable to the parent. In 2025, group total net profit was RMB4.281 billion, of which RMB74 million was attributable to owners of the parent [2].

概览

北汽集团（北京汽车集团有限公司）是北京市国资委监管的国有独资汽车集团，历史可追溯至 1958 年北京汽车制造厂生产「井冈山」牌轿车，总部位于北京市顺义区。集团拥有自主品牌北京汽车、极狐（北汽蓝谷）、享界（与华为合作）、福田，以及合资企业北京奔驰、北京现代、福建奔驰、福田戴姆勒 [5] [38]。业务覆盖整车及零部件研发制造、汽车服务贸易、金融与投资等，是北京汽车工业的战略规划与资本运营中心 [1]。

北京汽车持有北京奔驰 51% 股权，并将其纳入合并报表 [12]。2025 年集团合并营业收入 2814.92 亿元、净利润 42.81 亿元，其中归属于母公司股东的净利润仅 0.74 亿元，其余利润归属少数股东（如梅赛德斯-奔驰集团）[2]。2025 年整车销量 175.2 万辆、出口 30.8 万辆，拥有中国商用车销量第一的福田汽车和豪华车制造企业北京奔驰 [5]。

集团由北京市国资委通过唯一股东北京国有资本运营管理有限公司全资控股，经北京汽车（01958.HK）、北汽蓝谷（600733.SH）、福田汽车（600166.SH）三家上市主体运营；现任董事长张建勇（2024 年 3 月上任）、总经理常瑞（2026 年 1 月上任）[1] [7]。

历史沿革

  1. 1958

    北京汽车制造厂生产「井冈山」牌轿车，北汽以此作为历史起点。[38]

  2. 1966

    第一代轻型越野车 BJ212 量产、轻型载货车 BJ130 试制成功。[17]

  3. 1983

    1983 年与美国汽车公司（AMC）合资成立北京吉普汽车有限公司，为中国第一家整车制造合资企业；1984 年 1 月 15 日正式开业。[36]

  4. 2002

    与韩国现代汽车合资成立北京现代汽车有限公司，成为中国加入 WTO 后第一家整车制造合资企业。[17]

  5. 2005-08

    北京奔驰汽车有限公司成立（与戴姆勒克莱斯勒合资），北京汽车工业进入豪华车制造领域。[17]

  6. 2009-12

    与瑞典萨博（Saab）达成技术合作，收购部分 Saab 9-3、当时的 9-5 及动力总成技术与工装（并非萨博全部知识产权），作为后续绅宝平台的技术基础。[23]

  7. 2010

    布局新能源汽车产业，构建北汽新能源汽车标准体系。[18]

  8. 2012-11

    绅宝（Senova）品牌正式发布，首款中高级轿车定名为绅宝 D 系列。[24]

  9. 2014-12

    北京汽车股份有限公司（01958.HK）在港交所主板上市，成为集团乘用车资源整合平台。[12]

  10. 2020-08

    北汽集团累计整车产量突破 3000 万辆。[17]

    张建勇接替姜德义出任北汽集团党委书记、董事长。[19]

现状

业务结构

按板块看，2025 年整车制造实现营业收入 2192.47 亿元（毛利率 15.39%）、零部件 378.92 亿元（毛利率 5.79%）、其他业务 243.53 亿元（毛利率 17.22%），合计营业收入 2814.92 亿元，综合毛利率 14.26% [1]。集团围绕三家上市主体展开：乘用车资源平台北京汽车（01958.HK）2025 年整车销量 86.8 万辆、营业收入 1640.47 亿元、归母净利润 1.23 亿元 [12]；新能源汽车主体北汽蓝谷（600733.SH）2025 年销量 20.96 万辆、收入 279.40 亿元、归母净亏损 45.63 亿元 [8]；商用车主体福田汽车（600166.SH）2025 年销量 65.01 万辆、收入 612.47 亿元、归母净利润 13.64 亿元 [14]。

2025 年集团整车销量 175.2 万辆（同比 +5.6%），其中自主品牌销量 107 万辆、占比 61%；新能源汽车销量突破 39 万辆（同比 +95%）。自主乘用车中，极狐销售 16.3 万辆（同比 +101%）、北京越野超 20 万辆（同比 +38%）、享界月销破万；福田商用车年销超 65 万辆 [5]。对比 2023 年，北汽整车销量 170.8 万辆（同比 +17.6%），自主乘用车销量 19 万辆（同比 +69.1%）、出口 19 万辆（同比 +73.5%），福田汽车位居商用车行业第一 [6]。

最新业绩（截至 2026 年 10 月 29 日已公布的最近半年度）

2026 年上半年，三家上市主体业绩如下：北京汽车营业收入 577.26 亿元（同比 -29.94%），归母净亏损 15.94 亿元（上年同期为盈利 3.6 亿元）[28]；北汽蓝谷营业收入 115.97 亿元（同比 +21.86%），归母净亏损 19.38 亿元（上年同期亏损 23.08 亿元，同比减亏）[29]；福田汽车营业收入 336.68 亿元（同比 +10.86%），归母净利润 9.05 亿元（同比 +16.54%）[30]。

集团层面，2026 年上半年累计销量 79.5 万辆，其中自主品牌 53.5 万辆（同比 +14.2%）、新能源汽车 18.1 万辆（同比 +22.6%）、出口 18.4 万辆（同比 +30.8%）[33]。分品牌看，极狐交付 8.06 万辆（同比 +65.88%）、享界 2.1 万辆（同比 +119.58%）、北京越野 8.46 万辆（其中方盒子车型 6.67 万辆、同比 +33.9%）；福田商用车销量超 36 万辆（同比 +10.1%），其中新能源 5.9 万辆、重卡超 9 万辆、海外 10.9 万辆（同比 +38.9%）[33]。产品层面，极狐进一步完善阿尔法、贝塔、问道三大产品系列（贝塔 S3 布局换电家轿、贝塔 T1 切入入门纯电 SUV）；北京越野 BJ40 增程长续航版、BJ30 旅行家高光版上市，并推出 BIP 用户共创模式及北京 81、北京·城市猎人两款新品类 [33]。此后，豪华越野 SUV 泰钽 700 已于 2026 年 8 月 25 日上市 [37]。2026 年上半年，奔驰乘用车整体在华销量为 210,245 辆、同比下降 28.3%；其中北京奔驰国产车辆销量为 165,876 辆、同比下降 31.1%。描述北京奔驰时采用其中的国产车辆口径 [42]。

核心技术与平台

三电领域自研固液混合极光电池、第三代神擎动力系统；智能驾驶完成 L2 至 L4 级全技术路线布局，L3 级在北京开展常态化试点运营，L4 级 Robotaxi 落地商业化场景；并打造极盾车身安全、极净座舱健康两大技术体系。截至 2026 年 8 月累计申请专利约 4.2 万件，7 次获中国汽车工业科技进步一等奖 [34]。集团与华为、宁德时代共建电池生产基地 [34]。新华网 2026 年 7 月 12 日报道极狐正完善阿尔法、贝塔、问道三大产品系列，北汽集团 2026 年 10 月 9 日官方稿也确认了这一布局 [33] [44]。2026 年 6 月 16 日上市的贝塔 T1 焕新版，发布时限时优惠价为 6.28 万元起；该价格仅对应此次焕新上市的限时优惠，不代表所有 T1 版本截至研究截止日的售价 [35]。

创始人与管理层

创始人

北汽集团并非民营意义上的单一创始人，其历史起点为北京汽车制造厂于 1958 年生产「井冈山」牌轿车 [38]，现由北京市国资委通过北京国有资本运营管理有限公司全资持有 [1]。

  • 姜德义——前任董事长，2024 年 3 月因到法定退休年龄卸任 [19]。

股权与治理

集团唯一股东为北京国有资本运营管理有限公司（持股 100%），其上为北京市国资委，实际控制人为北京市国资委；集团注册资本约 199.57 亿元，法定代表人、董事长为张建勇 [1]。2024 年 4 月集团公告的董事及高管调整中，任命王建平为职工董事、蒋自力为党委副书记、董事等 [19]。

竞争格局

我们的判断。北汽在国有汽车集团中的主要对手是上汽、广汽、东风、长安、一汽；商用车领域福田与中国重汽、东风竞争；豪华车领域北京奔驰与华晨宝马、一汽奥迪竞争；自主新能源与比亚迪、吉利、长安等竞争。在豪华新能源轿车领域，享界 S9 与同属华为鸿蒙智行体系的问界（赛力斯）、智界（奇瑞）、尊界（江淮）车型展开竞争 [20]。我们对其优势的判断基于三点已披露事实：福田为中国商用车销量第一 [14]；北京奔驰使集团在豪华车市场占有一席之地 [10]；与华为合作（享界）使其获得高阶辅助驾驶技术 [20]。

我们的判断。其主要短板（依据 2025 年数据）：利润高度依赖北京奔驰——2025 年奔驰乘用车整体在华销量为 551,932 辆（约 55.19 万辆）、同比下降 19% [43]，北京奔驰收入和税后利润分别同比下降 23.1% 和 38.6% [10]；自主板块持续亏损——北汽蓝谷自 2020 年起归母净利润连年亏损，六年累计亏损 341.02 亿元 [8]。北京现代 2025 年销量 21 万辆 [21]。乘用车平台北京汽车 2025 年归母净利润 1.23 亿元、同比下降 87.2%，连续第三年下滑 [12]。以上为我们的判断，依据为前述披露的财务数据。

未来展望

公司公开目标。北汽集团于 2025 年 6 月 5 日发布的《2024 年可持续发展（ESG）报告》记载，集团在 2024 年提出「三年跃升行动」计划，目标到 2027 年整车销量 300 万辆，自主品牌销量力争超过 200 万辆、自主品牌乘用车销量力争超过 100 万辆 [32] [41]；2026 年计划实现整车销量 220 万辆 [5]。福田汽车董事会于 2026 年 9 月 29 日通过、9 月 30 日披露的「十五五」战略（2026–2030 年）提出 2030 年整车销量 100 万辆、新能源销量 50 万辆、海外销量 40 万辆 [13] [40]；北京现代「智启 2030」计划提出未来 4–5 年推出 20 款新产品、5 年达成年销 50 万辆，并计划 2026 年上市 2 款新能源车型 [21]。福田汽车 2026 上半年销量 36.04 万辆（同比 +10.1%）、归母净利润 9.05 亿元（同比 +16.54%）[30]。

我们的判断。上述目标依赖三个前提：北京奔驰在 2025 年下滑后企稳；自主品牌在放量的同时不再加深亏损；新能源转型提速。另一种可能情形是，价格竞争持续与豪华车市场疲软使利润维持低位，届时 300 万辆目标难以达成，且集团归母净利润仍可能接近零。此外，两项风险压制前景：集团自主新能源车型尚未形成规模效应（北汽蓝谷 2025 年整车业务毛利率仍为负 [8]）；出口业务面临目标市场关税与政策变化风险（2025 年出口 30.8 万辆 [5]）。以上为我们的判断，依据为前述披露的财务数据与目标。

风险

  • 北京奔驰业绩下滑。2025 年北京奔驰收入同比 -23.1%、税后利润同比 -38.6% [10]；2026 年上半年奔驰乘用车在华国产车辆（北京奔驰）销量为 165,876 辆、同比下降 31.1% [42]。
  • 自主新能源板块亏损。北汽蓝谷 2025 年归母净利润亏损 45.63 亿元，自 2020 年起累计亏损 341.02 亿元 [8]。
  • 综合毛利率下降。集团综合毛利率由 2024 年的 17.05% 降至 2025 年的 14.26% [1]。
  • 归母净利润规模。2025 年集团净利润 42.81 亿元，其中归母净利润为 0.74 亿元 [2]。
