Overview

Linghui (领汇) is an independent automotive brand formally unveiled by BYD Company Limited on 2 February 2026, targeting both B-end fleet operations (ride-hailing, taxi, official vehicles) and C-end household use, with the positioning "practitioner of ultra-value mobility", the vision "redefining mobility through technological innovation" and the principle "technology without compromise, prices more affordable"[7][13]. It is BYD's fifth auto brand after the Dynasty/Ocean series, Denza, Fangchengbao and Yangwang (fourth independent sub-brand if the Dynasty/Ocean main marque is excluded), operated by BYD Auto Industry Co., Ltd. with an independent sales and service channel[7][12]. The brand was created because BYD's mainstream models are so widely used as ride-hailing cars that they acquired a "workhorse" image; spinning off the fleet business into a rebadged, independently channelled brand lets Dynasty and Ocean focus on private buyers and premiumisation while serving commercial operators with purpose-built vehicles[12][20]. The roll-out has been fast: after the unveiling, the e7 launched in April, the e9 in May and the M9 in June, followed by concentrated deliveries in Chongqing and Beijing[4][19][18]. As of 2026-10-29 Linghui has launched four models (e5, e7, e9, M9) and filed a fifth (e3); as a brand it does not disclose financials separately, which are consolidated into parent BYD (HKEX: 1211; SZSE: 002594)[5].

Current operations

Positioning and background

Linghui was created to separate BYD's fleet (B-end) business from its consumer-facing Dynasty and Ocean lineups. Because BYD's mainstream models are widely used as ride-hailing cars, the main marque carries a "workhorse" image, and Linghui absorbs corporate and government fleet procurement so the main brand can focus on private buyers and premiumisation[12][13]. People close to BYD said Linghui consolidates the e-series models of BYD's former fleet-business division and runs an independent channel[26][27]. BYD says Linghui targets a gap left by a polarising NEV market, combining "operational-grade durability" with "household-grade comfort"[3]. In BYD's brand architecture the main marque (Dynasty and Ocean) serves the mass market, Denza is premium, Fangchengbao is specialist-personalisation, Yangwang is million-yuan luxury, and Linghui adds the mobility/fleet segment[12]. BYD said Linghui's business focuses on taxis, ride-hailing, leasing companies and official vehicles, clearly separated from the main marque's consumer market[7].

Origin and operating entity

The "Linghui" trademark first appeared in the MIIT batch 403 catalog in January 2026, and BYD formally unveiled the brand on 2 February 2026[12][14]. Linghui consolidates the e-series models of BYD's former fleet-business division, which had already launched the e7 and e5; the first Linghui models were adapted from these cars[14][26]. The first four models span pure-electric and plug-in hybrid power: the e5, e7 and e9 are pure-electric sedans and the M9 is the only plug-in hybrid MPV[12][14]. The operating entity, BYD Auto Industry Co., Ltd., was established on 2006-08-03 with registered capital of USD 375,765.452367, held 96.79% by BYD Company Limited and 3.21% by BYD (H.K.) Co., Limited[5][25].

Model lineup and pricing

The first four models are adapted from BYD's mature Dynasty/Ocean models to reuse tooling and production lines: the e5 corresponds to the Qin Plus EV, the e7 to the Ocean-network Sealion platform, the e9 to the Han and the M9 to the Xia[27][14]. On pricing, the e7 launched on 2026-04-15 in five trims at RMB 95,800-115,800 (China market, official guidance price, including the battery)[3][16]; by the research date third-party price-tracking sites listed further fleet trims extending the band to RMB 145,800 (550 flash-charging fleet trim), without a new official launch release[30]. The e7's trims split into household versions and ride-hailing "fleet" versions, offering 450 km, 520 km and 550 km (flash-charging) ranges on third-party price-tracking sites, with fleet versions generally priced above household versions of the same range (clue only)[30]. The e9 launched on 2026-05-09 in three trims at RMB 150,800-169,800[1], and the M9 on 2026-06-29 in two trims at RMB 188,800 and RMB 199,800[4]. By price and use case the four models form a ladder: the e5 targets entry fleet use (taxis and ride-hailing), the e7 mid-size flash charging (premium ride-hailing and family), the e9 C-class business and official use, and the M9 business-MPV reception[27][1]. The e5 went on sale without a dedicated launch event; third-party price-tracking sites list three 530 km trims at RMB 95,800-129,800 (clue only)[28].

Technology and charging

Linghui shares BYD's electric-drive technology. The company said future models will integrate the God's Eye driving-assist system, DiSus intelligent body control, e-Platform 3.0 and fifth-generation DM[7]. The e7 and e9 carry the second-generation Blade Battery and flash charging (10%-70% in about 5 minutes, 10%-97% in about 9 minutes)[3][1], and the M9 uses the fifth-generation DM hybrid system[4]. BYD unveiled the second-generation Blade Battery and flash charging in March 2026, saying the battery can charge at minus 30 degrees Celsius with only about three extra minutes[11]. On charging, BYD announced its "Flash Charging China" strategy in March 2026, planning 20,000 stations nationwide by year-end[11]; the company said 10,000 stations covering 332 cities had been built by August 2026, with plans for a further 6,000 overseas within a year[9].

Channels, warranty and operations

Linghui runs an independent fleet sales and service network distinct from Dynasty-network 4S stores, with longer, core-component warranties for operating vehicles: launch benefits on the e9 and M9 include a 6-year/600,000 km operational three-electric warranty[1][20]. On 2026-05-08 Linghui signed with Chongqing mobility operator Chesheng Huidi and held a first concentrated delivery, adding BYD's nationwide after-sales network to lower driver repair costs and downtime[19]. On 2026-06-27 Linghui delivered the e7 to Beijing users[18]. For operating scenarios, Dahecube reported that the Linghui M9 keeps many physical buttons and wear-resistant interior materials to suit high-frequency, long-cycle commercial use[20].

Sales and market performance

BYD does not break out Linghui in its monthly production and sales bulletins. On retail insurance registrations, Gasgoo data show the Linghui e9 accumulated 1,526 units from February to September 2026[22]. For context, parent BYD sold 4.6024 million NEVs in 2025 (up 7.73% year on year), comprising 2.2567 million battery-electric passenger vehicles (up 27.86%) and 2.2887 million plug-in hybrid passenger vehicles (down 7.91%), with 1.0496 million passenger vehicles and pickups sold overseas (up 145%) across 119 countries and regions[8][10]; full-year 2025 R&D spending was RMB 63.4 billion, lifting cumulative R&D investment past RMB 240 billion[11]. In H1 2026 BYD reported revenue of about RMB 344.8 billion (down 7.13% year on year), net profit attributable to owners of the parent of about RMB 12.3 billion, R&D spending of about RMB 28.9 billion, overseas sales of 790,000 units (up 68%) and overseas revenue of about RMB 181.3 billion (52.57% of total revenue); its three premium brands Fangchengbao, Denza and Yangwang sold 228,000 units combined (up 61%), reaching 12.6% of total sales[9][5]. Linghui was born within this multi-brand structure.

Founders and leadership

Linghui has no separate listed-company governance structure; its operating entity is BYD Auto Industry Co., Ltd., which media report is the certified owner of the brand's official website and Weibo account[13]. BYD's 2026 interim report shows BYD Auto Industry Co., Ltd. was established on 2006-08-03 with registered capital of USD 375,765.452367, held 96.79% by BYD Company Limited and 3.21% by BYD (H.K.) Co., Limited[5][25]. As of 2026-10-29 BYD had not announced a dedicated "Linghui brand general manager"; the most senior publicly named figure is Huang Yongming, assistant general manager of BYD's fleet-business division, who spoke for the brand at the 2026-06-27 Beijing delivery ceremony; his appointment date has not been disclosed[18]. The brand originated from BYD's internal fleet-business division and operates within that organisation[12]. Linghui has no separate legal entity; its models are filed under the "Linghui" trademark by BYD Auto Industry Co., Ltd. through the MIIT product catalog[6]; the brand's internal structure and executive appointments had not been fully disclosed in public channels as of the research date. The parent, BYD Company Limited, is listed on the HKEX (1211) and SZSE (002594); see the BYD deep dive for board and governance details[5]. The e-series fleet business that Linghui absorbed already had models: BYD had launched the e7 sedan in May 2025 from RMB 103,800[14]. On launch day Linghui opened its official Weibo account with a "Hello, Linghui" post and BYD said "Welcome Linghui"[17].

Competition

In ride-hailing and taxi markets, Linghui competes with incumbents such as GAC Aion (AION S and others) and Geely's Cao Cao Mobility, which runs its own fleet; in the official/business MPV segment it faces the long-standing dominance of the Buick GL8. Dahecube reported that the GL8 has built barriers in government and corporate reception and business leasing through market accumulation and high resale value, while the Linghui M9 enters that B-end business-travel territory from RMB 188,800, competing on durability and whole-life operating efficiency rather than luxury premium[20]. Linghui's strengths are access to BYD's electric-drive technology and supply-chain scale and the operating-turnover advantage of flash charging, while dedicated operational three-electric warranties (6 years/600,000 km on the e9 and M9) lower driver holding costs[1][3]. Its weaknesses are a brand-new nameplate, cautious B-end procurement decisions, and first models that are rebadged versions of existing cars with limited differentiation. On retail insurance registrations, the Linghui e9 totalled 1,526 units from February to September 2026, far smaller than the incumbent fleet volumes of GAC Aion and Cao Cao Mobility (our assessment)[22]. Our assessment: Linghui's core value is clearing the way for the main marque's de-commercialisation and premiumisation; its own retail volume is still small, and scale in a fleet market that is no longer uncontested will depend on partnerships with mobility platforms and on delivery of the operational warranty and flash-charging network[22]. Separately, a Gasgoo Research Institute analyst told Dahecube that as robotaxi and other emerging mobility ecosystems commercialise, running Linghui as a standalone brand makes it easier to cooperate deeply with mobility platforms and could even support BYD building its own mobility service platform[20].

Outlook

Company plans: Linghui says it will launch a model series covering multiple scenarios (entry sedans, multi-purpose MPVs and multi-use functional vehicles) and integrate God's Eye, DiSus, e-Platform 3.0 and fifth-generation DM technologies[18]; on charging it rides the "Flash Charging China" plan for 20,000 stations by end-2026 with 5 km urban and 50 km highway coverage[3]. BYD has also said the M9 will later add a 1163 DiSus flagship trim with God's Eye driving assist (highway NOA and automated parking)[4]. The brand promotes a cadence of "fast brand launch, fast store roll-out, fast model updates and fast flash charging" and "a new model every month"[1]. Our analysis: Linghui launched the e7, e9 and M9 in rapid succession from April to June 2026 and lowered B-end purchasing thresholds with free flash-charging benefits and financing on the e7[16], but the differing "first model" descriptions for the e5 and e7 and the unconfirmed timing of the e3 mean the product cadence and official messaging still need monitoring. Volume scaling depends on bulk procurement by mobility platforms and government/enterprise clients, and on delivery of the operational warranty and flash-charging network; if fleet sales undershoot, the brand could remain small and low-profile for some time[22]. In addition, if the already-filed e3 launches, it would fill the brand's entry-level pure-electric sedan tier (our analysis).

Risks

  • Sales and disclosure risk: BYD does not break out Linghui in monthly bulletins, and third-party retail (insurance-registration) data capture only the e9 at small volumes, so the true scale of bulk fleet deliveries is hard to verify[22].
  • Competition risk: GAC Aion and Geely's Cao Cao Mobility are established in the fleet market, and the Buick GL8 has entrenched positions in business MPVs[20].
  • Brand and channel investment risk: the independent channel, dedicated warranties and flash-charging network require ongoing investment that may add group cost before volumes ramp up[19].
  • Product differentiation risk: the first models are rebadged versions of existing cars, and a badge swap plus de-contenting alone may not support long-term brand value[16].
  • Overseas and policy risk: overseas entry (including right-hand-drive versions) appears only in encyclopedia-style and self-media hints and is not officially confirmed; overseas expansion and local taxi-electrification policies remain uncertain[12].
  • Technology and charging-infrastructure risk: flash charging, the second-generation Blade Battery and the charging network are still in a large-scale build-out phase, and Linghui's differentiation could narrow if rivals quickly catch up on charging and battery experience[3].

概览

领汇（Linghui）是比亚迪股份有限公司于 2026 年 2 月 2 日正式发布的独立汽车品牌，面向 B 端对公营运（网约车、出租车、公务车）与 C 端个人家用双场景，品牌定位「超价值出行践行者」、愿景「以技术创新重新定义出行」、主打「技术不妥协、价格更亲民」[7][13]。领汇是比亚迪继王朝/海洋系列、腾势、方程豹、仰望之后的第五个汽车品牌（不计王朝/海洋主品牌则为第四个子品牌），由比亚迪汽车工业有限公司运营，拥有独立的销售与售后渠道[7][12]。设立背景是比亚迪主品牌车型因性价比高被大量用作网约车、形成「工具车」印象，将对公业务换标独立可让王朝网、海洋网专注个人家用与高端化，同时以专业车型服务营运市场[12][20]。领汇落地节奏较快：品牌官宣后，4 月上市 e7、5 月上市 e9、6 月上市 M9，并先后在重庆、北京开展集中交付[4][19][18]。截至 2026-10-29，领汇已上市 e5、e7、e9、M9 四款车型并申报 e3；作为品牌，其财务数据未单独披露，相关指标并入母公司比亚迪（港交所 1211、深交所 002594）[5]。

现状

品牌定位与设立背景

领汇的设立目的是把比亚迪的对公（B 端）业务与面向个人的王朝网、海洋网业务区隔开。比亚迪主品牌车型因性价比高被大量用作网约车，带来「工具车」的品牌印象；领汇承接政府公务、出租、网约等批量采购，让主品牌专注个人家用与高端化[12][13]。据接近比亚迪的知情人士，领汇整合了比亚迪原对公事业部的 e 系列新能源车型，并拥有独立的渠道体系[26][27]。官方称领汇瞄准新能源市场分化留下的空白，主打「营运级耐久」与「家用级舒适」兼顾的产品[3]。从品牌架构看，比亚迪主品牌（王朝网、海洋网）面向主流大众市场，腾势定位高端、方程豹定位专业个性化、仰望定位百万级豪华，领汇补上出行/营运市场一环[12]。官方称其业务范围重点覆盖出租车、网约车、租赁公司、公务用车等对公营运市场，与主品牌面向个人消费的市场形成区隔[7]。

品牌溯源与运营主体

「领汇牌」商标与全新标识于 2026 年 1 月经工信部第 403 批产品公告首次亮相，品牌于 2 月 2 日由比亚迪正式官宣[12][14]。领汇整合了比亚迪原对公事业部的 e 系列车型——该事业部此前已推出 e7、e5 等对公车型，领汇首批车型在此基础上改款而来[14][26]。首批四款车型涵盖纯电与插混两种动力：e5、e7、e9 为纯电轿车，M9 为唯一的插电式混合动力 MPV[12][14]。品牌运营主体比亚迪汽车工业有限公司成立于 2006-08-03，注册资本 375,765.452367 万美元，由比亚迪股份有限公司持股 96.79%、BYD (H.K.) CO., LIMITED 持股 3.21%[5][25]。

车型谱系与价格

首批四款车型均基于比亚迪王朝网/海洋网成熟车型改款而来，以复用模具与产线、控制成本：e5 对应秦 PLUS EV、e7 对应海洋网海狮纯电平台、e9 对应汉、M9 对应夏[27][14]。价格上，e7 于 2026-04-15 上市，5 款配置 9.58 万-11.58 万元（中国内地市场、正式指导价、含动力电池）[3][16]；截至研究截止日，第三方报价网站另列多款「出行版」配置，价格带上沿至 14.58 万元（550 闪充高能出行版），新增配置未见官方上市稿[30]。e7 配置分为家用版与面向网约车的「出行版」两类，据第三方报价网站提供 450 km、520 km、550 km（闪充）三种续航，同续航的出行版指导价普遍高于家用版（仅作线索）[30]。e9 于 2026-05-09 上市，3 款配置 15.08 万-16.98 万元[1]；M9 于 2026-06-29 上市，首批两款 18.88 万、19.98 万元[4]。按价格与场景，四款车型形成梯度：e5 主打入门级营运（出租、网约），e7 主打中级闪充（专车与家用），e9 主打 C 级商务接待与公务，M9 主打商务 MPV 接待[27][1]。e5 未单独举办上市发布，第三方报价网站列 530 km 三款配置 9.58 万-12.98 万元（仅作线索）[28]。

核心技术与补能

领汇共享比亚迪三电技术。官方称未来车型将整合「天神之眼」辅助驾驶、云辇智能车身控制、e 平台 3.0 与第五代 DM 技术[7]。e7、e9 搭载第二代刀片电池与闪充技术（10%-70% 约 5 分钟、10%-97% 约 9 分钟）[3][1]；M9 采用第五代 DM 插混系统[4]。第二代刀片电池与闪充技术由比亚迪于 2026 年 3 月发布，官方称可在零下 30℃ 环境下充电、仅比常温多约 3 分钟[11]。补能上，比亚迪 2026 年 3 月发布「闪充中国」战略，计划年底在全国建设 20000 座闪充站[11]；据公司披露，截至 2026 年 8 月已建成 10000 座、覆盖 332 座城市，并计划一年内在海外部署 6000 座[9]。

渠道、质保与运营

领汇以独立的对公销售与售后体系区别于王朝网 4S 店，并针对营运车辆推出更长周期、覆盖核心部件的专属质保：e9、M9 上市权益含营运三电 6 年或 60 万公里质保[1][20]。2026-05-08，领汇与重庆出行服务商车胜惠迪签约并举行首批集中交付，叠加比亚迪全国售后网络以降低司机维修成本与停运损失[19]。2026-06-27，领汇在北京为用户集中交付 e7[18]。针对营运场景，大河财立方报道领汇 M9 保留了大量实体按键与耐磨内饰，以适配高频次、长周期的营运环境[20]。

销量与市场表现

比亚迪未在月度产销快报中单列领汇销量。以零售上险口径看，据盖世汽车数据，领汇 e9 自 2026 年 2 月上市以来至 2026 年 9 月累计上险 1,526 辆[22]。作为参照，母公司比亚迪 2025 年新能源汽车累计销量 460.24 万辆（同比增长 7.73%），其中纯电动乘用车 225.67 万辆（同比增长 27.86%）、插电式混合动力乘用车 228.87 万辆（同比下降 7.91%），乘用车及皮卡海外累计销售 104.96 万辆（同比增长 145%），出海足迹覆盖全球 119 个国家和地区[8][10]；2025 年全年研发投入 634 亿元，累计研发投入突破 2400 亿元[11]。2026 年上半年，比亚迪营业收入约 3448 亿元（同比下降 7.13%）、归母净利润约 123 亿元、研发投入约 289 亿元、海外销量 79 万辆（同比增长 68%）、海外营收约 1813 亿元（占总营收 52.57%）；方程豹、腾势、仰望三大高端品牌合计销量 22.8 万辆、同比增长 61%，占总销量比重升至 12.6%[9][5]。领汇即诞生于这一集团多品牌布局之下。

创始人与管理层

领汇未单独设立上市公司治理架构，品牌运营主体为比亚迪汽车工业有限公司；据媒体报道，该公司官网与官方微博的认证主体均为比亚迪汽车工业有限公司[13]。比亚迪 2026 年半年度报告显示，比亚迪汽车工业有限公司成立于 2006-08-03，注册资本 375,765.452367 万美元，由比亚迪股份有限公司持股 96.79%、BYD (H.K.) CO., LIMITED 持股 3.21%[5][25]。截至 2026-10-29，比亚迪未公布「领汇品牌总经理」一职；公开露面的最高职务者为比亚迪对公事业部总经理助理黄永明，他在 2026-06-27 北京交付仪式上代表品牌发言，其上任时间未披露[18]。领汇品牌源自比亚迪内部的对公事业部，由该事业部体系运营[12]。领汇未单独设立法人主体，其车型以「领汇牌」通过工信部产品公告由比亚迪汽车工业有限公司申报[6]；品牌层面的组织架构与高管任命，截至研究截止日未在公开渠道完整披露。母公司比亚迪股份有限公司为港交所（1211）与深交所（002594）上市公司，其董事会与股权治理信息见比亚迪深度研究[5]。比亚迪原对公 e 系列此前已有车型——比亚迪曾于 2025 年 5 月以 10.38 万元起价推出 e 系列 e7 轿车[14]。品牌发布当日，领汇汽车在官方微博发布「你好，领汇」等内容，比亚迪官方称「欢迎领汇」[17]。

领汇为比亚迪旗下独立品牌，未单独披露财务数据，故本表为空。其母公司比亚迪股份有限公司（港交所 1211、深交所 002594）按中国企业会计准则编制财报，并按国际财务报告准则补充披露，币种为人民币。净利润口径为「归属于母公司股东的净利润」（即归母净利润，非「归属于普通股股东」）。营收、毛利率、归母净利润、研发投入等指标见比亚迪 2025 年年度报告及 2026 年半年度报告（巨潮资讯）。

竞争格局

在网约车与出租市场，领汇面对广汽埃安（AION S 等）和吉利曹操出行（自有车队）等先行者；在公务/商务 MPV 领域则面对别克 GL8 的长期主导地位。大河财立方报道称，GL8 凭借市场积淀与高保值率在政企接待与商务租赁领域建立了壁垒，而领汇 M9 以 18.88 万元起价切入该 B 端商旅市场，主打耐用性与全生命周期运营效率而非豪华溢价[20]。领汇的优势在于共享比亚迪三电技术与供应链规模，以及闪充补能带来的营运周转效率；营运车专属三电质保（e9、M9 为 6 年或 60 万公里）进一步降低司机持有成本[1][3]。短板是品牌全新、B 端客户采购决策谨慎，且首批车型为既有车型改款、与主品牌区隔有限。以零售上险口径看，领汇 e9 在 2026 年 2-9 月累计 1,526 辆，规模远小于广汽埃安、曹操出行等在网约车市场的既有体量（我们的判断）[22]。我们的判断：领汇的核心价值在于为主品牌「去营运化」与高端化扫清障碍；其自身零售上险规模尚小，能否在已非蓝海的对公市场规模化，取决于与出行平台的合作落地、营运级质保与闪充网络的实际兑现[22]。另据盖世汽车分析师对大河财立方表示，随着 Robotaxi 等新兴出行生态商业化落地，领汇以独立品牌运作，便于未来与各大出行平台开展深度合作，甚至支撑比亚迪自建出行服务平台[20]。

未来展望

公司公开规划：领汇官方称将推出覆盖多场景的车型系列（入门级轿车、多功能 MPV、多用型功能车），并整合天神之眼、云辇、e 平台 3.0、第五代 DM 等资源[18]；补能上配合「闪充中国」战略，计划 2026 年底建成 20000 座闪充站，实现城区 5 公里、高速 50 公里覆盖[3]。官方还透露 M9 后续将推出 1163 云辇旗舰版，并搭载天神之眼辅助驾驶（高速 NOA、自动泊车）[4]。官方提出「品牌发布快、门店落位快、车型上新快、闪充补能快」与「一月一上新」的节奏口号[1]。我们的分析：领汇已在 4-6 月连续推出 e7、e9、M9 三款车型，落地速度较快，并以 e7 的免费闪充权益、金融方案降低 B 端客户购车门槛[16]；但 e5 与 e7 的「首款」表述存在出入、e3 尚未确认正式上市时间，产品节奏与官方口径仍需持续观察。放量的前提是出行平台与政企客户批量采购的落地，以及营运级质保与闪充网络的实际兑现；若对公放量不及预期，品牌可能长期停留在小规模、低曝光状态[22]。此外，若已申报的 e3 正式上市，将补足品牌在入门级纯电轿车一档的产品覆盖（我们的分析）。

风险

  • 销量与披露风险：比亚迪未在月度产销快报中单列领汇，第三方零售（上险）数据仅覆盖 e9 一款且规模很小，对公/车队批量交付的真实规模难以核实[22]。
  • 竞争风险：对公市场已有广汽埃安、吉利曹操出行等成熟玩家，商务 MPV 领域有别克 GL8 的既有壁垒[20]。
  • 品牌与渠道投入风险：独立渠道、专属质保与闪充网络建设需要持续投入，在规模未起之前可能增加集团成本[19]。
  • 产品差异化风险：首批车型均为既有车型改款，若仅靠换标与减配，可能难以支撑独立品牌长期价值[16]。
  • 海外与政策风险：出海（含右舵版本）仅见于百科类与自媒体线索，未经官方确认；海外进入及当地出租车电动化政策存在不确定性[12]。
  • 技术迭代与补能建设风险：闪充、第二代刀片电池等技术与补能网络仍处大规模建设期，若竞品在补能与电池体验上快速跟进，领汇的差异化优势可能被削弱[3]。
