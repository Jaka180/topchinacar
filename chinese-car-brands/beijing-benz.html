Overview

Beijing Benz Automotive Co., Ltd. (BBAC, 北京奔驰) is a passenger-vehicle joint venture between BAIC Motor Corporation Limited (北京汽车, 01958.HK) and Mercedes-Benz Group AG, with BAIC Motor holding 51% and Mercedes-Benz Group (together with its wholly owned subsidiary Mercedes-Benz Group China Ltd.) holding 49% [4][5]. Founded on 8 August 2005, it is a Sino-German venture integrating vehicle, engine and power-battery production, and describes itself as one of the largest and most advanced passenger-car production bases in Mercedes-Benz's global production network [1][3].

Its predecessor, Beijing Jeep, was founded in 1984 as China's first automotive joint venture [13]. After a 2010 renaming and the 2013 'BAIC-Daimler' restructuring, the Chinese side's stake rose to 51% and BBAC became a pillar of BAIC Motor's earnings [15]. Its revenue and profit after taxes peaked in 2022 (EUR 24.82 billion and EUR 3.649 billion) [18] and have declined for three consecutive years: in 2025 revenue was EUR 16.72 billion (-23.1%) and profit after taxes EUR 1.5 billion (-38.6%) [6].

BBAC has produced Mercedes-Benz passenger cars since 2005 and engines since 2013, and also exports engine core components and complete engines [3]. In 2025–2026 it has been switching its line-up toward pure-electric models on the MMA and MB.EA platforms — the Electric CLA and Electric GLC SUV — alongside new combustion models such as the long-wheelbase GLE SUV [29][31][24].

Current operations

Business structure

BBAC is a manufacturing joint venture spanning three vehicle platforms — front-wheel drive, rear-wheel drive and electric — plus engine and power-battery plants, and is part of Mercedes-Benz's global production network; it also exports engine core components and complete engines [1][3]. Sales of Mercedes-Benz cars in China are carried out through the separate joint venture Beijing Mercedes-Benz Sales Service Co., Ltd., set up by the two shareholders [15].

Products

The company profile lists seven models currently in production: the Electric GLC SUV, Electric CLA, GLE L SUV, CLA, E-Class, GLC SUV and C-Class [1]. The Mercedes-Benz Group's Beijing plant page separately lists twelve models under 'Production 2025 & 2026' — C-, E- and A-Class long versions, CLA, GLA, GLB, GLC long version, AMG A 35 L, EQA, EQB, EQE and EQE SUV — reflecting a different reference point [3]. By its 21st anniversary in August 2026, BBAC had cumulatively launched 15 models across sedans, SUVs and pure-electric vehicles [21]. The all-new Electric GLC SUV, which rolled off the line in June 2026, is BBAC's first locally produced model on the MB.EA pure-electric platform [31]. The all-new long-wheelbase GLE SUV, which rolled off the line at the Shunyi plant in September 2026, has a wheelbase over 3.1 metres and a 3.0-litre inline-six engine with 48-volt mild hybrid [24].

Plants and technology

BBAC operates in the Yizhuang Industrial Park (Beijing Economic-Technological Development Area), with front-wheel-drive, rear-wheel-drive and electric vehicle plants, an engine plant producing engines since 2013, and a battery plant supplying battery systems for the all-electric models since 2019; a second plant in Shunyi district has produced since 2020 [3]. The company has about 11,000 employees (as of December 2025) and applies the Mercedes-Benz MO360 production system, using the Internet of Things, big data, the industrial internet, digital twins and AI [3][1]. The Shunyi plant's stamping workshop uses Zeiss optical measuring equipment with micron-level precision [24].

Production and sales

BBAC sold 610,800 vehicles in 2020 (+7.67% year on year) and 561,000 in 2021 (-8.15%) [10]. In 2025, Mercedes-Benz Cars locally produced 453,198 vehicles in China (-19.5%) and its China unit sales totalled 551,932 including imports (-19.3%) [19][8]. In the first half of 2026, local production fell to 165,876 vehicles (-31.1%) [9]. As of the research date, the Mercedes-Benz Cars fact sheet for the third quarter of 2026 shows China unit sales of 86,792 vehicles in July–September (down 30.6% from 125,133 a year earlier) and 297,037 in the first nine months (down 29.0%) [41]. The group has cut its 2026 outlook for Mercedes-Benz Cars' adjusted return on sales from 3%–5% to 2%–3%, citing both a more challenging market environment in China and higher fourth-quarter costs for raw materials and product lifecycle measures [42][43].

Founders and leadership

Origin

As a joint venture, BBAC has no individual founder. In 2005 Beijing Jeep was restructured by Beijing Automotive Industry Holding Co., Ltd. together with DaimlerChrysler AG and DaimlerChrysler (China) Investment Co., Ltd. into Beijing Benz-DaimlerChrysler Automotive Co., Ltd. [12]. BAIC Motor Corporation Limited was incorporated on 20 September 2010 [40]; in 2010, after the Daimler–Chrysler split, the venture's shareholders became BAIC Motor and Daimler and the company was renamed Beijing Benz Automotive Co., Ltd. [15]. The company profile now lists the investors as BAIC Motor, Mercedes-Benz Group AG and Mercedes-Benz (China) Investment Co., Ltd. [1]. Its lineage goes back to Beijing Jeep, founded in 1984 as China's first automotive joint venture [13].

Current management

  • Jens Bühler (严博霖, also transliterated 比勒) — President & CEO, in the role since 1 May 2026, succeeding Jörg Bartels [32][33]. Immediately before the appointment he was Head of Production at the Kecskemét plant and at Mercedes-Benz Manufacturing Hungary [32].
  • Wei Zhilan (魏芝兰) — Deputy Party Secretary and Senior Executive Vice President (the Chinese-side executive), in office as of August 2026 [21][22].

Recent personnel changes

  • Jörg Bartels (柏睿凯) — previous President & CEO, who left BBAC to become head of electrified drive systems after Torsten Eder's departure on 1 May 2026 [32].
  • Zhang Zhengye (张正业) — Party Secretary and Senior Executive Vice President of BBAC as of January 2026; around late March 2026 he moved to Beijing Mercedes-Benz Sales Service Co., Ltd. as Senior Executive Vice President [23][28][25].

Ownership and governance

BAIC Motor holds 51% of BBAC and consolidates the venture; Mercedes-Benz Group and its wholly owned subsidiary Mercedes-Benz Group China Ltd. hold 49% [4][5]. At the listed-parent level, BAIC Group was the sole controlling shareholder of BAIC Motor (46.90% as at the annual-report date), with Shougang Group at 12.83%, Mercedes-Benz Group at 9.55%, other domestic shareholders at 8.82% and H-share public shareholders at 21.90% [4]. The 9.55% figure is the stake shown in the 2025 annual report [4]. The 2013 agreement was for Daimler to acquire 12% of BAIC Motor [15]. By the 25 April 2018 H-share placing announcement, Daimler AG already held 765,818,182 shares, equal to 10.08% of the issued share capital; the placing of 420 million new H shares, completed on 3 May 2018, left that share count unchanged and reduced the percentage to 9.55% [38][39]. The ultimate controller is the Beijing State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission, which beneficially owns BAIC Group and thereby BAIC Motor and BBAC [5][4]. At the parent level, Wang Hao stepped down as BAIC Motor's chairman on 14 April 2026 and Zhang Guofu was appointed chairman and non-executive director [20].

Competition

Our judgment. BBAC competes in China's premium segment mainly against BMW Brilliance and FAW-Audi among foreign joint ventures, and increasingly against domestic luxury and NEV brands such as NIO, Li Auto, Huawei Harmony Intelligent Mobility Alliance models (AITO/Wenjie, Stelato), BYD's Denza and Yangwang, and Zeekr. Its strengths rest on the Mercedes-Benz brand franchise and a broad locally produced line-up. The company describes itself as one of the largest and most advanced passenger-car production bases in Mercedes-Benz's global network [1]; the Group's Beijing plant page describes BBAC only as an important part of that network [3]. Its weakness is the three-year decline: revenue and profit after taxes fell in 2023, 2024 and 2025, with 2025 revenue down 23.1% and profit after taxes down 38.6%, and H1 2026 locally produced sales down 31.1% [6][9].

Our judgment. The decline reflects a slow electrification transition amid an intense domestic price war. In 2025, Mercedes-Benz Cars' China unit sales fell 19.3%, while BBAC's profit after taxes fell 38.6% — the profit decline was steeper than its own volume decline (note the two measures are not the same scope: one is Mercedes-Benz Cars' total China sales including imports, the other is BBAC's venture-level profit after taxes), pointing to pressure on per-vehicle earnings [8][6]. Against a market where China's NEV penetration passed 50% for the first time in 2025 (CPCA data reported at about 54% for the year), a line-up still anchored by combustion models is structurally disadvantaged [27].

Our judgment. BBAC's transition from the first-generation EQ models (EQC, EQA, EQB, EQE, EQE SUV) to the MMA and MB.EA 'Electric' models is the key variable: the Electric CLA rolled off the line in September 2025 and the Electric GLC SUV in June 2026 [29][31]. Its recovery depends on whether these new models can regain share without losing its combustion-vehicle base.

Outlook

Company plans. Mercedes-Benz Group says it will launch more than 40 new models globally by 2027, including several China-first models, and that it is investing more than RMB 14 billion with its Chinese partner in new technology and products [17][16]. At BBAC, the Electric CLA 260 L went on sale in April 2026 from RMB 229,000 [16], and the Electric GLC SUV went on sale in July 2026 (the Gilt Gold at RMB 339,800, with the Silver on presale at RMB 299,900) [36]. BBAC also passed cumulative six-million-vehicle output in January 2026 and had launched 15 models cumulatively by its August 2026 anniversary, with the long-wheelbase GLE SUV rolling off the line in September 2026; the company describes 2026 as a 'product year' and its 'AI era' [23][21][24].

Our judgment. Whether BBAC stabilises depends on the new electric range (Electric GLC SUV, Electric CLA, GLE L SUV) regaining volume lost by the declining combustion models, and on the MMA/MB.EA line-up arriving fast enough to compete with domestic NEV leaders. The 31.1% H1 2026 locally produced-sales decline, followed by a 30.6% fall in Mercedes-Benz Cars' China unit sales in Q3 2026 (86,792 vehicles, from 125,133 a year earlier), suggests the slide has not yet reversed [9][41]. A key precondition is that Mercedes-Benz's renewed China focus translates into concrete product and technology investment at BBAC [17].

Our judgment. A further consideration is BBAC's centrality to BAIC Motor: the parent's profit attributable to equity holders fell 87.2% in 2025, so a continued BBAC decline would directly constrain the parent's earnings and, in turn, the resources available for the venture's electrification [4].

Risks

  • Three-year financial decline. Revenue and profit after taxes fell for a third straight year in 2025 (revenue -23.1%, profit after taxes -38.6%), with H1 2026 locally produced sales down a further 31.1% [6][9].
  • China market headwinds. Mercedes-Benz Cars' China unit sales were 86,792 in Q3 2026, down 30.6% from 125,133 a year earlier [41]. The group cut its 2026 outlook for Mercedes-Benz Cars' adjusted return on sales from 3%–5% to 2%–3%, citing both a more challenging market environment in China and higher fourth-quarter costs for raw materials and product lifecycle measures [42][43].
  • Price war and competition. Intense domestic price competition and rising local NEV brands continue to pressure the premium joint-venture segment [8][27].
  • Technology-transition risk. The shift to the MMA and MB.EA electric platforms is capital-intensive and its market reception is not yet proven; the first-generation EQ models (EQC, EQA, EQB, EQE, EQE SUV) have given way to the new Electric CLA and Electric GLC SUV, which only entered production in 2025–2026 [29][31].
  • Single-brand and single-partner dependence. BBAC's results depend entirely on the Mercedes-Benz brand and on the continued cooperation of BAIC Motor and Mercedes-Benz Group [4].
  • Parent-profit sensitivity. BAIC Motor's profit attributable to equity holders fell 87.2% in 2025, leaving the parent's earnings highly sensitive to BBAC's performance [4].
  • Limited disclosure. As an unlisted joint venture, BBAC does not publish separate full financial statements; gross margin and R&D are not disclosed, so its operations are visible only through its two shareholders' reports [7].

概览

北京奔驰汽车有限公司（简称北京奔驰，英文 Beijing Benz Automotive Co., Ltd.，BBAC）是北京汽车股份有限公司（01958.HK）与梅赛德斯-奔驰集团股份公司合资的乘用车企业，北京汽车持股 51%，梅赛德斯-奔驰集团（连同其全资子公司梅赛德斯-奔驰（中国）投资有限公司）持股 49% [4][5]。公司成立于 2005 年 8 月 8 日，是中德合资企业，集整车、发动机与动力电池生产于一体，自称是梅赛德斯-奔驰全球生产网络中规模最大、最前沿的乘用车生产基地之一 [1][3]。

其前身北京吉普成立于 1984 年，是中国第一家汽车合资企业 [13]。经 2010 年更名与 2013 年「北戴合」重组，中方持股升至 51%，北京奔驰成为北京汽车的核心利润来源 [15]。公司营收与除税后溢利于 2022 年达到峰值（248.2 亿欧元、36.49 亿欧元）[18]，此后连续三年下滑：2025 年收入 167.2 亿欧元（同比 -23.1%）、除税后溢利 15 亿欧元（同比 -38.6%）[6]。

北京奔驰自 2005 年起生产奔驰乘用车、自 2013 年起生产发动机，并实现发动机核心零部件与整机的出口 [3]。2025–2026 年，公司正把产品线切换至 MMA、MB.EA 纯电平台的纯电 CLA、纯电 GLC SUV，同时继续投放长轴距 GLE SUV 等新燃油车型 [29][31][24]。

现状

业务结构

北京奔驰是集前驱车、后驱车、电动车三大车型平台以及发动机与动力电池工厂于一体的制造型合资企业，是梅赛德斯-奔驰全球生产网络的一部分，并实现发动机核心零部件与整机的出口 [1][3]。奔驰在华销售由双方股东另设的合资销售公司北京梅赛德斯-奔驰销售服务有限公司负责 [15]。

产品

公司官网「公司介绍」现列在产车型 7 款：纯电 GLC SUV、纯电 CLA、长轴距 GLE SUV、CLA、E 级车、GLC SUV、C 级车 [1]。奔驰集团北京工厂页的「Production 2025 & 2026」清单则另列 12 款（C/E/A 级长轴距、CLA、GLA、GLB、GLC 长轴距、AMG A 35 L、EQA、EQB、EQE、EQE SUV），参考时点不同 [3]。截至 2026 年 8 月成立 21 周年，北京奔驰已累计投产 15 款车型，覆盖轿车、SUV 与纯电领域 [21]。2026 年 6 月下线的全新纯电 GLC SUV，是北京奔驰基于 MB.EA 纯电平台的首款国产量产车型 [31]。2026 年 9 月在顺义工厂下线的全新长轴距 GLE SUV，轴距超 3.1 米，搭载直列六缸 3.0 升发动机与 48 伏轻混系统 [24]。

工厂与技术

北京奔驰位于北京经济技术开发区（亦庄），拥有前驱、后驱、纯电整车工厂，2013 年起生产发动机的发动机工厂，以及 2019 年起为纯电车型供应电池系统的动力电池工厂；顺义第二工厂自 2020 年起投产 [3]。公司员工约 1.1 万人（2025 年 12 月），引入梅赛德斯-奔驰乘用车生产运营 360（MO360）体系，应用物联网、大数据、工业互联网、数字孪生与 AI 等技术 [3][1]。顺义工厂冲压车间配备测量精度达微米级的蔡司光学检测仪器 [24]。

产销

北京奔驰 2020 年整车销量 61.08 万辆（同比 +7.67%）、2021 年 56.10 万辆（同比 -8.15%）[10]。2025 年，奔驰在华本地生产车辆 453,198 辆（同比 -19.5%）、奔驰乘用车在华总销量（含进口）551,932 辆（同比 -19.3%）[19][8]。2026 上半年本地生产 165,876 辆（同比 -31.1%）[9]。截至研究截止日，奔驰乘用车 2026 年三季度 Fact Sheet 显示：7–9 月在华销量为 86,792 辆，同比 -30.6%（2025 年三季度为 125,133 辆）；前三季度 297,037 辆，同比 -29.0% [41]。集团并将 2026 年奔驰乘用车调整后销售利润率展望由 3%–5% 下调至 2%–3%，原因同时包括中国市场环境更趋挑战，以及第四季度原材料与产品生命周期措施的成本上升 [42][43]。

创始人与管理层

历史渊源

作为合资企业，北京奔驰没有单一创始人。2005 年，北京汽车工业控股有限责任公司与戴姆勒·克莱斯勒股份公司、戴姆勒·克莱斯勒（中国）投资有限公司重组北京吉普，成立北京奔驰-戴姆勒·克莱斯勒汽车有限公司 [12]。北京汽车股份有限公司于 2010 年 9 月 20 日才注册成立 [40]；同年，随着戴姆勒与克莱斯勒拆分，合资公司股东变更为北京汽车股份有限公司与戴姆勒，并更名为北京奔驰汽车有限公司 [15]。公司官网现在的投资方表述是北京汽车股份有限公司、梅赛德斯-奔驰集团股份公司与梅赛德斯-奔驰（中国）投资有限公司 [1]。其谱系可追溯至 1984 年成立的中国第一家汽车合资企业北京吉普 [13]。

现任核心管理层

  • 比勒（Jens Bühler，中文名严博霖）——总裁兼首席执行官，自 2026 年 5 月 1 日起接替柏睿凯上任 [32][33]。官方任命稿确认，他接任前是匈牙利凯奇凯梅特工厂及 Mercedes-Benz Manufacturing Hungary 的生产负责人 [32]。
  • 魏芝兰——党委副书记、高级执行副总裁（中方负责人），截至 2026 年 8 月在任 [21][22]。

近期人事变动

  • 柏睿凯（Jörg Bartels）——前任总裁兼首席执行官，返回德国出任电驱系统负责人（接替 2026 年 5 月 1 日离任的 Torsten Eder）[32]。
  • 张正业——2026 年 1 月时任北京奔驰党委书记、高级执行副总裁；2026 年 3 月下旬起转任北京梅赛德斯-奔驰销售服务有限公司高级执行副总裁 [23][28][25]。

股权与治理

北京汽车持股北京奔驰 51% 并将其纳入合并报表；梅赛德斯-奔驰集团及其全资子公司梅赛德斯-奔驰（中国）投资有限公司持股 49% [4][5]。上市公司层面，北汽集团是北京汽车唯一控股股东（年报报告出具日持股 46.90%），首钢集团持股 12.83%、梅赛德斯-奔驰集团 9.55%、其他内资股东 8.82%、H 股公众股东 21.90% [4]。9.55% 是 2025 年报所列持股 [4]。2013 年协议口径是戴姆勒收购北京汽车 12% 股权 [15]。到 2018 年 4 月 25 日《配售 H 股》公告时，戴姆勒已持股 765,818,182 股，占已发行股本 10.08%；该批 4.2 亿股新 H 股于 2018 年 5 月 3 日发行完成，戴姆勒股数不变，比例降至 9.55% [38][39]。实际控制人为北京市人民政府国有资产监督管理委员会，其受益拥有北汽集团，进而控制北京汽车与北京奔驰 [5][4]。母公司层面，2026 年 4 月 14 日王昊卸任北京汽车董事长，张国富获委任为董事长、非执行董事 [20]。

币种为欧元、单位为百万欧元、会计准则为国际财务报告准则（IFRS）。北京奔驰为非上市合资企业（北京汽车持股 51%、梅赛德斯-奔驰集团持股 49%），不单独编制并披露完整财务报表；北京汽车通过港交所公告，转发摘录自梅赛德斯-奔驰集团财务报表的北京奔驰主要财务信息（北京奔驰在梅赛德斯-奔驰集团报表中为按权益法核算的联营公司）。表中「净利润（除税后溢利）」为北京奔驰公司自身的除税后利润，即该合资公司全体股东（北京汽车 51%、梅赛德斯-奔驰集团 49%）合计应享的税后利润，既不是「归属于母公司股东的净利润」口径（北京奔驰无单一上市母公司），也不是北京汽车作为股东应占的 51% 份额。具体数字：2022 年收入 24,820 百万欧元、除税后溢利 3,649 百万欧元，2023 年收入 22,484 百万欧元、除税后溢利 2,999 百万欧元（均见 2024 年 3 月 17 日港交所公告）；2024 年收入 21,747 百万欧元、除税后溢利 2,443 百万欧元（见 2025 年 3 月 13 日港交所公告）；2025 年收入 16,720 百万欧元、除税后溢利 1,500 百万欧元（见 2026 年 3 月 5 日港交所公告）。毛利率与研发投入未单独披露，故列为 null。以上仅为财务披露数据，本页不构成任何交易推荐。

竞争格局

我们的判断。北京奔驰在中国豪华车市场的主要对手，外资阵营是华晨宝马与一汽奥迪，同时日益面对蔚来、理想、华为鸿蒙智行（问界、享界）、比亚迪腾势与仰望、极氪等本土豪华及新能源品牌的竞争。其优势在于梅赛德斯-奔驰的品牌力，以及较完整的本地化产品矩阵。公司自称是梅赛德斯-奔驰全球生产网络中规模最大、最前沿的乘用车生产基地之一 [1]；奔驰集团北京工厂页只称北京奔驰是该生产网络的重要组成部分 [3]。其短板是连续三年的下滑：收入与除税后溢利在 2023、2024、2025 三年连降，2025 年收入同比 -23.1%、除税后溢利同比 -38.6%，2026 上半年本地生产销量再降 31.1% [6][9]。

我们的判断。下滑背后是电动化切换偏慢叠加国内价格战。2025 年奔驰乘用车在华销量同比 -19.3%，而北京奔驰除税后溢利同比 -38.6%——利润跌幅大于其自身销量跌幅（需注意两者口径不同：前者为奔驰乘用车在华总销量含进口，后者为北京奔驰合资公司整体除税后溢利），显示单车盈利承压 [8][6]。在中国新能源乘用车渗透率于 2025 年首次过半的市场环境下（乘联分会口径全年约 54%），以燃油车型为锚的产品结构存在结构性劣势 [27]。

我们的判断。北京奔驰从第一代 EQ 车型（EQC、EQA、EQB、EQE、EQE SUV）切换到 MMA、MB.EA「纯电（Electric）」车型是核心变量：纯电 CLA 于 2025 年 9 月下线、纯电 GLC SUV 于 2026 年 6 月下线 [29][31]。其能否复苏，取决于这些新车型能否在燃油基本盘不流失的前提下重新抢回份额。

未来展望

公司公开规划。梅赛德斯-奔驰集团表示，到 2027 年将在全球推出超 40 款新车，其中包括多款专为中国市场打造的车型，并称正与中国合作伙伴共同投资超过 140 亿元人民币用于新技术与新产品的研发落地 [17][16]。在北京奔驰，纯电 CLA 260 L 已于 2026 年 4 月上市（起售价 22.9 万元）[16]，纯电 GLC SUV 于 2026 年 7 月上市（鎏金版 33.98 万元、晖银版预售 29.99 万元）[36]。北京奔驰 2026 年 1 月整车累计产量突破 600 万辆，8 月成立 21 周年时累计投产 15 款车型，9 月全新长轴距 GLE SUV 下线；公司把 2026 年定位为「产品大年」和「AI 元年」[23][21][24]。

我们的判断。北京奔驰能否企稳，取决于新纯电车型（纯电 GLC SUV、纯电 CLA、长轴距 GLE SUV）能否补上燃油车型流失的销量，以及 MMA/MB.EA 产品阵能否足够快地投放、与本土新能源头部品牌竞争。2026 上半年本地生产销量同比 -31.1%，三季度奔驰乘用车在华销量再降 30.6%（86,792 辆，上年同期 125,133 辆），显示下滑尚未扭转 [9][41]。一个关键前提是梅赛德斯-奔驰重振中国市场的表态能否转化为在北京奔驰的具体产品与技术投入 [17]。

我们的判断。另一重考量是北京奔驰对北京汽车的核心地位：母公司 2025 年权益持有人应占净利润下滑 87.2%，北京奔驰的持续下滑将直接制约北京汽车的盈利，进而可能影响可供合资公司电动化投入的资源 [4]。

风险

  • 财务连续三年下滑。2025 年收入与除税后溢利连续第三年下滑（收入 -23.1%、除税后溢利 -38.6%），2026 上半年本地生产销量再降 31.1% [6][9]。
  • 中国市场逆风。奔驰乘用车 2026 年三季度在华销量为 86,792 辆，同比 -30.6%（上年同期 125,133 辆）[41]。集团将 2026 年奔驰乘用车调整后销售利润率展望由 3%–5% 下调至 2%–3%，原因同时包括中国市场环境更趋挑战，以及第四季度原材料与产品生命周期措施的成本上升 [42][43]。
  • 价格战与竞争。国内激烈的价格竞争与本土新能源品牌的崛起，持续挤压豪华合资阵营 [8][27]。
  • 技术路线转型风险。向 MMA 与 MB.EA 纯电平台的切换投入大、市场接受度尚未被验证；第一代 EQ 车型（EQC、EQA、EQB、EQE、EQE SUV）已让位于 2025–2026 年才投产的纯电 CLA、纯电 GLC SUV [29][31]。
  • 单一品牌与单一伙伴依赖。北京奔驰的业绩完全取决于梅赛德斯-奔驰品牌，以及北京汽车与梅赛德斯-奔驰集团两方合作的持续 [4]。
  • 母公司利润敏感。北京汽车 2025 年权益持有人应占净利润下滑 87.2%，母公司盈利高度敏感于北京奔驰表现 [4]。
  • 披露有限。作为非上市合资企业，北京奔驰不单独编制完整财务报表，毛利率与研发投入未披露，其经营仅能通过两方股东的报告窥见 [7]。
