Overview

Great Wall Pickup (长城皮卡) is the pickup business and founding business line of Great Wall Motor Company Limited (GWM). It dates to 1996, when the first Deer pickup rolled off the line, with first exports to the Middle East following in 1997 and a first-place ranking in both domestic sales and exports by 1998.[6][13] Media report that in 2018 Great Wall Pickup became an independently operated brand, GWM's fourth vehicle brand, and in 2019 GWM launched the Poer (Cannon) series, which brought passenger-car features such as a multi-link rear suspension and 8AT into China's pickup segment.[12] Today the brand spans the Wingle, Jingangpo, Poer and Shanhaipo families, covering petrol, diesel and Hi4-T/PHEV plug-in-hybrid powertrains.[7]

Per the 2025 annual results, Great Wall Pickup sold 178,936 vehicles in 2025 (+2.22% year on year), including 60,699 exports (+20.98%), with domestic terminal market share near 50%; it has been China's best-selling pickup for 28 consecutive years, with cumulative global sales above 2.85 million units and the Poer line above 800,000.[1][2] In the first nine months of 2026 the brand sold 135,826 vehicles, down 0.29% year on year on the monthly-brief basis.[3] As a brand of a listed automaker, Great Wall Pickup publishes no separate financial statements; this page covers the brand's products, history, leadership and competition, with parent-level financials referenced from GWM's filings.[1]

History

  1. 1996

    The first Deer (迪尔) pickup rolls off the line, starting GWM's pickup business and leading what the brand later calls the '1.0 tool-vehicle era' of China's pickup market.[6][13]

  2. 1997-10

    The first batch of Great Wall pickups is exported to the Middle East, GWM's earliest whole-vehicle exports.[6]

  3. 1998

    Great Wall Pickup ranks first in China's pickup market for the first time, starting the run of consecutive annual championships that reached 28 years by 2025, and the brand describes 1998 as the year it began selling vehicles overseas.[13][18]

  4. 2006

    The Wingle (风骏) pickup is launched; it has since sold for 20 years with cumulative global sales above 1.8 million units, in the brand's framing as the '2.0 multi-purpose commercial era'.[21]

  5. 2018

    Per media reporting, Great Wall Pickup becomes an independently operated brand, GWM's fourth vehicle brand.[12]

  6. 2019-08-18

    The new pickup brand Great Wall Poer (长城炮) is launched, initially covering passenger, commercial and off-road categories.[13]

  7. 2019-08-30

    GWM's Chongqing Yongchuan smart factory is completed and put into production, with the global 'P series' passenger pickup (Poer) rolling off the line; the plant has a planned capacity of 160,000 vehicles and total investment of RMB 4.5 billion.[16]

  8. 2019-09-05

    The Poer passenger pickup goes on sale at the Chengdu auto show, priced from RMB 126,800, positioned as China's first passenger-oriented pickup with multi-link rear suspension and a ZF 8AT.[13]

  9. 2019-10-24

    The Poer commercial pickup goes on sale, priced RMB 97,800-128,800, with petrol and diesel, 2WD/4WD and manual/8AT variants.[13]

  10. 2019-12-05

    The 10,000th Poer rolls off the line at the Chongqing Yongchuan plant, three months after the first unit.[13]

  11. 2022-03-22

    The Jingangpo (King Kong Poer), a fashion-commercial pickup sharing the Poer platform, is launched with a 2.0T + 6MT powertrain.[8]

  12. 2022-05-08

    The 300,000th Poer rolls off the line at the Chongqing smart plant, reaching 300,000 units in 2 years and 8 months.[6]

  13. 2022-10-27

    The CAAM Pickup Branch is established; GWM is elected the first rotating-chairman member and Poer brand CEO Zhang Haobao becomes its first chairman. A day earlier the State Council had called for lifting urban pickup-access restrictions.[9]

  14. 2022-12-30

    The Shanhaipo, a large high-performance luxury pickup, is launched at the Guangzhou auto show with a 3.0T V6 petrol engine and longitudinal 9AT, priced RMB 228,800-301,800.[6]

  15. 2023

    The 500,000th Poer rolls off the line, making it the first Chinese premium pickup brand to pass 500,000 units; full-year sales reach 202,330 vehicles (monthly-brief basis), and the brand leads China's pickup market for a 26th consecutive year.[15]

  16. 2024

    Full-year sales reach 175,057 vehicles (-13.07% YoY) including 50,172 exports (regular-report basis; the monthly brief records 177,100). The brand also rolled out the 2.4T Poer line and the Shanhaipo cross-country edition.[5][14]

  17. 2025-06-23

    The Shanhaipo Hi4-T, positioned as the world's first off-road hybrid pickup and built on GWM's Hi4-T architecture, goes on sale (Global Edition RMB 228,800, Performance Edition RMB 249,800), alongside the Shanhaipo 2.4T Global Edition at RMB 193,800.[11]

  18. 2025

    Full-year sales reach 178,936 vehicles (+2.22% YoY) including 60,699 exports, with domestic terminal market share near 50% and China's pickup sales leadership for a 28th consecutive year; cumulative global sales exceed 2.85 million units and Poer exceeds 800,000.[1][20]

  19. 2026-03

    The new Wingle 7 goes on sale, per media reporting, extending the Wingle line that has sold for 20 years.[21]

  20. 2026-04

    Per media reporting, the Poer Hi4-T series begins pre-sales at the Beijing auto show, extending GWM's Hi4-T off-road hybrid architecture into the Poer line.[12]

Current operations

Model line-up and pricing

As of the research cut-off date, the official Great Wall Pickup China website lists four model families: the Poer series (2026 Commercial Poer, 2026 Passenger Poer, 2026 Off-road Poer, Passenger Poer Hi4-T, Commercial Poer PHEV, Huopao and V6 Huopao), the Shanhaipo series (Passenger, Hi4-T, 2.4T Global, Cross-country and Performance editions), the Jingangpo, and the Wingle 5 and Wingle 7. Official suggested retail starting prices, accessed on 2026-10-29, run from the 2026 Wingle 5 at RMB 69,800 up to the Shanhaipo Performance Edition at RMB 279,800; within the range, the 2026 Commercial Poer starts at RMB 99,800, the 2026 Passenger Poer at RMB 126,800, the 2026 Off-road Poer at RMB 179,800, the Passenger Poer Hi4-T at RMB 176,800, the Commercial Poer PHEV at RMB 149,800, the Jingangpo at RMB 89,800, and the Shanhaipo Hi4-T at RMB 228,800.[7] Plug-in-hybrid (Hi4-T/PHEV) prices are for the whole vehicle including the traction battery; petrol and diesel versions do not carry a traction battery.[7]

Technology and platforms

The Poer is described by media as China's first passenger-oriented pickup, with a multi-link rear suspension, a ZF 8AT and L2 driving assistance, and it is built on GWM's Chongqing Yongchuan smart plant.[13] The Jingangpo shares the Poer platform and is built at the Hubei Jingmen smart plant.[8] The Shanhaipo fuel line uses a self-developed 3.0T V6 petrol engine with a longitudinal 9AT.[5] On the new-energy side, the Shanhaipo Hi4-T is built on GWM's Hi4-T off-road super-hybrid architecture with a Miller-cycle 2.0T engine, a 9HAT and an integrated P2 front motor, delivering combined power of 300 kW and combined torque of 750 N·m; it carries a 37.1 kWh battery with NEDC pure-electric range of 115 km, NEDC combined range of about 1,000 km and combined fuel consumption of 1.7 L/100 km, plus a BorgWarner 4A+Mlock four-wheel-drive system and three locking differentials.[11] Media report the 2026 Poer range is built on GWM's Tank platform, sharing the off-road architecture used by the Tank brand.[18]

Product iterations

In 2024 the brand rolled out the Jingangpo 8AT, the 2.4T Passenger/Commercial/Off-road Poer and the Shanhaipo Cross-country edition, and previewed the Shanhaipo Hi4-T built on the Hi4-T architecture; the Poer alone sold 130,416 units in 2024, its 51st consecutive month above 10,000 units, per media.[14] In 2025 the brand launched the Shanhaipo Hi4-T, the V6 Huopao and the 2026 Poer and 2026 Jingangpo ranges, and the new Wingle 7 followed in March 2026.[18][21]

Brand positioning

Media describe the brand as spanning four tracks: the Wingle series as the commercial-tool base, the Jingangpo as fashion-commercial, the Poer as passenger-leisure, and the Shanhaipo as high-end luxury and performance.[12]

Plants and capacity

The main pickup production base is the Chongqing Yongchuan smart plant, GWM's fifth full-process vehicle base and its first in South China, completed and put into production on 30 August 2019 with a planned capacity of 160,000 vehicles and total investment of RMB 4.5 billion; it produces the Poer 'P series' as well as HAVAL SUVs.[16] By 2025 the Chongqing government described the Yongchuan base as having total investment of RMB 9.88 billion and designed annual capacity of 250,000 pickups and SUVs, claiming one Poer rolls off every 72 seconds and one in four China-built pickups is made in Yongchuan.[17] The Jingangpo and other commercial models are built at the Hubei Jingmen smart plant.[8]

Overseas business

Great Wall Pickup is China's earliest vehicle exporter and has kept the leading position in China-brand pickup exports. In 2025 it exported 60,699 pickups (+20.98% year on year) on the annual-report basis.[1] Media report that the 2.4T Poer was launched in Chile, South Africa, Australia and Peru in 2025, and that the Shanhaipo Hi4-T entered Australia in May 2025 and won a '2025 best new-energy pickup' award from Australian outlet CarExpert; the Shanhaipo HEV won a 'best hybrid pickup' award in Thailand in March 2025, and the Poer took the top spot in Chile's new-pickup sales chart in May 2025.[22] The company said the Shanhaipo Hi4-T will next launch in Thailand and Mexico, and that the Shanhaipo now spans Hi4-T, HEV, 3.0T petrol and 2.4T diesel powertrains.[11]

Policy and market position

The brand has been a key promoter of relaxing China's pickup-access restrictions: GWM was the lead organiser of the CAAM Pickup Branch, established on 27 October 2022, and a State Council notice the day before called for lifting urban pickup-access restrictions.[9] Media report that over 90% of prefecture-level-and-above cities have opened up pickup access. In 2025 the Poer, Wingle and Jingangpo held the top three places among China's pickup models, per the annual results.[2]

Founders and leadership

Founder

Great Wall Motor founder Wei Jianjun is the parent company's chairman and actual controller, and Great Wall Pickup is the business line he built from the first Deer pickup in 1996. The 2025 annual report lists Great Wall Pickup among the group's brands (alongside Haval, WEY, Tank and ORA) and identifies Wei Jianjun as the group's actual controller.[1] Further detail on the founder and the parent group is covered in the Great Wall Motor (gwm) deep research.

Current brand CEO

Zhang Haobao (张昊保) is the CEO of the Great Wall Pickup brand and chairman of the CAAM Pickup Branch. When the CAAM Pickup Branch was founded on 27 October 2022, GWM was elected the first rotating-chairman member and Zhang Haobao, then Poer brand CEO, became its first chairman.[9] Media report that Zhang has more than 20 years in the pickup industry; before joining GWM he worked at Zhengzhou Nissan in roles including deputy head of the marketing-PR department, head of the market-vehicle-type department and head of the market department. He joined GWM in 2019 as pickup marketing director, led the Poer brand launch, and rose through pickup-brand marketing general manager and pickup-brand company general manager to brand CEO.[19][12] GWM has not issued a formal exchange announcement of this appointment, so the exact start date of his CEO title is not disclosed; the reporting is based on media and his public titles.

Brand ownership and governance

Great Wall Pickup is not a separately listed company but a brand operated within Great Wall Motor, whose actual controller is Wei Jianjun. The brand does not separately disclose a board of directors; group governance is covered in the Great Wall Motor (gwm) deep research.[1] A December 2019 media report identified Zhang Jiaming as then general manager of the pickup brand, a different person from Zhang Haobao, speaking at the 10,000th-Poer ceremony.[13]

Competition

Our judgment: competitive scope. Great Wall Pickup competes in China's pickup market and increasingly in export markets. Domestic peers include Jiangling (JMC), Zhengzhou Nissan, Foton, SAIC Maxus and Changan, and in new-energy pickups Geely's Radar; internationally it faces the incumbent leaders Ford and Toyota. This is our analytical peer selection, not a sales ranking.[12] Per CAAM data quoted by media, China's pickup sales were about 548,000 in 2024 (+5.3%), a fourth consecutive year above 500,000, with exports the main growth driver.[10]

Our judgment: strengths. The brand holds a large installed base — 28 consecutive years as China's pickup sales leader, cumulative global sales above 2.85 million units, and the top three China pickup models in 2025 — plus a complete fuel, diesel and Hi4-T hybrid line-up and a first-mover position in passenger-oriented pickups.[2][12][13] Export strength (60,699 vehicles in 2025, +20.98%) provides a second growth axis.[1]

Our judgment: weaknesses and uncertainty. Volume has been flat to declining: 175,057 in 2024 (-13.07%), 178,936 in 2025, and 135,826 in the first nine months of 2026 (-0.29%).[1][5][3] Third-party terminal data cited by media show domestic terminal share fell to 29.7% in the first quarter of 2026 from a long-stable near-50%, as the tool-vehicle base (Wingle) came under pressure and new-energy rivals such as Geely Radar gained share in the only growing sub-segment.[12] The brand's new-energy pickups (Shanhaipo Hi4-T, Poer Hi4-T/PHEV) only began scaling in 2025-2026, later than some rivals.[11][12]

Outlook

Company plans. The brand's stated direction is to advance the new-energy and globalisation of pickups. The Shanhaipo Hi4-T was described at launch as opening the 'first year of pickup new energy', and the company said the model will next enter Thailand and Mexico, with the Shanhaipo family spanning Hi4-T, HEV, 3.0T petrol and 2.4T diesel powertrains.[11] Per media, the Poer Hi4-T series began pre-sales at the April 2026 Beijing auto show to fill the new-energy gap.[12] These are disclosed directions, not completed results.

Our judgment. The near-term question is whether export growth can offset domestic share erosion. Exports rose to 60,699 vehicles in 2025 (+20.98%) and September 2026 sales grew 16.43% year on year on export strength, while domestic terminal share declined.[1][3][12] Restoring volume depends on scaling the Hi4-T/PHEV pickups against Geely Radar and other new-energy entrants and on the strength of the domestic commercial market; the 0.29% nine-month decline does not yet confirm a turnaround.[3][12] Sustained export growth (up 20.98% in 2025) is a structural advantage relative to domestic peers, but converting it into stable overseas share and profit depends on local operations and the trade environment.[1]

Risks

  • Domestic share erosion. Third-party terminal data cited by media show domestic terminal share fell to 29.7% in the first quarter of 2026 from a long-stable near-50%, under weak commercial demand and new-energy competition.[12]
  • Volume decline. Sales were 175,057 vehicles in 2024 (-13.07%) and 135,826 in the first nine months of 2026 (-0.29%).[5][3]
  • New-energy gap. The brand's electric and hybrid pickups only began scaling in 2025-2026, later than rivals such as Geely Radar, whose pure-electric pickups have taken a large share of that sub-segment.[12]
  • Policy dependence. Pickup demand has been supported by the relaxation of urban access restrictions and by policies on scrapping and annual inspection; a reversal would weigh on the category.[9]
  • Dependence on the parent. The brand does not publish separate financials, so its standalone economics cannot be assessed from public filings; its results sit within Great Wall Motor, whose 2025 attributable net profit fell 22.07% year on year.[1]
  • Overseas risks. The parent's 2025 annual report identifies geopolitical conflict and rising trade barriers as sources of uncertainty in overseas markets, which likewise affect pickup exports.[1]

概览

长城皮卡是长城汽车股份有限公司的皮卡业务，也是公司的起家业务。其历史可追溯至 1996 年第一辆迪尔皮卡下线，1997 年首批皮卡出口中东，1998 年即实现国内、出口销量双第一。[6][13] 据媒体报道，2018 年长城皮卡独立运营，成为长城汽车第四大品牌；2019 年公司推出长城炮系列，把多连杆后悬架、8AT 等乘用车技术引入中国皮卡市场。[12] 如今品牌覆盖风骏、金刚炮、长城炮、山海炮四大车系，横跨汽油、柴油与 Hi4-T/PHEV 插电混动多动力路线。[7]

据 2025 年业绩公告，2025 年长城皮卡销售 178,936 辆（同比 +2.22%），其中出口 60,699 辆（同比 +20.98%），国内终端市占率近 50%，连续 28 年蝉联中国皮卡销量冠军，全球累计销量超 285 万辆，其中长城炮超 80 万辆。[1][2] 2026 年前三季度销售 135,826 辆，同比 -0.29%（产销快报口径）。[3] 作为上市车企旗下品牌，长城皮卡不单独披露财务数据；本页覆盖其产品、历史、管理层与竞争格局，母公司层面财务数据以长城汽车公告为参照。[1]

历史沿革

  1. 1996

    第一辆迪尔皮卡下线，开启长城汽车皮卡业务，引领品牌后来所称的中国皮卡「1.0 工具车时代」。[6][13]

  2. 1997-10

    首批长城皮卡出口中东，为长城汽车最早的整车出口。[6]

  3. 1998

    长城皮卡首次夺得中国皮卡市场销量第一，开启连续多年蝉联销冠的纪录（至 2025 年已连续 28 年）；品牌亦将 1998 年视为其整车走向海外市场的起点。[13][18]

  4. 2006

    风骏皮卡上市，此后持续销售 20 年、全球累计销量超 180 万辆，被品牌称为中国皮卡「2.0 多用途商用化时代」。[21]

  5. 2018

    据媒体报道，长城皮卡正式独立运营，成为长城汽车旗下第四大品牌。[12]

  6. 2019-08-18

    皮卡新品牌长城炮正式发布，初期涵盖乘用、商用、越野三大板块。[13]

  7. 2019-08-30

    长城汽车重庆永川智慧工厂建成投产，全球「P 系列」乘用皮卡（长城炮）下线；工厂规划年产能 16 万辆、总投资 45 亿元。[16]

  8. 2019-09-05

    长城炮乘用皮卡在成都车展正式上市，起售价 12.68 万元，定位中国首款多连杆后悬架、采埃孚 8AT 的乘用化皮卡。[13]

  9. 2019-10-24

    长城炮商用皮卡上市（9.78 万-12.88 万元），提供汽/柴油、两/四驱、6MT/8AT 等多种版本。[13]

  10. 2019-12-05

    第 1 万台长城炮在重庆永川智慧工厂下线，距首台下线仅三个月。[13]

  11. 2022-03-22

    与长城炮同平台的时尚商用皮卡金刚炮上市，搭载 2.0T + 6MT。[8]

  12. 2022-05-08

    长城炮第 30 万台整车在重庆智慧工厂下线，从 0 到 30 万辆用时 2 年零 8 个月。[6]

  13. 2022-10-27

    中国汽车工业协会皮卡分会成立，长城汽车当选首届理事长单位，皮卡品牌 CEO 张昊保任首届理事长；此前一日国务院通知要求「推动取消皮卡车进城限制」。[9]

  14. 2022-12-30

    大型高性能豪华皮卡山海炮于广州车展上市，搭载 3.0T V6 汽油发动机 + 纵置 9AT，售价 22.88 万-30.18 万元。[6]

  15. 2023

    长城炮第 50 万台整车下线，成为首个突破 50 万辆的中国高端皮卡品牌；全年销售 202,330 辆（产销快报口径），连续 26 年蝉联中国皮卡销量第一。[15]

  16. 2024

    全年销售 175,057 辆（同比 -13.07%），其中出口 50,172 辆（定期报告口径；产销快报口径 177,100 辆）；年内密集投放 2.4T 长城炮、山海炮穿越版等车型。[5][14]

  17. 2025-06-23

    山海炮 Hi4-T 正式上市，定位全球首款越野混动皮卡、基于长城 Hi4-T 架构打造：全球版 22.88 万元、性能版 24.98 万元；同步上市山海炮 2.4T 全球版 19.38 万元。[11]

  18. 2025

    全年销售 178,936 辆（同比 +2.22%），其中出口 60,699 辆，国内终端市占率近 50%，连续 28 年蝉联中国皮卡销量冠军；全球累计销量超 285 万辆，长城炮超 80 万辆。[1][20]

  19. 2026-03

    据媒体报道，新风骏 7 上市，延续风骏系列 20 年产品生命周期。[21]

  20. 2026-04

    据媒体报道，长城炮 Hi4-T 系列在北京车展开启预售，将长城 Hi4-T 越野混动架构延展至长城炮车系。[12]

现状

车型谱系与定价

截至研究截止日，长城皮卡中国官网列示四大车系：长城炮系列（2026 款商用炮、2026 款乘用炮、2026 款越野炮、乘用炮 Hi4-T、商用炮 PHEV、火炮、V6 火炮），山海炮系列（乘用版、Hi4-T、2.4T 全球版、穿越版、性能版），金刚炮，以及风骏 5、风骏 7。2026-10-29 访问官网的官方建议零售起售价自 2026 款风骏 5 的 6.98 万元起，最高至山海炮性能版 27.98 万元；其中 2026 款商用炮 9.98 万元、2026 款乘用炮 12.68 万元、2026 款越野炮 17.98 万元、乘用炮 Hi4-T 17.68 万元、商用炮 PHEV 14.98 万元、金刚炮 8.98 万元、山海炮 Hi4-T 22.88 万元。[7] 插电混动（Hi4-T/PHEV）车型价格为含动力电池的整车价格，汽油、柴油版不含动力电池。[7]

技术与平台

据媒体报道，长城炮是中国首款乘用化皮卡，配备多连杆后悬架、采埃孚 8AT 与 L2 级辅助驾驶，在长城汽车重庆永川智慧工厂生产。[13] 金刚炮与长城炮同平台，在湖北荆门智慧工厂生产。[8] 山海炮燃油版搭载自研 3.0T V6 汽油发动机 + 纵置 9AT。[5] 新能源方面，山海炮 Hi4-T 基于长城 Hi4-T 越野超级混动架构打造，采用米勒循环 2.0T 发动机 + 9HAT + 集成式 P2 前置电机，综合功率 300kW、综合扭矩 750N·m，搭载 37.1kWh 电池，NEDC 纯电续航 115km、NEDC 综合续航约 1000km、百公里综合油耗 1.7L，并配博格华纳 4A+Mlock 四驱与「真三把锁」。[11] 据媒体报道，2026 款长城炮全系基于长城坦克平台打造，与坦克品牌共享越野架构。[18]

产品迭代

2024 年品牌密集投放金刚炮 8AT、2.4T 乘用炮/商用炮/越野炮、山海炮穿越版等车型，并亮相基于 Hi4-T 架构打造的山海炮 Hi4-T；据媒体报道，仅长城炮 2024 年就销售 130,416 辆、累计 51 个月销量过万。[14] 2025 年品牌先后上市山海炮 Hi4-T、V6 火炮、2026 款长城炮、2026 款金刚炮等车型，2026 年 3 月新风骏 7 上市。[18][21]

品牌定位

据媒体报道，品牌覆盖四大核心赛道：风骏系列主打商用工具，金刚炮主打时尚商用，长城炮主打乘用休闲，山海炮主打高端豪华与性能。[12]

工厂与产能

主要生产基地为重庆永川智慧工厂，是长城汽车全球第五个整车生产基地、南方首个整车基地，2019 年 8 月 30 日建成投产，规划年产能 16 万辆、总投资 45 亿元，主要生产长城炮「P 系列」及哈弗 SUV。[16] 重庆市政府 2025 年介绍，永川基地总投资 98.8 亿元、设计年产皮卡及 SUV 达 25 万辆，号称「每 72 秒下线一台长城炮皮卡，全国每 4 辆皮卡就有 1 辆永川造」。[17] 金刚炮等商用车型在湖北荆门智慧工厂生产。[8]

海外业务

长城皮卡是中国最早的整车出口车企之一，长期保持中国品牌皮卡出口领先。2025 年按年报口径出口 60,699 辆（同比 +20.98%）。[1] 据媒体报道，2025 年 2.4T 长城炮先后在智利、南非、澳洲、秘鲁上市；山海炮 Hi4-T 于 2025 年 5 月登陆澳大利亚并获当地媒体 CarExpert「2025 年度最佳新能源皮卡」奖，山海炮 HEV 于 2025 年 3 月在泰国获「最佳混合动力皮卡」奖，5 月长城炮在智利皮卡新车畅销榜登顶。[22] 公司称山海炮 Hi4-T 后续将在泰国、墨西哥上市，山海炮已构建 Hi4-T 超混、HEV、3.0T 汽油、2.4T 柴油全动力矩阵。[11]

皮卡政策与行业地位

品牌是皮卡进城限制解禁的重要推动者：长城汽车是中汽协皮卡分会筹备牵头单位，该分会于 2022 年 10 月 27 日成立；此前一日国务院通知要求「推动取消皮卡车进城限制」。[9] 据媒体报道，全国超 90% 地级以上城市已放开皮卡进城限制。2025 年长城炮、风骏、金刚炮包揽国内皮卡单车销量前三，见 2025 年业绩公告。[2]

创始人与管理层

创始人

长城汽车创始人魏建军现任母公司董事长、实际控制人，长城皮卡即其自 1996 年第一辆迪尔皮卡起家打造的业务。2025 年年报将长城皮卡列为集团旗下品牌之一（与哈弗、魏牌、坦克、欧拉等并列），并认定魏建军为集团实际控制人。[1] 创始人及母公司更详细信息见长城汽车（gwm）深度研究。

现任品牌 CEO

张昊保现任长城汽车皮卡品牌 CEO、中国汽车工业协会皮卡分会理事长。2022 年 10 月 27 日中汽协皮卡分会成立，长城汽车当选首届理事长单位，时任长城汽车皮卡品牌 CEO 的张昊保任首届理事长。[9] 据媒体报道，张昊保深耕皮卡行业超 20 年，加入长城汽车前曾长期供职郑州日产，历任市场公关部副部长、市场车型部部长、市场部部长等职务；2019 年加入长城汽车任皮卡营销总监，主导长城炮品牌发布，后历任皮卡品牌营销总经理、皮卡品牌公司总经理，晋升至现任皮卡品牌 CEO。[19][12] 长城汽车未就该任命发布交易所正式公告，其 CEO 职务的精确上任日期未披露，相关表述依据媒体报道与其公开头衔。

品牌归属与治理

长城皮卡并非单独上市公司，而是长城汽车旗下品牌，长城汽车实际控制人为魏建军。品牌不单独披露董事会，集团治理详见长城汽车（gwm）深度研究。[1] 2019 年 12 月的一则媒体报道称，张佳明时任长城汽车皮卡品牌总经理，在长城炮第 1 万台下线仪式上致辞；这是与张昊保不同的前任品牌负责人。[13]

长城皮卡为长城汽车股份有限公司旗下品牌，未单独披露品牌层面的财务数据（营收、毛利率、净利润、研发投入、现金流等），故 financials.rows 为空。因不存在品牌层面的净利润，也就无从区分「归属于母公司股东」或「归属于普通股股东」的口径。作为参照，母公司长城汽车 2025 年年度报告（中国企业会计准则）披露营业总收入 2,228.24 亿元（同比增长 10.20%）、归属于上市公司股东的净利润 98.65 亿元（同比下降 22.07%）。母公司财务数据见长城汽车（gwm）深度研究。本页不构成任何交易推荐。

竞争格局

我们的判断：竞争范围。长城皮卡参与中国皮卡市场竞争，并日益进入出口市场。国内对手包括江铃（JMC）、郑州日产、福田、上汽大通、长安，新能源皮卡领域有吉利雷达；海外则面对福特、丰田等传统皮卡巨头。这是分析所选的竞争范围，不是销量排名。[12] 据中汽协会数据（经媒体引用），2024 年中国皮卡销量约 54.8 万辆、同比增长 5.3%，连续四年超 50 万辆，出口为主要增量来源。[10]

我们的判断：优势。品牌拥有庞大存量基础——连续 28 年蝉联中国皮卡销量第一、全球累计销量超 285 万辆、2025 年包揽皮卡单车销量前三，并具备燃油、柴油、Hi4-T 混动完整矩阵与乘用化皮卡先发优势。[2][12][13] 出口（2025 年 60,699 辆、同比 +20.98%）构成第二增长轴。[1]

我们的判断：短板与不确定性。销量呈持平或下滑：2024 年 175,057 辆（-13.07%）、2025 年 178,936 辆、2026 年前三季度 135,826 辆（-0.29%）。[1][5][3] 据媒体引用的第三方终端数据，2026 年一季度国内终端市占率已从长期稳定的近 50% 跌至 29.7%：工具车基本盘（风骏）承压，吉利雷达等新能源竞品在唯一增长的细分市场抢占份额。[12] 品牌的新能源皮卡（山海炮 Hi4-T、长城炮 Hi4-T/PHEV）自 2025-2026 年才逐步上量，晚于部分对手。[11][12]

未来展望

公司公开规划。品牌披露的方向是推进皮卡新能源化与全球化。山海炮 Hi4-T 上市时被表述为开启「皮卡新能源元年」，公司称该车后续将在泰国、墨西哥上市，山海炮已构建 Hi4-T 超混、HEV、3.0T 汽油、2.4T 柴油全动力矩阵。[11] 据媒体报道，长城炮 Hi4-T 系列于 2026 年 4 月北京车展开启预售以补齐新能源短板。[12] 以上为公司披露的方向，不是已实现的业绩。

我们的判断。近期的关键问题是出口增长能否抵消国内份额的收缩。2025 年出口增至 60,699 辆（+20.98%），2026 年 9 月销量在出口拉动下同比增长 16.43%，而同期国内终端份额下滑。[1][3][12] 恢复销量取决于 Hi4-T/PHEV 皮卡能否在与吉利雷达等新能源入局者的竞争中上量，以及国内商用市场需求能否回暖；前三季度 0.29% 的下滑尚不足以确认拐点。[3][12] 出口的持续高增长（2025 年品牌出口同比增长 20.98%）是相对国内同行的结构性优势，但能否转化为稳定的海外份额与利润，取决于本地化运营与贸易环境。[1]

风险

  • 国内份额收缩。据媒体引用的第三方终端数据，2026 年一季度国内终端市占率从长期稳定的近 50% 跌至 29.7%，主因是商用需求疲软与新能源竞争。[12]
  • 销量下滑。2024 年销售 175,057 辆（-13.07%），2026 年前三季度 135,826 辆（-0.29%）。[5][3]
  • 新能源短板。品牌的纯电、混动皮卡自 2025-2026 年才逐步上量，晚于吉利雷达等对手，后者的纯电皮卡已占据该细分市场较大份额。[12]
  • 政策依赖。皮卡需求长期受益于进城限制放宽及报废、年检等政策，若政策收紧将拖累品类。[9]
  • 依赖母公司。品牌未单独披露财务，无法从公开文件评估其独立盈利能力；其经营成果并入长城汽车，而母公司 2025 年归母净利润同比下降 22.07%。[1]
  • 海外风险。母公司 2025 年年报将国际地缘冲突、贸易壁垒增多列为海外市场不确定性来源，同样影响皮卡出口。[1]
