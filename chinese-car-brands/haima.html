Overview

Haima Automobile Co., Ltd. (SZSE: 000572) is a privately-owned passenger-vehicle manufacturer headquartered in Haikou, Hainan, within the Hainan Free Trade Port. Its main business is the R&D, manufacture and sale of vehicles and powertrains, plus auto finance through its subsidiary Haima Finance Co., Ltd. [1]. In FY2025 it reported operating revenue of RMB1.849 billion (total operating income RMB1.876 billion including financial services) and a net loss attributable to owners of the parent of RMB148.42 million — its fourth consecutive loss-making year; it sold 17,025 vehicles, of which 16,811 were exported, with exports heavily concentrated in the Middle East [1][7].

Haima traces its roots to the Hainan Automobile Stamping Plant founded in 1988; it once built Mazda-based models such as the Premacy and Familia before turning to its own brand after the Mazda partnership ended in 2006 [5]. Founder Jing Zhu is the actual controller; the controlling shareholders are Haima Investment Group and its concert party Haima (Shanghai) Investment, holding 34.46% combined [3]. After peaking at 216,468 vehicles in 2016, sales have fallen sharply; capacity utilisation is now around 6%, and the company is pivoting toward hydrogen, B-end mobility and contract manufacturing, establishing the Chujie R&D joint venture with battery maker Chuneng in September 2026 [9][10].

History

    The Hainan Automobile Stamping Plant is established and renamed the Hainan Automobile Manufacturing Plant in the same year, following the founding of Hainan province as a special economic zone.[5]

    Haima's first complete vehicle, the HMC6470, rolls off the line, filling Hainan's gap in automobile production.[5]

  3. 1992

    Hainan Mazda Automobile Co., Ltd. is established with Mazda on a technology-cooperation basis; assembly of Mazda vehicles begins the following year.[5]

  4. 1994-08-08

    The listed shell (Hainan Jinpan Industrial Co., Ltd.) is listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange under code 000572.[6]

  5. 1997-06

    Jing Zhu, aged 31, takes over as head of the Hainan Automobile Manufacturing Plant, launching Haima's second phase of entrepreneurship.[5]

    FAW-Haima Automobile Co., Ltd. is formed by merging FAW's and Haima's Hainan auto assets into China's first mixed-ownership automaker.[5]

  12. 2007

    The listed company is renamed Haima Automobile Group Co., Ltd., with the stock short name changed from 'Haima Shares' to 'Haima Auto'.[6]

Current operations

Business structure

Haima's main business is manufacturing and selling vehicles and powertrains, plus auto finance. In FY2025 the automotive-manufacturing segment contributed revenue of RMB1.751 billion, or 94.67% of operating revenue; overseas sales accounted for 80.46% of operating revenue and domestic sales 19.54% [1]. Customer concentration is extreme: the top five customers made up 94.23% of sales, with the largest, Green City General Trading LLC, alone accounting for 74.12% [1]. Battery-electric vehicles remained negligible in 2025, with production and sales of 110 and 108 units respectively and sales revenue of RMB11.49 million [1]. Headcount fell 24.48% year on year to 1,684 employees at end-2025 [7].

Products and technology

Disclosed main models are the Haima 7X seven-seat MPV, the 7X-E battery-electric MPV (510 km CLTC range) [16], the 7X-H hydrogen fuel-cell MPV and the 8S compact SUV, plus the S5 Pro and S7 Pro [1]. The company holds production qualifications for conventional fuel, battery-electric and hydrogen passenger vehicles — a scarce combination — and both its Haikou and Zhengzhou bases are qualified for fuel and NEV production [10]. On hydrogen, Haima signed a strategic framework agreement with Toyota Motor (China) in March 2023; two demonstration projects entered operation in March and September 2024, and by end-2024 the first batch of 50 hydrogen vehicles had each covered about 16,000 km on average [5][2]. The Zhengzhou base develops B-end mobility products: the Haima EX00 'Box' car has been launched, and the EX02 battery-swap model obtained an MIIT product announcement in November 2025 [1].

Factories and capacity

Haima has two passenger-vehicle bases with stamping, welding, painting and final-assembly processes — Haikou (Hainan) and Zhengzhou (Henan) — with a combined originally-planned capacity of 300,000 units per year (150,000 each); 2025 sales of 17,025 units imply roughly 6% utilisation [10]. As of end-June 2026, the Zhengzhou segment had total assets of RMB1.642 billion and total liabilities of RMB1.588 billion [10]. On 22 April 2026, controlling subsidiary Hainan Haima Automobile signed an agreement to contribute buildings and structures, machinery and land-use rights to its wholly-owned subsidiary Hainan Haima Park Operation Management Co., Ltd., increasing that company's registered capital by RMB405,055,309, in order to revitalise base assets [19]. Cailian Press quoted a Zhengzhou-base employee on 21 October 2026 as saying that, once the cooperation with Chery ends, the base would next contract-manufacture for a Hubei company; Sina Finance reprinted the report on 23 October 2026 [18].

Overseas business

Haima's sales are now almost entirely export-driven: in 2025, 16,811 of 17,025 vehicles were sold overseas and only 214 domestically, with exports heavily concentrated in the Middle East [1]. Its largest customer, Green City General Trading LLC, accounted for 74.12% of sales [1]. While consolidating its traditional Middle East market, the company has been pushing products into Southeast Asia and other emerging markets [2]. That concentration left it exposed when geopolitical conflict disrupted H1 2026 order shipment and payment collection, cutting export volume 66.43% [4].

Financial position

At end-2025 Haima's total assets were RMB4.919 billion (-11.86% year on year) with a debt-to-asset ratio of 55.25%; net assets attributable to the parent were RMB1.607 billion, or RMB0.98 per share [1]. Given the parent company's negative undistributed profit, the board proposed no cash dividend, no bonus shares and no capitalisation of reserves for 2025 [7].

Sales trajectory and latest results

Haima's sales were 27,957 vehicles in 2023 (+14.62% YoY) and fell to 15,497 in 2024, with exports the overwhelming majority [1][2]. The deterioration accelerated in 2026. For H1 2026, the interim report shows operating revenue of RMB314.27 million (-52.04% YoY) and a net loss attributable to the parent of RMB97.36 million; export volume fell 66.43% and overseas sales revenue fell 64.55% to RMB173.09 million as geopolitical conflict disrupted order shipment and payment collection [4]. For the first nine months of 2026, the Q3 report shows total operating income of RMB436 million (-65.78% YoY) and a net loss attributable to the parent of RMB112 million, versus a RMB74.44 million loss a year earlier; operating cash flow turned positive at RMB23.38 million [3].

Founders and leadership

Founder

Jing Zhu (景柱), born 1966, is Haima's founder and actual controller. He joined the Hainan Automobile Stamping Plant as a technician in 1988, rising to workshop director, assistant plant head and acting plant head; from June 1997 he served as head (and party secretary) of the Hainan Automobile Manufacturing Plant, and from January 1998 to January 2001 as general manager and party secretary of FAW-Hainan Automobile. He founded the private Haima Group in 2000 and was its chairman from December 2004 to December 2006. He holds a doctorate in management science and engineering from Hunan University and was a postdoctoral researcher in applied economics at Peking University, later serving as a full professor and doctoral supervisor at Hunan University. He returned as Haima Automobile chairman in May 2019 [1][5].

Shareholders and governance

According to the Q3 2026 report, the largest shareholders are Haima (Shanghai) Investment Co., Ltd. at 28.80% and Haima Investment Group Co., Ltd. at 5.66%; the two concert parties together hold 34.46% and are the controlling shareholders [3]. Jing Zhu holds 73% of Haima Investment Group, which in turn wholly owns Haima (Shanghai) Investment, making him the actual controller [11].

Competition

Main competitors

In its domestic heyday Haima competed in the mainstream family-sedan and SUV segments against brands such as Geely, Chery and GAC Trumpchi; today its MPV and SUV models (7X, 8S) compete in crowded self-brand segments, while its export business competes with other Chinese exporters in the Middle East and Southeast Asia. In 2016 it sold 216,468 vehicles; by 2025 it sold 17,025, a fraction of the monthly volume of leading domestic brands [9][1].

Strengths (our assessment)

  • Scarce production qualifications: Haima holds fuel, battery-electric and hydrogen passenger-vehicle qualifications across two bases, an asset increasingly valuable after MIIT largely stopped issuing new NEV passenger-vehicle qualifications from 2022 [10].
  • Contract-manufacturing experience: its Zhengzhou base OEM-built the Xpeng G3 from 2017 to 2021, giving it a proven line and trained workforce [10].
  • Hainan Free Trade Port location and an early hydrogen partnership with Toyota [5].

Weaknesses (our assessment)

  • Tiny scale and idle capacity: 2025 sales of 17,025 vehicles versus 300,000-unit planned capacity, about 6% utilisation [10].
  • Extreme customer concentration: one customer, Green City General Trading LLC, was 74.12% of 2025 sales [1].
  • Four consecutive loss-making years (2022-2025) and shrinking R&D (expensed R&D fell from RMB211.93 million in 2023 to RMB109.47 million in 2025) [1].
  • Negligible electric-vehicle scale: only 108 battery-electric units sold in 2025, so Haima is not yet a meaningful player in China's NEV transition [1].

Our assessment: Haima has been marginalised out of the mainstream passenger-vehicle market. The disclosed figures describe a company shifting from volume sales toward exporting to a single Middle East buyer, monetising its production qualifications and plants through contract manufacturing, and betting on hydrogen and B-end mobility. The 66.43% year-on-year fall in H1 2026 export volume highlights the fragility of that export concentration [4].

Outlook

Company targets and plans

In its 2024 annual report, Haima described its approach as stabilising existing export business, expanding contract manufacturing and exploring new-energy transformation, and its 2026 interim report frames operations around cost reduction, stable operations and improving efficiency [2][4]. On hydrogen, the company said it remains in the demonstration phase with limited impact on results [5]. Through the Chujie joint venture, media report that partner Chuneng's first vehicle is positioned in the RMB150,000–200,000 range and is aimed at mass production around mid-2027, with Haima expected to provide qualifications and manufacturing [10].

Our analysis

Our assessment: Haima's near-term fortunes depend mainly on whether its Middle East export orders and payments recover from the geopolitical disruption that cut H1 2026 export volume by 66.43% and pushed nine-month revenue down 65.78% [4][3]. The Chujie partnership could fill idle Zhengzhou capacity and generate contract income, but the joint venture is registered for R&D and NEV sales without in-house production, and the target model is not expected to mass-produce before 2027 [10]. Hydrogen remains a demonstration rather than a commercial business. None of these paths is yet proven to restore sustainable profit. The 2026 interim report sets the operating keynote as “cost reduction, stable operations and higher efficiency”, and separately says the company will keep revitalising existing assets; the 2025 annual report states the Zhengzhou base's goal as “survive, seek development and return to profit” [4][1].

Risks

  • Customer concentration: the top five customers were 94.23% of 2025 sales, and the largest, Green City General Trading LLC, 74.12% [1].
  • Geopolitical risk: H1 2026 export volume fell 66.43% as international conflict disrupted order shipment and payment collection, and overseas payment channels were strained; nine-month revenue fell 65.78% [4][3].
  • Financial risk: four consecutive loss-making years (2022-2025); net assets attributable to the parent fell from RMB1.897 billion (end-2023) to RMB1.607 billion (end-2025) and further to RMB1.510 billion (end-June 2026), while 2025 operating cash flow was RMB-403.64 million [1][4].
  • Scale risk: about 6% capacity utilisation means revenue cannot cover costs, and the company keeps shrinking headcount and R&D [10].
  • Technology-route risk: hydrogen fuel-cell business is still in demonstration and contributes little, while battery-electric sales were only 108 units in 2025 [1].
  • Regulatory risk: NEV production-qualification rules could affect how Haima monetises its qualifications through partnerships; the company's own disclosures flag competitive and policy pressures [10].

概览

海马汽车股份有限公司（深交所 000572）是总部位于海南省海口市（地处海南自贸港）的民营乘用车制造企业，主营业务为汽车及汽车动力总成的研发、制造、销售，以及通过子公司海马财务开展的汽车金融服务 [1]。2025 财年实现营业收入 18.49 亿元（营业总收入 18.76 亿元，含金融服务收入），归属于上市公司股东的净利润为 -1.48 亿元，为连续第四年亏损；全年整车销量 17,025 辆，其中境外销量 16,811 辆，出口高度依赖中东市场 [1][7]。

海马前身为 1988 年成立的海南汽车冲压件厂，曾与马自达合作生产普力马、福美来等车型，2006 年与马自达合作到期后转为自主品牌 [5]。公司实际控制人为创始人景柱，控股股东为海马投资集团有限公司及其一致行动人海马（上海）投资有限公司（合计持股 34.46%）[3]。公司销量自 2016 年峰值 21.65 万辆持续下滑，目前产能利用率约 6%，正处于向氢能、B 端出行与对外加工制造转型的阶段，并于 2026 年 9 月与电池企业楚能合资成立楚界研发公司 [9][10]。

历史沿革

  1. 1988

    海南汽车冲压件厂成立，同年更名为海南汽车制造厂，乘海南建省办经济特区之势起步。[5]

  2. 1990-10

    海马第一款整车 HMC6470 下线，填补海南省汽车生产空白。[5]

  3. 1992

    海南马自达汽车有限公司成立，与日本马自达以技术合作方式合作，次年开始组装销售马自达汽车。[5]

  4. 1994-08-08

    上市主体（原海南金盘实业股份有限公司）在深交所挂牌交易，证券代码 000572。[6]

  5. 1997-06

    31 岁的景柱临危受命出任海南汽车制造厂厂长，开启海马第二次创业。[5]

  6. 1998

    国家财政部以资产划转方式将海南汽车制造厂国有资产 100% 划转给一汽集团，更名一汽海南汽车有限公司。[5]

  7. 2000

    民营海马集团成立，与日本马自达再次合作。[5]

  8. 2001

    普力马上市，为国内第一款 SMPV（小型 MPV），迅速占据细分市场龙头。[5]

  9. 2002

    福美来上市，与凯越、伊兰特并称家用轿车「新三样」；海马集团收购面临退市的 *ST 琼金盘，并将民营海马汽车公司装入上市公司。[5]

  10. 2004

    海马集团与一汽集团在海南的汽车资产合并，成立混合所有制的一汽海马汽车有限公司。[5]

  11. 2006

    海马自主品牌亮相，与马自达合作到期后转向自主开发，并启动国际化战略。[5]

  12. 2007

    海马收购郑州轻型汽车制造厂（现海马新能源汽车有限公司）；1 月上市公司由海南金盘实业更名海马投资集团股份有限公司。[5][6]

  13. 2011-05-12

    上市公司更名海马汽车集团股份有限公司，证券简称由「海马股份」变更为「海马汽车」。[6]

  14. 2016

    海马销量达到历史峰值 21.65 万辆。[9][13]

  15. 2017

    销量跌至 14.04 万辆（-35.13%）；海马郑州基地开始为小鹏汽车代工 G3（合作延续至 2021 年）。[9][10][13]

  16. 2019-05-16

    因 2017、2018 连续两年亏损，2019 年 4 月被实施退市风险警示（*ST 海马）；5 月 16 日董事会选举创始人景柱为董事长，开启第四次创业；同期公司开始变卖海口、上海共 401 套闲置房产「保壳」。5 月 15 日的公告是出售海口房产，不是董事长选举。[1][9][17]

  17. 2019-06-26

    上市公司更名为海马汽车股份有限公司（Haima Automobile Co.,Ltd.）。[6]

  18. 2021

    海马向海马投资旗下企业出售金盘实业 100% 股权与郑州兰马实业 95% 股权，为归母净利润增加约 3.02 亿元，实现账面扭亏。[9]

  19. 2022

    海马全年归母净利润亏损 15.74 亿元，为有记录以来最深年度亏损。[2][12]

  20. 2023-03

    海马与丰田汽车（中国）投资有限公司签署氢燃料电池汽车研发与产业化战略合作框架协议，并在海口启动氢燃料电池汽车示范运营。[5][9]

  21. 2023

    全年销量回升至 27,957 辆（同比 +14.62%），产量 30,371 台（+28.33%）。[2][12]

  22. 2024

    销量下滑至 1.55 万辆，出口仍为绝对主力；海马新能源郑州资产托管给郑州国资经营（为期五年）。[2][10]

  23. 2025

    全年销量 17,025 辆（同比 +9.86%），几乎全部为出口（16,811 辆）；营业收入 18.49 亿元，归母净亏损 1.48 亿元，为连续第四年亏损。[1][7]

  24. 2026-09

    郑州楚界汽车研发有限公司注册成立，海马汽车有限公司持股 51%、武汉楚能乘用车有限公司持股 49%，从事汽车零部件研发与新能源汽车整车销售，为与电池企业楚能新能源的合作。[10]

现状

业务结构

海马主业为汽车及动力总成的制造销售，外加汽车金融。2025 财年汽车制造板块收入 17.51 亿元，占营业收入的 94.67%；境外销售占营业收入的 80.46%，境内销售占 19.54% [1]。客户集中度极高：前五大客户占年度销售总额的 94.23%，最大客户 Green City General Trading LLC 一家即占 74.12% [1]。2025 年纯电动汽车产销仅 110 辆、108 辆，销售收入 1,148.84 万元，规模极小 [1]。截至 2025 年末员工总数 1,684 人，同比减少 24.48% [7]。

产品与技术

披露的主力产品为海马 7X（七座 MPV）、7X-E（纯电 MPV，CLTC 续航 510 公里） [16]、7X-H（氢燃料电池 MPV）、8S（紧凑型 SUV），以及 S5 Pro、S7 Pro [1]。公司拥有传统燃油乘用车、纯电动乘用车和氢能乘用车三种生产资质，海口、郑州两大基地均具备燃油车与新能源汽车生产资质 [10]。氢能方面，海马 2023 年 3 月与丰田汽车（中国）签署战略合作框架协议，两个示范运营项目于 2024 年 3 月、9 月投入运营，截至 2024 年底首批 50 台氢能车单台平均行驶里程约 1.6 万公里 [5][2]。郑州基地则开发 B 端出行产品：海马 EX00「盒子汽车」已上市发布，EX02 换电车型于 2025 年 11 月取得工信部产品公告 [1]。

工厂与产能

海马拥有海口（海南）、郑州（河南）两个具备冲压、焊装、涂装、总装四大工艺的乘用车生产基地，原有规划产能合计 30 万辆/年（各 15 万辆），但 2025 年 17,025 辆的销量仅相当于约 6% 的产能利用率 [10]。截至 2026 年 6 月末，郑州分部资产总额 16.42 亿元、负债总额 15.88 亿元 [10]。2026 年 4 月 22 日，控股子公司海南海马汽车与其全资子公司海南海马园区运营管理有限公司签订增资协议，以房屋建（构）筑物、机器设备和土地使用权增资 405,055,309 元，用于盘活基地资产 [19]。另据财联社 2026 年 10 月 21 日对郑州基地工作人员的采访（新浪财经 2026-10-23 转载），结束与奇瑞的合作后，郑州基地接下来将为一家湖北企业代工 [18]。

海外业务

海马如今几乎完全依赖出口：2025 年 17,025 辆销量中境外 16,811 辆、境内仅 214 辆，出口高度集中于中东 [1]。其最大客户 Green City General Trading LLC 一家即占销售额的 74.12% [1]。公司在巩固传统中东市场的同时，产品已推向东南亚等新兴市场 [2]。这一集中度使其在地缘冲突导致 2026 年上半年订单发运与回款受阻时首当其冲，出口销量同比下滑 66.43% [4]。

财务状况

截至 2025 年末，海马总资产 49.19 亿元（同比 -11.86%），资产负债率 55.25%，归属于上市公司股东的净资产 16.07 亿元，每股净资产 0.98 元 [1]。因母公司未分配利润为负，2025 年度拟不派发现金红利、不送红股、不进行公积金转增股本 [7]。

销量轨迹与最新业绩

海马 2023 年销量 27,957 辆（同比 +14.62%），2024 年降至 15,497 辆，出口为绝对主力 [1][2]。进入 2026 年后下滑加速。半年报显示，2026 年上半年实现营业收入 3.14 亿元（同比 -52.04%），归母净利润亏损 9,736.48 万元；受国际地缘政治冲突影响，订单发运与销售回款受阻，出口销量同比下降 66.43%，境外销售收入 1.73 亿元、同比下降 64.55% [4]。三季报显示，2026 年前三季度营业总收入 4.36 亿元（同比 -65.78%），归母净利润亏损 1.12 亿元（上年同期亏损 7,443.71 万元），经营活动现金流量净额转正为 2,338.29 万元 [3]。

创始人与管理层

创始人

景柱（1966 年生）是海马创始人兼实际控制人。1988 年进入海南汽车冲压件厂任技术员，历任车间主任、厂长助理、代理厂长；1997 年 6 月起任海南汽车制造厂厂长、党委书记，1998 年 1 月至 2001 年 1 月任一汽海南汽车有限公司总经理、党委书记。2000 年创办民营海马集团，2004 年 12 月至 2006 年 12 月任海马集团董事长。他获湖南大学管理科学与工程专业全日制博士学位，并做过北京大学应用经济学博士后，后任湖南大学全职教授、博士生导师。2019 年 5 月回任海马汽车董事长至今 [1][5]。

股权与治理

据 2026 年三季报，前两大股东为海马（上海）投资有限公司（持股 28.80%）与海马投资集团有限公司（持股 5.66%），二者为一致行动人、合计持股 34.46%，为公司控股股东 [3]。景柱持有海马投资集团 73% 股权，海马投资集团又全资持有海马（上海）投资，从而形成景柱为实际控制人的控制链条 [11]。

竞争格局

主要对手

在其国内鼎盛期，海马在家用轿车与 SUV 市场与吉利、奇瑞、广汽传祺等自主品牌竞争；如今其 7X（MPV）、8S（SUV）在拥挤的自主品牌细分市场参与竞争，出口业务则在中东、东南亚与中国其他出口车企竞争。2016 年海马销量 21.65 万辆，到 2025 年仅 17,025 辆，不及头部自主品牌单月销量 [9][1]。

优势（我们的判断）

  • 稀缺生产资质：公司拥有燃油、纯电、氢能三种乘用车生产资质，覆盖两个基地，在 2022 年起工信部原则上不再新增新能源乘用车资质的背景下更具价值 [10]。
  • 代工经验：郑州基地 2017—2021 年为小鹏汽车代工 G3，拥有成熟产线与造车工人 [10]。
  • 海南自贸港区位，以及与丰田较早布局的氢能合作 [5]。

短板（我们的判断）

  • 规模极小、产能闲置：2025 年销量 17,025 辆，对比 30 万辆规划产能，利用率约 6% [10]。
  • 客户集中度极高：单一客户 Green City General Trading LLC 占 2025 年销售额 74.12% [1]。
  • 连续四年亏损（2022—2025），研发收缩（费用化研发费用由 2023 年 2.12 亿元降至 2025 年 1.09 亿元）[1]。
  • 电动车规模几乎为零：2025 年纯电动汽车仅销售 108 辆，尚未成为中国新能源转型中的有效参与者 [1]。

我们的判断：海马已被边缘化出主流乘用车市场。披露数据呈现的是一家从「以量卖车」转向「对单一中东买家出口、以生产资质和厂房承接代工、押注氢能与 B 端出行」的企业。2026 年上半年出口销量同比下滑 66.43%，凸显这种出口集中的脆弱性 [4]。

未来展望

公司目标与规划

2024 年年报将公司经营思路描述为稳定现有出口业务、拓展对外加工业务、探索新能源转型；2026 年半年报则把经营总基调定位为「降成本、稳经营、提人效」[2][4]。氢能方面，公司称仍处于示范阶段、对业绩影响较小 [5]。通过楚界合资，有消息称，合作方楚能的首款车型定位 15 万—20 万元区间，目标在 2027 年年中前后量产上市，海马预计提供资质与制造能力 [10]。

我们的分析

我们的判断：海马近期的命运主要取决于其中东出口订单与回款能否从地缘冲突冲击中恢复——该冲击使 2026 年上半年出口销量下滑 66.43%、前三季度营收下滑 65.78% [4][3]。楚界合作有望填满郑州闲置产能并带来代工收入，但该合资公司注册为研发与新能源整车销售、不含整车生产，且目标车型预计 2027 年才量产 [10]。氢能仍是示范而非商业化业务。以上路径均尚未被证明能恢复可持续盈利。2026 年半年报把经营总基调写为「降成本、稳经营、提人效」，并另写持续盘活存量资产；2025 年年报对郑州基地的经营目标是「保生存、谋发展、扭亏为盈」 [4][1]。

风险

  • 客户集中风险：2025 年前五大客户占销售总额 94.23%，最大客户 Green City General Trading LLC 占 74.12% [1]。
  • 地缘政治风险：2026 年上半年出口销量下滑 66.43%，国际地缘冲突影响订单发运与销售回款，外汇结算通道不畅；前三季度营收下滑 65.78% [4][3]。
  • 财务风险：连续四年亏损（2022—2025）；归母净资产由 2023 年末 18.97 亿元降至 2025 年末 16.07 亿元，2026 年 6 月末进一步降至 15.10 亿元，2025 年经营活动现金流量净额为 -4.04 亿元 [1][4]。
  • 规模风险：产能利用率约 6%，营收难以覆盖成本，公司持续压缩人员与研发 [10]。
  • 技术路线风险：氢燃料电池业务仍处示范阶段、贡献有限，纯电动汽车 2025 年销量仅 108 辆 [1]。
  • 政策与资质风险：新能源汽车生产资质管理规定可能影响海马通过合作变现资质的路径；公司披露亦提示竞争与政策压力 [10]。
