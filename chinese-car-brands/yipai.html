Overview

Dongfeng Yipai (eπ) is the mainstream new-energy vehicle brand of Dongfeng Motor Corporation, one of China's centrally administered automotive SOEs. Dongfeng released the brand in Wuhan on 10 November 2023 as a mainstream technology electric brand, unveiling the eπ007 sedan as its first model. Xinhua describes the target as the RMB 150,000-250,000 mainstream market; CNR describes it as the RMB 200,000-class market [13][14]. In June 2025, Dongfeng merged Yipai together with its Fengshen (Aeolus) and Nami (Nammi) brands into Dongfeng Yipai Automobile Technology Co. (Yipai Technology), folding Nami into Yipai to run the group's mainstream passenger-vehicle business under one management [2][11]. The line-up spans the eπ007/007+ sedans, the eπ008 family SUV and the M8 six-seat flagship, plus the Nami entry-EV series; in 2025 the Yipai brand (including Nami) sold 138,000 vehicles, up 20% [4]. The integrated Yipai Technology sold 275,752 vehicles in 2025 and delivered 132,900 units in the first half of 2026, up 21% [8][7]. The brand does not disclose standalone financials and is not separately listed; the parent group is covered in the Dongfeng Motor Group deep-dive [1].

Current operations

Brand structure

Since 26 June 2025, the Yipai, Fengshen and Nami brands have been run under Yipai Technology, which consolidates product planning, R&D, production, supply chain, sales and service; Nami was merged into Yipai, so the Yipai brand now spans the eπ series and the Nami entry-EV series, while Fengshen stays a separate division focused on global energy-efficient vehicles [2][11][18]. General manager Wang Junjun frames the strategy as 'Yipai upward, Fengshen renewed' [2]. Per Beijing News (贝壳财经), the Yipai brand sits in the RMB 100,000-200,000 band, Nami is positioned under RMB 100,000, and Fengshen is concentrated in RMB 100,000-150,000 [19].

Models and pricing

The line-up is led by the eπ007/007+ sedans, the eπ008 five- and six-seat family SUV and the M8 six-seat flagship, with the Nami 01 and 06 entry EVs listed on the same official site [2]. The eπ007 launched in March 2024 at RMB 159,600-234,600 (the top figure is a fully-loaded price), and the eπ008 in June 2024 at RMB 216,600 for both versions, with a limited-time benefit price of RMB 188,600. The M8 launched on 23 July 2026: Dongfeng's official site states a limited-time super trade-in price from RMB 169,800, while Economic Observer separately reports a guide price of RMB 179,800-219,800 and a limited-time benefit price from RMB 165,800. Those are two price statements, not one official package [1][16][5][22].

Technology

The brand is built on Dongfeng's Quantum architecture and MACH powertrain, complemented by the Tianyuan cockpit and Tianyuan assisted-driving stack [2]. At the 1 August 2025 event, the eπ007+ debuts the Tianyuan assisted-driving system T200 and, according to Dongfeng, comes standard with an 8295 chip [2]. The M8 is fully equipped with the Huawei Qiankun six-piece suite (Qiankun ADS 5 Pro, HarmonySpace 5.2 cockpit, Qiankun vehicle cloud, vehicle control, whale-fin connectivity and HUAWEI SOUND) and a CATL high-energy-density-cell Yipai Golden Shield battery, offering 800V pure-electric (CLTC 600 km, 4C) and range-extender (300 km electric / 1,281 km combined) options [5]. The M8 is the first production model of the deepened Dongfeng Yipai-Huawei Qiankun cooperation, marking Yipai's shift toward a deep Huawei-based intelligentization route [5][21]. Dongfeng's 16,000-tonne integrated die-casting line has entered production and was first applied to the M8's body parts, cutting weight by 10%-15% [7]. A 350Wh/kg solid-state battery test vehicle has completed cold-weather calibration, and Dongfeng planned volume installation from September 2026 with the eπ007 as the first model; as of this page's as-of date (2026-10-29), no follow-up announcement confirming installation has been seen [7].

Production and capacity

Yipai Technology operates three whole-vehicle production bases in Hubei province — Wuhan Plant 1, Wuhan Plant 2 and the Xiangyang plant — with combined annual capacity of more than 500,000 units [7]. According to a Changjiang Daily report republished on the Wuhan municipal government portal, Yipai Technology entered the top ten of China's new-energy vehicle startups by sales about one year after its formation [7][9]. On the sales and service side, per Dongfeng's official site, Yipai's nationwide network exceeded 320 outlets by the time of the M8 launch [5][6].

Sales

By Dongfeng's own account, the Yipai brand (including the merged Nami series) sold 138,000 vehicles in 2025, up 20% year-on-year [4]. On a wider basis that also includes Fengshen, Yipai Technology sold 275,752 vehicles in 2025, up 28.3% [8], and delivered 132,900 units in the first half of 2026, up 21%, with June overseas sales up 186% year-on-year [7]. Looking only at the eπ series, Economic Observer puts the line that the core eπ007 and eπ008 models 'lingered at 2,000 to 4,000 units a month' in its 2024 sentence: retail of 46,000 and wholesale of 58,000 that year, which the article does not attribute to CPCA. For 2025 it only says wholesale sales fell to 50,800 (CPCA data). First-half 2026 volume is 17,400 (again labeled CPCA). The 50,800 figure is not rewritten here as a full-year 2025 monthly band of 2,000-4,000 [22].

Overseas business

In 2025 the Yipai brand exported to more than 20 countries and regions, with the eπ007 sold overseas as the DONGFENG 007 [4][3]. In September 2025 the right-hand-drive DONGFENG 007 launched in Hong Kong in three versions alongside the Dongfeng brand's new Kowloon Bay showroom [3]. In the 'Wings of the Future' strategy released on 1 August 2025, Dongfeng wrote that locally built vehicles would account for more than 20% of sales in 2026, and that it would aim for localized manufacturing to exceed 50% by 2030 [2].

Founders and leadership

Founder

Yipai has no private founder. It is a brand created by the central SOE Dongfeng Motor Corporation, released on 10 November 2023 by Chen Hao, a member of Dongfeng's Party standing committee, vice-president and general manager of Dongfeng Passenger Vehicle Company's sales company, together with Yipai marketing division head Yu Fei, who presented the first model [13][14].

  • Wang Junjun (汪俊君) - general manager of Yipai Technology, appointed when the company was established on 26 June 2025. He previously served as deputy general manager (CTO) of Voyah [11][19].
  • Zhang Jun (张俊) - member of the party committee, party secretary and deputy general manager of Yipai Technology. He previously served as general manager of Dongfeng Motor Group's operation management department and marketing management department, and as general manager and party secretary of Dongfeng Passenger Vehicle Sales Company [11][19].

  • Deng Liuchao (邓留超) - in August 2026 he became general manager of Yipai Technology's sales company, overseeing marketing for both Dongfeng Fengshen and Dongfeng Yipai. Yu Fei was general manager of the Yipai marketing division when the brand was released (November 2023) and during the 2024 eπ007 and eπ008 launches; his departure date and next role were not confirmed by the sources this study retrieved [13]. Competition

Competitive position: our assessment

Our assessment: Yipai sits in China's most crowded band - the RMB 100,000-250,000 mainstream NEV market - competing against BYD's Ocean and Dynasty series, Geely Galaxy, Changan Qiyuan, Leapmotor and other domestic brands, with the M8 moving it into the RMB 200,000-level six-seat SUV segment contested by the AITO M7, Li Auto L8 and Leapmotor C16 [22]. Economic Observer describes that segment as a 'red ocean' - cumulative sales fell about 14.24% year-on-year to roughly 432,200 units in the first four months of 2026, even as new entrants kept arriving [22]. The brand's 2025 result of 138,000 units (Yipai including Nami) is modest next to the segment leaders, and Economic Observer places the 2,000-4,000 monthly range for the core eπ007 and eπ008 models in its 2024 sentence, while for 2025 it only reports wholesale sales of 50,800 (CPCA data) [22][4].

Strengths and constraints: our assessment

Our assessment: Yipai's advantages are Dongfeng's decades of manufacturing, engineering and supply-chain resources, its in-house technology base (Quantum architecture, MACH powertrain, Tianyuan cockpit/assisted driving) and, from 2026, deep Huawei Qiankun cooperation - the M8 offers the Huawei six-piece suite from a limited-time super trade-in price of RMB 169,800, bringing configurations previously found on RMB 300,000-plus models down to the RMB 160,000 level [5][22]. Its constraints are a slow all-new-model cadence (more than two years between the eπ008 and the M8), heavy dependence on the Nami low-price EV series for Yipai Technology's overall volume, and the fact that its higher-priced eπ and M8 models have yet to prove they can contribute profitable volume [22]. The above is based on the sales, product and pricing data cited in this section.

Outlook

Company plans

The January 2026 dealer conference set out three all-new models and five facelifts for 2026. Two of the Huawei-equipped models were to use 16,000-tonne integrated die-casting, a high-energy-density solid-state battery, Huawei Qiankun assisted driving and the Harmony cockpit [4]. A 1 February 2026 report on Autoreport, a Yiche outlet, separately describes a high-performance coupe that would use Dongfeng's in-house hybrid solid-liquid battery and reach 1,000 km of range, plus a large-space SUV. That article does not call the coupe's battery a solid-state battery. The dealer-conference 'high-energy-density solid-state battery' refers to the two Huawei-equipped models, and the Wuhan government reprint's 350Wh/kg solid-state battery planned for the eπ007 in September 2026 is a third, separate item [7][21]. These are stated plans and one media report, not achieved results.

Our analysis

Our assessment: the Huawei partnership is a meaningful differentiator, but the RMB 200,000-level six-seat SUV segment shrank about 14.24% year-on-year in the first four months of 2026 (cumulative sales about 432,200 units) and is crowded with new entrants, so the M8's pricing-led entry faces intensifying competition [22]. The August 2026 marketing leadership change (Deng Liuchao in, Yu Yuefeng out) adds execution risk mid-year [23]. Overseas, the brand's base across more than 20 markets is still small and exports are in a growth phase, but changes to target-market tariffs and certification on Chinese-built EVs remain a constraint [4]. In addition, whether Dongfeng's self-developed solid-state battery enters volume installation on schedule in September 2026 will test whether Yipai's in-house battery story holds, a milestone that matters to the brand's differentiation narrative [7]. The above is our judgement, based on the data cited earlier in this page.

Risks

  • Slow new-model cadence: the more-than-two-year gap between the eπ008 (June 2024) and the M8 (July 2026) sits next to Economic Observer's 2024 description of the core eπ007 and eπ008 models lingering at 2,000-4,000 units a month. For 2025 the same article only says wholesale sales fell to 50,800 (CPCA data). The 2026 plan depends on new models ramping fast [22].
  • Dependence on the low-price Nami series: Yipai Technology's overall volume leans on the Nami entry-EV series, so the higher-priced eπ and M8 models must still prove they can contribute profitable volume [22][7].
  • Intense price competition: the RMB 100,000-250,000 mainstream NEV segment is contested by BYD, Geely, Changan and Leapmotor, and the RMB 200,000-level six-seat SUV segment the M8 targets shrank about 14.24% year-on-year in the first four months of 2026 [22].
  • Leadership churn: the marketing division changed hands in August 2026 (Deng Liuchao in, Yu Yuefeng out), which can disrupt execution and channel relationships mid-year [23].
  • Technology-timeline uncertainty: Dongfeng's plan to install its solid-state battery from September 2026 (first on the eπ007) had no follow-up confirmation by the as-of date, so the rollout timing carries uncertainty [7].
  • Overseas trade-policy risk: the brand exports to more than 20 countries and regions. The 'Wings of the Future' strategy of 1 August 2025 says locally built vehicles should exceed 20% of sales in 2026 and that localized manufacturing should be pushed above 50% by 2030, so the overseas build-out is exposed to changes in target-market tariffs and certification [4][2].
  • No separate financial disclosure: the brand does not publish standalone financials, so external assessment of its profitability depends on the parent's consolidated disclosures [1].

概览

东风奕派（eπ）是中央企业东风汽车集团有限公司旗下的主流新能源乘用车品牌。东风于 2023 年 11 月 10 日在武汉发布该品牌，定位主流科技电动品牌，首款车型为 eπ007 轿车。新华网称其锚定 15–25 万级主流市场，央广网称其瞄准 20 万级市场[13][14]。2025 年 6 月，东风将奕派与风神、纳米两大自主品牌整合成立东风奕派汽车科技公司（奕派科技），并把纳米并入奕派，统一运营集团主流乘用车业务[2][11]。品牌车型谱系覆盖 eπ007/007+ 轿车、eπ008 家用 SUV、M8 六座旗舰及纳米入门纯电序列；2025 年奕派品牌（含纳米）销量 13.8 万辆、同比增长 20%[4]。整合后的奕派科技（含风神、奕派、纳米三品牌）2025 年销量 275,752 辆，2026 年上半年交付 13.29 万辆、同比增长 21%[8][7]。品牌不单独披露财务数据、也未单独上市，母集团情况见「东风汽车集团」深度研究[1]。

现状

品牌架构

自 2025 年 6 月 26 日起，奕派、风神、纳米三大品牌统一由奕派科技运营，整合商企、研发、生产、供应链、销售与服务全价值链；纳米并入奕派，故奕派品牌现涵盖 eπ 系列与纳米入门纯电序列，风神则作为独立事业部专注全球节能车市场[2][11][18]。总经理汪俊君将这一策略概括为「奕派向上、风神向新」[2]。据新京报贝壳财经报道，奕派价格区间为 10 万–20 万元，纳米聚焦 10 万元以下，风神价位集中在 10 万–15 万元[19]。

车型与价格

产品线以 eπ007/007+ 轿车、eπ008 五座/六座家用 SUV、M8 六座旗舰为主力，纳米 01、06 入门纯电车型同列于官网[2]。eπ007 于 2024 年 3 月以 15.96 万–23.46 万元上市（顶格为带选装满配价），eπ008 于 2024 年 6 月上市，增程与纯电两款售价均为 21.66 万元，限时权益价为 18.86 万元。M8 于 2026 年 7 月 23 日上市：东风官网写限时超级置换价 16.98 万元起；经济观察报另写官方指导价 17.98 万–21.98 万元、限时权益价 16.58 万元起。这是官网和该报道的两套说法，不并成一套[1][16][5][22]。

技术

品牌基于东风量子架构与马赫动力，配合天元智舱、天元智驾[2]。2025 年 8 月 1 日的发布会上，eπ007+ 首发天元智驾 T200，东风称标配 8295 芯片[2]。M8 全系满配华为乾崑六件套（乾崑智驾 ADS 5 Pro、鸿蒙座舱 HarmonySpace 5.2、乾崑车云、乾崑车控、鲸鳍通信、HUAWEI SOUND），采用宁德时代高比能电芯的奕派金盾电池，提供 800V 纯电（CLTC 600 公里、4C）与增程（纯电 300 公里/综合 1281 公里）两种动力[5]。M8 是东风奕派与华为乾崑深化合作后落地的首款车型，标志着奕派在智能化路线上转向深度绑定华为方案的取向[5][21]。东风 16000 吨一体化压铸产线已投产，率先用于 M8 车身部件，减重 10%–15%[7]。350Wh/kg 固态电池试验样车已完成极寒标定，东风计划于 2026 年 9 月量产装车、首搭车型为 eπ007；截至本页研究截止日（2026-10-29），未见装车完成的后续公告[7]。

生产与产能

奕派科技在湖北省布局三个整车生产基地，即武汉一厂、武汉二厂与襄阳工厂，整车年产能合计超过 50 万辆[7]。据武汉市政府门户网站转载《长江日报》的报道，奕派科技在成立约一年之际已跻身造车新势力销量前十[7][9]。销售与服务网络方面，据东风官网，奕派 M8 上市时全国销售网点已超过 320 家[5][6]。

销量

按东风官方口径，2025 年奕派品牌（含并入的纳米序列）销量 13.8 万辆、同比增长 20%[4]。按更宽的、含风神的口径，奕派科技 2025 年销量为 275,752 辆、同比增长 28.3%[8]；2026 年上半年交付 13.29 万辆、同比增长 21%，其中 6 月海外销量同比增长 186%[7]。若单看 eπ 系列，经济观察报把「核心车型 eπ007 和 eπ008 月销量长期徘徊在 2000 辆至 4000 辆」放在 2024 年那一句：当年零售 4.6 万辆、批发 5.8 万辆，原文没有标乘联会。2025 年只写批发销量下滑至 5.08 万辆（乘联会数据）。2026 年上半年累计 1.74 万辆（再次标明乘联会）。这里不把 5.08 万辆改写成 2025 年全年月销都在 2000–4000 辆[22]。

海外业务

2025 年，奕派品牌出口至 20 多个国家和地区，eπ007 以「DONGFENG 007」之名在海外销售[4][3]。2025 年 9 月，右舵版 DONGFENG 007（即 eπ007）在香港上市，推出三个版本，与东风香港九龙湾新展厅同场发布[3]。2025 年 8 月 1 日发布的「未来之翼」战略写明，2026 年本地化制造销量占比达 20% 以上，本地化制造力争 2030 年达 50% 以上[2]。

竞争格局

竞争地位：我们的判断

我们的判断：奕派身处中国竞争最激烈的 10 万–25 万元主流新能源价格带，对手包括比亚迪王朝/海洋、吉利银河、长安启源、零跑等本土品牌；M8 则使其进入 20 万元级大六座 SUV 这一与问界 M7、理想 L8、零跑 C16 短兵相接的细分市场[22]。经济观察报将这一细分市场称为「红海」——2026 年前 4 个月累计销量约 43.22 万辆、同比下滑 14.24%，市场整体收缩的同时新车型仍密集入市[22]。品牌 2025 年 13.8 万辆（奕派含纳米）的成绩相较头部玩家仍偏小，经济观察报则把核心车型 eπ007 和 eπ008 月销 2000–4000 辆写在 2024 年；2025 年只写批发销量下滑至 5.08 万辆（乘联会数据）[22][4]。

优势与短板：我们的判断

我们的判断：奕派的核心优势在于东风数十年造车与供应链资源、自研技术底座（量子架构、马赫动力、天元座舱/智驾），以及 2026 年起与华为乾崑的深度合作——M8 以 16.98 万元起的限时超级置换价提供华为六件套，将过去 30 万元以上车型的配置下探至 16 万元级[5][22]。其短板在于全新车型投放节奏偏慢（eπ008 至 M8 之间两年多空窗），奕派科技整体销量较大程度依赖纳米低价纯电序列，且价位更高的 eπ 与 M8 车型尚未证明能贡献有利润的销量[22]。上述判断依据为本节所引销量、产品与价格数据。

未来展望

公司公开规划

2026 年 1 月招商大会写明，当年推出 3 款全新车型与 5 款改款，其中两款搭载华为技术的车型将应用 16000 吨一体化压铸、高能量密度固态电池、华为乾崑智驾与鸿蒙座舱[4]。汽车产经网 2026 年 2 月 1 日另报道，产品计划里还有一款搭载东风自研混合固液电池、续航 1000 公里的高性能轿跑，以及一款大空间 SUV。这篇报道没有把该轿跑写成固态电池。招商大会稿里的「高能量密度固态电池」指的是上述两款华为技术车型；武汉市政府转载稿里计划 2026 年 9 月首搭 eπ007 的 350Wh/kg 固态电池是另一项，三者不并成一件事[7][21]。以上是公开规划与一篇媒体报道，并非已实现结果。

我们的分析

我们的判断：华为合作是重要差异化，但 20 万元级大六座 SUV 市场 2026 年前 4 个月累计约 43.22 万辆、同比下滑约 14.24%，且新进入者众多，M8 以价格切入将面临更激烈竞争[22]。2026 年 8 月营销负责人更替（邓留超接任、余岳峰调离）也带来年中执行层面的不确定性[23]。海外方面，品牌在 20 余个市场的基数仍小、出口处于增长阶段，但目标市场对中国产纯电动车的关税与认证政策仍是制约[4]。此外，东风自研固态电池能否按计划于 2026 年 9 月量产装车，将检验奕派在电池技术上的自研故事能否兑现，这一节点对品牌的差异化叙事同样关键[7]。上述为我们的判断，依据为前文所引数据。

风险

  • 新车节奏偏慢：eπ008（2024 年 6 月）至 M8（2026 年 7 月）之间有两年多的全新车型空窗。经济观察报称 2024 年核心车型 eπ007 和 eπ008 月销量长期徘徊在 2000 辆至 4000 辆；2025 年只写批发销量下滑至 5.08 万辆（乘联会数据）。2026 年计划高度依赖新车型快速上量[22]。
  • 依赖低价纳米序列：奕派科技整体销量较大程度依赖纳米入门纯电序列，价位更高的 eπ 与 M8 车型仍需证明能贡献有利润的销量[22][7]。
  • 价格竞争激烈：10 万–25 万元主流新能源市场面对比亚迪、吉利、长安、零跑竞争；M8 所处的 20 万元级大六座 SUV 市场 2026 年前 4 个月同比下滑约 14.24%[22]。
  • 管理层更替：2026 年 8 月营销负责人换人（邓留超接任、余岳峰调离），可能带来年中执行与渠道层面的波动[23]。
  • 技术落地节奏不确定：东风原计划 2026 年 9 月量产装车固态电池（首搭 eπ007），截至研究截止日未见后续确认公告，装车节奏存在不确定性[7]。
  • 海外贸易政策风险：品牌出口至 20 多个国家和地区。2025 年 8 月 1 日「未来之翼」战略提出，2026 年本地化制造销量占比达 20% 以上，本地化制造力争 2030 年达 50% 以上。海外拓展面临目标市场关税与认证政策变化的风险[4][2]。
  • 无单独财务披露：品牌不单独披露财务报表，外部对其盈利情况的判断需依赖母公司的合并披露[1]。
