Overview

Anhui Jianghuai Automobile Group Corp., Ltd. (JAC; SSE 600418) is a state-controlled automaker headquartered in Hefei, Anhui, whose core business is commercial vehicles — light, medium and heavy trucks, pickups, vans and buses — plus passenger vehicles including MPVs, SUVs and sedans [2]. Its controlling shareholder is Jianghuai Automobile Group Holdings (江汽控股), which held 27.35% of the listed company as of 30 June 2026 and is owned 50/50 by Anhui SASAC and Volkswagen (China) Investment [3]. In FY2025 JAC reported total operating revenue of RMB46.57 billion (+10.35%) and a net loss attributable to shareholders of RMB1.70 billion, and sold 384,100 vehicles and chassis, including 221,900 exported [1][10].

JAC builds MAEXTRO (尊界), an ultra-luxury brand, with Huawei under Huawei's Harmony Intelligent Mobility Alliance (HIMA) partnership model [35]; the first model, the MAEXTRO S800 sedan, launched on 30 May 2025 at RMB708,000-1,018,000 [33]. The company is also a 25% shareholder in the Volkswagen Anhui joint venture and a former contract manufacturer for NIO, and it controls the separately listed bus maker Ankai [17]. Its FY2025 annual report states that JAC ranked first in medium- and high-end light-truck exports, while pickup exports exceeded 50,000 units and ranked second [1].

In FY2025, JAC reported a net loss attributable to shareholders of RMB2.50 billion excluding non-recurring items [1].

Current operations

Business structure

Commercial vehicles are JAC's core. In 2025 the company sold 384,100 vehicles and chassis — 235,000 commercial vehicles and 149,000 passenger vehicles — and posted total operating revenue of RMB46.57 billion [1]. By product, against 2025 operating revenue of RMB46.48 billion, commercial-vehicle revenue was RMB20.47 billion (44.04%, down 7.64%), passenger-vehicle revenue RMB18.00 billion (38.72%, up 46.99%), bus revenue RMB3.16 billion (6.79%, up 24.34%) and chassis revenue RMB0.16 billion (0.34%) [1][19]. By region, overseas revenue accounted for 47.15% of main operating revenue, while overseas main operating revenue was 44.19% of consolidated operating revenue [1][10]. New-energy commercial-vehicle sales rose 70.76% to 20,700 units in 2025, ranking fourth in the segment [23].

The sales trend requires care over measurement bases. Under the original 2023 annual-report basis, JAC sold 592,500 vehicles in 2023 (+18.40%) [6]. In 2024 it sold 403,094 vehicles (-7.42%); the 2023 comparative figure of 435,389 used in the 2024 report excludes the NIO contract-manufactured models and other items [4]. Sales fell further to 384,100 in 2025 [1]. The company said it sold more than 2 million vehicles and generated over RMB210 billion revenue during the 14th Five-Year Plan period (2021-2025) [23].

Products and technology

On the commercial-vehicle side, JAC's line-up centres on the 'JAC 1ka' light-truck family, 'JAC 1van' light buses and JAC pickups; in 2025 light trucks ranked No.2 in China and pickups sold over 60,000 units (No.2). In 2024 JAC exported 36,000 light trucks (No.1 for a 14th consecutive year), over 53,000 pickups (No.2) and 21,000 light buses [20][23]. The passenger-vehicle range includes the MAEXTRO S800; as of 29 October 2026, HIMA listed its suggested retail price from RMB728,000 [34]; the company's disclosed range also includes the Refine RF8 MPV and new-energy light trucks such as the ES9 and Kunpeng ET9 [3]. JAC has developed the DEFINE platform — which will cover SUVs, sedans and MPVs across pure-electric and hybrid powertrains —, and it works with Huawei on the Tuling Longxing platform used by MAEXTRO [20][28]. In a technical introduction published on 25 November 2024, JAC described its full-domain by-wire intelligent chassis as using XYZ coordinated control, linking chassis and electric-drive software algorithms to control the vehicle in six degrees of freedom [32]. It has started L3 road testing, holds more than 16,000 authorised patents, and operates China's first CNAS-accredited 'Automotive Intelligent Connected Technology Anhui Provincial Key Laboratory' [20][14].

Factories and overseas operations

JAC now has 31 global production bases and more than 5,000 user/service centres, and has cumulatively delivered over 12 million products; it sells to more than 130 countries and regions with cumulative exports of nearly 2 million vehicles [14]. The MAEXTRO super factory was completed in December 2024 with planned annual capacity of 200,000 vehicles [17]. In 2019 JAC took part in the acquisition of Allur Group, Kazakhstan's largest automaker, as a starting point for developing the customs-union and Central Asian markets [14]. In its 2025 annual report, JAC lists its Tashkent entity as a joint venture and its Kazakhstan entity as a subsidiary of an associate, rather than as controlled subsidiaries [1].

Latest results

In H1 2026, operating revenue was RMB22.13 billion (+14.31%), gross margin rose to 11.14% (from 8.98% a year earlier), net loss attributable to shareholders was RMB749 million and vehicle sales fell 9.56% to 172,400 units [3]. For the single third quarter of 2025, JAC reported operating revenue of RMB11.513 billion (+5.54%) and a net loss attributable to shareholders of RMB661 million; the quarterly report was unaudited [31].

Founders and leadership

Founder

JAC has no individual founder. Its predecessor, the state-run Hefei Jianghuai Automobile Plant, was founded in 1964; the joint-stock company was incorporated on 30 September 1999 [14].

Key management

  • Xiang Xingchu (born August 1970), chairman and party secretary. He was elected chairman on 26 April 2021 and re-elected at the first meeting of the ninth board on 18 November 2025. A senior engineer, he previously served as general manager and vice chairman of the listed company. He is also party secretary, chairman and general manager of Jianghuai Holdings [8][7].
  • Li Ming (born July 1977), director and general manager. He was first appointed general manager on 24 December 2021 and re-appointed at the first meeting of the ninth board on 18 November 2025. A doctor of engineering and senior engineer, he previously served as assistant general manager and deputy general manager, concurrently general manager of the engine branch and passenger-vehicle company, and a director and general manager of the JAC-Volkswagen (now Volkswagen Anhui) joint venture [8][7].
  • Zhang Peng (born November 1970), deputy general manager since 24 March 2022 and a director since 30 June 2026. Previously party secretary and general manager of the international company, and party secretary of the pickup business unit [8][3].
  • Luo Shicheng (born December 1974), deputy general manager since 18 November 2025. Previously deputy general manager of the passenger-vehicle manufacturing company, general manager of Anhui Jianghuai Anchi Automobile and of the commercial-vehicle company. He is also chairman of Anhui Jianghuai Intelligent Technology Co. [8].
  • Li Weihua (born March 1979), deputy general manager since 18 November 2025. Previously deputy head of the enterprise-management department, deputy general manager of the light commercial-vehicle manufacturing company and general manager of the Shandong branch. He is also party secretary and general manager of the human-resources management centre [8].
  • Chen Jiming (born March 1980) is also a deputy general manager [8].

Shareholding and governance

As of 30 June 2026, controlling shareholder Jianghuai Holdings held 27.35% of the listed company, and Hong Kong Securities Clearing Company held 4.03% (90,809,756 shares) [3]. Jianghuai Holdings is owned 50/50 by Anhui SASAC and Volkswagen (China) Investment; the annual reports and announcements identify Anhui SASAC as the actual controller [2][9]. At the first meeting of the ninth board on 18 November 2025, Xiang Xingchu was elected chairman, the members of the board committees were selected, and Li Ming was appointed general manager alongside other senior management appointments [8].

Competition

Competitive scope

Our judgement: based on truck and pickup product coverage, Beiqi Foton, Dongfeng, JMC, FAW Jiefang and Great Wall provide useful comparisons for JAC's commercial-vehicle business; MAEXTRO belongs in the premium NEV passenger-car comparison set. These comparisons reflect product coverage and price positioning, rather than direct competition across every business. JAC reports commercial-vehicle, passenger-vehicle and bus businesses, while the S800 launched at RMB708,000-1,018,000 [1][33].

Business base and constraints

In 2025, JAC ranked second in N2 light trucks and first in medium- and high-end light-truck exports; pickup exports exceeded 50,000 units and ranked second. Overseas main operating revenue accounted for 47.15% of main operating revenue. In its official news release dated 2 July 2026, JAC reported cumulative S800 deliveries of over 19,000 units [1][10].

In its FY2025 annual report, JAC stated that its passenger-vehicle brands other than MAEXTRO lacked market competitiveness. The FY2025 net loss attributable to shareholders was RMB1.70 billion, or RMB2.50 billion excluding non-recurring items [1]; the H1 2026 attributable net loss was RMB749 million [3].

Our analysis

Our judgement: commercial-vehicle revenue fell in 2025 while its gross margin increased, so the revenue change alone does not establish stronger price competition. Volume and product mix may also affect revenue; assessing operating improvement requires considering revenue, gross margin and expenses together. [1].

Outlook

Company targets and plans

The FY2025 annual report, disclosed on 4 April 2026, sets expected 2026 vehicle sales of 424,000 (+10.4%) and total operating revenue of RMB65 billion (+39.58%). JAC plans to continue developing MAEXTRO, focus its commercial-vehicle business on light trucks, pickups and electrification, and strengthen local operations overseas. The report states that forward-looking plans and strategies do not constitute substantive commitments [1].

At the supply-chain expo on 22 June 2026, Xiang Xingchu reiterated the 15th Five-Year Plan targets of one million vehicle sales and RMB100 billion revenue, through independent development and open cooperation, with four areas of focus: MAEXTRO, premium passenger vehicles, light commercial vehicles and international operations [12]. On 5 August 2026, JAC announced the launch of the MAEXTRO V800 and V680 MPVs. The three V800 versions start at RMB766,000, RMB866,000 and RMB1,016,000, respectively, while the V680 starts at RMB648,000 [11].

Our analysis

Our judgement: converting revenue growth into profit depends on deliveries of new models, product margins and control of selling expenses; brand positioning and sales targets alone do not establish a profit recovery. Commercial-vehicle revenue fell while its margin rose in 2025, and selling expenses increased 97.50%, so revenue, mix and costs require separate assessment [1]. Volkswagen Anhui's losses and unpaid equity subscriptions also require separate monitoring from JAC's own manufacturing operations [3]. Better new-model deliveries and spending efficiency could support recovery; insufficient growth or rising investment could prolong losses. These are conditional assessments, not company commitments.

Risks

  • Operating risk: in its FY2025 annual report, JAC stated that its passenger-vehicle brands other than MAEXTRO lacked market competitiveness [1].
  • Financial risk: the consolidated debt-to-asset ratio was 79.07% at end-2025 (liabilities RMB40.23 billion, assets RMB50.88 billion) [1]; as of 30 June 2026, JAC had RMB812.5 million in unpaid equity subscriptions to Volkswagen Anhui [3].
  • Overseas risk: overseas main operating revenue accounted for 47.15% of main operating revenue in 2025; JAC identified the complex international environment, rising trade barriers and stronger competition overseas as export challenges in its annual report [1].
  • Competition risk: commercial-vehicle revenue fell 7.64% in 2025, while the segment gross margin rose 0.85 percentage points to 10.05% [1].
  • Cost and investment risk: selling expenses rose 97.50% to RMB2.90 billion in 2025, with advertising and sales-service fees increasing from RMB176 million to RMB1.57 billion; the annual report attributed the increase in selling expenses to higher advertising and sales-service fees [1].

概览

安徽江淮汽车集团股份有限公司（江淮汽车，上交所 600418）是一家总部位于合肥的国有控股整车企业，主业为商用车——轻、中、重型卡车、皮卡、轻客与客车——以及乘用车（MPV、SUV、轿车）[2]。公司控股股东为安徽江淮汽车集团控股有限公司（江汽控股），截至 2026 年 6 月 30 日持有上市公司 27.35% 股权，后者由安徽省国资委与大众汽车（中国）投资有限公司各持 50% 股权 [3]。2025 财年公司实现营业总收入 465.69 亿元（同比增 10.35%）、归母净利润亏损 17.03 亿元，销售整车及底盘 38.41 万辆，其中出口 22.19 万辆 [1][10]。

江淮与华为以鸿蒙智行（HIMA，Harmony Intelligent Mobility Alliance）合作模式打造超豪华品牌尊界（MAEXTRO）[35]，首款车型尊界 S800 轿车于 2025 年 5 月 30 日上市（70.8 万–101.8 万元）[33]。公司同时是大众安徽合资公司 25% 股东、蔚来此前的代工方，并控股独立上市的安凯客车 [17]。公司 2025 年年报披露，其中高端轻卡出口居行业第一，皮卡出口超 5 万辆、位居行业第二 [1]。

2025 年公司扣非净亏损 25.00 亿元 [1]。

现状

业务结构

商用车是江淮的核心。2025 年公司销售整车及底盘 38.41 万辆——商用车 23.5 万辆、乘用车 14.9 万辆，实现营业总收入 465.69 亿元 [1]。分产品看，按 2025 年营业收入 464.76 亿元计，商用车收入 204.69 亿元（占 44.04%，同比下降 7.64%）、乘用车收入 179.96 亿元（占 38.72%，同比增 46.99%）、客车收入 31.57 亿元（占 6.79%，增 24.34%）、底盘收入 1.58 亿元（占 0.34%）[1][19]。分地区看，境外收入占主营业务收入 47.15%，境外主营业务收入占合并营业收入 44.19% [1][10]。新能源商用车方面，2025 年销量 2.07 万辆、同比增 70.76%，居行业第四 [23]。

销量轨迹需要区分口径。按 2023 年年度报告原报口径，2023 年全年销量 59.25 万辆（同比增 18.40%） [6]。2024 年销量 40.31 万辆、同比下降 7.42%，其 2023 年比较基数 43.54 万辆已剔除代工蔚来车型等口径 [4]。2025 年进一步降至 38.41 万辆 [1]。公司称「十四五」期间累计销售超 200 万辆、营收超 2100 亿元 [23]。

产品与技术

商用车端，江淮产品以「江淮1卡」轻卡、「江淮1van」轻客与江淮皮卡为核心；2025 年轻卡居行业第二、皮卡销售超 6 万辆（行业第二）。2024 年江淮轻卡出口 3.6 万台（连续十四年行业出口第一）、皮卡出口超 5.3 万辆（行业第二）、轻客出口 2.1 万辆 [20][23]。乘用车产品包括尊界 S800；截至 2026 年 10 月 29 日，鸿蒙智行官网列示该车建议零售价为 72.8 万元起 [34]；公司披露的产品线还包括瑞风 RF8 MPV、江淮新能源 ES9 纯电轻卡、坤鹏 ET9 纯电轻卡等 [3]。公司开发了 DEFINE 平台（未来覆盖 SUV、轿车、MPV，兼容纯电与混动），并与华为共同打造尊界所用的途灵龙行平台 [20][28]。江淮 2024 年 11 月 25 日官网技术介绍称，其全域线控智慧底盘采用 XYZ 协控，通过底盘与电驱动系统的软件算法联动，实现车辆六个自由度控制 [32]。公司已启动 L3 级道路测试，累计拥有授权专利超 1.6 万件，拥有国内第一个获 CNAS 认可的「汽车智能网联技术安徽省重点实验室」[20][14]。

工厂与海外业务

公司现有 31 个全球生产基地及超 5000 家全球用户中心，已累计向全球交付各类产品 1200 万辆；产品远销全球 130 多个国家和地区，累计出口近 200 万辆 [14]。尊界超级工厂于 2024 年 12 月建成，规划年产能 20 万辆 [17]。2019 年江淮参与收购哈萨克斯坦最大汽车工业集团 Allur 集团，成为其开发关税同盟市场和中亚市场的起点 [14]。2025 年年度报告按关联关系口径将塔什干公司列为合营企业、哈萨克斯坦相关主体列为联营企业的子公司，而非江淮控股子公司 [1]。

最新业绩

2026 年上半年，公司营业收入 221.3 亿元（同比增 14.31%），毛利率升至 11.14%（上年同期 8.98%），归母净亏损 7.49 亿元，销量 17.24 万辆（同比降 9.56%）[3]。2025 年第三季度单季，公司营业收入 115.13 亿元（同比增 5.54%）、归母净亏损 6.61 亿元；该季报未经审计 [31]。

竞争格局

竞争范围

我们的判断：按卡车与皮卡产品线，北汽福田、东风、江铃、一汽解放和长城可作为江淮商用车业务的比较对象；尊界则应放在高端新能源乘用车市场观察。上述比较以产品覆盖和价格定位为依据，不代表各公司所有业务直接竞争。江淮年报披露的业务包括商用车、乘用车和客车，尊界 S800 的上市价格为 70.8 万–101.8 万元 [1][33]。

业务基础与约束

2025 年，江淮 N2 类轻卡居行业第二，中高端轻卡出口保持行业第一，皮卡出口超 5 万辆、位居行业第二；境外主营业务收入占主营业务收入 47.15%。公司在 2026 年 7 月 2 日官网新闻中披露，尊界 S800 累计交付超 1.9 万辆 [1][10]。

公司在 2025 年年报中指出，除尊界外的乘用车品牌市场竞争力不强。2025 年归母净亏损 17.03 亿元，扣非净亏损 25.00 亿元 [1]；2026 年上半年归母净亏损 7.49 亿元 [3]。

我们的分析

我们的判断：2025 年商用车收入下降而毛利率上升，仅凭收入变化不能判定价格竞争加剧。收入变动还可能涉及销量和产品结构，判断经营改善需要同时观察收入、毛利率与费用 [1]。

未来展望

公司目标与规划

2025 年年报（2026 年 4 月 4 日披露）列示，2026 年预计销售汽车 42.4 万辆、同比增 10.4%，预计营业总收入 650 亿元、同比增 39.58%；公司拟继续打造尊界品牌，商用车聚焦轻卡、皮卡及新能源转型，国际业务强化属地化运营。年报同时说明，经营计划与发展战略等前瞻性表述不构成实质承诺 [1]。

2026 年 6 月 22 日，项兴初在链博会表示，「十五五」期间锚定「百万销量、千亿营收」目标，坚持自主发展与开放合作，聚焦尊界、乘用车高端化、轻型商用车和国际化四个方向 [12]。2026 年 8 月 5 日，公司官网宣布尊界 V800、V680 MPV 上市，V800 三款配置分别为 76.6 万、86.6 万、101.6 万元起，V680 为 64.8 万元起 [11]。

我们的分析

我们的判断：收入扩大能否转化为盈利，取决于新增车型的交付规模、产品毛利和销售费用控制，不能仅凭品牌定位或销量目标推定。2025 年商用车收入下降而毛利率上升、销售费用增长 97.50%，说明收入、产品结构与费用需要分别观察 [1]。大众安徽的亏损和仍未支付的股权认购款，也需要与江淮自身的制造业务分别跟踪 [3]。如果新车型交付和费用效率改善，盈利可能修复；若增量不足或投入继续增加，亏损也可能持续。这些是条件判断，并非公司承诺。

风险

  • 经营风险：公司在 2025 年年报中披露，除尊界外的乘用车品牌市场竞争力不强 [1]。
  • 财务风险：2025 年末合并资产负债率为 79.07%（负债总额 402.31 亿元、资产总额 508.80 亿元）[1]；截至 2026 年 6 月 30 日，公司对大众安徽仍有 8.125 亿元股权认购款尚未支付 [3]。
  • 海外风险：2025 年境外主营业务收入占主营业务收入 47.15%；公司在年报中将国际形势复杂、贸易壁垒升级及境外市场竞争加剧列为出口业务面临的挑战 [1]。
  • 竞争风险：2025 年商用车收入同比下降 7.64%；同期该业务毛利率为 10.05%，同比提高 0.85 个百分点 [1]。
  • 费用与投入风险：2025 年销售费用同比增 97.50% 至 28.97 亿元，其中广宣及销售服务费从上期的 1.76 亿元升至 15.72 亿元；年报将销售费用增长归因于广宣及销售服务费增加 [1]。
