Overview

Enovate Motors (Tianji Auto) is a Chinese new-energy-vehicle startup whose predecessor, Dearcc (Electric Coffee Auto), was founded in June 2015 as a micro-EV brand incubated within LeEco's auto business. The premium brand Tianji (Enovate) was launched in 2018, and the corporate entity was renamed Enovate Motors Technology Group Co., Ltd. in 2019. The company was founded and led by Zhang Hailiang, a former SAIC Volkswagen general manager and LeEco Auto executive.[11][4][11][4][5][8][9][1][7][3][15][16]

The company launched two production models — the battery-electric mid-size SUV ME7 (September 2020) and the extended-range compact SUV ME5 (July 2021) — raised cumulative financing of about RMB10 billion, built vehicle plants in Shaoxing (Zhejiang) and Changsha (Hunan), and in December 2022 signed a joint-venture contract to build a plant in Saudi Arabia with Sumou Holding.[5][8][11][4][5][8][9][1][7][3][15][16]

Because sales fell far short of targets and funding dried up, the company halted work and production on 1 April 2023. Its Changsha subsidiary was subject to a bankruptcy-liquidation application in August 2024; in December 2024 the MIIT flagged its ME5 extended-range model for production-consistency non-compliance; the Shaoxing base's core assets were auctioned in August 2025 for about RMB64.96 million (less than one-tenth of book value); the Changsha subsidiary entered pre-reorganization in June 2026, but on 17 August 2026 the court terminated the pre-reorganization administrator-selection after the applicant withdrew. As of 29 October 2026 the group had not completed liquidation or formal bankruptcy and remained shut down.[9][1][16][7][3][15][11][4][5][8][9][1][7][3][15][16]

History

  1. 2015-06

    The predecessor Dearcc (Electric Coffee Auto) is founded as a micro-EV brand incubated within LeEco's auto business. Hubei Daily records incorporation in June 2015; Interface News notes the predecessor was founded in Zhejiang in 2015.[11][5]

  2. 2017-10

    Zhang Hailiang, former LeEco Auto executive, takes over as Dearcc's chairman and CEO (China Economic Net records 2017; Sohu Auto records October 2017).[10][13]

  3. 2017-11

    Dearcc launches its first model, the A0-class micro-EV EV10, with a post-subsidy price below RMB70,000.[12]

  4. 2018

    The company launches the premium brand Tianji (Enovate) as part of a move upmarket; the first model ME7 debuts at the Guangzhou Auto Show in November. In the same year it acquires the Quanzhou Xihu auto production base and obtains its new-energy-vehicle production qualification.[8][11]

  5. 2019-03

    Zhejiang Dearcc Auto Technology Co., Ltd. is renamed Enovate Motors Technology Group Co., Ltd. (China Economic Net/Lanjing Finance records March 2019; Hubei Daily records 2019).[12][11]

  6. 2019-04-16

    ME7 opens pre-sale at the Shanghai Auto Show at RMB366,800–381,800 before subsidies (a limited run of 6,666 units). Zhang Hailiang announces a completed RMB2 billion Series A round and cumulative financing of over RMB6.5 billion.[4]

  7. 2019-10

    The Shaoxing passenger-vehicle base enters the MIIT catalogue of road-vehicle manufacturers.[8]

  8. 2019-12-28

    The first model, ME7, rolls off the production line at the Shaoxing base.[8]

  9. 2020-09

    ME7 officially launches at RMB218,800–289,800 after subsidies (three trims, with a maximum NEDC range of 530 km). China Economic Net reports deliveries began in October 2020; Hubei Daily states delivery was delayed until 2021, and the two accounts differ.[8][11][10]

  10. 2020-10

    Enovate completes a new financing round of over RMB5 billion, led by local-government industrial guidance funds and large state-owned banks.[5][12]

  11. 2020-12

    The Changsha passenger-vehicle base enters the MIIT catalogue of road-vehicle manufacturers.[8]

  12. 2021-06

    The Changsha production base starts operation; in July the second model, the extended-range compact SUV ME5, launches with a guide price of RMB149,900–159,900.[8][12]

  13. 2022-12-12

    Enovate signs a joint-venture contract with Saudi firm Sumou Holding to invest about USD500 million in two phases for a NEV manufacturing and R&D base in Saudi Arabia with planned annual capacity of about 100,000 units. The project did not proceed to production.[5][6]

  14. 2023-03

    Media report a planned financing round of over RMB750 million for wage arrears and production resumption; no completion was subsequently announced.[12]

  15. 2023-04-01

    Enovate halts work and production for some positions from 1 April 2023, citing its funding situation and production and sales plans.[9][10]

  16. 2024-08-13

    A creditor, Shen Yuqing, applies to the Changsha Yuelu District People's Court for bankruptcy liquidation of Enovate Auto (Changsha) Group Co., Ltd. (case (2024) Xiang 0104 Po Shen No. 31).[1]

  17. 2024-12

    The MIIT notification on the results of 2023 new-energy-vehicle supervision inspections names Fujian Enovate Automobile Manufacturing Co., Ltd.'s plug-in extended-range hybrid multipurpose passenger vehicle (model QAC6452SHEVBB6, i.e. the ME5) for energy-consumption production-consistency non-compliance with GB/T 19753-2021; media reported the company said it would not voluntarily recall, citing the large number of vehicles involved and no impact on use.[16]

  18. 2025-08-22

    Machinery, fixtures and materials of Fujian Enovate Automobile Manufacturing Co., Ltd.'s Shaoxing branch are auctioned for RMB64,955,890.70 (appraised value RMB53,622,701), less than one-tenth of the book value recorded in the last audit report.[7][8]

  19. 2026-04-07

    The Hunan Xiangjiang New Area Party and Government Office replies that Enovate is in a work-and-production halt and is advancing strategic-investor introduction and work resumption through bankruptcy reorganization judicial proceedings.[2]

  20. 2026-06-04

    The Changsha Intermediate People's Court publishes a notice on selecting a temporary administrator for the pre-reorganization case of Enovate Auto (Changsha) Group Co., Ltd., applied for by Shanghai Huanben Catering Management Co., Ltd.[3]

  21. 2026-08-17

    The Changsha Intermediate People's Court announces that, after Shanghai Huanben Catering Management Co., Ltd. withdrew its bankruptcy reorganization application against Enovate Auto (Changsha) Group Co., Ltd. and the court approved the withdrawal, it terminates the administrator-selection procedure for the pre-reorganization case.[15]

Current operations

Business and products

The company's business was new-energy-vehicle development, manufacturing and sales. It launched two production models: the battery-electric mid-size SUV ME7 (launched September 2020 at RMB218,800–289,800 after subsidies, with a maximum NEDC range of 530 km) and the extended-range compact SUV ME5 (launched July 2021 with a guide price of RMB149,900–159,900). The ME-S concept sedan was shown at the 2019 Shanghai Auto Show but never mass-produced. No primary disclosure of revenue by business segment was obtained.[8][12][10][4][5][8][9][11][2][3][7][1][12][10][4][6][16][15]

Technology

The ME7 was built on the iMA digital architecture, which the company described as integrating vehicle, IT and internet technologies, and featured a 5+X multi-screen cockpit, facial recognition and L2-level driving assistance. At pre-sale the company highlighted a Bosch flat-wire permanent-magnet synchronous motor with 98% efficiency and a 0–100 km/h time of 4.9 seconds for the all-wheel-drive version.[4] The ME5 used the iMES extended-range system pairing a 1.5-litre engine with an electric motor, with a claimed pure-electric NEDC range of 155 km and a combined NEDC range of 1,012 km.[14] These descriptions come from launch materials and media reports; no independently tested current specifications were obtained.[5][8][9][11][2][3][7][1][12][10][4][6][16][15]

Plants and capacity

The Shaoxing base entered the MIIT manufacturer catalogue in October 2019, and the ME7 rolled off the line there on 28 December 2019. Interface News reported the Shaoxing plant planned about 1,000 mu of land, RMB5.5 billion of total investment and 180,000 units of designed annual capacity; the Changsha plant started production in June 2021 with 60,000 units of designed annual capacity. Media differ on total three-site capacity: China Economic Net reports 220,000 units across Changsha, Shaoxing and Nanning, while Hubei Daily reports 340,000 units of passenger-vehicle capacity across the three sites plus planned Qingdao and Cixi commercial-vehicle bases. The Nanning, Qingdao and Cixi bases were never built or put into production.[5][10][11][12][5][8][9][11][2][3][7][1][12][10][4][6][16][15]

Production qualification

In 2018 the company acquired the Quanzhou Xihu automobile production base and obtained its new-energy-vehicle production qualification; the Shaoxing base entered the MIIT manufacturer catalogue in October 2019 and the Changsha base in December 2020.[8][5][8][9][11][2][3][7][1][12][10][4][6][16][15]

Overseas plans

Overseas, the company signed a joint-venture contract with Saudi firm Sumou Holding on 12 December 2022 to invest about USD500 million in two phases for a NEV manufacturing and R&D base in Saudi Arabia with planned annual capacity of about 100,000 units; media also reported talks with Saudi investors including the sovereign wealth fund and Aramco. The contract did not proceed beyond signing, and by the cutoff no vehicles had been produced from or exported via the Saudi base, with no overseas sales disclosed.[5][6][5][8][9][11][2][3][7][1][12][10][4][6][16][15]

Domestic sales record

China Economic Net reports that the ME7 sold only 97 units cumulatively by the end of 2020; CPCA data cited by media show 1,778 units in 2021, 5,321 units in 2022 and 186 units in the first two months of 2023; Interface News reported 4,837 insured registrations in January–October 2022.[10][5][9][5][8][9][11][2][3][7][1][12][10][4][6][16][15]

Production-consistency issue

In December 2024 the MIIT notification on the results of 2023 new-energy-vehicle supervision inspections listed Fujian Enovate Automobile Manufacturing Co., Ltd.'s plug-in extended-range hybrid multipurpose passenger vehicle (model QAC6452SHEVBB6, the ME5) for energy-consumption production-consistency non-compliance with GB/T 19753-2021. Media reported that the company said it would not voluntarily recall, citing the large number of vehicles involved and no impact on use.[16][5][8][9][11][2][3][7][1][12][10][4][6][16][15]

Current status and judicial process

As of 29 October 2026 the company had been halted since 1 April 2023. Its Changsha subsidiary was subject to a bankruptcy-liquidation application in August 2024 and entered pre-reorganization in June 2026, but on 17 August 2026 the Changsha Intermediate People's Court terminated the pre-reorganization administrator-selection after the applicant Shanghai Huanben Catering Management Co., Ltd. withdrew its bankruptcy reorganization application and the court approved the withdrawal. The company was never listed and did not disclose any quarterly or half-year results.[9][1][3][15][5][8][9][11][2][3][7][1][12][10][4][6][16][15]

Founders and leadership

Founder and public roles

Zhang Hailiang was born in 1970 and graduated from Tongji University before joining Shanghai Volkswagen in 1994. China Economic Net reports that he became general manager of Shanghai Volkswagen in September 2010 (after the incumbent died in an accident) and rose to SAIC Group vice president in August 2014; Sohu Auto records the same months. He left SAIC in April 2016 to join LeEco Auto as president and COO, became global CEO of LeSEE (LeEco's super-car arm) in March 2017, and took over as chairman and CEO of Dearcc in October 2017. People's Daily Online identifies him as Enovate's chairman and CEO; Hubei Daily likewise lists him as chairman. China Economic Net reported in January 2026 that he had not resigned as chairman but had been unreachable to investors since late 2023.[10][13][4][11][4][10][11][5][13][3]

Other executives

Hubei Daily names Niu Shengfu (CTO) and Xiang Dongping (CMO) as senior executives from the SAIC system alongside chairman Zhang Hailiang. These are dated role records from media reports; this review obtained no primary announcement of the complete current management or board as of October 2026, and does not present historical names as current appointments.[11][4][10][11][5][13][3]

Ownership and governance

Hubei Daily lists shareholders including Shanghai Electric Technology Venture Capital Co., Ltd., Xingzheng Investment Management Co., Ltd. and Changsha Tianji New Energy Industry Investment Fund (LP), and reports the parent's registered capital at about RMB880 million. Interface News reported Changsha Tianji New Energy Industry Investment Fund (LP) as the largest shareholder with 24.625%. China Economic Net reported that the Shanghai Quhe fund controlled by Shi Yongmin held 8.59%. This review obtained no primary document identifying the group's actual controller, so the actual controller is not publicly confirmed; registered shareholdings and any actual-controller designation are listed separately.[11][5][10][4][10][11][5][13][3]

On financing, media citing Tianyancha records report that the company raised eight rounds by March 2023, including a round of over RMB5 billion completed in October 2020 led by local-government industrial guidance funds and large state-owned banks, with cumulative disclosed financing of about RMB10 billion.[12][5][4][10][11][5][13][3]

The Changsha Intermediate People's Court announcement records that Enovate Auto (Changsha) Group Co., Ltd. was incorporated on 3 September 2018 as a limited-liability company (Hong Kong/Macau/Taiwan-invested, not wholly owned) with registered capital of RMB620,618,536.67, and had fallen into a work-and-production halt due to a broken funding chain and operating difficulties.[3][4][10][11][5][13][3]

Competition

Historical positioning

The company positioned the ME7 as a premium mid-size electric SUV, with pre-sale prices of RMB366,800–381,800 before subsidies (April 2019) and launch prices of RMB218,800–289,800 after subsidies (September 2020); the ME5 was an extended-range compact SUV with a guide price of RMB149,900–159,900 (July 2021). These prices placed the brand in the mainstream-to-premium band of China's NEV market.[4][5][11][8][4][9][11][5][10]

Sales record

CPCA data cited by media show 1,778 units in 2021, 5,321 units in 2022 and 186 units in the first two months of 2023; Interface News reported 4,837 insured registrations in January–October 2022. These figures were far below leading new-EV makers' monthly deliveries.[9][5][11][8][4][9][11][5][10]

For context, media reported in April 2023 that the NEV market had entered an elimination phase, with brands such as GAC Fiat Chrysler, Qoros, Borgward and Ziyoujia exiting around the same period, and that Enovate's halt was part of this broader shakeout.[9][8][4][9][11][5][10]

Our assessment

Our assessment: the company had an early start, a qualified production permit and about RMB10 billion in cumulative financing, but its two models did not achieve meaningful volume, and it stopped producing in April 2023. Its competitive weakness was a slow product cadence — the ME7 was shown in November 2018 and pre-sold in April 2019 but only launched in September 2020, and it sold 97 units by the end of 2020 — and a lack of a distinctive product position. The basis is the media-reported sales data and timeline above.[8][10][8][4][9][11][5][10]

Our assessment: Enovate belongs to the first wave of new-EV makers that obtained production permits and financing but failed to reach scale. A China Economic Net report in September 2025 groups Enovate with WM Motor as cases where the founder left the country after the company ran into trouble, in contrast with HiPhi and Neta, whose founders stayed to push restructuring. The basis is that media report.[8][8][4][9][11][5][10]

Our assessment: Enovate's rise and fall reflects a common path among first-wave new-EV makers — raising funds and building plants at scale before demand was proven, then breaking the funding chain after a single weak model. The concrete evidence is the gap between planned capacity (180,000 units in Shaoxing and 60,000 in Changsha, per Interface News) and the 5,321 units actually sold in 2022. This is our judgement, not a company statement.[5][9][8][4][9][11][5][10]

Outlook

Historical company plans

In December 2022 the company announced the Saudi joint-venture plan and said it was also in talks with Saudi investors including the sovereign wealth fund and Aramco; media also reported the company had completed pre-IPO preparation work for a US listing. These were pre-halt plans and did not materialize. This review obtained no current official business plan or resumption target as of the cutoff.[5][6][10][5][6][10][2][3][1][7][15][9]

Reorganization status

The Changsha subsidiary's bankruptcy-liquidation application was filed in August 2024; a government reply on 7 April 2026 said the company was advancing strategic-investor introduction and work resumption through bankruptcy reorganization judicial proceedings; and on 4 June 2026 the Changsha Intermediate People's Court announced it was selecting a temporary administrator for the pre-reorganization case. However, on 17 August 2026 the court terminated the pre-reorganization administrator-selection after the applicant Shanghai Huanben Catering Management Co., Ltd. withdrew its bankruptcy reorganization application and the court approved the withdrawal. As of the cutoff, no primary announcement confirmed a completed reorganization plan, a selected strategic investor or a resumption of production.[1][2][3][15][5][6][10][2][3][1][7][15][9]

Our analysis

Our assessment: the June 2026 pre-reorganization was a procedural step, not proof of revival, and its termination in August 2026 leaves the company without a confirmed active reorganization proceeding as of the cutoff. Any restart would require an investor, funding, a usable production permit and a functioning after-sales and parts network. The basis is the June 2026 court notice, the August 2026 termination announcement, the April 2026 government reply and the Shaoxing asset auction. Whether the company ends in liquidation, a new reorganization filing or continued limbo cannot be determined from the evidence so far.[3][15][2][7][5][6][10][2][3][1][7][15][9]

Our analysis: to actually resume production, an investor would need not only to settle debts but also to rebuild the product line, production base and after-sales network. The Shaoxing assets have been auctioned, and the vehicle app and parts services have been affected, both weakening the basis for a restart. The basis is the auction record and the April 2026 government reply.[7][2][5][6][10][2][3][1][7][15][9]

Our analysis: Enovate's failure shows that an early start, a production permit and ample financing do not guarantee survival; sustained sales volume and product-market fit matter more. This is our judgement, based on the sales-versus-capacity gap and the halt timeline cited in this research.[9][5][5][6][10][2][3][1][7][15][9]

Risks

  • Reorganization and liquidation risk: a bankruptcy-liquidation application against the Changsha subsidiary was filed in August 2024 and pre-reorganization began in June 2026, but the pre-reorganization administrator-selection was terminated on 17 August 2026 after the applicant withdrew; as of the cutoff no completed plan, selected investor or confirmed active reorganization proceeding existed.[1][3][15][1][3][2][7][8][9][10][15][16][11]
  • Asset-disposal risk: the Shaoxing branch's machinery, fixtures and materials were auctioned for about RMB64.96 million against a book value of nearly RMB1 billion, meaning most of the manufacturing base was disposed of.[7][8][1][3][2][7][8][9][10][15][16][11]
  • After-sales and service risk: a government reply in April 2026 confirmed the vehicle app's biometric-service module was suspended and parts suppliers were unable to serve normally during the halt, with services to resume only after operations normalise.[2][1][3][2][7][8][9][10][15][16][11]
  • Production-resumption risk: work and production halted on 1 April 2023, sales and financing had been weak for years before the halt, and the company had not resumed production by the cutoff.[9][1][3][2][7][8][9][10][15][16][11]
  • Production-consistency and recall risk: in December 2024 the MIIT flagged the ME5 extended-range model for energy-consumption production-consistency non-compliance, and media reported the company said it would not voluntarily recall, which exposes owners to unresolved compliance issues.[16][1][3][2][7][8][9][10][15][16][11]
  • Governance risk: media reported that the founder had not resigned as chairman but had been unreachable to investors since late 2023, and that the company had long been neither liquidated nor formally bankrupt. This is a media report, not an official announcement.[10][1][3][2][7][8][9][10][15][16][11]
  • Procedural-stage risk: by August 2026 the court had terminated the pre-reorganization administrator-selection, and public records did not confirm any reorganization-plan timetable.[3][15][1][3][2][7][8][9][10][15][16][11]
  • Enforcement and credit risk: Hubei Daily reported in July 2023 that the company was listed as a dishonest judgment debtor by the Cixi People's Court for violating the property-reporting system, with over RMB50.36 million enforced against the company and its subsidiary Dearcc Auto Parts Manufacturing (Cixi) Co., Ltd.[11][1][3][2][7][8][9][10][15][16][11]

概览

天际汽车（ENOVATE）是中国新能源汽车造车新势力之一，前身电咖汽车于 2015 年 6 月成立，为乐视汽车体系内孵化的微型电动车品牌；2018 年发布高端品牌「天际」，2019 年公司主体更名为天际汽车科技集团有限公司。公司由曾任上汽大众总经理、乐视汽车高管的张海亮创立并出任董事长兼 CEO。[11][4][11][4][5][8][9][1][7][3][15][16]

公司先后推出两款量产车型——纯电中型 SUV ME7（2020 年 9 月上市）与增程式紧凑型 SUV ME5（2021 年 7 月上市），累计融资约百亿元，在浙江绍兴、湖南长沙布局整车工厂，并于 2022 年 12 月与沙特 Sumou Holding 签署合资合同、宣布赴沙特建厂。[5][8][11][4][5][8][9][1][7][3][15][16]

因销量远低于预期、资金链断裂，公司于 2023 年 4 月 1 日停工停产；此后经历 2024 年 8 月长沙子公司被申请破产清算、2024 年 12 月工信部通报 ME5 生产一致性不合规、2025 年 8 月绍兴基地核心资产以约 6495.6 万元（不足账面价值一成）被拍卖、2026 年 6 月长沙子公司进入预重整、2026 年 8 月 17 日该预重整案因申请人撤回而终止选任管理人程序等事件。截至 2026 年 10 月 29 日，集团尚未完成清算或正式破产，处于停摆状态。[9][1][16][7][3][15][11][4][5][8][9][1][7][3][15][16]

现状

业务与产品

公司业务为新能源汽车研发、制造与销售。共推出两款量产车型：纯电中型 SUV ME7（2020 年 9 月上市，补贴后 21.88 万–28.98 万元，NEDC 续航最高 530 公里）与增程式紧凑型 SUV ME5（2021 年 7 月上市，市场指导价 14.99 万–15.99 万元）。ME-S 概念轿车于 2019 年上海车展亮相但未量产。本次未取得各业务收入占比的一手财务披露。[8][12][10][4][5][8][9][11][2][3][7][1][12][10][4][6][16][15]

技术

ME7 基于 iMA 智能数字化架构打造，公司称其融合整车、IT、互联网技术，配备 5+X 多屏智能座舱、人脸识别与 L2 级驾驶辅助；预售时公司宣传采用博世扁铜线永磁同步电机、效率 98%，四驱版 0–100km/h 加速 4.9 秒。[4] ME5 采用 iMES 智慧增程系统，由 1.5 升发动机与电机组成，宣称纯电 NEDC 续航 155 公里、综合 NEDC 续航 1012 公里。[14] 上述为公司发布资料及媒体报道的介绍，本次未取得独立实测的当前全系规格。[5][8][9][11][2][3][7][1][12][10][4][6][16][15]

工厂与产能

绍兴基地于 2019 年 10 月进入工信部生产企业名录，同年 12 月 28 日 ME7 下线。界面新闻报道绍兴工厂规划占地约 1000 亩、总投资 55 亿元、设计年产能 18 万辆；长沙工厂 2021 年 6 月投产、设计年产能 6 万辆。关于三地总规划产能，媒体口径不一：中国经济网称长沙、绍兴、南宁三地规划总产能达 22 万辆，湖北日报称三地规划 34 万辆乘用车产能并另规划青岛、慈溪商用车基地；南宁、青岛、慈溪基地均未建成投产。[5][10][11][12][5][8][9][11][2][3][7][1][12][10][4][6][16][15]

生产资质

2018 年收购泉州西虎汽车生产基地获得新能源汽车生产资质；绍兴基地 2019 年 10 月、长沙基地 2020 年 12 月先后进入工信部道路机动车辆生产企业名录。[8][5][8][9][11][2][3][7][1][12][10][4][6][16][15]

海外布局

海外方面，公司于 2022 年 12 月 12 日与沙特企业 Sumou Holding 签署合资合同，计划分两期投资约 5 亿美元在沙特建设新能源汽车制造与研发基地，规划年产约 10 万辆；媒体报道其同时与沙特主权基金、沙特阿美等投资方洽谈。但该合资仅到签约阶段，未落地建设或投产，截至研究截止日无车辆从沙特基地下线或出口，亦无海外销量披露。[5][6][5][8][9][11][2][3][7][1][12][10][4][6][16][15]

国内销量记录

中国经济网报道 ME7 至 2020 年末累计销量仅 97 辆；媒体引用的乘联会数据显示 2021 年 1778 辆、2022 年 5321 辆、2023 年前两个月 186 辆；界面新闻报道 2022 年 1–10 月上险量 4837 辆。[10][5][9][5][8][9][11][2][3][7][1][12][10][4][6][16][15]

生产一致性问题

2024 年 12 月，工信部《关于 2023 年度新能源汽车监督检查结果的通报》将福建天际汽车制造有限公司旗下插电式增程混合动力多用途乘用车（QAC6452SHEVBB6，即 ME5）列入生产一致性不合规名单，原因为能量消耗量不符合 GB/T 19753-2021《轻型混合动力电动汽车能量消耗量试验方法》要求；媒体报道公司以涉及产品数量过多、不影响使用为由表示不会主动召回。[16][5][8][9][11][2][3][7][1][12][10][4][6][16][15]

现状与司法程序

截至 2026 年 10 月 29 日，公司自 2023 年 4 月 1 日起停工停产。长沙子公司 2024 年 8 月被申请破产清算、2026 年 6 月进入预重整选任临时管理人阶段，但 2026 年 8 月 17 日长沙市中级人民法院因申请人上海环本餐饮管理有限公司撤回破产重整申请并经裁定准许，终止了该预重整案选任管理人程序。公司从未上市，亦未披露任何季度或半年业绩。[9][1][3][15][5][8][9][11][2][3][7][1][12][10][4][6][16][15]

竞争格局

历史定位

公司将 ME7 定位为高端中型电动 SUV，2019 年 4 月预售补贴前 36.68 万–38.18 万元、2020 年 9 月上市补贴后 21.88 万–28.98 万元；ME5 为增程式紧凑型 SUV，2021 年 7 月市场指导价 14.99 万–15.99 万元。上述价格使其处于国内新能源市场的中端偏上区间。[4][5][11][8][4][9][11][5][10]

销量记录

媒体引用的乘联会数据显示，2021 年销量 1778 辆、2022 年 5321 辆、2023 年前两个月 186 辆；界面新闻报道 2022 年 1–10 月上险量 4837 辆。这些数字远低于头部造车新势力的单月交付量。[9][5][11][8][4][9][11][5][10]

作为背景，中国经济网 2023 年 4 月报道称新能源市场已进入淘汰赛，广汽菲克、观致、宝沃、自游家等品牌同期退出，天际汽车的停产属于这轮洗牌的一部分。[9][8][4][9][11][5][10]

我们的判断

我们的判断：天际汽车起步早、手握生产资质、累计融资约百亿元，但两款车型均未形成规模销量，并于 2023 年 4 月停产。其竞争短板在于产品节奏缓慢（ME7 于 2018 年 11 月亮相、2019 年 4 月预售、2020 年 9 月才上市，至 2020 年末仅售 97 辆）及产品定位缺乏鲜明特色。依据为上述媒体报道的销量数据与时间线。[8][10][8][4][9][11][5][10]

我们的判断：天际汽车属于第一代造车新势力中拿到生产资质与融资、但未能形成规模销量的阵营。中国经济网 2025 年 9 月的报道将天际与威马归为「公司遇困后创始人离境」的情形，与高合、哪吒创始人留在国内推进重整形成对比。这一对比依据为该媒体报道。[8][8][4][9][11][5][10]

我们的判断：天际汽车的兴衰折射出第一代造车新势力的一种常见路径——在需求未验证前大举融资建厂，随后因单一车型失利而资金链断裂。具体证据是规划产能（界面新闻报道绍兴 18 万辆、长沙 6 万辆）与 2022 年实际销量 5321 辆之间的巨大落差。这是我们的判断，非公司表述。[5][9][8][4][9][11][5][10]

未来展望

公司规划的历史记录

2022 年 12 月公司宣布沙特合资计划，并称正与沙特主权基金、阿美石油等投资方洽谈；媒体还报道公司已完成美股上市前期准备工作。上述均为停工前的历史规划，均未落地。本次未取得截至研究截止日的公司现行经营计划或复产目标。[5][6][10][5][6][10][2][3][1][7][15][9]

重整进展

长沙子公司于 2024 年 8 月被申请破产清算；2026 年 4 月 7 日政府回复称企业正通过破产重组司法程序推进战略投资人引进及复工复产；2026 年 6 月 4 日长沙中院公告为该预重整案选任临时管理人。但 2026 年 8 月 17 日，长沙中院因申请人上海环本餐饮管理有限公司撤回破产重整申请并经裁定准许，终止了该预重整案选任管理人程序。截至截止日，本次核查未取得确认重整计划获批、战略投资人确定或复产的一手公告。[1][2][3][15][5][6][10][2][3][1][7][15][9]

我们的分析

我们的判断：长沙子公司 2026 年 6 月进入预重整属程序性进展，不等于业务复活；而 2026 年 8 月预重整案因申请人撤回而终止，使公司截至截止日再无确认的进行中的重整程序。任何重启都需落实投资人、资金、可用生产资质及售后配件体系。依据为 2026 年 6 月法院公告、2026 年 8 月终止公告、2026 年 4 月政府回复及绍兴基地资产拍卖事实。公司最终走向清算、重新申请重整还是继续搁置，现有证据尚不足以判断。[3][15][2][7][5][6][10][2][3][1][7][15][9]

我们的分析：若要真正复产，投资人不仅需清偿债务，还需重建产品、生产基地与售后网络；绍兴资产已被拍卖、车辆 App 与配件服务已受影响，均削弱了重启基础。依据为拍卖记录与 2026 年 4 月政府回复。[7][2][5][6][10][2][3][1][7][15][9]

我们的分析：天际汽车失败的教训在于，起步早、有生产资质、融资充足并不保证存活，持续的销量规模与产品市场匹配度更为关键。这是我们的判断，依据为本研究引用的销量与产能落差及停工时间线。[9][5][5][6][10][2][3][1][7][15][9]

风险

  • 重整与清算风险：2024 年 8 月长沙子公司被申请破产清算，2026 年 6 月进入预重整，但 2026 年 8 月 17 日该预重整案因申请人撤回而终止选任管理人程序；截至截止日无确认的重整计划、选定投资人或进行中的重整程序。[1][3][15][1][3][2][7][8][9][10][15][16][11]
  • 资产处置风险：绍兴分公司机器设备等以约 6495.6 万元成交，账面价值近 10 亿元，主要生产基地已被处置。[7][8][1][3][2][7][8][9][10][15][16][11]
  • 售后与服务风险：2026 年 4 月政府回复确认，停工停产期间车辆 App 生物识别服务模块暂停、零配件供应商暂难正常服务，待企业运营正常后逐步恢复。[2][1][3][2][7][8][9][10][15][16][11]
  • 复产风险：2023 年 4 月 1 日停工停产；媒体报道停工前销量、融资已持续疲弱，截至截止日公司未复产。[9][1][3][2][7][8][9][10][15][16][11]
  • 生产一致性与召回风险：2024 年 12 月工信部通报 ME5 增程式车型能量消耗量生产一致性不合规，媒体报道公司表示不会主动召回，车主面临合规问题未解决的风险。[16][1][3][2][7][8][9][10][15][16][11]
  • 治理风险：媒体报道，创始人未卸任董事长，但自 2023 年底起投资人无法与其取得联系，公司长期未清算、未宣布破产。此为媒体报道，非官方公告。[10][1][3][2][7][8][9][10][15][16][11]
  • 程序阶段风险：截至 2026 年 8 月，长沙中院已终止预重整案选任管理人程序，公开记录未确认任何重整计划时间表。[3][15][1][3][2][7][8][9][10][15][16][11]
  • 执行与信用风险：湖北日报 2023 年 7 月报道，公司因违反财产报告制度被慈溪市人民法院列为失信被执行人，公司及旗下电咖汽车零部件制造（慈溪）有限公司被执行 5036 万余元。[11][1][3][2][7][8][9][10][15][16][11]
