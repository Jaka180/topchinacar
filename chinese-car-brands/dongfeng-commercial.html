Overview

Dongfeng Commercial Vehicle Co., Ltd. (东风商用车有限公司) is the core medium- and heavy-duty commercial-vehicle business of the Dongfeng group, headquartered in Shiyan, Hubei, with origins tracing to the Second Automobile Works established in 1969 [15]. On 26 January 2015 the company, a joint venture between Dongfeng Motor Group Co., Ltd. and AB Volvo on a 55:45 basis with RMB 9.2 billion registered capital, was formally inaugurated in Shiyan to develop, produce and sell "Dongfeng"-brand medium/heavy trucks, buses, special vehicles and chassis, engines and transmissions [10][9]. It is one of China's leading medium/heavy-truck makers. In 2025 its wholesale volume was 131,000 units (up 24% year on year) and new-energy sales exceeded 10,000 units (up 98%). It is now driving electrification and intelligence through its D600 "Green-Smart" platform [6][4]. It is built around the Dongfeng Tianlong and Tianjin platforms and uses the brand promise "everything is rooted in reliability" [1].

Current operations

Business structure

Dongfeng Commercial Vehicle makes medium and heavy trucks, special vehicles and key assemblies — cabs, frames, engines, transmissions and axles — together with casting/forging and aftermarket businesses. Its range spans heavy-truck, medium-truck and special-vehicle lines, built on the Tianlong and Tianjin platforms plus the Changxing, Tuoxing and Jincheng application series [15][1]. On 28 March 2024 Dongfeng held the launch meeting of its Commercial Vehicle Division, announcing a commercial-vehicle "Leapfrog Project" (跃升工程) and a new division to coordinate Dongfeng Commercial Vehicle, Dongfeng Automobile Co., Ltd., Dongfeng Liuzhou and Dongfeng Special Commercial Vehicle (Dongfeng Huashen); Dongfeng Commercial Vehicle is the core medium/heavy-truck entity under that division [27].

Sales and operating performance

In 2024 the company sold 106,000 units (up 6%), including 5,058 new-energy vehicles (up 211%) and more than 20,000 exports (up 60%), with the Tianlong GX high-end truck selling more than 11,000 units in a single overseas market [13]. In 2025 it reported wholesale sales of 131,000 units (up 24%), new-energy sales exceeding 10,000 units (up 98%) and exports of 20,000 units [6][21]. The company said its 2025 domestic conventional-energy share rose from 14.0% in 2024 to above 16.0%, its resource-transport share rose from 6.0% to 12.4%, its tractor share reached 11.3% (up two percentage points), and it kept a leading position in cargo trucks and the No.1 position in special vehicles [18]. In January–April 2025 its domestic retail share reached 22.7% (23.6% in April alone), with first-half 2025 wholesale sales of 63,000 units (up 4.3%) and retail sales of 60,000 units [14].

As of the research cut-off date, the latest disclosed result is first-half 2026: the company's mid-year work meeting stated only that first-half sales "maintained year-on-year growth" and called for "winning the battle to turn losses into profit" [8], while the Shiyan Daily reported first-half overseas sales up 75% [12]. The industry outlet Operator Finance reported in October 2026 that first-half wholesale and retail sales were 66,000 and 61,000 units respectively and that this year's sales target was 153,000 units; these specific figures have not been confirmed in any official company announcement [26][8].

Plants and capacity

The company is based in Shiyan, with a whole-vehicle plant and the new D600 smart factory. According to a September 2025 media visit, the existing whole-vehicle plant has four assembly lines and five inspection lines, can build new-energy, gas and diesel vehicles on the same line, has annual capacity of about 280,000 units and turns out a vehicle roughly every 90 seconds [14]. The company's campus-recruitment profile states annual capacity of 200,000 units and 10 plants and subsidiaries [15]. On 24 January 2026 the D600 smart factory — total investment RMB 1.838 billion, 670 mu of land, 288 robots, 100% automated welding and painting, production efficiency 30% higher than a conventional plant — formally started production in Shiyan [3]. On a design basis, once fully completed it will turn out one body every 87 seconds and reach annual output of 260,000 units and output value of RMB 6 billion (not yet the achieved run-rate) [3].

Technology and platforms

On 10 October 2026 the company globally launched its new "Green-Smart" platform (the D600 platform) in Wuhan. Built over five years through 11 parallel projects, it covers five energy routes — diesel, gas, electric, hydrogen and ammonia — 34 market segments and 143 typical scenarios, is compatible with five global regulatory systems and 28 key countries, and cuts energy consumption 8% versus "oil-to-electric" conversions [4]. On powertrains, the self-developed Longqing 3.0 chain, still being iterated, covers six medium- and heavy-duty powertrain lines and four emissions stages (China VI / Euro VI / Euro V / Euro III) [19]. In new energy the company says it has broken through modular batteries, high-speed motors, integrated electric drive axles, central integrated domain control and full-vehicle thermal management, and has launched more than 70 models including hydrogen-fuel and hybrid variants [18][21]. On assisted driving, cumulative sales of high-level assisted-driving tractors have exceeded 1,000 units with more than 200 million kilometres of public-road safe operation, and the company is advancing routine L4 autonomous mining-truck and terminal-truck testing [21].

Product portfolio

For 2026 the company announced at its December 2025 partner conference 214 new products across the tractor, cargo, engineering and special-vehicle categories and diesel, gas, pure-electric and hybrid routes; conventional-energy tractors cover seven sub-markets and 25 typical scenarios with 67 model-year variants, plus a new Cummins 16-litre high-horsepower gas product (above 720 hp), while new-energy tractors target eight scenarios with 13 model-year variants across charging, battery-swap, range-extender and P2-hybrid routes [18]. In new energy the company had already launched more than 70 models including hydrogen-fuel and hybrid variants in 2025, covering 24 customer operating scenarios; its pure-electric tractors cover the mainstream market within 300 km with energy consumption 3%–5% better than rivals, and hydrogen-fuel models are in demonstration operation on the Xiong'an and Han-Yi expressways, now in their third generation [18].

Overseas business

The company is expanding in 28 key countries and regions across Asia, Europe, Africa, Australia and Latin America, and works with Dongfeng Import & Export to build an integrated export-and-service system and advance KD localised production [19][12]. Exports were 20,000 units in 2025 [21], and first-half 2026 overseas sales rose 75% year on year [12]. In 2024 the Tianlong GX high-end truck sold more than 11,000 units in a single overseas market [13].

Founders and leadership

Founder

As a joint-venture subsidiary of the state-owned Dongfeng group, the company has no private founder; its history traces to the state-established Second Automobile Works of 1969 [15].

Current management

Liu Licheng (刘利成) — general manager. The company's 20 January 2026 work-conference report confirms him in the post, where he delivered the general-manager's work report [6]. According to the industry outlet CVWorld (tier C), Liu was born in 1968 and graduated from Zhejiang University; he served as head of the manufacturing headquarters and in procurement-and-supply roles, was assistant general manager for about nine years from 2015, became deputy party secretary, deputy general manager and trade-union chairman in March 2024, and succeeded to the general-manager post in May 2025 [11].

Zeng Miao (曾苗) — party secretary. At the 11 December 2025 partner conference he still attended as discipline-inspection secretary [19]; official company reports confirm him as party secretary from 26 August 2026 [7], and the 10 October 2026 platform launch again lists him as party secretary [4]. According to Operator Finance (tier C), Zeng was born in March 1975, graduated from Beihang University, joined Dongfeng in 1996 and served in Dongfeng's personnel (cadres) department and its import/export, passenger-vehicle and logistics units before becoming discipline-inspection secretary (and chief compliance officer) of Dongfeng Commercial Vehicle in June 2022 [16]. His predecessor Zheng Hua still held the post of party secretary and deputy general manager as of January 2026 [6].

The management team listed at the December 2025 partner conference also included deputy general managers Liu Zhensheng, Wu Huaizhu, Huang Zheng, Bai Weizhi and Yang Ruoqing, and assistant general managers Frank, Fang Yong, Zhang Da and Liu Yi [19]. By October 2026, Wu Huaizhu was appearing as deputy secretary-general of the China Association of Automobile Manufacturers, and the role of head of the technology centre was held by assistant general manager Xiang Xusheng [4].

Former chairman Zhang Xiaofan (张小帆) moved to FAW in January 2026. According to China Securities Journal and Economic Observer reports, Zhang was born in March 1970 and joined Dongfeng (the Second Automobile Works) in July 1992; his earlier roles included assistant general manager of Dongfeng Automobile, general manager of Dongfeng Light Commercial Vehicle Marketing Co., Ltd., deputy mayor of Xiangyang (on secondment), deputy general manager of Dongfeng Special Commercial Vehicle, and director of the Dongfeng Group Party Committee and Board office, and board secretary. He became general manager of Dongfeng Commercial Vehicle in February 2023, deputy general manager of the Commercial Vehicle Division in March 2024, general manager of that division in April 2025 and chairman of Dongfeng Commercial Vehicle in May 2025; on 28 January 2026 he was appointed member of the Standing Committee of the Party Committee and vice president of China FAW Group [24][23][27]. After moving to FAW, Dongfeng Automobile Co., Ltd. (600006.SH) said on 15 July 2026 that Zhang continued to perform his chairman duties there through internal coordination or authorisation [25]. As of the cut-off date, Dongfeng had not announced a new chairman for Dongfeng Commercial Vehicle.

Ownership and governance

The company is a Sino-foreign joint venture in which Dongfeng Motor Group Co., Ltd. holds 55% and AB Volvo (publ) holds 45%, with registered capital of RMB 9.2 billion [10]. It is not separately listed, and its board composition and controlling shareholder are not separately disclosed in public annual reports or announcements; this page states only the shareholding structure [10][15].

Competition

Our judgement. The leading players in China's medium/heavy-truck market include FAW Jiefang, Sinotruk, Shaanxi Automobile and Foton, and competition is intense. The Economic Observer, citing industry data, puts 2025 domestic medium/heavy-truck sales at about 1.25 million units, up 20% year on year [23]. FAW Jiefang sold 280,000 vehicles in 2025, including 255,000 medium/heavy trucks (up 19.0%), 45,000 new-energy vehicles (up 184.3%) and 60,000 exports [20]. Sinotruk Group's heavy-truck production and sales exceeded 300,000 units in 2025, a new record, and it has ranked first in China in heavy-truck exports for 21 consecutive years [22]. Dongfeng Commercial Vehicle itself sold 131,000 units (wholesale) in 2025 [6], a scale below both Jiefang and Sinotruk — which is why at its 2026 work conference the company set the task of "striving for the industry top three" and adopted "returning to No.1 in the medium/heavy-truck industry" as its direction of endeavour [6]. Its stated strengths are the self-developed Longqing powertrain, the No.1 position in special vehicles and a leading position in cargo trucks, with domestic conventional-energy share above 16% in 2025 [18]. In new-energy medium/heavy trucks its 2025 new-energy sales had just passed 10,000 units, still well below FAW Jiefang's 45,000 new-energy vehicles [6][20]. Our judgement: the company retains solid brand and product foundations in the medium/heavy-truck segment, but its heavy-truck scale now trails Jiefang and Sinotruk, and its official use of "turn losses into profit" as an operating goal points to profitability pressure [8].

Outlook

Company plans. At its December 2025 partner conference Dongfeng Commercial Vehicle set a 2026 sales target of 176,000 units and plans to launch 214 new products in 2026 across tractor, cargo, engineering and special-vehicle lines covering diesel, gas, battery-electric and hybrid routes [2]. Over the 15th Five-Year Plan period the company says it will keep the vision of becoming a world-class commercial-vehicle enterprise, anchoring the new-energy track (the "Xiangxin" project), the globalisation track (the "Yangfan" project) and the conventional-energy track (the "Jiazhi" project) [2]. In new energy it plans to break through in five scenarios — resource transport, regional distribution, engineering heavy-haul, medium municipal use and engineering operations — aiming for the industry top five, along pure-electric, hydrogen-fuel and hybrid routes; it also targets a first-tier share of above 18% and an overseas sales share above 30% [18]. The D600 (Green-Smart) platform, covering five energy routes, is its next-generation modular strategic platform for the 15th Five-Year Plan period [4].

Our judgement. At its July 2026 mid-year meeting the company said only that first-half sales "maintained year-on-year growth", without publishing specific volumes or cutting its year-start 176,000-unit target in the public release; the 153,000-unit target and first-half wholesale/retail figures of 66,000/61,000 units reported by Operator Finance in October 2026 are not confirmed by any official announcement [8][26]. On product cadence, the company has said its next-generation full range is expected to launch in the second half of 2026, and the D600 platform was launched globally in October 2026 with intent orders for 8,700 units from 10 strategic customers, making new-product ramp-up the key variable for second-half volume [18][4]. For the second half the company has called for winning three campaigns — domestic market, new-energy market and overseas market — and stressed that its subsidiaries should keep improving profitability so as to support the company's own return to profit [8]. Whether it can "return to No.1 in the industry" and turn a profit depends on D600 ramp-up, new-energy volume growth and overseas breakthroughs, all of which remain uncertain [4][8].

Risks

  • Profitability pressure. The company's official statements repeatedly describe "winning the battle to turn losses into profit" as a core task, though it discloses no specific profit or loss figures; on this basis we judge that it faces near-term profitability pressure [8][6].
  • Competitive gap. Its heavy-truck volume trails FAW Jiefang and Sinotruk: FAW Jiefang sold 255,000 medium/heavy trucks in 2025 and Sinotruk's heavy-truck production and sales topped 300,000 units, both above Dongfeng Commercial Vehicle's 131,000 units [20][22][6].
  • New-energy transition. New-energy sales exceeded 10,000 units in 2025 (up 98%) but remain a small share of the 131,000-unit total, and the shift to the D600 platform requires sustained investment [6][4].
  • Overseas and policy risks. The company targets 28 key countries and regions and is advancing KD localised production, exposing it to trade barriers, exchange-rate moves and local regulatory changes [19][12].
  • Disclosure perimeter. As an unlisted 55:45 joint-venture subsidiary, the company does not separately publish financial statements, limiting external visibility into its operating results [10].
  • Management succession. Former chairman Zhang Xiaofan was transferred in January 2026, and as of the cut-off date no new chairman of Dongfeng Commercial Vehicle has been announced [24][25].

概览

东风商用车有限公司是东风汽车集团旗下中重型商用车核心事业板块，总部位于湖北省十堰市，起源于 1969 年成立的中国第二汽车制造厂[15]。2015 年 1 月 26 日，东风汽车集团股份有限公司与沃尔沃集团（AB Volvo）以 55:45 股比共同投资组建的合资公司正式挂牌运营，注册资本 92 亿元，研发、生产、销售「东风」品牌中重型卡车、客车、专用车及底盘、发动机、变速箱等[10][9]。公司是中国中重卡市场头部企业之一，2025 年批发销量 13.1 万台（同比增长 24%）、新能源销量破万（同比增长 98%），正以 D600「绿智」平台推进新能源与智能化转型[6][4]。公司以东风天龙、东风天锦等平台为核心，并将「一切源于可靠」作为品牌承诺[1]。

现状

业务结构

东风商用车主营中重型卡车、专用车及驾驶室、车架、发动机、变速箱、车桥等关键总成，并涵盖铸锻、后市场等业务，产品覆盖重卡、中卡、专用车及核心零部件，旗下有东风天龙、东风天锦两大整车平台，以及东风畅行、东风拓行、东风锦程等系列品牌[15][1]。2024 年 3 月 28 日，东风公司召开商用车事业部启动会，宣布实施商用车「跃升工程」、成立商用车事业部，统筹管理东风商用车、东风汽车股份有限公司、东风柳州汽车、东风特种商用车（东风华神）等业务，东风商用车为该事业部体系下的中重卡核心业务主体[27]。

销量与经营

2024 年，公司实现销量 10.6 万辆（同比增长 6%），其中新能源 5058 辆（同比增长 211%）、出口突破 2 万辆（同比增长 60%），天龙 GX 高端车在单一海外市场销量超 1.1 万辆[13]。2025 年，公司实现批发销量 13.1 万台（同比增长 24%）、新能源销量破万（同比增长 98%）、出口销量 2 万台[6][21]。据公司介绍，2025 年国内传统能源份额由 2024 年的 14.0% 提升至 16.0% 以上，资源运输份额由 6.0% 提升至 12.4%，牵引车份额达 11.3%（同比增长两个百分点），载货车保持领先，专用车持续保持行业第一[18]。2025 年 1–4 月，公司国内终端市占率达 22.7%，4 月单月升至 23.6%；2025 年上半年批发销量 6.3 万台（同比增长 4.3%）、终端销量 6.0 万台[14]。

截至研究截止日，已公布的最近一期业绩为 2026 年上半年：公司年中工作会仅表示「上半年销量同比保持增长」，并提出「打赢扭亏为盈攻坚战」[8]；十堰日报报道，上半年海外销量同比增长 75%[12]。行业媒体运营商财经网 2026 年 10 月报道，上半年批发销量 6.6 万辆、终端 6.1 万辆，并称公司今年销量目标为 15.3 万辆；上述具体数字未见公司官方公告证实[26][8]。

工厂与产能

公司以十堰为制造基地，拥有整车工厂与新建的 D600 智慧工厂。据 2025 年 9 月媒体探访报道，公司现有整车工厂有 4 条整车装配线和 5 条检测线，可实现新能源车、燃气车、油车混流生产，年产能约 28 万辆，平均每 90 秒下线一辆整车[14]。公司校园招聘简介称其年产能达 20 万台，拥有 10 家工厂、分（子）公司[15]。2026 年 1 月 24 日，总投资 18.38 亿元、占地 670 亩的 D600 智慧工厂在十堰正式投产，拥有 288 台机器人协同作业，焊接、涂装实现 100% 自动化，生产效率较传统工厂提升 30%[3]。按设计口径，该工厂建成后平均 87 秒下线一台车身，年产量将达 26 万辆、产值 60 亿元（非当前已实现产量）[3]。

技术与平台

2026 年 10 月 10 日，公司在武汉全球发布全新「绿智平台」（即 D600 平台），该平台历时五年、历经 11 个平行项目，实现燃油、燃气、电、氢、氨五种能源路线全覆盖，覆盖 34 个细分市场、143 个典型场景，兼容全球 5 大法规体系、28 个重点国家，能耗较「油改电」车型降低 8%[4]。动力总成方面，龙擎 3.0 持续迭代，基于平台化技术架构拓展中重型 6 条动力链，覆盖国六/欧六/欧五/欧三四大法规阶段[19]。新能源方面，公司已突破模块化电池、高速电机、高效集成电驱桥、中央集成域控、全域热管理等核心技术，推出包含氢燃料与混动在内的 70 余款车型[18][21]。智能驾驶方面，据公司介绍，高阶辅助驾驶牵引车累计销量已达 1000 台以上，公开道路安全运营超过 2 亿公里，并正在推进 L4 级无人矿卡、无人集卡的常态化运营测试[21]。

商品布局

面向 2026 年，公司在 2025 年 12 月合作伙伴大会上宣布投放覆盖牵引、载货、工程、专用四大品系、燃油/燃气/纯电/混动多种能源路线的 214 款新商品；其中传统能源牵引车针对 7 大细分市场、25 个典型场景推出 67 款年型车，并新增康明斯 16L 大马力燃气商品（动力提升至 720 马力以上），新能源牵引车聚焦 8 大场景、布局充电、换电、增程、P2 混动等多技术路线并投放 13 款年型车[18]。在新能源领域，公司 2025 年已推出包含氢燃料和混动在内的 70 余款车型、覆盖 24 个客户运营场景，其中纯电牵引车覆盖 300 公里以内主流市场、能耗优于竞品 3%–5%，氢燃料车型已在雄安和汉宜高速示范运营并迭代至第三代[18]。

海外业务

公司面向亚欧非澳拉 28 个重点国家和地区推进海外业务，并联合东风进出口公司搭建一体化出海运营体系，推进 KD 属地化生产[19][12]。2025 年公司出口销量 2 万台[21]；2026 年上半年海外销量同比增长 75%[12]。2024 年，东风天龙 GX 高端车在单一海外市场销量超 1.1 万辆[13]。

东风商用车有限公司未单独上市，不单独披露财务报表。其为东风汽车集团股份有限公司持股 55%、沃尔沃集团（AB Volvo）持股 45% 的合资子公司，财务并入东风汽车集团股份有限公司合并报表，但集团公开文件不单独披露东风商用车有限公司的损益表与资产负债表明细，因此营收、毛利率、净利润等指标均无独立公开数字，本表留空。公司无单一上市母公司，没有已公布的「归属于母公司股东的净利润」。若披露利润，应为合资公司自身、由全体股东（东风 55%、沃尔沃 45%）合计应享的利润，而不是东风汽车集团单独应占的 55% 份额。集团层面财务数据见东风汽车集团深度研究。本页仅为财务数据披露，不构成任何交易推荐。

竞争格局

我们的判断。中国中重卡市场头部企业包括一汽解放、中国重汽、陕汽、福田等，竞争激烈。据经济观察报援引行业数据，2025 年国内中重卡市场全年销量约 125 万辆、同比增长 20%[23]。一汽解放 2025 年整车销售 28 万辆，其中中重卡 25.5 万辆（同比增长 19.0%）、新能源整车 4.5 万辆（同比增长 184.3%）、海外出口 6 万辆[20]。中国重汽集团 2025 年重卡产销规模突破 30 万辆、再创历史新高，重卡出口连续 21 年居中国首位[22]。东风商用车自身 2025 年批发销量为 13.1 万台，规模低于解放与重汽，这也解释了公司为何在 2026 年工作会上提出「力争行业前三」、并将「重返中重卡行业第一」作为奋斗方向[6]。公司公开表述的自身优势是：龙擎自主动力总成、专用车行业第一、载货车保持领先，2025 年国内传统能源份额超 16%[18]。在新能源中重卡方面，公司 2025 年新能源销量刚破万辆，与一汽解放 4.5 万辆的新能源整车规模仍有明显差距[6][20]。我们的判断：公司在中重卡领域仍有较强的品牌与商品基础，但重卡规模已落后于解放、重汽，且官方以「扭亏为盈」表述经营目标，反映出一定的盈利压力[8]。

未来展望

公司公开规划。2025 年 12 月合作伙伴大会上，东风商用车提出 2026 年销量目标 17.6 万辆，并计划 2026 年投放覆盖牵引、载货、工程、专用四大品系、燃油/燃气/纯电/混动多种能源路线的 214 款新商品[2]。「十五五」期间公司表示将坚持「成为世界一流商用车企业」的愿景，锚定新能源赛道推进「向新工程」、锚定全球化赛道推进「扬帆工程」、锚定传统能源赛道推进「价值工程」[2]。在新能源领域，公司计划以资源运输、区域配送、工程重载、中型市政、工程作业五大场景重点突破，力争进入行业前五，并坚持纯电、氢燃料、混动三条路线布局；公司还提出份额稳定在第一阵营（18% 以上）、海外销量占比大于 30% 的目标[18]。D600（绿智）平台覆盖五种能源路线，是公司面向「十五五」的新一代模块化战略平台[4]。

我们的判断。公司 2026 年 7 月年中工作会仅表述「上半年销量同比保持增长」，未公布具体销量数字，也未在公开稿中下调年初 17.6 万辆目标；行业媒体运营商财经网 2026 年 10 月报道的 15.3 万辆目标与上半年批发 6.6 万辆、终端 6.1 万辆，均未见官方公告证实[8][26]。从商品节奏看，公司表示下一代全系产品有望 2026 年下半年上市，D600 平台已于 2026 年 10 月全球发布，并在发布会上与 10 家战略客户达成 8700 台意向订单，新品爬坡是下半年冲量的关键变量[18][4]。公司下半年提出坚决打赢国内市场、新能源市场、海外市场三大战役，并强调以各子分公司持续提升盈利能力为企业扭亏提供支撑[8]。能否实现「重回行业第一」并扭亏为盈，取决于 D600 产品爬坡、新能源放量与海外突破，均存在不确定性[4][8]。

风险

  • 盈利压力。公司官方口径多次把「打赢扭亏为盈攻坚战」作为核心任务，但未披露具体盈亏数字；据此判断其近期存在盈利压力[8][6]。
  • 竞争差距。重卡规模落后于一汽解放、中国重汽：一汽解放 2025 年中重卡销量 25.5 万辆、中国重汽重卡产销突破 30 万辆，均高于东风商用车的 13.1 万台[20][22][6]。
  • 新能源转型。2025 年新能源销量破万（同比增长 98%），但在 13.1 万台总量中占比仍低，向 D600 平台转型需持续投入[6][4]。
  • 海外与政策风险。公司面向 28 个重点国家和地区并推进 KD 属地化，面临贸易壁垒、汇率及当地法规变化风险[19][12]。
  • 披露范围。作为未单独上市的 55:45 合资子公司，公司不单独披露财务报表，外部对其经营结果的可见度有限[10]。
  • 管理层更替。原董事长张小帆于 2026 年 1 月调离，截至研究截止日尚未公开宣布东风商用车的新任董事长[24][25]。
