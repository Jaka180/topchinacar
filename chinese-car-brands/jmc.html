Overview

Jiangling Motors Corporation, Ltd. (JMC; SZSE 000550 A-share, 200550 B-share) is a commercial-vehicle and SUV manufacturer headquartered in Nanchang, Jiangxi. It is the listed vehicle-making arm of Jiangling Motors Group (JMCG) and a long-standing partner of Ford Motor Company, which has held a stake in JMC since 1995 and now owns 32.00% [4]. JMC makes and sells JMC-brand light trucks, pickups and light buses, and Ford-brand light buses, MPVs, SUVs and pickups; in FY2025 it reported operating revenue of RMB39.17 billion and profit attributable to owners of the parent of RMB1.19 billion, and sold 377,253 vehicles including 160,000 exports [1][8].

JMC is a state-controlled Sino-foreign listed company: its controlling shareholders are Nanchang Jiangling Investment Co., Ltd. (41.03%, a state-owned legal person) and Ford Motor Company (32.00%); the annual report identifies Jiangling Motors Group Co., Ltd. and Chongqing Changan Automobile Co., Ltd. as the actual controllers [1]. Its products ranked first in China's light-bus segment, second in pickups and seventh in light trucks in 2025 [8].

Current operations

Business structure

JMC's main business is manufacturing and selling commercial vehicles and SUVs plus related parts. In FY2025 whole-vehicle revenue was RMB35.96 billion (91.8% of operating revenue), materials and parts RMB2.11 billion (5.39%), and maintenance and technical services RMB1.10 billion (2.81%); gross margins for the three lines were 11.88%, 30.23% and 36.99% respectively [1][9]. In 2025 the company produced 378,338 and sold 377,253 vehicles, comprising 99,770 light buses, 83,207 trucks, 59,681 pickups and 134,595 SUVs [1]. Beyond complete vehicles, the annual report's description of the company's basic situation covers production and sales of vehicles and parts, after-sales service, import and export, used vehicles, management consulting and freight-capacity services [1].

Products and models

JMC's JMC-brand lineup includes the Shunda light truck, the Baodian, Domain Tiger (Yuhu) and Dadao pickups, and the Teshun light bus; Ford-brand products include the Transit (Quanshun) light bus, the Tourneo MPV, the Equator and Equator Sport SUVs, the Ranger pickup and the Bronco off-road SUV. The 2025 annual report also records the launch of the Ford Zhiqu Liema new-energy SUV in Nanchang in December 2025 [1]. The locally built Ranger launched in December 2023 from RMB145,800, and the Bronco launched on 25 April 2024 from RMB299,800 [14][4].

New energy

In 2025 JMC produced 37,304 and sold 35,806 NEVs, with NEV revenue of RMB2.958 billion; of this, NEV commercial vehicles accounted for production of 32,373 and sales of 32,557 (revenue RMB2.404 billion), and NEV passenger vehicles for production of 4,931 and sales of 3,249 (revenue RMB554 million) [1][8]. The company has laid out a full NEV lineup across light buses, pickups and light trucks, and in January 2025 launched the E Fushun light bus and E Shunda light truck, both pure-electric commercial vehicles [4].

Technology and platforms

In March 2025 the company released the new 'Blue Flame' powertrain platform, and in April 2025 jointly released a 'super light-truck system solution' with Bosch at the Shanghai Auto Show; the group has also self-developed the IDEA electronic/electrical architecture and a 'nine-in-one' central computing platform for commercial vehicles [4]. FY2025 R&D investment was RMB1.490 billion, down 12.31% year on year, with capitalised R&D at 18.41% of the total [1][9].

Plants and capacity

JMC's main manufacturing base is the Fushan plant in the Xiaolan Economic Development Zone, Nanchang, which went into full production on 9 September 2021. It covers about 1,500 mu (100 hectares), involved total investment of about RMB12.8 billion and has annual capacity of 300,000 vehicles, producing JMC-brand light trucks (conventional and new-energy) and Ford-brand SUVs such as the Equator and Equator Sport [18][4][13].

Overseas business

The listed company exported 160,000 vehicles in 2025, up 38.4% year on year [1][8]. At the group level, exports rose from about 34,000 vehicles in 2021 to about 169,900 in 2025, a compound annual growth rate of about 49% during the 14th Five-Year Plan period, with overseas sales concentrated in the Middle East, Africa, Latin America, Southeast Asia and the Caribbean [15]. The group operates 102 overseas dealer channels and 13 KD (knocked-down) assembly plants; in Algeria it produces locally via KD assembly with annual exports exceeding 10,000 units, and in 2025 six markets (including Vietnam, Argentina and Saudi Arabia) each exceeded 10,000 units, with a further 16 markets above 1,000 units [15]. In 2025 the group exported more than 34,000 vehicles to the Middle East region, where Saudi Arabia became its first 10,000-unit market [15]. Products aimed at overseas markets include the E-luda electric light truck meeting European standards, a new-generation pickup and a pure-electric SUV [15].

Latest results (as of 2026-10-29)

As of 29 October 2026, JMC's most recently disclosed results are for the first nine months of 2026: revenue of RMB28.786 billion (up 5.49% year on year) and profit attributable to owners of the parent of RMB907 million (up 21.02%), with operating cash flow of RMB1.782 billion; cumulative vehicle sales rose 9.32% to 285,285 units, comprising 82,132 light buses, 58,583 trucks, 45,058 pickups and 99,512 SUVs [3][11]. Third-quarter profit attributable to the parent rose 922.92% year on year to RMB168 million. The report attributes the increases in profit attributable to the parent and profit excluding non-recurring items for the first nine months mainly to the effect of deferred tax assets reversed following JMC Ford Automotive Technology (Shanghai)'s business adjustment in the third quarter of 2025. Income tax expense in the first nine months of 2026 fell by RMB507 million, or 77.84%, mainly because of that prior-year tax effect [3].

Founders and leadership

Founder

JMC has no individual founder. Its predecessor is the state-run Nanchang Automobile Maintenance Plant (founded April 1947), renamed Jiangxi Automobile Manufacturing Plant in October 1967, which began vehicle production in 1968; the financial reports record that the company was restructured into a Sino-foreign joint-stock company on 16 June 1992, while the group's official history records the founding of the joint-stock company as 22 November 1993 [1][4].

Key management

  • Qiu Tiangao (born 1966), chairman since 2016-04-07. He is also chairman of Jiangling Motors Group (since April 2014), Nanchang Jiangling Investment, Jiangxi Isuzu Motors and Jiangling Group New Energy Vehicle. He previously served as general manager and chairman of Nanchang Gear Co., chairman of Jiangxi Jiangling Gear Co., vice president of Jiangling Holdings, and director, general manager and chairman of Jiangling Motors Group [1][4].
  • Xiong Chunying (born 1964), director (since 2021-06-25) and president (since 2021-05-01). A senior engineer, she previously served as head of the quality-management department, assistant to the president, vice president, executive vice president and director [1].
  • Wu Shengbo (born 1966, US citizen), vice chairman since 2022-11-24. He is group vice president of Ford Motor Company, president and CEO of Ford's China and International Markets Group, and chairman, president and CEO of Ford Motor (China) [1].
  • Ding Wenmin (born 1972), first executive vice president since 2026-09-29 (previously executive vice president since 2022-06-01). He is a standing committee member of the party committee of Jiangling Motors Group and secretary of the party committee of JMC. He previously served as deputy director of the product-development technology centre, head of product planning and project management, assistant to the president, vice president, and a director of Jiangling Group [7].
  • Zhong Junhua (born 1976), director since 2024-04-11. In September 2026 she resigned as executive vice president (a post she had held since 2024-03-26) due to organisational arrangements and was reassigned as deputy party secretary, general manager and director of Jiangling Motors Group; she remains a director of the listed company [1][6].
  • Zeng Fafa (born 1978), executive vice president since 2026-09-29. He previously served as deputy head of the quality-management department, head of the new-model launch management department, head of quality management, head of manufacturing, assistant to the president and vice president [7].
  • Li Weihua (female, born 1977), chief financial officer since 2025-11-01, also a member of the executive committee, secretary of the board's audit committee, and a director of JMC Ford Automotive Technology (Shanghai) and Hanon Automotive Parts (Nanchang). She holds a bachelor's degree in international economic law from Shanghai University of Finance and Economics and an MBA from York University's Schulich School of Business; she previously served as a Ford China financial analyst, financial analyst and manager at Ford Automotive Engineering Research (Nanjing), various Ford Asia-Pacific finance posts, CFO of Ford Lio Ho Motor, CFO of JMC, and CFO of Changan Ford Automobile [16].

Competition

Main competitors

In light commercial vehicles JMC competes mainly with Beiqi Foton, JAC Motors, Dongfeng and Great Wall Motor's pickup line; in Ford-brand SUVs it competes with Changan Ford and domestic brands. In 2025 JMC's products led the light-bus segment and were No.2 in pickups but only No.7 in light trucks [8].

Strengths (our assessment)

  • A leading position in light buses and a strong position in pickups, backed by the Ford Transit franchise and the JMC commercial-vehicle network [8].
  • Growing exports: 160,000 vehicles in 2025, up 38.4% year on year, and a group-level network of 102 overseas dealer channels and 13 KD plants [8][15].
  • A long record of cash dividends since listing [12].

Weaknesses (our assessment)

  • Revenue growth lagged volume growth in 2025 (revenue +2.07% versus volume +10.56%), and the overall gross margin fell to 13.58% [1][9].
  • Small NEV scale: NEV sales of 35,806 units in 2025 are a small fraction of total volume [8].
  • The passenger-vehicle push via JMC Ford Automotive Technology (Shanghai) accumulated losses of about RMB2.37 billion over 2022-2025, and the joint venture is scheduled for liquidation and deregistration after debt settlement [1][17].

Our assessment: JMC is a solid but narrow player, strong in light buses and pickups but weak in passenger vehicles and new energy. NEV sales of 35,806 vehicles in 2025, about 9.5% of total volume, indicate that its new-energy business remains limited in scale. The disclosed figures show the commercial-vehicle business carrying the group, while the Ford passenger-vehicle joint venture incurred losses of about RMB2.37 billion over 2022-2025 and has stopped operating [1]. Profit also depends partly on government grants: in 2025 government grants recognised in the income statement were RMB464.32 million, or roughly 40% of profit attributable to the parent [1].

Outlook

Company targets and plans

JMC set a 2026 operating target of 430,000 vehicles sold and RMB42.0 billion in revenue, and said it would anchor its strategy on three directions: intelligent connectivity, new energy and globalisation [8]. At the group level, JMCG has outlined a '1236' strategy for 2030: one million vehicles, RMB200 billion in revenue, 30% of sales overseas and 60% NEV share, using a dual-brand, dual-channel export approach [4].

Our analysis

Our assessment: the disclosed figures point to exports and, to a lesser degree, NEV commercial vehicles as the main growth drivers, while the domestic light-truck market remains competitive and price-sensitive. Whether JMC can lift profitability depends on several conditions: whether NEV commercial-vehicle volumes scale without further eroding gross margin (the overall gross margin fell to 13.58% in FY2025), whether revenue and expenses improve following the passenger-vehicle distribution adjustment, and whether export growth continues in the face of tariffs and geopolitical measures [9][17]. None of these conditions is guaranteed.

Our assessment: the year-on-year profit improvement in the first nine months of 2026 includes a comparative-base effect from the prior-year reversal of deferred tax assets. Sustained improvement needs to be assessed against subsequent revenue, gross margins and expenses; if operating performance does not improve, the planned liquidation alone is insufficient evidence of a recovery in profitability [3][17].

Risks

  • Operating risk: revenue grew more slowly than volume in 2025 (revenue +2.07% versus volume +10.56%), and the overall gross margin fell to 13.58% [1][9].
  • Financial risk: JMC Ford Automotive Technology (Shanghai) recorded a net loss of RMB751 million in 2025; consolidated accounts receivable at end-2025 were RMB6.141 billion, up 46.89% year on year [1]. Announcement 2025-042 provides for liquidation and deregistration after debt settlement and states that the capital increase and debt-settlement arrangements have no effect on JMC's total profit (before income tax) [17]. The 2026 third-quarter report attributes the year-on-year increases in profit attributable to the parent and profit excluding non-recurring items for the first nine months mainly to the effect of deferred tax assets reversed following the subsidiary's business adjustment in the third quarter of 2025 [3].
  • Customer concentration risk: in 2025 the top five customers accounted for RMB16.54 billion, or 42.22% of sales [1].
  • Government grants: government grants of RMB464.32 million recognised in the income statement in 2025 accounted for about 40% of profit attributable to the parent [1][12].
  • Policy and regulation risk: the commercial-vehicle business faces tightening emissions and safety regulations, which the annual report lists among its core future risks [8].
  • Overseas risk: exports face uncertainty from tariffs and geopolitical measures in key markets [8].
  • Technology-route risk: JMC's NEV sales were 35,806 units in 2025, about 9.5% of total vehicle sales [8].

概览

江铃汽车股份有限公司（深交所 000550 A 股、200550 B 股）是总部位于江西省南昌市的商用车与 SUV 制造企业，是江铃汽车集团有限公司的上市整车主体，也是福特汽车公司的长期合作伙伴——福特自 1995 年起持股，现持股 32.00% [4]。公司生产销售 JMC 品牌轻卡、皮卡、轻客，以及福特品牌轻客、MPV、SUV 与皮卡；2025 财年实现营业收入 391.70 亿元、归属于母公司股东的净利润 11.87 亿元，销售整车 37.73 万辆、其中出口 16 万辆 [1][8]。

江铃汽车为国有控股的中外合资上市公司：控股股东为南昌市江铃投资有限公司（持股 41.03%，国有法人）与福特汽车公司（持股 32.00%）；年报认定的实际控制人为江铃汽车集团有限公司、重庆长安汽车股份有限公司 [1]。2025 年公司产品轻客市占份额居行业第一、皮卡第二、轻卡第七 [8]。

历史沿革

  1. 1947-04

    江铃集团前身南昌汽车保养厂成立。[4]

  2. 1968-04

    江西汽车制造厂生产出第一批 12 辆“井冈山”牌 2.5 吨载重汽车，开始整车制造。[4]

  3. 1985-06

    引进日本五十铃技术，第一辆江铃 N 系列轻卡下线。[4]

  4. 1992-06-16

    在江西汽车制造厂基础上改组设立中外合资股份制企业（财务报告记载口径）。[1]

  5. 1993-11-22

    江铃汽车股份有限公司创立（集团官网记载口径，与财务报告的 1992-06-16 口径不同）。[4]

  6. 1993-12-01

    A 股在深圳证券交易所挂牌上市（000550），为江西省首家上市公司。[4]

  7. 1995-08-21

    与美国福特汽车公司签署长期合作协议，福特出资 4000 万美元购入 B 股、占 20% 股权。[4]

  8. 1997-12-02

    江铃全顺汽车厂建成投产，第一辆国产全顺（Ford Transit）下线。[4]

  9. 1998-11

    1998 年 11 月 12 日增发 B 股后，福特持股比例提高至 29.96%（按年报“历次控股股东的变更情况”记载口径；集团官网对此事件日期记载略有不同）。[1][4]

  10. 2004-11-01

    江铃集团与长安汽车成立江铃控股有限公司。[20]

  11. 2005-12-08

    江铃集团将所持江铃汽车 354,176,000 股（占总股本 41.03%）过户至江铃控股；此为股份转让完成日期，区别于此前的增资安排。同年较早的 8 月 5 日，福特在 8 月 2、3、5 日累计购入 321,400 股 B 股后，持股达到 258,964,200 股（30%）；收购报告书全文于 9 月 22 日披露。[1][20][21]

  12. 2010-10

    江铃首款自主 SUV“驭胜”下线。[4]

  13. 2013-02

    位于山西太原的江铃重型汽车有限公司揭牌成立。[4]

  14. 2013-12

    福特完成股份收购计划，持有江铃股份 32% 股份。[4]

  15. 2019-07-26

    新江铃控股完成重组，爱驰汽车、江铃集团、长安汽车分别持股 50%、25%、25%（涉及陆风品牌与生产资质）。[4]

  16. 2021-09-09

    富山工厂举行全面投产活动，占地 1500 亩、总投资约 128 亿元、具备 30 万辆整车生产能力。[4][13][18]

  17. 2021-12-07

    江铃福特汽车科技（上海）有限公司成立（江铃持股 51%、福特 49%，注册资本 2 亿元），运营福特品牌乘用车与皮卡。[5]

  18. 2023-12

    国产福特游骑侠 Ranger 皮卡上市（14.58 万元起）。[14]

  19. 2024-04-25

    福特烈马（Bronco）越野 SUV 上市，售价 29.98 万元起。[4]

  20. 2025-09

    江铃汽车决议将福特品牌乘用车与皮卡分销委托给福特全资的福特汽车销售服务（上海）有限公司，并与福特共同增资用于清偿江铃福特汽车科技（上海）负债；该合资公司拟在债务清偿完成后清算注销。[17][12]

现状

业务结构

江铃汽车主业为生产和销售商用车、乘用车 SUV 及相关零部件。2025 财年整车收入 359.56 亿元（占营业收入 91.8%）、材料及零部件收入 21.11 亿元（5.39%）、保养及技术服务等收入 11.02 亿元（2.81%），三项毛利率分别为 11.88%、30.23%、36.99% [1][9]。2025 年整车产量 378,338 辆、销量 377,253 辆，分车型为轻客 99,770 辆、卡车 83,207 辆、皮卡 59,681 辆、SUV 134,595 辆 [1]。除整车外，年报对“公司基本情况”的描述涵盖汽车及零部件生产销售、售后服务、进出口、二手车、管理咨询与运力服务 [1]。

产品谱系

JMC 品牌阵容含顺达轻卡，宝典、域虎、大道皮卡与特顺轻客；福特品牌产品含全顺（Transit）轻客、途睿欧 MPV、领睿、领裕 SUV、游骑侠 Ranger 皮卡与烈马 Bronco 越野 SUV。2025 年年报另披露，福特智趣烈马新能源 SUV 于 2025 年 12 月在南昌正式上市 [1]。国产游骑侠于 2023 年 12 月上市（14.58 万元起），福特烈马于 2024 年 4 月 25 日上市（29.98 万元起）[14][4]。

新能源

2025 年新能源整车产销 37,304 台、35,806 台，销售收入 29.58 亿元；其中新能源商用车产销 32,373 台、32,557 台、收入 24.04 亿元，新能源乘用车产销 4,931 台、3,249 台、收入 5.54 亿元 [1][8]。公司已布局轻客、皮卡、轻卡全谱系新能源产品，2025 年 1 月推出全新纯电商用车 E 福顺轻客与 E 顺达轻卡 [4]。

技术与平台

2025 年 3 月公司发布全新动力品牌“蓝焰动力平台”，2025 年 4 月与博世在上海车展联合发布“超级轻卡系统解决方案”；集团还自主研发了 IDEA 电子电气架构和商用车“九合一”中央计算平台 [4]。2025 财年研发投入 14.90 亿元，同比下降 12.31%，资本化研发投入占比 18.41% [1][9]。

工厂与产能

江铃汽车主要生产基地为位于南昌小蓝经开区的富山工厂，2021 年 9 月 9 日全面投产，占地 1500 亩、总投资约 128 亿元、具备年产 30 万辆整车能力，生产 JMC 品牌轻卡（传统能源与新能源）及领睿、领裕等福特品牌 SUV [18][4][13]。

海外业务

上市公司 2025 年出口 16 万辆，同比增长 38.4% [1][8]。集团层面，出口量从 2021 年约 3.4 万台增至 2025 年约 16.99 万台，“十四五”期间复合增长率约 49%，海外销售集中于中东、非洲、拉美、东南亚及加勒比地区 [15]。集团在海外拥有 102 家经销渠道与 13 个 KD 组装工厂；在阿尔及利亚通过 KD 散件组装实现本地化生产、年出口超万台，2025 年越南、阿根廷、沙特等 6 个国家销量突破万台，另有 16 个市场超千台 [15]。2025 年集团对中东区域累计出口 3.4 万余台，沙特成为江铃在中东的第一个万台市场 [15]。面向海外的产品包括符合欧洲标准的电动轻卡 E 路达、新一代皮卡与纯电 SUV [15]。

最新业绩（截至 2026-10-29）

截至 2026 年 10 月 29 日，江铃汽车已披露的最近一期业绩为 2026 年前三季度：实现营业收入 287.86 亿元（同比增长 5.49%）、归属于母公司股东的净利润 9.07 亿元（同比增长 21.02%）、经营活动现金流净额 17.82 亿元；累计销售整车 285,285 辆（28.53 万辆），同比增长 9.32%，其中轻客 82,132 辆、卡车 58,583 辆、皮卡 45,058 辆、SUV 99,512 辆 [3][11]。单第三季度归母净利润 1.68 亿元，同比增长 922.92%。三季报说明，前三季度归母净利润及扣非归母净利润同比增长，主要受江铃福特汽车科技（上海）在 2025 年第三季度业务调整带来的递延所得税资产转回影响；2026 年前三季度所得税费用同比减少 5.07 亿元（下降 77.84%），主要由于上年同期该项税务影响 [3]。

创始人与管理层

创始人

江铃汽车并无单一创始人。其前身为 1947 年 4 月成立的国有南昌汽车保养厂，1967 年 10 月更名为江西汽车制造厂、1968 年开始整车制造；财务报告记载公司于 1992 年 6 月 16 日在江西汽车制造厂基础上改组设立为中外合资股份制企业，集团官网另记载股份公司 1993 年 11 月 22 日创立 [1][4]。

股权与治理

截至 2025 年末，控股股东为南昌市江铃投资有限公司（持股 41.03%，国有法人）与福特汽车公司（持股 32.00%）；年报认定的实际控制人为江铃汽车集团有限公司、重庆长安汽车股份有限公司 [1]。2026 年三季报披露的前十大股东中，前两大股东持股比例仍约 41.03% 与 32.00% [3]。福特在董事会派驻董事，包括副董事长吴胜波及另一名福特委派董事 Ryan Anderson；2025 年董事会就与福特的分销委托及江铃福特汽车科技（上海）增资等关联交易作出决议 [17]。

竞争格局

主要对手

轻型商用车领域，江铃主要与北汽福田、江淮汽车、东风及长城皮卡竞争；福特品牌 SUV 领域与长安福特及自主品牌竞争。2025 年江铃产品轻客市占第一、皮卡第二，但轻卡仅列第七 [8]。

优势（我们的判断）

  • 轻客龙头、皮卡靠前，依托福特全顺品牌与江铃商用车网络 [8]。
  • 出口持续增长：2025 年出口 16 万辆，同比增长 38.4%；集团层面拥有 102 家海外经销渠道与 13 个 KD 工厂 [8][15]。
  • 上市以来长期保持现金分红 [12]。

短板（我们的判断）

  • 2025 年营收增速（+2.07%）低于销量增速（+10.56%），综合毛利率降至 13.58% [1][9]。
  • 新能源规模偏小：2025 年新能源销量 3.58 万台，占总销量比重有限 [8]。
  • 通过江铃福特汽车科技（上海）推进的乘用车业务 2022—2025 年累计亏损约 23.73 亿元，该合资公司已停止运营、拟在债务清偿后清算注销 [1][17]。

我们的判断：江铃是一家业务扎实但较窄的企业，在轻客、皮卡领域较强，乘用车与新能源较弱。2025 年新能源整车销量 35,806 辆、约占总销量的 9.5%，显示其新能源业务规模仍有限。披露数据显示商用车业务支撑整体，而福特乘用车合资公司 2022—2025 年累计亏损约 23.73 亿元、已停止运营 [1]。盈利对政府补助存在一定依赖：2025 年计入当期损益的政府补助 4.64 亿元，约占归母净利润的四成 [1]。

未来展望

公司目标与规划

江铃汽车制定 2026 年经营目标为销量 43 万辆、营业收入 420 亿元，并表示将锚定智能化、新能源化、全球化三大核心方向 [8]。集团层面提出面向 2030 年的“1236”战略：整车销量 100 万辆、营业收入 2000 亿元、海外销量占比 30%、新能源销量占比 60%，并采用“双品牌+双渠道”出口策略 [4]。

我们的分析

我们的判断：披露数据显示出口以及（程度较轻的）新能源商用车是主要增长来源，而国内轻卡市场竞争激烈、价格敏感。江铃盈利能否改善取决于多个条件——新能源商用车能否在不进一步侵蚀毛利率（2025 财年综合毛利率降至 13.58%）的前提下放量，乘用车分销调整后收入与费用表现能否改善，以及出口能否在关税与地缘措施下持续增长 [9][17]。这些条件并非必然成立。

我们的判断：2026 年前三季度利润同比改善包含上年同期递延所得税资产转回形成的基数影响。持续盈利改善仍需后续收入、毛利率及费用数据验证；若经营表现未改善，拟清算安排本身不足以支持盈利修复的结论 [3][17]。

风险

  • 经营风险：2025 年营收增速（+2.07%）低于销量增速（+10.56%），综合毛利率降至 13.58% [1][9]。
  • 财务风险：江铃福特汽车科技（上海）2025 年净亏损 7.51 亿元；江铃汽车合并口径年末应收账款为 61.41 亿元，同比增长 46.89% [1]。2025-042 号公告约定该合资公司完成债务清偿后清算注销，并明确增资和债务清偿安排对江铃汽车利润总额无影响 [17]。2026 年三季报解释，前三季度归母净利润及扣非归母净利润同比增长，主要受该子公司在 2025 年第三季度业务调整带来的递延所得税资产转回影响 [3]。
  • 客户集中度风险：2025 年前五名客户销售额 165.40 亿元，占年度销售总额 42.22% [1]。
  • 政府补助：2025 年计入当期损益的政府补助 4.64 亿元，约占归母净利润的四成 [1][12]。
  • 政策与法规风险：商用车业务面临排放与安全法规趋严，公司年报将其列为核心未来风险之一 [8]。
  • 海外风险：出口面临重点市场关税与地缘措施的不确定性 [8]。
  • 技术路线风险：2025 年公司新能源销量为 35,806 台，约占整车销量的 9.5% [8]。
