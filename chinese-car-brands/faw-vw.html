Overview

FAW-Volkswagen Automotive Co., Ltd. (一汽-大众, FAW-VW) is a Sino-German passenger-car joint venture founded on 6 February 1991 and held 60% by China FAW Co., Ltd. (中国第一汽车股份有限公司; the English company page says China FAW Group Co., Ltd.) and 40% in total by the Volkswagen Group (Volkswagen AG 25%, Audi AG 5%, Volkswagen (China) Investment Co., Ltd. 10%); it was China's first modern passenger-car maker built to economic scale [1]. It operates three brands — Volkswagen, Audi and Jetta — with more than 30 models across five whole-vehicle bases in Changchun, Chengdu, Foshan, Qingdao and Tianjin [2][3].

Its revenue comes almost entirely from building and selling passenger cars and related parts. In 2025 it sold 1,587,065 vehicles (including imported Audis) — Volkswagen 902,066, Audi 566,988 (including imports) and Jetta 118,011 — again ranking first among Chinese joint-venture automakers and first in fuel-vehicle sales; in October 2025 it became the first Chinese passenger-car maker to reach 30 million cumulative units [4]. Because it is a non-listed JV, its profit is not separately disclosed [1].

One of China's oldest and largest joint ventures, FAW-Volkswagen is a core sales and profit pillar of the FAW Group, but volume has broadly declined since the record 2.16 million units of 2020 and its electric transition lags the market [34][16].

Current operations

Business structure and brands

FAW-Volkswagen operates three brands. In 2025 it sold 1,587,065 vehicles (including imported Audis), of which the Volkswagen brand sold 902,066, the Audi brand 566,988 (including imports) and the Jetta brand 118,011 [4]. For 2024, the split was Volkswagen 928,018, Audi 611,088 (including imports) and Jetta 120,001 out of 1,659,107 units [9]. In 2025 its fuel-vehicle market share rose 0.9 percentage points year on year, the Audi brand returned to first place in the luxury fuel-vehicle market for the first time in six years, and the Volkswagen brand's fuel-vehicle share rose 0.6 percentage points; high-end models accounted for 54% of Volkswagen-brand volume [4][28]. FAW-Volkswagen set 'breakthrough' as its 2025 theme and pursues a 'stabilise fuel, add electric, seek exports' course [14].

Sales trajectory

FAW-Volkswagen's annual retail sales were 2.13 million units in 2019 (retaking the Chinese passenger-car crown), a record 2.16 million in 2020, 1.86 million in 2021 (down 14.07% amid chip shortages), 1.82 million in 2022, 1.91 million in 2023, 1.66 million in 2024 and 1.59 million in 2025 [33][34]. In 2021 the Volkswagen brand sold 989,000 units, the Audi brand more than 700,000 and the Jetta brand 169,000, with tax paid of more than RMB 41.8 billion [32].

Products and technology

FAW-Volkswagen self-developed a SOA electrical/electronic architecture and says it now holds core technologies including the Shuangqing transmission, a 1.5-litre hybrid-dedicated engine and Ni60 battery cells [22]. The Volkswagen brand's new-energy products will adopt the new CMP and CSP platforms and the CEA electrical/electronic architecture, while the first Jetta-brand pure-electric model carries the in-house SOA architecture [23]. On the Audi side, the A5L is built on the PPC premium-fuel platform (fifth-generation EA888 engine, E³ 1.2 architecture) and the Q6L e-tron family on the PPE premium-electric platform (107 kWh CATL battery, 765 km CLTC range, 800 V); both launched on 7 August 2025 in Hangzhou, the A5L at RMB 239,800-289,800 and the Q6L e-tron family at a limited-time price of RMB 348,800-398,800 [31]. The A5L Qiankun Intelligent Driving Edition, launched on 21 November 2025 at RMB 286,800, is the world's first fuel vehicle to carry Huawei's Qiankun assisted-driving system [8]. The Jetta M6 pure-electric sedan launched on 22 October 2026 at RMB 79,800-99,800 using the self-developed SOA architecture and the Horizon Journey 6M chip [30]. On 28 August 2026 the Volkswagen brand opened pre-sales for the ID.AURA T6 pure-electric SUV at RMB 135,900-169,900, announcing a 'smart-electric 2.0' phase; the ID.AURA series is planned to cover 10 models across battery-electric, plug-in hybrid and range-extended forms [35]. Its current electrified lineup also includes the ID.4 CROZZ and ID.AURA battery-electric models plus plug-in hybrids such as the Magotan PHEV and Tayron L PHEV [7]. The company has deployed about 3,000 R&D staff focused on intelligent connectivity, smart cockpit and driver assistance [35].

Production bases and capacity

FAW-Volkswagen has five whole-vehicle bases — Changchun (northeast), Chengdu (southwest), Foshan (south), Qingdao (east) and Tianjin (north) — works with nearly 2,000 tier-1 suppliers and more than 1,500 dealers, and employs about 36,000 people across the five bases [1][3]. The Foshan base hosts the MEB smart plant for Volkswagen and Audi electrified products [3]. Over more than three decades the five bases have anchored an automotive industry cluster, supporting more than 500,000 jobs nationwide and working with more than 3,400 upstream and downstream partners, with procurement of over RMB 160 billion [3]. The company also operates a vehicle proving ground, a new-technology development centre, a new-energy centre, bench-testing and acoustic-testing facilities, with whole-process test capability from parts to complete vehicles [1].

Domestic position

FAW-Volkswagen has ranked first among Chinese joint-venture automakers and first in fuel-vehicle sales for seven consecutive years [4][36]. According to CAAM data cited by media, Chinese-brand passenger-vehicle market share rose to 71.8% in the first half of 2026, leaving joint ventures and foreign brands a combined 28.2%; a JV executive told the 2026 China Auto Forum that their combined share had fallen to 24.5% in June [35].

Overseas business

FAW-Volkswagen's exports achieved a start in 2025 with the first whole vehicles shipped to the Middle East; the Jetta brand moved first and the Volkswagen brand followed, targeting the Middle East, Africa, ASEAN and South America. A separate export volume has not been disclosed [28][14].

Recent performance (first half of 2026)

As of the research date, FAW-Volkswagen has not published a 2026 half-year results announcement. According to CPCA data cited by media, its first-half 2026 retail sales were about 558,000 units, down 25% year on year, and Volkswagen Group's equity profit from its Chinese joint ventures fell about 63% in the first half of 2026 [16]. The ID. series has contracted sharply: the ID.4 CROZZ sold only 135 units in July 2026, versus about 20,500 for all of 2025 [35]. In the ID 1.0 phase the company sold about 230,000 ID.-series vehicles in total, with the ID.4 averaging roughly 5,000-6,000 units a month in 2021-2022 [35]. For 2026, China FAW chairman Qiu Xiandong has set group targets of 3.546 million vehicle sales and RMB 570.04 billion in revenue [10].

Founders and leadership

Ownership and governance

FAW-Volkswagen is held 60% by China FAW Co., Ltd. (中国第一汽车股份有限公司; the English company page says China FAW Group Co., Ltd.) and 40% in total by the Volkswagen Group (Volkswagen AG 25%, Audi AG 5%, Volkswagen (China) Investment Co., Ltd. 10%). It is not listed, and the structure has been stable for more than three decades [1][2]. Day-to-day decisions are made by a management committee whose members are appointed by both shareholders [3].

Current management

  • Qiu Xiandong (邱现东) — chairman; also chairman and party secretary of China FAW Group. Ralf Brandstätter — vice-chairman; also chairman and CEO of Volkswagen Group China. The chairman and vice-chairman titles are per business-registration records; Qiu Xiandong's FAW Group role and Brandstätter's Volkswagen Group China role are confirmed by official FAW and Volkswagen China pages [19][21][3].
  • Dong Xiuhui (董修惠) — general manager and party secretary, appointed 2025-12-31. He joined FAW in 1991, was FAW-Volkswagen commercial deputy general manager and general manager of the sales company from July 2015 to December 2020, then returned to China FAW as deputy general manager of the joint-venture cooperation management department and later the strategy management and business planning department, and from December 2022 served as deputy general manager of FAW Toyota and party secretary and general manager of FAW Toyota Sales [25][12].
  • Wang Shengli (王胜利) — (commercial) deputy general manager and party secretary and general manager of FAW-Volkswagen Sales Co., Ltd., appointed 2026-01-17. He joined FAW-Volkswagen in 2002, moved into sales in 2013, became a deputy general manager of the sales company in 2018 and executive deputy general manager in 2020, then served as deputy general manager of FAW Bestune and general manager of FAW Hongqi Sales, during which Hongqi's annual volume grew from about 310,000 units in 2022 to about 412,000 in 2024 [27][12].

Recent management changes

On 2024-12-25 Pan Zhanfu stepped down and Chen Bin, a China FAW vice president, took over concurrently as general manager and party secretary; on 2025-12-31 Dong Xiuhui succeeded him. The commercial deputy general manager and sales-company general manager role passed from Wu Yingkai to Wang Shengli [10][12]. Media reported that Chen Bin's concurrent tenure lasted one year because of a group rule on concurrent senior posts [12]. The Jetta automotive technology company is led by general manager Gao Jiefang [30].

Competition

Our judgment. FAW-Volkswagen's main rivals include the other large joint ventures (SAIC Volkswagen, SAIC-GM, GAC Toyota, GAC Honda, Dongfeng Nissan) and fast-growing domestic brands and new-energy makers such as BYD, Geely, Changan and Chery [14]. Its strengths are scale, brand breadth across economy (Jetta), volume (Volkswagen) and premium (Audi) segments, a seven-year run as the top-selling Chinese joint venture, and a still-leading fuel-vehicle franchise: in 2025 its fuel-vehicle share rose 0.9 percentage points and the Audi brand regained first place in the luxury fuel-vehicle market [4][36]. Its scale remains well ahead of the other German JV: in 2024 SAIC Volkswagen sold about 1.2 million units against FAW-Volkswagen's 1.659 million [14]. In the luxury segment the Audi A6L and Q5L were each first in their fuel-vehicle classes in 2025 [4].

Our judgment. Its weakness is the speed of the electric transition. Volume has fallen from the record 2.16 million units of 2020 to 1,587,065 in 2025, and retail sales fell about 25% in the first half of 2026 [34][16]. The Volkswagen Group's equity profit from its Chinese joint ventures (about 63% lower in H1 2026) indicates the pressure is financial as well as volumetric [16]. The company is responding with 16 China-tailored new models from 2026 and localized platforms (CMP/CSP/CEA, PPC/PPE) plus Huawei cooperation, but as of the research date these remain plans whose commercial results have not yet shown [22].

Outlook

Company plans. FAW-Volkswagen has stated that from 2026 its three brands will introduce 16 China-tailored new models across multiple segments, and that Volkswagen-brand new-energy products will use the CMP and CSP platforms with the CEA electrical/electronic architecture [22][23]. Under the March 2025 strategic agreement, the Volkswagen and Jetta brands will add 11 new models, 10 of them new-energy [5]. For 2026 the company plans 13 new-model launches (six fuel and seven new-energy) [28][36], and has set a target of about 60% new-energy share of sales by 2030 [36]. Under the August 2025 Jetta agreement, a new Jetta company was set up and the brand plans four new-energy models by 2028 [26][29]. The company is also extending exports from Jetta and the Volkswagen brand [14]. Separately, the Volkswagen Group is mounting its largest-ever NEV product offensive in China, planning about 50 new-energy models by 2030, including about 30 battery-electric models [26]. The Volkswagen-brand head of FAW-Volkswagen Sales said the first HEV model will arrive in the second half of 2026, a three-box battery-electric sedan by year-end, and 2027-2028 will be a product-heavy period [35].

Our judgment. These product and platform plans are commitments rather than completed results. Whether they halt the volume decline depends on execution of the new-model launches and on whether the localized electric and intelligent products can compete on price and features; the roughly 25% first-half retail decline in 2026 shows the baseline has not yet stabilised [16]. The Audi-Huawei cooperation and the CEA/CMP/CSP architecture give FAW-Volkswagen a route to close its intelligence gap, but sales effects cannot be assumed before the models actually launch [8]. Frequent top-management changes since late 2024 add execution uncertainty [12]. Exports are still at an early stage and are unlikely to offset the domestic decline in the near term [6].

Risks

  • Volume decline. Sales fell from the record 2.16 million units of 2020 to 1,587,065 in 2025, and retail sales fell about 25% in the first half of 2026 [34][16].
  • Electric-transition gap. FAW-Volkswagen has relied mainly on fuel vehicles and is only now rolling out localized new-energy products from 2026, while its ID. series has contracted sharply [22][35]. Domestic brands such as BYD and Geely have built a full new-energy product and technology loop and lead the segments FAW-Volkswagen is only now entering [14].
  • Profit pressure on the German partner. Volkswagen Group's equity profit from its Chinese joint ventures fell about 63% in the first half of 2026, indicating financial stress on the JV system [16].
  • Management turnover. The general manager position changed three times between late 2024 and end-2025, adding execution risk [12].
  • Price competition. The Chinese market's price war has intensified, with joint-venture brands cutting prices to defend share, pressuring transaction prices [14].
  • Disclosure limits. As a non-listed joint venture, FAW-Volkswagen does not separately publish financial statements, so profitability and financial position cannot be independently assessed from public data [1].

概览

一汽-大众汽车有限公司（简称一汽-大众、FAW-VW）是一家成立于 1991 年 2 月 6 日的中德合资乘用车企业，由中国第一汽车股份有限公司持股 60%（官网英文版写作 China FAW Group Co., Ltd.），大众集团合计持股 40%（德国大众汽车股份公司 25%、德国奥迪汽车股份公司 5%、大众汽车（中国）投资有限公司 10%），是中国第一家按经济规模起步建设的现代化乘用车生产企业 [1]。公司现经营大众、奥迪、捷达三大品牌、30 余款车型，拥有长春、成都、佛山、青岛、天津五大整车生产基地 [2][3]。

一汽-大众的收入几乎全部来自乘用车整车及零部件的制造与销售。2025 年销售整车 1,587,065 辆（含奥迪进口车），其中大众品牌 902,066 辆、奥迪品牌 566,988 辆（含进口车）、捷达品牌 118,011 辆，再次蝉联合资车企销量第一、燃油车销量第一；同年 10 月成为国内首家产销突破 3000 万辆的乘用车企业 [4]。由于是非上市合资企业，其盈利数据未单独披露 [1]。

作为中国历史最久、规模最大的合资车企之一，一汽-大众是中国一汽销量与利润的基本盘，但其销量自 2020 年 216.19 万辆的历史峰值以来整体回落，电动化转型步伐落后于市场 [34][16]。

历史沿革

  1. 1990-11

    中国一汽与德国大众在北京人民大会堂就 15 万辆轿车合资项目正式签约。[1][2]

  2. 1991-02

    1991 年 2 月 6 日一汽-大众汽车有限公司成立，成为中国第一家按经济规模起步建设的现代化乘用车生产企业；同年首辆捷达轿车在长春下线。[1][3]

  3. 1996-07

    一汽-大众全面建成投产，形成年产 15 万辆轿车、27 万台发动机和 18 万台传动器的生产能力。[1][2]

  4. 2006-07

    第 100 万辆捷达轿车下线。[6]

  5. 2010

    捷达车型生产转移至成都，为日后捷达品牌独立运营奠定产地基础。[18]

  6. 2014-12

    第 1000 万辆整车下线。[13]

  7. 2017-08

    第 1500 万辆整车下线。[13]

  8. 2019-03

  9. 2019

    全年销量 212.99 万辆，时隔五年重夺中国乘用车企业销量冠军。[34][33]

  10. 2020

    全年销量 216.19 万辆（同比增长 1.5%），创历史最高销量，成为国内唯一产销均突破 200 万辆的乘用车企业。[33][34]

  17. 2025-08

    中国一汽、大众汽车集团（中国）与成都经开区签署《捷达事业发展合作协议》，筹备成立捷达品牌新公司。[26][28]

  18. 2025-09-23

    一汽-大众在长春基地举行首批出口车辆下线仪式，海外事业正式启航。[3]

  19. 2025-10-30

    第 3000 万辆整车（一汽奥迪 A5L 领航版）在长春下线，一汽-大众成为国内首家产销突破 3000 万辆的乘用车企业。[5][6]

现状

业务结构与品牌

一汽-大众经营大众、奥迪、捷达三大品牌。2025 年销售整车 1,587,065 辆（含奥迪进口车），其中大众品牌 902,066 辆、奥迪品牌 566,988 辆（含进口车）、捷达品牌 118,011 辆 [4]。2024 年对应为大众 928,018 辆、奥迪 611,088 辆（含进口车）、捷达 120,001 辆，全年 1,659,107 辆 [9]。2025 年其燃油车市场份额同比提升 0.9 个百分点，奥迪品牌燃油车份额时隔六年重回豪华车市场第一，大众品牌燃油车份额同比提升 0.6 个百分点，高端车型占大众品牌销量 54% [4][28]。一汽-大众以「突破」为 2025 年主题，坚持「稳油、加电、谋出海」方针 [14]。

历年销量

一汽-大众历年终端销量为：2019 年 212.99 万辆（重夺中国乘用车企销量冠军）、2020 年 216.19 万辆（历史新高）、2021 年 185.78 万辆（缺芯下同比下滑 14.07%）、2022 年 182.36 万辆、2023 年 191.02 万辆、2024 年 165.91 万辆、2025 年 158.71 万辆 [33][34]。2021 年大众品牌销售 98.9 万辆、奥迪品牌超 70 万辆、捷达品牌 16.9 万辆，全年上缴税金超 418 亿元 [32]。

产品与技术

一汽-大众自主研发 SOA 电子电气架构，并称已掌握「双擎」变速箱、1.5L 混动专用发动机、Ni60 电池等核心技术 [22]。大众品牌新能源车型将采用全新 CMP、CSP 平台并搭载 CEA 电子电气架构，首款捷达品牌纯电车型搭载自研 SOA 架构 [23]。奥迪方面，A5L 基于 PPC 豪华燃油平台（第五代 EA888 发动机、E³ 1.2 电子电气架构），Q6L e-tron 家族基于 PPE 豪华纯电平台（107kWh 宁德时代电池、CLTC 续航 765 公里、800V 平台）；两者于 2025 年 8 月 7 日在杭州上市，A5L 售价 23.98 万—28.98 万元，Q6L e-tron 家族限时尊享价 34.88 万—39.88 万元 [31]。2025 年 11 月 21 日上市的 A5L 乾崑智驾版（28.68 万元）为全球首款搭载华为乾崑智驾技术的燃油车 [8]。捷达 M6 纯电轿车于 2026 年 10 月 22 日上市（7.98 万—9.98 万元），搭载自研 SOA 架构与地平线征程 6M 芯片 [30]。2026 年 8 月 28 日，大众品牌纯电 SUV ID.AURA T6 开启预售（13.59 万—16.99 万元），公司宣布进入「智电 2.0」阶段；ID.AURA 系列规划 10 款产品，涵盖纯电、插混与增程 [35]。 其现有电动化阵容还包括 ID.4 CROZZ、ID. AURA 系列纯电车型，以及迈腾 PHEV、探岳 L PHEV 等插电混动车型 [7]。公司已投入近 3000 名研发人员，聚焦智能网联、智能座舱与辅助驾驶业务 [35]。

生产基地与产能

一汽-大众拥有东北长春、西南成都、华南佛山、华东青岛、华北天津五大整车生产基地，与近 2000 家一级供应商、超过 1500 家经销商合作，五大基地员工约 3.6 万人 [1][3]。佛山基地建有 MEB 智慧工厂，是大众与奥迪双品牌电动化产品的重要产地 [3]。三十多年来，一汽-大众以五大基地为中心牵引汽车产业集群，在全国范围带动就业超 50 万人，与 3400 余家上下游合作伙伴合作，采购额 1600 多亿元 [3]。公司还拥有汽车试验场、新技术开发中心、新能源中心、台架试验、声学试验等研发试验设施，具备从零件到整车的全过程试验能力 [1]。

国内市场地位

一汽-大众连续七年位居中国合资车企销量第一、燃油车市场销量第一 [4][36]。据中汽协数据并经媒体援引，2026 年上半年中国品牌乘用车市场份额升至 71.8%，合资及外资品牌合计份额仅 28.2%；在 2026 中国汽车论坛上，有合资车企高管披露 6 月合资与外资品牌整体市占率已跌至 24.5% [35]。

海外业务

一汽-大众出海业务自 2025 年实现「从 0 到 1」突破，首批整车出口中东市场；捷达品牌率先启动，大众品牌同步跟进，覆盖中东、非洲、东盟、南美等区域。单独出口量未披露 [28][14]。

近期业绩（2026 年上半年）

截至研究截止日，一汽-大众未发布 2026 年半年度业绩公告。据媒体援引乘联分会数据，其 2026 年上半年零售销量约 55.8 万辆、同比下降约 25%；大众汽车集团财报显示其 2026 年上半年在华合资企业权益利润同比下滑约 63% [16]。ID 系列销量显著收缩，ID.4 CROZZ 2026 年 7 月单月仅售 135 辆，而该车型 2025 年全年销量约 2.05 万辆 [35]。在 ID 1.0 阶段，一汽-大众累计销售约 23 万辆 ID. 系列车型，ID.4 在 2021—2022 年月均销量约 5000—6000 辆 [35]。中国一汽董事长邱现东提出 2026 年集团销量 354.6 万辆、营业收入 5700.4 亿元的目标 [10]。

股权与治理

  • 董修惠——党委书记、总经理，2025 年 12 月 31 日上任。1991 年入职一汽，2015 年 7 月至 2020 年 12 月任一汽-大众商务副总经理兼销售公司总经理，2020 年 12 月调回中国一汽，历任合资合作事业管理部副总经理、战略管理及商业规划部副总经理，2022 年 12 月起任一汽丰田副总经理兼一汽丰田销售公司党委书记、总经理 [25][12]。
  • 王胜利——（商务）副总经理兼一汽大众销售有限责任公司党委书记、总经理，2026 年 1 月 17 日上任。2002 年加入一汽-大众，2013 年转入营销，2018 年任销售公司副总经理、2020 年任执行副总经理，此后历任（一汽奔腾副总经理、）一汽红旗销售公司总经理，期间红旗年销量由 2022 年约 31 万辆增至 2024 年约 41.2 万辆 [27][12]。

近期管理层变动

2024 年 12 月 25 日潘占福卸任，中国一汽副总经理陈彬兼任一汽-大众总经理、党委书记；2025 年 12 月 31 日董修惠接任；商务副总经理兼销售公司总经理一职先后由吴迎凯、王胜利担任 [10][12]。据媒体报道，陈彬因集团高管兼任原则仅兼任一年 [12]。一汽-大众捷达汽车科技有限公司总经理为高解放 [30]。

竞争格局

我们的判断。一汽-大众的主要对手包括上汽大众、上汽通用、广汽丰田、广汽本田、东风日产等大型合资车企，以及比亚迪、吉利、长安、奇瑞等快速成长的自主品牌与新能源企业 [14]。其优势在于规模与品牌覆盖：捷达（经济型）、大众（主流）、奥迪（豪华）三个层级齐全，连续七年位居中国合资车企销量第一，燃油车基盘依然领先——2025 年燃油车份额同比提升 0.9 个百分点，奥迪品牌重回豪华燃油车市场第一 [4][36]。其规模仍明显领先于另一家德系合资企业：2024 年上汽大众销量约 120 万辆，而一汽-大众为 165.9 万辆 [14]。在豪华车细分市场，奥迪 A6L、Q5L 2025 年分别位居同级燃油车销量第一，构成其品牌与利润的重要支撑 [4]。

我们的判断。其短板在于电动化转型速度。销量已从 2020 年 216.19 万辆的历史峰值降至 2025 年的 1,587,065 辆，2026 年上半年零售销量同比下滑约 25% [34][16]。大众汽车集团在华合资企业权益利润 2026 年上半年同比下滑约 63%，说明压力不止于销量、也体现在盈利层面 [16]。公司以 2026 年起 16 款本土化新车、CMP/CSP/CEA 与 PPC/PPE 平台及华为合作作为应对，但这些截至研究截止日仍是计划，其商业结果尚未显现 [22]。

未来展望

公司公开规划。一汽-大众表示，自 2026 年起三大品牌将在多个细分市场引入 16 款为中国市场量身打造的全新车型，大众品牌新能源产品将采用 CMP、CSP 平台并搭载 CEA 电子电气架构 [22][23]。按 2025 年 3 月战略合作协议，大众品牌和捷达品牌将新增 11 款全新车型、其中 10 款为新能源 [5]。2026 年当年计划投放 13 款新车（6 款燃油、7 款新能源）[28][36]，并明确提出到 2030 年新能源销量占比约 60% 的目标 [36]。按 2025 年 8 月捷达事业发展合作协议，捷达品牌新公司成立，计划 2028 年前推出 4 款新能源车型 [26][29]。公司还自捷达、大众品牌起步拓展整车出口 [14]。大众汽车集团正在华推进史上最大规模新能源产品攻势，计划至 2030 年在华推出约 50 款新能源汽车，其中约 30 款纯电动 [26]。一汽-大众销售公司大众品牌负责人班永盛表示，2026 年下半年将推出首款 HEV 车型，年底还有一款三厢纯电轿车，2027—2028 年将是产品大年 [35]。

我们的判断。上述产品与平台规划是承诺而非已经实现的结果。能否止住销量下滑，取决于新车型投放的执行，以及本土化电动与智能产品在价格和功能上的竞争力；2026 年上半年约 25% 的零售下滑显示其基盘尚未企稳 [16]。奥迪与华为的合作以及 CEA/CMP/CSP 架构为一汽-大众提供了补足智能化短板的路径，但在车型真正上市之前，不能预设其销量效果 [8]。2024 年底以来高层的频繁更替，也为这些计划的执行增加了不确定性 [12]。出口业务尚处起步阶段、规模有限，短期难以对冲国内销量下滑 [6]。

风险

  • 销量下滑。销量从 2020 年 216.19 万辆的历史峰值降至 2025 年的 1,587,065 辆，2026 年上半年零售销量同比下滑约 25% [34][16]。
  • 电动化差距。一汽-大众长期以燃油车为主，本土化新能源产品自 2026 年起才陆续投放，ID 系列销量明显收缩 [22][35]。比亚迪、吉利等自主品牌已构建起完整的新能源产品与技术闭环，领先于一汽-大众正在进入的细分市场 [14]。
  • 德方股东盈利压力。大众汽车集团 2026 年上半年在华合资企业权益利润同比下滑约 63%，显示合资体系面临财务压力 [16]。
  • 管理层更替频繁。2024 年底至 2025 年底总经理一职三次变动，增加执行风险 [12]。
  • 价格竞争。中国车市价格战加剧，合资品牌通过降价争夺份额，对成交价形成压力 [14]。
  • 信息披露有限。作为非上市合资企业，一汽-大众不单独发布财务报表，盈利与财务状况难以从公开数据独立判断 [1]。
