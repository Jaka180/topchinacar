Overview

Founded in 2017, Human Horizons develops, manufactures and sells new-energy vehicles. Its luxury battery-electric brand HiPhi was introduced in July 2019. The administrator identifies three models launched into production: HiPhi X, Z and Y.[12][8][2][12][8][2][1][3][4]

The administrator dates the production halt to 18 February 2024. On 2 April 2026 the Yancheng Economic Development Zone court approved the 52-company restructuring plan and terminated the reorganization procedure. The administrator continued recruiting investors on 2 September. This review has not obtained a public primary announcement confirming selection or production resumption by 29 October 2026.[2][1][3][12][8][2][1][3][4]

The 2025 acceptance ruling records consolidated book assets of RMB5.983 billion and liabilities of RMB15.781 billion at 31 August 2024, and filed claims of about RMB22.8 billion at 25 March 2025. These concern the 52-company restructuring perimeter; they are neither annual revenue or earnings nor final admitted claims.[4][12][8][2][1][3][4]

Current operations

Business and products

The official corporate profile organizes its activities around smart vehicles, smart transportation and smart cities. The administrator identifies three production models, X, Z and Y, and two models under development. No primary financial disclosure of revenue shares by business was obtained for this review.[12][2][12][2][6][14][13][8][5][4][1][3]

The official milestones record the X Founders Edition launch on 24 September 2020 at RMB680,000 for six seats and RMB800,000 for four seats, with March 2021 configurations at RMB570,000–800,000. Z launched on 26 August 2022 at RMB610,000–630,000. Y launched on 15 July 2023 and deliveries began on 29 July. Its product page advertises a starting MSRP of RMB339,000. These are historical launch prices or website quotations.[6][14][12][2][6][14][13][8][5][4][1][3]

Technology and platforms

A 2019 company strategy announcement describes vehicle development, vehicle-road cooperation and interconnected sharing, listing the HOA architecture, road monitoring platform and V2X system. Yancheng SASAC’s HiPhi 1 launch account lists over 500 sensors, 5G and V2X, NT doors, programmable projection headlights and interactive displays. These describe the contemporary product and strategy materials, rather than independently tested current specifications across the range.[13][8][12][2][6][14][13][8][5][4][1][3]

Plant and operating locations

The administrator’s 2024 notice describes the Yancheng HiPhi City plant as an upgrade of former Yueda Kia factory premises. Its 2026 announcement identifies Shanghai, Yancheng and Qingdao Laixi as the main operating locations. The official milestones separately record a January 2022 agreement bringing the China headquarters to Qingdao. This review has not obtained primary confirmation of the group headquarters or available capacity at the cutoff; current headquarters and available capacity remain unconfirmed.[5][2][6][12][2][6][14][13][8][5][4][1][3]

Production access

Page 17 of the 2025 acceptance ruling states that Human Horizons lacked an independent vehicle-manufacturing qualification. The Jiangsu company cooperated with Yueda Kia on capacity and production and applied for product access through Yueda Kia’s access platform. The Jiangsu company was the cooperation agreement’s contracting entity. This describes the arrangement at the time of acceptance.[4][12][2][6][14][13][8][5][4][1][3]

Domestic market record

The official milestones report leadership in December 2021 monthly sales and second-half 2021 sales among luxury-brand EVs priced above RMB500,000. They also describe X as the full-year 2022 single-model leader among luxury EVs with starting prices above RMB500,000, measured by domestic insurance registrations. This review has not obtained a verifiable primary full-year total sales table and omits platform-derived annual totals.[6][12][2][6][14][13][8][5][4][1][3]

Overseas record

The official milestones record an overseas strategy announcement in April 2023, initially targeting Munich and Oslo; European pricing and sales of X and Z in Germany and Norway in June; and experience-centre openings on 5 and 29 September respectively. No primary overseas delivery disclosure was obtained, so overseas sales remains null. These historical records do not confirm that the outlets still operate at the cutoff.[6][12][2][6][14][13][8][5][4][1][3]

Restructuring and stakeholders

The 2026 ruling records submission of the draft plan on 12 January and revision on 10 February following investor-recruitment developments and major changes in factory usage-right assets. Voting ran from 11 February to 16 March. Approval on 2 April terminated the procedure; investor selection forms part of subsequent plan execution.[1][12][2][6][14][13][8][5][4][1][3]

The administrator’s April 2026 reference to nearly 4,000 in-service staff and employee creditors combines different populations and is not current headcount; the employee card therefore remains null. The administrator also identifies over 2,500 other creditors and over 16,000 owners, and describes pooling funds, realizing asset value and introducing common-benefit financing to sustain operations and basic after-sales services.[2][12][2][6][14][13][8][5][4][1][3]

The September recruitment notice required investment proposals before 8 October. Existing applicants need not register again but must supplement their deposits and may revise proposals. This deadline preceded the cutoff. This review has not obtained primary confirmation of a selected investor, investment proceeds received or production resumption.[3][12][2][6][14][13][8][5][4][1][3]

Competition

Historical positioning and design evidence

The official milestones record historical X and Z prices above RMB500,000; Y’s product page advertises RMB339,000 as a starting price. CNIPA’s China Design Gold Award decision is dated 22 July 2022 and was published on 26 July. Item 9 of the appendix lists the Jiangsu company’s automobile design patent ZL201930350828.3. January 2023 was the online ceremony for the 23rd awards. The company identifies the winning model as HiPhi X.[6][14][9][11][10][6][14][9][11][10][2][4][1][3]

Our assessment: differentiation and competitive limits

Our assessment: historical prices and the award-winning design gave HiPhi a recognizable luxury-EV position. A design award does not establish sales scale, profitability or comprehensive technological leadership. Comparable primary competitor-share data and latest annual sales totals were unavailable to this review, preventing confirmation of its market share or industry rank. The basis is official pricing records and CNIPA’s award documents.[6][14][9][11][6][14][9][11][10][2][4][1][3]

Our assessment: renewed sales would expose HiPhi to competition on luxury-EV design, user experience and service capability. Brand and development work may offer a basis for restarting, but disclosed factory usage-right changes and production-access partnerships affect execution. Historical factories and access relationships cannot simply be treated as assets already available to an investor. The basis is the administrator’s development-model disclosure and the court’s descriptions of access and usage rights.[2][4][1][6][14][9][11][10][2][4][1][3]

Our assessment: evaluating the company’s competitive position at the cutoff first requires confirmation of investor selection, funding performance and production arrangements before product expansion. Continued September recruitment, the administrator-confirmed production halt and the court’s inclusion of investor selection in execution support that sequence, without proving that a restart must fail.[3][2][1][6][14][9][11][10][2][4][1][3]

Outlook

Historical company plans

The official milestones record an April 2023 overseas strategy initially targeting Munich and Oslo, followed by X and Z sales in Germany and Norway in June. These records predate the production halt. No primary disclosure of current overseas sales or restart targets was obtained for the cutoff.[6][2][6][2][1][3][4]

Execution and elapsed deadlines

Page 5 of the 2026 approval ruling records the original plan to select an investor within three to six months after the plan was passed, using public auction or competitive negotiations following public recruitment. This is a historical timetable, not an unexpired future commitment as of 29 October 2026. Investment proceeds are to pay bankruptcy expenses, common-benefit debts and bankruptcy claims under the plan.[1][6][2][1][3][4]

The administrator’s 2 September notice continued recruitment and required proposals before 8 October. Selection considers investment amount, payment timing, investment model, creditor protection and the operating plan. The notice requires an investment agreement and performance deposit after selection. This review has not obtained a public primary document confirming the selected investor, agreement signing or receipt of funds by the cutoff.[3][6][2][1][3][4]

Our analysis

Our assessment: plan approval establishes a procedural milestone; restarting the business still requires an investor, funding and workable production and access arrangements. The basis is the court’s inclusion of selection in execution, its record of major factory usage-right changes and continued September recruitment. Unconfirmed here describes the limits of the evidence obtained, not a proven absence of investment.[1][3][4][6][2][1][3][4]

Our assessment: if an investor performs and production and service arrangements are secured, a phased restart may be possible. If funding, factory or access issues remain unresolved, operational recovery may be delayed or further asset disposals may be needed. These are conditional scenarios, not established outcomes. Without the administrator’s original draft, this review cannot verify the detailed treatment of each creditor category or applicable recovery rates.[1][3][6][2][1][3][4]

Risks

  • Plan-execution evidence: the court terminated the procedure on 2 April 2026 and included investor selection in execution. Recruitment continued on 2 September with proposals due before 8 October. This review has not obtained primary confirmation of the result at the cutoff.[1][3][1][3][4][2]
  • Balance-sheet evidence: consolidated book liabilities were RMB15.781 billion against assets of RMB5.983 billion at 31 August 2024; filed claims were about RMB22.8 billion at 25 March 2025. These are book balances and submitted claims, not final repayment amounts or recovery rates.[4][1][3][4][2]
  • Production-arrangement evidence: the administrator confirms a halt on 18 February 2024. The court records major factory usage-right changes in the February 2026 draft update. The acceptance ruling also describes product access through the Jiangsu entity’s Yueda Kia cooperation.[2][1][4][1][3][4][2]
  • After-sales and stakeholder evidence: the administrator identifies over 16,000 owners affected by restructuring and describes efforts to maintain basic after-sales services. No primary material confirming fulfillment of all historical service promises by the cutoff was obtained.[2][1][3][4][2]

我们的判断：差异化与竞争边界

我们的判断：历史产品价格及获奖设计，为高合在豪华纯电市场建立了可辨识的产品定位；但设计奖项不能证明销量规模、盈利能力或整车技术全面领先。本次未取得可比的一手竞品份额及最新全年销量资料，无法据此确认其市场份额或行业排名。判断依据为官网价格记录与国家知识产权局奖项文件。[6][14][9][11][6][14][9][11][10][2][4][1][3]

我们的判断：高合未来若恢复销售，需要同时面对豪华纯电产品的设计、使用体验与服务能力竞争。品牌及研发积累可能为投资人提供业务重启基础，但已披露的工厂使用权变化及生产准入合作安排会影响实施路径；不能直接把历史工厂或准入关系当成投资人已取得的可用资产。依据为管理人披露的在研车型、法院对准入安排和使用权变化的记载。[2][4][1][6][14][9][11][10][2][4][1][3]

我们的判断：截至截止日，评估该公司的竞争地位应先核实投资人选择、资金履约和生产安排，再讨论产品扩张。9 月仍继续招募投资人、管理人确认过停工停产、法院把投资人遴选纳入计划执行，这些事实支持上述次序，但不足以证明重启必然失败。[3][2][1][6][14][9][11][10][2][4][1][3]

2026 年批准裁定书第 5 页记载，原计划由管理人在重整计划通过后 3 至 6 个月内，以公开拍卖或公开招募后的竞争性磋商等市场化方式选定投资人。该条为历史计划安排，不能在 2026 年 10 月 29 日写成尚未到期的未来承诺；投资款按计划用于清偿破产费用、共益债务和破产债权。[1][6][2][1][3][4]

我们的分析

我们的判断：计划获批只确认了法律程序节点，业务重启仍需落实投资人、资金及可执行的生产和准入安排。依据是法院将投资人遴选作为执行内容，并记载工厂使用权资产发生重大变化，9 月公告又继续招募。这里的未确认指本次核查的证据边界，不能直接等同于已确认无人投资。[1][3][4][6][2][1][3][4]

我们的判断：若投资人完成履约且重新落实生产和服务安排，可能出现分阶段重启；若履约、工厂或准入问题迟迟未解决，则可能延后经营恢复，甚至需要另行处置资产。两者均为条件情景，并非已经发生的结果。本次未取得管理人草案原件，无法核实分组债权的具体清偿安排及可适用的清偿比例。[1][3][6][2][1][3][4]
