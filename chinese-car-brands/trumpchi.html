Overview

Trumpchi (广汽传祺) is the self-owned mainstream passenger-car brand of Guangzhou Automobile Group Co., Ltd. (GAC Group), operated by GAC Trumpchi Automobile Co., Ltd., a wholly owned GAC subsidiary whose predecessor was registered on 21 July 2008. The Trumpchi name was unveiled in 2010 and the first model rolled off the line in October 2010 and went on sale the same December [4][12]. The brand's first car, the GA5 sedan, was built on a platform and production line bought from Fiat's Alfa Romeo 166, reflecting GAC's entry into self-owned cars through a purchased European platform [23].

Trumpchi covers sedans, SUVs and MPVs, with a particular strength in MPVs, and positions itself as a China MPV specialist; since 2025 it has added the high-end new-energy sub-series Xiangwang (向往) [4][7]. Volume rose from about 17,000 units in 2011 to a peak of 535,000 in 2018, then contracted, recovering to 415,000 in 2024 before falling to 319,200 in 2025 (-23.02% year on year), and rebounding to 164,400 in H1 2026 (+12.36%) [13][8][1][2]. Within GAC's self-owned business, Trumpchi sits alongside GAC Aion (290,100 units in 2025), the two forming the core of the group's self-owned brands [1].

The brand started late and its early models sold modestly; volume only took off with the GS4 launched in April 2015, and sales have since swung widely with its dependence on SUVs and single hit models [23][12]. In January 2026 GAC Group set up the Trumpchi business unit (传祺 BU) for integrated operation, making the brand a core object of the group's three-year Panyu Action reform [9].

Current operations

Business structure and recent performance

Trumpchi is a single-brand passenger-car business within GAC Group; its results are consolidated at the group level and not reported separately [1]. Sales fell 23.02% to 319,200 units in 2025; GAC's 2025 annual report shows Trumpchi's designed capacity at 720,000 units with output of 328,700 units, about 46% utilisation [1][11]. In H1 2026 the brand sold 164,400 units, up 12.36% year on year, with GAC's interim report attributing the recovery to strengthened MPV value, the refreshed Xiangwang E8 PHEV and M8 PHEV L, an updated Xiangwang S7, and the new boxy Yue7 entering the new-energy off-road segment [2][3]. Media citing GAC's production/sales bulletins reported cumulative 241,200 units for the first three quarters of 2026 (+3.47% YoY) and H1 2026 revenue of RMB 20.23 billion (+12.66% YoY) [27].

Over the longer term the trajectory is uneven: after the 535,000-unit peak in 2018, sales fell to about 385,000 in 2019 (the CAAM basis is about 343,000) and about 322,000 in 2021, then recovered to 362,548 in 2022 (+11.8%), 406,500 in 2023 (+12.1%) and 415,000 in 2024 (+2%), before declining again in 2025 [12][33][36][34][8]. MPVs are the anchor: the 2023 MPV family sold 164,200 units (+59% YoY, No.1 in China's MPV market), 184,000 in 2024, and a cumulative 900,000+ by September 2026 [34][24][17]. Trumpchi new-energy vehicle sales exceeded 150,000 units in 2025 [32].

Production bases and capacity

Trumpchi has four production bases in Guangzhou (Panyu), Hangzhou, Xinjiang and Yichang, forming a layout covering the Pearl River Delta, the Yangtze River Delta, the Yangtze River Economic Belt and the Belt and Road [4]. The Yichang plant was completed in June 2019, covering about 1,400 mu with total investment of RMB 3.53 billion, an annual capacity of 200,000 vehicles and a single line able to roll out one vehicle every 52 seconds [4][6]. GAC's 2025 annual report discloses Trumpchi's combined designed capacity of 720,000 units and output of 328,700 units [1].

Core technology and platforms

Trumpchi began in-house hybrid development in 2006, focusing on dual-motor series-parallel DHT technology and accumulating over 400 core patents; its GMC 1.0 and GMC 2.0 systems won industry awards including World's Best Transmission and World's Best Hybrid System [17]. In 2022 GAC released the hybrid technology brand Juliang Hybrid (钜浪混动), and in 2023 the technology brand i-GTEC [4][16]. At the 2026 GAC Tech Day the company released the Xingyuan (Star Source) powertrain brand, covering Xingyuan PHEV (GMC 3.0 Thunder and Gale DHT, fourth-generation DHE engine, P4 rear drive), Xingyuan Super Twin HEV and Xingyuan range-extender [17][18]. In intelligent driving, the Xiangwang series carries Huawei Qiankun ADS and HarmonyOS cockpit, while the S7 uses GAC's Xingling Zhixing ADiGO GSD system [7][22][29].

Overseas business

Trumpchi has distribution across five overseas regions (Middle East, Southeast Asia, Eastern Europe, Africa and the Americas) covering 22 countries [4]. In 2025 GAC's self-owned-brand overseas sales reached nearly 130,000 units, up 47% year on year; the group introduced five new models overseas and opened 16 new markets. By the end of 2025, GAC's business covered 86 countries and regions, with more than 630 outlets and five KD plants in Nigeria, Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia and Cambodia [32]. Media reported Trumpchi exports of 4,388 units to North America in January-February 2025, and whole-vehicle exports to markets including Russia via the China-Europe freight train [19][20].

Founders and leadership

Brand ownership and governance

Trumpchi is not a separately listed company; it is operated by GAC Trumpchi Automobile Co., Ltd., a wholly owned subsidiary of GAC Group. GAC Group is an A+H listed state-controlled group (SSE 601238, HKEX 02238), founded in June 1997 and headquartered in the Panyu Auto City of Guangzhou. Per the 2025 annual report, its controlling shareholder is Guangzhou Automobile Industry Group Co., Ltd. (about 54.02%), and the Guangzhou SASAC, which holds 90% of the industry group, is the actual controller [1][25]. Guangzhou municipal government corporate information lists Feng Xingya as chairman and secretary of the party committee and Kan Xianqing as general manager [25].

Founder

Trumpchi is a self-owned brand launched by GAC Group in 2008 and has no individual founder; the first model was built on a platform and production line bought from Fiat's Alfa Romeo 166 under the group's then management [23]. GAC Group's self-owned brands are Trumpchi, GAC Aion, GAC Haobo and Qijing (with Huawei), alongside its GAC Honda and GAC Toyota joint ventures [25].

Current Trumpchi BU management

Li Yong (李勇) became Trumpchi BU president in early October 2026; a 16 October 2026 business-registration change showed Li Yong succeeding Huang Jian as chairman and legal representative of GAC Trumpchi Automobile Co., Ltd. [9][27]. Li Yong holds a master's degree from Tsinghua University and joined GAC Group in 2002; he served as assistant general manager of GAC Hino (now GAC Lingcheng), assistant general manager of GAC Motor, and deputy general manager of GAC Motor concurrently general manager of GAC Trumpchi Sales Co., Ltd. In November 2022 he became deputy party secretary and general manager of GAC Trading, and in 2025 party secretary and chairman of GAC Trading [27].

Huang Jian (former president) joined GAC in 1998 and was head of the group's planning and development department; from 2020 he served as a GAC Toyota deputy general manager overseeing procurement, finance, HR and digitalisation, and from 2024 also as deputy plant director. He became Trumpchi BU president on 16 January 2026 and moved to GAC Toyota as executive deputy general manager on 30 September 2026 [9][10]. Li An is Trumpchi BU vice president in charge of sales and service, with a long GAC Toyota sales-channel background; Wang Yu is Trumpchi BU vice president in charge of products, previously at GAC R&D Centre where he led development of the GS5, E9 and M8 [28]. The above are public posts and business records only.

Shareholding and governance

In January 2026 GAC Group established the Trumpchi BU to integrate R&D, manufacturing, sales and service resources under one business unit [28]. At group level, Feng Xingya is party secretary and chairman, and Kan Xianqing is deputy party secretary, director and general manager (professional manager) [25].

Competition

Our judgment: competitive scope. Based on Trumpchi's SUV, MPV and sedan line-up, its main domestic competitors in the mainstream passenger-car market are BYD, Geely, Changan and Chery, while in MPVs it competes with the Denza D9, Buick GL8 and Toyota Sienna [4]. The Xiangwang S7/S9 and Yue7 put the brand into direct competition with new-energy SUVs in the RMB 160,000-250,000 band. This is our analytical selection, not a sales ranking [7][22].

Our judgment: strengths. Trumpchi retains a leading MPV franchise: cumulative MPV family sales above 900,000 units, the M8 above 500,000, and the E8 the mid-size MPV leader for 24 consecutive months [17]. A 2026 H1 resale-value report cited by media ranks Trumpchi first among Chinese self-owned brands with a 59.38% three-year residual value, and the M8 first among self-owned MPVs for a fifth straight year with 61.57% [17].

Our judgment: MPV competition. Trumpchi's MPV strength rests on long-term iteration and segment coverage (M6 in the RMB 100,000-150,000 family band, M8 across RMB 200,000-400,000, E8/E9 in new energy), but the segment is being contested by the Denza D9, Buick GL8, Toyota Sienna and multiple new-energy MPVs, so its leadership depends on sustained model renewals [15][17].

Our judgment: weaknesses and cost pressure. Volume is far below the 2018 peak: after 535,000 units in 2018, sales fell to about 385,000 in 2019 (CAAM basis about 343,000), about 322,000 in 2021 and 319,200 in 2025 [12][1]. The former volume and price anchors GS4 and GS8 have fallen sharply: the GS4 contributed about 335,000 of 2017's 500,000 units and then shrank, while the GS8 fell from 102,000 in 2017 to about 28,096 in 2019 [12]. We judge that the roughly 46% capacity utilisation in 2025 implies higher fixed cost per vehicle [11]. The electrification mix is still limited, and models such as Xiangwang and Yue7 have not yet proven sustainable scale [2].

Outlook

Company plans. GAC has positioned Trumpchi as a core part of the three-year Panyu Action, whose target is for self-owned brands to exceed 60% of group sales by 2027 and to challenge self-owned-brand sales of 2 million units [26]. Under the plan Trumpchi will cooperate deeply with Huawei to launch new mid-to-high-end SUV, MPV and sedan series carrying Huawei high-level intelligent driving [26]. GAC said at the 2025 Guangzhou Auto Show it will build the Xiangwang series into a range-anxiety-free new-energy series and expand the MPV line-up [17]. CNR reports that Trumpchi will use the Xingyuan powertrain to build a multi-category matrix spanning MPVs, urban SUVs and hardcore off-road SUVs, with the new-generation Xiangwang E8 PHEV (CLTC feed-in consumption 3.98L/100km, 281 km pure-electric range) and the Xiangwang M8 PHEV L forming the plug-in MPV pairing, and the 2027 Xiangwang S7 PHEV becoming the first Xingyuan PHEV SUV [17]. Overseas, GAC has set a 2026 target of 250,000 exported units and plans to add over 300 sales outlets, with Trumpchi a core export brand [21]. These are disclosed directions and targets, not completed results.

Our judgment. Whether Trumpchi restores scale depends on three conditions: sustained demand for the Xiangwang series and Yue7, continued MPV leadership as that segment electrifies, and overseas growth holding up. The main alternative is that domestic price competition keeps per-vehicle margins thin while the MPV franchise faces new entrants. This is our judgment, not a share-price forecast, based on the disclosed sales and capacity-utilisation data cited above [2][11].

Risks

  • Volume and capacity. 2025 sales were 319,200 units (-23.02% YoY); designed capacity was 720,000 units with output of 328,700 units, about 46% utilisation [1][11].
  • Dependence on a declining SUV base. The GS4 and GS8, once the volume anchors, have fallen sharply since their peaks, concentrating risk on the MPV line [12].
  • Electrification mix. The brand's new-energy share is still limited, and the Xiangwang and Yue7 models are early in their product cycles [2].
  • MPV competition. Its core MPV franchise faces a growing field of new-energy competitors [17].
  • Overseas business. Self-owned-brand overseas sales were nearly 130,000 units in 2025 (+47% YoY); media reported whole vehicles exported to markets including Russia via the China-Europe freight train, and the overseas channel network is still expanding [32][20].
  • No separate financial disclosure. Brand-level revenue and profit are not published, so standalone profitability cannot be assessed from public filings [1].
  • Management transition. The Trumpchi BU president and the chairman of GAC Trumpchi Automobile changed from Huang Jian to Li Yong in late September to October 2026, alongside the ongoing BU reform [9][27].

概览

广汽传祺（Trumpchi）是广州汽车集团股份有限公司（广汽集团）旗下的自主主流乘用车品牌，由广汽集团全资子公司广汽传祺汽车有限公司运营；该公司前身为 2008 年 7 月 21 日注册成立的广州汽车集团乘用车有限公司，「传祺」品牌名于 2010 年发布，首款车型同年 10 月下线、12 月上市 [4][12]。品牌首款轿车 GA5 基于从菲亚特旗下阿尔法·罗密欧 166 购入的平台与生产线打造，反映广汽通过购入欧洲平台起步自主造车的路径 [23]。

传祺覆盖轿车、SUV、MPV 三大品类，尤以 MPV 见长，并以「中国 MPV 专家」自居；2025 年起推出高端新能源子系列「向往」 [4][7]。销量由 2011 年的约 1.7 万辆升至 2018 年 53.5 万辆的峰值，其后回落，2024 年回升至 41.5 万辆，2025 年跌至 31.92 万辆（同比 -23.02%），2026 年上半年回升至 16.44 万辆（+12.36%） [13][8][1][2]。作为广汽自主板块的一部分，传祺与广汽埃安（2025 年销量 29.01 万辆）共同构成集团自主品牌基本盘 [1]。

品牌起步较晚、早期车型市场反响平淡，真正放量始于 2015 年 4 月上市的 GS4，此后销量随 SUV 与单一爆款的依赖而大起大落 [23][12]。2026 年 1 月，广汽集团设立传祺 BU（事业单元）推进一体化运营，传祺成为集团三年「番禺行动」改革的核心对象之一 [9]。

现状

业务结构与近期表现

传祺是广汽集团内部的单一乘用车品牌，经营结果并入集团合并报表，不单独披露品牌层面财务数据 [1]。2025 年销量同比下滑 23.02% 至 31.92 万辆；广汽 2025 年年报显示，广汽传祺设计产能 72 万辆、报告期产量 32.87 万辆，产能利用率约 46% [1][11]。2026 年上半年品牌销售 16.44 万辆、同比增长 12.36%，广汽半年报将回升归因于 MPV 系列价值强化、向往 E8 PHEV 与 M8 PHEV L 焕新、向往 S7 年款升级，以及全新「方盒子」越 7 切入新能源泛越野市场 [2][3]。媒体引述产销快报称，2026 年前三季度广汽传祺累计销售 24.12 万辆、同比增长 3.47%，上半年营业收入 202.3 亿元、同比增长约 12.66% [27]。

从更长周期看，销量轨迹起伏明显：2018 年 53.5 万辆峰值之后，2019 年降至约 38.5 万辆（中汽协口径约 34.31 万辆）、2021 年约 32.2 万辆，随后回升至 2022 年 36.25 万辆（+11.8%）、2023 年 40.65 万辆（+12.1%）、2024 年 41.5 万辆（约 +2%），2025 年再度下滑 [12][33][36][34][8]。MPV 是品牌基本盘：2023 年 MPV 家族销量 16.42 万辆、同比增长 59%、居中国 MPV 市场第一，2024 年 MPV 家族年销 18.4 万辆，截至 2026 年 9 月累计突破 90 万辆 [34][24][17]。2025 年传祺新能源车型销量突破 15 万辆 [32]。

生产基地与产能

传祺在广州（番禺）、杭州、新疆、宜昌设有四大生产基地，形成覆盖珠三角、长三角、长江经济带及「一带一路」的整体布局 [4]。宜昌工厂于 2019 年 6 月竣工，总占地约 1400 亩、总投资 35.3 亿元，整车年产能 20 万辆，单线可达每 52 秒下线一台车 [4][6]。广汽 2025 年年报披露传祺合计设计产能 72 万辆、产量 32.87 万辆 [1]。

核心技术与平台

传祺自 2006 年起启动混动技术自研，主攻双电机串并联 DHT 路线，掌握 400 余项核心专利，GMC 1.0、GMC 2.0 系统先后斩获「世界十佳变速器」「世界十佳混合动力系统」等行业奖项 [17]。2022 年发布混动技术品牌「钜浪混动」，2023 年发布技术品牌 i-GTEC [4][16]。2026 年广汽科技日发布「星源动力」动力技术品牌，涵盖星源插混（GMC 3.0 雷霆版/疾风版 DHT、第四代 DHE 发动机、P4 后电驱）、星源超级双擎 HEV 与星源增程三大分支 [17][18]。智驾方面，向往系列搭载华为乾崑 ADS 与鸿蒙座舱，S7 采用广汽星灵智行 ADiGO GSD 智驾系统 [7][22][29]。

海外业务

传祺已在海外中东、东南亚、东欧、非洲、美洲五大板块 22 个国家布局销售网络 [4]。2025 年广汽自主品牌海外销量近 13 万辆、同比增长 47%；全年向海外市场导入 5 款新车型、新开拓 16 个市场。截至 2025 年底，广汽业务已覆盖 86 个国家和地区，网点超过 630 个，并在尼日利亚、泰国、马来西亚、印尼、柬埔寨建成 5 家 KD 工厂 [32]。媒体报道 2025 年 1–2 月传祺向北美市场出口 4388 辆，并曾通过中欧班列向俄罗斯等市场出口整车 [19][20]。

创始人与管理层

品牌归属与治理

传祺并非单独上市公司，而是由广汽集团全资子公司广汽传祺汽车有限公司运营的品牌。广汽集团为 A+H 股上市的国有控股集团（上交所 601238、港交所 02238），成立于 1997 年 6 月，总部位于广州番禺汽车城；据 2025 年年报，其控股股东为广州汽车工业集团有限公司（持股约 54.02%），广州市国资委持有广汽工业 90% 股权，为实际控制人 [1][25]。广州市政府公开的企业信息显示，广汽集团董事长为冯兴亚、总经理为閤先庆 [25]。

创始人

传祺为广汽集团 2008 年启动的自主品牌，无自然人创始人；首款车型由广汽集团时任管理层推动，采用购入的阿尔法·罗密欧 166 平台与生产线起步 [23]。广汽集团自主品牌包括广汽传祺、广汽埃安、广汽昊铂及与华为合作的启境汽车，另有广汽本田、广汽丰田等合资品牌 [25]。

现任传祺 BU 管理层

李勇 于 2026 年 10 月初接任传祺 BU 总裁；10 月 16 日工商变更显示李勇接任广汽传祺汽车有限公司董事长、法定代表人，接替黄坚 [9][27]。李勇拥有清华大学硕士学位，2002 年毕业后加入广汽集团，历任广汽日野（现广汽领程）总经理助理、广汽乘用车总经理助理、广汽乘用车副总经理兼广汽传祺销售公司总经理；2022 年 11 月调任广汽商贸党委副书记、总经理，2025 年任广汽商贸党委书记、董事长 [27]。

黄坚（前任总裁）1998 年加入广汽集团，曾任集团规划发展部部长；2020 年起任广汽丰田分管采购、财务、人事与数字化的副总经理，2024 年起兼任副工厂长；2026 年 1 月 16 日出任传祺 BU 总裁，2026 年 9 月 30 日调任广汽丰田执行副总经理 [9][10]。李安任传祺 BU 副总裁、负责销售服务领域，长期在广汽丰田聚焦销售渠道与终端运营；王彧任传祺 BU 副总裁、负责产品领域，此前任职广汽研究院，曾主导传祺 GS5、E9、M8 等车型研发 [28]。以上均为公开职务与商业履历。

股权与治理

2026 年 1 月，广汽集团设立传祺 BU，整合研发、制造、销售与服务资源推进一体化运营 [28]。集团层面，冯兴亚任党委书记、董事长，閤先庆任党委副书记、董事、总经理（职业经理人） [25]。

竞争格局

我们的判断：竞争范围。按传祺的 SUV、MPV 与轿车谱系，其在主流乘用车市场的主要国内对手为比亚迪、吉利、长安、奇瑞；在 MPV 市场则与腾势 D9、别克 GL8、丰田赛那竞争 [4]。向往 S7/S9 与越 7 使品牌直接进入 16 万–25 万元价位新能源 SUV 的竞争。此为分析所选的竞争范围，不是销量排名 [7][22]。

我们的判断：优势。传祺保有领先的 MPV 基盘：截至 2026 年 9 月 MPV 家族累计销量超 90 万辆、M8 超 50 万辆、E8 连续 24 个月中型 MPV 销冠 [17]。媒体引用的 2026 上半年保值率报告显示，广汽传祺以 59.38% 的三年保值率居自主品牌总榜第一，M8 以 61.57% 连续五年蝉联自主 MPV 保值率第一 [17]。

我们的判断：MPV 竞争。传祺的 MPV 优势建立在长期迭代与细分卡位上（M6 主打 10 万–15 万元家用、M8 覆盖 20 万–40 万元、E8/E9 切入新能源），但该细分市场正被腾势 D9、别克 GL8、丰田赛那及多款新能源 MPV 分流，其领先地位依赖持续换代 [15][17]。

我们的判断：短板与成本压力。品牌销量远低于 2018 年峰值：2018 年 53.5 万辆之后，2019 年降至约 38.5 万辆（中汽协口径约 34.31 万辆）、2021 年约 32.2 万辆、2025 年 31.92 万辆 [12][1]。曾经的销量与价格支柱 GS4、GS8 大幅下滑：GS4 在 2017 年贡献约 33.5 万辆、其后收缩，GS8 由 2017 年的 10.2 万辆降至 2019 年的约 2.81 万辆 [12]。我们判断，2025 年约 46% 的产能利用率意味着较高的单位固定成本压力 [11]。电动化结构仍有限，向往与越 7 等新车型尚未证明可持续的规模 [2]。

未来展望

公司公开规划。广汽将传祺列为三年「番禺行动」的核心，目标是到 2027 年自主品牌占集团总销量 60% 以上、挑战自主品牌销量 200 万辆 [26]。番禺行动提出传祺将与华为深入合作，推出全新中高级 SUV、MPV 和轿车三大系列车型并搭载华为高阶智驾 [26]。广汽在 2025 广州车展表示将把向往系列打造成「无里程焦虑的新能源车系」并扩充 MPV 阵容 [17]。央广网报道称，传祺将依托星源动力构建 MPV、城市 SUV、硬核越野 SUV 多品类矩阵，全新一代向往 E8 PHEV（CLTC 亏电油耗 3.98L/100km、纯电续航 281 公里）与向往 M8 PHEV L 组成插混 MPV 组合，2027 款向往 S7 PHEV 将成为首款搭载星源插混的 SUV [17]。在海外，广汽提出 2026 年挑战全年 25 万辆的出口销量目标、并计划新增超 300 家渠道网点，传祺是核心出口品牌之一 [21]。以上为公司披露的方向与目标，不是已实现的业绩。

我们的判断。传祺能否恢复规模取决于三个条件：向往系列与越 7 的持续需求、MPV 电动化进程中领导地位的延续，以及海外增长能否维持。另一种主要情形是国内价格竞争继续挤压单车利润，而其最强资产 MPV 基盘面临新进入者。以上为我们的判断，不构成股价预测，依据为前述披露的销量与产能利用率数据 [2][11]。

风险

  • 销量与产能。2025 年销量 31.92 万辆（同比 -23.02%），设计产能 72 万辆、产量 32.87 万辆、产能利用率约 46% [1][11]。
  • 对下滑 SUV 基盘的依赖。曾经的销量支柱 GS4、GS8 自峰值大幅回落，风险向 MPV 产品线集中 [12]。
  • 电动化结构。品牌新能源占比仍有限，向往与越 7 尚处于产品周期早期 [2]。
  • MPV 竞争。核心 MPV 基盘面临日益增多的新能源竞争对手 [17]。
  • 海外业务。2025 年自主品牌海外销量近 13 万辆（同比 +47%），媒体曾报道其通过中欧班列向俄罗斯等市场出口整车，海外渠道网络仍在拓展期 [32][20]。
  • 无单独财务披露。品牌层面营收与利润未公开，无法从公开文件评估其独立盈利能力 [1]。
  • 管理层交接。2026 年 9 月末至 10 月传祺 BU 总裁与广汽传祺董事长由黄坚更替为李勇，叠加 BU 改革进行中 [9][27]。
