Overview

Hycan Automobile Technology Co., Ltd. operated the Hycan (合创) electric-vehicle brand. It began as GAC-NIO New Energy Vehicle Technology Co., Ltd., a joint venture between GAC Group and NIO announced on 28 December 2017 and registered on 10 April 2018.[1][7]

In early 2021 Guangdong Zhutou Intelligent Technology Investment, an affiliate of Pearl River Investment Management Group (珠江投管集团), subscribed RMB1.923 billion and became the 68.56% majority shareholder; the company was renamed Hycan and NIO later withdrew from the shareholder register.[9][7]

The company launched four battery-electric models — the 007 mid-size SUV (April 2020), Z03 compact SUV (October 2021), A06 coupe-sedan (December 2022) and V09 MPV (October 2023) — but annual sales never exceeded roughly 20,000 units, and the business accumulated large losses.[5][6][8][11]

After severe operating difficulties through 2024, GAC Group and GAC Aion — which together hold 25% — agreed in January 2025 to provide shareholder loans for employee wages and to have GAC Aion take over after-sales service. On 30 December 2025 the Guangzhou Bay Area International Restructuring Center accepted a restructuring case for the company. As of 29 October 2026 the company was in restructuring with investor recruitment underway, and no resumption of production had been confirmed.[1][3]

Current operations

Business and legal form

Hycan's stated main business is new-energy-vehicle technology R&D and vehicle sales. It is a limited-liability company (foreign-invested, non-wholly-owned) with unified social credit code 91440101MA5ARYR74A, registered at the Xiangjiang Financial Business Centre in Guangzhou's Nansha District, with registered capital of RMB5,523,044,649 fully paid up and Wang Huipu as legal representative.[1][3]

Products

The product line spanned SUV, sedan and MPV segments. The 007 was a mid-size electric SUV launched on 10 April 2020 with post-subsidy prices of RMB262,600-303,000 plus a limited Starship edition from RMB340,000 and a range of roughly 500km; the Z03 was a compact SUV launched on 18 October 2021 at RMB132,800-168,800; the A06 was a coupe-sedan launched on 3 December 2022 at RMB179,800, RMB199,800 and RMB269,800 for the 550A, 630C and PERFORMANCE versions; and the V09 was a mid-to-large MPV launched on 13 October 2023 at RMB318,800-438,800 across five versions. A lower-priced A06 PLUS variant was sold from RMB139,800. The company did not disclose a revenue breakdown by model.[5][6][11][7][15]

On specifications: the Z03 measures 4,602×1,900×1,645mm with a 2,750mm wheelbase, offers 55.5/63.98/76.8kWh batteries with CLTC ranges of 430/510/620km, and a 135kW or 160kW front motor with 225Nm of torque. The V09 measures 5,215×1,967×1,910mm with a 3,170mm wheelbase, carries 92/95.16/114.19kWh batteries for 620/762km ranges, and a 202kW (275PS) motor with 335Nm. The A06 offers 63/71kWh batteries for 550-630km ranges in front- and all-wheel-drive forms, the all-wheel-drive version combining 340kW (462PS) and 534Nm for a claimed 3.9-second 0-100km/h sprint; the A06 PLUS carries a 60.04kWh battery, a 160kW motor and up to 520km of range.[15]

Technology and platforms

The company presented its own H-GEA global battery-electric architecture and the H-VIP intelligent-driving and connectivity system. The A06 was the first model on H-GEA and the first to carry H-VIP 2.0, with a Qualcomm 8155 chip and the self-developed HI-OS system, a 0.217 drag coefficient and a 4,965mm body with a 2,850mm wheelbase. The V09 carried H-VIP 3.0 with a standard 800V silicon-carbide platform and 4C super-fast charging, and a LiDAR detecting up to 300m. The first model, the 007, was instead built on GAC's GEP 2.0 platform and produced at a GAC new-energy plant, reflecting the company's early dependence on GAC technology.[6][11][16]

Factories and capacity

Around end-2021 Hycan acquired a 49% stake in GAC Motor (Hangzhou) Co., Ltd. via auction. The plant flexibly builds GAC fuel vehicles and Hycan EVs on the same line, and the Z03, A06 and V09 were produced there. The June 2026 restructuring notice describes the Hangzhou plant as a world-class smart-manufacturing plant with annual capacity of 200,000 units and a complete four-process production line.[10][3]

Domestic position and sales

Hycan did not publish monthly sales, and its volume was not separately broken out in GAC Group's monthly production and sales reports. Media citing third-party data reported 2022 sales of about 19,600 units and 2023 sales of about 16,000 units, while another report put the figures at 18,600 (2022) and 18,900 (2023). GAC's production and sales report showed Hycan 2024 volume at no more than 4,950 units, down 82.32% year on year. By December 2022 the company said cumulative deliveries had passed 20,000 units.[7][9]

Overseas business

In May 2024 Hycan shipped its first export batch (V09, A06 PLUS, Z03) from Hangzhou to Southeast Asia, the Middle East and Europe. In September 2024 it appointed Malaysian firm Concept Fields Sdn Bhd as master distributor, with plans to launch the Z03, V09 and A06/A06 Plus in Malaysia in February or March 2025 and to arrange local CKD assembly; the distributor also said it would cover Middle East and Africa markets. No confirmed overseas sales figures or evidence of completed entry into those markets had been published as of 29 October 2026.[14][15]

Founders and leadership

Founding and early management

At its founding, NIO founder Li Bin served as chairman, GAC Research Institute president Wang Qiujing as vice-chairman, and Liao Bing (an assistant president of GAC Research Institute) as CEO; Liao Bing was described as a founder and CEO of GAC-NIO. Around February 2021 Liao Bing resigned as CEO and Li Bin stepped back, and GAC and NIO executives subsequently left the board after the capital increase. These are dated historical roles recorded in media.[9][10][12]

Current officers

The 13 January 2025 GAC announcement and the June 2026 restructuring notice both list Wang Huipu as legal representative of Hycan. After the 2021 restructuring, Pearl River Investment Management Group president Li Zhihong served as chairman and legal representative, succeeding the departing Li Bin as legal representative. Media additionally reported Yang Ying as CEO and Wu Song (a GAC Group executive) as co-chairman, with He Kaixin as chief technology officer, all drawn from GAC. This review has not obtained a primary document listing the complete current board and management at October 2026, and does not present historical names as current appointments.[1][3][8]

Controlling shareholder background

Media reported that the controlling shareholder, Guangdong Zhutou Intelligent Technology Investment, is an affiliate of Pearl River Investment Management Group, whose main business is energy and infrastructure investment; that Zhu Yihang (son of Hopsen Group chairman Zhu Mengyi and founder of the EDG esports club) is the actual controller of Zhutou Intelligent; and that Zhu Mengyi attended the GAC Group and Pearl River Investment Management strategic-cooperation signing in January 2021. These are media reports, not announcement confirmations.[8][13]

Shareholding and governance

The 2025 announcement lists the shareholders as Guangdong Zhutou Intelligent 68.5594%, GAC Aion 20.5411%, GAC Group 4.4589%, Yaoyu Investment Holdings 4.4589% and Kaichuang Gongjin 1.9817%, and does not identify an actual controller. This document states registered shareholdings only and makes no actual-controller determination.[1]

Competition

Positioning and differentiation

Hycan positioned itself between GAC Aion and NIO, using GAC's manufacturing and platform base with NIO-oriented user operations. In practice its products were widely described as sharing Aion platforms, which limited differentiation: the 007 was compared with the Aion LX (media called it an Aion LX 'sister car'), and the Z03 with the Aion Y. The 007 was priced at RMB262,600-303,000 after subsidy, near the Aion LX with which it shared the GEP 2.0 platform.[5][8][12]

Our assessment: competitive limits

Our assessment: Hycan's reliance on GAC platforms and its late entry meant it competed largely on price against stronger brands such as Aion and the established new forces, without a clear technology or brand advantage. The basis is the shared-platform reporting and the launch-price records above; even the V09's 800V platform did not by itself establish scale or profitability, as annual sales remained below roughly 20,000 units while losses were large.[8][11][7]

Our assessment: industry shake-out context

Our assessment: Hycan's exit fits a broader shake-out of under-scaled new-energy start-ups during an intensifying price war. As a reference point, Jiyue delivered 13,561 units in 2024, more than double Hycan's sub-4,950-unit volume, and also failed in late 2024. GAC Group attributed its 2024 profit decline partly to about RMB18 billion of additional sales subsidies across the group. The basis is media-reported comparative delivery figures and GAC's 2024 profit-forecast commentary.[7]

Outlook

Historical company plans

In 2021, after Pearl River Investment Management took control, media reported a company target of delivering 250,000 vehicles by 2025, and that the Zhutou side planned about RMB3 billion in the first phase and over RMB10 billion of follow-on investment for the intelligent-vehicle project. These were pre-difficulty targets recorded here as historical information only; actual deliveries never approached the 2025 goal.[13]

Restructuring progress

The Guangzhou Bay Area International Restructuring Center accepted the restructuring case on 30 December 2025 and appointed a coordinator; the 12 June 2026 notice began pre-recruitment of restructuring investors, with registration closing 15 July 2026 and due diligence by 15 August 2026. The notice lists the 49% Hangzhou plant stake and the four model platforms and tooling as the main assets. This review has not obtained a primary document confirming a selected investor or a resumption plan by 29 October 2026.[3]

Our analysis

Our assessment: the accepted restructuring and investor recruitment create the procedural possibility of a sale or partial restart of the brand or its assets, but a return to vehicle production would require an investor, new funding and renewed after-sales arrangements; there is no confirmation any of these has occurred. The basis is the restructuring notice's asset list and its statement that investor selection is in progress.[3]

Our assessment: the value of the estate most plausibly lies in the Hangzhou plant stake and the four model platforms rather than in the retail brand, given that the brand has halted production and after-sales has passed to GAC Aion. This is a conditional judgment, not an established outcome; the restructuring could equally end in an asset disposal without a restart.[3][1]

Risks

  • Restructuring and continuity: the company entered restructuring on 30 December 2025 and investor recruitment began in June 2026; no investor or restart had been confirmed as of the cutoff, so the outcome and any resumption of production remain uncertain.[3]
  • Balance-sheet evidence: total profit was RMB-1,840.27 million for 2023 and RMB-1,130.02 million for the eleven months to 30 November 2024; equity fell from RMB1,360.52 million to RMB374.39 million over the same period, with an asset-liability ratio of about 92.94% at end-November 2024, indicating continuing losses and thin equity.[1]
  • After-sales and customer obligations: GAC Aion took over after-sales service in January 2025 with up to RMB174 million in shareholder loans over a 10-year term; the long-term adequacy of this arrangement is not independently established.[1]
  • Shareholder and funding constraints: the controlling shareholder is an investment vehicle of Pearl River Investment Management Group, a group without a core automotive operating business; its capacity to sustain further funding is not disclosed.[8]
  • Legal and enforcement: Hycan was listed as a dishonest judgment debtor on 16 January 2025 over an unpaid RMB2.12 million obligation, indicating outstanding legal liabilities.[7][17]
  • Production and market access: production depends on the Hangzhou plant in which Hycan holds only 49%, and that stake is now a restructuring asset, so any future production depends on the restructuring outcome and on car-industry entry qualifications.[3]

概览

合创汽车科技有限公司经营合创（HYCAN）电动汽车品牌，前身是广汽集团与蔚来汽车于 2017 年 12 月 28 日宣布、2018 年 4 月 10 日注册成立的广汽蔚来新能源汽车科技有限公司。[1][7]

2021 年初，珠江投管集团旗下广东珠投智能增资 19.23 亿元、成为持股 68.56% 的第一大股东，公司更名为合创汽车，蔚来其后退出股东行列。[9][7]

公司先后量产四款纯电车型——007 中型 SUV（2020 年 4 月）、Z03 紧凑型 SUV（2021 年 10 月）、A06 轿跑（2022 年 12 月）与 V09 MPV（2023 年 10 月），但年销量始终未突破约 2 万辆，并持续大幅亏损。[5][6][8][11]

经历 2024 年严重经营困难后，合计持股 25% 的广汽集团与广汽埃安于 2025 年 1 月同意按股比提供借款支付员工工资，并由广汽埃安承接售后服务。2025 年 12 月 30 日，广州市湾区国际重组中心受理公司重组案件。截至 2026 年 10 月 29 日，公司处于重组阶段、正预招募投资人，未见复产确认。[1][3]

历史沿革

  1. 2017-12-28

    广汽集团宣布与上海蔚来汽车有限公司合作设立广汽蔚来，计划总投资 12.8 亿元，广汽、蔚来分别持股 45%、55%。[7]

  2. 2018-04-10

    广汽蔚来新能源汽车科技有限公司注册成立，初始股东为蔚来汽车、广汽集团、湖北长江蔚来新能源产业发展基地合伙企业、广汽新能源（今广汽埃安）各持 22.5%，员工持股平台开创共进持 10%。蔚来创始人李斌任董事长、广汽研究院院长王秋景任副董事长、广汽研究院院长助理廖兵任 CEO。[9][12]

  3. 2019-12-27

    发布「HYCAN 合创」品牌并亮相首款车型 HYCAN 007，开启预售（预售价 26 万–40 万元）。[9][16]

  4. 2020-04-10

    首款车型 007 中型纯电 SUV 上市，补贴后 BASE 版 26.26 万元、PLUS 版 28.28 万元、TOP 版 30.30 万元，星舰版（限量 500 台/年）34 万元起；基于广汽 GEP 2.0 平台、由广汽新能源工厂生产。其后报道其 2020 年销量仅约 508 台，与此前 1.5 万辆年度目标相去甚远。[5][7][16]

  5. 2021-02

    广汽蔚来完成约 24.05 亿元增资：广东珠投智能增资 19.23 亿元、广汽埃安增资约 4.82 亿元，珠投智能成为持股 68.56% 的第一大股东。CEO 廖兵辞任，李斌退出，珠江投资管理集团总裁李志红继任法定代表人。[7][9][10]

  6. 2021-04

    公司更名：2021 年 4 月 1 日先更名「合创智慧科技」，约一个月后再更名「合创汽车科技有限公司」（部分报道记为 5 月）。[7][17]

  7. 2021-10-18

    第二款车型合创 Z03 紧凑型纯电 SUV 上市，共四个版本，售价 13.28 万–16.88 万元，CLTC 续航 510km/620km。[8]

  8. 2021-12

    合创通过竞拍取得广汽乘用车（杭州）有限公司 49% 股权，该工厂实现广汽燃油车与合创新能源车柔性共线生产（另有报道记为 2022 年上半年）。[9][10]

  9. 2022-08-17

    上海蔚来汽车彻底退出合创股东行列，新增股东耀煜投资控股有限公司。[9][17]

  10. 2022-12-03

    第三款车型 A06 纯电轿跑上市，550A / 630C / PERFORMANCE 分别售 17.98 万、19.98 万、26.98 万元，为 H-GEA 架构首款车型、首次搭载 H-VIP 2.0。2022 年 12 月交付 3,134 辆，累计交付突破 2 万辆。[6][9]

  11. 2023-02-10

    合创与广汽集团旗下广州巨湾技研有限公司达成战略合作，以大湾区为示范点打造「XFC 极速充电」生态圈。[9]

  12. 2023-02-23

    广汽集团第六届董事会第 34 次会议通过增资议案：广汽及广汽埃安按 4.46%、20.54% 分别增资 1.07 亿元、4.93 亿元；非执行董事管大源以「未来发展存在不确定性」投弃权票。[4][9]

  13. 2023-10-13

    第四款车型 V09 中大型纯电 MPV 在上海上市，共五款车型，售价 31.88 万–43.88 万元，标配 800V 碳化硅平台、搭载 H-VIP 3.0。[11]

  14. 2024-05

    合创首批出口车（V09、A06 PLUS、Z03）自杭州发运，面向东南亚、中东、欧洲市场。[14]

    合创宣布进军马来西亚，委任当地 Concept Fields 为总代理，计划于 2025 年 2–3 月引入 Z03、V09、A06/A06 Plus 三款车型并规划本地组装（CKD）；该总代理另称将负责中东、非洲等多个市场。[15]

  18. 2025-01-16

  19. 2025-12-30

  20. 2026-06-12

    重组协调人发布意向投资人预招募公告，列明主要资产为杭州工厂 49% 股权及 007、Z03、A06、V09 四款车型平台和模具；报名截止 2026 年 7 月 15 日、尽职调查截止 8 月 15 日。[3]

现状

业务与法律形式

合创汽车主营业务为新能源汽车技术研发及整车销售。公司为有限责任公司（外商投资、非独资），统一社会信用代码 91440101MA5ARYR74A，注册地址为广州市南沙区香江金融商务中心，注册资本 552,304.4649 万元、已全部实缴，法定代表人为王会普。[1][3]

产品

产品线覆盖 SUV、轿车、MPV。007 为中型纯电 SUV，2020 年 4 月 10 日上市、补贴后 26.26 万–30.30 万元，另有星舰版 34 万元起、续航约 500 公里；Z03 为紧凑型 SUV，2021 年 10 月 18 日上市，13.28 万–16.88 万元；A06 为轿跑，2022 年 12 月 3 日上市，550A / 630C / PERFORMANCE 三个版本分别 17.98 万、19.98 万、26.98 万元；V09 为中大型 MPV，2023 年 10 月 13 日上市，五款车型 31.88 万–43.88 万元；另有定位更低的 A06 PLUS 售价 13.98 万元。公司未按车型披露收入构成。[5][6][11][7][15]

尺寸与动力方面：Z03 车身 4,602×1,900×1,645mm、轴距 2,750mm，电池 55.5/63.98/76.8kWh，CLTC 续航 430/510/620km，前置电机 135kW 或 160kW、扭矩 225Nm；V09 车身 5,215×1,967×1,910mm、轴距 3,170mm，电池 92/95.16/114.19kWh、续航 620/762km，电机 202kW（275PS）、扭矩 335Nm；A06 电池 63/71kWh、续航 550–630km，分前驱与四驱，四驱综合 340kW（462PS）、534Nm，官方 0–100km/h 3.9 秒；A06 PLUS 电池 60.04kWh、电机 160kW、续航最高 520km。[15]

技术与平台

公司发布了自研 H-GEA 合创全球纯电架构及 H-VIP 智驾互联系统。A06 为 H-GEA 架构首款车型、首次搭载 H-VIP 2.0，配备高通 8155 芯片与自研 HI-OS 车机系统，风阻系数 0.217，车长 4,965mm、轴距 2,850mm；V09 搭载 H-VIP 3.0、标配 800V 碳化硅平台与 4C 超充、配备最远探测 300m 的激光雷达。首款车型 007 则基于广汽 GEP 2.0 平台、由广汽新能源工厂生产，体现早期对广汽技术的依赖。[6][11][16]

工厂与产能

约 2021 年底，合创通过竞拍取得广汽乘用车（杭州）有限公司 49% 股权，该工厂以柔性线共线生产广汽燃油车与合创新能源车，Z03、A06、V09 均在此投产。2026 年 6 月重组公告记载杭州工厂为年产 20 万辆、拥有完整四大工艺的世界级智能制造工厂。[10][3]

国内地位与销量

合创未按月公布销量，其销量也未在广汽集团每月产销快报中单列。媒体引第三方数据称 2022 年销量约 1.96 万辆、2023 年约 1.60 万辆；另一报道记为 2022 年 1.86 万辆、2023 年 1.89 万辆。广汽产销快报显示 2024 年合创销量不高于 4,950 辆、同比 -82.32%。截至 2022 年 12 月，公司称累计交付突破 2 万辆。[7][9]

海外业务

2024 年 5 月，合创首批出口车（V09、A06 PLUS、Z03）自杭州发运，面向东南亚、中东、欧洲。2024 年 9 月，合创委任马来西亚 Concept Fields Sdn Bhd 为总代理，计划 2025 年 2–3 月在马来西亚推出 Z03、V09、A06/A06 Plus 并规划本地 CKD 组装，该总代理另称将覆盖中东、非洲市场。截至 2026 年 10 月 29 日，未见已确认的海外销量或已完成进入这些市场的证据。[14][15]

创始人与管理层

创始人与早期管理层

现任管理层

2025 年 1 月 13 日广汽公告与 2026 年 6 月重组公告均列王会普为法定代表人。2021 年重组后，珠江投资管理集团总裁李志红曾任董事长、法定代表人，并继退出的李斌出任法定代表人。媒体另报道杨颖任 CEO、吴松（广汽集团高管）任联席董事长、何凯欣任首席技术官，均出自广汽体系。本次核查未取得截至 2026 年 10 月的完整现任董事会及管理层名单的一手文件，不将历史名单写成现任名单。[1][3][8]

控股股东背景

竞争格局

定位与差异化

合创定位于广汽埃安与蔚来之间，借助广汽的制造与平台基础、叠加蔚来式的用户运营。但实践中其产品被普遍描述为与埃安共享平台，差异化有限：007 被与埃安 LX 比较、被称为埃安 LX「姊妹车」，Z03 被与埃安 Y 比较。首款 007 补贴后售价 26.26 万–30.30 万元，与同平台的埃安 LX 价格接近。[5][8][12]

我们的判断：竞争边界

我们的判断：合创依赖广汽平台、且入局偏晚，使其在与埃安及头部新势力的竞争中主要靠价格周旋，缺乏清晰的技术或品牌优势。判断依据为上述共享平台的报道与上市价记录；V09 的 800V 平台等产品亮点本身不能证明规模或盈利，其年销量长期低于约 2 万辆而亏损巨大。[8][11][7]

我们的判断：行业洗牌背景

我们的判断：合创的退出是价格战加剧背景下、规模不足的新能源初创企业加速出清的一部分。作为参照，极越 2024 年交付 13,561 辆、仍多于合创不足 4,950 辆的销量，且同样于 2024 年末倒下；广汽集团自身也将 2024 年利润下滑部分归因于集团及投资企业追加约 180 亿元销售补贴。判断依据为媒体转引的交付量对比及广汽 2024 年业绩预告表述。[7]

未来展望

公司规划的历史记录

2021 年珠江投管入主后，媒体报道公司曾定下力争 2025 年实现交付 25 万辆的目标；媒体还报道朱氏智能汽车项目首期合作预计投入 30 亿元、后续投资将超过 100 亿元。以上均为经营困难前的历史目标，本文仅作历史信息记录；实际交付量远未达到 2025 年目标。[13]

重组进展

广州市湾区国际重组中心于 2025 年 12 月 30 日受理重组并选定协调人；2026 年 6 月 12 日公告开始预招募重整投资人，报名截止 2026 年 7 月 15 日、尽调截止 8 月 15 日，列明主要资产为杭州工厂 49% 股权及四款车型平台和模具。截至 2026 年 10 月 29 日，本次核查未取得确认中选投资人或复产方案的一手文件。[3]

我们的分析

我们的判断：重组获受理并启动投资人招募，为品牌或资产的处置、局部重启提供了程序上的可能；但恢复整车生产仍需落实投资人、资金及售后安排，目前均无确认。判断依据为重组公告的资产清单及其「投资人招募进行中」的表述。[3]

我们的判断：鉴于品牌已停产、售后渠道已转由广汽埃安承接，财产价值更可能落在杭州工厂股权与四款车型平台，而非零售品牌本身。此为条件判断而非既成结果；重组亦可能以资产处置收场而不再重启。[3][1]

风险

  • 重组与存续风险依据：公司于 2025 年 12 月 30 日进入重组、2026 年 6 月启动投资人招募，截至研究截止日未确认投资人及复产，结果与是否恢复生产仍不确定。[3]
  • 资产负债风险依据：2023 年利润总额 -18.40 亿元，2024 年前 11 个月 -11.30 亿元；同期所有者权益由 13.61 亿元降至 3.74 亿元，2024 年 11 月末资产负债率约 92.94%，反映持续亏损与权益薄弱。[1]
  • 售后与客户义务风险依据：2025 年 1 月广汽埃安承接售后，按 10 年测算由股东提供不超过 1.74 亿元借款，该安排的长期充足性未经独立确认。[1]
  • 股东与资金风险依据：控股股东为珠江投管集团旗下投资主体，该集团无核心整车经营业务，其持续注资能力未公开披露。[8]
  • 法律与执行风险依据：2025 年 1 月 16 日因未履行 212.06 万元给付义务被列为失信被执行人，表明存在未结法律债务。[7][17]
  • 生产与准入风险依据：公司生产依赖其持股 49% 的杭州工厂，该工厂股权现为重组资产，未来任何生产安排均取决于重组结果及汽车行业准入资质。[3]
