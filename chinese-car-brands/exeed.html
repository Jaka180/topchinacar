Overview

Exeed (Xingtu) is the premium brand of Chery Automobile Co., Ltd. (HKEX: 9973.HK), headquartered in Wuhu, Anhui. It was first shown as the 'EXEED' premium product sequence at the 2017 Frankfurt Motor Show and was upgraded to a standalone brand in 2018, Chery's third attempt to move upmarket after Riich and Qoros.[1][11] Chery chairman Yin Tongyue positioned Exeed as the 'tip of the pyramid' of the Chery group, likened to Audi for Volkswagen or Lexus for Toyota, with a stated mission of technology output, brand upmarket and globalisation.[11][12] At its Brand Night on 22 December 2025 the brand entered its '3.0 era' and repositioned itself as a 'global performance-luxury brand'.[10] Its official positioning is 'global performance-luxury innovator', built on performance as the core, luxury as the base and innovation first.[1]

Exeed sells mainly SUVs and sedans across two series: the Exeed series of fuel and plug-in-hybrid SUVs (Zhuifeng, Lingyun, Yaoguang, Lanyue) and the Sterra new-energy series (the ES sedan and ET SUV), joined from 2026 by the EX7 crossover and ET5. Exeed is one of Chery Automobile's five passenger-vehicle brands, alongside Chery, Jetour, iCAR and Luxeed. After the December 2025 Brand Night the 'Sterra' series name has been phased out in official materials, and the site now lists the sedan as the Exeed ES, alongside the EX7, ET5 and the Sterra ET.[2][3] Sales rose from under 20,000 units in 2019 to a peak of 140,959 in 2024 (up 12.3%), then fell 15% to 120,369 in 2025, the only declining sub-brand in Chery's 2025 line-up.[8][19] The brand already has meaningful overseas scale, covering more than 30 markets with an overseas average selling price above RMB 400,000.[10][13]

Because Exeed is a brand rather than a separately listed company, it does not disclose standalone financial statements; parent figures are covered in the Chery deep research, where Chery Automobile reported FY2025 revenue of RMB 300.287 billion and attributable net profit of RMB 19.019 billion.[5] In 2026, with domestic sales contracting, the brand was moved into Chery's international business group, shifting its growth focus overseas.[12]

History

  1. 2017-09

    Chery unveils the 'EXEED' premium product sequence and its first concept car, the EXEED TX, at the Frankfurt Motor Show — Chery's third move upmarket after Riich and Qoros.[1][11]

  2. 2018

    EXEED is upgraded from a product sequence to a standalone brand and named 'Xingtu' (Exeed). Chery chairman Yin Tongyue says the brand took 10 years to build, with an initial annual sales target of 60,000 and a goal of 100,000.[11]

  3. 2019-01

    The first model, the Exeed TX, rolls off the production line.[1]

  4. 2019-04

    The first production models, the TX/TXL mid-size SUVs, go on sale at around RMB 150,000 (media cite a RMB 125,900-175,900 range); the brand sells under 20,000 units that year.[11][16]

  5. 2019-10

    The compact SUV Exeed LX is launched.[1]

  6. 2020

    Exeed launches an LX 1.5T version at a limited-time price of RMB 99,900, sharply lowering the entry price.[16]

  7. 2021-03

    Exeed launches the Lanyue seven-seat SUV, lifting the average selling price toward RMB 200,000, and fixes the 'wind, cloud, moon, light' naming system (TX/TXL become Lingyun, VX becomes Lanyue).[11][4]

  8. 2022

    Exeed sells about 51,000 vehicles for the year, still in an early scale-up phase.[11]

  9. 2023-02

    Exeed launches the Yaoguang mid-size SUV.[16]

  10. 2023-04-07

    Exeed launches the 'Sterra' high-end new-energy series at Chery's New Energy Night, debuting the STERRA ES sedan and STERRA ET SUV.[11]

  11. 2023-12

    The Sterra ES, the series' first model and Exeed's first model starting above RMB 200,000, is launched at RMB 225,800-339,800.[1][6]

  12. 2024-05-09

    The Sterra ET mid-to-large SUV is launched with range-extended and battery-electric versions.[1][12]

  13. 2025-07-07

    Chery creates the 'Chery Brand Domestic Business Group' and folds Exeed in as a business division overseen by executive vice president Li Xueyong; Exeed marketing-centre general manager Huang Zhaogen becomes executive deputy general manager of the division.[11]

  14. 2025-11-28

    The Exeed ET5 (new-luxury smart electric mid-size SUV) launches; presales opened in November at RMB 159,900 / 174,900 for two trims, and the launch offered limited-time trade-in prices of RMB 134,900-149,900.[18]

  15. 2025-12-22

    At its Brand Night in Beijing, Exeed enters its '3.0 era', repositioning as a 'global performance-luxury brand'; it unveils the ES and ET series (ES7 GT, ES8, ET8, ET9), technologies including the 2400MPa Panshi body 3.0, Falcon driving assist and Xiniu all-solid-state battery, and launches the 'EXEED Le Mans plan'.[10][14]

  16. 2026-04-19

    The Exeed EX7, the opening model of the brand's 3.0 era, launches globally in Zhuhai at RMB 199,900-263,900, in battery-electric and range-extended versions and first to carry aviation-grade electromechanical braking (EMB).[3]

  17. 2026-08-19

    Exeed is moved from the domestic business group to Chery's international business group, managed by executive vice president and international-business-group general manager Zhang Guibing, reporting to executive vice president Zhang Guozhong; the change is confirmed internally on 19 August 2026.[12][13]

  18. 2026-09-01

    The upgraded 2027 Exeed Lanyue is launched (fuel RMB 189,900-209,900; C-DM RMB 209,900-229,900) on the M3X Mars architecture with the Feiyu chassis and L2 driving assist.[4]

  19. 2026-09-23

    Chery announces Zhang Guozhong moves to Chery Holding Group as executive vice president and Zhang Guibing becomes Chery Automobile's executive vice president, a further step in its 'globalisation 3.0' reorganisation.[15]

Current operations

Model line-up

As of the research cut-off (2026-10-29), the official Exeed site lists the EX7 and ET5, the Sterra ET, the Exeed ES, and the fuel/hybrid line of Lanyue (C-DM), Yaoguang (C-DM), Lingyun and Zhuifeng C-DM, spanning compact to large SUVs and a mid-to-large sedan.[2] The December 2025 Brand Night formalised two product series: an ES series focused on performance (the ES7 GT performance sedan and ES8 shooting-brake) and an ET series focused on luxury (the ET8 and ET9 large SUVs), with three or more new models planned over the following 10-20 months.[10][20] Per the Chery prospectus, the Exeed series covers mid-to-high-end SUVs with fuel and plug-in-hybrid powertrains at roughly RMB 109,900-233,900, while the Sterra series covers sedans and SUVs with range-extended and pure-electric powertrains at roughly RMB 152,800-319,800.[6] The ES7 GT is claimed to hit 0-100 km/h in the 3-second range, the ES8 uses a ONE-BOX shooting-brake design, and the ET9 is a large six-seat SUV with coach doors and a split tailgate.[10]

Technology platforms

Exeed draws on Chery's technology stack. Fuel models such as the Lanyue use the M3X Mars architecture (the 2027 Lanyue C-DM uses a 44.5%-thermal-efficiency 1.5TGDI hybrid engine, a 3-speed DHT and three motors for 175 km CLTC electric and 1,300 km combined range), while new-energy models such as the EX7 use the E0X platform supporting both pure-electric and range-extended powertrains.[4][3] At the Brand Night the brand announced forward-looking technologies: the Panshi body 3.0 with 2400 MPa hot-stamped steel, Falcon driving assist spanning L2+ to L4, the Lingxi cockpit, the Xiniu all-solid-state battery, a Kunpeng Tianqing hybrid engine with 48% thermal efficiency, and the Mars architecture plus Snow Leopard all-wheel drive and Feiyu chassis.[10][14] On batteries, Chery said a solid-liquid hybrid (semi-solid) battery will be carried first by the Exeed EX7 in Q4 2026, and the all-solid-state battery will be carried first by the Exeed ES8, targeting batch installation and road validation from 2027.[21]

Production and capacity

Exeed does not disclose brand-level plant and capacity data; its models are produced within Chery's manufacturing base, and parent-level capacity is covered in the Chery deep research.[5] Chery's 2026 interim announcement shows the group's overseas revenue reached RMB 98.968 billion in H1 2026, about 69% of total revenue, up 51.0% year on year, reflecting the parent's overseas production and sales network on which Exeed's international push depends.[9]

Overseas business

Exeed sells overseas under the EXEED and EXLANTIX names; by December 2025 it covered more than 30 markets, with key regions including Europe, the Middle East and Latin America.[10][15] Media citing third-party data reported that in H1 2025 Exeed ranked first among luxury brands in Russia, with an overseas average selling price above RMB 400,000.[12][13] Parent Chery has ranked first in Chinese-brand vehicle exports for 23 consecutive years, with exports about 70% of group sales in H1 2026.[15] After being moved into Chery's international business group in August 2026, the brand's stated priority shifted to overseas markets, where its pricing power is stronger than at home.[12]

Recent results

Exeed's sales fell 15% to 120,369 units in 2025 and continued to fall in 2026: 41,600 units in January-July (down 39.3%) and 51,966 units in January-September (down 40.9%), with domestic retail of about 10,900 units in January-July.[8][12][17] Because Exeed does not report separately, the most recent published financials are the parent's: Chery Automobile's H1 2026 revenue was RMB 143.280 billion (up 1.2%) with attributable net profit of RMB 8.567 billion and a gross margin of 16.1%, up from 13.0% a year earlier.[9] Chairman Yin Tongyue said Chery 'shipped 150,000 fewer cars' domestically in H1 2026 by proactively cutting dealer inventory, part of the backdrop to Exeed's steep domestic decline.[15]

Founders and leadership

Brand ownership

Exeed is not a separate listed company but a brand of Chery Automobile Co., Ltd. It was first run as an independent brand and, in July 2025, was folded into Chery's newly created 'Chery Brand Domestic Business Group' as the Exeed business division; in August 2026 it was shifted to Chery's international business group.[11][12] Chery Automobile is a mixed-ownership enterprise with no actual controller, listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange on 25 September 2025.[5][6]

Current management line

Since August 2026 Exeed is managed by Chery executive vice president and international-business-group general manager Zhang Guibing, reporting to executive vice president Zhang Guozhong; the change was confirmed internally on 19 August 2026.[12][13] Zhang Guibing joined Chery in July 1999, started as a quality engineer, took over Chery's first overseas CKD project in 2004, and from 2018 led Chery's international business; in December 2024 he became executive vice president and international-business-group general manager, and in September 2026 he became Chery Automobile's executive vice president (常务副总裁).[15] Zhang Guozhong joined Chery in July 1997, became an executive director in March 2024 and executive vice president in December 2024, and moved to Chery Holding Group as executive vice president in September 2026.[6][15] Before that, from July 2025, Exeed was overseen by executive vice president Li Xueyong as head of the domestic business group; at the December 2025 Brand Night Li Xueyong laid out the brand's 3.0-era positioning.[11][10] After the August 2026 shift, Li Xueyong no longer directly oversees Exeed and continues to lead the Chery, Fulwin and QQ brands under the domestic business group.[15]

Marketing leadership and hires

Huang Zhaogen, Chery president assistant and former Exeed marketing-centre general manager, became executive deputy general manager of the Exeed division in July 2025; media later reported that he was transferred out and succeeded as Exeed marketing-centre general manager by Liu Hening, who joined Chery about a year earlier and previously worked on Great Wall Motor's WEY brand marketing. The exact timing of the handover is reported differently across outlets (July, September or end-2025).[11][18][17]

Founder's role

Chery's founder and chairman Yin Tongyue is the key figure behind Exeed. He positioned the brand as the 'tip of the pyramid' and 'Chery's Audi', and has said the brand was built over 10 years with an initial annual sales target of 60,000 and a goal of 100,000.[11][12] In 2019 he said the brand's launch and listing were preparation for Chery's entry into mature European and North American markets.[12] At the December 2025 Brand Night he said brand-building should rest on product strength, culture and user relationships rather than feature-stacking.[10] Media noted that Exeed's leadership has changed repeatedly since 2018, with further adjustments in 2025 and 2026. The above reflects only public roles and business history.[11][12]

Competition

Our judgment: the competitive field. With its SUV-and-sedan line-up spanning roughly RMB 110,000-400,000, Exeed competes against other Chinese brands that moved upmarket in the same period, notably Lynk & Co (Geely) and WEY (Great Wall Motor), and in new energy against AITO (Seres/Huawei), Li Auto and Zeekr, as well as the incumbent joint-venture SUVs it targets such as the Audi Q5L and BMW X3.[13][12] This is an analytical selection of the competitive set, not a sales ranking.

Our judgment: strengths. Exeed's clearest asset is Chery's technology base and export infrastructure: it reuses the M3X and E0X platforms and, in 2026, was moved into Chery's international business group precisely to draw on the parent's overseas channels.[4][12] Its overseas results are concrete: media cited it as first among luxury brands in Russia in H1 2025, with an overseas average selling price above RMB 400,000.[12] On technology, it announced 2400 MPa body steel, L2+-L4 driving assist and a solid-state-battery roadmap that places the EX7 and ES8 at the front of Chery's rollout.[10][21] Chery has said it will give its best technology and most advanced intelligence to Exeed first.[3]

Our judgment: weaknesses. Sales remain small and are now falling: 120,369 units in 2025 (down 15%) and 51,966 in January-September 2026 (down 40.9%), while domestic retail dropped from about 90,000 in 2024 to about 53,000 in 2025 and about 10,900 in January-July 2026.[8][17][13] Media and analysts criticise the brand's repeated repositioning and scattered product line, and its pricing has moved downward; analyst Mei Songlin told Jiemian that Exeed, as Chery's self-owned premium brand, has been in a state of trial and exploration.[12][13] The Huawei-partnered Luxeed sibling brand has taken over the group's premium new-energy positioning, which media see as squeezing Exeed's space.[12] By contrast, the Chery-Huawei Luxeed brand sold 90,493 units in 2025, up 56%.[8]

Outlook

Company plans. Exeed's stated direction is the '3.0 era' positioning as a 'global performance-luxury brand', anchored on base performance, value experience and emotional expression, with an ES performance series and an ET luxury series and three or more new models over the next 10-20 months.[10][20] It also announced the 'EXEED Le Mans plan' to become, in its words, the first Chinese brand to compete in the world's top three racing series as a factory team, with a three-stage route from a domestic one-make series to the Asian Le Mans Series and then the Le Mans 24 Hours.[10] On batteries, Chery said the solid-liquid hybrid battery will be carried first by the Exeed EX7 in Q4 2026 and the all-solid-state battery by the Exeed ES8, targeting batch installation and road validation from 2027.[21] These are disclosed plans and targets, not completed results.

Our judgment. The move of Exeed into Chery's international business group in August 2026 signals that its growth focus is shifting overseas, where its pricing power is far stronger than at home.[12][13] Whether the brand stabilises depends on at least three conditions: whether the EX7, ET5 and the new ES/ET models can rebuild domestic volume, whether its high-priced overseas business can keep growing amid trade and currency risks, and whether the solid-state-battery rollout materialises on schedule to support the premium positioning. These conditions are not guaranteed, and none of this is a forecast of financial results or share price.[21][13]

Risks

  • Declining sales. Exeed's 2025 sales fell 15% to 120,369 units and its January-September 2026 sales fell 40.9% to 51,966 units, with domestic retail dropping to about 10,900 units in January-July 2026.[8][17][13]
  • Unstable brand positioning. The brand has repeatedly adjusted its positioning and naming (the Sterra series name has been phased out) and its pricing has moved downward, which media and analysts see as blurring its premium image.[12][13]
  • Management turnover. Exeed's leadership has changed repeatedly since 2018, most recently in 2025 and 2026, adding execution risk.[11][12]
  • No separate financial disclosure. Exeed publishes no brand-level revenue or profit, so standalone profitability cannot be assessed from public filings.[5]
  • Overseas and trade risks. The brand's growth is increasingly reliant on overseas markets; geopolitical tensions, exchange-rate volatility and stricter regulation (notably in Europe, where it sells as EXLANTIX) could affect this.[13]
  • Intra-group competition. The Chery-Huawei Luxeed brand overlaps with Exeed in premium new energy, and Chery has called the Huawei partnership a top-priority strategy, which media see as squeezing Exeed's resources and positioning.[12]
  • Domestic price war and demand pressure. Amid an intense domestic price war, chairman Yin Tongyue said Chery proactively cut domestic wholesale in H1 2026 to reduce dealer inventory, weighing on Exeed's domestic sales.[15]

概览

星途（Exeed）是奇瑞汽车股份有限公司（港交所：9973.HK）旗下高端品牌，总部位于安徽芜湖。它于 2017 年法兰克福车展以「EXEED」高端产品序列首次亮相，2018 年升级为独立品牌，是奇瑞继瑞麒、观致之后第三次冲击高端市场。[1][11] 奇瑞董事长尹同跃将星途定位为奇瑞集团的「金字塔尖」，类比大众的奥迪、丰田的雷克萨斯，承担技术输出、品牌向上与全球化三重任务。[11][12] 2025 年 12 月 22 日的「星途品牌之夜」上，品牌进入「3.0 时代」，重新定位为「全球性能豪华品牌」。[10] 星途官方将品牌定位概括为「全球性能豪华创新者」，以性能为核、豪华为底、创新为先。[1]

星途以 SUV 与轿车为主，产品分两大序列：星途序列（追风、凌云、瑶光、揽月等燃油与插混 SUV）与星纪元新能源序列（ES 轿车、ET SUV），2026 年起又加入 EX7 跨界车与 ET5。星途与奇瑞、捷途、iCAR、智界并列为奇瑞汽车旗下五大乘用车品牌。2025 年 12 月品牌之夜后，「星纪元」序列名称在官方口径中逐渐淡出，官网现以「星途 ES」列示该轿车，与 EX7、ET5 及星纪元 ET 并列。[2][3] 销量从 2019 年不足 2 万辆升至 2024 年 140,959 辆（同比 +12.3%）的高点，2025 年同比下滑 15% 至 120,369 辆，是奇瑞汽车 2025 年唯一下滑的子品牌。[8][19] 品牌海外已有规模，覆盖 30 余个市场，海外车型平均售价超 40 万元。[10][13]

由于星途是品牌而非单独上市公司，不披露独立财务报表，母公司数据见奇瑞深度研究——母公司奇瑞汽车 2025 年营收 3002.87 亿元、归母净利润 190.19 亿元。[5] 2026 年，随着国内销量收缩，品牌被划归奇瑞国际业务事业群，增长重心转向海外。[12]

历史沿革

  1. 2017-09

    奇瑞在法兰克福车展发布高端产品序列「EXEED」及首款概念车 EXEED TX，是继瑞麒、观致之后奇瑞第三次冲击高端市场。[1][11]

  2. 2018

    EXEED 由产品序列升级为独立品牌并命名为「星途」。奇瑞董事长尹同跃称该品牌历时 10 年打造，上市后年销量目标 6 万辆、力争 10 万辆。[11]

  3. 2019-01

    首款车型星途 TX 下线。[1]

  4. 2019-04

    首款量产车型 TX/TXL 中型 SUV 上市，售价在 15 万元左右（有媒体记载为 12.59 万—17.59 万元）；当年销量不足 2 万辆。[11][16]

  5. 2019-10

    紧凑型 SUV 星途 LX 上市。[1]

  6. 2020

    星途推出限时售价 9.99 万元的 LX 1.5T 版本，大幅拉低价格门槛。[16]

  7. 2021-03

    星途推出七座 SUV 揽月，平均售价上探至近 20 万元；同年确立「风、云、月、光」命名体系，将 TX/TXL 改名凌云、VX 改名揽月。[11][4]

  8. 2022

    星途全年销量约 5.1 万辆，仍处于规模爬坡期。[11]

  9. 2023-02

    星途推出中型 SUV 瑶光。[16]

  10. 2023-04-07

    星途在「奇瑞新能源之夜」发布高端新能源序列「星纪元」（STERRA），首发 STERRA ES 轿车与 STERRA ET SUV。[11]

  11. 2023-12

    星纪元序列首款车型、也是星途首款起售价突破 20 万元的车型星纪元 ES 上市，售价 22.58 万—33.98 万元。[1][6]

  12. 2024-05-09

    中大型 SUV 星纪元 ET 上市，提供增程版与纯电版。[1][12]

  13. 2025-07-07

    奇瑞成立「奇瑞品牌国内业务事业群」，星途并入为星途事业部，由执行副总裁李学用统管；星途营销中心总经理黄招根改任星途事业部常务副总经理。[11]

  14. 2025-11-28

    星途 ET5（新奢智电中型 SUV）上市；11 月以 15.99 万元、17.49 万元两个版型开启预售，上市推出限时超级置换价 13.49 万—14.99 万元。[18]

  15. 2025-12-22

    星途在北京举行「星途品牌之夜」，进入「3.0 时代」，重新定位为「全球性能豪华品牌」；发布 ES、ET 两大产品系列（ES7 GT、ES8、ET8、ET9），公布 2400MPa 磐石车身 3.0、猎鹰辅助驾驶、犀牛全固态电池等技术，并启动「EXEED 勒芒计划」。[10][14]

  16. 2026-04-19

    星途 3.0 时代开山之作 EX7 在珠海全球上市，官方指导价 19.99 万—26.39 万元，提供纯电与增程双动力，全球首搭航空级 EMB 线控制动。[3]

  17. 2026-08-19

    星途由国内业务事业群划归奇瑞国际业务事业群，由执行副总裁兼国际业务事业群总经理张贵兵分管、向常务副总裁张国忠汇报；2026 年 8 月 19 日奇瑞内部确认该调整。[12][13]

  18. 2026-09-01

  19. 2026-09-23

    奇瑞宣布张国忠调任奇瑞控股集团常务副总裁，张贵兵接任奇瑞汽车常务副总裁，是其「全球化 3.0」组织调整的进一步动作。[15]

现状

车型谱系

截至研究截止日（2026-10-29），星途官网列示 EX7、ET5、星纪元 ET、星途 ES，以及燃油/混动序列中的揽月（C-DM）、瑶光（C-DM）、凌云、追风 C-DM，覆盖紧凑型到中大型 SUV 与中大型轿车。[2] 2025 年 12 月品牌之夜正式确立两大产品系列：以性能为核心的 ES 系列（性能轿车 ES7 GT、猎装车 ES8）与以豪华为核心的 ET 系列（大型 SUV ET8、ET9），并计划未来 10-20 个月投放 3 款以上新车。[10][20] 据奇瑞招股书，星途序列主打中高端 SUV、覆盖燃油与插混，售价约 10.99 万—23.39 万元；星纪元序列主打轿车与 SUV、覆盖增程与纯电，售价约 15.28 万—31.98 万元。[6] 其中 ES7 GT 官方称零百加速进入 3 秒级，ES8 采用 ONE-BOX 星际新猎装设计，ET9 定位大六座 SUV、采用对开门与天地尾门设计。[10]

技术平台

星途复用奇瑞技术栈。燃油车型如揽月基于 M3X 火星架构（其 2027 款 C-DM 版搭载热效率 44.5% 的 1.5TGDI 混动专用发动机、3 挡超能混动 DHT 与三电机，CLTC 纯电续航 175km、综合续航 1300km），新能源车型如 EX7 基于可同时支持纯电与增程的 E0X 平台。[4][3] 品牌之夜公布了多项前瞻技术：2400MPa 热成形钢打造的磐石车身 3.0、覆盖 L2+ 至 L4 的猎鹰辅助驾驶、灵犀座舱、犀牛全固态电池、热效率 48% 的鲲鹏天擎混动发动机，以及火星架构＋雪豹四驱＋飞鱼底盘。[10][14] 固态电池方面，奇瑞表示固液混合（半固态）电池将于 2026 年第四季度由星途 EX7 率先搭载，全固态电池由星途 ES8 首搭、力争 2027 年启动批量装车与道路验证。[21]

生产与产能

星途未单独披露品牌层面的工厂与产能信息，其车型在奇瑞体系的生产基地生产，母公司产能与制造布局见奇瑞深度研究。[5] 奇瑞 2026 年中期业绩公告显示，集团上半年海外市场收入 989.68 亿元、占总收入约 69%、同比增长 51.0%，反映出星途国际化所依托的母公司海外产销网络。[9]

海外业务

星途以 EXEED、EXLANTIX 之名出海，截至 2025 年 12 月已覆盖 30 余个市场，海外重点市场包括欧洲、中东、拉美等区域。[10][15] 媒体引用第三方数据称，2025 年上半年星途位居俄罗斯豪华品牌销量第一，海外车型平均售价超 40 万元。[12][13] 母公司奇瑞已连续 23 年位居中国品牌整车出口第一，2026 年上半年出口销量约占集团总销量的 70%。[15] 2026 年 8 月划归奇瑞国际业务事业群后，品牌重心转向定价能力更强的海外市场。[12]

现状（最近业绩）

星途 2025 年销量同比降 15% 至 120,369 辆，2026 年继续下滑：1-7 月 4.16 万辆（同比 -39.3%）、1-9 月 51,966 辆（同比 -40.9%），9 月单月销量 4,905 辆、同比下降 54.6%；其中 1-7 月国内零售仅约 1.09 万辆。[8][12][17] 由于星途不单独披露财务，最近一期财务为母公司口径：奇瑞汽车 2026 年上半年营收 1432.80 亿元（同比 +1.2%）、归母净利润 85.67 亿元，毛利率 16.1%（上年同期 13.0%）。[9] 奇瑞董事长尹同跃表示，2026 年上半年奇瑞在国内「少发了 15 万台车」，主动降低经销商库存、挤掉渠道水分，这也是星途国内销量大幅收缩的背景之一。[15]

创始人与管理层

品牌归属

星途并非单独上市公司，而是奇瑞汽车股份有限公司旗下品牌。它最初独立运营，2025 年 7 月被并入奇瑞新设的「奇瑞品牌国内业务事业群」成为星途事业部，2026 年 8 月又划归奇瑞国际业务事业群。[11][12] 母公司奇瑞汽车为混合所有制企业、无实际控制人，于 2025 年 9 月 25 日在港交所上市。[5][6]

现任管理层

自 2026 年 8 月起，星途由奇瑞汽车执行副总裁兼国际业务事业群总经理张贵兵分管，向常务副总裁张国忠汇报；2026 年 8 月 19 日奇瑞内部确认该调整。[12][13] 张贵兵 1999 年 7 月加入奇瑞，从质量工程师做起，2004 年接手奇瑞首个海外 CKD 项目并担任项目总经理，2018 年起全面执掌奇瑞国际业务；2024 年 12 月升任执行副总裁兼国际业务事业群总经理，2026 年 9 月接任奇瑞汽车常务副总裁。[15] 张国忠 1997 年 7 月进入奇瑞，2024 年 3 月出任奇瑞汽车执行董事、同年 12 月起任奇瑞汽车常务副总裁，2026 年 9 月调任奇瑞控股集团常务副总裁。[6][15] 此前自 2025 年 7 月起，星途由执行副总裁李学用以国内业务事业群总经理身份统管；2025 年 12 月品牌之夜上，李学用阐述了品牌 3.0 时代定位。[11][10] 2026 年 8 月调整后，李学用不再直接负责星途，继续统筹奇瑞品牌国内事业群下的奇瑞、风云、QQ 等品牌。[15]

营销负责人

奇瑞总裁助理、原星途营销中心总经理黄招根于 2025 年 7 月改任星途事业部常务副总经理；此后媒体报道其调离，由加入奇瑞约一年、此前负责长城魏牌营销的刘赫宁接任星途营销中心总经理。媒体对交接时间的记载不一（7 月、9 月或 2025 年底）。[11][18][17]

创始人的角色

奇瑞创始人、董事长尹同跃是星途背后的关键人物。他将星途定位为奇瑞集团的「金字塔尖」「奇瑞的奥迪」，并称该品牌历时 10 年打造、上市后年销量目标 6 万辆、力争 10 万辆。[11][12] 2019 年他曾表示，星途的发布与上市，是在为奇瑞进入欧美成熟市场做准备。[12] 2025 年 12 月品牌之夜上，他表示品牌建设应靠产品力、文化与用户关系，而非配置堆砌。[10] 媒体注意到，星途自 2018 年以来负责人多次更替，2025 年、2026 年管理层又接连调整。以上均为公开职务与商业履历。[11][12]

财务数据

星途为奇瑞汽车股份有限公司（港交所：9973.HK）旗下品牌，不单独披露品牌层面财务报表，故本表不列营收、毛利率、净利润、研发投入、现金流等品牌级财务数字（rows 为空）。母公司奇瑞汽车合并财务报表按《香港财务报告准则》（HKFRS）编制、以人民币列示（单位：百万元），净利润按「本公司拥有人应占利润」（即归属于母公司股东）口径披露：2025 年全年营业收入 3002.87 亿元、归母净利润 190.19 亿元；2026 年上半年营业收入 1432.80 亿元、归母净利润 85.67 亿元（未经审计）。母公司完整财务数据见奇瑞（chery）深度研究。本页不构成任何交易推荐。

竞争格局

我们的判断：竞争范围。按星途覆盖约 11 万—40 万元的 SUV 与轿车产品线，其对手包括同期冲高的自主品牌——吉利领克、长城魏牌，以及在新能源领域与问界（赛力斯/华为）、理想、极氪等竞争，同时对标奥迪 Q5L、宝马 X3 等在位合资 SUV。[13][12] 此为分析所选的竞争范围，不是销量排名。

我们的判断：优势。星途最突出的资产是奇瑞的技术基座与出口基础设施：它复用 M3X、E0X 平台，2026 年划归奇瑞国际业务事业群，正是为了借助母公司海外渠道。[4][12] 其海外成绩是实打实的：媒体引用数据称 2025 年上半年位居俄罗斯豪华品牌销量第一，海外车型平均售价超 40 万元。[12] 技术上，它公布了 2400MPa 车身钢、L2+ 至 L4 辅助驾驶，并将 EX7、ES8 置于奇瑞固态电池落地的首位。[10][21] 奇瑞官方称，将把最好的技术、最前沿的智能优先赋予星途。[3]

我们的判断：短板。销量规模偏小且正在下滑：2025 年 120,369 辆（同比降 15%），2026 年 1-9 月 51,966 辆（同比降 40.9%），国内零售则从 2024 年约 9 万辆降至 2025 年约 5.3 万辆，再跌至 2026 年 1-7 月约 1.09 万辆。[8][17][13] 媒体与分析师批评其定位反复、产品线分散，且定价不断下探；分析师梅松林对界面新闻表示，星途作为奇瑞自主高端品牌，一直处于尝试和探索阶段。[12][13] 与华为合作的兄弟品牌智界承接了集团高端新能源的定位，媒体认为这挤压了星途的空间。[12] 对比之下，兄弟品牌智界（奇瑞与华为合作）2025 年销量 90,493 辆、同比增长 56%。[8]

未来展望

公司公开规划。星途公开的方向是「3.0 时代」的「全球性能豪华品牌」定位，围绕基础性能、价值体验、情绪表达三大维度展开，设 ES 性能序列与 ET 豪华序列，并计划未来 10-20 个月投放 3 款以上新车。[10][20] 品牌并启动「EXEED 勒芒计划」，目标是成为首个以厂家车队身份参加世界三大顶级汽车赛事体系的中国品牌，路径分三阶段：本土统规赛、亚洲勒芒系列赛（ALMS）、勒芒 24 小时耐力赛正赛。[10] 电池方面，奇瑞表示固液混合电池将于 2026 年第四季度由星途 EX7 率先搭载，全固态电池由星途 ES8 首搭、力争 2027 年启动批量装车与道路验证。[21] 以上为披露的计划与目标，不是已实现的业绩。

我们的判断。2026 年 8 月星途划归奇瑞国际业务事业群，表明其增长重心转向海外——那里的定价能力远强于国内。[12][13] 品牌能否企稳，取决于至少三个条件：EX7、ET5 及新 ES/ET 车型能否重建国内销量，高溢价的海外业务能否在贸易与汇率风险下继续增长，以及固态电池路线能否按计划落地以支撑高端定位。这些条件并非必然成立；以上不构成对财务结果或股价的任何预测。[21][13]

风险

  • 销量下滑。星途 2025 年销量同比降 15% 至 120,369 辆，2026 年 1-9 月同比降 40.9% 至 51,966 辆，同期（1-7 月）国内零售仅约 1.09 万辆。[8][17][13]
  • 品牌定位不稳。品牌多次调整定位与命名（星纪元序列名称已淡出），车型定价不断下探，媒体与分析师认为这模糊了高端形象。[12][13]
  • 管理层频繁更替。星途自 2018 年以来负责人多次更换，最近一次发生在 2025 年与 2026 年，带来执行风险。[11][12]
  • 无单独财务披露。星途不披露品牌层面营收与利润，无法从公开文件评估品牌独立盈利能力。[5]
  • 海外与贸易风险。品牌增长越来越依赖海外市场，地缘政治、汇率波动与更严格的监管（尤其在欧洲，其以 EXLANTIX 名义销售）可能影响其扩张。[13]
  • 集团内部竞争。奇瑞与华为合作的智界与星途在高端新能源领域存在重叠，奇瑞亦将华为合作置于优先地位，媒体认为这挤压了星途的资源与定位。[12]
  • 国内价格战与需求压力。国内车市价格战持续内卷，奇瑞董事长尹同跃称 2026 年上半年公司在国内主动收缩批发以去库存，星途国内销量因此明显承压。[15]
