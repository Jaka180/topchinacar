Overview

Changan Qiyuan (CHANGAN NEVO) is the digital-intelligent new-energy brand series under the Changan brand of Chongqing Changan Automobile Co., Ltd. (SZSE 000625), a state-controlled automaker. It was launched on 27 August 2023 in Chongqing, positioned as "digital-intelligent evolution new cars" for mainstream family buyers, and is built on Changan's SDA software-defined architecture ([1], [25]). Together with Deepal and Avatr it forms Changan's three-brand new-energy matrix, sitting between Deepal (youth tech-sport) and Avatr (premium) and anchored in the RMB 80,000-250,000 mainstream family market ([2], [6]). In Europe, Changan positions Qiyuan as an "intelligent, comfortable family mobility" brand, Deepal as "young, tech, sporty" and Avatr as "original design, intelligent new luxury" ([12]).

Qiyuan is the volume engine of Changan's own-brand electrification. It sold 146,349 vehicles in 2024 and 411,000 in 2025, and passed one million cumulative sales in September 2026, roughly 37 months after launch, with 46,507 delivered that month ([7], [9], [11]). Its 411,000 units in 2025 made it the largest of Changan's three NEV brands that year, ahead of Deepal (333,000) and Avatr (over 120,000) ([16]). In H1 2026 Qiyuan delivered 173,822 units (AQ-series up 102.6% YoY), and its Q05 sold over 20,000 a month with more than 80,000 units in the half ([22], [10]). Unlike Deepal and Avatr, which are separate companies, Qiyuan is a brand series within the Changan brand and is led by a product CEO; it does not disclose standalone financials ([8]).

The brand's product plan spans three series — A (sedans), Q (SUVs) and E0 (digital new cars) — and at launch Changan set a 2030 target of 1.5 million units for Qiyuan, with 10 new models planned globally by 2025 ([1], [25]). Qiyuan sits within the "third venture" transformation Changan began in 2017 and is a carrier of its Shangri-La (electrification), Beidou Tianshu (intelligence) and Haina Baichuan (globalisation) programmes ([18]). In October 2026 the brand made its European debut as CHANGAN NEVO, with the Q05 scheduled to go on sale in Europe in early 2027 ([12]).

Current operations

Model line-up

As of the research cut-off (2026-10-29), Qiyuan sells seven models across sedans and SUVs: the A05 compact sedan, A06 mid-large liftback coupe and A07 mid-large sedan (A-series), the Q05 compact SUV, Q06 mid-size SUV and Q07 mid-size SUV (Q-series), and the E07 variable-form mid-large SUV (E0-series) ([4], [5]). Prices span roughly RMB 79,900 (all-new Q05) to 319,900 (E07). The volume leader is the all-new pure-electric Q05 (launched November 2025), described by media as the only lidar-equipped BEV SUV under RMB 100,000; per executive VP Yang Dayong it passed 76,000 units in H1 2026 (May 19,350, June 23,523) ([16], [21]). The Q07, launched in April 2025, reached cumulative sales above 90,000 within about eight months ([16]). The brand targets the RMB 80,000-250,000 mainstream family market, with most volume below RMB 200,000 ([15]).

Technology and platforms

Qiyuan is built on Changan's SDA software-defined architecture; its first production model, the E07, offers SUV, sedan, coupe, expansion and off-road forms with pluggable hardware and definable software, with more than 3,500 configurations ([5]). The E07 carries two Hesai AT128 lidars among 30 smart-driving sensors, per Hesai's official release ([24]). The powertrain stack centres on the Blue Whale hybrid family — the A05 and first-generation Q05 use a 1.5L hybrid, and the Q07 uses the smart Blue Whale 3.0 system with 1.5L (72 kW) and 1.5T (110 kW) options and a 165 kW drive motor, offering 145 km / 215 km of CLTC pure-electric range ([13]). The E07's BEV version uses an 800V silicon-carbide "AI platinum" platform (0-100 km/h in 3.96 s, over 650 km range); its EREV version uses the Tianyu smart range extender (up to 245 km pure-electric, 1,190 km combined) ([5]). The newer models (Q06, and the 630 km A06 BEV) use an 800V silicon-carbide platform with 6C fast charging ([27], [23]). The in-house Tianshu (Tianshu Pilot) system spans Pro, Max, Ultra and Ultimate tiers; the Q06 is the first production model to carry it, with the whole range fitted with lidar and the Ultra tier (560 TOPS, 256-line lidar) offered as an option ([27], [10]). At the Paris show, Changan said Tianshu Pilot uses a one-stage end-to-end architecture and multimodal large models, while the Tianshu chassis uses distributed electric drive that keeps the body stable through a blowout at 225 km/h ([12]).

Manufacturing and capacity

Qiyuan has no standalone plants; production runs on Changan's group capacity, including the Changan digital factory opened on 21 October 2024 alongside the E07 ([7]). In July 2026, executive VP Yang Dayong said the Nanchang plant had formally started production, easing the capacity bottleneck that constrained H1 output ([21]). At group level, Changan has 22 manufacturing bases and 93 plants worldwide, and the new China Changan Automobile Group (established July 2025) has 143 controlled subsidiaries, 145,000 employees, RMB 308.7 billion in assets and nine overseas plants with 260,000 units of usable capacity ([12], [16]).

Domestic position and overseas business

Within Changan, Qiyuan plays the role of the new-energy "scale base", concentrated in the RMB 100,000-200,000 band and sold through Changan's existing dealer network rather than a separate sales system ([15]). In September 2026 Changan delivered 228,900 vehicles overall, of which 113,200 were new-energy and 96,200 were overseas (+73.8% YoY); Qiyuan contributed 46,507 that month ([11]). Qiyuan made its European debut at the 2026 Paris show and is part of Changan's European push, which has invested EUR 1.18 billion, set up eight subsidiaries and 800+ employees, and built a network of more than 350 sales-service outlets across 15 countries; Changan plans 180,000 units of European capacity by 2028 and 350,000 by 2030 ([12]).

Founders and leadership

Structure and governance

Qiyuan is a brand series under the Changan brand, not a separate legal entity, so it has no independent board, chairman or listed-company executives; its operations are consolidated into Changan (SZSE 000625), a state-controlled automaker ([6]). The brand is run by a product CEO within Changan's brand organisation, and no separate actual controller is identified for Qiyuan beyond Changan's own control chain ([8]). Changan itself sits under the China Changan Automobile Group, the central state-owned group established in July 2025 ([16]).

Key management

  • Di Zhirui — Changan Qiyuan product CEO (general-manager level). Born 1981, a Jiangsu Liyang native, he is an engineer with a bachelor's degree in engineering and joined Changan in July 2003. He held roles as deputy general manager, executive deputy general manager and general manager (also party branch secretary) of Changan's product-planning department, Deepal product marketing director, general manager (also party committee secretary) of the Auchan business division, general manager of the OX project department, executive deputy general manager (general-manager level), party committee secretary and company spokesperson of the Changan brand business division, Qiyuan marketing general manager, and Changan Qiyuan product CEO (general-manager level). On 15 June 2026 Changan's ninth board at its 59th meeting appointed him a company vice president for a term expiring with the ninth board ([8], [9]).

Media read the June 2026 appointment — alongside that of Avatr's Chen Zhuo — as embedding the heads of the two NEV brands directly into Changan's group decision-making tier ([9]).

Competition

Our judgment: competitive scope. In Qiyuan's core RMB 80,000-250,000 mainstream NEV band, its main rivals include BYD, Geely Galaxy, Leapmotor and Wuling's NEV line, as well as Changan's own Deepal brand, which overlaps Qiyuan's price band. This is an analytical selection, not a sales ranking ([12], [2]).

Our judgment: strengths. Qiyuan's clearest advantages are price-to-value ("value beyond class, supply from a higher tier"), Changan's state-backed supply chain and dense dealership network, and rapid product iteration — it reached one million cumulative sales in about 37 months ([6], [11]). State media report that the globally-developed Q05 topped China's compact-SUV segment for six consecutive months, indicating category strength; however, a July 2026 report put June Q05 sales of 23,523 units at fourth in the compact-SUV segment, so the two claims use different windows or rankings and we present both ([12], [21]). In 2025 Changan's NEV sales exceeded 1.109 million units (+51%), of which Qiyuan contributed 411,000, or roughly 37% (calculated from disclosed figures), the largest of the three brands ([16]).

Our judgment: product mix. Qiyuan runs a dual track of low-price volume (the all-new Q05) and an upward push (the Q07 and Q06): the Q07 has held monthly sales around the ten-thousand level with cumulative sales above 90,000, while the Q06 launched in September 2026 with the Tianshu Pilot system and drew over 35,000 cumulative orders within 24 hours, becoming a new volume driver ([16], [11]).

Our judgment: weaknesses. Volume is concentrated in the low-priced Q05, and the brand missed its 2024 target — the 250,000-unit partners-conference goal versus 146,349 actual deliveries ([20], [7]). Its positioning also overlaps Deepal, so Changan must manage internal segmentation carefully ([9]).

Outlook

Company plans. At launch, Changan set a 2030 target of 1.5 million units for Qiyuan and said it would launch 10 new models globally by 2025; these are disclosed targets, not completed results ([1]). At the January 2024 partners conference, Changan described Qiyuan as the future cornerstone of the company and named the SDA-based E07 for an October 2024 mass production ([20]). In July 2026, executive VP Yang Dayong said the H2 2026 target was 300,000 units with a full-year aim of about 400,000 ([21]). In Europe, Qiyuan debuted at the 2026 Paris show with the Q05 scheduled to go on sale in early 2027, on top of Changan's broader European plan for 180,000 units of capacity by 2028 and 350,000 by 2030 ([12]).

Our judgment. With one million cumulative sales, a first quarterly profit reported for Q2 2026 and a European launch path, Qiyuan has moved from volume accumulation toward profit and overseas expansion. The key tests are whether it can sustain full-year profitability, lift average selling price beyond the low-priced Q05, and differentiate from Deepal. The 2030 target of 1.5 million units is far above the roughly 400,000-unit full-year run-rate Changan itself set for 2026, so reaching it depends on continued domestic share gains plus a successful European ramp. This is our analysis, not a forecast of financial results ([10], [21], [12]).

Risks

  • Sales concentration. Volume relies on the low-priced all-new Q05; a slowdown in the compact-SUV segment would weigh on overall sales ([12]).
  • Price-band overlap. Qiyuan overlaps Deepal within Changan's own brand portfolio, requiring careful segmentation ([9]).
  • Profitability is recent. Qiyuan's first quarterly profit was reported only for Q2 2026, by media; sustained full-year profitability is not yet established ([10]).
  • Missed targets. The brand fell short of its 250,000-unit 2024 goal, indicating execution and planning risk ([20], [7]).
  • Overseas execution. The European entry is at an early stage, with the Q05 not due on sale until early 2027, exposing it to tariffs, homologation and brand-building costs ([12]).
  • Dependence on the parent. As a brand series without standalone financials or capacity, Qiyuan's funding, production and sales network depend on Changan, so group-level decisions and resource allocation directly shape its trajectory ([8]).

概览

长安启源（CHANGAN NEVO）是重庆长安汽车股份有限公司（深交所 000625）旗下长安品牌的数智新能源序列品牌，长安汽车为国有控股车企。品牌于 2023 年 8 月 27 日在重庆发布，定位「数智进化新汽车」，面向主流家庭用户，并以长安 SDA 软件定义架构为技术底座（[1]、[25]）。它与深蓝、阿维塔构成长安汽车三大新能源品牌矩阵，介于深蓝（年轻科技运动）与阿维塔（高端）之间，主打 8 万-25 万元主流家庭市场（[2]、[6]）。在欧洲市场，长安将启源定位为「智能舒享家用出行」、深蓝为「年轻科技运动」、阿维塔为「原创设计、智能新豪华」，三者形成差异化区隔（[12]）。

启源是长安自主新能源的规模基盘：2024 年销量 146,349 辆，2025 年达 41.1 万辆，并于 2026 年 9 月累计突破 100 万辆、当月交付 46,507 辆，距品牌发布约 37 个月（[7]、[9]、[11]）。2025 年启源 41.1 万辆的销量在长安三大新能源品牌中居首（同期深蓝 33.3 万辆、阿维塔突破 12 万辆）（[16]）。2026 年上半年启源交付 173,822 辆（AQ 系列同比 +102.6%），其中 Q05 月销超 2 万辆、上半年累计超 8 万辆（[22]、[10]）。与独立运营公司的深蓝、阿维塔不同，启源是长安品牌下的序列品牌，由产品 CEO 负责，不单独披露财务数据（[8]）。

品牌规划 A（轿车）、Q（SUV）、E0（数智新汽车）三大产品序列，发布之初长安汽车即提出启源 2030 年 150 万辆目标，并计划到 2025 年面向全球推出 10 款新车（[1]、[25]）。启源处于长安汽车 2017 年开启的「第三次创业」转型之中，是「香格里拉」电动化、「北斗天枢」智能化、「海纳百川」全球化三大计划的落地载体之一（[18]）。2026 年 10 月，启源以 CHANGAN NEVO 之名完成欧洲首秀，Q05 计划 2027 年初在欧洲上市（[12]）。

现状

车型谱系

截至研究截止日（2026-10-29），启源在售 7 款车型，覆盖轿车与 SUV：A 系列的 A05 紧凑型轿车、A06 中大型掀背轿跑、A07 中大型轿车，Q 系列的 Q05 紧凑型 SUV、Q06 中型 SUV、Q07 中型 SUV，以及 E0 系列的 E07 可变形中大型 SUV（[4]、[5]）。价格带约覆盖 7.99 万元（全新 Q05）至 31.99 万元（E07）。销量主力为 2025 年 11 月上市的全新纯电 Q05，媒体称其为 10 万元以内唯一搭载激光雷达的纯电 SUV；据执行副总裁杨大勇，其 2026 上半年累计超 7.6 万辆（5 月 19,350 辆、6 月 23,523 辆）（[16]、[21]）。2025 年 4 月上市的 Q07 上市后约 8 个月累计销量突破 9 万辆（[16]）。品牌主要面向 8 万-25 万元主流家庭市场，销量主力集中在 20 万元以下（[15]）。

技术与平台

启源基于长安 SDA 软件定义架构打造，其首款量产车型 E07 提供 SUV、轿车、轿跑、拓展、越野五种形态，硬件可插拔、软件可定义，拥有 3,500 多种配置（[5]）。据禾赛官方，E07 搭载包括 2 颗禾赛 AT128 激光雷达在内的 30 个智驾传感器（[24]）。动力以蓝鲸混动家族为核心——A05 与首代 Q05 采用 1.5L 混动，Q07 采用智慧新蓝鲸 3.0，提供 1.5L（72kW）与 1.5T（110kW）两种选择、驱动电机峰值功率 165kW，CLTC 纯电续航 145km/215km（[13]）。E07 纯电版采用 800V 碳化硅「AI 铂金」平台（百公里加速 3.96 秒、续航超 650km），增程版搭载天驭智慧增程（纯电最高 245km、综合最高 1190km）（[5]）。较新车型（Q06 及 630km 版 A06 纯电）采用 800V 碳化硅平台与 6C 快充（[27]、[23]）。自研天枢（天枢领航）智驾分为 Pro、Max、Ultra、Ultimate 四档，Q06 为启源首款搭载该系统的量产车型，全系配备激光雷达，Ultra（560TOPS、256 线激光雷达）为选装（[27]、[10]）。据长安在巴黎车展介绍，天枢领航采用一段式端到端架构与多模态大模型，天枢底盘采用分布式电驱、可在时速 225 公里爆胎时保持车身平稳（[12]）。

制造与产能

启源无独立工厂，依托长安集团产能生产，包括 2024 年 10 月 21 日与 E07 同日启用的长安数智工厂（[7]）。2026 年 7 月，执行副总裁杨大勇称南昌工厂已正式投产，缓解了制约上半年产量的产能瓶颈（[21]）。集团层面，长安在全球布局 22 个制造基地、93 座工厂；2025 年 7 月挂牌成立的中国长安汽车集团拥有 143 家控股子公司、从业 14.5 万人、资产总额 3,087 亿元，并建成 9 家海外工厂、可利用产能 26 万辆（[12]、[16]）。

国内地位与海外业务

在长安体系内，启源承担新能源「规模基盘」角色，产品集中于 10 万-20 万元区间，依托长安现有经销商网络销售，而非另建独立销售体系（[15]）。2026 年 9 月长安汽车整车交付 22.89 万辆，其中新能源 11.32 万辆、海外 9.62 万辆（同比 +73.8%），启源当月贡献 46,507 辆（[11]）。启源于 2026 巴黎车展完成欧洲首秀，并纳入长安欧洲布局——长安在欧洲已投资 11.8 亿欧元、设立 8 家子公司、员工超 800 人、在 15 国布局超 350 家销售服务网点，规划 2028 年建成 18 万辆产能、2030 年落地 35 万辆（[12]）。

创始人与管理层

架构与治理

启源是长安品牌下的序列品牌，非独立法人，无独立董事会、董事长或上市公司高管；其经营并入长安汽车（深交所 000625）合并报表，长安汽车为国有控股车企（[6]）。品牌由长安品牌体系内的产品 CEO 负责运营，启源本身无单独认定的实际控制人，其控制链归于长安汽车（[8]）。长安汽车隶属 2025 年 7 月挂牌成立的中国长安汽车集团（中央企业集团）（[16]）。

竞争格局

我们的判断：竞争范围。在启源主攻的 8 万-25 万元主流新能源价格带，主要对手包括比亚迪、吉利银河、零跑、五菱新能源，以及价格带与启源重叠的长安自有深蓝品牌。此为分析所选的竞争范围，不是销量排名（[12]、[2]）。

我们的判断：优势。启源最突出的优势是性价比（「越级价值+降维供给」）、长安央企背书的供应链与密布渠道，以及快速的产品迭代——约 37 个月达成百万累计销量（[6]、[11]）。央广网称面向全球打造的 Q05 连续 6 个月居国内紧凑型 SUV 销量第一，显示品类竞争力；但 2026 年 7 月报道称 6 月 Q05 销量 23,523 辆在紧凑型 SUV 市场位列第四，两者统计窗口或口径不同，本文并列呈现（[12]、[21]）。2025 年长安汽车新能源销量突破 110.9 万辆（同比 +51%），其中启源贡献 41.1 万辆，占约 37%（按披露数字计算），是三大品牌中的主力（[16]）。

我们的判断：产品结构。启源并行推进低价位走量（全新 Q05）与向上突围（Q07、Q06）两条路线：Q07 上市后月销稳定在万辆级、累计突破 9 万辆；Q06 于 2026 年 9 月上市，首搭天枢领航系统，上市 24 小时累计订单破 3.5 万台，成为新的冲量主力（[16]、[11]）。

我们的判断：短板。销量集中于低价位的 Q05，且品牌未达成 2024 年目标——全球伙伴大会设定的 25 万辆对实际交付的 146,349 辆（[20]、[7]）。其定位与深蓝存在重叠，长安需统筹管理以控制内部内耗（[9]）。

未来展望

公司公开规划。发布之初，长安汽车提出启源 2030 年 150 万辆目标，并称到 2025 年面向全球推出 10 款全新车型；以上为披露的目标，不是已实现的业绩（[1]）。2024 年 1 月全球伙伴大会上，长安称启源是公司未来发展的基石，并将基于 SDA 架构的数智新汽车定名 E07、计划 10 月量产（[20]）。2026 年 7 月，执行副总裁杨大勇称下半年目标 30 万辆、全年向 40 万辆冲击（[21]）。欧洲方面，启源在 2026 巴黎车展完成首秀，Q05 计划 2027 年初上市；长安在欧洲的整体规划为 2028 年建成 18 万辆产能、2030 年落地 35 万辆（[12]）。

我们的判断。启源已具备百万累计销量、2026 年二季度首次单季度盈利（媒体口径）与欧洲上市路径，正从规模积累转向盈利与海外扩张。关键考验在于能否实现全年盈利、提升低价位 Q05 之外的单价，并与深蓝形成区隔。2030 年 150 万辆目标远高于长安自身设定的 2026 年约 40 万辆全年目标规模，其达成有赖于国内份额的持续提升与欧洲市场的成功放量。以上为我们的分析，不构成对财务结果的预测（[10]、[21]、[12]）。

风险

  • 销量集中。销量依赖低价位的全新 Q05，紧凑型 SUV 细分市场若放缓将拖累整体（[12]）。
  • 价格带重叠。启源与深蓝在长安自有品牌矩阵内价格带重叠，需精细区隔（[9]）。
  • 盈利刚起步。启源 2026 年二季度才首次单季度盈利（媒体报道口径），全年持续盈利尚未确立（[10]）。
  • 未达目标。品牌未达成 2024 年 25 万辆目标，存在执行与规划风险（[20]、[7]）。
  • 海外执行。欧洲布局尚处早期，Q05 至 2027 年初才上市，面临关税、准入与品牌建设成本（[12]）。
  • 对母公司的依赖。作为无独立财务与产能的序列品牌，启源的资金、生产与销售网络均依赖长安汽车，集团层面的决策与资源配置直接决定其走向（[8]）。
