Overview

Brilliance Auto refers here to Brilliance Auto Group Holdings Co., Ltd., a Shenyang-based company established on 16 September 2002. It entered bankruptcy reorganization in November 2020; the court approved the plan and terminated proceedings on 2 August 2023, and its 100% equity was transferred to Shenyang Automobile on 15 March 2024 [18]. An April 2026 listed-company announcement identifies Shenyang Automobile Group as its sole shareholder [12].

Brilliance China Automotive Holdings Limited (01114.HK) is a separate related listed company. Its 2026 interim report identifies Shenyang Sanshi as a directly wholly-owned subsidiary of Shenyang Automobile Group holding about 29.99% of Brilliance China [3]. Brilliance China indirectly holds 25% of BMW Brilliance [7]. This disclosed ownership chain does not pass through Brilliance Auto Group Holdings; the BMW investment and the associated earnings discussed below are Brilliance China figures [3][1].

Brilliance Group has disclosed its own consolidated accounts, including unaudited H1 2024 revenue of RMB 5,276,918,311.57 [19]. Its 2023 accounts received a disclaimer of audit opinion [20]. Separately, Brilliance China reports BMW investment income, Jinbei vehicles, components and auto-finance operations; those activities provide related-company context rather than a current consolidated business profile for Brilliance Group [5].

Current operations

Related-company businesses and ownership

Brilliance China's principal activities are, through its 25%-owned associate BMW Brilliance, the manufacture and sale of BMW vehicles and components; through subsidiaries the manufacture and sale of Jinbei (Shenyang) minibuses and multi-purpose vehicles and of automotive components (Ningbo Yumin Machinery, Mianyang Brilliance Ruian); and auto financing through Brilliance-BEA Auto Finance [4]. It also indirectly holds a 31.20% interest in engine maker Xinchen China Power Holdings [5]. On the ownership side, Shenyang Automobile Group Co., Ltd. (formerly Shenyang Automobile Co., Ltd.) wholly owns Brilliance Auto Group Holdings, and indirectly holds about 29.99% of Brilliance China through its directly wholly-owned subsidiary Shenyang Sanshi Automobile Industry Development Co., Ltd. [12][3]. The December 2025 changes at Jinbei Automobile (600609.SH) and the April 2026 changes at Liaoning Shenhua Holdings Co., Ltd. (600653.SH) were internal ownership adjustments within the existing Shenyang Automobile Group control structure. Both companies explicitly stated that their controlling shareholders, indirect controlling shareholders and ultimate controllers remained unchanged [15][21][16].

BMW Brilliance

Founded in May 2003, BMW Brilliance is a premium passenger-vehicle joint venture; since 11 February 2022 BMW holds 75% and Brilliance China 25% [6][7]. Its Shenyang base (Dadong, Tiexi and Lydia plants plus a powertrain plant) is BMW's largest production base worldwide, with more than RMB 116 billion invested since 2010, over 20,000 employees and about 460 local suppliers, and has been Shenyang's largest taxpayer for 19 consecutive years [6]. BMW Brilliance domestic sales fell to 534,001 units in 2025 from 603,807 in 2024 (-11.6%); 2025 included 47,762 BEVs, and exports were 2,162 units (down 89.8% from 21,284 in 2024) [1]. The 2025 top sellers were the 3 Series (180,457 units), 5 Series (126,444), X3 (82,850), X5 (72,400) and X1 (63,004) [1].

Jinbei and commercial vehicles

The Jinbei brand (born 1989) is operated by Brilliance China's subsidiary Jinbei (Shenyang) Automotive [17][4]. Its interim report describes plans for CNG, automatic-transmission and overseas customised vehicles [3].

Brilliance China: components and auto finance

The components arm Ningbo Yumin supplies aluminium structural parts for BYD models, while Mianyang Ruian has entered the supplier systems of Chery and Quanchai; the auto-finance subsidiary Brilliance-BEA Auto Finance is diversifying toward NEV manufacturers [1]. The YuXin smart-cockpit joint venture with TCL started production at its Shenyang plant on 8 June 2026 [13].

Brilliance China results (H1 2026)

For the six months ended 30 June 2026, Brilliance China reported revenue of RMB 676 million (up 20.3% year on year) and profit attributable to equity holders of RMB 779 million (down 54.2%); the share of results of associates was RMB 974 million (down 52.5%) [3]. BMW Brilliance sold 212,782 vehicles in H1 2026 (-18.8%), including 14,442 BEVs (-50.0%) [3]. Jinbei (Shenyang) sold 2,815 light buses and multi-purpose vehicles, up from 277 in H1 2025; the board declared an interim dividend of HKD 0.50 per share [3].

Founders and leadership

Company formation

The financial-statement notes record that Liaoning Province established a working group to receive state-owned Brilliance assets in April 2002 and approved formation of Brilliance Auto Group Holdings in July 2002. The company was incorporated on 16 September 2002 using the net assets of Zhuhai Brilliance Holdings. This institutional establishment is distinct from the incorporation and listing history of Brilliance China [18][5].

Ownership and governance

Brilliance Auto Group Holdings is wholly owned by Shenyang Automobile Group Co., Ltd. [12]. Shenyang Automobile Group indirectly holds about 29.99% of Brilliance China through its directly wholly-owned subsidiary Shenyang Sanshi Automobile Industry Development, and is the single largest shareholder; Brilliance Auto Group Holdings' ultimate controller is the Shenyang Municipal SASAC [3][21]. Brilliance China's board consists of seven members - three executive directors and four independent non-executive directors (Song Jian, Jiang Bo, Dong Yang and Zhu Zheyu) [4][9].

Competition

Brilliance China indirectly holds 25% of BMW Brilliance; Shenyang Automobile Group holds about 29.99% of Brilliance China through directly wholly-owned Shenyang Sanshi. These investments do not sit in the disclosed ownership chain below Brilliance Auto Group Holdings [3][7]. BMW Brilliance domestic sales fell from 709,954 in 2023 and 603,807 in 2024 to 534,001 in 2025; Jinbei (Shenyang), a Brilliance China subsidiary, sold 2,815 minibuses and MPVs in H1 2026 [4][3].

Our judgment. In assessing related-company competition, Mercedes-Benz, Audi and domestic premium NEV makers are relevant comparison groups for BMW Brilliance; SAIC Maxus and Jiangling are relevant comparisons for Jinbei. The sales figures above suggest a large scale gap between the two vehicle businesses within Brilliance China. They do not establish Brilliance Group's own current market share or consolidated sales [4][3].

Our judgment. Brilliance China's BMW stake provides equity-method income but also substantial earnings concentration. Its share of all associates' results fell 41.2% in 2025 and 52.5% in H1 2026. The 2025 announcement links weaker BMW Brilliance results to lower volumes and higher dealer support costs. This exposure belongs to Brilliance China; it cannot be used as evidence that Brilliance Group currently owns the BMW interest or earns the corresponding profit [1][3].

Outlook

Disclosed related-company plans. BMW's 24 April 2026 release states that the long-wheelbase Neue Klasse BMW iX3 was presented at the Beijing auto show and is scheduled for market launch in Q4 2026 [11]. Brilliance China's 2026 interim report describes plans to launch local Neue Klasse volume production in H2 2026, introduce Jinbei automatic-transmission, CNG and overseas customised models, deliver the Egyptian KD project and develop South American markets including Bolivia. It reports five dedicated YuXin production lines installed and commissioned, with capacity expansion planned for H2 2026 [3]. These are plans of the named related companies.

Our judgment. The 52.5% decline in Brilliance China's share of associates' results and the 50.0% decline in BMW Brilliance BEV sales in H1 2026 indicate earnings pressure at the listed company. If Neue Klasse production and customer demand meet the disclosed timetable, the new product cycle could help; a slower ramp or further dealer-support costs could offset that benefit. Jinbei's 2,815 H1 sales and Brilliance China's RMB 62.010 million share of YuXin's H1 loss provide no basis yet for assuming those activities will replace BMW-related earnings. None of this is a forecast for Brilliance Group's consolidated results [3].

Risks

  • Group audit limitations. Brilliance Group published 2023 financial statements, but Tianzhi International issued a disclaimer of audit opinion, as confirmed by CICC's May 2024 trustee report. Its H1 2024 consolidated financial report is unaudited; the issue is audit assurance, rather than absence of disclosure [20][19].
  • Reorganization execution. The court approved Brilliance Group's reorganization plan and terminated proceedings on 2 August 2023; its 100% equity transfer was completed on 15 March 2024. The H1 2024 report states that the companies were in the plan-execution period [18].
  • Brilliance China earnings concentration. Brilliance China indirectly owns 25% of BMW Brilliance. Brilliance China's share of all associates' results fell 41.2% in 2025 and 52.5% in H1 2026; these are related listed-company figures, not Brilliance Group earnings [7][1][3].
  • BMW Brilliance BEV sales. Sales were 47,762 in 2025 versus 95,083 in 2024. The announcement prints a 48.0% decline, while those volumes imply about 49.8%; both the reported figure and the arithmetic discrepancy are identified here. H1 2026 BEV sales fell 50.0% to 14,442 [1][3].
  • Brilliance China guarantee-related items. Its 2025 announcement attributes part of the increase in other net income to an adjustment of excess provisions for unauthorized guarantees. This is a disclosure in Brilliance China's accounts [1].

概览

本页研究主体为华晨汽车集团控股有限公司，总部位于沈阳，成立于 2002 年 9 月 16 日。集团于 2020 年 11 月进入破产重整，法院于 2023 年 8 月 2 日批准重整计划并终止重整程序，2024 年 3 月 15 日其 100% 股权过户至沈阳汽车名下 [18]。2026 年 4 月上市公司公告列示其唯一股东为沈阳汽车集团有限公司 [12]。

华晨中国汽车控股有限公司（01114.HK）是另一家关联上市公司。其 2026 年中期报告明确，沈阳三实是沈阳汽车集团的直接全资子公司，持有华晨中国约 29.99% 股份 [3]；华晨中国间接持有华晨宝马 25% 股权 [7]。这条已披露股权链不经过华晨集团，下文所述宝马投资及其利润贡献均属于华晨中国口径 [3][1]。

华晨集团已披露自身合并报表，其中未经审计的 2024 年上半年合并营业收入为人民币 5,276,918,311.57 元 [19]；其 2023 年度财务报表的审计意见为无法表示意见 [20]。华晨中国另行披露宝马投资收益、金杯整车、零部件与汽车金融业务；这些信息用于说明关联企业情况，不代表华晨集团现时的合并业务结构 [5]。

现状

关联公司业务与股权

华晨中国的主要业务为：透过持股 25% 的联营公司华晨宝马制造及销售宝马汽车及零部件；透过附属公司制造及销售金杯（沈阳）面包车及多用途车，以及汽车零部件（宁波裕民机械、绵阳华晨瑞安）；并透过华晨东亚汽车金融提供汽车金融服务 [4]。此外还间接持有发动机企业新晨中国动力 31.20% 权益 [5]。股权方面，沈阳汽车集团有限公司（前称沈阳汽车有限公司）全资持有华晨汽车集团控股有限公司，并通过其直接全资附属公司沈阳三实汽车产业发展有限公司间接持有华晨中国约 29.99% [12][3]。金杯汽车（600609.SH）2025 年 12 月、辽宁申华控股（600653.SH）2026 年 4 月完成的调整，均属既有沈阳汽车集团控制体系内的持股结构内部调整。两家公司均明确公告：控股股东、间接控股股东及实际控制人不发生变化 [15][21][16]。

华晨宝马

华晨宝马成立于 2003 年 5 月，是高端乘用车合资企业；自 2022 年 2 月 11 日起宝马持股 75%、华晨中国持股 25% [6][7]。其沈阳基地（大东、铁西、里达三大整车工厂及动力总成工厂）为宝马全球最大生产基地，2010 年以来累计投资超 1160 亿元、员工逾 2 万人、本土供应商约 460 家，连续 19 年为沈阳最大纳税企业 [6]。华晨宝马国内销量由 2024 年 603,807 辆降至 2025 年 534,001 辆（-11.6%）；2025 年其中纯电 47,762 辆，出口 2,162 辆（较 2024 年 21,284 辆减少 89.8%） [1]。2025 年主力车型依次为 3 系（180,457 辆）、5 系（126,444 辆）、X3（82,850 辆）、X5（72,400 辆）、X1（63,004 辆） [1]。

金杯与商用车

金杯品牌（诞生于 1989 年）由华晨中国附属公司金杯（沈阳）汽车运营 [17][4]。中期报告列示 CNG、自动挡与海外定制车型计划 [3]。

华晨中国：零部件与汽车金融

零部件企业宁波裕民为比亚迪多款车型供应铝合金车身结构件并实现量产，绵阳瑞安进入奇瑞及全柴供应商体系；汽车金融平台华晨东亚汽车金融正加大对新能源整车厂商的业务拓展 [1]。与 TCL 的驭新智能座舱合资企业沈阳工厂于 2026 年 6 月 8 日投产 [13]。

华晨中国业绩（2026 上半年）

截至 2026 年 6 月 30 日止六个月，华晨中国实现收益 6.76 亿元（同比 +20.3%）、本公司股权持有人应占溢利 7.79 亿元（同比 -54.2%），应占联营公司业绩 9.74 亿元（同比 -52.5%） [3]。华晨宝马 2026 年上半年销量 212,782 辆（同比 -18.8%），其中纯电 14,442 辆（同比 -50.0%） [3]。金杯（沈阳）售出 2,815 辆轻型客车及多用途汽车（2025 年同期为 277 辆）；董事会宣派中期股息每股 0.50 港元 [3]。

竞争格局

华晨中国间接持有华晨宝马 25% 股权；沈阳汽车集团通过直接全资子公司沈阳三实持有华晨中国约 29.99%。已披露的这条股权链不经过华晨汽车集团控股有限公司 [3][7]。华晨宝马国内销量由 2023 年 709,954 辆、2024 年 603,807 辆降至 2025 年 534,001 辆；华晨中国附属公司金杯（沈阳）2026 年上半年售出 2,815 辆轻型客车及多用途车 [4][3]。

我们的判断。分析关联企业竞争时，可将奔驰、奥迪及国内高端新能源厂商作为华晨宝马的比较对象，将上汽大通、江铃作为金杯业务的比较对象。上述销量表明华晨中国内部两项整车业务规模相差较大，但不能据此确定华晨集团本身现时的市场份额或合并销量 [4][3]。

我们的判断。华晨中国的宝马股权带来权益法收益，也形成显著的盈利集中度。华晨中国全部联营公司业绩贡献在 2025 年下降 41.2%、2026 年上半年下降 52.5%；2025 年公告将华晨宝马业绩转弱与销量下降及经销商支持费上升联系起来。这是华晨中国的业务敞口，不能用来认定华晨集团现时持有宝马权益或取得对应利润 [1][3]。

未来展望

关联公司公开规划。宝马 2026 年 4 月 24 日新闻稿称，新世代 BMW iX3 长轴距版在北京车展亮相，计划于 2026 年第四季度上市 [11]。华晨中国 2026 年中期报告列示：下半年启动新世代车型本地批量生产；推进金杯自动挡、CNG 与海外定制车型、埃及 KD 项目及包括玻利维亚在内的南美市场；驭新已安装并调试五条专用生产线，计划下半年扩产 [3]。这些规划均属于上述具名关联企业。

我们的判断。2026 年上半年华晨中国应占联营公司业绩下降 52.5%、华晨宝马纯电销量下降 50.0%，显示上市公司的盈利承压。如果新世代车型生产及需求符合已披露进度，新产品周期可能提供帮助；若爬坡较慢或经销商支持费继续上升，则可能抵销其作用。金杯上半年 2,815 辆销量及华晨中国应占驭新上半年 62.010 百万元亏损，尚不足以支持这些业务可替代宝马利润的假设。以上不构成对华晨集团合并业绩的预测 [3]。

风险

  • 集团审计限制。华晨集团已披露 2023 年度财务报表，但天职国际出具无法表示意见的审计报告，中金公司 2024 年 5 月受托管理报告予以确认；集团 2024 年半年度合并财务报告未经审计。问题在于审计保证程度，不能写成未披露 [20][19]。
  • 重整计划执行。法院于 2023 年 8 月 2 日批准华晨集团重整计划并终止重整程序，2024 年 3 月 15 日完成 100% 股权过户；2024 年半年报附注说明相关企业已进入重整计划执行期 [18]。
  • 华晨中国盈利集中度。华晨中国间接持有华晨宝马 25% 股权。华晨中国全部联营公司业绩贡献在 2025 年下降 41.2%、2026 年上半年下降 52.5%；这些属于关联上市公司的数字，不是华晨集团利润 [7][1][3]。
  • 华晨宝马纯电销量。2025 年为 47,762 辆、2024 年为 95,083 辆；公告列示降幅 48.0%，按所列销量计算约为 49.8%，此处同时指出公告数值及算术差异。2026 年上半年纯电销量下降 50.0% 至 14,442 辆 [1][3]。
  • 华晨中国担保相关项目。其 2025 年公告将部分其他收入净额增长归因于未经授权担保超额拨备的调整；此项披露属于华晨中国财务报表口径 [1]。
