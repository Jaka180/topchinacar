Overview

smart (smart Automobile Co., Ltd., 智马达汽车有限公司) is a 50:50 global joint venture between Mercedes-Benz AG and Geely Automobile Group Co., Ltd. (under Geely Holding), established in December 2019 and headquartered in Ningbo's Hangzhou Bay, China, to develop, produce and sell smart-brand electric and plug-in-hybrid vehicles worldwide.[1][21] Mercedes-Benz leads design while Geely leads engineering, manufacturing and supply; under the official description the two sides each hold 50%.[1]

The brand itself dates to 1994, when Daimler partnered with Nicholas Hayek of Swatch Group to jointly establish smart, and its first mass-produced model, the two-seater City-Coupé, entered production in 1998; for two decades smart was defined by the ultra-compact city car.[2] In 2019, the last year before the reset, the smart brand sold about 116,800 vehicles globally, down 9.3% year on year.[18] After electrification, smart repositioned as a premium brand built on Geely's SEA architecture and Mercedes-Benz design, rolling out the #1 (2022), #3 (2023) and #5 (2024), and from 2025 adding plug-in hybrids (#5 EHD, #6); the two-seater #2 returned in October 2026.[3][4]

smart does not publish standalone financials. On volume, its 2024 global deliveries rose 7% year on year with cumulative deliveries near 130,000 units, while 2025 China retail was 30,799 units, down more than 7%.[11][17] As of the research cut-off the brand's footprint spans six continents: its H1 2026 release cited 41 markets and more than 700 sales outlets, and it subsequently entered Argentina (September 2026) and Thailand (October 2026).[8][29][28]

Current operations

Corporate structure and shareholders

smart Automobile Co., Ltd. is registered in Ningbo with registered capital of RMB 5.4 billion at formation (each side RMB 2.7 billion), later raised to RMB 5.75 billion and again to about RMB 6.99 billion in October 2025 (up about 21%).[21][12][23] The official description is a 50:50 venture between Mercedes-Benz AG and Geely, with Mercedes-Benz responsible for design and Geely leading engineering, manufacturing and supply.[1] The registered holding structure is layered: Tianqi Lithium's 2023-07-13 announcement states that smart Mobility Pte. Ltd. (a Singapore company) is a controlling shareholder of the China JV, and corporate-registry reports show the China entity held by SMART MOBILITY PTE. LTD. and related entities.[9][12] Before the Series-A round, Mercedes-Benz AG held 46.000% of SM, Geely's offshore subsidiary Sunrise Mobility Limited 41.400%, Geely affiliate ZMD Capital Ltd 4.600% and other shareholders/reserved shares 8.000% (Class B, non-voting). After the round (a total of US$250-300 million, illustrated at US$300 million), Mercedes-Benz holds 43.397%, Sunrise 39.057%, ZMD 4.340%, other shareholders/reserved shares 7.546%, Tianqi Lithium Hong Kong 2.830% and other co-investors 2.830%.[9]

Development phases

smart's development falls roughly into three phases: the petrol two-seater era under Daimler (1994-2019), anchored on Europe's urban-commuter market; the 2019-2022 period of joint-venture formation and product renewal; and the era from 2022 to date of pure-electric and plug-in-hybrid expansion and globalisation on Geely's SEA architecture.[2][1]

Model line-up and pricing

As of the cut-off the line-up comprises the #1 compact SUV, #3 compact coupe SUV, #5 mid-size SUV (BEV and #5 EHD PHEV), #6 fastback sedan (PHEV) and the newly unveiled two-seater #2.[3][20][4] China prices span roughly RMB 112,999 to RMB 368,000 across models, with the volume band around RMB 150,000-300,000: the #1 launched at RMB 194,200-239,200 (June 2022), the #5 at an effective RMB 229,900-368,000 (October 2024), the #2 Couture Edition at RMB 112,999, and the new-generation #1 from RMB 149,900 (August 2026).[31][3][26][30] The #6 has a 2,926 mm wheelbase and is the brand's largest model to date, while the #2 returns to the brand's original two-seater form.[20][4]

Technology and platforms

The new-generation range is engineered by smart's R&D team on Geely's SEA (Sustainable Experience Architecture) platform, with the Mercedes-Benz global design team responsible for design.[2][25] The #5 uses an 800V platform, while the #5 EHD is a 1.5T plug-in hybrid and the #6 uses Geely's Leishen electric-hybrid system (claimed thermal efficiency of 47.26%) with standard LiDAR and a claimed 1,810 km CLTC combined range.[3][24][20] The #2 is built on smart's self-developed ECA (Electric Compact Architecture) purpose-built for two-seaters.[4][27]

Production and capacity

In 2021 it was reported that the #1 would be built at a new Xi'an plant with planned annual capacity of about 150,000 units.[25][18] The two-seater #2, by contrast, is produced in full at Geely's Jinan manufacturing base, where its production version rolled off the line on 2026-10-20.[27] smart does not publish per-plant capacity or utilisation figures, so precise capacity is not publicly disclosed.[1]

China market

smart's China retail volume has declined for two consecutive years: 42,292 units in 2023, 33,280 in 2024 and 30,799 in 2025 (down more than 7%), with the #1 contributing 20,836 units, the #3 4,451 and the #5 5,512 in 2025.[17] A separate media outlet reports 2025 China volume of about 27,000 units (-6.9%) and a #5 full-year figure of 3,670 units, so the two sources disagree on the precise level.[19] The same 42,292 figure has also been used as a CPCA wholesale 'China sales' number, with 24,732 exports bringing 2023 wholesale to 67,024 units.[13] For 2026, one media report puts China retail at 8,516 units in the first five months, down more than 24% year on year.[32]

Overseas business

smart operates a 'China-Europe dual-home' structure, with smart Europe GmbH handling sales, marketing and after-sales in Europe via a direct-to-customer agency model.[1] Its 2025 overseas volume was about 12,700 units, down about 14.4% year on year.[19] The market footprint grew from 39 countries and regions in February 2026 to 41 markets across six continents (with more than 700 sales outlets) by July 2026; smart then entered Argentina (September 2026) and Thailand (October 2026).[5][8][29][28] In Thailand, Thonburi Group is the official importer and distributor, launching the #1, #3 and #5 and planning a flagship Bangkok showroom supported by the Mercedes-Benz retail network.[28]

Founders and leadership

Founding

smart has no single natural-person founder: its predecessor was set up by Daimler and Swatch Group's Nicholas Hayek in 1994, and the current joint venture was formed by Mercedes-Benz and Geely in 2019, so this section focuses on the two shareholders and the current management.[2][1]

Ownership and governance

smart Automobile is a 50:50 Mercedes-Benz-Geely joint venture; its board has six members, with each shareholder appointing three. The initially announced members were, on the Geely side, Li Shufu, An Conghui and Li Donghui, and on the Mercedes-Benz side, Hubertus Troska, Britta Seeger and Markus Schäfer.[21] Per corporate registry information reported by media, Li Shufu serves as chairman and Tong Xiangbei as legal representative and general manager.[23] No annual report or announcement designates a single actual controller, and the company publishes no standalone financial statements.[1]

Global CEO

Tong Xiangbei has been global CEO since January 2020. He graduated from Beijing University of Technology in 1996 in automotive and internal-combustion-engine design, worked at Ford China from 1998 for about 17 years in plant and programme roles across China, the US and Asia-Pacific, and joined Geely in 2015, where he helped build the Lynk & Co Zhangjiakou plant and served as assistant to the Geely Auto Group president.[1][13]

Global CMO

Kang Yi became global CMO on 2026-04-01, succeeding Mandy Zhang, and also serves as Chair of the Supervisory Board of smart Europe GmbH. Kang Yi has worked for Mercedes-Benz for 20 years; in 2015 he was appointed Head of Sales and Marketing of smart in China, and at the time of the announcement he was Head of Sales and Marketing at Mercedes-Benz Auto Finance Ltd. Mandy Zhang concluded her four-year assignment as global CMO, during which she oversaw the global launches of the #1, #3 and #5, and took up a new role at Beijing Mercedes-Benz Sales Service Co., Ltd.[5]

China and Europe executives

Yi Han has been deputy global CMO and CEO of smart China's marketing company since 2024-01-09, reporting to the global CMO; he previously held marketing roles at Geely, Volvo and Lynk & Co, and was smart's China sales and after-sales head from 2020 to 2021.[14][15] The 2020 executive team included Daniel Lescow (sales, marketing and after-sales), Jun Yang (R&D) and Ulf Nestler (CFO), with Dirk Adelmann as the first smart Europe GmbH CEO; Wolfgang Ufer currently serves as smart Europe GmbH CEO with Martin Günther as CFO.[6][1]

Competition

Main rivals

In China's compact-to-mid-size premium electric segment, smart competes with the electric MINI (built by the BMW-Great Wall Spotlight joint venture), the Volvo EX30 and Geely siblings such as Lynk & Co and Zeekr; at the lower price end it overlaps with BYD's Yuan Plus and the Leapmotor B10.[17][13] In Europe it faces the electric MINI and a new generation of city cars such as the Renault Twingo.[19]

Strengths

smart's assets are a genuinely global brand with Mercedes-Benz design; the models on sale in early 2026 (#1, #3, #5) held five-star Euro NCAP safety results and Red Dot design awards, and the brand can draw on Geely's SEA architecture, EV supply chain and low-cost manufacturing.[7][25] Its 'China-Europe dual-home' footprint, with a D2C agency model, gives it distribution that many Chinese EV start-ups lack.[1]

Weaknesses

smart is a small-volume player: China retail fell from 42,292 units in 2023 to 30,799 in 2025.[17] It leans on Geely platforms rather than proprietary core EV technology, and its move into larger SUVs and sedans blurs the small-car identity on which the brand was built.[19] Because it publishes no financials, its losses and cash position are not externally verifiable.[9]

Our judgement

In our view, smart's core challenge is positioning consistency: the data show the #1, the model closest to the brand's traditional image, still contributed about two-thirds of China volume in 2025, while the larger #3, #5 and #6 have not reached comparable scale.[17] The return of the #2 two-seater is an attempt to re-anchor the brand's identity; whether it grows the business depends on whether smart can hold a premium price in a segment that cheaper rivals are pressing downward.[4][19]

Outlook

Company plans

smart has named 2026 its 'Year of Opening New Frontiers'. CEO Tong Xiangbei said the brand would grow its product family from three to five models with the #6 and #2, accelerate market entries and upgrade its smart care services.[7] The #2 began early ordering in Europe and China after its Paris premiere, with the China Couture Edition already on sale and the Premium variant scheduled for early November 2026.[4][26][32] On profitability, the CEO said in January 2025 that the business had reached positive gross margin and that pre-tax and after-tax profit turnarounds were the next goals.[11]

Our analysis

In our judgement, smart's near-term growth hinges on the #2 rather than on further enlargement: its China volume has declined for two straight years and the larger models have not scaled, so re-establishing the two-seater as a premium icon is a plausible way to restore differentiation.[17][19] The caveat is that this is a low-margin, small-volume segment, and EU tariffs on Chinese-built EVs raise the cost of its European business, so revenue growth may come more from overseas distribution than from China.[19] On the other hand, continued capital increases by the two shareholders suggest Mercedes-Benz and Geely are still willing to keep funding the venture in the near term.[12][7]

Risks

Commercial risks

smart's China retail volume has declined for two consecutive years, leaving it dependent on a single model, the #1, for most of its volume.[17] A price war in China's EV market has forced repeated cuts, which pressure both volume and the brand's premium positioning.[17] The brand's positioning swings between a premium small car and a mainstream larger car, drawing media criticism that it is building ever-bigger cars.[17]

Financial risks

smart does not publish financials, so its losses and cash position are not externally verifiable; it has relied on capital increases and external financing (the 2023 Series-A round) to fund operations.[9][12]

Policy and overseas risks

Because its vehicles are built in China, smart is exposed to EU import tariffs on Chinese-made EVs, which its CEO has cited as a factor that raised costs and reshaped product planning.[19] Overseas expansion into right-hand-drive and emerging markets adds compliance and currency complexity.[8]

Technology-route and governance risks

smart depends on Geely for its platform, electric-drive and hybrid technology and on Mercedes-Benz for design, so its independence is limited; its dual BEV-plus-PHEV route is still unproven at volume.[18][20] As a 50:50 joint venture without a single actual controller, its strategy depends on continued alignment between the two shareholders.[1]

概览

smart（智马达汽车有限公司，smart Automobile Co., Ltd.）是梅赛德斯-奔驰股份公司与吉利汽车集团有限公司（属吉利控股）于 2019 年 12 月合资成立、双方各持股 50% 的全球汽车公司，总部位于中国宁波杭州湾，负责在全球研发、生产与销售 smart 品牌纯电与插混汽车。[1][21] 官方口径下奔驰负责设计，吉利主导工程、制造与供应链。[1]

品牌历史可追溯至 1994 年，戴姆勒与斯沃琪集团的尼古拉斯·海耶克联合创立 smart 品牌，1998 年首款量产车型双座 City-Coupé 投产；此后约二十年 smart 一直以超紧凑城市小车著称。[2] 在合资转型前的 2019 年，smart 品牌全球销量约 11.68 万辆、同比下滑 9.3%。[18] 电动化焕新后，smart 转型为依托吉利 SEA 架构与奔驰设计的高端品牌，先后推出 #1（2022）、#3（2023）、#5（2024），并自 2025 年起在中国引入插混车型（#5 EHD、#6）；双座 #2 于 2026 年 10 月回归。[3][4]

smart 不单独披露财务数据。销量方面，2024 年全球交付同比增 7%、累计交付近 13 万台；2025 年中国市场零售 30,799 辆、同比下滑超 7%。[11][17] 截至研究截止日，品牌已覆盖六大洲：2026 年上半年业绩稿称已达 41 个市场、销售网点超 700 家，其后于 2026 年 9 月进入阿根廷、10 月进入泰国。[8][29][28]

发展脉络

smart 的发展大致分三个阶段：1994-2019 年戴姆勒旗下的燃油双座小车时期，长期依赖欧洲城市通勤市场；2019-2022 年梅赛德斯-奔驰与吉利合资组建与产品焕新期；2022 年至今依托吉利 SEA 架构的纯电/插混扩张与全球化期。[2][1]

截至研究截止日，在售/已亮相车型包括 #1 紧凑型 SUV、#3 紧凑型轿跑 SUV、#5 中型 SUV（纯电及 #5 EHD 插混）、#6 掀背轿车（插混）以及新亮相的双座 #2。[3][20][4] 在中国，车型价格带从 #2 鎏金高定版 11.2999 万元到 #5 顶配 36.8 万元，主销区间大致 15 万-30 万元：#1 上市价 19.42 万-23.92 万元（2022 年 6 月）、#5 到手价 22.99 万-36.8 万元（2024 年 10 月）、#2 鎏金高定版 11.2999 万元、全新一代 #1 14.99 万元起（2026 年 8 月）。[31][3][26][30] #6 轴距 2,926 mm，为品牌迄今尺寸最大的车型；#2 则回到品牌最初的双座形态。[20][4]

技术与平台

新一代车型由 smart 研发团队基于吉利 SEA 浩瀚架构开发，梅赛德斯-奔驰全球设计团队负责设计。[2][25] #5 采用 800V 平台；#5 EHD 为 1.5T 插混，#6 采用吉利雷神电混系统（官方称热效率 47.26%），全系标配激光雷达、CLTC 综合续航 1,810 km。[3][24][20] #2 则基于 smart 自研、专为双座车打造的 ECA 电动紧凑型架构。[4][27]

2021 年有报道称 #1 将在西安新工厂生产，规划年产能约 15 万辆。[25][18] 双座 #2 则全部在吉利济南制造基地生产，量产版于 2026-10-20 在该基地下线。[27] smart 不公布各基地产能与利用率，精确产能未公开披露。[1]

smart 中国市场零售已连续两年下滑：2023 年 42,292 辆、2024 年 33,280 辆、2025 年 30,799 辆（同比下滑超 7%），其中 2025 年 #1 为 20,836 辆、#3 为 4,451 辆、#5 为 5,512 辆。[17] 另一家媒体则记 2025 年中国约 2.7 万辆（-6.9%）、#5 全年 3,670 辆，两套口径的具体数字存在分歧。[19] 同一 42,292 也被用作乘联会批发「中国销量」口径，加出口 24,732 辆后 2023 年批发为 67,024 辆。[13] 2026 年前 5 个月，媒体报道中国零售为 8,516 辆、同比下滑超 24%。[32]

海外业务

smart 实行「中欧双核」布局，由 smart Europe GmbH 负责欧洲销售、市场与售后，采用 D2C 直营代理模式。[1] 2025 年海外销量约 12,700 辆、同比下滑约 14.4%。[19] 市场覆盖从 2026 年 2 月的 39 个国家/地区，增至 2026 年 7 月的六大洲 41 个市场（销售网点超 700 家），随后于 2026 年 9 月进入阿根廷、10 月进入泰国。[5][8][29][28] 在泰国，吞武里集团为官方进口与经销商，首批引入 #1、#3、#5，并计划在曼谷开设旗舰展厅、依托奔驰零售网络开展销售与售后。[28]

创始人与管理层

创始人

smart 品牌无单一自然人创始人：其前身由戴姆勒与斯沃琪集团的尼古拉斯·海耶克于 1994 年联合创立，现行合资公司则由梅赛德斯-奔驰与吉利于 2019 年共同组建，故本节重点介绍合资双方与现任管理层。[2][1]

股权与治理

smart 智马达为梅赛德斯-奔驰与吉利各持 50% 的合资公司，董事会由 6 名成员组成，双方各委派 3 名；最初公布的人选中，吉利方为李书福、安聪慧、李东辉，奔驰方为唐仕凯（Hubertus Troska）、贝思格（Britta Seeger）、马库斯·谢弗（Markus Schäfer）。[21] 据媒体转引的企业工商登记信息，李书福任董事长，佟湘北任法定代表人兼总经理。[23] 年报与公告均未认定单一实际控制人，公司也不单独公布财务报表。[1]

全球 CEO

佟湘北自 2020 年 1 月起任全球 CEO。他 1996 年毕业于北京工业大学汽车与内燃机设计专业，1998 年起在福特中国工作约 17 年，曾在中国、美国及亚太多个工厂任职；2015 年加入吉利，主导筹建领克张家口工厂，并任吉利汽车集团总裁助理。[1][13]

全球 CMO

康毅于 2026-04-01 起接替张明霞出任全球 CMO，并兼任 smart Europe GmbH 监事会主席。康毅在梅赛德斯-奔驰工作 20 年，2015 年被任命为 smart 在华销售与市场负责人，公告时其职务为梅赛德斯-奔驰汽车金融有限公司销售与市场负责人。张明霞结束四年全球 CMO 任期，其间主导 #1、#3、#5 全球上市，其后调任北京梅赛德斯-奔驰销售服务有限公司。[5]

中国与欧洲高管

易寒自 2024-01-09 起任 smart 全球公司副 CMO 兼中国营销公司 CEO，向全球 CMO 汇报；此前历任吉利汽车、沃尔沃、领克的市场相关职务，2020-2021 年任 smart 中国市场销售及售后负责人。[14][15] 2020 年公布的高管团队包括 Daniel Lescow（销售/市场/售后）、羊军（研发）、Ulf Nestler（CFO），Dirk Adelmann 为首任 smart Europe GmbH CEO；Wolfgang Ufer 现任 smart Europe GmbH CEO，Martin Günther 任 CFO。[6][1]

竞争格局

主要对手

在中国紧凑至中型高端纯电市场，smart 面临电动 MINI（由宝马与长城合资的光束汽车生产）、沃尔沃 EX30，以及同门领克、极氪的竞争；在更低价格带又与比亚迪元 PLUS、零跑 B10 等重叠。[17][13] 在欧洲，它面对电动 MINI 以及雷诺 Twingo 等新一代城市小车。[19]

优势

smart 的核心资产是真正的全球品牌：奔驰设计；2026 年初在售的 #1、#3、#5 拥有 Euro NCAP 五星安全认证与红点设计奖背书，并可调用吉利 SEA 架构、新能源供应链与低成本制造。[7][25] 其「中欧双核」布局与 D2C 代理模式，也使其具备许多中国新势力所没有的分销渠道。[1]

短板

smart 体量小：中国市场零售从 2023 年的 42,292 辆降至 2025 年的 30,799 辆。[17] 它依赖吉利平台而非自有核心三电技术，且向大 SUV、轿车扩张模糊了其赖以立身的小车形象。[19] 由于不披露财务数据，其亏损与现金状况无法外部核实。[9]

我们的判断

我们认为，smart 的核心挑战在于定位的一致性：数据显示，最贴近品牌传统形象的 #1 在 2025 年仍贡献约三分之二的中国销量，而更大的 #3、#5、#6 尚未带来同等规模。[17] #2 双座车的回归，是重新锚定品牌定位的尝试；它能否带来增长，取决于能否在被低价车型压价的细分市场守住高端溢价。[4][19]

未来展望

公司规划

smart 将 2026 年定为「开拓新境」之年。全球 CEO 佟湘北表示，品牌将借 #6 与 #2 把全球车型矩阵从 3 款扩充至 5 款，加速进入新市场并升级 smart care 客户服务。[7] #2 在巴黎首秀后已在欧洲与中国开启预订，中国鎏金高定版已上市，Premium 版计划于 2026 年 11 月初上市。[4][26][32] 盈利方面，CEO 于 2025 年 1 月表示公司已实现正毛利，下阶段目标为税前及税后利润转正。[11]

我们的分析

我们认为，smart 的短期增长更依赖 #2 而非继续做大：其中国销量已连续两年下滑，大车型未形成规模，因此重建双座车的高端标签是恢复差异化的合理路径。[17][19] 需要警惕的是，这是低毛利、小体量的细分市场，且欧盟对中国产电动车加征关税抬高了欧洲业务成本，故营收增长可能更多来自海外分销而非中国市场。[19] 另一方面，股东双方持续增资，表明梅赛德斯-奔驰与吉利短期内仍愿继续投入。[12][7]

风险

经营风险

smart 中国市场零售已连续两年下滑，且销量高度依赖单一车型 #1。[17] 中国新能源市场的价格战迫使品牌多次降价，既压制销量也侵蚀高端定位。[17] 品牌定位在「精品小车」与「主流大车」之间摇摆，媒体批评其「将车越造越大」。[17]

财务风险

smart 不公布财务报表，其亏损与现金状况无法外部核实；公司依赖增资与外部融资（2023 年 A 轮）支撑运营。[9][12]

政策与海外风险

由于车型在中国生产，smart 面临欧盟对中国产电动车的进口关税，CEO 曾称这抬高了成本并改变了产品规划。[19] 拓展右舵及新兴市场还增加了合规与汇率复杂性。[8]

技术路线与治理风险

smart 的平台、电驱与混动技术依赖吉利、设计依赖梅赛德斯-奔驰，独立性有限；其「纯电 + 插混」双路线能否上量尚待验证。[18][20] 作为无单一实际控制人的 50:50 合资公司，其战略依赖双方股东的持续协同。[1]
