Overview

Sinotruk (Hong Kong) Limited (HKEX: 03808) is the Hong Kong-listed entity of CNHTC, focused on commercial vehicles and related assemblies and parts. According to the company, it ranked first globally in heavy-truck sales for the first time in 2025 [2]. The listed group develops and manufactures heavy and medium trucks, light trucks and buses, together with key assemblies and parts such as engines, gearboxes, axles, cabs and frames, and also provides financial services [1]. In FY2025 it reported revenue of RMB109.54 billion and net profit attributable to shareholders of RMB7.02 billion, both record highs, and sold 292,140 heavy trucks (including 153,368 exports) and 123,136 light trucks [1]. In H1 2026 revenue rose 39.24% year on year to RMB70.84 billion and net profit attributable to shareholders rose 26.22% to RMB4.33 billion; heavy-truck sales reached 193,644 units (domestic 85,293 and export including affiliated export 108,351, up 57.1%) [7].

The company is a state-owned enterprise. Its 51% controlling shareholder is China National Heavy Duty Truck Group Co., Ltd. (CNHTC), which holds 1,408,106,603 shares; the ultimate controller is the Shandong Provincial SASAC, which holds about 70% of Shandong Heavy Industry Group, the parent that owns about 65% of CNHTC [12][13]. Germany's MAN SE took a 25%+1 share stake through its 2009 strategic investment; after a block-trade transfer of 56,000,000 shares on 24 July 2026, the direct holder MAN Finance and Holding S.A. holds 496,198,671 shares, about 17.97% [8]. The current group website dates its predecessor, Jinan Auto Works, to 1930 and records production of the Huanghe JN150 heavy truck in 1960 [31]. An older SAE-China profile still gives 1956 as the founding year; this report follows the current company disclosure [29]. CNHTC and Weichai Power belong to the same Shandong Heavy Industry Group system and maintain continuing connected transactions in engines and parts [13].

The group's A-share subsidiary Sinotruk Jinan Truck (000951.SZ, 51.26%-owned by Sinotruk Hong Kong) produces the HOWO and Huanghe heavy-truck brands [15]. The listed group also includes the light-vehicle brand VGV, whose operating subsidiary is within the listed group's consolidation scope [30].

Current operations

Business structure

Since 2025 the group reports three operating segments: heavy trucks (manufacture and sale of heavy and medium-heavy trucks and parts, now including the engine business), light trucks and others, and finance. In FY2025 the heavy-truck segment generated revenue of RMB97,179 million (+13.7%), light trucks and others RMB14,550 million (+30.4%), and finance RMB744 million (+20.6%) [2]. Heavy trucks are the core business, with 292,140 units sold in 2025 [1]. In H1 2026 the group sold 193,644 heavy trucks, of which 85,293 were sold domestically (ranking second in the domestic market) and 108,351 were exported (including affiliated export) [7].

Technology and platforms

The group develops and manufactures key assemblies and parts including engines, gearboxes, axles, cabs and frames. Its MC-series engines originated in the MAN technology cooperation. Its new-energy routes include battery electric, hybrid and hydrogen fuel cell. Per the annual results announcement, new-energy heavy-truck sales rose 248.9% and new-energy light-truck sales rose about 220% in 2025. FY2025 expensed research and development cost was RMB2,902 million. [2][9][1]

The 2025 annual report states that regulatory-compliant, enhanced and advanced assisted-driving platforms achieved batch application. The company reports commercial deployment of L2+ trunk-logistics assisted driving and L4 autonomous-driving solutions for confined scenarios across ports, mines and trunk logistics operations. These are company disclosures of product deployment. [2]

Manufacturing and capacity

The 2025 annual report places the main heavy-truck production bases in Jinan and Jining, and light-truck bases mainly in Jinan, Fujian and Chengdu. A company release dated 15 September 2026 reports 37 overseas KD plants, more than 1,000 service outlets and sales to over 150 countries and regions. A comparable global annual production-capacity figure was not confirmed in the sources reviewed. [2][32]

Domestic position

In its 2025 annual report, the company states that it ranked first in China in heavy-truck sales for the fourth consecutive year and first globally for the first time in 2025. This is the company’s annual ranking statement. The listed group disclosed 292,140 heavy-truck sales. Its 2026 interim report records domestic heavy-truck sales of 85,293 units in H1, ranking second in the domestic market. [2][7]

Overseas business

In 2025 the listed group exported 153,368 heavy trucks, including affiliated export; the company reports ranking first in Chinese heavy-truck exports for the 21st consecutive year. In H1 2026 consolidated overseas revenue was RMB29.84 billion, while heavy-truck export revenue including affiliated export was RMB30.91 billion, up 54%. These two revenue figures use different reporting scopes. [1][7]

The 2025 annual report identifies Africa and Southeast Asia as established markets and sets out plans for localisation through overseas subsidiaries, parts warehouses and local factories. The 37 KD plants and service network reported in September 2026 represent the company’s overseas network at that date. [2][32]

Founders and leadership

Predecessor and restructuring

The current group website dates its predecessor, Jinan Auto Works, to 1930, also used in a September 2026 company release. An older SAE-China member profile gives 1956. This report follows the current company disclosure and records the disagreement without claiming that the dates represent verified, separate establishment or restructuring events. [31][32][29]

The official group history records the establishment of the restructured CNHTC on 18 January 2001 and the listing of the Hong Kong entity on 28 November 2007. Strategic cooperation with MAN followed in 2009, involving engine and vehicle technology. These dates refer to different entities and events: the predecessor, the restructured group and the Hong Kong-listed entity. [33]

Key management

  • Liu Zhengtao (刘正涛), chairman since 27 June 2025. An engineer with a university degree, he is also party secretary and chairman of CNHTC. He previously served as deputy party secretary, director, general manager, executive deputy general manager and procurement director of CNHTC, as a director of Sinotruk Jinan Truck, and as executive director and president of Sinotruk (Hong Kong) from 2020 to 2022 and again as president from 27 March 2025 to 27 June 2025 [2].
  • Liu Wei (刘伟), executive director and president since 27 June 2025. He holds a bachelor's degree in internal-combustion engines from Wuhan Institute of Technology (now Wuhan University of Technology) and an MBA from Shandong University, and is currently deputy party secretary, director and general manager of CNHTC [2].

Other executive directors are Li Xia, Han Feng, Zhao Hua, Wang Dechun and Han Xing; non-executive directors are Cheng Guangxu and Karsten Oellers (a MAN SE representative); independent non-executive directors are Wang Dengfeng, Zhao Hang, Lyu Shousheng, Zhang Zhong and Liu Xiaolun [14].

Equity and governance

CNHTC directly holds 51% of Sinotruk (Hong Kong) (1,408,106,603 shares, transferred from its BVI subsidiary in December 2024) [12]. MAN Finance and Holding S.A. holds 496,198,671 shares, about 17.97%, after the 24 July 2026 block-trade transfer of 56,000,000 shares; MAN is now indirectly held by TRATON SE, the commercial-vehicle business under the Volkswagen Group [8]. About 65% of CNHTC is held by Shandong Heavy Industry Group, 70% of which is held by the Shandong Provincial SASAC, making the SASAC the ultimate controller [13]. The company holds 51.26% of the SZSE-listed subsidiary Sinotruk Jinan Truck (000951.SZ) [15].

Competition

Comparison scope and market context

Our assessment: the heavy-truck business should primarily be compared with the heavy-truck operations of FAW Jiefang, Dongfeng Commercial Vehicle, Shaanxi Automobile and Foton, distinguishing domestic sales, exports and new-energy products. The company’s global sales ranking in its 2025 annual report and domestic ranking in its interim report use different geographical scopes. The global ranking does not directly establish its domestic competitive position. [2][7]

Per CAAM data cited in the interim report, H1 2026 Chinese heavy-truck industry sales totalled about 660,900 units, up 22.59%, including 222,500 exports, up 43.02%. Subtracting exports gives domestic sales of about 438,400 units. The company’s industry-total sales share includes exports and is reported separately from its domestic-market ranking. [7]

Our assessment: strengths and weaknesses

Our assessment: export scale and the ability to develop and manufacture key assemblies form a competitive foundation. The evidence is 2025 heavy-truck exports of 153,368 units including affiliated export, and company disclosures of engine, gearbox, axle, cab and frame capabilities. Its September 2026 disclosure of 37 KD plants adds an overseas production and service network. Whether these foundations create sustained cost and service advantages still needs to be assessed market by market. [1][2][32]

Our assessment: new-energy sales growth indicates a changing product mix but does not establish a profitability advantage. New-energy heavy-truck sales rose 248.9% in 2025, while consolidated gross margin fell from 15.6% in 2024 to 15.1% and the light-truck and others segment remained loss-making. Product volumes, segment profitability and consolidated margins need to be assessed together. [1][2]

Our assessment: an expanding overseas business may increase sensitivity to external demand and exchange rates. In H1 2026 heavy-truck exports including affiliated export reached 108,351 units, exceeding domestic sales of 85,293; the annual report also identifies foreign-exchange risk. Export leadership and risk exposure can coexist, with future performance dependent on orders, collections and localisation costs. [7][2]

Outlook

Company targets and plans

In its 2025 annual report, signed on 27 March 2026, the company sets out 2026 plans for premium products, coordinated brand development and full-category exports, supported by overseas subsidiaries, parts warehouses and local factories. Its new-energy strategy puts battery electric at the core, supported by hybrid and fuel-cell technologies, alongside battery-as-a-service and integrated vehicle, energy and service solutions. Intelligent-technology plans include mid- and high-level assisted driving, autonomous driving, intelligent cabins and AI applications. These are company plans rather than completed or assured outcomes. [2]

Our analysis

Our assessment: exports and new energy remain business areas to monitor, supported by growth in both exports and new-energy heavy-truck sales in 2025, followed by further export expansion in H1 2026. Converting volume growth into higher profitability requires collections, local service capabilities and product margins to support one another. Improvement in the loss-making light-truck and others segment also matters. If pricing or localisation costs offset incremental gains, greater scale may not raise profit margins. [1][7][2]

Our assessment: multiple powertrain routes can address more operating scenarios, while requiring continued allocation of development and service resources. Given the 2025 gross-margin decline and the planned expansion of new-energy and overseas operations, segment profitability, consolidated margins and actual deployment will be the tests of progress. Management plans should not be treated as certain industry forecasts. [1][2]

Risks

  • Profitability pressure: FY2025 consolidated gross margin was 15.1%, compared with 15.6% in FY2024; the light-truck and others segment had a 1.5% operating loss margin in FY2025 [1][2].
  • Financial risk: the light-truck and others segment remained loss-making in FY2025 [2]; at the A-share subsidiary level, 2025 operating cash flow fell 89.34% year on year on higher bill-based collection and higher cash payments for goods and services [17].
  • Subsidiary leverage and working capital: the A-share subsidiary Sinotruk Jinan Truck had an end-2025 gearing ratio of 65.35%, with inventories up 79.6% and notes receivable and accounts receivable up 37.8% year on year [17].
  • Customer concentration: the A-share subsidiary's top five customers accounted for 58.36% of 2025 sales [17].
  • Foreign-exchange risk: the annual report identifies exports, imports, overseas operations and Hong Kong financing as the main sources of foreign-exchange exposure. The main non-RMB monetary assets and liabilities are in US dollars, euros and Hong Kong dollars. The company uses instruments including forward foreign-exchange contracts to manage this exposure [2].
  • Credit, interest-rate and liquidity risk: note 45 to the 2025 annual report identifies foreign-currency, interest-rate, credit and liquidity risk as the main risks arising from financial instruments and describes its management policies. Exposures include borrowings, receivables, cash and payables [2].

概览

中国重汽（香港）有限公司（港交所：03808）是中国重汽集团的香港上市主体，主营商用车及相关总成、零部件。据公司披露，2025 年重卡销量首次位居全球第一 [2]。上市集团研发和制造重卡、中重卡、轻卡及客车，以及发动机、变速箱、车桥、驾驶室、车架等关键总成与零部件，并提供金融服务 [1]。2025 财年实现营收 1095.41 亿元、归母净利润 70.19 亿元，均创历史新高；销售重卡 292,140 辆（其中出口 153,368 辆）、轻卡 123,136 辆 [1]。2026 年上半年营收同比增长 39.24% 至 708.41 亿元，归母净利润同比增长 26.22% 至 43.25 亿元；重卡销量 193,644 辆（内销 85,293 辆、出口含联营出口 108,351 辆，同比 +57.1%）[7]。

公司为国有企业：持股 51% 的控股股东为中国重型汽车集团有限公司（中国重汽集团），持有 1,408,106,603 股；最终控制方为山东省国资委——山东省国资委持有山东重工集团约 70% 股权，山东重工集团持有中国重汽集团约 65% 股权 [12][13]。德国曼公司（MAN SE）2009 年战略入股 25%+1 股；2026 年 7 月 24 日经大宗交易转让 5,600 万股后，直接持股主体 MAN Finance and Holding S.A. 持有 496,198,671 股、约 17.97% [8]。集团官网现将集团前身济南汽车制造总厂的始建年份记为 1930 年，1960 年生产黄河 JN150 重卡 [31]；学会旧介绍仍记载始建于 1956 年，本文采用现行公司口径 [29]。中国重汽集团与潍柴动力同属山东重工集团体系，双方存在发动机、零部件等方面的持续关连交易 [13]。

集团 A 股子公司中国重汽集团济南卡车（000951.SZ，由中国重汽香港持股 51.26%）生产豪沃、黄河两大重卡品牌 [15]。上市集团还包括轻型汽车品牌 VGV，其运营子公司属于上市集团合并范围 [30]。

历史沿革

  1. 1930

    按集团官网现行介绍，中国重汽集团前身济南汽车制造总厂始建于 1930 年；2026 年官方新闻采用相同年份。中国汽车工程学会旧会员介绍仍记载 1956 年，此处采用现行公司一手口径。[31][32][29]

  2. 1960

    生产中国第一辆重型汽车——黄河牌 JN150 八吨载货汽车，结束中国不能生产重型汽车的历史。[9]

  3. 1978

    以 SKD 方式组装生产罗曼卡车，至 1991 年累计生产 8000 余辆。[9]

  4. 1983

    与奥地利斯太尔-戴姆勒-普赫公司签订斯太尔 91 系列重型汽车技术引进合同，为中国政府批准的第一个重卡技术引进项目。[9]

  5. 1985

    第一辆国产斯太尔卡车在济南汽车制造总厂下线。[9]

  6. 2001-01-18

    集团官网发展历程记载，重组后的中国重汽于 1 月 18 日正式宣告成立。[33]

  7. 2002-08

    建成全国最大的重型汽车桥箱生产基地，10 月推出斯太尔王重卡。[9]

  8. 2003

    与沃尔沃就重型卡车合资项目签约，双方合资公司成立。[9]

  9. 2004

    HOWO（豪沃）系列重卡下线。[9]

  10. 2006

    重型汽车发动机生产线正式投入运行。[9]

  11. 2007-11-28

    中国重汽以红筹股形式在香港主板上市，股票代码 03808。[33]

  12. 2009

    与德国曼公司签署战略合作协议，曼公司入股中国重汽（香港），双方在整车、发动机、底盘和车桥等领域展开合作。[9]

  13. 2010

    推出轻卡产品，拓展产品型谱。[9]

  14. 2012

    推出引进曼技术国产化的 MC 系列发动机。[9]

  15. 2013

    与德国曼合作的首辆 SITRAK（汕德卡）重卡下线。[9]

  16. 2018

    公司称全球首台无人驾驶电动卡车在天津港开启试运营。[9]

  17. 2020

    全新一代黄河重卡在济南发布，重新定位为自主高端品牌。[9]

  18. 2024-12-24

    中国重汽集团完成从 BVI 子公司受让中国重汽（香港）51% 股份，成为直接控股股东。[12]

  19. 2025-06-27

    刘正涛接任董事长（原董事长王志坚辞任），刘伟获任执行董事、总裁。[2]

  20. 2025

    据公司 2025 年年报披露，2025 年重卡销量首次位居全球第一，并连续四年位居中国重卡行业第一。[2]

现状

业务结构

自 2025 年起，集团报告分部为三个：重卡（重卡、中重卡及零部件制造销售，发动机业务并入其中）、轻卡及其他、金融。2025 财年重卡分部收入 971.79 亿元（+13.7%）、轻卡及其他分部 145.50 亿元（+30.4%）、金融分部 7.44 亿元（+20.6%）[2]。重卡是核心业务，2025 年销售 292,140 辆 [1]。2026 年上半年集团销售重卡 193,644 辆，其中内销 85,293 辆（国内排名第二）、出口（含联营出口）108,351 辆 [7]。

技术与平台

集团自行研发和制造发动机、变速箱、车桥、驾驶室、车架等关键总成与零部件；MC 系列发动机源于曼技术合作。新能源方面布局纯电、混动、氢燃料三大路线。据年度业绩公告，2025 年新能源重卡销量同比增长 248.9%、新能源轻卡增长约 220%。2025 财年费用化研发费用为 29.02 亿元。 [2][9][1]

2025 年年报披露，法规版、增强版和高阶版辅助驾驶平台均已批量应用；公司称 L2+ 干线物流辅助驾驶和 L4 封闭场景无人驾驶方案已在港口、矿区及干线物流等场景商业化部署。上述为公司产品部署披露。 [2]

制造与产能

2025 年年报列示，重卡主要生产基地位于济南和济宁，轻卡生产基地主要位于济南、福建和成都。2026 年 9 月 15 日公司新闻称，其海外网络包括 37 座 KD 工厂和 1000 多个服务网点，产品销往 150 多个国家和地区。所核对资料未确认可直接比较的全球统一年产能数字。 [2][32]

国内市场地位

据公司 2025 年年报披露，2025 年重卡销量连续四年位居中国第一，并首次位居全球第一；这是公司的年度排名陈述。上市集团公告重卡销量为 292,140 辆。2026 年中期报告披露，上市集团上半年内销重卡 85,293 辆，国内市场排名第二。 [2][7]

海外业务

2025 年上市集团重卡出口（含联营出口）153,368 辆；公司披露连续 21 年居中国重卡出口首位。2026 年上半年合并口径海外收入为 298.40 亿元，含联营出口的重卡出口收入为 309.12 亿元（同比增长 54%）。两项收入采用不同统计口径。 [1][7]

2025 年年报将非洲和东南亚列为优势市场，并表示将通过海外附属公司、配件中心库与本地工厂推进本地化经营。2026 年 9 月官方新闻披露的 37 座 KD 工厂及服务网络，属于该时点的公司海外网络口径。 [2][32]

创始人与管理层

前身与重组

集团官网现将集团前身济南汽车制造总厂的始建年份记为 1930 年，2026 年官方新闻沿用该年份；中国汽车工程学会旧会员介绍则记载 1956 年。本文采用现行公司一手口径，保留两项来源的分歧，不将其解释为已经证实的不同建厂或改制事件。 [31][32][29]

集团官网发展历程记载，重组后的中国重汽于 2001 年 1 月 18 日正式宣告成立，香港上市主体于 2007 年 11 月 28 日上市。2009 年与曼开展战略合作，引进发动机和整车等技术。集团前身、重组后集团和香港上市主体的日期属于不同实体与事件口径。 [33]

竞争格局

比较范围与市场背景

我们的判断：重卡业务宜主要与一汽解放、东风商用车、陕汽及福田的重卡业务比较，并区分国内销售、出口和新能源产品。公司 2025 年年报披露的全球销量排名与中期报告中的国内排名采用不同地域口径，不能将前者当作国内竞争地位的直接证明。 [2][7]

据中期报告引用的中汽协数据，2026 年上半年中国重卡行业总销量约 66.09 万辆，同比增长 22.59%，其中出口 22.25 万辆，同比增长 43.02%；相减得到国内销量约 43.84 万辆。公司披露的行业总销量份额包含出口，与国内市场排名分别列示。 [7]

我们的判断：优势与短板

我们的判断：出口规模和关键总成研发制造能力是其竞争基础。依据是 2025 年重卡出口（含联营出口）153,368 辆，以及公司披露的发动机、变速箱、车桥、驾驶室和车架等研发制造能力；2026 年 9 月公司披露的 37 座 KD 工厂为海外服务与生产网络提供了补充。上述基础能否转化为持续的成本和服务优势，仍需按市场验证。 [1][2][32]

我们的判断：新能源销量增长表明产品结构正在变化，但不能直接证明盈利优势。2025 年新能源重卡销量增长 248.9%，同时合并毛利率从 2024 年的 15.6% 降至 15.1%，轻卡及其他分部仍亏损。应同时观察产品放量、分部盈利与合并毛利率，而非以单一销量增速作结论。 [1][2]

我们的判断：海外业务扩大可能增加对外部需求和汇率变化的敏感度。2026 年上半年含联营出口的重卡出口销量为 108,351 辆，高于内销 85,293 辆；年报也明确列示外汇风险。出口领先与风险暴露可以同时存在，未来表现取决于订单、收款和本地化成本。 [7][2]

未来展望

公司目标与规划

公司在 2026 年 3 月 27 日签署的 2025 年年报中提出，2026 年将推进高端化、多品牌协同与全品类出海，加速海外附属公司注册、配件中心库运营和本地化工厂落地。新能源规划以纯电动为核心，以混动和燃料电池为支撑，推广车电分离及“车辆+能源+服务”一体化方案；智能化规划包括中高阶辅助驾驶、无人驾驶、智能座舱及人工智能应用。以上为公司计划，不代表已经完成或必然实现。 [2]

我们的分析

我们的判断：出口与新能源仍是值得跟踪的业务方向，依据是 2025 年出口销量和新能源重卡销量均增长，以及 2026 年上半年出口继续扩大。但销量增长能否转为更高盈利，需要出口收款、当地服务能力与产品毛利共同支撑；轻卡及其他分部能否改善亏损也会影响合并结果。若产品价格或本地化成本抵消增量收益，规模扩张可能未必提高利润率。 [1][7][2]

我们的判断：同时推进多种动力路线可以覆盖更多运营场景，也需要持续分配研发和服务资源。鉴于 2025 年毛利率下降且公司拟继续扩展新能源与海外体系，我们将以分部盈利、合并毛利率及实际部署进展检验转型效果，不把管理层规划写成确定的行业预测。 [1][2]

风险

  • 盈利压力：2025 财年合并毛利率为 15.1%，2024 财年为 15.6%；轻卡及其他分部 2025 财年营运亏损率为 1.5% [1][2]。
  • 财务风险：轻卡及其他分部 2025 财年仍亏损 [2]；A 股子公司层面 2025 年经营活动现金流量净额同比大降 89.34%，系票据回款增加及购买商品、接受劳务支付现金增加所致 [17]。
  • 子公司杠杆与营运资金：A 股子公司中国重汽集团济南卡车 2025 年末资产负债率 65.35%，存货同比增长 79.6%、应收票据及应收账款同比增长 37.8% [17]。
  • 客户集中：A 股子公司前五大客户 2025 年销售占比 58.36% [17]。
  • 外汇风险：年报明确指出，出口、进口、境外经营和香港融资形成主要外汇风险，主要非人民币货币资产与负债涉及美元、欧元和港元。公司采用远期外汇合约等进行管理 [2]。
  • 信贷、利率与流动资金风险：2025 年年报附注 45 将外汇、利率、信贷及流动资金列为金融工具的主要风险，并披露管理政策；风险敞口涉及借款、应收款项、现金及应付款项等 [2]。
