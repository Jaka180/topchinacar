Overview

Dongfeng Motor Co., Ltd. (东风汽车有限公司, DFL) is a whole-vehicle joint venture. Its direct shareholders are Dongfeng Motor Group Company Limited and Nissan (China) Investment Co., Ltd. (NCIC, a wholly owned subsidiary of Nissan Motor Co., Ltd.), each holding 50% [6][28]. It was formed under an agreement signed on 19 September 2002, formally established on 9 June 2003 and in operation from 1 July 2003, with registered capital of RMB 16.7 billion and a 50-year term; at its founding it was the largest Sino-foreign whole-vehicle joint venture in China's auto industry [1][2]. Headquartered in Wuhan, it comprises three business segments: Dongfeng Nissan Passenger Vehicle Company (operating the Nissan, Venucia and Infiniti passenger-vehicle brands), Zhengzhou Nissan Automobile Co., Ltd. (light commercial vehicles under the Nissan and Dongfeng brands) and Dongfeng Auto Parts (Group) Co., Ltd. [3]. In 2025, Nissan's China sales (passenger plus light commercial vehicles) totalled 653,024 units, of which the three Dongfeng Nissan passenger-vehicle brands accounted for 600,959 units [3]. After peaking at 1.6 million vehicles of annual production and sales in 2018, DFL has entered a period of multi-year volume contraction running in parallel with its NEV (N-series) and export transition [1].

History

  1. 2002-09-19

    Dongfeng and Nissan sign a long-term comprehensive cooperation agreement in Beijing, forming Dongfeng Motor Co., Ltd. on a 50:50 basis with registered capital of RMB 16.7 billion and a 50-year term — at the time the largest auto JV project in China.[1][2]

  2. 2003-06-09

    Dongfeng Motor Co., Ltd. is formally established in Wuhan.[1][2]

  3. 2003-06-16

    Dongfeng Nissan Passenger Vehicle Company, the passenger-vehicle arm of the JV, is established.[16]

  4. 2003-07-01

    Dongfeng Motor Co., Ltd. begins formal operations with about 74,000 employees; Dongfeng's Miao Wei becomes chairman and Nissan's Katsuji Nakamura becomes president. The first passenger car, the Sunny, goes on sale in June.[2]

  5. 2010-09

    Dongfeng Nissan releases its own brand 'Venucia' (启辰), moving to dual-brand operation.[16]

  6. 2014-11

    The Xiangyang plant begins local production of Infiniti-brand models.[16]

  7. 2018

    Dongfeng Motor Co., Ltd. reaches annual production and sales of 1.6 million vehicles across Dongfeng, Nissan, Infiniti and Venucia brands. Separately, Dongfeng Nissan's full-year retail (terminal) sales reached 1,166,696 units, up 3.9% and a record high.[1][24]

  8. 2020-11-18

    The Qashqai rolls off the line and the Changzhou plant formally starts production.[17]

  9. 2024-06

    On 21 June Nissan announces the closure of the Changzhou passenger-vehicle plant — its first passenger-car plant closure in China — with annual capacity of about 130,000 units. On 16 June Dongfeng Nissan launches the 'New Endeavour 100' plan. The Dongfeng group report says the plan will put seven new NEV models on the market by the end of 2026. A Huadu government page reprinting Dongfeng Nissan's own text sets R&D spending above RMB 10 billion over three years, a medium- to long-term goal of raising the R&D team above 4,000 people, and an initial export target of 100,000 units a year. That text does not say the team was growing from 1,600.[17][29][30]

  10. 2024-12

    On 11 December 2024 Nissan appoints Stephen Ma (马智欣), then CFO, chairman of the Nissan China Management Committee effective 1 January 2025, succeeding Shohei Yamazaki. The announcement does not appoint him president of Dongfeng Motor Co., Ltd.[5]

  11. 2025-04-27

    The N7, the first NEV strategic model built on the Tianyan (天演) architecture and the first China-developed all-electric sedan under the Nissan brand, goes on sale at RMB 119,900–149,900.[12]

  12. 2025-06-25

    Dongfeng Motor Group Company Limited signs a joint-venture contract with NCIC (Nissan's wholly owned subsidiary) to set up a company that will export vehicles, parts and accessories to agreed overseas markets. Registered capital is RMB 1 billion, Dongfeng holds 40% and NCIC 60%, and the term is 28 years.[6]

  13. 2025-10

    The Teana HarmonySpace edition rolls off the Xiangyang production line; local media described it as 'the world's first fuel vehicle with a HarmonyOS cockpit', marking the implementation of Dongfeng Nissan's 'oil-electric same intelligence' (油电同智) strategy.[18]

  14. 2025-11

    Nissan Import & Export (Guangzhou) Co., Ltd. is inaugurated at the China International Import Expo; the first export models are the N7 and the Frontier Pro PHEV pickup.[20]

  15. 2025-12-01

    The N6 plug-in hybrid sedan goes on sale and deliveries begin.[3]

  16. 2026-05-25

    Dongfeng Motor Co., Ltd. recalls 68,265 N7 and N6 vehicles over an accelerator-pedal design defect (49,465 N7 and 18,800 N6 units).[9][19]

  17. 2026-06-12

    The 100,000th N-series NEV rolls off the line in Guangzhou, 14 months after the first model's launch.[26]

  18. 2026-08-14

    Nie Chunfei replaces Zhou Feng as deputy general manager of Dongfeng Nissan Passenger Vehicle Company, becoming the Chinese-side head of the JV.[25][14]

Current operations

Business structure

DFL now consists of three business segments: Dongfeng Nissan Passenger Vehicle Company (operating the Nissan, Venucia and Infiniti brands), Zhengzhou Nissan Automobile Co., Ltd. (light commercial vehicles under the Nissan and Dongfeng brands) and Dongfeng Auto Parts (Group) Co., Ltd., which handles research, manufacturing and sales of automotive equipment and components [3]. At its founding DFL was China's first Sino-foreign JV covering the full range of trucks, buses, light commercial vehicles and passenger cars [2]. Nissan China's January 2026 release describes the present business as the three segments above [3].

Sales and current status

In 2025, Nissan's China sales (passenger plus light commercial vehicles) reached 653,024 units, down about 6.3% from 696,631 in 2024 (calculated from the two official releases) [3][4]; the three Dongfeng Nissan passenger-vehicle brands accounted for 600,959 units — Nissan China's release says 销量 and does not say retail — of which the Sylphy alone sold 320,000 (more than half), and Dongfeng Nissan's NEV sales surpassed 50,000 for the year [3]. On Zhengzhou Nissan, that release says full-year sales rose 39% and gives no absolute total; December sales were 5,603 units, up 28% [3]. The HKEX express report's 销售量 column lists Zhengzhou Nissan at 52,140 units for the year, against 39,902 a year earlier, up 30.67%; December sales volume is also 5,603, against 4,576 (about 22.4% on those two figures, which does not match the 28% in the Nissan release). The two A-level series are shown side by side [3][10]. The same express report lists Dongfeng Nissan (including Dongfeng Infiniti and Venucia) sales volume at 600,006 units, down 4.94% (2024 sales volume 631,155). The column is headed 销售量 and does not say wholesale. December sales volume in that column is 53,784 units, which is not the same figure as the 52,344 units in Nissan China's December release [10][3].

According to the Economic Observer, Dongfeng Nissan passenger-vehicle annual sales have fallen for a seventh consecutive year: over 1.16 million in 2018, below one million in 2022, 723,100 in 2023, 631,200 in 2024 and about 600,900 in 2025 [25]. For 2018, China News Service separately reported full-year retail (terminal) sales of 1,166,696 units, up 3.9% and a record high [24]; for 2024, the express report's 销售量 column shows 631,155 units [10].

In the first half of 2026, Dongfeng Nissan sold 196,000 units, down 19.93% year on year, of which the Sylphy was 100,500 units (about half) [25]. In July 2026, Nissan's China sales totalled 23,677 units, down 58.7% year on year, including 21,702 units for Dongfeng Nissan (three brands) and 1,975 for Zhengzhou Nissan [8].

Technology and NEV platform

Dongfeng Nissan has built its N-series line-up on the locally developed Tianyan (天演) NEV architecture: the N7, its first model, is a pure-electric sedan (launched 27 April 2025 at RMB 119,900–149,900), followed by the N6 PHEV sedan (on sale and in delivery from 1 December 2025) and the NX8 SUV with BEV and EREV powertrains (cumulative deliveries over 10,000 in H1 2026) [12][3][23]. The 100,000th N-series NEV rolled off the line in Guangzhou on 12 June 2026, 14 months after the first model's launch [26]; N-series NEV sales grew 192% in H1 2026 [23]. On intelligence, the N7 carries a Qualcomm Snapdragon 8295P chip, the NISSAN OS system and an end-to-end assisted-driving system co-developed with Momenta, measuring 4,930×1,895×1,487 mm with a 2,915 mm wheelbase [12]. In cooperation with Huawei, the Teana HarmonySpace edition rolled off the Xiangyang line in October 2025; local media described it as 'the world's first fuel vehicle with a HarmonyOS cockpit', under the 'oil-electric same intelligence' (油电同智) route [18].

Plants and capacity

Dongfeng Nissan Passenger Vehicle Company grew from bases in Huadu (Guangzhou), Xiangyang, Zhengzhou and Dalian; the Xiangyang plant has built Infiniti models locally since November 2014 [16]. On 18 November 2020 the Qashqai rolled off the line and the Changzhou plant formally started production. A June 2024 report, citing the company's website, describes six production bases (Guangzhou, Xiangyang, Zhengzhou, Dalian, Changzhou and Wuhan) and annual vehicle capacity of 1.6 million units; that description is not dated to 18 November 2020 [17]. As sales shrank, Nissan announced the closure of the Changzhou plant in June 2024 (annual capacity about 130,000 units) — its first passenger-car plant closure in China — with production transferred to other bases [17].

Overseas business and exports

On 1 April 2026, the first Frontier Pro pickups were dispatched in a ceremony at Zhengzhou Nissan [13]. In the first quarter of Nissan's FY2026, the first 900 Frontier Pro pickups departed Shanghai Port for Latin America; on 9 September a second batch sailed to the Gulf region, and on 11 September the first N7s and Frontier Pros left for Southeast Asia [7]. Under the export plan, the N7 goes to Latin America and Southeast Asia, the Frontier Pro to Latin America, Southeast Asia and the Middle East, and the NX8 and other models are planned for specific global markets [7]. The export vehicle Nissan Import & Export (Guangzhou) Co., Ltd. was set up by Dongfeng Motor Group Co., Ltd. (40%) and NCIC, a wholly owned Nissan subsidiary (60%), with RMB 1 billion in registered capital, and was inaugurated during the November 2025 China International Import Expo. The HKEX announcement says the business is exporting vehicles, parts and accessories to agreed overseas markets [6][20].

Founders and leadership

Founder

As a joint venture, DFL has no single entrepreneur-founder; it was created by a 50:50 cooperation agreement between the state-owned Dongfeng group and Nissan [1].

Current management

  • Stephen Ma (马智欣) — member of Nissan's global executive committee and chairman of Nissan China, and president of Dongfeng Motor Co., Ltd. [13]. A Nissan China release dated 31 May 2025 identifies him as chairman of the Nissan China Management Committee and president of Dongfeng Motor Co., Ltd. [11]. On 11 December 2024 Nissan named him, then its chief financial officer, chairman of the Nissan China Management Committee effective 1 January 2025, succeeding Shohei Yamazaki and reporting directly to the CEO; that announcement does not appoint him president [5]. The 2 April 2026 Xinhua report names Liu Xinyu (刘新宇) as general manager of Nissan (China) Investment Co., Ltd. [13]. According to media reports (Securities Times), he served as a DFL vice president from 2012, returned to Nissan's headquarters in 2018 and was CFO from November 2019 to November 2024 [21].
  • Nie Chunfei (聂春飞) — vice president of Dongfeng Motor Co., Ltd. and deputy general manager of Dongfeng Nissan Passenger Vehicle Company (the Chinese-side head), appointed on 14 August 2026 to succeed Zhou Feng [25][14]. According to the Economic Observer, Nie worked early in his career in R&D and procurement at Dongfeng Nissan and was deputy head of its procurement division; in 2024 he was posted to Wuhan as party secretary and deputy general manager of Zhixin Technology, Dongfeng's NEV core-component platform, becoming its chairman and party secretary in January 2025; in September 2025 he returned to Dongfeng Nissan as a Dongfeng Motor Co., Ltd. vice president and assistant to the Dongfeng Nissan general manager, before this promotion to deputy general manager; he also concurrently serves as deputy general manager of the components division of Dongfeng Motor Group Company Limited [25].
  • Zhou Feng (周锋) (former Chinese-side head) — served as deputy general manager of Dongfeng Nissan Passenger Vehicle Company from December 2023; in July 2025 he was promoted to deputy general manager and member of the standing party committee of Dongfeng Motor Group while retaining the Dongfeng Nissan role; from 14 August 2026 he no longer holds the Dongfeng Nissan post after Nie Chunfei succeeded him [25]. A Dongfeng Motor Group website report of October 2026 confirms his current title as member of the standing party committee and deputy general manager of Dongfeng [14].

Ownership and governance

DFL's registered capital is RMB 16.7 billion [1]. Its direct shareholders are Dongfeng Motor Group Company Limited and Nissan (China) Investment Co., Ltd. (NCIC, a wholly owned subsidiary of Nissan), each holding 50% [6][28]. The HKEX announcement of 25 June 2025 says Dongfeng Motor Co., Ltd. is jointly controlled by the listed company and NCIC, and that under the listing rules it must be regulated in the same way as a subsidiary [6]. The cited filings do not name a single actual controller.

Competition

Our judgement. Dongfeng Nissan's main domestic competitors are the other Sino-Japanese joint ventures — GAC Toyota, FAW Toyota, GAC Honda and Dongfeng Honda — as well as self-owned-brand NEV makers such as BYD, Geely, Changan and Chery. The evidence: in 2025 the three Dongfeng Nissan brands sold 600,959 units, with the Sylphy alone at 320,000, a concentration on a single model that leaves it exposed as petrol demand shrinks [3]. Its relative strength is a full value-chain footprint — local R&D, multiple production bases, a dealer network built over two decades and Nissan's global channels — which it is now using to develop the N-series locally and export China-made models [7]. Its weaknesses are a seventh consecutive year of declining sales (from over 1.16 million in 2018 to about 600,900 in 2025 [25]) and the fact that the N-series, despite growing 192% in H1 2026, has not yet offset the fall in petrol-car volume [23].

Outlook

Company plans. On 16 June 2024 Dongfeng Nissan launched the 'New Endeavour 100' plan [29][30]. The Dongfeng group report says the plan will put seven new NEV models on the market by the end of 2026 [29]. A Huadu government page reprinting Dongfeng Nissan's own text sets R&D spending above RMB 10 billion over three years, a medium- to long-term goal of raising the R&D team above 4,000 people, and an initial export target of 100,000 units a year; it does not say the team was growing from 1,600 [30]. Nissan said it is bringing in a line-up of sedans, SUVs and pickups, with petrol, battery-electric, plug-in hybrid and range-extender powertrains. Separately, it said that by summer 2027 it will launch 10 new-energy vehicles in China, nine of them Nissan-brand NEVs [11].

Our judgement. The export pivot is the most concrete near-term change: in 2026 the first 900 Frontier Pro pickups left for Latin America and the first N7s and Frontier Pros for Southeast Asia, giving the JV a way to use idle capacity and Nissan a lower-cost NEV supply for overseas markets [7]; whether this offsets the domestic decline depends on demand in target markets and on trade barriers beyond the JV's control. The N-series reached 100,000 cumulative units in 14 months and grew 192% in H1 2026, but from a small base and against a continuing fall in petrol-car sales, so overall volume stabilisation is not yet proven [26][23]. Separately, Nissan posted a net loss of 670.9 billion yen in FY2024 and is executing the 'Re:Nissan' restructuring (about 20,000 job cuts and seven plant closures); whether the parent's financial strain affects investment and product support in China is a variable to watch in assessing the transition's sustainability [15]. The May 2026 recall of 68,265 N7 and N6 vehicles is an early quality signal as the locally developed NEV line-up scales up [9].

Risks

  • Prolonged sales decline. Dongfeng Nissan passenger-vehicle sales have fallen for a seventh consecutive year, from over 1.16 million in 2018 to about 600,900 in 2025 [25]; in July 2026 Nissan's China sales were 23,677 units, down 58.7% year on year [8].
  • Reliance on a single model. In 2025 the Sylphy accounted for 320,000 of 600,959 units (about 53%), and in H1 2026 it was still about half of volume (100,500 units) [3][25].
  • Quality risk in the NEV ramp. In May 2026 the JV recalled 68,265 N7 and N6 vehicles over an accelerator-pedal design defect (49,465 N7 and 18,800 N6 units) [9].
  • Nissan's global restructuring and losses. Nissan announced the 'Re:Nissan' revival plan in May 2025; media (People's Daily Japan, citing Kyodo) reported plans to cut about 20,000 jobs and close seven plants globally, after a net loss of 670.9 billion yen in FY2024 [15][3].
  • Overcapacity and asset restructuring. The Changzhou plant (about 130,000 units of annual capacity) was closed in 2024, evidence of capacity pressure as sales shrank from a 1.6-million-unit footprint [17].
  • Export trade barriers. The export strategy targets Southeast Asia, the Middle East and Latin America, exposing it to tariff and non-tariff barriers on China-made vehicles [7].

概览

东风汽车有限公司（Dongfeng Motor Co., Ltd.，简称 DFL）是整车合资企业，直接股东为东风汽车集团股份有限公司与日产（中国）投资有限公司（NCIC，日产汽车公司的全资子公司），各持股 50%[6][28]。公司于 2002 年 9 月 19 日签约、2003 年 6 月 9 日成立、同年 7 月 1 日运营，注册资本 167 亿元人民币、合资期限 50 年，成立时为中国汽车行业规模最大的中外合资整车项目[1][2]。合资公司总部设于武汉，旗下包括东风日产乘用车公司（运营日产、启辰、英菲尼迪三个乘用车品牌）、郑州日产汽车有限公司（日产、东风双品牌轻型商用车）和东风汽车零部件（集团）有限公司三大业务板块[3]。2025 年，日产汽车中国区（乘用车＋轻型商用车）销量 653,024 台，其中东风日产三个乘用车品牌合计 600,959 台[3]。DFL 在 2018 年达到年产销 160 万辆的峰值后，进入销量多年收缩与新能源（N 序列）、出口转型并行的阶段[1]。

现状

业务结构

DFL 现由三大业务板块构成：东风日产乘用车公司运营日产、启辰、英菲尼迪三个乘用车品牌；郑州日产汽车有限公司运营日产、东风双品牌轻型商用车；东风汽车零部件（集团）有限公司负责汽车装备与汽车零部件的研发、制造与销售[3]。成立之初，DFL 是国内首家同时拥有卡车、客车、轻型商用车与乘用车全系列产品的中外合资汽车企业[2]。日产中国 2026 年 1 月稿将当前业务写为上述三大板块[3]。

销量与现状

2025 年，日产汽车中国区（乘用车＋轻型商用车）销量 653,024 台，较 2024 年的 696,631 台下降约 6.3%（据官方两稿数字计算）[3][4]；其中东风日产三个乘用车品牌合计销量 600,959 台（日产中国稿写「销量」，未写「零售」），仅轩逸一款全年即 32 万台（占比过半），东风日产新能源车型全年累计突破 5 万台[3]。郑州日产方面，日产中国稿只写全年累计销量同比增长 39%，没有绝对台数，并写 12 月销量 5,603 台、同比增长 28%[3]。东风集团产销快报的「销售量」栏写郑州日产全年 52,140 辆、同期 39,902 辆、累计同比 +30.67%；12 月销售量同为 5,603 辆、同期 4,576 辆（按两数计算约 +22.4%，与日产稿的当月 +28% 不一致）。两份均为 A 级来源，数字并列[3][10]。同一快报中，东风日产（含东风英菲尼迪、启辰）销售量 600,006 台、同比下降 4.94%（2024 年销售量 631,155 台）；表头为「销售量」，未写「批发」。2025 年 12 月该栏为 53,784 台，与日产中国稿的 52,344 台不是同一列数[10][3]。

据经济观察报，东风日产乘用车年销量已连续第七年下滑：2018 年超 116 万辆、2022 年跌破百万辆、2023 年 72.31 万辆、2024 年 63.12 万辆、2025 年约 60.09 万辆[25]。其中 2018 年终端销量另有中新网报道为 1,166,696 辆（同比＋3.9%，为当年新高）[24]；2024 年产销快报销售量为 631,155 辆[10]。

2026 年上半年，东风日产累计销量 19.60 万辆、同比下滑 19.93%，其中轩逸 10.05 万辆（约占一半）[25]。7 月，日产汽车中国区销量 23,677 台、同比下降 58.7%，其中东风日产（三品牌）21,702 台、郑州日产 1,975 台[8]。

技术平台与新能源

东风日产以本土研发的「天演」新能源架构为基础布局 N 序列：N7 为该架构首款纯电轿车（2025 年 4 月 27 日上市，售价 11.99 万–14.99 万元），随后推出插混轿车 N6（2025 年 12 月 1 日上市并交付）与纯电/增程双动力 SUV NX8（2026 年上半年累计交付破万）[12][3][23]。N 序列累计第 10 万辆整车于 2026 年 6 月 12 日在广州下线，从首款车上市起用时 14 个月[26]；2026 年上半年 N 序列新能源销量同比增长 192%[23]。智能化方面，N7 搭载高通骁龙 8295P 芯片、NISSAN OS 系统及与 Momenta 协同开发的一段式端到端组合驾驶辅助，长宽高 4930×1895×1487mm、轴距 2915mm[12]。东风日产与华为合作，2025 年 10 月在襄阳工厂量产下线天籁·鸿蒙座舱，当地报道称其为全球首款鸿蒙座舱燃油车，由此推出「油电同智」路线[18]。

工厂与产能

东风日产乘用车公司由广州花都、襄阳、郑州、大连等基地起家，襄阳工厂自 2014 年 11 月起国产英菲尼迪车型[16]。2020 年 11 月 18 日，逍客下线，常州工厂正式投产。2024 年 6 月的一篇报道称，按官网信息，东风日产有广州、襄阳、郑州、大连、常州、武汉六个生产基地，整车年产能 160 万辆；该表述没有系到 2020 年 11 月 18 日[17]。因销量收缩，日产 2024 年 6 月宣布关闭常州工厂（年产能约 13 万辆），为其在华首次关闭乘用车工厂，其生产转移至其他基地[17]。

海外业务与出口

2026 年 4 月 1 日，锋坦 Frontier Pro 首批车辆出海发车仪式在郑州日产举行[13]。2026 财年第一季度，首批 900 台锋坦 Frontier Pro 从上海港启航驶向拉美；9 月 9 日第二批锋坦发往海湾地区，9 月 11 日首批 N7 与锋坦起航发往东南亚[7]。按规划，N7 出口拉美、东南亚，锋坦面向拉美、东南亚和中东，NX8 及其他车型将规划出口至全球特定市场[7]。出口主体日产进出口（广州）有限公司由东风汽车集团股份（40%）与日产全资子公司 NCIC（60%）共同出资 10 亿元设立，2025 年 11 月进博会期间揭牌。港交所公告写明，业务为向协商一致的海外市场出口车辆、零部件及配件[6][20]。

  • 周锋（前任中方负责人）——自 2023 年 12 月起任东风日产乘用车公司副总经理；2025 年 7 月升任东风汽车集团副总经理、党委常委后仍兼任该职；2026 年 8 月 14 日起由聂春飞接替、不再兼任[25]。东风汽车集团官网 2026 年 10 月的报道确认其现为东风公司党委常委、副总经理[14]。

股权与治理

DFL 注册资本 167 亿元[1]。直接股东为东风汽车集团股份有限公司与日产（中国）投资有限公司（NCIC，日产全资子公司），各持股 50%[6][28]。2025 年 6 月 25 日港交所公告写明，东风汽车有限公司由上市公司与 NCIC 共同控制，并按上市规则以与附属公司相同的方式监管[6]。所引公告未认定单一实际控制人。

竞争格局

我们的判断：东风日产在国内的主要竞争对手包括广汽丰田、一汽丰田、广汽本田、东风本田等日系合资企业，以及比亚迪、吉利、长安、奇瑞等自主新能源车企。依据是：2025 年东风日产三个品牌销量 600,959 台，其中轩逸一款即占 32 万台，对单一车型的高度依赖使其在燃油车需求收缩时承压[3]。其相对优势是具备完整全价值链布局——本地研发、多个生产基地、二十余年积累的经销网络以及日产全球渠道——现正以此本地化开发 N 序列，并把中国产车型出口海外[7]。其短板在于销量连续第七年下滑（由 2018 年超 116 万辆降至 2025 年约 60.09 万辆[25]），且 N 序列虽在 2026 年上半年增长 192%，尚不足以抵消燃油车下滑[23]。

未来展望

公司公开规划。2024 年 6 月 16 日，东风日产发布「新奋斗100」行动计划[29][30]。东风集团官网稿写明，计划在 2026 年底前投放 7 款全新新能源产品[29]。花都区政府转载的东风日产原文写：未来三年研发投入超过 100 亿元，中长期将研发团队提升到 4000 人以上，初期出口目标设定为每年 10 万辆；原文没有「由 1600 人扩至 4000 人」[30]。日产中国 2025 年 5 月 31 日稿分两句：前一句是产品组合涵盖轿车、SUV、皮卡，以及燃油、纯电、插混、增程；后一句是到 2027 年夏季将在中国市场投放 10 款新能源汽车，其中日产品牌新能源汽车 9 款。10 款指新能源汽车，不含燃油[11]。

我们的判断。出口转向是近期最具体的改变：2026 年首批 900 台锋坦 Frontier Pro 已发往拉美，首批 N7 与锋坦已发往东南亚，为合资公司提供了消化闲置产能的途径，也为日产提供了更低成本的新能源海外供应[7]；但其能否对冲国内下滑，取决于目标市场需求与关税壁垒等合资公司难以控制的因素。N 序列累计第 10 万辆下线用时 14 个月、2026 年上半年增长 192%，但基数尚小，且燃油车销量仍在下降，整体销量企稳尚未得到验证[26][23]。此外，日产全球 2024 财年净亏损 6,709 亿日元、正推进「Re:Nissan」重组（裁员约 2 万人、关闭 7 家工厂），母公司财务压力是否会影响在华投资与产品支持，是观察其转型可持续性的一个变量[15]。2026 年 5 月对 68,265 辆 N7、N6 的召回，是本地化研发新能源产品放量过程中需关注的质量信号[9]。

风险

  • 销量持续下滑。东风日产乘用车年销量连续第七年下滑，由 2018 年超 116 万辆降至 2025 年约 60.09 万辆[25]；2026 年 7 月日产汽车中国区销量 23,677 台、同比下降 58.7%[8]。
  • 单一车型依赖。2025 年 600,959 台销量中轩逸一款占 32 万台（约 53%），2026 年上半年轩逸 10.05 万辆、仍约占整体一半[3][25]。
  • 新能源放量中的质量风险。2026 年 5 月，合资公司因油门踏板总成设计缺陷召回 N7、N6 共 68,265 辆（N7 49,465 辆、N6 18,800 辆）[9]。
  • 日产全球重组与亏损。日产 2025 年 5 月发布「Re:Nissan」复兴计划，媒体（人民网转载共同社）报道拟在全球裁员约 2 万人并关闭 7 家工厂，2024 财年净亏损 6,709 亿日元[15][3]。
  • 产能过剩与资产重组。2024 年关闭常州工厂（年产能约 13 万辆），反映销量由 160 万辆产能规模收缩带来的产能压力[17]。
  • 出口贸易壁垒。出口战略面向东南亚、中东、拉美市场，面临各市场对中国产整车的关税与非关税壁垒[7]。
