How to read these numbers口径说明

  • Monthly tables group the reported basis labels and default to company-name order, without ranks. Company reports may cover different scopes. Rankings require an explicitly shared sales basis, other than company reports.月度数据按公告口径标签分表，默认按公司名排序，不列名次。公司公告的统计范围可能不同；只有明确相同的销量口径、且不是公司公告的数据才展示排名。
  • Domestic retail, wholesale, deliveries and exports are kept apart and are never added together.国内零售、批发、交付、出口分开列示，互不合并。
  • A missing value shows “—”. Missing is not zero.缺失显示「—」。缺失不是零。
  • Rows marked “Preliminary” may still be revised by the company later.标了「初步数据」的数字，公司后续可能修正。
  • Every row carries its basis and a source link, so you can check the original.每行都带口径和来源链接，可以直接核对原文。
