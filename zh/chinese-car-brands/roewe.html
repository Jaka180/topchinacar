Overview

Roewe (荣威) is SAIC Motor's first self-owned passenger-car brand, launched in October 2006 as a medium-to-high-end self-owned marque and built on technology SAIC acquired from Britain's MG Rover [1][7]. It is operated by SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle, the passenger-car unit of the listed SAIC Motor Corporation (Shanghai Stock Exchange 600104) that also runs the MG brand; the group's ultimate controller is the Shanghai State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission [3].

Today Roewe is positioned as SAIC's mainstream domestic family brand, spanning fuel, plug-in hybrid, extended-range and pure-electric models, while MG is positioned as the group's young, global brand [14][4]. After peaking at about 460,000 units in 2018 (Yicai), Roewe's sales fell for several years before recovering to about 177,000 units of domestic retail in 2025, up 25% year-on-year [24][10]. In 2026, its 20th anniversary, the brand is pursuing an AI-native product push led by the Jiayue 07, its first AI-native extended-range SUV, launched on 23 October 2026 at a guide price of RMB127,800-152,800 [13].

SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle executives have said Roewe has a solid technology base in the DMH super hybrid and needs to polish the brand with technology rather than compete only on price [12]. Brand general manager Qian Yang said Roewe will keep cultivating the RMB150,000-class mainstream family market and pursue technology parity [25]. Brand-level financials are not separately disclosed; Roewe's results are consolidated within SAIC Motor [3].

History

  1. 2004

    SAIC acquires Rover intellectual property from Britain's MG Rover in stages: about GBP37 million in 2004 for the Rover 75, Rover 25 and K-series engine IP, and about GBP30 million more in January 2005. Media differ on whether the second payment was for additional IP or financial support.[19]

  2. 2006-10

    SAIC announces the Roewe brand name on 12 October 2006, positioning it as a medium-to-high-end self-owned marque.[1][7][17]

  3. 2006-11

    The first model, the Roewe 750 (built on the Rover 75 platform), debuts at the 2006 Beijing International Auto Show.[1]

  4. 2007-01

    On 31 January 2007 the Roewe 750's three 2.5L variants are priced at RMB231,800 / 243,800 / 276,800 and orders open.[18]

  5. 2008

    The Roewe 550 makes its debut at the Shanghai International Auto Show.[1]

  6. 2010-04

    The fully self-developed Roewe 350 is released; it later becomes the brand's volume sedan in the RMB70,000-140,000 band.[1][24]

  7. 2011-08

    The Roewe W5, the brand's first SUV, is released.[1]

  8. 2011-11

    The Roewe E50 pure-electric car is released (the brand's official history dates it to November 2011; another media account places the launch in 2012).[1][12]

  9. 2016-07

    The Roewe RX5, marketed as the world's first production internet car, is launched with Alibaba's YunOS for Car (later Banma); it sells 237,000 units in its first full year (2017).[12]

  10. 2018

    Roewe reaches its sales peak of about 460,000 units, up 20% (Yicai). That year it also launches the Marvel X high-end EV priced at RMB260,000-310,000, which sells only about 5,000 units over two years.[24]

  11. 2019

    As the market weakens, then-SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle general manager Wang Xiaoqiu shifts to a downmarket strategy, launching models priced from about RMB60,000.[24]

  12. 2020

    Roewe introduces the R badge for mid-to-high-end new-energy vehicles, alongside the refreshed lion badge, forming a dual-badge strategy.[24]

  13. 2021-10

    The R brand is spun off as Feifan Auto, an independent company with registered capital of RMB7 billion focusing on mid-to-high-end smart EVs.[8][9]

  14. 2023-11-08

    The Roewe D7, first model of the D-family series, is launched (D7 DMH at RMB121,800-141,800 and D7 EV at RMB143,800-170,800 launch benefit prices).[11]

  15. 2024-10-28

    SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle announces the merger of Roewe and Feifan; Feifan returns to the Roewe lineup.[8][9]

  16. 2024-10

    The Roewe D7 DMH World Champion Edition sets a Guinness World Record of 2,208 km on a single full tank and charge, with average fuel consumption of 2.49 L/100km.[20]

  17. 2025

    Roewe's domestic retail sales recover to about 177,000 units, up 25% year-on-year.[10]

  18. 2026-03-10

    At the spring media briefing, Roewe announces its 20th-anniversary plan: seven new models in 2026 at roughly one per quarter, plus an AI-native high-end series positioned above RMB200,000.[10][21]

  19. 2026-07-28

    Lu Xiao, previously general manager of SAIC-GM, replaces Wang Jun as general manager of SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle, overseeing both Roewe and MG.[22]

  20. 2026-10-23

    The Roewe Jiayue 07, the first AI-native extended-range SUV of the new Jiayue series, is launched with a guide price of RMB127,800-152,800 (limited-time benefit price RMB119,800-144,800).[13][26]

Current operations

Positioning and business structure

Roewe is SAIC's mainstream domestic family brand; management has set a clear division of labour in which MG targets young, global buyers while Roewe focuses on mainstream domestic family users [14]. The in-sale lineup on the official site spans the i5 and i6 fuel sedans, the pure-electric D6, the D7 EV and D7 DMH, the M7 DMH, the iMAX8 MPV family, the new R7 and 2025 F7 (the former Feifan models), and the Jiayue 07 extended-range SUV [4]. Most of its new-energy models sit in the RMB70,000-150,000 household band [10]. With the i6 launched in April 2026 the brand has returned to the entry fuel-sedan band, where the i6 carries a 15.6-inch 2.5K screen and a Qualcomm Snapdragon 8155 chip [10].

Technology and platforms

Roewe draws on SAIC's shared technology base. The DMH super hybrid, used by the D7 DMH and M7 DMH, features a five-in-one powertrain control unit (PICU) and a P1+P3 dual-motor hybrid transmission, and was named a 2023 top-ten engine/hybrid system by the China Heart awards [20]. SAIC's plug-in hybrid line, which the DMH system continues, won a second prize of the State Science and Technology Advancement Award in 2017; in October 2024 the D7 DMH World Champion Edition set a Guinness World Record of 2,208 km on a single tank and charge, with average fuel consumption of 2.49 L/100km [20]. The D7 EV is built on the Nebula pure-electric platform and uses the Rubik's Cube battery [11]. In the cockpit, the brand has moved from the RX5's Banma system in 2016 to the Jiayue 07's Doubao cockpit assistant, developed with ByteDance's Volcano Engine, plus the Momenta R7 world model and XHEART X7 chip on the CPP AI-native architecture [26].

Sales and market position

Roewe's annual sales peaked in 2018 and then fell for several years. Yicai reported about 460,000 units in 2018 (up 20%) and about 420,000 in 2019 (up 4%); later brand-level annual totals are not given in Yicai or in company filings [24]. In 2025 domestic retail recovered to about 177,000 units, up 25% year-on-year, but media reported January-August 2026 volume of 62,200 units, down 42.29% year-on-year (August terminal sales of 5,285 units) [10][27].

The 2025 recovery was highly concentrated in one fuel model: the i5 sold about 81,000 units, nearly half the brand's total, at a terminal price from about RMB44,900; new-energy volume remained small, with the D7 at about 24,000, the M7 DMH at about 11,000 and the i6 MAX EV at about 6,320 [10]. Media reported that in January-August 2026 the i5 fell to about 25,200 units, the i6 sold about 6,743 units after launch, and the pure-electric D6 reached about 11,443 units [27].

Overseas and production

Roewe is primarily a domestic brand; the group's overseas business is led by MG, with 2025 exports and overseas-base sales of 1.071 million units (up 3.1%) and MG selling over 300,000 units in Europe [23]. Roewe does not separately disclose production capacity or plants at brand level; its vehicles are built within SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle's production system [3].

Founders and leadership

Founder

As a brand of the state-owned SAIC Motor Corporation, Roewe has no individual founder; the brand was created by SAIC in 2006 after the group acquired Rover technology [7].

Current management

Roewe is run by a brand business unit inside SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle, which also operates MG [16].

  • Lu Xiao (卢晓) — general manager of SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle since 28 July 2026, replacing Wang Jun. He was previously general manager of SAIC-GM, and earlier a long-time executive at the Pan Asia Technical Automotive Center, which he joined in 1997, leading development of the Buick Regal, LaCrosse and Chevrolet Malibu; media describe him as a Chinese chief engineer on a GM global platform [22][15].
  • Yu Jingmin (俞经民) — executive deputy general manager of SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle; previously party secretary of SAIC Volkswagen and executive deputy general manager of sales and marketing there [16][12][9][29].
  • Zhang Liang (张亮) — deputy general manager of SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle and general manager of its sales company [14][10].
  • Qian Yang (钱漾) — general manager of the Roewe brand business unit, who moved from SAIC Volkswagen (media-reported) [14][27].

At the March 2026 media briefing, Zhang Liang said Roewe must 'regain its original aspiration, go upmarket, and break away from low-efficiency internal competition, returning to the RMB200,000-class market' [10].

Ownership and governance

Roewe is not a separate listed entity; it belongs to SAIC Motor (Shanghai Stock Exchange 600104), whose ultimate controller, per the annual report, is the Shanghai State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission [3].

Competition

In the mainstream household market, Roewe's new-energy models (D7, M7 DMH, D6) are positioned against BYD's two sales networks and Geely's Galaxy on value for money, while the i6 fuel sedan targets the Geely 5th-gen Emgrand, Nissan Sylphy Classic and Lavida XR [10]. The Jiayue 07 enters the RMB120,000-150,000 extended-range SUV segment as a large five-seat SUV, differentiating through its AI-native positioning (Momenta R7 world model and Doubao cockpit) [13][26].

Our judgment. Roewe's competitive base rests on the SAIC group's technology platforms (DMH hybrid, Nebula pure-electric platform, Banma/Doubao cockpit) and a large-volume fuel-car base in the i5 [12][10]. Its relative weaknesses are sales concentration in a single fuel model (the i5 was nearly half of 2025 volume), a small new-energy share, and a long decline from the 2018 peak, which together suggest weakened brand equity in a crowded mainstream market [10][28]. Wu Bing, a former general manager of SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle, once reflected in media interviews that Roewe had lost its way pursuing leads and value-for-money input-output ratios and forgot to tell consumers what the brand is [28].

Outlook

Company plans. For 2026, its 20th anniversary, Roewe announced a plan to launch seven new models at a roughly one-per-quarter cadence and set an AI-native product direction; at the March 2026 briefing the company also said the AI-native high-end series targets the market above RMB200,000, the i6 would launch in April, and a brand night on 21 April would unveil the new series [10][21]. The first AI-native model, the Jiayue 07, launched on 23 October 2026 at a guide price of RMB127,800-152,800 and a limited-time benefit price of RMB119,800-144,800 [13][26]. Brand general manager Qian Yang said Roewe will keep cultivating the RMB150,000-class mainstream family market and pursue technology parity, adapting premium smart features and scenario-based experiences to mainstream consumers [25].

Our judgment. Whether Roewe can sustain its 2025 recovery depends on two premises: broadening a lineup still concentrated in the i5, and turning the AI-native Jiayue series into volume. Media reported January-August 2026 sales down 42.29% year-on-year, so the recovery is not yet established [27]. The AI-native positioning and the group's hybrid and battery technology provide a basis for differentiation, but their contribution in a price-competitive mainstream segment remains to be demonstrated [25][14].

Risks

  • Sales concentration and decline. The i5 was nearly half of 2025 volume, and media reported January-August 2026 sales down 42.29% year-on-year, concentrating risk in a single fuel model amid a shrinking fuel-car market [10][27].
  • Weak new-energy traction. Media data show the D7, M7 DMH and other new-energy models selling at modest scale, leaving the brand exposed in the segment that is growing fastest [10].
  • Price competition. Roewe's mainstream positioning places it in the most price-competitive household band, where its value-for-money approach faces sustained pressure from BYD and Geely Galaxy [10].
  • Brand-equity erosion. Years of declining sales since the 2018 peak and repeated positioning adjustments have left the brand's identity unclear, as management acknowledged in media interviews [10][28].
  • AI-native execution and delivery. The Jiayue 07's AI-native cockpit and assisted-driving features are new, and the conversion of pre-sale orders (over 51,000 reported) into deliveries, as well as the real-world performance of the Doubao cockpit and assisted driving, remain to be verified after launch [13].
  • Group dependence. Roewe does not report standalone financials, so its performance and funding depend on the SAIC group's passenger-vehicle business [3].

概览

荣威（Roewe）是上汽集团首个自主乘用车品牌，2006 年 10 月发布，定位中高端自主品牌，基于上汽从英国 MG 罗孚收购的技术打造 [1][7]。它由上汽乘用车运营——该板块隶属上交所上市（600104）的上汽集团，同时运营 MG 品牌；集团最终实际控制人为上海市国有资产监督管理委员会 [3]。

如今荣威定位为上汽面向国内主流家庭用户的品牌，覆盖燃油、插电混动、增程与纯电车型；MG 则定位年轻化、全球化品牌 [14][4]。荣威销量在 2018 年达到约 46 万辆峰值（第一财经口径）后连续多年下滑，2025 年国内零售回升至约 17.7 万辆、同比增长 25% [24][10]。2026 年品牌迎来 20 周年，正以 AI 原生序列为先导推进产品攻势，其首款 AI 原生增程 SUV 家越 07 已于 2026 年 10 月 23 日以 12.78 万—15.28 万元指导价上市 [13]。

上汽乘用车管理层多次表示，荣威在 DMH 超级混动等技术上底子扎实，需要用技术擦亮品牌、而非只靠价格竞争 [12]。品牌事业部总经理钱漾表示，荣威将持续深耕 15 万级主流家用市场、推进技术平权 [25]。品牌层面经营与财务数据未单独披露，荣威业绩并入上汽集团合并报表 [3]。

历史沿革

  1. 2004

    上汽分阶段取得英国 MG 罗孚的车型与发动机知识产权：2004 年以约 3700 万英镑取得罗孚 75、25 及 K 系列发动机等知识产权，2005 年 1 月再支付约 3000 万英镑（后一笔究竟是追加知识产权还是资金支持，媒体说法不一）。[19]

  2. 2006-10

    上汽于 2006 年 10 月 12 日公布「荣威」品牌名，定位中高端自主品牌。[1][7][17]

  3. 2006-11

    首款车型荣威 750（基于罗孚 75 平台）在 2006 年北京国际车展首发。[1]

  4. 2007-01

    2007 年 1 月 31 日，荣威 750 三款 2.5L 车型公布售价（23.18 万 / 24.38 万 / 27.68 万元）并接受预订。[18]

  5. 2008

    荣威 550 在上海国际车展首发。[1]

  6. 2010-04

    完全自主开发的荣威 350 上市，此后长期作为主销车型（官方指导价 7 万—14 万元）。[1][24]

  7. 2011-08

    荣威首款 SUV 荣威 W5 上市。[1]

  8. 2011-11

    荣威 E50 纯电动汽车投放市场（品牌官网沿革记为 2011 年 11 月，另有媒体报道称 2012 年上市）。[1][12]

  9. 2016-07

    荣威 RX5 上市，被称为全球首款量产互联网汽车，搭载阿里 YunOS for Car（后为斑马智行）；2017 年首个完整年销量 23.7 万辆。[12]

  10. 2018

    荣威年销量达到约 46 万辆的峰值、同比增长 20%（第一财经口径）；同年推出定价 26 万—31 万元的 Marvel X 高端纯电车型，但上市两年累计仅售 5000 余辆。[24]

  11. 2019

    市场下行，时任上汽乘用车总经理王晓秋调整策略走低价下沉路线，推出最低售价约 6 万元的车型。[24]

  12. 2020

    荣威发布全新「R」标（中高端新能源），与焕新的「狮标」组成双标战略。[24]

  13. 2021-10

    R 品牌独立为飞凡汽车（注册资本 70 亿元），聚焦中高端智能电动车。[8][9]

  14. 2023-11-08

    2023 年 11 月 8 日，D 家族首款车型荣威 D7 上市（D7 DMH 上市权益价 12.18 万—14.18 万元、D7 EV 14.38 万—17.08 万元）。[11]

  15. 2024-10-28

    2024 年 10 月 28 日，上汽乘用车官宣荣威与飞凡合并，飞凡回归荣威。[8][9]

  16. 2024-10

    荣威 D7 DMH 世界冠军版以满油满电续航 2208 公里、平均油耗 2.49 升/百公里创吉尼斯世界纪录。[20]

  17. 2025

    2025 年荣威国内零售销量回升至约 17.7 万辆、同比增长 25%。[10]

  18. 2026-03-10

    2026 年 3 月 10 日春季媒体沟通会，荣威公布 20 周年规划：2026 年以约每三个月一款的节奏推出 7 款全新车型，并推出聚焦 20 万元以上市场的 AI 原生高端序列。[10][21]

  19. 2026-07-28

    2026 年 7 月 28 日，原上汽通用总经理卢晓接替王骏出任上汽乘用车总经理，统管荣威与 MG 两大品牌。[22]

  20. 2026-10-23

    2026 年 10 月 23 日，荣威全新「家越」序列首款 AI 原生增程 SUV 家越 07 上市，官方指导价 12.78 万—15.28 万元（限时权益价 11.98 万—14.48 万元）。[13][26]

现状

定位与业务结构

荣威是上汽面向国内主流家庭用户的品牌，管理层明确双品牌分工——MG 面向年轻与全球市场，荣威聚焦国内主流家庭用户 [14]。官网在售产品线包括 i5、i6 燃油轿车，纯电 D6，D7 EV 与 D7 DMH，M7 DMH，iMAX8 MPV 车系，原飞凡的「全新 R7」「2025 款 F7」，以及增程 SUV 家越 07 [4]。其新能源车型大多分布在 7 万—15 万元家用市场 [10]。凭借 2026 年 4 月上市的 i6，品牌重回入门燃油家轿价格带，i6 搭载 15.6 英寸 2.5K 中控屏与高通骁龙 8155 芯片 [10]。

核心技术与平台

荣威依托上汽集团共享技术底座。D7 DMH、M7 DMH 搭载的 DMH 超级混动采用五合一动力控制单元（PICU）与 P1+P3 双电机混动专用变速箱，获评 2023 年「中国心」十佳发动机及混动系统 [20]。上汽插电混动技术（DMH 前身）2017 年获国家科技进步二等奖；2024 年 10 月 D7 DMH 世界冠军版以满油满电续航 2208 公里、平均油耗 2.49 升/百公里创吉尼斯世界纪录 [20]。D7 EV 基于星云纯电平台、搭载魔方电池 [11]。座舱方面，从 2016 年 RX5 的斑马智行，演进到 2026 年家越 07 基于 CPP AI 原生架构、与字节跳动火山引擎联合开发的豆包座舱助手及 Momenta R7 世界模型、XHEART X7 芯片 [26]。

销量与市场地位

荣威年销量 2018 年达峰值后连年下滑。第一财经报道 2018 年约 46 万辆（同比 +20%）、2019 年约 42 万辆（同比 +4%）；此后荣威品牌单独年销量未再出现在第一财经或公司公告里 [24]。2025 年国内零售回升至约 17.7 万辆、同比增长 25%；但据媒体报道，2026 年 1—8 月累计销量回落至 6.22 万辆、同比下降 42.29%（8 月单月终端销量 5285 辆）[10][27]。

2025 年的回升高度依赖单一燃油车型：i5 全年零售约 8.1 万辆、接近品牌总量的近一半，终端起售价约 4.49 万元；新能源规模仍小，D7 约 2.4 万辆、M7 DMH 约 1.1 万辆、i6 MAX EV 约 6320 辆 [10]。据媒体报道，2026 年 1—8 月 i5 销量回落至约 2.52 万辆、i6 上市后累计约 6743 辆、纯电 D6 累计约 1.14 万辆 [27]。

海外与生产

荣威以国内市场为主，集团海外业务主要由 MG 承担——2025 年上汽出口及海外基地销售 107.1 万辆、同比增长 3.1%，MG 在欧洲市场销量超 30 万辆 [23]。荣威品牌层面未单独披露产能与工厂信息，产品由上汽乘用车生产体系统一制造 [3]。

创始人与管理层

创始人

荣威是国有控股上汽集团旗下品牌，无单一创始人；品牌由上汽在取得罗孚技术后于 2006 年创建 [7]。

现任核心管理层

荣威由上汽乘用车旗下的品牌事业部运营，上汽乘用车同时运营 MG 品牌 [16]。

  • 卢晓——上汽乘用车总经理，2026 年 7 月 28 日起任，接替王骏；此前任上汽通用总经理，更早长期在泛亚汽车技术中心工作（1997 年加入），主导别克君威、君越、雪佛兰迈锐宝等车型研发，据媒体报道为通用全球平台中国籍总工程师 [22][15]。
  • 俞经民——上汽乘用车常务副总经理，此前任上汽大众党委书记、销售与市场执行副总经理 [16][12][9][29]。
  • 张亮——上汽乘用车副总经理、销售公司总经理 [14][10]。
  • 钱漾——荣威品牌事业部总经理，由上汽大众调任（据媒体报道）[14][27]。

在 2026 年 3 月媒体沟通会上，张亮提出荣威要「找回初心、要贵、要脱离低效的内卷，重新杀回 20 万级市场」[10]。

股权与治理

荣威并非独立上市主体，隶属上交所上市（600104）的上汽集团；据 2025 年年度报告，集团最终实际控制人为上海市国有资产监督管理委员会 [3]。

竞争格局

在主流家用市场，荣威的新能源车型（D7、M7 DMH、D6 等）对标比亚迪两网及吉利银河、走性价比路线；2026 年上市的 i6 燃油家轿对标吉利 5 代帝豪、轩逸经典、朗逸新锐等主流车型 [10]。家越 07 进入 12 万—15 万元级增程 SUV 细分市场，定位大五座增程 SUV，以「AI 原生」（Momenta R7 世界模型、豆包座舱助手）作为差异化卖点 [13][26]。

我们的判断。荣威的竞争基础在于上汽集团的技术平台（DMH 混动、星云纯电平台、斑马/豆包座舱）与 i5 构成的燃油车销量基本盘 [12][10]。相对短板是销量高度集中于单一燃油车型（i5 约占 2025 年销量的近一半）、新能源占比偏低，以及自 2018 年峰值以来的长期下滑，反映出在竞争激烈的主流市场中品牌影响力有所削弱 [10][28]。上汽集团副总裁吴冰（曾任上汽乘用车总经理）曾反思，荣威过去几年「迷失在追求线索、性价比的投入产出比」，忘了告诉大家荣威品牌到底是什么 [28]。

未来展望

公司公开规划。2026 年是荣威品牌 20 周年，荣威宣布以约每三个月一款的节奏全年推出 7 款全新车型，并确立 AI 原生方向；3 月沟通会上公司另表示 AI 原生高端序列聚焦 20 万元以上市场、i6 于 4 月上市、4 月 21 日品牌之夜揭晓新序列 [10][21]。首款 AI 原生车型家越 07 已于 2026 年 10 月 23 日上市，官方指导价 12.78 万—15.28 万元、限时权益价 11.98 万—14.48 万元 [13][26]。品牌事业部总经理钱漾表示，荣威将持续深耕 15 万级主流家用市场、推进技术平权，把高端智能配置与场景化体验适配主流消费群体 [25]。

我们的判断。荣威能否延续 2025 年的回升，取决于两点——一是拓宽目前仍高度依赖 i5 的产品结构，二是让 AI 原生家越序列真正放量。据媒体报道 2026 年 1—8 月销量同比下滑 42.29%，显示回升尚未稳固 [27]。AI 原生定位及集团在混动、电池方面的技术为其差异化提供了基础，但在价格竞争激烈的主流市场能否兑现为销量与利润，仍有待验证 [25][14]。

风险

  • 销量集中与下滑。i5 约占 2025 年销量的近一半，且媒体报道 2026 年 1—8 月销量同比下滑 42.29%，在燃油车市场整体收缩背景下风险集中于单一车型 [10][27]。
  • 新能源乏力。媒体数据显示 D7、M7 DMH 等新能源车型销量规模偏小，品牌在增长最快的新能源细分市场中竞争力偏弱 [10]。
  • 价格竞争。荣威主打主流家用区间，处于价格竞争最激烈的市场，其性价比路线面临比亚迪、吉利银河的持续压力 [10]。
  • 品牌资产削弱。自 2018 年峰值以来的多年下滑与反复的定位调整，使品牌形象有所失焦（管理层在媒体沟通会中亦提及）[10][28]。
  • AI 原生落地与交付。家越 07 的 AI 原生座舱与辅助驾驶均为新功能，预售订单（荣威称超 5.1 万辆）能否转化为实际交付，以及豆包座舱、辅助驾驶的实际表现，仍有待上市后验证 [13]。
  • 集团依赖。荣威不单独披露财务，其业绩与资金支持依赖于上汽集团乘用车板块 [3]。
