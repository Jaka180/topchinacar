Overview

Zeekr (极氪) is the premium smart battery-electric brand of Zhejiang Geely Holding Group, launched in March 2021 and named from a combination of "ZE" (zero), "E" (evolving the electric era) and "Kr" (the element krypton). It unveiled its first model, the ZEEKR 001 shooting-brake crossover, in April 2021 and began deliveries that October, positioning itself in China's RMB 200,000-plus high-end new-energy market. The brand focused on pure electric vehicles at first and added plug-in hybrid models from September 2025, running alongside Lynk & Co as one of Geely's premium electrified brands. [1][17]

Zeekr's listed vehicle, ZEEKR Intelligent Technology Holding Limited (a Cayman Islands holding company), floated on the New York Stock Exchange in May 2024 under the ticker ZK, issuing 21 million ADSs at US$21 each and raising about US$441 million, and closing its first day up about 35%. Before the IPO it had raised about US$1.25 billion over two rounds, reaching a US$13 billion valuation in its 2023 Series A. In February 2025 it acquired 51% of Lynk & Co to form Zeekr Group, and in December 2025 Geely Automobile Holdings (0175.HK) completed a privatisation that made Zeekr a wholly-owned subsidiary and took it off the NYSE. In 2025 the Zeekr brand delivered 224,133 vehicles, and Geely Auto has set a 2026 target of 300,000 units for the brand. [24][10][19][25][4][9][16]

Zeekr's revenue is dominated by vehicle sales, supplemented by powertrain and battery components and R&D services from its 51%-owned subsidiary Ningbo Viridi. Because it once listed independently, its historical financials are reported under U.S. GAAP: revenue rose from RMB6.53 billion in 2021 to RMB75.91 billion in 2024, but the brand remained loss-making. After the December 2025 delisting, its results are consolidated into Geely Automobile Holdings, which has a separate group-level deep dive on this site. [2][3][14]

Current operations

Ownership and business structure

Since December 2025 Zeekr has been an indirect wholly-owned subsidiary of Geely Automobile Holdings (0175.HK), which is itself controlled by Geely Holding. Before that, Zeekr sat directly under Geely Holding and, from February 2025, controlled 51% of Lynk & Co (with the other 49% held by Geely Auto's Ningbo Geely). Zeekr Group, the two-brand structure formed in February 2025, runs both Zeekr and Lynk & Co. Zeekr's revenue is split between vehicle sales, batteries and other components (from the 51%-owned Ningbo Viridi) and R&D services; in 2024 about 87.6% of revenue came from mainland China and about 8.9% from Europe. In 2023 its non-vehicle revenue — RMB14.69 billion from powertrain components plus RMB3.07 billion from R&D and other services — totalled RMB17.76 billion, about 34.4% of total revenue of RMB51.67 billion, of which over 95% came from Geely-affiliated companies. [2][19][25][4]

Technology and platforms

Zeekr's vehicles are built on Geely's SEA (Sustainable Experience Architecture) scalable electric platform, with a self-developed electrical/electronic architecture (ZEEA), ZEEKR OS and FOTA updates. The brand develops its own batteries, battery-management systems and e-motors, and has adopted an 800-volt (and, on newer models, 900-volt) high-voltage system; the self-developed LFP "Golden Battery" supports ultra-fast charging. Its assisted-driving stack is branded Navigation Zeekr Pilot (NZP), with partnerships with Waymo (the Zeekr RT robotaxi), Mobileye and NVIDIA (a Thor-based domain controller). The 9X introduced the in-house Super Hybrid (SEP) system, a 2.0T-engine plug-in hybrid on the Haohan-S architecture; the 7X offers 75kWh LFP or 100kWh ternary batteries, and the 9X's tri-motor system is rated above 1,400 hp with a 0-100 km/h time of about 3.1 seconds. [1][7][17][27]

Manufacturing and capacity

Zeekr's headquarters and main manufacturing base are in Ningbo, Zhejiang, with R&D centres and design studios in Ningbo, Hangzhou, Gothenburg and Shanghai. Deliveries grew from about 6,000 vehicles in 2021 and about 72,000 in 2022 to 118,685 in 2023, 222,123 in 2024 and 224,133 in 2025; December 2025 set a monthly record of 30,267. The brand states that cumulative global deliveries reached nearly 650,000 by end-2025 (a figure slightly above the sum of its disclosed annual deliveries, which totals about 643,000 including 2021). Zeekr had about 7,427 R&D staff at end-2023 and spent close to RMB17 billion on R&D cumulatively from 2021 to 2023. It relies on Geely's vehicle, powertrain and chassis supply chain for vertical integration, and has adopted processes such as integrated die-casting. [1][9][15]

Domestic position

Within China, Zeekr competes in the RMB 200,000-plus premium new-energy segment. Its stated records include the 001 as the top-selling RMB 300,000-plus BEV in 2022-2023 and the 009 leading the RMB 400,000-plus MPV segment; in 2023 its vehicle gross margin of 15% trailed only Tesla among pure-BEV makers. For 2024 the brand targeted 230,000 deliveries and reached 222,123; for 2025 Zeekr Group had set a 320,000-unit target for the Zeekr brand, against actual deliveries of 224,133. [12][19][25][9]

Overseas business

By end-2025 Zeekr had entered more than 50 countries and regions across five continents, including the Netherlands, Sweden, Thailand, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Mexico and Australia, with nearly 640 stores worldwide of which over 100 are overseas. In 2025 it topped Hong Kong's luxury-automobile sales, took the No.1 spot for pure-electric brands in Egypt, and ranked among the top three pure-electric brands in Brazil and Kazakhstan; the 009 led the luxury pure-electric MPV segment in Thailand and Malaysia, and the 7X had entered more than 40 markets. [15][9]

H1 2026 after consolidation into Geely Auto

Following the privatisation, Zeekr no longer reports standalone financials; its results are consolidated into Geely Automobile Holdings. In the first half of 2026, the Zeekr brand delivered about 178,000 vehicles, up 97% year on year, and the 9X sold more than 46,000 units to rank first among all models priced above RMB 500,000 in China in H1 2026. Zeekr contributed about 31.7% of Geely Auto's H1 2026 revenue, with an average transaction price of about RMB 350,000. Geely Auto as a whole reported H1 2026 revenue of RMB173.6 billion (up 15%), profit attributable to owners of RMB9.09 billion, and a core profit of RMB9.68 billion (up 46%). [26][28]

Founders and leadership

Founder and brand origin

Zeekr was created within Geely Holding under founder Li Shufu as a premium brand to move the group upmarket in the new-energy era; its first chief executive was An Conghui, a veteran Geely executive. Li Shufu, born in 1963 in Taizhou, Zhejiang, founded Geely in 1986 and entered the auto industry in 1997. He served as chairman of Zhejiang Zeekr Intelligent Technology until stepping down in June 2026, and remains chairman of Zhejiang Geely Holding Group. He is Zeekr's ultimate controlling person through Geely Holding's control of Geely Automobile Holdings. [2][22][23]

Current management

  • An Conghui (安聪慧) — chief executive officer of Zeekr and chairman and CEO of Zeekr Group. He joined Geely in 1996 after graduating, became a vice-president of Geely Holding in 2003 and its president in December 2011, and now also serves as CEO of Geely Holding. He stepped down as legal representative of Zhejiang Zeekr Intelligent Technology in June 2026 while remaining Zeekr CEO, and became chairman of Geely Automobile Holdings (0175.HK) on 18 August 2026. [1][22][23]
  • Gan Jiayue (淦家阅) — CEO of Geely Auto Group. Born in 1981, he joined Geely in 2003, became a vice-president of Geely Auto Group in charge of procurement in June 2020 and its CEO in 2021. In June 2026 he became legal representative of Zhejiang Zeekr Intelligent Technology (and of Lynk & Co), and on 18 August 2026 he became chief executive officer of Geely Automobile Holdings. [21][22][23]
  • Gui Shengyue (桂生悦) — joined Geely Automobile in June 2005 and was its CEO from February 2006. He served as a director of Zeekr until stepping down in June 2026. On 18 August 2026 he stepped down as CEO of Geely Automobile Holdings and became its vice-chairman, remaining an executive director. [12][22][23]
  • Li Donghui (李东辉) — executive director of Geely Automobile Holdings and vice-chairman and CFO of Geely Holding. He served as a director of Zeekr until stepping down in June 2026, and stepped down as vice-chairman of Geely Automobile Holdings on 18 August 2026 while remaining an executive director. [22][23]
  • Dai Yong (戴永) — CFO of Geely Auto Group, who joined Geely in 2018. In June 2026 he became finance head of Zhejiang Zeekr Intelligent Technology, replacing Yuan Jing. [22]
  • Yuan Jing (袁璟) — former CFO of Zeekr from April 2021; he had previously been an executive director at UBS investment bank. He stepped down as finance head of Zhejiang Zeekr Intelligent Technology in June 2026. [12][22]

According to a May 2024 media report, Zeekr's then vice-presidents also included Xu Yun, Zhao Yuhui and Zhu Ling; their roles after the June 2026 corporate-registry change have not been separately disclosed. [12]

Shareholding and governance

In its May 2024 IPO, Zeekr issued 21 million ADSs at US$21 each, raising about US$441 million; Geely Automobile subscribed to about 12.9 million ADSs (about 61.4% of the offering) for about US$271 million. According to Geely Auto's HKEX announcement, after the offering Geely Auto held about 52.1% of Zeekr (on a fully diluted basis) and Geely Holding about 11.4%, with Li Shufu as the ultimate controlling person through Geely Holding. After the February 2025 acquisition, Lynk & Co was consolidated into Zeekr, and after the December 2025 privatisation Zeekr became a wholly-owned subsidiary of Geely Automobile Holdings. [24][4][25]

Competition

Zeekr competes in China's premium new-energy market against NIO, Li Auto, XPeng and Tesla, as well as Xiaomi's SU7 in the RMB 200,000-plus sedan segment and, higher up, BYD's Denza and Huawei-backed AITO. Its advantages are Geely's SEA architecture and a vertically integrated powertrain and battery supply chain, a model line-up spanning BEV and hybrid, and an expanding overseas footprint. Its stated records include the 001 as the top-selling RMB 300,000-plus BEV in 2022-2023 and the 009 leading the RMB 400,000-plus MPV segment; in 2023 its vehicle gross margin of 15% trailed only Tesla among pure-BEV makers. [12][9][11]

Our judgment: Zeekr's scale still trails China's volume leaders. It delivered 224,133 vehicles in 2025, well below BYD and the leading new-energy players, and the brand has yet to turn a profit under U.S. GAAP, reporting a net loss attributable to shareholders of RMB6.42 billion in 2024 despite a 16.4% gross margin; by contrast, NIO and XPeng each reported 2023 net losses exceeding RMB20 billion and RMB10 billion respectively, which shows Zeekr's loss is comparatively contained for its stage but still material in absolute terms. This is our assessment, based on the disclosed financials and delivery figures above. [3][11][9]

Outlook

Company targets. Geely Automobile Holdings has set a 2026 sales target of 300,000 units for the Zeekr brand (up 34%), as part of a group goal of 3.45 million vehicles; in H1 2026 the brand had delivered about 178,000 units. Zeekr has also said it plans to expand into Japan and continue localised production abroad, and it expects the Zeekr-Lynk & Co integration to save 10-20% of combined R&D spending through joint development. For 2024 the brand targeted 230,000 deliveries and reached 222,123. [16][26][14][10]

Our analysis. These plans rest on three premises: that the hybrid 9X and 8X can lift volume without diluting the premium image; that overseas sales and local production keep ramping despite tariffs; and that the brand keeps narrowing losses toward profitability. The main alternatives are a prolonged domestic price war, tighter EU trade measures on China-made BEVs, and slower-than-expected synergy from the Geely Auto integration. This is our judgment and does not predict any share price. [17][14]

Risks

  • Persistent losses. Zeekr has yet to be profitable: net loss attributable to shareholders was RMB6.42 billion in 2024 (narrowed from RMB8.35 billion in 2023), and 2022-2024 cumulative losses are large relative to its scale. [3]
  • Price competition. The brand operates in a segment hit by intense price cuts, and it has repeatedly refreshed models (for example multiple 001 updates) to stay competitive, which can pressure margins and resale values. [12]
  • Overseas and tariff exposure. Zeekr exports to Europe, where the EU imposed definitive countervailing duties on China-made battery-electric vehicles from October 2024, adding 18.8% for Geely on top of the standard 10% duty; this raises the cost of its China-built exports. [18]
  • Integration execution. Folding Zeekr and Lynk & Co into Geely Automobile Holdings requires organisational, channel and R&D integration that may take time to deliver the expected savings. [14]
  • Technology-route transition. Shifting from a pure-BEV positioning to a hybrid line-up (9X, 8X) is a strategic change whose market reception is not yet proven at scale. [17]
  • Liquidity and funding. Zeekr long relied on external funding; at end-2023 it had cash and equivalents of about RMB3.26 billion against total current liabilities of about RMB32.32 billion (current assets of about RMB20.48 billion, implying net current liabilities of about RMB11.84 billion), leaving near-term refinancing pressure. [2]
  • Premium-segment concentration. A large share of revenue depends on a few high-priced models, so a single model's failure could significantly affect results; the prospectus flags this risk. [1]

概览

极氪（Zeekr）是浙江吉利控股集团旗下的高端智能纯电品牌，2021 年 3 月发布，品牌名由「ZE」（Zero，无限可能的起点）、「E」（Evolving the Electric Era）与「Kr」（稀有气体氪）组成。首款车型极氪 001 猎装轿跑于 2021 年 4 月亮相、10 月开启交付，定位中国 20 万元以上高端新能源市场。品牌早期专注纯电，2025 年 9 月起新增插电式混合动力车型，与领克并列为吉利旗下高端电动化品牌。 [1][17]

极氪的上市主体 ZEEKR Intelligent Technology Holding Limited（开曼群岛控股公司）于 2024 年 5 月在纽交所上市（代码 ZK），发行 2100 万份 ADS、每股 21 美元、募资约 4.41 亿美元，上市首日收涨约 35%。上市前极氪经两轮融资共筹得约 12.5 亿美元，2023 年 A 轮投后估值 130 亿美元。2025 年 2 月收购领克 51% 股权组建极氪科技集团；2025 年 12 月吉利汽车控股（0175.HK）完成私有化，极氪成为其全资附属公司并自纽交所退市。2025 年极氪品牌交付 224,133 辆，吉利汽车将极氪品牌 2026 年销量目标定为 30 万辆。 [24][10][19][25][4][9][16]

极氪的收入以整车销售为主，辅以持股 51% 的电池与动力系统子公司宁波威睿提供的三电部件收入及研发服务收入。因曾独立上市，其历史财务数据按美国公认会计原则披露：营收由 2021 年的 65.28 亿元增至 2024 年的 759.13 亿元，但品牌仍处亏损。2025 年 12 月退市后，业绩并入吉利汽车控股合并报表，后者另有集团层面的深度研究。 [2][3][14]

现状

归属与业务结构

自 2025 年 12 月起，极氪成为吉利汽车控股（0175.HK）的间接全资附属公司，而吉利汽车本身由吉利控股控制；此前极氪直接隶属于吉利控股，并自 2025 年 2 月起持有领克 51% 股权（其余 49% 由吉利汽车旗下宁波吉利持有）。2025 年 2 月组建的极氪科技集团同时运营极氪与领克两个品牌。极氪的收入由整车销售、电池及其他部件（来自持股 51% 的宁波威睿）与研发服务构成；2024 年约 87.6% 的收入来自中国大陆、约 8.9% 来自欧洲。2023 年极氪非整车业务收入——三电部件 146.93 亿元加研发及其他服务 30.68 亿元——合计 177.6 亿元，占当年总营收 516.73 亿元的约 34.4%，其中超过 95% 来自吉利系关联方。 [2][19][25][4]

技术与平台

极氪车型基于吉利 SEA 浩瀚可扩展纯电架构开发，自研电子电气架构（ZEEA）、ZEEKR OS 与 FOTA 升级，自研电池、电池管理系统与电驱，采用 800 伏（新车型达 900 伏）高压平台，自研磷酸铁锂「金砖电池」支持超快充；智能驾驶方案为浩瀚智驾（NZP），并与 Waymo（极氪 RT 原生自动驾驶车型）、Mobileye 合作，采用英伟达 Thor 平台域控制器。极氪 9X 首搭自研浩瀚超级电混（SEP），基于浩瀚-S 架构、配 2.0T 发动机；7X 提供 75kWh 磷酸铁锂或 100kWh 三元锂两种电池；9X 三电机系统综合功率超 1400 马力、零百加速约 3.1 秒。 [1][7][17][27]

工厂与产能

极氪总部与主要制造基地位于浙江宁波，并在宁波、杭州、哥德堡、上海设研发中心与设计工作室。交付量从 2021 年约 6,000 辆、2022 年约 7.2 万辆增至 2023 年 118,685 辆、2024 年 222,123 辆、2025 年 224,133 辆，2025 年 12 月单月交付 30,267 辆创新高。品牌口径的「截至 2025 年末全球累计交付近 65 万辆」，略高于其分年披露交付量之和（含 2021 年约 6,000 辆在内合计约 64.3 万辆）。截至 2023 年末极氪拥有研发人员约 7,427 名，2021 年至 2023 年累计研发投入近 170 亿元；并借助吉利的整车、三电与底盘产业链实现零部件垂直整合，采用一体式压铸等工艺。 [1][9][15]

国内市场地位

在国内，极氪定位 20 万元以上高端新能源市场。据其披露，001 曾为 2022—2023 年 30 万元以上纯电销冠，009 领跑 40 万元以上 MPV 市场；2023 年整车毛利率 15%，在纯电企业中仅次于特斯拉。2024 年公司设定 23 万辆交付目标、实际交付 222,123 辆；2025 年极氪科技集团曾为极氪品牌设定 32 万辆目标，实际交付 224,133 辆。 [12][19][25][9]

海外业务

截至 2025 年末，极氪已进入荷兰、瑞典、泰国、阿联酋、沙特、墨西哥、澳大利亚等 50 多个国家和地区、覆盖 5 大洲，全球门店近 640 家、其中海外门店超 100 家。2025 年极氪登顶香港豪华汽车品牌销量榜首，拿下埃及纯电品牌销量冠军，并跻身巴西、哈萨克斯坦纯电品牌销量前三甲；009 在泰国、马来西亚均为豪华纯电 MPV 销量冠军，7X 已进入 40 多个全球市场。 [15][9]

2026 年上半年（并入吉利汽车后）

私有化后极氪不再单独披露财务数据，业绩并入吉利汽车控股合并报表。2026 年上半年，极氪品牌交付约 17.8 万辆、同比增长 97%，其中 9X 销量超 4.6 万辆、居中国 50 万元以上全品类销量第一；极氪贡献吉利汽车当期约 31.7% 的收入、平均客单价约 35 万元。吉利汽车整体上半年营收 1736 亿元（同比增长 15%），归母净利润 90.9 亿元、核心归母净利润 96.8 亿元（同比增长 46%）。 [26][28]

创始人与管理层

创始人

极氪由吉利控股创始人李书福推动设立，作为集团在新能源时代向上突围的高端品牌，首任 CEO 为吉利老将安聪慧。李书福 1963 年生于浙江台州，1986 年创办吉利，1997 年进入汽车领域；他于 2026 年 6 月卸任浙江极氪智能科技董事长，仍任浙江吉利控股集团董事长，并通过吉利控股控制吉利汽车，为极氪最终控制人。 [2][22][23]

现任核心管理层

  • 安聪慧——极氪首席执行官、极氪科技集团董事长兼 CEO；1996 年毕业后加入吉利，2003 年任吉利控股副总裁，2011 年 12 月起任吉利控股总裁，现任吉利控股 CEO。2026 年 6 月卸任浙江极氪智能科技法定代表人（仍任极氪 CEO），2026 年 8 月 18 日起任吉利汽车控股（0175.HK）董事会主席。 [1][22][23]
  • 淦家阅——吉利汽车集团 CEO；1981 年生，2003 年加入吉利，2020 年 6 月任吉利汽车集团副总裁（负责采购），2021 年起任吉利汽车集团 CEO。2026 年 6 月起任浙江极氪智能科技法定代表人（兼领克法定代表人），2026 年 8 月 18 日起任吉利汽车控股行政总裁。 [21][22][23]
  • 桂生悦——2005 年 6 月加入吉利汽车，2006 年 2 月起任吉利汽车行政总裁；曾任极氪董事，2026 年 6 月卸任。2026 年 8 月 18 日辞任吉利汽车控股行政总裁、转任董事会副主席，继续担任执行董事。 [12][22][23]
  • 李东辉——吉利汽车控股执行董事、吉利控股副董事长兼首席财务官；曾任极氪董事，2026 年 6 月卸任。2026 年 8 月 18 日辞任吉利汽车控股董事会副主席，继续担任执行董事。 [22][23]

股权与治理

2024 年 5 月 IPO 中，极氪发行 2100 万份 ADS、每股 21 美元，募资约 4.41 亿美元；吉利汽车认购约 1290.1 万份 ADS（约占发行量的 61.4%），支付约 2.71 亿美元。据吉利汽车港交所公告，IPO 完成后吉利汽车持有极氪约 52.1% 权益（按悉数摊薄基准）、吉利控股约 11.4%，李书福通过吉利控股为最终控制人。2025 年 2 月收购完成后领克并入极氪合并报表；2025 年 12 月私有化后，极氪成为吉利汽车控股全资附属公司。 [24][4][25]

竞争格局

极氪在国内高端新能源市场与蔚来、理想、小鹏、特斯拉竞争，在 20 万元以上轿车市场与小米 SU7 竞争，更高价位则面对比亚迪腾势、华为系问界等。其优势在于吉利 SEA 架构与垂直整合的三电、电池供应链，纯电与电混并行的产品矩阵，以及不断扩大的海外版图。据其披露，001 曾为 2022—2023 年 30 万元以上纯电销冠，009 领跑 40 万元以上豪华 MPV 市场；2023 年整车毛利率 15%，在纯电企业中仅次于特斯拉。 [12][9][11]

我们的判断：极氪的规模仍落后于国内头部品牌。2025 年交付 224,133 辆，明显低于比亚迪及头部新势力；品牌在美会计准则下尚未盈利，2024 年归母净亏损 64.24 亿元，尽管毛利率已达 16.4%。对照来看，蔚来、小鹏 2023 年净亏损分别超 200 亿元、100 亿元，说明极氪同期的亏损体量相对其发展阶段仍属可控，但绝对规模依然不小。以上为我们的判断，依据为前述披露的财务与销量数据。 [3][11][9]

未来展望

公司公开目标。吉利汽车控股将极氪品牌 2026 年销量目标定为 30 万辆（同比增长 34%），为集团整体 345 万辆目标的一部分；2026 年上半年极氪已交付约 17.8 万辆。极氪还表示计划进入日本市场并继续推进海外本地化生产，并预计极氪与领克整合后通过联合开发可节省 10%–20% 的研发投入。2024 年公司曾设定 23 万辆交付目标，全年实际交付 222,123 辆。 [16][26][14][10]

我们的分析。上述规划依赖三个前提：一是电混车型 9X、8X 能在不稀释高端定位的情况下拉动规模，二是海外销售与本地化生产在关税环境下继续放量，三是亏损持续收窄、逐步走向盈利。主要变数在于国内价格战延续、欧盟对中国产纯电动车的贸易措施收紧，以及并入吉利汽车后的协同兑现慢于预期。以上为我们的判断，不构成任何股价预测。 [17][14]

风险

  • 持续亏损。极氪尚未盈利：2024 年归母净亏损 64.24 亿元（较 2023 年的 83.47 亿元收窄），2022—2024 年累计亏损相对其规模仍然较大。 [3]
  • 价格竞争。品牌所处细分市场竞争激烈，且车型更新频繁（如 001 多次改款），可能对利润率与保值率形成压力。 [12]
  • 海外与关税风险。极氪对欧洲出口，欧盟自 2024 年 10 月起对中国产纯电动车征收终裁反补贴税，吉利系被加征 18.8%（叠加 10% 基础关税），推高其中国产出口成本。 [18]
  • 整合执行风险。极氪、领克并入吉利汽车后的组织、渠道与研发整合需要时间，协同节省的兑现存在不确定性。 [14]
  • 技术路线切换。由纯电定位转向纯电与电混并行（9X、8X）是战略调整，其规模化市场接受度尚待验证。 [17]
  • 流动性与融资压力。极氪长期依赖外部融资：截至 2023 年末现金及现金等价物约 32.6 亿元，流动负债合计约 323.2 亿元（流动资产约 204.8 亿元，净流动负债约 118.4 亿元），短期再融资压力较大。 [2]
  • 高端细分集中度高。收入较大比例依赖少数高价车型，单一车型失利可能显著影响业绩，招股书亦提示这一风险。 [1]
