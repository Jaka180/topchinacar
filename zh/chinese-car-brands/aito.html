Overview

AITO (问界) is the premium intelligent new-energy vehicle brand of Seres Group Co., Ltd. (SSE 601127.SH / HKEX 9927.HK), created with Huawei under the Smart Selection model that later became the Harmony Intelligent Mobility Alliance (HIMA). Seres is responsible for vehicle engineering and manufacturing, while Huawei contributes the HarmonySpace cockpit, the Qiankun ADS intelligent-driving stack, product definition and the retail channel; Huawei holds no equity in the brand.[4][15] The brand launched on 2 December 2021 at Seres's Liangjiang smart factory in Chongqing, and its name stands for "Adding Intelligence to Auto".[7]

The line-up spans five SUV series — M5, M6, M7, M8 and M9, plus the long-wheelbase M9 Ultimate — in extended-range (EREV) and pure-electric versions, with suggested retail prices from RMB229,800 to RMB649,800.[4] Per Seres's 2025 annual report, AITO delivered 426,000 vehicles in 2025 and was, by the company's account, the best-selling Chinese premium brand in China's domestic market that year; cumulative users passed 1.2 million by October 2026.[21][5]

In July 2024 Seres Auto bought the AITO/问界 trademarks and related design patents from Huawei for RMB2.5 billion, making Seres the brand owner.[3] From 15 September 2026, product definition, marketing, retail and service are led by Seres, with Huawei providing technology empowerment, while AITO remains a member of HIMA.[9] Seres's new-energy vehicles — overwhelmingly AITO — drove 2025 operating revenue of RMB165.054 billion, though the group does not break out AITO's own revenue.[21]

Current operations

Positioning and product line

AITO sits in the RMB230,000–650,000 premium SUV segment and competes with Tesla, Li Auto and electric offerings from German premium brands. The current official line-up and starting prices are: M5 (RMB229,800), M6 (RMB229,800), M7 (RMB279,800), M8 (RMB359,800), M9 (RMB479,800) and M9 Ultimate (RMB649,800).[4] The flagship M9 has a CLTC combined range of 1,405 km, the M7 reaches 1,690 km, the M8 1,526 km and the M6 up to 1,605 km in EREV form.[4]

Technology

On the Huawei side, AITO uses the HarmonySpace cockpit and Qiankun ADS intelligent driving, from ADS 2.0 on the 2023 new M7 through later versions, plus the Tuling chassis platform and Huawei battery platforms.[8][14] On the Seres side, the brand is built on the Magic-cube (魔方) technology platform, whose 2.0 version was unveiled in November 2025, and the Super EREV system; Seres says its range-extender business held more than 37.5% segment share in 2025 and its Super EREV system reaches 44.8% peak thermal efficiency.[6][24] The company reports that AITO users added 3.8 billion km of assisted-driving mileage in 2025, with 95.4% of users active, and that by September 2026 assisted-driving active users had passed one million with over 10 billion km of cumulative assisted-driving mileage.[2][6]

Manufacturing

In its January 2025 inquiry reply Seres described three Chongqing plants — the Liangjiang factory, the Fenghuang factory and the Super Factory in Liangjiang New Area (commissioned February 2024), with combined capacity of about one million vehicles a year and the Super Factory planned for 700,000 units.[26] By the 2026 interim report, Seres had acquired Longsheng New Energy (the Super Factory) as a wholly-owned subsidiary and described the Super Factory — with 100% automation of key processes — as the core production base for the AITO range.[32]

Delivery trajectory and latest results

AITO deliveries rose from roughly 78,000 in 2022 and 103,000 in 2023 (prospectus basis) to 387,000 in 2024 and 426,000 in 2025 per the 2025 annual report, with the September 2023 new-M7 relaunch as the turning point.[34][21] The 2025 annual report also shows the AITO average transaction price rising from RMB377,000 in 2024 to RMB391,000 in 2025, with R&D staff up from 6,201 to 9,019.[21] In the latest period, Seres's 2026 interim report (PRC GAAP) shows H1 2026 operating revenue of RMB57,493 million (down 7.87% year on year) and a net loss attributable to shareholders of the listed company of RMB1,717 million (versus a profit of RMB2,941 million a year earlier), with H1 new-energy-vehicle sales of 178,800 units (up 3.87%).[19] The September 2026 production-and-sales report shows January–September 2026 new-energy-vehicle sales of 256,521 units, down 15.79% year on year.[20]

Overseas

AITO's overseas entry is at an early stage. In February 2026 it signed a strategic cooperation agreement with Abu Dhabi Motors (ADM), a UAE luxury-car dealer group, under which ADM will handle AITO sales, delivery and after-sales in the UAE; AITO said its models had completed local adaptation and certification and that the M9 was in local customer test drives.[25] At the October 2026 Paris Motor Show AITO announced a plan to enter the European market in autumn 2027, targeting EU whole-vehicle type approval and a five-star safety standard under Euro NCAP 2026, with localised adaptation for European roads and driving preferences.[5][16]

Founders and leadership

Brand governance

AITO has no separate chief executive; it is operated within Seres Group under the HIMA model. Seres owns the brand after buying the AITO/问界 trademarks from Huawei in July 2024, and Huawei holds no equity in the brand; Seres Auto separately holds a 10% stake in the Huawei intelligent-vehicle unit Yinwang, giving the group a 9.36% equity interest.[3][32]

Founder

Zhang Xinghai (张兴海) founded the group's predecessor as a spring factory in Chongqing in 1986, moved into mini-vehicles with Dongfeng in 2003 and pivoted to new-energy vehicles in 2016. He is today chairman (founder) of Seres Group and was identified as actual controller in the 2025 annual report.[23][21]

Key figures

  • Zhang Xinghai (张兴海) — chairman (founder) and actual controller of Seres Group; he stepped down as chairman of Seres Auto in June 2026.[21][35]
  • Zhang Zhengping (张正萍) — chairman of Seres Auto since a June 2026 registry change; he is Zhang Xinghai's son and previously led Seres's US company and Seres Auto.[36][35]
  • Yin Xianzhi (尹先知) — executive director and president of Seres Group.[32]
  • He Liyang (何利扬) — president of Seres Auto, promoted from director to vice-chairman in the June 2026 change; he represented Seres at AITO launches such as the M8.[36][14]
  • Kang Bo (康波) — executive director and vice-president of Seres Group, a public spokesperson for the 2026 cooperation-model adjustment and the Europe plan.[10][16]
  • Yu Chengdong (Richard Yu, 余承东) — Huawei executive director, chairman of the Product Investment Review Board and chairman of the Device BG, the public face of the AITO/HIMA partnership; he is also vice-chairman of Yinwang.[33][29]

Ownership and governance

At end-2025 the largest shareholder of Seres Group was Chongqing Xiaokang Holding (20.98% directly plus 2.01% via a guarantee-and-trust account, 22.99% in total), followed by Dongfeng Motor Group (18.79%) and Chongqing Yu'an Automobile Industry (3.79%). Zhang Xinghai holds 50% of Xiaokang Holding; he also holds 11.97% of Yu'an personally while Xiaokang Holding holds 17.33% of Yu'an. The annual report identifies Zhang Xinghai as the actual controller.[21] The 2026 interim report lists Zhang Xinghai as executive director and chairman, Yin Xianzhi as executive director and president, and Kang Bo as executive director.[32]

Competition

AITO competes mainly in China's RMB230,000–650,000 premium SUV segment. Its direct reference set includes Tesla's Model Y, Li Auto's L-series, XPeng, Zeekr and, in the upper band, the Mercedes-Benz, BMW and Audi electric SUVs; within HIMA it coexists with Luxeed (Chery), Stelato (BAIC), Maextro (JAC) and Shangjie (SAIC), and is HIMA's volume leader.[4][2]

Our judgment. AITO's strengths are Huawei's intelligent-driving and cockpit stack and brand pull, Seres's EREV and manufacturing capability, and scale in the premium segment (426,000 deliveries in 2025 and more than 1.2 million cumulative users), which underpins its sales-and-service network and a large assisted-driving data base.[21][5] Its main weaknesses are reliance on Huawei's product momentum and channel — a dependence the September 2026 handover to Seres-led operations is meant to address but has not yet proven — a 2026 volume decline, and the fact that AITO's own profitability cannot be assessed because Seres does not break out the brand's financials.[9][20][1]

Outlook

Company plans. Seres has said it will focus on the premium intelligent-EV business and aims for a second one million AITO vehicles within two years, while accelerating overseas model development.[2] AITO announced at the 2026 Paris Motor Show that it plans to enter the European market in autumn 2027, targeting EU whole-vehicle type approval and a five-star safety standard under Euro NCAP 2026, and pursuing localised adaptation for European roads, driving preferences and lifestyle.[5][16] The 15 September 2026 cooperation-model adjustment transfers product definition, marketing, retail and service to Seres, with Huawei continuing technology empowerment; the other four HIMA brands keep the Huawei-led model.[9]

Our analysis. Our judgment: AITO's 2024–2025 growth was driven by Huawei's brand pull and a strong M7/M8/M9 product cycle; whether it continues depends on premises that are not guaranteed — a successful handover to Seres-led operations without losing Huawei's traffic, a fresh product cycle to reverse the 2026 decline (Seres reported new-energy-vehicle sales down 15.79% year on year in January–September 2026), and whether Europe and the new M6 can diversify the brand's domestic concentration. If these conditions fail, the H1 2026 group loss could persist.[19][20]

Risks

  • Dependence on Huawei. AITO relies on Huawei's intelligent-driving and cockpit technology, channel and brand pull; the September 2026 move to Seres-led operations is a structural change whose execution has yet to be demonstrated.[9]
  • Volume decline and group loss. Seres's new-energy-vehicle sales fell 15.79% year on year in January–September 2026, and the group posted an attributable net loss in H1 2026, indicating a product-cycle cooling.[19][20]
  • No standalone financial disclosure. AITO does not report separate financials, so its profitability cannot be assessed independently; Seres does not break out AITO's revenue or profit.[1]
  • Intense premium competition. The RMB300,000-plus segment faces price pressure from traditional luxury brands and rival NEV flagships.[4]
  • Overseas entry risks. The autumn-2027 Europe plan is subject to EU type approval, Euro NCAP, data-compliance and trade-policy requirements, and to local adaptation; it is a plan, not yet realised sales.[16]

概览

问界（AITO）是赛力斯集团股份有限公司（上交所 601127.SH / 港交所 9927.HK）旗下高端智能新能源汽车品牌，由赛力斯与华为在「智选车」（后升级为鸿蒙智行，HIMA）模式下联合打造。赛力斯负责整车工程与制造，华为提供鸿蒙座舱、乾崑智驾 ADS 智能驾驶技术、产品定义与销售渠道，华为不持有品牌股权。[4][15] 品牌于 2021 年 12 月 2 日在赛力斯重庆两江智慧工厂发布，AITO 意为「Adding Intelligence to Auto」（为汽车加入智能）。[7]

问界产品矩阵覆盖 M5、M6、M7、M8、M9 五款 SUV 系列及 M9 Ultimate 领世加长版，提供增程（EREV）与纯电两种动力，建议零售价 22.98 万–64.98 万元。[4] 据赛力斯 2025 年年报，问界 2025 年交付 42.6 万辆，为公司所称当年国内市场销量最高的中国豪华品牌；截至 2026 年 10 月累计用户突破 120 万。[21][5]

2024 年 7 月赛力斯汽车以 25 亿元收购华为持有的问界/AITO 商标及外观设计专利，品牌归赛力斯所有。[3] 自 2026 年 9 月 15 日起，问界产品定义、营销、零售与服务由赛力斯主导、华为提供技术赋能，问界仍属鸿蒙智行成员。[9] 以问界为主体的新能源汽车业务带动赛力斯 2025 年营业总收入达 1,650.54 亿元，但集团未单独披露问界品牌收入。[21]

现状

定位与产品线

问界定位于 23 万–65 万元的高端 SUV 市场，与特斯拉、理想及德系豪华品牌的电动车型竞争。现售车型与起售价为：M5 22.98 万元、M6 22.98 万元、M7 27.98 万元、M8 35.98 万元、M9 47.98 万元、M9 Ultimate 64.98 万元。[4] 旗舰 M9 的 CLTC 综合续航达 1,405 公里，M7 达 1,690 公里，M8 达 1,526 公里，M6 增程版综合续航最高 1,605 公里。[4]

技术

华为侧，问界采用鸿蒙座舱与乾崑智驾 ADS 智驾系统，自 2023 年新 M7 的 ADS 2.0 迭代至后续版本，并搭载途灵底盘平台与华为电池平台。[8][14] 赛力斯侧，品牌依托魔方技术平台（2.0 版于 2025 年 11 月发布）与超级增程系统，公司称 2025 年增程器业务市占率超 37.5%、超级增程系统最高热效率 44.8%。[6][24] 公司披露，2025 年问界用户新增智能辅助驾驶里程 38 亿公里、活跃用户占比 95.4%；截至 2026 年 9 月辅助驾驶活跃用户突破 100 万、累计辅助驾驶里程超 100 亿公里。[2][6]

制造

赛力斯在 2025 年 1 月的审核问询函回复中披露重庆三座工厂——两江工厂、凤凰工厂，以及 2024 年 2 月投产的两江新区超级工厂，合计年产能约 100 万辆，其中超级工厂规划年产能 70 万辆。[26] 至 2026 年中期报告，赛力斯已收购龙盛新能源（超级工厂）为其全资子公司，并称关键工序 100% 自动化的超级工厂系问界系列车型的核心生产基地。[32]

销量轨迹与最新业绩

问界交付量由 2022 年约 7.8 万辆、2023 年约 10.3 万辆（招股书口径），增至 2024 年 38.7 万辆、2025 年 42.6 万辆（2025 年年报口径），2023 年 9 月新 M7 焕新上市成为转折点。[34][21] 2025 年年报还显示，问界成交均价由 2024 年的 37.7 万元升至 2025 年的 39.1 万元，研发人员由 6,201 人增至 9,019 人。[21] 最新一期，赛力斯 2026 年半年报（中国企业会计准则）显示，2026 年上半年集团营业总收入 574.93 亿元（同比下降 7.87%）、归属于上市公司股东的净利润亏损 17.17 亿元（上年同期盈利 29.41 亿元），上半年新能源汽车销量 17.88 万辆（同比增长 3.87%）。[19] 2026 年 9 月产销快报显示，2026 年 1–9 月新能源汽车销量 25.65 万辆、同比下降 15.79%。[20]

海外

问界出海尚处早期。2026 年 2 月，AITO 与阿联酋豪华汽车经销商集团 Abu Dhabi Motors（ADM）签署战略合作协议，由 ADM 负责 AITO 在阿联酋的销售、交付与售后服务；公司称其车型已完成本地化适配与当地认证，M9 已在当地开展用户试驾。[25] 2026 年 10 月巴黎车展上，问界宣布计划于 2027 年秋季进入欧洲市场，以欧盟整车型式认证与 Euro NCAP 2026 五星安全标准为目标，并围绕欧洲道路环境与驾驶偏好做本地化适配。[5][16]

问界没有独立的首席执行官，由赛力斯集团在鸿蒙智行体系下运营。2024 年 7 月赛力斯从华为收购问界/AITO 商标后，品牌归赛力斯所有，华为不持有品牌股权；赛力斯汽车另持有华为智能汽车业务主体引望 10% 股权，集团持有引望 9.36% 权益。[3][32]

创始人

张兴海 1986 年在重庆创办弹簧厂，2003 年与东风合作进入微车领域，2016 年转型新能源汽车，现任赛力斯集团董事长（创始人），2025 年年报认定其为公司实际控制人。[23][21]

关键人物

  • 张兴海——赛力斯集团董事长（创始人）、实际控制人；2026 年 6 月卸任赛力斯汽车董事长。[21][35]
  • 张正萍——2026 年 6 月工商变更后任赛力斯汽车董事长，为张兴海之子，此前曾任赛力斯汽车总经理等职。[36][35]
  • 尹先知——赛力斯集团执行董事兼总裁。[32]
  • 何利扬——赛力斯汽车总裁，2026 年 6 月由董事升任副董事长，代表赛力斯出席问界新品发布（如 M8 上市）。[36][14]
  • 康波——赛力斯集团执行董事、副总裁，2026 年问界合作模式调整与欧洲拓展计划的公司公开发言人。[10][16]
  • 余承东（Richard Yu）——华为常务董事、产品投资评审委员会主任、终端 BG 董事长，问界/鸿蒙智行合作的华为方代表，多次共同发布品牌车型；同时任引望副董事长。[33][29]

股权与治理

截至 2025 年末，赛力斯集团第一大股东为重庆小康控股有限公司（直接持股 20.98%、另通过担保及信托财产专户持股 2.01%，合计 22.99%），东风汽车集团有限公司持股 18.79%，重庆渝安汽车工业有限公司持股 3.79%。张兴海持有重庆小康控股 50% 股权，本人另持有渝安 11.97% 股权，小康控股持有渝安 17.33% 股权。年报认定张兴海为实际控制人。[21] 2026 年中期报告列示张兴海为执行董事、董事长，尹先知为执行董事兼总裁，康波为执行董事。[32]

财务数据

币种：CNY · 单位：hundred million (亿元) · 会计准则：PRC GAAP (China Accounting Standards)

问界是赛力斯集团旗下品牌，并非单独上市或单独披露报表的主体，不发布独立财务报表，因此品牌层面无单独的营收、毛利率、净利润或研发投入数据，本表留空。以下母公司参考数据均出自赛力斯集团在中国企业会计准则（CAS）下的定期报告，币种为人民币、单位为亿元。2025 年度：赛力斯营业总收入 1,650.54 亿元，同比增长 13.69%；归属于上市公司股东的净利润 59.57 亿元（公司新闻稿四舍五入为 1,650.5 亿元、13.7%、59.6 亿元）。该净利润口径为「归属于上市公司股东（即归属于母公司所有者）」，并非单独列示的「归属于普通股股东」口径；赛力斯无优先股，二者实质相同。2025 年研发投入 125.1 亿元（另有研发费用 79.5 亿元，为不同口径），新能源汽车毛利率约 28.8%。2026 年上半年：营业总收入 574.93 亿元，同比下降 7.87%；归属于上市公司股东的净利润亏损 17.17 亿元。赛力斯未单独披露问界品牌的收入或利润。本页仅呈现公开披露信息，不构成任何交易推荐。

竞争格局

问界主要在中国 23 万–65 万元的高端 SUV 市场参与竞争，直接参照对象包括特斯拉 Model Y、理想 L 系列、小鹏、极氪，以及高端区间的奔驰、宝马、奥迪电动 SUV；在鸿蒙智行内部，其与智界（奇瑞）、享界（北汽）、尊界（江淮）、尚界（上汽）并存，问界为鸿蒙智行的销量主力。[4][2]

我们的判断。问界的优势在于华为智驾与座舱全栈技术与品牌势能、赛力斯的增程与制造能力，以及高端市场的规模（2025 年交付 42.6 万辆、累计用户超 120 万），这为其销售服务体系与大规模智驾数据提供了基础。[21][5] 主要短板在于对华为产品势能与渠道的依赖——2026 年 9 月转向赛力斯主导正是为应对这一依赖，但尚未得到验证；2026 年销量下滑，以及赛力斯未单独披露问界财务数据，使品牌自身盈利能力难以评估。[9][20][1]

未来展望

公司公开规划。赛力斯表示将坚定聚焦高端智能电动汽车主业，力争两年内实现第二个百万辆目标，同时加快海外车型研发。[2] 问界在 2026 巴黎车展宣布计划于 2027 年秋季进入欧洲市场，以欧盟整车型式认证与 Euro NCAP 2026 五星安全标准为目标，并围绕欧洲道路环境、驾驶偏好与生活方式做本地化适配。[5][16] 2026 年 9 月 15 日的合作模式调整将产品定义、营销、零售与服务转由赛力斯主导、华为继续技术赋能；鸿蒙智行其余四界仍采用华为全流程主导模式。[9]

我们的分析。我们的判断：问界 2024–2025 年的增长由华为品牌势能与强劲的 M7/M8/M9 产品周期驱动；能否延续取决于若干并不必然成立的前提——在不流失华为流量前提下顺利完成向赛力斯主导运营的交接、以新一轮产品周期扭转 2026 年下滑（赛力斯披露 2026 年 1–9 月新能源汽车销量同比下滑 15.79%），以及欧洲市场与新 M6 能否对冲国内集中度。若这些条件不成立，2026 年上半年的集团亏损可能延续。[19][20]

风险

  • 对华为的依赖。问界依赖华为的智驾与座舱技术、渠道与品牌势能；2026 年 9 月转向赛力斯主导运营是结构性调整，其执行效果尚未得到验证。[9]
  • 销量下滑与集团亏损。赛力斯 2026 年 1–9 月新能源汽车销量同比下滑 15.79%，2026 年上半年归母亏损，显示产品周期出现回落。[19][20]
  • 无单独财务披露。问界不单独披露财务，盈利能力无法独立评估；赛力斯未单独披露问界品牌的收入或利润。[1]
  • 高端市场竞争激烈。30 万元以上区间面临传统豪华品牌降价与新能源旗舰的双重挤压。[4]
  • 出海风险。2027 年秋季进入欧洲的计划受欧盟型式认证、Euro NCAP、数据合规与贸易政策要求及本地化适配约束，属于计划、尚未形成实际销量。[16]
