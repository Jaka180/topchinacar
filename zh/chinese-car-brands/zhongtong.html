Overview

Zhongtong Bus Holding Co., Ltd. (SZSE: 000957) is a bus and coach manufacturer based in Liaocheng, Shandong province, and one of China's earliest bus makers and bus-listed companies [1]. It develops, manufactures and sells large, medium and light buses spanning 5.5 to 27 metres with more than 140 model variants, covering highway passenger transport, urban transit, tourism, group commute, school buses, logistics and special vehicles, sold in more than 100 countries and regions [8]. In FY2025 it reported revenue of RMB7.329 billion (+27.82%) and net profit attributable to shareholders of RMB359.65 million (+44.18%), on sales of 12,363 buses (+8.36%), of which 6,335 were exported [1][3].

Zhongtong is a state-controlled enterprise within the Shandong Heavy Industry Group system: its controlling shareholder is China National Heavy Duty Truck Group Co., Ltd. (21.07%), with Shandong Heavy Industry Group as the actual controller and the Shandong Provincial SASAC as the ultimate controlling party [7]. Exports are the core growth engine: overseas revenue reached RMB4.303 billion in FY2025, or 58.71% of total revenue [1]. In the first nine months of 2026 revenue was RMB5.937 billion (+4.98%) and net profit attributable to shareholders RMB402.37 million (+45.36%) [2].

Zhongtong sits alongside Weichai Power, Sinotruk, Shantui and Yangzhou Asiastar within the Shandong Heavy Industry (Weichai) system [18]. By 2025 its cumulative bus output exceeded 300,000 units, including over 100,000 new-energy buses [8].

History

  1. 1958

    The predecessor, Liaocheng vehicle repair and assembly plant, was founded.[9][15]

  2. 1971

    The first bus (Q650, nicknamed 'Golden Phoenix') rolled off the line on 1 July, marking the start of professional bus production.[9][15]

  3. 1981

    Became an enterprise directly under the Shandong Provincial Transport Department, entering a faster development phase.[9]

  4. 1984

    The plant was renamed Shandong Liaocheng Bus Works (山东省聊城客车厂).[15]

  5. 1992

    Output value exceeded RMB100 million; the LCK6931 bus won a national gold award for consumer-trusted domestically produced vehicles.[9]

  6. 1993-03-16

    With approval under Liaocheng reform document No. 15 (1993), a joint-stock company was established by directed placement on the basis of Shandong Liaocheng Bus Works, jointly initiated by Shandong Liaocheng Bus Works, China National Heavy Duty Truck Corporation and China Highway Vehicle & Machinery Corporation.[1]

  7. 1994-04-07

    The company was registered as Shandong Bus Co., Ltd. (山东客车股份有限公司).[1][15]

  8. 1996

    Annual bus production and sales exceeded 2,000 units, a record at the time.[9]

  9. 1998-04-29

    The company was renamed Zhongtong Bus Co., Ltd. (中通客车股份有限公司).[15]

  10. 2000-01-13

    Listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange under ticker 000957; in November the same year the BOVA (Netherlands) licence and technology-transfer agreement was approved, with 180 staff sent to BOVA for training.[15][9]

  11. 2001-06

    The BOVA-technology-based 'Bofa' premium coach rolled off the line, positioned as a domestically made luxury coach.[15][9]

  12. 2003

    Began systematic overseas expansion after receiving an 8-bus order from the UAE, opening the international market.[9][15]

  13. 2004

    Set up an overseas sales office; the following year an overseas marketing company was established, with annual exports reaching 222 buses.[15]

  14. 2006

    The first new-energy (electric) bus rolled off the line, among the first Chinese bus makers to master NEV bus technology.[9]

  15. 2007

    The company's laboratory passed CNAS accreditation, becoming the industry's first national-level laboratory.[9]

  16. 2008

    Pure-electric buses served the Beijing Olympics/Paralympics; signed a US$64 million sales contract with Saudi Arabia, then the largest single order in China's bus-export history.[9][15]

  17. 2019-10

    Shandong Heavy Industry Group obtained 100% of Shandong Traffic Industrial Group via a free transfer of state-owned equity, thereby indirectly controlling Zhongtong's 21.07% stake and bringing it into the Shandong Heavy Industry / Weichai system.[10][18]

  18. 2024-11-28

    Controlling shareholder Zhongtong Automobile Industry Group transferred its 124,941,288 shares (21.07%) to China National Heavy Duty Truck Group; the controlling shareholder changed but the actual controller was unchanged.[7][12]

  19. 2025-01-17

    The 9th meeting of the 11th board accepted Hu Haihua's resignation as director and chairman, elected Wang Xingfu as chairman, and appointed Li Pengcheng as general manager and Zhang Feng as board secretary.[24]

Current operations

Business structure

Zhongtong's business is a single segment: bus development, manufacturing and sales, which accounted for 100% of revenue in FY2025 [1]. By product, FY2025 revenue split into new-energy buses RMB3.651 billion (49.82%, +113.12%), conventional buses RMB3.376 billion (46.06%, -11.66%) and other business RMB302 million (4.12%), so new-energy buses overtook conventional buses as the largest product category [1]. By region, FY2025 revenue was RMB3.026 billion domestic (41.29%) and RMB4.303 billion overseas (58.71%); by channel, distribution was 52.65% (up from 22.26% in 2024) and direct sales 47.35%, a marked expansion of the distribution channel [1]. Overseas gross margin (22.18%) was higher than domestic (11.14%). At end-2025 total assets were RMB9.877 billion and net assets attributable to shareholders RMB3.141 billion [1].

Production capacity and plants

The 2025 annual report states the company has annual production capacity of 20,000 buses, including 20,000 new-energy buses [1]; its official website says it has capacity for 30,000 large, medium and light buses per year across a built-up area of 471,700 square metres [8]. It also has a subsidiary, Xinjiang Zhongtong Bus Co., Ltd., to serve western and overseas markets [15].

Core technology and platforms

Zhongtong's new-energy products are built around its sixth-generation new-energy energy-saving control system, with three-electric (battery, motor, electronic control) optimisation based on overseas field-test data [21]. Its L-series pure-electric models use an integrated electric drive axle with transmission efficiency above 95%, and the N12D double-decker offers a range of up to 600km with 8% lower energy consumption [21]. The company states the Shandong Heavy Industry group has strategically invested in overseas high-tech firms such as Canada's Ballard Power Systems and completed product and industrial deployment across the three technology routes of pure electric, hybrid and fuel cell [21].

In 2025 the company's R&D investment was RMB255.0 million (+8.05%), 3.48% of revenue and fully expensed with zero capitalisation, and R&D staff numbered 614, or 17.30% of the workforce [1].

Overseas markets

Overseas business is Zhongtong's core growth engine. In FY2025 overseas revenue was RMB4.303 billion, 58.71% of total revenue, with an overseas gross margin of 22.18% versus 11.14% for domestic sales [1]. In H1 2026 the company exported 4,390 buses (+23.4%), 58% of the period's total sales [21][11]. In its official news the company states that it is the largest new-energy bus supplier in Portugal, has delivered the UAE's largest batch of pure-electric buses, operates nearly 2,000 pure-electric buses in Chile, and is rolling out diversified products in Mexico and Vietnam (N11D double-decker sightseeing buses) [21]. Northern Europe is a recent focus in the high-end overseas market: the company made its first Nordic delivery on Denmark's Bornholm island in September 2025, and in October 2026 exhibited the H12E Plus, N15E and V7 at the Persontrafik show in Stockholm [21]. The company says its products reach more than 100 countries and regions, with over 100,000 new-energy buses produced cumulatively [21].

Sales and latest results

In 2025 the company produced 12,930 buses (+11.05%) and sold 12,363 (+8.36%), comprising 7,172 large, 4,361 medium and 830 light and logistics vehicles; domestic sales were 6,028 and overseas 6,335 [3][1]. In H1 2026 it sold 7,600 buses (+30.16%), with 3,210 domestic (+40.7%) and 4,390 exports (+23.4%), exports at 58% of the total [11][21]. For the first nine months of 2026 revenue was RMB5.937 billion (+4.98%) and net profit attributable to shareholders RMB402.37 million (+45.36%) [2].

Founders and leadership

Zhongtong is a state-controlled enterprise with no individual founder; its predecessor plant was founded in 1958 [9].

Current management

Chairman Wang Xingfu (born 1969, master's degree, engineer and economist) was elected chairman on 2025-01-17; he built his career within the company through roles including designer, production scheduler, head of material procurement, deputy director of quality inspection, workshop director, sales-company branch manager, head of the market department, deputy and executive deputy general manager of the marketing company, head of the market research centre, general manager of the marketing company, assistant general manager, assistant chairman, general manager of the new-energy bus sales company and board secretary; he was appointed general manager on 2023-11-17 before becoming chairman [24][13]. The 2026 interim report lists Wang Xingfu as the company's legal representative [19]. General manager Li Pengcheng (born 1984, bachelor's degree) was appointed on 2025-01-17 after serving at Weichai Power as head of its market department, general manager of its heavy-duty and commercial-vehicle powertrain sales companies, and later vice president and executive president [24][13]. Board secretary Zhang Feng (born 1980, bachelor's degree, political-work engineer) was appointed on 2025-01-17; he previously served as board secretary of Sinotruk Jinan Truck Co., Ltd. and head of the investment-management department of Shantui Construction Machinery [24][13]. The 2025 annual report names He Wei as the executive in charge of accounting work (finance head) [1]. The previous chairman, Hu Haihua (born 1980, master's degree, senior engineer), served as assistant general manager of Weichai Deutz, director of Weichai Power's No. 2 plant, assistant to the operations director of Ferretti (Italy), general manager of Maz-Weichai, and vice president, assistant to the president, chief quality officer and manufacturing director of Weichai Power, then party secretary and general manager of Weichai Group and audit director of Shandong Heavy Industry Group; he was elected a director on 2023-11-03 and chairman at the 11th board's first meeting on 2023-11-17, then resigned as director and chairman on 2025-01-17; he is now a member of the Shandong Heavy Industry Group party committee and deputy general manager [23][24][22].

Shareholding and governance

At end-2025 the controlling shareholder China National Heavy Duty Truck Group Co., Ltd. held 124,941,288 shares (21.07%), and the second-largest shareholder Shandong Provincial State-owned Capital Investment Holding Co., Ltd. held 70,981,592 shares (11.97%) [6][20]. The control chain is: Shandong Provincial SASAC (holding 70% of Shandong Heavy Industry Group) → Shandong Heavy Industry Group (holding 65% of China National Heavy Duty Truck Group) → China National Heavy Duty Truck Group (holding 21.07% of Zhongtong), with Jinan SASAC holding the remaining 35% of China National Heavy Duty Truck Group [7][1].

Competition

Zhongtong competes in China's bus industry alongside Yutong Bus, the King Long group (Xiamen King Long, Golden Dragon and Higer), Ankai Bus and Yangzhou Asiastar Bus — the last of which, like Zhongtong, sits within the Shandong Heavy Industry Group system [18]. Industry-wide, sales of buses of 6 metres and above reached 137,212 units in 2025, up 8.84% to a seven-year high [1]. In H1 2026 the company's gross margin was 17.20% (up 1.82 percentage points year on year) and net margin 7.04% (up 2.21 percentage points) [11]. In H1 2026 Zhongtong's total sales of 7,600 buses included 3,210 domestic (+40.7%) and 4,390 exports (+23.4%), with exports at 58% of the total [21][11].

Our assessment: Zhongtong's strengths are its export position and the Shandong Heavy Industry group's powertrain and supply-chain synergies (Shandong Heavy Industry Group was its largest supplier in 2025, at 8.36% of total purchases). Exports reached 6,335 buses in 2025 and 4,390 in H1 2026, giving overseas markets a weight unusual among China's second-tier bus makers [1][21]. Its scale is far smaller than the industry leader's, and profitability has historically been thin: net profit attributable to shareholders was RMB69.65 million in 2023 before recovering to RMB359.65 million in 2025 [1]. Total sales fell for four consecutive years (2020-2023) before rebounding, leaving it dependent on export demand and domestic policy support such as trade-in subsidies for new-energy buses [16][11].

Our assessment (market share): dividing Zhongtong's 12,363 buses in 2025 by the industry-wide 137,212 buses of 6 metres and above gives about 9.0%; this is our own calculation, not disclosed by the company or an industry association, and Zhongtong's figure includes 830 light and logistics vehicles, so the denominators are not fully comparable [3][1]. Our assessment: this export orientation sets Zhongtong apart from peers that rely mainly on the domestic market, but also concentrates its risk in currency movements and overseas demand cycles [14][1].

Outlook

In its 2025 annual report, Zhongtong states it is steadily moving toward its goal of becoming a leading brand in the global bus industry, leveraging the Belt and Road Initiative and the global NEV transition to keep raising its overseas market share [1]. It says domestic transit demand is supported by the continued roll-out of trade-in policies, while tourist-coach demand is shifting toward premium, customised and smaller vehicles, and new-energy buses are extending into highway passenger use for airport shuttles, short-haul and group rental [11][1].

Our analysis: the 2026 trajectory has so far been volume-led and export-led — H1 2026 sales rose 30.16% while revenue grew only 1.85%, and nine-month profit grew 45.36% (with Q3 alone contributing revenue of RMB1.923 billion, +12.18%, and net profit of RMB119.63 million, +38.43%); H1 operating cash flow of RMB1.55 billion (+1,800.9%) and nine-month operating cash flow of RMB1.685 billion (+274.85%) reflect improved cash collection [2][20]. Continued growth therefore hinges on sustained export demand and domestic subsidy continuity; a slowdown in either would weigh on a company that has historically operated on thin margins [16]. Our analysis also notes that H1 2026 average selling price came under pressure as low-priced conventional-fuel exports to Africa rose as a share of the mix, a factor to watch for gross-margin sustainability [11].

Risks

  • Accounts-receivable and contract-asset impairment: at end-2025 the gross carrying amount of receivables and contract assets was RMB3.682 billion with impairment provisions of RMB822.5 million, which the auditor identified as a key audit matter [17].
  • Foreign-exchange and export risk: financial expenses rose 65.67% in 2025 due to increased foreign-exchange losses, and exports account for the majority of sales, exposing earnings to currency movements and overseas-market volatility [1][14].
  • Policy risk: domestic transit demand depends on new-energy bus subsidies and trade-in programmes; withdrawal or adjustment of these policies could dampen orders [11].
  • Competition and scale: Zhongtong's output is a fraction of the industry leader's, and it competes with several similarly positioned bus makers, limiting pricing power; its top five customers made up 29.31% of 2025 sales, indicating some customer concentration [1].
  • Leverage and liquidity: the debt-to-asset ratio stood at 68.20% at end-2025, up 4.24 percentage points year on year, a relatively high level for a bus maker [1][14].
  • Technology-route risk: the company pursues several new-energy routes (pure electric, hybrid, fuel cell), and a slowdown in the commercialisation of any one route could affect competitiveness [21].
  • Supply-chain and raw-material risk: the company depends on upstream battery, motor and electronic-control suppliers and on procurement within the Shandong Heavy Industry group; its top supplier in 2025 was Shandong Heavy Industry Group (8.36% of total purchases), and raw-material price swings could squeeze margins [1].

概览

中通客车股份有限公司（Zhongtong Bus Holding Co., Ltd.，深交所：000957）总部位于山东聊城，是中国最早的客车生产企业和客车上市企业之一 [1]。公司研发、制造和销售大型、中型、轻型客车，覆盖公路客运、公交、旅游、团体通勤、校车、物流及专用车等细分市场，车长 5.5—27 米、140 余个品种，产品销往全球 100 多个国家和地区 [8]。2025 财年实现营业收入 73.29 亿元（+27.82%）、归母净利润 3.60 亿元（+44.18%），销售客车 12,363 辆（+8.36%），其中境外销售 6,335 辆 [1][3]。

中通客车是山东重工集团体系内的国有控股企业：控股股东为中国重型汽车集团有限公司（持股 21.07%），实际控制人为山东重工集团，最终控制方为山东省国资委 [7]。出口是公司核心增长引擎：2025 财年海外收入 43.03 亿元，占营业收入 58.71% [1]。2026 年前三季度营业收入 59.37 亿元（+4.98%）、归母净利润 4.02 亿元（+45.36%）[2]。

中通客车与潍柴动力、中国重汽、山推、扬州亚星客车等同属山东重工（潍柴系）体系 [18]。截至 2025 年累计产销客车 30 万余辆，其中新能源客车 10 万余辆 [8]。

历史沿革

  1. 1958

    前身聊城车辆制修厂成立。[9][15]

  2. 1971

    第一辆客车（Q650 型，俗称「金凤凰」）于 7 月 1 日下线，正式开启专业生产客车历程。[9][15]

  3. 1981

    成为山东省交通厅直属企业，进入提速发展阶段。[9]

  4. 1984

    更名为「山东省聊城客车厂」。[15]

  5. 1992

    产值突破亿元大关；LCK6931 客车获消费者信得过国产车金奖。[9]

  6. 1993-03-16

    经聊体改〔1993〕15 号文批准，在山东省聊城客车厂基础上，由山东省聊城客车厂、中国重型汽车集团公司、中国公路车辆机械总公司三家共同发起，以定向募集方式设立股份有限公司。[1]

  7. 1994-04-07

    注册登记为「山东客车股份有限公司」。[1][15]

  8. 1996

    年产销客车突破 2000 辆，创历史新高。[9]

  9. 1998-04-29

    公司更名为「中通客车股份有限公司」。[15]

  10. 2000-01-13

    在深圳证券交易所上市（股票代码 000957）；同年 11 月与荷兰 BOVA 签署的《许可证及技术转让协议》获批，先后派遣 180 人赴 BOVA 学习。[15][9]

  11. 2001-06

    引进 BOVA 技术的中通「博发」高档豪华客车下线，定位国产豪华客车。[15][9]

  12. 2003

    接到阿联酋 8 辆客车订单，敲开国际市场大门，开启规模化出海。[9][15]

  13. 2004

    成立海外销售处；次年成立海外营销公司，当年累计出口客车 222 辆。[15]

  14. 2006

    第一台新能源客车下线，成为行业率先掌握新能源客车研发与生产能力的企业之一。[9]

  15. 2007

    试验室通过 CNAS 审核，成为行业首个国家级实验室。[9]

  16. 2008

    纯电动客车服务北京奥运会、残奥会；与沙特签署 6400 万美元销售合同，为当时中国客车出口第一大单。[9][15]

  17. 2019-10

    山东重工集团通过国有股权无偿划转取得山东交通工业集团 100% 股权，从而间接控制中通客车 21.07% 股权，中通客车纳入山东重工（潍柴系）体系。[10][18]

  18. 2024-11-28

    控股股东中通汽车工业集团将其所持 124,941,288 股（21.07%）无偿划转至中国重型汽车集团，控股股东变更，实际控制人不变。[7][12]

  19. 2025-01-17

    第十一届董事会第九次会议同意胡海华辞去董事、董事长职务，选举王兴富为董事长，聘任李鹏程为总经理、张峰为董事会秘书。[24]

现状

业务结构

中通客车业务为单一主业——客车研发、制造与销售，2025 财年占营业收入 100% [1]。分产品看，2025 财年新能源客车收入 36.51 亿元（49.82%，+113.12%）、传统客车收入 33.76 亿元（46.06%，-11.66%）、其他业务 3.02 亿元（4.12%），新能源客车首次超过传统客车成为第一大产品类别 [1]。分地区看，2025 财年国内收入 30.26 亿元（41.29%）、海外收入 43.03 亿元（58.71%）；分销售模式，经销占 52.65%（2024 年 22.26%）、直销占 47.35%，经销渠道明显扩张 [1]。海外业务毛利率（22.18%）高于国内（11.14%）。截至 2025 年末，公司总资产 98.77 亿元、归属于上市公司股东的净资产 31.41 亿元[1]。

产能与工厂

2025 年年报称公司具备年产能 2 万辆的规模化制造能力（含新能源客车整车 2 万辆）[1]；公司官网则称拥有建筑面积 47.17 万平方米、具备年产 3 万辆大中轻客车生产能力 [8]。公司另设有子公司新疆中通客车有限公司，面向西部及海外市场 [15]。

核心技术平台

中通新能源产品依托其第六代新能源节能控制系统，可根据海外实地测试数据对三电系统进行优化 [21]。L 系纯电动车型采用集成电驱桥，传动效率大于 95%；N12D 双层巴士最高续航 600 公里、整车能耗下降 8% [21]。公司称山东重工方面已战略投资加拿大巴拉德等海外高科技公司，完成纯电动、混合动力、燃料电池三大技术路线的产品布局与产业化落地 [21]。

2025 年公司研发投入 2.55 亿元（+8.05%），占营业收入的 3.48%，全部费用化、资本化率为 0；研发人员 614 人，占员工总数的 17.30% [1]。

海外市场

海外业务是中通客车的核心增长引擎。2025 财年海外收入 43.03 亿元，占营业收入的 58.71%，海外业务毛利率 22.18%，高于国内的 11.14% [1]。2026 年上半年公司出口 4,390 辆（+23.4%），占当期总销量的 58% [21][11]。公司在官网新闻中称，其已成为葡萄牙第一大新能源客车供应商，完成阿联酋最大批量纯电动客车交付，近两千辆纯电动客车在智利运营，并在墨西哥、越南（N11D 双层纯电观光巴士）等市场推进多元化产品布局 [21]。北欧是公司近期重点深耕的海外高端市场：2025 年 9 月在丹麦博恩霍尔姆岛完成北欧首次交付，2026 年 10 月携 H12E Plus、N15E、V7 亮相斯德哥尔摩 Persontrafik 展会 [21]。公司称产品足迹已遍及全球 100 多个国家和地区，累计产销新能源客车超 10 万辆 [21]。

历年销量与最新业绩

2025 年公司生产客车 12,930 辆（+11.05%）、销售 12,363 辆（+8.36%），其中大型 7,172 辆、中型 4,361 辆、轻型及物流车 830 辆；境内销售 6,028 辆、境外 6,335 辆 [3][1]。2026 年上半年销售 7,600 辆（+30.16%），其中国内 3,210 辆（+40.7%）、出口 4,390 辆（+23.4%），出口占总销量 58% [11][21]。2026 年前三季度营业收入 59.37 亿元（+4.98%）、归母净利润 4.02 亿元（+45.36%）[2]。

股权与治理

截至 2025 年末，控股股东中国重型汽车集团有限公司持股 124,941,288 股（21.07%），第二大股东山东省国有资产投资控股有限公司持股 70,981,592 股（11.97%）[6][20]。产权控制链为：山东省国资委（持有山东重工集团 70%）→ 山东重工集团（持有中国重汽集团 65%）→ 中国重汽集团（持有中通客车 21.07%），另济南市国资委持有中国重汽集团 35% [7][1]。

竞争格局

中通客车在中国客车行业与宇通客车、金龙系（厦门金龙、金旅、海格）、安凯客车及扬州亚星客车等企业竞争——其中亚星客车与中通同属山东重工集团体系 [18]。行业层面，2025 年 6 米以上客车销售 137,212 台（+8.84%），创近 7 年新高 [1]。2026 年上半年公司毛利率 17.20%，同比上升 1.82 个百分点，净利率 7.04%，同比上升 2.21 个百分点 [11]。2026 年上半年中通销售 7,600 辆，其中国内 3,210 辆（+40.7%）、出口 4,390 辆（+23.4%），出口占总销量 58% [21][11]。

我们的判断：中通的强项在于出口地位与山东重工体系的动力总成与供应链协同（2025 年公司第一大供应商即山东重工集团，关联采购占年度采购总额的 8.36%）。2025 年出口 6,335 辆、2026 年上半年出口 4,390 辆，海外权重在中国二线客车企业中较为突出 [1][21]。但其规模远小于行业龙头，历史上盈利能力偏弱：归母净利润 2023 年仅 0.70 亿元，2025 年回升至 3.60 亿元 [1]。总销量曾连续四年下滑（2020—2023 年）后回升，使其依赖出口需求以及新能源公交以旧换新补贴等国内政策支持 [16][11]。

我们的判断（市占率）：以中通 2025 年 12,363 辆除以行业 6 米以上客车 137,212 台，约为 9.0%；此为本研究的自行计算，非公司或行业协会直接披露，且中通口径含轻型及物流车 830 辆，两者分母不完全可比 [3][1]。我们的判断：这种出口导向使中通区别于主要依赖国内市场的同行，但也将其风险集中于汇率波动与海外需求周期 [14][1]。

未来展望

中通客车在 2025 年年报中表示，公司依托「一带一路」倡议和全球新能源汽车产业变革机遇，稳步向全球客车行业领军品牌的目标迈进，持续提升海外市场占有率 [1]。公司称国内公交需求受以旧换新政策延续支撑，旅游客车需求向高端化、定制化、小型化转型，新能源客车加快向公路客运领域渗透（用于机场接驳、短途客运、团体租赁等场景）[11][1]。

我们的分析：2026 年以来的增长到目前为止主要由销量和出口驱动——2026 年上半年销量增长 30.16% 而营收仅增长 1.85%，前三季度利润增长 45.36%（其中第三季度营业收入 19.23 亿元、归母净利润 1.20 亿元，同比分别 +12.18%、+38.43%）；上半年经营现金流净额 15.48 亿元（+1,800.9%）、前三季度经营活动产生的现金流量净额 16.85 亿元（+274.85%）也反映回款改善 [2][20]。持续增长因此取决于出口需求的延续与国内补贴的连续性；两者任一放缓，都会给这家历史上毛利率偏薄的企业带来压力 [16]。我们的分析还注意到，2026 年上半年由于面向非洲的低价燃油车出口占比上升，单车平均售价承压，这是观察毛利率能否持续改善的关键变量 [11]。

风险

  • 应收账款与合同资产减值：截至 2025 年末应收账款及合同资产原值合计 36.82 亿元、减值准备 8.23 亿元，被审计机构列为关键审计事项 [17]。
  • 汇率与出口风险：2025 年财务费用因汇兑损失增加同比上升 65.67%，且出口占销售大头，盈利受汇率波动与海外市场波动影响 [1][14]。
  • 政策风险：国内公交需求依赖新能源客车补贴与以旧换新政策，政策退出或调整可能抑制订单 [11]。
  • 竞争与规模：中通产量仅为行业龙头的零头，并与多家定位相近的客车厂商竞争，议价能力受限；2025 年前五大客户占销售总额 29.31%，存在一定客户集中度 [1]。
  • 杠杆与流动性：2025 年末资产负债率 68.20%，同比上升 4.24 个百分点，在客车企业中处于相对较高水平 [1][14]。
  • 技术路线风险：公司同时布局纯电动、混合动力、燃料电池等新能源技术路线，任一技术商业化放缓都可能影响竞争力 [21]。
  • 供应链与原材料风险：公司依赖上游电池、电机、电控供应商及山东重工集团体系内采购，2025 年第一大供应商即山东重工集团（占年度采购总额 8.36%），原材料价格波动可能挤压毛利率 [1]。
