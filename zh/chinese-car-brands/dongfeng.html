Overview

Dongfeng Motor Corporation (东风汽车集团有限公司, Dongfeng) is a state-owned, centrally administered automobile group headquartered in Wuhan, Hubei. Its predecessor, the Second Automobile Works, began construction in Shiyan, Hubei in 1969 [1]. The group's main business is vehicle manufacturing, sales, service and R&D, spanning passenger and commercial vehicles, new-energy vehicles, military vehicles, key assemblies, parts, equipment and auto finance, with production bases in more than 20 cities and exports to over 100 countries and regions [1]. In 2025 the parent group sold 2,472,536 vehicles [12]; its 20 March 2026 disclosure reported over 1.55 million self-owned-brand vehicles (over 60% of the total) and 295,000 exports (up 20%) [2]. For 2025 NEV sales, Dongfeng disclosed 1.053 million units and 22.6% year-on-year growth on 20 March 2026 [2], but 1.04 million units and 21% growth on 24 July 2026 [7]. The cited official releases do not explain the discrepancy; both figures are presented here without treating either as a reconciled final figure. It is one of China's three central automobile SOEs; Dongfeng Motor Group Company Limited (formerly 0489.HK) withdrew its H-share listing on 18 March 2026, and premium NEV brand Voyah (07489.HK) listed by introduction on the HKEX on 19 March 2026 [42][2]. As of end-2024 the group had total assets of RMB 470.386 billion, 117,000 employees and cumulative vehicle production of over 62 million units [1].

Current operations

Business structure

Dongfeng's business is divided into self-owned brands (Voyah, M-Hero, Fengshen, Yipai, Nammi, Forthing) and commercial vehicles, plus the joint ventures Dongfeng Motor Co., Ltd. (with Nissan), Dongfeng Honda and DPCA (Shenlong) [1]. In 2025 the parent group sold 2,472,536 vehicles [12]; its 20 March 2026 disclosure reported self-owned-brand sales exceeding 1.55 million (over 60% of volume) and 295,000 exports [2]. For 2025 NEV sales, Dongfeng disclosed 1.053 million units and 22.6% year-on-year growth on 20 March 2026 [2], but 1.04 million units and 21% growth on 24 July 2026 [7]. The cited official releases do not explain the discrepancy; both figures are presented here without treating either as a reconciled final figure. The former listed unit Dongfeng Motor Group Company Limited (0489.HK) sold 1,896,185 vehicles in 2025, including 562,833 NEVs [12]. In H1 2026 the group sold 1,021,000 vehicles, with NEV sales of 493,000 (up 27.5%), self-owned-brand sales of 725,000 (up 13.6%, 71% of the total) and exports up 97% [5][5]. For 2024 the group sold 2.48 million vehicles (2.55 million terminal deliveries), including 860,000 NEVs (up 64.4%) and 1.37 million self-owned-brand vehicles (up 34.8%) [1]. For 2023, the parent group sold 2,421,227 vehicles (down about 17%), while the listed unit sold 2,088,185 vehicles (down about 15.27%) [25].

Technology and platforms

Dongfeng pursues pure-electric, super-hybrid and hydrogen routes, with three passenger-vehicle NEV platforms (M-Hero MORA, Quantum Architecture and DSMA) and T1/T2 commercial-vehicle platforms [20]. It has developed the Mach hybrid powertrain (its 2.0T hybrid-specific engine reaches 45.5% thermal efficiency, the first mass-produced 2.0T hybrid engine to exceed 45%), the automotive-grade MCU chip DF30, a solid-state battery planned for volume vehicle loading in H2 2026, the 'Tianyuan' cockpit and intelligent-driving systems, and a 16,000-tonne integrated die-casting line [5]. The company also leads a Hubei provincial key laboratory for embodied AI in vehicles and leads the Hubei solid-state battery industrial technology innovation consortium [17][23].

Factories and capacity

The group's production bases span more than 20 cities including Wuhan, Shiyan, Xiangyang and Guangzhou [1]. Dongfeng disclosed on 27 April 2026 that Voyah had two factories with combined annual capacity of 450,000 vehicles [48]. In January 2026 the world's largest 16,000-tonne integrated die-casting line achieved volume delivery of battery trays, making Dongfeng the industry's first whole-vehicle maker to cover front, middle and rear integrated die-casting; a concurrently commissioned smart plant for medium/heavy commercial vehicles — claimed to be the world's largest single such plant — operates 288 robots with 100% welding and painting automation and about 30% higher production efficiency than conventional plants [23].

Overseas business

According to Dongfeng's 22 June 2026 release, the right-hand-drive DONGFENG 007, DONGFENG VIGO and DONGFENG BOX had been launched and were on sale in Hong Kong [47]. Dongfeng exported 295,000 vehicles in 2025 (up 20%), and in H1 2026 exports grew 97% [2][5].

Domestic market position

Dongfeng is one of China's three central automobile SOEs and a leading Chinese medium/heavy-truck maker [1][20]. According to Dongfeng's 9 July 2026 disclosure, its H1 2026 total, domestic, NEV and self-owned-brand sales growth rates exceeded industry growth by 1.1, 9.6, 21.2 and 13.2 percentage points respectively. Export sales grew 97% year on year; the company did not disclose the export growth gap over the industry in percentage points [5].

Founder

As a central SOE, Dongfeng has no private entrepreneur-founder; it traces to the state-built Second Automobile Works of 1969 [1].

Competition

Our judgement. Dongfeng's main domestic comparators include large automotive groups such as FAW, SAIC and Changan, as well as self-owned-brand manufacturers BYD, Geely, Chery and Great Wall Motor. Dongfeng spans passenger vehicles, commercial vehicles and joint ventures; its parent group sold 2,472,536 vehicles in 2025 [1][12]. Voyah reported 2025 revenue of RMB 34.86 billion and net profit of RMB 1.02 billion [2]. However, its unaudited consolidated H1 2026 results under IAS 34 showed revenue of approximately RMB 18.159 billion, a net loss attributable to owners of the parent of approximately RMB 389 million (versus a net profit of approximately RMB 464 million in H1 2025) and gross margin of 17.7%, down from 21.4% a year earlier [44]. Our judgement: Voyah's return to loss despite delivery growth means its 2025 profit alone cannot establish sustained profitability for Dongfeng's self-owned NEV business. Declining joint-venture sales are a competitive pressure: Dongfeng Nissan (including Venucia and Infiniti) sold 600,006 vehicles in 2025, down 4.94%, while Dongfeng Honda sold 325,798, down 23.92% [12]. The former listed subsidiary reported a 2025 net loss attributable to owners of RMB 1.708 billion [8]. These figures cover different entities and do not establish parent-group profitability.

For 2025 NEV sales, Dongfeng disclosed 1.053 million units and 22.6% year-on-year growth on 20 March 2026 [2], but 1.04 million units and 21% growth on 24 July 2026 [7]. The cited official releases do not explain the discrepancy; both figures are presented here without treating either as a reconciled final figure. Our judgement. Both releases put annual NEV sales above one million, but assessing the transition also requires attention to entity scope and profitability. At the listed-subsidiary scope in 2023, joint-venture sales were 1,397,400 units versus 347,400 self-owned passenger vehicles, while Dongfeng Nissan (including Venucia and Infiniti) and Dongfeng Honda fell 21.5% and 8.5% respectively [21]. Against this business base, whether self-owned NEV growth can deliver sustained profit improvement depends on volumes, product mix and cost control; joint-venture performance could also change the outcome.

Outlook

Company plans. Dongfeng set a 2026 target of 3.25 million vehicle sales, including 1.7 million new-energy vehicles and 600,000 exports, under its 'Dongfang Fengqi 2030' plan [18]. In February 2026 the company announced that in 2026 it would scale up the Mach powertrain and DF30 chip, launch the 'Tianyuan' cockpit and intelligent-driving systems, and globally unveil the Huawei co-created 'Yijing' brand in March 2026 [17]. By H1 2026 the Yijing brand had already made its debut with the flagship six-seat SUV Yijing X9, and the DF30 chip had completed verification on multiple models and was moving into volume production [23]. The Shenlong Auto Technology (Wuhan) JV plans four new NEV models from 2027 [41].

Our judgement. The 2026 target of 3.25 million units is well above the 2.47 million achieved in 2025, and H1 2026 sales of 1.021 million mean the second half must accelerate sharply for the target to be met; the target therefore looks demanding [12][5]. Dongfeng's profitability hinges on whether the self-owned NEV ramp (Voyah, Yipai, M-Hero) can offset continued joint-venture decline and price competition. On central-SOE restructuring, Dongfeng Automobile's 5 June 2025 announcement refers back to Dongfeng's February notification of planned restructuring with other central SOEs and states that Dongfeng notified it on 4 June that no relevant asset or business restructuring was currently involved [38]. Separately, China Changan Automobile Group was formed on 29 July 2025 by spin-off from China South Industries Group as an independent central SOE, so the two restructuring threads should be distinguished [26]. Voyah listed independently in March 2026 [2]. In our judgement, its long-term financing and governance outcomes require assessment against subsequent disclosures. The above are our judgements, based on the figures cited.

Our judgement: overseas expansion. The EU imposes countervailing duties on China-origin battery-electric vehicles [40][43], while Dongfeng has disclosed export growth [2][5]. If Voyah or M-Hero exports China-origin BEVs covered by the regulation to the EU, the additional duties could affect pricing, margins and market expansion. The effect depends on the models, applicable rates and local operating arrangements; it does not imply an identical impact on every export model or every European market.

Risks

  • Declining joint-venture sales. In 2025 Dongfeng Nissan (including Venucia and Infiniti) sold 600,006 vehicles, down 4.94%, while Dongfeng Honda sold 325,798, down 23.92% [12].
  • Loss at the former listed subsidiary. Dongfeng Motor Group Company Limited recorded a 2025 net loss attributable to owners of the parent of RMB 1.708 billion [8].
  • Restructuring disclosures. Dongfeng Automobile's 5 June 2025 announcement states that its indirect controlling shareholder Dongfeng had notified it in February of plans to restructure with other central SOEs. On 4 June Dongfeng notified it that no relevant asset or business restructuring was currently involved and that Dongfeng Automobile's normal production and operations would not be affected [38].
  • Overseas trade barriers. Under Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2754, the EU introduced five-year countervailing duties on battery-electric vehicles from China effective 30 October 2024, with initially determined rates ranging from 7.8% to 35.3% [40][43]. Dongfeng reported exports of 295,000 vehicles in 2025 and 97% year-on-year export growth in H1 2026 [2][5].
  • Financial reporting perimeter. This research covers Dongfeng Motor Corporation, whereas the financial table uses the consolidated statements of Dongfeng Motor Group Company Limited; these are different entities [1][8].

概览

东风汽车集团有限公司（简称东风）是总部位于湖北省武汉市、由国务院国资委履行出资人职责的汽车制造中央企业（商业一类央企），前身是 1969 年在湖北十堰建设的第二汽车制造厂[1]。公司以汽车制造、销售、服务和技术研发为主业，业务涵盖乘用车、商用车、新能源汽车、军车、关键总成、零部件、汽车装备与汽车金融，生产基地分布在武汉、十堰、襄阳、广州等 20 多个城市，产品出口 100 多个国家和地区[1]。2025 年母集团销售整车 247.25 万辆[12]；据 2026 年 3 月 20 日披露，自主品牌销量超 155 万辆（占比超 60%）、出口 29.5 万辆（同比增长 20%）[2]。关于 2025 年新能源销量，东风 2026 年 3 月 20 日披露 105.3 万辆、同比增长 22.6%[2]；2026 年 7 月 24 日披露 104 万辆、同比增长 21%[7]。两项官方披露存在差异，所引稿件未解释差异原因，本研究并列呈现，不将任一数值视为已获统一的最终口径。它是中国三大汽车央企之一；2026 年 3 月 18 日，东风汽车集团股份有限公司（原 0489.HK）撤销 H 股上市地位；3 月 19 日，高端新能源品牌岚图汽车（07489.HK）以介绍方式在香港上市[42][2]。截至 2024 年底，公司总资产 4703.86 亿元、从业人数 11.7 万人，累计产销汽车超 6200 万辆[1]。

现状

业务结构

东风业务分为自主板块（岚图、猛士、风神、奕派、纳米、风行）与商用车，以及东风汽车有限公司（与日产合资）、东风本田、神龙汽车三大合资企业[1]。2025 年母集团销售整车 247.25 万辆[12]；据 2026 年 3 月 20 日披露，自主品牌销量超 155 万辆（占比超 60%）、出口 29.5 万辆[2]。关于 2025 年新能源销量，东风 2026 年 3 月 20 日披露 105.3 万辆、同比增长 22.6%[2]；2026 年 7 月 24 日披露 104 万辆、同比增长 21%[7]。两项官方披露存在差异，所引稿件未解释差异原因，本研究并列呈现，不将任一数值视为已获统一的最终口径。原上市公司东风汽车集团股份有限公司（0489.HK）2025 年销售 189.62 万辆，其中新能源汽车 56.28 万辆[12]。2026 年上半年集团销量 102.1 万辆，其中新能源 49.3 万辆（同比增长 27.5%）、自主品牌 72.5 万辆（同比增长 13.6%，占比 71%）、出口同比增长 97%[5][5]。回溯来看，2024 年集团销售汽车 248 万辆、终端交付 255 万辆，其中新能源汽车 86 万辆（同比增长 64.4%）、自主品牌 137 万辆（同比增长 34.8%）[1]；2023 年母集团销售 242.12 万辆（同比下降约 17%），上市子公司销售 208.82 万辆（同比下降约 15.27%）[25]。

技术与平台

东风坚持纯电、超级混动、氢能多路线并进，已形成猛士 MORA、量子架构、DSMA 三大乘用车新能源平台及 T1、T2 商用车平台[20]。已开发马赫混动动力（其 2.0T 混动专用发动机热效率达 45.5%，为首款量产突破 45% 的 2.0T 混动机型）、车规级 MCU 芯片 DF30、计划 2026 年下半年量产装车的固态电池、「天元」智舱与智驾系统，以及 16000 吨一体化压铸产线[5]。公司还牵头建设汽车具身智能技术湖北省重点实验室、牵头组建湖北省固态电池产业技术创新联合体[17][23]。

工厂与产能

集团生产基地分布在武汉、十堰、襄阳、广州等 20 多个城市[1]。东风 2026 年 4 月 27 日披露，岚图拥有两座工厂，合计年产能 45 万辆[48]。2026 年 1 月，全球最大 16000 吨一体化压铸产线实现电池箱体量产交付，东风成为行业首家覆盖前中后全车体一体化压铸布局的整车企业；同期投产的全球单体最大中重型商用车智慧工厂配备 288 台机器人协同作业、焊接涂装自动化率 100%，生产效率较传统工厂提升 30%[23]。

海外业务

据东风 2026 年 6 月 22 日官方披露，DONGFENG 007、DONGFENG VIGO、DONGFENG BOX 右舵车型已在香港上市在售[47]。2025 年东风出口 29.5 万辆（同比增长 20%），2026 年上半年出口同比增长 97%[2][5]。

国内市场地位

东风是中国三大汽车央企之一，也是中国中重卡龙头之一[1][20]。据东风 2026 年 7 月 9 日披露，2026 年上半年整体、国内、新能源、自主品牌销量的同比表现分别好于行业 1.1、9.6、21.2、13.2 个百分点；出口销量同比增长 97%，公司未披露出口增速高于行业的具体百分点数[5]。

竞争格局

我们的判断：东风在国内的主要比较对象包括一汽、上汽、长安等大型汽车集团，以及比亚迪、吉利、奇瑞、长城等自主品牌车企。东风的业务覆盖乘用车、商用车及合资板块，2025 年母集团销量为 247.25 万辆[1][12]。2025 年岚图营收 348.6 亿元、净利润 10.2 亿元[2]；但按国际会计准则第 34 号编制的 2026 年上半年未经审计合并业绩显示，营收约 181.59 亿元，归属于母公司拥有人的净亏损约 3.89 亿元（2025 年同期净利润约 4.64 亿元），毛利率由上年同期 21.4% 降至 17.7%[44]。我们的判断：岚图在交付增长的同时重新录得亏损，说明仅凭其 2025 年盈利尚不足以确认东风自主新能源业务已形成持续盈利能力。合资板块的销量下滑是其竞争压力之一：2025 年东风日产（含启辰、英菲尼迪）销量 600,006 辆、同比下降 4.94%，东风本田销量 325,798 辆、同比下降 23.92%[12]；原上市子公司 2025 年归母净亏损 17.08 亿元[8]。这些数据对应不同经营主体，不能直接等同于母集团整体盈利。

关于 2025 年新能源销量，东风 2026 年 3 月 20 日披露 105.3 万辆、同比增长 22.6%[2]；2026 年 7 月 24 日披露 104 万辆、同比增长 21%[7]。两项官方披露存在差异，所引稿件未解释差异原因，本研究并列呈现，不将任一数值视为已获统一的最终口径。我们的判断：两项披露均显示新能源年销量超过百万辆，但评估转型成效还应结合业务主体与盈利口径。2023 年上市子公司口径下，合资业务销量 139.74 万辆、自主乘用车销量 34.74 万辆，东风日产（含启辰、英菲尼迪）、东风本田分别同比下降 21.5%、8.5%[21]；在这一业务基础上，自主新能源增长能否持续改善盈利，仍取决于销量增长、产品结构与成本控制，合资业务走势也可能改变转型结果。

未来展望

公司公开规划。东风锚定 2026 年整体销量 325 万辆、新能源汽车 170 万辆、出口 60 万辆的目标，并推进「东方风起 2030」计划[18]。2026 年 2 月公司披露，2026 年将推动马赫动力、DF30 芯片规模化上车，加速「天元」智舱与智驾系统落地，并于 3 月全球亮相与华为共创的「奕境」品牌[17]。到 2026 年上半年，奕境品牌已携旗舰大六座 SUV 奕境 X9 首发登场，DF30 芯片也已在多款车型完成验证并推进量产上车[23]。神龙汽车科技（武汉）规划 2027 年起投放 4 款全新新能源车型[41]。

我们的判断。325 万辆的 2026 年目标明显高于 2025 年实际 247.25 万辆；2026 年上半年 102.1 万辆意味着下半年需明显提速方能达成全年目标，目标较有挑战[12][5]。东风的盈利改善取决于岚图、奕派、猛士等自主新能源的爬坡能否对冲合资板块的持续下滑与价格竞争。关于央企重组：东风股份 2025 年 6 月 5 日公告回溯了 2 月东风筹划与其他国资央企重组的通知，并披露其 6 月 4 日接到东风通知，东风「暂不涉及相关资产和业务重组」[38]。此外，中国长安汽车集团已于 2025 年 7 月 29 日由原兵器装备集团分立成立、成为独立央企，两条重组线索应加以区分[26]。岚图已于 2026 年 3 月独立上市[2]；我们的判断：其长期融资与治理成效仍需结合后续披露检验。以上为我们的判断，依据为前述所引数据。

我们的判断：海外拓展。欧盟对中国原产电池电动汽车征收反补贴税[40][43]，东风已披露出口业务增长[2][5]。若岚图、猛士向欧盟出口适用该条例的中国原产纯电车型，额外税负可能影响定价、利润和市场拓展；影响程度取决于具体车型、适用税率与当地经营安排，不能据此推断所有出口车型或所有欧洲市场均受到相同影响。
