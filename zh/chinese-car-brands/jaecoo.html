Overview

JAECOO (杰酷) is the export-focused elegant off-road SUV brand of Chery Automobile Co., Ltd., created alongside its sister brand OMODA under the OMODA & JAECOO (O&J) dual-brand family. The name combines the German word Jäger (hunter) with the English word Cool, and the brand's philosophy is 'From Classic, Beyond Classic', positioning it for refined off-road SUVs that blend city refinement with outdoor capability.[3][12]

JAECOO made its global debut with OMODA in April 2023, and its first model, the JAECOO 7, went on sale in Russia in September 2023 as the brand's first market.[14][8] It then expanded into Europe, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, Latin America, Africa and Australia. By late October 2026 the O&J brands had entered 79 markets with more than 1,700 showrooms, and their combined cumulative global sales had surpassed 1.4 million units, with Europe accounting for nearly half of recent global sales.[21][18]

In the UK, its first Western European volume market, Jaecoo managed 28,232 units in all of 2025 and shifted 22,789 in January-April 2026, according to figures O&J's global CEO gave Motor Trader. The article uses those words, not SMMT registrations; it uses 'registered' for Omoda's 12,324 over the same 2026 period.[9] The JAECOO 7 was the UK's best-selling Chinese-brand car in October 2025, and the UK's overall best-selling car in September 2026 with 10,813 registrations, when OMODA & JAECOO UK recorded a 6.37% market share.[5][24]

JAECOO is not separately listed and does not disclose standalone financials; its parent, Chery Automobile, is listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange (9973.HK).[1] The wider Chery group sold 2,806,393 vehicles in 2025 (including 1,344,020 exports), ranking first in China-brand passenger-vehicle exports for a 23rd consecutive year and 233rd on the 2025 Fortune Global 500.[2]

Current operations

Model line-up

As of the research cut-off, JAECOO's range spans the J7 (compact/mid-size urban off-road SUV and volume model), the J8 (flagship seven- or six-seat plug-in hybrid), the J5 (compact SUV), the all-electric E5/J5 EV and the boxy J6 EV off-roader. Powertrains cover petrol, the SHS plug-in hybrid (SHS-P) and battery-electric.[4][13][16] In the UK the current range is priced from £24,555 (J5), £29,210 (J7) and £45,500 (J8), with the E5 from £27,505 OTR.[4][23]

The J7 measures about 4,500 mm long with a 2,672 mm wheelbase, claims a 600 mm wading depth and seven drive modes, and features a 14.8-inch touchscreen and a 540-degree camera; the UK petrol version uses a 1.6-litre turbo engine of 147 PS and 275 Nm, while the plug-in hybrid delivers 204 PS with about 56 miles of electric range and up to 745 miles combined.[12] The flagship J8 uses the SHS-P plug-in hybrid with 428 hp, 0-62 mph in 5.8 seconds, about 83 miles of electric range and up to 700 miles combined, plus JAECOO's first lockable differentials and a 600 mm wading depth, in six- or seven-seat layouts.[13] The compact J5 uses a 140 PS 1.6-litre engine with a Getrag seven-speed dual-clutch transmission, a 1.45-square-metre glass roof and up to 1,180 litres of boot space; the E5 uses a 60.9 kWh battery and a 207 PS motor for about 248 miles of WLTP range.[23][19]

Technology and platform

JAECOO's core technology is the SHS (Super Hybrid System), covering the SHS-P plug-in hybrid and SHS-H hybrid routes, marketed around super performance, super range, super efficiency and super safety.[7] In October 2026 the company announced an upcoming 5-kWh HEV system (first on the OMODA 7) with a 'super silent' mode and 2.2 kW of HEV vehicle-to-load (V2L) output.[21] On the intelligent side, the October 2026 release names two systems: SIVP (Super Intelligent Valet Parking) and SIAS (Super Intelligent AI Space).[22] The parent group reported R&D spending of RMB 6.672 billion for the first half of 2026, up 28.3% year-on-year.[22]

Production and markets

JAECOO does not disclose brand-level production capacity and relies on Chery's global manufacturing network. Local assembly has begun in Malaysia, where the CKD iCaur 03 (the J6 EV's local name) rolled off the line at Chery Corporate Malaysia's Shah Alam plant in May 2026.[25] In the UK, JAECOO entered the market in January 2025 and by October 2026 the OMODA & JAECOO UK retailer network had grown from 81 to 146 sites, with the dual brand passing 100,000 cumulative UK sales within two years.[24] In September 2026, O&J's global monthly sales reached 73,421 units (up 123% year-on-year), with Europe about half of the total; in that month OMODA & JAECOO ranked second in the UK market and the JAECOO 7 was the UK's best-selling car with 10,813 registrations.[27][24]

Founders and leadership

Brand governance and position in the group

JAECOO is not a separate legal entity or listed company; it is a brand operated within Chery Automobile's international business, managed jointly with OMODA as a dual-brand operation sharing overseas channels and dealers.[3][1] Chery's 2025 annual report describes the brand portfolio around Chery, Jetour, Exeed, iCAR and Luxeed, with OMODA & JAECOO managed as export-oriented brands and not broken out as a separate line in the financials.[1] On 7 October 2026 Chery announced a division of responsibilities: Executive Vice President (常务副总裁) Zhang Guibing took direct charge of the Chery-brand domestic business group while continuing to serve as general manager of the international business group, within which the overseas brands operate; Executive Vice President (执行副总裁) Li Xueyong moved to lead the Jetour brand division.[17]

Key executives

  • Xu Jun (Shawn Xu) — CEO of OMODA & JAECOO International, the global head of the O&J dual-brand operation. At the October 2026 OMODA X NIGHT he stated the two brands had surpassed 1.4 million cumulative global sales in three and a half years; in a June 2026 interview he outlined a 2026 combined target of one million units (480,000 Omoda and 520,000 Jaecoo).[21][9] His appointment date is not publicly disclosed.
  • Gary Lan — CEO of JAECOO UK, quoted in the brand's November 2025 UK sales announcement.[5] His appointment date is not publicly disclosed.
  • Victor Zhang — UK Country Director of JAECOO UK when quoted in July 2025; by October 2026 he was titled Managing Director of OMODA & JAECOO UK in the company's releases.[15][24] His appointment date is not publicly disclosed.

Design

JAECOO's 'From Classic, Beyond Classic' design philosophy runs across its range.[3] A CAR Magazine interview identifies Chery design vice president Steve Eum as responsible for the design direction of brands including OMODA and JAECOO.[20] Beyond these roles, JAECOO does not separately disclose a brand-level board or detailed management biographies.[3]

Competition

Our judgment: the competitive field. JAECOO competes in the compact-to-large SUV segment on price and specification. In the UK, its J7 is pitched against mainstream compact SUVs, and the J8 is positioned against models such as the Kia Sportage and Hyundai Santa Fe; its electric E5 competes with value EVs such as the MG ZS EV. In export markets it also faces other Chinese brands including BYD and MG.[13][5] This is an analytical selection of comparators, not a sales ranking.

Our judgment: strengths. JAECOO benefits from Chery's manufacturing scale, an aggressive price-to-specification ratio, the SHS plug-in hybrid, a fast product cadence, and a long seven-year/100,000-mile warranty.[12][5] As of the research cut-off the UK model page lists the J7 from £29,210 OTR, which is the self-charging hybrid (SHS-H Pure), not the petrol model. Petrol front-wheel-drive Deluxe is £30,165 OTR and the plug-in hybrid (SHS-P) Luxury is £35,170 OTR. The £29,435 petrol figure was the November 2024 order-book price.[28][12] Hybrid power, four-wheel drive on some trims and the long warranty at these prices remain central to its appeal. The brand's UK traction is documented: 28,232 units managed in 2025 (Motor Trader's wording, not an SMMT registration total), the J7 as the best-selling Chinese car in October 2025, and the J7 as the overall best-selling UK car in September 2026 with 10,813 registrations.[9][24]

Our judgment: weaknesses and uncertainties. The brand is young, its scale is concentrated in a few fast-growing export markets, and its brand recognition trails established rivals. JAECOO does not disclose standalone financials, so its profitability cannot be assessed separately from the group; and the O&J family faces quality and regulatory exposure, including Chery's October 2026 recall of 8,878 OMODA 5 vehicles in Malaysia over a rear-axle welding defect.[17][1]

Our judgment: overall. JAECOO is an export brand scaling rapidly on aggressive pricing and hybrid technology, drawing on Chery's group capability; the key question is whether it can convert fast volume growth into sustainable profitability while managing quality, regulatory and trade-policy risks. Because O&J's official releases frequently report the two brands' sales combined, gauging JAECOO's standalone scale requires cross-checking against the merged figures.[7][21]

Outlook

Company plans. O&J states a goal of becoming the world's leading hybrid brand and is expanding market coverage, having announced plans to enter France in April 2026 and Germany in May 2026 to complete European coverage, and to expand to 99 markets by 2027, including Japan, South Korea and Canada.[18][21] These are disclosed plans, not completed results.

Sales targets at different dates. In a June 2026 interview, global CEO Xu Jun outlined a 2026 combined target of one million units (480,000 Omoda and 520,000 Jaecoo).[9] Separately, in an October 2026 release, the company said it expected 2026 annual sales to exceed 800,000 units, up about 110% year-on-year.[21] These two figures are stated at different times and use different scopes, and neither is a completed result.

Product and technology plans. On product, the company listed the Omoda 7, JAECOO 8 and Omoda 4 as 2026 launches, with an all-wheel-drive Omoda crossover (code T1QC) due in the first or second quarter of 2027 and a large pickup (code KP31) mentioned without a stated timing.[9] On technology, O&J announced the upcoming 5-kWh HEV system (first on the OMODA 7), automated valet parking and AI cockpit features.[21][22]

Our analysis. Our judgment: JAECOO's disclosed trajectory is export-led rapid volume growth, with Europe contributing roughly half of recent sales.[27] Whether this pace continues depends on at least three conditions: demand for the new hybrid and EV models, the ability to contain quality and recall incidents in tightly regulated markets, and the trade-policy environment in Europe and elsewhere. The one-million-unit figure is a management target, not a result, and some of the brand's own sales releases mix annual and cumulative scopes, complicating external verification. Because JAECOO does not disclose standalone financials, its independent profitability cannot be observed and must be tracked through Chery's consolidated reports and subsequent sales disclosures.[21][1]

Risks

  • No separate financial disclosure: JAECOO does not publish standalone revenue or profit, so its independent profitability cannot be assessed from public filings.[1]
  • Young brand and limited track record: the brand has only been on sale since 2023, so its long-term quality, residual value and repeat-purchase record are not yet established.[3]
  • Concentration in fast-growing export markets: growth depends on markets such as the UK, Europe and Russia, where trade measures, tariffs and local demand shifts can affect sales and local production.[7]
  • Quality and regulatory exposure in the group: in October 2026 Chery recalled 8,878 Omoda 5 vehicles in Malaysia over a rear-axle welding defect, highlighting quality and regulatory risk across the O&J family.[17]
  • Statistical-scope ambiguity: the brand's own releases give headline figures whose annual-versus-cumulative scope is not always clearly stated, complicating external verification.[7][21]
  • Technology-route and competition risk: the brand leans on hybrid (SHS) technology and an off-road SUV positioning; intensifying EV and smart-driving competition or a shift in technology routes could dilute its differentiation.[7]

概览

JAECOO（杰酷）是奇瑞汽车股份有限公司面向海外市场的精致越野 SUV 品牌，与姊妹品牌 OMODA 共同构成「OMODA & JAECOO」（O&J）双品牌。品牌名由德语 Jäger（猎人）与英语 Cool（酷）组合而来，主张「From Classic, Beyond Classic」（源于经典、高于经典），定位兼具城市精致与户外能力的越野 SUV。[3][12]

JAECOO 于 2023 年 4 月与 OMODA 一同全球首秀，首款车型 JAECOO 7 于 2023 年 9 月在俄罗斯（品牌首个市场）开售。[14][8] 此后陆续进入欧洲、中东、东南亚、拉美、非洲与澳洲。截至 2026 年 10 月下旬，O&J 双品牌已进入 79 个市场、拥有超 1,700 家门店，全球累计销量突破 140 万辆，欧洲占近期全球销量约一半。[21][18]

在其首个西欧销量市场英国，据 O&J 全球 CEO 向 Motor Trader 给出的数字，2025 年全年售出（managed）28,232 辆、2026 年 1–4 月售出（shifted）22,789 辆，并非 SMMT 注册量；原文里 “registered” 只用于同期 Omoda 的 12,324 辆。[9] JAECOO 7 于 2025 年 10 月成为英国销量最高的中国品牌车型，并于 2026 年 9 月以 10,813 辆注册量成为英国全市场销量第一车型，当月 OMODA & JAECOO 英国市占率达 6.37%。[5][24]

JAECOO 未单独上市、不单独披露财务数据，母公司为奇瑞汽车（港交所 9973.HK）。[1] 母公司所属的奇瑞集团 2025 年销量 2,806,393 辆（其中出口 1,344,020 辆），连续 23 年居中国品牌乘用车出口第一，位列 2025 年《财富》世界 500 强第 233 位。[2]

现状

车型谱系

截至研究截止日，JAECOO 已形成以越野 SUV 为主的产品谱系：J7（紧凑/中型城市越野 SUV，销量主力）、J8（旗舰七座或六座插电混动）、J5（紧凑型 SUV）、纯电 E5/J5 EV 以及方正造型的 J6 EV 越野车，动力覆盖燃油、SHS 插电混动（SHS-P）与纯电。[4][13][16] 英国市场现售车型起售价为 J5 24,555 英镑、J7 29,210 英镑、J8 45,500 英镑，纯电 E5 27,505 英镑（OTR）起。[4][23]

就具体规格而言，J7 车长约 4,500 毫米、轴距 2,672 毫米，宣称 600 毫米涉水深度与七种驾驶模式，配备 14.8 英寸中控屏与 540 度全景影像；英国燃油版搭载 1.6 升涡轮增压发动机（147 马力 / 275 牛·米），插混版系统输出 204 马力、纯电续航约 56 英里、综合最高 745 英里。[12] 旗舰 J8 采用 SHS-P 插电混动，系统输出 428 马力、0–62 英里/时 5.8 秒，纯电续航约 83 英里、综合最高 700 英里，并首次配备可锁止差速器与 600 毫米涉水深度，提供六座与七座两种布局。[13] 紧凑型 J5 搭载 140 马力 1.6 升发动机与 Getrag 7 速双离合变速箱，配 1.45 平方米天窗与最高 1,180 升行李空间；纯电 E5 搭载 60.9 千瓦时电池与 207 马力电机，WLTP 续航约 248 英里。[23][19]

技术与平台

JAECOO 的核心技术为 SHS 超级混动系统，覆盖 SHS-P 插电混动与 SHS-H 油电混动两条路线，官方主打「超级性能、超级续航、超级高效、超级安全」。[7] 2026 年 10 月，公司宣布即将推出 5 千瓦时混动（HEV）技术（率先搭载于 OMODA 7），具备「超静音模式」与 2.2 千瓦的 HEV 对外放电（V2L）。[21] 在智能化方面，2026 年 10 月的新闻稿写的是两套系统：SIVP（Super Intelligent Valet Parking，超级智能代客泊车）和 SIAS（Super Intelligent AI Space，超级智能 AI 空间）。[22] 母公司 2026 年上半年研发投入 66.72 亿元，同比增长 28.3%。[22]

生产与市场

JAECOO 未单独披露品牌产能，依托奇瑞全球制造网络。本地组装已在马来西亚落地——CKD 版 iCaur 03（即 J6 EV 的当地名称）于 2026 年 5 月在奇瑞马来西亚公司莎阿南工厂下线。[25] 在英国，JAECOO 于 2025 年 1 月进入市场，截至 2026 年 10 月 OMODA & JAECOO 英国零售商网络已从 81 家增至 146 家，双品牌两年内英国累计销量突破 10 万辆。[24] 2026 年 9 月，O&J 全球月销量达 73,421 辆（同比增 123%），欧洲约占一半；当月 OMODA & JAECOO 在英国市场排名第二，JAECOO 7 以 10,813 辆注册量成为英国销量第一车型。[27][24]

竞争格局

我们的判断：竞争范围。JAECOO 在紧凑型到大型 SUV 细分市场以价格与配置参与竞争。在英国，J7 对标主流紧凑型 SUV，J8 对标起亚 Sportage、现代 Santa Fe 等车型；纯电 E5 则与 MG ZS EV 等平价电动车竞争。在海外市场，其还面对比亚迪、MG 等中国品牌。[13][5] 此为分析所选的对比范围，不是销量排名。

我们的判断：优势。JAECOO 受益于奇瑞的制造规模、较激进的性价比、SHS 插电混动技术、较快的产品节奏与 7 年/10 万英里的长质保。[12][5] 截至研究截止日，英国官网车型页 J7 起售价 29,210 英镑（OTR）是自充电混动（SHS-H Pure），不是燃油版现价；燃油前驱 Deluxe 为 30,165 英镑（OTR），插电混动（SHS-P）Luxury 为 35,170 英镑（OTR）。29,435 英镑是 2024 年 11 月开预订时的燃油起价。[28][12] 混动、部分配置的四驱与长质保仍是其价位上的核心卖点。品牌在英国的表现有据可查：2025 年售出 28,232 辆（Motor Trader 采访口径，并非 SMMT 注册量），J7 于 2025 年 10 月成为当地销量最高的中国品牌车型，并于 2026 年 9 月以 10,813 辆注册量成为英国全市场销量第一车型。[9][24]

我们的判断：短板与不确定性。品牌成立时间短，规模集中于少数快速增长的出口市场，品牌认知度不及成熟对手；JAECOO 不单独披露财务，盈利能力无法脱离集团单独评估；且 O&J 家族面临质量与监管风险，包括奇瑞 2026 年 10 月因后桥焊接缺陷在马来西亚召回 8,878 辆 OMODA 5。[17][1]

我们的判断：总体。JAECOO 是依托奇瑞集团能力、以激进定价与混动技术快速起量的出口品牌；关键问题在于能否在管控质量、监管与贸易政策风险的同时，把快速放量转化为可持续的盈利规模。此外，O&J 官方发布常将两品牌销量合并，单独评估 JAECOO 体量时需要对照合并数字甄别。[7][21]

未来展望

公司公开规划。O&J 提出成为全球领先混动品牌的目标并持续扩张市场，曾宣布计划于 2026 年 4 月进入法国、5 月进入德国以完成欧洲覆盖，并计划到 2027 年扩展至 99 个市场，包括日本、韩国与加拿大。[18][21] 以上为披露的计划，不是已实现的业绩。

不同时点的销量目标。2026 年 6 月的采访中，全球 CEO 徐军提出 2026 年双品牌合计 100 万辆的销量目标（欧萌达 48 万、杰酷 52 万）。[9] 而 2026 年 10 月的公司新闻稿则称，预计 2026 年全年销量将突破 80 万辆、同比约增 110%。[21] 这两个数字发布于不同时点、口径不同，且均非已实现业绩。

产品与技术规划。在产品端，公司列明欧萌达 7、JAECOO 8、欧萌达 4 为 2026 年车型，另有一款四驱欧萌达跨界车（代号 T1QC）计划于 2027 年第一或第二季度推出，大型皮卡（代号 KP31）被提及但未给出时间。[9] 在技术路线上，O&J 宣布即将推出 5 千瓦时混动系统（率先搭载于 OMODA 7），以及自动代客泊车与 AI 座舱能力。[21][22]

我们的分析。我们的判断：JAECOO 披露的轨迹是以出口驱动的快速放量，欧洲贡献近期约一半销量。[27] 这一节奏能否延续，取决于至少三个条件——混动与纯电新车型的需求、品牌能否在监管严格的市场控制质量与召回等事件、以及欧洲等地的贸易政策环境。100 万辆是管理层的目标而非已完成结果，且品牌官方发布的部分销量数字存在年度与累计口径混用的情形，增加外部核验难度。因 JAECOO 不单独披露财务，其独立盈利能力无法观察，需通过奇瑞合并财报及后续销量披露持续跟踪。[21][1]

风险

  • 无单独财务披露：JAECOO 不单独披露营收与利润，无法从公开文件评估品牌独立盈利能力。[1]
  • 品牌年轻、历史数据有限：品牌自 2023 年起才上市销售，长期品质、保值率与复购记录尚未建立。[3]
  • 依赖快速增长的出口市场：增长依赖英国、欧洲、俄罗斯等市场，贸易措施、关税与当地需求变化可能影响销售与本地生产。[7]
  • 集团层面的质量与监管风险：2026 年 10 月，奇瑞因后桥焊接缺陷在马来西亚召回 8,878 辆 OMODA 5，凸显 O&J 家族面临的质量与监管风险。[17]
  • 统计口径不清晰：品牌官方发布的部分销量数字未明确年度或累计口径，增加外部核验难度。[7][21]
  • 技术路线与竞争风险：品牌高度依赖混动（SHS）与越野 SUV 定位，若纯电或智能驾驶竞争加剧、技术路线转向，其差异化可能被稀释。[7]
