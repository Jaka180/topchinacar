Overview

Farizon (远程) is the new-energy commercial-vehicle business within the Geely Holding system. The group entity was registered on 1 February 2016 and the Farizon brand was launched in October 2016 as what Geely Holding calls China's first commercial-vehicle brand focused on new energy [6][7]. It makes heavy trucks, light trucks, mini/small trucks, vans (LCV) and buses/coaches across two technology routes — pure electric and methanol (醇氢) electric — and has ranked first in China's new-energy commercial-vehicle market for four consecutive years (2022–2025), selling 162,019 units in 2025 [14]. In January–July 2026 it sold 107,589 units, up 37.8% year on year [3].

Farizon itself is unlisted. Its shareholders are Geely Group (Ningbo) Co., Ltd. 59.9%, Zhejiang Geely Holding Group 40% and Ningbo Yima Enterprise Management Partnership 0.1%, with registered capital of RMB 500 million [10]. Its controlling listed subsidiary, Hanma Technology Group (SSE: 600375, formerly Hualing Xingma), carries the heavy-truck business and is headquartered in Ma'anshan, Anhui [8]. The ultimate controlling person is Geely Holding founder Li Shufu [17]. The group's vehicle businesses comprise the Farizon brand, the Ouling (Tangjun Ouling) light/mini-truck business in Zibo and Hanma Technology, supported by ecosystem platforms such as Wanwu Youhao and Green E-Link for green freight and energy services [1].

Farizon's distinguishing feature is its methanol (醇氢) electric route alongside pure electric, built on Geely's methanol supply-chain expertise, together with a fast-growing overseas footprint: in August 2026 it said it had become the first new-energy commercial-vehicle brand to reach a 600,000-unit cumulative milestone, with H1 2026 exports up 123% and coverage of 64 countries/regions [3].

Current operations

Business structure

Farizon organises its vehicles around five product lines — heavy trucks, light trucks, mini/small trucks, LCV/vans and buses — and describes itself as the first Chinese commercial-vehicle brand to electrify its entire line-up across multiple energy forms [6]. Its vehicle businesses are the Farizon brand, the Ouling (Tangjun Ouling) business in Zibo and Hanma Technology; the ecosystem platforms include Green E-Link (city-distribution freight) and Wanwu Youhao / Sunshine Mingdao (green freight and smart fleet) [1]. The heavy-truck arm, Hanma Technology, reported 2025 revenue of RMB 6.553 billion (up 55.20%), of which complete-vehicle and chassis revenue was RMB 4.378 billion (up 83.46%), special-purpose-vehicle revenue RMB 1.408 billion (up 33.75%) and auto-parts revenue RMB 531 million (down 13.72%), and stopped domestic production and sales of conventional fuel vehicles from 31 December 2025 [17][26]. Beyond vehicle sales, Farizon describes itself as a full-lifecycle ecosystem service provider, offering battery leasing (separation of vehicle and battery), flexible rental and purchase schemes, a sky-earth integrated smart freight platform and the Chengxin after-sales service brand [3].

Technology and routes

Farizon's core routes are pure electric and methanol (醇氢) electric. The M100 methanol heavy truck launched in April 2019 was, per the company, the world's first mass-produced methanol heavy truck [1][7]. The group says it has built China's largest new-energy commercial-vehicle research institute with more than 2,000 R&D engineers, and reports over 4,000 cumulative patent applications and participation in 86 national, industry and group standards [2][20]. In 2026 it introduced a commercial-vehicle version of Geely's Golden Brick battery and supercharging ecosystem, and disclosed that the Xinghan H pure-electric tractor supports platooning of one lead vehicle plus one to four followers [3]. In April 2026 the Global Methanol-Electric Ecosystem Alliance was established during the 2026 Intelligent Electric Vehicle Development Forum; Farizon vice president and chief brand officer Song Zhaohuan said Geely had cultivated methanol technology for over two decades and that green fuel was written into the government work report for the first time, with more than 80 national and local support policies issued [25].

Products

Representative models include the Xingxiang V series city-distribution vans (Xingxiang V7E launched in April 2024 from RMB 112,800) and the Farizon Super VAN, described as the world's first purpose-built electric large van (launched in April 2024 from RMB 128,800) [5].

Manufacturing bases

The official website lists five specialist bases — Nanchong (Sichuan), Shangrao (Jiangxi), Ma'anshan (Anhui), Zibo (Shandong) and Xiangtan (Hunan) [2]; the development history also records the Jinzhong methanol-heavy-truck base in Shanxi and a bus plant in Huzhou, Zhejiang [1]. The Nanchong base produces buses and V-series vans; the former Tangjun Ouling Zibo base was renamed Shandong Geely Farizon Automobile Manufacturing in October 2026 [21][18].

Latest performance (as of 2026-10-29)

For the group, January–July 2026 sales were 107,589 units (up 37.8%), with H1 2026 exports up 123% and coverage of 64 countries/regions [3]. For the listed subsidiary Hanma Technology, the 2026 half-year report (published 22 August 2026) shows H1 revenue of RMB 3.831 billion (up 34.56%), net profit attributable to shareholders of RMB 33.21 million (up 19.77%), net profit excluding non-recurring items of RMB 2.887 million (turning positive), and sales of 7,808 trucks (up 25.43%), of which 6,756 new-energy medium/heavy trucks (up 52.37%) and 966 methanol-hybrid power systems [13].

Domestic and overseas position

Farizon ranked first in China's new-energy commercial-vehicle market for the fourth consecutive year in 2025 with 162,019 units [14]. Overseas, it covered 26 countries/regions in 2025 (overseas business up nearly 500%) and expanded to 64 countries/regions in H1 2026 [14][3]. Hanma Technology's 2025 overseas (国外) main-business revenue was RMB 2.613 million (up 351.25% year on year) with an export gross margin of 23.58%, versus domestic revenue of RMB 6.337 billion; its production-and-sales table shows 13,967 medium/heavy trucks sold in China and 8 sold overseas (2 a year earlier) [26][29]. It made its IAA Transportation debut in Hanover in September 2026 and the European version of the Xinghan H rolled off the line in August 2026 [4][3].

Founders and leadership

Origin

Farizon was created within Geely Holding, which laid out its commercial-vehicle business in 2014 and launched the Farizon brand in 2016 [6][7]. Geely Holding's founder and chairman Li Shufu is the ultimate controlling person; the listed subsidiary Hanma Technology discloses Li Shufu as its actual controller [17][8].

Current management

  • Dai Qing (戴庆), chairman and legal representative. Born September 1983; joined Zhejiang Geely Holding in December 2000 and headed its finance-management and operation-management centres; he has been Geely Holding's first rotating president since March 2025 and currently serves as rotating president of Geely Holding and chairman of Zhejiang Farizon New Energy Commercial Vehicle Group [11]. Hanma Technology's 27 March 2026 announcement already recorded him as chairman of Farizon [11].
  • Fan Xianjun (范现军), CEO. Born November 1970; graduated from Shandong University of Technology in automobile application engineering in 1996. He worked at Beiqi Foton from 1993, serving as vice president of Beiqi Foton from December 2017 to June 2018, and joined the Geely commercial-vehicle group in June 2018, holding roles including head of the commercial-vehicle research institute, group president and CEO. He is also chairman of Hanma Technology [9].
  • Lou Yuanfa (娄源发), president. Born May 1976, senior engineer; graduated from Zhengzhou University in mechanical and electronic engineering in 1997. He joined Geely Holding in March 2004, served as rotating vice president of Geely Auto (December 2020–January 2022), co-president of Lifan Technology and chairman and CEO of Livan Auto (February 2022–April 2024), and vice president of Geely Auto Group (April 2024–March 2026), before becoming president of Farizon [19].

Former chairman Zhou Jianqun passed away due to illness on 15 February 2026, according to Hanma Technology announcements reported by media [22][23].

Ownership and governance

Farizon's shareholders are Geely Group (Ningbo) 59.9%, Zhejiang Geely Holding Group 40% and Ningbo Yima Enterprise Management Partnership 0.1%, with registered capital of RMB 500 million and Dai Qing as legal representative [10]. Its listed subsidiary Hanma Technology is 19.98% held by the Farizon group (320,293,659 shares) with Li Shufu as actual controller [12][17]. In October 2026 Farizon Commercial Vehicle Technology added Gotion High-Tech and CITIC Securities Investment as shareholders, raising registered capital to RMB 1.06 billion [18].

Competition

Farizon's competitive set differs by segment: in new-energy vans and light trucks it competes with SAIC-GM-Wuling, Chery, Foton and Dongfeng; in new-energy heavy trucks, Hanma Technology competes with Sany, XCMG, FAW Jiefang and Dongfeng, plus new entrants such as DeepWay, Lingyi Auto (零一汽车) and Supao Technology (速豹) [17][25]. Farizon's disclosed strengths are first-mover scale in China's new-energy commercial-vehicle market, the dual pure-electric and methanol-electric route, and integration with Geely Holding's passenger-car R&D, supply chain and global channels [14].

Our assessment: scale leadership in new-energy commercial vehicles is well documented, but growth partly relies on low-price, thin-margin city-distribution vehicles where SAIC-GM-Wuling and others compete on price and channel reach, so market-share leadership does not by itself establish profit leadership. The basis is that the heavy-truck subsidiary Hanma Technology remained loss-making on a recurring basis in 2025 (net profit attributable to shareholders RMB 47.58 million, but net profit excluding non-recurring items RMB -68.81 million) [17].

Our assessment: the methanol-electric route is a genuine differentiator but depends on methanol supply and refuelling infrastructure, which limits how fast it can scale outside regions with methanol availability [3]. In new-energy heavy trucks, Hanma Technology's methanol-electric and hybrid positioning differentiates it from pure-electric rivals such as Sany and XCMG; its 2025 hybrid heavy-truck sales rose 363.5% year on year with 16 consecutive months at industry first, but the segment also attracts well-funded new entrants such as DeepWay and Lingyi Auto, so the differentiation must be continuously validated in capital-intensive competition [25].

Outlook

Company targets and plans

Farizon set a 2026 sales target of 260,000 units and announced the "30111" strategy at its January 2026 ecosystem partner conference, aiming for one million annual sales by 2030 and to become China's No.1 commercial-vehicle seller and the world's No.1 new-energy commercial-vehicle seller; CEO Fan Xianjun stated the 2030 goal publicly [15]. At the August 2026 10th-anniversary event the company said it would build the Xinghan H into a long-haul trunk-line champion under the "one Geely" strategy and that its first overseas plant would soon begin production [3]. The heavy-truck subsidiary Hanma Technology separately set a 2026 goal of 30,000 heavy-truck sales and more than 10% market share [25]. For context, the 2025 sales target announced at the December 2024 partner conference was 200,000 units, against actual 2025 sales of 162,019 [24][14].

Our analysis

Our assessment: the one-million-unit 2030 goal implies a much faster pace than recent results — roughly 162,000 units in 2025 and 107,589 in January–July 2026 — so reaching it depends on sustained export growth, new heavy-truck volume from Hanma Technology and continued city-delivery-vehicle share gains, all of which face price competition; and the 2025 target of 200,000 units was not met, which suggests near-term targets carry execution risk [14][3]. Our assessment: overseas expansion is the clearest growth driver given H1 2026 export growth of 123%, but Europe in particular carries homologation, tariff and local-service costs that the company has not quantified [3]. Whether overall profitability improves also depends on Hanma Technology sustaining its post-restructuring turnaround amid heavy-truck price competition and on city-delivery vehicles holding share against low-price rivals [17].

Risks

  • Profitability pressure. Hanma Technology reported 2025 net profit attributable to shareholders of RMB 47.58 million (down 69.74% year on year) and a recurring net loss of RMB 68.81 million excluding non-recurring items [17].
  • Restructuring and integration. Hanma Technology's restructuring plan was confirmed fully implemented by the court on 30 December 2024 with Farizon as industrial investor; consolidating the heavy-truck, light-truck and van businesses (including the October 2026 Tangjun Ouling renaming) carries execution risk [32][18].
  • Overseas and tariff exposure. Export growth to Europe and other regions is exposed to import duties, homologation and localisation costs, which the company's disclosures do not quantify [3].
  • Technology-route dependence. The methanol-electric route depends on methanol supply, refuelling infrastructure and policy support, which vary by region [3].
  • Non-disclosure of group financials. Farizon itself is an unlisted subsidiary and does not publish standalone financial statements, limiting external visibility into its consolidated profitability [6].
  • Customer concentration and receivables. Hanma Technology's 2025 annual report shows high customer concentration — its top five customers accounted for 80.45% of sales — and its accounting-estimate-change announcement disclosed end-2025 receivables and other receivables of about RMB 1.455 billion aged 0–6 months and about RMB 37 million aged 7–12 months [27][30].

概览

远程是吉利控股体系内的新能源商用车业务。集团主体于 2016 年 2 月 1 日工商注册，2016 年 10 月发布远程品牌，吉利控股称其为中国首个专注新能源领域的商用车品牌 [6][7]。远程生产重卡、轻卡、小微卡、VAN（LCV）与客车，走「纯电动＋甲醇（醇氢）电动」两大技术路线，2022–2025 年连续四年居中国新能源商用车行业销量第一，2025 年销量 162,019 辆 [14]；2026 年 1–7 月销量 107,589 辆，同比增长 37.8% [3]。

远程本身未上市，股东为吉利集团（宁波）有限公司 59.9%、浙江吉利控股集团有限公司 40%、宁波翊马企业管理合伙企业（有限合伙）0.1%，注册资本 5 亿元 [10]。其控股上市公司汉马科技集团（上交所：600375，原华菱星马）承担重卡业务，总部位于安徽马鞍山 [8]。最终实际控制人为吉利控股创始人李书福 [17]。集团整车业务包括远程品牌、淄博的欧铃（唐骏欧铃）轻卡/微卡业务与汉马科技，并配套万物友好、绿色慧联等绿色运力与能源服务生态平台 [1]。

远程的特点在于，在纯电之外另辟甲醇（醇氢）电动路线，并依托吉利的甲醇产业链积累；2026 年 8 月远程称成为全球首个达成 60 万台累计里程碑的新能源商用车品牌，2026 上半年出口同比增长 123%、覆盖 64 个国家和地区 [3]。

现状

业务结构

远程围绕重卡、轻卡、小微卡、LCV/VAN、客车五大产品线组织产品，自称中国首个实现多能源布局、全系产品新能源化的商用车品牌 [6]。整车业务包括远程品牌、淄博的欧铃（唐骏欧铃）业务与汉马科技；生态平台包括绿色慧联（城配运力）、万物友好／阳光铭岛（绿色运力与智慧车联网）[1]。重卡板块汉马科技 2025 年营收 65.53 亿元（同比 +55.20%），其中整车及底盘收入 43.78 亿元（+83.46%）、专用车收入 14.08 亿元（+33.75%）、汽车零部件收入 5.31 亿元（-13.72%）[17][26]，并自 2025 年 12 月 31 日起停止国内传统燃油整车生产销售 [17]。在整车销售之外，远程自称升级为全生命周期生态服务商，购车端推出车电分离、租售灵活方案，运营端搭建「天地一体化」智慧运力平台，服务端打造「程心服务」品牌 [3]。

技术与路线

远程核心技术路线为「纯电动＋甲醇（醇氢）电动」。2019 年 4 月上市的 M100 甲醇重卡，公司称是全球首款量产甲醇重卡 [1][7]。集团称建成中国最大的新能源商用车研究院，研发工程师超 2000 人，累计申请专利 4000 余项、主导参与 86 项国家与行业及团体标准 [2][20]。2026 年远程引入吉利金砖电池商用车版与超充生态，并披露星瀚 H 纯电牵引车实现 1+1 至 1+4 编队能力 [3]。2026 年 4 月，全球甲醇电动生态联盟在 2026 智能电动汽车发展高层论坛期间成立；吉利远程副总裁兼首席品牌官宋兆桓称吉利在甲醇领域已深耕二十余年，绿色燃料首次被写入政府工作报告、国家及地方相关政策已出台 80 多项 [25]。

产品

五大产品线的代表车型包括：星享 V 系列城配 VAN（星享 V7E 于 2024 年 4 月上市、定价 11.28 万元起），以及被称为全球首款正向研发电动大 VAN 的远程超级 VAN（2024 年 4 月上市、12.88 万元起）[5]。

制造基地

官网列示四川南充、江西上饶、安徽马鞍山、山东淄博、湖南湘潭五大产品线专业制造基地 [2]；发展历程另记山西晋中甲醇重卡基地与浙江湖州客车工厂 [1]。南充基地承担客车与 V 系列 VAN 生产；原唐骏欧铃的淄博基地 2026 年 10 月更名山东吉利远程汽车制造有限公司 [21][18]。

最近业绩（截至 2026-10-29）

集团层面，2026 年 1–7 月销量 107,589 辆（同比 +37.8%），上半年出口同比增长 123%、覆盖 64 个国家和地区 [3]。控股上市公司汉马科技 2026 年半年度报告（2026 年 8 月 22 日披露）显示，上半年营收 38.31 亿元（同比 +34.56%）、归母净利润 3,321.13 万元（同比 +19.77%）、扣非净利润 288.67 万元（扭亏为盈），销售货车 7,808 辆（同比 +25.43%），其中新能源中重卡 6,756 辆（同比 +52.37%）、醇氢动力系统 966 台 [13]。

国内外市场地位

远程 2025 年以 162,019 辆连续四年居中国新能源商用车行业销量第一 [14]。海外方面，2025 年覆盖 26 个国家和地区（海外业务同比增长近 500%），2026 上半年扩展至 64 个国家和地区 [14][3]；汉马科技 2025 年国外主营业务收入 261.31 万元（同比 +351.25%）、外销毛利率 23.58%，国内主营业务收入 63.37 亿元；产销表中重卡境内销量 13,967 辆、境外销量 8 辆（上年 2 辆）[26][29]；2026 年 9 月首次亮相德国汉诺威商用车展（IAA），星瀚 H 欧洲版于 2026 年 8 月下线 [4][3]。

远程新能源商用车集团是吉利控股体系内的非上市子公司，不单独披露合并财务报表，故本表列示控股上市公司汉马科技集团股份有限公司（上交所：600375，合并重卡业务）的财务数据作为参考，并非远程集团口径。币种人民币、单位百万元、准则为中国企业会计准则；净利润为「归属于上市公司股东（母公司股东）的净利润」，毛利率为「主营业务（加工制造业）毛利率」。FY2023 数据取自 2025 年年度报告近三年主要会计数据：营收 38.77 亿元、归母净利润 -9.63 亿元。FY2024 数据取自同一张表：营收 42.22 亿元、归母净利润 1.57 亿元、加工制造业毛利率 2.98%，扣非净利润 -10.79 亿元。FY2025 数据取自 2025 年年度报告：营收 65.53 亿元（同比 +55.20%）、归母净利润 0.48 亿元（同比 -69.74%）、加工制造业毛利率 8.86%（同比 +5.88 个百分点）、扣非净利润 -0.69 亿元。研发投入 2024 年为 1.61 亿元（占营收 3.82%），2025 年为 2.03 亿元（占营收 3.10%，全部费用化、无资本化）。本页仅列示公开披露信息，不构成任何交易推荐。

竞争格局

远程的竞争格局因细分市场而异：在新能源 VAN 与轻卡领域，与上汽通用五菱、奇瑞、福田、东风等竞争；在新能源重卡领域，汉马科技与三一、徐工、一汽解放、东风及 DeepWay、零一汽车、速豹等新进入者竞争 [17][25]。远程披露的优势在于：在中国新能源商用车市场先发规模领先、「纯电＋甲醇电动」双路线，以及与吉利控股乘用车研发、供应链、全球渠道的协同 [14]。

我们的判断：远程在新能源商用车领域的规模领先有据可查，但其增长部分依赖低价、薄利的城配车型，而上汽通用五菱等对手在价格与渠道覆盖上同样强势，因此市占率领先并不直接等于盈利领先。依据是：重卡子公司汉马科技 2025 年归母净利润仅 0.48 亿元、扣非净利润 -0.69 亿元，经常性层面仍亏损 [17]。

我们的判断：甲醇电动路线是差异化优势，但依赖甲醇供应与加注基础设施，在甲醇资源不足的区域规模化速度受限 [3]。在新能源重卡领域，汉马科技以甲醇电动与混动形成差异化，区别于三一、徐工等纯电路线对手，其 2025 年混动重卡销量同比增长 363.5%、连续 16 个月行业第一可作支撑；但该赛道也吸引 DeepWay、零一汽车等新进入者获得大额融资，差异化优势需在资本密集竞争中被持续验证 [25]。

未来展望

公司目标与规划

远程在 2026 年 1 月生态伙伴大会上提出 2026 年销量目标 26 万辆，并发布「30111」战略，力争 2030 年实现年销 100 万辆、成为国内商用车销量第一与全球新能源商用车销量第一；CEO 范现军公开宣布了这一 2030 年目标 [15]。2026 年 8 月十周年活动上，公司表示将在「一个吉利」战略下把星瀚 H 打造为干线运输冠军车型，首个海外工厂将于近期下线 [3]。重卡子公司汉马科技另提出 2026 年重卡销量 3 万辆、市场份额突破 10% 的目标 [25]。作为参照，2024 年 12 月生态伙伴大会公布的 2025 年销量目标为 20 万辆，实际 2025 年销量为 162,019 辆 [24][14]。

我们的分析

我们的判断：2030 年 100 万辆目标意味着明显快于近期的节奏——2025 年约 16.2 万辆、2026 年 1–7 月 10.76 万辆，达成需依赖出口持续放量、汉马科技重卡增量与城配车型份额继续提升，而这些均面临价格竞争；且 2025 年 20 万辆目标未完成，说明近期目标存在执行风险 [14][3]。我们的判断：2026 上半年出口同比增长 123%，海外扩张是最清晰的增长来源，但欧洲市场尤其涉及认证、关税与本地服务成本，公司披露中尚未量化这些成本 [3]。整体盈利能否改善，还取决于汉马科技在重卡价格竞争中维持重整后的扭亏势头，以及城配车型能否在低价对手的冲击下守住份额 [17]。

风险

  • 盈利压力。重卡子公司汉马科技 2025 年归母净利润 0.48 亿元（同比 -69.74%），扣非净利润 -0.69 亿元，经常性层面仍亏损 [17]。
  • 重整与整合。汉马科技重整计划于 2024 年 12 月 30 日获法院裁定执行完毕、远程为产业投资人；重卡、轻卡、VAN 业务的整合（含 2026 年 10 月唐骏欧铃更名）存在执行风险 [32][18]。
  • 海外与关税风险。面向欧洲等地的出口面临进口关税、认证与本地化成本，公司披露未量化这些成本 [3]。
  • 技术路线依赖。甲醇电动路线依赖甲醇供应、加注基础设施与相关政策支持，区域差异较大 [3]。
  • 集团财务未单独披露。远程本身为非上市子公司，不单独披露财务报表，外部对其整体盈利可见度有限 [6]。
  • 客户集中与应收账款风险。汉马科技 2025 年年报显示客户集中度较高——前五大客户占销售额的 80.45%——其会计估计变更公告披露，截至 2025 年末 0–6 个月账龄应收账款与其他应收款约 14.55 亿元、7–12 个月约 0.37 亿元 [27][30]。
