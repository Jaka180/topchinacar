Overview

SAIC Motor Corporation Limited (上海汽车集团股份有限公司) is a state-owned automaker headquartered in Shanghai and ultimately controlled by the Shanghai State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission. Its roots trace to 1955, when the predecessor Shanghai Internal Combustion Engine Parts Manufacturing Company was founded; the listed company was incorporated and listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange (600104) in 1997 and completed an overall listing in 2011 [5]. It is one of China's largest vehicle groups: in 2025 it sold 4.507 million vehicles, with self-owned brands (Roewe, MG, IM, Maxus, Wuling, Baojun) contributing 65% of volume [10].

Revenue comes mainly from vehicle and parts manufacturing, plus financial services, and from three large joint ventures with Volkswagen, General Motors and, through SAIC-GM-Wuling, with GM and Guangxi Automobile Group. Its business spans six segments: complete vehicles, parts, mobility and services, finance, international operations and innovation technology [5]. Consolidated total operating revenue reached RMB656.24 billion in 2025; SAIC ranked 125th on the 2026 Fortune Global 500 (revenue USD91.30 billion, its 22nd appearance, up 13 places from 138th a year earlier) [5]. In May 2026 it delivered its 100 millionth vehicle, becoming the first Chinese automaker to cross that cumulative milestone [25]. The 2025 result marked a rebound from a difficult 2024, when net profit attributable to owners of the parent fell 88.19% year-on-year amid asset impairments at SAIC-GM [2].

History

  1. 1955

    The predecessor Shanghai Internal Combustion Engine Parts Manufacturing Company is established, marking SAIC's beginnings.[33][34]

  2. 1958

    The first Fenghuang (Phoenix) sedan is trial-produced, later renamed the Shanghai brand in the 1960s.[13]

  3. 1985

    SAIC Volkswagen is established with Volkswagen as China's first passenger-car joint venture, starting Santana localization.[13]

  4. 1997

    SAIC-GM is set up with General Motors as Shanghai's 'No.1 project'; SAIC Motor Corporation is incorporated and listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange (600104).[13][1]

  5. 2002-11

    SAIC-GM-Wuling (SGMW) is formed by SAIC, GM and Wuling in Liuzhou.[15][23]

  6. 2004-06

    On 16 June 2004 SAIC signs a cooperation agreement with Britain's MG Rover to fund the development of new MG and Rover models; SAIC then acquires Rover intellectual property in stages — GBP 37 million in 2004 for other Rover models and the K-series engine, and a further GBP 30 million on 13 January 2005 for the Rover 25.[29][19]

  7. 2005-07

    Nanjing Auto acquires the MG brand and remaining MG Rover assets for about GBP 53 million.[19]

  8. 2006-10

    SAIC launches Roewe, its first self-owned passenger-car brand.[13]

  9. 2007-01

    The Roewe 750, built on the Rover 75 platform, goes on sale.[19]

  10. 2007-12

    SAIC merges with Nanjing Auto (the 'SAIC-Nanjing' deal), acquiring the Yuejin auto assets including MG for RMB2.095 billion, China's largest automaker merger at the time.[20][19]

  11. 2010

    Baojun, the first own brand of SAIC-GM-Wuling, is launched.[13][23]

  12. 2011

    SAIC launches its commercial-vehicle brand SAIC MAXUS (Maxus), built on LDV technology, and completes an overall listing of the group.[13][5]

  13. 2016

    SAIC launches the Roewe RX5, which SAIC marketed as an 'internet car'.[13]

  14. 2020-12

    IM Motors (Zhiji Auto) is founded by SAIC, Zhangjiang Hi-Tech and Alibaba Group.[14]

  15. 2021-10

    Feifan (Rising Auto), spun off from Roewe's R brand, begins operating as an independent company.[22]

  16. 2024-07

    Wang Xiaoqiu is elected chairman and Jia Jianxu is appointed president on 10 July 2024, forming a new management team; chairman Chen Hong retires.[30][17]

  17. 2024-10

    Roewe and Feifan brands are officially merged, and SAIC forms the 'big passenger-vehicle' unit.[24][22]

  18. 2025

    SAIC reports 2024 net profit attributable to owners of the parent of RMB1.67 billion, down 88.19% year-on-year, after SAIC-GM recorded asset impairments in Q4 2024; in 2025 SAIC launches the Shangjie brand with Huawei.[2][3][16]

  19. 2026-05

    SAIC delivers its 100 millionth vehicle, becoming the first Chinese automaker with cumulative production and sales exceeding 100 million units.[13][25]

  20. 2026-08

    SAIC and General Motors sign a renewal agreement extending the SAIC-GM joint venture by 20 years to 2047.[11]

Current operations

Business structure

By product, the 2025 vehicle business recorded operating revenue of RMB410.20 billion (62.51% of total operating revenue), parts RMB203.02 billion (30.94%), services and others RMB32.94 billion (5.02%), and the finance business RMB10.09 billion (1.54%). Segment gross margins were 4.30% for whole vehicles, 19.15% for parts, 26.38% for services and 88.47% for the finance business. By region, revenue from China was RMB504.14 billion (gross margin 10.82%) and from other regions RMB152.10 billion (gross margin 12.88%) [1].

In 2025, selling expenses were RMB20.90 billion (up 4.07%), administrative expenses RMB22.45 billion (up 6.37%), R&D expenses RMB18.10 billion (up 2.57%) and investment income RMB13.43 billion (up 87.15%) [1]. In the first half of 2026, SAIC reported consolidated total operating revenue of RMB298.65 billion and net profit attributable to owners of the parent of RMB5.15 billion; core net profit (excluding foreign-exchange and impairment effects) was RMB7.87 billion, up 72% year-on-year, with gross margin of 12.6% and operating cash flow of RMB54.3 billion (up 158%) [25].

Technology and platforms

SAIC is advancing solid-state and semi-solid-state batteries, the Lingxi digital chassis, the DMH super hybrid and Hengxing super extended-range systems, and end-to-end assisted-driving large models. In 2025 it disclosed that its second-generation semi-solid-state battery was fitted and delivered in volume on the new MG4, and that the Hengxing super extended-range and Lingxi chassis 2.0 entered volume production [10].

Representative products include the new MG4 with a semi-solid-state battery version [10]. SAIC's August 2025 pre-sale release for the new-generation IM LS6 specifies a Lingxi digital chassis, a turning radius as low as 4.79 m, and mapless NOA through IM AD 3.0 [32]. The IM LS9 uses the Hengxing extended-range system, with a company-disclosed combined range of 1,508 km [16].

In the joint-venture segment, SAIC-GM's Buick launched the high-end new-energy sub-brand Zhijing with the 'Xiaoyao' super fusion architecture in April 2025, and SAIC Audi's new AUDI brand launched its first production model, the E5 Sportback, in September 2025 [16]. The company works with Huawei, Momenta, Horizon Robotics, OPPO and Doubao; in 2025 it launched the Shangjie brand with Huawei, whose first model the Shangjie H5 topped 10,000 monthly sales [16]. In 2025, consolidated R&D investment was RMB21.71 billion, with 30,300 R&D staff, 17.6% of the workforce [1]. The company said it has invested over RMB150 billion in R&D over more than a decade and holds nearly 25,000 valid patents [13]. In industrial investment, according to a People's Daily Wenchuang (人民日报文创) report on 11 August 2026, SAIC is the largest institutional shareholder of the autonomous-driving firm Momenta, which listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in July 2026; the memory-chip company CXMT, in which SAIC Financial Holdings had earlier invested, listed on the STAR Market in July 2026 [12].

Plants and capacity

SAIC operates 13 complete-vehicle bases plus supporting parts and logistics bases across northeast, east, south, southwest, central-south and northwest China; overseas it has nine complete-vehicle and KD plants, with products and services reaching more than 170 countries and regions and over 3,800 sales and service outlets [5]. According to a People's Daily Wenchuang (人民日报文创) report on 11 August 2026, SAIC announced in June 2026 its first wholly owned European production base in Spain, investing about EUR200 million for planned annual capacity of 120,000 vehicles, with production expected to start by the end of 2028 [12]. In 2024–2025 the company formed a 'big passenger-vehicle' unit, integrating SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle (Roewe/Feifan and MG), SAIC International, the innovation R&D institute, Z-one Technology and the overseas mobility company [24].

Domestic market position

In 2025 SAIC sold 4.507 million vehicles, up 12.3% year-on-year, with a market share of 13.1% (up 0.3 percentage points) and retail sales of 4.67 million units; self-owned brands sold 2.928 million units, up 21.6%, or 65% of group volume [10]. BYD sold 4.6024 million vehicles in 2025, ahead of SAIC's wholesale volume for the full year [31]. According to a People's Daily Wenchuang (人民日报文创) report on 11 August 2026, SAIC sold 2.384 million vehicles in January–July 2026, which the company said kept it leading Chinese automakers, with self-owned brands at 72.4% of volume (1.725 million units, up 13.6%) [12]. New-energy vehicles totalled 1.643 million units in 2025, up 33.1% [10].

Overseas business

In 2025 SAIC exported and sold overseas 1.071 million vehicles, up 3.1%, with cumulative overseas sales exceeding 6 million units; it has one 300,000-unit-scale regional market (Europe) and five 50,000-unit-scale markets (Americas, Middle East, Australia-New Zealand, ASEAN and South Asia) [10]. SGMW's Indonesia base raised new-energy vehicles to 80% of its production and sales, and SAIC is building a plant in Egypt with Mansour Group while advancing a European base plan [10]. Among overseas entities, SAIC CP (Thailand) sold 29,391 units, Wuling Indonesia 21,016 units and MG Motor India 69,997 units in 2025 [27]. According to a People's Daily Wenchuang (人民日报文创) report on 11 August 2026, MG sold 218,000 units in Europe in January–July 2026, up 22.8%, and SAIC's total overseas sales were 876,000 units, up 52.1% [12]. The company's self-operated ocean-going ro-ro fleet has grown to 21 vessels [10].

Founders and leadership

Founder

As a state-owned group, SAIC has no single entrepreneur-founder; its history traces back to the 1955 Shanghai Internal Combustion Engine Parts Manufacturing Company, and the listed company dates from its 1997 incorporation [13].

Current management

  • Wang Xiaoqiu (王晓秋) — chairman and party secretary. Born August 1964; PhD in engineering, professor-level senior engineer. He held quality-assurance roles at Shanghai Volkswagen, served as general manager of Shanghai GM, and as SAIC vice president with responsibility for the passenger-vehicle unit and technical centre. He was appointed SAIC president on 19 July 2019 [28], and was elected chairman on 10 July 2024 [30]; his career details are in the official board biography [6].
  • Jia Jianxu (贾健旭) — director, president and deputy party secretary. Born February 1978; MBA. He was deputy general manager and executive deputy general manager of Shanghai Yanfeng Johnson Controls Seating, and deputy general manager and then general manager of Yanfeng Automotive Trim Systems, and managed SAIC's Luxembourg, Europe and Hong Kong operations, before serving as SAIC vice president and president of SAIC Volkswagen. He was appointed SAIC president on 10 July 2024 [30]; his career details are in the official board biography [6].
  • The board also includes external directors Ge Dawei and Huang Jian, independent directors Chen Naiwei, Sun Zheng and Song Xiaoyan, and employee representative director Hua Ende [6].

Ownership and governance

The Wang Xiaoqiu and Jia Jianxu team took over in July 2024 (former chairman Chen Hong retired at the mandatory age), driving reform of the self-owned-brand business [17]. As of 30 June 2026, the controlling shareholder SAIC Motor (Group) Corporation held 63.71%, China COSCO Shipping Group 5.91% and Nanjing Yuejin Automobile Group 3.60%, with total share capital of 11.495 billion shares; the actual controller is Shanghai SASAC [7].

Competition

Our judgment. SAIC competes domestically with BYD, Geely, Changan, Chery and Great Wall Motor, and internationally with global automakers. Its competitive base rests on vehicle scale, coverage across self-owned and joint-venture brands, parts operations and MG's overseas channels. Supporting facts include 4.507 million vehicle sales in 2025, with self-owned brands accounting for 65% [16], and more than 300,000 MG sales in Europe that year; SAIC described MG as Europe's best-selling Chinese brand [10].

In 2024, SAIC's auto-manufacturing gross margin was 9.38% and net profit attributable to owners of the parent fell 88.19%. The annual report attributed the decline mainly to lower vehicle sales, price competition reducing revenue and gross profit, and impairments at SAIC-GM and its subsidiaries reducing investment income [35].

Our judgment. These disclosures indicate that SAIC's results are sensitive to domestic price competition and joint-venture performance. Whether the 2025 recovery continues depends in part on the quality of self-owned-brand growth and a recovery in joint-venture profitability [35][10].

Outlook

Company plans. SAIC and General Motors renewed the SAIC-GM joint venture to 2047 (agreement signed 5 August 2026), with the 50:50 split unchanged [11]. According to a People's Daily Wenchuang (人民日报文创) report on 11 August 2026, SAIC Volkswagen was renewed in November 2024 through 2040, planning 18 new models by 2030 (15 China-specific) and seven new-energy products in 2026, including the ID. ERA 9X [12]. The same report said that in April 2026 SAIC signed a strategic agreement with Audi to set up Audi's first electric-intelligent technology centre in Shanghai, developing models on the ADP platform such as the AUDI E7X [12]. The company also plans more than 20 new models in 2026 across self-owned and joint-venture brands, and is advancing solid-state batteries, the Shangjie brand with Huawei, and the overseas 'Glocal 3.0' strategy [10].

Our judgment. Whether the recovery continues depends on two premises: self-owned brands continuing to gain share (65% of volume in 2025; 72.4% in January–July 2026, according to a People's Daily Wenchuang report on 11 August 2026) and the joint-venture business stabilising after the renewal agreements. Continued domestic price competition, tighter trade restrictions or faster assisted-driving and battery technology cycles could impede the recovery. Whether brand and organisational integration improves coordination, and whether production technologies achieve sustained scale, requires further operating evidence [35][12].

Risks

  • Price competition and margins. In its 2024 annual report, SAIC cited lower sales and price competition reducing revenue and gross profit as reasons for the earnings decline; auto-manufacturing gross margin was 9.38% [35].
  • Joint-venture business. The 2024 annual report disclosed that impairments at SAIC-GM and its subsidiaries in the fourth quarter substantially reduced SAIC's investment income [35].
  • Trade and tariff barriers. The 2024 annual report disclosed adverse effects from the EU anti-subsidy investigation on overseas operations and responses including accelerated vehicle shipments and additional HEV models [35].
  • Foreign-exchange risk. The 2024 annual report identified foreign-exchange exposure from assets, liabilities and transactions denominated in currencies including US dollars, euros and Hong Kong dollars; SAIC also stated that it had no significant foreign-exchange risk at the end of 2024 [35].
  • Technology and product cycles. In its 2024 annual-report risk disclosures, SAIC said that competition over features and faster product cycles driven by intelligent technologies posed challenges to automakers' profitability [35].

概览

上海汽车集团股份有限公司是一家总部位于上海、最终由上海市国有资产监督管理委员会控股的国有汽车集团。其前身可追溯至 1955 年成立的上海市内燃机配件制造公司，1997 年设立并在上海证券交易所上市（600104），2011 年实现整体上市 [5]。它是中国规模最大的汽车集团之一：2025 年销售整车 450.7 万辆，其中荣威、MG、智己、大通、五菱、宝骏等自主品牌销量占比达 65% [10]。

公司收入主要来自整车与零部件制造，以及金融业务，并依托上汽大众、上汽通用、上汽通用五菱三大合资企业，业务覆盖整车、零部件、移动出行和服务、金融、国际经营、创新科技六大板块 [5]。2025 年合并营业总收入达 6562.44 亿元，位列 2026 年《财富》世界 500 强第 125 位（营收 913.01 亿美元，第 22 次上榜，较上年第 138 位上升 13 位）[5]。2026 年 5 月，上汽交付第 1 亿辆车，成为中国汽车工业首家累计产销突破 1 亿辆的汽车集团 [25]。2025 年业绩从艰难的 2024 年回升：2024 年归母净利润受上汽通用大额资产减值影响同比下降 88.19% [2]。

历史沿革

现状

业务结构

分产品看，2025 年整车业务收入 4101.96 亿元（占营业总收入 62.51%）、零部件 2030.20 亿元（30.94%）、劳务及其他 329.36 亿元（5.02%）、金融业务 100.92 亿元（1.54%）。分业务毛利率分别为：整车 4.30%、零部件 19.15%、劳务及其他 26.38%、金融业务 88.47%。分地区看，中国地区收入 5041.39 亿元（毛利率 10.82%），其他地区 1521.05 亿元（毛利率 12.88%）[1]。

2025 年销售费用 208.98 亿元（同比 +4.07%）、管理费用 224.53 亿元（+6.37%）、研发费用 181.04 亿元（+2.57%）、投资收益 134.33 亿元（+87.15%）[1]。2026 年上半年，上汽实现合并营业总收入 2986.5 亿元、归母净利润 51.5 亿元；剔除汇兑及减值影响后的核心归母净利润 78.7 亿元，同比增长 72%，毛利率 12.6%，经营活动现金流量净额 543 亿元（同比 +158%）[25]。

技术与平台

上汽推进固态与半固态电池、灵蜥数字底盘、DMH 超级混动与恒星超级增程、端到端智驾大模型等技术。2025 年公司披露，其第二代半固态电池技术在全新 MG4 上实现搭载并批量交付，恒星超级增程、灵蜥底盘 2.0 等实现量产装车 [10]。

代表性产品包括推出半固态电池版本的全新 MG4 [10]。上汽 2025 年 8 月的新一代智己 LS6 预售新闻稿披露，该车搭载灵蜥数字底盘，转弯半径最低为 4.79 米，IM AD 3.0 提供无图 NOA 功能 [32]。智己 LS9 搭载恒星超级增程，公司披露其综合续航为 1508 公里 [16]。

合资板块同步转型：2025 年 4 月上汽通用别克推出高端新能源子品牌“至境”及“逍遥”超级融合架构，2025 年 9 月上汽奥迪新品牌 AUDI 首款量产车型 E5 Sportback 上市 [16]。公司与华为、Momenta、地平线、OPPO、豆包等合作，2025 年与华为推出“尚界”品牌，首款车型尚界 H5 单月销量破万 [16]。2025 年合并口径研发投入 217.06 亿元，研发人员 3.03 万人、占员工总数 17.6% [1]。公司称其十余年累计研发投入超 1500 亿元、获得有效专利近 2.5 万项 [13]。在产业投资方面，据《人民日报文创》2026 年 8 月 11 日报道，上汽是智驾方案商 Momenta 的第一大机构股东，Momenta 于 2026 年 7 月在港交所主板上市；上汽金控此前投资的存储芯片企业长鑫科技于 2026 年 7 月在科创板上市 [12]。

工厂与产能

上汽在东北、华东、华南、西南、中南与西北建有 13 个整车基地及配套零部件、物流基地；海外设有 9 家整车制造基地和 KD 工厂，产品和服务进入全球 170 多个国家和地区，营销服务网点超 3800 家 [5]。据《人民日报文创》2026 年 8 月 11 日报道，2026 年 6 月上汽宣布在西班牙建设欧洲首个全资生产基地，投资约 2 亿欧元、规划年产能 12 万辆，预计 2028 年底投产 [12]。2024–2025 年，公司组建“大乘用车”板块，将上汽乘用车（荣威飞凡、名爵）、上汽国际、创新研发总院、零束科技、海外出行五家企业一体化管理 [24]。

国内市场地位

2025 年上汽销售整车 450.7 万辆，同比增长 12.3%，市场占有率 13.1%（同比上升 0.3 个百分点），终端零售 467 万辆；自主品牌销量 292.8 万辆，同比增长 21.6%，占集团销量 65% [10]。比亚迪 2025 年销量 460.24 万辆，全年批售量高于上汽 [31]。据《人民日报文创》2026 年 8 月 11 日报道，2026 年 1–7 月上汽累计销售整车 238.4 万辆，公司称其继续位居中国汽车企业首位，自主品牌销量 172.5 万辆、同比增长 13.6%，占集团总销量 72.4% [12]。2025 年新能源车销量 164.3 万辆，同比增长 33.1% [10]。

海外业务

2025 年上汽出口及海外市场销售 107.1 万辆，同比增长 3.1%，海外累计销量突破 600 万辆；已形成 1 个“三十万辆级”（欧洲）和 5 个“五万辆级”（美洲、中东、澳新、东盟、南亚）海外区域市场 [10]。上汽通用五菱印尼基地新能源汽车产销占比提升至 80%，公司还与埃及曼苏尔集团合作在埃及建厂、并推进欧洲基地建设方案 [10]。海外主体中，上汽正大（泰国）2025 年销量 29,391 辆、印尼五菱 21,016 辆、名爵印度 69,997 辆 [27]。据《人民日报文创》2026 年 8 月 11 日报道，2026 年 1–7 月 MG 在欧洲市场销量 21.8 万辆、同比增长 22.8%；上汽同期海外累计销售 87.6 万辆、同比增长 52.1% [12]。公司自营远洋滚装船已达 21 艘 [10]。

  • 董事会还包括外部董事葛大维、黄坚，独立董事陈乃蔚、孙铮、宋晓燕，以及职工代表董事华恩德 [6]。

股权与治理

王晓秋、贾健旭团队于 2024 年 7 月完成换届接棒（原董事长陈虹到龄退休），推动自主品牌业务改革 [17]。截至 2026 年 6 月 30 日，控股股东上海汽车工业（集团）有限公司持股 63.71%，中国远洋海运集团有限公司持股 5.91%，跃进汽车集团有限公司持股 3.60%，总股本 114.95 亿股；实际控制人为上海市国资委 [7]。

竞争格局

我们的判断。上汽在国内与比亚迪、吉利、长安、奇瑞、长城等车企竞争，在海外面对跨国车企。其竞争基础是整车规模、自主与合资品牌覆盖、零部件业务及 MG 的海外渠道。事实依据包括：2025 年整车销量 450.7 万辆、自主品牌占集团销量 65% [16]；MG 当年欧洲销量超过 30 万辆，上汽称其为欧洲最畅销的中国品牌 [10]。

2024 年上汽汽车制造业毛利率为 9.38%，归母净利润同比下降 88.19%。年报将整车销量下降、价格战导致销售收入及毛利下降，以及上汽通用及其控股子公司计提减值导致投资收益减少，列为业绩下降的主要原因 [35]。

我们的判断。上述披露表明，上汽业绩对国内价格竞争及合资业务表现敏感；2025 年业绩回升能否持续，还需观察自主品牌的增长质量和合资业务的盈利恢复 [35][10]。

未来展望

公司公开规划。上汽与通用汽车于 2026 年 8 月 5 日签署协议，将上汽通用合资续约至 2047 年（50:50 股比不变）[11]。据《人民日报文创》2026 年 8 月 11 日报道，上汽大众已于 2024 年 11 月续约至 2040 年，计划到 2030 年推出 18 款新车（其中 15 款专为中国市场开发）、2026 年投放 7 款新能源产品（含 ID. ERA 9X）[12]。同篇报道称，2026 年 4 月上汽与奥迪签署战略协议，在上海设立奥迪全球首个电动智能技术中心，基于 ADP 智能数字平台开发车型（如 AUDI E7X）[12]。公司还计划 2026 年投放自主与合资品牌二十余款新车，并加速固态电池、与华为的“尚界”品牌及海外“Glocal 3.0”战略 [10]。

我们的判断。上汽 2025 年业绩回升能否延续，取决于两个前提：自主品牌持续提升份额（2025 年占 65%；据《人民日报文创》2026 年 8 月 11 日报道，2026 年 1–7 月占 72.4%），以及合资板块在续约后企稳。若国内价格竞争持续、贸易限制加剧，或辅助驾驶与电池技术迭代加快，复苏也可能受阻。品牌与组织整合能否提高协同效率、量产技术能否持续规模化，仍需后续经营结果验证 [35][12]。

风险

  • 价格竞争与利润率。公司在 2024 年报中将销量下降和价格战导致收入及毛利下降列为利润下滑的原因；当年汽车制造业毛利率为 9.38% [35]。
  • 合资业务。2024 年报披露，上汽通用及其控股子公司在第四季度计提相关资产减值准备，导致公司投资收益同比大幅减少 [35]。
  • 贸易与关税壁垒。公司在 2024 年报中披露，海外业务受到欧盟反补贴调查等因素影响，并采取新车抢运、增加 HEV 车型等措施应对 [35]。
  • 汇率风险。2024 年报披露，以美元、欧元、港币等计价的外币资产、负债及交易存在外汇风险；公司同时表示，2024 年末无重大外汇风险 [35]。
  • 技术与产品迭代。公司在 2024 年报的风险提示中指出，竞争向配置延伸、智能化技术加快产品迭代，车企盈利能力面临挑战 [35]。
