Overview

SAIC Volkswagen Automotive Co., Ltd. (上汽大众汽车有限公司, formerly Shanghai Volkswagen) is the 50/50 passenger-car joint venture between the state-owned SAIC Motor and Germany's Volkswagen Group, and China's first passenger-car joint venture. The contract was signed on 10 October 1984 in the Great Hall of the People, the company was registered on 16 February 1985 and its headquarters are in Anting, Shanghai [1][2].

The company played a formative role in China's auto industry: its Santana localization programme in the 1980s and 1990s helped take China's auto-parts industry from almost nothing to a full supply chain, as the two shareholders put it, and by its 40th anniversary in November 2024 it had accumulated more than 28 million vehicles and employed more than 26,000 people [2][7]. Financially, its net profit peaked at RMB 28.01 billion in 2018 before narrowing to RMB 4.74 billion in 2024 [9][15].

SAIC Volkswagen builds Volkswagen and, until mid-2026, Skoda vehicles, and houses the SAIC Audi premium business with the AUDI electric brand. It has long been one of China's largest passenger-car makers and a core profit source for SAIC Motor: it sold 1.024 million vehicles in 2025 (down 10.81%), though company-reported terminal sales reached 1.06 million [6][4]. In November 2024 the two shareholders extended the joint venture to 2040 and committed to 18 new models by 2030 [2].

Current operations

Business structure and brands

SAIC Volkswagen's business runs on three brands: the Volkswagen brand (its volume business), Skoda (which Volkswagen China confirmed in March 2026 will exit the Chinese market by mid-2026, with after-sales taken over by SAIC Volkswagen; Skoda's China sales fell from a 2018 peak of 341,000 units to about 15,000 in 2025), and the SAIC Audi premium business [10][11]. In 2025 the Volkswagen brand sold over 1 million vehicles at retail, retaining the title of best-selling single joint-venture brand in China; within it, the Lavida family sold over 270,000 units, the Passat family nearly 234,000, the Tiguan family over 203,000, the Tharu family over 148,000 and the Teramont family nearly 38,000. The 'Pro' family (Teramont Pro, Passat Pro, Lavida Pro) reached nearly 122,000 units, 3.2 times the prior year [4]. SAIC Audi sold over 47,000 units in 2025, up 23%, a third consecutive record year [4]. The Volkswagen-brand line-up spans the Santana, Passat, Lavida, Tiguan, Teramont, Tharu, Lamando and Polo families, plus the ID. series and ID. ERA range [1].

Technology and platforms

The company builds its Volkswagen-brand EVs on the MEB platform: the first MEB model, the ID.4 X, launched in March 2021, followed by the ID.6 X in June 2021 [1]. From 2026 it is rolling out the ID. ERA line-up across extended-range, plug-in-hybrid and pure-electric powertrains. The ID. ERA 9X, a large six-seat extended-range SUV, launched on 25 April 2026 with an official guide price of RMB 309,800–359,800 and a limited-time price from RMB 299,800 [22]. The ID. ERA 8X, a large five-seat extended-range SUV, launched on 12 October 2026 with a limited-time price of RMB 206,800–269,800 [23]. The ID. ERA 5S, a plug-in-hybrid family sedan, launched at the August 2026 Chengdu auto show with a limited-time price from RMB 89,900 [9]. Two compact EVs on the China-specific CMP platform are due from 2026 [2]. On intelligent driving, the Volkswagen brand uses the IQ.Pilot assisted-driving system co-developed with Zoyu to bring high-speed NOA to fuel cars, and the Audi A5L Sportback uses Huawei's Qiankun technology, described by the company as the first fuel car to carry Huawei technology [4].

Plants and capacity

The company's headquarters are in Anting, Shanghai, with production bases in Nanjing, Yizheng, Ningbo, Changsha and Urumqi [7]. Its MEB new-energy plant in Anting, with design capacity of 240,000 units, was completed in November 2019 [1]. Per SAIC Motor's 2023 annual report, SAIC Volkswagen had design capacity of 2.088 million units with capacity utilization of about 58% [24]. In 2025 it closed its Nanjing Jiangning plant (annual capacity about 360,000 units, building the Passat and Skoda Superb), moving Passat production to Yizheng [25]. Chinese media report that by 2025 SAIC Volkswagen's design capacity had fallen to about 1.92 million units with production of about 1.058 million and utilization of about 55% [24].

Volume trajectory and latest results

SAIC Volkswagen's wholesale sales peaked at 2.0651 million units in 2018 and have mostly declined since: 2.0018 million in 2019, 1.5055 million in 2020, 1.242 million in 2021, 1.3208 million in 2022, 1.215 million in 2023, 1.148 million in 2024 and 1.024 million in 2025 [9][21]. On the financial side, H1 2025 revenue was RMB 60.147 billion and net profit attributable to owners of the parent was RMB 0.884 billion; because the company dropped out of SAIC Motor's 'major investees' list, full-year 2025 financials are no longer separately disclosed [5][24]. On the operational side, the most recent A-level disclosure is SAIC Motor's September 2026 production-and-sales bulletin: SAIC Volkswagen sold 492,643 units in the first nine months of 2026, down 34.52% year-on-year, with 60,402 units in September (down 35.80%) [13].

Founders and leadership

Founder

As a Sino-German joint venture, SAIC Volkswagen has no individual entrepreneur-founder. It was created through the 1984 contract between SAIC Motor's predecessor and Volkswagen Group, and its management and strategy are set jointly by the two shareholders [2].

Current management

  • Wu Yun (吴赟) — general manager since 3 August 2026, succeeding Tao Hailong. Born 1979, he holds a bachelor's degree in engineering and joined SAIC Volkswagen in 2003, working in product engineering, manufacturing, marketing, strategic planning and model-line management; he helped drive the AUDI brand's China localization. In 2025 he served as general manager of Bosch Huayu Steering Systems before returning to SAIC Volkswagen in 2026 as executive deputy general manager of personnel and organization [3].
  • Tao Hailong (陶海龙) — former general manager (July 2024 to August 2026). Born 1968, he spent more than thirty years in the industry and long worked in SAIC Volkswagen's quality and manufacturing departments, later serving as executive director of quality assurance and deputy general manager of SAIC Motor's passenger-vehicle division, general manager of Shanghai Automotive Transmission Co., and general manager of Huayu Automotive Systems. He drove the 'oil-and-electric' strategy and the launch of the ID. ERA series, Audi E5 Sportback and E7X, and later moved to Huayu Automotive Systems as general manager [3][26].
  • Jia Jianxu (贾健旭) — earlier general manager (February 2023 to July 2024). Born 1978, he was appointed SAIC Volkswagen general manager in February 2023, became SAIC Motor vice president in December 2023 and SAIC Motor president on 10 July 2024 [s28b][27].
  • Fu Qiang (傅强) — executive deputy general manager of sales and marketing since August 2024, and general manager of Shanghai SAIC Volkswagen Sales Co. Born 1977, he joined SAIC Volkswagen in 1999 and rose through sales-service-centre, network-development, powertrain and platform, and ID. marketing roles before leading Volkswagen-brand marketing operations [28].

Ownership and governance

The company is 50% owned by SAIC Motor and 50% by the Volkswagen side (German Volkswagen and its affiliates); the official wording is that the Chinese and German sides each hold 50% [7]. Registered capital is RMB 11.5 billion, and the company is carried as an equity-method investee in SAIC Motor's financial statements [5]. A full list of the board and executive committee has not been found in A-level public disclosure.

Competition

SAIC Volkswagen competes in China's mass-market and premium passenger-car segments. In the fuel-car market, the Volkswagen brand ranked first among single brands with about 8.3% market share in 2025 [14], with the Lavida, Passat and Tiguan families leading their segments [4]. In new-energy vehicles, it positions the ID. ERA range against domestic extended-range and pure-electric rivals; media report that the ID. ERA 8X (limited-time price from RMB 206,800) is priced below the Li Auto L6, while the ID. ERA 5S (limited-time price from RMB 89,900) undercuts the BYD Qin L DM-i [9]. The SAIC Audi/AUDI business competes with BMW, Mercedes-Benz, FAW Audi and Chinese premium EV brands; in October 2026 Audi China split the four-ring brand to FAW while SAIC Audi focuses on the AUDI electric line-up, and under the transition arrangement SAIC Audi may keep selling four-ring models until end-2027 [12].

Our judgment. SAIC Volkswagen's strengths are its manufacturing system, a dealer network of over 1,000 Volkswagen-brand outlets and a still-strong fuel-car franchise, which supports the transition financially [4]. Its weakness is structural: overall volume fell 10.81% in 2025, after a 5.51% decline in 2024, and the decline widened to 34.52% in the first nine months of 2026, while its ID. ERA flagship models have not sustained launch momentum — media report the ID. ERA 9X's monthly retail falling from 5,004 units in May 2026 to 910 in September [6][21][13][9]. Profit has narrowed sharply, from RMB 28.01 billion in 2018 to RMB 4.74 billion in 2024, reflecting margin pressure from aggressive new-energy pricing [9]. In the premium segment, SAIC Audi's volume remains much smaller than FAW Audi's — media report that FAW Audi accounts for the large majority of Audi's China sales, so SAIC Audi's scale advantages are limited [12].

Outlook

Company plans. At the November 2024 extension, the shareholders committed to 18 new models by 2030, of which 15 are developed specifically for China; from 2026 two compact EVs on the China-specific CMP platform will launch, followed by three plug-in hybrid and two extended-range models [2]. The company said the Volkswagen brand will launch six new-energy models in 2026 [14], and in April 2026 Audi and SAIC agreed to deepen cooperation and jointly develop the next generation of AUDI models, with an Audi innovation technology centre planned for Shanghai [7]. The company frames this as its 'joint-venture 2.0' phase, combining German manufacturing with Chinese intelligent-electric technology [3].

Our judgment. Whether SAIC Volkswagen stabilizes its volume depends on two premises: first, whether the ID. ERA extended-range, plug-in-hybrid and pure-electric line-up and the AUDI brand can offset the structural decline in its fuel-car base and the loss of Skoda (about 15,000 units in 2025); second, whether aggressive pricing on new-energy models can coexist with recovering profitability, given net profit has already narrowed from RMB 28.01 billion in 2018 to RMB 4.74 billion in 2024 [9][10]. The planned capacity reduction for fuel vehicles and the Anting base upgrade are the stated levers for this shift, but their execution and timing remain to be seen [2].

Risks

  • Volume decline. Sales fell 10.81% in 2025 (1.024 million units) and 34.52% in the first nine months of 2026 (492,643 units), a sustained contraction in the company's core business [6][13].
  • Profit compression. Net profit narrowed from RMB 28.01 billion in 2018 to RMB 4.74 billion in 2024; management has said the new-energy models are profitable but with far thinner margins than past fuel-car launches [9].
  • Brand-portfolio change. Skoda's exit by mid-2026 removes a brand (about 15,000 units in 2025) and the Audi four-ring models are being transferred to FAW, concentrating SAIC Volkswagen's premium business on the younger AUDI electric brand [10][12].
  • New-energy competition and pricing. The company competes on price in the extended-range and pure-electric segments (e.g. the ID. ERA 8X priced below the Li Auto L6), which pressures margins in an intensely price-competitive market [9].
  • Capacity restructuring. The shareholders plan to gradually cut fuel-vehicle capacity and upgrade the Anting base, a process whose cost and execution timing carry uncertainty; idle capacity and the 2025 Nanjing plant closure reflect this adjustment [2][25].
  • Overseas-trade exposure. SAIC Volkswagen's parent SAIC Motor has disclosed that its overseas business is affected by the EU countervailing-duty investigation and rising trade-protection measures, and external trade friction could affect the wider group [6].

概览

上汽大众汽车有限公司（前身上海大众）是国有控股的上汽集团与德国大众汽车集团按 50:50 合资经营的轿车企业，也是中国第一家轿车合资企业。1984 年 10 月 10 日在北京人民大会堂签署合资合营合同，1985 年 2 月 16 日注册成立，总部位于上海安亭[1][2]。

这家公司在中国汽车工业中扮演过奠基性角色：上世纪八九十年代的桑塔纳国产化，助力中国汽车零部件产业实现「从无到有」的飞跃（双方股东语）；到 2024 年 11 月 40 周年时，累计产销超 2800 万辆、员工超 2.6 万名[2][7]。财务上，其净利润 2018 年达到 280.1 亿元的历史高点，此后收窄至 2024 年的 47.4 亿元[9][15]。

上汽大众生产大众品牌及（截至 2026 年年中）斯柯达品牌车型，并经营上汽奥迪高端业务与 AUDI 电动品牌。它长期是中国规模最大的乘用车企业之一，也是上汽集团的核心利润来源：2025 年销售整车 102.4 万辆（同比下降 10.81%），公司口径终端销售达 106 万辆[6][4]。2024 年 11 月，双方股东将合资期延长至 2040 年，并承诺到 2030 年推出 18 款新车型[2]。

现状

业务结构与品牌

上汽大众的业务覆盖三个品牌：大众品牌（销量基本盘）、斯柯达（大众中国 2026 年 3 月确认将于 2026 年年中退出中国市场，售后服务由上汽大众接手；其在华销量从 2018 年 34.1 万辆的峰值降至 2025 年的约 1.5 万辆），以及上汽奥迪高端业务[10][11]。2025 年大众品牌零售销量超 100 万辆，蝉联合资单一品牌销量冠军；其中朗逸家族全年超 27 万辆、帕萨特家族近 23.4 万辆、途观家族超 20.3 万辆、途岳家族超 14.8 万辆、途昂家族近 3.8 万辆，「Pro 家族」（途昂 Pro、帕萨特 Pro、朗逸 Pro）全年终端销量近 12.2 万辆，为上一年 Pro 产品的 3.2 倍[4]。上汽奥迪 2025 年销量超 4.7 万辆、同比增长 23%，连续三年创新高[4]。大众品牌车型覆盖桑塔纳、帕萨特、朗逸、途观、途昂、途岳、凌渡、Polo 等燃油家族，以及 ID. 系列与 ID. ERA 序列等新能源产品[1]。

技术与平台

大众品牌电动车基于 MEB 平台生产：首款 MEB 车型 ID.4 X 于 2021 年 3 月上市，ID.6 X 于 2021 年 6 月上市[1]。自 2026 年起，公司推出覆盖增程、插混、纯电三条技术路线的 ID. ERA 序列。其中大型六座增程 SUV ID. ERA 9X 于 2026 年 4 月 25 日上市，官方指导价 30.98 万—35.98 万元、限时权益价 29.98 万元起[22]；大五座增程 SUV ID. ERA 8X 于 2026 年 10 月 12 日上市，限时权益价 20.68 万—26.98 万元[23]；插混家用轿车 ID. ERA 5S 于 2026 年 8 月成都车展上市，限时权益价 8.99 万元起[9]。自 2026 年起，基于专为中国市场打造的 CMP 平台的两款紧凑级电动车也将投入市场[2]。智能驾驶方面，大众品牌携手卓驭推出 IQ.Pilot 增强驾驶辅助 2.0，将端到端高快 NOA 普及至燃油车；奥迪 A5L Sportback 采用华为乾崑技术，公司称其为全球首款搭载华为技术的燃油车[4]。

生产基地与产能

公司总部位于上海安亭，并先后在南京、仪征、宁波、长沙、乌鲁木齐等地建立生产基地[7]。其位于安亭的 MEB 新能源工厂设计产能 24 万辆，于 2019 年 11 月落成[1]。据上汽集团 2023 年年度报告，上汽大众设计产能 208.8 万辆、产能利用率约 58%[24]。2025 年公司关闭了南京江宁工厂（年产能约 36 万辆，生产帕萨特与斯柯达速派），帕萨特产线迁至仪征工厂[25]。据中国媒体报道，到 2025 年上汽大众设计产能约 192 万辆、产量约 105.8 万辆、产能利用率约 55%[24]。

销量轨迹与最新业绩

上汽大众批发销量于 2018 年达到 206.51 万辆的历史峰值，此后大体逐年回落：2019 年 200.18 万辆、2020 年 150.55 万辆、2021 年 124.2 万辆、2022 年 132.08 万辆、2023 年 121.5 万辆、2024 年 114.81 万辆、2025 年 102.4 万辆[9][21]。财务方面，2025 年上半年营业总收入 601.47 亿元、归母净利润 8.84 亿元；因公司退出上汽集团「主要参股公司」名单，2025 年全年财务数据未再单独披露[5][24]。经营层面，最近一次 A 级披露为上汽集团 2026 年 9 月产销快报：上汽大众 2026 年前三季度销量 49.26 万辆、同比下降 34.52%，其中 9 月单月 6.04 万辆、同比下降 35.80%[13]。

竞争格局

上汽大众在国内大众化与豪华乘用车市场参与竞争。燃油车领域，2025 年大众品牌以约 8.3% 的市占率位列单一品牌燃油车市场第一[14]，朗逸、帕萨特、途观等家族在各细分市场保持领先[4]。新能源领域，其 ID. ERA 序列对标国内增程与纯电车型：媒体报道 ID. ERA 8X（限时权益价 20.68 万元起）定价低于理想 L6，ID. ERA 5S（限时权益价 8.99 万元起）则低于比亚迪秦 L DM-i[9]。上汽奥迪/AUDI 业务与宝马、奔驰、一汽奥迪及中国高端电动车品牌竞争；2026 年 10 月奥迪中国将四环品牌划归一汽，上汽奥迪专注 AUDI 电动车型，按过渡安排，上汽奥迪在 2027 年底前仍可销售四环车型[12]。

我们的判断。上汽大众的优势在于成熟的制造体系、超 1000 家大众品牌经销商网点，以及仍较稳固的燃油车基本盘，可为转型提供资金支撑[4]。其短板是结构性的：整体销量 2025 年同比下降 10.81%（2024 年已下降 5.51%），2026 年前三季度降幅扩大至 34.52%；其 ID. ERA 旗舰车型的上市势头未能持续，媒体报道 ID. ERA 9X 月零售量从 2026 年 5 月的 5004 辆回落至 9 月的 910 辆[6][21][13][9]。利润基本盘也大幅收窄，从 2018 年的 280.1 亿元降至 2024 年的 47.4 亿元，反映新能源车型进攻型定价带来的利润挤压[9]。在豪华细分市场，上汽奥迪的体量相对一汽奥迪仍很小——媒体报道一汽奥迪占奥迪在华销量的绝对多数，上汽奥迪的规模效应受限[12]。

未来展望

公司公开规划。2024 年 11 月续约时，双方股东承诺到 2030 年推出 18 款新车型，其中 15 款专为中国市场开发；自 2026 年起，基于专为中国市场打造的 CMP 平台的两款紧凑级电动车将投入市场，3 款插混、2 款增程车型也将陆续推出[2]。公司表示大众品牌 2026 年将推出 6 款新能源车型[14]，2026 年 4 月奥迪与上汽达成深化合作，将联合开发 AUDI 品牌下一代车型，并计划在上海设立奥迪创新技术中心[7]。公司将其定位为「合资 2.0」阶段，即德系制造与中国智电技术相结合[3]。

我们的判断。上汽大众能否稳住销量取决于两个前提：其一，ID. ERA 增程、插混与纯电序列以及 AUDI 品牌能否对冲燃油车基本盘的结构性下滑和斯柯达（2025 年约 1.5 万辆）退出造成的缺口；其二，新能源车型的进攻型定价能否与利润修复并存——其净利润已从 2018 年的 280.1 亿元收窄至 2024 年的 47.4 亿元[9][10]。逐步缩减燃油车产能、升级安亭基地是公司公开提出的转型抓手，但其执行节奏与效果仍有待观察[2]。

风险

  • 销量下滑。2025 年销量同比下降 10.81% 至 102.4 万辆，2026 年前三季度同比下降 34.52% 至 49.26 万辆，核心业务持续收缩[6][13]。
  • 利润承压。净利润从 2018 年的 280.1 亿元收窄至 2024 年的 47.4 亿元；管理层称新能源车型虽盈利，但利润空间已远不如早年燃油车新车上市时丰厚[9]。
  • 品牌组合调整。斯柯达于 2026 年年中退出（2025 年约 1.5 万辆），奥迪四环车型划归一汽，上汽大众的高端业务进一步集中到更年轻的 AUDI 电动品牌[10][12]。
  • 新能源竞争与定价。公司在增程与纯电市场以价格竞争（如 ID. ERA 8X 定价低于理想 L6），在价格竞争激烈的市场环境下对利润率构成压力[9]。
  • 产能调整。双方股东计划逐步缩减燃油车产能、升级安亭基地，这一过程在成本与执行节奏上存在不确定性；产能闲置与 2025 年南京工厂关停正反映了这一调整[2][25]。
  • 海外贸易环境。母公司上汽集团披露其海外业务受欧盟反补贴调查及欧美贸易保护措施升级影响，外部贸易摩擦可能波及整个集团[6]。
