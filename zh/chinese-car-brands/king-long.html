Overview

Xiamen King Long Motor Group Co., Ltd. (SSE: 600686) is one of China's largest bus manufacturers and China's first listed bus company. Through three whole-vehicle subsidiaries — Xiamen King Long United Automotive Industry Co., Ltd., Xiamen Golden Dragon Bus Co., Ltd. and Higer Bus Company Limited (Suzhou) — it operates the King Long, Golden Dragon and Higer brands, covering 4.5-metre to 18-metre buses for highway, tourism, transit, group commute, school and special-purpose markets, sold in more than 180 countries and regions [1][7]. In FY2025 it reported revenue of RMB 24.55 billion (+6.89%) and net profit attributable to owners of the parent of RMB 468 million (+196.90%), and sold 51,250 buses [1][4]. In H1 2026 revenue reached RMB 11.085 billion (+7.34%) and net profit attributable to shareholders RMB 276 million (+137.58%) [3].

King Long is a Fujian provincial state-controlled enterprise: controlling shareholder Fujian Motor Industry Group Co., Ltd. (福汽集团) held 32.25% at end-2025, and after buying 7,276,800 shares through centralised bidding between 2 June and 20 October 2026 its stake rose to 33.26%; the actual controller is the State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission of the Fujian Provincial People's Government [6][2]. In 2025 overseas sales revenue of RMB 12.98 billion exceeded domestic sales revenue of RMB 10.31 billion, making export the group's primary growth driver [1].

After a 2024-2025 restructuring, King Long and Golden Dragon are wholly owned subsidiaries. Suzhou King Long (Higer) remained a proposed transfer as of 2026-10-29: under the board-approved plan of October 2025, the listed company's direct stake would rise from 2.1405% to 65.2162% (before the transfer, Xiamen King Long holds 63.0757% and Suzhou Chuangyuan 34.7838%; afterwards, King Long would hold 65.2162% and Suzhou Chuangyuan 34.7838%). Tonghuashun's capital-operation page marks this transfer as in progress, and no announcement of a completed industrial-and-commercial registration was found. The company says the plan is intended to coordinate R&D, procurement, sales and after-sales across the three brands [24][25][32][16].

For FY2025 the company proposed a cash dividend of RMB 2.00 per 10 shares (before tax), totalling RMB 143.41 million; combined with the interim dividend this gives a full-year cash dividend of RMB 164.92 million, or 35.21% of net profit attributable to shareholders [1][7].

Current operations

Business structure

King Long's core business is bus development, manufacturing and sales. In FY2025 the 'automobiles and body parts' segment generated revenue of RMB 23.29 billion (94.89% of operating revenue) at a gross margin of 12.14%, with the remainder from other business [1]. By region, overseas sales reached RMB 12.98 billion (+30.56%, gross margin 16.69%) and domestic sales RMB 10.31 billion (-13.52%, gross margin 6.42%), overseas revenue exceeded domestic and was about 52.9% of total revenue [1][30]. In 2025 the group sold 51,250 buses (18,560 large, 10,528 medium, 22,162 light) and produced 52,723 (19,362 large, 11,024 medium, 22,337 light); it exported about 30,300 buses (+34.47%) [4][1]. For industry context, CAAM data cited in the 2025 annual report show China sold 566,800 buses in 2025 (+11.9%), of which 150,300 were exported (+18.51%), while the domestic large and medium bus segment fell 10% [7]. King Long sells large and medium buses mainly through direct sales supplemented by dealers, and light buses mainly through dealers [7]. Its top five customers accounted for RMB 3.29 billion of sales (13.41% of the total) and its top five suppliers RMB 4.83 billion (24.9% of purchases) in 2025, and the group had 12,619 employees at end-2025; operating cash flow was RMB 1.664 billion (+33.01%) [1][12].

Recent performance

In H1 2026 the group reported revenue of RMB 11.085 billion (+7.34%), net profit attributable to shareholders of RMB 276 million (+137.58%) and deducted (non-GAAP) net profit of RMB 194 million (+1,472.77%); it sold 23,900 buses (+5.97%) and exported 17,800 (+26.76%), including 2,777 new-energy buses (+35.2%) [3][13][15]. Overseas revenue reached RMB 7.336 billion (+21.84%), or 66.18% of operating revenue [33]. For January-September 2026 the group sold 35,679 buses; September single-month sales were 4,137 units and cumulative large-bus sales 11,923 units [27].

Products and technology

The group's portfolio spans highway coaches, city buses, school buses and special vehicles. In H1 2026 it completed development and upgrade of highway models (MC, C and Qiantu series) and transit models (M, MU9 and seventh-generation premium city bus) [28]. On electrification it has its own ISG system and 'Yiqu' new-energy management system, plus the G-BOS telematics platform; a high-performance silicon-carbide controller is already in mass production and delivery, and a flat-wire motor product matrix is being expanded [21][28]. The Longwei II scored 98.02 in C-SCAP and was rated 'China's super-five-star safety bus'; the Merry Combo's streamlined shape cuts drag by 11.8%; King Long has served the National People's Congress and CPPCC (lianghui) since 2001 with a 26-year unbroken attendance record [8]. On autonomy and hydrogen, the group runs the Robovan A3 delivery vehicle, Apolong and M6S autonomous buses, and a 49-tonne hydrogen fuel-cell semi-truck (the company website says a -30°C quick start and a range of more than 600 km; Fujian Daily says 10-15 minute refuelling, a range of more than 500 km and a -30°C cold start); it helped draft national standard GB/T 47341-2026 and completed trial builds of a low-platinum fuel-cell system and an oxide solid-state hydrogen storage system [23][8][14].

Production bases

The group's main subsidiaries include Xiamen King Long United, Xiamen Golden Dragon, Suzhou King Long (Higer), King Long Intelligent Technology, King Long (Longhai) Investment, King Long Ceremony Vehicle and King Long New Energy [7]. Higer's new-energy bus base in Suzhou covers 430 mu (about 28.7 hectares) with phase-one investment of RMB 1.5 billion and planned annual capacity of 10,000 new-energy buses, and the Scania-Higer luxury coach plant (operational since 9 November 2016) has annual capacity of over 1,000 units [21][20]. King Long's engineering research institute is in Jimei District, Xiamen [14].

Overseas and domestic markets

King Long's internationalisation is long-standing: volume exports to Iraq began in 2001, 82 buses went to Malta in 2003, and it passed UK VCA certification in 2005, and it now sells in more than 150 countries and regions with cumulative exports above 160,000 units [8]. In 2025 it marked 20 years of cooperation with a Saudi partner, with the in-service King Long fleet in Saudi Arabia exceeding 10,000 units; 121 new-energy buses went to Chile to help build Latin America's first 100% electric-bus city, and 150 natural-gas buses entered Argentina [14]. At the October 2025 Busworld expo in Belgium it launched the MU12E, MC13E, A02 and V8 new-energy models [14]. In H1 2026 the King Long brand exported 8,075 buses (+38%), ranking first among China's 3.5-metre-and-above bus exporters, while Higer (5,110) and Golden Dragon (4,656) ranked third and fourth [8][16]. Domestically, the group delivered 300 new-energy buses to Liaoning, 65 M-series buses to Heilongjiang, 228 new-energy buses to Urumqi and 145 to Chongqing [14].

Founders and leadership

Founder and corporate history

King Long has no single founder in the usual sense: its origins lie in the state-owned Xiamen King Long United Automotive Industry Co., Ltd. founded in 1988, and the modern listed company took shape through a 1993 listing on the Shanghai Stock Exchange [8][1].

Competition

Main competitors

In China's bus market King Long competes mainly with Yutong Bus (the industry leader), Zhongtong Bus, Anhui Ankai and Beiqi Foton (AUV) [16]. The phrase '一通三龙' refers to Yutong plus Suzhou King Long (Higer), Xiamen King Long and Xiamen Golden Dragon — the group's own three whole-vehicle plants — not Zhongtong, Ankai or Foton AUV [31]. The company's own H1 2026 interim report states that its large and medium bus output and sales ranked second in the industry and its export sales ranked first [28]. By growth, the group's three-year revenue CAGR of 10.4% ranked 7th among 12 commercial-vehicle companies that had disclosed 2025 data [12].

Strengths

  • Export scale: the group exported about 30,300 buses in 2025, keeping it among the industry leaders; in H1 2026 its three brands — King Long (8,075), Higer (5,110) and Golden Dragon (4,656) — ranked first, third and fourth respectively among China's exporters of buses of 3.5 metres and above, according to media statistics [8][16].
  • Full line-up and brand matrix: products span 4.5m-18m, letting the group bid for transit, tourism and light-coach tenders simultaneously [16].
  • Profitability has improved: overall gross margin rose from 9.71% (2023) to 10.21% (2024) and 12.72% (2025), and weighted-average return on equity reached 13.91% in 2025 [1][12].

Weaknesses

  • Thin margins and reliance on non-recurring items: deducted (non-GAAP) net profit was negative in 2022-2024 (-RMB 596 million, -RMB 355 million and -RMB 158 million) and only turned positive (RMB 199 million) in 2025; non-recurring gains totalled about RMB 270 million in 2025 [2][1].
  • Weak domestic business: 2025 domestic sales revenue fell 13.52% with a 6.42% gross margin, far below the 16.69% overseas margin [1].
  • High leverage: the asset-liability ratio was about 84.81% at end-2025 [1].

Our assessment: the disclosed figures show King Long's recent profit growth is concentrated in the overseas business — its higher margin (16.69% versus 6.42%) and faster growth offset the shrinking domestic market; but profitability remains weaker than Yutong's, and whether the improvement continues depends on export momentum and whether the 'three dragons integration' keeps lifting gross margin [1][16].

Outlook

Company targets and plans

In its 2025 annual report the company said the domestic large and medium bus market shrank in 2025, while the light-bus segment and exports grew; it described the overseas market as the core force driving industry growth, with Belt and Road countries and emerging markets increasing infrastructure spending and adopting electrification plans [7]. The company's stated strategy is to build 'four centres' (R&D, procurement, sales, after-sales) to coordinate the three brands and keep their respective strengths ('three dragons dancing together'), and to continue the integration and internationalisation push [16]. In a February 2026 interview, chairman Chen Feng said that over the next five years the group will focus on 'strong management and control, optimised systems, market expansion and greater efficiency', deepening technology in autonomous driving and green low-carbon areas, and consolidating its position as an export leader while deepening localised cooperation [14]. On technology the company says it will keep investing in smart driving, hydrogen storage and green transport [8].

Our analysis

Our assessment: export remains the most visible growth driver — H1 2026 overseas revenue rose 21.84% to RMB 7.336 billion, 66.18% of operating revenue — but this exposes the group to tariff, currency and geopolitical risks [33]. The domestic bus business is structurally soft and partly policy-dependent; the group's domestic revenue fell 13.52% in 2025, and the September 2026 monthly figures (single-month sales -15.16%, large-bus cumulative sales -10.82%) suggest the domestic large-bus market remains under pressure [1][27]. Whether profit growth continues depends on whether export volume and the overseas margin premium can keep outpacing the domestic decline, which is not assured [13].

Risks

  • Operating risk: the domestic large and medium bus market is shrinking; the group's 2025 domestic sales revenue fell 13.52%, and September 2026 single-month sales fell 15.16% with cumulative large-bus sales down 10.82% [1][27].
  • Financial risk: the asset-liability ratio was about 84.81% at end-2025, and the group relies partly on non-recurring items (government subsidies, financial asset gains) to support profit; non-recurring gains totalled RMB 270 million in 2025 [1].
  • Overseas risk: with overseas revenue at 66.18% of H1 2026 operating revenue, the group is highly exposed to tariffs, export-tax rebate changes, currency swings and geopolitical events [33].
  • Policy risk: a significant part of domestic bus demand depends on the trade-in subsidy policy and new-energy purchase-tax policy; changes in these policies affect demand timing and volume [7].
  • Technology-route risk: the group runs pure-electric and hydrogen fuel-cell programmes in parallel — for example a 49-tonne hydrogen fuel-cell semi-truck — and new-energy buses are central to its export growth; a wrong bet on a technology route could hurt competitiveness [23].

概览

厦门金龙汽车集团股份有限公司（上交所：600686）是中国最大的客车制造商之一、中国客车行业首家上市公司。公司通过厦门金龙联合汽车工业有限公司、厦门金龙旅行车有限公司、金龙联合汽车工业（苏州）有限公司三家整车子公司，运营「金龙」「金旅」「海格」三大品牌，产品覆盖 4.5 米至 18 米客车，面向公路客运、旅游、公交、团体通勤、校车及专用车市场，销往全球 180 多个国家和地区 [1][7]。2025 财年实现营业收入 245.47 亿元（+6.89%）、归母净利润 4.68 亿元（+196.90%），销售客车 51,250 辆 [1][4]。2026 年上半年营业收入 110.85 亿元（+7.34%）、归母净利润 2.76 亿元（+137.58%）[3]。

金龙汽车为福建省属国有控股企业：控股股东福建省汽车工业集团有限公司（福汽集团）2025 年末持股 32.25%，2026 年 6 月 2 日至 10 月 20 日通过集中竞价方式购入公司股份 7,276,800 股后，持股比例升至 33.26%；实际控制人为福建省人民政府国有资产监督管理委员会 [6][2]。2025 年海外销售收入 129.83 亿元，超过国内销售（103.09 亿元），出口成为集团首要增长引擎 [1]。

经 2024—2025 年重组，金龙、金旅为上市公司全资子公司。苏州金龙截至 2026-10-29 仍是拟划转：按 2025 年 10 月董事会通过的方案，上市公司直接持股将由 2.1405% 升至 65.2162%（划转前厦门金龙 63.0757%、苏州创元 34.7838%；划转后金龙汽车 65.2162%、苏州创元 34.7838%）。同花顺资本运作栏把这笔划转标为「进行中」，未见完成工商变更的公告。公司称该方案用于统筹三大品牌的研发、采购、销售、售后 [24][25][32][16]。

2025 年度利润分配预案为每 10 股派发现金股利 2.00 元（含税），合计派现 1.4341 亿元；叠加中期分红后全年现金分红 1.6492 亿元，占归母净利润的 35.21% [1][7]。

现状

业务结构

金龙以客车研发、制造与销售为主业。2025 财年「汽车及车身件」业务收入 232.92 亿元，占营业收入的 94.89%，毛利率 12.14%，其余为其他业务 [1]。分地区看，海外销售收入 129.83 亿元（+30.56%，毛利率 16.69%）、国内销售 103.09 亿元（-13.52%，毛利率 6.42%），海外收入超过国内，约占总营收的 52.9% [1][30]。2025 年销售客车 51,250 辆（大型 18,560、中型 10,528、轻型 22,162），生产 52,723 辆（大型 19,362、中型 11,024、轻型 22,337）；出口约 3.03 万辆（+34.47%）[4][1]。行业背景方面，据年报引述中汽协数据，2025 年我国累计销售客车 56.68 万辆（+11.9%），其中出口 15.03 万辆（+18.51%），国内大中型客车销售下降 10% [7]。金龙大中型客车以直销为主、经销为辅，轻型客车以经销为主、直销为辅 [7]；2025 年前五大客户合计销售金额 32.92 亿元，占年度销售总额 13.41%，前五名供应商合计采购金额 48.32 亿元，占年度采购总额 24.9%；截至 2025 年末员工 12,619 人，经营活动现金流量净额 16.64 亿元（+33.01%）[1][12]。

近期经营

2026 年上半年，集团实现营业收入 110.85 亿元（+7.34%）、归母净利润 2.76 亿元（+137.58%）、扣非净利润 1.94 亿元（+1472.77%）；销售客车 2.39 万辆（+5.97%）、出口 1.78 万辆（+26.76%），其中新能源客车出口 2,777 辆（+35.2%）[3][13][15]。上半年境外收入 73.36 亿元（+21.84%），占营业总收入 66.18% [33]。2026 年 1—9 月累计销售客车 35,679 辆，其中 9 月单月 4,137 辆、大型客车累计 11,923 辆 [27]。

产品与技术

集团产品谱系覆盖公路客车、城市公交、校车与专用车。2026 年上半年完成 MC 系列、C 系列、乾途系列等多款公路车型及 M 系列、MU9 系列、七代高端公交等多款公交车型的开发与升级拓展 [28]。电动化方面，公司拥有自研 ISG 系统与「易驱」新能源管理系统、G-BOS 智慧运营车联网平台，高性能碳化硅控制器已量产并批量交付、扁线电机产品矩阵持续完善 [21][28]。龙威Ⅱ代在 C-SCAP 测评中以 98.02 分获评「中国超五星级安全客车」，Merry Combo 通过流线造型降低 11.8% 风阻；金龙客车最早自 2001 年服务全国两会，实现 26 年无缺席、无故障的服务纪录 [8]。智能驾驶与氢能方面，公司布局 Robovan A3 无人配送车、Apolong 与 M6S 自动驾驶巴士，以及 49 吨氢燃料电池半挂牵引车（官网称 -30°C 快速启动、续航超过 600 公里；福建日报称 10—15 分钟快速加氢、续航突破 500 公里，并可在 -30°C 冷启动）；还参与编制国标 GB/T 47341-2026，并完成低铂高安全燃料电池系统及氧化物固态储氢系统的整车试制 [23][8][14]。

生产基地

集团主要子公司包括厦门金龙联合、厦门金龙旅行车、苏州金龙（海格）、金龙智能科技、金龙（龙海）投资、金龙礼宾车、金龙新能源等 [7]。海格苏州新能源客车基地占地 430 亩、一期投资 15 亿元，规划年产 1 万辆新能源车；斯堪尼亚·海格豪华客车工厂（2016-11-09 投产）年产能 1000 台以上 [21][20]。金龙客车工程研究院位于厦门集美区 [14]。

海外与国内市场

金龙的国际化起步较早：2001 年批量出口伊拉克、2003 年 82 辆出口马耳他、2005 年通过英国 VCA 认证，如今已进入全球 150 多个国家和地区、累计出口超 16 万辆 [8]。2025 年与沙特伙伴合作满 20 周年，金龙客车在沙特市场保有量突破 1 万台；121 台新能源公交出口智利，助力智利打造拉美首个 100% 电动公交城市；150 台天然气公交落地阿根廷 [14]。2025 年 10 月比利时 Busworld 展上，金龙发布 MU12E、MC13E、A02、V8 四款新能源车型 [14]。2026 年上半年，金龙客车品牌出口 8,075 辆（+38%），居 3.5 米以上客车出口行业第一；海格（5,110 辆）、金旅（4,656 辆）分列第三、第四 [8][16]。国内方面，公司向辽宁交付 300 台新能源公交、向黑龙江交付 65 台 M 系列地铁巴士、向乌鲁木齐交付 228 台新能源客车、向重庆交付 145 台新能源公交 [14]。

创始与沿革

竞争格局

主要对手

国内客车市场，金龙主要与行业龙头宇通客车、中通客车、安徽安凯、北汽福田（欧辉）等竞争 [16]。业内所称「一通三龙」指宇通，加上苏州金龙（海格）、厦门金龙（大金龙）和厦门金旅；三龙是金龙集团自己的三家整车厂，不是中通、安凯、欧辉 [31]。公司 2026 年半年报自述，报告期内大中型客车产销量位居行业第二、出口销量行业第一 [28]。就成长性而言，公司三年营业总收入复合增长率 10.4%，在商用车行业已披露 2025 年数据的 12 家公司中排名第 7 [12]。

优势

  • 出口规模：2025 年集团出口约 3.03 万辆、保持行业领先；2026 年上半年三大品牌金龙（8,075 辆）、海格（5,110 辆）、金旅（4,656 辆），据媒体统计，分列 3.5 米以上客车出口第一、第三、第四位 [8][16]。
  • 全谱系与多品牌矩阵：产品覆盖 4.5—18 米，可同时竞逐公交、旅游、轻客标单 [16]。
  • 盈利能力改善：整体毛利率由 2023 年 9.71% 升至 2024 年 10.21%、2025 年 12.72%，2025 年加权平均净资产收益率达 13.91% [1][12]。

短板

  • 利润率偏薄、依赖非经常性损益：2022—2024 年扣非净利润连续为负（-5.96 亿、-3.55 亿、-1.58 亿），2025 年才转正为 1.99 亿元；2025 年非经常性损益合计约 2.7 亿元 [2][1]。
  • 国内业务偏弱：2025 年国内销售收入下降 13.52%，毛利率 6.42%，远低于海外 16.69% [1]。
  • 杠杆偏高：2025 年末资产负债率约 84.81% [1]。

我们的判断：披露数据显示，金龙近期的利润增长集中在海外业务——其更高毛利（16.69% 对 6.42%）与更快增速对冲了国内市场的萎缩；但盈利能力仍弱于宇通，能否延续改善取决于出口动能与「三龙整合」能否持续抬升毛利率 [1][16]。

未来展望

公司目标与规划

公司在 2025 年年报中称，2025 年国内大中型客车市场萎缩，轻型客车与出口则实现增长；公司把海外市场视为拉动行业增长的核心力量，「一带一路」沿线及新兴市场基础设施建设需求旺盛、多国出台电动化规划 [7]。公司称将推进「建四大中心、促三龙共舞」战略，统筹研发、采购、销售、售后，保留三品牌各自优势，并继续推进整合与国际化 [16]。在 2026 年 2 月的访谈中，董事长陈锋表示，未来 5 年集团将坚持「强管控、优体系、拓市场、增效能」方针，聚焦智能驾驶、绿色低碳深耕技术，以全球视野巩固出口龙头地位、深化本土化合作 [14]。技术方面，公司表示将在智能驾驶、氢能储运、绿色交通等领域持续深耕 [8]。

我们的分析

我们的判断：出口仍是最可见的增长引擎——2026 年上半年境外收入 73.36 亿元（+21.84%），占营业总收入 66.18%——但这使公司更暴露于关税、汇率与地缘风险 [33]。国内客车业务结构偏弱且部分依赖政策，2025 年国内收入下降 13.52%；2026 年 9 月单月销量 -15.16%、大型客车累计销量 -10.82%，显示国内大客市场仍承压 [1][27]。利润能否延续增长，取决于出口量与海外毛利溢价能否持续快于国内下滑，这一条件并非必然成立 [13]。

风险

  • 经营风险：国内大中型客车市场萎缩，2025 年公司国内销售收入同比下降 13.52%；2026 年 9 月单月销量 -15.16%、大型客车累计销量 -10.82% [1][27]。
  • 财务风险：2025 年末资产负债率约 84.81%，且利润部分依赖政府补助、金融资产收益等非经常性损益（2025 年非经常性损益合计约 2.7 亿元）[1]。
  • 海外风险：2026 年上半年境外收入占营业总收入 66.18%，公司高度暴露于关税、出口退税、汇率波动及地缘事件 [33]。
  • 政策风险：国内客车需求相当部分依赖「以旧换新」补贴与新能源购置税政策，政策变化会影响需求释放时点与规模 [7]。
  • 技术路线风险：公司并行推进纯电与氢燃料电池路线（例如 49 吨氢燃料电池半挂牵引车），新能源客车是其出口增长核心，技术路线押注失误会影响竞争力 [23]。
