Overview

Beijing Offroad (北京越野) is the off-road vehicle brand of Beijing Automotive Group Co., Ltd. (BAIC Group), and part of the Beijing brand under Hong Kong-listed BAIC Motor Corporation Limited (01958.HK). Public filings do not show Beijing Automotive Group Off-road Vehicle Co., Ltd. as a subsidiary of 01958.HK or as the brand's operating company [3][37]. It presents itself as China's 'off-road family' (越野世家) and the originator of the country's off-road vehicle, tracing its lineage to the Beijing BJ212 light off-road vehicle approved for production in May 1966 [1]. Its business is passenger off-road vehicles: hardcore off-roaders (BJ40, BJ60, BJ80), a light off-road SUV (BJ30), and, since 2026, the luxury Taitan sub-brand (Taitan 700) and the mainstream NEV sub-brand Xingtan (Xingtan 5X), with petrol, range-extended (EREV), plug-in hybrid and battery-electric powertrains and, increasingly, Huawei Qiankun assisted driving [24][31][33].

After several quiet years, sales rebounded strongly: full-year 2025 sales exceeded 200,000 units, up 38% year on year on BAIC Group's basis, with 147,499 boxy 'box' models delivered (+72%), BAIC Group saying the BJ40 led hardcore off-road sales for 11 consecutive months and the BJ40 EREV led the EREV box segment for nine months, while BAIC Motor's reprint of China Securities Journal says the BJ40 series has ranked first since July 2025. The two accounts conflict and both are kept [2][3]. In the first half of 2026 the brand sold 84,600 vehicles, with 66,700 box models delivered (+33.9% year on year) [15].

As a brand it is not separately listed and discloses no standalone financials; its results are consolidated in BAIC Motor. The Beijing brand, Beijing Benz, Beijing Hyundai and Fujian Benz are the four vehicle businesses under that listed company, not peers sitting beside it. Consolidated revenue is BAIC Motor's figure, and Beijing Offroad sits inside the Beijing brand [3][30].

History

  1. 1960

    Beijing Automobile Works formally starts developing a light off-road vehicle, building on the earlier Jinggangshan and Dongfanghong sedan programmes.[11]

  2. 1963

    The BJ210 light off-road vehicle developed by BAW is formally named the 'Beijing' (北京) brand.[1]

  3. 1966-05

    The Beijing BJ212 light off-road vehicle is approved for series production in May 1966. The BJ212 and its derivatives go on to sell over 1.2 million units over more than 50 years, taking over 80% of China's light off-road market at its peak.[1][20][27]

  4. 1983

    Beijing Jeep Corporation is established with American Motors (AMC) as China's first Sino-foreign whole-vehicle joint venture (formally opening in January 1984), a partnership through which the BJ212 line was upgraded.[29]

  5. 2013-12

    The Beijing 40 (BJ40), BAIC's first modern hardcore off-road vehicle and a tribute to the BJ212, is launched at the Water Cube, priced RMB146,800-186,800; BAIC chairman Xu Heyi says it reopens the 'off-road family' (越野世家).[27]

  6. 2016-04

    The BJ80, a full-size hardcore off-roader with a Mercedes-Benz G-Class-like silhouette, is launched from RMB298,000; in 2025 it served as a parade gun-salute-towing and press-support vehicle at the commemoration of the 80th anniversary of the end of the War of Resistance.[28][2]

  7. 2019-10

    BAIC Group relaunches the 'BEIJING' brand, folding the Senova nameplate into it; the off-road line continues under the Beijing brand as the '越野世家' core.[21]

  8. 2023-11

    A fully redesigned BJ40 is unveiled at the Guangzhou auto show, ten years after the original; in 2023 the four main models (BJ40/BJ60/BJ80/BJ90) sold about 34,000 units.[10][28]

  9. 2024-05

    The BJ30 light off-road SUV is launched in May as a deep-hybrid light off-road four-wheel-drive model, the first application of the 'Magic Core Electric Drive' (魔核电驱) off-road electrification solution that BAIC released that year.[9][1]

  10. 2025-04

    The BJ40 EREV (range-extended) goes on sale on 23 April at the Shanghai auto show with an official guide price of RMB179,800-199,800 (from RMB154,800 after national and trade-in subsidies); the BJ40 petrol facelift follows in July.[12][11]

  11. 2025

    Beijing Offroad sells over 200,000 units (+38%); NEV share nears 40%. Current operations

Business structure

Beijing Offroad is a pure passenger off-road-vehicle business: it runs no separate commercial-vehicle, battery or component segment, and its whole-vehicle results are consolidated in BAIC Motor (01958.HK). The Beijing brand, Beijing Benz, Beijing Hyundai and Fujian Benz are the four vehicle businesses under that listed company. Beijing Offroad is part of the Beijing brand, not a peer of the three joint ventures. The consolidated revenue figure is BAIC Motor's [3][30]. It is the core of BAIC Motor's own 'Beijing' brand, which also covers sedans and urban SUVs [3].

Model line-up and brand matrix

As of the research cut-off (2026-10-29), Beijing Offroad's matrix is organised in three lines: the BJ series (BJ30, BJ40, BJ60, BJ80) defends the core, the Taitan series (Taitan 700) pushes upward, and the Xingtan series (Xingtan 5X) widens volume [34][24]. The BJ40 is the volume anchor. BAIC Group says it led hardcore off-road sales for 11 months in 2025; BAIC Motor's reprint of China Securities Journal says the BJ40 series has ranked first since July 2025. The two accounts conflict and both are kept, and the BJ30 is the entry point around the RMB100,000 class [2][9]. The Beijing Auto official site also lists a 'Yueji travel' series (X7, EU5, U5, EU7) of non-off-road cars, which belong to the Beijing brand rather than the off-road core [24].

Latest performance (as of 2026-10-29)

For full-year 2025, Beijing Offroad sold more than 200,000 vehicles (+38%), with boxy 'box' models delivering 147,499 units (+72%); the hardcore off-road models built at the smart factory sold 76,000 units, roughly double 2024, and the BJ40 EREV sold 38,000 units in eight months, topping the EREV box segment; individual buyers made up 83% of box-model deliveries, up 13 percentage points; and the new 'Yuanjing Zhixing' model took over 5,000 orders within 24 hours of launch [3][4]. In Q1 2026, Beijing Offroad delivered 43,200 vehicles (+67%), including over 30,900 box models (+89%) and over 15,100 BJ40-series vehicles (+144%) [32]. In H1 2026 the brand sold 84,600 vehicles, with 66,700 box models delivered (+33.9%), and added models such as the BJ40 EREV long-range edition and the BJ30 Traveller High-light edition [15]. Parent BAIC Motor reported FY2025 consolidated revenue of RMB164.047 billion and net profit attributable to the parent of RMB123 million (down 87.2% year on year; prepared under IFRS) [30]. For H1 2026 (six months ended 30 June 2026, unaudited, prepared under IAS 34) it reported revenue of RMB57.726 billion, down 29.9% year on year, and a net loss attributable to equity holders of the company of RMB1.594 billion, against a profit of RMB360 million a year earlier [36]. For the first nine months of 2026 it reported revenue of RMB120.8 billion and attributable net profit of RMB51.29 million (down 98.3% year on year) under PRC GAAP, according to Hong Kong media [35].

Technology and platforms

The electrification path centres on the 'Magic Core Electric Drive' (魔核电驱) solution released in 2024, applied to the BJ30 deep hybrid, BJ40 EREV and BJ60 EREV; the BJ40 EREV uses a 40.3kWh battery for 152 km of electric range and an 82L fuel tank for over 1,000 km of combined range [9][12]. On intelligence, the brand co-develops with Huawei: the Taitan 700 carries Huawei Qiankun ADS 5 MAX and the Xingtan 5X carries ADS 5 Pro, and in July 2026 Beijing Offroad and Huawei Yinwang inaugurated a 'joint off-road centre' [31][34]. On batteries it co-developed the 'Xiaoyao off-road edition' battery with CATL [34].

Plant and capacity

The main production base is the Beijing Offroad Smart Factory in Zhaoquanying, Shunyi District, Beijing (about 540,000 sq m; media report annual capacity of roughly 100,000 vehicles), capable of flexible petrol, diesel and EREV production [18][19]. In September 2025 the factory's monthly output passed 10,000 units for the first time. The same ceremony's figure of 3.2 million vehicles is the combined output of four bases — the Shunyi smart factory plus BAIC Zhuzhou, Guangzhou and Kunming — not Beijing Offroad off-road vehicles alone [19][40].

Overseas business

Beijing Offroad's exports are handled centrally by BAIC International, and no separate brand-level export figure is disclosed. BAIC International covers more than 90 countries and regions under the BAIC, ARCFOX and STELATO brand matrix [32]. Beijing Offroad plans to develop high-temperature, cold-climate and EU versions of its 2026 models for export to Europe, Central and South America and North Africa from late 2026 into early 2027 [3].

Founders and leadership

Founder

Beijing Offroad has no single entrepreneur-founder: it is a state-created brand whose lineage runs from the Beijing Automobile Works' BJ212, developed from 1960 and approved for production in 1966 [1]. The BJ212 and its derivatives long served as parade command and gun-salute-towing vehicles at Tiananmen Square [1].

Current management

  • Zhang Guofu (张国富) — deputy general manager of BAIC Group and chairman of Beijing Offroad; he has chaired BAIC Motor (01958.HK) since 14 April 2026. On 20 March 2026 the board only proposed appointing him as a non-executive director and, if shareholders approved, nominating him as chairman; Wang Hao's resignation took effect when that appointment was approved. The EGM on 14 April 2026 approved it, and the board appointed him chairman the same day. His resignation as BAIC Bluepark chairman took effect on 19 March 2026 (announced 20 March), which is not the effective date of the BAIC Motor chairmanship. Born July 1977, he holds a project-management engineering master's degree from Beijing Institute of Technology and is a senior engineer. From 2019 he served successively as deputy party secretary and executive deputy general manager, then general manager, of Beijing Automotive Group Off-road Vehicle Co., Ltd., and as deputy party secretary and executive vice president of BAIC Motor; he became general manager of BAIC Bluepark (and party secretary and general manager of Beijing New Energy Vehicle) in July 2024, BAIC Bluepark chairman in March 2025. He stepped down as BAIC Bluepark chairman with effect from 19 March 2026, and became chairman of BAIC Motor on 14 April 2026 [26][37][38][39][31].
  • Chen Geng (陈更) — president of BAIC Motor (北汽股份总裁) since 29 November 2025, when Song Wei ceased to be president. In a January 2026 interview he described the company's marketing, quality and cost work across 2025; that full-year programme is not his record as president for the whole year [3][41].
  • Xu Feng (徐峰) — general manager of the Beijing Offroad product centre, who set out the 2026 launch plan and the 'off-road democratisation' roadmap [3][5].
  • Wang Chunfeng (王春风) — general manager of the Beijing Offroad sales company, who described the channel expansion and user operations [3].
  • Zhu Baiqing (朱百庆) — general manager of the Beijing Offroad Smart Factory, who described the flexible and digital manufacturing [3].

Predecessor and governance

Former BAIC Motor chairman Wang Hao (born December 1982, PhD in economics) served as chairman from 13 March 2025 until 14 April 2026, when Zhang Guofu succeeded him [30][38]. Beijing Offroad is not an independently listed legal entity; its parent BAIC Motor is controlled by BAIC Group, a Beijing municipal wholly state-owned enterprise ultimately controlled by the Beijing Municipal SASAC (see the BAIC Group deep research for the ownership chain) [2].

Competition

Our judgment: the competitive field. Beijing Offroad competes in China's fast-growing hardcore and 'boxy' (方盒子) off-road segment. Its direct rivals include Great Wall Motor's Tank brand, BYD's Fangchengbao (Bao 5), Chery's Jetour Traveller and the Haval Big Dog [28]. Tank in particular had led the Chinese hardcore off-road SUV segment for 39 consecutive months as of early 2024 [28]. The market is expanding: domestic boxy-SUV sales reached about 1.02 million units in 2025, and media cite an institutional forecast of up to 1.5 million units in 2026 [34][13]. This is an analytical selection of competitors, not a sales ranking.

Our judgment: strengths. The brand's clearest asset is its heritage: as the maker of the BJ212 it can credibly claim to be China's original off-road brand, and the BJ212 series has sold over 1.2 million units. The September 2025 ceremony figure of 3.2 million vehicles is, in the same report, the combined output of four bases — the Shunyi smart factory plus BAIC Zhuzhou, Guangzhou and Kunming — not Beijing Offroad off-road production alone [1][40]. Its 2025 results show it has converted that into a volume recovery — over 200,000 units, with BAIC Group saying the BJ40 led hardcore off-road for 11 months and the BJ40 EREV topped the EREV box segment, while BAIC Motor's reprint of China Securities Journal says the BJ40 series has ranked first since July 2025. Those two accounts conflict and both are kept. The volume recovery is supported by a growing individual-buyer base (83% of box-model deliveries) [2][3][6]. The Huawei assisted-driving partnership and the CATL battery co-development give it a route into the intelligent off-road tier, backed by its accumulated off-road driving data [34].

Our judgment: weaknesses. The same heritage did not stop rivals overtaking it: in 2023 its four main models sold only about 34,000 units, far behind Tank, and media noted it once missed the first-mover bonus of the hardcore off-road boom [28]. Its recovery leans heavily on the sub-RMB200,000 BJ40/BJ30 volume tier, while the above-RMB300,000 Taitan 700 is an unproven new model whose pre-sale orders (over 10,000) have yet to be matched by sustained deliveries [13].

Outlook

Company plans. For 2026, BAIC Motor president Chen Geng disclosed a target of over 200,000 domestic sales plus 100,000 overseas, for a total of more than 300,000 units [5]. The brand plans several new models through 2026: the BJ40 Explorer in Q1, the BJ40 EREV 2026 update and a new BJ60 with Huawei assisted driving in Q2, the BJ30 update and the user-co-created C-class BJ81 in Q3, and an A+-class light off-road model (petrol, BEV and PHEV) in Q4 [5][3]. Product-centre general manager Xu Feng said the brand would cover the A0-to-D segments across light, professional and luxury off-road series over three years, targeting one million units by 2030 [3]. On technology it is pushing 'off-road democratisation' (越野平权) across fuel, EREV, PHEV and BEV; the Taitan 700 is positioned as an 'all-scenario luxury intelligent off-road SUV', which BAIC Group chairman Zhang Jianyong said is meant to 'redefine luxury off-road' [13]. These are disclosed plans and targets, not achieved results.

Our judgment. The 300,000-unit 2026 target implies roughly 216,000 units in H2 after 84,600 in H1 [15], a significant step-up that depends on the new-model cadence and on the Taitan/Xingtan launches converting orders into sustained volume [5][33]. The brand's recovery rests on two premises: that the BJ40/BJ30 base keeps growing without a repeat of the 2023-2024 slowdown, and that the Taitan move above RMB300,000 holds up against the Tank 500/700 and Fangchengbao. An alternative is that the crowded 'boxy' segment — media report nearly 20 new off-road launches in the first seven months of 2026 — intensifies price competition and stalls the up-market push [13]. This is our analysis and does not constitute a forecast of financial results.

Risks

  • Concentrated volume base. Growth depends heavily on the sub-RMB200,000 BJ40 and BJ30; a slowdown in this tier would directly hit overall volume, as happened in 2023 when the four main models sold only about 34,000 units [28].
  • Unproven premium push. The Taitan 700 is a first attempt above RMB300,000; its pre-sale orders (over 10,000) have yet to be matched by sustained deliveries [13].
  • Intense competition. The boxy off-road segment is crowded, with media reporting nearly 20 new off-road launches in the first seven months of 2026 and established rivals such as Tank and Fangchengbao [13][28].
  • No separate financial disclosure. Beijing Offroad publishes no brand-level revenue or profit, so standalone profitability cannot be assessed from public filings; its results are consolidated in BAIC Motor, whose FY2025 attributable net profit was RMB123 million, down 87.2% year on year, and whose H1 2026 attributable net loss was RMB1,594 million, swinging from a profit [36] [30].
  • Leadership churn. The BAIC Motor chairmanship changed twice in about a year: Wang Hao from 13 March 2025, then Zhang Guofu with effect from 14 April 2026. The 20 March 2026 board meeting only proposed that appointment, adding execution uncertainty around the handover [37][38].
  • Technology dependence on partners. The intelligent off-road positioning leans on Huawei's assisted-driving stack and CATL batteries, tying part of the brand's differentiation to third parties [14][34].

概览

北京越野（Beijing Offroad）是北汽集团（北京汽车集团有限公司）旗下的越野车品牌，属于港交所上市公司北京汽车股份有限公司（01958.HK）整车板块中的北京品牌。公开文件没有把「北京汽车集团越野车有限公司」列为 01958.HK 的子公司，也没有列示它运营该品牌 [3][37]。品牌自居中国「越野世家」与国产越野车开创者，渊源可追溯至 1966 年 5 月定型投产的北京牌 BJ212 轻型越野车 [1]。其业务为乘用越野车：硬派越野（BJ40、BJ60、BJ80）、轻越野（BJ30），以及 2026 年新推出的豪华越野子品牌泰钽（泰钽 700）与主流新能源 SUV 子品牌星钽（星钽 5X），动力覆盖燃油、增程（EREV）、插混与纯电，并越来越多地搭载华为乾崑智驾 [24][31][33]。

经历多年沉寂后，品牌销量强劲回升：2025 年全年销量超 20 万辆、同比增长 38%（北汽集团口径），其中方盒子车型交付 147,499 辆、同比增长 72%，北汽集团称 BJ40 连续 11 个月居硬派越野销量第一、BJ40 增程连续 9 个月居增程式方盒子销量第一，北京汽车官网转载的《中国证券报》则写 BJ40 系列自 2025 年 7 月起稳居榜首。两说冲突，并列保留 [2][3]。2026 年上半年品牌销量 8.46 万辆，方盒子车型交付 6.67 万辆（同比 +33.9%）[15]。

作为品牌，北京越野未单独上市、也不单独披露财务，业绩并入北京汽车股份的合并报表。北京品牌、北京奔驰、北京现代、福建奔驰是这家上市主体下面的四个整车板块，不是与北京汽车股份并列的四家主体。合并收入是北京汽车股份的合并数，北京越野在北京品牌里面 [3][30]。

历史沿革

  1. 1960

    北京汽车制造厂在「井冈山」「东方红」轿车试制基础上，正式启动轻型越野车开发。[11]

  2. 1963

    北汽研制的 BJ210 轻型越野车被正式定名为「北京」牌。[1]

  3. 1966-05

    1966 年 5 月，北京牌 BJ212 轻型越野车定型并安排生产；此后 BJ212 及衍生车型畅销 50 多年、累计超 120 万辆，鼎盛时占全国轻型越野车市场份额 80% 以上。[1][20][27]

  4. 1983

    与美国汽车公司（AMC）合资成立北京吉普汽车有限公司，为中国第一家整车制造合资企业（1984 年 1 月正式开业），BJ212 由此得以改进升级。[29]

现状

业务结构

北京越野是纯乘用车越野业务，不经营商用车、动力电池等独立业务板块，整车经营业绩并入北京汽车股份有限公司（01958.HK）合并报表。北京品牌、北京奔驰、北京现代、福建奔驰是这家上市主体下面的四个整车板块。北京越野属于北京品牌，不是与三家合资并列的另一家主体。合并收入是北京汽车股份的合并数 [3][30]。它是北京汽车自主品牌「北京」品牌的核心组成，与轿车、城市 SUV 等产品共同构成北京汽车的自主乘用车序列 [3]。

车型谱系与品牌矩阵

截至研究截止日（2026-10-29），北京越野的产品矩阵分三条线：BJ 系列（BJ30、BJ40、BJ60、BJ80）守基本盘，泰钽系列（泰钽 700）向上突破，星钽系列（星钽 5X）向宽走量 [34][24]。BJ40 是销量基盘。北汽集团称其 2025 年连续 11 个月居硬派越野销量第一；北京汽车官网转载的《中国证券报》写 BJ40 系列自 2025 年 7 月起稳居榜首。两说冲突，并列保留；BJ30 是 10 万元级的轻越野入口 [2][9]。此外，北京汽车官网还列示「悦己出行」系列（X7、EU5、U5、EU7）等非越野车型，属于北京品牌而非北京越野主业 [24]。

近期业绩（截至 2026-10-29）

2025 年全年，北京越野销量超 20 万辆（+38%），方盒子车型交付 147,499 辆（+72%）；智能工厂生产的硬派越野车销量 7.6 万辆、较 2024 年近乎翻倍，BJ40 增程 8 个月售出 3.8 万辆、登顶增程式方盒子市场；方盒子车型个人用户占比达 83%、提升 13 个百分点；全新车型「元境智行」上市 24 小时订单破 5000 辆 [3][4]。2026 年一季度，北京越野终端交付 4.32 万辆、同比增长 67%，其中方盒子家族交付超 3.09 万辆（+89%）、BJ40 系列交付突破 1.51 万辆（+144%）[32]。2026 年上半年品牌销量 8.46 万辆，方盒子车型交付 6.67 万辆（+33.9%），BJ40 增程长续航版、BJ30 旅行家高光版等新品陆续上市 [15]。母公司北京汽车 2025 财年合并营业收入 1640.47 亿元、归属于母公司股东净利润 1.23 亿元（同比降 87.2%）[30]；2026 年中期（截至 2026-06-30，按《国际会计准则》第 34 号编制、未经审计）营业收入 577.26 亿元、同比降 29.9%，本公司权益持有人应占净亏损 15.94 亿元、由盈转亏（上年同期盈利 3.60 亿元）[36]；其 2026 年前三季按中国会计准则实现营业收入 1,208 亿元、归属股东净利润 5,129 万元（同比降 98.3%，据港媒报道）[35]。

技术与平台

电动化路径以 2024 年发布的「魔核电驱超级驱动方案」为核心，落地「三剑客」——BJ30 深混、BJ40 增程、BJ60 增程；其中 BJ40 增程采用 40.3kWh 电池、纯电续航 152 公里，配 82L 油箱综合续航超 1000 公里 [9][12]。智能化上，品牌与华为深度共创：泰钽 700 搭载华为乾崑智驾 ADS 5 MAX、星钽 5X 搭载 ADS 5 Pro，并于 2026 年 7 月与华为引望挂牌成立「联合越野中心」[31][34]。电池层面联合宁德时代开发「骁遥电池越野专研版」[34]。

工厂与产能

主要生产基地为北京越野智能工厂（北京顺义区赵全营镇，占地约 54 万平方米，媒体披露年产能力约 10 万辆），可实现汽油、柴油、增程等多车型柔性生产 [18][19]。2025 年 9 月该工厂单月产量首次突破 1 万辆。同一仪式报道的累计生产 320 万辆，是以顺义智能工厂为核心，加上北汽株洲、广州、昆明基地的四大基地合计产量，不是北京越野越野车的单一产量 [19][40]。

海外业务

北京越野的出口由北汽国际统筹，未单独披露品牌级海外销量。北汽国际已覆盖 90 多个国家和地区，形成 BAIC、ARCFOX、STELATO 三大海外品牌矩阵 [32]。北京越野计划为 2026 年新车同步开发高温版、高寒版、欧盟版等国际版型，拟于 2026 年底至 2027 年初出口欧洲、中南美、北非等地区 [3]。

竞争格局

我们的判断：竞争范围。北京越野处于中国快速扩容的硬派越野与「方盒子」细分赛道，直接对手包括长城坦克、比亚迪方程豹（豹 5）、奇瑞捷途旅行者、哈弗大狗等 [28]。其中坦克品牌截至 2024 年初已连续 39 个月蝉联中国硬派越野 SUV 销量冠军 [28]。市场正在扩容：国内方盒子 SUV 2025 年销量约 102 万辆，媒体引机构预测 2026 年有望冲击 150 万辆 [34][13]。此为分析所选竞争范围，并非销量排名。

我们的判断：优势。品牌最核心的资产是历史：作为 BJ212 的制造者，它有底气自称中国越野车开创者，BJ212 系列累计超过 120 万辆。2025 年 9 月仪式上的「累计生产突破 320 万辆」，据同一报道，是以顺义智能工厂为核心，加上北汽株洲、广州、昆明基地的四大基地合计产量，不是北京越野越野车的单一产量 [1][40]。2025 年的数据表明其已把历史转化为销量复苏——超 20 万辆。北汽集团称 BJ40 连续 11 个月硬派越野第一、BJ40 增程登顶增程方盒子；北京汽车官网转载的《中国证券报》写 BJ40 系列自 2025 年 7 月起稳居榜首。两说冲突，并列保留。个人用户占比达 83% [2][3][6]。与华为的智驾合作、与宁德时代的电池合作，加上其积累的越野实测数据，为其打开智能越野层级提供了路径 [34]。

我们的判断：短板。同样的历史并未阻止其长期被对手反超：2023 年其四款主力越野车合计仅售约 3.4 万辆、与坦克品牌差距悬殊，媒体也指出其一度错过硬派越野爆发期的先发红利 [28]。其复苏高度依赖 20 万元以下的 BJ40/BJ30 走量区间，而 30 万元级以上的泰钽 700 是未经验证的新车型，预售热度（订单超 1 万辆）尚未被持续交付证实 [13]。

未来展望

公司公开规划。北京汽车总裁陈更披露 2026 年目标：内销 20 万辆以上、外销 10 万辆，冲击 30 万辆以上总销量 [5]。品牌计划 2026 年投放多款新车：一季度 BJ40 探险家，二季度 BJ40 增程 2026 款及搭载华为智驾的全新 BJ60，三季度 BJ30 改款及用户共创的 C 级 SUV 北京 81，四季度一款 A+ 级轻越野车型（燃油、纯电、插混多种动力）[5][3]。产品中心总经理徐峰披露，未来三年将以轻越野、专业越野、豪华越野三大系列布局 A0 到 D 级市场，2030 年冲击百万销量规模 [3]。技术层面在燃油、增程、插混、纯电多动力上推进「越野平权」；泰钽 700 被定位为「全场景豪华智能越野 SUV」，北汽集团董事长张建勇称其意在「重新定义豪华越野」[13]。以上为披露的计划与目标，并非已实现的业绩。

我们的判断。2026 年 30 万辆目标意味着在上半年 8.46 万辆之后，下半年需交付约 21.6 万辆，显著提速 [15]，取决于新车节奏以及泰钽/星钽能否把预售订单转化为持续销量 [5][33]。品牌复苏依赖两个前提：BJ40/BJ30 基盘持续增长、不再重演 2023—2024 年的放缓；以及泰钽在 30 万元以上与坦克 500/700、方程豹的竞争中站住脚。另一种可能是，「方盒子」赛道拥挤——媒体称 2026 年前 7 个月主流越野新车投放近 20 款——价格竞争加剧，使向上突破受阻 [13]。以上为我们的分析，不构成对财务结果的预测。

风险

  • 销量基盘集中。增长高度依赖 20 万元以下的 BJ40、BJ30；该区间一旦放缓将直接冲击总量，2023 年四款主力车型合计仅约 3.4 万辆即为例证 [28]。
  • 向上突破未经验证。泰钽 700 是品牌首次冲击 30 万元以上市场，预售订单（超 1 万辆）尚未被持续交付证实 [13]。
  • 竞争激烈。方盒子赛道拥挤，媒体称 2026 年前 7 个月主流越野新车投放近 20 款，且面临坦克、方程豹等既有对手 [13][28]。
  • 无单独财务披露。北京越野未披露品牌层面营收与利润，独立盈利能力无法从公开文件评估；业绩并入北京汽车，后者 2025 财年归母净利润 1.23 亿元、同比降 87.2%，2026 年中期权益持有人应占净亏损 15.94 亿元、由盈转亏 [36] [30]。
  • 管理层更替。北京汽车董事长大约一年内两次变动：王昊自 2025 年 3 月 13 日起任职，张国富自 2026 年 4 月 14 日起接任。2026 年 3 月 20 日董事会只是提议委任，交接窗口带来执行层面的不确定性 [37][38]。
  • 对合作伙伴的技术依赖。智能越野定位倚重华为智驾体系与宁德时代电池，品牌差异化部分系于第三方 [14][34]。
