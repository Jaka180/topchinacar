Overview

Forthing (东风风行, Fengxing) is the passenger-vehicle brand of Dongfeng Liuzhou Motor Co., Ltd. in Liuzhou. The brand-history page calls that company a controlled subsidiary of Dongfeng Motor Corporation (东风汽车集团有限公司) [1]. The company profile states the direct shareholders as Dongfeng Motor Group Company Limited (东风汽车集团股份有限公司) with 75% and Guangxi Liuzhou Industrial Investment & Development Group Co., Ltd. with 25% [2]. The 2024 annual results announcement of the listed company states that its holding company and ultimate holding company is Dongfeng Motor Corporation, a state-owned enterprise established in the PRC [22]. Its predecessor, the Liuzhou Agricultural Machinery Factory, was founded in 1954 and entered vehicle production in 1969; the Forthing brand was born in September 2001 with the Lingzhi MPV, described as China's first self-owned-brand MPV [1]. Today the brand spans MPVs, SUVs and sedans plus the Xinghai new-energy series released in April 2024 (V9, S7, V6), running alongside the Chenglong commercial-vehicle brand of the same company [2]. In 2025 Dongfeng Liuzhou Motor sold 133,000 vehicles, up 12% year-on-year, with commercial vehicles up 40.3%, new-energy commercial vehicles up 181% and new-energy passenger vehicles up 25.1% [4]. Dongfeng Liuzhou Motor says it has won the trust of nearly 2 million global customers [15]. The brand is not separately listed and publishes no standalone financials; the parent group is covered in the Dongfeng Motor Group deep-dive [2].

Current operations

Business structure

Dongfeng Liuzhou Motor runs two brands: the Forthing passenger-vehicle brand (MPV, SUV, sedan, pure-electric MPV and pure-electric sedan) and the Chenglong commercial-vehicle brand (tractors, cargo trucks, dump trucks, special vehicles and pure-electric logistics and sanitation vehicles) [2]. In 2025 the company sold 133,000 vehicles, up 12% year-on-year, with commercial vehicles up 40.3%, new-energy commercial vehicles up 181% and new-energy passenger vehicles up 25.1% [4]. At its December 2025 partner conference the company forecast 2025 terminal sales of 145,300 units (up 22.9%), with more than 50,000 commercial vehicles sold (up 43%) and new-energy sales above 8,200 units (up 140%) [11]. In H1 2026 the Forthing brand sold 42,371 units, up 40% year-on-year [14]; by July 2026 Dongfeng Liuzhou Motor had cumulatively sold 82,807 units (up 33%) [12], and by August 89,439 units (up 26%) [19]. Domestically the company has nearly 2,500 marketing and service outlets covering more than 30 provinces, and nearly 300 overseas outlets serving more than 70 countries and regions [2].

Technology and platforms

The Xinghai series builds on Dongfeng's EMA modular platform and Mach power. The EMA platform covers SUVs, MPVs and sedans with wheelbases from 2,615 mm to 3,000 mm, supports pure-electric, plug-in hybrid and range-extender powertrains, and holds an ASEAN-NCAP five-star safety rating [15]. Forthing's powertrain and safety portfolio includes the Mach hybrid engine, an 800V silicon-carbide electric drive, the 'armour' battery that has passed more than 500 tests, and the Xinghai Pilot L2 assisted-driving system [15]. A 30 August 2024 Forthing article gives the Xinghai S7 a 0.191 drag coefficient, a 555 km range, an 11.9 kWh/100 km energy consumption and a 68.53% high-strength-steel ratio; that article does not name a test cycle such as CLTC [17]. The brand-history page also records a 650 km long-range version released on 21 December 2024 and a range-extender version launched at the Shanghai Auto Show on 24 April 2025, so the S7 line is not only the September 2024 pure-electric car [1]; the 2027 Xinghai V9 uses the Mach hybrid with 200 km CLTC pure-electric and 1,300 km combined range [18]. From June 2026, Forthing deepened cooperation with Huawei to equip the Xinghai series with Huawei Qiankun intelligent driving, landing first on the V6 [4]. On the commercial side, Dongfeng Liuzhou first showed the group's T1 hydrogen-electric platform at the June 2026 customer day and released the Chenglong H7 PRO hydrogen version and the Chenglong Yiwei 5 electric heavy truck [4].

Factories and capacity

Dongfeng Liuzhou Motor occupies about 5,180 mu (3.454 million square metres) and operates the Liudong commercial-vehicle and passenger-vehicle bases, with annual capacity of 100,000 commercial vehicles and 400,000 passenger vehicles; its B+ engine project builds EW-series 2.0 and 1.8T engines at 100,000 units per year [2]. The company also runs full-vehicle environment and NVH laboratories and a proving ground that simultaneously supports passenger vehicles under 3.5 tonnes and commercial vehicles up to 55 tonnes [2].

Domestic position

Dongfeng Liuzhou Motor is one of Dongfeng's 'four bases' in the south: a commercial-vehicle base, passenger-vehicle base, self-owned-brand R&D base and Southeast-Asia export base [2]. Forthing was founded in the MPV segment, where the Lingzhi has long served the commercial/utility MPV niche; for the '15th Five-Year Plan' the company targets 250,000 passenger vehicles focused on the MPV track [4].

Overseas business

A 17 September 2026 Dongfeng Liuzhou article says the business covered more than 120 countries and regions, with eight KD plants in Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Africa and Central Asia, and more than 200 overseas sales and service networks [6]. A 19 September 2026 article on the same site says overseas KD plants had reached nine [19]. The two official figures, two days apart, are both shown here. In 2025 overseas exports rose 32.7% year-on-year, covering more than 80 countries and regions (per the Thailand-market release) [15]; the company's corporate profile page states products are sold to more than 70 countries and regions through nearly 300 overseas outlets, a differing figure presented here alongside the newer one [2]. In the first eight months of 2026, overseas whole-vehicle exports surpassed 33,000 units [6]. On 9 March 2026 Forthing held a brand launch in Bangkok and entered the Thai market, debuting the right-hand-drive FRIDAY SUV and the V9 [15]. On 16 September 2026 the Pinglu Canal opened. It is 134.2 km long. Dongfeng Liuzhou's passenger-vehicle KD kits were among the first cargoes and were sent through the canal toward ASEAN [6]. The same article says the canal can shorten the sea route for cargo from southwest China by more than 560 km. That distance is about southwest cargo in general, not a stated saving on Dongfeng Liuzhou's own KD shipment [6]. In ASEAN the company plans three '10,000-unit markets': Vietnam (led by the KD plant), Thailand (right-hand-drive passenger vehicles) and Indonesia (a reserve market) [6]. In Thailand, Forthing said it will gradually build a nationwide sales and service network and introduce more family models [15].

Competition

Competitive position: our assessment

Our assessment: Forthing competes mainly in the MPV segment, where the Lingzhi line has been a long-standing incumbent, and enters the mainstream RMB 100,000-250,000 new-energy market through the Xinghai series, where it faces the large established players that dominate that price band [4]. Its scale is modest: 133,000 vehicles in 2025 is small against the segment leaders [4]. The Xinghai models enter at low prices: the S7 launched from RMB 119,800 [9], the 2027 V9 is guided from RMB 179,900 [18], and the V6 launched with a launch-period super trade-in price from RMB 99,900 [19]. The above is based on the sales, product and pricing data cited in this study.

Strengths and constraints: our assessment

Our assessment: Forthing's strengths are its MPV heritage and the Lingzhi line's 'wealth-creation tool' positioning [1], Dongfeng's manufacturing and supply-chain backing as one of the group's southern bases [2], and low entry pricing on the Xinghai models [9]. Its constraints are a limited brand presence in the mainstream new-energy market and no dominant technology label in the RMB 100,000-200,000 band. Cited 2025 results do not break out sales by model, so this assessment does not treat volume as dependent on the Lingzhi line [4]. The Huawei Qiankun partnership from 2026 is intended to fill the technology gap in the family large-six-seat segment, but that segment is itself competitive. The above is based on the sales, product and pricing data cited in this study.

Outlook

Company plans

Dongfeng Liuzhou Motor's '15th Five-Year Plan' ('725·Dual Leadership') strategy, released in June 2026, sets commercial vehicles at 70,000 units (leading the southern regional market), passenger vehicles at 250,000 units (focused on the MPV track), and overseas sales of 155,000 units by 2030, described as 'rebuilding a Dongfeng Liuzhou Motor overseas' [4]. The earlier 'Chengfeng Twin-Engine (2030)' plan, released in June 2025, targeted cumulative investment of RMB 20 billion, nine overseas smart manufacturing bases and 300+ new sales networks by 2030, with an initial annual overseas sales target of 300,000 units [5][13]; by 2026 official releases stated that plan's overseas target as 155,000 units [6]. In ASEAN it plans three '10,000-unit markets': Vietnam (led by the KD plant), Thailand (right-hand-drive passenger vehicles) and Indonesia (a reserve market) [6]. On the commercial side, the December 2025 partner conference set a 2026 target of 45,000 domestic commercial vehicles, with new-energy units striving to exceed 16,000 [11]. These are the company's stated targets and plans, not achieved results.

Our analysis

Our assessment: the 250,000-unit figure is a passenger-vehicle target. The 133,000 units reported for 2025 are Dongfeng Liuzhou Motor's total sales, not passenger-vehicle sales. The same release separates the growth of commercial vehicles, new-energy commercial vehicles and new-energy passenger vehicles, and does not call 133,000 a passenger-vehicle total, so the passenger-vehicle base is below 133,000 and the step up to 250,000 is wider than a near-doubling [4]. The 19 September 2026 launch article says the Xinghai V6's launch-period super trade-in price starts at RMB 99,900, and that this is the first time Huawei Qiankun intelligent driving has gone below RMB 100,000 [19]. The family large-six-seat segment is competitive, so the commercial impact remains to be proven. The overseas 2030 target was stated as 300,000 units in the June 2025 plan and as 155,000 units in later official releases, indicating the plan has been revised downward [5][6]. The above is our assessment, based on the data cited in this study.

Risks

  • Model sales mix not disclosed: the 2025 results give company sales of 133,000 units and separate growth rates for commercial vehicles, new-energy commercial vehicles and new-energy passenger vehicles, but not a Lingzhi share. This page does not state that volume depends on the Lingzhi line [4].
  • Intense competition in the mainstream new-energy market: the RMB 100,000-250,000 band is contested by many established players, and Forthing enters it mainly through low pricing [4].
  • Early-stage Huawei cooperation: the Huawei Qiankun partnership was signed in June 2026, the V6 began pre-sale in August 2026 and only launched in September 2026, so its commercial impact is unproven [4][19].
  • Heavy new-energy transition spending: Forthing announced in 2023 a plan to invest over RMB 20 billion in new-energy competitiveness and to stop producing fuel vehicles by 2030, so the transition carries cost and timing risk [8].
  • Overseas trade and logistics risk: overseas expansion depends on logistics channels such as the Pinglu Canal, which only opened in September 2026, and on destination-market trade policy and certification requirements [6].
  • No standalone financial disclosure: the brand publishes no standalone financial statements, so outside assessment of profitability relies on the parent's consolidated disclosures [2].

概览

东风风行（Fengxing / Forthing）是总部位于广西柳州的东风柳州汽车有限公司的乘用车品牌。品牌历史页称东风柳汽是东风汽车集团有限公司的控股子公司[1]。公司概况写直接股东为东风汽车集团股份有限公司持股 75%、广西柳州市产业投资发展集团有限公司持股 25%[2]。港交所 2024 年业绩公告写明，股份公司的控股公司及最终控股公司是在中国成立的国有企业东风汽车集团有限公司[22]。其前身柳州农业机械厂创立于 1954 年、1969 年进入汽车生产领域；2001 年 9 月，东风风行以 MPV 菱智诞生，该车型被称为国内首款自主品牌 MPV[1]。品牌现覆盖 MPV、SUV、轿车及 2024 年 4 月发布的「星海」新能源序列（V9、S7、V6 等），与同属东风柳汽的商用车品牌「乘龙」并行[2]。2025 年东风柳汽销量 13.3 万辆、同比 +12%，其中商用车 +40.3%、新能源商用车 +181%、新能源乘用车 +25.1%[4]。东风柳汽表示已累计收获近 200 万全球客户信赖[15]。品牌不单独披露财务数据、也未单独上市，母集团情况见「东风汽车集团」深度研究[2]。

现状

业务结构

东风柳汽运营两大品牌：乘用车品牌「东风风行」（MPV、SUV、轿车、纯电动 MPV 与纯电动轿车）与商用车品牌「乘龙」（牵引车、载货车、自卸车、专用车及纯电动物流车、纯电动环卫车）[2]。2025 年公司销售 13.3 万辆、同比 +12%，其中商用车 +40.3%、新能源商用车 +181%、乘用车新能源 +25.1%[4]。据 2025 年 12 月合作伙伴大会（预计口径），公司 2025 年预计实现终端汽车销量 14.53 万辆、同比 +22.9%，其中商用车全年实销超 5 万辆、同比 +43%，新能源销量突破 8200 辆、同比 +140%[11]。2026 年上半年东风风行销量 42,371 辆、同比 +40%[14]；截至 2026 年 7 月，东风柳汽累计销售 82,807 辆、同比 +33%[12]，1–8 月累计销售 89,439 辆、同比 +26%[19]。渠道上，公司在国内拥有近 2500 个营销服务网点、覆盖 30 多个省市自治区，海外拥有近 300 个营销服务网点、产品远销 70 余个国家和地区[2]。

技术与平台

星海序列基于东风 EMA 模块化平台与马赫动力：EMA 平台覆盖 SUV、MPV、轿车三大车系，轴距覆盖 2615–3000 毫米，兼容纯电、插混、增程，并获 ASEAN-NCAP 五星安全认证[15]。动力与安全上，东风风行手握获评「中国心十佳发动机」的马赫混动高效发动机、全域 800V 高压碳化硅电驱总成，以及历经 500 余项严苛测试的「铠甲电池」，并搭载星海 Pilot L2 级智能驾驶辅助系统[15]。2024 年 8 月 30 日东风风行稿写星海 S7 风阻系数 0.191、续航 555 公里、百公里电耗 11.9 千瓦时、高强钢占比 68.53%，该稿未写 CLTC 等测试循环[17]。品牌历史页另记 2024 年 12 月 21 日星海 S7 650 公里长续航版发布、2025 年 4 月 24 日星海 S7 增程版在上海车展上市，因此 S7 的产品现状不只有 2024 年 9 月那次纯电上市[1]；2027 款星海 V9 采用马赫混动，CLTC 纯电 200 公里、综合 1300 公里[18]。2026 年 6 月起与华为深化合作，风行星海将搭载华为乾崑智驾，首款落地车型为星海 V6[4]。商用车侧，东风柳汽在第十届 67 品牌客户日首次展示集团 T1 氢电平台成果，并发布乘龙 H7 PRO 氢耀版与乘龙翼威 5 超能版电动重卡[4]。

工厂与产能

东风柳汽占地面积约 5180 亩（345.4 万平方米），拥有柳东商用车、乘用车两大新生产基地，具备年产 10 万辆商用车、40 万辆乘用车生产能力；已投建 B+ 发动机项目生产 EW 系列 2.0、1.8T 发动机，形成 10 万台/年产能[2]。公司建有整车环境模拟试验室、NVH 试验室等大型综合试验室，并建成同时满足 3.5 吨以下乘用车及最大至 55 吨商用车整车开发的专业试验场[2]。

国内市场地位

东风柳汽是东风公司在南方的商用车生产基地、乘用车生产基地、自主品牌研发基地和东南亚出口基地「四大基地」之一[2]。东风风行起家于 MPV 市场，菱智长期深耕商用兼用 MPV 细分；面向「十五五」，公司提出乘用车冲刺 25 万辆、聚焦 MPV 赛道[4]。

海外业务

2026 年 9 月 17 日东风柳汽稿写业务已覆盖全球 120 多个国家和地区，在东南亚、中东、非洲、中亚等区域建成 8 座 KD 工厂，海外销售和服务网络超过 200 家[6]。2026 年 9 月 19 日同一官网的秋季发布会稿写海外 KD 工厂已达 9 家[19]。两份官网稿相差两天，数字不同，此处并列。2025 年海外出口同比增长 32.7%、覆盖 80 多个国家和地区（泰国市场发布稿口径）[15]；公司官网「公司概况」页则写产品远销 70 余个国家和地区、海外近 300 个营销服务网点，与较新稿件口径存在差异，此处并列呈现[2]。2026 年 1–8 月，东风柳汽海外整车出口突破 3.3 万辆[6]。2026 年 3 月 9 日，东风风行在泰国曼谷举行品牌发布会，右舵新能源 SUV Friday 与星海 V9 亮相，正式进入泰国市场[15]。2026 年 9 月 16 日平陆运河通航，全长 134.2 公里，东风柳汽乘用车 KD 件作为首航货物之一，经运河发往东盟[6]。同一篇稿写西南货物出海航程可缩短 560 公里以上。这 560 公里以上说的是西南货物出海航程，不是东风柳汽 KD 件自身航程的缩短数字[6]。在东盟，公司规划打造越南（依托 KD 工厂）、泰国（右舵乘用车）、印尼（战略储备）三个「万辆级」市场[6]。在泰国，东风风行表示将逐步搭建覆盖全泰国的销售与服务网络，并持续引入更多家用车型[15]。

竞争格局

竞争地位：我们的判断

我们的判断：东风风行主要竞争于 MPV 细分市场——凭借菱智深耕多年——并通过星海序列切入 10 万–25 万元主流新能源市场，该市场由多家头部企业主导[4]。其体量偏小：2025 年东风柳汽销量 13.3 万辆、同比 +12%，与主流新能源市场头部玩家差距明显[4]。星海序列以低起售价进入市场：星海 S7 上市价 11.98 万元起[9]，2027 款星海 V9 官方指导价 17.99 万元起[18]，星海 V6 上市期间超级置换价低至 9.99 万元起[19]。上述判断依据为本研究所引销量、产品与价格数据。

优势与约束：我们的判断

我们的判断：东风风行的优势在于 MPV 积淀与菱智系列的「创富工具」定位[1]、作为东风南方基地的制造与供应链支撑[2]，以及星海车型的低起售价[9]。其约束在于主流新能源市场品牌声量不足、10 万–20 万元价格带缺乏绝对技术标签。所引 2025 年成绩没有分车型销量，因此这一判断不把销量写成依赖菱智序列[4]。2026 年起与华为乾崑的合作意在补齐家用大六座市场的技术短板，但该细分市场竞争激烈[4]。上述判断依据为本研究所引销量、产品与价格数据。

未来展望

公司公开规划

东风柳汽 2026 年 6 月发布的「十五五」（「725·双领先」）战略提出：商用车 7 万辆（实现南方区域市场领先）、乘用车 25 万辆（聚焦 MPV 赛道），海外 2030 年销量达 15.5 万辆，称「在海外再造一个东风柳汽」[4]。此前 2025 年 6 月发布的「乘风双擎（2030）」计划提出：至 2030 年累计投入 200 亿元、共建 9 个海外智能制造基地、新增 300 家以上销售网络，当时提出年海外销售 30 万辆的目标[5][13]；到 2026 年，官方稿件将该计划的海外目标表述为 15.5 万辆[6]。在东盟，公司规划打造越南（依托 KD 工厂）、泰国（右舵乘用车）、印尼（战略储备）三个「万辆级」市场[6]。商用车侧，2025 年 12 月合作伙伴大会提出 2026 年冲刺国内商用车 4.5 万辆产销目标、新能源力争突破 1.6 万辆[11]。以上为公司目标与规划表述，并非已实现结果。

我们的分析

我们的判断：25 万辆写的是乘用车目标。2025 年披露的 13.3 万辆是东风柳汽公司总销量，不是乘用车销量。同一篇稿分别给出商用车、新能源商用车和乘用车新能源的增速，没有把 13.3 万辆称为乘用车销量，所以乘用车基数低于 13.3 万辆，冲到 25 万辆的幅度比把总销量看成接近翻倍更大[4]。2026 年 9 月 19 日上市稿写星海 V6 上市期间超级置换价低至 9.99 万元起，并写这是华为乾崑智驾首次进入 10 万元以内[19]。家用大六座细分市场竞争激烈，其商业成效尚待观察。海外 2030 年目标在 2025 年 6 月计划中表述为 30 万辆、在 2026 年官方稿件中表述为 15.5 万辆，说明该规划已向下修订[5][6]。上述为我们的判断，依据为前文所引数据。

风险

  • 分车型销量未披露：2025 年成绩给出公司销量 13.3 万辆，以及商用车、新能源商用车、乘用车新能源的增速，没有菱智占比。本页不把销量写成依赖菱智序列[4]。
  • 主流新能源市场竞争激烈：10 万–25 万元主流新能源市场头部企业众多，东风风行主要以低起售价切入，面临价格与品牌双重压力[4]。
  • 华为合作尚处早期：与华为乾崑智驾的合作于 2026 年 6 月签约、首款车型星海 V6 于 2026 年 8 月预售、9 月才正式上市，其商业成效尚未经市场充分验证[4][19]。
  • 新能源转型投入大：东风风行 2023 年提出投入超 200 亿元构建新能源竞争力、2030 年停产燃油车，转型成本与节奏存在不确定性[8]。
  • 海外贸易与物流风险：海外拓展依赖平陆运河等物流通道及所在国贸易政策与认证要求，平陆运河 2026 年 9 月才通航，出海链路尚在磨合[6]。
  • 无单独财务披露：品牌不单独披露财务报表，外部对其盈利情况的判断需依赖母公司的合并披露[2]。
