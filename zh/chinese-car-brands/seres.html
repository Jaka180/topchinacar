Overview

Seres Group Co., Ltd. is a Chongqing-headquartered automaker that makes premium new energy vehicles and owns the AITO (问界) brand built with Huawei. The company traces its roots to a 1986 spring factory and is now dual-listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange (601127) and the Hong Kong Stock Exchange (9927).[6][17] Its revenue comes overwhelmingly from new energy vehicle sales: in 2025 new energy vehicles contributed RMB155,611 million (about 94% of revenue), on total revenue of RMB165,054 million and attributable net profit of RMB5,957 million.[1][1][4][5][6][11][17][21]

AITO is a brand within HIMA. The company reported that AITO delivered more than 420,000 vehicles in 2025 and was the best-selling Chinese premium brand in China's domestic market that year.[5] The group sold 472,269 new energy vehicles in 2025.[1] Under the Smart Selection model Seres handles vehicle manufacturing while Huawei contributes the HarmonySpace cockpit, the Qiankun ADS intelligent-driving stack and channel support; from 15 September 2026, product definition, marketing, retail and service are led by Seres, with Huawei providing technology.[21][1][4][5][6][11][17][21]

The group also runs a legacy Dongfeng Sokon (DFSK)/Fengon commercial-vehicle and export business, and divested its mass-market Landian brand in 2026.[6] After four consecutive loss-making years from 2020 to 2023, Seres returned to profit in 2024 and 2025, but swung back to an attributable net loss of RMB1,717 million in the first half of 2026, while January–September 2026 new energy vehicle sales fell 15.8% year on year.[4][11][1][4][5][6][11][17][21]

Current operations

Business structure

Seres's business is concentrated in new energy vehicles. In 2025 new energy vehicle revenue was RMB155,611 million (94.3% of revenue) and legacy fuel-vehicle revenue was RMB1,903 million (1.2%), the remainder being parts and other income; this NEV share is not the same as an AITO-brand revenue share, which the company does not disclose separately.[1] Operating cash flow reached RMB28,914 million in 2025, up 28.4% year on year; net assets attributable to the parent rose to RMB40,918 million, the asset-liability ratio fell to 70.91%, and cash reached RMB87,287 million (60.66% of total assets) at year-end.[1] Shareholders approved the FY2025 final dividend of RMB0.80 per share (tax inclusive) on 22 April 2026; A-share cash dividends were paid on 3 June. The implementation notice lists a final dividend total of about RMB1,392 million.[12] The annual report's approximately RMB1,900 million full-year total (31.90% of attributable net profit) includes interim dividends and differs from the final dividend.[1] The group also holds a 10% stake in Shenzhen Yinwang Intelligent Technology, Huawei's smart-car unit, acquired for RMB11.5 billion (registered 31 March 2025, fully paid by September 2025).[19][1][3][4][5][6][10][12][14][15][16][19][23][26][27]

Products and technology

The AITO line-up spans five SUVs — M5, M6, M7, M8 and M9 — with starting prices from RMB229,800 to RMB479,800, plus the M9 Ultimate from RMB649,800, in extended-range (EREV) and pure-electric variants.[16] The M9 flagship has a CLTC range of 1,405 km.[16] The M6, positioned between the M5 and M7, opened pre-orders on 23 March 2026 with more than 60,000 orders within weeks.[5] Seres's in-house technology centres on the Cube (魔方) platform, its super range-extender system and the Super Factory manufacturing system; the company says its range-extender business held more than 37.5% of the segment market in 2025.[15][1][3][4][5][6][10][12][14][15][16][19][23][26][27]

R&D and intelligence

R&D investment was RMB12,511.9 million in 2025 (up 77.4%), with 9,019 R&D staff, 41.1% of the workforce.[1] AITO users added 3.8 billion km of assisted-driving mileage in 2025.[5] By September 2026 AITO had passed one million assisted-driving active users and 10 billion km of cumulative assisted-driving mileage.[15] The company reports a maximum thermal efficiency of 44.8% and generation exceeding 3.65 kWh per litre for its super range-extender.[15] It received MSCI ESG's top grade, AAA, in October 2025.[5][1][3][4][5][6][10][12][14][15][16][19][23][26][27]

Manufacturing

The company's inquiry response gives the plant allocation as of 30 September 2024: Liangjiang produced the M5 series (designed capacity 50,000 units/year), Fenghuang the M7 series (100,000/year), and the Super Factory the M9 series (150,000/year). Other vehicle plants produced Fengon, Landian and Dongfeng Sokon models, among others (300,000/year). This is a historical allocation and does not establish model assignments or capacity at the research cutoff.[10] A subsequent company investor-relations record states that the Super Factory achieved shared-line production of the AITO M8 and M9 in H1 2025.[27] The Super Factory was previously leased; in 2025 the group completed a share-issue acquisition of 100% of the owner, Longsheng New Energy, for RMB8,164 million, bringing the plant onto its own balance sheet.[1][1][3][4][5][6][10][12][14][15][16][19][23][26][27]

Domestic market position

AITO is the group's principal brand. In 2025 AITO delivered more than 420,000 vehicles, including more than 110,000 M9s (retaining the crown in the RMB500,000-plus segment), more than 150,000 M8s (first in the RMB400,000 segment) and more than 110,000 new M7s.[23] Group-wide, total vehicle sales were 516,860 in 2025 (new energy vehicles 472,269), up from 497,008 in 2024 (new energy vehicles 426,885).[1][3] The group's H1 2026 gross margin was 23.30%; Q2 inventory days were 7.5. At 30 June, cash reserves exceeded RMB73,150 million (57.0% of assets; including monetary funds, financial assets held for trading and other items).[4][1][3][4][5][6][10][12][14][15][16][19][23][26][27]

Overseas business

The group's overseas business centres on Dongfeng Sokon/Fengon traditional exports and its own SERES NEV brand. In 2025 overseas sales were 28,641 units (7,778 new energy vehicles and 20,863 others), with overseas revenue of RMB2,379 million.[1] The SERES 5 has been sold in Europe, the Middle East and Latin America.[6] An AITO release dated 6 February 2026 disclosed a UAE channel agreement with Performance Plus Motors, an ADM subsidiary responsible for local sales, delivery and after-sales service; the agreement itself does not establish an official launch or completed customer deliveries.[26] In October 2026 AITO appeared at the Paris Motor Show, where Seres announced a plan to enter the European market in autumn 2027.[14][1][3][4][5][6][10][12][14][15][16][19][23][26][27]

Founders and leadership

Founder and actual controller

Zhang Xinghai (张兴海), born 1963 with a postgraduate degree and the title of senior economist, is the founder, actual controller and chairman of Seres Group. He started the Fenghuang Electric Spring Factory in 1986 and later founded the Yu'an Group.[17] He holds 50% of Chongqing Sokon Holdings; the 2025 annual report identifies him as the actual controller.[1] His current public roles include membership of the 14th CPPCC Standing Committee, vice chairmanship of the All-China Federation of Industry and Commerce, and chairmanship of the Chongqing Federation of Industry and Commerce. He previously served as a delegate to the 13th National People's Congress.[7] He was elected chairman of the sixth board on 22 April 2026.[18][1][7][13][17][18]

Key management

  • Yin Xianzhi (尹先知) — director and president of the company, appointed 22 April 2026. Born January 1968, a CCP member with a party-school postgraduate degree; previously held positions in Chongqing Shapingba District government (including township head and district finance director) and state-linked urban-investment firms, and was previously a director and vice president of Seres.[18][1][7][13][17][18]
  • Kang Bo (康波) — director and vice president. Born December 1975 with a doctorate in business administration; nearly 30 years in China's auto and parts industry, including 12 years at BMW China and BMW's Germany headquarters at China and group vice-president level, and roles at Delphi as China managing director of a business division and head of the independent aftermarket for Asia-Pacific.[7][1][7][13][17][18]
  • Zhou Lin (周林) — vice president. Born September 1980 with an executive MBA; previously assistant general manager and CTO of Dongfeng Sokon, executive deputy general manager of the Seres Auto technology centre and deputy general manager of Seres Auto, and the company's chief technology officer (CTO); now vice president.[7][1][7][13][17][18]
  • Shen Wei (申薇) — vice president and board secretary. Born May 1982 with an LL.M.; previously at Beijing King & Wood Mallesons, Yushang Investment Group and Chongqing Xizheng Yufu Equity Investment Fund Management, and a former supervisor and director of the company; now vice president and board secretary.[7][1][7][13][17][18]
  • Wang Ping (王平) — vice president. Born October 1973 with a bachelor's degree; previously at Qingling Motors, Zongshen Technology Development and Changan Ford; now vice president.[7][1][7][13][17][18]
  • Liu Lian (刘联) — chief financial officer. Born November 1967 with an executive MBA and the title of accountant; previously at Chongqing Hongyan Internal Combustion Engine, then deputy general manager of the shock-absorber division and assistant to the president of Chongqing Sokon Auto Group, and a director and vice president of the company; now chief financial officer.[7][1][7][13][17][18]

Shareholding and governance

As of 31 December 2025 the largest shareholder was Chongqing Sokon Holdings (22.99%), followed by Dongfeng Motor Group (18.79%), HKSCC Nominees (6.23%), Chongqing Yu'an Auto Industry (3.79%) and Chongqing Industrial Investment Mother Fund (3.05%).[1] By 24 September 2026 Chongqing Sokon Holdings had raised its holding to 23.23%, with persons acting in concert (Zhang Zhengping and Chongqing Yu'an Auto Industry) holding 27.03% in total.[13] The sixth board elected on 22 April 2026 has Zhang Xinghai as chairman and Yin Xianzhi as president.[18][1][7][13][17][18]

Competition

Main competitors

Our judgment: given AITO's SUV line-up and official starting prices, a useful comparison set includes Tesla, Li Auto, XPeng, Zeekr, Avatr, other HIMA brands and electric offerings from premium German brands. This is our analytical peer selection.[16][1][4][5][11][15][16][21]

Strengths

  • Our judgment: Deep Huawei integration: the HarmonySpace cockpit and Qiankun ADS give AITO a strong intelligent-driving and brand story, and Huawei's retail reach supported its rise.[21][1][4][5][11][15][16][21]
  • A premium scale position: AITO delivered over 420,000 vehicles in 2025. Our judgment: this provides a customer base for its sales and service operations.[5][1][4][5][11][15][16][21]
  • Our judgment: Vertical manufacturing and in-house EREV technology, including the Super Factory and a leading range-extender position (over 37.5% segment share in 2025).[15][1][4][5][11][15][16][21]
  • Our judgment: Scale in intelligent driving: by September 2026 AITO had passed one million assisted-driving active users and 10 billion km of cumulative assisted-driving mileage, a large real-world data base.[15][1][4][5][11][15][16][21]

Weaknesses

  • NEV business concentration: this business represented about 94.3% of group revenue in 2025. The annual report does not separately disclose AITO revenue, so this cannot establish that AITO or five SUVs generated nearly all revenue.[1][1][4][5][11][15][16][21]
  • Our judgment: a sales recovery remains challenging. January–September 2026 NEV sales fell 15.8% year on year; a sustained recovery from new models remains to be demonstrated.[11][1][4][5][11][15][16][21]
  • Our judgment: A thin net margin: attributable net profit of RMB5,957 million on revenue of RMB165,054 million (about 3.6%) is far below the 29.1% gross margin, reflecting heavy R&D, selling and channel spend.[1][1][4][5][11][15][16][21]
  • Our judgment: Non-AITO diversification remains limited: overseas NEV sales were 7,778 in 2025, and Saidou Technology was deconsolidated in July 2026.[1][4][1][4][5][11][15][16][21]

Our judgment

Our judgment: AITO's premium volume shows the Huawei partnership works commercially, but the 2026 revenue decline, the first-half loss and the shift of operations to Seres's leadership indicate that profitability is not yet independent of Huawei's product momentum and the five-SUV model cycle. The gross margin cited here is the group's consolidated 29.14%; the company does not disclose an AITO-brand gross margin, so it cannot be used directly to argue for AITO's pricing power.[1][4][1][4][5][11][15][16][21]

Our judgment: following the partnership changes, Seres needs to demonstrate its ability to organise product development, marketing and retail. An uneven handover or product updates that fail to revive sales could delay earnings recovery. This assessment does not substitute for undisclosed AITO revenue or profit data.[4][15][21][1][4][5][11][15][16][21]

Outlook

Company targets and latest disclosures

In its 2025 annual report the company said it would focus on the premium intelligent EV business, accelerate overseas model development and operations, and push into robotics and other new businesses, targeting a second million AITO vehicles within two years.[5] In early 2026 it refreshed its line-up — the M6 opened pre-orders on 23 March 2026 and the M8 and M7 received upgrades.[5] For overseas, Seres announced at the 2026 Paris Motor Show a plan to enter the European market in autumn 2027.[14] The latest operating figures were as follows: H1 2026 revenue was RMB57,493 million with an attributable net loss of RMB1,717 million, and January–September 2026 new energy vehicle sales fell 15.8% year on year to 256,521 units.[4][11] The 15 September 2026 cooperation-model adjustment transfers product definition, marketing, retail and service to Seres while Huawei continues technology empowerment.[21][4][5][11][14][15][21]

Our analysis

Our judgment: Seres's 2024–2025 profitability was driven by AITO's product cycle and Huawei's brand pull; whether it is sustained depends on conditions that are not guaranteed — a successful handover of marketing and retail to Seres without losing Huawei's traffic, a fresh product cycle to reverse the 2026 volume decline (the company said the new-generation M9 delivered over 40,000 units in its first 16 weeks), and a path for overseas and non-AITO businesses to offset domestic concentration. If these conditions fail, the first-half loss and volume decline could extend into the second half.[15][4][11][4][5][11][14][15][21]

Risks

Disclosed operating and financial conditions

  • Operations: H1 2026 attributable net loss was RMB1,717 million; January–September NEV sales fell 15.8% year on year.[4][11][1][4][11]
  • Concentration: NEVs generated about 94.3% of FY2025 revenue; AITO revenue and profit were not separately disclosed.[1][1][4][11]
  • Cash flow: FY2025 financing cash flow increased mainly through H-share proceeds.[1] H1 2026 investing net outflow was RMB21,544 million (prior-year period: RMB6,966 million). The interim report attributes the change mainly to cash management using idle funds to optimise allocation.[4][1][4][11]

Company-disclosed risks

The H1 2026 report lists policy, demand, competition, supply-chain and international risks, including materials and component supply, trade barriers, exchange rates and market access.[4][1][4][11]

概览

赛力斯集团股份有限公司是一家总部位于重庆的高端新能源汽车制造商，也是与华为联合打造的 AITO（问界）品牌的所有者与制造商。公司前身可追溯至 1986 年的一家弹簧厂，现于上海证券交易所（601127）与香港联合交易所（9927）两地上市。[6][17] 公司收入几乎全部来自新能源汽车销售：2025 年新能源汽车收入 1,556.11 亿元（约占营收 94%），全年实现营收 1,650.54 亿元、归母净利润 59.57 亿元。[1][1][4][5][6][11][17][21]

问界为鸿蒙智行体系内的品牌。公司披露，2025 年问界交付超 42 万辆，为当年国内市场销量最高的中国豪华品牌。[5] 集团 2025 年新能源汽车销量为 472,269 辆。[1] 在智选车（鸿蒙智行）模式下，赛力斯负责整车制造，华为提供鸿蒙座舱、乾崑智驾 ADS 等技术赋能与渠道支持；自 2026 年 9 月 15 日起，产品定义、营销、零售与服务改由赛力斯主导，华为提供技术赋能。[21][1][4][5][6][11][17][21]

集团还经营东风小康（DFSK）/风光这一传统商用车与出口业务，并于 2026 年剥离了大众化品牌蓝电。[6] 公司在 2020–2023 连续四年亏损后，于 2024 年扭亏、2025 年续盈，但 2026 年上半年归母净亏损 17.17 亿元，1–9 月新能源汽车销量同比下滑 15.8%。[4][11][1][4][5][6][11][17][21]

现状

业务结构

赛力斯业务集中于新能源汽车。2025 年新能源汽车收入 1,556.11 亿元（占营收 94.3%），传统燃油车收入 19.03 亿元（占 1.2%），其余为零部件及其他收入；需说明该口径不等同于问界品牌收入占比，公司未单独披露问界品牌收入。[1] 2025 年经营活动现金流量净额 289.14 亿元，同比增长 28.4%；年末归母净资产增至 409.18 亿元，资产负债率降至 70.91%，货币资金 872.87 亿元（占总资产 60.66%）。[1] 2025 年末期分红每股 0.80 元（含税）已于 2026 年 4 月 22 日获股东会通过，A 股现金红利于 6 月 3 日发放；实施公告列示末期分红总额约 13.92 亿元。[12] 年报列示的全年现金分红约 19.00 亿元（占归母净利润 31.90%）包含中期分红，须与末期分红区分。[1] 集团另持有华为智能汽车业务主体深圳引望智能技术 10% 股权（对价 115 亿元，2025 年 3 月 31 日过户、9 月付清）。[19][1][3][4][5][6][10][12][14][15][16][19][23][26][27]

产品与技术

问界产品线覆盖 M5、M6、M7、M8、M9 五款 SUV，起售价自 22.98 万至 47.98 万元，另有 M9 Ultimate 领世加长版 64.98 万元起，提供增程（EREV）与纯电两种动力。[16] 旗舰 M9 的 CLTC 综合续航达 1,405 公里。[16] 定位介于 M5 与 M7 之间的 M6 于 2026 年 3 月 23 日开启小订，订单很快突破 6 万。[5] 赛力斯自研技术集中在魔方平台、超级增程与超级工厂智能制造体系，公司称 2025 年增程器业务市占率超 37.5%。[15][1][3][4][5][6][10][12][14][15][16][19][23][26][27]

智能化与研发

2025 年研发投入 125.12 亿元（同比增长 77.4%），研发人员 9,019 人、占员工总数 41.1%。[1] 问界用户 2025 年新增智能辅助驾驶里程 38 亿公里。[5] 截至 2026 年 9 月，问界辅助驾驶活跃用户突破 100 万、累计辅助驾驶里程超 100 亿公里。[15] 公司披露超级增程系统最高热效率为 44.8%、1 升油发电超 3.65 度。[15] 公司于 2025 年 10 月获得 MSCI ESG 最高等级 AAA。[5][1][3][4][5][6][10][12][14][15][16][19][23][26][27]

制造

公司问询回复披露，截至 2024 年 9 月末，两江工厂生产 M5 系列（设计产能 5 万辆/年）、凤凰工厂生产 M7 系列（10 万辆/年）、超级工厂生产 M9 系列（15 万辆/年）；其他整车工厂生产风光、蓝电、东风小康等系列（30 万辆/年）。该表为历史分布，不能代表研究截止日的车型分工或产能。[10] 后续公司投资者关系活动记录披露，2025 年上半年超级工厂已实现问界 M8 与 M9 共线生产。[27] 超级工厂此前为租赁使用，2025 年集团以发行股份方式完成对业主龙盛新能源 100% 股权的收购（作价 81.64 亿元），将工厂纳入自有资产体系。[1][1][3][4][5][6][10][12][14][15][16][19][23][26][27]

国内市场地位

问界是集团的主力品牌。2025 年问界交付超 42 万辆，其中 M9 超 11 万辆（蝉联 50 万元级销量冠军）、M8 超 15 万辆（位居 40 万元级细分市场第一）、全新 M7 超 11 万辆。[23] 集团口径下，2025 年汽车总销量 516,860 辆（新能源汽车 472,269 辆），高于 2024 年的 497,008 辆（新能源汽车 426,885 辆）。[1][3] 2026 年上半年集团毛利率为 23.30%；第二季度存货周转天数为 7.5 天；截至 6 月末，现金储备超 731.5 亿元（占总资产 57.0%，该指标包含货币资金、交易性金融资产等）。[4][1][3][4][5][6][10][12][14][15][16][19][23][26][27]

海外业务

集团海外业务以东风小康/风光传统出口与 SERES 自有新能源品牌为主。2025 年境外销量 28,641 辆（其中新能源汽车 7,778 辆、其他车型 20,863 辆），国外营业收入 23.79 亿元。[1] SERES 5 已在欧洲、中东及拉美等市场销售。[6] 2026 年 2 月 6 日，问界品牌新闻稿披露与 ADM 旗下 Performance Plus Motors 签署阿联酋渠道合作协议，由后者负责当地销售、交付及售后；协议披露本身不代表正式上市或已完成客户交付。[26] 2026 年 10 月问界亮相巴黎车展，赛力斯官宣计划 2027 年秋季进入欧洲市场。[14][1][3][4][5][6][10][12][14][15][16][19][23][26][27]

竞争格局

主要对手

我们的判断：结合问界的 SUV 产品与官方起售价，竞品对比可重点考察特斯拉、理想、小鹏、极氪、阿维塔及鸿蒙智行其他品牌，并关注德系豪华品牌的电动车产品。该名单是研究比较范围。[16][1][4][5][11][15][16][21]

优势

  • 我们的判断：深度绑定华为：鸿蒙座舱与乾崑智驾 ADS 赋予问界较强的智能化与品牌势能，华为的零售渠道亦助推其崛起。[21][1][4][5][11][15][16][21]
  • 高端规模地位：2025 年问界交付超 42 万辆；我们的判断是，这为其销售与服务体系提供了用户基础。[5][1][4][5][11][15][16][21]
  • 我们的判断：垂直制造与自研增程技术，包括超级工厂与领先的增程器地位（2025 年市占率超 37.5%）。[15][1][4][5][11][15][16][21]
  • 我们的判断：智能驾驶规模：截至 2026 年 9 月，问界辅助驾驶活跃用户突破 100 万、累计辅助驾驶里程超 100 亿公里，积累了大规模真实路况数据。[15][1][4][5][11][15][16][21]

短板

  • 新能源汽车业务集中度：2025 年该业务收入约占集团营收 94.3%；年报未单独披露问界品牌收入，不能据此断言问界或五款 SUV 贡献几乎全部收入。[1][1][4][5][11][15][16][21]
  • 我们的判断：销量修复仍有压力；2026 年 1–9 月新能源汽车销量同比下滑 15.8%，新车型能否带动持续恢复仍待观察。[11][1][4][5][11][15][16][21]
  • 我们的判断：净利率偏低：归母净利润 59.57 亿元÷营收 1,650.54 亿元（约 3.6%）远低于 29.1% 的毛利率，反映研发、销售与渠道投入较重。[1][1][4][5][11][15][16][21]
  • 我们的判断：非问界业务的分散作用尚有限：2025 年境外新能源汽车销量为 7,778 辆；2026 年 7 月赛豆科技不再并表。[1][4][1][4][5][11][15][16][21]

我们的判断

我们的判断：问界的高端销量说明华为合作模式在商业上成立，但 2026 年营收下滑、上半年亏损以及运营转向赛力斯主导，表明公司盈利尚未独立于华为的产品势能与五款 SUV 的车型周期。此处引用的毛利率为集团综合口径 29.14%，公司未单独披露问界品牌毛利率，故不能据此直接论证问界的定价能力。[1][4][1][4][5][11][15][16][21]

我们的判断：合作模式调整后，赛力斯需要验证自主组织产品、营销与零售的执行能力；若交接不顺或产品迭代未能带动销量恢复，盈利修复可能承压。这一判断不能替代尚未披露的问界品牌收入或利润数据。[4][15][21][1][4][5][11][15][16][21]

未来展望

公司目标与最新披露

赛力斯在 2025 年年报中表示，将坚定聚焦高端智能电动汽车主业，加快面向海外市场的车型研发与运营体系搭建，并推进智能机器人等新业务落地，力争两年内实现第二个百万辆目标。[5] 2026 年初公司加快产品迭代——M6 于 3 月 23 日开启小订，M8、M7 迎来焕新升级。[5] 海外方面，赛力斯在 2026 巴黎车展上官宣计划 2027 年秋季进入欧洲市场。[14] 最新经营数据为：2026 年上半年营收 574.93 亿元、归母净亏损 17.17 亿元，1–9 月新能源汽车销量 25.65 万辆、同比下滑 15.8%。[4][11] 2026 年 9 月 15 日的合作模式调整将产品定义、营销、零售与服务转由赛力斯主导、华为继续技术赋能。[21][4][5][11][14][15][21]

我们的分析

我们的判断：赛力斯 2024–2025 两年的盈利由问界的车型周期与华为的品牌势能驱动；能否持续，取决于若干并不必然成立的条件——在不流失华为流量前提下顺利完成营销与零售交接、以新一轮产品周期扭转 2026 年销量下滑（公司称全新一代 M9 上市 16 周交付超 4 万辆），以及海外与非问界业务能否对冲国内集中度。若这些条件不成立，上半年亏损与销量收缩可能延续至下半年。[15][4][11][4][5][11][14][15][21]

风险

已披露的经营与财务情况

  • 经营表现：2026 年上半年归母净亏损 17.17 亿元；1–9 月新能源汽车销量同比下滑 15.8%。[4][11][1][4][11]
  • 业务集中度：2025 年新能源汽车业务收入约占集团营收 94.3%，年报未单独披露问界品牌收入或利润。[1][1][4][11]
  • 现金流：2025 年筹资现金流增加主要来自 H 股募集资金。[1] 2026 年上半年投资活动现金净流出 215.44 亿元（上年同期 69.66 亿元）；半年报将其变动主要归因为优化资金配置、利用闲置资金开展现金管理。[4][1][4][11]

公司披露的风险

2026 年半年报列示政策支持变动、市场需求波动、行业竞争加剧、供应链以及国际经济政治形势变化等风险；其中供应链风险涉及原材料价格、芯片与电池等关键零部件供给，海外风险涉及贸易壁垒、汇率与准入规则。[4][1][4][11]
