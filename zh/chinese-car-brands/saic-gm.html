Overview

SAIC General Motors Corporation Limited (上汽通用汽车有限公司) is a 50/50 passenger-vehicle joint venture between SAIC Motor Corporation and General Motors, founded in Shanghai on 12 June 1997. It was originally named Shanghai General Motors and was renamed SAIC-GM with effect from 1 July 2015. It produces Buick, Chevrolet and Cadillac vehicles, is headquartered in Pudong Jinqiao, Shanghai, and has four manufacturing bases — Pudong Jinqiao, Yantai Dongyue, Shenyang Beisheng and Wuhan — with the Pan Asia Technical Automotive Center (PATAC) as its R&D arm [1][14][18].

SAIC-GM was once among China's largest joint-venture automakers: annual sales peaked at about 2.0 million units in 2017, then declined to 435,007 units in 2024 with a net loss of RMB 26.688 billion after RMB 23.212 billion of asset impairments in Q4 2024. Sales recovered to 535,000 units in 2025 (up 22.99%), and on 5 August 2026 SAIC Motor and GM renewed the joint venture through 2047 [3][5][7][12].

As a landmark Sino-American joint venture, SAIC-GM set up its manufacturing plant and the PATAC R&D centre in parallel from the outset, and in 2010 became China's first traditional passenger-vehicle maker to reach one million annual units [20]. At the 2026 renewal, SAIC Motor chairman Wang Xiaoqiu said that 29 years ago the joint venture answered the question of 'whether we have it', whereas now it must answer 'whether we are strong', and that SAIC-GM should be a pioneer of the 'JV 2.0' era [24].

Current operations

Brands and products

SAIC-GM runs three brands: Buick (mainstream-to-premium; the GL8 business MPV, Regal/LaCrosse/Excelle sedans, Envision SUVs and NEV models), Cadillac (luxury; CT5, XT5, LYRIQ) and Chevrolet (volume; historically the Sail, Cruze, Malibu, Equinox). The company says its products cover MPV, SUV and sedan segments with pure-electric, plug-in-hybrid and range-extended drivetrains [1]. According to a media report on GM's 2025 China sales, Buick's MPV family sold over 120,000 units (up 23% year-on-year), Envision family sales rose 76.4% and LaCrosse sales more than doubled, while Cadillac's LYRIQ and XT5 deliveries rose 90% and 32.4% respectively [32].

At the 2025 Shanghai auto show, the new large NEV MPV GL8 Lushang (陆尚) was formally launched in three variants priced RMB 249,900-289,900, shown alongside the GL8 Luzun PHEV (陆尊 PHEV); Cadillac presented the LYRIQ-V, its first pure-electric V-series model [13][19].

New-energy transition

According to China Economic Information Network, SAIC-GM's 2025 full-year retail sales were 562,185 units (including exports), of which new-energy vehicles (NEVs) totalled 88,833 units, ranking first among joint-venture brands in NEV volume and penetration [31]. SAIC Motor's 2026 interim report states that SAIC-GM's H1 2026 NEV sales grew by more than 80%, and the Buick Zhijing E7 became the best-selling NEV model among joint-venture brands in its first month on sale [10].

In April 2025 Buick launched the 'Zhijing' high-end NEV sub-brand and the 'Xiaoyao' super-integrated architecture, and within the year introduced models such as the Zhijing L7 range-extended sedan and the Zhijing Shijia; in H1 2026 it added the Zhijing E7 large six-seat NEV SUV [13][23][10].

Manufacturing bases and capacity

The company has four production bases — Pudong Jinqiao, Yantai Dongyue, Shenyang Beisheng and the Wuhan branch — which at the 2017 20th anniversary comprised nine vehicle plants and four powertrain plants [1][21].

As sales declined, capacity utilisation fell sharply: media reported designed capacity of 1.908 million units and output of 422,000 units in 2024 (about 22% utilisation), and designed capacity cut to 1.452 million units with output of 534,000 units in 2025 (about 37% utilisation) [27]. Media also reported that the Shenyang Beisheng plant completed its asset handover to local government [33]. SAIC Motor's 2025 annual report said it would 'adjust capacity': SAIC Volkswagen and SAIC-GM would optimise their base layouts, releasing capacity for self-owned brand products such as 'Shangjie' [27].

R&D and technology

PATAC was founded on 12 June 1997 as a 50/50 joint venture between GM China and SAIC Motor; it was China's first Sino-foreign joint-venture automotive design and development centre, and the first joint-venture auto enterprise to receive the 'State-recognised Enterprise Technology Centre' designation [14].

In April 2025 Buick released the 'Xiaoyao' super-integrated architecture, described as the first region-integrated NEV architecture developed by a local team for a joint-venture automaker, supporting MPV/SUV/sedan bodies, pure-electric/plug-in-hybrid/range-extended powertrains and front/rear/all-wheel drive [13][23].

Recent performance

Per SAIC Motor's monthly sales releases, SAIC-GM's H1 2026 wholesale sales were 231,200 units (down 5.68% year-on-year) with output of 231,300 units (down 12.17%) [6]. For January-August 2026, cumulative wholesale sales were 300,935 units (down 9.14% year-on-year), with 35,008 units sold in August alone (down 20.25%) [8]. In equity-method terms, SAIC-GM's cumulative unrecognised loss narrowed from RMB 8.980 billion at end-2025 to RMB 8.401 billion at end-June 2026 [10].

Founders and leadership

Founder

As a 50/50 joint venture, SAIC-GM has no single entrepreneur-founder; it was established in 1997 by SAIC Motor and General Motors [1].

Current management

  • Xu Ping (徐平) — general manager, appointed on 28 July 2026, succeeding Lu Xiao. Born May 1969, he holds an engineering master's degree and the title of professor-level senior engineer. He has worked at SAIC-GM and PATAC since 1998, serving as PATAC powertrain deputy director, project-management executive director and executive deputy general manager, and as executive director of SAIC-GM's planning and development department. Within SAIC he also served as deputy director and then executive deputy director of the SAIC Motor technology centre, deputy general manager of SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle, and general manager of SAIC Motor UK Holding; immediately before this appointment he was general manager of HASCO (Huayu Automotive Systems). From September 2026 he also became the legal representative of PATAC, per a commercial-registration change [25][26][30].
  • Cesar Toledo — executive vice president of SAIC-GM, effective 5 June 2026 (per the GM press release). He previously served as vice president, Vehicle Sales, Service and Marketing at SAIC-GM [16].
  • Elbi Kremer (柯睿铭) — general manager of the Pan Asia Technical Automotive Center (PATAC), appointed 1 March 2025; he previously served as GM South America product engineering, programme and planning director [17].

Predecessor general manager Lu Xiao (August 2024 to 28 July 2026) joined PATAC in 1997, rose to PATAC executive deputy general manager, and became the first Chinese chief engineer on a GM global platform, leading development of the Buick Regal and LaCrosse and the Chevrolet Malibu; he has since moved to SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle as its general manager [22][26]. Former executive vice president Kaher Kazem retired on 1 August 2026 [16].

Ownership and governance

SAIC Motor and General Motors each hold 50%, with the board composed according to the shareholding split. SAIC Motor accounts for SAIC-GM under the equity method and, because it does not bear excess losses, has written its long-term equity investment down to zero and no longer recognises excess losses [5]. The chairman of SAIC-GM (the manufacturing company) was not stated in the public documents reviewed for this research. PATAC's legal representative is Xu Ping, changed from Lu Xiao in September 2026 [30].

Competition

Our judgment. SAIC-GM competes in China's passenger-vehicle market against domestic brands such as BYD, Geely, Changan and Chery, and against other joint-venture automakers including Volkswagen, Toyota and Honda. Its competitive base rests on a three-brand portfolio (Buick, Chevrolet, Cadillac), the Buick GL8's position in the business-MPV segment, and PATAC's local R&D capability [21]. Its main weaknesses are a belated NEV transition and a sharp decline in scale: annual sales fell roughly 78% from the 2017 peak of about 2.0 million to 435,007 units in 2024, before recovering to 535,000 in 2025 [22][7].

Our judgment. The 2024 results — a net loss of RMB 26.688 billion after RMB 23.212 billion of impairments and negative net assets — indicate that SAIC-GM's results are highly sensitive to domestic price competition and the speed of its NEV transition. Whether the 2025 sales recovery and the H1 2026 improvement under equity-method accounting can be sustained depends on the ramp-up of the 'Zhijing'/'Xiaoyao' NEV line-up and on stabilising Buick and Cadillac [5][28].

Our judgment. In the business-MPV segment the Buick GL8 retained a benchmark position, but in NEVs and intelligent driving media assessed SAIC-GM's competitiveness as limited relative to domestic peers. Sustained domestic price competition has also forced the company to defend volume with 'one-price' promotions [22]. Compared with sister joint ventures SAIC Volkswagen (1.024 million units in 2025) and SAIC-GM-Wuling (1.6151 million units in 2025), SAIC-GM's 535,000 units in 2025 has clearly fallen behind; it was once an important profit source for SAIC Motor, and the 2024 loss weighed on group results [7][5].

Outlook

Company plans. On 5 August 2026 SAIC Motor and General Motors signed an agreement extending the SAIC-GM joint venture by 20 years to 2047, with the 50:50 split unchanged; the previous contract was due to expire in June 2027 [12][24]. Media described the renewed cooperation as 'local-led', with product definition, technology-route selection, vehicle validation and production handed to the Chinese team centred on PATAC [24]. SAIC-GM describes its goal as becoming a leader of the 'JV 2.0' era built on electrification, intelligentisation and high value [1]. Buick said that from 2025 all its new models launched in China would be NEVs, with six new models on the 'Xiaoyao' architecture within a year; media reported SAIC-GM planned 12 all-new NEV models from 2025 to 2027 covering BEV, PHEV and range-extended powertrains [23][22].

Our judgment. The renewal removes the near-term risk that the JV contract expires in June 2027. Whether the recovery continues depends on the commercial success of the locally developed 'Xiaoyao'-based NEV line-up and on retaining Buick and Cadillac share, against a backdrop of continued price competition and a shrinking joint-venture-brand share of the market. Chevrolet's sharply reduced sales are another uncertainty [24][27].

Risks

  • Sales and capacity. Annual sales fell about 78% from the 2017 peak to 2024; capacity utilisation was about 22% in 2024, 37% in 2025 and about 32% in H1 2026, leaving large amounts of idle capacity [27].
  • Financial position. SAIC-GM posted a 2024 net loss of RMB 26.688 billion and negative net assets at end-2024 after RMB 23.212 billion of impairments; SAIC Motor accounts for it under the equity method and does not bear excess losses [5].
  • Brand concentration. Chevrolet's sales have collapsed (media reported licensed sales of one unit in June 2026), and media have speculated about its withdrawal from China; SAIC-GM denied such plans, but no official exchange announcement had been found as of the research date [27][28].
  • Competition and technology cycle. NEV penetration has risen above 50% in China and domestic brands have squeezed joint-venture share; media reported that the joint-venture model's longer decision chains lag domestic competitors' product-cycle speed [24][22].
  • Joint-venture contract. SAIC-GM's original contract was due to expire in June 2027 and was renewed only about ten months beforehand, later than peers such as SAIC Volkswagen; the new term now runs to 2047 [24][12].
  • Profit sustainability. The recovery relies on voluntary output cuts, destocking and 'one-price' price protection, while the ramp-up of new NEV models is still to be proven; media reported its NEV and intelligent-driving competitiveness still lags domestic brands [22][23].

概览

上汽通用汽车有限公司是上海汽车集团股份有限公司与通用汽车公司各持股 50% 的中外合资乘用车企业，1997 年 6 月 12 日在上海成立，原名上海通用汽车有限公司，2015 年 7 月 1 日起更名为上汽通用。公司生产别克、雪佛兰、凯迪拉克三大品牌车型，总部位于上海浦东金桥，拥有浦东金桥、烟台东岳、沈阳北盛、武汉四大生产基地，并以泛亚汽车技术中心为研发机构 [1][14][18]。

上汽通用曾是中国规模最大的合资车企之一：2017 年销量达到约 200 万辆的历史峰值，此后持续下滑，2024 年降至 43.50 万辆、净亏损 266.88 亿元（当年四季度计提资产减值 232.12 亿元）。2025 年销量回升至 53.5 万辆（同比 +22.99%），2026 年 8 月 5 日上汽与通用续约至 2047 年 [3][5][7][12]。

作为中美合资的代表项目，上汽通用诞生之初就同步设立整车厂与泛亚研发中心，走“整车厂 + 研发中心”双轨并行的本土化路线，2010 年成为国内首家年产销百万辆级的传统乘用车公司 [20]。2026 年续约时，上汽集团董事长王晓秋称“29 年前合资合作解决了‘有没有’的问题，如今要回答‘强不强’”，上汽通用要“勇做合资 2.0 时代的开拓者” [24]。

历史沿革

  1. 1997-06-12

    上海通用汽车有限公司（2015 年更名上汽通用）成立，上汽集团与通用汽车各出资 50%；泛亚汽车技术中心同日成立。[1][14][20]

  2. 1998-12-17

    第一辆别克新世纪轿车在浦东金桥工厂下线，公司进入量产阶段。[20]

  3. 2000-12-12

    别克赛欧下线，以“十万元家轿”定位切入中国家庭轿车市场。[20]

  4. 2001-10

    别克 GL10 批量出口菲律宾，为中高档轿车批量出口的早期案例。[20]

  5. 2003-04

    别克凯越中级家轿发布，开启全新的中级车细分市场。[20]

  6. 2004-06-07

    发布凯迪拉克品牌，进入豪华车市场。[20][21]

  7. 2005-01-18

    发布雪佛兰品牌，形成别克、雪佛兰、凯迪拉克三大品牌矩阵。[20][21]

  8. 2006-01

    累计产销突破 100 万辆。[20]

  9. 2008-02

    第 200 万辆整车下线。[20]

  10. 2009-04

    雪佛兰科鲁兹上市，系通用汽车新一代全球战略车型。[20]

  11. 2009-12-17

    第 300 万辆整车下线。[20]

  12. 2010-12

    年产销率先突破 100 万辆，成为中国汽车工业史上首个年产销跃上“百万量级”的传统乘用车公司。[20]

  13. 2011-09

    第 500 万辆整车下线，成为中国用时最短实现 500 万辆产品的乘用车企业。[20]

  14. 2015-07

    自 2015 年 7 月 1 日起由“上海通用汽车有限公司”更名为“上汽通用汽车有限公司”。[18][29]

  15. 2017

    年销量达到历史峰值约 200 万辆（200.02 万辆），此后进入持续下滑通道直至 2024 年。[24][27]

  16. 2024-08

    卢晓接替庄菁雄出任总经理，主导去库存与产品本土化改革。[22][25]

  17. 2025-01

    上汽集团公告，上汽通用及其控股子公司将在 2024 年第四季度计提资产减值准备 232.12 亿元。[5]

  18. 2025-04-21

    别克推出高端新能源子品牌“至境”并发布“逍遥”超级融合架构。[13][23]

  19. 2026-07-28

    徐平接任上汽通用总经理（卢晓调任上汽乘用车总经理）。[25][26]

  20. 2026-08-05

    上汽集团与通用汽车签署续约协议，将上汽通用合资期限延长 20 年至 2047 年，50:50 股比不变。[12]

现状

品牌与产品

上汽通用运营别克（主流/中高档：GL8 商务 MPV、君威/君越/英朗轿车、昂科威 SUV 及新能源车型）、凯迪拉克（豪华：CT5、XT5、LYRIQ）与雪佛兰（大众化：历史上有赛欧、科鲁兹、迈锐宝、探界者）三大品牌。公司称其产品覆盖 MPV、SUV、轿车细分市场，提供纯电、插混、增程多元驱动技术 [1]。据媒体对通用汽车 2025 年在华销量的报道，别克 MPV 家族销量逾 12 万辆（同比增长 23%）、昂科威全年销量同比增长 76.4%、君越销量同比增长逾一倍，凯迪拉克锐歌、XT5 交付量分别同比增长 90%、32.4% [32]。

2025 年上海车展上，别克全新大型新能源 MPV GL8 陆尚正式上市，共 3 款车型，售价 24.99 万—28.99 万元，并携手 GL8 陆尊 PHEV 展出；凯迪拉克带来 V 系列首款纯电车型 LYRIQ-V [13][19]。

新能源转型

据中国经济信息网报道，上汽通用 2025 年全年终端销量 562,185 辆（含出口），其中新能源累计销量 88,833 辆，其新能源销量与渗透率在合资品牌阵营中位列第一 [31]。上汽集团 2026 年半年报显示，上汽通用上半年新能源车销量增速超过 80%，别克至境 E7 上市首月即成为合资品牌新能源车型销量冠军 [10]。

别克 2025 年 4 月发布高端新能源子品牌“至境”与“逍遥”超级融合架构，年内推出至境 L7（增程轿车）、至境世家等车型，2026 年上半年再推至境 E7 大六座新能源 SUV [13][23][10]。

生产基地与产能

公司拥有浦东金桥、烟台东岳、沈阳北盛、武汉四大生产基地，2017 年成立 20 周年时形成 9 个整车厂、4 个动力总成厂 [1][21]。

随着销量下滑，产能利用率大幅走低：媒体报道 2024 年设计产能 190.8 万辆、产量 42.2 万辆（利用率约 22%），2025 年设计产能压减至 145.2 万辆、产量 53.4 万辆（利用率约 37%）[27]。媒体还报道，沈阳北盛工厂已完成资产交割交由地方政府处置 [33]。上汽集团 2025 年年报表示将“调产能”，上汽大众、上汽通用优化基地布局，释放出的产能用于“尚界”等自主品牌产品 [27]。

研发与技术

泛亚汽车技术中心成立于 1997 年 6 月 12 日，由通用汽车中国有限责任公司与上汽集团各持股 50% 组建，是中国首家合资汽车设计开发中心，也是首家获“国家认定企业技术中心”的合资汽车企业 [14]。

2025 年 4 月，别克发布“逍遥”超级融合架构——合资车企首个由中国团队主导研发的全域融合新能源架构，支持 MPV/SUV/轿车三种车身、纯电/插混/增程三种动力、前驱/后驱/四驱三种驱动，并推出高端新能源子品牌“至境” [13][23]。

近期业绩

据上汽集团产销快报，上汽通用 2026 年上半年批发销量 23.12 万辆（同比下降 5.68%）、产量 23.13 万辆（同比下降 12.17%）[6]。2026 年前 8 个月累计批发销量 300,935 辆（同比下降 9.14%），其中 8 月单月销量 35,008 辆（同比下降 20.25%）[8]。权益法核算下，上汽通用累计未确认亏损由 2025 年末的 89.80 亿元收窄至 2026 年 6 月末的 84.01 亿元 [10]。

股权与治理

上汽集团与通用汽车各持股 50%，董事会由双方按股比组成。上汽集团对上汽通用采用权益法核算，因不承担超额亏损义务，已将长期股权投资减记至零、不再确认超额亏损 [5]。上汽通用（制造公司）董事长未在本研究所查阅的公开文件中载明。泛亚汽车技术中心法定代表人为徐平，系 2026 年 9 月由卢晓变更而来 [30]。

竞争格局

我们的判断。上汽通用在中国乘用车市场与比亚迪、吉利、长安、奇瑞等自主品牌，以及大众、丰田、本田等合资品牌竞争。其竞争基础在于别克、雪佛兰、凯迪拉克三大品牌矩阵、别克 GL8 在商务 MPV 市场的地位，以及泛亚的本土研发能力 [21]。其主要短板是新能源转型滞后与规模大幅收缩：年销量从 2017 年约 200 万辆的峰值下降约 78% 至 2024 年的 43.50 万辆，2025 年回升至 53.5 万辆 [22][7]。

我们的判断。2024 年净亏损 266.88 亿元（含 232.12 亿元减值）且净资产为负，表明上汽通用业绩对国内价格竞争及新能源转型节奏高度敏感。2025 年销量回升、2026 年上半年权益法核算转正能否持续，取决于“至境”“逍遥”新能源产品线的放量，以及别克、凯迪拉克品牌的企稳 [5][28]。

我们的判断。在商务 MPV 市场，别克 GL8 仍具标杆地位；但在新能源与智能化方面，媒体评价上汽通用竞争力相对有限。持续的价格战也迫使其以“一口价”促销保量 [22]。与同集团合资公司上汽大众（2025 年 102.4 万辆）、上汽通用五菱（2025 年 161.51 万辆）相比，上汽通用 2025 年 53.5 万辆的规模已明显落后；其曾是上汽集团重要的利润来源，2024 年的巨额亏损也对集团业绩形成拖累 [7][5]。

未来展望

公司公开规划。2026 年 8 月 5 日，上汽集团与通用汽车签署协议，将上汽通用合资期限延长 20 年至 2047 年（50:50 股比不变）；原合约原定 2027 年 6 月到期 [12][24]。媒体将新一轮合作概括为“本土主导”，产品定义、技术路线选择、整车验证与量产等权限交由以泛亚为核心的本土团队 [24]。上汽通用将自身目标定位为“合资 2.0 时代的引领者”，以电动化、智能化、高价值化为目标 [1]。别克表示自 2025 年起在中国推出的全新车型序列将全部为新能源车，未来一年推出 6 款基于“逍遥”架构的新车型；媒体报道上汽通用计划 2025 至 2027 年推出 12 款全新新能源车型，覆盖纯电、插混、增程 [23][22]。

我们的判断。续约消除了合资合约 2027 年 6 月到期的近期风险。复苏能否延续，取决于本土研发的“逍遥”架构新能源车型能否在市场上取得成功，以及在价格战持续、合资品牌份额收缩的背景下稳住别克与凯迪拉克的市场地位。雪佛兰销量大幅萎缩是另一项不确定性 [24][27]。

风险

  • 销量与产能。年销量从 2017 年峰值下降约 78% 至 2024 年；产能利用率 2024 年约 22%、2025 年约 37%、2026 年上半年约 32%，存在大量闲置产能 [27]。
  • 财务状况。上汽通用 2024 年净亏损 266.88 亿元、年末净资产为负（含 232.12 亿元减值）；上汽集团以权益法核算、不承担超额亏损 [5]。
  • 品牌集中度。雪佛兰销量大幅萎缩（媒体披露 2026 年 6 月挂牌销量仅 1 辆），媒体出现“退出中国”的猜测；上汽通用否认相关计划，但截至研究日未查到官方交易所公告 [27][28]。
  • 竞争与技术迭代。中国新能源汽车渗透率已超 50%，自主品牌挤压合资份额；媒体报道合资模式的长决策链条难以跟上国内车企的产品迭代节奏 [24][22]。
  • 合资合约。上汽通用原合约 2027 年 6 月到期，续约在到期前约十个月才落地，晚于上汽大众等同行；新合资期限现延至 2047 年 [24][12]。
  • 盈利持续性。公司盈利恢复依赖主动减产、去库存与“一口价”保价，新能源新品放量尚待观察；媒体报道其新能源与智能化竞争力仍落后于自主品牌 [22][23]。
