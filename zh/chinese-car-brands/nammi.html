Overview

Dongfeng Nammi (东风纳米) is the small pure-electric passenger-vehicle brand of Dongfeng Motor Group, one of China's three central automotive state-owned enterprises. Dongfeng released the brand in Chengdu on 23 August 2023, positioning it as a 'national pure-electric professional brand' [1]; Hubei Daily described it as China's first national pure-electric professional brand [17]. The predecessor was Dongchuang Zilian (Wuhan) New Energy Technology Co., Ltd., founded on 28 October 2020 to market Dongfeng's entry-level EVs; it was renamed Dongfeng Motor Nammi Technology Co., Ltd. in February 2023, and again to Dongfeng Passenger Vehicle Sales Co., Ltd. on 28 September 2023 [16][21]. Nammi targets the roughly RMB 50,000-100,000 small pure-electric segment and has launched two models, the Nammi 01 hatchback (January 2024) and the Nammi 06 SUV (May 2025) [6][2].

On 26 June 2025 Dongfeng consolidated Nammi, eπ/Yipai and Fengshen into a single company, Dongfeng Yipai Automotive Technology Co., Ltd., so the Nammi name now operates mainly as an economy-EV model line within Yipai Technology rather than as a separately run brand [4][19]. In 2025 the two Nammi models together sold about 49,400 units, and the Nammi 01 passed 110,000 cumulative sales by year-end [23][11]. In June 2026 the Nammi 06 sold 5,938 units in a single month and entered the top three of the new-energy small-SUV segment [25]. This page studies the Nammi brand; the parent Dongfeng Motor Group and the merged Yipai Technology have separate deep dives.

Current operations

Business structure and integration

Nammi's business is single-focus: the design, manufacture and sale of entry-level pure-electric passenger cars. Since 26 June 2025 the brand has been folded into Dongfeng Yipai Automotive Technology Co., Ltd. (Yipai Technology), which integrates the Yipai (eπ), Fengshen and Nammi brands across product planning, R&D, production, supply chain, sales and service [4][19]. Under the 'Yipai upward, Fengshen renewed' positioning, Nammi is merged into Yipai as its economy pure-electric line, Yipai itself moves upmarket in cooperation with Huawei, and Fengshen focuses on energy-saving family cars; Yipai targets RMB 150,000-250,000 while Nammi covers RMB 60,000-100,000 [19][20]. The integration was preceded by Dongfeng's August 2023 'leap' (跃迁) reform, which placed the Fengshen, eπ/Yipai and Nammi brands under group-level integrated management of marketing and manufacturing [18][17].

Yipai Technology sold 275,752 vehicles in 2025 (up 28.3%), and in H1 2026 delivered 132,857 vehicles (up 21%, including 23,231 units in June 2026), with overseas sales up 186% [11][12]. Within this, the Nammi 01 passed 110,000 cumulative sales by end-2025, and the Nammi 06 passed 4,000 monthly exports in December 2025 [11]. The merged company rests its products on six technology bases - quantum architecture, Mach power, styling, perceived quality, Tianyuan cockpit and Tianyuan intelligent driving - and launched seven new models in 2025 [11].

Products and models

The Nammi line has two active models. The Nammi 01, a small pure-electric hatchback launched on 7 January 2024, is priced at RMB 74,800-104,800 at launch and offers 330 km / 430 km CLTC ranges [6][7]. Its 2026 model launched on 16 October 2025 with eight versions and a limited-time renewal guide price of RMB 58,800-88,800 [24]. The Nammi 06, a small pure-electric SUV, debuted in Beijing on 8 April 2025, opened pre-sale on 28 April at RMB 89,900-119,900 (11,601 orders in one hour), and launched on 27 May 2025 at a limited-time RMB 79,900-109,900 [13][3][2]. In mid-2026 the Nammi 06 Smart Edition (智趣版) added the Tianyuan intelligent-driving T100 system with city NOA, a 2,715 mm wheelbase and a 471 km CLTC range; June 2026 single-month sales reached 5,938 units, ranking the model in the top three of the new-energy small-SUV segment [25].

Two predecessor models predate the brand. The predecessor Dongchuang Zilian went from zero to over 10,000 units in its first year (2021) and passed 35,000 units in 2022 [16]. The Dongfeng EX1, an A0-class pure-electric car, was its first product, and the Nammi BOX, a small pure-electric SUV launched on 17 July 2022, was the main 2022 driver [16][18]. Both are now discontinued legacy models.

Technology and platforms

Nammi models are built on Dongfeng's self-developed Quantum Architecture; the Quantum Architecture 3 platform is dedicated to the small pure-electric market and, per the company, offers four advantages - extreme space, extreme safety, extreme efficiency and extreme intelligent control [1]. The Nammi 01 uses the Mach-E 'ten-in-one' electric drive with a hairpin motor across the range; Dongfeng said energy consumption is as low as 11 kWh per 100 km with 89% CLTC overall efficiency, and disclosed three technology reserves - in-wheel motors, solid-state batteries in demonstration operation, and mass-production-ready super-fast charging [1]. The Nammi 06 carries Dongfeng's 'Tianyuan intelligence' (Tianyuan intelligent driving and Tianyuan cockpit), a third-generation 'Zhihan' thermal-management system, 3C super-fast charging (about 100 km added in five minutes), and a Mach-E drive with a 92.7% high-efficiency ratio certified by CATARC as an 'efficiency star' [2]. The Nammi 01 adds a 540° surround view and ACC adaptive cruise among eight mainstream driver-assistance functions [7]. The 2026 Nammi 06 Smart Edition further equips an 8-megapixel inertial binocular camera, city and highway NOA, a Qualcomm Snapdragon 8155 cockpit chip with 8.8-inch instrument and 12.8-inch central screens, an electric split tailgate, and 3C fast charging up to 154 kW (30% to 80% in about 18 minutes) [25].

Factory and capacity

Nammi models are produced at Dongfeng's Nammi intelligent eco-factory in Xiangyang, Hubei, which grew out of a RMB 5.31 billion new-energy passenger-vehicle project signed by Dongfeng and Xiangyang in February 2023, with a planned annual capacity of 120,000 units [16][7]. In H1 2025 the Xiangyang factory produced 28,580 units, of which 19,670 were for the domestic market and about 8,900 for export, against a full-year production target of 100,000 units [9]. Cumulative Nammi 01 production passed 100,000 units on 23 October 2025, with January-September 2025 output of 44,280 units, up 64.17% year on year [10].

Overseas business

Exports extend a channel that began with the predecessor Nammi BOX, whose eGT-produced models were shipped to Europe and Latin America [18]. The Nammi 01 has entered more than 40 countries and regions, with cumulative overseas sales above 24,000 units as of October 2025 [10]. The Nammi 06 exported more than 4,000 units in December 2025 alone [11], and its overseas version, sold as the Dongfeng VIGO, launched in San José, Costa Rica on 28 January 2026 with 270 terminal orders signed at the event [8].

Competition

Competitive position: our assessment

Our assessment: Nammi operates in China's fiercely contested small pure-electric market, where the leaders are the BYD Seagull and Dolphin, the Wuling Bingo and the Geely Xingyuan, joined by the ORA Good Cat and others. With combined 2025 sales of about 49,400 units across its two models, Nammi's scale is a fraction of the segment leaders' [23]. This judgment rests on the sales figures cited above and on the segment's price band of roughly RMB 50,000-100,000, where the Nammi 01 and Nammi 06 are priced [2].

Our assessment: the Nammi 06's June 2026 single-month sales of 5,938 units, which entered the top three of the new-energy small-SUV segment, shows the brand can gain ground when it pushes advanced driving-assistance into the RMB 100,000 class [25]. However, this is one model in one month, and the brand's overall domestic retail base remains small relative to the segment leaders [23].

Strengths and constraints: our assessment

Our assessment: Nammi's strengths are the manufacturing, engineering and supply-chain resources of a central SOE parent, a self-developed platform (Quantum Architecture 3) and powertrain (Mach-E), the Tianyuan intelligent system carried on the Nammi 06, and an export channel built from the predecessor's Nammi BOX in Europe [1][18]. Its constraints are a narrow two-model line-up in a low-margin segment, modest volume relative to the leaders, and reduced brand independence after being merged into Yipai Technology - the 'Nammi' name now exists mainly as a model line rather than a separately run brand [19]. The brand's earlier target of 400,000 units by 2025 was not met; the two Nammi models together sold well below that level in 2025 [15][23].

Outlook

Company plans

At its 2023 partner conference, Dongfeng Nammi announced a plan to launch one to two new models a year and to exceed 400,000 units of sales by 2025, with the aim of leading the small pure-electric market [15]. These are stated plans, and the 400,000-unit target was not achieved. The company also said in April 2025 that it expected 2025 Nammi 01 exports to jump to about 30,000 units [5]. After the integration, forward-looking statements are made at the Yipai Technology level: Yipai plans six all-new models in 2026, longer-range solid-state batteries to enter volume production in 2026, and the Tianyuan T500 'parking-space-to-parking-space' pilot system, with an annual sales target of 1.5 million units (1 million for Yipai, 500,000 for Fengshen) [11][19]. Dongfeng has said the Nammi 01 has already reached more than 40 countries and regions [10]. On the product side, Yipai is iterating the Nammi 06 through the Smart Edition (智趣版), which brings the Tianyuan T100 city-NOA system into the RMB 100,000 class [25].

Our analysis

Our assessment: the Nammi brand's trajectory has effectively converged with Yipai Technology, so its future depends on how the merged company deploys the economy EV line against BYD and Wuling in a price-war segment. The near-term question is whether Nammi 01 and Nammi 06 exports can keep scaling - the December 2025 single-month export of over 4,000 Nammi 06 units and the January 2026 VIGO launch in Costa Rica are early, positive signals, but the base is small [11][8]. The June 2026 Nammi 06 sales jump to 5,938 units, driven by the Smart Edition, is another early signal that advanced driver-assistance can revive demand in the segment, but a single monthly figure does not yet establish a trend [25]. A separate risk is that, as an entry brand merged into a larger unit, Nammi's product identity could be diluted while it carries the low-margin end of the portfolio [19]. These judgments rest on the sales, product and integration facts cited above.

Risks

  • Intense price competition in the small EV segment: Nammi competes directly with the BYD Seagull/Dolphin, Wuling Bingo and Geely Xingyuan in a RMB 50,000-100,000 band where price cuts are common; the Nammi 01's entry price was lowered over successive model years (limited-time RMB 58,800-88,800 for the 2026 model versus RMB 74,800-104,800 at launch) [24][6].
  • Low margin and narrow line-up: the brand has only two active models in a low-margin segment, concentrating its exposure [2][19].
  • Reduced brand independence: since the June 2025 integration, Nammi operates within Yipai Technology and its products are counted in Yipai Technology's figures, so brand-level performance is no longer separately reported [4].
  • Overseas trade-policy and execution risk: exports to Europe and the Americas are exposed to tariffs, certification and localisation requirements, and the overseas network is still young (the first Central America launch was in January 2026) [8].
  • Dependence on the parent group: Nammi relies on Dongfeng for platforms, manufacturing and capital, and does not separately disclose financials, limiting external visibility into its standalone economics [22][18].

概览

东风纳米（Nammi）是中国三大汽车央企之一东风汽车集团有限公司旗下聚焦小型纯电市场的乘用车品牌。东风于 2023 年 8 月 23 日在成都发布该品牌，定位「国民纯电专业品牌」[1]；湖北日报称其为中国首个国民纯电专业品牌[17]。品牌前身为 2020 年 10 月 28 日成立的东创紫联（武汉）新能源科技有限公司，负责东风入门级纯电车型的营销，2023 年 2 月更名为东风汽车纳米科技有限公司，同年 9 月 28 日又更名为东风乘用车销售有限公司[16][21]。纳米主攻约 5 万–10 万元级小型纯电市场，先后推出纳米 01（2024 年 1 月上市）与纳米 06（2025 年 5 月上市）两款车型[6][2]。

2025 年 6 月 26 日，东风将纳米、奕派（eπ）、风神整合进东风奕派汽车科技公司统一运营，「纳米」自此更多作为奕派科技旗下的经济型纯电产品谱系存在，而非独立运营的品牌[4][19]。2025 年，纳米两款车型合计销量约 4.94 万辆，其中纳米 01 累计销量至年底突破 11 万辆[23][11]。2026 年 6 月，纳米 06 单月销量达 5,938 辆，跻身新能源小型 SUV 细分市场前三[25]。本页为东风纳米这一品牌的研究，母集团东风汽车集团与整合后的奕派科技另有深度研究。

现状

业务结构与整合现状

东风纳米业务高度聚焦，即入门级纯电乘用车的设计、制造与销售。自 2025 年 6 月 26 日起，品牌被并入东风奕派汽车科技公司（奕派科技），该公司将奕派（eπ）、风神、纳米三大品牌的商企、研发、生产、供应链、销售与服务全链条整合至同一主体[4][19]。按「奕派向上、风神向新」的定位，纳米融入东风奕派成为其经济型纯电产品线，奕派自身携手华为进军中高端，风神专注节能家享市场；奕派主打 15 万–25 万元区间，纳米聚焦 6 万–10 万元区间[19][20]。这一整合的前奏是 2023 年 8 月东风实施的乘用车新能源「跃迁行动」，将风神、奕派（eπ）、纳米三大产品系列品牌纳入营销与制造的集团一体化管理[18][17]。

奕派科技 2025 年销售 275,752 辆（同比增长 28.3%），2026 年上半年交付 132,857 辆（同比增长 21%，其中 6 月交付 23,231 辆），海外销量同比增长 186%[11][12]。其中纳米 01 累计销量至 2025 年底突破 11 万辆，纳米 06 于 2025 年 12 月单月出口突破 4,000 辆[11]。整合后的奕派科技以「量子架构、马赫动力、造型美学、感知品质、天元智舱、天元智驾」六大技术底座支撑产品，2025 年共推出 7 款新车型[11]。

产品与车型

纳米产品线现有两款在售车型。纳米 01 为小型纯电两厢车，2024 年 1 月 7 日上市，上市价 7.48 万–10.48 万元，提供 330 km / 430 km 两种 CLTC 续航[6][7]；其 2026 款于 2025 年 10 月 16 日上市，推出 8 款车型，限时焕新指导价 5.88 万–8.88 万元[24]。纳米 06 为小型纯电 SUV，2025 年 4 月 8 日北京首秀，4 月 28 日以 8.99 万–11.99 万元开启预售（1 小时订单 11,601 辆），5 月 27 日以限时先享价 7.99 万–10.99 万元正式上市[13][3][2]。2026 年年中，纳米 06「智趣版」推向市场，新增天元智驾 T100 城市 NOA、2715mm 轴距与 471km CLTC 续航；2026 年 6 月单月销量达 5,938 辆，跻身新能源小型 SUV 细分市场前三[25]。

品牌诞生前还有两款遗留车型。前身东创紫联首年（2021 年）实现从 0 到破万辆，2022 年销量突破 3.5 万辆[16]。东风 EX1 为前身东创紫联销售的首款 A0 级纯电车型，纳米 BOX 为 2022 年 7 月 17 日上市的小型纯电 SUV，是东创紫联 2022 年的销量主力[16][18]。二者现均已停售。

技术与平台

纳米车型基于东风自研的量子架构打造，其中针对小型纯电市场的是量子架构 3 号平台，官方称其具备「极致空间、极致安全、极致能效、极致智控」四大优势[1]。纳米 01 采用马赫E「十合一」电驱系统、全系扁线电机，官方称能耗低至百公里 11 千瓦时、CLTC 综合效率 89%；发布时东风还披露了平台的三大技术储备——轮毂电机、示范运营中的固态电池、成熟可量产的超级快充[1]。纳米 06 搭载东风「天元智能」技术——天元智驾与天元智舱，配备第三代「智焓」热管理系统、3C 超级快充（5 分钟约补能 100 公里）以及高能效占比 92.7% 的马赫E电驱，其马赫E电驱获中汽研「能效之星」认证[2]。纳米 01 则配备 540° 环视系统与 ACC 自适应巡航等 8 种主流智能辅助驾驶功能[7]。2026 款纳米 06 智趣版进一步配备 800 万像素惯导双目摄像头、城市与高速 NOA、高通骁龙 8155 芯片（8.8 英寸仪表 + 12.8 英寸中控）、电动天地门，以及最高 154kW 的 3C 快充（30% 充至 80% 约 18 分钟）[25]。

工厂与产能

纳米车型产自湖北襄阳的东风纳米智能生态工厂，该工厂源于 2023 年 2 月东风与襄阳签署的总投资 53.1 亿元新能源乘用车项目，规划年产 12 万辆[16][7]。2025 年上半年襄阳工厂生产 28,580 辆，其中国产 19,670 辆、出口约 8,900 辆，对照全年 10 万辆的生产目标[9]。纳米 01 累计产量于 2025 年 10 月 23 日突破 10 万辆，2025 年 1–9 月产量 44,280 辆，同比增长 64.17%[10]。

海外业务

纳米出口延续了前身时期的渠道：纳米 BOX 时期易捷特生产车型即已出口欧洲、拉美[18]。纳米 01 已进入全球 40 多个国家和地区，截至 2025 年 10 月海外累计销量超 2.4 万辆[10]。纳米 06 于 2025 年 12 月单月出口突破 4,000 辆[11]，其海外版「东风 VIGO」于 2026 年 1 月 28 日在哥斯达黎加首都圣何塞上市，活动现场达成 270 辆终端订单[8]。

股权与治理

品牌层面未在公告中单独认定「控股股东」。原运营主体东创紫联于 2023 年 2 月更名为东风汽车纳米科技有限公司，同年 9 月 28 日再更名为东风乘用车销售有限公司，由东风汽车集团股份有限公司（0489.HK）直接全资持股[21]。襄阳生产主体东风汽车纳米科技（襄阳）有限公司于 2023 年 1 月前后成立，注册资本 5 亿元，由集团间接全资控股[22]。最终控制方为国务院国资委监管的中央企业东风汽车集团有限公司[1]。

竞争格局

竞争地位：我们的判断

我们的判断：东风纳米所处的中国小型纯电市场，头部玩家为比亚迪海鸥、海豚，五菱缤果，吉利星愿，另有欧拉好猫等。纳米两款车型 2025 年销量合计约 4.94 万辆，规模与细分市场头部车型相比差距明显[23]。上述判断依据为前述销量数据，以及该细分市场约 5 万–10 万元的价格带——纳米 01 与纳米 06 均定价于此区间[2]。

我们的判断：纳米 06 于 2026 年 6 月实现单月销量 5,938 辆、跻身新能源小型 SUV 细分市场前三，显示品牌把高阶智驾下放到十万元级价位时能够获得市场增量[25]。但这只是单一车型的月度表现，品牌整体国内零售基数相对细分市场头部仍偏小[23]。

优势与约束：我们的判断

我们的判断：东风纳米的优势在于央企母集团的制造、工程与供应链资源，自研的量子架构 3 号平台与马赫E动力，纳米 06 搭载的天元智能系统，以及从前身纳米 BOX 欧洲出口延续而来的海外渠道[1][18]。其约束在于仅两款在售车型、身处低毛利细分市场，规模相对头部偏小，且并入奕派科技后品牌独立性下降——「纳米」如今更多作为车型谱系存在，而非独立运营的品牌[19]。品牌早期提出的 2025 年销量 40 万辆目标未达成，2025 年纳米两款车型合计销量明显低于该水平[15][23]。

未来展望

公司公开规划

在 2023 年伙伴大会上，东风纳米宣布每年上市 1 至 2 款新车型、到 2025 年销量突破 40 万辆的目标，并确立「小型纯电动市场领导者」愿景[15]。以上为公司规划表述，其中 40 万辆目标未达成。公司还曾于 2025 年 4 月预计当年纳米 01 出口量跃升至 3 万辆[5]。整合后，前瞻性规划主要由奕派科技层面发布：奕派科技计划 2026 年推出 6 款全新车型、更长续航固态电池 2026 年量产装车、搭载「车位到车位」全程领航的天元 T500 系统，并设定 150 万辆年度销量目标（奕派 100 万辆、风神 50 万辆）[11][19]。东风方面称纳米 01 已进入全球 40 多个国家和地区[10]。产品层面，奕派正通过纳米 06「智趣版」把天元智驾 T100 城市 NOA 引入十万元级价位[25]。

我们的分析

我们的判断：东风纳米的品牌轨迹实际上已与奕派科技合流，其未来取决于合并后的公司如何在价格战激烈的小型纯电市场，以经济型产品线对抗比亚迪与五菱。近期看点在于纳米 01、纳米 06 的出口能否持续放量——2025 年 12 月纳米 06 单月出口破 4,000 辆、2026 年 1 月 VIGO 在哥斯达黎加上市是积极但基数仍小的信号[11][8]。2026 年 6 月纳米 06 在智趣版带动下单月销量升至 5,938 辆，是高阶智驾拉动细分市场需求的又一早期信号，但单月数据尚不足以确立趋势[25]。另一风险在于，作为并入更大主体的入门品牌，纳米在承担组合中低毛利一端的同时，产品身份可能被稀释[19]。上述判断依据为前述销量、产品与整合相关事实。

风险

  • 小型纯电市场价格竞争激烈：纳米直接对标比亚迪海鸥/海豚、五菱缤果、吉利星愿等，处于 5 万–10 万元价格带、价格战频发；纳米 01 的起售价随年款下调（2026 款限时焕新价 5.88 万–8.88 万元，低于上市时的 7.48 万–10.48 万元）[24][6]。
  • 毛利低、产品线单一：品牌仅两款在售车型，处于低毛利细分市场，经营暴露集中[2][19]。
  • 品牌独立性下降：2025 年 6 月整合后，纳米并入奕派科技运营、销量计入奕派科技口径，品牌层面业绩不再单独披露[4]。
  • 海外贸易政策与执行风险：对欧洲、美洲的出口面临关税、认证与属地化要求，且海外网络仍处早期（中美洲首次上市在 2026 年 1 月）[8]。
  • 对母公司依赖：纳米在平台、制造与资金上依赖东风，且不单独披露财务，外界难以评估其独立经营状况[22][18]。
