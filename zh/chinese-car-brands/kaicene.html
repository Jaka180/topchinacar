Overview

Changan Kaicene (长安凯程) is the commercial-vehicle brand of Chongqing Changan Automobile Co., Ltd. (SZSE 000625), operated by Chongqing Changan Kaicene Auto Technology Co., Ltd., in which Changan directly held 65.90% after a capital increase completed in December 2024 and consolidates it — a stake restated in Changan's 2026 interim report ([3], [18]). The brand descends from Changan's light-vehicle business, renamed 'Kaicene Automobile' in April 2018 under Changan's 'Third Venture — Innovation and Entrepreneurship' plan and positioned as a smart-logistics commercial brand, one of Changan's four independent brands ([6]); its roots reach back to Changan's first micro-vehicles rolling off the line in 1984 ([15]).

Kaicene's line-up spans pickups, light vans, micro-trucks, light trucks and commercial MPVs, under series including Changan Ruixing, Shenqi, Star (Zhixing), Xingka, Ono and Changan Pickup ([1]). Milestones include the F70 mid-to-large pickup co-developed with PSA and launched globally in 2019 ([9]), the first super extended-range pickup Changan Hunter in 2024 ([7]), and the V919 pure-electric van, first production model on the K01 architecture, in 2025 ([10]). The brand is repositioning as a 'digital-NEV commercial-vehicle technology brand' under its electrification, intelligence, pickup and globalisation strategy ([12]).

In 2025 Kaicene sold 261,000 vehicles (+17.8% YoY), including 49,000 NEVs (+80.5%) and 87,000 exports (+31.1%), with brand-level revenue of RMB 13.6 billion ([12], [19]). In July 2025 China Changan Automobile Group was established in Chongqing as a first-tier central SOE directly under SASAC, making Kaicene the commercial-vehicle carrier of the 'new central SOE'; it has completed a Series A round of over RMB 2 billion and is advancing further equity financing and an IPO ([5], [16]).

Current operations

Product range

As of the research cut-off (2026-10-29), Kaicene's range covers pickups, trucks, buses/vans, NEVs, MPVs and passenger-style vehicles, with pickups including the F70 (and its BlueCore version), Hunter, Hunter K50 and Lantuoer/Explorer ([1]). The strategic NEV products are the V919 pure-electric van, the Ruixing EM60/EM80 electric vans, the Xingka EV micro-truck and the Shenqi T30EV light truck ([1]). Since November 2024 the brand has said it will launch only new-energy products from 2025, with more than six NEV models planned over three years, and four NEV pickups across three product series within three years ([13], [16]).

Brand identity

Under the November 2024 strategy, Changan-brand commercial vehicles keep the classic sun logo (太阳标) for conventional products, while Kaicene adopts a new cube logo (立方标) for its digital-NEV products, reflecting its repositioning as a digital-NEV commercial-vehicle technology brand ([13]).

Technology and platforms

Kaicene's core technology is K01, its first native digital-NEV commercial-vehicle architecture, described as the embodiment of Changan's six-layer architecture technology with 'decoupling of upper and lower bodies, separation of software and hardware, and vehicle-cloud integration'; at the 15 November 2024 launch Kaicene said that, compared with traditional architectures, products developed on K01 shorten the development cycle by one year, lower R&D investment by 40% and raise manufacturing efficiency by 30%. Those figures are the company's statements at that launch, not independently verified results ([13]). The architecture is open to the industry through whole-vehicle sales, platform/technology export and ecosystem services ([13]). Kaicene reuses Changan's Tianheng intelligent chassis (the V919 has line-controlled braking IBCU as standard) and the commercial Jinzhongzhao battery platform (商用金钟罩) supporting 2C/4C fast charging ([13]). On pickups, Changan said it would keep deepening platform cooperation with Stellantis ([7]).

Smart-logistics ecosystem

Since the 2022 'Photosynthesis Forest Plan', Kaicene has extended from vehicle sales into logistics ecosystems through the 'Changan Kaicene Intelligent Butler' platform, building 'smart vehicles + a smart cloud', and is developing unmanned logistics vehicles (Robovan/Robotruck), with small-batch production planned from 2026 ([13]). Kaicene also said it is deepening cooperation with CATL and JD.com on a NEV commercial-vehicle intelligent operation system ([12]).

Manufacturing and capacity

As Changan's commercial-vehicle arm, Kaicene's production is integrated into Changan's vehicle-manufacturing system; the brand does not separately disclose its factory list or capacity, so these are not stated here ([15]). Kaicene shares Changan's global R&D network, which spans a 'six-country, ten-site' layout with more than 18,000 R&D staff across over 30 countries and regions ([7]).

Domestic position

Kaicene says its NEV mini-van line has repeatedly been the segment sales champion, including six consecutive months in 2025 ([12], [17]). The company positions itself as the strategic carrier of Changan's commercial-vehicle business, which it says has operated in commercial vehicles for 41 years with cumulative sales exceeding 10 million units ([11]).

Overseas business

Exports are a growth engine: 66,700 units in 2024 (+106.2%) and 87,000 in 2025 (+31.1%) ([20], [12]). In early 2025 Kaicene said it would launch at least four new products overseas over three years ([15]).

Recent performance

The most recent complete fiscal year is 2025 (261,000 units and RMB 13.6 billion revenue). For the first half of 2025, Xinhua reported revenue of RMB 6.599 billion (+5.8% YoY) ([17]). China Economic Net reported first-half sales of 140,300 units (+10.1% YoY) ([11]). In the first quarter of 2026, media reported overseas sales of 27,000 units (+29% YoY) and monthly pickup sales topping 10,000 in March (up 94% month-on-month) ([12]). As Kaicene is not separately listed, its 2026 half-year results are not separately disclosed and are consolidated within Changan's 2026 interim report ([3]).

Founders and leadership

Ownership and governance

Chongqing Changan Kaicene Auto Technology Co., Ltd. was incorporated on 27 July 2020 with RMB 1.0 billion registered capital, initially owned 83.64% by Changan and 16.36% by Chongqing Airport Development Investment Group, as an independent legal entity under mixed-ownership reform ([14]). After the capital increase completed in December 2024, the shareholder structure is: Changan 65.90%, Chongqing Airport Development Investment Group 11.49%, CCB Investment 5.01%, Hengtai Dingzhou Fund 4.29%, Chenzhi Automotive Technology Group Co., Ltd. (formerly China Changan Automobile Group Co., Ltd.) 2.86%, Jianyuan Yinhe 2.15%, Southern Assets 2.15%, Bingqi New Momentum 2.15%, Bethel 1.43%, Changyu Fund 1.14%, Southern Xinyu Fund 0.72% and Yuekai Zhidong Fund 0.72%; registered capital rose from RMB 1.0 billion to RMB 1.4237145 billion with total funds of RMB 2.08 billion ([18]). The December 2024 announcement records that 2.86% subscriber under the name China Changan Automobile Group Co., Ltd. Changan's 2025 annual report says this company was renamed Chenzhi Automotive Technology Group Co., Ltd. in June 2025, and that the renaming did not change its nature, ownership or control relationship with Changan; a separate China Changan Automobile Group, formed by a demerger in July 2025, is the listed company's indirect controlling shareholder ([26]). No announcement transferring this 2.86% Kaicene stake to the new group has been seen, so the stake is shown under the renamed company. Kaicene has no separately identified actual controller beyond Changan's control chain, where the actual controller is the State Council SASAC ([3]).

Competition

Our judgment: competitive scope. Kaicene's own line-up sits in China's light commercial vehicle market, covering pickups, light and micro trucks, vans and commercial MPVs ([1]). The sources used for this page do not name a set of rival companies, so none is listed here.

Our judgment: strengths. First, Kaicene shares Changan's R&D and supply chain — the K01 architecture reuses Changan's passenger-car six-layer architecture technology, the V919 has the Tianheng chassis line-control braking as standard, and pickups deepen platform cooperation with Stellantis ([13], [7]). Second, strong export momentum (exports +106.2% in 2024 and +31.1% in 2025) ([20], [12]). Third, NEV growth faster than overall growth (NEVs +80.5% versus +17.8% overall in 2025), with its NEV mini-van repeatedly the segment champion ([12], [19]). Fourth, an early move into extended-range pickups with the Hunter ([7]).

Our judgment: weaknesses. NEV sales are still a small share of the total (49,000 of 261,000 units in 2025, about 19%), and the revenue base remains fuel micro-trucks and vans, which face tightening emission rules and price competition ([12], [15]). As an unlisted subsidiary, Kaicene does not disclose profit independently, so its profitability cannot be observed directly — media reports citing exchange listing materials indicate it was not yet profitable in recent years ([3], [21]).

Outlook

Company plans. At the August 2025 Chengdu Auto Show, Kaicene set 2030 goals of over 70% NEV penetration, leadership in unmanned logistics vehicles, over 200,000 annual pickup sales and over 40% overseas share, aiming for the global top five in light commercial vehicles ([17]). Earlier guidance differed: in January 2025, then general manager Lan Xiangwen said Kaicene expected overseas sales to reach 50% of total sales by 2030, 'i.e. 600,000 units' — which read literally implies 600,000 overseas and roughly 1.2 million total ([15]); a Gasgoo report republished by Shanghai Metals Market wrote of 'total sales of 600,000 units by 2030, with overseas sales contributing 50%' ([16]), and Changjiang Business Daily similarly quoted a 2030 total of 600,000 units with 50% overseas ([20]). These earlier figures are not fully consistent with each other, and the August 2025 Chengdu Auto Show guidance no longer used the 600,000-unit figure; this page presents them by date without reconciling them.

Other disclosed plans: launching only new-energy products from 2025 with more than six NEV models over three years and four NEV pickups across three series within three years, and small-batch production of unmanned logistics vehicles from 2026 ([13], [16]). On later financing, two media accounts are set side by side. Changjiang Business Daily reported on 21 January 2025 that Kaicene would keep advancing equity financing and an IPO ([20]). China Economic Net reported on 30 July 2025 that, after the Series A of more than RMB 2 billion, Kaicene 'is now starting a Series B' and that the new group's establishment would speed an independent listing ([11]). As of 2026-10-29, no A-tier company announcement has been found that the Series B has closed or that an IPO filing has been made. The July 2025 report is the latest statement located, and there is no later official update. These are disclosed plans and media reports, not completed results.

Our judgment. Whichever 2030 figure is used, the target implies a substantial expansion from the 261,000 units sold in 2025, depending heavily on the NEV transition (70% penetration) and overseas expansion (40%+ share) both executing as planned. The central-SOE status, the completed Series A round and the K01 architecture provide funding and platform foundations, but whether Kaicene can turn scale and NEV pickup/van volumes into observable profit remains to be seen — it does not disclose profit independently, and media-cited listing materials indicate it was not yet profitable in recent years ([8], [3], [21]). Whether strategy execution continues under new chairman Wang Xiaofei from April 2026 is another key variable for the transition ([8]). The above is our analysis, not a forecast of financial results.

Risks

  • Transition execution. Kaicene still sells mostly fuel micro-trucks and vans, and its shift to only NEV products from 2025 starts from a small base (49,000 NEVs of 261,000 total in 2025) ([12]).
  • Overseas concentration and trade barriers. Exports (87,000 units in 2025) depend on emerging-market demand and face tariff and regional-policy risk ([12]).
  • Competitive and pricing pressure. The LCV market faces intense price competition and tightening emission rules, noted in Kaicene's own strategy communications ([15]).
  • Information opacity. As an unlisted subsidiary, Kaicene does not publish standalone audited financials, so profitability is not independently verifiable ([3]).
  • Losses and leverage. Media reports citing exchange listing materials showed net losses in recent years and a debt ratio of 90.74% as of end-Q3 2024 ([21]).
  • NEV commercial-vehicle technology challenges. Kaicene's strategy communications note that NEV commercial vehicles must still overcome battery-life, heavy-load stability and charging-infrastructure issues ([15]).
  • Financing and listing uncertainty. The media-reported follow-on equity financing and IPO plan are in progress and not guaranteed to complete ([20]).

概览

长安凯程（Changan Kaicene）是重庆长安汽车股份有限公司（深交所 000625）旗下的商用车品牌，经营主体为重庆长安凯程汽车科技有限公司。2024 年 12 月增资完成后，长安汽车直接持有其 65.90% 股权并纳入合并报表，2026 年半年度报告仍载明该比例（[3]、[18]）。品牌前身是长安轻型车业务，2018 年 4 月随长安「第三次创业—创新创业计划」更名为「凯程汽车」，定位智慧物流商用品牌，为长安四大自主品牌之一（[6]）；其源头可追溯至 1984 年长安第一批微型汽车下线（[15]）。

凯程产品覆盖皮卡、轻客、微卡、轻卡、商用 MPV 等品类，旗下有长安睿行、长安神骐、长安之星、长安星卡、长安欧诺、长安皮卡等产品系列（[1]）。重要节点包括 2019 年与 PSA（标致雪铁龙）联合开发的中大型皮卡 F70 全球上市（[9]）、2024 年首款超级增程皮卡长安猎手上市（[7]）、2025 年首款基于 K01 架构的纯电后驱宽体轻客 V919 上市（[10]），并以「电动化、智慧化、皮卡化、全球化」四化战略向数智新能源商用车科技品牌转型（[12]）。

2025 年凯程全年销售 26.1 万辆（同比 +17.8%），其中新能源 4.9 万辆（+80.5%）、出口 8.7 万辆（+31.1%），品牌口径营收 136 亿元（[12]、[19]）。2025 年 7 月中国长安汽车集团在重庆挂牌成立、成为国务院国资委直管一级央企，凯程成为这家「新央企」的商用车战略承载者，并已完成超 20 亿元 A 轮融资、推进股权融资与 IPO（[5]、[16]）。

历史沿革

  1. 1984

    长安第一批微型汽车剪彩出厂，被称为中国最早的微型汽车，是后来成为凯程的微车业务的源头。[15]

  2. 1998

    「长安之星」诞生，后被誉为中国商用车市场的品质标杆。[15]

  3. 2003

    长安推出星卡 1022，为国内最早采用凸头设计的微卡之一。[15]

  4. 2010

    长安金牛星上市，为首款达到 C-NCAP 准五星安全标准的微车；截至 2010 年「太阳标」微车累计销量达 900 万辆。[15]

  5. 2014

    长安之星系列累计销量突破 500 万辆，曾连续 8 年稳居细分市场销量冠军。[15]

  7. 2017-09-27

现状

产品谱系

截至研究截止日（2026-10-29），凯程产品覆盖皮卡、卡车、客车/轻客、新能源、MPV、乘用化车型六大品类，皮卡含 F70（及蓝鲸版）、猎手、猎手 K50、览拓者/探索版（[1]）。战略新能源产品包括纯电宽体轻客 V919、睿行 EM60/EM80 纯电轻客、星卡 EV 纯电微卡、神骐 T30EV 纯电轻卡（[1]）。自 2024 年 11 月起品牌宣布 2025 年起只投放全新新能源产品，未来三年推出 6 款以上新能源车型，皮卡三年内完成 3 大系列 4 款新能源皮卡（[13]、[16]）。

品牌标识

按 2024 年 11 月发布的品牌战略，长安品牌商用车沿用传统「太阳标」承载经典燃油产品，凯程则以全新「立方标」承载数智新能源产品，体现其「数智新能源商用车科技品牌」定位（[13]）。

技术与平台

凯程的核心技术是首个原生数智新能源商用车架构 K01，为长安汽车六层架构技术在商用车领域的具象化，具备「上下解耦、软硬分离、车云一体」特点；公司在 2024 年 11 月 15 日发布会上称，与传统架构相比，基于 K01 开发的产品开发周期缩短 1 年、研发投入降低 40%、制造效率提升 30%。这是发布会口径，不是已经独立核实的结果（[13]）。该架构面向行业开放共享，通过整车研发销售、平台与技术输出、生态服务三类合作（[13]）。凯程复用长安天衡智能底盘（V919 全系标配线控制动 IBCU）与商用金钟罩电池平台，支持 2C/4C 快充（[13]）。皮卡方面，长安表示将继续与 Stellantis 深化皮卡平台合作（[7]）。

智慧物流生态

自 2022 年发布「光合森林计划」以来，凯程将业务从整车销售延伸至物流生态，依托「长安凯程智慧管家」平台打造「聪明的车+智慧的云」，并开发无人物流车（Robovan/Robotruck 平台），计划 2026 年小批量投产（[13]）。凯程另称正深化与宁德时代、京东等合作，构建新能源商用车智能化运营体系（[12]）。

产能与制造

作为长安汽车商用车板块，凯程的生产纳入长安整车制造体系，品牌层面未单独披露工厂清单与产能，故本文不列示（[15]）。凯程共享长安「六国十地」全球研发布局，其研发体系覆盖全球 30 多个国家和地区、1.8 万余人研发团队（[7]）。

国内市场地位

凯程称其新能源微客「持续斩获细分市场销量冠军」，2025 年曾连续 6 个月位居该细分市场冠军（[12]、[17]）。凯程自称为长安商用车业务的战略承载者，深耕商用车领域 41 年、累计销量超 1000 万辆（[11]）。

海外业务

出口是增长引擎：2024 年出口 6.67 万辆（+106.2%）、2025 年 8.7 万辆（+31.1%）（[20]、[12]）。2025 年初凯程表示未来三年将向海外投放不少于四款全新产品（[15]）。

近期业绩

最近一个完整财年为 2025 财年（26.1 万辆、营收 136 亿元）。2025 年上半年营收 65.99 亿元（同比 +5.8%）见新华网客户端（[17]）；同期销量 14.03 万辆（同比 +10.1%）见中国经济网（[11]）。2026 年一季度，媒体披露其海外销量 2.7 万辆（同比 +29%）、3 月皮卡月销破万（环比 +94%）（[12]）。因凯程未单独上市，其 2026 年上半年业绩未单独披露，归入长安汽车 2026 年半年度报告合并范围（[3]）。

重庆长安凯程汽车科技有限公司于 2020 年 7 月 27 日成立，注册资本 10 亿元，成立时由长安汽车持股 83.64%、重庆临空开发投资集团持股 16.36%，是混合所有制改革下的独立法人（[14]）。2024 年 12 月增资完成后，股权结构为：长安汽车 65.90%、临空投 11.49%、建信投资 5.01%、恒泰定州基金 4.29%、辰致汽车科技集团有限公司（原中国长安汽车集团有限公司）2.86%、建源银河 2.15%、南方资产 2.15%、兵器新动能 2.15%、伯特利 1.43%、长渝基金 1.14%、南方新域基金 0.72%、粤凯智动基金 0.72%；注册资本由 10 亿元增至 14.237145 亿元，增资总额 20.8 亿元（[18]）。2024 年 12 月公告里，这 2.86% 的认缴股东名称是中国长安汽车集团有限公司。长安汽车 2025 年年报写明，该公司于 2025 年 6 月更名为辰致汽车科技集团有限公司，更名后公司性质、所有权属及与长安汽车的控制关系不变；2025 年 7 月分立新设的中国长安汽车集团有限公司是另一主体，为上市公司的间接控股股东（[26]）。未见把这 2.86% 凯程股权划给新集团的公告，因此按更名后的主体列示。凯程品牌本身无单独认定的实际控制人，其控制链归于母公司长安汽车（实际控制人为国务院国资委）（[3]）。

竞争格局

我们的判断：竞争范围。凯程自身产品处于中国轻型商用车市场，覆盖皮卡、轻卡/微卡、轻客/微客与商用 MPV（[1]）。本文所用出处没有点名一组对标企业，因此不列具体竞争者。

我们的判断：优势。一是共享长安研发与供应链：K01 架构复用长安乘用车六层架构技术，V919 全系标配天衡智能底盘线控制动，皮卡与 Stellantis 深化平台合作（[13]、[7]）。二是出口动能强：2024 年出口 +106.2%、2025 年 +31.1%（[20]、[12]）。三是新能源增速快于整体：2025 年新能源 +80.5% 高于整体 +17.8%，新能源微客多次位居细分市场销量冠军（[12]、[19]）。四是在增程皮卡上先发，以长安猎手卡位（[7]）。

我们的判断：短板。新能源销量占比较小（2025 年为 26.1 万辆中的 4.9 万辆、约占 19%），营收基盘仍是燃油微卡与微客，面临排放法规收紧与价格竞争（[12]、[15]）。品牌未单独上市、不披露利润，盈利状况无法独立观察；媒体转引挂牌信息显示其近年尚未盈利（[3]、[21]）。

未来展望

公司公开规划。2025 年 8 月成都车展，凯程提出 2030 年目标：新能源渗透率超 70%、无人物流车引领行业、皮卡年销量突破 20 万辆、海外销量占比超 40%，力争进入全球轻型商用车品牌前五（[17]）。更早口径不同：2025 年 1 月，时任凯程总经理兰祥文称「预计 2030 年海外销量将达到总销量的 50%，即 60 万辆」——按字面意为海外 60 万辆、总销量约 120 万辆（[15]）；同期 Gasgoo 经上海有色网转载写为「到 2030 年总销量 60 万辆，海外占 50%」（[16]），长江商报亦转引「2030 年总销量 60 万辆、海外占比 50%」（[20]）。这些早期口径彼此不完全一致，且 2025 年 8 月成都车展口径未再沿用 60 万辆总销量数字；本文按时间分别标注，不强行统一。

其他披露规划包括：自 2025 年起只投放全新新能源产品、未来三年推出 6 款以上新能源车型，皮卡三年内完成 3 大系列 4 款新能源皮卡，2026 年起小批量投产无人物流产品（[13]、[16]）。融资口径并列如下。长江商报 2025 年 1 月 21 日报道，凯程将持续推进股权融资和 IPO 上市（[20]）。中国经济网 2025 年 7 月 30 日报道，在完成超 20 亿元 A 轮之后，「目前正启动 B 轮融资」，并称新集团成立将加速独立上市进程（[11]）。截至 2026-10-29，未见 B 轮完成或 IPO 申报的 A 级公司公告。2025 年 7 月这一口径是能找到的最新表述，其后没有新的官方进展。以上是已披露的计划和媒体报道，不是已完成的结果。

我们的判断。无论取上述哪种 2030 口径，目标都意味着在 2025 年 26.1 万辆基础上大幅扩张，高度依赖新能源转型（70% 渗透率）与海外扩张（40% 以上占比）同步兑现。央企身份、已完成的 A 轮融资与 K01 平台化为凯程提供了资金与技术基础，但其竞争地位最终取决于能否把规模与皮卡/轻客新能源放量转化为可观察的盈利——目前凯程并不单独披露利润，媒体转引挂牌信息显示其近年尚未盈利（[8]、[3]、[21]）。2026 年 4 月王孝飞接任董事长后战略执行能否延续，也是观察其转型进度的关键变量（[8]）。以上为我们的分析，不构成对财务结果的预测。

风险

  • 转型执行。凯程销量仍以燃油微卡、微客为主，其「2025 年起只投放新能源产品」的转型基数较小（2025 年新能源仅 4.9 万辆、占 26.1 万辆的比例不高）（[12]）。
  • 海外集中与贸易壁垒。出口（2025 年 8.7 万辆）依赖新兴市场需求，面临关税与区域政策风险（[12]）。
  • 竞争与价格压力。轻型商用车市场价格竞争激烈、排放法规趋严，凯程自身战略沟通中亦提及（[15]）。
  • 信息不透明。作为未上市子公司，凯程不单独披露经审计财报，盈利状况无法独立核实（[3]）。
  • 亏损与负债。媒体转引挂牌信息显示其近年净利润为亏损，截至 2024 年三季度末资产负债率达 90.74%（[21]）。
  • 新能源商用车技术难点。凯程战略沟通中提到，新能源商用车仍需攻克电池寿命、重载工况稳定性、充电配套不完善等难题（[15]）。
  • 融资与上市不确定性。媒体报道的后续股权融资与 IPO 计划仍在推进中，不保证完成（[20]）。
