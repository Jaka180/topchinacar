Overview

FAW Jiefang Group Co., Ltd. (000800.SZ) is the listed commercial-vehicle arm of China FAW, headquartered in Changchun, Jilin. It develops, manufactures and sells heavy, medium and light trucks and buses, together with core assemblies and parts such as engines, gearboxes and axles [2]. Its predecessor FAW Car Co., Ltd. was founded in 1997 and listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange the same year; in 2020 a major asset restructuring injected the Jiefang commercial-vehicle business and the company was renamed FAW Jiefang [1].

The Jiefang brand traces to the first Jiefang CA10 truck of 1956, the first domestically produced vehicle of the People's Republic of China [12]. In 2025 the company sold 280,000 vehicles (up 11.5%), including 254,743 medium/heavy trucks and about 45,000 new-energy vehicles, and exported about 60,000 units; it reported revenue of RMB62.678 billion and net profit attributable to shareholders of RMB724.5 million [1][5].

The company is state-owned: its controlling shareholder is China FAW Co., Ltd. (holding 62.19%), with FAW Bestune Automobile Co., Ltd. holding a further 15.94% as a concert party, and the ultimate controller is the State Council SASAC [2]. It positions itself as the leader in China's medium/heavy truck industry and aims to become a world-class provider of green and intelligent transport solutions [5]. Since the First Automobile Works broke ground in 1953, the Jiefang brand has produced over nine million vehicles cumulatively, making it one of China's longest-established commercial-vehicle brands [17].

Current operations

Business structure

Commercial vehicles are the core business. In FY2025 commercial-vehicle revenue was RMB58.087 billion, accounting for 92.68% of operating revenue, with spare parts and other revenue of RMB4.591 billion (7.32%) [9]. The company sells tractors, cargo trucks, dump trucks, special vehicles and buses, and makes its own engines, gearboxes and axles, which are mainly produced in-house; it also runs the Jiefang Lvdong aftermarket platform for whole-lifecycle vehicle services and treats power-generation equipment as a new growth engine [5] [2].

By segment, in 2025 the company sold 254,743 medium/heavy trucks (up 19.02%), 25,109 light trucks (down 31.14%) and 148 buses (down 74.44%); the declines in light trucks and buses were attributed to industry competition and product structure [1].

Sales trajectory

Whole-vehicle sales have recovered from the 2022 industry trough: 241,700 units in 2023 (up 42.11%), 251,100 units in 2024 (up 3.90%) and 280,000 units in 2025 (up 11.52%). Medium/heavy truck sales were 205,200 units in 2023 and 214,000 in 2024 before rising to 254,743 in 2025. Overseas sales grew from 45,000 units in 2023 to 57,000 in 2024 and about 60,000 in 2025, while new-energy sales rose from 7,200 units in 2023 to 15,900 in 2024 and about 45,000 in 2025 [10][16].

Manufacturing and capacity

The company has five whole-vehicle production bases in Changchun, Qingdao, Guanghan, Liuzhou and Foshan, with a combined capacity of 418,000 units/year, of which Changchun 153,000, Qingdao 200,000, Guanghan 40,000, Liuzhou 20,000 and Foshan 5,000 [2]. In 2024 the J7 intelligent factory was rated among the first batch of MIIT excellent-level intelligent factories, the 6DV engine super-factory was completed, and the Qingdao commercial-vehicle R&D base was put into use; the Wuxi R&D base was completed in September 2025 and the Foshan branch plant started production in December 2025 [6][18].

Technology and platforms

In new energy the company follows the Lantu strategy with battery-electric, hybrid and fuel-cell routes. In December 2025 it released the Lantu 3.0 platform with four base models and 165 series models, and four NG heavy-duty engines (6SV3, 6SX1 PRO, 6SM7, 6SM6) [8]. In 2024 it launched the J7 Chuangling edition and industry-first hydrogen-engine and liquid-hydrogen fuel-cell tractors [6]. In intelligent driving it has mass-produced highway L2 assisted driving and commercialised L4 autonomous driving in ports, mines and sanitation scenarios [10]. In 2025 it rolled out 139 whole-vehicle models and self-developed its own motor, electronic-control and electric-axle assemblies [18]. In 2025 R&D investment was RMB2.582 billion with 3,397 R&D staff (17.77% of employees) [1].

Domestic position

In 2024 the company's medium/heavy truck terminal share was 26.60%, first in China for nine consecutive years; in 2025 its domestic medium/heavy truck terminal share was 22.6%, still first, with tractor, cargo and NG truck terminal shares of 23%, 28.9% and 27.8% respectively [10][18]. The Jiefang brand value exceeded RMB145 billion in 2025, ranking first in the commercial-vehicle industry for 14 consecutive years, with domestic medium/heavy truck sales first for 10 consecutive years and tractor sales first for 19 consecutive years [17].

Powertrain and innovation

In powertrain the company covers diesel, gas and electric across the board: the GS280 and ER260 intelligent power-domain products, the 6DV1 engine and the T-shift gearbox are in production, along with self-developed electric drive axles and electric gearboxes [18]. Its innovation index has ranked first in the commercial-vehicle industry for eight consecutive years, and it has self-developed fuel-cell and ammonia-hydrogen engines; its CA10TAX130S ten-speed gearbox was named among the world's top ten gearboxes [18].

Overseas business

Exports reached about 60,000 units in 2025 (up 5.2%), a roughly tenfold rise over six years. Products are exported to more than 100 countries, with 23 KD plants overseas, 169 sales outlets and 402 service outlets [5][18]. Under the SPRINT 2030 strategy it released five overseas sub-brands (CORTRON, DORTRON, LITRON, PITRON and BOTRON) in December 2025, opened a Saudi Arabia subsidiary, and is building a 200,000-unit overseas operating capability through eight overseas subsidiaries [18].

Latest results (H1 2026)

In H1 2026 the company reported revenue of RMB37.571 billion (up 33.81% YoY) and net profit attributable to shareholders of RMB306 million (up 1,459.16% YoY); its non-recurring-adjusted net profit turned positive at RMB33.17 million from a loss a year earlier [3].

Founders and leadership

Founder

As a state-owned enterprise, FAW Jiefang has no entrepreneur-founder in the private-company sense. The Jiefang brand originates from the First Automobile Works built by the state in the 1950s, which produced the first Jiefang truck in 1956 [12].

Current management

  • Li Sheng (李胜), chairman and party secretary. Born 1976; holds a doctorate in engineering and is a professorate-level senior engineer. Per the board-election announcement, he previously served in a series of R&D and management roles at the commercial-vehicle development institute and the Qingdao vehicle business unit, then as deputy general manager of the company and general manager of the Qingdao vehicle business unit, before becoming general manager of the company. He was appointed general manager on 31 October 2023, elected chairman on 26 December 2024, and re-elected chairman of the 11th board on 29 June 2026 [7][15].
  • Yu Changxin (于长信), general manager, chief financial officer and general counsel. Born 1972; holds a bachelor's degree in engineering and is a senior economist. He previously served as deputy head of the marketing headquarters, general manager of the commercial-vehicle overseas marketing department, and deputy general manager then general manager of China FAW Group Import and Export Co., Ltd., before becoming executive deputy general manager of the company. He was appointed general manager on 26 December 2024 and additionally appointed chief financial officer and general counsel on 29 June 2026 [7][15].

Ownership and governance

The controlling shareholder China FAW Co., Ltd. held 62.19% (3,060,649,901 shares) at end-2025, and FAW Bestune Automobile Co., Ltd., its subsidiary, held 15.94% (784,500,000 shares) as a concert party; the ultimate controller is the State Council SASAC [2]. The 11th board elected on 29 June 2026 has nine directors: five non-independent (Li Sheng, Yu Changxin, Chen Hua, Deng Weigong, Jiao Youlin), three independent (Zhao Fuquan, Shen Jinjun, Wang Xu) and one employee-representative director (Wang Hao) [15].

Competition

Our assessment. Jiefang competes mainly with Sinotruk, Dongfeng Commercial Vehicle, Shaanxi Automobile (Shaanxi Heavy Truck) and Foton in medium/heavy trucks. Its medium/heavy-truck terminal share of 26.60% in 2024 and first place in 2025 (22.6% domestic terminal share) support treating it as the domestic medium/heavy-truck leader [10][18]. At the same time, Sinotruk's heavy-truck exports of about 153,000 units in 2025 were well above Jiefang's roughly 60,000 units, so the two companies' leadership positions differ by region [5].

Our assessment. Jiefang's vertical integration — self-made engines, gearboxes, axles and a complete chain from raw blanks to whole vehicles — supports product and cost competitiveness, but its company-wide gross margin was only 7.05% in 2025 and net margin 1.26%, so profitability depends heavily on volume and non-recurring items such as government grants [9]. In H1 2026 its revenue ranked second and net profit fifth among eight listed commercial-vehicle peers, per a media summary [11].

Our assessment. In light trucks Jiefang entered the segment relatively late, later than rivals such as Foton, Dongfeng and JMC [12]. In new energy, its roughly 45,000 units in 2025 mark three consecutive years of doubling but remain small against its diesel base, while rivals are also expanding in new energy and overseas markets, so its domestic leadership may not translate directly into the new-energy and export races [5].

Our assessment. In 2025 the commercial-vehicle industry saw intensifying competition, with industry prices down about 5%–8% and weak freight rates squeezing customer profits; Jiefang chose value marketing over aggressive discounting, accepting near-term pressure to protect product structure and quality over the longer term [17].

Outlook

Company targets and plans. The company set a 2026 sales target of 320,000 vehicles (up 14%), including 75,000 new-energy vehicles and 80,000 for overseas [17]. Under its SPRINT 2030 internationalisation strategy it aims for global sales above 500,000 units by 2030, including 180,000 overseas [13]. Its new-energy roadmap puts battery electric at the core with hybrid and fuel-cell support, and it plans to roll out 165 Lantu 3.0 models in 2026 [8]. Chairman Li Sheng stated the goal of reaching, by 2030, brand value above RMB180 billion, revenue above RMB100 billion and market value above RMB100 billion — the three RMB100-billion targets [17]. In addition, the company joined 25 banks and financial institutions to launch a RMB100-billion financial pool under a 1+N+X industry-finance model, providing capital support for technology development, green transition and overseas expansion [8]. These are company plans rather than completed results.

Our analysis. The 2026 target of 320,000 units compares with 280,000 sold in 2025, implying roughly 14% growth; achieving it depends on continued new-energy expansion and overseas execution [17]. H1 2026 revenue grew 33.81% year on year, but attributable net profit of RMB306 million remains small relative to revenue, so whether volume growth converts into profitability will depend on product mix, new-energy margins and cost control. At the industry level, China's commercial-vehicle market is expected to stay roughly flat at about 4.25 million units in 2026, with heavy trucks around 800,000 and new-energy heavy-truck penetration potentially reaching 35%–40%, leaving overseas and new energy as the main incremental drivers [18] [3].

Risks

  • Cyclical demand. The commercial-vehicle industry is cyclical and sensitive to freight demand, fuel prices and replacement policies; 2024 revenue fell 8.93% year on year amid weak industry demand [4].
  • Thin margins. Company-wide gross margin was 7.05% in 2025 and net margin 1.26%; attributable net profit includes large non-recurring items such as government grants [9].
  • New-energy profitability. New-energy sales grew 184.3% in 2025 but the overall margin remains low; converting NEV volume into profit is not yet proven [5].
  • Overseas and foreign-exchange exposure. Exports reached about 60,000 units in 2025 and the company is expanding overseas subsidiaries; overseas operations expose it to demand, policy and currency risks [5].
  • Leverage and working capital. End-2025 gearing was 63.01% and inventory rose 32.6% year on year, increasing working-capital pressure [9].
  • Overseas investment integration. The company plans to set up wholly-owned subsidiaries in eight countries and build KD plants, an expansion that carries execution, localisation and geopolitical risk [13].
  • Competition and price pressure. The commercial-vehicle market is fiercely competitive with price erosion of about 5%–8% in 2025 and weak freight rates weighing on customer demand, pressuring sales volume and per-unit profitability [17].
  • Policy and regulation. The industry saw frequent policy moves in 2025 on market access, vehicle replacement and new-energy support, which materially affect the company's sales and product mix [2].

概览

一汽解放集团股份有限公司（000800.SZ）是中国一汽旗下的商用车上市公司，总部位于吉林省长春市，研发、制造并销售重型、中型、轻型卡车与客车，以及发动机、变速箱、车桥等核心总成与零部件 [2]。其前身一汽轿车股份有限公司成立于 1997 年并于同年登陆深交所；2020 年通过重大资产重组注入解放商用车业务，公司更名为一汽解放 [1]。

解放品牌源自 1956 年新中国第一辆汽车——解放牌 CA10 卡车 [12]。2025 年公司销售整车 28 万辆（同比 +11.5%），其中中重卡 254,743 辆、新能源约 4.5 万辆，出口约 6 万辆；实现营业收入 626.78 亿元、归母净利润 7.245 亿元 [1][5]。

公司为国有企业：控股股东为中国第一汽车股份有限公司（持股 62.19%），一致行动人一汽奔腾汽车股份有限公司持股 15.94%，实际控制人为国务院国资委 [2]。公司定位为中国中重卡行业龙头，致力于成为「中国第一、世界一流」的绿色智能交通运输解决方案提供者 [5]。从 1953 年第一汽车制造厂奠基至今，解放品牌累计产销汽车 900 余万辆，是中国历史最悠久的商用车品牌之一 [17]。

历史沿革

  1. 1956-07-13

    第一辆解放牌 CA10 卡车在第一汽车制造厂下线，成为新中国第一辆自主生产的汽车。[12]

  2. 1997-06-10

    前身一汽轿车股份有限公司成立，并于 1997 年 6 月 18 日在深交所上市。[1][11]

  3. 2003-01

    一汽对中重型卡车业务进行重组，成立一汽解放汽车有限公司（解放有限），成为主营运营主体。[12]

  4. 2011-06

    控股股东中国一汽完成主业重组改制，成立中国第一汽车股份有限公司。[1]

  5. 2018

    第七代重卡 J7 上市，并实现第 700 万辆解放卡车下线。[12]

  6. 2020-05

    一汽轿车完成重大资产重组，置出乘用车业务、置入解放商用车业务，并更名为一汽解放集团股份有限公司。[1][12]

  7. 2021-09

    一汽解放发布「15333」新能源战略，布局纯电、混动、燃料电池三条技术路线。[12][18]

  8. 2023-10-31

    董事会选举吴碧磊为董事长、聘任李胜为总经理，胡汉杰卸任董事长。[14]

  9. 2024-08

    设立一汽解放集团国际汽车有限公司（解放国际），作为海外运营主体。[4][10]

  10. 2024-12

    完成同一控制下企业合并，以现金 3.28 亿元收购一汽非洲投资有限公司 55% 股权，并完成向特定对象发行 A 股 298,507,462 股。[4]

  11. 2024-12-26

    董事会选举李胜为董事长，聘任于长信为总经理；吴碧磊辞去董事长职务。[7]

  12. 2025-09

    无锡研发基地建成投用，成为公司在长三角的创新枢纽。[18]

  13. 2025-12

    在全球合作伙伴大会上发布蓝途 3.0 新能源平台、与宁德时代和特来电合作的鲸E租租赁品牌、四款 NG 重型发动机及全新海外品牌架构，并宣布成立沙特子公司。[8][13]

  14. 2026-06-29

    完成第十一届董事会换届，李胜当选董事长，于长信获聘总经理兼财务负责人、总法律顾问。[15]

现状

业务结构

商用车是核心业务。2025 财年商用车收入 580.87 亿元，占营业收入的 92.68%；备品及其他收入 45.91 亿元，占 7.32% [9]。公司销售牵引车、载货车、自卸车、专用车与客车，并自产发动机、变速箱、车桥等核心总成，核心总成以自主生产为主；此外，公司依托后市场平台「解放绿动」提供车辆全生命周期服务，并将发电动力业务作为新的增长极 [5] [2]。

按车型看，2025 年公司销售中重型卡车 254,743 辆（同比 +19.02%）、轻型卡车 25,109 辆（-31.14%）、客车 148 辆（-74.44%）；轻卡与客车下滑受行业竞争与产品结构影响 [1]。

历年销量

整车销量自 2022 年行业低谷以来持续回升：2023 年 24.17 万辆（同比 +42.11%）、2024 年 25.11 万辆（+3.90%）、2025 年 28 万辆（+11.52%）。中重卡销量由 2023 年 20.52 万辆、2024 年 21.4 万辆升至 2025 年 25.47 万辆；海外销量由 2023 年 4.5 万辆增至 2024 年 5.7 万辆、2025 年约 6 万辆；新能源销量由 2023 年 0.72 万辆、2024 年 1.59 万辆增至 2025 年约 4.5 万辆 [10][16]。

制造与产能

公司布局长春、青岛、广汉、柳州、佛山五大整车生产基地，总产能 41.8 万辆/年，其中长春 15.3 万辆、青岛 20 万辆、广汉 4 万辆、柳州 2 万辆、佛山 0.5 万辆 [2]。2024 年 J7 智能工厂获评工信部首批「卓越级智能工厂」，6DV 发动机超级工厂建成投产，青岛商用车研发基地落成；2025 年 9 月无锡研发基地建成投用，12 月佛山分公司新工厂投产 [6][18]。

技术与平台

新能源方面，公司按「蓝途」战略布局纯电、混动、燃料电池三条路线。2025 年 12 月发布蓝途 3.0 平台（含 4 款基本型产品和 165 款系列化产品）及 6SV3、6SX1 PRO、6SM7、6SM6 四款 NG 重型发动机 [8]。2024 年发布 J7 创领版，行业首发「星熠」氢气发动机牵引车与液氢燃电牵引车 [6]。智能驾驶方面，公司已实现高速 L2 辅助驾驶量产，并在港口、矿区、环卫场景实现 L4 无人驾驶商业化运营 [10]。2025 年公司投放 139 款整车产品，并实现电机、电控、电驱桥等「大三电」自主研发制造 [18]。2025 年公司研发投入 25.82 亿元，研发人员 3,397 人（占员工 17.77%） [1]。

国内市场地位

2024 年公司中重卡终端份额 26.60%，连续九年国内第一；2025 年国内中重卡终端份额 22.6%，持续位居行业首位，牵引车、载货车、NG 车终端份额分别达 23%、28.9%、27.8% [10][18]。2025 年解放品牌价值突破 1450 亿元、连续十四年居商用车行业第一，国内中重卡销量连续十年第一、牵引车连续十九年第一 [17]。

动力总成与创新

动力总成方面，公司实现「油气电」全域布局，GS280、ER260 等智慧动力域产品及 6DV1 发动机、T-shift 变速箱量产，自主电驱桥、电驱箱全面量产 [18]。公司创新指数连续八年位居商用车行业第一，自主研发燃电发动机、氨氢融合发动机，CA10TAX130S 10 挡变速器获评第九届「世界十佳变速器」 [18]。

海外业务

2025 年出口约 6 万辆（同比 +5.2%），实现六年十倍跃迁；产品出口全球 100 多个国家，在海外建有 23 座 KD 工厂、169 家销售网络和 402 家服务网络 [5][18]。在 SPRINT 2030 战略下，公司 2025 年 12 月发布 CORTRON、DORTRON、LITRON、PITRON、BOTRON 五大海外子品牌，落地沙特子公司，并通过 8 个海外子公司加快构建 20 万辆级海外经营能力 [18]。

最新业绩（2026 年上半年）

2026 年上半年公司实现营业收入 375.71 亿元（同比 +33.81%），归母净利润 3.06 亿元（同比 +1459.16%），扣非净利润 3317 万元、由上年同期亏损转为盈利 [3]。

创始人与管理层

创始人

一汽解放为国有企业，无民营意义上的单一创始人。解放品牌源自 20 世纪 50 年代国家建设的第一汽车制造厂，1956 年造出第一辆解放卡车 [12]。

现任核心管理层

  • 李胜，董事长、党委书记。1976 年出生，研究生学历、工学博士学位，正高级工程师。据换届选举公告简历，其历任解放有限商用车开发院院长助理、青岛整车事业部副总经理、商用车开发院副院长、青岛整车事业部常务副总经理等职，后任公司副总经理兼青岛整车事业部总经理，再任公司总经理、党委副书记。2023 年 10 月 31 日获聘总经理，2024 年 12 月 26 日当选董事长，2026 年 6 月 29 日当选第十一届董事会董事长 [7][15]。
  • 于长信，总经理、财务负责人、总法律顾问。1972 年出生，大学学历、工学学士，正高级经济师。据换届选举公告简历，其历任解放有限营销总部副部长、商用车海外营销部总经理、中国第一汽车集团进出口有限公司副总经理、总经理等职，后任公司常务副总经理。2024 年 12 月 26 日获聘总经理，2026 年 6 月 29 日续任并兼任财务负责人、总法律顾问 [7][15]。

竞争格局

我们的判断。一汽解放主要与中国重汽、东风商用车、陕汽（陕重汽）、福田在中重卡市场竞争。2024 年中重卡终端份额 26.60%、2025 年国内中重卡终端份额 22.6% 且位居行业首位，是将其视为国内中重卡龙头的事实基础 [10][18]。与此同时，2025 年中国重汽重卡出口约 15.3 万辆，明显高于一汽解放的约 6 万辆，因此两家的领先地位存在地域差异 [5]。

我们的判断。一汽解放拥有从毛坯原材料到核心零部件、从关键大总成到整车的完整制造体系，自产发动机、变速箱、车桥支撑了产品与成本竞争力，但 2025 年公司整体毛利率仅 7.05%、净利率 1.26%，盈利对销量规模与政府补助等非经常性损益的依赖较高 [9]。据媒体报道，2026 年上半年其营收在八家上市商用车同行中排名第二、净利润排名第五 [11]。

我们的判断。在轻卡领域，一汽解放拓展轻型车较晚，晚于福田、东风、江铃等对手 [12]。新能源方面，2025 年约 4.5 万辆虽连续三年翻倍增长，但相对于其柴油车基本盘仍偏小，而中国重汽、东风、福田均在加码新能源与海外市场，公司国内的领先地位未必能直接平移到新能源与出口竞赛中 [5]。

我们的判断。2025 年商用车行业竞争加剧，行业价格降幅约 5%–8%，运价低迷挤压用户盈利空间；一汽解放选择坚持价值营销而非大幅降价，短期承受一定压力，但有助于中长期产品结构与质量提升 [17]。

未来展望

公司目标与规划。公司确定 2026 年整车销量目标 32 万辆（同比 +14%），其中新能源 7.5 万辆、海外 8 万辆 [17]；并按「SPRINT 2030」国际化战略提出到 2030 年全球销量超 50 万辆、其中海外 18 万辆 [13]。新能源以纯电动为核心、混动与燃料电池为支撑，计划 2026 年投放 165 款蓝途 3.0 车型 [8]。董事长李胜提出，力争到 2030 年实现品牌价值超 1800 亿元、营收规模超 1000 亿元、市值超 1000 亿元的「三个千亿级」目标 [17]。此外，公司联合 25 家银行及金融机构发布「千亿金融池」，构建「1+N+X」产业融合模式，为技术攻关、绿色转型与海外拓展提供资本支持 [8]。以上为公司规划，并非已经实现的结果。

我们的分析。2026 年 32 万辆目标相较 2025 年 28 万辆需约 14% 的增长，实现与否取决于新能源放量与海外执行 [17]。2026 年上半年营收同比增长 33.81%，但归母净利润 3.06 亿元相对于营收规模仍偏小，销量增长能否转化为盈利，取决于产品结构、新能源毛利与成本控制。从行业看，2026 年中国商用车市场预计约 425 万辆、与 2025 年基本持平，其中重卡约 80 万辆，新能源重卡渗透率有望达 35%–40%，行业增量主要来自海外与新能源 [18] [3]。

风险

  • 需求周期性。商用车行业具有周期性，受货运需求、油价与置换政策影响；2024 年行业需求不振，公司营收同比下降 8.93% [4]。
  • 盈利偏薄。2025 年公司整体毛利率 7.05%、净利率 1.26%；归母净利润中含政府补助等较大规模非经常性损益 [9]。
  • 新能源盈利。2025 年新能源销量同比增长 184.3%，但整体毛利仍低，新能源放量转化为盈利尚未得到验证 [5]。
  • 海外与汇率敞口。2025 年出口约 6 万辆并加速海外子公司布局，海外经营使其面临需求、政策与汇率风险 [5]。
  • 杠杆与营运资金。2025 年末资产负债率 63.01%，存货同比增长 32.6%，营运资金压力上升 [9]。
  • 海外投资整合。公司计划在八个国家设立全资子公司并建设 KD 工厂，扩张伴随执行、本地化与地缘政治风险 [13]。
  • 政策与法规。2025 年行业在市场准入、以旧换新、新能源扶持等领域密集出台政策，公司销量与产品结构受政策节奏影响较大 [2]。
  • 竞争与价格压力。商用车行业竞争激烈、价格内卷，2025 年行业价格降幅约 5%–8%，运价低迷影响用户购车意愿，对公司销量与单车盈利形成压力 [17]。
