Overview

Haval is the core SUV brand of Great Wall Motor Company Limited, positioned as a global SUV specialist. The name first appeared on the Great Wall Haval CUV, which rolled off the line in March 2005 and went on full sale in June 2005; on 29 March 2013 Haval became an independent brand with its own logo, entering a dual-brand era alongside the Great Wall brand.[2][26][9] Haval is one of Great Wall Motor's vehicle brands, alongside WEY, Tank, ORA, Great Wall Pickup, Great Wall Soul and Great Wall Commercial Vehicle, and is the largest of them by passenger-vehicle volume.[1]

In 2025 Haval sold 761,487 vehicles (+7.69% year on year), and by year-end its cumulative global users surpassed 10 million, per the Great Wall Motor 2025 annual report.[1] Per the June 2026 production and sales brief, Haval sold 327,290 vehicles in the first half of 2026 (+1.82% year on year), with June sales of 60,288 vehicles (-3.38% year on year).[3] Per the September 2026 brief, Haval sold 66,558 vehicles in September 2026 (-18.76% year on year) and 515,080 vehicles in January-September 2026 (-2.54% year on year).[22][8]

Per the 2025 annual report, Haval rests on three core technologies, the Hi4 intelligent electric-hybrid four-wheel-drive, the Coffee OS 3 smart cockpit and the Coffee Pilot Plus advanced driving-assistance system, and covers three segments: mainstream urban, universal off-road and off-road.[1] From 2026 some models have adopted the GWM mother-brand name (the GWM H10 and others), while the brand keeps its global-SUV-specialist positioning.[4][13]

The brand's best-known model is the H6, launched in August 2011. Per China Economic Net, the H6 led China's SUV market in monthly sales for 108 consecutive months, was overtaken by the BYD Song and Tesla Model Y in 2022 and ranked sixth in 2023, and had surpassed 4 million cumulative global sales by the June 2024 new-generation launch.[12][7] The 2025 annual report records 2025 sales of 89,304 vehicles for the Raptor (+33.84%) and 180,201 for the Dargo series (+18.13%).[1]

Current operations

Model line-up

As of the research cut-off date, the Haval China website lists a new-energy line-up (GWM H10, GWM Raptor PLUS, Dargo PLUS PHEV, Raptor 2026, second-generation Xiaolong MAX) and a fuel/HEV line-up (GWM Dargo, second-generation H9 2026, H6L, Dargo PLUS, Raptor fuel, new H5, new-generation H6, H6 Classic 2026).[4] The new-generation H6 launched on 19 June 2024 at RMB 117,900-143,900 (limited-time RMB 103,900-129,900), and the H6L launched on 18 November 2025 at a suggested retail price from RMB 117,900 with a limited-time price from RMB 103,900.[12][17] The Raptor launched from RMB 165,800 in October 2023, while the GWM H10, launched 5 August 2026 at RMB 209,800-231,800, is the top of the range.[21][15]

Positioning evolution and GWM mother-brand naming

The brand evolved from a mainstream SUV specialist into a three-segment structure. Per the 2025 annual report, Haval covers mainstream urban, universal off-road and off-road segments with a full-category, full-scenario, multi-powertrain matrix, and upholds the brand values of focus, technology, reassurance and an enterprising spirit.[1] Media reported that after Lu Wenbin took charge of Haval in May 2026 he started a naming-adjustment plan, and that the H10, whose name was chosen by a public vote of about 700,000 netizens, is the first GWM model to use the mother-brand name while its front still carries the HAVAL badge.[13] Historically Haval split into Red Label and Blue Label sub-brands from 2015, and the black badge replaced the red-blue scheme in March 2019.[23]

Product cadence

GWM's official history records a steady cadence of Haval launches: the second-generation H9 (25 September 2024), the second-generation Xiaolong MAX (16 April 2025), the Dargo 2026 (17 July 2025), the H6L (18 November 2025), the Raptor PLUS (15 May 2026) and the H10 (5 August 2026).[2] The Dargo (September 2020) opened the boxy universal off-road segment and the Raptor (October 2023) extended it into plug-in-hybrid new-energy off-road.[19][21]

Technology and platforms

Per the 2025 annual report, Haval is built on the Hi4 intelligent electric-hybrid four-wheel-drive (with the Hi4 2.0 upgrade), the Coffee OS 3 smart cockpit and the Coffee Pilot Plus advanced driving-assistance system, and uses the Guiyuan (GWM ONE) platform to expand its new-energy range.[1] The H10 uses the second-generation Hi4 performance hybrid and Coffee Pilot 3 with lidar as standard.[15] As of the cut-off date, the website line-up contains no pure-electric model, only fuel, HEV and plug-in-hybrid variants.[4]

Plants and capacity

Haval is produced in Great Wall Motor's plants at home and abroad. GWM's Tula plant in Russia (completed 5 June 2019) was its first full-process overseas plant, the Rayong plant in Thailand (started 9 June 2021) was its second, and the Iracemapolis plant in Brazil (completed 16 August 2025, initial capacity 50,000 vehicles a year) is its third full-process overseas base, with the Haval H6 GT as the first model off the line.[2][20] A KD plant in Pakistan began assembly in September 2022 with the third-generation Haval H6.[2]

Overseas business

Haval is Great Wall Motor's main export brand. GWM sold 453,141 vehicles overseas in 2024 (+43.39% year on year) and 506,066 in 2025 (+11.68%).[2] Russia is the largest single market; per Phoenix Auto, GWM sold about 229,800 vehicles there in 2024 (about 12% local share and nearly half of its overseas volume), while in the first half of 2025 the Russian passenger-car market shrank 27.6% and Haval's Russia sales fell to about 64,000 vehicles (-21.5%).[25] Brazil is growing, with the Iracemapolis plant targeting South America.[20]

Founders and leadership

Brand ownership and governance

Haval is not a separate listed company; it is a brand operated within Great Wall Motor. Per the 2025 annual report, Great Wall Motor's actual controller is Wei Jianjun, its founder and chairman. Wei Jianjun joined the predecessor business as general manager in 1990 and has chaired the joint-stock company since June 2001.[1][2]

Current brand head

According to media reports, Lu Wenbin was appointed Haval-ORA brand general manager of GWM's marketing centre in May 2026, overseeing both the Haval and ORA brands. Lu Wenbin was born in 1983, joined Great Wall Motor in 2004 and worked long-term in technology and R&D, having served as pickup product director, deputy general manager of the Chongqing branch and the Yongchuan production base, and Haval brand technical general manager; he became ORA brand general manager on 20 June 2025.[11][13]

Previous brand general manager

Zhao Yongpo was appointed Haval brand general manager in November 2023; GWM's chief growth officer Li Ruifeng described him as a technology veteran of over 20 years who was formerly deputy general manager of the GWM technology centre, leading vehicle platform, lower body, performance and chassis development.[10] On 8 February 2025 Zhao announced he would also manage the ORA brand, and on 21 December 2025 he was transferred to WEY (Wei brand) CEO, no longer heading Haval.[24][27] The above are all public roles and commercial history.

Competition

Our judgment: competitive scope. Based on Haval's compact-to-full-size SUV line-up, the main domestic competitors are BYD (Song series), Geely, Changan (CS75 series) and Chery, which overlap in the mainstream and boxy SUV segments. In the off-road segment it faces the Tank brand within its own group and rivals such as Chery's JETOUR and JAECOO and BYD's Fang Cheng Bao. The GWM H10, a large boxy 5/6-seat SUV launched at RMB 209,800-231,800, enters the 200,000-yuan large family SUV segment, overlapping with new-energy rivals in the same class. This is our analytical selection, not a sales ranking.[4][16][15]

Our judgment: strengths. Haval retains a large installed base (over 10 million cumulative users by end-2025), a long H6 sales record, and a differentiated boxy/off-road line-up (Dargo 180,201 and Raptor 89,304 vehicles in 2025). The 2025 annual report records the Raptor topping the three-year resale value list for plug-in hybrids, and GWM's history records the second-generation H9 taking a top-three result in the T2.1 mass-production fuel class at the 2025 Taklimakan Rally. It also benefits from Great Wall Motor's overseas plants and network, where Haval is the main export brand.[1][2]

Our judgment: weaknesses and uncertainties. The H6, once the brand's anchor, led China's SUV market for 108 consecutive months before being overtaken by the BYD Song and Tesla Model Y in 2022 and falling to sixth in 2023; the brand's annual sales remain well below the 2016 peak of about 938,000. Its electrified mix is limited and, as of the cut-off date, the line-up has no pure-electric model. Its reliance on the Russian market concentrates overseas risk, and the shift of some models to the GWM mother-brand name is still in transition.[12][16][25][13]

Outlook

Company plans. The 2025 annual report states that Haval will deepen its global footprint, accelerate the deployment of Hi4 technology and the all-scenario adaptation of intelligent driving assistance, and use the Guiyuan (GWM ONE) platform to expand the new-energy market, aiming for growth in both sales volume and value.[1] Media reported that, at the 2025 Guangzhou Auto Show, Zhao Yongpo said the brand would use its best technology to build a GWM flagship large six-seat SUV, which materialised as the GWM H10 launched in August 2026.[13][14] Media also reported that Great Wall Motor set a 2026 group sales target of at least 1.8 million vehicles (1.2 million domestic plus 600,000 overseas), versus about 1.3237 million in 2025, with Haval as the core volume brand.[13][2] These are disclosed directions and targets, not completed results.

Our judgment. The H10 and the GWM mother-brand naming give the brand a higher-price flagship and a clearer group-level identity; the H10's reported 31,826 firm orders in 24 hours indicate strong initial demand, but this is an order figure, not sustained sales.[14][15] Whether the brand can restore scale and improve its electrified mix depends on continued demand for the H10 and boxy models, execution of the naming transition, and overseas conditions such as Russia demand and trade barriers. First-half 2026 sales grew only 1.82% year on year, so a return to the 2016 peak is not yet in evidence.[3] The 5-2-1 target announced in January 2019 (2 million vehicles a year within five years) was not met: actual sales were 706,234 vehicles in 2024 and 761,487 in 2025.[9][6][1]

Risks

  • Domestic competition. China's SUV market is highly competitive and price-driven; media reported that the H6 lost its monthly sales lead after 108 months and was overtaken by the BYD Song and Tesla Model Y in 2022.[12]
  • Electrification mix. As of the cut-off date, Haval's line-up contains no pure-electric model, which weighs on its sales structure as China's new-energy penetration rises.[4]
  • H6 dependence and decline. The H6, the brand's long-time anchor, has declined from its 2016 peak of 580,683 units; a continued fall would pressure the whole brand.[16][12]
  • Overseas concentration. Haval depends heavily on the Russian market; per media reports, Russia contributed nearly half of GWM's overseas volume in 2024, and in the first half of 2025 the Russian market shrank and Haval's Russia sales fell about 21.5%, while trade barriers rose.[25]
  • No separate financial disclosure. Haval does not publish brand-level revenue or profit, so standalone profitability cannot be assessed from public filings.[1]
  • Management transition and renaming. The brand head changed from Zhao Yongpo to Lu Wenbin between December 2025 and May 2026, alongside a naming adjustment that shifts some models to the GWM mother-brand name, adding execution uncertainty.[27][11][13]

概览

哈弗（Haval）是长城汽车股份有限公司的核心 SUV 品牌，定位「全球 SUV 专家」。品牌名最早用于 2005 年 3 月下线、6 月全面上市的长城哈弗 CUV，2013 年 3 月 29 日作为独立品牌运营，拥有独立标识，进入哈弗与长城双品牌时代。[2][26][9] 哈弗是长城汽车旗下整车品牌之一，与魏牌、坦克、欧拉、长城皮卡、长城灵魂、长城商用车等并列，也是其中乘用车销量规模最大的品牌。[1]

据长城汽车 2025 年年度报告，哈弗 2025 年销售 761,487 辆（同比 +7.69%），截至年末全球累计用户突破 1000 万。[1] 据公司 2026 年 6 月产销快报，哈弗 2026 年上半年销售 327,290 辆（同比 +1.82%），其中 6 月销售 60,288 辆（同比 -3.38%）。[3] 据公司 2026 年 9 月产销快报，哈弗 2026 年 9 月销售 66,558 辆（同比 -18.76%），1-9 月累计销售 515,080 辆（同比 -2.54%）。[22][8]

据 2025 年年报，哈弗依托 Hi4 智能电混四驱、Coffee OS 3 智能座舱、Coffee Pilot Plus 高阶智驾三大核心技术，布局主流都市、泛越野、越野三大品类。[1] 2026 年起部分车型改用「长城」母品牌命名（长城 H10 等），品牌仍保持「全球 SUV 专家」定位。[4][13]

品牌最知名的车型为 2011 年 8 月上市的哈弗 H6。据中国经济网，H6 曾连续 108 个月蝉联 SUV 销量第一，2022 年被比亚迪宋、特斯拉 Model Y 超越，2023 年排名第六；至 2024 年 6 月新一代车型上市时全球累计销量已超 400 万辆。[12][7] 2025 年年报载明猛龙年销 89,304 辆（同比 +33.84%）、大狗品类 180,201 辆（同比 +18.13%）。[1]

现状

车型谱系

截至研究截止日，哈弗中国官网列示新能源车型（长城 H10、长城猛龙 PLUS、大狗 PLUS PHEV、猛龙 2026 款、二代枭龙 MAX）与燃油/HEV 车型（长城大狗、二代 H9 2026 款、H6L、大狗 PLUS、猛龙燃油版、新 H5、新一代 H6、H6 经典版 2026 款）。[4] 新一代 H6 于 2024 年 6 月 19 日上市（11.79 万-14.39 万元，限时 10.39 万-12.99 万元）；H6L 于 2025 年 11 月 18 日上市，建议零售价 11.79 万元起、限时权益价 10.39 万元起。[12][17] 猛龙 2023 年 10 月上市（16.58 万元起）；长城 H10 于 2026 年 8 月 5 日上市（20.98 万-23.18 万元），为品牌最高价位车型。[21][15]

定位演变与「长城」母品牌命名

品牌定位由主流 SUV 专家演化为三大品类结构。据 2025 年年报，哈弗布局主流都市、泛越野、越野三大品类，构建全品类、全场景、多动力产品矩阵，并践行「专注、科技、安心、进取」品牌价值。[1] 据媒体报道，吕文斌 2026 年 5 月接管哈弗后启动命名调整计划；H10 的命名由约 70 万网友投票确定，为长城旗下首款采用「长城」母品牌命名的车型，其前脸仍挂 HAVAL 标识。[13] 历史上哈弗还曾于 2015 年起拆分红标、蓝标两大子品牌，2019 年 3 月黑标取代红蓝双标。[23]

产品投放节奏

长城汽车官网发展历程记录了哈弗稳定的投放节奏：二代 H9（2024 年 9 月 25 日）、二代枭龙 MAX（2025 年 4 月 16 日）、大狗 2026 款（2025 年 7 月 17 日）、H6L（2025 年 11 月 18 日）、猛龙 PLUS（2026 年 5 月 15 日）、长城 H10（2026 年 8 月 5 日）。[2] 2020 年 9 月大狗上市开启方盒子泛越野品类，2023 年 10 月猛龙上市将这一品类延伸至插混新能源越野领域。[19][21]

技术与平台

据 2025 年年报，哈弗依托 Hi4 智能电混四驱（含 Hi4 2.0 升级）、Coffee OS 3 智能座舱、Coffee Pilot Plus 高阶智驾，并借助归元平台拓展新能源产品。[1] 长城 H10 搭载第二代 Hi4 性能版电混四驱，标配激光雷达的 Coffee Pilot 3 智驾系统。[15] 截至截止日，官网在售车型均为燃油、HEV 或插混，无纯电车型。[4]

工厂与产能

哈弗在国内外的长城汽车工厂生产。俄罗斯图拉工厂（2019 年 6 月 5 日竣工投产）为长城首个海外全工艺整车厂，泰国罗勇工厂（2021 年 6 月 9 日投产）为第二个，巴西伊拉塞马波利斯工厂（2025 年 8 月 16 日竣工投产，初期年产能 5 万辆）为第三个海外全工艺整车基地，首台下线车型为哈弗 H6 GT。[2][20] 2022 年 9 月巴基斯坦 KD 工厂投产，首台当地组装第三代哈弗 H6 下线。[2]

海外业务

哈弗是长城汽车海外主力品牌。长城汽车 2024 年海外销售 453,141 辆（同比 +43.39%）、2025 年海外销售 506,066 辆（同比 +11.68%）。[2] 俄罗斯是最大单一市场；据凤凰网汽车，长城 2024 年在俄销量约 22.98 万辆（当地份额约 12%、贡献海外销量近半），2025 年上半年俄罗斯乘用车市场萎缩 27.6%、哈弗在俄销量降至约 6.4 万辆（同比 -21.5%）。[25] 巴西市场增长较快，伊拉塞马波利斯工厂面向整个南美市场。[20]

哈弗并非单独上市公司，而是长城汽车旗下品牌。据 2025 年年报，长城汽车实际控制人为创始人、董事长魏建军；魏建军 1990 年加入公司前身并担任总经理，2001 年 6 月起任股份公司董事长。[1][2]

现任品牌负责人

据媒体报道，吕文斌于 2026 年 5 月出任长城汽车营销中心哈弗欧拉品牌总经理，统管哈弗、欧拉两大品牌。吕文斌 1983 年出生，2004 年加入长城汽车，长期从事技术与研发工作，历任皮卡产品总监、重庆分公司副总经理、永川生产基地副总经理、哈弗品牌技术总经理等职；2025 年 6 月 20 日起任欧拉品牌总经理。[11][13]

上一任品牌总经理

赵永坡于 2023 年 11 月出任哈弗品牌总经理，长城汽车首席增长官（CGO）李瑞峰称其深耕技术领域 20 年、曾担任长城汽车技术中心副总经理，主导整车平台、下车体、性能和底盘研发等工作。[10] 2025 年 2 月 8 日赵永坡宣布同时负责欧拉品牌；2025 年 12 月 21 日调任魏牌 CEO，不再负责哈弗品牌。[24][27] 以上均为公开职务与商业履历。

竞争格局

我们的判断：竞争范围。按哈弗紧凑型至全尺寸 SUV 的产品线，国内主要竞争对手为比亚迪（宋系列）、吉利、长安（CS75 系列）、奇瑞等，在主流与方盒子 SUV 细分市场存在重叠；越野细分市场则面对同集团的坦克品牌及奇瑞捷途、奇瑞 JAECOO、比亚迪方程豹等。长城 H10 以 20.98 万-23.18 万元切入 20 万级大型家庭 SUV 市场，与同级新能源车型存在价格重叠。此为分析所选的竞争范围，不是销量排名。[4][16][15]

我们的判断：优势。哈弗保有庞大存量用户（截至 2025 年末累计超 1000 万）、长期的 H6 销量纪录，以及差异化的方盒子/越野产品线（2025 年大狗 180,201 辆、猛龙 89,304 辆）。2025 年年报载明猛龙登顶插电混动车型三年保值率榜首；长城汽车官网发展历程载明二代 H9 柴油版获 2025 环塔拉力赛 T2.1 量产燃油组前三名。同时哈弗受益于长城汽车海外工厂与渠道，是集团海外主力品牌。[1][2]

我们的判断：短板与不确定性。H6 曾连续 108 个月蝉联 SUV 销量第一，2022 年被比亚迪宋、特斯拉 Model Y 超越，2023 年排名第六；品牌年销量仍明显低于 2016 年约 93.80 万辆的峰值。品牌电动化结构有限，截至截止日在售车型无纯电产品；对俄罗斯市场的依赖集中了海外风险；部分车型向「长城」母品牌命名的切换仍在过渡中。[12][16][25][13]

未来展望

公司公开规划。2025 年年报称，哈弗将深化全球化布局，加速 Hi4 技术输出与智驾辅助全域适配，依托归元平台拓展新能源市场，实现销量与价值双增长。[1] 据媒体报道，赵永坡在 2025 广州车展曾表示将用哈弗最好的技术打造长城旗舰大型六座 SUV，即 2026 年 8 月上市的长城 H10。[13][14] 据媒体报道，长城汽车 2026 年集团销量目标为不低于 180 万辆（国内 120 万辆、海外 60 万辆），较 2025 年约 132.37 万辆有所上调，哈弗为核心走量品牌。[13][2] 以上为公司披露的方向与目标，不是已实现的业绩。

我们的判断。H10 与「长城」母品牌命名，为品牌带来更高价位的旗舰产品与更清晰的集团级身份；H10 上市 24 小时大定 31,826 台显示初始需求较强，但这只是订单数字，不等于持续销量。[14][15] 品牌能否恢复规模并改善电动化结构，取决于 H10 及方盒子车型的持续需求、命名切换的执行，以及俄罗斯需求、贸易壁垒等海外环境。2026 年上半年销量同比仅 +1.82%，回归 2016 年峰值尚无数据支撑。[3] 2019 年 1 月提出的「5-2-1」目标（5 年内年销 200 万辆）并未实现：实际销量 2024 年 706,234 辆、2025 年 761,487 辆。[9][6][1]

风险

  • 国内竞争。中国 SUV 市场竞争激烈、价格敏感；据媒体报道，H6 在连续 108 个月销冠后失去月销冠，2022 年被比亚迪宋、特斯拉 Model Y 超越。[12]
  • 电动化结构。截至截止日，哈弗在售车型无纯电产品，在中国新能源渗透率持续提升的环境下对销量结构形成压力。[4]
  • 对 H6 的依赖与下滑。H6 由 2016 年 580,683 辆的峰值回落；若继续下滑将拖累整个品牌。[16][12]
  • 海外集中风险。哈弗高度依赖俄罗斯市场；据媒体报道，俄罗斯 2024 年贡献长城海外销量近半，2025 年上半年当地市场萎缩、哈弗在俄销量同比下滑约 21.5%，且贸易壁垒上升。[25]
  • 无单独财务披露。哈弗未披露品牌层面营收与利润，无法从公开文件评估品牌独立盈利能力。[1]
  • 管理层交接与命名切换。2025 年 12 月至 2026 年 5 月，品牌负责人由赵永坡更替为吕文斌，并伴随部分车型向「长城」母品牌命名的调整，带来执行层面的不确定性。[27][11][13]
