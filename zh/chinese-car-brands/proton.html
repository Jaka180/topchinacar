Overview

Proton Holdings Berhad — the brand name derived from Perusahaan Otomobil Nasional (“National Automobile Enterprise”) — is Malaysia's first national car brand, incorporated on 7 May 1983 and headquartered in Shah Alam, Selangor. It is majority-owned by the Malaysian conglomerate DRB-HICOM (50.1%), with China's Geely side holding the remaining 49.9%; since April 2023 that 49.9% stake has been held by the Hong Kong-listed Geely Automobile Holdings (0175.HK), which accounts for Proton as an associate under the equity method. [5][12]

Proton builds affordable A/B/C/D-segment sedans and SUVs for the Malaysian and Southeast Asian market, and since 2024 has sold battery-electric and plug-in hybrid models under the e.MAS sub-brand. It has held second place in Malaysia's passenger-car market every year since 2019, behind Perodua; group sales reached 157,976 units in 2025 (+3.3% year on year) with exports of 6,000 units. [3][1]

Current operations

Business structure and model line-up

Proton's business is conventional passenger vehicles sold under the Proton brand, plus the e.MAS new-energy sub-brand distributed through its subsidiary PRO-NET (Proton New Energy Technology Sdn Bhd). The internal-combustion line-up consists of the Saga, Persona and Iriz sedans/hatchbacks, the X50, X70 and X90 SUVs and the S70 sedan; the e.MAS line-up comprises the e.MAS 7 and e.MAS 5 battery-electric models and the e.MAS 7 PHEV. The X70 is based on the Geely Boyue, and the X50 and X90 SUVs were likewise introduced from Geely's model range; the S70 sedan was added later in the Geely partnership. [1][17]

Technology and platforms

Proton's electrified models draw on Geely technology: the e.MAS 7 battery-electric SUV shares its 60.22 kWh battery solution with the Geely Galaxy E5 (the Malaysian CKD version kept the 60.22 kWh pack rather than the larger 68.39 kWh unit fitted to the 2026 Galaxy E5 in China), while the e.MAS 7 PHEV is built on the GMA platform with an EM-i drive system combining a 1.5-litre dedicated hybrid engine and an 11-in-1 dedicated hybrid transmission, for a total range exceeding 1,400 km. The all-new 2026 Saga uses a new AMA platform and is the first model fully designed and validated by Malaysian talent, with Proton holding full intellectual-property rights. Proton opened its first overseas R&D centre in Ningbo, China in April 2024. [28][8][1][17]

New-energy model specifications

The e.MAS 7 launched in December 2024 as a CBU import from RM109,800 (on-the-road, without insurance). A locally assembled (CKD) version followed on 20 January 2026 at RM103,800 (Prime) and RM119,800 (Premium); at that launch a limited-time RM4,000 rebate brought them to RM99,800 and RM115,800 in Peninsular Malaysia. The Prime uses a 49.52 kWh LFP battery for 345 km WLTP range and the Premium a 60.22 kWh battery for 410 km. On 11 June 2026 Proton added an e.MAS 7 Premium Plus variant with a 68.39 kWh battery, up to 450 km WLTP range and 115 kW DC fast charging; Paul Tan recorded a launch on-the-road price of RM118,800 (about RM119,000). The 2026 buying-guide listing puts the e.MAS 7 range at RM103,800–125,800. The e.MAS 5 (launched 30 October 2025, from RM59,800) is a B-segment EV with a CATL LFP battery and up to 325 km WLTP range; the e.MAS 7 PHEV (4 February 2026, from RM109,800) has a total range exceeding 1,400 km. [6][28][37][38][36][7][8]

Plants and capacity

On 4 September 2025 Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim opened Proton's first dedicated EV assembly plant in Tanjung Malim, on a 5.57-acre site within the Automotive High-Tech Valley (AHTV); its RM47 million first phase gives an initial capacity of 20,000 units a year, scalable to 45,000. The e.MAS 7 was the first CKD model planned for the line, followed by the e.MAS 5. On 30 September 2025 Proton ended 40 years of vehicle production at Shah Alam, moving about 1,400 employees to Tanjung Malim, which now houses Malaysia's first dedicated EV assembly line. Proton also assembles and exports Geely-branded vehicles from Tanjung Malim — official releases do not name specific models. [4][1][2]

Sales and market position

Proton closed 2025 with 157,976 units (+3.3%), which it then estimated at 19.4% share against an estimated TIV of 814,949 units; the Malaysian Automotive Association's later TIV figure was 820,752 units (about 19.25% on that denominator). Exports were 6,000 units (+26%). In the first nine months of 2026 group sales reached 160,886 units (+40.8% year on year), already exceeding full-year 2025, with an estimated year-to-date share of 26.9%; exports rose to 7,370 units. For the e.MAS sub-brand, Proton's 2026 nine-month domestic sales were 31,104 units — e.MAS 5 (20,327), e.MAS 7 PHEV (6,247) and e.MAS 7 (4,530) — a 46.4% share of Malaysia's local EV market, or 32,200 units including exports, with cumulative deliveries of 41,749 units since December 2024. CEO Li Chunrong has said Proton launched 34 new models since the Geely tie-up, a count that includes special editions, R3 limited editions and smart-brand cars; Proton adopted Geely's GCPA quality standard and cut its demerit score from 6,388 to 677, while expanding 3S/4S showrooms from 75 in 2017 to 173 plus 48 e.MAS outlets. [1][2][30][21][34]

Founders and leadership

Founder and origins

Proton originated as Malaysia's National Car Project, driven by Mahathir Mohamad (Malaysia's fourth prime minister, who conceived the idea in 1979 while deputy prime minister). It was incorporated on 7 May 1983; on 23 May a joint-venture agreement was signed among HICOM (70%), Mitsubishi Motors (15%) and Mitsubishi Corporation (15%). The first model, the Saga, followed in 1985. [22][26][35][39]

Chairman and chief executive

Tan Sri Syed Faisal Albar is chairman of Proton and group managing director of DRB-HICOM. Dr Li Chunrong, an engineer by training from the Huazhong University of Science and Technology, was executive vice-president of Dongfeng Honda Engine Co before being hired by Geely to run Proton; media date his joining Geely to 22 August 2017 and his taking office as Proton chief executive officer to October 2017 (the new board naming him CEO was announced around the 29 September 2017 equity transfer). [5][18][17]

Other senior management

  • Dato' Ir. Abdul Rashid Musa — deputy chief executive officer since 1 November 2025, reporting to the CEO; previously Proton's chief technical officer and later CEO of Proton Edar (overseeing the 2018 X70 launch), then held portfolios at UMW Holdings including its aerospace division. [9]
  • Zhang Qiang — chief executive officer of PRO-NET, the subsidiary that distributes the e.MAS brand. [2][30]
  • Chen Limin — Proton chief financial officer (Chinese name not confirmed in the sources reviewed). [10]
  • Roslan Abdullah — chief executive officer of Proton Edar. [3]

Ownership and governance

Proton is held 50.1% by DRB-HICOM and 49.9% by Geely Automobile Holdings (0175.HK) through its wholly-owned subsidiary Linkstate Overseas Ltd. In the January–April 2023 internal restructuring, Geely Automobile Holdings acquired that stake, plus a 49.9% stake in DRB-HICOM Geely Sdn Bhd (DHG), from Geely International (Hong Kong) Ltd for RMB1.063 billion plus about US$56.39 million in loans, and accounts for Proton under the equity method. Proton does not separately disclose the full composition of its board; the chairman is from the DRB-HICOM side (Syed Faisal Albar) and the CEO is appointed from the Geely side (Li Chunrong). [12][5][18]

Competition

In Malaysia, Proton competes with Perodua, the long-standing market leader that sold 359,904 units in 2025 (about 44% share, roughly 2.3 times Proton's volume), and with Toyota, Honda and a growing group of Chinese entrants including BYD, Chery and Jaecoo. Proton's strengths are its national-brand status, a large dealer network (187 Proton Edar outlets plus 48 e.MAS outlets at end-2025), Geely-derived products and local CKD assembly. Its market share rose from 11% in 2018 to 19% in 2025, and the company now runs second in Malaysia. [33][13][1]

Our judgment: Proton's principal structural weaknesses are the scale gap against Perodua and dependence on Geely for platforms and electrified powertrains, which limits its room to differentiate on cost and product cadence; its EV share leadership (e.MAS held about 46.4% of Malaysia's local EV market in the first nine months of 2026) is also exposed to price competition from BYD and Tesla, as Chinese brands expand rapidly in the Malaysian market. These are our assessments, based on the sales and share figures cited above. [30][34]

Outlook

Company targets. Proton has stated a target of 200,000 units in 2026 (up 26% on 2025). At the media night of 28 January 2026, CEO Li Chunrong set out a roadmap of over 227,000 units by 2027 (Proton's highest-ever annual total) and becoming Malaysia's best-selling carmaker by 2029, and reiterated the “30+30+30 by 2030” plan of 330,000 units with 30% electrified, 30% exported across 30 markets. At the AHTV level, Geely and DRB-HICOM target 500,000-unit annual capacity by 2035 (half exported) and a one-million-set component supply chain, and Geely's chairman said the plan is to help Proton launch at least one new-energy vehicle a year and cultivate 5,000 local new-energy talents within five years. [29][15][23][27]

Our analysis. These targets rest on three premises: that the 2026 volume momentum (160,886 units in nine months, already above full-year 2025) continues through the year; that the e.MAS PHEV and EV ramp plus the new Saga sustain demand without heavy discounting; and that export expansion through Proton International Corporation and AHTV local assembly materialises. The main alternatives are a cooling of the Malaysian total market after the record 820,752-unit 2025 (MAA projects a decline to about 790,000 units in 2026), intensifying price competition from Chinese brands, and execution risk on the AHTV build-out. This is our assessment; it is not a forecast of any share price. [2][34]

Risks

Competition. Proton's volume remains far below Perodua, and Chinese brands such as BYD, Chery and Jaecoo are expanding in Malaysia, raising the intensity of price competition in both ICE and EV segments. [13][1]

Technology dependence. Most of Proton's new products and its electrified powertrains are derived from Geely platforms, so its product cadence and cost structure depend on continued technology transfer from the Chinese partner. [5][8]

Market and policy. Malaysia's total industry volume hit a record 820,752 units in 2025, and MAA projects a decline to about 790,000 units in 2026, so any post-record cooling would weigh on volumes; the EV transition also depends on government incentives and charging infrastructure. [13][34]

Overseas and concentration. Proton's business is heavily concentrated in its home market, and its export base of 6,000 units in 2025 remains small relative to total sales, so overseas expansion targets carry execution risk. [10][1]

Financial transparency. As an unlisted company, Proton does not separately disclose financial statements, so outside visibility into its profitability and capital position is limited. Media reports citing CEO Li Chunrong put Proton's 2017 loss at RM934 million and its 2019 return to profit at about RM160 million; another media report (citing the MITI minister) put the pre-partnership annual loss at RM1.2 billion — the two figures differ and there is no audited, separately disclosed set of accounts. [17][21]

概览

宝腾控股有限公司（Proton Holdings Berhad，品牌名源自马来语 Perusahaan Otomobil Nasional，即“国民汽车企业”）是马来西亚首个国民汽车品牌，1983 年 5 月 7 日注册成立，总部位于雪兰莪州莎阿南。它由马来西亚综合企业 DRB-HICOM 持股 50.1%（多数股），吉利方持有其余 49.9%；自 2023 年 4 月起，该 49.9% 股权由香港上市的吉利汽车控股（0175.HK）持有，并按联营公司以权益法入账。 [5][12]

宝腾面向马来西亚及东南亚市场生产 A/B/C/D 级轿车与 SUV，2024 年起以 e.MAS 子品牌销售纯电与插电混动车型。自 2019 年起，宝腾在马来西亚乘用车市场连续每年位居第二（仅次于 Perodua）；2025 年集团销量 157,976 辆（同比 +3.3%），出口 6,000 辆。 [3][1]

历史沿革

  1. 1979

    时任副总理马哈蒂尔提出国民车构想；内阁于 1982 年 11 月批准国民车计划。[26][35]

  2. 1983-05

    5 月 7 日注册成立 Perusahaan Otomobil Nasional（PONSB）；5 月 23 日与三菱签署合资协议（HICOM 70%、三菱汽车 15%、三菱商事 15%），由三菱提供技术。[26][35][17][39]

  3. 1985-07

    7 月 9 日首款量产车型 Proton Saga 上市，基于三菱车型。[35][17]

  4. 1986

    首款车下线一年后开始出口，首个市场为孟加拉。[10]

  5. 1992

    宝腾在吉隆坡证券交易所主板上市；同年成为英国增长最快的新车品牌。[26][10]

  6. 1996

    宝腾收购莲花集团（Lotus Group）控股权。[26][22]

  7. 2004

    三菱汽车与三菱商事于 2004–2005 年先后出售所持宝腾股份，股权由马来西亚政府（国库控股等）接手。[17][26]

  8. 2012-01

    DRB-HICOM 与国库控股签署协议收购其战略股权，成为宝腾控股股东。[16][26]

  9. 2017-06

    6 月 23 日，吉利控股集团与 DRB-HICOM 签署最终协议，收购宝腾 49.9% 股权及莲花 51% 股权。[22]

  10. 2017-09

    9 月 29 日股权交割完成，并公布新一届董事会、李春荣出任宝腾 CEO；媒体另记其 2017 年 8 月 22 日加盟吉利、2017 年 10 月正式就任 CEO。[22][18][17]

  11. 2018-12

    12 月 12 日上市 X70，为吉利入股后首款车型（基于吉利博越）。[22][18]

  12. 2019

    宝腾扭亏为盈，当年盈利约 1.6 亿马币；销量回升至 100,821 辆（同比 +55.7%）。[17][18]

  13. 2023-04

    吉利汽车控股（0175.HK）从吉利控股关联公司收购宝腾 49.9% 股权及 DRB-HICOM Geely Sdn Bhd（DHG）49.9% 股权，完成内部重组，并以权益法入账。[12]

  14. 2023-05

    宝腾迎来成立 40 周年，并上市 X90，为品牌首款轻混（新能源）SUV。[5][20]

  15. 2024-04

    宝腾首个海外研发中心在中国宁波揭幕，与吉利研发体系深度融合。[17]

  16. 2024-12

    12 月上市 e.MAS 7，为宝腾首款纯电动车、马来西亚首款国民电动车（媒体记 12 月 16 日，宝腾官网里程碑记 12 月 17 日）。[6][24]

  17. 2025-09

    9 月 4 日总理安瓦尔·易卜拉欣为丹绒马林 AHTV 的宝腾首个专用电动车工厂揭幕；9 月 30 日宝腾结束莎阿南工厂 40 年的整车生产，约 1,400 名员工转入丹绒马林。[4][1]

  18. 2025-10

    10 月 30 日上市 e.MAS 5，定位为马来西亚首款平价电动车。[7][25]

  19. 2025-11

    11 月 27 日上市全新一代 Saga，基于全新 AMA 平台并由本地团队全流程设计。[15][1]

  20. 2026-02

    2 月 4 日上市 e.MAS 7 PHEV，为马来西亚国产品牌首款插电式混合动力车型。[8][13]

现状

业务结构与车型谱系

宝腾的主营业务为以 Proton 品牌销售的燃油乘用车，以及通过子公司 PRO-NET（Proton New Energy Technology Sdn Bhd）销售的 e.MAS 新能源子品牌车型。燃油车型包括 Saga、Persona、Iriz 等轿车/两厢车，以及 X50、X70、X90 三款 SUV 和 S70 轿车；e.MAS 系列则包括 e.MAS 7、e.MAS 5 两款纯电车型与 e.MAS 7 PHEV 插混车型。其中 X70 基于吉利博越，X50、X90 两款 SUV 由吉利的车型谱系导入，S70 轿车为吉利合作后期新增车型。 [1][17]

技术与平台

宝腾的新能源车型依托吉利技术：纯电的 e.MAS 7 与吉利银河 E5 共享 60.22 千瓦时电池方案（其本地组装版沿用 60.22 千瓦时电池，未采用 2026 款银河 E5 在华升级的 68.39 千瓦时电池）；e.MAS 7 PHEV 基于 GMA 平台、搭载由 1.5 升专用混动发动机与 11 合 1 专用混动变速器组成的 EM-i 驱动系统，综合续航超过 1,400 公里。全新一代 2026 Saga 采用全新 AMA 平台，是首款由本地人才全流程设计与验证、宝腾保有全部知识产权的车型。2024 年 4 月，宝腾首个海外研发中心在中国宁波揭幕。 [28][8][1][17]

新能源车型参数

e.MAS 7 于 2024 年 12 月以整车进口（CBU）形式上市，起售价 109,800 马币（不含保险）；2026 年 1 月 20 日推出本地组装（CKD）版，Prime 售价 103,800 马币、Premium 119,800 马币，该次上市时限时 4,000 马币优惠后在西马分别为 99,800 与 115,800 马币。其中 Prime 搭载 49.52 千瓦时磷酸铁锂电池、WLTP 续航 345 公里，Premium 搭载 60.22 千瓦时电池、WLTP 续航 410 公里。2026 年 6 月 11 日宝腾新增 e.MAS 7 Premium Plus 版本，搭载 68.39 千瓦时电池、WLTP 续航最高 450 公里、直流快充最高 115 千瓦；Paul Tan 记上市落地价 118,800 马币（约 11.9 万马币）。2026 年购车指南所列 e.MAS 7 价带为 103,800–125,800 马币。e.MAS 5（2025 年 10 月 30 日上市，起售价 59,800 马币）为搭载宁德时代磷酸铁锂电池的 B 级纯电车型，WLTP 续航最高 325 公里；e.MAS 7 PHEV（2026 年 2 月 4 日上市，起售价 109,800 马币）综合续航超过 1,400 公里。 [6][28][37][38][36][7][8]

工厂与产能

2025 年 9 月 4 日，总理安瓦尔·易卜拉欣为宝腾位于丹绒马林、汽车高科技谷（AHTV）内的首个专用电动车工厂揭幕；该厂占地 5.57 英亩，第一阶段投资 4,700 万马币，初始年产能 2 万辆，可扩展至最高 4.5 万辆，e.MAS 7 为首款规划的 CKD 车型，随后是 e.MAS 5。9 月 30 日，宝腾结束莎阿南工厂 40 年的整车生产，约 1,400 名员工转入丹绒马林，后者现拥有马来西亚首条专用电动车装配线。丹绒马林工厂还为吉利品牌组装并出口车辆，但官方稿未点名具体车型。 [4][1][2]

销量与市场地位

宝腾 2025 年销量 157,976 辆（同比 +3.3%），按当时估计 TIV 814,949 辆计算市占率约 19.4%；马来西亚汽车协会后来公布的 TIV 为 820,752 辆（按该分母约 19.25%）。出口 6,000 辆（+26%）；2026 年前 9 个月集团销量达 160,886 辆（同比 +40.8%），已超过 2025 全年，年内累计市占率约 26.9%，出口升至 7,370 辆。e.MAS 子品牌 2026 年前 9 个月国内销量 31,104 辆——e.MAS 5（20,327 辆）、e.MAS 7 PHEV（6,247 辆）、e.MAS 7（4,530 辆）——占马来西亚本地电动汽车（EV）市场 46.4%，含出口合计 32,200 辆，自 2024 年 12 月上市以来累计交付 41,749 辆。CEO 李春荣称，宝腾自吉利合作以来已推出 34 款新车型，但该口径包含特别版、R3 限量版乃至 smart 品牌车型；宝腾还引入吉利 GCPA 质量标准，缺陷扣分由 6,388 降至 677，3S/4S 经销店由 2017 年的 75 家增至 173 家，另有 48 家 e.MAS 网点。 [1][2][30][21][34]

创始人与管理层

创始人渊源

宝腾源自马来西亚国民车计划，由该国第四任首相马哈蒂尔主导推动（构想于其任副首相的 1979 年提出），1983 年 5 月 7 日注册成立；5 月 23 日与三菱签署合资协议（HICOM 70%、三菱汽车 15%、三菱商事 15%）。1985 年推出首款车型 Saga。 [22][26][35][39]

董事长与首席执行官

丹斯里赛·法依沙·阿尔巴（Tan Sri Syed Faisal Albar）任宝腾董事长，同时是 DRB-HICOM 集团董事总经理。李春荣（Li Chunrong）为华中科技大学工科出身，出任宝腾 CEO 前是东风本田发动机公司的执行副总裁，由吉利聘请执掌宝腾；媒体记其 2017 年 8 月 22 日加盟吉利、2017 年 10 月就任宝腾 CEO（宣布其任 CEO 的新一届董事会约在 2017 年 9 月 29 日股权交割前后公布）。 [5][18][17]

股权与治理

宝腾由 DRB-HICOM 持股 50.1%、吉利汽车控股（0175.HK）通过全资子公司 Linkstate Overseas Ltd 持股 49.9%。在 2023 年 1–4 月的内部重组中，吉利汽车控股以人民币 10.63 亿元加约 5,639 万美元贷款为对价，从吉利国际（香港）有限公司收购该股权及 DRB-HICOM Geely Sdn Bhd（DHG）49.9% 股权，并以权益法入账。宝腾不单独披露董事会完整席位名单；董事长由 DRB-HICOM 方出任（Syed Faisal Albar），CEO 由吉利方委派（李春荣）。 [12][5][18]

竞争格局

在马来西亚市场，宝腾的主要对手是长期占据第一的 Perodua（2025 年销量 359,904 辆、市占率约 44%，约为宝腾的 2.3 倍），以及丰田、本田和比亚迪、奇瑞、Jaecoo 等不断涌入的中国品牌。宝腾的优势在于国民品牌地位、庞大的经销商网络（2025 年末 187 家 Proton Edar 网点加 48 家 e.MAS 网点）、吉利系产品以及本地 CKD 组装。其市占率由 2018 年的 11% 升至 2025 年的 19%，目前稳居马来西亚第二。 [33][13][1]

我们的判断：宝腾的主要结构性短板在于与 Perodua 的规模差距，以及对吉利在平台与电动化动力系统上的依赖，这限制了其在成本与产品节奏上的差异化空间；其在电动汽车市场的份额领先（2026 年前 9 个月 e.MAS 约占马来西亚本地 EV 市场 46.4%）也面临比亚迪、特斯拉等品牌的价格竞争，中国品牌正在马来西亚快速扩张。以上为我们的判断，依据为前述销量与份额数据。 [30][34]

未来展望

公司公开目标。宝腾提出 2026 年销售 20 万辆的目标（较 2025 年 +26%）。在 2026 年 1 月 28 日的媒体晚宴上，CEO 李春荣给出的路线图是：2027 年超 22.7 万辆（为宝腾历史最高年度销量）、2029 年成为马来西亚销量第一的车企，并重申 “30+30+30 by 2030” 计划（2030 年 33 万辆、30% 电气化、30% 出口至 30 个市场）。在 AHTV 层面，吉利与 DRB-HICOM 的目标是到 2035 年形成 50 万辆年产能（一半出口）与百万套零部件供应链；吉利董事长表示将帮助宝腾每年至少推出 1 款新能源车型，并在五年内培养 5,000 名本地新能源人才。 [29][15][23][27]

我们的分析。上述目标依赖三个前提：一是 2026 年的销量势头（9 个月 160,886 辆，已超 2025 全年）能延续全年；二是 e.MAS 插混与纯电放量及全新 Saga 无需大幅让利即可维持需求；三是通过 Proton International Corporation 与 AHTV 本地组装实现的出口扩张如期落地。主要变数在于 2025 年总销量创新高（820,752 辆）后马来西亚整体车市转冷（MAA 预计 2026 年回落至约 79 万辆）、中国品牌的价格竞争加剧，以及 AHTV 建设的执行风险。以上为我们的判断，不构成任何股价预测。 [2][34]

风险

竞争风险。宝腾的销量仍明显低于 Perodua，且比亚迪、奇瑞、Jaecoo 等中国品牌正加速进入马来西亚，燃油与纯电细分市场的价格竞争都在加剧。 [13][1]

技术依赖风险。宝腾多数新产品及电动化动力系统源自吉利平台，其产品节奏与成本结构有赖于中方合作伙伴的持续技术导入。 [5][8]

市场与政策风险。2025 年马来西亚总销量创历史新高达 820,752 辆，MAA 预计 2026 年回落至约 79 万辆，若创新高后转冷将拖累销量；电动化转型还依赖政府补贴与充电基础设施。 [13][34]

海外与集中度风险。宝腾业务高度集中于本土市场，2025 年出口仅 6,000 辆、占比较小，海外扩张目标存在执行风险。 [10][1]

财务透明度风险。作为未上市企业，宝腾不单独披露财务报表，外界对其盈利能力与资本状况的可见度有限。媒体援引 CEO 李春荣称，宝腾 2017 年亏损 9.34 亿马币、2019 年扭亏为盈约 1.6 亿马币；另有媒体（引贸工部长）称合作前一年亏损 12 亿马币，两个口径不一，且无经审计的单独披露。 [17][21]
