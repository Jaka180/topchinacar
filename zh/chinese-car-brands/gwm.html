Overview

Great Wall Motor Company Limited is a privately owned automaker headquartered in Baoding, Hebei. Its official chronology dates the predecessor factory to 1984. The joint-stock company was incorporated in June 2001 and listed in Hong Kong (02333.HK) in 2003 and Shanghai (601633.SH) in 2011.[1][5] Its business covers vehicle and component design, R&D, production, sales and service. Brands include Haval, WEY, Tank, ORA, Great Wall Pickup, SOUO and Great Wall Commercial Vehicle, spanning SUVs, sedans, pickups, MPVs, motorcycles and heavy trucks. Spotlight Automotive is a separate 50/50 joint venture with BMW Group.[1][18]

In fiscal 2025 Great Wall Motor sold 1,323,811 vehicles (+7.23% YoY), including 506,787 overseas (+11.60% YoY) and 406,000 new-energy vehicles (+26.00% YoY); revenue was RMB 222.824 billion (+10.20% YoY) and net profit attributable to shareholders of the listed company was RMB 9.865 billion (−22.07% YoY).[1] The 2025 annual report gives comparative 2024 sales of 1,234,528 vehicles.[1]

The 2025 annual report describes Great Wall Pickup as China's pickup sales leader for 28 consecutive years. GWM sold 506,787 vehicles overseas in 2025, about 38.3% of its total. The company has three full-process vehicle plants overseas, plus several KD assembly plants. By the research cut-off date, it had also disclosed its 2026 interim report: first-half attributable net profit was RMB 2.465 billion, down 61.11% year on year.[1][4]

Current operations

Business structure

Great Wall Motor's revenue is dominated by whole-vehicle sales, with a smaller parts and other business. For full-year 2025, operating revenue was RMB 222.824 billion (+10.20% YoY).[1] In the first half of 2026, operating revenue was RMB 102.10 billion (+10.58% YoY), of which vehicle sales were RMB 88.82 billion and parts and other revenue RMB 13.29 billion; gross margin was 18.37%. Half-year net profit attributable to shareholders was RMB 2.465 billion (−61.11% YoY), net profit excluding non-recurring items was RMB 1.610 billion (−55.04% YoY), second-quarter net profit was RMB 1.519 billion (+60.67% QoQ), and operating cash flow was RMB 10.436 billion (+13.25% YoY). At end-June 2026 the headcount was about 99,600.[4] By vehicle category, 2025 sales comprised 1,033,100 SUVs (+4.45% YoY), 111,800 sedans and others (mainly new-energy vehicles, +58.73% YoY) and 178,900 pickups (+2.22% YoY).[1] The Tank brand sold 234,442 vehicles in 2025, remaining China's best-selling off-road SUV brand.[1]

Brand premiumisation and revenue per vehicle

Per the company's 2025 results announcement, revenue per vehicle was RMB 168,300 in 2025 (up about RMB 4,500 YoY), and models priced above RMB 200,000 sold 534,000 units, taking their share of sales to 41% (up 6 percentage points from 2024); in 2025 the company's MSCI ESG rating was A.[10]

Technology and platforms

Technologies include the Hi4 intelligent four-wheel-drive hybrid family, including Hi4-T and Hi4-Z architectures, and the Coffee OS smart cockpit and Coffee Pilot driving-assistance systems.[1][2] In 2025 the company launched a new-generation Hi4 — Hi4 won the China Society of Automotive Engineers special award and the Hi4-Z architecture was named among the "China Heart" 2025 top ten engines and hybrid systems — put its VLA driving-assistance large model into production, first on the WEY Lanshan intelligent version, and developed a 4.0T V8 engine; it also opened an advanced safety laboratory with RMB 510 million of investment and 57,000 square metres of floor area in 2025. In January 2026 it announced the Guiyuan platform, a native-AI all-powertrain platform compatible with gasoline, diesel, BEV, hybrid, PHEV and hydrogen.[10][5] On the smart side, it rolled out the Coffee Pilot Ultra driving-assistance system and Coffee OS 3 smart cockpit, and co-developed the RISC-V automotive-grade MCU chip Zijin M100.[2]

R&D investment and patents

In 2025 total R&D investment was RMB 10.378 billion, or 4.66% of operating revenue, while expensed R&D cost was RMB 10.432 billion.[1] Total R&D investment by year was RMB 9.067 billion (2021), RMB 12.181 billion (2022), RMB 11.034 billion (2023), RMB 10.446 billion (2024) and RMB 10.378 billion (2025).[8][3][9][2][1] The 2025 annual report records 27,097 R&D personnel at year-end, or 27.76% of total employees; this is the report's R&D personnel measure.[1] For the 2023 Global Automotive Patent Big Data Platform ranking, disclosed by the company on 26 January 2024, the reporting entity is Great Wall Holdings (including Great Wall Motor and its parts and components subsidiaries): 6,888 patent publications and 4,665 grants, each ranking first among Chinese private automaker groups for three consecutive years; in the new-energy vehicle field, 1,838 publications and 1,375 grants, each first among automakers in China for three consecutive years; and in intelligent technology, 1,131 publications, also first among private automaker groups.[11]

Factories and capacity

Great Wall Motor operates multiple whole-vehicle plants in China, and has built three full-process plants overseas — Tula (Russia), Rayong (Thailand) and Iracemápolis (Brazil) — plus several KD assembly plants.[1][13] The Tula plant, its first full-process plant abroad, started production in June 2019.[5] The Rayong plant was acquired from General Motors in 2020.[5] The Iracemápolis plant in Brazil was completed and started production in August 2025, with an annual capacity of 50,000 vehicles, a site of 1.2 million square metres and the Haval H6 GT as its first model off the line, witnessed by Brazilian President Lula.[13] The 2018 joint-venture contract announcement planned initial standard annual capacity of 160,000 vehicles for Spotlight Automotive.[18]

Domestic market position

At home, Great Wall Pickup has long ranked first in China's pickup market in both domestic sales and exports; the Tank 300 has been China's best-selling off-road SUV for five consecutive years and the Tank 500 has ranked first in luxury off-road sales above RMB 300,000 for four consecutive years; and the new Gaoshan topped China's MPV sales in July–December 2025. In 2025, models priced above RMB 200,000 sold 534,000 units, a 41% share.[5][10]

Overseas business

In 2025 the company sold 506,787 vehicles overseas (+11.60% YoY), about 38% of total sales.[1] In the first half of 2026 overseas sales reached 289,000 units (+45.46% YoY), accounting for just over half of total sales for the first time, with overseas revenue rising to about 55% of total revenue.[4] The 2025 annual report states that, as of 2025, products had been exported to more than 170 countries and regions, with over 1,500 overseas sales outlets.[1] In its August 2025 plant-opening release, the company described plans to use Brazil as a hub for Mexico, Argentina, Chile and other Latin American markets.[13] The company pursues an "ecosystem globalisation" strategy covering the whole chain of R&D, production, supply, sales and service.[1]

Founders and leadership

Founder and business development

Wei Jianjun joined the predecessor business as general manager in 1990 and has chaired the joint-stock company since June 2001, overseeing group strategy and business development. The company chronology records the first Deer pickup in 1996, the first pickup exports to the Middle East in 1997 and the Saifei SUV in the market in 2002.[1][5]

Current management

According to the 2026 interim report, the board comprises executive directors, an employee director, a non-executive director and independent non-executive directors: Wei Jianjun is chairman and executive director; Zhao Guoqing is vice chairman, executive director and deputy general manager; Li Hongshuan is executive director and chief financial officer (finance director); Zhao Gai is the employee director; He Ping is a non-executive director; and Fan Hui, Zou Zhaolin and Tian Yajuan are independent non-executive directors.[4] Among senior management, Mu Feng is general manager (president), Shi Qingke is international president, and Yuan Yuan is board secretary (joint company secretary).[4][6]

In June 2026, employee director Lu Caijuan and independent non-executive director Le Ying stepped down; Zhao Gai succeeded as employee director and Tian Yajuan as independent non-executive director; Li Hongshuan no longer serves concurrently as board secretary, and Yuan Yuan was appointed board secretary in July 2026.[4]

The 2025 annual report records that Zhao Guoqing joined in 2000, previously worked as deputy head of the technology research institute and head of procurement management, became deputy general manager in June 2010 and vice chairman and executive director in March 2022. Li Hongshuan joined in 2007, became CFO in June 2020 and an executive director in March 2022. Mu Feng joined in 2007, held responsibility for R&D, product strategy and vehicle operations, and became general manager in July 2022. These are the company's disclosed career histories.[1]

Ownership and governance

The company's 16 October 2026 related-party transaction announcement identifies Baoding Innovation Great Wall Asset Management Co., Ltd. as the controlling shareholder, holding about 59.63% of the issued share capital; Wei Jianjun indirectly holds 99% of Great Wall Holdings, which holds 62.85% of Innovation Great Wall, and is the ultimate beneficial owner and actual controller.[7] As of 31 December 2025, total share capital was 8,557,872,087 shares, comprising 6,239,096,087 A shares and 2,318,776,000 H shares.[1]

Competition

Our judgment: competitive scope and differentiation. Based on the company's disclosed SUV, pickup, MPV and new-energy passenger-vehicle portfolio, we select BYD, Geely, Chery, Changan and SAIC as competitors to monitor, alongside global brands such as Toyota in pickups and off-road vehicles. This is our analytical peer selection, not a sales ranking. GWM sold 178,936 pickups and 234,442 Tank vehicles in 2025. Its 2025 annual report describes Great Wall Pickup as China's pickup sales leader for 28 consecutive years and Tank as China's off-road SUV sales leader. Our judgment is that this existing product scale in pickups and off-road SUVs provides a basis for differentiation.[1]

Our judgment: profit recovery needs further evidence. The company disclosed revenue per vehicle of RMB 168,300 in 2025, while group gross margin was 18.04%, attributable net profit fell 22.07% year on year, and first-half 2026 attributable net profit fell 61.11%. Higher revenue per vehicle can coexist with lower net profit; a shift toward higher price bands alone does not establish better profitability. About 406,000 new-energy vehicles divided by 1,323,811 total vehicles gives approximately 30.7% of the company's global 2025 sales. This internal sales-mix measure alone cannot establish whether its transition lags the industry. Subsequent assessment should track sales mix, expenses and profit on a consistent basis.[10][1][4]

Outlook

Company plans. The operating plan in the 2025 annual report, published on 27 March 2026, calls for a broader all-powertrain product matrix, further development of Hi4 technology and the VLA driving-assistance model, and continued ecosystem globalisation. The report also calls for deeper regional operations, local manufacturing and local supply chains to address overseas risks. These are disclosed directions, not completed results.[1]

Our judgment. With 506,787 overseas vehicle sales in 2025 and ongoing investment in local manufacturing, overseas demand and plant utilisation warrant attention. Better coordination of local production, distribution and supply chains could support sales growth; weak local demand or higher trade barriers could instead make capacity investment a burden. In China, the question is whether sales of new-energy and higher-priced models translate into profit. Penetration rates from different periods, geographies and wholesale or retail measures do not establish an industry lag. This analysis rests on the company's disclosed product portfolio, profit changes and overseas operations; outcomes depend on demand, cost control and local execution.[1][10][13]

Risks

  • Overseas risks disclosed by the company. The 2025 annual report identifies international geopolitical conflict and growing trade barriers as sources of uncertainty in overseas markets.[1]
  • Domestic competition risk disclosed by the company. The 2025 annual report identifies an expanding range of products in China and intensifying competition among similar products.[1]
  • Profit and margin changes. In 2025, attributable net profit fell 22.07% year on year and group gross margin was 18.04%, versus 19.51% in 2024. First-half 2026 attributable net profit fell 61.11% year on year.[1][4]
  • New-energy product mix. New-energy vehicles accounted for approximately 30.7% of the company's full-year global sales in 2025; ORA sold 48,312 vehicles that year.[1]

概览

长城汽车股份有限公司是一家总部位于河北省保定市的民营整车制造企业。官网发展历程将前身工厂的成立追溯至 1984 年；公司于 2001 年 6 月改制设立股份公司，2003 年、2011 年先后在香港联合交易所（02333.HK）和上海证券交易所（601633.SH）上市。[1][5] 公司业务包括整车及零部件设计、研发、生产、销售和服务，旗下拥有哈弗、魏牌、坦克、欧拉、长城皮卡、长城灵魂和长城商用车等品牌，覆盖 SUV、轿车、皮卡、MPV、摩托车和重卡；另与宝马集团设立 50:50 合资企业光束汽车。[1][18]

2025 财年，公司销售新车 1,323,811 辆（同比 +7.23%），其中海外销售 506,787 辆（同比 +11.60%）、新能源车型全球销售 40.60 万辆（同比 +26.00%）；实现营业总收入 2,228.24 亿元（同比 +10.20%），归属于上市公司股东的净利润 98.65 亿元（同比 −22.07%）。[1] 按 2025 年报列示的比较数，2024 年公司销售新车 1,234,528 辆。[1]

据 2025 年报，长城皮卡连续 28 年居中国皮卡销量第一；2025 年海外销售 506,787 辆，占公司总销量约 38.3%。公司已在海外建立三个全工艺整车生产基地，另设多家 KD 工厂。截至研究截止日，公司已披露 2026 年半年度报告：上半年归母净利润 24.65 亿元，同比下降 61.11%。[1][4]

现状

业务结构

长城汽车收入以整车销售为主，另有规模较小的零部件及其他业务。2025 年全年营业总收入 2,228.24 亿元（同比 +10.20%）。[1] 2026 年上半年，公司营业总收入 1,021.01 亿元（同比 +10.58%），其中整车销售收入 888.16 亿元、零部件及其他收入 132.86 亿元，毛利率 18.37%；上半年归母净利润 24.65 亿元（同比 −61.11%）、扣非归母净利润 16.10 亿元（同比 −55.04%），其中第二季度归母净利润 15.19 亿元（环比 +60.67%），经营活动产生的现金流量净额 104.36 亿元（同比 +13.25%）；截至 2026 年 6 月末员工总数约 9.96 万人。[4] 按车型类别，2025 年公司销售 SUV 103.31 万辆（同比 +4.45%）、轿车及其他（主要为新能源车）11.18 万辆（同比 +58.73%）、皮卡 17.89 万辆（同比 +2.22%）。[1] 坦克品牌 2025 年销售 23.44 万辆，蝉联中国越野 SUV 销量第一。[1]

品牌向上与单车收入

据公司 2025 年业绩公告，2025 年单车收入达 16.83 万元（同比提升约 4,500 元），20 万元以上车型销售 53.4 万辆，销量占比提升至 41%（较 2024 年提升 6 个百分点）；2025 年公司 MSCI（明晟）ESG 评估结果为 A。[10]

技术与平台

技术体系包括 Hi4 智能四驱电混技术家族（含 Hi4-T、Hi4-Z 架构），以及 Coffee OS 智能座舱与 Coffee Pilot 辅助驾驶系统。[1][2] 2025 年公司推出新一代 Hi4 技术，Hi4 获中国汽车工程学会科学技术奖特等奖，Hi4-Z 架构获「中国心」2025 年度十佳发动机及混动系统称号；VLA 辅助驾驶大模型正式落地并首搭于魏牌蓝山智能进阶版；公司还成功研发 4.0T V8 发动机，并于 2025 年启用总投资 5.1 亿元、总建筑面积 5.7 万平方米的先进安全试验室。2026 年 1 月官宣「归元」平台，为兼容汽油、柴油、纯电、混动、插混、氢能的原生 AI 全动力整车平台。[10][5] 智能化方面，公司推出 Coffee Pilot Ultra 智驾系统和 Coffee OS 3 智慧空间系统，并联合培育 RISC-V 车规级 MCU 芯片紫荆 M100。[2]

研发投入与专利

2025 年公司研发投入合计 103.78 亿元，占营业收入比例 4.66%；利润表口径的研发费用为 104.32 亿元。[1] 研发投入合计历年为：2021 年 90.67 亿元、2022 年 121.81 亿元、2023 年 110.34 亿元、2024 年 104.46 亿元、2025 年 103.78 亿元。[8][3][9][2][1] 据 2025 年报「研发人员情况表」，2025 年末公司研发人员为 27,097 人，占员工总数的 27.76%；此处采用年报研发人员口径。[1] 专利方面，据公司 2024 年 1 月 26 日披露的 2023 年全球汽车专利大数据平台榜单，统计主体为长城控股（含长城汽车及控股旗下零部件等子公司），其专利公开量 6,888 件、授权量 4,665 件，均连续三年居中国民营车企集团第一；新能源汽车领域专利公开量 1,838 件、授权量 1,375 件，均连续三年居在华车企第一；智能化领域专利公开量 1,131 件，同样居民营车企集团第一。[11]

主要工厂与产能

公司在国内拥有多个整车生产基地，海外已建成俄罗斯图拉、泰国罗勇、巴西伊拉塞马波利斯三个全工艺整车生产基地及多家 KD 组装工厂。[1][13] 图拉工厂于 2019 年 6 月投产，是公司海外首个全工艺整车制造厂。[5] 泰国罗勇工厂于 2020 年收购自通用汽车。[5] 巴西伊拉塞马波利斯工厂于 2025 年 8 月竣工投产，年产能 5 万辆、总占地 120 万平方米，首台下线车型为哈弗 H6 GT，巴西总统卢拉现场见证。[13] 据 2018 年合资合同公告，光束汽车规划初期年标准产能 16 万辆。[18]

国内市场地位

在国内市场，长城皮卡长期位居皮卡市场国内销量与出口第一，坦克 300 连续五年位居越野 SUV 销量冠军、坦克 500 连续四年位列 30 万级以上豪华越野销量第一，全新高山问鼎 2025 年 7–12 月全国 MPV 销冠；2025 年公司 20 万元以上车型销售 53.4 万辆、占比 41%。[5][10]

海外业务

2025 年公司海外销售新车 50.68 万辆（同比 +11.60%），占公司总销量约 38%。[1] 2026 年上半年海外销售 28.90 万辆（同比 +45.46%），占上半年总销量的比例首次超过五成，境外收入占总营收比重提升至约 55%。[4] 据 2025 年报，截至 2025 年，公司产品已出口至 170 多个国家和地区，海外销售渠道超过 1,500 家。[1] 公司在 2025 年 8 月巴西工厂投产新闻稿中称，计划以巴西为中心辐射墨西哥、阿根廷、智利等拉美市场。[13] 公司持续推进研、产、供、销、服全链条「生态出海」。[1]

创始人与管理层

创始人与创业历程

魏建军于 1990 年加入公司前身并担任总经理，2001 年 6 月起任股份公司董事长，负责集团整体战略规划及业务发展。公司官网发展历程载明，1996 年首辆迪尔皮卡下线，1997 年首批皮卡出口中东，2002 年赛弗 SUV 进入市场。[1][5]

现任核心管理层

据 2026 年半年度报告披露，公司董事会由执行董事、职工董事、非执行董事和独立非执行董事构成：魏建军任董事长、执行董事；赵国庆任副董事长、执行董事、副总经理；李红栓任执行董事、财务负责人（财务总监）；赵改任职工董事；何平任非执行董事；范辉、邹兆麟、田雅娟任独立非执行董事。[4] 高级管理人员中，穆峰任总经理（总裁），史青科任国际总裁，袁媛任董事会秘书（联席公司秘书）。[4][6]

2026 年 6 月，原职工董事卢彩娟、独立非执行董事乐英离任，由赵改接任职工董事、田雅娟接任独立非执行董事；李红栓不再兼任董事会秘书，袁媛于 2026 年 7 月获聘董事会秘书。[4]

据 2025 年报，赵国庆于 2000 年加入公司，曾任技术研究院副院长、配套管理本部本部长等职务，2010 年 6 月起任副总经理，2022 年 3 月起任副董事长、执行董事；李红栓于 2007 年加入公司，2020 年 6 月起任财务总监，2022 年 3 月起任执行董事；穆峰于 2007 年加入公司，曾负责研发、商品战略与整车业务，2022 年 7 月起任总经理。以上为公司披露的职业履历。[1]

股权与治理

据公司 2026 年 10 月 16 日关联交易公告，控股股东为保定创新长城资产管理有限公司，持有公司约 59.63% 已发行股本；魏建军间接持有长城控股 99% 股权，长城控股持有创新长城 62.85% 股权，魏建军为公司最终实益拥有人和实际控制人。[7] 截至 2025 年 12 月 31 日，公司总股本 8,557,872,087 股，其中 A 股 6,239,096,087 股、H 股 2,318,776,000 股。[1]

竞争格局

我们的判断：竞争范围与差异化基础。按公司披露的 SUV、皮卡、MPV 和新能源乘用车布局，我们将比亚迪、吉利、奇瑞、长安、上汽列为主要跟踪对象，并在皮卡和越野细分市场关注丰田等全球品牌。这是分析所选的竞争范围，不是销量排名。公司 2025 年皮卡销售 178,936 辆、坦克销售 234,442 辆；2025 年报称长城皮卡连续 28 年居中国皮卡销量第一、坦克居中国越野 SUV 销量第一。我们的判断是，皮卡与越野 SUV 的既有产品规模构成差异化竞争的基础。[1]

我们的判断：利润修复仍需观察。公司披露 2025 年单车收入 16.83 万元；同期集团毛利率为 18.04%，归母净利润同比下降 22.07%，2026 年上半年归母净利润同比下降 61.11%。单车收入提高与净利润下降可以同时发生，不能仅凭产品价格带上移判断盈利能力改善。2025 年公司新能源车型全球销量约 40.60 万辆，除以总销量 1,323,811 辆，约为 30.7%；这一公司内部结构比例本身不足以判断转型是否落后于行业。后续应观察同口径销量结构、费用与利润的变化。[10][1][4]

未来展望

公司公开规划。2025 年报（2026 年 3 月 27 日发布）的经营计划提出完善全动力产品矩阵，迭代 Hi4 技术及 VLA 辅助驾驶大模型，并推进「生态出海」。报告还提出以区域深耕、本土化运营、海外工厂及本地供应链配套应对海外经营风险。以上为公司披露的方向，不是已实现的业绩。[1]

我们的判断。2025 年海外销量为 506,787 辆，公司同时推进海外本地产能建设；海外市场需求和工厂利用情况因此是后续观察重点。若本地生产、渠道与供应链协同改善，海外业务可能支持销量增长；若当地需求不足或贸易壁垒增加，产能投入也可能形成负担。国内方面，应观察新能源车型及高价位车型的销售能否转化为利润，而不以不同期间、地域和批零口径的渗透率推出行业落后结论。上述分析以公司披露的产品布局、利润变化和海外业务为依据，结果取决于需求、费用控制与本地化执行。[1][10][13]

风险

  • 公司披露的海外风险。2025 年报将国际地缘冲突、贸易壁垒增多列为海外市场不确定性的来源。[1]
  • 公司披露的国内竞争风险。2025 年报指出，国内汽车市场产品持续丰富，同质化竞争加剧。[1]
  • 利润与毛利率变化。2025 年归母净利润同比下降 22.07%、集团毛利率为 18.04%（2024 年为 19.51%）；2026 年上半年归母净利润同比下降 61.11%。[1][4]
  • 新能源产品结构。2025 年新能源车型约占公司全年全球销量 30.7%；欧拉品牌销售 48,312 辆。[1]
