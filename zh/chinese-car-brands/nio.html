Overview

NIO Inc. (NYSE: NIO; HKEX: 9866; SGX: NIO) is a Shanghai-headquartered smart electric vehicle company founded in November 2014, and the first automaker to list on the New York, Hong Kong and Singapore exchanges.[8] It designs, develops, manufactures and sells premium smart electric vehicles, and earns most of its revenue from vehicle sales, with the remainder coming from energy, accessories and services.[1] The company's stated mission is "Blue Sky Coming", and it positions itself as a user enterprise built around innovative technology and services.[8][1][8][12][22]

The company operates a battery-swapping network (NIO Power) and offers Battery-as-a-Service (BaaS) and related services.[1] It now operates three brands — NIO (premium), ONVO (family/mass market, launched May 2024) and firefly (a small high-end global brand launched December 2024) — plus the NIO MENA joint venture with CYVN for the Middle East and North Africa.[12][22] In 2025 it delivered 326,028 vehicles, up 46.9% year over year, and posted total revenues of RMB87,487.5 million; Q4 2025 was its first profitable quarter, although the full year remained loss-making.[1][1][8][12][22]

Current operations

Business structure

NIO generates the bulk of its revenue from vehicle sales. In FY2025, vehicle sales were RMB76,883.9 million (about 87.9% of total revenues) and other sales — largely energy, accessories and services — were RMB10,603.6 million.[1] The group operates three brands: NIO (premium), ONVO (family/mass market, launched May 2024) and firefly (small high-end, launched December 2024).[8] The NIO brand covers SUVs and sedans such as the ES8, ES6, EC6, ES7/EL7, EC7, ET7, ET5, ET5T and the ET9 and ES9 executive flagships, while ONVO focuses on the mainstream family segment and firefly on small high-end cars.[8] As of 2026-10-29, ONVO's China website lists the L60, L80 and L90 SUVs.[35][36][37] For the Middle East and North Africa, NIO operates through the NIO MENA joint venture with CYVN Holdings, whose first market is the UAE.[22] Its European business is run separately — through its own operations in the Nordic and Western European markets and, since 2025, through local distributors.[26][1][3][6][8][12][14][19][22][23][24][26][31][32][35][36][37]

Core technologies

NIO reports in-house development across 12 technology domains — chips and smart hardware, battery systems, electric drive and high-voltage systems, vehicle engineering, vehicle operating system, connectivity, assisted and intelligent driving, smart cabin, smart power, smart manufacturing, artificial intelligence and global digital operations.[19] Its proprietary 5nm smart-driving chip NX9031 entered mass production in 2025.[31] NX6031 is NIO's in-house developed LiDAR control SoC.[32] It has also released the full-domain vehicle operating system SkyOS and introduced the NIO WorldModel (NWM) smart-driving model and the NADArch 2.0 architecture.[12] The ET9 executive flagship, built on the SkyRide chassis system, carries 1,081 filed and obtained patents; in December 2024 it became China's first production model with a steer-by-wire system approved for mass production.[12] By end-December 2025 NIO had filed for or obtained over 9,600 patents.[8][1][3][6][8][12][14][19][22][23][24][26][31][32][35][36][37]

Energy and battery-swap network

As of 6 February 2026, cumulative battery swaps surpassed 100 million; the 2025 ESG report records 4,877 charging stations and 27,728 charging piles, with a 96% battery-recycling rate under the swap system.[23] Per its 2025 annual report (Form 20-F), NIO operated 3,737 power swap stations worldwide as of 31 December 2025.[6] By comparison, at end-2024 NIO operated 3,054 swap stations (904 on Chinese highways), 4,292 charging stations and 25,096 charging piles, and connected more than 1.77 million third-party chargers.[12] The Battery-as-a-Service (BaaS) model lets users subscribe to the battery separately, lowering the upfront purchase price.[1][1][3][6][8][12][14][19][22][23][24][26][31][32][35][36][37]

Manufacturing

NIO manufactures vehicles in Hefei, Anhui province. Until late 2023 it used JAC's manufacturing services; in December 2023 it signed an agreement to acquire the production equipment and assets of JAC's first (F1) and second (F2) advanced manufacturing bases for about RMB3.16 billion (excluding tax).[14] Separately, NIO disclosed in its FY2023 earnings release that it had completed the filing process for its electric passenger vehicle investment project with the relevant authorities in Anhui Province and had been included in the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology's catalogue of approved manufacturers, giving it independent production qualification.[3] Its China R&D and manufacturing footprint covers Shanghai, Hefei, Beijing, Nanjing, Shenzhen, Hangzhou and Wuhan, with overseas R&D centres in San Jose, Munich, Oxford, Berlin, Budapest, Singapore and Abu Dhabi.[26][1][3][6][8][12][14][19][22][23][24][26][31][32][35][36][37]

Overseas business

NIO entered Europe in 2021 with Norway as its first market.[24] By mid-2025 its models were available in five European markets — Sweden, Norway, the Netherlands, Germany and Denmark.[26] It has continued to expand through local distributors: in June 2025 Hedin Mobility Group was appointed exclusive distributor for the NIO and firefly brands in Belgium and Luxembourg, with go-to-market activities in Belgium starting in September 2025 and Luxembourg to follow in 2026.[26] In the Middle East and North Africa, NIO operates through the NIO MENA joint venture with the UAE as its first market.[22] NIO does not separately disclose its overseas delivery volumes.[1][1][3][6][8][12][14][19][22][23][24][26][31][32][35][36][37]

Founders and leadership

Founder

William Bin Li (Li Bin) is the founder, chairman and chief executive officer. He has been chairman since the company's inception and CEO since March 2018.[21] In 2000 he co-founded Beijing Bitauto E-Commerce Co., Ltd., serving as director and president until 2006, and chaired Bitauto Holdings Limited (formerly NYSE: BITA) from 2010 to 2020; he has also served as a director of Uxin (Nasdaq: UXIN) since July 2021 and of Dida (HKEX: 2559) from 2015 to 2025. He holds a bachelor's degree in sociology from Peking University.[21][6][9][16][20][21]

Key management

  • Lihong Qin — co-founder, director and president since inception. Previously chief marketing officer and executive director of Longfor Properties (2008-2014), deputy general manager of Anhui Chery Automobile Sales & Service (2005-2008) and senior consultant/project manager at Roland Berger (2003-2005).[21][6][9][16][20][21]
  • Yu Qu — chief financial officer, appointed 2024-07-05. Joined NIO in October 2016 (previously senior vice president of finance); before that he held finance roles at Lear Corporation and Johnson Controls (2013-2016) and worked at PwC for ten years.[20][6][9][16][20][21]
  • Feng Shen — executive vice president and chairman of the Quality Management Committee. Joined NIO in December 2017; previously president of Polestar China and global CTO of Polestar, and president of Volvo Cars China R&D.[9][6][9][16][20][21]
  • Xin Zhou — executive vice president, and chairman of the Product Committee since 2017. Joined NIO in April 2015; previously executive director at Qoros Auto and engagement manager at McKinsey.[9][6][9][16][20][21]
  • Ganesh V. Iyer — chief executive officer of NIO US since December 2018.[9][6][9][16][20][21]

Ownership and governance

NIO has a dual-class share structure: Class A ordinary shares carry one vote per share and Class C ordinary shares eight votes per share.[6] According to the 2025 annual report, as of 31 March 2026 founder Li Bin had 7.1% beneficial ownership under SEC rules and 34.0% voting power, while CYVN Investments RSC had 16.7% beneficial ownership and 11.8% voting power. Li Bin's beneficial ownership includes shares issuable upon exercise of options within 60 days and shares held through NIO Users Trust; it is not equivalent to his personal economic interest. The annual report describes Li Bin as having significant influence over major matters but does not identify an actual controller.[6] NIO holds about 91.8% of the controlling interest in NIO Holding Co., Ltd. (NIO China), above the 75.9% agreed at the time of the 2020 Hefei investment.[6] The board of directors comprises chairman William Bin Li; director Lihong Qin; non-executive directors Eddy Georges Skaf and Nicholas Paul Collins; and independent directors Hai Wu, Denny Ting Bun Lee, Yu Long and Yonggang Wen.[16][6][9][16][20][21]

Competition

Main competitors

Our judgment: based on its three brands' positioning, NIO competes in China's premium and mass-market EV segments against Tesla, BYD, Li Auto, XPeng, Zeekr and Huawei-backed brands. Its NIO brand targets the premium segment above RMB300,000, while ONVO competes in the mainstream family SUV segment and firefly addresses the compact segment where it faces Chinese rivals and international small EVs. Our judgment: in the mainstream family SUV segment, ONVO's key competitors include the Tesla Model Y and BYD models.[12][1][8][12][15][16]

Strengths

  • Our judgment: the combination of a battery-swap network, BaaS and in-house technology can differentiate NIO's services. NIO reported 3,054 swap stations at end-2024 and the release of its SkyOS operating system and NWM smart-driving model during 2024.[12][1][8][12][15][16]
  • Our judgment: the three-brand portfolio (NIO, ONVO, firefly), positioned for premium, family and small-car buyers, broadens its addressable market beyond the premium segment.[8][1][8][12][15][16]
  • Recovering profitability metrics: FY2025 gross margin was 13.6% and vehicle margin 14.6%, and Q2 2026 gross margin reached 18.4%.[1][15][1][8][12][15][16]

Weaknesses

  • FY2025 net loss attributable to ordinary shareholders was RMB15,570.7 million.[1][1][8][12][15][16]
  • NIO delivered 326,028 vehicles in 2025.[1][1][8][12][15][16]
  • FY2025 R&D expenses were RMB10,605.0 million.[1][1][8][12][15][16]

Our judgment

Our judgment: the improvement in deliveries, gross margin and operating cash flow is supported by NIO's own disclosures, but its full-year net loss and reliance on external capital indicate that scale and cost control — rather than brand alone — remain the decisive variables in this segment.[1][15][1][8][12][15][16]

Outlook

Company guidance and results

In its FY2025 release (published 10 March 2026), NIO guided for Q1 2026 deliveries of 80,000-83,000 vehicles and total revenues of RMB24,482-25,176 million; that guidance is now historical information, and actual Q1 2026 deliveries of 83,465 were reported in the Q2 2026 release.[1][15] As of the most recent disclosure before the as-of date, Q2 2026 total revenues were RMB32,136.9 million with a gross margin of 18.4% and a US GAAP net loss of RMB528.0 million; H1 2026 revenues reached RMB57.67 billion, and cash reserves stood at RMB56.7 billion as of 30 June 2026.[15] For Q3 2026, NIO guided for deliveries of 108,000-111,000 vehicles and total revenues of RMB33,285-34,051 million; actual Q3 2026 deliveries were 109,178.[15][16][1][15][16][6]

Our analysis

Our judgment: the improvement in volume, margins and operating cash flow is supported by NIO's own disclosed figures, but whether it can sustain full-year profitability depends on conditions that are not guaranteed — continuing to scale ONVO and firefly without eroding NIO-brand pricing, converting battery-swap and technology spending into higher utilisation, and controlling costs while expanding overseas.[15] If deliveries or margins fall short of expectations, continued losses and further fundraising remain possible.[1][1][15][16][6]

Our judgment: NIO's 3,737 swap stations at end-2025 and investment in charging and swapping equipment make station utilisation relevant to spreading infrastructure costs. Whether rising use improves returns also depends on operating costs and future investment requirements.[6][1][15][16][6]

Risks

  • Operating risk: NIO remained loss-making for FY2025, with a net loss attributable to ordinary shareholders of RMB15,570.7 million.[1][1][6][12][30]
  • Competition risk: NIO's 2025 annual report warns that intensifying competition may lead to pricing pressure and margin compression.[6][1][6][12][30]
  • Financial risk: The company has repeatedly raised equity and convertible capital (including a HK$4.03 billion placement and a US$1.16 billion equity offering in 2025). Its annual report warns that further equity financing could dilute existing shareholders.[6][1][6][12][30]
  • Technology and infrastructure risk: NIO's 2025 annual report identifies risks in expanding its battery-swap network relating to funding, construction progress, permits and regional service capacity.[6][1][6][12][30]
  • Overseas risk: European expansion faces the EU's definitive countervailing duties on battery electric vehicles imported from China (imposed in October 2024); NIO does not separately disclose overseas delivery volumes.[30][12][1][6][12][30]
  • Governance risk: As of 2026-03-31, Li Bin had 7.1% beneficial ownership under SEC rules and 34.0% voting power. The beneficial ownership calculation includes shares acquirable within 60 days and shares held through NIO Users Trust, and is not equivalent to his personal economic interest. The annual report warns that the dual-class share structure limits other shareholders' influence over corporate matters.[6][1][6][12][30]

概览

蔚来集团（NIO Inc.）是一家总部位于上海的智能电动汽车公司，2014 年 11 月成立，2018 年 9 月美国存托股份在纽约证券交易所上市，2022 年先后在香港、新加坡上市，成为首家在纽约、香港、新加坡三地上市的汽车企业。[8] 公司设计、开发、制造并销售高端智能电动汽车，收入主要来自整车销售，其余来自能源、配件与服务。[1] 公司以「Blue Sky Coming」为使命，将自己定位为以创新技术与服务为核心的用户企业。[8][1][8][12][22]

蔚来运营换电网络（NIO Power），并提供电池租用服务（BaaS）及相关服务。[1] 公司现经营三大品牌——蔚来（高端）、乐道（家庭/大众，2024 年 5 月发布）、萤火虫（面向全球的小型高端品牌，2024 年 12 月发布），另与 CYVN 成立中东北非合资公司 NIO MENA。[12][22] 2025 年公司交付 32.60 万辆（同比增长 46.9%），营收 874.88 亿元，第四季度首次实现单季盈利，但全年仍处亏损。[1][1][8][12][22]

现状

业务结构

蔚来收入主要来自整车销售。2025 财年汽车销售收入 768.84 亿元（约占营收 87.9%），其他销售收入（主要为能源、配件与服务）106.04 亿元。[1] 公司经营蔚来（高端）、乐道（家庭/大众，2024 年 5 月发布）、萤火虫（小型高端，2024 年 12 月发布）三大品牌。[8] 蔚来品牌覆盖 SUV 与轿车，包括 ES8、ES6、EC6、ES7/EL7、EC7、ET7、ET5、ET5T 以及行政旗舰 ET9、ES9；乐道主攻主流家庭市场，萤火虫面向小型高端车。[8] 截至 2026 年 10 月 29 日，乐道中国官网列示 L60、L80、L90 三款 SUV。[35][36][37] 在中东与北非，蔚来通过与 CYVN Holdings 的合资公司 NIO MENA 运营，首个市场为阿联酋。[22] 其欧洲业务另行运营——通过自身在北欧与西欧市场的直营网络，以及 2025 年起引入的本地经销商合作。[26][1][3][6][8][12][14][19][22][23][24][26][31][32][35][36][37]

核心技术

蔚来披露其在 12 个技术领域开展全栈自研——芯片与智能硬件、电池系统、电驱与高压系统、车辆工程、整车操作系统、车联网、辅助与智能驾驶、智能座舱、智慧能源、智能制造、人工智能与全球数字化运营。[19] 自研 5 纳米智驾芯片 NX9031 于 2025 年实现量产。[31] NX6031 为蔚来自研激光雷达主控芯片。[32] 公司还发布了整车全域操作系统 SkyOS，并推出智驾模型 NIO WorldModel（NWM）与 NADArch 2.0 架构。[12] 基于 SkyRide 底盘系统的行政旗舰 ET9 拥有 1,081 件申请及已授权专利；2024 年 12 月，它成为中国首款获准量产的线控转向量产车型。[12] 截至 2025 年 12 月底，公司累计申请或获得专利 9,600 余件。[8][1][3][6][8][12][14][19][22][23][24][26][31][32][35][36][37]

能源与换电网络

截至 2026 年 2 月 6 日累计换电突破 1 亿次，2025 年 ESG 报告载充电站 4,877 座、充电桩 27,728 根，换电体系下电池回收率达 96%。[23] 据 2025 年年报，截至 2025 年 12 月 31 日全球换电站 3,737 座。[6] 作为对比，截至 2024 年底其运营换电站 3,054 座（其中 904 座位于中国高速）、充电站 4,292 座、充电桩 25,096 根，接入第三方充电桩超 177 万根。[12] 电池租用服务（BaaS）让用户可单独订阅电池，从而降低购车价格。[1][1][3][6][8][12][14][19][22][23][24][26][31][32][35][36][37]

制造

蔚来在安徽合肥制造整车。2023 年底前由江淮汽车代工；2023 年 12 月签约以约 31.6 亿元（不含税）收购江淮第一、第二先进制造基地（F1/F2 工厂）的生产设备和资产。[14] 另据 2023 财年业绩公告，蔚来已完成在安徽省的纯电动乘用车投资项目备案，并已列入工业和信息化部获准生产企业目录，由此获得独立生产资质。[3] 其中国研发与制造布局覆盖上海、合肥、北京、南京、深圳、杭州、武汉等地，海外研发中心位于圣何塞、慕尼黑、牛津、柏林、布达佩斯、新加坡和阿布扎比。[26][1][3][6][8][12][14][19][22][23][24][26][31][32][35][36][37]

海外业务

蔚来 2021 年进入欧洲，以挪威为首个市场。[24] 至 2025 年年中，其车型已在瑞典、挪威、荷兰、德国、丹麦五个欧洲市场销售。[26] 此后通过本地经销商继续扩张：2025 年 6 月，Hedin Mobility Group 获任为蔚来与萤火虫品牌在比利时和卢森堡的独家经销商，按公告比利时自 2025 年 9 月起启动上市销售，卢森堡计划于 2026 年跟进。[26] 在中东与北非，蔚来通过 NIO MENA 合资公司运营，以阿联酋为首个市场。[22] 蔚来未单独披露海外交付量。[1][1][3][6][8][12][14][19][22][23][24][26][31][32][35][36][37]

股权与治理

蔚来采用双重股权结构：A 类普通股每股 1 票，C 类普通股每股 8 票。[6] 据 2025 年年报，截至 2026 年 3 月 31 日，创始人李斌按 SEC 规则实益拥有 7.1%、拥有 34.0% 投票权，CYVN Investments RSC 实益拥有 16.7%、拥有 11.8% 投票权。李斌的实益拥有统计包含 60 日内可行权取得的股份及通过蔚来用户信托持有的股份，不能等同于其个人经济权益。年报表述为李斌对重大事项具有重大影响力，并未将其认定为实际控制人。[6] 蔚来持有蔚来中国（NIO Holding Co., Ltd.）约 91.8% 的控股权益，高于 2020 年合肥注资时约定的 75.9%。[6] 董事会由董事长李斌、董事秦力洪、非执行董事 Eddy Georges Skaf 与 Nicholas Paul Collins，以及独立董事吴海、李廷斌、龙宇、文勇刚组成。[16][6][9][16][20][21]

竞争格局

主要对手

我们的判断：根据三品牌的产品定位，蔚来在中国高端与大众纯电市场与特斯拉、比亚迪、理想、小鹏、极氪及华为系品牌竞争。蔚来品牌主攻 30 万元以上高端市场，乐道进入主流家庭 SUV 细分市场，萤火虫则面向小型车市场，与中国品牌及海外小型电动车展开竞争。我们的判断：在主流家庭 SUV 细分市场，乐道的主要竞品包括特斯拉 Model Y 与比亚迪车型。[12][1][8][12][15][16]

优势

  • 我们的判断：换电网络、BaaS 与自研技术的结合可形成服务差异化。蔚来披露截至 2024 年底运营换电站 3,054 座，并于 2024 年发布 SkyOS 操作系统与 NWM 智驾模型。[12][1][8][12][15][16]
  • 我们的判断：蔚来、乐道、萤火虫分别面向高端、家庭与小型车用户，三品牌矩阵将市场覆盖面拓展至高端以外的细分市场。[8][1][8][12][15][16]
  • 盈利指标回升：2025 年综合毛利率 13.6%、车辆毛利率 14.6%，2026 年第二季度综合毛利率升至 18.4%。[1][15][1][8][12][15][16]

  • 2025 财年研发投入 106.05 亿元。[1][1][8][12][15][16]

我们的判断

我们的判断：交付增长、毛利率回升与经营现金流改善的方向有蔚来自行披露的数据支撑，但 2025 年全年归母净亏损与对外部资本的依赖表明，在这一细分市场，规模与成本控制而非品牌本身仍是决定性变量。[1][15][1][8][12][15][16]

未来展望

公司指引与已公布业绩

在 2025 年财报（2026 年 3 月 10 日发布）中，蔚来指引 2026 年第一季度交付 8.0 万-8.3 万辆、营收 244.82 亿-251.76 亿元；该指引现为历史信息，2026 年第二季度业绩公告已披露第一季度实际交付 83,465 辆。[1][15] 截至研究截止日前最近一次披露，2026 年第二季度营收 321.37 亿元、综合毛利率 18.4%、US GAAP 净亏损 5.28 亿元；上半年营收 576.7 亿元，截至 2026 年 6 月 30 日现金储备 567 亿元。[15] 对 2026 年第三季度，蔚来指引交付 10.8 万-11.1 万辆、营收 332.85 亿-340.51 亿元，实际第三季度交付 109,178 辆。[15][16][1][15][16][6]

我们的分析

我们的判断：销量、毛利率与经营现金流的改善方向有蔚来自行披露的数据支撑，但能否实现全年盈利，取决于若干并不必然成立的条件——在不侵蚀蔚来品牌定价的前提下持续放量乐道与萤火虫、将换电与技术投入转化为更高利用率，并在拓展海外时控制成本。[15] 若销量或毛利不及预期，持续亏损与再融资需求仍可能出现。[1][1][15][16][6]

我们的判断：截至 2025 年底的 3,737 座换电站及充换电设备投入，使站点利用率成为分摊基础设施成本的重要变量。使用量增加能否改善回报，还取决于运营成本及后续投入需求。[6][1][15][16][6]

风险

  • 经营风险：蔚来 2025 财年仍处亏损，归母净亏损 155.71 亿元。[1][1][6][12][30]
  • 竞争风险：蔚来在 2025 年年报中提示，竞争加剧可能带来定价下行压力与利润率压缩。[6][1][6][12][30]
  • 财务风险：公司多次进行股权与可转债融资（2025 年含 40.3 亿港元配售与 11.6 亿美元股权增发）；年报提示，后续股权融资可能稀释现有股东权益。[6][1][6][12][30]
  • 技术与基础设施风险：蔚来在 2025 年年报中列明，换电网络扩张涉及资金、建设进度、许可及区域服务能力等方面的风险。[6][1][6][12][30]
  • 海外风险：欧洲扩张面临欧盟于 2024 年 10 月对中国产纯电动汽车加征的最终反补贴税；蔚来未单独披露海外交付量。[30][12][1][6][12][30]
  • 治理风险：截至 2026-03-31，李斌按 SEC 规则实益拥有 7.1%、拥有 34.0% 投票权。实益拥有统计包含 60 日内可取得的股份及通过蔚来用户信托持有的股份，不能等同于其个人经济权益。年报提示，双重股权结构限制了其他股东对公司事务的影响力。[6][1][6][12][30]
