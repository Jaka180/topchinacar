Overview

Shangjie (SAIC Shangjie, 尚界) is the new-energy vehicle brand jointly created by the state-owned SAIC Motor Corporation (Shanghai Stock Exchange 600104.SH) and Huawei, operating under Huawei's Harmony Intelligent Mobility Alliance (HIMA). It was unveiled on 16 April 2025 as HIMA's fifth 'jie' brand after AITO (Seres), Luxeed (Chery), Stelato (BAIC) and Maextro (JAC), and uses Huawei's Smart Selection (智选车) model, in which Huawei participates deeply in product definition, design, cockpit and driving-assistance technology, marketing and channel, while SAIC handles manufacturing and the supply chain [1][2].

The brand targets the mainstream RMB 150,000-300,000 new-energy segment, below the pricing of the other HIMA brands. Its first model, the mid-size SUV Shangjie H5, launched on 23 September 2025 at RMB 159,800-199,800 and sold about 25,800 units in 2025 [3][18]. Its second model family, the pure-electric coupe Z7 and shooting brake Z7T, launched on 22 April 2026 at RMB 219,800-309,800 and became the brand's volume line [4].

In the first eight months of 2026 the brand wholesaled more than 50,000 units [10]. In September 2026, after AITO's operations were handed to Seres, Huawei said HIMA would focus resources on four brands (Maextro, Stelato, Luxeed and Shangjie), leaving Shangjie as the lowest-priced of the four, and confirmed a mid-to-large family SUV for autumn 2026 [17][25].

Current operations

Business and positioning

Shangjie is SAIC's dedicated channel into the mainstream intelligent new-energy market, positioned below SAIC's own premium IM Motors brand and below the other HIMA brands, which mainly sit above RMB 200,000 [11]. It follows Huawei's Smart Selection model and sells through HIMA channels. As of late September 2026 the brand had nearly 1,000 dealers and about 200 dedicated-network stores, with channel coverage of 75% and near-100% coverage in tier-1 to tier-3 cities [10]. SAIC said it will further push a 'dedicated network + shopping-mall' channel model [10].

Technology

The brand's models are built around Huawei's core technology stack: Huawei Qiankun ADS intelligent driving, the HarmonyOS cockpit, the Tuling chassis platform and the Huawei 'Whale' 800V battery platform [13]. The H5 launched with Qiankun ADS 4, and the refreshed H5 (June 2026) upgraded to ADS 5; the Z7/Z7T carry the 896-line dual-beam image-grade lidar and ADS 4.1, later upgradable to ADS 5 via OTA [3][14][9]. SAIC contributes chassis engineering and manufacturing; Huawei contributes the intelligent-driving and cockpit stack [1].

Manufacturing

Shangjie models are built within SAIC's passenger-vehicle manufacturing system. The Z7/Z7T are produced at SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle's Nanjing Jiangning plant [14]. SAIC is also building a dedicated Shangjie plant in Jinqiao, Shanghai with a planned annual capacity of 200,000 units [14]; in September 2026 SAIC added about RMB 1.0 billion to upgrade it to Industry 4.0 standards [17]. SAIC's initial investment was RMB 6.0 billion with a dedicated team of over 5,000 people [10].

Safety and performance

The Z7/Z7T use a cage body with 91% high-strength steel and aluminium, 14 hot-stamped 2000 MPa steel members in the passenger-cell core structure, and 5.8-times-body-weight roof crush resistance; the 'Whale' 800V battery platform has 15 layers of safety protection; and all versions carry an 896-line lidar plus 36 high-precision sensors. The fastest 0-100 km/h time is 3.44 seconds, 100 km/h-to-zero braking is 33.1 metres, top moose-test speed is 86.2 km/h and minimum turning radius is 5.5 metres [13][9].

Sales trajectory

The H5's monthly sales peaked above 10,600 in November 2025 before declining to about 2,700 by May 2026, and the brand's volume centre of gravity then shifted to the Z7 series. In July 2026 the three models sold 11,017 units combined (Z7 6,225, Z7T 2,917, H5 1,875), and the Z7 series passed 20,000 cumulative deliveries within three months of launch [14]. For the first eight months of 2026, wholesale sales exceeded 50,000 units [17][10]; CPCA-linked data put the figure at about 51,800 units, second within HIMA behind AITO [21].

Geographic scope

Shangjie sells only in the domestic Chinese market, and the brand has disclosed no overseas sales volume, exports or overseas production plans. SAIC's own overseas business is led by brands such as MG, which sold more than 300,000 units in Europe in 2025 [18].

Founders and leadership

Founders and group sponsors

Shangjie has no private founder: it was jointly created by SAIC and Huawei. On the SAIC side, chairman Wang Xiaoqiu and president Jia Jianxu have been the public faces of the project; Jia Jianxu presented the brand at the April 2025 launch and the H5 launch, and Huawei's Richard Yu (Yu Chengdong, Huawei's terminal BG chairman) co-presented [1][3]. The cooperation agreement was signed on 21 February 2025 by SAIC president Jia Jianxu and Huawei Terminal Smart Selection business president Wang Yanmin [2].

Brand-level management

  • Zhu Yong (祝勇) — general manager of Shangjie and deputy general manager of SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle. Media report he became SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle deputy general manager in 2024 and led the SAIC-Huawei cooperation project in that capacity; he appeared publicly as Shangjie general manager, but his exact Shangjie appointment date is not disclosed. Competition

Shangjie competes in China's RMB 150,000-300,000 mainstream new-energy segment, the largest and most price-competitive part of the market. The H5 (RMB 159,800-199,800) competes with the BYD Song Plus and other mainstream family SUVs in the RMB 150,000-200,000 band [11], while the Z7 (RMB 219,800-299,800) is positioned in the RMB 200,000-plus pure-electric sedan segment, where a Sina Finance report places it against the Xiaomi SU7 [18]. Within HIMA it coexists with AITO, Luxeed, Stelato and Maextro, occupying the lowest price band [11]. CPCA-linked data put Shangjie's January-August 2026 sales at about 51,800 units, second within HIMA behind AITO [21]; in July 2026 the AITO brand sold 22,572 units against Shangjie's 11,017 [14].

Our judgment. Shangjie's main strength is the combination of Huawei's intelligent-driving and cockpit stack with SAIC's manufacturing and long vehicle-engineering heritage, at prices below the other HIMA brands [1]. Its main weaknesses are a short track record, small absolute scale relative to top-tier rivals, and single-model dependence: the H5 cooled sharply after its launch peak, and the brand's momentum now rests heavily on the Z7 series [14]. Media have also noted potential positioning overlap with SAIC's own IM brand, whose L6 sits in a similar price band [18].

Outlook

Company plans. Huawei's Richard Yu confirmed on 24 September 2026 that Shangjie's next all-new model is a mid-to-large family SUV to be released in autumn 2026 with Huawei's latest intelligent mobility solutions [25]. Shangjie general manager Zhu Yong said SAIC and Huawei planned four to five models from the outset, and that a new Z7 version would appear in the fourth quarter of 2026 [15]. SAIC said it will continue to expand the Z and H series, and that the Jinqiao dedicated plant (planned 200,000-unit annual capacity) has been funded for an Industry 4.0 upgrade [14][17].

Our judgment. Whether Shangjie sustains its trajectory depends on three premises: first, broadening a line-up still concentrated in the Z7 series and reversing the H5's post-launch decline; second, converting launch-month order momentum into stable flat-period sales in a crowded segment; and third, keeping the brand's Huawei-supplied intelligence current while managing potential overlap with SAIC's own IM brand [14][18]. The planned mid-to-large SUV is a step up the price ladder and will test whether the brand can move beyond the RMB 150,000-300,000 mainstream band it currently occupies [25]. The brand also depends heavily on HIMA channels and on Huawei's driving-assistance brand; with AITO going solo and HIMA refocusing on four brands, the allocation of ecosystem resources is an external variable for Shangjie's future trajectory [25].

Risks

  • Single-model dependence and demand cooling. The H5 peaked above 10,600 monthly units in November 2025 then fell to about 2,700 by May 2026, and the brand's momentum now rests on the Z7 series; a similar cooling cycle would weigh on overall volume [14].
  • Intense price competition. Shangjie operates in the most price-competitive RMB 150,000-300,000 segment, where it faces the BYD Song Plus, Xiaomi SU7 and others [11][18].
  • No separate financial disclosure. As a brand within SAIC, Shangjie does not report standalone financials, so its profitability cannot be assessed independently [6].
  • Channel dependence on HIMA. Sales run through Huawei's HIMA channel; any change in the SAIC-Huawei cooperation or HIMA structure would directly affect the brand's distribution. A July 2026 rumour of Shangjie leaving HIMA, later denied, illustrates the sensitivity [23].
  • Internal brand overlap. Media have flagged potential overlap between Shangjie and SAIC's own IM Motors in the RMB 200,000-250,000 band [18].
  • Management transition. Media reported in late September 2026 that general manager Zhu Yong would be replaced by Gu Yebin; a change of brand leadership during the scale-up phase adds execution uncertainty until a formal announcement is made [22].
  • Investment and payback. SAIC has put RMB 6.0 billion of initial funding plus about RMB 1.0 billion for the Jinqiao plant into Shangjie and formed a dedicated team of over 5,000 people; as a new brand without separate financial disclosure, its payback depends on sustained volume growth, and its profitability cannot be independently verified [10][17].

概览

尚界（SAIC 尚界）是国有控股的上海汽车集团股份有限公司（上交所 600104.SH）与华为联合打造的新能源汽车品牌，隶属华为鸿蒙智行（HIMA）生态。它于 2025 年 4 月 16 日发布，是继问界（赛力斯）、智界（奇瑞）、享界（北汽）、尊界（江淮）之后的鸿蒙智行第五「界」，采用华为「智选车」模式——华为深度参与产品定义、设计、座舱与智驾技术、营销和渠道，上汽负责整车制造与供应链 [1][2]。

品牌瞄准 15 万–30 万元主流新能源市场，价位低于鸿蒙智行其余「界」。首款车型中型 SUV 尚界 H5 于 2025 年 9 月 23 日上市，售价 15.98 万–19.98 万元，2025 年全年零售销量约 2.58 万辆 [3][18]。第二款车型家族——纯电轿跑 Z7 与猎装版 Z7T——于 2026 年 4 月 22 日上市，售价 21.98 万–30.98 万元，成为品牌销量主力 [4]。

2026 年前 8 个月品牌累计批售超 5 万辆 [10]。2026 年 9 月，问界经营主导权交回赛力斯后，华为宣布鸿蒙智行将聚焦尊界、享界、智界、尚界四界，尚界成为其中定位最亲民的品牌，并计划于 2026 年秋季推出中大型家用 SUV [17][25]。

现状

业务与定位

尚界是上汽切入主流智能新能源市场的品牌，定位低于上汽自有的高端品牌智己，也低于鸿蒙智行其余主要布局 20 万元以上的「界」 [11]。品牌采用华为智选车模式，通过鸿蒙智行渠道销售。截至 2026 年 9 月下旬，经销商近 1000 家、专网约 200 家，渠道覆盖率达 75%，一到三线城市近 100% 覆盖 [10]。上汽表示后续将推动「专网＋商超」渠道模式 [10]。

技术与平台

品牌车型围绕华为核心技术矩阵构建：华为乾崑智驾、鸿蒙座舱、途灵平台与华为巨鲸 800V 电池平台 [13]。H5 上市即搭载乾崑智驾 ADS 4，2026 年 6 月新款升级为 ADS 5；Z7/Z7T 搭载 896 线双光路图像级激光雷达与 ADS 4.1，并可通过 OTA 升级至 ADS 5 [3][14][9]。上汽贡献底盘工程与制造，华为贡献智驾与座舱全栈技术 [1]。

制造与产能

尚界车型在上汽乘用车制造体系内生产，Z7/Z7T 由上汽乘用车南京江宁基地生产 [14]。上汽同时在建设上海金桥尚界专属工厂，规划年产 20 万台 [14]，2026 年 9 月追加约 10 亿元按工业 4.0 标准升级 [17]。上汽对尚界首期投入 60 亿元专项资金、组建超 5000 人专属团队 [10]。

安全与性能

Z7/Z7T 采用高强钢与铝合金占比 91% 的笼式车身，14 处乘员舱核心骨架采用 2000MPa 热成型钢，车顶抗压 5.8 倍车重；华为巨鲸 800V 电池平台具备 15 层安全防护；全系标配 896 线激光雷达等 36 个高精度传感器。零百加速最快 3.44 秒、百公里制动 33.1 米、麋鹿测试最高车速 86.2km/h、最小转弯半径 5.5 米 [13][9]。

销量轨迹

尚界 H5 月销于 2025 年 11 月冲高至超 1.06 万辆，随后回落，2026 年 5 月降至约 2700 辆，品牌销量重心此后转向 Z7 系列。2026 年 7 月尚界三款车型合计销量 11017 辆（Z7 6225、Z7T 2917、H5 1875），Z7 系列上市三个月累计交付突破 2 万台 [14]。2026 年 1–8 月累计批售超 5 万辆 [17][10]；乘联分会相关口径约 5.18 万辆，位居鸿蒙智行体系内第二、仅次于问界 [21]。

市场范围

尚界目前面向国内市场销售，品牌层面未披露海外销量、出口或海外生产计划。上汽集团自身的海外业务以 MG 等品牌为主，2025 年 MG 在欧洲销量超 30 万辆 [18]。

竞争格局

尚界在中国 15 万–30 万元主流新能源市场参与竞争，这是规模最大、价格竞争最激烈的区间。H5（15.98 万–19.98 万元）在 15 万–20 万元区间对标比亚迪宋 PLUS 等主流家用 SUV [11]；Z7（21.98 万–29.98 万元）处于 20 万元以上纯电轿车区间，新浪财经报道将其与小米 SU7 对标 [18]。在鸿蒙智行体系内部，它与问界、智界、享界、尊界并存，占据最低价位带 [11]。乘联分会相关数据显示，尚界 2026 年 1–8 月销量约 5.18 万辆，位居鸿蒙智行体系内第二、仅次于问界 [21]；作为对比，2026 年 7 月问界系列合计 22572 辆、尚界 11017 辆 [14]。

我们的判断。尚界的主要优势是把华为智驾与座舱全栈技术与上汽制造及长期的整车工程底蕴结合，且价位低于鸿蒙智行其余品牌 [1]。主要短板是历史短、绝对规模仍小于头部对手，且表现出单一车型依赖：H5 在上市峰值后迅速回落，品牌势头当前高度依赖 Z7 系列 [14]。媒体亦提示其与上汽自有智己品牌存在定位重叠，智己 L6 价位带与之相近 [18]。

未来展望

公司公开规划。华为余承东于 2026 年 9 月 24 日确认，尚界下一款全新车型定位中大型家用 SUV，计划 2026 年秋季发布，将搭载华为最新智慧出行解决方案 [25]。尚界汽车总经理祝勇表示，上汽与华为从合作初期即规划约 4–5 款产品，2026 年四季度将推出 Z7 的一个新版本 [15]。上汽称将持续拓展 Z、H 两大系列，金桥专属工厂（规划年产 20 万台）已获追加资金按工业 4.0 标准升级 [14][17]。

我们的判断。尚界能否延续势头取决于三个前提：其一，拓宽目前仍集中于 Z7 系列的产品线，并扭转 H5 上市后的回落；其二，在竞争拥挤的区间把上市首月订单动能转化为稳定的平销期销量；其三，在保持华为智驾技术领先的同时，处理好与上汽自有智己品牌的潜在定位重叠 [14][18]。规划中的中大型 SUV 是品牌向上的一步，将检验其能否突破目前 15 万–30 万元的主流价格带 [25]。此外，品牌当前高度依赖鸿蒙智行渠道与华为智驾的品牌号召力，在问界单飞、鸿蒙智行聚焦四界的背景下，生态资源分配与品牌协同将是决定尚界后续走势的外部变量 [25]。

风险

  • 单一车型依赖与需求回落。H5 于 2025 年 11 月单月销量一度突破 1.06 万辆，随后回落至 2026 年 5 月约 2700 辆，品牌势头当前依赖 Z7 系列；若出现类似降温周期将拖累整体销量 [14]。
  • 价格竞争激烈。尚界处于 15 万–30 万元这一价格竞争最激烈的区间，面对比亚迪宋 PLUS、小米 SU7 等对手 [11][18]。
  • 无单独财务披露。作为上汽旗下品牌，尚界不单独披露财务，其盈利能力无法独立评估 [6]。
  • 渠道依赖鸿蒙智行。销售通过华为鸿蒙智行渠道进行，上汽与华为合作或鸿蒙智行体系结构的任何变化都会直接影响品牌分销。2026 年 7 月一度出现的「尚界脱离鸿蒙智行」传言（后被否认）即体现这一敏感性 [23]。
  • 内部品牌重叠。媒体提示尚界与上汽自有智己品牌在 20 万–25 万元区间存在潜在定位重叠 [18]。
  • 管理层变动。媒体 2026 年 9 月末报道总经理祝勇将由顾晔斌接替；在规模化阶段更换品牌一把手，在正式公告落地前增加执行不确定性 [22]。
  • 投入与盈利回报。上汽对尚界首期投入 60 亿元、追加约 10 亿元建设金桥工厂，并组建超 5000 人专属团队；作为处于投入期且未单独披露财务的新品牌，其投资回收依赖销量规模持续放大，而盈利状况无法独立验证 [10][17]。
