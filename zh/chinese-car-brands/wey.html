Overview

WEY is a premium brand of Great Wall Motor Company Limited, positioned as a premium intelligent new-energy vehicle brand. The brand was launched in Guangzhou on 16 November 2016 and named after founder Wei Jianjun's surname (WEY is the romanisation of 魏), making it China's first car brand named after its founder; its vertical logo is derived from the flagpole of the Zhili Governor-General's office in Baoding.[5][7][8]

Per the Great Wall Motor 2025 annual report, WEY sold 99,617 vehicles in 2025 (+79.36% year on year), a near-six-year high, built on a "Gaoshan + Lanshan" large six/seven-seat dual-flagship matrix; in December 2025 the all-new Lanshan Intelligent version was launched as the first model to carry the VLA driving-assistance large model.[1] Per the company's June 2026 sales brief, WEY sold 44,514 vehicles in the first half of 2026 (+29.05% year on year).[3] Per the September 2026 brief, January–September 2026 sales were 66,582 vehicles (+4.70%).[25]

WEY is one of Great Wall Motor's five vehicle brands alongside Haval, Tank, ORA and Great Wall Pickup, and carries the group's premiumisation mandate.[1] It entered the RMB 150,000–200,000 band with the fuel VV-series SUVs at launch, repositioned itself in 2021 toward a new-generation intelligent vehicle brand and then toward new energy; its current line-up centres on the Lanshan six-seat SUV, the Gaoshan MPV and the V9X flagship.[4][5]

In 2026 WEY entered a fresh product wave, launching the V9X (May), the V9X Family (July), the V8X (August) and the Gaoshan 8 HEV (October), reshaping its flagship line-up around the Guiyuan S platform.[16][28][27][20]

Current operations

Model line-up and pricing

As of the research cut-off date (2026-10-29), the WEY China website lists, with suggested retail starting prices: the V9X from RMB 349,800 (AI luxury six-seat flagship, in Luxury Family, Luxury Extended and Executive Extended configurations), the all-new Lanshan Intelligent version, the all-new Gaoshan from RMB 285,800 (six/seven seats) and the New Mocha Hi4 Max from RMB 185,800.[4][15] The V8X launched in August 2026 at a limited-time price from RMB 226,800, across Luxury Family and Sport versions targeting the RMB 200,000–300,000 band, and the Gaoshan 8 HEV launched on 26 October 2026 at a limited-time price from RMB 259,800.[27][20] The all-new Lanshan launched on 21 August 2024 (Max RMB 299,800 / Ultra RMB 326,800), and its Intelligent version launched on 22 December 2025 at a limited-time price from RMB 275,800.[22][21]

In 2026 WEY accelerated its model cadence: alongside the V9X and V8X it also launched the V9X Family version (limited-time price from RMB 316,800, July 2026) and the Gaoshan 8 HEV.[28][20] The Gaoshan 8 HEV uses a 2.0T hybrid-specific engine with front and rear electric motors, 322 kW combined power and 782 N·m torque, 0–100 km/h in 5.9 seconds, with a 192-line lidar, an Orin-Y chip and 27 sensors as standard.[20]

Technology and platforms

WEY draws on Great Wall Motor's in-house technologies: the Hi4 intelligent hybrid four-wheel-drive system, the 2.0T super Hi4 hybrid, the VLA driving-assistance large model, Coffee OS cockpit and the Coffee Pilot driving-assistance family. The Guiyuan S platform, activated in 2026 after six years of development, is compatible with fuel, hybrid, PHEV, BEV and hydrogen powertrains with over 90% component commonality, and underpins the V9X and V8X.[16][15] The V9X uses a dual-VLA large-model architecture; the V8X carries the 2.0T super Hi4 with an 800V platform and 6C fast charging, a 66.6 kWh battery for 402 km CLTC electric range and up to 1,659 km combined range.[27] The Guiyuan S platform decomposes the vehicle into 49 core modules and 329 shared components, with 100% commonality in the chassis core structure.[16] The V9X's body uses 88.8% high-strength steel and lightweight alloy.[16]

Production

WEY models are produced within Great Wall Motor's manufacturing network. The brand's 300,000th vehicle rolled off the line at the Xushui plant in Baoding on 22 October 2019, at the time the fastest 300,000-unit milestone for a Chinese premium brand.[5] The company does not publish separate plant-by-plant capacity figures for the WEY brand in its annual report.[1]

Sales performance

WEY's 2025 sales of 99,617 vehicles (+79.36%) was a near-six-year high, driven by the Lanshan and Gaoshan flagships.[1] In 2026 the brand's monthly sales weakened through the year: June 7,191 (−29.48%), July 7,725 (−23.10%), August 7,367 (−8.23%) and September 6,976 (−36.73%), with cumulative growth slowing from +29.05% in the first half to +4.70% in the first nine months.[3][23][24][25] On a longer horizon, per media reports WEY's sales peaked at about 140,000 in 2018, then fell to about 36,000 in 2022 before recovering to about 42,000 in 2023, 55,000 in 2024 and near 100,000 in 2025 (99,617 per the annual report).[18][10][1]

For parent-level context, Great Wall Motor sold 1.324 million vehicles in 2025 (+7.23%), including 506,800 overseas (+11.60%) and 406,000 new-energy vehicles (+26.00%); by brand, Haval sold 761,487 (+7.69%), Tank 234,442, Great Wall Pickup 178,936 (+2.22%) and ORA 48,312. Operating revenue was RMB 222.824 billion (+10.20%) and net profit attributable to shareholders of the parent was RMB 9.865 billion (−22.07%).[1] In H1 2026 the group's operating revenue was RMB 102.101 billion (+10.58%) with net profit attributable to shareholders of the parent of RMB 2.465 billion (−61.11%).[32]

Overseas

The Mocha PHEV (Coffee 01) earned a five-star Euro NCAP result in September 2022 and was sold in Europe.[5] Per media interviews with WEY management in December 2025, the brand plans to release an overseas product line on its new platform and accelerate the Gaoshan family's globalisation, targeting sales in 17 countries and regions by 2027; these are disclosed plans, not completed results.[17][20]

Founders and leadership

Founder and brand origin

WEY was created in 2016 by Great Wall Motor's founder and chairman Wei Jianjun, who named the brand after his own surname, the first such case among Chinese car brands.[5][8] At the V9X launch in May 2026, Wei Jianjun served as the model's first founder spokesperson.[15][16]

Parent chairman and governance

Per Great Wall Motor's 2025 annual general meeting circular, Wei Jianjun (aged 62) graduated from the Party School of the CPC Hebei Provincial Committee in enterprise management in 1999, joined Baoding Great Wall Auto Industrial Company (the company's predecessor) in 1990 as general manager, and has served as chairman since June 2001; he is also chairman and general manager of the major shareholder Baoding Innovation Great Wall Asset Management.[31] Per the 2025 annual report summary, the controlling shareholder Baoding Innovation Great Wall Asset Management holds 59.77% of Great Wall Motor's A shares; Baoding Great Wall Holdings Group holds 62.854% of that vehicle, and Wei Jianjun is the actual controller (holding 58.2% of Baoding Great Wall Holdings Group, 99% of Beijing Lvzhi Chaowan Technology which holds a further 41.2% of the holdings group, and 0.125% of Innovation Great Wall directly); A-share holders hold 13.13% and H-share holders 27.10%.[26] At the June 2026 board re-election, Wei Jianjun was re-elected chairman and Zhao Guoqing re-elected vice chairman.[31]

Current WEY CEO

Per media reports, Zhao Yongpo announced on 21 December 2025 that he was taking over as WEY CEO, and made his first public appearance in the role at the all-new Lanshan Intelligent version launch on 22 December 2025. Zhao Yongpo joined Great Wall Motor around 2000, rose from technical positions, became deputy general manager of the technology centre in 2021, was appointed Haval brand general manager in November 2023, and concurrently headed the ORA brand from February 2025.[11][12][13] As of the research cut-off date, Zhao Yongpo remained WEY CEO, per media reports from August 2026.[19]

CEO succession

Per multiple media reports, WEY has changed CEO frequently since 2016. Media counts differ: some say eight CEOs in nine years, others nine people across eleven appointments. The individuals reported, in order, are: Jens Steingraeber (first CEO, a former Audi executive), Liu Yan, Li Ruifeng, Hu Shujie, Yu Fei, then Li Ruifeng again, Liu Yanzhao (Tank brand CEO, concurrent), Chen Siying, Liu Yanzhao again, Feng Fuzhi, and Zhao Yongpo.[29][11][12][30] Chen Siying left after about eight months in October 2023; Feng Fuzhi (a consumer-electronics background across Sony, Apple, Samsung and Xiaomi, who entered autos in 2016 and later became a Li Auto retail director before joining Great Wall Motor at the end of 2023) first appeared publicly as WEY CEO on 20 May 2025. Per media reports, chairman Wei Jianjun said in December 2025 that the frequent changes were not firings but that successive CEOs felt the pressure was too great themselves. The above covers only public roles and business history.[29][30][13]

Competition

Our judgment: competitive scope. Based on WEY's premium new-energy positioning and its large six-seat SUV (Lanshan / V9X) and MPV (Gaoshan) line-up, we select Li Auto (six-seat SUVs), Denza (MPVs such as the D9), Voyah (Dreamer MPV), Geely's Lynk & Co, Changan's Avatr and Huawei-linked brands (AITO, Luxeed) as the main domestic reference points. This is our analytical peer selection, not a sales ranking.[14][18]

Our judgment: strengths. WEY draws on Great Wall Motor's Hi4 hybrid, VLA driving-assistance and Coffee OS cockpit technologies, giving the Lanshan Intelligent version and V9X differentiated intelligent features. Per the 2025 annual report, the Gaoshan repeatedly topped China's MPV sales charts in 2025.[1] By growth, WEY's 2025 sales rose 79.36% year on year, faster than Haval (+7.69%) and Great Wall Pickup (+2.22%).[1] Per media reports, China's MPV market retail sales were about 417,000 in the first half of 2026 (down 17.9% year on year), and the Gaoshan ranked second in MPV sales with about 34,227 units in the half.[19] Media also reported that WEY's transaction price in the RMB 280,000-plus segment rose to about RMB 293,700, above Audi's reported RMB 256,500.[17]

Our judgment: weaknesses and uncertainties. WEY's absolute scale remains small: media reported about 100,000 units in 2025 versus about 300,000 for Lynk & Co over the same period.[14] Since 2026 sales have fallen back, the Lanshan's monthly sales have slipped below 1,000 units and the Gaoshan has become the single main model, concentrating the brand's sales mix.[18][19] Frequent CEO changes add uncertainty over strategic execution, and the brand publishes no standalone financials, so its independent profitability cannot be assessed from public filings. Per media reports, the V9X sold 1,018 units in May, 1,505 in June and 1,855 in July 2026, not yet at scale.[19][12][1]

Outlook

Company plans. Per the 2025 annual report, WEY is positioned as a premium intelligent new-energy brand meeting family demand through its Gaoshan and Lanshan dual flagships.[1] Per media interviews with WEY management in December 2025, the brand plans to launch a new platform and premium models in 2026, with an overseas product line for the new platform, and to accelerate the Gaoshan family's globalisation, targeting sales in 17 countries and regions by 2027.[17][20] Per media reports, WEY CEO Zhao Yongpo said in August 2026 that the Lanshan is not being discontinued and that a new product is planned for the fourth quarter of 2026. These are disclosed directions and targets, not completed results.[19]

Our judgment. The V9X (Guiyuan S platform flagship) and V8X extend WEY into the RMB 350,000-class six-seat and RMB 200,000-class five-seat segments, forming a fuller product ladder. However, first-half 2026 growth (+29.05%) relied mainly on the single Gaoshan model, and September 2026 sales fell 36.73% year on year, so the brand's near-term momentum is weakening. Whether WEY can hold ground in the premium market depends on sustained demand for the V9X and V8X, a more diversified sales mix and the pace of overseas expansion. This analysis rests on the company's disclosed sales and product plans; outcomes depend on market demand and execution.[3][25][18][19][20]

Risks

  • Frequent CEO turnover. Per media reports, WEY has changed CEO about eight or nine times (across eleven appointments) in nine years, adding uncertainty over strategic continuity.[29][12]
  • Concentrated sales mix. Per media reports, the Gaoshan accounted for the large majority of WEY sales in the first half of 2026, while Lanshan sales fell sharply, creating dependence on a single model.[18][19]
  • Slowing recent sales. September 2026 sales fell 36.73% year on year, and January–September growth slowed to +4.70%, per the company's sales briefs.[25]
  • Intense competition. The large six-seat SUV and new-energy MPV segments are crowded, with rivals including Li Auto, Denza, Voyah and Huawei-linked brands, and pricing pressure is high.[14][18]
  • No separate financial disclosure. WEY does not publish brand-level revenue or profit, so its standalone profitability cannot be assessed from public filings.[1]
  • Overseas uncertainty. WEY's overseas expansion depends on execution in Europe, Australia and Southeast Asia and is exposed to local demand and trade-policy changes.[17][20]
  • Positioning and channel adjustments. The brand name and channel model (direct sales / agency) have been adjusted several times in recent years, and the effect on consumer recognition remains to be seen.[14]
  • Direct-sales channel cost. Per media reports, WEY's full shift to direct sales involved large investment; chairman Wei Jianjun said the direct-sales input was at least RMB 2 billion, pressuring profitability.[14]

概览

魏牌（WEY）是长城汽车股份有限公司旗下的高端品牌，定位高端智能新能源品牌。品牌于 2016 年 11 月 16 日在广州发布，以创始人魏建军的姓氏命名（WEY 为「魏」的罗马化拼写），是中国首个以创始人姓氏命名的汽车品牌；其竖型标识取自保定直隶总督府大旗杆。[5][7][8]

据长城汽车 2025 年年度报告，魏牌 2025 年销售 99,617 辆（同比 +79.36%），创近六年销量新高，以「高山+蓝山」构成的大六/七座双旗舰产品矩阵满足家庭用户需求；2025 年 12 月全新蓝山智能进阶版上市，首搭 VLA 辅助驾驶大模型。[1] 据公司 2026 年 6 月产销快报，魏牌 2026 年上半年销售 44,514 辆（同比 +29.05%）。[3] 据 2026 年 9 月产销快报，2026 年 1–9 月销售 66,582 辆（同比 +4.70%）。[25]

魏牌是长城汽车五大整车品牌之一，与哈弗、坦克、欧拉、长城皮卡并列，承担长城汽车品牌向上与高端新能源突破的任务。[1] 品牌成立初期以 VV 系列燃油 SUV 切入 15 万–20 万元价格带，2021 年起转向新一代智能汽车并逐步聚焦新能源；当前主力车型为蓝山（大六座 SUV）、高山（MPV）及 2026 年基于归元 S 平台上市的旗舰 V9X。[4][5]

2026 年魏牌进入新一轮产品投放期，先后推出 V9X（5 月）、V9X 家庭版（7 月）、V8X（8 月）与高山 8 HEV（10 月），以归元 S 平台重塑旗舰产品线。[16][28][27][20]

车型谱系与定价

截至研究截止日（2026-10-29），魏牌中国官网列示车型及建议零售起售价为：魏牌 V9X 34.98 万元起（AI 豪华六座旗舰，共豪华家庭版、豪华加长版、行政加长版三款配置）、全新蓝山智能进阶版、全新高山 28.58 万元起（6/7 座）、新摩卡 Hi4 Max 18.58 万元起。[4][15] 2026 年 8 月上市的 V8X 限时权益价 22.68 万元起，分豪华家庭版、运动版，卡位 20 万–30 万元价格带；2026 年 10 月 26 日上市的高山 8 HEV 限时权益价 25.98 万元起。[27][20] 全新蓝山于 2024 年 8 月 21 日上市（智驾 Max 版 29.98 万元、智驾 Ultra 版 32.68 万元），智能进阶版于 2025 年 12 月 22 日上市（限时焕新价 27.58 万元起）。[22][21]

2026 年，魏牌产品投放节奏加快：除 V9X、V8X 外，还推出 V9X 家庭版（2026 年 7 月上市，限时权益价 31.68 万元起）与高山 8 HEV。[28][20] 其中高山 8 HEV 搭载 2.0T 混动专用发动机加前后双电机，系统综合功率 322kW、扭矩 782N·m，零百加速 5.9 秒，全系标配 192 线激光雷达、Orin-Y 芯片与 27 个传感器。[20]

技术与平台

魏牌依托长城汽车自研技术：Hi4 智能电混四驱、2.0T 超级 Hi4 混动、VLA 大模型辅助驾驶与 Coffee OS 智能座舱，以及 Coffee Pilot 智驾系列。2026 年启用的归元 S 平台历时 6 年研发，兼容燃油、混动、插混、纯电、氢能五种动力，零部件通用率超 90%，为 V9X、V8X 的平台基础。[16][15] V9X 采用双 VLA 大模型架构；V8X 搭载 2.0T 超级 Hi4 与 800V 平台、6C 快充，66.6 度电池包 CLTC 纯电续航 402 公里、综合续航最高 1,659 公里。[27] 归元 S 平台将整车解构为 49 个核心模块与 329 个共用组件，底盘核心结构 100% 通用。[16] V9X 车身高强钢及轻量化合金占比 88.8%。[16]

生产

魏牌车型在长城汽车生产体系内制造。2019 年 10 月 22 日，魏牌第 30 万辆整车在保定徐水工厂下线，为当时最快达成 30 万辆的中国豪华品牌。[5] 公司在年报中未单独披露魏牌各工厂产能。[1]

创始人与管理层

创始人与品牌渊源

魏牌由长城汽车创始人、董事长魏建军于 2016 年创立，以自己的姓氏命名品牌，为中国汽车品牌中的首例。[5][8] 2026 年 5 月 V9X 上市时，魏建军首次以创始人身份为该车型代言。[15][16]

集团董事长与治理

据长城汽车 2025 年年度股东会相关公告，魏建军（62 岁）1999 年毕业于中共河北省委党校企业管理专业，1990 年加入保定长城汽车工业公司（公司前身）并担任总经理，2001 年 6 月至今任董事长，同时兼任主要股东保定创新长城资产管理有限公司董事长、总经理。[31] 据 2025 年年度报告摘要，控股股东保定创新长城资产管理有限公司持有长城汽车 59.77% A 股；保定市长城控股集团有限公司持有创新长城 62.854% 股权，魏建军为实际控制人（持长城控股 58.2%、持北京绿智潮玩科技有限公司 99%——后者持长城控股 41.2%、直接持创新长城 0.125%）；A 股股东持股 13.13%、H 股股东持股 27.10%。[26] 2026 年 6 月董事会改选中，魏建军续任董事长、赵国庆续任副董事长。[31]

据媒体报道，赵永坡于 2025 年 12 月 21 日宣布接任魏牌 CEO，并于 12 月 22 日全新蓝山智能进阶版上市发布会上首次公开亮相。赵永坡约 2000 年加入长城汽车，从技术岗位起步，2021 年任技术中心副总经理，2023 年 11 月任哈弗品牌总经理，2025 年 2 月起兼任欧拉品牌总经理。[11][12][13] 截至研究截止日，赵永坡仍担任魏牌 CEO（据 2026 年 8 月媒体报道）。[19]

CEO 更迭

据多家媒体报道，魏牌自 2016 年成立以来 CEO 更迭频繁，媒体统计口径不一（有称 9 年 8 位，也有称 9 人 11 任）。据媒体报道的先后顺序，历任者包括：首任 CEO 严思（Jens Steingraeber，原奥迪高管）、柳燕、李瑞峰、胡树杰、余飞，其后李瑞峰复任，刘艳钊（坦克品牌 CEO 兼任）、陈思英、刘艳钊再度兼任、冯复之、赵永坡。[29][11][12][30] 其中，陈思英约任职 8 个月后于 2023 年 10 月离任；冯复之（早年曾在索尼、苹果、三星、小米等消费电子企业任职，2016 年转入汽车行业，曾任理想汽车零售总监，2023 年底加入长城汽车）2025 年 5 月 20 日首次以魏牌 CEO 身份公开亮相。据媒体报道，长城汽车董事长魏建军 2025 年 12 月回应称，魏牌 CEO 更换频繁不是公司辞退对方，而是历任 CEO「自己感觉压力特别大」。以上均为公开职务与商业履历，不含私人信息。[29][30][13]

竞争格局

我们的判断：竞争范围。按魏牌高端新能源定位及其大六座 SUV（蓝山/V9X）与 MPV（高山）产品线，我们将理想（六座 SUV）、腾势（MPV，如 D9）、岚图（梦想家 MPV）、吉利领克、长安阿维塔及华为系（问界、智界）列为主要国内参照对象。这是分析所选的竞争范围，不是销量排名。[14][18]

我们的判断：优势。魏牌依托长城汽车的 Hi4 电混、VLA 智驾与 Coffee OS 座舱技术，蓝山智能进阶版与 V9X 在智能化配置上具有差异化；据 2025 年年报，2025 年高山多次登顶中国 MPV 销冠。[1] 从增速看，魏牌 2025 年销量同比 +79.36%，高于哈弗（+7.69%）与长城皮卡（+2.22%）等集团主力品牌。[1] 据媒体报道，2026 年上半年中国 MPV 市场零售销量约 41.7 万辆（同比 −17.9%），高山上半年累计零售约 34,227 辆，位列 MPV 销量亚军。[19] 媒体还报道，魏牌 28 万元以上区间成交均价升至约 29.37 万元，高于奥迪的约 25.65 万元。[17]

我们的判断：短板与不确定性。魏牌绝对规模仍偏小：据媒体报道，2025 年约 10 万辆，同期领克约 30 万辆。[14] 2026 年以来销量回落，蓝山月销跌至千辆以下，高山成为单一主力，品牌销量结构集中。[18][19] CEO 频繁更迭带来战略执行的不确定性，且品牌无独立财务披露，无法从公开文件评估其独立盈利能力。据媒体报道，V9X 上市后 5 月首销 1,018 辆、6 月 1,505 辆、7 月 1,855 辆，尚未形成规模。[19][12][1]

未来展望

公司公开规划。据长城汽车 2025 年年度报告，魏牌定位高端智能新能源品牌，以「高山+蓝山」双旗舰满足家庭用户需求。[1] 据媒体报道（魏牌管理层 2025 年 12 月接受采访），魏牌 2026 年计划推出全新平台和高端车型，全新平台也将发布海外产品线，并加快高山家族全球化，目标到 2027 年在 17 个国家和地区销售。[17][20] 据媒体报道，魏牌 CEO 赵永坡 2026 年 8 月表示蓝山未停产、也没有停产规划，计划 2026 年四季度推出全新产品。以上为公司披露的方向与目标，不是已实现的业绩。[19]

我们的判断。V9X（归元 S 平台旗舰）与 V8X 的推出，使魏牌向 35 万元级六座与 20 万元级五座市场延伸，形成更完整的产品梯度。但 2026 年上半年销量增长（+29.05%）主要依赖高山单一车型，且 9 月销量同比下滑 36.73%，品牌短期动能正在减弱；能否在高端市场站稳，取决于 V9X、V8X 的持续需求、产品结构的多元化以及海外扩张的落地节奏。上述分析以公司披露的销量与产品规划为依据，结果取决于市场需求与执行。[3][25][18][19][20]

风险

  • CEO 频繁更迭。据媒体报道，魏牌约 9 年间更换 8 至 9 位（共 11 任）CEO，战略执行的连续性存在不确定性。[29][12]
  • 销量结构集中。据媒体报道，2026 年上半年高山占魏牌销量绝大部分，蓝山销量大幅下滑，品牌对单一车型依赖度高。[18][19]
  • 近期销量放缓。据公司产销快报，2026 年 9 月销量同比下滑 36.73%，1–9 月增速放缓至 +4.70%。[25]
  • 竞争激烈。大六座 SUV 与新能源 MPV 市场竞争者众多（理想、腾势、岚图、华为系等），价格竞争激烈。[14][18]
  • 无单独财务披露。魏牌未披露品牌层面营收与利润，无法从公开文件评估其独立盈利能力。[1]
  • 海外不确定性。魏牌海外扩张依赖欧洲、澳大利亚、东南亚等市场落地，面临当地需求与贸易政策变化的风险。[17][20]
  • 定位与渠道调整。品牌名称与渠道模式（直营/代理）近年多次调整，对消费者认知的影响尚待观察。[14]
  • 直营渠道成本。据媒体报道，魏牌全面转向直营模式后投入较大，长城汽车董事长魏建军称直营投入至少 20 亿元，对盈利形成压力。[14]
