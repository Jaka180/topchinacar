Overview

Aion is the mainstream new-energy vehicle brand of Guangzhou Automobile Group (GAC Group, SSE 601238 / HKEX 02238). Its predecessor, GAC New Energy, was established on 28 July 2017 as GAC's carrier for intelligent connected new-energy vehicles, and the brand became independent at the Guangzhou Auto Show on 20 November 2020.[6][13][14] The operating entity is GAC Aion New Energy Automobile Co., Ltd., which brought in 53 strategic investors in a Series-A round in October 2022 (RMB18.294 billion, post-money valuation RMB103.239 billion), leaving GAC Group with 76.89%.[13][16] Aion sells pure-electric and range-extended models mainly in the RMB 70,000-150,000 mainstream band, plus the high-end Hyptec (Hyper) sub-brand.[3]

Aion grew from about 42,000 vehicles in 2019 to a peak of about 480,000 in 2023, driven heavily by ride-hailing fleets, then fell to 374,884 in 2024 and 290,081 in 2025 as the ride-hailing market saturated.[18][2] From late 2025 the brand shifted to a dual-brand strategy (Aion as 'national good car', Hyptec as 'elite car') and a retail-first push; the merged Hyptec-Aion BU sold 181,579 units in H1 2026, up 67.08% year on year with the C-end share above 80%, and 35,494 units in September 2026, up 21.91%, its seventh straight month of year-on-year growth.[10][11][24] Within GAC Group, Aion is the core of the group's own-brand new-energy business and was merged with Hyptec into the 'Hyptec-Aion BU' in December 2025.[1][30]

Current operations

Model line-up and positioning

As of the research cut-off (2026-10-29), Aion's China line-up spans the AION S and RT sedans, the AION Y and V SUVs, the small UT hatchback, the upper-end LX, the i60 (BEV/EREV) and N60, plus the Ray 7 in pre-sale, spanning a mainstream band of roughly RMB 70,000-150,000, plus the upper-end AION LX (about RMB 286,600-469,600).[3] The N60 carries LiDAR and high-level driving assist as standard from RMB 109,800-129,800, with a 4,615 mm body and 2,775 mm wheelbase; within about two months it delivered over 10,000 units and passed 20,000 users in under five months.[5][11][24] The i60 (launched at a limited-time RMB 104,800, 2027 model from RMB 106,800) is the current volume anchor; GAC said it stayed above 10,000 units a month after launch, and the refreshed 2027 model took over 20,000 orders in its first month.[32][11][24] In April 2026 the brand completed a renewal with a new logo and the 'Easy Life' proposition, repositioning around 'fashion, smart, peace of mind' for young families; under the dual-brand strategy Aion is positioned as the 'national good car' while Hyptec focuses on the 'elite car'.[11][20]

Core technology

Aion's technology stack includes the AEP pure-electric platform (AEP 3.0 released November 2022), the Magazine Battery safety system, the Xingling electronic/electrical architecture (upgraded to Xingling 4.0 in 2026), the ADiGO cockpit system and the self-developed GSD driving-assist system.[17][15] The brand self-supplies batteries through Inpai Battery (established October 2022, GAC Aion-controlled; media report a RMB10.9 billion total investment and over 300,000 battery packs produced by October 2026) and e-drives through Ruipai Power.[21][25] GAC says the Magazine Battery 2.0 has logged 50 billion kilometres without a self-ignition incident; GAC's 2025 'Xingyuan EREV' system (oil-to-electricity conversion 3.73 kWh/L, sustained output 85 kW, 5.5 L/100km at low charge) is fitted to the i60 and Hyptec HL.[32] GAC says its upgraded ADiGO GSD driving assist covers 99.9% of road scenarios, and its self-developed amorphous-alloy e-drive (0.025mm ribbon, co-developed with the CAS Dongguan Institute) is used on the RT, N60 and i60.[1][24]

Plants and capacity

Aion's main base is the Guangzhou Panyu intelligent new-energy eco-plant; after the second smart manufacturing centre was completed in October 2022, planned annual capacity reached 400,000 units (up to 600,000 in multi-shift operation), of which 200,000 units is dedicated to the AION Y Plus.[17] The refitted former GAC Mitsubishi Changsha plant was completed and started production on 21 August 2024.[7] Overseas, the GAC Aion Thailand smart factory in Rayong (Eastern Economic Corridor) opened on 17 July 2024 with a THB2.3 billion investment and a 50,000-unit first phase, expandable to 100,000 units; it produces the second-generation AION V and AION Y Plus, and Aion has said an Indonesia plant was planned as its second overseas base.[8][9][39]

Overseas business

GAC said its business covered 86 countries and regions by end-2025 with more than 630 outlets; H1 2026 group self-brand exports reached 121,500 units, up 132% year on year, with Hyptec-Aion models present in Singapore, Thailand, Colombia, Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Greece (where it ranked second and third in the pure-electric segment in July and August 2026).[1][40][41] The second-generation AION V won Euro NCAP and ANCAP five-star ratings and entered European markets including the UK and Spain.[12] The first stage of channel integration for the Hyptec-Aion BU was completed, with 254 stores across 147 cities upgraded for dual-brand operation.[41]

Founders and leadership

Ownership and governance

Aion is not a separate listed company. It is operated by GAC Aion New Energy Automobile Co., Ltd., a GAC-controlled subsidiary. After the October 2022 Series-A financing (53 strategic investors, 17.72% of shares), GAC Group's direct and indirect holding fell from 93.45% to 76.89%, and Aion remained GAC's controlled subsidiary.[13][16] On listing, GAC said in mid-2025 it would 'proceed with capitalisation at the right time'; in August 2026 GAC told investors Aion was 'focusing on its main business' without confirming IPO progress; in September 2026 financial media reported, citing a Beijing Equity Exchange document, that China Cinda was transferring part of its Aion stake while Aion prepared for a Hong Kong IPO.[27][28]

Current management

The chairman and legal representative of GAC Aion is Xia Xianqing (閤先庆), who took over from GAC Group chairman Feng Xingya in a business-registration change in June 2025. Xia (born December 1973, bachelor's degree) was appointed general manager of GAC Group on 16 November 2025; he previously served as GAC Group deputy general manager, head of its strategic development division, chairman and general manager of GAC Trading, and deputy general manager and head of sales at GAC Honda. GAC's 2025 annual report lists his current posts as executive director and general manager, and concurrently chairman of GAC Toyota, chairman of GAC Aion, chairman of Qijing Auto, vice chairman of GAC Toyota Engine and a director of GAC International. The report's glossary identifies Qijing Auto as Qijing Intelligent Automobile Technology (Guangzhou) Co., Ltd., formerly Huawang Automobile Technology (Guangzhou) Co., Ltd.[26][38][44] Gu Huinan (古惠南), Aion's founding general manager since 2017, retired at the statutory age in March 2025.[22] China National Radio reported on 18 December 2025 that Zhang Xiong (张雄) would serve as president of the Hyptec-Aion BU; that account is a media report, not a board announcement. The same report says public information shows he graduated from Tsinghua University in 2007 and later joined the GAC R&D Institute as an engineer.[33]

Founding management

Gu Huinan joined Guangzhou Peugeot after graduating in 1987, then moved to the GAC Toyota engine plant and became a co-founder of GAC Trumpchi in 2007; from 2017 he led GAC Aion and grew its sales to about 480,000 in 2023.[31][22][18]

Competition

Our judgment: the competitive field. By Aion's RMB 70,000-150,000 mainstream pure-electric and EREV line-up, its main rivals include BYD's Dynasty/Ocean models, Geely Galaxy, Changan's Deepal and Nevo, Leapmotor, and Xiaomi's entry models, plus the SAIC-GM-Wuling and other small-EV lines in the entry segment. This is an analytical selection, not a sales ranking.[20][15]

Our judgment: strengths. Aion retains GAC's full-stack manufacturing base, a self-supplied battery and e-drive chain (Inpai Battery, Ruipai Power), a state-owned group's capital and channel support, and an early overseas manufacturing footprint in Thailand.[21][8] The i60 and N60 show the brand can re-gain momentum with retail-targeted products: H1 2026 sales rose 67.08% year on year with the C-end share above 80%, and September 2026 retail sales rose 21.91% year on year for the seventh straight month.[11][24]

Our judgment: weaknesses. The ride-hailing label is slow to fade: in 2023 about 45% of Aion's sales were in the online-hailing market, and the brand's earlier upmarket attempt (the LX) gained little traction, leaving it concentrated in the low-margin mainstream band.[20] Sales fell for two consecutive years (2024 and 2025) before the 2026 rebound, and the brand remains smaller than BYD and several new-energy rivals in absolute volume; the 2026 rebound also relies heavily on the two new products (i60 and N60), so the line-up is not yet balanced.[2]

Outlook

Company plans. Aion's disclosed direction is to deepen the dual-brand strategy ('Aion as the national good car, Hyptec as the elite car'), push the retail share higher, and expand overseas manufacturing and export. GAC's H1 2026 report said the group would launch several key models in H2 2026 including the Aion Ray 7, the first model of the new Ray series, which opened its pre-sale programme in September 2026.[34][4] Media reported that the Hyptec-Aion BU set a 2026 full-year sales goal of 350,000 units and delivered 281,494 units in January-September 2026, about 80.4% of that goal.[41] Aion previously set a long-term target, reported by media in 2022, of exceeding 1.5 million vehicles of annual production and sales by 2030; this is a historical target statement, not a committed result.[18] On listing, GAC has said Aion will 'proceed at the right time' and, more recently, that Aion is 'focusing on its main business', so the IPO timetable is not confirmed.[28]

Our judgment. The 2026 rebound rests on the i60 and N60 and the incoming Ray 7; whether it is durable depends on sustaining the i60/N60 run, converting the Ray series into volume, and turning overseas plants (Thailand first) into profitable local sales amid import-duty and cost pressures. This is our analysis, not a forecast of financial results.[24][8]

Risks

  • New-product reliance. The 2026 rebound depends heavily on the i60 and N60; the line-up is not yet balanced, so fading momentum in these models could pull sales back down.[24]
  • Ride-hailing dependence. Aion's past growth leaned on the ride-hailing market, which has been saturating; about 45% of 2023 sales were in that channel.[20]
  • Incomplete separate financial disclosure. GAC Group's table of principal subsidiaries discloses the operating company's sales revenue (RMB37.421 billion in 2024 and RMB28.384 billion in 2025), but net profit, gross margin and R&D are not broken out, so standalone profitability cannot be read from a full income statement.[43][44]
  • Parent-group loss. GAC Group's audited net loss attributable to owners of the parent was RMB8.784 billion in 2025 (the board approved the annual report on 27 March 2026), after a 2024 profit attributable to owners of the parent of RMB824 million, reflecting the wider profitability pressure on the own-brand new-energy business that Aion sits within.[44][43]
  • Mainstream price-band margin pressure. The brand competes mostly in the RMB 70,000-150,000 band against BYD and others, where price competition is intense.[20]
  • Overseas cost and policy risks. Aion's Thai production carries local cost and subsidy-rollback pressures, and export growth faces import-duty and local-competition uncertainty.[8][15]
  • Management transition. The founding general manager retired in March 2025 and the marketing leadership changed in September-December 2025, adding execution uncertainty during the brand renewal.[22]

概览

埃安（Aion）是广州汽车集团股份有限公司（广汽集团，上交所 601238／港交所 02238）旗下的主流新能源汽车品牌。其前身广汽新能源于 2017 年 7 月 28 日成立，是广汽发展智能网联新能源汽车的载体，2020 年 11 月 20 日在广州车展上宣布品牌独立。[6][13][14] 运营主体为广汽埃安新能源汽车股份有限公司，2022 年 10 月完成 A 轮融资、引入 53 名战略投资者（融资 182.94 亿元、投后估值 1032.39 亿元），广汽直接及间接持股降至 76.89%。[13][16] 埃安主销约 7 万-15 万元主流价位的纯电与增程车型，另有高端子品牌昊铂（Hyptec）。[3]

埃安销量从 2019 年约 4.2 万辆增长到 2023 年约 48 万辆的峰值，主要依靠网约车市场拉动，随后在网约车市场饱和的背景下回落至 2024 年 374,884 辆、2025 年 290,081 辆（同比 -22.62%）。[18][2] 2025 年底起，品牌转向「埃安国民好车、昊铂精英座驾」的双品牌战略并主攻 C 端零售，2026 年上半年合并口径的昊铂埃安 BU 累计销量回升至 181,579 辆，同比增长 67.08%，C 端用户占比超 80%；2026 年 9 月终端销量 35,494 辆，同比增长 21.91%，连续 7 个月同比正增长。[10][11][24] 在广汽集团内部，埃安是自主新能源业务的核心，2025 年 12 月与昊铂合并组建「昊铂埃安 BU」。[1][30]

现状

车型谱系与定位

截至研究截止日（2026-10-29），埃安国内在售车型包括 AION S、RT 轿车，AION Y、V 两款 SUV，小型两厢车 UT，上攻车型 LX，以及 i60（纯电／增程）、N60 和开启先享计划的 Ray7，整体覆盖约 7 万-15 万元主流价位，另有上攻车型 AION LX（约 28.66 万-46.96 万元）。[3] N60 全系标配激光雷达与高阶智驾，指导价 10.98 万-12.98 万元，车长 4615 mm、轴距 2775 mm，上市约两个月交付突破万辆、不足 5 个月用户突破 2 万。[5][11][24] i60（上市限时焕新价 10.48 万元起、2027 款 10.68 万元起）是当前销量主力：广汽称其上市后月销持续破万辆，2027 款焕新上市首月订单突破 2 万辆。[32][11][24] 2026 年 4 月品牌完成焕新，启用新 LOGO 与「智悦生活 Easy Life」主张，重新聚焦「时尚、智能、安心」，瞄准年轻家庭用户；双品牌战略下，埃安主打「国民好车」、昊铂聚焦「精英座驾」。[11][20]

核心技术

埃安的技术栈包括 AEP 纯电专属平台（2022 年 11 月发布 AEP 3.0）、弹匣电池安全技术、星灵电子电气架构（2026 年升级至星灵架构 4.0）、ADiGO 智能座舱与自研 GSD 智驾系统。[17][15] 品牌通过因湃电池（2022 年 10 月成立，由广汽埃安控股，媒体称总投资 109 亿元、截至 2026 年 10 月累计电池包下线突破 30 万套）与锐湃动力自研自产电池与电驱。[21][25] 广汽称弹匣电池 2.0 已达成 500 亿公里 0 自燃纪录；广汽 2025 年发布的「星源增程」技术（油电转化率高达 3.73kWh/L、持续发电功率 85kW、亏电油耗 5.5L/100km）已搭载于 i60 与昊铂 HL。[32] 广汽称其升级后的 ADiGO GSD 智驾系统已覆盖 99.9% 道路场景；自研非晶合金电驱（0.025mm 薄带，与中科院东莞材料所联合攻关）已应用于 RT、N60、i60 等车型。[1][24]

工厂与产能

埃安主基地为广州番禺智能新能源汽车生态工厂，2022 年 10 月第二智造中心竣工后规划年产能达 40 万辆（多班生产最高 60 万辆），其中 20 万辆产能专注于 AION Y Plus 车型生产。[17] 接管改造的原广汽三菱长沙工厂于 2024 年 8 月 21 日竣工投产。[7] 海外方面，广汽埃安泰国罗勇智能工厂（位于东部经济走廊）2024 年 7 月 17 日竣工投产，总投资 23 亿泰铢、一期年产能 5 万辆、可扩能至 10 万辆，生产第二代 AION V 与 AION Y Plus；埃安曾表示印尼工厂是其规划中的第二个海外基地。[8][9][39]

海外业务

广汽称截至 2025 年底业务已覆盖 86 个国家和地区、网点超 630 个；2026 年上半年集团自主品牌出口达 12.15 万辆、同比增长 132%，昊铂埃安车型已进入新加坡、泰国、哥伦比亚、沙特、阿联酋、希腊等市场（其中 2026 年 7 月、8 月在希腊纯电细分市场分列第二、第三）。[1][40][41] 第二代 AION V 已获 Euro NCAP、ANCAP 双五星安全认证，登陆英国、西班牙等欧洲市场。[12] 昊铂埃安 BU 第一阶段渠道融合已完成，全国 147 个城市 254 家门店完成双品牌融合升级。[41]

创始人与管理层

股权与治理

埃安并非单独上市公司，运营主体为广汽集团控股的广汽埃安新能源汽车股份有限公司。2022 年 10 月 A 轮融资（53 名战投、释放 17.72% 股份）后，广汽集团直接及间接持股由 93.45% 降至 76.89%，埃安仍为广汽集团控股子公司。[13][16] 就上市，广汽集团 2025 年年中称「等待合适时机推进资本化进程」，2026 年 8 月在投资者互动中称埃安「聚焦主业」、未确认 IPO 进展；2026 年 9 月有财经媒体援引北京产权交易所招商文件称，中国信达正挂牌转让所持埃安部分股权、埃安正推进港股 IPO。[27][28]

现任管理层

广汽埃安法定代表人、董事长为閤先庆，2025 年 6 月工商变更中接替广汽集团董事长冯兴亚出任。閤先庆 1973 年 12 月生、大学本科学历，2025 年 11 月 16 日获聘为广汽集团总经理；此前曾任广汽集团副总经理、战略发展本部本部长，广汽商贸董事长、总经理，广汽本田副总经理、销售本部本部长。广汽集团 2025 年年报列其现任为公司执行董事、总经理，兼任广汽丰田董事长、广汽埃安董事长、启境汽车董事长、广汽丰田发动机副董事长、广汽国际董事。年报释义写明，启境汽车指启境智能汽车科技（广州）有限公司，前称华望汽车技术（广州）有限公司。[26][38][44] 埃安首任总经理古惠南于 2025 年 3 月到龄退休。[22] 央广网 2025 年 12 月 18 日报道，张雄将担任昊铂埃安 BU 总裁；该说法来自媒体报道，不是董事会公告。同一报道称，公开信息显示张雄 2007 年毕业于清华大学，后加入广汽研究院成为工程师。[33]

创始管理层

古惠南 1987 年毕业后进入广州标致，后转战广汽丰田发动机工厂，2007 年成为广汽传祺联合创始人；2017 年起执掌广汽埃安，带领品牌销量增长至 2023 年约 48 万辆。[31][22][18]

竞争格局

我们的判断：竞争范围。按埃安 7 万-15 万元主流的纯电与增程产品线，其主要对手包括比亚迪王朝／海洋系列、吉利银河、长安深蓝与启源、零跑、小米入门车型，以及入门级市场的上汽通用五菱等小车产品。此为分析所选的竞争范围，不是销量排名。[20][15]

我们的判断：优势。埃安保有广汽集团的全栈制造基础、自供的电池与电驱链条（因湃电池、锐湃动力）、国有集团的资本与渠道支持，以及泰国等地的早期海外制造布局。[21][8] i60 与 N60 表明品牌能以零售导向产品重新起量：2026 年上半年销量同比增长 67.08%，C 端占比超 80%；2026 年 9 月终端销量同比增长 21.91%，连续 7 个月同比正增长。[11][24]

我们的判断：短板。网约车标签消退较慢：2023 年约 45% 的销量来自网约车市场，此前的高端上攻（LX）成效有限，使品牌集中于利润率较低的主流价位。[20] 销量在 2026 年回升前连续两年（2024、2025）下滑，绝对规模仍小于比亚迪及多家新能源竞品；且 2026 年的回升主要依赖 i60、N60 两款新品的零售放量，产品结构尚未恢复均衡。[2]

未来展望

公司公开规划。埃安披露的方向是深化「埃安国民好车、昊铂精英座驾」的双品牌战略、提升 C 端零售占比，并扩大海外制造与出口。广汽集团 2026 年半年报称，下半年将推出包括埃安 Ray7 在内的多款重点车型；Ray7 为全新 Ray 系列首款车型，2026 年 9 月已开启先享计划。[34][4] 据媒体报道，昊铂埃安 BU 2026 年全年销量目标为 35 万辆，2026 年 1-9 月累计交付 281,494 辆，完成约 80.4%。[41] 埃安曾提出长期产销目标：据媒体报道，其 2022 年提出到 2030 年产销突破 150 万辆。此为历史目标表述，而非已兑现的业绩。[18] 就上市，广汽集团曾表示埃安将「等待合适时机推进」，近期又称埃安「聚焦主业」，IPO 时间表尚未确认。[28]

我们的判断。2026 年的回升建立在 i60、N60 与即将上市的 Ray7 之上；能否持续，取决于能否稳住 i60／N60 的势头、把 Ray 系列转化为销量，并在进口关税与成本压力下将海外工厂（以泰国为先）转化为有盈利的本地销量。以上为我们的分析，不构成对财务结果的预测。[24][8]

风险

  • 新品依赖。2026 年的回升高度依赖 i60、N60 两款新品，产品结构尚不均衡，若新品热度消退，存在销量回落风险。[24]
  • 网约车依赖。埃安过去的增长倚重网约车市场，而该市场正趋于饱和；2023 年约 45% 的销量来自这一渠道。[20]
  • 财务披露不完整。广汽集团主要控股公司表已列示运营主体销售收入（2024 年 374.21 亿元、2025 年 283.84 亿元），但净利润、毛利率、研发投入未单列，无法从完整利润表评估其独立盈利能力。[43][44]
  • 母公司亏损。广汽集团 2025 年经审计归母净利润为亏损 87.84 亿元（董事会于 2026 年 3 月 27 日批准年报），2024 年归母净利润为盈利 8.24 亿元，反映出埃安所处的自主新能源业务的整体盈利压力。[44][43]
  • 主流价位毛利压力。品牌主要在 7 万-15 万元区间与比亚迪等竞争，价格竞争激烈。[20]
  • 海外成本与政策风险。埃安泰国生产面临本地成本与补贴退坡压力，出口增长受进口关税与当地竞争不确定性影响。[8][15]
  • 管理层更替。创始总经理 2025 年 3 月退休、营销负责人 2025 年 9-12 月更替，品牌焕新期存在执行层面的不确定性。[22]
