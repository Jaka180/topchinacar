Overview

Bestune (奔腾, BESTUNE) is the mainstream self-owned passenger-car brand of China FAW Group Co., Ltd., the state-owned automaker headquartered in Changchun, Jilin. The brand first appeared on 18 May 2006 and launched its first model, the B70 mid-size sedan built on the Mazda 6 platform, in August of that year.[8][16] Within FAW, Bestune sits alongside the premium Hongqi brand and the commercial-vehicle Jiefang brand, under the group's stated direction of 'doing Hongqi well, strengthening Jiefang and enlarging Bestune'.[3]

In 2025 Bestune sold 200,017 vehicles (+33% year on year), a record for the brand, of which 171,957 were new-energy vehicles (+107%), with NEV penetration above 85%.[1][2] In September 2025 it completed an equity-diversification reform that raised RMB 8.55 billion in total. Four outside investors contributed RMB 3.31 billion (Yueda Auto Group RMB 1.71 billion, ABC Financial Asset Investment RMB 1.0 billion, China Telecom RMB 500 million and Nanjing Horizon RMB 100 million), and the controlling shareholder, China FAW Co., Ltd., contributed a further RMB 5.24 billion. China FAW Co., Ltd. and FAW Equity Investment (Tianjin) together held 79.0394% afterwards.[3][10] Its core products are the Yueyi (JOYEE) NEV series and the Bestune Xiaoma mini EV, with legacy B-series sedans and T-series SUVs still exported to Gulf markets.[7]

In 2026 the picture turned: media reported H1 2026 domestic retail of about 39,000 units, down about 35% year on year as the Xiaoma slowed after subsidy cuts, prompting the brand to put its weight behind the newly independent Yueyi brand and the Yueyi 08 flagship launched in July 2026.[18][6]

Current operations

Business structure and model line-up

Bestune operates within FAW as a mainstream passenger-car brand now centred on NEVs. Per the official 2025 sales release, its sales split into 171,957 NEVs and the remainder fuel vehicles, with the Yueyi series (Yueyi 03 and Yueyi 07) and the Bestune Xiaoma as the main NEV products.[1] The Yueyi 03 pure-electric SUV launched on 27 March 2025 at a limited-time price of RMB 92,900-117,900, and the Yueyi 07 super-hybrid SUV launched on 26 May 2025 from RMB 99,800.[27][26] The Bestune Xiaoma mini EV launched on 28 May 2024 at RMB 26,900, 29,900 and 31,900, and its 2026 model year (launched 27 July 2025) is priced at RMB 34,900-45,900.[29][23] On 29 May 2026 Yueyi was upgraded to China FAW's first independent NEV brand, and the flagship Yueyi 08 B+-class sedan went on sale on 12 July 2026 in range-extended and 565 km/650 km pure-electric versions. FAW's headline is 'on sale from RMB 99,900' and the chart is captioned 'launch price benefits'; the body does not state a guide price of RMB 99,900-139,900. Autohome reported the price as still RMB 104,900-144,900, or RMB 99,900-139,900 after benefits. Xinhua Jilin reported an official guide price of RMB 99,900-139,900.[6][19][30]

The brand's NEV transition is measurable. In 2023 FAW set an 'All in NEV' strategy positioning Bestune as a 'new mainstream' brand; 2024 sales were 150,777 units (+25%) including 82,872 NEVs (+214%) for 55% NEV penetration, and 2025 rose to 200,017 with penetration above 85%.[18][1] Media reported Bestune was loss-making after its 2019 spin-off, returning above 100,000 units in 2023 and reaching about 151,000 in 2024.[9] After the May 2026 upgrade, the Yueyi 03, 07 and 08 moved under the independent Yueyi brand, while the Bestune name continues to carry the Xiaoma mini EV and the B-series and T-series fuel models.[5]

Technology and platforms

At the February 2025 Yueyi Tech Day, Bestune unveiled the Yueying platform, the Zhuri powertrain (with drive efficiency up to 95.5%) and the Ruyi cockpit; the Yueyi 07 carries FAW's BMP super-hybrid technology.[4][1] All Yueyi products sit on the Yueying platform with Zhuri powertrains across pure-electric, hybrid and range-extended routes, plus the Ruyi cockpit and a 'Lingzhi' assisted-driving stack developed with Huawei and Horizon Robotics.[27] The Yueyi 08 flagship uses a 202 kW rear motor, an 800V platform with 6C fast charging (30%-80% in 7.5 minutes), a Qualcomm Snapdragon 8295P dual-NPU cockpit and the Huawei Qiankun assisted-driving computing platform paired with lidar, supporting 24 assisted-driving functions.[6] FAW's global R&D network, which brings together about 19,000 R&D staff and over 16,000 patents, underpins this platform, powertrain and cockpit work.[4]

Manufacturing and channels

Bestune runs a dual Changchun-Yancheng manufacturing base with total design capacity of 350,000 vehicles a year. The Yancheng base was built and put into production in nine months and produced 170,000 vehicles in 2025 at a stated 240% utilisation rate; per earlier reporting its planned capacity was to exceed 150,000 a year by end-2025.[1][9] By end-2025 its nationwide authorised network exceeded 500 outlets, of which 300-plus were NEV-dedicated stores or zones.[1]

In H1 2026 the brand's momentum reversed. Media citing CPCA data reported domestic retail of about 39,000 units in the half, down about 35% year on year, with the Xiaoma at about 26,000 units and the Yueyi 03 averaging under 3,000 a month.[18] As the fuel line-up moved overseas, FAW shifted the domestic business to the independent Yueyi brand and began replacing store signage from 'FAW Bestune' to 'FAW Yueyi'; media noted only three outlets in Beijing (Shijingshan, Shunyi and Miyun).[18] FAW also introduced the 'Joy Pai' user brand, iterated its '518 service rights 4.0' and launched the 'Yueyi 321' service commitment offering lifetime warranty on the whole vehicle and the three-electric (battery, motor, electronic-control) system.[1]

Overseas business

In 2025 FAW established a Bestune international company to systemise its overseas expansion, focusing on Eastern Europe and the Gulf.[1] At the December 2025 Riyadh International Auto Show, Bestune showed the JOYEE series alongside its fuel models and signed memoranda with the GOCAB and HANCO groups, planning a dual fuel-plus-NEV line-up in Saudi Arabia.[7]

Founders and leadership

Brand ownership and governance

Bestune is not a separately listed company. Its operating entity, FAW Bestune Automobile Co., Ltd., is controlled by China FAW, which held 79.0394% after the September 2025 capital increase (via FAW Co., Ltd. and FAW Equity Investment). The other shareholders are Yueda Auto Group (second largest; the First Financial/JW View report put it at 10.3358%, while the Daily Economic News reported 11.2897%), ABC Financial Investment (6.0443%), China Telecom (3.0222%) and Nanjing Horizon Robotics (0.6044%).[3][9][10] Adding the same other stakes (China FAW 79.0394%, ABC Financial Investment 6.0443%, China Telecom 3.0222%, Nanjing Horizon 0.6044%) to the Daily Economic News figure of 11.2897% equals 100.0000%. Adding them to the JW View figure of 10.3358% equals about 99.05%. The two Yueda figures do not belong in one cap table that sums to 100%; the Daily Economic News set is the one that does. There is no public 'actual controller' statement for the unlisted brand; the shareholding and voting structure above is the relevant disclosure.

Competition

Our judgment: competitive scope. With the Yueyi series priced from about RMB 92,900 to 139,900 and the Xiaoma mini EV at about RMB 26,900-45,900, Bestune competes in China's roughly 70,000-150,000-yuan mainstream NEV segment and the A00 mini-EV segment.[6][18] In the mainstream band it faces BYD, Geely, Changan (Deepal/Qiyuan), Leapmotor and XPeng; in the mini-EV band it faces Wuling (Hongguang MINI EV) and Geely (Xingyuan). The Yueyi 08 enters the 100,000-yuan-class NEV sedan segment against same-class pure-electric and range-extended sedans.[12] This is our analytical selection, not a sales ranking.

Our judgment: strengths. Bestune has a state-owned 'national team' backing from FAW, a dual Changchun-Yancheng manufacturing base with 350,000-unit design capacity, and 2025 volume of 200,017 units with NEV penetration above 85%.[1] Its December 2025 entry into the Saudi market with the JOYEE series gives it an early overseas NEV channel in the Gulf.[7]

Our judgment: weaknesses. The brand depends heavily on the Xiaoma mini EV, which media said contributed 65% of 2025 volume (about 55% if computed against 200,017 units); when national NEV subsidies were cut in 2026, Xiaoma sales fell sharply and pulled the brand's domestic retail down about 35% in H1 2026.[18] The Yueyi series has not yet reached mainstream scale: media reported the Yueyi 03 averaged below 3,000 units a month in H1 2026, and the Yueyi 07 averaged under 500 a month in its eight months on sale in 2025, with 227 and 170 units in the first two months of 2026.[18]

Outlook

Company plans. At the February 2026 global partner conference, FAW group general manager Liu Yigong said Bestune must charge toward the 600,000-unit class, and the brand released its 'Optimus Prime Twin-Engine' global strategy.[13] The independent Yueyi brand and its Yueyi 08 flagship are the main vehicles for the push into the RMB 100,000-150,000 mainstream NEV market; at the Yueyi 08 launch the company stressed a 'good and affordable' NEV positioning.[12][19] These are disclosed directions and targets, not completed results.

At group level, FAW's 2030 plan sets 'six over' metrics: vehicle sales, intelligent-connected NEV sales, self-owned-brand sales, self-owned-brand intelligent-connected NEV sales and overseas sales are to exceed 5 million, 3 million, 2 million, 1.5 million and 0.7 million respectively, with employee average-income growth above the central-SOE average.[10] For 2026, FAW's group-wide vehicle sales target is 3.546 million, with Hongqi aiming at 550,000.[17]

Our judgment. The 600,000-unit class is a medium-to-long-term aspiration, roughly three times 2025 volume, and the brand has not disclosed a specific year for reaching it; media cited a 2026 target of about 200,000 units or a wholesale 230,000-300,000 range with divergent figures.[13] Whether Bestune can grow depends on whether the Yueyi 08 and the independent Yueyi brand can offset Xiaoma's decline and the subsidy-driven A00 contraction, and on overseas demand in the Gulf and Eastern Europe.[18] The large gap between H1 2026 cumulative sales of about 87,000 (of which NEVs were about 72,000; the Daily Economic News did not call this wholesale) and domestic retail of about 39,000 means the cumulative figure alone may not reflect end-demand, so near-term sales should be read with that caveat; the Yueyi 08, launched in July 2026, is the key variable to watch for the second half.[17][18] A NetEase account article that names CNR as its source reported, citing CAAM, that China FAW's H1 2026 NEV penetration was 32.7%, behind Changan and Dongfeng, and that this figure still included Leapmotor's sales. No CAAM original was found. FAW's 28 December 2025 deal was an equity investment in Leapmotor by FAW Equity Investment, not a consolidation of Leapmotor.[18][33]

Risks

  • Dependence on the Xiaoma mini EV. Media reported the Xiaoma contributed 65% of 2025 volume (about 55% against 200,017 units); its decline after the 2026 subsidy cut dragged domestic retail down about 35% year on year in H1 2026.[18]
  • Subsidy and policy exposure. A00 mini-EV demand is sensitive to national NEV purchase subsidies and purchase-tax breaks, which were scaled back in 2026.[18]
  • Weak mainstream-NEV traction. The Yueyi 03 averaged below 3,000 units a month in H1 2026 and the Yueyi 07 averaged under 500 a month during 2025, limiting the brand's move up-market.[18]
  • Sales-basis gap. The Daily Economic News reported H1 2026 cumulative sales of about 87,000 (NEVs about 72,000) and did not call them wholesale. Set against domestic retail of about 39,000, the gap leaves end-demand and channel inventory uncertain.[17][18]
  • No separate financial disclosure. Bestune does not publish brand-level revenue or profit, so its standalone profitability cannot be assessed from public filings; media reported the brand was loss-making.[9]
  • Overseas concentration. Overseas expansion focuses on Eastern Europe and the Gulf, leaving exposure to regional demand and trade conditions.[1]

概览

奔腾（BESTUNE）是中国第一汽车集团有限公司旗下自主主流乘用车品牌，一汽总部位于吉林省长春市。品牌于 2006 年 5 月 18 日首次亮相，同年 8 月上市首款车型——基于马自达 6 平台开发的中高级轿车奔腾 B70。[8][16] 在一汽体系内，奔腾与高端品牌红旗、商用车品牌解放并列，被集团定位为「做优红旗、做强解放、做大奔腾」中的一员。[3]

2025 年奔腾全年销量 200,017 辆（同比 +33%），创品牌成立以来新高，其中新能源 171,957 辆（同比 +107%），新能源渗透率超 85%。[1][2] 2025 年 9 月，奔腾完成股权多元化改革，共募集资金 85.5 亿元。四家外部投资者合计出资 33.1 亿元（悦达汽车集团 17.1 亿元、农银金融资产投资 10 亿元、中国电信 5 亿元、南京地平线 1 亿元），控股股东中国第一汽车股份有限公司同步增资 52.4 亿元；增资后中国第一汽车股份有限公司及一汽股权投资（天津）有限公司合计持股 79.0394%。[3][10] 品牌核心产品为悦意（JOYEE）新能源序列与奔腾小马微型电动车，B 系列轿车、T 系列 SUV 等燃油车型仍出口海湾市场。[7]

2026 年形势转向：媒体报道上半年国内零售约 3.9 万辆、同比下滑约 35%，小马在补贴退坡后失速，品牌遂将重心押注新独立的悦意品牌及 2026 年 7 月上市的悦意 08 旗舰。[18][6]

现状

业务结构与车型谱系

奔腾在一汽体系内是主打新能源的主流乘用车品牌。据官方 2025 年销量通报，全年销量中新能源 171,957 辆、其余为燃油车，新能源主力为悦意序列（悦意 03、悦意 07）与奔腾小马。[1] 悦意 03 纯电 SUV 于 2025 年 3 月 27 日上市（限时售价 9.29 万-11.79 万元），悦意 07 超级电混 SUV 于 2025 年 5 月 26 日上市（9.98 万元起）。[27][26] 奔腾小马于 2024 年 5 月 28 日上市，三款售价 2.69 万元、2.99 万元、3.19 万元；2026 款于 2025 年 7 月 27 日上市，售价 3.49 万-4.59 万元。[29][23] 2026 年 5 月 29 日悦意升级为中国一汽首个独立新能源品牌，旗舰悦意 08（B+ 级轿车）于 2026 年 7 月 12 日上市，提供增程版及 565km、650km 纯电版。一汽官网标题为「9.99 万元起售」，配图说明为「上市价格权益」，正文没有「指导价 9.99 万-13.99 万元」。汽车之家写售价仍为 10.49 万-14.49 万元，权益后为 9.99 万-13.99 万元。新华网吉林则写成官方指导价 9.99 万-13.99 万元。[6][19][30]

品牌新能源转型轨迹清晰：2023 年一汽提出「All in 新能源」战略，奔腾定位「中国新锐主流乘用车优秀品牌」；2024 年全年销量 150,777 辆（同比 +25%），其中新能源 82,872 辆（同比 +214%）、渗透率 55%；2025 年进一步升至 200,017 辆、新能源渗透率超 85%。[18][1] 据媒体报道，奔腾自 2019 年独立后一度销量承压、处于亏损状态，2023 年重返 10 万辆大关，2024 年约 15.1 万辆。[9] 2026 年 5 月升级后，悦意 03、悦意 07、悦意 08 等车型纳入独立悦意品牌体系；奔腾品牌自身继续承载奔腾小马及 B、T 系列燃油车型。[5]

技术与平台

2025 年 2 月悦意科技日上，奔腾发布越影平台、逐日动力（电驱最高效率达 95.5%）、如意座舱等技术集群；悦意 07 搭载中国一汽 BMP 超级电混技术。[4][1] 悦意全系产品基于越影平台、搭载逐日动力，覆盖纯电、电混、增程三大技术路线，并配如意座舱及与华为、地平线合作开发的「灵机智驾」。[27] 悦意 08 旗舰搭载 202kW 后驱电机、800V 平台与 6C 超充（7.5 分钟完成 30%-80% 补能）、高通骁龙 8295P 双 NPU 座舱，配华为乾崑辅驾计算平台与激光雷达，支持 24 项辅助驾驶功能。[6] 中国一汽全球研发布局汇聚约 1.9 万名研发人才、拥有超 1.6 万件专利，为奔腾的平台、动力与座舱研发提供支撑。[4]

制造与渠道

奔腾拥有长春与盐城双基地，总设计产能 35 万辆/年；盐城基地 9 个月建成投产，2025 年实现年产整车 17 万辆、产能利用率 240%，此前规划 2025 年底产能突破 15 万辆/年。[1][9] 截至 2025 年底，全国授权网络超 500 家，其中新能源专属门店/专区达 300 家以上。[1]

2026 年上半年品牌增势逆转。媒体引乘联分会数据称，上半年国内零售约 3.9 万辆、同比下滑约 35%，其中小马约 2.6 万辆、悦意 03 月均不足 3000 辆。[18] 随着燃油车转向海外，一汽将国内重心转向独立悦意品牌，推动门店标识由「一汽奔腾」更换为「一汽悦意」；媒体报道北京地区仅石景山、顺义、密云 3 家门店。[18] 服务层面，一汽发布用户品牌「Joy Pai」、迭代「518 服务权益 4.0」，并推出「悦意 321 服务承诺」，提供整车、三电终身质保及终身道路救援等权益。[1]

海外业务

2025 年一汽成立奔腾国际公司，推动海外事业体系化、本土化，聚焦东欧及海湾等重点市场。[1] 2025 年 12 月，奔腾携 JOYEE 系列与燃油车型亮相沙特利雅得国际车展，与 GOCAB、HANCO 集团签署合作备忘录，规划以「燃油+新能源」双线布局沙特市场。[7]

竞争格局

我们的判断：竞争范围。悦意系列定价约 9.29 万-13.99 万元、奔腾小马约 2.69 万-4.59 万元，奔腾主要竞争于中国约 7 万-15 万元主流新能源市场与 A00 级微型电动车市场。[6][18] 主流价位段面对比亚迪、吉利、长安（深蓝/启源）、零跑、小鹏等；微型电动车段面对五菱（宏光 MINI EV）、吉利（星愿）等。悦意 08 切入 10 万级新能源家轿市场，与同级纯电与增程轿车直接竞争。[12] 此为分析所选的竞争范围，不是销量排名。

我们的判断：优势。奔腾拥有中国一汽的「国家队」背书、长春与盐城双基地（35 万辆设计产能），以及 2025 年 200,017 辆、新能源渗透率超 85% 的规模。[1] 2025 年 12 月携悦意系列进入沙特市场，为其在海湾地区开辟了较早的新能源渠道。[7]

我们的判断：短板。品牌高度依赖奔腾小马，据媒体报道其贡献 2025 年约 65% 销量（若按 200,017 辆计算约为 55%）；2026 年国补退坡后小马销量骤降，拖累品牌上半年国内零售同比下滑约 35%。[18] 悦意序列尚未达到主流规模：媒体报道悦意 03 上半年月均不足 3000 辆，悦意 07 上市 8 个月月均不足 500 辆、2026 年前两月分别为 227 辆和 170 辆。[18]

未来展望

公司公开规划。2026 年 2 月全球生态伙伴大会上，一汽集团总经理刘亦功提出奔腾须「向 60 万辆级发起冲锋」，品牌同步发布「擎天柱·双擎」全球战略。[13] 独立后的悦意品牌及旗舰悦意 08，是品牌冲击 10 万-15 万元主流新能源市场的主要载体；悦意 08 上市时公司强调「好而不贵」的新能源定位。[12][19] 以上为公司披露的方向与目标，不是已实现的业绩。

集团层面，一汽 2030 年规划提出「六个超过」：整车销量、智能网联新能源汽车销量、自主品牌销量、自主品牌智能网联新能源汽车销量、海外销量分别超过 500 万辆、300 万辆、200 万辆、150 万辆、70 万辆，职工平均收入增速超过央企平均水平。[10] 2026 年一汽整车销量目标为 354.6 万辆，红旗冲刺 55 万辆。[17]

我们的判断。「60 万辆级」为中长期愿景，约为 2025 年销量的三倍，公司未披露具体达成年份；媒体对 2026 年目标报道口径不一（约 20 万辆，或批发 23 万辆必达、30 万辆挑战）。[13] 奔腾能否增长，取决于悦意 08 与独立悦意品牌能否对冲小马下滑及补贴退坡带来的 A00 市场收缩，以及海湾、东欧等海外需求。[18] 2026 年上半年累计销量约 8.7 万辆（其中新能源约 7.2 万辆；每日经济新闻未写明为批发）与国内零售约 3.9 万辆之间的缺口很大，累计销量未必反映终端需求，短期销量需结合这一口径差异解读；2026 年 7 月上市的悦意 08 后续销量爬坡，是观察品牌下半年表现的关键变量。[17][18] 一篇网易号稿（正文自称来源央广网）称，中汽协数据显示中国一汽上半年新能源渗透率 32.7%，落后于长安与东风，且该数字合并了零跑汽车销量。未见中汽协原文。一汽 2025 年 12 月 28 日的动作是一汽股权投资入股零跑，并非把零跑并入合并报表。[18][33]

风险

  • 对奔腾小马的依赖。媒体报道小马贡献 2025 年约 65% 销量（若按 200,017 辆计算约为 55%）；2026 年补贴退坡后其下滑，拖累上半年国内零售同比下滑约 35%。[18]
  • 补贴与政策敞口。A00 级微型电动车需求对国补与购置税优惠敏感，2026 年相关政策收紧。[18]
  • 主流新能源突破乏力。悦意 03 上半年月均不足 3000 辆、悦意 07 上市 8 个月月均不足 500 辆，制约品牌向上。[18]
  • 销量口径差异。每日经济新闻写 2026 年上半年累计销量约 8.7 万辆（其中新能源约 7.2 万辆），并未称为批发。与国内零售约 3.9 万辆对照，缺口较大，终端需求与渠道库存仍不确定。[17][18]
  • 无单独财务披露。奔腾未披露品牌层面营收与利润，无法从公开文件评估品牌独立盈利能力；媒体报道品牌处于亏损状态。[9]
  • 海外集中风险。海外扩张聚焦东欧与海湾，存在区域需求与贸易环境敞口。[1]
