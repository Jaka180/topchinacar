Overview

Yibin Kaiyi Automobile Co., Ltd. (Kaiyi Auto) is a state-controlled passenger-vehicle maker headquartered in Yibin, Sichuan. It began in January 2014 as Wuhu Kaiyi Automobile Co., Ltd., a wholly-owned 'internet car' subsidiary of Chery Automobile, and relocated to Yibin in December 2017, after which a Yibin municipal state-owned entity took control [1][7]. It is the only automaker in Sichuan holding the full passenger-vehicle catalogue (sedan, SUV, MPV) with both fuel and new-energy qualifications, selling models mainly in the RMB50,000-160,000 band such as the Kunlun mid-size SUV, the Xuandu sedan and the Shiyue electric city car, plus the Jiangtun electric logistics vans [1][2]. Backed by Chery's technology platforms and Yibin's state capital, Kaiyi's 2025 output first exceeded 100,000 units, at 123,000. A January 2026 reprint quotes exports of 33,000 units, up 257%; the company's 27 April 2026 conference reported 2025 export-sales growth of 233% [9][18][38].

Kaiyi's shareholding history reflects its dual identity of 'Chery technology plus Yibin capital': in early 2018 Chery transferred 51% for RMB2.494 billion to Yibin Automobile Industry Development Investment Co., Ltd. and Sichuan Yibin Pushi Group Co., Ltd., keeping 49% [10]. A December 2024 announcement showed Yibin Auto Investment at 80.67%, Chery at 18.00% and Pushi at 1.33%, with registered capital of RMB4.727 billion [3]. On 4 January 2026 the registry changed: capital rose to RMB6,066,617,700, Yibin Auto Investment held 62.8579% and new shareholder Tianjin Xinkai Enterprise Management Partnership, under China Cinda, held 22.0802%. Jiemian reported that Chery and Pushi remained shareholders; the opened registry page did not show their new percentages [36][37].

Current operations

Business structure

Kaiyi is a single-plant passenger-vehicle maker that has recently expanded into new-energy logistics vehicles. Its core domestic products are the Kunlun and Kunlun iHD mid-size SUVs, the Xuandu sedan and its EV variant, the Xuanjie and Xuanjie Pro compact SUVs, and the Shiyue family of A00 electric city cars [2]. It also sells the Jiangtun E5 and E7 electric microvans, an entry into the commercial-vehicle segment [2]. On exports, Kaiyi began shipping vehicles in 2021 via CBU and KD; by April 2025 cumulative exports exceeded 60,000 units, with 57 overseas partners, channels in 54 countries and regions and nearly 400 sales outlets [5]. For 2025, output reached 123,000 units. A January 2026 reprint quotes exports of 33,000 units (+257% YoY); the 27 April 2026 company conference reported export-sales growth of 233% [9][38].

The product line has been broadening at both ends of the price ladder. In June 2025 Kaiyi launched the Shiyue Mate, a five-seat A00 electric car priced at RMB59,900-75,900 with 305-405 km CLTC range [24]; in April 2026 it opened pre-sales of the Shiyue max (RMB47,900-51,900) and then launched it with a 310 km range [25]. In September 2024 the company unveiled three export-oriented new-energy models — the X7 iHD, e-Qute 02 and X3 Pro EV [25].

Latest disclosed operating data

As of the research cut-off date, the most recent complete-period disclosure is full-year 2025: general manager Gao Lei stated that 2025 output exceeded 100,000 units for the first time, reaching 123,000 units, with full-year exports of 33,000 units (+257% YoY). The 27 April 2026 company conference reported 2025 export-sales growth of 233%. The two export-growth figures are not the same [9][38]. No audited half-year 2026 results have been published, consistent with the company's unlisted status.

Technology and platforms

Kaiyi develops models on the i-FA modular architecture (mid-to-large SUVs such as the Kunlun), the i-EA pure-electric platform (A00/A0 city cars such as the Shiyue) and the i-XA platform, alongside the i-CAR intelligent cockpit system [16][15]. In traditional powertrains it relies on Chery's technology supply chain, while in electrification it cooperates with CATL, and in intelligent features with Huawei and Tencent [13][1].

Factory and capacity

The Yibin smart factory covers 973 mu in phase one, with an approved annual capacity of 150,000 units and up to 250,000 units attainable; it was completed in December 2019 with total investment of RMB5.3 billion (about RMB3.7 billion for the phase-one plant) [1][7]. The company's layout was described in 2020 as 'one headquarters, two centres and three bases' — Yibin headquarters, a Chengdu R&D and sales centre, and Wuhu, Dalian and Jiaozuo production bases [7].

Domestic market position

Kaiyi's domestic network exceeds 300 outlets covering northeast China, Shandong, Hebei, the northwest, and Hubei and Jiangxi provinces [6]. Yibin government reporting stated that Kaiyi crossed 50,000 units in sales for two consecutive years and exceeded 60,000 units in 2024, while its global sales passed 70,000 units in 2024 [6][4]. Yibin municipality aims to build Kaiyi into the cornerstone of a RMB100-billion-level intelligent connected NEV industry base [5].

Overseas business

Kaiyi's export markets are centred on four regions — Eastern Europe, Central Asia, Central/South America and Southeast Asia — with sales already reaching Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Egypt, Iran, Russia, Azerbaijan, Madagascar, Angola, Costa Rica, Peru, Panama, Venezuela, the Dominican Republic, Chile, Bolivia, Ecuador and Libya, among others [6]. In Russia it sold 7,893 vehicles in 2024 (+7.2% YoY). By October 2026 Avtotor's CEO told Izvestia that Kaiyi assembly at the Kaliningrad plant had been discontinued and that distribution had passed to Votur Motor Rus LLC, which has certified the X7 Kunlun crossover [22][27].

Founders and leadership

Founder

Kaiyi has no single individual founder in the formal sense; it emerged from Chery's system. It was established in 2014 by Lu Jianhui, a former Chery Automobile deputy general manager, who led a team of more than 200 people (largely from Chery's Qiyun division) to form the 'Jiangbei project group' in Wuhu's Jiangbei industrial zone [29][30]. Lu stepped back at the end of 2016, succeeded by fellow Chery veteran Feng Wutang, and passed away in February 2019, according to Wuhu Daily [30].

Key management

  • Jia Yaquan, chairman and party secretary, also a vice president of Chery Automobile Co., Ltd. People's Daily Sichuan reported this title in September 2026; his precise appointment date at Kaiyi was not publicly disclosed [19].
  • Gao Lei, general manager, deputy party secretary and legal representative. He has been described as deputy party secretary and general manager since at least October 2022, and a December 2024 listed-company announcement records him as legal representative. At the 27 April 2026 international business conference he was also introduced as vice chairman [15][5][3][38].
  • He Yongping, general manager of the Kaiyi sales company, quoted in Yibin government reporting in February 2025 [6].
  • Han Ren, deputy general manager and general manager of Yibin Kaiyi International Trade Co., Ltd., quoted in Yibin government reporting in June 2025 on export operations [5].
  • Former management: Pan Yanlong, a Chery Automobile deputy general manager, served as Kaiyi chairman around 2022-2023; Hu Mingjing served as party secretary [15].

Ownership and governance

A December 2024 listed-company announcement showed Yibin Automobile Industry Development Investment at 80.67%, Chery at 18.00% and Sichuan Yibin Pushi Group at 1.33%, with registered capital of RMB4.727 billion [3]. On 4 January 2026 the registry changed: capital is RMB6,066,617,700, Yibin Auto Investment holds 62.8579% and Tianjin Xinkai Enterprise Management Partnership, under China Cinda, holds 22.0802%. Jiemian reported that Chery and Pushi remain shareholders; the opened registry page did not show their new percentages [36][37]. Yibin Automobile Industry Development Investment is wholly owned by the Yibin municipal state-assets authority, and Pushi Group is wholly owned by the liquor maker Wuliangye Group [11]. No public announcement formally designates an 'actual controller', so this research records only the shareholding structure.

Competition

Competitive scope

Our judgement: in its RMB50,000-160,000 home-market band, Kaiyi competes mainly with other value-focused Chinese brands — for the Shiyue electric city car against the Wuling Hongguang MINIEV and Changan Lumin, and for the Kunlun and Kunlun iHD SUVs against compact/mid-size SUVs from domestic makers in a similar price range. This comparison is based on price positioning and body segments reported by the media, not on any single market-share dataset [17][21].

Business base

Kaiyi's scale is small by industry standards: 123,000 units produced in 2025 against a Chinese passenger-vehicle market of tens of millions of units a year [9]. Its strengths are Chery-shared platforms and powertrains, Yibin state-capital backing, a low-price positioning and exports, for which 2025 growth is reported as both 257% and 233% depending on the source [14][9][18][38]. Its weaknesses include a small brand footprint, a domestic dealer network of just over 300 outlets, and dependence on Chery for technology [6].

In the A00 electric city-car segment, the Shiyue family competes with the Wuling Hongguang MINIEV, Changan Lumin and Leapmotor T03, with the Shiyue Mate positioned as a five-seat model in the RMB59,900-75,900 band [24]. On exports, Kaiyi's main markets are Eastern Europe, Central Asia, Central/South America and Southeast Asia; in Russia it sold 7,893 vehicles in 2024 [22][5].

Our analysis

Our judgement: government reporting put 2024 domestic sales at just over 60,000 units and global sales above 70,000, a gap of about 10,000 units, so 2024 exports were not most of the volume. That gap does not show that 2025 growth was mainly export-led, or that domestic retail was far below wholesale. Output of 123,000 units in 2025 is a separate figure [6][4][9]. Whether it can hold a durable position in the intensely competitive Chinese market depends on converting Chery platforms into differentiated, cost-competitive products, and on the continuation of export momentum.

Outlook

Company targets and plans

In September 2026, chairman Jia Yaquan said that, with Chery Group's deep empowerment, Kaiyi's 2026 production and sales are expected to exceed 140,000 units and exceed 200,000 units in 2027; the Yibin base has been incorporated into Chery Group's key capacity layout, with the local sourcing rate rising from 16% in 2023 to 27.7% and targeting above 40% by 2027 [19][20]. Yibin municipal government has stated it aims to build Kaiyi into a cornerstone of a RMB100-billion-level intelligent connected NEV industry base [5]. General manager Gao Lei said in early 2026 that growth would continue to come mainly from export expansion and resource-integration synergies, with a continued push into new-energy models and deeper localised overseas operations [18].

For context on the gap between targets and outcomes, the Yibin economic cooperation bureau projected in May 2025 that Kaiyi's 2025 sales would exceed 150,000 units [4]. That figure is a sales forecast. Full-year 2025 output of 123,000 units is a different metric [9].

Our analysis

Our judgement: the 140,000-unit 2026 target implies roughly 14% growth over 2025 output of 123,000 units, which is a company forecast rather than a committed outcome [9]. Achieving it depends on sustained export demand, especially in Russia and emerging markets, and on domestic acceptance of the new-energy models. Because Kaiyi does not publish financial statements, profitability and cash-flow trends cannot be independently verified, and these targets should be read alongside the risks facing a small automaker in a highly competitive market.

Risks

  • Operating and scale risk: Kaiyi is a small player (123,000 units in 2025) with a domestic dealer network of just over 300 outlets, competing in a crowded market [6][9].
  • Technology dependence: its key platforms and powertrains are shared from Chery, so product competitiveness is tied to Chery's continued support [14].
  • Overseas risk: overseas operations face exchange-rate, tariff and geopolitical risk, including the Russian market, where Avtotor's chief executive said in October 2026 that Kaiyi assembly had been discontinued and distribution had passed to Votur Motor Rus LLC [22][27].
  • Financial disclosure risk: as an unlisted company, Kaiyi publishes no audited financial statements, so external parties cannot independently verify profitability, leverage or cash flow [1].
  • Competition risk: its core price band (RMB50,000-160,000) is among the most intensely contested segments of the Chinese market [21].
  • Transition investment risk: Kaiyi is adding new-energy models alongside its fuel range, which requires continued product and marketing investment [1].

概览

宜宾凯翼汽车有限公司（凯翼汽车）是一家总部位于四川宜宾的国有控股乘用车整车企业。其前身是奇瑞汽车 2014 年 1 月设立的全资互联网汽车子公司芜湖凯翼汽车有限公司，2017 年 12 月迁入宜宾后由宜宾市属国企取得控股地位 [1][7]。公司是四川省唯一同时具备轿车、SUV、MPV 车型及燃油与新能源双资质的乘用车生产企业，主力产品集中在 5 万–16 万元价格带，包括昆仑中型 SUV、轩度轿车、拾月纯电小车以及江豚新能源物流车 [1][2]。依托奇瑞的技术平台与宜宾国资的资金支持，凯翼 2025 年产量首次突破 10 万辆、达 12.3 万辆。2026 年 1 月的转载称出口 3.3 万辆、增 257%；2026 年 4 月 27 日公司大会称 2025 年出口销量同比增长 233% [9][18][38]。

凯翼的股权沿革体现了「奇瑞技术＋宜宾资本」的双重身份：2018 年初，奇瑞将 51% 股权作价 24.94 亿元转让给宜宾市汽车产业发展投资有限责任公司与四川省宜宾普什集团有限公司，奇瑞保留 49% [10]。2024 年 12 月公告显示宜宾汽投 80.67%、奇瑞 18.00%、普什 1.33%，注册资本 47.27 亿元 [3]。2026 年 1 月 4 日工商变更后，注册资本增至 606,661.77 万元，宜宾汽投持股 62.8579%，新增中国信达旗下天津信凯企业管理合伙企业持股 22.0802%。界面新闻报道奇瑞与普什仍为股东，已打开的工商页面没有这两家的新比例 [36][37]。

现状

业务结构

凯翼是一家单一工厂的乘用车整车企业，近年向新能源物流车延伸。其国内核心产品为昆仑、昆仑 iHD 中型 SUV，轩度轿车及其 EV 版，炫界、炫界 Pro 紧凑型 SUV，以及拾月系列 A00 纯电小车 [2]。公司还销售江豚 E5、E7 纯电微面，进入商用车领域 [2]。出口方面，凯翼 2021 年起以整车（CBU）与散件（KD）方式出海，截至 2025 年 4 月累计出口超 6 万辆，拥有 57 个海外合作伙伴、覆盖 54 个国家和地区的渠道与近 400 个销售网点 [5]。2025 年产量 12.3 万辆。2026 年 1 月的转载称出口 3.3 万辆、同比增 257%；2026 年 4 月 27 日公司大会称出口销量同比增长 233% [9][38]。

产品线正沿价格梯度的两端扩张。2025 年 6 月，凯翼推出五座 A00 纯电小车拾月 Mate，售价 5.99 万–7.59 万元，CLTC 续航 305–405 公里 [24]；2026 年 4 月开启拾月 max 预售（4.79 万–5.19 万元）并随后上市，续航 310 公里 [25]。2024 年 9 月，公司发布三款面向海外的新能源车型——X7 iHD、e-Qute 02 与 X3 Pro EV [25]。

最近已披露经营数据

截至研究截止日，最近的完整期间披露为 2025 全年：总经理高雷表示，2025 年产量首次突破 10 万辆、达 12.3 万辆，全年出口 3.3 万辆（同比增 257%）。2026 年 4 月 27 日公司大会称 2025 年出口销量同比增长 233%。两处出口增速不是同一个数 [9][38]。截至 2026 年 10 月，公司未公布 2026 年半年度的经审计业绩，与其非上市身份一致。

技术与平台

凯翼依托 i-FA 模块化架构（中型/中大型 SUV，如昆仑）、i-EA 纯电平台（A00/A0 小车，如拾月）与 i-XA 平台开发车型，并配套 i-CAR 智能座舱系统 [16][15]。传统动力方面依托奇瑞的技术供应体系，电动化方面与宁德时代合作，智能化方面与华为、腾讯合作 [13][1]。

工厂与产能

宜宾智慧工厂一期占地 973 亩，获批年产能 15 万辆、实际可达 25 万辆，2019 年 12 月建成投产，总投资 53 亿元（其中一期工厂约 37 亿元）[1][7]。2020 年披露的布局为「一总部两中心三基地」：总部宜宾，成都研发中心与销售中心，以及芜湖、大连、焦作三个生产基地 [7]。

国内市场地位

凯翼国内销售网点逾 300 家，覆盖东北、山东、河北、西北以及湖北、江西等区域 [6]。宜宾市政府报道称，凯翼连续两年销量跨越 5 万辆、2024 年突破 6 万辆，全球销量 2024 年突破 7 万辆 [6][4]。宜宾市政府表示，目标是把凯翼打造为千亿级智能网联新能源汽车产业基地的核心 [5]。

海外业务

凯翼出口聚焦东欧、中亚、中南美、东南亚四大区域，产品已进入沙特、阿联酋、埃及、伊朗、俄罗斯、阿塞拜疆、马达加斯加、安哥拉、哥斯达黎加、秘鲁、巴拿马、委内瑞拉、多米尼加、智利、玻利维亚、厄瓜多尔、利比亚等国家和地区 [6]。在俄罗斯，凯翼 2024 年销售 7893 辆（同比增 7.2%），据 Izvestia 报道，至 2026 年 10 月 Avtotor 已停止在加里宁格勒组装凯翼车型，分销改由 Votur Motor Rus LLC 负责，后者已认证 X7 昆仑跨界车 [22][27]。

创始人与管理层

创始人

凯翼并无严格意义上的单一创始人，而是奇瑞体系的产物。公司由奇瑞汽车原副总经理陆建辉于 2014 年牵头创立，他带领 200 余人团队（主要来自奇瑞旗云事业部）在芜湖江北产业集中区组建「江北项目组」[29][30]。陆建辉 2016 年底隐退，由同为奇瑞体系出身的冯武堂接任，并于 2019 年 2 月离世（据《芜湖日报》）[30]。

  • 前任管理层：潘燕龙（奇瑞汽车股份有限公司副总经理）曾于 2022–2023 年间任凯翼董事长，胡明镜曾任党委书记 [15]。

股权与治理

上市公司常熟汽饰 2024 年 12 月公告中，宜宾凯翼股东为宜宾市汽车产业发展投资有限责任公司（80.67%）、奇瑞汽车股份有限公司（18.00%）与四川省宜宾普什集团有限公司（1.33%），注册资本 47.27 亿元 [3]。2026 年 1 月 4 日工商变更后，注册资本为 606,661.77 万元，宜宾汽投持股 62.8579%，中国信达旗下天津信凯企业管理合伙企业持股 22.0802%。界面新闻报道奇瑞与普什仍为股东，已打开的工商页面没有这两家的新比例 [36][37]。宜宾市汽车产业发展投资有限责任公司为宜宾市政府国资委独资公司，普什集团由白酒企业五粮液集团全资持有 [11]。公开公告中未见明确的「实际控制人」认定，故本研究只写持股结构，不作实际控制人认定。

财务数据

竞争格局

竞争范围

我们的判断：在其 5 万–16 万元的国内价格带内，凯翼主要与其他主打性价比的中国品牌竞争——拾月纯电小车对标五菱宏光 MINIEV、长安 Lumin，昆仑与昆仑 iHD SUV 则与同价位国产紧凑/中型 SUV 竞争。该比较依据媒体披露的价格定位与车身级别，而非单一市场份额数据 [17][21]。

业务基础

按行业标准，凯翼体量偏小：2025 年产量 12.3 万辆，而中国乘用车市场年销以千万辆计 [9]。其优势在于共享奇瑞的平台与动力总成、宜宾国资的资金支持、低价定位以及出口（2025 年增速一处写 257%，另一处写 233%）[14][9][18][38]。其短板是品牌体量小、国内销售网点仅 300 余家、技术上依赖奇瑞 [6]。

在 A00 纯电小车细分市场，拾月系列与五菱宏光 MINIEV、长安 Lumin、零跑 T03 竞争，拾月 Mate 以 5.99 万–7.59 万元的五座定位切入 [24]。出口方面，凯翼主要市场为东欧、中亚、中南美、东南亚；在俄罗斯 2024 年销售 7893 辆 [22][5]。

我们的分析

我们的判断：政府报道称 2024 年国内销量 6 万余辆、全球销量突破 7 万辆，差额大约 1 万辆，所以 2024 年出口并不是销量的大头。这个差额说明不了 2025 年的增长主要由出口拉动，也说明不了国内零售远小于批发。2025 年产量 12.3 万辆是另一个数字 [6][4][9]。在竞争激烈的中国市场能否持续立足，取决于把奇瑞平台转化为差异化、有成本竞争力的产品，以及出口动能能否延续。

未来展望

公司目标与规划

2026 年 9 月，董事长贾亚权表示，在奇瑞集团深度赋能下，凯翼 2026 年产销量预计突破 14 万辆、2027 年突破 20 万辆；宜宾基地已纳入奇瑞集团重要产能布局，本地化配套率由 2023 年的 16% 提升至 27.7%，目标 2027 年突破 40% [19][20]。宜宾市政府表示，目标是把凯翼打造为千亿级智能网联新能源汽车产业基地的核心 [5]。总经理高雷在 2026 年初表示，今年增量仍将主要来自品牌出口扩大与多方资源整合的协同效应，并继续加强新能源车型布局、深化国际市场本地化运营 [18]。

关于目标与结果之间的差距，宜宾市经济合作和新兴产业局在 2025 年 5 月曾预计凯翼 2025 年销量将突破 15 万辆 [4]。这是销量预测。2025 年全年产量 12.3 万辆是另一个口径 [9]。

我们的分析

我们的判断：2026 年 14 万辆的目标意味着较 2025 年 12.3 万辆的产量增长约 14%，这属于公司预测而非已实现的承诺 [9]。能否达成取决于出口需求（尤其是俄罗斯及新兴市场）能否延续，以及新能源车型在国内的接受度。由于凯翼不公开财务报表，其盈利能力与现金流趋势无法独立核实，上述目标应与一家小型车企在激烈竞争市场中的风险一并看待。

风险

  • 经营与规模风险：凯翼体量小（2025 年 12.3 万辆），国内销售网点仅 300 余家，身处竞争激烈的市场 [6][9]。
  • 技术依赖风险：其关键平台与动力总成共享自奇瑞，产品竞争力取决于奇瑞的持续支持 [14]。
  • 海外风险：海外业务面临汇率、关税与地缘政治风险，包括俄罗斯市场。Avtotor 首席执行官 2026 年 10 月表示，凯翼在加里宁格勒的组装已经停止，分销改由 Votur Motor Rus LLC 负责[22][27]。
  • 财务披露风险：作为非上市公司，凯翼不发布经审计财务报表，外界无法独立核实其盈利、负债与现金流 [1]。
  • 竞争风险：其 5 万–16 万元的核心价格带是中国市场竞争最激烈的区间之一 [21]。
  • 转型投入风险：公司在燃油车之外布局新能源车型，转型需要持续的产品与营销投入 [1]。
