Overview

YUTONG BUS CO., LTD. (SSE: 600066) is China's largest bus and coach manufacturer, with products spanning 5-metre to 18-metre buses across highway passenger, tourism, city transit, group commuting, school and airport-apron segments, plus pure-electric and hybrid new-energy models, across more than 100 product series [1]. In FY2025 it reported revenue of RMB41.43 billion (+11.31%) and net profit attributable to shareholders of RMB5.55 billion (+34.94%), and sold 49,518 buses, of which 17,149 were exported [4][12]. The company says it is shifting from a manufacturing-plus-product-sales model toward manufacturing-services-plus-solutions, and describes its 'Yutong model' of export as an example of China's auto industry moving from product export to technology export [1].

Yutong is a privately controlled enterprise: controlling shareholder Zhengzhou Yutong Group Co., Ltd. directly held 37.70% at end-2025, and Tang Yuxiang has been the actual controller since March 2023 [7][5]. Its buses are sold in more than 60 countries and regions, and it says its large and medium bus production and sales rank first in the industry [3].

Yutong maintains a high-dividend policy: the FY2025 profit-distribution plan was implemented on 2026-05-15, paying a final cash dividend of RMB2 per share (tax inclusive) totalling RMB4.43 billion; including the interim dividend, total FY2025 cash dividends reached RMB5.53 billion, 99.65% of the year's net profit attributable to shareholders [22]. Since listing it has paid dividends 29 times totalling RMB31.56 billion, exceeding its capital-market fundraising [12].

History

  1. 1963

    The predecessor, Henan Provincial Transport Department Zhengzhou Bus Repair Plant (河南省交通厅郑州客车修配厂), is founded; the same year it produces Henan's first long-distance coach, the JT660.[8][10]

  2. 1985

    The plant is renamed Zhengzhou Bus Factory (郑州客车厂) and listed among China's 14 key large-bus manufacturers.[8][10]

  3. 1991

    The self-developed ZK6980 series, led by the ZK6980W sleeper coach, is launched and becomes a market hit.[8]

  4. 1993

    Yutong takes the lead in shareholding reform in the industry and incorporates Zhengzhou Yutong Bus Co., Ltd., shifting from factory system to corporate system.[8][10]

  5. 1997

    Yutong lists on the Shanghai Stock Exchange, becoming the first listed large-bus maker in China.[8][10]

  6. 1999

    Yutong launches its first pure-electric bus with wholly owned intellectual property.[10]

  7. 2003

    Zhengzhou Yutong Group Co., Ltd. is established; Yutong Bus tops the domestic large and medium bus market for the first time with about 15,000 units sold.[10][11]

  8. 2005

    Yutong becomes China's largest bus exporter, a position it has maintained since.[10]

  9. 2008

    Yutong launches its first dedicated school bus and takes the lead in drafting the national school-bus safety standard.[10][11]

  10. 2010

    Annual production and sales exceeded 40,000 units in 2010 (41,894 produced and 41,169 sold), making Yutong the world's largest single-plant bus manufacturer.[10][11][23]

  11. 2012

    The energy-saving and new-energy bus base (over 2,000 mu) comes into operation, described as the world's largest new-energy bus base.[10][11]

  12. 2013

    Yutong releases its self-developed 'Ruikong' (睿控) new-energy control technology.[8][10]

  13. 2015

    The T7 premium coach is launched after 11 years of development, and Yutong obtains the official product announcement for its first fuel-cell bus the same year.[8][10]

  14. 2016-01-08

    Yutong is presented with the second prize of the 2015 National Science and Technology Progress Award for the project 'R&D and industrialisation of key technologies for energy-saving and new-energy buses'.[25][26]

  15. 2018

    Yutong wins the China Grand Award for Industry (中国工业大奖), the country's top industrial honour.[8][10]

  16. 2019

    Yutong's self-developed L4 autonomous bus begins trial operation in Zhengzhou; it also launches a 5G smart-bus solution.[8][10]

  17. 2022

    888 Yutong pure-electric buses serve the FIFA World Cup in Qatar, the first large-scale use of Chinese new-energy buses at a major international sporting event.[8][10]

  18. 2023-03-13

    The change of actual controller is completed: Tang Yuxiang becomes the actual controller of Yutong Group and Yutong Bus after acquiring a controlling stake in Zhengzhou Tongtai Hezhi Management Consulting Co., Ltd.[5]

  19. 2023

    Yutong releases the Ruikong E platform, described as the commercial-vehicle industry's first hardware-software integrated electric platform; 800 buses are exported to Uzbekistan, and 921 buses serve the Hangzhou Asian Games.[8][10]

  20. 2024

    The battery-technology project in which Yutong participated wins a National Science and Technology Progress Award (second prize), its third such honour; annual revenue reaches RMB37.22 billion (+37.63%).[8][10]

  21. 2025

    The premium Tianyu S12 coach goes on sale; full-year bus sales reach 49,518 units (+5.54%); on 26 June 2025 the 10,000th vehicle exported to Central Asia rolls off the line in Zhengzhou, and a Kazakhstan customer signs an order for 1,000 buses at the same event; Yutong wins seven Busworld awards in Belgium, including Best Coach (T14E) and Best Bus (U15).[8][4][28]

Current operations

Business structure

Yutong's core business is bus development, manufacturing and sales. In FY2025 the bus-product segment (classified as 'industry' in the report) generated revenue of RMB36.23 billion, 87.45% of the total, with other business contributing the remainder [13][1]. By region, overseas sales reached RMB21.11 billion (50.95% of revenue) and domestic sales RMB15.40 billion (37.18%), the first year overseas revenue exceeded domestic [1][11]. In 2025 the company sold 49,518 buses: 24,943 large, 15,964 medium and 8,611 light [4]. By segment, in 2025 large and medium transit-bus sales reached 5,631 units (+8.6%) and new-energy transit-bus sales 5,549 units (+7%), while its share of the school-bus market reached 49.7%, first in that segment [12]; in 2025 the top five customers accounted for RMB6.251 billion of sales, 15.09% of annual sales [13][1].

Technology and platforms

Yutong invests heavily in R&D: FY2025 R&D spending was RMB1.81 billion, 4.36% of revenue [12][1]. Its Ruikong E platform, released in 2023, is described as the commercial-vehicle industry's first hardware-software integrated electric platform [8]. The company develops electric drive, battery and electronic-control systems in-house and has pushed into intelligent connected buses, with an L4 autonomous bus in trial operation since 2019 and a 5G smart-bus solution [11]; in 2020 its 'Xiaoyu' 2.0 intelligent connected bus rolled off the line in volume and it built China's first L4 ride-hailing bus route [8]. In H1 2026 it launched new models including the Yuwei E9PRO+, E8PRO and Yuchang E6MAX for micro-circulation and on-demand bus scenarios, and delivered 235 new-energy buses to Tianjin Public Transport in June 2026 [21]. At end-2025 it had 4,180 R&D staff (21.35% of the total) and 2,743 valid patents and software copyrights [1][16]. In H1 2026 R&D spending was RMB829 million (4.96% of revenue), and the company continued telematics, intelligent-driving and smart-cockpit development, releasing the Xiaoyu AI assistant and finalising its second-generation L3 intelligent-driving functions for small-batch rollout [22].

Manufacturing and capacity

Production is concentrated in Zhengzhou, Henan, where Yutong has its industrial park and the energy-saving and new-energy bus base that began operation in 2012 covering over 2,000 mu; the Zhengzhou Yutong Industrial Park (1,700 mu) was opened in 1998 [11][8]. The 2025 annual report discloses three design-capacity lines of 30,000, 30,000 and 5,000 buses a year, totalling 65,000 units a year (flexible production lines) [1][18].

Domestic position and overseas business

Domestically, its large and medium bus production and sales rank first in the industry, and Chinese media reported it has ranked first in national sales for 23 consecutive years [11]. Overseas, its buses are sold in more than 60 countries and regions, with KD cooperation in 16 countries and regions [3][12]; by end-June 2026 its cumulative bus exports exceeded 138,000 units, of which over 13,000 were new-energy buses [3]. Overseas operations are run through country subsidiaries, including Yutong France SAS and Yutong Chile SPA, plus Hong Kong trading companies, under a 'direct sales, direct service, direct financing' model [22].

Latest results

In H1 2026 the company sold 20,100 buses and reported revenue of RMB16.72 billion (+3.65% YoY) and non-recurring-items-adjusted net profit of RMB1.80 billion (+15.83% YoY), with a weighted-average return on equity of 11.85% [22]. In the first three quarters of 2026 it reported revenue of RMB25.82 billion (-2.09% YoY), net profit attributable to shareholders of RMB3.18 billion (-3.31% YoY) and non-recurring-items-adjusted net profit of RMB3.10 billion (+18.45% YoY) [15].

Founders and leadership

Founder

Yutong has no individual founder in the usual sense: its predecessor was a state-run bus repair plant founded in 1963, and the modern company was formed through a 1993 shareholding reform [8]. Tang Yuxiang, who joined the management when the company was incorporated in 1993, has led the company for more than two decades and became its actual controller in 2023 [5].

Key management

  • Tang Yuxiang (汤玉祥, born 1954), chairman. He joined the company as director and deputy general manager in 1993, became general manager and CFO in 1996, and has served as chairman since 2001; he was re-elected by the 12th board on 2026-04-27. He is also party secretary of the company and chairman of Yutong Group, and served as a deputy to the 10th to 13th National People's Congress [6].
  • Li Panpan (李盼盼, born 1985), director, general manager, CFO and legal representative. He previously held roles including bus product development manager, deputy head of the quality assurance department, quality director, assistant general manager and deputy general manager; he became general manager in 2024 [6].
  • Wang Wentao (王文韬, born 1984), director and deputy general manager, previously head of the marketing department and deputy director of domestic marketing [6].
  • Yu Li (于莉, born 1975), board secretary, who has served as board secretary since 2012 [6].

Ownership and governance

Controlling shareholder Zhengzhou Yutong Group Co., Ltd. held 834,755,347 shares (37.70%) at end-2025; Mengshi Bus Co., Ltd., a subsidiary of Yutong Group, held 87,428,292 shares (3.95%) [7]. In addition, as of 2026-09-30, Hong Kong Securities Clearing Company Limited held 244,203,910 shares (11.03%), with China Life Insurance, CPIC Life and the National Social Security Fund also among the top-10 shareholders [15]. Tang Yuxiang is the actual controller: after completing the share transfer on 2023-03-13, he indirectly controls Yutong Group through Zhengzhou Tongtai Hezhi Management Consulting Co., Ltd. [5].

Competition

Main competitors

In China's bus market Yutong competes mainly with Xiamen King Long Motor Group (King Long, Golden Dragon, Higer), Zhongtong Bus, Anhui Ankai Automobile and Beiqi Foton (AUV) [11]. In new-energy buses and exports it also faces manufacturers such as BYD and CRRC Electric Vehicle [14]. In H1 2026 industry sales of large and medium buses totalled 53,686 units, up 5.73% year on year, with the market showing mild growth and structural divergence [3].

Strengths

  • Market leadership: large and medium bus production and sales rank first in China. In H1 2026 Yutong sold 15,779 buses of 6m and above, the industry's only maker above 10,000, and held a 37.1% share of the domestic 7m-and-above large and medium segment (8,763 units) [21]. As of the end of 2025, Yutong Group had cumulatively sold more than 260,000 new-energy commercial vehicles, a group total that includes non-bus businesses [27].
  • Export leadership: 17,149 buses exported in 2025, first in the industry, with KD cooperation in 16 countries and regions [12].
  • Sustained R&D: R&D spending has exceeded RMB1.5 billion a year since 2018, supporting premium and new-energy products [16].

Weaknesses

  • Exposure to a shrinking domestic market: in H1 2026 domestic large and medium bus demand fell, and the company's total sales fell 5.73% [3].
  • Cash-flow volatility: operating cash flow fell 55.67% in FY2025 on higher raw-material payments [1].
  • Policy-dependent transit demand: part of domestic transit demand depends on the continuation of the trade-in subsidy policy [3].

Our assessment: the disclosed data show Yutong's strengths concentrated in scale, exports and a 'premiumisation plus new-energy' product mix, while its domestic large and medium bus business faces structural softness; the 34.94% profit growth in 2025 came mainly from overseas volume and sales-mix improvement rather than margin expansion, since gross margin only rose from 22.94% in 2024 to 24.14% in 2025 [1][12].

Outlook

Company targets and plans

In its FY2025 annual report the company said it expects domestic large and medium bus demand to grow slightly in 2026, supported by the start of the 15th Five-Year Plan, transport and tourism policies, and the continuation of the new-energy bus trade-in policy; overseas demand is expected to keep growing, led by European electrification and recovering emerging markets [1]. According to China Bus Statistics Network data cited in the 2026 interim report, industry exports of large and medium buses rose 18.45% year on year in the first half of 2026, including a 29.77% rise in new-energy exports, with growth expected to continue in the second half [3]. The company says it will continue its 'four modernisations' strategy (electrification, intelligent connectivity, premiumisation and internationalisation) and deepen the 'direct sales, direct service, direct financing' model overseas [16]. A securities-research report cited by media says Yutong's first overseas new-energy commercial-vehicle plant, a KD facility in Qatar with an initial designed capacity of 300 units a year, expandable to 1,000 units a year, has been set up [17].

Our analysis

Our assessment: exports and new-energy buses are the two visible growth drivers, but both carry conditions. Export growth depends on continued demand in Europe and emerging markets and is exposed to tariff and geopolitical changes; the domestic bus market, in contrast, is soft and partly policy-dependent, as shown by the 5.73% sales decline and 3.52% profit decline in H1 2026 [3]. Whether Yutong can keep growing profit will depend on whether export volume and new-energy mix keep improving faster than the domestic market declines, none of which is guaranteed [14]. In the first three quarters of 2026, net profit attributable to shareholders fell 3.31% year on year to RMB3.18 billion, while non-recurring-items-adjusted net profit rose 18.45% to RMB3.10 billion, indicating improving underlying operating quality even as headline profit was affected by non-recurring base effects [15].

Risks

  • Operating risk: the domestic large and medium bus market is shrinking overall; the company's own H1 2026 sales fell 5.73% on lower domestic demand [3].
  • Financial risk: FY2025 operating cash flow fell 55.67% year on year, which the company attributed to higher raw-material procurement payments [1].
  • Policy risk: a significant part of domestic bus demand depends on the trade-in subsidy policy and new-energy purchase-tax policy; any change in these policies affects the timing and level of demand [3].
  • Overseas risk: overseas sales accounted for 50.95% of operating revenue in FY2025, so the company is highly exposed to tariffs, export-tax rebate changes and geopolitical events; it cited Middle East instability as affecting demand in H1 2026 [1][3].
  • Technology-route risk: Yutong pushes pure-electric, hybrid and fuel-cell routes in parallel, and new-energy bus economics in overseas markets are still maturing [11].
  • Export-policy and localisation risk: analysts told media that a lower export-tax rebate could push manufacturers to accelerate overseas local production, affecting export pace and cost structure [17].

概览

宇通客车股份有限公司（YUTONG BUS CO., LTD.，上交所：600066）是中国最大的客车制造商，产品覆盖 5 米至 18 米全谱系，涵盖公路客运、旅游客运、公交客运、团体通勤、校车、机场摆渡车等细分市场以及纯电、混动等新能源车型，拥有 100 余个产品系列 [1]。2025 财年实现营业收入 414.26 亿元（+11.31%）、归母净利润 55.54 亿元（+34.94%），销售客车 49,518 辆，其中出口 17,149 辆 [4][12]。公司正从「制造型＋销售产品」向「制造服务型＋解决方案」转型，并称独创了中国制造出口的「宇通模式」，成为中国汽车工业由产品输出走向技术输出的代表之一 [1]。

宇通为民营企业：控股股东郑州宇通集团有限公司 2025 年末直接持股 37.70%，汤玉祥自 2023 年 3 月起为实际控制人 [7][5]。公司产品批量销售至全球 60 多个国家和地区，大中型客车产销量稳居行业第一 [3]。

宇通延续高分红传统：2025 年度利润分配方案已于 2026-05-15 实施，向全体股东每股派发现金股利 2 元（含税），末期派现合计 44.28 亿元；含中期分红的年度现金分红总额 55.35 亿元，占当年度归母净利润的 99.65% [22]。上市以来累计分红 29 次、分红总额 315.6 亿元，超过资本市场融资额 [12]。

历史沿革

  1. 1963

    前身「河南省交通厅郑州客车修配厂」成立，同年试制成功河南首辆 JT660 型长途客车。[8][10]

  2. 1985

    更名为「郑州客车厂」，被列为全国 14 家大客车生产骨干企业之一。[8][10]

  3. 1991

    自主研发的 ZK6980 系列客车面世，其中 ZK6980W 卧铺客车投放市场后走红。[8]

  4. 1993

    率先在行业实行股份制改造，成立郑州宇通客车股份有限公司，实现从工厂制向公司制的转变。[8][10]

  5. 1997

    在上海证券交易所挂牌上市，成为国内大客车行业第一家上市公司。[8][10]

  6. 1999

    推出第一辆完全自主知识产权的纯电动客车。[10]

  7. 2003

    郑州宇通集团有限公司成立；宇通客车以约 1.5 万辆年销量首次登顶国内大中型客车市场。[10][11]

  8. 2005

    出口量跃居中国第一，并保持至今。[10]

  9. 2008

    推出第一款专业校车，并牵头制定专用校车安全国家标准。[10][11]

  10. 2010

    2010 年产销突破 4 万辆（产量 41,894 辆、销量 41,169 辆），成为世界单厂规模最大的客车企业。[10][11][23]

  11. 2012

    占地 2000 余亩的节能与新能源客车基地投产，成为全球规模领先的新能源客车生产基地。[10][11]

  12. 2013

    发布自主研发的「睿控」新能源控制技术。[8][10]

  13. 2015

    历经 11 年研发的高端公商务客车 T7 正式推出；同年取得首个燃料电池客车正式公告。[8][10]

  14. 2016-01-08

    获颁「2015年度国家科学技术进步奖」二等奖，获奖项目为「节能与新能源客车关键技术研发及产业化」。[25][26]

  15. 2018

    荣获我国工业领域最高奖项「中国工业大奖」。[8][10]

  16. 2019

    自主研发的 L4 级自动驾驶巴士在郑州试运行，并推出 5G 智能公交系统解决方案。[8][10]

  17. 2022

    888 辆宇通纯电动客车服务卡塔尔世界杯，为国际大型体育赛事首次大批量引入中国新能源客车。[8][10]

  18. 2023-03-13

    实际控制人变更完成：汤玉祥受让郑州通泰合智管理咨询有限公司控股权后，成为宇通集团及宇通客车实际控制人。[5]

  19. 2023

    发布商用车行业首个软硬件一体化电动专属平台「睿控 E 平台」；800 辆客车出口乌兹别克斯坦；同年 921 辆客车服务杭州亚运会。[8][10]

  20. 2024

    参与的动力电池项目荣获国家科学技术进步奖二等奖（第 3 次获此殊荣）；全年营业收入 372.18 亿元，同比增长 37.63%。[8][10]

  21. 2025

    高端「天域 S12」上市；全年销售客车 49,518 辆（+5.54%），全球大中型客车市场占有率接近 10%；2025 年 6 月 26 日，出口中亚地区的第 1 万台车辆在郑州下线，同场签署哈萨克斯坦客户 1,000 台订单；在比利时世界客车博览会获 7 项 Busworld 大奖，T14E、U15 分获年度最佳客车、最佳巴士大奖。[8][4][28]

现状

业务结构

宇通以客车研发、制造与销售为主业。2025 财年客车产品（报表中归入「工业」）收入 362.29 亿元，占营业收入的 87.45%，其余为其他业务 [13][1]。分地区看，海外销售收入 211.08 亿元（占 50.95%）、国内销售收入 154.02 亿元（占 37.18%），海外收入首次超过国内 [1][11]。2025 年销售客车 49,518 辆，其中大型 24,943 辆、中型 15,964 辆、轻型 8,611 辆 [4]。分市场看，2025 年大中型公交销量 5,631 辆（+8.6%）、新能源公交销量 5,549 辆（+7%），校车市场占有率达 49.7%、稳居细分市场第一 [12]；2025 年前五大客户销售额 62.51 亿元，占年度销售总额 15.09% [13][1]。

技术与平台

宇通保持高研发投入：2025 财年研发支出 18.08 亿元，占营业收入的 4.36% [12][1]。其 2023 年发布的「睿控 E 平台」被称为商用车行业首个软硬件一体化电动专属平台 [8]。公司自主攻关电池、电机、电控等核心部件，并推进智能网联化，2019 年起 L4 级自动驾驶巴士试运行，并推出 5G 智能公交方案 [11]；2020 年智能网联巴士「小宇」2.0 批量下线，公司建成国内首条 L4 级网约巴士线路 [8]。2026 年上半年，公司依托睿控 E+ 平台及智能辅助驾驶技术，推出宇威 E9PRO⁺、宇威 E8PRO、宇畅 E6MAX 等新车型，适配微循环、网约公交等场景，并于 2026 年 6 月向天津公交批量交付 235 辆新能源公交 [21]。截至 2025 年末拥有研发人员 4,180 人（占 21.35%），国内外有效专利及软件著作权 2,743 件 [1][16]。2026 年上半年公司研发支出 8.29 亿元、占营业收入的 4.96%，并持续推进车联网、智能驾驶与智能座舱研发，发布小宇 AI 助手，完成二代 L3 级智能驾驶功能定型并小批量推广 [22]。

制造与产能

生产集中于河南郑州，建有宇通工业园及 2012 年投产、占地 2000 余亩的节能与新能源基地；1998 年占地 1700 亩的郑州宇通工业园正式启用 [11][8]。2025 年年报披露公司三处设计产能分别为 30,000、30,000、5,000 辆/年，合计 65,000 辆/年（柔性生产线）[1][18]。

国内地位与海外业务

国内方面，公司大中型客车产销量稳居行业第一，据中国工业新闻网报道已连续 23 年居全国销量第一 [11]。海外方面，产品批量出口至全球 60 多个国家和地区，并在 16 个国家和地区开展 KD 合作 [3][12]；截至 2026 年 6 月底，公司累计出口客车超 13.8 万辆，其中新能源客车超 1.3 万辆 [3]。海外业务以国别子公司运营，包括法国宇通（YUTONG FRANCE SAS）、智利宇通（YUTONG CHILE SPA）及香港贸易公司，并推行「直销、直服、直融」模式 [22]。

最新业绩

2026 年上半年公司销售客车 20,100 辆，实现营业收入 167.19 亿元（同比增长 3.65%）、扣非净利润 17.96 亿元（同比增长 15.83%），加权平均净资产收益率 11.85% [22]。2026 年前三季度，公司实现营业收入 258.16 亿元（同比下降 2.09%）、归母净利润 31.84 亿元（同比下降 3.31%）、扣非净利润 30.99 亿元（同比增长 18.45%）[15]。

创始人与管理层

创始人

宇通并无通常意义上的单一创始人：其前身为 1963 年成立的国有客车修配厂，1993 年经股份制改造成立现公司 [8]。汤玉祥自 1993 年公司成立后进入管理层，此后长期执掌公司，并于 2023 年成为实际控制人 [5]。

核心管理层

  • 汤玉祥（1954 年生），董事长。1993 年公司成立后任董事、副总经理，1996 年任总经理、财务负责人，2001 年起担任董事长，2026-04-27 第十二届董事会连任；同时任公司党委书记、宇通集团董事长。其为第十届至十三届全国人大代表，曾获郑州市「五一」劳动奖章、河南省劳动模范、全国劳动模范 [6]。
  • 李盼盼（1985 年生），董事、总经理、财务负责人、法定代表人。历任公交产品开发经理、质量保证部副部长、质量总监兼质量保证处处长、总经理助理、副总经理，2024 年起任总经理 [6]。
  • 王文韬（1984 年生），董事、副总经理，曾任市场营销部部长、国内营销副总监等 [6]。
  • 于莉（1975 年生），董事会秘书，自 2012 年起担任 [6]。

股权与治理

控股股东郑州宇通集团有限公司 2025 年末持有 834,755,347 股（37.70%）；宇通集团子公司猛狮客车有限公司持有 87,428,292 股（3.95%）[7]。此外，截至 2026-09-30，香港中央结算有限公司持有 244,203,910 股（11.03%），中国人寿、太平洋人寿、全国社保基金等机构亦位列前十大股东 [15]。实际控制人为汤玉祥，其自 2023-03-13 完成股权转让后，通过郑州通泰合智管理咨询有限公司间接控制宇通集团 [5]。

竞争格局

主要对手

国内客车市场，宇通主要与厦门金龙汽车集团（金龙、金旅、海格）、中通客车、安徽安凯、北汽福田（欧辉）等竞争 [11]；在新能源客车与出口领域还面临比亚迪、中车电动等制造商 [14]。2026 年上半年行业大中型客车总销量 53,686 辆、同比增长 5.73%，客车市场整体呈总量微增、结构分化特征 [3]。

优势

  • 行业领先：大中型客车产销量行业第一；2026 年上半年宇通 6 米以上客车销量 15,779 辆、是业内唯一破万企业，国内 7 米以上大中型客车销量 8,763 辆、市占率 37.1% [21]。据中国工业新闻网报道，公司已连续 23 年居全国销量第一 [11]。截至 2025 年底，宇通集团累计销售新能源商用车超过 26 万辆，该口径包含客车以外业务 [27]。
  • 出口领先：2025 年出口 17,149 辆居行业首位，并在 16 个国家和地区开展 KD 合作 [12]。
  • 持续研发与高端化：2018 年以来每年研发投入均超 15 亿元；高端产品 T14E、U15 在 2025 年比利时 Busworld 分获年度最佳客车、最佳巴士大奖，为中国客车首次包揽两项年度大奖 [16][10]。

短板

  • 受国内市场需求下滑影响：2026 年上半年国内大中型客车需求下降，公司总销量同比下降 5.73% [3]。
  • 现金流波动：2025 财年经营活动现金流量净额同比下降 55.67%，主要因原材料采购付款增加 [1]。
  • 依赖政策驱动的公交需求：国内公交市场部分依赖「以旧换新」补贴政策延续 [3]。

我们的判断：披露数据显示，宇通的优势集中在规模、出口与「高端化＋新能源」产品结构，而其国内大中型客车业务面临结构性疲软的市场；2025 年 34.94% 的利润增长主要来自海外放量与销售结构改善，而非毛利率扩张——毛利率仅从 2024 年的 22.94% 升至 2025 年的 24.14% [1][12]。

未来展望

公司目标与规划

公司在 2025 年年报中预计，随着「十五五」开局、交通强国与旅游强国建设推进及新能源公交「以旧换新」政策延续，2026 年国内大中型客车需求将小幅增长；海外受欧洲电动化及新兴市场恢复性增长带动，需求总体仍将增长 [1]。据中国客车统计信息网数据，2026 年上半年行业大中型客车出口量同比增长 18.45%，其中新能源出口同比增长 29.77%，下半年预计延续增长势头 [3]。公司表示将继续推进电动化、智能网联化、高端化、国际化「四化」战略，并在海外深化「直销、直服、直融」模式；其中电动化方面深化产业链协同、强化平台化布局，智能网联化方面以「车—路—云」协同架构为核心构建全场景方案 [16]。媒体引述券商研报称，宇通首个海外新能源商用车工厂已落地卡塔尔，采用 KD 模式，设计初始年产能 300 辆、可扩展至 1000 辆 [17]。

我们的分析

我们的判断：出口与新能源客车是两个可见的增长引擎，但均带有条件。出口增长依赖欧洲与新兴市场的持续需求，并受关税与地缘变化影响；国内市场则偏弱且部分依赖政策——2026 年上半年销量下降 5.73%、归母净利润下降 3.52% 即为体现 [3]。宇通能否延续利润增长，取决于出口放量与新能源结构改善能否快于国内市场的下滑，这些条件并非必然成立 [14]。一个值得关注的信号是，公司 2026 年前三季度归母净利润同比下降 3.31%，而扣非净利润 30.99 亿元、同比增长 18.45%，说明主业经营质量在改善，但整体利润仍受非经常性损益基数等因素影响 [15]。

风险

  • 经营风险：国内大中型客车市场整体下滑，2026 年上半年公司销量同比下降 5.73%，主因国内需求下降 [3]；2025 年国内大中型座位客车市场需求同比下滑 13.01%，公司在销量上仍跑赢行业平均水平 [12]。
  • 财务风险：2025 财年经营活动现金流量净额同比下降 55.67%，公司称系原材料采购付款增加所致 [1]。
  • 政策风险：国内公交需求相当部分依赖「以旧换新」补贴与新能源购置税政策，政策变化会影响需求释放的时点与规模 [3]。
  • 海外风险：2025 年海外销售收入占营业收入的 50.95%，海外业务高度依赖关税、出口退税及地缘环境；公司提及 2026 年上半年中东局势影响需求 [1][3]。
  • 技术路线风险：宇通并行推进纯电、混动、燃料电池路线，而海外新能源客车经济性仍在成熟过程中 [11]。
  • 出口政策与本地化风险：有专家对媒体表示，出口退税下调可能推动车企加快海外本地化生产，进而影响出口节奏与成本结构 [17]。
