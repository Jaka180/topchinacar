Overview

MG (Morris Garages, 名爵) is a British-origin car brand founded in 1924 at Morris Garages in Oxford by Cecil Kimber alongside William Morris, best known for its sports-car heritage and models such as the MGB. Today it is part of the state-owned SAIC Motor Corporation, whose ultimate controller is the Shanghai State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission, and is operated by SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle alongside the Roewe brand [1][7]. MG is SAIC's flagship brand for overseas markets and the core growth pole of its international business [3].

In 2025 MG sold more than 300,000 vehicles in Europe, up nearly 30% year-on-year, and remained the best-selling Chinese car brand in Europe for the 11th consecutive year; it is described as the first Chinese brand to surpass a cumulative one million vehicles in Europe [2][13]. Within the group, SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle (Roewe plus MG) sold 886,742 vehicles in 2025, up 25.42% year-on-year [7]. The MG4 EV, launched in 2022, is described by SAIC as the Chinese auto industry's first true 'global car' [4].

    The MG K3 Magnette wins its class at the Mille Miglia in Italy, making MG the first foreign marque to win the race.[1]

Current operations

Positioning and business structure

MG positions itself as a young-oriented brand for the RMB100,000-200,000 mainstream new-energy segment. Media reports put its 2025 domestic sales in China at about 137,000 units, up 59% year-on-year, driven mainly by the new MG4; SAIC said the new MG4 has sold over 10,000 units a month in China since September 2025 [3][16]. In sales structure, MG is heavily overseas-weighted: with about 137,000 domestic units (media-reported) and about 591,000 overseas retail units in 2025, overseas markets account for the large majority of volume, consistent with its role as SAIC's flagship export brand [18]. Strategically, management has framed fuel cars as 'the present' and electrification as 'the future', protecting the fuel-vehicle base while pushing the new-energy transition [10].

Product lineup and technology platforms

MG's lineup spans the pure-electric MG4 EV hatchback, the compact MG5 sedan, the midsize MG7 coupe, the Cyberster electric convertible, the MG 07 new-energy coupe, and export-focused SUVs such as the ZS and HS. Its technology stack includes SAIC's Nebula pure-electric platform, second-generation semi-solid-state batteries (the MG 07 610km version uses SAIC QingTao cells), the DMH hybrid system, mCDC intelligent electromagnetic suspension, and high-level driver assistance on the MG 07 based on Momenta [4][25][12]. SAIC said it was the first in China (在国内率先) to put second-generation semi-solid-state battery technology on the new MG4 and deliver it at scale [3].

Plants and capacity

MG's domestic production is integrated into SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle's Chinese plants. Overseas, JSW MG Motor India produces at the former General Motors Halol plant in Gujarat; director Parth Jindal said current capacity is about 110,000 units a year, rising to 220,000 by January 2028, with potential to reach about 400,000, and that the company does not see a need for a new plant site for at least the next three to four years [34]. In Thailand, SAIC Motor-CP (51% SAIC / 49% CP Group) has operated a Chonburi plant since 2014; its second Chonburi plant (completed 2017) has annual capacity of about 100,000 right-hand-drive units, and a battery plant producing about 50,000 CTP packs a year opened in October 2023 [28][26][29].

Overseas business

Europe is MG's largest overseas market: 2025 sales exceeded 300,000 units, with the MG Hybrid+ family at 137,000 units (up 300%) and the pure-electric family at 46,000 units under countervailing-duty pressure; cumulative sales in Italy, Spain and France each surpassed 100,000 units, and the UK surpassed 370,000 units [2]. In India, sales were about 70,000 units in 2025, up 17.8%; in Thailand, domestic sales plus exports reached 35,872 units, up 44% [17]. SAIC's Glocal 3.0 overseas strategy plans 17 new overseas models over three years [2].

Latest results as of the cut-off date

In H1 2026, MG sold over 190,000 vehicles in Europe, up over 20% year-on-year, keeping the title of China's best-selling brand in Europe for the 11th straight year; SAIC said MG aims to sprint toward 400,000 units in Europe for the full year [32]. In the first eight months of 2026, MG's cumulative sales in Europe reached 241,000 units, up 22.3% [23]. SAIC's H1 2026 report disclosed consolidated total operating revenue of RMB298.65 billion and net profit attributable to owners of the parent of RMB5.15 billion; core net profit (excluding foreign-exchange and impairment effects) was RMB7.87 billion, up 72% year-on-year [22].

Founders and leadership

Founders

MG's founding is credited to William Morris, the brand's 'father', and Cecil Kimber, the co-founder who shaped the brand at Morris Garages in Oxford from 1924; John Thornley is credited with globalising the brand through the MG Car Club [1].

Current management

The MG brand is run by a brand business unit inside SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle, which also operates Roewe. At a dealer meeting on 10 June 2025, SAIC's passenger-vehicle management line-up included general manager Wang Jun, executive deputy general manager Yu Jingmin, MG brand business unit general manager Chen Cui, and Roewe brand business unit general manager Qian Yang [6].

  • Chen Cui (陈萃) — general manager of the MG brand business unit, appointed in June 2025 (reported on 11 June 2025); he previously served as ID marketing senior director at SAIC Volkswagen and worked on the SAIC-Huawei Shangjie project [9]. At his first public appearance on 30 June 2025 he announced the plan to invest over RMB10 billion and launch 13 new-energy models within two years [10].
  • Yu Jingmin (俞经民) — executive deputy general manager of SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle, appointed in August 2024 (announced 14 August 2024); he previously served as party secretary and executive vice president for sales and marketing at SAIC Volkswagen, and was a deputy general manager of SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle from 2014 [27][6].
  • Wang Jun (王骏) — general manager of SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle (appointment date not publicly disclosed) [6].
  • Zhang Liang (张亮) — deputy general manager of SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle and general manager of SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle Sales Company [13]. The previous MG brand business unit general manager, Zhou Xing (周钘), took the post on 9 December 2024 and stepped down in June 2025 [30][9].

Ownership and governance

MG is not a separately listed entity and sits within SAIC Motor, which is listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange (600104). According to SAIC's annual report, the group's ultimate actual controller is the Shanghai State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission [7].

Competition

In China, the new MG4 competes in the A0-segment pure-electric hatchback market against models such as the BYD Dolphin and Geely Xingyuan; it entered with a guide price from RMB68,800 and a limited-time subsidised starting price of RMB65,800, with a semi-solid-state battery version at RMB99,800 [11]. The first-generation MG4 EV was the best-selling compact pure-electric car in Europe in 2023, and the Cyberster occupies a niche in the small pure-electric roadster segment [1][5]. In Europe, MG competes with mainstream and Chinese brands in the compact-EV and hybrid segments; SAIC attributed pressure on MG's pure-electric family to the European Union's countervailing measures, and the brand's general manager cited high EU tariffs as the reason the first-generation MG4 EV was temporarily overtaken in Europe [2][12]. Domestically, MG uses 'technology parity' as its differentiator, bringing semi-solid-state batteries and in-car connectivity down to mainstream price points [12].

Our judgment. MG's competitive base rests on a century-old brand name, an established European dealer network (about 1,300 sales and service outlets across 34 European countries), and overseas scale, with Europe exceeding 300,000 units in 2025 and cumulative Europe deliveries passing one million [13]. Its relative weakness is product concentration: media reports indicate the domestic lineup leaned heavily on the MG4 in early 2026, and the roughly 137,000-unit domestic scale in 2025 remains far smaller than its overseas business, so the domestic recovery still hinges on a single model [16][18].

Outlook

Company plans. MG has announced an 'All in new energy' strategy to invest more than RMB10 billion and launch 13 new-energy models within two years (by the end of 2027), covering pure-electric, plug-in hybrid and extended-range powertrains [10][12]. For 2026, SAIC listed the MG07 and MG4X among the new models to be launched; the MG 07 went on sale on 21 August 2026, and the brand plans to further expand domestic dealer coverage [3][24]. Under the group's overseas 'Glocal 3.0' strategy, SAIC plans 17 new overseas models across SUV, sedan, MPV and pickup segments within three years, with new HEV systems and solid-state battery application [2]. In India, JSW MG Motor India is expanding the Halol plant toward 220,000 units a year by January 2028 [34].

Our judgment. Whether MG can sustain its recovery depends on two premises: first, that the new-energy rollout (13 models by end-2027) broadens a domestic lineup that currently leans heavily on the MG4; second, that the brand manages trade barriers in Europe, its largest market, while the India and Thailand operations scale up. The semi-solid-state battery's safety and cost positioning is the brand's stated differentiator, but its contribution to profitability in the low-price mainstream segment remains to be seen [12][2].

Risks

  • Trade barriers and tariffs. SAIC disclosed that MG's pure-electric family in Europe sold 46,000 units in 2025 under the pressure of countervailing duties, and company statements attributed a temporary loss of ground in Europe to high EU tariffs [2].
  • Market concentration in Europe. Europe is MG's largest overseas market and main source of overseas scale; EU trade measures therefore weigh disproportionately on the brand, while India and Thailand remain smaller in absolute terms [2][17].
  • Domestic price competition. The new MG4 entered China at a limited-time subsidised price from RMB65,800, and the brand has positioned semi-solid-state technology at mainstream price points in a price-competitive market [11][12].
  • Product concentration. Domestic volume leans heavily on a single model; media reports indicate the MG4 contributed the majority of MG's domestic retail sales in early 2026, and SAIC said the MG4 family has maintained monthly deliveries above 10,000 units, concentrating risk in one product line [16][32].
  • India joint-venture finances. SAIC's announcement disclosed that JSW MG Motor India had total assets of INR76.5 billion and total liabilities of INR73.1 billion at 31 December 2024, an asset-liability ratio of 95.5%, and that the company is working to improve its operating performance and cash flow [8].
  • Management change and transition execution. MG changed its brand general manager in June 2025 and is accelerating its new-energy rollout; the delivery of the 13-model plan and channel build-out remains to be watched [9][12].
  • Currency and overseas operating risks. MG has a high overseas mix and is exposed to exchange-rate swings and regulatory and trade-policy changes in Europe, India and ASEAN; SAIC disclosed it has responded to EU countervailing measures by adding HEV models [2].

概览

MG（Morris Garages，名爵）是一个起源于英国的汽车品牌，1924 年由塞西尔·金伯与创始人威廉·莫里斯在牛津的 Morris Garages 创立，以跑车传统和 MGB 等车型闻名。如今它隶属于国有控股的上海汽车集团股份有限公司（实际控制人为上海市国有资产监督管理委员会），由上汽乘用车与荣威品牌一起运营 [1][7]。MG 是上汽的海外主力品牌，也是其国际业务的核心增长极 [3]。

2025 年 MG 在欧洲销量超过 30 万辆、同比增长近 30%，连续 11 年蝉联中国汽车品牌欧洲销量冠军；据称是首个在欧洲累计交付突破 100 万辆的中国品牌 [2][13]。集团层面，上汽乘用车（荣威与 MG 合计）2025 年销量 886,742 辆、同比增长 25.42% [7]。2022 年上市的 MG4 EV 被上汽称为中国汽车工业首款真正意义上的「全球车」 [4]。

现状

定位与业务结构

MG 定位为面向年轻人的 10 万–20 万元级主流新能源品牌。据媒体报道，2025 年国内销量约 13.7 万辆、同比增长 59%，主要由全新 MG4 拉动；上汽称全新 MG4 自 2025 年 9 月起国内月销破万 [3][16]。从销量结构看，MG 呈现「海外为主、国内为辅」特征：2025 年国内约 13.7 万辆（媒体报道口径）、海外零售约 59.1 万辆，海外贡献了品牌绝大多数销量，与其作为上汽海外主力品牌的定位相符 [18]。经营策略上，品牌管理层提出「燃油车是现在、电动化是未来」，守住燃油车基本盘的同时推进新能源转型 [10]。

产品谱系与技术平台

MG 的产品谱系覆盖纯电两厢 MG4 EV、紧凑级轿车 MG5、中级轿跑 MG7、纯电敞篷跑车 Cyberster、新能源轿跑 MG 07，以及以出口为主的 ZS、HS 等 SUV。技术栈包括上汽星云纯电专属平台、第二代半固态电池（MG 07 纯电 610km 版采用上汽清陶电芯）、DMH 超级混动系统、mCDC 智能电磁悬架，以及 MG 07 上基于 Momenta 的高阶辅助驾驶 [4][25][12]。上汽称在国内率先实现第二代半固态电池技术在全新 MG4 上搭载并批量交付 [3]。

工厂与产能

MG 的国内生产整合于上汽乘用车在华工厂。海外方面，JSW MG Motor India 在古吉拉特邦的原通用 Halol 工厂生产；董事 Parth Jindal 称现产能约 11 万辆/年，2028 年 1 月扩至 22 万辆，并可扩至约 40 万辆，公司表示至少未来三到四年无需新厂址 [34]。泰国方面，上汽正大（上汽 51%／正大 49%）自 2014 年起运营春武里工厂；2017 年竣工的第二工厂年产能约 10 万辆右舵车，2023 年 10 月投产电池工厂、年产约 5 万块 CTP 电池 [28][26][29]。

海外业务

欧洲是 MG 最大的海外市场：2025 年销量超 30 万辆，其中 MG Hybrid+ 家族 13.7 万辆（同比大涨 300%）、纯电家族在反补贴税压力下销售 4.6 万辆；在意大利、西班牙、法国的历史累计销量均破 10 万辆，英国历史累计破 37 万辆 [2]。印度市场 2025 年销量约 7 万辆、同比增长 17.8%；泰国国内销量加出口合计 35,872 辆、同比增长 44% [17]。上汽的海外 Glocal 3.0 战略计划三年内打造 17 款全新海外车型 [2]。

截至研究日的最新业绩

2026 年上半年 MG 在欧洲销量超 19 万辆、同比增长 20% 以上，连续十一年蝉联中国品牌欧洲销量冠军；上汽称 MG 全年欧洲销量将冲刺 40 万辆 [32]。2026 年 1–8 月 MG 欧洲累计销量 24.1 万辆、同比增长 22.3% [23]。上汽 2026 年半年报披露，合并营业总收入 2986.5 亿元、归母净利润 51.5 亿元；剔除汇兑及减值影响后的核心归母净利润 78.7 亿元、同比增长 72% [22]。

竞争格局

国内方面，全新 MG4 主攻 A0 级纯电两厢市场，与比亚迪海豚、吉利星愿等车型竞争，以官方指导价 6.88 万元起、限时补贴价 6.58 万元起入市，半固态电池版本 9.98 万元 [11]。第一代 MG4 EV 曾获 2023 年欧洲紧凑型纯电销量第一，Cyberster 则占据纯电敞篷跑车这一小众细分市场 [1][5]。欧洲方面，MG 在紧凑型电动车与混动细分市场与主流及中国品牌竞争；上汽将纯电家族的压力归因于欧盟反补贴措施，MG 品牌总经理亦表示第一代 MG4 EV 在欧洲被短暂反超的原因是欧盟高额关税 [2][12]。在国内，MG 以「技术平权」作为差异点，将半固态电池、手车互联等配置下放至主流价位 [12]。

我们的判断。MG 的竞争基础在于百年品牌、覆盖欧洲 34 国约 1300 个销售服务网点的成熟经销网络与海外规模，欧洲年销超 30 万辆、累计交付破百万 [13]。相对短板是产品集中度：媒体报道显示 2026 年初国内产品线高度依赖 MG4，且 2025 年国内约 13.7 万辆的规模仍远小于其海外业务，国内回暖仍系于单一车型 [16][18]。

未来展望

公司公开规划。MG 已宣布「All in 新能源」战略，两年内投入超 100 亿元、推出 13 款新能源车型（截至 2027 年底），动力形式覆盖纯电、插混、增程 [10][12]。2026 年，上汽将 MG07、MG4X 列入计划投放的新车型；MG 07 已于 2026 年 8 月 21 日上市，品牌并计划进一步扩充国内经销商覆盖 [3][24]。在集团海外「Glocal 3.0」战略下，上汽计划三年内打造 17 款全新海外车型，覆盖 SUV、轿车、MPV、皮卡，搭载全新 HEV 混动系统并落地固态电池技术 [2]。印度方面，JSW MG Motor India 正将 Halol 工厂扩至 2028 年 1 月年产 22 万辆 [34]。

我们的判断。MG 能否延续回暖，取决于两个前提：其一，2027 年底前 13 款新能源产品能否拓宽目前高度依赖 MG4 的国内产品线；其二，在欧洲这一最大市场能否应对贸易壁垒，同时印度、泰国业务能否同步放量。半固态电池的安全与成本定位是其公开强调的差异化点，但它在低价主流区间对盈利的贡献仍有待观察 [12][2]。

风险

  • 贸易与关税壁垒。上汽披露 2025 年 MG 纯电家族在欧洲于反补贴税压力下销售 4.6 万辆，公司亦将欧洲市场阶段性失守归因于欧盟高额关税 [2]。
  • 欧洲市场集中。欧洲是 MG 最大的海外市场和海外规模的主要来源，欧盟贸易措施对品牌海外业务的影响因此更突出；相比之下，印度、泰国等市场的绝对体量仍较小 [2][17]。
  • 国内价格竞争。全新 MG4 以限时补贴价 6.58 万元起入市，品牌并将半固态电池技术下放至主流价位，置身于价格竞争激烈的市场 [11][12]。
  • 产品结构集中。国内销量高度依赖单一车型，媒体报道显示 2026 年初全新 MG4 贡献了 MG 国内零售销量的多数份额，上汽亦披露 MG4 家族月交付持续破万，风险集中于单一产品线 [16][32]。
  • 印度合资公司财务状况。上汽公告披露，截至 2024 年 12 月 31 日 JSW MG Motor India 资产总额 765 亿卢比、负债总额 731 亿卢比、资产负债率 95.5%，公司正努力改善经营绩效与现金流 [8]。
  • 管理层与转型执行。MG 于 2025 年 6 月更换品牌总经理，并加速推进新能源投放，13 款车型计划与渠道建设的落地成效需持续关注 [9][12]。
  • 汇率与海外经营风险。MG 海外占比高，受汇率波动及欧洲、印度、东盟等地监管与贸易政策变化影响；上汽披露已就欧盟反补贴措施采取增加 HEV 车型等应对 [2]。
