Overview

Skyworth Auto is the passenger-car brand of Skywell New Energy Vehicles Group ("Skywell"), founded in Nanjing in December 2010 by Huang Hongsheng, the founder of the Skyworth home-appliance group. Public sources do not agree on the group's legal form: a February 2020 Beijing News report and the March 2021 HKEX announcement use Skywell New Energy Vehicles Group Co., Ltd. (有限公司); the group's website as read for this review, and a July 2026 Sina Finance article citing Tianyancha, use the joint-stock name 开沃新能源汽车集团股份有限公司. The exact name on the day of incorporation is not confirmed as a single legal form. Skywell began as a new-energy bus maker, acquired and reorganised Nanjing Golden Dragon Bus Manufacturing Co., Ltd. in 2011, and entered passenger vehicles after obtaining a production qualification in 2017.[14][9][17][1][2] Skyworth Auto is not a subsidiary of the listed Skyworth Group (00751.HK). Under a 26 March 2021 HKEX announcement, the listed group's indirect wholly-owned subsidiary transferred 11 Class-12 '创维'/'Skyworth' trademarks to Skywell New Energy for RMB28 million; the royalty-free licensee named 'Skyworth Auto' in that filing is Shenzhen Skyworth Auto Intelligence Co., Ltd., an indirect non-wholly-owned subsidiary of the listed group. The licence covers specified electronic products related to cars and vehicles, such as display panels, navigation equipment, detectors and rear-view mirrors. That company is not the passenger-car brand.[1] The media-quoted Skywell PR line that 'Skyworth Auto only uses the name under licence' is a simplification.[9]

The group runs a commercial-vehicles-plus-passenger-cars strategy. Its passenger line has two volume models, the EV6 battery-electric SUV and the HT-i plug-in hybrid SUV (BYD DM-i system); the commercial line produces buses, light vehicles, special-purpose vehicles and trucks under Nanjing Golden Dragon and other names.[3][10] Sales have fallen from a 2022 peak: 21,916 units total in 2022, 18,600 domestic units in 2023, about 11,800 domestic units in 2024 (Yiche), 3,763 units total in 2025, and 387 domestic units in H1 2026.[3][9][19] Sources disagree on the UK launch date: Skywell UK's site says January 2025, while Sina Finance and Jinan Times say December 2024. In June 2026 the Skywell UK site still said it was operating through 16 retail points in England and Wales and recruiting more dealers; July 2026 media reported that sales had paused, the importer had stopped and 16 dealers had closed. The two accounts are left side by side, and a UK exit is not stated as fact as of 29 October 2026. In September 2026 the group made its first appearance at the Hannover international transport show. People's Daily reported that debut and the global premiere, with Zhiyu Weike, of the L4 battery-electric heavy truck Z9, and did not report orders.[9][19][27][23]

Current operations

Business structure

According to the Skywell Group official website, the group's core businesses are commercial vehicles, Heyi mobility (合一出行) and Chuangyuan power (创源动力), with several R&D centres and production bases nationwide; the site states global sales coverage across six continents, more than 45 countries and more than 100 cities.[2] The passenger brand Skyworth Auto is operated by Jiangsu Kaiwo Automobile Co., Ltd., while the commercial-vehicle line rests on Nanjing Golden Dragon, covering a full range from 4-18m buses to logistics, special-purpose vehicles, trucks, passenger cars and autonomous vehicles.[17][24] The official timeline records commercial-vehicle milestones: the Chuangyuan core-components company was founded in 2015, an autonomous 'Blue Whale' bus rolled off the line in 2019, an autonomous mining truck followed in 2020, and in 2022 the group led drafting of a battery-swap standard for electric heavy trucks.[2]

Technology and platforms

The passenger unit's three-electric and driver-assistance capabilities remain largely externally supplied: the HT-i's hybrid system is sourced from BYD (DM-i), and the volume models stay at basic L2 driver assistance.[10][9] At the February 2025 innovation conference the company proposed BE, AE and CE passenger-vehicle platforms and claimed an 800V high-voltage integrated system with 4C batteries ('8 minutes charging for 800 li of range'), V2G charging and the Skylink cockpit with the Xiaowei GPT AI.[5] As its differentiator the company promotes a 'Healthy Cabin 3.0' concept (health monitoring, sleep cabin); in May 2024 it responded that its health-technology claims are limited to improving sleep and similar effects, with no 'cure' or 'life-extension' wording, and that Huang's remarks were quoted out of context.[9]

Models and pricing

At its March 2025 global dealer conference the company announced event prices for the 2025 EV6 intelligent-driving edition (RMB109,800-139,800) and the 2025 HT-i smart-power edition (RMB89,800-155,800), and gave the flagship Skyworth Panshi its global debut. Those were that day's event prices, not the guide prices as of 29 October 2026.[4] The official 2026 EV6 configuration table lists manufacturer guide prices of RMB98,800 (Huixiang), RMB129,800 (Shuxiang), RMB139,800 (Pro), RMB189,800 (health prestige), RMB125,800 (Zhengjie) and RMB199,800 (2026 Shenchí). The same page's navigation also lists Skyworth S710, the 2026 HT-i, HT-i II and EV6 II; their current guide prices were not taken from opened configuration pages and are not stated here.[28] At the September 2022 HT-i launch there were seven variants priced RMB146,800-242,800, with a 200km pure-electric range and 1,267km combined range; Autohome describes the powertrain as a Xiaoyun 1.5L plug-in hybrid, while Jiemian reports the hybrid system is BYD's DM-i.[15][10] In June 2023 the company followed with the EV6 II (RMB156,800-289,800) and HT-i II (RMB128,000-259,800) facelifts.[24] The earlier Tianmei ET5 offered 404/410/520km NEDC ranges at RMB152,800-198,800.[10][14]

Factories and capacity

The Skywell Group headquarters is in the Lishui development zone of Nanjing.[17] A February 2020 Beijing News report, citing the group's own website, put the headquarters at 830,000 sq m of land and 500,000 sq m of floor space with RMB4.3 billion of investment, capable of 20,000 large/medium buses, 200,000 light buses and 100,000 passenger cars a year, with more than 4,000 employees at the time.[17] The group has production bases in Shenzhen, Wuhan, Xuzhou, Hohhot and Weinan, among others.[2][24] The passenger-car operating entity Jiangsu Kaiwo Automobile Co., Ltd. was established on 29 September 2018. As of February 2020 its registered capital was RMB200 million, it was 100%-owned by Skywell, and Huang Hongsheng was legal representative and chairman.[17]

Founders and leadership

Founder

Huang Hongsheng, born 1956, is the founder of the Skyworth group and known as the 'TV king'. Media report that he registered Skyworth in Hong Kong in 1989 (starting from a remote-control factory), and that Skyworth Digital Holdings listed on the HKEX main board in 2000 (today's Skyworth Group, 00751.HK).[10][24] According to Hong Kong ICAC and government announcements, on 13 July 2006 the District Court sentenced Huang to six years' imprisonment for conspiracy to steal and conspiracy to defraud, involving more than HK$50 million, after convicting him the previous week.[7][8] Media report he was released early on bail in 2009, founded Skywell New Energy in 2010 and reorganised Nanjing Golden Dragon in 2011, becoming its chairman.[10][21]

Current management

An official February 2025 release lists: Zhu Ping as Skywell general manager and co-CEO; Han Biwen as Skywell deputy general manager and Skyworth Auto co-CEO; Dong Zhaozhi as executive vice president; Jiang Min as vice president and party secretary; Zhang Jianbang as chief financial officer; Chen Zhijun as vice president; Jia Fan as vice president (a 2023 release called him Skyworth Auto executive vice president and co-founder); and Zi Aijun as chief quality officer.[5][6] A January 2023 release listed Wu Longba as Skyworth Auto president and co-founder; at the September 2026 Hannover show the group chief technology officer was Liu Qian.[6][23] Start dates for most of these roles are not publicly disclosed.

The 8 February 2025 official release lists Zhu Ping, Han Biwen and others among those present. It does not name Lin Jin, and it does not list a chairman. Qichacha lists Zhu Ping as the group's legal representative. That listing does not identify who the chairman was as of 29 October 2026. A February 2024 Autohome Chejiahao article, citing Tianyancha, described a chairmanship change and a rise in registered capital; it is a C-tier account, later registry mirrors do not agree with one another, and those figures are not used here.[5][26][24] As of August 2025, an official group activity report still described Huang as 'founder' and Zhu Ping as 'group general manager and co-CEO', and the February 2025 official roster does not list Huang.[25][5] Wu Longba previously put forward the '4390 quality strategy' (4 certification systems, 9 quality-management tools, no fewer than 3 million km of whole-vehicle testing per model, and zero delivery complaints).[10]

Ownership and governance

Under the March 2021 HKEX announcement, Huang indirectly held about 88.97% of Skywell New Energy and about 46.75% of the listed Skyworth Group; an April 2021 STAR Market Daily report put his direct stake in Skywell New Energy at about 39.76% (indirect vs direct measures).[1][14] The company is unlisted, publishes no separate governance structure for the passenger-car business, and there is no 'actual controller' determination in any annual report or announcement. The 2021 figures above are dated and do not represent the current shareholding structure. A February 2024 Autohome Chejiahao article, citing Tianyancha, gave other direct stakes and a registered-capital figure. That article is C-tier, later registry mirrors disagree with each other, and those February 2024 figures are not stated as the current register.[1][24]

Competition

Competitive position

Our assessment: Skyworth Auto's core weakness is the disconnect between its brand and its product. Many consumers assume it is a Skyworth Group product, but it has no equity relationship with the listed group, so the home-appliance brand's goodwill does not convert into car demand; the two volume models have lacked meaningful refreshes for years, and the HT-i uses BYD's earlier-generation DM-i hybrid technology.[9][10]

Our assessment: its difficulties also reflect the gap between a home-appliance operating model and the logic of carmaking. Appliances are hardware-centric and iterate yearly, whereas intelligent vehicles are software-defined and iterate monthly; Skyworth's models lagged mainstream rivals on three-electric and driver-assistance technology, which contributed to the slide from the 21,916-unit peak in 2022.[10][9]

Our assessment: in the RMB100,000-150,000 band against BYD, GAC Aion, Leapmotor and others, Skyworth's models lack scale and cost advantages and trail on driver assistance (while mainstream rivals popularise 800V and urban NOA, Skyworth remains largely at L2). In January 2023 the EV6 520 intelligent edition received a C-NCAP one-star rating with a 39.9% combined score rate, and in the side-pole crash the chest compression exceeded the limit with a zero score for that item, weakening safety-based confidence.[13][10][11]

Advantages and gaps

Our assessment: its remaining advantages are a valid vehicle-production qualification, an established multi-base manufacturing layout, and a commercial-vehicle business that predates its passenger line. At the 2026 Hannover show, People's Daily confirmed a first appearance and the premiere of the L4 heavy truck Z9; it did not report overseas orders. The gaps are a narrow product line-up and core technology that is largely bought in. The UK channel cannot be written as an exit already completed: July 2026 media reported a pause, while the June 2026 official site still described 16 retail points in operation.[2][9][23][27]

Outlook

Public targets

The company's announced targets have repeatedly fallen short. In 2021 Huang said he hoped to file for the STAR Market that year and list in 2022, proposed '250,000 units in 2025 and 500,000 in 2030', later raised to one million units by 2030, said he would 'invest another RMB30 billion to make Skyworth Auto a global top-10', and separately set a goal of entering the global EV first tier within ten years with a RMB300 billion market value.[10][9] The January 2023 opening ceremony set a 40,000-unit target for the year (32,000 domestic, 8,000 overseas). Two accounts exist for 2024: Jiemian, quoting Huang, put the 2024 sales target at 50,000 units; Jinan Times wrote that the 2024 sales target was 40,000 units and that 11,000 were completed, without calling the 11,000 figure domestic or approximate. The two targets are left side by side.[6][10][19] In March 2025 it announced a 'double-speed growth' strategy, and the February 2025 conference proposed 'striving to rank among the world's leading new-energy-vehicle brands by 2030'.[4][5] These are dated company statements, not achieved results; as of 29 October 2026 the company had still not listed.[10]

Our analysis

Our assessment: the gap between targets and results is structural. Total sales fell from 21,916 units in 2022 to 3,763 in 2025, with 387 domestic units in H1 2026. On the UK business, July 2026 media reported a pause, while the June 2026 official site still described the retail network as operating, so an exit is not stated as fact. Media also report that the Xuzhou government halted further support payments and reclaimed offices while cutting utilities. Any recovery depends on sustained funding, a product refresh and a functioning channel — none of which can currently be verified from primary sources.[9][11][27]

Our assessment: the most likely near-term scenario is continued contraction, because the company has no verifiable funding or product plan to reverse the decline. Other possibilities include restructuring, asset sales, or a pivot back toward its commercial-vehicle base. The 2026 Hannover show, as reported by People's Daily, confirms only a first commercial-vehicle appearance and the Z9 premiere, not orders already won. The available evidence is insufficient to determine which outcome will prevail.[2][19][23]

Risks

Operating and sales risk

Total sales have collapsed from 21,916 units in 2022 to 3,763 in 2025, with 387 domestic units in H1 2026; with a shrinking dealer network, the company lacks the scale to cover fixed costs and after-sales obligations.[9][19] Its sales channel uses a low-barrier 'Skyworth partnership' franchise (per Jiemian, a RMB50,000 deposit plus purchase of three cars and a fitted-out store is enough to open a shop), so part of the recorded volume lands with dealer outlets rather than end consumers.[10]

Financial and funding risk

Media report that the Xuzhou Economic Development Zone promised RMB900 million in industry support to bring in Skyworth Auto, then stopped the remaining payments after the sales, tax and listing milestones in the agreement were missed (a Tencent repost says 'the remaining nearly RMB300 million'; self-media 'Xueshan Caijin' says RMB630 million, about 70%, had been disbursed), while reclaiming offices, cutting utilities and clearing executives out of free hotel rooms. This is media-reported and not confirmed by any official filing, but it points to funding risk for the unlisted company.[11][18]

Compliance risk

The passenger-car production qualification is registered in Nanjing under Nanjing Golden Dragon, while vehicles are reported to be assembled in Xuzhou, which media call 'produced in Xuzhou, licensed in Nanjing'; self-media add that the Xuzhou plant was only approved for parts production. No regulator has ruled, but the ambiguity itself is a compliance exposure.[11][18]

Technology and safety risk

Core three-electric and driver-assistance capabilities are largely outsourced, and volume models stay at L2 assistance; the EV6's January 2023 C-NCAP one-star result (39.9% combined score, zero in the side-pole crash item) is a dated and specific safety signal.[10][13]

Overseas risk

The UK channel has two accounts, and an exit is not stated as fact as of 29 October 2026. In July 2026 Sina Finance and Jinan Times reported that sales had paused, the importer had stopped operating and 16 dealers had closed, with 127 registrations of which more than half were importer stock. Sina says six vehicles were registered in 2024; Jinan Times calls that figure six genuine end-customer registrations, and says only one of the ten largest UK insurers would underwrite the BE11. Skywell UK's June 2026 post still described 16 retail points in England and Wales, with more dealers being recruited, and the January 2026 model-year page footer still names Innovation Automotive Limited (company no. 11065805) as distributor.[9][19][27][16]

Industry and policy risk

Domestic new-energy vehicle market concentration is rising fast, with leading makers compressing price bands through scale and cost advantages while 800V fast charging and urban NOA trickle down; tail-end brands that lack a technology moat and rely on bought-in components have less room to manoeuvre in the price war.[9][11]

概览

创维汽车是开沃新能源汽车集团（Skywell，下称「开沃集团」）旗下的乘用车品牌。集团法律形式在公开来源里并不一致：2020 年 2 月新京报与 2021 年 3 月港交所公告写「开沃新能源汽车集团有限公司」；开沃集团官网现用名，以及 2026 年 7 月新浪财经援引天眼查，写「开沃新能源汽车集团股份有限公司」。成立当日全称未确认为同一种法律形式。开沃集团由创维集团创始人黄宏生于 2010 年 12 月在南京创立，最初是新能源客车制造商，2011 年收购重组南京金龙客车制造有限公司，2017 年取得新能源乘用车生产资质后进入乘用车市场。[14][9][17][1][2] 创维汽车并非港交所上市公司创维集团（00751.HK）的子公司。据创维集团 2021 年 3 月 26 日港交所公告，其间接全资附属公司以人民币 2,800 万元将 11 个第十二类「创维」「Skyworth」商标转让给开沃新能源；同日免费被授权方为深圳创维汽车智能有限公司（公告中称「创维汽车」，系上市公司间接非全资附属公司）。许可范围是与汽车和车辆有关的指定电子产品，例如显示板、导航设备、探测器、后视镜。该公司并非乘用车品牌。[1] 媒体转述的开沃公关口径「创维汽车只是用了名称使用权」属简化表述。[9]

公司实行「商乘并举」。乘用车现有创维 EV6（纯电 SUV）与创维 HT-i（插混 SUV，采用比亚迪 DM-i 系统）两款量产车型；商用车以南京金龙等品牌生产客车、轻型车、专用车、重卡等。[3][10] 销量自 2022 年高点一路下滑：2022 年全年总销量 21,916 辆，2023 年国内销量 1.86 万辆，2024 年国内销量约 1.18 万辆（易车口径），2025 年总销量 3,763 辆，2026 年上半年国内仅 387 辆。[3][9][19] 英国上市时间来源不一致：Skywell UK 官网写 2025 年 1 月，新浪财经与新黄河写 2024 年 12 月。2026 年 6 月 Skywell UK 官网仍称通过英格兰和威尔士 16 个零售点运营并在招募经销商；2026 年 7 月媒体则报道销售暂停、进口商停止运营、16 家经销商关闭。两说并列，不把退出英国写成截至 2026 年 10 月 29 日的既成事实。2026 年 9 月首次参加汉诺威国际交通运输博览会，人民日报称首次参展并与智御维科联合全球首发 L4 级纯电重卡 Z9，没有写订单。[9][19][27][23]

业务结构

据开沃集团官网，集团三大核心产业为商用车、合一出行与创源动力，全国布局多个研发中心与生产基地；官网并称其全球销售覆盖 6 大洲、45 个以上国家、100 个以上城市。[2] 乘用车品牌创维汽车由江苏开沃汽车有限公司运营，商用车板块以南京金龙为基础，覆盖 4–18 米客车、物流车、专用车、卡车、乘用车、无人驾驶等全系产品。[17][24] 官网发展历程记录了商用车与核心零部件板块的多项里程碑：2015 年成立创源动力核心零部件公司，2019 年无人驾驶客车「蓝鲸号」下线，2020 年无人驾驶矿卡下线，2022 年牵头起草纯电重卡换电标准。[2]

技术与平台

乘用车板块的「三电」与智驾能力长期依赖外部供应：HT-i 的混动系统采购自比亚迪（DM-i 系统），主力车型停留在 L2 级基础辅助驾驶。[10][9] 2025 年 2 月创新大会上，公司提出 BE、AE、CE 三大新能源乘用车平台，并宣称开发 800V 高压集成系统搭配 4C 电池（「充电 8 分钟续航 800 里」）、V2G 双向充电与 Skylink 智能座舱（小维 GPT AI）。[5] 差异化方面，公司主打「健康座舱 3.0」概念（健康检测、睡眠座舱等）；2024 年 5 月公司官方回应称其健康科技外宣口径仅止于改善睡眠等，无「治病」「延寿」表述，黄宏生相关言论被断章取义。[9]

竞争格局

竞争位置

我们的判断：创维汽车的核心短板是「品牌与产品脱节」——不少消费者误以为它是创维集团的产品，但它与上市公司无股权从属关系，家电品牌的好感难以转化为购车需求；且两款主力车型多年缺乏实质迭代，HT-i 采用比亚迪上一代 DM-i 混动技术。[9][10]

我们的判断：其困境还折射出「家电模式」与「造车逻辑」的差异——家电以硬件为核心、迭代以年计，而智能汽车以软件定义、迭代以月计；创维车型在三电与智驾上的迭代明显慢于主流车企，这是其从 2022 年 21,916 辆高点滑落的重要原因。[10][9]

我们的判断：在 10 万-15 万元价格带面对比亚迪、广汽埃安、零跑等主流对手时，创维车型缺乏规模与成本优势，辅助驾驶落后（当主流车企普及 800V 与城市 NOA 时，创维仍以 L2 为主）。2023 年 1 月创维 EV6 520 智能版获 C-NCAP 一星评价（综合得分率 39.9%），侧面柱碰撞中胸部压缩变形量超出极限值、该项目得分为 0，削弱了安全层面的信任。[13][10][11]

优势与差距

我们的判断：其现存优势是拥有合规的整车生产资质、已建成的多基地制造布局，以及先于乘用车起步的商用车业务。2026 年汉诺威车展上，人民日报只确认首次参展和 L4 重卡 Z9 首发，没有写海外订单。差距在于产品覆盖窄、核心技术依赖外采。英国渠道不能写成已经退出：2026 年 7 月媒体报道停摆，2026 年 6 月官网仍称 16 个零售点在运营。[2][9][23][27]

未来展望

公开目标

公司公开的目标一再落空。2021 年黄宏生曾表示希望当年申报、2022 年挂牌科创板，提出「2025 年销售 25 万辆、2030 年 50 万辆」，后上调至 2030 年年销百万辆，并称「再投 300 亿把创维汽车做到全球 TOP10」；同年他还提出「十年内进入全球电动车领域第一阵营、目标 3,000 亿市值」。[10][9] 2023 年开工仪式设定全年 4 万辆目标（国内 3.2 万、海外 8,000）。2024 年目标有两个口径：界面新闻引黄宏生称 2024 年销量目标 5 万辆；新黄河写 2024 年销售目标 4 万辆、仅完成 1.1 万辆，未写「国内」或「约」。两处目标并列，不把其中一个写成唯一口径。[6][10][19] 2025 年 3 月发布「双倍速增长」战略，2025 年 2 月创新大会提出「力争在 2030 年跻身全球新能源汽车品牌前列」。[4][5] 这些均为有日期的公司表态，不是已实现的结果；截至 2026 年 10 月 29 日公司仍未上市。[10]

我们的分析

我们的判断：目标与现实的差距是结构性的。总销量由 2022 年 21,916 辆降至 2025 年 3,763 辆，2026 年上半年国内销量仅 387 辆。英国业务方面，2026 年 7 月媒体报道停摆，2026 年 6 月官网仍称零售网络在运营，不能把退出写成既成事实。媒体另报道徐州政府停止后续扶持款并收回办公场地、切断水电。任何恢复都依赖持续资金、产品更新和可运转的渠道，而这三者目前均无法从一手来源得到核验。[9][11][27]

我们的判断：近期最可能的情景是继续收缩，因为公司没有可核验的资金或产品计划来扭转下滑。其他可能包括重组、出售资产，或转向其商用车基础。2026 年汉诺威车展在人民日报的报道里只确认商用车首次参展和 Z9 首发，没有写已经拿到的订单。现有证据不足以确定其中某一种结果。[2][19][23]

风险

经营与销量风险

总销量由 2022 年 21,916 辆降至 2025 年 3,763 辆、2026 年上半年国内销量 387 辆，经销商网络收缩，公司缺乏覆盖固定成本与售后义务的规模。[9][19] 其销售端采用低门槛的「创维合伙模式」加盟（据界面新闻，提交 5 万元保证金、购买 3 台创维汽车、租门店装修即可开店），部分销量落在经销商门店头上而非终端消费者。[10]

财务与资金风险

媒体报道徐州经开区为引入创维汽车承诺 9 亿元产业扶持资金，后因产销、纳税、上市等对赌目标未达成而停止拨付剩余款项（腾讯转载稿称「剩下近 3 个亿」；自媒体「雪山财金」称已兑现 6.3 亿元、约占 70%），并收回总部办公场地、切断水电、清退高管免费酒店。上述均为媒体报道、无官方文件确认，但指向这家未上市企业的资金风险。[11][18]

合规风险

乘用车生产资质登记在南京（南京金龙）名下，而整车据称在徐州下线，媒体称之为「徐州生产、南京领证」；自媒体称徐州工厂实际只获批零部件生产权限。尚无监管结论，但该模糊状态本身即构成合规风险。[11][18]

技术与安全风险

三电与智驾核心能力大量外采，主力车型停留在 L2 级辅助驾驶；2023 年 1 月创维 EV6 获 C-NCAP 一星评价（综合得分率 39.9%）、侧面柱碰撞项目得 0 分，是有日期且具体的安全信号。[10][13]

海外风险

英国渠道有两套说法，不能把「已经退出英国」写成截至 2026 年 10 月 29 日的事实。2026 年 7 月新浪财经与新黄河报道销售暂停、进口商停止运营、16 家经销商关闭，累计注册 127 辆、超半数为进口商库存；新浪写 2024 年注册 6 辆，新黄河写 2024 年真实终端注册 6 辆，并称英国十大保险公司仅一家愿意承保 BE11。Skywell UK 官网 2026 年 6 月稿则称仍通过英格兰和威尔士 16 个零售点运营并在招募经销商，2026 年 1 月改款页页脚仍列 Innovation Automotive Limited（公司编号 11065805）为分销商。[9][19][27][16]

行业与政策风险

国内新能源汽车市场集中度快速提升，头部车企以规模与成本优势压缩价格带，800V 高压快充、城市 NOA 等加速下放；缺乏技术护城河、依赖外采的尾部品牌在价格战中回旋空间收窄。[9][11]
