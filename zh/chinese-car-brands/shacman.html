Overview

Shaanxi Automobile Group (SHACMAN) is a large heavy-duty vehicle manufacturer in northwest China and one of China's large full-series commercial-vehicle manufacturers, tracing its origins to the Shaanxi Automobile Manufacturing Plant founded in 1968 under China's 'Third Front' construction programme, with headquarters in Xi'an [7][1]. The group has about 25,000 employees and a brand value of RMB63.176 billion; on total assets its two official pages differ slightly (RMB92.0 billion on the company-profile page versus RMB92.67 billion on the group-introduction page), and its China Top-500 Enterprises ranking is given as 395th and 373rd respectively [1][2]. The business spans heavy, medium and light trucks, buses and special vehicles, plus key components (axles, engines) and commercial-vehicle aftermarket services.

The group's core heavy-truck entity is Shaanxi Heavy Duty Automobile Co., Ltd. (Shaanxi Zhongqi), 51%-owned by Weichai Power (000338.SZ / 2338.HK) and 49% by Shaanxi Automobile Group; around it sit Shaanxi Automobile Commercial Vehicle (about 68.51% Shaanxi Automobile Group), component JVs Hande Axle and Xi'an Cummins, and the HK-listed Deewin Tianxia (2418.HK) [8][30]. The group itself is not listed; its holding company is 51%-owned by the Shaanxi Provincial SASAC and 49% by Chang'an Huitong [10].

In 2025 the group sold 184,009 heavy trucks, third in China by CAAM data, and more than 200,000 vehicles in total (+22.1%) [14][15]. Born of the military, Shaanxi Auto has developed China's first, second and third generations of heavy military off-road trucks and reports developing China's first LNG heavy truck [7][1].

Current operations

Business structure

The group runs a 'dual-wheel' model centred on heavy trucks (Shaanxi Heavy Duty Automobile) and Shaanxi Automobile Commercial Vehicle. Heavy trucks are the core: in 2025 the group sold 184,009 heavy trucks (third in China), and per a Huachuang Securities reading of Weichai Power's 2025 annual report, Shaanxi Heavy Duty Automobile's own 2025 sales were about 153,000 units including about 59,000 exports, with revenue of about RMB52.6 billion [14][25]. In 2025 Shaanxi Automobile Commercial Vehicle's vehicle sales rose 15%, new-energy vehicles 66% and exports 104% year on year; its absolute sales volume is not disclosed [19]. Components (Hande Axle, Xi'an Cummins, Zhide Electric Control) and aftermarket services (Deewin Tianxia) complement the vehicle business [11][16]. The HK-listed Deewin Tianxia (2418.HK) recorded H1 2026 revenue of RMB1.435 billion (+26.2%) with net profit of RMB33.9 million (-63.0%), and 2025 full-year revenue of RMB2.762 billion (+5.1%) [36][35].

Technology and platforms

The group's flagship is the Delong X6000 high-end platform, launched on 11 November 2020 after a decade of forward development [31]. Its new-energy routes span pure-electric, hybrid and hydrogen fuel-cell; it reports developing China's first LNG heavy truck, holding 1,170 new-energy and intelligent-connectivity patents, and holding the country's first heavy-truck autonomous-driving test licence [1]. Per Shaanxi Daily, the group has put 'full-scenario L2 combination driving assistance and closed-scenario L4 autonomous-driving technology into mass production', and is the first company in China to pass the MIIT's 'information security + software upgrade' dual-standard review; in H1 2026 Hande Axle's electric-drive axle sales rose 45% with over 70% market share, and Zhide Electric Control sold nearly 10,000 e-drive systems [5].

Manufacturing and capacity

The group builds heavy trucks and new-energy vehicles in Xi'an's Jingwei Industrial Park; one final-assembly line can assemble more than ten models at once, daily output was raised from 500 to 625 vehicles in 2025, and 13,700 vehicles rolled off the line in January alone, a record [15]. Per Huashang Daily, one new-energy heavy truck leaves the line every 4.8 minutes on average, and new-energy model orders across the group exceeded 27,000 units in 2025 [24]. Shaanxi Automobile Commercial Vehicle's full-series commercial-vehicle industrial park in Caijiapo, Baoji covers 2,000 mu with first-phase investment of about RMB1.5 billion and began production in December 2019 (a widely cited '20万辆产能、总投资45.76亿元' figure could not be traced to any disclosed source and is not used here) [28]. Hande Axle runs four production bases (Xi'an, Baoji, Zhuzhou, Tongchuan) with annual capacity of 1.5 million axle assemblies [11].

Domestic market position

In 2025 Shaanxi Auto sold 184,009 heavy trucks, third in China (CAAM), behind Sinotruk (304,857) and FAW Jiefang (215,526), ahead of Dongfeng (181,459) and Foton (142,185), in a market of 1.1449 million units [14]. At its December 2025 business conference the company said 2025 group industrial output value was expected to be RMB86.8 billion (+3%), and that Shaanxi Heavy Duty's domestic civilian sales were expected at 88,000 units (+60%) with orders exceeding 94,000 (+64%) [34][3].

Overseas business

On overseas network figures two sets of numbers coexist. The company-profile page lists 40 overseas marketing areas, more than 200 first-tier overseas dealers, more than 390 overseas service outlets and more than 160 overseas parts outlets, covering more than 150 countries and regions with KD assembly plants in 19 countries [1]; a media account (Asia-Pacific Times) reports 41 overseas marketing areas, more than 200 first-tier distributors, more than 600 overseas service outlets and more than 180 parts channels across more than 160 countries and regions [15]. Exports account for about 20% of China's heavy-truck exports, and Shaanxi Auto holds over 40% share in Central Asia [22]. The first export was 13 Yan'an-brand off-road trucks in 1988; cumulative exports have since exceeded 360,000 units [26].

New-energy development

Shaanxi Auto says it began new-energy R&D in 1999, developed China's first single-fuel CNG heavy truck in 2005, the world's first self-pressurised LNG heavy truck in 2008, and China's first national-IV dual-fuel heavy truck in 2014 [15]. New-energy heavy-truck sales rose from about 210 units in 2021 to 773 (2022), 3,103 (2023), 6,900 (2024) and 26,000 (2025, +280%, second-fastest in China), accounting for about 10% of the domestic new-energy heavy-truck market; group new-energy vehicle sales rose 172.5% in 2025 [15].

Ownership and governance

Shaanxi Automobile Holding Group is 51%-owned by the Shaanxi Provincial SASAC and 49% by Chang'an Huitong Co., Ltd. (a Shaanxi state-owned investment firm), and was registered on 20 August 2012 [10]. Shaanxi Automobile Holding Group holds about 67.06% of Shaanxi Automobile Group Co., Ltd., which in turn holds 49% of Shaanxi Heavy Duty Automobile and about 68.51% of Shaanxi Automobile Commercial Vehicle; Weichai Power holds 51% of Shaanxi Heavy Duty Automobile [8]. Deewin Tianxia (2418.HK) is controlled by Shaanxi Automobile Group [16]. As the group is unlisted, no annual report or announcement identifies an actual controller, so only shareholding percentages are stated here.

Competition

Comparison scope

Our assessment: in heavy trucks Shaanxi Auto should be compared with Sinotruk, FAW Jiefang, Dongfeng Commercial Vehicle and Foton, distinguishing domestic sales, exports and new-energy products. By 2025 CAAM data, Sinotruk sold 304,857 heavy trucks (first), FAW Jiefang 215,526 (second), Shaanxi Auto 184,009 (third), Dongfeng 181,459 (fourth) and Foton 142,185 (fifth); in a 1.1449-million-unit market Shaanxi Auto's share was about 16% [14].

Our assessment: strengths and weaknesses

Our assessment: export scale and a military-off-road heritage are structural advantages. Evidence includes exports at about 20% of China's heavy-truck exports, over 40% share in Central Asia, coverage of more than 160 markets and KD plants in 19 countries [22][15]. New-energy momentum is a second strength: 26,000 new-energy heavy trucks sold in 2025 (+280%) and H1 2026 new-energy growth of 39.2% [15][4].

Our assessment: a structural weakness is that its core heavy-truck asset is not wholly owned - Weichai Power holds 51% of Shaanxi Heavy Duty Automobile, which constrains the group's own capital-market plans; Shaanxi Automobile Group's A-share IPO has not completed and is reported to bypass the heavy-truck asset [8][17]. Whether scale and new-energy growth translate into durable profitability still needs to be tested segment by segment.

Outlook

Company targets and plans

At its December 2025 business conference the company set a '15th Five-Year Plan' goal of staying in the heavy-truck industry's top three by 2030 with full commercialisation of intelligent driving [3]. For 2026, Shaanxi Heavy Duty Automobile's domestic civilian sales target is 100,000 units, comprising diesel 32,000, natural-gas 28,000 and new-energy 40,000 (new-energy about 40%) - note this is a domestic civilian target, not the group's total heavy-truck volume [27]. The '2035 Strategy', released on 28 February 2018, aims for an internationally leading full-series commercial-vehicle business [33]. Media report that Proton Motors has considered a Hong Kong IPO of about USD200 million [17]. These are plans and reports, not completed outcomes.

Our analysis

Our assessment: exports and new energy are the most concrete growth levers, supported by H1 2026 export growth of 34.2% and new-energy growth of 39.2% [4]. Whether these convert into profit depends on export collections, localised service costs and product margins. The unresolved IPO path and the 51%-Weichai structure of the heavy-truck asset mean the group's capitalisation progress depends on moves outside its core asset, so actual progress should be tracked against announcements rather than plans [17].

Risks

  • Ownership structure of the core asset: Weichai Power holds 51% of Shaanxi Heavy Duty Automobile, so the group's most important asset is consolidated by a peer headquartered in Shandong (Weichai Power, listed 000338.SZ / 2338.HK) rather than by Shaanxi Auto itself; Shaanxi Automobile Group's IPO has not completed [8][17].
  • Heavy-truck cyclicality: the business is exposed to the heavy-truck market cycle; the company's own framing emphasises counter-cyclical structural adjustment, and results depend on domestic demand recovery [3].
  • Related-party dependence: Deewin Tianxia (2418.HK) is deeply tied to the Shaanxi Auto system: in 2025 its logistics & supply-chain segment contributed RMB2.057 billion, 74.5% of revenue, with customers concentrated across the commercial-vehicle industry chain [35]. China Securities Journal reported in December 2025 that revenue from Shaanxi Automobile Group and related parties reached RMB1.635 billion, or 62.2% of 2024 revenue, a customer-concentration risk for the listed service arm [16].
  • New-energy competition and transition costs: the new-energy heavy-truck market is crowded, and sustained new-energy growth requires continued R&D and service-network investment; the profitability of the new-energy portfolio is not separately disclosed [15].
  • Overseas exposure: large export volumes create exposure to foreign demand, localisation costs and policy changes in overseas markets [15].

概览

陕汽集团（SHACMAN）是我国西北地区大型重型汽车制造企业，也是我国大型全系列商用车制造企业之一，前身是 1968 年在「三线建设」背景下于宝鸡岐山麦李西沟创建的陕西汽车制造厂，总部位于西安 [7][1]。集团现有员工约 2.5 万人，品牌价值 631.76 亿元；关于资产总额，官网「企业概况」页为 920 亿元、「集团简介」页为 926.7 亿元，两页数字略有出入，中国企业 500 强排名两页也分别为第 395 位与第 373 位 [1][2]。业务覆盖重、中、轻型卡车、客车与专用车，并延伸至车桥、发动机等关键零部件及商用车后市场服务。

集团核心重卡主体为陕西重型汽车有限公司（陕重汽），由潍柴动力（000338.SZ / 2338.HK）持股 51%、陕汽集团持股 49%；围绕它还有陕汽商用车（陕汽集团持股约 68.51%）、零部件合资公司汉德车桥与西安康明斯，以及港股上市公司德银天下（2418.HK）[8][30]。集团本身未上市，其控股公司陕汽控股由陕西省国资委持股 51%、长安汇通有限责任公司持股 49% [10]。

2025 年集团销售重卡 184,009 辆、位居中国行业第三（中汽协口径），汽车总销量超 20 万辆（+22.1%）[14][15]。陕汽因军而生，先后研制我国第一、二、三代重型军用越野车，并称研发出我国首辆 LNG 重卡 [7][1]。

历史沿革

  1. 1968-02

    陕西汽车制造厂在宝鸡岐山麦李西沟创建（三线建设），建成我国第一座重型军用越野汽车生产基地。[7][1]

  2. 1974

    延安 250（SX250）型重型军用越野汽车设计定型，为中国第一代重型军用越野车。[13]

  3. 1984-10

    延安 250 军车参加新中国成立 35 周年国庆阅兵。[13][7]

  4. 1985

    陕汽走出蔡家坡，建设西安生产基地，开启第二次创业。[33]

  5. 1988

    陕汽实现首次出口：13 辆延安牌越野车走出国门。[26]

  6. 2002-09

    陕西重型汽车有限公司（陕重汽）成立，由陕汽集团（49%）与湘火炬汽车集团（51%）合资组建。[30][29]

现状

业务结构

集团以重卡（陕重汽）与陕汽商用车「双轮驱动」。重卡是核心：2025 年集团重卡销量 184,009 辆（行业第三）；据华创证券对潍柴动力 2025 年报的解读，陕重汽自身 2025 年重卡销量约 15.3 万辆、其中出口约 5.9 万辆，营收约 526 亿元 [14][25]。陕汽商用车 2025 年整车销量同比增长 15%、新能源车增长 66%、出口增长 104%，绝对销量未披露 [19]。零部件（汉德车桥、西安康明斯、智德电控）与后市场服务（德银天下）与整车业务协同 [11][16]。港股上市的德银天下（2418.HK）2026 年上半年收入 14.35 亿元（+26.2%）、净利润 3390 万元（-63.0%），2025 全年收入 27.62 亿元（+5.1%）[36][35]。

技术与平台

集团旗舰为 2020 年 11 月 11 日发布、历时十年正向研发的德龙 X6000 高端平台 [31]。新能源布局纯电、混动、氢燃料三条路线，称研发了我国首辆 LNG 重卡，拥有 1170 项新能源与智能网联专利，并持有国内第一张重卡自动驾驶测试牌照 [1]。据陕西日报，集团实现「全场景 L2 级组合驾驶辅助、封闭场景 L4 级自动驾驶技术量产」，并成为国内首家通过工信部「信息安全+软件升级」双重标准审查的企业；2026 年上半年汉德车桥电驱桥销量增长 45%、市占率超 70%，智德电控电驱系统销售近万套 [5]。

制造与产能

集团在西安经开区泾渭工业园布局重卡与新能源制造；总装线可同时装配十多款车型，2025 年日产量由 500 辆提升至 625 辆，1 月单月下线 1.37 万辆创历史新高 [15]。据华商报，新能源智能工厂平均每 4.8 分钟下线一辆新能源重卡，2025 年新能源车型全系列接单突破 2.7 万辆 [24]。陕汽商用车在宝鸡蔡家坡建设的全系列商用车产业园总占地 2000 亩、一期累计投资约 15 亿元，2019 年 12 月投产（此前流传的「规划总产能 20 万辆、总投资 45.76 亿元」未能查到公开出处，本文不采用）[28]。汉德车桥在西安、宝鸡、株洲、铜川布局四大基地，具备年产各类桥总成 150 万根能力 [11]。

国内市场地位

2025 年陕汽销售重卡 184,009 辆、位居行业第三（中汽协），排在中国重汽（304,857 辆）、一汽解放（215,526 辆）之后，东风汽车（181,459 辆）、福田汽车（142,185 辆）之前，全年重卡市场总量 114.49 万辆 [14]。商务年会上披露 2025 年集团工业总产值预计 868 亿元（+3%），陕重汽国内民品预计销售 8.8 万辆（+60%）、订单突破 9.4 万辆（+64%）[34][3]。

海外业务

海外网络数字存在两套口径。官网「企业概况」页称设 40 个海外营销区、200 余家海外一级经销商、390 余家海外服务网点、160 余家海外配件网点，产品覆盖 150 多个国家和地区、在 19 个国家建立散件组装厂 [1]；媒体（亚太时报）报道为全球 41 个海外营销区、200 多家一级转销商、600 多个海外服务网点、180 多个配件渠道，覆盖 160 多个国家和地区 [15]。陕汽出口量约占全国重卡出口总量的 20%，在中亚市场占有率超 40% [22]。1988 年 13 辆延安牌越野车首次出口，累计出口已超 36 万辆 [26]。

新能源发展

陕汽称 1999 年在国内率先尝试新能源汽车技术研发，2005 年研发国内首款单一燃料压缩天然气（CNG）重卡，2008 年研发世界首辆自增压液化天然气（LNG）重卡，2014 年推出国内首家国四双燃料重卡 [15]。新能源重卡销量由 2021 年约 210 辆，增至 2022 年 773 辆、2023 年 3103 辆、2024 年 6900 辆、2025 年 2.6 万辆（+280%、增速全国第二），占国内新能源重卡市场约 10%；2025 年集团新能源车销量同比增长 172.5% [15]。

  • 刘义，陕汽控股党委书记、董事长，2026 年 1 月 22 日就任。1978 年 12 月生，工学硕士、正高级工程师；此前历任陕西法士特齿轮有限责任公司常务副总经理、总经理，陕西法士特汽车传动集团有限责任公司总经理；现任陕重汽副董事长，2026 年 7 月获潍柴动力董事会提名为执行董事 [6][18]。

股权与治理

陕汽控股由陕西省国资委持股 51%、长安汇通有限责任公司（陕西省属国有投资平台）持股 49%，于 2012 年 8 月 20 日注册 [10]。陕汽控股持有陕汽集团（股份）约 67.06% 股权，陕汽集团再持有陕重汽 49%、陕汽商用车约 68.51%；潍柴动力持有陕重汽 51% [8]。德银天下（2418.HK）由陕汽集团控股 [16]。因集团未上市、无年报或公告对「实际控制人」的明确认定，此处仅列示持股比例。

竞争格局

比较范围

我们的判断：重卡业务宜与中国重汽、一汽解放、东风商用车、福田汽车比较，并区分国内、出口与新能源产品。按中汽协数据，2025 年中国重汽销售重卡 304,857 辆（第一）、一汽解放 215,526 辆（第二）、陕汽 184,009 辆（第三）、东风 181,459 辆（第四）、福田 142,185 辆（第五）；以全年重卡市场 114.49 万辆计算，陕汽份额约 16% [14]。

我们的判断：优势与短板

我们的判断：出口规模与军工越野基因是其结构性优势，依据是出口约占全国重卡出口 20%、中亚市占率超 40%、覆盖 160 多个市场、在 19 国建散件组装厂 [22][15]。新能源动能是另一优势：2025 年新能源重卡 2.6 万辆（+280%）、2026 年上半年新能源 +39.2% [15][4]。

我们的判断：结构性短板在于核心重卡资产并非全资持有——潍柴动力持有陕重汽 51% 股权，制约集团自身的资本运作；陕汽集团 A 股 IPO 尚未完成，且被报道绕开重卡资产 [8][17]。规模与新能源增速能否转化为持续盈利，仍需分板块验证。

未来展望

公司目标与规划

公司在 2025 年 12 月商务年会上确立「十五五」到 2030 年稳居重卡行业前三、推动智驾全面商业化的目标 [3]。2026 年陕重汽国内民品销量目标为冲刺 10 万辆，其中柴油 3.2 万辆、天然气 2.8 万辆、新能源 4 万辆（新能源占比约 40%）——需注意这是国内民品目标，而非集团重卡总销量 [27]。2018 年 2 月 28 日发布的「2035 战略」提出全系列商用车业务达到国际领先水平 [33]。媒体称质子汽车曾考虑赴港上市、计划募资约 2 亿美元 [17]。以上为公司计划与媒体报道，不代表已经完成。

我们的分析

我们的判断：出口与新能源是最具体的增长抓手，依据是 2026 年上半年出口 +34.2%、新能源 +39.2% [4]。但销量能否转化为利润，取决于出口回款、本地化服务成本与产品毛利。集团 IPO 路径未明、重卡资产由潍柴控股 51% 的结构，意味着其资本化进展依赖核心资产之外的布局，实际进展应以公告而非规划为准 [17]。

风险

  • 核心资产股权结构：潍柴动力持有陕重汽 51% 股权，集团最重要的资产由总部位于山东的上市公司潍柴动力（000338.SZ / 2338.HK）并表，而非陕汽自身；陕汽集团 IPO 尚未完成 [8][17]。
  • 重卡周期波动：业务受重卡市场周期影响，公司自身亦强调逆周期调结构，业绩取决于内需复苏 [3]。
  • 关联方依赖：德银天下（2418.HK）业务深度绑定陕汽体系：2025 年其物流及供应链服务板块收入 20.57 亿元、占总收入 74.5%，客户集中于商用车产业链 [35]；据中国证券报 2025 年 12 月报道，其 2024 年来自陕汽集团及关联方的收入达 16.35 亿元、占营收 62.2%，构成上市服务子公司的客户集中风险 [16]。
  • 新能源竞争与转型成本：新能源重卡市场竞争激烈，新能源高速增长需持续投入研发与服务网络，其盈利性未单独披露 [15]。
  • 海外风险：出口规模大，面临海外需求、本地化成本与政策变动风险 [15]。
