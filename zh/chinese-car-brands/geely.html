Overview

Zhejiang Geely Holding Group Co., Ltd. is a privately owned, Hangzhou-headquartered automotive group founded by Li Shufu, which started in 1986 and entered the car industry in 1997. Its portfolio spans Geely Auto (0175.HK), Volvo Cars, Zeekr, Lynk & Co, Polestar, Lotus, smart, Proton, Radar and Farizon, together with ride-hailing (Cao Cao Mobility), methanol energy and satellite businesses; it aims to lead a green intelligent mobility ecosystem by 2030 and reach carbon neutrality across the value chain by 2045. [6]

In 2025 the group sold 4.12 million vehicles worldwide (+26% year on year), ranking eighth among global automakers, with new-energy vehicles at 2.29 million units (56% of the total). On a consolidated basis the group reported 2025 revenue of RMB631.56 billion and a net loss attributable to the parent of RMB6.44 billion; its core listed subsidiary Geely Automobile Holdings reported 2025 revenue of RMB345.23 billion and attributable profit of RMB16.85 billion. In the first half of 2026, Geely Automobile Holdings reported revenue of RMB173.60 billion (+15%) and exports of 474,228 units (+158%). [26][1][30][12]

Geely stands out for combining a broad multi-brand portfolio with vertical integration in vehicle architectures and electrified powertrains, assembled through a series of overseas acquisitions: Volvo Cars (2010), the London Taxi Company (2013), Proton and Lotus (2017), a stake in Mercedes-Benz (2018) and the smart joint venture (2019). In 2025 it folded Zeekr and Lynk & Co into the listed Geely Automobile Holdings, concentrating its China passenger-car brands under a single listed entity. [16][21]

Current operations

Business structure

Geely Holding's core business is passenger vehicles, carried mainly by the Hong Kong-listed Geely Automobile Holdings (0175.HK) together with Volvo Cars, Polestar, Lotus and the smart and Proton joint ventures; it also runs the Farizon new-energy commercial-vehicle unit, Cao Cao Mobility and digital and satellite operations. Farizon sold 162,000 commercial vehicles in 2025, ranking first in China's new-energy commercial-vehicle market for four straight years. On the group's own consolidated accounts, the Geely series (Geely Auto) contributed RMB345.23 billion and the Volvo Cars series RMB259.34 billion of 2025 revenue. [26][6]

In 2025 Geely concentrated its self-owned passenger-car brands under a single listed entity: in February 2025 Zeekr acquired 50% of Lynk & Co (30% from Volvo Cars China and 20% from Geely Holding) and subscribed about RMB367 million of new capital to reach 51%, forming Zeekr Group; Geely Galaxy was upgraded from a series to a standalone brand in March 2025; and in December 2025 Geely Automobile Holdings merged with Zeekr, which then delisted from the NYSE. This left Geely Auto with four self-owned passenger brands: Geely Galaxy, Geely (fuel series), Zeekr and Lynk & Co. After the integration, the two brands expect to save 10–20% of R&D spending through joint development. [16][23]

Technology and platforms

Geely's technology base is built around the SEA (Sustainable Experience Architecture) scalable electric architecture, plus the CMA and BMA modular architectures and the Volvo-led SPA platform, spanning battery-electric, super-hybrid (PHEV/EREV) and methanol-hydrogen powertrains. Under its 2021 "Smart Geely 2025" strategy the group has built a system covering intelligent driving, smart cockpits, electronic architecture, vehicle architecture, batteries, electric drives and super-hybrid powertrains, and deployed the G-ASD assisted-driving system and a computing centre. Geely Automobile Holdings' expensed R&D was RMB17.62 billion in 2025, and the group has invested over RMB250 billion in R&D over the past decade with more than 30,000 R&D staff. [5][14][6]

Plants and capacity

Geely Automobile Holdings operates 13 vehicle bases in China (Chunxiao, Xiangtan, Baoji, Linhai, Jinzhong, Qiantang, Hangzhou Bay, Guiyang, Changxing, Xi'an, Yuyao, Chengdu and Zhangjiakou) with double-shift annual capacity of about 2.61 million units at end-2025. Volvo Cars builds cars in Gothenburg (Sweden), Ghent (Belgium), South Carolina (US) and Chengdu, Daqing and Taizhou (China); Proton produces at Tanjung Malim in Malaysia; Lotus has a global smart factory in Wuhan. The group has more than 10 R&D and design centres and over 7,000 sales outlets worldwide, with vehicle and powertrain plants in China, the US, the UK, Sweden, Belgium and Malaysia. [5][6]

Market position and sales

In 2025 Geely Holding sold 4.12 million vehicles worldwide, ranking eighth among global automakers, with a 56% new-energy share. Geely Automobile Holdings sold 3.02 million vehicles (+39%), including 1.69 million new-energy vehicles (+90%) and exports of 420,097 units. By brand in 2025: Geely Galaxy 1.24 million (+150%), Lynk & Co 350,000 (+23%), Zeekr 224,000, and Geely China Star 1.21 million (+3%); Volvo Cars 710,042 units; Proton 162,601 (+6.5%); Polestar over 60,000; Farizon 162,019. For 2026, the group plans to build three 150,000-unit-scale overseas markets in Europe, Eastern Europe and ASEAN, plus two 100,000-unit-scale markets in Latin America & Africa and Central/West Asia. In H1 2026 Geely Automobile Holdings sold 1.42 million units (+1%), with new-energy vehicles at 56.2% of the total, and exported 474,228 units (+158%). [29][30][12]

Founders and leadership

Founder

Li Shufu was born in 1963 in Taizhou, Zhejiang, started a refrigerator and refrigerator-parts business in 1986, moved into motorcycles around 1993, and entered the car industry in 1997; he led the 2010 acquisition of Volvo Cars. He is chairman of Zhejiang Geely Holding Group and long served as chairman of Geely Automobile Holdings (0175.HK). In August 2026 he stepped down from the Geely Auto chairmanship, becoming honorary life chairman, while remaining the controlling shareholder. Beyond Geely's own brands, he has also taken stakes in Mercedes-Benz, Aston Martin, AB Volvo and Renault Korea. [24][17][22]

  • An Conghui — CEO of Geely Holding Group; chairman of Geely Automobile Holdings (0175.HK) since 18 August 2026; CEO of Zeekr Group. He previously served as CEO of Geely Auto Group and CEO of Zeekr Intelligent Technology. [17][32]

  • Li Donghui — executive director of Geely Automobile Holdings (0175.HK); vice chairman of Geely Holding Group (formerly its CEO). He stepped down as Geely Auto vice chairman in August 2026 and remains an executive director. [32]

Competition

In China, Geely Auto, Galaxy, Lynk & Co and Zeekr compete directly with BYD, Chery, Changan, Great Wall Motor and newer entrants such as Xiaomi; Volvo Cars, Zeekr and Lynk & Co contest the premium segment against Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Audi and Tesla. Geely's advantages are a multi-brand portfolio spanning mass-market to super-luxury, a global footprint (Volvo, Proton, Lotus, Polestar), and in-house architectures and powertrains; the group ranked eighth globally by 2025 volume. In H1 2026 China's domestic passenger-car sales fell 24.3% year on year and domestic NEV passenger-car sales fell 16.8%, pushing the domestic market into stock-based competition. On exports, Chery sold 1.344 million units overseas and BYD 1.05 million in 2025, keeping Geely Auto in the second tier of Chinese exporters; in H1 2026 Geely Auto's NEV sales of 799,500 units kept it second among China's NEV makers. [41][12]

Our judgment: Geely's main weakness is scale and profitability relative to China's most vertically integrated rival. In 2025 BYD sold 4,602,436 vehicles, above Geely Holding's 4,116,321, and Geely Automobile Holdings' new-energy scale (1.69 million units) trails the domestic leader. At the listed-company level, 2025 gross margin was 16.61% and attributable profit rose only 0.2%, although core attributable profit (excluding forex, impairments and disposal gains) rose 36% to RMB14.41 billion, which the company cites as evidence of improving quality. The multi-brand integration and the losses at associates such as Polestar and Lotus Technology add management and financing burdens. This is our assessment, based on the reported figures above. [1][29][33]

Outlook

Company targets. Geely Automobile Holdings set a 2026 sales target of 3.45 million vehicles, including 2.22 million new-energy vehicles; by brand, the 2026 goals are 300,000 for Zeekr, 400,000 for Lynk & Co, 1.55 million for Geely Galaxy and 1.2 million for the China Star series, with an export target of 640,000 units. Geely Holding CEO An Conghui said the group aims to exceed 6.5 million global sales (including commercial vehicles) and RMB 1 trillion in revenue by 2030, ranking among the top five global automakers. In the first half of 2026, Geely Automobile Holdings reported revenue of RMB173.60 billion (+15%), core attributable profit of RMB9.68 billion (+46%), and exports of 474,228 units (+158%), and raised its full-year export target to 920,000 units. In H1 2026 it sold 949,000 vehicles in China, lifting its domestic passenger-car market share to 11.4%; NEV sales were 799,500 units (+10%, 56.2% of the total) and NEV exports 277,200 units (+585%). Volvo Cars plans year-on-year volume growth in 2026 and stronger cash flow than in 2025. [26][13][34][30][32][35]

Our analysis. These targets rest on three premises: that the Zeekr–Lynk & Co integration delivers the planned R&D and channel synergies; that exports and overseas local production keep ramping; and that new-energy profitability continues to improve. The main alternatives are a prolonged domestic price war, tariff measures in the EU and elsewhere, and slower-than-expected consolidation of the newly merged brands. This is our judgment, not a forecast of share-price performance. [13][16]

Risks

  • Profitability and margin pressure. At group level, 2025 revenue rose 9.87% but attributable profit was a loss of RMB6.44 billion (consolidated net profit RMB3.03 billion, the difference being minority interests), with gross margin down 1.23 percentage points to 18.56%; at the listed level, gross margin was 16.61%. [26]
  • Integration execution. Folding Zeekr, Lynk & Co and Galaxy into Geely Auto (0175.HK) requires organisational and channel integration that may take time. [16]
  • Overseas and tariff exposure. Export growth and localised production in Europe and Southeast Asia are exposed to import tariffs and policy changes; under the EU's October 2024 definitive countervailing duties on China-made battery-electric vehicles, Geely faces an additional 18.8% duty (a combined rate of 28.8%). [13][41]
  • Losses and financing needs of associates. Polestar, which the group's financial-statement notes classify as an associate under the same ultimate controlling person, reported a 2025 net loss of USD 2,357 million; Lotus Technology, an associate, reported a 2025 net loss of USD 464 million. Both require ongoing funding. [37][38][39]
  • Foreign-exchange and impairment. In H1 2026, attributable profit under HKFRS fell 1.8% year on year, partly on foreign-exchange losses. [30][36]
  • Technology-route competition. Rivalry in assisted driving, batteries and solid-state technology could erode Geely's technology position. [14]
  • Leverage and debt. At end-2025 the group's consolidated debt-to-asset ratio was 69.90% and interest-bearing debt was RMB154.36 billion, with the parent company's ratio at 86.81%. [40]

概览

浙江吉利控股集团有限公司是一家总部位于杭州的民营汽车集团，由李书福创立，1986 年起家、1997 年进入汽车行业，旗下拥有吉利汽车（0175.HK）、沃尔沃汽车、极氪、领克、极星、路特斯、smart、宝腾、雷达、远程等乘用车与商用车品牌，并涉足出行服务（曹操出行）、甲醇生态与卫星业务，并提出 2030 年「引领绿色智能通行生态」愿景、计划 2045 年实现全产业链碳中和。 [6]

2025 年集团全球总销量 411.63 万辆，同比增长 26%，首次突破 400 万辆、居全球车企销量第八；其中新能源汽车 229.31 万辆、渗透率 56%。集团合并口径 2025 年营收 6315.55 亿元、归母净利润 -64.44 亿元；其核心上市子公司吉利汽车控股有限公司 2025 年营收 3452.32 亿元、归母净利润 168.52 亿元。2026 年上半年，吉利汽车控股营收 1736.00 亿元（同比 +15%）、出口 474,228 辆（同比 +158%）。 [26][1][30][12]

吉利的特点在于，通过一连串海外并购——2010 年收购沃尔沃汽车、2013 年收购伦敦出租车公司、2017 年收购宝腾与路特斯、2018 年入股梅赛德斯-奔驰、2019 年组建 smart 合资公司——形成了覆盖大众到超豪华的多品牌矩阵，并在整车架构与电动化动力系统上实现垂直整合。2025 年集团将极氪、领克并入上市公司吉利汽车（0175.HK），把自主乘用车品牌集中到单一上市主体之下。 [16][21]

现状

业务结构

吉利控股以乘用车为核心，主要由香港上市的吉利汽车控股有限公司（0175.HK）连同沃尔沃汽车、极星、路特斯以及 smart、宝腾合资公司承担，另经营远程新能源商用车、曹操出行及数字科技、卫星等业务，其中远程新能源商用车 2025 年销量 16.2 万辆、连续四年居中国新能源商用车行业第一。按集团合并口径，2025 年吉利系列（吉利汽车）贡献收入 3452.32 亿元、沃尔沃汽车系列贡献 2593.37 亿元。 [26][6]

2025 年吉利将自主乘用车品牌集中到单一上市主体：2 月极氪收购领克 50% 旧股（沃尔沃汽车中国 30%、吉利控股 20%），再认购约 3.67 亿元新增资本、最终持股 51%，组建极氪科技集团；3 月吉利银河由系列升级为独立品牌；12 月吉利汽车控股与极氪合并，极氪自纽交所退市。至此吉利汽车形成吉利银河、吉利（燃油系列）、极氪、领克四大自主乘用车品牌。极氪与领克整合后，双品牌协同研发预计可节省 10%–20% 的研发投入。 [16][23]

技术与平台

吉利技术体系以 SEA 浩瀚智能进化体验架构为核心，辅以 CMA、BMA 模块化架构和沃尔沃主导的 SPA 平台，动力路线覆盖纯电、超级混动（插混/增程）与醇氢电动。2021 年发布「智能吉利 2025」战略后，逐步构建智能驾驶、智能座舱、电子架构、整车架构、电池、电驱、超级混动的技术体系，部署 G-ASD 辅助驾驶系统与智算中心；吉利汽车控股 2025 年计入损益的研发费用 176.24 亿元，集团近 10 年累计研发投入超 2500 亿元、研发设计人员超 3 万人。 [5][14][6]

工厂与产能

吉利汽车控股在中国拥有 13 座整车基地（春晓、湘潭、宝鸡、临海、晋中、钱塘、杭州湾、贵阳、长兴、西安、余姚、成都、张家口），2025 年末双班年产能约 261 万辆。沃尔沃汽车在瑞典哥德堡、比利时根特、美国南卡罗来纳及中国成都、大庆、台州设厂；宝腾在马来西亚丹绒马林生产，路特斯全球智能工厂位于武汉；集团在全球建有超 10 个研发与设计中心，并拥有各类销售网点超 7000 家，在中国、美国、英国、瑞典、比利时、马来西亚建有整车与动力总成制造工厂。 [5][6]

市场地位与销量

2025 年吉利控股全球总销量 411.63 万辆，为全球第八大车企，新能源渗透率 56%。吉利汽车控股 2025 年销量 302.46 万辆（同比 +39%），其中新能源汽车 168.78 万辆（同比 +90%）、出口 420,097 辆。分品牌看，吉利银河 124 万辆（同比 +150%）、领克 35 万辆（同比 +23%）、极氪 22.4 万辆、吉利中国星 121 万辆（同比 +3%）；沃尔沃汽车 710,042 辆、宝腾 162,601 辆（同比 +6.5%）、极星超 6 万辆、远程新能源商用车 162,019 辆。在海外，吉利汽车 2026 年将重点攻坚欧洲、东欧、东盟三个「15 万级」市场，以及拉美非、中东亚两个「10 万级」市场。2026 年上半年，吉利汽车控股销量 142.3 万辆（同比 +1%），新能源汽车占比 56.2%，出口 474,228 辆（同比 +158%）。 [29][30][12]

竞争格局

在中国市场，吉利汽车、银河、领克与极氪直接面对比亚迪、奇瑞、长安、长城以及小米等新进入者的竞争；沃尔沃、极氪、领克则在豪华与高端新能源市场与奔驰、宝马、奥迪及特斯拉角力。吉利的优势在于覆盖大众到超豪华的多品牌矩阵、全球化布局（沃尔沃、宝腾、路特斯、极星）以及自研整车架构与动力系统，2025 年集团销量位居全球第八。2026 年上半年中国乘用车国内销量同比下降 24.3%、新能源乘用车国内销量同比下降 16.8%，国内市场转入存量竞争。在出口方面，2025 年奇瑞海外销量 134.4 万辆、比亚迪 105 万辆，吉利汽车海外规模仍处第二梯队；2026 年上半年吉利汽车新能源汽车销量 79.95 万辆，连续位居中国新能源车企销量第二。 [41][12]

我们的判断：吉利的短板在于规模与盈利相对国内垂直整合程度最高的对手仍有差距。2025 年比亚迪销量 460.24 万辆，高于吉利控股的 411.63 万辆；吉利汽车控股的新能源销量（168.78 万辆）也落后于国内龙头。在上市公司层面，2025 年毛利率 16.61%、归母净利润仅 +0.2%，但扣除汇兑、减值及出售收益后的核心归母净利润同比增长 36% 至 144.1 亿元，公司以此作为盈利质量改善的依据。多品牌整合以及极星、路特斯科技等联营企业的亏损也带来管理与融资负担。以上为我们的判断，依据为前述披露的财务与销量数据。 [1][29][33]

未来展望

公司公开目标。吉利汽车控股将 2026 年销量目标定为 345 万辆，其中新能源汽车 222 万辆；分品牌看，2026 年目标为极氪 30 万辆、领克 40 万辆、吉利银河 155 万辆、吉利中国星 120 万辆，出口目标 64 万辆。吉利控股集团 CEO 安聪慧表示，预计 2030 年集团全球销量（含商用车）有望突破 650 万辆、营收超 1 万亿元，跻身全球车企销量前五。2026 年上半年，吉利汽车控股营收 1736.00 亿元（同比 +15%）、核心归母净利润 96.8 亿元（同比 +46%）、出口 474,228 辆（同比 +158%），并上调全年出口目标至 92 万辆。2026 年上半年国内销量 94.9 万辆、国内乘用车市占率 11.4%，新能源汽车 79.95 万辆（同比 +10%、占总销量 56.2%），新能源出口 27.72 万辆（同比 +585%）。沃尔沃汽车计划 2026 年全年销量同比增长、现金流优于 2025 年水平。 [26][13][34][30][32][35]

我们的分析。上述目标依赖三个前提：一是极氪、领克整合后研发与渠道协同如期兑现，二是出口与海外本地化生产持续放量，三是新能源盈利持续改善。主要变数在于国内价格战延续、欧盟等地的关税政策，以及新并入品牌的整合慢于预期。以上为我们的判断，不构成任何股价预测。 [13][16]

风险

  • 盈利与利润率压力。集团层面，2025 年营收同比增长 9.87%，归母净利润为 -64.44 亿元（合并净利润 30.30 亿元，差额为少数股东损益），毛利率同比下降 1.23 个百分点至 18.56%；上市公司层面毛利率 16.61%。 [26]
  • 整合执行风险。极氪、领克、银河并入吉利汽车（0175.HK）后的组织与渠道整合仍需时间。 [16]
  • 海外与关税风险。出口增长与欧洲、东南亚本地化布局面临进口关税与政策变化；欧盟 2024 年 10 月终裁对中国产纯电动车加征反补贴税，吉利被加征 18.8%（综合税率 28.8%）。 [13][41]
  • 联营企业的亏损与融资需求。极星在集团财务附注中列为「联营企业且同一最终控制人」、路特斯科技列为联营企业；极星 2025 年净亏损 23.57 亿美元、路特斯科技 2025 年净亏损约 4.64 亿美元，均存在持续融资需求。 [37][38][39]
  • 汇率与减值风险。2026 年上半年按香港财务报告准则核算的归母净利润同比下降 1.8%，部分受汇兑损失影响。 [30][36]
  • 技术路线竞争。辅助驾驶、电池、固态电池等领域的竞争可能削弱其技术优势。 [14]
  • 杠杆与债务风险。截至 2025 年末集团合并资产负债率 69.90%、有息负债 1,543.58 亿元，母公司口径资产负债率 86.81%，债务规模较高。 [40]
