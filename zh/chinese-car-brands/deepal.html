Overview

Deepal (Shenlan, 深蓝) is the new-energy-vehicle brand of Chongqing Changan Automobile Co., Ltd. (SZSE 000625), operated by Deepal Automobile Technology Co., Ltd., a consolidated subsidiary in which Changan held 50.9959% after the C-round completed on 25 December 2025 ([22]). The brand was launched on 13 April 2022 as "Changan Deepal" at Changan's Global Partners Conference in Chongqing, positioned as a digital pure-electric brand for mainstream NEV buyers ([2]); in April 2023 the operating company was renamed Deepal Automobile Technology Co., Ltd. and began operating independently under the English name "Deepal" ([3]). Today the brand positions itself as a "young, technology-driven sporty brand" and offers extended-range (EREV) and battery-electric models spanning sedans, SUVs and hard-style off-roaders ([10], [1]).

Deepal is the volume mainstay of Changan's three own-brand NEV families alongside Avatr (premium) and Qiyuan. On the brand's own disclosed global basis, 2025 sales were 333,117 vehicles (+36.6%), while Changan's 2025 annual report recorded 325,000 (+44.4%). The +44.4% uses an annual-report 2024 base of about 225,000, not the delivery figure of about 243,900 ([44], [11], [6], [43]). The brand remains loss-making but has narrowed losses sharply: Gasgoo's reading of Changan's 2025 annual report gives 2025 revenue of RMB 50.245 billion and a net loss of RMB 899 million, and puts the 2024 comparison loss at RMB 1.571 billion ([52], [6]). The 2024 annual report figure used here for that year's net loss is RMB 1.572 billion ([5]). Its 1,000,000th vehicle rolled off the line on 28 September 2026 ([25]).

To fund its next stage, Deepal completed a C-round capital increase of RMB 6.122 billion on 25 December 2025 (Changan RMB 3.122 billion, Chongqing Yufu RMB 2.5 billion, CMB Financial Assets RMB 500 million) ([22]). Its core strengths are a "Super Range Extender" technology and a dual EREV-plus-BEV route, supplemented by Huawei's Qiankun intelligent-driving system on higher models; in December 2025 it received one of China's first batch of L3 conditional autonomous-driving licence plates ([11], [22]).

Current operations

Model line-up

As of the research cut-off (2026-10-29), Deepal sells seven models: the SL03, L06 and L07 sedans, the S05 compact SUV, the S07 mid-size SUV, the G318 hard-style off-road SUV and the S09 six-seat flagship large SUV ([1]). Its intelligent-driving route is split between the in-house Deepal AD / Deepal AD Pro (standard on the S05) and Huawei's Qiankun ADS (on the S07, L07 and S09); Deepal describes this as "Super Range Extender in the left hand, Huawei Qiankun intelligence in the right" ([11]). The full-line price band spans roughly RMB 119,900 (S05) to RMB 309,900 (S09) ([11]).

Technology and platforms

Deepal's models are built on Changan's EPA1 all-electric digital platform, which supports battery-electric, extended-range and hydrogen fuel-cell powertrains on a rear-drive-based architecture ([2]). Its core in-house stack includes the Force ultra-integrated electric drive and the Force intelligent range extender, plus a "Golden Bell" battery using CATL cells ([13]). In June 2024, a project Deepal completed together with CATL — "key technologies for the R&D and manufacturing of power batteries for large-scale industrialisation" — won a second-class National Science and Technology Progress Award ([36]). The Super Range Extender 2.0 powertrain is fitted to the G318 and S09 ([28]). In December 2025 Deepal received one of China's first batch of dedicated L3 licence plates, with the SL03 among the first two models approved for L3 market access ([22]).

Production and capacity

Deepal does not separately disclose capacity; its vehicles are built at Changan plants in Chongqing and Nanjing (Lishui), where the 400,000th and 1,000,000th vehicles respectively rolled off the line ([13], [20]). Overseas, Changan's first NEV plant in Rayong, Thailand started production on 16 May 2025 with a total investment of about THB 10 billion and phase-one capacity of 100,000 units a year, the first model being the right-hand-drive Deepal S05 ([10]).

Latest operating results

For the most recent completed fiscal year, Changan's 2025 annual report records Deepal 2025 revenue of RMB 50.245 billion and a net loss of RMB 899 million. The 2024 annual report puts that year's net loss at RMB 1.572 billion, while Gasgoo's reading of the 2025 annual report puts the comparison loss at RMB 1.571 billion ([5], [52], [6]). Shenzhen Business Daily, relaying Changan's 12 December 2025 announcement, reported a net loss of RMB 1.025 billion for the first ten months of 2025 ([23]). Chairman Deng Chenghao has said Deepal's breakeven point is about 30,000 vehicles a month and that the brand achieved monthly profit in some months from September 2025 onward ([39]). For 2026, Deepal sold 30,185 vehicles in September (-10.23% YoY) and 252,213 in the first nine months (+8.59%) ([24]); its standalone 2026 interim financials are not separately disclosed.

Founders and leadership

Ownership and governance

Deepal Automobile Technology Co., Ltd. was founded in May 2018 as Chongqing Changan New Energy Vehicle Technology Co., Ltd. and renamed in April 2023; its legal representative is Deng Chenghao ([3]). Its registered capital has changed over time: to RMB 200 million after a first-round financing in January 2020, and to about RMB 330 million after a financing round in March 2022, when Changan's stake was diluted further to 40.7% (per media) ([39]). In the C-round, new registered capital of RMB 138.048787 million was added, lifting registered capital from RMB 328.108278 million to RMB 466.157065 million (about RMB 466 million) ([37]). In November 2022 Changan spent RMB 1.33 billion to buy 10.34% of the equity and lifted its stake from 40.7% to 51%, consolidating the company ([39]). After the C-round completed on 25 December 2025, Changan held 50.9959% and remained the controlling shareholder, while new shareholders Chongqing Yufu Holding held 12.0934% and CMB Financial Assets Investment 2.4187% ([45], [46]). Changan's own actual controller is the State Council SASAC; Deepal, as a consolidated subsidiary, has no separately identified actual controller beyond its parent's control chain ([6]).

Key management

  • Deng Chenghao — chairman of Deepal, and executive vice president of Changan since 16 October 2026 (previously a Changan vice president). Born in 1986, he earned a master's degree in mechanical engineering from Tsinghua University in 2011 and then joined Changan, holding roles at Chongqing Changan New Energy Vehicle Technology including deputy general manager of the powertrain development department, director of the C385EV-E project, assistant to the general manager, and general manager. He became Deepal's CEO when the brand was established in 2022 and was appointed a Changan vice president in December 2023 while remaining Deepal CEO. On 5 September 2025 media reported his move to chairman, with the CEO role passing to Jiang Hairong, and his Weibo verification changed to chairman the same day. Jiang's public start as CEO was 8 September 2025 ([54], [33], [9], [57], [31]).
  • Jiang Hairong — CEO since 8 September 2025. He joined Huawei in 2005 as a chief engineer in Huawei's mobile phone product unit, moved to Honor in 2014 as head of product marketing and later head of overseas marketing, and after Honor became independent in 2021 served as CMO of Honor Device China until leaving in January 2025 ([35], [9]).
  • Wang Jun (former chairman) — president of Changan from July 2023 to April 2025, and chairman of Deepal from January 2024; when he resigned from his Changan positions on 10 April 2025, media still described him as Deepal's chairman, after which Deng Chenghao took over the chairmanship (media reported the change in September 2025) ([32]).

Competition

Our judgment: competitive scope. In Deepal's core RMB 120,000-250,000 mainstream NEV band, its main rivals include BYD, Geely Galaxy, Leapmotor, XPeng and Changan's own Qiyuan brand; the S09 large SUV competes against the Li Auto L9 and AITO M9. This is an analytical selection, not a sales ranking ([11]).

Our judgment: strengths. Deepal's clearest advantages are the dual EREV-plus-BEV route (which addresses range anxiety), Changan's vertically integrated supply chain and manufacturing, and Huawei's Qiankun intelligent driving on higher models — a pairing no direct rival at the same price offered when the S07 launched in 2024 ([13], [11]). Its 2025 sales made it the leading NEV brand among China's central-SOE automakers ([10]). The second-class National Science and Technology Progress Award and the L3 licence plate received in December 2025 are potential differentiators for moving upmarket ([36], [22]).

Our judgment: weaknesses. Sales are concentrated in the lower-priced S05 and S07, and the push into higher segments has been uneven: media reported that the G318 and S09, positioned above RMB 200,000, opened strongly but sold modestly, and that Changan's cheaper Qiyuan brand overlaps Deepal's price band and dilutes differentiation ([14]). Media also noted that among Deepal's seven models only the entry-level S05 exceeded 10,000 monthly sales, and that as of 2025 Deepal had missed its annual sales targets for three consecutive years ([29], [13]). As of the cut-off, September 2026 sales were 30,185 (-10.23% YoY), a cooling of growth momentum ([24]).

Outlook

Company plans. Deepal has stated a 2026 sales target of one million vehicles and a 2030 target of two million with overseas sales at 35%, plus a plan to launch 30 new products cumulatively by 2030 ([22], [38]). Its second-generation products were already in development as of late 2025 and were planned for launch in 2026 ([38]). Chairman Deng Chenghao has said Deepal will "not be Huawei's sixth 'Jie'-series partner but its best CP" — referring to Huawei's line of jointly branded car models — and that the two companies' ADSSE intelligent-driving solution was being pushed down from the RMB 200,000 class to the RMB 150,000 class ([34]). The C-round proceeds are directed to new-vehicle R&D, intelligent and electric core technology and global brand building ([22]). These are disclosed plans and targets, not completed results.

Our judgment. With the 1,000,000th vehicle produced, a 100,000-unit-a-year Thailand plant, a first-batch L3 licence plate and the completed C-round, Deepal has scale and funding to support a product-platform upgrade; the key test is whether it can lift average selling price and turn scale into full-year profit rather than only monthly profitability ([25], [22]). Its dual-track strategy — in-house Deepal AD on cheaper models and Huawei Qiankun on pricier ones — controls cost while borrowing Huawei's brand, and the L3 licence plate could become a lever for moving upmarket ([11]). The 2026 sales trajectory (252,213 units in the first nine months, +8.59%) leaves a large gap to the one-million annual target, so the fourth quarter would need a sharp acceleration ([24]).

Risks

  • Persistent losses. Deepal was still loss-making in 2025 (net loss RMB 899 million). The 2024 annual report puts that year's net loss at RMB 1.572 billion, and Gasgoo's reading of the 2025 annual report puts the comparison loss at RMB 1.571 billion. Those two years had not reached full-year profit ([6], [5], [52]).
  • Balance-sheet strain before the C-round. Shenzhen Business Daily on 13 December 2025, relaying Changan's 12 December announcement, reported that as of 31 October 2025 Deepal's total assets were RMB 31.47423 billion, total liabilities RMB 35.98594 billion and owners' equity RMB -4.51171 billion, i.e. liabilities exceeded assets; the C-round was intended to relieve this ([23]). Media reported that after the C-round Deepal's net assets turned positive ([29]).
  • Price-band overlap and internal competition. Changan's cheaper Qiyuan brand overlaps Deepal's price band, and Avatr is pricing down into Deepal's territory, diluting differentiation ([14], [39]).
  • Sales concentration and slowing momentum. Volume is concentrated in the low-priced S05 and S07; higher-priced G318 and S09 have sold modestly, and September 2026 sales fell 10.23% year on year ([14], [24]).
  • Overseas exposure. The Rayong plant's capacity of 100,000 units a year depends on Thai and surrounding right-hand-drive markets, exposing it to tariffs, regional demand and currency risk ([10]).
  • Management transition. A senior executive from outside the automotive industry became CEO in September 2025; this is a judgment that execution risk exists during the brand's expansion phase, and its basis is the leadership change itself ([9]).

概览

深蓝（Deepal）是重庆长安汽车股份有限公司（深交所 000625）旗下的新能源汽车品牌，经营主体为深蓝汽车科技有限公司；2025 年 12 月 25 日 C 轮融资完成后，长安汽车持股 50.9959%、纳入合并报表（[22]）。品牌于 2022 年 4 月 13 日在长安汽车全球伙伴大会上以「长安深蓝」之名发布，定位面向主流新能源用户的数字纯电品牌（[2]）；2023 年 4 月经营主体更名为深蓝汽车科技有限公司并独立运营，英文名为「Deepal」（[3]）。如今品牌定位「年轻科技运动品牌」，提供增程（EREV）与纯电车型，产品覆盖轿车、SUV 与硬派越野（[10]、[1]）。

深蓝是长安汽车三大自主新能源品牌（阿维塔、深蓝、启源）中的销量主力。品牌自行披露的全球口径 2025 年销量为 333,117 辆（约 33.3 万辆，+36.6%），长安汽车 2025 年年报口径为 32.5 万辆（+44.4%）。年报同比对应的 2024 年基数约为 22.5 万辆，不是交付口径的约 24.39 万辆（[44]、[11]、[6]、[43]）。品牌仍处亏损但大幅减亏：盖世汽车转述 2025 年年报，深蓝 2025 年营业收入 502.45 亿元、净亏损 8.99 亿元，并把 2024 年比较亏损写成 15.71 亿元（[52]、[6]）。2024 年年报口径的当年净亏损为 15.72 亿元（[5]）。2026 年 9 月 28 日深蓝第 100 万辆整车量产下线（[25]）。

为支撑下一步发展，深蓝于 2025 年 12 月 25 日完成 C 轮融资 61.22 亿元（长安汽车增资 31.22 亿、重庆渝富 25 亿、招银金融资产 5 亿）（[22]）。其核心长板是「超级增程」技术与「增程+纯电」双路线，高配车型同时搭载华为乾崑智驾；2025 年 12 月获颁中国首批 L3 级自动驾驶专用正式号牌（[11]、[22]）。

现状

车型谱系

截至研究截止日（2026-10-29），深蓝在售 7 款车型：轿车 SL03、L06、L07，紧凑型 SUV S05，中型 SUV S07，硬派越野 SUV G318，以及大六座旗舰 SUV S09（[1]）。智驾路线分两条：自研的 Deepal AD / Deepal AD Pro（S05 全系搭载）与华为乾崑智驾 ADS（S07、L07、S09 搭载），深蓝概括为「左手深蓝超级增程，右手华为乾崑智驾」（[11]）。全系价格带约覆盖 11.99 万（S05）至 30.99 万元（S09）（[11]）。

技术与平台

深蓝车型基于长安 EPA1 全电数字平台打造，可在后驱架构上兼容纯电、增程与氢电三种动力（[2]）。核心自研技术包括原力超集电驱、原力智能增程，以及采用宁德时代电芯的「金钟罩」电池（[13]）。2024 年 6 月，深蓝参与完成、与宁德时代联合的「面向大规模产业化的动力电池研发与制造关键技术」项目获国家科学技术进步奖二等奖（[36]）。超级增程 2.0 动力总成搭载于 G318、S09 车型（[28]）。2025 年 12 月，深蓝获颁中国首批 L3 级自动驾驶专用正式号牌，SL03 为率先进入产品准入的两款车型之一（[22]）。

生产与产能

深蓝未单独披露产能，产品在长安重庆、南京（溧水）等基地生产，第 40 万辆与第 100 万辆整车分别在此下线（[13]、[20]）。海外方面，长安首个新能源整车基地泰国罗勇工厂于 2025 年 5 月 16 日投产，总投资约 100 亿泰铢、一期年产能 10 万辆，首款车型为深蓝 S05 右舵版（[10]）。

竞争格局

我们的判断：竞争范围。在深蓝主攻的 12 万-25 万元主流新能源价格带，主要对手包括比亚迪、吉利银河、零跑、小鹏，以及长安自有的启源品牌；大型 SUV S09 则对标理想 L9、问界 M9。此为分析所选的竞争范围，不是销量排名（[11]）。

我们的判断：优势。深蓝最突出的优势是「增程+纯电」双路线（缓解里程焦虑）、长安垂直整合的供应链与制造体系，以及高配车型搭载的华为乾崑智驾——2024 年 S07 上市时同价位尚无直接竞品提供同等方案（[13]、[11]）。其 2025 年销量使其成为央企车企中销量最高的新能源品牌（[10]）。国家科学技术进步奖二等奖与 2025 年 12 月获颁的 L3 号牌，有望成为推动品牌向上的差异化抓手（[36]、[22]）。

我们的判断：短板。销量集中于低价位的 S05、S07，向中高端突围不均衡：媒体称定位 20 万元以上的 G318 与 S09 上市初期订单可观但实际销量有限，且定价更低的启源与深蓝价格带重叠、削弱区隔度（[14]）。另有媒体称，深蓝 7 款产品中月销过万的仅入门级 S05 一款；并对照其公开目标与交付统计，称截至 2025 年深蓝已连续三年未达成年度销量目标（[29]、[13]）。截至研究截止日，2026 年 9 月销量 30,185 辆（同比 −10.23%），增长动能有所降温（[24]）。

未来展望

公司公开规划。深蓝提出 2026 年销量 100 万辆、2030 年销量 200 万辆且海外占比 35% 的目标，并计划到 2030 年累计推出 30 款新产品（[22]、[38]）。截至 2025 年底，其第二代产品已开始开发、计划 2026 年投放（[38]）。董事长邓承浩称深蓝「不做华为第六界，只做华为最佳 CP」（「界」指华为与车企联合打造的系列车型），并表示双方合作的 ADSSE 智驾方案正由 20 万元级下探至 15 万元区间（[34]）。C 轮融资资金用于新汽车研发、智能化与电动化核心技术以及全球品牌力提升（[22]）。以上为披露的计划与目标，不是已实现的业绩。

我们的判断。深蓝已实现第 100 万辆整车下线、拥有泰国 10 万辆/年工厂、L3 号牌与已到账的 C 轮融资，规模与资金足以支撑其产品平台升级；关键考验在于能否提升单车均价、将规模转化为全年盈利（而非仅月度盈利）（[25]、[22]）。其双轨智驾策略——低配车型采用自研 Deepal AD、高配车型采用华为乾崑——既控制成本又借力华为品牌，L3 号牌或成为其推动品牌向上的抓手（[11]）。2026 年前三季度累计 252,213 辆（+8.59%），距 100 万辆年度目标仍有较大缺口，四季度需大幅放量（[24]）。

风险

  • 持续亏损。深蓝 2025 年仍净亏损 8.99 亿元。2024 年年报口径的当年净亏损为 15.72 亿元，盖世汽车对 2025 年年报的转述把比较亏损写成 15.71 亿元。这两个年份都还没有全年盈利（[6]、[5]、[52]）。
  • C 轮前的资产负债表压力。深圳商报 2025 年 12 月 13 日转述长安汽车 12 月 12 日公告：截至 2025 年 10 月 31 日，深蓝资产总额 314.7423 亿元、负债总额 359.8594 亿元、所有者权益 −45.1171 亿元（资不抵债）；C 轮融资旨在缓解这一状况（[23]）。媒体称 C 轮后深蓝净资产已转正（[29]）。
  • 价格带重叠与内部竞争。定价更低的启源品牌与深蓝价格带重叠，阿维塔也在价格下探切入深蓝腹地，削弱区隔度（[14]、[39]）。
  • 销量集中与动能降温。销量集中于低价位的 S05、S07，定位更高的 G318、S09 销量有限，2026 年 9 月销量同比下滑 10.23%（[14]、[24]）。
  • 海外敞口。罗勇工厂 10 万辆/年产能依赖泰国及周边右舵市场，面临关税、区域需求与汇率风险（[10]）。
  • 管理层更替。2025 年 9 月由非汽车行业背景高管出任 CEO——此为我们的判断，即品牌扩张期存在执行层面不确定性，依据是本次领导层变更本身（[9]）。
