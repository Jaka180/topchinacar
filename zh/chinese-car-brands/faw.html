Overview

China FAW Group Co., Ltd. (中国第一汽车集团有限公司, FAW) is a state-owned, centrally administered automobile group headquartered in Changchun, Jilin, with the State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission (SASAC) of the State Council exercising investor duties on behalf of the State. Its predecessor, the First Automobile Works, broke ground on 15 July 1953, produced the first domestically produced vehicle of the People's Republic of China (the Jiefang truck) in 1956, and trial-built its first Hongqi luxury sedan in 1958 [12][26].

FAW earns money mainly from building and selling whole vehicles: its own brands are Hongqi (premium passenger cars), Jiefang (commercial vehicles, listed as 000800.SZ) and Bestune (mainstream passenger cars), alongside two large joint ventures, FAW-Volkswagen and FAW Toyota [8][20]. In 2025 it sold 3.302 million vehicles and reported operating revenue of more than RMB541.5 billion, with own brands contributing 940,000 units and joint ventures 2.362 million units [1][10].

FAW is a tier-one central SOE directly supervised by SASAC and, after more than 70 years, has produced and sold more than 64 million vehicles; it has been graded A in the annual performance assessment of central-enterprise leaders for 17 consecutive years, and ranked 183rd on the 2026 Fortune Global 500 [5][21].

History

  1. 1953-07

    The First Automobile Works (FAW) breaks ground in Changchun, Jilin, as one of the key 'First Five-Year Plan' projects.[12][13]

  2. 1956

    On 13 July 1956 the first Jiefang (Liberation) truck rolls off the line, the first domestically produced vehicle of the People's Republic of China.[26]

  3. 1958

    The first Dongfeng passenger car and the first Hongqi (Red Flag) luxury sedan are trial-produced, founding FAW's passenger-car business.[26]

  4. 1990-11

    FAW and Volkswagen sign the 150,000-unit passenger-car joint-venture agreement in Beijing.[8]

  5. 1991-02

    FAW-Volkswagen Automotive Co., Ltd. is formally established, China's first modern passenger-car JV built to economic scale.[8]

  6. 2000-06

    The predecessor of FAW Toyota (Tianjin Toyota) is founded, later forming the FAW-Toyota 50/50 joint venture.[20]

  7. 2011-06

    FAW restructures its core business and establishes China FAW Co., Ltd., which holds the vehicle and JV interests.[12][13]

  8. 2017-12

    The group completes corporate-system reform, renaming itself China FAW Group Co., Ltd.[13]

  9. 2023-08

    Qiu Xiandong is appointed chairman and party secretary of China FAW Group.[6][15]

  10. 2024-03

    Liu Yigong is appointed director, general manager and deputy party secretary of China FAW Group.[16]

  12. 2026-09-14

    GAC Group signs a letter of intent with China FAW Co., Ltd. to buy part of a FAW-held vehicle JV via share issuance.[11][14]

Current operations

Business structure

By brand, 2025 sales of 3.302 million units split into own brands of 940,000 units (+15%) and joint ventures of 2.362 million units, with own-brand NEV sales of 366,000 units (+71.4%) [1]. Key brands sold: Hongqi more than 460,000 units, Jiefang more than 280,000, Bestune 200,017, FAW-Volkswagen 1,587,065 (including imports) and FAW Toyota 805,518 [5][24]. Note that FAW-Volkswagen's 1,587,065 includes imported vehicles and is not on the same statistical basis as the group's 2.362 million joint-venture sales, so the two cannot be added directly [24]. The group does not disclose a segment revenue breakdown; computed from the SASAC sales figures, joint-venture brands accounted for about 71.5% of 2025 group sales (2.362 divided by 3.302) [1]. Hongqi completed a brand renewal into a master-brand structure in which the Hongqi, Hongqi Tiangong (NEV) and Hongqi JinKuihua (ultra-luxury) sub-brands develop in concert; own-brand sales and own-brand NEV sales grew 15% and 71.4% respectively in 2025. For comparison, in 2024 own-brand sales were 819,000 units (+14.7%) and intelligent-connected NEV sales were 339,000 units (+41%) [2][1].

Technology and platforms

Around Hongqi, FAW has built three technology platforms — Tiangong (pure electric), Honghu (hybrid) and Jiuzhang (intelligence) — plus a 900V high-voltage platform, and iteratively released the Shengqi 'SIGHT (531)' technology strategy [1]. In 2023 the group broke through 378 key core technologies, filed 7,589 patent applications in the year (cumulative applications 20,772), invested RMB12.58 billion in NEV operations (+22%) and spent RMB16.61 billion on R&D [3][26]. In 2025 it obtained 2,650 granted patents including 1,805 invention patents, and broke through more than 300 key core technologies [5]. Jiefang released its Lantu 3.0 commercial-vehicle NEV platform [1].

The JV side is also localizing: FAW-Volkswagen independently developed a SOA electrical/electronic architecture and holds core technologies such as the twin-engine (双擎) transmission, a 1.5L hybrid-dedicated engine and Ni60 battery cells, and plans to introduce 16 new China-specific models across its three brands in multiple segments from 2026 [1]. FAW Toyota, under an 'oil-and-electric co-advance' strategy, launched the new bZ5 together with the new RAV4 as a 'twin engine' and released the 'IT'S TiME 3.0' technology brand covering intelligent driving, smart cockpit and powertrain [1].

Plants and capacity

The Hongqi Fanrong plant in Changchun was completed in 2021 and has run near full capacity since, rolling out about 1,000 vehicles a day; it has stamping, welding, painting, final-assembly and battery-motor five workshops, with 71.4% automation across the stamping line and 739 robots giving 100% welding automation, and the final-assembly line supports flexible A-to-C+ class production [33]. FAW-Volkswagen operates five production bases in Changchun, Chengdu, Foshan, Qingdao and Tianjin, and produced its 30-millionth vehicle in 2025 [8][1]. FAW-Volkswagen's Changchun base and Jiefang Dachai were named 'excellent-grade' smart factories [5].

Overseas business

Jiefang has expanded to 105 countries and regions; its 2025 overseas sales exceeded 60,000 units for the first time (a ten-fold rise in six years), with Southeast Asia, West Asia, Southeast Africa and South Africa up over 50% year on year and Northwest Africa up 277.2%; a Saudi subsidiary was established [1]. FAW-Volkswagen's first export vehicles rolled off the line and were shipped to the Middle East in 2025, marking an overseas 'from zero to one' step [5]. Bestune set up FAW Bestune International to focus on Eastern Europe and the Gulf region [1]. In 2024 the group exported 125,000 vehicles [2].

Half-year sales disclosure

SASAC's 3 July 2025 release states that China FAW sold more than 1.571 million vehicles in the first half of 2025 (+6.1% year on year), including more than 449,800 own-brand vehicles and 1.121 million joint-venture vehicles [34].

Founders and leadership

Founder

As a state-owned central enterprise, FAW has no single entrepreneur-founder; it evolved from the state-built First Automobile Works established in 1953 [12].

Current management

  • Qiu Xiandong (邱现东) — chairman and party secretary. Born November 1969; graduated from Tsinghua University (forging technology and business management), a professorate-level senior accountant (正高级会计师). He held finance roles at Dongfeng Motor, served as general manager of the Dongfeng-PSA joint venture Shenlong Automobile, and later as a vice president of Dongfeng Motor Group; he joined FAW in March 2017 as a standing committee member and deputy general manager, became director, general manager and deputy party secretary in July 2020, and chairman and party secretary on 31 August 2023 [6][38].
  • Liu Yigong (刘亦功) — director, general manager, deputy party secretary and president of the Hongqi brand operation committee. Born April 1968; graduated from Wuhan Institute of Technology (forging), a professorate-level senior engineer (正高级工程师). He was general manager of FAW-Volkswagen, then a standing committee member and deputy general manager of FAW, and became director, general manager and deputy party secretary on 15 March 2024, while concurrently serving as president of the Hongqi brand operation committee [32][16][17].
  • Other current core officers (as of 29 October 2026): Liang Guiyou (director, deputy party secretary and trade union chairman); Sun Jiguang (member of the Party Committee Standing Committee and head of the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection and National Commission of Supervision inspection and supervision team stationed at China FAW Group Co., Ltd.); Fang Shili (standing committee member and chief accountant); and standing committee members and deputy general managers Wu Bilei, Chen Bin, Gao Pu and Zhang Xiaofan [37][39].

Ownership and governance

FAW Group is an unlisted central SOE, with SASAC holding 100% (per FAW Jiefang announcement 2025-069), and there is no listed-company-style disclosure of a 'controller'; the group's vehicle and JV assets are held by China FAW Co., Ltd., established in June 2011 [25][12]. In the listed FAW Jiefang (000800.SZ), as of 2026-06-30 China FAW Co., Ltd. held 62.19% and FAW Bestune held 15.94% [28].

Competition

Our judgment. FAW competes with groups such as SAIC, Dongfeng, Changan, BYD, Geely, Chery and Great Wall Motor. Its scale and commercial-vehicle position are competitive assets: 2025 group sales were 3.302 million vehicles, and the group release reported Jiefang as No.1 in medium/heavy-truck terminal share [1]. Jiefang's annual report disclosed 45,063 NEV sales (+184.3%) and a No.1 position specifically in NEV tractor trucks [27]. The group release uses a broader NEV ranking description; we adopt the annual report's narrower segment definition. Hongqi exceeded 460,000 sales in 2025 [5]. These figures underpin our view that FAW has substantial established vehicle businesses.

Our judgment. FAW remains materially exposed to the performance of joint ventures: their 2.362 million sales represented about 71.5% of 2025 group volume. Own-brand sales were 940,000 units, including 366,000 NEVs, up 71.4% year on year [1]. The growth shows an expanding own-brand NEV business, but does not by itself establish a lead over competitors or prove that it can offset any future loss of JV volume. Assessing that requires comparable later-period sales and profitability disclosures.

Outlook

Company plans. FAW set a 2026 sales target of 3.546 million vehicles, with Hongqi aiming for 550,000 units and pushing toward one million by 2028 [10]. Jiefang targets 320,000 units for 2026 (75,000 NEVs and 80,000 overseas), and under its SPRINT2030 strategy aims for more than 500,000 global sales by 2030 including 180,000 overseas (36%) [22]. On restructuring, GAC Group announced on 28 September 2026 a plan to acquire China FAW Co., Ltd.'s 50% stake in FAW Toyota via share issuance; per the announcement, China FAW Co., Ltd. would become a significant strategic shareholder of GAC after completion, while FAW Toyota would remain an independent operating entity. On 29 September 2026 GAC's controlling shareholder Guangzhou Automobile Industry Group signed a strategic-cooperation framework agreement with China FAW, and on 8 October 2026 China FAW, Toyota and GAC signed a new framework agreement under which the three parties will cooperate across R&D, procurement, production and sales and jointly support FAW Toyota and GAC Toyota to develop in tandem [23]. As of the cut-off date (2026-10-29), the share transaction had not been completed [11][19].

Our judgment. The 2026 target of 3.546 million vehicles is a company plan rather than a completed result [10]. Relative to 2025 sales of 3.302 million, meeting it requires about 7.4% growth (a simple calculation). The 2025 own-brand NEV growth of 71.4% provides a basis for monitoring further expansion, but its 366,000-unit scale must be assessed alongside 2.362 million JV sales [1]. If own-brand NEV sales continue to grow while the JV business remains stable, the target has a stronger operating basis; if either weakens, the outcome could differ. The proposed FAW Toyota stake transaction also makes future sales comparisons sensitive to any change in consolidation scope: until completion terms and subsequent disclosures are available, we cannot assume either a particular volume impact or realized synergies [11]. Overseas expansion likewise requires execution in local markets; Jiefang's disclosed 60,000 exports in 2025 establish a starting volume, not a guarantee of future growth [27].

Risks

  • Joint-venture exposure. JV brands accounted for about 71.5% of 2025 group sales [1].
  • NEV business scale. Own-brand NEV sales were 366,000 units in 2025, within own-brand sales of 940,000 units [1].
  • Pending restructuring. GAC's announcement describes a proposed acquisition of a 50% stake in FAW Toyota [11].
  • Overseas exposure. Jiefang reported 60,000 overseas exports in 2025 [27].
  • Financial information available to this research. The group financial table leaves gross margin and attributable net profit null; the cited audited 2024 disclosure gives group net profit without identifying it as attributable to owners of the parent [25].

概览

中国第一汽车集团有限公司（简称「中国一汽」、FAW）是一家总部位于吉林省长春市、由国务院国资委代表国务院履行出资人职责的国有汽车集团。其前身第一汽车制造厂于 1953 年 7 月 15 日奠基，1956 年造出新中国第一辆汽车（解放牌卡车），1958 年试制成功第一辆红旗牌高级轿车 [12][26]。

一汽主要依靠整车制造与销售盈利：自主品牌为红旗（高端乘用车）、解放（商用车，主体为上市公司一汽解放 000800.SZ）、奔腾（主流乘用车），并经营一汽-大众、一汽丰田两大合资企业 [8][20]。2025 年销售整车 330.2 万辆、营业收入突破 5415 亿元，其中自主品牌 94 万辆、合资品牌 236.2 万辆 [1][10]。

一汽是国务院国资委直接监管的一级央企，历经七十余年发展，累计产销汽车超 6400 万辆，连续 17 年获评年度中央企业负责人经营业绩考核 A 级，2026 年位列《财富》世界 500 强第 183 位 [5][21]。

历史沿革

  1. 1953-07

    第一汽车制造厂在吉林长春奠基，为国家「一五」计划重点建设项目之一。[12][13]

  3. 1958

    第一辆东风牌小轿车和第一辆红旗牌高级轿车试制成功，奠定一汽轿车事业基础。[26]

  4. 1990-11

    一汽与德国大众在北京签署 15 万辆轿车合资项目协议。[8]

现状

业务结构

分品牌看，2025 年整车销量 330.2 万辆中，自主品牌 94 万辆（同比 +15%）、合资品牌 236.2 万辆；自主新能源汽车销量 36.6 万辆（同比 +71.4%）[1]。主要品牌销量为：红旗超 46 万辆、解放超 28 万辆、奔腾 200,017 辆、一汽-大众 1,587,065 辆（含进口车）、一汽丰田 805,518 辆 [5][24]。需要说明，一汽-大众 1,587,065 辆为含进口车的口径，与集团「合资品牌销量 236.2 万辆」的统计范围并不一致，两者不可直接相加 [24]。集团未披露分业务收入构成；按国资委销量数据计算，2025 年合资品牌销量约占集团总销量的 71.5%（236.2÷330.2）[1]。红旗品牌完成焕新升级，构建起「红旗」母品牌统领，红旗、红旗天工（新能源）、红旗金葵花（超豪华）三大子品牌协同发展的体系；自主品牌与自主新能源 2025 年销量增速分别为 15% 与 71.4%。作为对比，2024 年自主品牌销量 81.9 万辆（同比 +14.7%）、智能网联新能源汽车销量 33.9 万辆（同比 +41%）[2][1]。

技术与平台

一汽围绕红旗构建了天工纯电、鸿鹄混动、九章智能三大技术平台及 900V 高压平台，并迭代发布阩旗技术「SIGHT（洞见）531」发展战略 [1]。2023 年一汽突破关键核心技术 378 项，全年申请专利 7,589 件（累计申请 20,772 件），新能源业务资金投入 125.8 亿元（同比 +22%）、研发投入 166.1 亿元 [3][26]。2025 年获得授权专利 2,650 件、授权发明专利 1,805 件，突破关键核心技术 300 余项 [5]。解放发布「蓝途3.0」商用车新能源平台 [1]。

合资板块同步加码本土化：一汽-大众自主研发 SOA 电子电气架构，掌握「双擎」变速箱、1.5L 混动专用发动机、Ni60 电池等核心技术，并计划自 2026 年起在三大品牌多个细分市场引入 16 款为中国市场打造的全新车型 [1]；一汽丰田以「油电共进」策略推出全新 bZ5 与全新荣放构成「双引擎」，并发布技术品牌「IT'S TiME 3.0」，覆盖智能驾驶、智慧座舱与动力系统 [1]。

工厂与产能

红旗繁荣厂区 2021 年建成投产，自投产以来基本满产，平均每天约 1,000 台整车下线；厂区建有冲压、焊装、涂装、总装、电池电驱五大车间，冲压线全过程自动化率 71.4%，焊装线 739 台机器人焊接自动化率 100%，总装线可实现 A 级到 C+ 级全平台柔性生产 [33]。一汽-大众拥有长春、成都、佛山、青岛、天津五大基地，2025 年迎来第 3000 万辆整车下线 [8][1]。一汽-大众长春基地、解放大柴公司获评卓越级智能工厂 [5]。

海外业务

解放品牌海外业务已拓展至 105 个国家和地区，2025 年海外销量首次突破 6 万辆，实现六年十倍增长；其中东南亚、西亚、东南非、南非市场同比增长超 50%，西北非大区增长 277.2%；沙特子公司正式落成 [1]。一汽-大众 2025 年首批出口车辆下线并出口中东，实现海外事业「从 0 到 1」的突破 [5]。奔腾成立一汽奔腾国际公司独立运营，聚焦东欧及海湾等重点市场 [1]。2024 年集团整车出口 12.5 万辆 [2]。

半年度销量披露

国务院国资委 2025 年 7 月 3 日披露，2025 年上半年中国一汽整车销量突破 157.1 万辆，同比增长 6.1%；其中自主品牌销量突破 44.98 万辆、合资品牌销量 112.1 万辆 [34]。

竞争格局

我们的判断。一汽与上汽、东风、长安、比亚迪、吉利、奇瑞、长城等集团存在竞争关系。规模和商用车业务是其竞争基础：2025 年集团整车销量 330.2 万辆，集团新闻稿披露解放中重卡终端份额国内第一 [1]。解放年报披露新能源销量 45,063 辆、同比增长 184.3%，排名明确为新能源牵引车行业第一 [27]。集团新闻稿对新能源排名的表述较宽，此处采用年报明确的细分口径。红旗 2025 年销量超过 46 万辆 [5]。上述数据支持我们对一汽既有整车业务基础的判断。

我们的判断。一汽仍较大程度受到合资业务表现的影响：2025 年合资品牌销量 236.2 万辆，约占集团总销量 71.5%；自主品牌销量 94 万辆，其中自主新能源 36.6 万辆、同比增长 71.4% [1]。这一增速显示自主新能源业务在扩大，但单凭增速不能证明其领先对手，也不能证明其足以抵消未来合资销量的任何减少；还需观察后续可比期间的销量与盈利披露。

未来展望

公司公开规划。一汽确定 2026 年整车销量目标 354.6 万辆，红旗品牌冲刺 55 万辆、2028 年向百万辆规模迈进 [10]。解放确定 2026 年目标整车销量 32 万辆（新能源 7.5 万辆、海外 8 万辆），并按「SPRINT2030」国际化战略提出到 2030 年全球销量超 50 万辆、海外 18 万辆（占比 36%）[22]。重组方面，广汽集团 2026 年 9 月 28 日公告拟发行股份购买一汽股份持有的一汽丰田 50% 股权并募集配套资金；按公告，交易完成后一汽股份将成为广汽集团重要战略股东，一汽丰田仍作为独立运营主体存续。2026 年 9 月 29 日广汽集团控股股东广汽工业与中国一汽签署战略合作框架协议，10 月 8 日中国一汽、丰田汽车、广汽集团签署全新战略合作框架协议，三方约定围绕研发、采购、生产、销售全链路合作，并共同支持一汽丰田与广汽丰田协同发展 [23]。截至研究截止日（2026-10-29），上述股权交易尚未完成 [11][19]。

我们的判断。2026 年整车 354.6 万辆是公司计划，并非已经实现的结果 [10]。相对 2025 年销量 330.2 万辆，实现这一目标需要约 7.4% 的增长（简单计算）。2025 年自主新能源销量增长 71.4%，是观察后续扩张的依据，但其 36.6 万辆的规模仍需与合资品牌 236.2 万辆并看 [1]。若自主新能源持续增长、合资业务保持稳定，目标的经营基础将更充分；若其中任一板块转弱，结果可能不同。一汽丰田股权交易还可能改变后续销量的统计范围；在交易完成条件及后续披露明确前，不能预设特定销量影响或协同效果已经实现 [11]。海外扩张同样取决于当地市场的执行，解放披露的 2025 年出口 6 万辆只是既有规模，不能保证未来继续增长 [27]。

风险

  • 合资业务敞口。2025 年合资品牌销量约占集团总销量 71.5% [1]。
  • 新能源业务规模。2025 年自主品牌销量 94 万辆，其中自主新能源销量 36.6 万辆 [1]。
  • 待完成的重组。广汽公告所述为拟收购一汽丰田 50% 股权的交易 [11]。
  • 海外业务敞口。解放披露 2025 年海外出口 6 万辆 [27]。
  • 本研究取得的财务信息。集团财务表的毛利率和归母净利润留空；所引经审计 2024 年披露列示集团净利润，但未将其标注为归母口径 [25]。
