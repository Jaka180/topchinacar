Overview

MAEXTRO (尊界) is the ultra-luxury new-energy brand jointly created by Anhui Jianghuai Automobile Group (JAC; Shanghai Stock Exchange 600418.SH) and Huawei under Huawei's Harmony Intelligent Mobility Alliance (HIMA) model. Under the cooperation agreement, JAC is responsible for product development and the production base, while Huawei handles global exclusive sales, marketing and user operations and participates in improving manufacturing; the '尊界'/'MAEXTRO' trademarks were transferred by Huawei to JAC in 2024 and belong to JAC [17][2].

The brand debuted with the MAEXTRO S800 ultra-luxury executive sedan, launched on 30 May 2025 at RMB 708,000-1,018,000 in pure-electric and EREV versions [1][19]. It is JAC's first brand to enter the million-yuan price band and achieve batch delivery [17].

It has since grown into a sedan-plus-MPV line-up: the S800 Grand Design 典藏大观 (from RMB 1,388,000, June 2026) and the V800/V680 MPVs (from RMB 648,000, August 2026) [11][3].

The S800 delivered over 10,000 units cumulatively by 31 December 2025 [7], and by September 2026 cumulative MAEXTRO deliveries exceeded 19,700 units; per the Anhui SASAC, the S800 has led China's million-yuan luxury car segment in sales for 10 consecutive months since scale deliveries began in September 2025 [17][3].

The name MAEXTRO derives from 'Maestro'; JAC's English site describes the brand as China's first 'tech-luxury' automotive brand, co-created by JAC Group and Huawei in 2023 [2]. Within HIMA, MAEXTRO sits at the top of a five-brand matrix (AITO, Luxeed, Stelato, MAEXTRO and Shangjie) that Huawei runs with Seres, Chery, BAIC, JAC and SAIC [9][24].

In October 2026 the V800 was drawn into a dispute after a video showed brake-pedal bracket fractures in an emergency-braking test, and three government departments opened a joint investigation, leaving the brand at a pivotal point between rebuilding trust and ramping up its MPVs [17].

Current operations

Positioning and business role

MAEXTRO is the flagship of JAC's premiumisation strategy. JAC describes the brand as its 'number-one project' and lists it first among its priorities for the 15th Five-Year Plan period; in its 2025 annual report JAC stated that the MAEXTRO business 'got off to a successful start' and that the S800 set a new benchmark for 'tech-luxury' [7][4]. JAC general manager Li Ming has described MAEXTRO as playing a symbolic and leading role in JAC's transformation [15].

Technology

The brand is built on Huawei's full-stack technology: the Tuling Longxing chassis platform, HUAWEI Qiankun ADS intelligent driving (the S800 debuted ADS 4; the Grand Design and the V800/V680 use ADS 5), the Huawei Whale Battery Platform, the Xuanwu body structure and the Harmony ALPS cockpit [1][11][14]. In perception, the 896-line dual-beam image-grade lidar first carried by the 2026 S800 and the Grand Design can stably detect an object as small as 14 cm at 120 metres, versus the previous 192-line unit [11][24]. JAC says the S800 covered 15 million kilometres of comprehensive testing, with key functional systems completing over 7 million cycles and more than 400,000 hours of stress testing [1]. JAC describes its R&D system as an open 'one base, two wings' technology ecosystem, and it assembled a dedicated R&D team of over 5,000 people for MAEXTRO and set up the MAEXTRO Shanghai R&D centre [12][17]. At group level, JAC's 2025 R&D investment was RMB4.179 billion, up 20.64% and equal to 8.99% of revenue [7].

Manufacturing

MAEXTRO vehicles are produced at the MAEXTRO Super Factory in Feixi County, Hefei, completed in December 2024 with a planned annual capacity of 200,000 vehicles [17][13]. JAC says the factory is a digital-twin plant with more than 1,800 robots automating stamping, welding, painting and final assembly, collecting 300,000 data points per second and monitoring 26,000 key quality points online [1]; media report that at full production it is expected to generate annual output value exceeding RMB100 billion [13]. The brand also relies on a 'Quality Alliance' of more than 200 supply-chain partners (the 2025 annual report's group-level figure; JAC's official communications cite over 220) [7][6].

Recent performance (parent-company figures)

Because MAEXTRO does not report standalone financials, its contribution is visible only through JAC's consolidated results. JAC reported H1 2026 operating revenue of RMB22.130 billion (+14.31%), with the growth attributed mainly to the commercial-vehicle base and S800 deliveries; the first-half attributable net loss was RMB749 million, and the gross margin improved to 11.14% from 8.98% a year earlier [23]. Media report that in insurance-registration terms, the S800's monthly volume climbed to a December 2025 peak of 4,223 units, then fell to 2,798 in January 2026, 593 in June and 367 in July [23]. In H1 2026 JAC sold 172,400 vehicles in total, down 9.56% year on year, comprising 123,000 commercial vehicles (-1%) and 49,000 passenger vehicles (-25.6%) [23]. The V800/V680 MPVs launched on 5 August 2026 took more than 3,500 firm orders in their first 24 hours, per China Economic News [25][14].

Founders and leadership

Founders and group sponsors

MAEXTRO has no private founder; it was jointly created by JAC and Huawei. JAC was founded in 1964, initially focusing on truck and bus chassis, later partnering with Hyundai to enter passenger vehicles and with NIO to build high-end manufacturing experience before joining Huawei in 2023 to create MAEXTRO [17]. On the JAC side, chairman Xiang Xingchu has been the public face of the project; on the Huawei side, executive director Richard Yu (Yu Chengdong) has co-presented the brand's launches [1].

Key executives

  • Xiang Xingchu (项兴初) — party secretary and chairman of JAC, and party secretary, chairman and general manager of Jianghuai Holdings. Born in 1970 and a senior engineer, he joined JAC in 1994 and rose from final-assembly workshop director to general manager of the listed company, becoming head of Jianghuai Holdings and chairman of the listed company in April 2021; he was re-elected chairman of JAC's ninth board on 18 November 2025 [17][22].
  • Li Ming (李明) — general manager of JAC (also deputy party secretary and director). Born in 1977 with a doctoral degree and the title of professoriate senior engineer, he joined JAC in 1999 and previously served as assistant to the general manager and deputy general manager, including as managing director of the JAC-Volkswagen JV; he was first appointed general manager in December 2021 and re-appointed on 18 November 2025 [22][15].
  • Richard Yu (余承东) — Huawei executive director, chairman of the Product Investment Review Committee and chairman of the Device BG, who has presented MAEXTRO launches on Huawei's behalf [24][1].

Ownership and governance

The MAEXTRO trademarks belong to JAC (transferred by Huawei in 2024); Huawei participates under the HIMA model, and neither side has disclosed any Huawei equity stake in the MAEXTRO brand [17]. JAC's controlling shareholder is Jianghuai Holdings, holding 27.35% as of 30 June 2026 (28.23% at end-2025); Jianghuai Holdings is owned 50/50 by the Anhui SASAC and Volkswagen (China) Investment, and the Anhui SASAC is JAC's actual controller per the 2025 annual report [20][7]. JAC's JV Volkswagen (Anhui), in which VW holds 75% and JAC 25%, is separate from the MAEXTRO brand [21].

Competition

MAEXTRO competes in China's RMB 1 million-plus ultra-luxury segment, traditionally held by the Mercedes-Benz S-Class, BMW 7 Series, Audi A8 and Porsche Panamera. Media report that the S800's 13,092 insurance registrations from September 2025 to January 2026 exceeded the combined same-period sales of the Porsche Panamera, Mercedes-Maybach S-Class and Mercedes-Benz S-Class [17]. Xinhua reported in June 2026 that the base S800 had delivered 19,000 units in 13 months since launch and ranked among the top of the million-yuan luxury segment for 9 consecutive months [11]; by August 2026 the Anhui SASAC described the S800 as the segment's sales leader for 10 consecutive months [3]. Within HIMA, MAEXTRO coexists with AITO (Seres), Luxeed (Chery), Stelato (BAIC) and Shangjie (SAIC), occupying the top, million-yuan band [9].

Our judgment. Based on reported figures, MAEXTRO's main strength is the combination of Huawei's intelligent-driving and cockpit stack with JAC's manufacturing, plus trademark ownership that sits with the automaker rather than Huawei and a 'Quality Alliance' of more than 200 suppliers [7]. Its main weaknesses are a short track record, a line-up still centred on the S800, and heavy dependence on Huawei's channel and technology; the V800/V680 MPVs only launched in August 2026 and are in an early ramp-up phase [3]. Monthly S800 insurance registrations declined after the December 2025 peak, and the brand also faces new Chinese ultra-luxury entrants such as the Yangwang U8 and NIO ET9 [23][17].

Outlook

Company plans. JAC has said it will keep expanding the MAEXTRO line-up to cover MPV, SUV and sports-luxury segments. Chairman Xiang Xingchu said at a delivery ceremony on 16 September 2026 that MAEXTRO's long-term investment in the ultra-luxury track would not stop and that the product matrix would be broadened; JAC's 2025 annual report states that MAEXTRO MPV and SUV models are 'under orderly development' [17][7]. On 26 April 2026, Jianghuai Group and Huawei Device signed a joint innovation cooperation agreement to deepen the partnership [10]. The RMB3.5 billion refinancing that JAC planned to fund MAEXTRO's development had passed regulatory review and, per the 2025 annual report and 2026 interim report, was completed with share registration on 12 February 2026, adding 70,168,404 new shares [7][20]. JAC's 2025 annual report sets a 2026 target of 424,000 vehicle sales and RMB65 billion total operating revenue, with MAEXTRO as a key pillar [7].

Our judgment. Whether MAEXTRO sustains its momentum depends on three premises: stabilising S800 volume and scaling the V800/V680 MPVs; extending beyond the sedan into the SUV segment; and managing the trust impact of the October 2026 V800 brake-pedal incident, which media report cooled new orders and is under joint investigation by three government departments [17]. On the other hand, HIMA's channel reach and Huawei's standing in the high-end market are important support for reaching high-net-worth customers [9]. MAEXTRO does not disclose standalone financials, and JAC posted an attributable net loss of RMB1.703 billion in 2025, so MAEXTRO's own profitability cannot yet be independently assessed [7].

Risks

  • Quality and safety incident. In October 2026 a video showed brake-pedal bracket fractures on three V800s during an emergency-braking test; MAEXTRO said it would optimise the part design and offer free upgrades, and three government departments opened a joint investigation on 15 October. Media report the incident cooled new orders [17].
  • Volume concentration and demand. The brand's volume is concentrated in the S800; media report monthly S800 insurance registrations declined from a December 2025 peak of 4,223 units to 593 in June and 367 in July 2026 [23].
  • Dependence on Huawei. Sales and technology rely on Huawei's HIMA channel and intelligent-driving/cockpit stack, while the trademark sits with JAC, so brand controversies fall on JAC directly [17].
  • No standalone financial disclosure. As a brand within JAC, MAEXTRO does not report separate financials; JAC posted an attributable net loss of RMB1.703 billion in 2025 and RMB749 million in H1 2026 [7][20].
  • Short track record. The brand has existed only since 2023-2024, and its long-term brand equity and residual value remain unproven [2].
  • Loss-making JV weighs on the group. The JAC 2025 annual report records an equity-method loss of RMB1.076 billion on Volkswagen (Anhui), in which JAC holds 25%, with a year-end carrying value of RMB238 million; media report the entity's losses exceeded RMB4.0 billion in 2025 and RMB2.3 billion in H1 2026, which may weigh on the group's overall financial strength [7][23].

概览

尊界（MAEXTRO）是安徽江淮汽车集团股份有限公司（江淮汽车，上交所 600418.SH）与华为以鸿蒙智行（HIMA）模式联合打造的超豪华新能源品牌。按合作协议，江淮负责产品开发和生产基地建设，华为负责全球独家销售、营销与用户运营并参与提升制造能力；「尊界」「MAEXTRO」商标于 2024 年由华为转让给江淮、归属江淮汽车[17][2]。

品牌以 2025 年 5 月 30 日上市的超豪华旗舰轿车尊界 S800（70.8 万–101.8 万元，纯电与增程两种动力）为起点[1][19]，成为江淮首个进入百万级价格区间并实现批量交付的品牌[17]。

随后拓展为「轿车＋MPV」矩阵：尊界 S800 Grand Design 典藏大观（138.8 万元起，2026 年 6 月）与尊界 V800/V680 MPV（64.8 万元起，2026 年 8 月）[11][3]。

S800 截至 2025 年 12 月 31 日累计交付超 1 万台[7]；截至 2026 年 9 月尊界累计交付超 1.97 万辆；据安徽省国资委，自 2025 年 9 月规模交付以来，S800 连续 10 个月位居百万豪车销量冠军[17][3]。

品牌名源自英文「Maestro」（指挥大师），江淮英文官网称尊界为 2023 年双方共同创立、中国首个「科技豪华」汽车品牌[2]。尊界位居鸿蒙智行「五界」品牌矩阵（问界、智界、享界、尊界、尚界）的最高端，由华为分别与赛力斯、奇瑞、北汽、江淮、上汽合作运营[9][24]。

2026 年 10 月，尊界 V800 因紧急制动测试中踏板支架断裂的事件陷入舆论风波，三部门已展开联合调查；品牌正处在重建信任与放量 MPV 的关键节点[17]。

现状

定位与业务角色

尊界是江淮高端化转型的旗舰，被江淮称为「头号工程」，并在「十五五」重点任务中位列第一；江淮 2025 年年报称「尊界业务首战告捷」，S800 树立了「科技豪华」新典范[7][4]。江淮汽车总经理李明表示尊界在江淮转型中扮演标志性和引领性角色[15]。

技术

品牌围绕华为全栈技术构建：途灵龙行平台、华为乾崑智驾 ADS（S800 首发 ADS 4；典藏大观与 V800/V680 采用 ADS 5）、华为巨鲸电池平台、玄武车身架构与鸿蒙 ALPS 健康座舱[1][11][14]。在智驾感知上，2026 款 S800 与典藏大观首发的 896 线双光路图像级激光雷达可稳定识别 120 米外 14 厘米高的低矮障碍物，较上一代 192 线方案明显提升[11][24]。据江淮介绍，S800 整车综合测试里程达 1500 万公里，关键功能系统完成超 700 万次、40 万小时以上压力测试[1]。江淮将其研发体系概括为「一基两翼」开放技术生态，为尊界组建了超 5000 人的专项研发团队，并成立尊界上海研发中心[12][17]。集团层面，江淮 2025 年研发投入 41.79 亿元、同比增长 20.64%、占营业收入 8.99%[7]。

制造

尊界车型在合肥市肥西县尊界超级工厂生产，该工厂 2024 年 12 月建成、规划年产能 20 万辆[17][13]。据江淮介绍，工厂为数字孪生工厂，配备超 1800 台机器人实现冲压、焊装、涂装、总装自动化，每秒采集 30 万条数据、对 2.6 万处关键质量点实施在线监控[1]；媒体报道，达产后预计年产值超千亿元[13]。品牌还依托超 200 家供应链伙伴的「品质同盟」（2025 年年报集团口径；江淮官方口径为超 220 家）[7][6]。

近期业绩（母公司口径）

由于尊界不单独披露财务，其贡献只能通过江淮合并报表观察。江淮 2026 年上半年营业收入 221.30 亿元、同比增长 14.31%，增长主要来自商用车基本盘与尊界 S800 交付；上半年归母净亏损 7.49 亿元，毛利率从上年同期的 8.98% 提升至 11.14%[23]。据媒体统计的上险量，S800 月销量在 2025 年 12 月达到 4223 辆的峰值后回落，2026 年 1 月为 2798 辆、6 月 593 辆、7 月 367 辆[23]。上半年江淮汽车整车销量 17.24 万辆、同比下滑 9.56%，其中商用车 12.3 万辆（同比 -1%）、乘用车 4.9 万辆（同比 -25.6%）[23]。8 月 5 日上市的 V800/V680 上市 24 小时大定订单突破 3500 台（据中国经济新闻网）[25][14]。

创始人与管理层

创始与集团层面

尊界并无民营创始人，由江淮与华为联合打造。江淮始建于 1964 年，早期主营卡车与客车底盘，后与现代合作进入乘用车、与蔚来合作积累高端制造经验，2023 年携手华为打造尊界[17]。江淮方面，董事长项兴初是项目的公开代表；华为方面，常务董事余承东多次共同发布品牌车型[1]。

核心管理层

  • 项兴初——江淮汽车党委书记、董事长，同时任江汽控股党委书记、董事长、总经理。1970 年生，高级工程师，1994 年进入江淮，从总装车间主任起步，后升任上市公司总经理，2021 年 4 月升任江汽控股一把手并兼任上市公司董事长，2025 年 11 月 18 日当选江淮第九届董事会董事长、连任[17][22]。
  • 李明——江淮汽车总经理（兼任党委副书记、董事）。1977 年生，博士研究生学历、正高级工程师，1999 年加入公司，历任总经理助理、副总经理，期间兼任江淮大众董事总经理等职，2021 年 12 月首次获聘总经理、2025 年 11 月 18 日续聘[22][15]。

股权与治理

尊界商标归属江淮汽车（2024 年由华为转让）；华为以鸿蒙智行模式参与，双方未披露华为在尊界品牌层面的持股[17]。江淮汽车控股股东为江汽控股，截至 2026 年 6 月 30 日持股 27.35%（2025 年末为 28.23%）；江汽控股由安徽省国资委与大众汽车（中国）投资有限公司各持 50%，据 2025 年年报，江淮汽车实际控制人为安徽省国资委[20][7]。江淮与大众的合资公司大众汽车（安徽）有限公司由大众中国投资持股 75%、江淮持股 25%，与尊界品牌相互独立[21]。

尊界为上市公司江淮汽车（600418.SH）旗下品牌，并非单独上市或单独披露报表的主体，不发布独立财务报表，因此品牌层面无单独的营业收入、毛利率、净利润或研发投入数据可列示，本表留空；母公司财务见江淮汽车深度研究。下文净利润为「归属于上市公司股东的净利润」（归母口径）；江淮汽车无优先股，该口径与「归属于公司普通股股东的净利润」一致。供参考：在中国企业会计准则（CAS）下，江淮汽车 2025 年营业收入 464.76 亿元（同比 +10.35%）、归属于上市公司股东的净利润 -17.03 亿元、研发投入 41.79 亿元（同比 +20.64%、占营业收入 8.99%）；2026 年上半年营业收入 221.30 亿元（同比 +14.31%）、归属于上市公司股东的净利润 -7.49 亿元（据 2025 年年度报告及 2026 年半年度报告）。本页不构成任何交易推荐。

竞争格局

尊界在中国百万元以上超豪华市场参与竞争，该区间长期由奔驰 S 级、宝马 7 系、奥迪 A8 与保时捷 Panamera 主导。媒体报道，S800 在 2025 年 9 月至 2026 年 1 月的累计上险 13092 辆，超过保时捷 Panamera、迈巴赫 S 级、奔驰 S 级同期的销量总和[17]。新华网 2026 年 6 月报道，基础版 S800 上市 13 个月累计交付 19000 辆、连续 9 个月位居百万级豪华车型销量前列[11]；至 2026 年 8 月，安徽省国资委称 S800 连续 10 个月稳居百万豪车销量冠军[3]。在鸿蒙智行体系内部，尊界与问界（赛力斯）、智界（奇瑞）、享界（北汽）、尚界（上汽）并存，占据最高端、百万元级区间[9]。

我们的判断。依据已披露数据，尊界的主要优势在于把华为智驾与座舱全栈技术与江淮制造结合，商标归属车企而非华为，并依托超 200 家供应链伙伴的「品质同盟」形成高端配套能力[7]；主要短板是品牌历史短、产品线集中于 S800、对华为渠道与技术依赖度高，V800/V680 MPV 于 2026 年 8 月刚上市、尚处放量初期[3]。S800 上险量在 2025 年 12 月峰值后回落，品牌同时面临仰望 U8、蔚来 ET9 等中国超豪华新进入者的竞争[23][17]。

未来展望

公司公开规划。江淮表示将持续完善尊界产品矩阵，覆盖 MPV、SUV、运动豪华等细分场景。董事长项兴初在 2026 年 9 月 16 日交付仪式上表态，尊界在超豪华赛道的长期投入不会止步、后续将扩充产品矩阵；江淮 2025 年年报称尊界 MPV 与 SUV 车型「有序开发中」[17][7]。2026 年 4 月 26 日，江汽集团与华为终端签署联合创新合作协议，深化合作[10]。江淮为尊界发展募资的 35 亿元再融资项目已通过监管审核，据 2025 年年报与 2026 年半年报，该次定增已于 2026 年 2 月 12 日完成股份登记、新增 70,168,404 股[7][20]。江淮 2025 年年报提出 2026 年销售汽车 42.4 万辆、营业总收入 650 亿元的目标，尊界为关键支柱[7]。

我们的判断。尊界能否延续势头取决于三个前提：其一，稳住 S800 销量并放量 V800/V680 MPV；其二，从轿车向 SUV 品类拓展；其三，妥善处理 2026 年 10 月 V800 刹车踏板事件的信任影响——媒体报道该事件已使新订单遇冷，且三部门正展开联合调查[17]。另一方面，鸿蒙智行整体渠道与华为在高端市场的号召力，是尊界拓展高净值客群的重要支撑[9]。尊界不单独披露财务，江淮 2025 年归母净亏损 17.03 亿元，尊界自身盈利能力尚无法独立评估[7]。

风险

  • 质量与安全事件。2026 年 10 月有视频显示三辆 V800 在紧急制动测试中出现踏板支架断裂；尊界回应将优化部件设计并提供免费升级，10 月 15 日三部门展开联合调查。媒体报道该事件已使新订单遇冷[17]。
  • 销量集中与需求波动。品牌销量集中于 S800；媒体统计的上险量显示，S800 月销量从 2025 年 12 月 4223 辆的峰值回落至 2026 年 6 月的 593 辆、7 月的 367 辆[23]。
  • 对华为的依赖。销售与技术依赖华为鸿蒙智行渠道与智驾/座舱全栈供应，而商标归属江淮，品牌负面舆论需由江淮直接承担[17]。
  • 无单独财务披露。作为江淮旗下品牌，尊界不单独披露财务；江淮 2025 年归母净亏损 17.03 亿元、2026 年上半年归母净亏损 7.49 亿元[7][20]。
  • 品牌历史短。品牌自 2023—2024 年才成立，长期品牌资产与保值率尚未得到验证[2]。
  • 合资公司亏损拖累集团。江淮 2025 年年报记录对大众汽车（安徽）的权益法投资损益为 -10.76 亿元、期末账面价值 2.38 亿元（江淮持股 25%）；媒体报道大众安徽 2025 年亏损超 40 亿元、2026 年上半年亏损超 23 亿元，可能影响集团整体资金实力[7][23]。
