Overview

Karry is the light-commercial-vehicle (LCV) brand of Chery Commercial Vehicle (Anhui) Co., Ltd., the commercial-vehicle arm of Chery Holding Group. The brand was founded in 2007, and its operating entity was established on 24 May 2001 (formerly named Wuhu FAW Yangzi Automobile Manufacturing Co., Ltd.); the LCV production base is in Kaifeng, Henan.[1][31] Karry began with fuel micro-vans, micro-trucks and MPVs, set up the Karry New Energy arm in 2017, and today its line-up centres on pure-electric city-logistics vehicles: the Dolphin and Porpoise electric vans, the Little Elephant/Little Truck micro and small trucks, and the Lingmi electric light truck.[1][6]

Karry is the mainstay of Chery's passenger-commercial synergy in light commercial vehicles. Chery Commercial Vehicle's business dates from 2001 and its products are sold in more than 80 countries and regions; the company says more than 500,000 people had chosen Karry by the end of 2023.[2][1] In 2026 its monthly sales broke 10,000 units for three consecutive months from April: 10,384 units in April (Karry brand up 12.1% year on year) and 10,022 units in June (up 61%, exports up 146%).[3][9] On third-party registration data, Karry's 2025 new-energy mini-truck sales were 18,223 units, second in the segment with a 14.7% share behind Farizon; by September 2026 Chery Commercial Vehicle ranked third in the new-energy micro/small-truck segment with 2,165 units and a 13.0% share.[12][18] The brand and its parent are unlisted and publish no separate financial statements.[31]

History

  1. 2001-05-24

    Chery Commercial Vehicle (Anhui) Co., Ltd. is established (formerly named Wuhu FAW Yangzi Automobile Manufacturing Co., Ltd.), with Yin Tongyue as legal representative; its business covers commercial vehicles, modified vehicles and new-energy products.[31][14]

  2. 2007

    The Karry brand is founded. Third-party sources record that its first product was the Youyi fuel mini-van and that Chery itself was among the first users.[1][30]

  3. 2009

    C&C Trucks (Jirui United Heavy Truck Co., Ltd.) is officially founded as a heavy-truck joint venture. Some third-party sources also record 2009 as the year Karry was formalised as a separate micro-vehicle company.[1][30]

  4. 2010-04

    The Chery Henan (Kaifeng) base breaks ground in late April 2010 with a planned capacity of 200,000 micro-vehicles and 80,000 light trucks a year plus engineering/agricultural machinery; the base mainly produces Karry micro-vehicles.[22]

  5. 2012

    Chery & Wanda Guizhou Bus Co., Ltd. is founded. Karry products are exported to Colombia (first entry into South America) and to Malaysia (first entry into Asia).[1]

  6. 2013

    On 25 May the first export batch marks the global expansion of C&C Trucks; in the same year Karry products are exported to Egypt, entering Africa for the first time and becoming Karry's first overseas CKD project.[1]

  7. 2017

    Karry New Energy is founded; the company describes itself as the first new-energy light-commercial-vehicle manufacturer in China.[1]

  8. 2021-10-27

    Karry launches a brand-renewal strategy centred on new value, new image and new strategy (the 'three-new' strategy) and the Chery Chitu platform and its first model, the Youyue.[32]

  9. 2023

    The company says a total of more than 500,000 people have chosen Karry as customers.[1]

  10. 2024

    The international company of Chery Commercial Vehicle is formed, combining Karry (LCV), C&C (heavy truck) and Wanda (bus). In June 2024, Gong Yueqiong joins Chery as general manager of Chery Commercial Vehicle.[1][15]

  11. 2025-12-24

    At the 2026 global partner conference, Chery Commercial Vehicle reports 2025 sales up 67%, revenue up 86% and exports up 155%, sets a 2026 target of 150,000 units (including 40,000 overseas) and a 2030 target of one million units, and announces 21 new models for 2026.[11][28]

  12. 2026-03-27

    Chery Commercial Vehicle launches the FSCV (Future Super Commercial Vehicle) plan and a quality-first strategy at the 2026 Hefei new-energy commercial vehicle exhibition.[3]

  13. 2026-04-30

    Per registry data, Chery Commercial Vehicle's registered capital is increased to RMB 2,500.9196 million (approval date 30 April 2026), with Agricultural Bank of China Financial Asset Investment (9.8348%) and CCB Financial Asset Investment (about 9.83%) joining as shareholders; Chery Holding Group's stake becomes 80.3305%.[31][14]

  14. 2026-04-21

    Chery Commercial Vehicle unveils DELIVAN, a digital intelligent electric light-commercial brand aimed at Europe, at the Commercial Vehicle Show in Birmingham; a British subsidiary is set up and a European headquarters opened in Liverpool.[10][7]

  15. 2026-05-27

    Chery Commercial Vehicle holds a light-commercial launch in Zhengzhou. Yiche reports three models: the Little Truck X3 Youth (from RMB 49,900), the Dolphin Xingyao 50 and the Lingmi Gold Rice long-range light truck; China Communications News covers only the first two.[17][25][33]

  16. 2026-06

    Chery Commercial Vehicle reports June 2026 sales of 10,022 units (+61% YoY, exports +146%), the third consecutive month above 10,000; in the month 500 Karry Porpoise E7 vans were delivered for the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei network and 40 Lingmi trucks were delivered in Wuhan on 30 June.[9]

Current operations

Business structure and brand matrix

Karry is the brand responsible for light commercial vehicles within Chery Commercial Vehicle (Anhui) Co., Ltd. Alongside Karry (LCV), Chery Commercial Vehicle operates C&C Trucks (heavy trucks), Chery Wanda (buses) and the RELY pickup brand; in 2024 it formed an international company combining Karry (LCV), C&C (heavy truck) and Wanda (bus).[1] In April 2026 it unveiled DELIVAN, a digital intelligent electric light-commercial brand aimed at Europe, as the first overseas rollout of its FSCV strategy.[10] Karry itself focuses on pure-electric vans (Dolphin, Porpoise), micro/small trucks (Little Elephant series, Little Truck X7) and the Lingmi light truck.[6]

Main models and prices

The Dolphin K5 is a rear-drive electric cargo van with a 5.3-7.1 cubic-metre cargo volume and a 3,185 mm cargo-box length; CLTC range is up to 446 km, with 2C fast charging and an 80 kW/230 N-m motor.[4] The Little Truck X7 is a short-nose electric micro truck with a 3.815 m cargo box, 251/260 km range and an 85 kW/256 N-m motor.[5] On pricing, the Little Truck X3 Youth launched in May 2026 at RMB 49,900, cutting the entry price of an electric light truck.[17] Guide prices listed by third-party auto portals (China market, 2026 model year, battery included) are about RMB 90,900-176,800 for the Dolphin, RMB 56,900-162,800 for the Porpoise, RMB 59,900-186,800 for the Little Elephant/Little Truck series and RMB 119,800-180,800 for the Lingmi light truck; the company's own site does not directly publish official retail prices.[21]

Technology platform

Karry draws on the Chery group's technology system under a passenger-commercial synergy strategy. In March 2026 Chery Commercial Vehicle launched the FSCV (Future Super Commercial Vehicle) plan, centred on zero-carbon technology and AI, with a 3.0 product roadmap.[3] At the 2026 partner conference the company said it would invest over RMB 30 billion cumulatively in commercial-vehicle R&D by 2030 and build a team of nearly 4,000 R&D staff.[2]

Production bases and capacity

Chery Commercial Vehicle's smart-manufacturing layout comprises the Kaifeng (Henan) LCV smart-manufacturing base, the Guiyang (Guizhou) bus base and the Wuhu (Anhui) super truck plant, with a super LCV plant and an unmanned-vehicle base planned.[2] The Chery Kaifeng base, where Chery has operated since 2010 and which mainly produces Karry micro-vehicles, recorded 274,000 vehicles and RMB 23.3 billion of output value in 2025, including about 109,000 exported vehicles, roughly a quarter of Henan province's total vehicle exports; those figures are for the entire Chery Kaifeng base, not Karry brand sales alone.[17][22]

Domestic market position

Per third-party registration data, Karry's 2025 new-energy mini-truck sales were 18,223 units, second in the industry behind Farizon.[12] In September 2026 Chery Commercial Vehicle ranked third in the new-energy micro/small-truck segment with 2,165 units and a 13.0% share, and fifth in the new-energy light-truck segment with 1,742 units.[18][19]

Overseas business

Chery Commercial Vehicle exports to more than 80 countries with over 450 overseas sales and service outlets and plans more than 11 overseas manufacturing bases and plants (Mexico, Dominican Republic, Venezuela, Brazil, Algeria, Egypt, Ethiopia, Angola, the Gulf, Myanmar, Vietnam and others).[2] Karry entered South America via Colombia and Asia via Malaysia in 2012, and Africa via Egypt in 2013 (its first overseas CKD project).[1] The Lingmi electric light truck entered the Americas market in March 2025.[6] In April 2026 Chery Commercial Vehicle reported 2,862 exported units, up 323.3% year on year.[3]

Founders and leadership

Brand ownership and equity

Karry is not a separate legal entity; it is the light-commercial brand operated within Chery Commercial Vehicle (Anhui) Co., Ltd. Per registry data, the company was established on 24 May 2001 (formerly Wuhu FAW Yangzi Automobile Manufacturing Co., Ltd.), has registered capital of RMB 2,500.9196 million (RMB 2.009 billion paid in) and is now held 80.3305% by Chery Holding Group, 9.8348% by Agricultural Bank of China Financial Asset Investment and about 9.83% by CCB Financial Asset Investment, following a capital increase approved on 30 April 2026.[31][14] The official English history page still describes the company as a wholly-owned subsidiary with nearly RMB 2.009 billion of registered capital, a description that predates the April 2026 capital increase.[1] Per Securities Times, Chery Holding's largest shareholder is Wuhu Investment Holding Group (29.47%), whose controller is Wuhu Municipal SASAC; no annual report or announcement separately determines an actual controller for the Karry brand, so only the shareholding chain is stated here.[13]

Group founder

Chery Holding Group chairman Yin Tongyue is the group's founder and also serves as legal representative of Chery Commercial Vehicle (Anhui) Co., Ltd.[31] At the 2026 partner conference he said Chery would devote the whole group's strength to the commercial-vehicle track, with commercial vehicles standing on the shoulders of the passenger-vehicle business.[11] His full career is covered in the Chery (chery) deep research.

Current management

Gong Yueqiong, vice president of Chery Holding Group and general manager of Chery Commercial Vehicle, has led the commercial-vehicle business since joining Chery in June 2024. Born in July 1976, he holds a bachelor's degree in economics and an accountant qualification and is an alumnus of the Tsinghua-CUHK finance MBA (class of 2015). He joined the BAIC group in 2004 and held a series of posts at Beiqi Foton, including deputy manager and manager of the financial planning department, assistant general manager, deputy general manager and general manager; in December 2017 he became party secretary, director and general manager of Beiqi Foton, in August 2019 he also became chairman of Foton Daimler, and in January 2021 he became chairman of Beiqi Foton. In January 2022 he was promoted to deputy general manager of BAIC Group, and in June that year he also became chairman of Beijing Hyundai; he left BAIC for Chery in June 2024.[15][16] Zhu Hong, formerly of Sany Heavy Truck, joined Chery Commercial Vehicle in early 2025 as legal representative and general manager of the C&C Trucks joint venture, per media reports.[24] The DELIVAN team includes Chery Commercial Vehicle (UK) COO Ross Davies, per media reports.[7][27] Karry does not separately disclose a brand general manager; its business is coordinated by Chery Commercial Vehicle's LCV division. The above covers public posts and business careers only.

Competition

Our judgment: the competitive field. Karry competes in China's new-energy micro-truck, small-truck, electric-van and light-truck segments against Geely's Farizon, Beiqi Foton, Lingxi (Wuling New Energy), Changan, Wuling, Dongfeng and BYD Commercial Vehicle. Per 360che, the top three in 2025 new-energy mini-trucks were Farizon (29,999 units), Karry (18,223) and Foton (16,880).[12] By September 2026, per EV Partner registration data, Farizon led the micro/small-truck segment with a 34.38% share and Lingxi had risen to second place (15.88%), pushing Chery Commercial Vehicle to third (13.00%).[18]

Our judgment: strengths. Karry benefits from Chery's passenger-commercial synergy, sharing the group's technology platforms, R&D system and supply chain, and offers a full range from vans to micro, small and light trucks.[2] Its overseas network (more than 80 countries and over 450 outlets) is a distinctive asset, and its 2025 results - mini-trucks second in the industry and light trucks rising to fifth by September 2026 - indicate progress in the new-energy transition.[2][19]

Our judgment: weaknesses. Karry's scale remains well below Farizon's - its 2025 new-energy mini-truck sales were roughly 60% of Farizon's - and in September 2026 its micro-truck sales fell 16.92% year on year while Farizon's grew, with Lingxi overtaking Karry into second place in micro/small trucks.[12][18] Because the commercial-vehicle business is unlisted and discloses no separate financials, Karry's standalone profitability cannot be assessed from public filings.[31]

Outlook

Company plans. Chery Commercial Vehicle targets 150,000 units in 2026 (including 40,000 overseas) and one million units by 2030, aiming to build a business comparable to the passenger-vehicle arm and to become a global leader in new-energy commercial vehicles.[11][28] It plans to launch 21 new models in 2026 (four heavy trucks, four light trucks, eight micro trucks and five small/medium vans).[11] The FSCV plan, launched in March 2026, centres on zero-carbon technology and AI.[3] In Europe it is localising through the DELIVAN brand, with a British subsidiary, a Liverpool headquarters and a planned European engineering centre.[10][7]

Our analysis. Our judgment: Karry's and Chery Commercial Vehicle's growth rests on rising new-energy light-truck and micro-truck penetration plus Chery's passenger-commercial synergy; the 2025 figures (sales up 67%, exports up 155%) show overseas becoming a growth engine.[11] Whether the targets are met depends on sustained city-logistics demand, the competitive evolution of the micro/small-truck segment (where Lingxi and Farizon are gaining share), and overseas localisation and trade conditions.[18] The 2026 figure of 150,000 units and the 2030 figure of one million units are company targets, not realised results. These are conditional judgments.

Risks

  • Intense competition. The new-energy micro/small-truck market is concentrated: Farizon leads with about a 34% share and Lingxi has risen to second, while Foton and Changan are transforming fast.[18][12]
  • No separate financial disclosure. Karry and Chery Commercial Vehicle are unlisted, so brand-level profitability and leverage cannot be assessed from public filings.[31][14]
  • Group dependence. Karry relies on Chery's technology, supply chain and channels through the passenger-commercial synergy strategy; a shift in group resource allocation could affect its competitiveness.[11]
  • Overseas and trade risks. The overseas push spans more than 80 countries and plans for over 11 plants, exposing the business to tariffs, localisation and regulatory risks.[2]
  • Policy dependence. New-energy commercial-vehicle demand partly relies on road-access rights and purchase policies in major cities; any policy retreat could affect demand.[26]
  • Product-iteration pace. New-energy commercial-vehicle technology evolves quickly; Karry's micro-truck sales fell year on year in September 2026 while leaders grew, showing iteration pressure from Farizon and Lingxi.[18]

概览

开瑞（Karry）是奇瑞控股集团旗下商用车板块——奇瑞商用车（安徽）有限公司——的轻型商用车（LCV）品牌，2007 年创立，所属主体成立于 2001 年 5 月 24 日（曾用名芜湖一汽扬子汽车制造有限公司），轻型商用车生产基地设在河南开封。[1][31] 开瑞早期以燃油微客、微货与 MPV 起步，2017 年成立开瑞新能源，现聚焦纯电动城配物流车，主力为海豚、江豚两款纯电厢式车（VAN）、小象/小卡纯电微卡与零米纯电轻卡。[1][6]

开瑞是奇瑞乘商协同战略在轻型商用车领域的主力。奇瑞商用车业务始于 2001 年，产品远销全球 80 多个国家和地区；公司称截至 2023 年累计超 50 万人选择开瑞。[2][1] 2026 年自 4 月起单月销量连续三个月破万：4 月 10,384 辆（开瑞品牌同比 +12.1%）、6 月 10,022 辆（同比 +61%、出口 +146%）。[3][9] 据第三方上险统计，2025 年开瑞新能源微卡销量 18,223 辆、占 14.7%、居行业第二（远程第一）；到 2026 年 9 月，奇瑞商用车在新能源微小卡细分市场以 2,165 辆、13.0% 的份额居行业第三。[12][18] 开瑞品牌及所属主体未上市，未单独披露财务数据。[31]

历史沿革

  1. 2001-05-24

    奇瑞商用车（安徽）有限公司成立（曾用名芜湖一汽扬子汽车制造有限公司），法定代表人为尹同跃，业务覆盖商用车、改装车及新能源产品。[31][14]

  2. 2007

    开瑞品牌创立。据第三方资料，首款燃油车型优翼上市，奇瑞自身成为首批用户。[1][30]

  3. 2009

    联合重卡（集瑞联合重工有限公司）作为重卡合资公司正式创立；部分第三方资料另将 2009 年记为开瑞微车公司正式建制之年。[1][30]

  4. 2010-04

    奇瑞河南（开封）基地于 2010 年 4 月下旬奠基动工，规划年产 20 万辆微车、8 万辆轻卡及工程/农用机械，基地主要生产开瑞微车。[22]

  5. 2012

    奇瑞万达贵州客车有限公司成立；开瑞产品出口哥伦比亚（首次进入南美）、马来西亚（首次进入亚洲）。[1]

  6. 2013

    5 月 25 日联合重卡首批出口启动全球化；同年开瑞产品出口埃及、首次进入非洲，成为开瑞首个海外 CKD（散件组装）项目。[1]

  7. 2017

    开瑞新能源成立；公司自述为中国首个新能源轻型商用车制造商。[1]

  8. 2021-10-27

    开瑞发布以「全新价值、全新形象、全新战略」为核心的品牌焕新（三新）战略，并发布奇瑞赤兔平台及首款车型优越。[32]

  9. 2023

    公司称累计超过 50 万人选择开瑞。[1]

  10. 2024

    奇瑞商用车国际公司组建，整合开瑞（轻商）、联合重卡（重卡）与万达（客车）。2024 年 6 月，巩月琼加入奇瑞，出任奇瑞商用车总经理。[1][15]

  11. 2025-12-24

    在 2026 全球合作伙伴大会上，奇瑞商用车报告 2025 年销量增长 67%、营收增长 86%、出口增长 155%，设定 2026 年 15 万辆（含海外 4 万辆）、2030 年 100 万辆目标，并公布 2026 年 21 款新车型计划。[11][28]

  12. 2026-03-27

    奇瑞商用车在 2026 中国（合肥）新能源商用车展览会上发布 FSCV（未来超级商用车）计划与「品质为先」战略。[3]

  13. 2026-04-30

    据工商信息，奇瑞商用车注册资本增至 250,091.96 万元（核准日期 2026 年 4 月 30 日），农银金融资产投资持股 9.8348%、建信金融资产投资持股约 9.83%，奇瑞控股集团持股变为 80.3305%。[31][14]

  14. 2026-04-21

    奇瑞商用车在伯明翰商用车展（CV Show）发布面向欧洲的数字化智能纯电轻客品牌 DELIVAN，成立英国子公司，并在利物浦设立欧洲总部。[10][7]

  15. 2026-05-27

    奇瑞商用车在郑州举办轻商新品发布会。易车报道同场三款：开瑞小卡 X3 青春版（4.99 万元起）、海豚星耀 50、零米轻卡金米版长续航；《中国交通报》只写前两款。[17][25][33]

  16. 2026-06

    奇瑞商用车 2026 年 6 月销量 10,022 辆（同比 +61%，出口 +146%），连续三个月破万；当月 500 辆开瑞江豚 E7 交付京津冀网络，6 月 30 日 40 台零米轻卡在武汉交付。[9]

现状

业务结构与品牌矩阵

开瑞是奇瑞商用车（安徽）有限公司旗下负责轻型商用车的品牌。除开瑞（轻商/LCV）外，奇瑞商用车还经营联合重卡 C&C（重卡）、奇瑞万达（客车）与 RELY 威麟（皮卡）品牌；2024 年组建国际公司，整合开瑞（轻商）、联合重卡（重卡）与万达（客车）三大板块。[1] 2026 年 4 月又面向欧洲推出数字化智能纯电轻客品牌 DELIVAN，作为 FSCV 战略的首个海外落地。[10] 开瑞品牌本身聚焦纯电厢式车（海豚、江豚）、微卡/小卡（小象系列、小卡 X7）与零米轻卡。[6]

主力车型与价格

海豚 K5 为后置后驱纯电厢式货车，货厢容积 5.3–7.1 立方米、货厢长 3185 毫米，CLTC 续航最高 446 公里，支持 2C 快充、80kW/230N·m 电机。[4] 小卡 X7 为短头电动大微卡，货箱长 3.815 米，续航 251/260 公里，85kW/256N·m。[5] 价格方面，2026 年 5 月发布的小卡 X3 青春版 4.99 万元起，将纯电小卡购车门槛压低。[17] 据第三方汽车网站列示的厂商指导价（中国内市场、2026 款、含动力电池）：海豚约 9.09–17.68 万元、江豚约 5.69–16.28 万元、小象/小卡系列约 5.99–18.68 万元、零米轻卡 11.98–18.08 万元；公司官网未直接公布官方零售价。[21]

技术平台

开瑞依托奇瑞集团乘商协同的技术体系。2026 年 3 月奇瑞商用车发布 FSCV（未来超级商用车）计划，以零碳技术与 AI 为核心，明确 3.0 产品路线图。[3] 在 2026 伙伴大会上，公司表示至 2030 年累计投入超 300 亿元用于商用车研发、组建近 4000 人研发团队。[2]

生产基地与产能

奇瑞商用车智能制造基地布局为：河南开封轻商智造基地、贵州贵阳客车智造基地、安徽芜湖超级卡车工厂，另规划超级轻商工厂与无人车智造基地。[2] 奇瑞自 2010 年扎根开封建设基地，该基地主要生产开瑞微车。2025 年奇瑞开封基地整车产量 27.4 万辆、产值 233 亿元，其中出口整车约 10.9 万辆，约占河南省汽车出口总量的四分之一；上述数字是奇瑞开封基地的整体产量，不是开瑞品牌单独销量。[17][22]

国内市场地位

据第三方上险统计，2025 年开瑞新能源微卡销量 18,223 辆、居行业第二（远程第一）。[12] 2026 年 9 月，奇瑞商用车在新能源微小卡细分市场以 2,165 辆、13.0% 的份额居行业第三，在新能源轻卡市场以 1,742 辆居行业第五。[18][19]

海外业务

奇瑞商用车产品远销全球 80 多个国家，拥有 450 余个海外销售服务网点，规划建成 11 个以上海外制造基地及工厂（墨西哥、多米尼加、委内瑞拉、巴西、阿尔及利亚、埃及、埃塞俄比亚、安哥拉、海湾、缅甸、越南等）。[2] 开瑞 2012 年经哥伦比亚进入南美、经马来西亚进入亚洲，2013 年经埃及进入非洲（首个海外 CKD 项目）。[1] 零米纯电轻卡于 2025 年 3 月进入美洲市场。[6] 2026 年 4 月奇瑞商用车出口 2,862 辆，同比增长 323.3%。[3]

创始人与管理层

品牌归属与股权

开瑞不是独立法人，而是奇瑞商用车（安徽）有限公司旗下经营的轻商品牌。据工商信息，该公司成立于 2001 年 5 月 24 日（曾用名芜湖一汽扬子汽车制造有限公司），注册资本 250,091.96 万元（实缴 20.09 亿元），2026 年 4 月 30 日核准增资后，由奇瑞控股集团持股 80.3305%、农银金融资产投资持股 9.8348%、建信金融资产投资持股约 9.83%。[31][14] 官网英文历程页仍写「全资子公司、注册资本近 20.09 亿元」，该表述早于 2026 年 4 月增资。[1] 据证券时报报道，奇瑞控股第一大股东为芜湖市投资控股集团有限公司（持股 29.47%），其控制方为芜湖市人民政府国资委；因无年报或公告单独认定开瑞品牌层面的实际控制人，此处仅列明股权链。[13]

集团创始人

奇瑞控股集团董事长尹同跃是集团创始人，同时担任奇瑞商用车（安徽）有限公司法定代表人。[31] 他在 2026 伙伴大会上表示「将举全集团之力坚定不移深耕商用车赛道」，要求商用车站在乘用车的「肩膀」上发展。[11] 其完整履历见奇瑞（chery）深度研究。

现任管理层

奇瑞控股集团副总裁、奇瑞商用车总经理巩月琼自 2024 年 6 月加入奇瑞后执掌商用车板块。他 1976 年 7 月生，拥有经济学学士学位与会计师职称，为清华-港中文金融财务 MBA 2015 级校友；2004 年加入北汽集团体系，历任北汽福田财务计划部副经理、经理、总经理助理、副总经理、总经理，2017 年 12 月任北汽福田党委书记、董事、总经理，2019 年 8 月兼任福田戴姆勒董事长，2021 年 1 月任北汽福田董事长；2022 年 1 月升任北汽集团副总经理、同年 6 月兼任北京现代董事长，2024 年 6 月离开北汽加盟奇瑞。[15][16] 据媒体报道，曾供职三一重卡的朱宏于 2025 年初加盟奇瑞商用车，担任联合重卡合资公司的法定代表人、总经理。[24] DELIVAN 团队包括奇瑞商用车（英国）首席运营官 Ross Davies 等（据媒体报道）。[7][27] 开瑞未单独披露品牌总经理，其业务由奇瑞商用车轻商事业部统筹。以上均为公开职务与商业履历。

竞争格局

我们的判断：竞争范围。开瑞在中国新能源微卡、小卡、纯电 VAN 与轻卡市场，主要与吉利旗下远程新能源商用车、北汽福田、菱势（五菱新能源）、长安、五菱、东风、比亚迪商用车等品牌竞争。据卡车之家统计，2025 年新能源微卡前三为远程（29,999 辆）、开瑞（18,223 辆）、福田（16,880 辆）。[12] 到 2026 年 9 月，据电车资源上险数据，远程以 34.38% 份额领先微小卡市场，菱势升至第二（15.88%），奇瑞商用车退至第三（13.00%）。[18]

我们的判断：优势。开瑞受益于奇瑞集团乘商协同，共享集团技术平台、研发体系与供应链，产品覆盖 VAN、微卡、小卡到轻卡全品类。[2] 其海外网络（80 多个国家、450 余网点）是独特优势；2025 年微卡居行业第二、2026 年 9 月新能源轻卡升至行业第五，显示新能源转型取得进展。[2][19]

我们的判断：短板。开瑞规模仍明显小于远程，2025 年新能源微卡销量约为远程的六成；2026 年 9 月其微卡销量同比下滑 16.92%，而远程保持增长，菱势在微小卡市场超越开瑞升至第二。[12][18] 因商用车业务未上市、未单独披露财务，开瑞独立盈利能力无法从公开文件评估。[31]

未来展望

公司公开规划。奇瑞商用车 2026 年销量目标 15 万辆（含海外 4 万辆），到 2030 年目标销量 100 万辆、再造一个比肩乘用车业务的千亿级产业，定位全球新能源商用车领军品牌。[11][28] 2026 年计划上市 21 款新车型（重卡 4 款、轻卡 4 款、微卡 8 款、中小 VAN 5 款）。[11] 2026 年 3 月发布的 FSCV 计划以零碳与 AI 为核心。[3] 欧洲市场通过 DELIVAN 品牌推进本地化，设英国子公司、利物浦欧洲总部，并规划欧洲工程中心。[10][7]

我们的分析。我们的判断：开瑞及奇瑞商用车的高增长，建立在新能源轻卡、微卡渗透率提升与奇瑞集团乘商协同之上；2025 年销量 +67%、出口 +155% 表明海外正成为增长极。[11] 但目标能否实现，取决于城配物流需求的持续、微卡/小卡竞争格局变化（远程与菱势正抢占份额），以及海外本地化与贸易环境。[18] 2026 年 15 万辆、2030 年 100 万辆均为公司目标而非已实现业绩。以上为有条件的判断。

风险

  • 市场竞争。新能源微卡/小卡头部效应明显，远程以约 34% 份额领先、菱势已升至第二，福田、长安等传统车企快速转型。[18][12]
  • 无单独财务披露。开瑞及奇瑞商用车未上市，品牌层面盈利与负债无法从公开文件评估。[31][14]
  • 集团依赖。开瑞通过乘商协同战略依赖奇瑞的技术、供应链与渠道，若集团资源分配调整，可能影响其竞争力。[11]
  • 海外与贸易风险。海外布局涉及 80 多个国家、规划 11 个以上工厂，面临关税、本地化与法规风险。[2]
  • 政策依赖。新能源商用车需求部分依赖主要城市的路权与购置政策，政策退坡可能影响需求。[26]
  • 产品迭代节奏。新能源商用车技术快速迭代，2026 年 9 月开瑞微卡销量同比下滑而头部品牌增长，显示来自远程、菱势的迭代压力。[18]
