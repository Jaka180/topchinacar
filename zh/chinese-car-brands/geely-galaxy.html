Overview

Geely Galaxy (吉利银河) is the mainstream new-energy-vehicle (NEV) brand of Geely Auto Group, positioned between Geely's conventional internal-combustion models and the higher-positioned Lynk & Co and Zeekr brands. It was unveiled in Hangzhou on 23 February 2023 as a mid-to-high-end NEV series under the Geely brand and upgraded to a standalone brand on 3 March 2025 [1][7].

At its February 2023 launch Geely framed Galaxy as part of the group's push toward mid-to-high-end new energy, announcing seven models over two years across a plug-in-hybrid L series and a pure-electric E series, alongside the Aegis battery system, the NordThor hybrid and Galaxy N OS [1]. It was upgraded from a series to a standalone brand on 3 March 2025, when the LEVC premium-MPV line was also merged in [7].

Over roughly three years the brand became Geely's volume engine in new energy: it sold over 83,000 units in about seven months from the first model launch in 2023, then about 494,000 units in 2024 (+80%) and about 1.24 million in 2025 (+150%), becoming the fastest brand to reach one million annual NEV sales, before easing to about 520,000 units in the first half of 2026 (-5% year on year) [8][13][14][19]. Within this, Geely Auto sold more than 1.68 million NEVs in 2025, of which Galaxy accounted for the large majority at about 1.24 million [13].

The brand spans A0 to C-segment sedans, SUVs and MPVs plus hard-core off-roaders, in both plug-in-hybrid and pure-electric forms. Its models are exported under the Geely name: the EX5 (Galaxy E5) had entered more than 40 markets by early 2026, and the STARRAY EM-i (Starship 7 EM-i) more than 10 countries, with the latter earning a Euro NCAP five-star rating in December 2025 [6][25][26].

The brand's stated value proposition is "build smart, high-value cars for everyone, for a better life", with Geely positioning it as a leader of premium new-energy vehicles on a "high quality, fair price" route; Geely Auto Group CEO Gan Jiayue introduced this proposition at the E5's global launch in August 2024 [5][6].

Current operations

Brand structure and position within Geely

Geely Galaxy is the mainstream-NEV arm of Geely Auto Group, whose brands also include Geely (conventional models and the China Star series), Lynk & Co (premium) and Zeekr (luxury). The brand was assembled through two consolidations: Geometry merged into Galaxy in October 2024 to form the Geome smart compact-car line, and the LEVC (Yizhen) premium-MPV line was merged in on 3 March 2025 [6][7]; the result is three product lines: smart compact cars (Geome), mainstream premium cars and premium MPVs [8]. Geely Auto Group itself is a subsidiary of Zhejiang Geely Holding Group, and Geely Automobile Holdings (0175.HK) is the listed holding company [25].

Under the Taizhou Declaration announced in September 2024, Geely said its new-energy business would focus on the Galaxy brand, building one premium new-energy brand serving mainstream users; Geely Auto Group CEO Gan Jiayue called Galaxy the mainstream NEV brand Geely wants to concentrate on [6]. Within the group, Galaxy, Lynk & Co and Zeekr form a mainstream-premium-luxury ladder that Geely says covers the mainstream, premium and luxury markets respectively [13].

In the wider group consolidation, Geometry was folded into Galaxy in October 2024; ZGH began restructuring the Zeekr-Lynk & Co equity relationship in November 2024; in February 2025 Zeekr completed its acquisition of and capital injection into Lynk & Co, making Lynk & Co a Zeekr subsidiary; in December 2025 Zeekr completed its privatisation to become a wholly-owned Geely Auto subsidiary; and LEVC and Radar were gradually brought under Geely Auto's management [21].

Model range and price bands

As of late October 2026 the Galaxy range runs from the Panda mini and the Xingyuan A0 hatchback (from RMB 69,800) through compact and mid-size sedans and SUVs (L6, L7, E5, E8, Xingyao 8, Starship 7 EM-i) up to the M9 full-size six-seat SUV (from RMB 173,800) and the V900 flagship MPV (from RMB 269,800); 2026 additions include the Starship 7 EV, the Warship 700 hard-core SUV and the TT electric sport sedan [6][11][12][15].

Sales milestones

The brand's first car rolled off the line on 23 April 2023, and from the L7's launch on 31 May 2023 to the end of 2023 the series sold over 83,000 units in about seven months, lifting the average transaction price of Galaxy models to around RMB 160,000 [10][19]. It reached annual sales of one million units in 29 months after the L7's launch, becoming the fastest NEV brand to hit that milestone; in October 2025 it passed the million mark year-to-date ahead of schedule, with 1,002,461 units in January-October and 127,476 units in October alone (+101%) [25][20]. In June 2026 Galaxy sold 108,206 units (+20% year on year), taking first-half sales past 510,000; the company said the brand ranked among the top three NEV brands globally by sales [15]. In July 2026 the Galaxy series sold 107,797 units (+13%), its first time above 100,000 in a single month [21].

Model-level sales

By model, the Xingyuan exceeded 40,000 monthly sales for seven consecutive months in 2025 with cumulative sales over 530,000 by year-end; the E5 sold more than 160,000 units in 2025 (cumulative over 230,000); the M9 reached nearly 40,000 units within four months of launch; and the Starship 7 EM-i and Panda mini each averaged over 10,000 monthly sales in 2025 [25]. In the first half of 2026 the Xingyuan sold 249,418 units, the Starship 7 EM-i kept monthly sales above 10,000, and the E5 sold 13,543 units in June (+65% year on year) [15].

Core technology and platforms

Galaxy models draw on the group's shared platforms and technology: the SEA and GEA architectures, the GTA native off-road NEV architecture used by the Warship 700, the NordThor EM hybrid family (EM-i, EM-P and the off-road EM-T), the Aegis (Shen Dun) battery safety system including the gold-brick battery, the Flyme Auto / Galaxy N OS cockpit, and the Qianli Haohan assisted-driving system with H1-H9 tiers (the H1 tier already exceeds 100 TOPS) [4][8][7][15]. Geely has also deployed satellite connectivity on models such as the Xingyao 8 and E8 [25].

Manufacturing and capacity

Galaxy vehicles are produced within Geely Auto Group's manufacturing footprint, which the group describes as 12 plants and five global R&D centres (Hangzhou, Ningbo, Gothenburg, Coventry and Frankfurt) [26]. For overseas localisation, the BAMC plant in Egypt began operations in 2025 rolling out the Coolray and Emgrand, and the Indonesian plant began mass production of the Geely EX5 and EX5 EM-i; Kazakhstan is advancing localised production, and a strategic partnership with Renault in Brazil plans localised production of the EX5 EM-i from the second half of 2026 [25][13].

Channel and overseas business

Galaxy reached about 650 stores in China by the end of 2023 [19]. At group level, Geely's global network covered 88 countries and regions with more than 1,200 outlets by the end of 2025 [25]. Geely Auto recorded 420,000 overseas sales in 2025, including more than 120,000 NEV exports; the EX5 topped Brazil's pure-electric C-segment SUV ranking for four consecutive months, and the STARRAY EM-i ranked second among PHEVs in Australia in November 2025 [25][26].

Founders and leadership

Geely Galaxy is a brand rather than a separately listed company, so it has no independent board or separately disclosed chief executive; its operations are run within Geely Auto Group's management structure. Key executives with direct responsibility for the brand, as publicly reported, include the following [21].

  • Gan Jiayue (淦家阅): CEO of Geely Auto Group, who announced the Galaxy launch and its upgrade to a standalone brand; from 18 August 2026 CEO of the listed Geely Automobile Holdings (0175.HK) [8][22].
  • Lin Jie (林杰): senior vice president of Geely Auto Group in charge of sales and marketing; from 2 August 2026 general manager of the group's new sales corporation and concurrently general manager of Zeekr sales [21].
  • Fan Junyi (范峻毅): general manager of Geely Auto's sales company as of May 2025; from August 2026 executive deputy general manager of the sales corporation and general manager of Lynk & Co sales, overseeing Galaxy alongside Lynk & Co [10][21].
  • Guan Jiuyang (关久旸): executive deputy general manager of the Galaxy sales company from August 2026 [21].

In an August 2026 governance change at the listed entity effective 18 August 2026, Li Shufu resigned as chairman and executive director of Geely Automobile Holdings (0175.HK) and was appointed honorary chairman for life, remaining chairman of Zhejiang Geely Holding Group; An Conghui became chairman, Gan Jiayue became CEO, and Gui Shengyue moved from CEO to vice-chairman [22]. The sales corporation set up on 2 August 2026 places the China Star, Galaxy, Lynk & Co and Zeekr sales organisations under one roof, keeping front-line brands independent while centralising channel, data and service standards [21]. Brand ownership is held through Geely Auto Group, a subsidiary of Zhejiang Geely Holding Group [25].

Competition

Geely Galaxy's model range spans roughly the RMB 70,000-330,000 band, from the A0 Xingyuan to the M9 and V900 flagships; its largest-volume models sit in the mainstream NEV segment where price competition is the most intense [6][11][12]. With the M9 full-size six-seat SUV and the V900 flagship MPV, Galaxy extended its price ceiling into the RMB 170,000-330,000 band, moving into the large six-seat SUV and premium MPV segments [11][12].

Our assessment: Galaxy's main rivals are BYD's Dynasty and Ocean series, and to a lesser extent Changan's Deepal, Leapmotor and GAC Aion, which together dominate the mainstream NEV segments Galaxy targets; this judgement rests on the brand's own mainstream pricing (roughly RMB 70,000-250,000 for its volume models) and its stated "high quality, fair price" positioning rather than on any single source listing competitors [6][5]. Its main strengths are scale and cost, backed by shared group platforms (SEA, GEA, GTA), powertrains (NordThor hybrids) and the Flyme Auto / Qianli Haohan software stack that are also used by Lynk & Co and Zeekr, plus a full segment-coverage lineup; this helped it reach roughly 1.24 million units in 2025 [13][15]. Its main weaknesses are lower brand equity than Lynk & Co or Zeekr, thin differentiation in a price-war-dominated market, and a heavy reliance on the Xingyuan and E5 for volume; the 5% decline in the first half of 2026 shows the strain when the domestic NEV market softens [14].

Outlook

Geely Auto has set a 2026 target of 1.55 million units for Geely Galaxy, up 25% year on year, inside a group sales target of 3.45 million units [16][25]. The brand plans new and refreshed models in 2026, including the Starship 7 EV (launched 2026-06-10), the Warship 700 hard-core SUV (launched 2026-10-10), the TT electric sport sedan, the Xingyao 7 MAX and the 2026-model A7 EM / A7 EV, while pushing the Qianli Haohan assisted-driving system down the range; Galaxy described 2026 as a big product year [15][23]. On exports, the STARRAY EM-i is set to enter Germany, France, the UK and Brazil in 2026 [26]. On intelligent driving, Geely said its Qianli Haohan G-ASD system obtained EU certification in March 2026, which the company described as the first Chinese high-level assisted-driving system to go overseas [13].

Our analysis: the 25% growth target is more demanding than the first-half performance, since Galaxy posted a 5% year-on-year decline in H1 2026 and would need to accelerate materially in the second half [14]. This assumes the new 2026 models land well and that overseas volumes ramp up; the outcome also depends on how the domestic NEV price war and the European market environment evolve. The brand's scale, cost base and shared technology offer a credible path, but the target is not a certainty.

Risks

  • Price competition: Galaxy operates in the most crowded mainstream NEV band, where rivals such as BYD have pushed advanced driver assistance down to entry-level prices, compressing margins [5].
  • Volume concentration: sales lean heavily on the Xingyuan and E5; the 5% first-half 2026 decline shows sensitivity to a softening domestic NEV market [14].
  • Internal brand overlap: Galaxy shares platforms and technology with Lynk & Co and Zeekr, which could create intra-group competition for resources and customers [21].
  • Group dependence: Galaxy's R&D, supply chain and channel are centrally managed by the Geely group, so the brand's own room for differentiated moves is limited [21].
  • Overseas and regulatory risk: Galaxy's exports (EX5, STARRAY EM-i) are sold under the Geely name and, as they expand into Europe and other markets, face local regulatory and competitive headwinds [27][25].
  • Technology-route exposure: the brand spans plug-in hybrid, extended-range and pure-electric; shifts in NEV subsidies or battery policy could alter the economics of any route [6].
  • Macro and competitive environment: Geely Auto itself cautioned in its interim results that the macro economy, geopolitics and market competition could still affect operating performance [14].

概览

吉利银河是吉利汽车集团旗下的主流新能源品牌，定位介于吉利传统燃油车型与更高端的领克、极氪之间。它于 2023 年 2 月 23 日在杭州作为吉利品牌的中高端新能源系列发布，2025 年 3 月 3 日升级为独立品牌 [1][7]。

2023 年 2 月发布时，吉利将银河定位为向中高端新能源发力的载体，宣布两年内推出 7 款车型，分为智能电混 L 系列与智能纯电 E 系列，并同步发布神盾电池、雷神电混、银河 N OS 等技术 [1]。2025 年 3 月 3 日，银河在集团「一个吉利」整合中由系列升级为独立品牌，翼真（LEVC）高端 MPV 系列同步并入 [7]。

大约三年间，银河成为吉利在新能源领域最主要的走量品牌：2023 年自首款车上市约 7 个月销量突破 8.3 万辆；2024 年约 49.4 万辆（同比增长 80%）；2025 年约 124 万辆（同比增长 150%），成为最快达成新能源汽车年销百万的品牌；2026 年上半年回落至约 52 万辆（同比下降 5%）[8][13][14][19]。其中，2025 年吉利汽车新能源总销量超 168 万辆，银河约 124 万辆、占其绝大部分 [13]。

品牌覆盖 A0 级到 C 级的轿车、SUV、MPV 及硬派越野，兼顾插混与纯电。其车型在海外以 Geely 名义销售，如 EX5（银河 E5）截至 2026 年初已进入 40 余个市场，STARRAY EM-i（星舰 7 EM-i）进入 10 余个国家，后者 2025 年 12 月获 Euro NCAP 五星安全认证 [6][25][26]。

品牌宣称的价值主张是「造每个人的智能精品车，为美好生活而来」，吉利将其定位为「新能源精品车引领者」，走「高质优价」路线；这一价值主张由淦家阅在 2024 年 8 月银河 E5 全球上市时正式发布 [5][6]。

现状

品牌架构与在集团中的位置

吉利银河是吉利汽车集团的主流新能源品牌，与吉利（传统车型、中国星系列）、领克（高端）、极氪（豪华）并列。品牌通过两次整合组建：2024 年 10 月几何并入银河形成 GEOME 智能精品小车系列，2025 年 3 月 3 日翼真（LEVC）高端 MPV 系列并入 [6][7]；调整后形成「智能精品小车（GEOME）」「主流精品车」「高端 MPV」三大产品线 [8]。吉利汽车集团为浙江吉利控股集团子公司，吉利汽车控股有限公司（0175.HK）为上市控股主体 [25]。

根据吉利 2024 年 9 月发布的《台州宣言》，吉利新能源将聚焦银河品牌，打造「一个」为主流用户服务的精品新能源产品品牌；淦家阅当时表示，银河是吉利要聚焦的主流新能源品牌 [6]。在集团内部，银河与领克、极氪构成「主流—高端—豪华」的阶梯，吉利称三者分别覆盖主流、高端与豪华市场 [13]。

在集团层面的整合中，2024 年 10 月几何并入银河；同年 11 月吉利控股启动对极氪与领克股权关系的优化调整；2025 年 2 月极氪完成对领克的收购及注资、领克转为极氪附属公司；2025 年 12 月极氪完成私有化、成为吉利汽车全资子公司；同期翼真、雷达等品牌逐步归入吉利汽车整体管理框架 [21]。

车型谱系与价格带

截至 2026 年 10 月底，银河产品线从熊猫 mini 和星愿 A0 级小车（6.98 万元起），经紧凑型和中型轿车、SUV（L6、L7、E5、E8、星耀 8、星舰 7 EM-i），上探至 M9 大六座旗舰 SUV（17.38 万元起）和 V900 旗舰 MPV（26.98 万元起）；2026 年新增星舰 7 EV、战舰 700 硬派 SUV 和 TT 纯电运动轿车 [6][11][12][15]。

销量里程碑

银河首款车于 2023 年 4 月 23 日下线，自 L7 于 2023 年 5 月 31 日上市至当年底，银河系列约 7 个月销量突破 8.3 万辆，并将单车平均售价拉高至约 16 万元 [10][19]。自 L7 发布起 29 个月，银河实现年销百万，成为最快达成该里程碑的新能源品牌；2025 年 10 月提前达成「年销百万」，1-10 月累计 1,002,461 辆、10 月单月 127,476 辆（同比 +101%）[25][20]。2026 年 6 月银河单月销量 108,206 辆（同比 +20%），上半年累计突破 51 万辆；公司称银河跻身新能源汽车品牌销量全球前三 [15]。2026 年 7 月银河系列单月销量 107,797 辆（同比 +13%），首次突破 10 万辆 [21]。

分车型销量

分车型看，2025 年吉利星愿连续 7 个月月销破 4 万、全年累计超 53 万辆；E5 全年销量超 16 万辆、累计超 23 万辆；M9 上市 4 个月累计近 4 万辆；星舰 7 EM-i 与熊猫 mini 月均销量均过万 [25]。2026 年上半年，星愿累计 249,418 辆，星舰 7 EM-i 月销持续过万，E5 6 月销量 13,543 辆（同比 +65%）[15]。

核心技术与平台

银河车型依托集团共享的平台与技术：SEA、GEA 架构，战舰 700 使用的 GTA 原生新能源越野架构，雷神 EM 电混家族（EM-i、EM-P 与越野 EM-T），神盾电池安全系统（含金砖电池），Flyme Auto / 银河 N OS 座舱，以及千里浩瀚智能驾驶系统（H1-H9 五个层级，H1 起步算力已超 100 TOPS）[4][8][7][15]。吉利还在星耀 8、E8 等车型上搭载卫星通信能力 [25]。

生产与产能

银河车型在吉利汽车集团的制造体系内生产，集团自称拥有 12 座工厂与 5 个全球研发中心（杭州、宁波、哥德堡、考文垂、法兰克福）[26]。海外本地化方面，埃及 BAMC 工厂 2025 年投产，下线 Coolray 与 Emgrand 两款车型；印尼工厂量产 Geely EX5 与 EX5 EM-i；哈萨克斯坦推进本地化生产；与雷诺在巴西的合作按计划于 2026 年下半年起本地化生产 EX5 EM-i [25][13]。

渠道与海外业务

银河截至 2023 年底在国内建成约 650 家门店 [19]。集团层面，吉利汽车全球网络截至 2025 年底覆盖 88 个国家和地区、网点超 1200 家 [25]。2025 年吉利汽车海外销量 42 万辆，其中新能源出口超 12 万辆；EX5 连续四个月位居巴西纯电 C 级 SUV 细分销量第一，STARRAY EM-i 2025 年 11 月位居澳大利亚 PHEV 细分销量第二 [25][26]。

吉利银河是品牌而非单独上市公司，没有独立的董事会或单独披露的行政总裁，其运营在吉利汽车集团的管理架构内进行。直接负责该品牌的关键高管及公开职务如下 [21]。

  • 淦家阅：吉利汽车集团 CEO，是银河发布及升级为独立品牌的宣布者；2026 年 8 月 18 日起任上市主体吉利汽车控股（0175.HK）行政总裁 [8][22]。
  • 林杰：吉利汽车集团分管营销的高级副总裁；2026 年 8 月 2 日起任集团销售总公司总经理，兼任极氪销售公司总经理 [21]。
  • 范峻毅：2025 年 5 月时任吉利汽车销售公司总经理；2026 年 8 月起任销售总公司常务副总经理兼领克销售公司总经理，分管银河 [10][21]。
  • 关久旸：2026 年 8 月起任银河销售公司常务副总经理 [21]。

2026 年 8 月（自 2026-08-18 生效）上市主体的治理层调整中，李书福辞任吉利汽车控股（0175.HK）董事长及执行董事、转任名誉主席，仍任浙江吉利控股集团董事长；安聪慧出任董事长，淦家阅出任行政总裁，桂生悦由行政总裁转任副主席 [22]。2026 年 8 月 2 日成立的销售总公司，将中国星、银河、领克、极氪等销售组织纳入同一体系，前台品牌保持独立、渠道与数据及服务标准统一收口 [21]。品牌所有权经吉利汽车集团持有，吉利汽车集团为浙江吉利控股集团子公司 [25]。本页仅记录公开职务与商业经历。

竞争格局

吉利银河的产品线大致横跨 7 万-33 万元区间，从 A0 级星愿到 M9、V900 旗舰车型，其走量车型集中在竞争最激烈的主流新能源区间 [6][11][12]。凭借 M9 大六座旗舰 SUV 与 V900 旗舰 MPV，银河将价格带上探至 17 万-33 万元区间，切入大六座 SUV 与高端 MPV 细分市场 [11][12]。

我们的判断：银河最主要的对手是比亚迪王朝网与海洋网，其次是长安深蓝、零跑、广汽埃安等，它们共同占据银河所切入的主流新能源细分市场；这一判断依据的是银河走量车型约 7 万-25 万元的主流定价与其「高质优价」定位，而非某一篇列出竞对名单的出处 [6][5]。主要优势是规模与成本，依托与领克、极氪共享的集团平台（SEA、GEA、GTA）、动力总成（雷神电混）和 Flyme Auto / 千里浩瀚软件体系，以及覆盖全品类的产品矩阵，这支撑其 2025 年销量达到约 124 万辆 [13][15]。主要短板是品牌溢价低于领克、极氪，在价格战主导的市场中差异化有限，且对星愿、E5 两款车型依赖较重；2026 年上半年销量同比下滑 5%，反映了国内新能源市场走弱时的压力 [14]。

未来展望

吉利汽车为吉利银河设定 2026 年销量目标 155 万辆，同比增长 25%，在集团 345 万辆（含新能源 222 万辆）的整体目标之内 [16][25]。品牌计划在 2026 年推出星舰 7 EV（2026-06-10 上市）、战舰 700 硬派 SUV（2026-10-10 上市）、TT 纯电运动轿车、星耀 7 MAX、2026 款 A7 EM / A7 EV 等新车型，并将「千里浩瀚」辅助驾驶下放至更多车型；银河官方称 2026 年是其「产品大年」 [15][23]。出口方面，STARRAY EM-i 计划 2026 年进入德国、法国、英国和巴西 [26]。在智驾层面，吉利称其千里浩瀚 G-ASD 于 2026 年 3 月获得欧盟认证，为公司所称中国首个成功出海的高阶辅助驾驶系统 [13]。

我们的分析：25% 的增长目标比上半年实际表现更具挑战，因为银河 2026 年上半年销量同比下降 5%，下半年需明显提速才能达成 [14]。这一判断的前提是 2026 年新车型顺利起量、海外销量爬坡，结果还取决于国内新能源价格战和欧洲市场环境的演变。品牌的规模、成本基础和共享技术为其提供了可信路径，但目标并非必然达成。

风险

  • 价格竞争：银河处于最拥挤的主流新能源区间，比亚迪等对手已将高阶智驾下探至入门价位，压缩利润空间 [5]。
  • 销量集中：销量对星愿、E5 两款车型依赖较重，2026 年上半年同比下滑 5% 显示其对国内新能源市场走弱的敏感性 [14]。
  • 集团内品牌重叠：银河与领克、极氪共享平台与技术，可能在集团内部形成资源与用户竞争 [21]。
  • 体系依赖：银河的研发、供应链与渠道由吉利集团统一收口，品牌自身可施展的差异化空间有限 [21]。
  • 海外与监管风险：银河出口车型（EX5、STARRAY EM-i）以 Geely 名义销售，向欧洲等市场扩张时面临当地监管与竞争环境变化 [27][25]。
  • 技术路线敞口：品牌同时布局插混、增程与纯电，新能源补贴或电池政策变化可能改变任一技术路线的经济性 [6]。
  • 宏观与竞争环境：吉利汽车在中报中提示，宏观经济、地缘政治及市场竞争仍可能影响经营表现 [14]。
