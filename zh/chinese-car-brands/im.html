Overview

IM Motors (Zhiji Auto, 智己汽车) is the premium smart electric-vehicle brand of the state-owned SAIC Motor Corporation (Shanghai Stock Exchange 600104). It was registered on 25 December 2020 in Shanghai Pudong by SAIC, Zhangjiang Hi-Tech and Alibaba Group, and grew out of SAIC's internal 'No.1 project' (the L project) [2][23]. As of 30 June 2025 SAIC held 7.19% directly and 52.34% indirectly, 59.53% in total, remaining the controlling shareholder [12].

IM sells premium new-energy passenger cars in roughly the RMB 200,000-400,000-plus segment, spanning the L6/L7 sedans and LS6/LS7/LS8/LS9 SUVs. In 2025 it sold 81,017 vehicles (wholesale), up 23.68% year-on-year [10]. Per SAIC's 2025 annual report, IM's 2025 operating revenue was RMB 15.411 billion and net profit attributable to the parent was RMB -3.598 billion [11]. In December 2025 IM announced its first full-cost-basis monthly profit [19], and from 2025 it began expanding overseas under the IM5/IM6 names [22].

Current operations

Strategic position

IM Motors is SAIC's premium electric-vehicle flagship and its internally-designated 'No.1 project', described by official channels as a high-end smart electric brand cultivated in Shanghai Pudong and a key move in the Shanghai state-owned system's transition to the intelligent-vehicle era [21][23]. Operationally it runs a 'pure-electric plus extended-range' product matrix and a 'domestic plus overseas' dual-track layout [29]. SAIC has told media that IM is the group's 'No.1 project' and that its latest technologies are first put into production on IM models [33].

Product lineup and domestic business

As of the cut-off date, IM fields six production models: the L7 (April 2022, RMB 368,800-408,800, mid-to-large sedan), LS7 (February 2023, RMB 309,800-459,800), LS6 (October 2023, guide RMB 229,900-291,900, the volume model), L6 (May 2024, launch-benefit RMB 199,900-325,900), LS9 (November 2025, launch-benefit RMB 322,800-352,800, large six-seat flagship) and LS8 (April 2026, RMB 261,800-311,800, mid-to-large extended-range SUV) [3][4][5][6][8][9].

The line-up is shifting from pure-electric to a 'pure-electric plus extended-range' mix: the new-generation LS6 (September 2025), LS9 (November 2025) and LS8 (April 2026) all use the 'Hengxing' super extended-range system on an 800V platform [29][9]. Sales remain concentrated in the LS6 - SAIC disclosed it passed 100,000 cumulative deliveries, the brand's first model to do so [21].

Manufacturing and capacity

IM vehicles are produced at SAIC's Lingang production base in the Shanghai Lingang Special Area. A SAIC-affiliated newspaper reported that the base builds the IM L7, LS7 and LS6 alongside Roewe/Feifan models, and that the base's new-energy monthly output reached a record 13,950 units in December 2023 [20]. IM does not disclose brand-specific capacity.

Sales trajectory

IM's annual sales climbed from a low base: 38,253 units in 2023 [28], 65,503 in 2024 [10], and 81,017 in 2025, up 23.68% [10]. Monthly sales exceeded 10,000 for four consecutive months from September to December 2025: November sales were 13,443 units, up 34.34% year-on-year (January-November cumulative 69,199) [13], and December sales were 11,818 [10]; SAIC's website noted on 1 December 2025 that IM had by then posted three consecutive months above 10,000 [17].

In 2026, SAIC's production-and-sales bulletin shows IM sold 39,652 units in January-June, up 106.97% year-on-year (8,000 in June, up 81.32%) [14]; IM's own figure was 40,087 deliveries in H1 2026, up 58% [37]. The two series (wholesale sales and deliveries) are separate statistical bases.

Recent financials (H1 2026)

According to media citing SAIC's 2026 interim report, IM's H1 2026 operating revenue was RMB 8.357 billion (RMB 3.556 billion a year earlier) and net profit attributable to the parent was RMB -1.324 billion (RMB -1.825 billion a year earlier), a narrower loss; total assets were RMB 22.506 billion and net assets attributable to the parent RMB 0.816 billion at 30 June 2026 [43]. For full-year 2025, per SAIC's annual report, IM's operating revenue was RMB 15.411 billion and net profit attributable to the parent RMB -3.598 billion [11].

Overseas business

2025 was IM's first overseas year: per IM's official community review, it entered 16 markets across five continents; the IM6 (LS6's overseas version) launched in Thailand in March 2025, the IM5 and IM6 launched in Singapore in October 2025, and the IM5 became the first Chinese car to win The Straits Times Car of the Year 2025 [22]. Overseas sales are not separately disclosed.

Founders and leadership

Founders

IM Motors has no single private entrepreneur-founder: it was incubated by SAIC as its 'No.1 project' (L project), personally championed by then-SAIC chairman Chen Hong with SAIC president Wang Xiaoqiu as overall commander; SAIC executives Jiang Jun and Liu Tao were, respectively, executive group leader and deputy group leader, and Wang Xiaoqiu served as founding chairman [23][2]. At registration on 25 December 2020 the board included Wang Xiaoqiu (chairman), Zu Sijie, Yang Xiaodong, Zhangjiang Hi-Tech chairman Liu Ying and Alibaba DAMO Academy president Zhang Jianfeng as directors [2].

Current management

  • Jia Jianxu (贾健旭) - chairman of IM Motors (since a business-registration change on 16 December 2024) and president of SAIC Motor. Born February 1978 with an MBA, he spent more than 20 years at Yanfeng Automotive Trim Systems (general manager from 2018) and led SAIC's European vehicle-market work; he became general manager of SAIC Volkswagen in February 2023, SAIC vice president in December 2023, and SAIC president in July 2024 [27][15][26][16].
  • Jiang Jun (蒋峻) - CEO of IM Motors and vice president of SAIC (appointed SAIC vice president on 22 December 2023 while remaining IM CEO; his exact IM CEO start date is not disclosed). Born March 1970 with a management master's degree, he previously served as marketing after-sales director at Shanghai GM, deputy general manager of SAIC Motor Manufacturing and marketing executive director at SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle, and led the L project from 2020 [25][15].
  • Liu Tao (刘涛) - co-CEO of IM Motors (exact appointment date not disclosed). He graduated from Jilin University of Technology in 1997 and joined SAIC the same year, worked on SAIC's SsangYong joint venture and the UK Longbridge acquisition, led development of the inkaNet connected-car system in 2010, served as product-planning director at SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle, moved to SAIC headquarters in 2017 and became deputy group leader of the L project in 2020; from August 2026 his focus shifted to overseas business [1][45][43].
  • Xu Yin (徐崟) - vice general manager of IM Motors (appointed August 2026, in charge of domestic marketing) [43].

Per the company's information-disclosure page, board members include Liu Ying, Zu Sijie, Zhang Jianfeng and Jiang Jun, with Jia Jianxu as chairman; the registered business scope covers R&D, manufacturing and sales of new-energy vehicles and components [1].

Ownership and governance

IM Motors is not listed. At founding SAIC held 54%, Zhangjiang Hi-Tech and Alibaba 18% each, with the remaining 10% split between ESOP (5.1%) and CSOP (4.9%) platforms; SAIC and Zhangjiang Hi-Tech initially held their stakes through the Shanghai Yuanjie Intelligent Technology Equity Investment Fund (limited partnership), where SAIC subscribed RMB 5.399 billion (74.986% of the fund) and Zhangjiang Hi-Tech RMB 1.8 billion (25%) [2][18]. SAIC's 2025 interim report shows that as of 30 June 2025 SAIC held 7.19% directly and 52.34% indirectly, 59.53% in total [12]. On 23 December 2025 SAIC announced (Lin 2025-056) that it would convert the RMB 5.399 billion registered capital in IM held via the Yuanjie fund into direct holding, stating its shareholding in IM is unchanged [18]. SAIC's 2025 annual report capital-reserve note discloses that after minority shareholders' capital increases, SAIC's share of IM's net assets fell from 50.93% to 50.27%, and that under the updated articles of association SAIC still controls IM [11].

Competition

IM Motors competes in China's RMB 200,000-400,000 premium new-energy segment. In the RMB 200,000-300,000 large five-seat extended-range SUV segment, media describe the Li Auto L6 as the benchmark that the new-generation LS6 targets on space and range, while in the large-SUV flagship segment, reports place the LS9 alongside the AITO M9, Lynk & Co 900, Denza N9 and Zeekr 9X [35][32]. Its stated differentiators are the Lingxi Digital Chassis, the 'Hengxing' super extended-range system, and the downward deployment of flagship technology to mainstream price points [40].

Our judgment. IM's competitive base rests on SAIC's manufacturing and supply-chain system and a technically dense product line-up, but its annual volume (about 81,000 units in 2025) remains well below head-tier rivals, and growth has been highly dependent on the LS6 [10][21]. Pricing has also drifted down over time from the L7's RMB 400,000-plus launch toward the RMB 200,000 segment, which some media have noted pressures its 'premium' positioning [29]. Within the group, the new 'Shangjie' brand (SAIC with Huawei) targets the mainstream market, and media have flagged potential internal positioning overlap with IM [33].

Outlook

Company plans. IM Motors released the 'NEXT 2028' strategy on 21 August 2026, built on the NEO three-electric architecture, a steer-by-wire chassis and the IM Claw agent, and said these would be standard across future models; the strategy's first production model, the new-generation L6, launched in August 2026, and a new-generation LS6 opened pre-sale on 10 September 2026 from RMB 209,900 [40][41][42]. Media report IM set a full-year 2026 delivery target of 110,000-130,000 units, and the company has said it will keep accelerating its overseas expansion (16 markets entered in 2025) [43][22].

Our judgment. Whether IM sustains its 2025-2026 momentum depends on two premises: first, that the LS8/LS9 and 'NEXT 2028' models broaden a line-up still concentrated in the LS6; second, that the brand converts its first full-cost-basis monthly profit (December 2025) into sustained profitability while its volume remains far below head-tier rivals [21][43]. The steer-by-wire chassis and semi-solid-state battery are the stated technology differentiators, but their contribution to profitability in the price-competitive RMB 200,000-300,000 segment remains to be seen [29].

Risks

  • Product concentration. IM's volume has been highly dependent on the LS6, the brand's first model to pass 100,000 cumulative deliveries, concentrating sales risk in a single product line [21].
  • Price competition and positioning. Pricing has drifted from the RMB 400,000-plus L7 launch toward the RMB 200,000 segment in a heavily price-competitive market, which pressures margins and the 'premium' positioning [29].
  • Channel and dealer risk. In July 2026 media reported that some IM dealers in Kunming and Zhuhai faced operating anomalies after cash-flow problems left some buyers unable to obtain vehicle certificates; IM said it was restoring certificate issuance and delivery and that channel operations were otherwise stable [38].
  • Scale gap versus rivals. IM's monthly deliveries have repeatedly fallen below 10,000 units, while head-tier premium NEV brands sustain monthly volumes above that level, limiting IM's economies of scale [39].
  • Persistent losses. IM recorded a net profit attributable to the parent of RMB -3.598 billion in 2025 and RMB -1.324 billion in H1 2026, and has not yet turned profitable [11][43].
  • Technology iteration and compliance. IM is moving its whole range to steer-by-wire and high-level assisted-driving hardware, which raises technical and regulatory execution risk relative to its current scale [40].
  • Overseas and trade barriers. IM is expanding in Europe and other markets; its parent SAIC has disclosed adverse effects from the EU anti-subsidy investigation on overseas operations, which could affect IM's European expansion [11].

概览

智己汽车（IM Motors）是国有控股的上海汽车集团股份有限公司（上交所 600104）旗下的高端智能电动车品牌，2020 年 12 月 25 日由上汽集团、张江高科与阿里巴巴集团在浦东注册成立，脱胎于上汽内部的「一号工程」（L 项目）[2][23]。截至 2025 年 6 月 30 日，上汽直接持股 7.19%、间接持股 52.34%，合计 59.53%，仍为控股股东[12]。

智己主营约 20 万元至 40 万元以上的高端新能源乘用车，产品覆盖 L6/L7 轿车与 LS6/LS7/LS8/LS9 SUV。2025 年销售 8.10 万辆（批发口径）、同比增长 23.68%[10]。据上汽 2025 年年度报告，智己 2025 年营业总收入 154.11 亿元、归母净利润 -35.98 亿元[11]。2025 年 12 月，智己宣布首次实现全成本口径单月盈利[19]，并从 2025 年起以 IM5/IM6 之名拓展海外市场[22]。

现状

战略定位

智己是上汽集团的高端电动车旗舰与内部认定的「一号工程」，官方渠道将其描述为浦东本土培育的高端智能电动汽车品牌、上海国资体系面向智能汽车时代的关键落子[21][23]。经营上，智己采取「纯电+增程」产品矩阵与「国内+海外」双线布局[29]。上汽方面对媒体强调，智己是集团「一号工程」，集团所有最新技术均会在智己车型上率先量产落地[33]。

产品谱系与国内业务

截至研究截止日，智己拥有六款量产车型：L7（2022 年 4 月，36.88 万–40.88 万元，中大型轿车）、LS7（2023 年 2 月，30.98 万–45.98 万元）、LS6（2023 年 10 月，指导价 22.99 万–29.19 万元，销量主力）、L6（2024 年 5 月，权益价 19.99 万–32.59 万元）、LS9（2025 年 11 月，权益价 32.28 万–35.28 万元，大型六座旗舰）、LS8（2026 年 4 月，26.18 万–31.18 万元，中大型增程）[3][4][5][6][8][9]。

产品线正从纯电向「纯电+增程」双线演进：新一代 LS6（2025 年 9 月）、LS9（2025 年 11 月）、LS8（2026 年 4 月）均采用 800V 平台上的「恒星」超级增程[29][9]。销量仍集中于 LS6——上汽披露其累计交付突破 10 万辆，为品牌首款破十万车型[21]。

制造与产能

智己车型由上汽集团位于上海临港新片区的临港生产基地生产。上汽旗下报纸报道，临港基地生产智己 L7、LS7、LS6 及荣威/飞凡等车型，2023 年 12 月该基地新能源汽车月产量达 13,950 辆的历史新高[20]。智己未披露品牌专属产能数据。

销售轨迹

智己年销量从低基数快速爬升：2023 年 3.83 万辆[28]，2024 年 6.55 万辆[10]，2025 年 8.10 万辆、同比增长 23.68%[10]。2025 年 9 月至 12 月连续四个月月销破万：11 月销量 13,443 辆、同比增长 34.34%（1–11 月累计 69,199 辆）[13]，12 月销量 11,818 辆[10]；上汽官网于 2025 年 12 月 1 日的快报中称智己当时已连续三个月月销破万[17]。

进入 2026 年，上汽产销快报显示智己 1–6 月累计销量 39,652 辆、同比增长 106.97%（其中 6 月 8,000 辆、同比增长 81.32%）[14]；智己官方口径称 2026 年上半年交付 40,087 辆、同比增长 58%[37]。两套口径（批发销量与交付量）为不同统计口径。

财务现状（截至 2026 年上半年）

据媒体引述上汽集团 2026 年半年度报告，2026 年上半年智己营业总收入 83.57 亿元（上年同期 35.56 亿元），归母净利润 -13.24 亿元（上年同期 -18.25 亿元），亏损同比收窄；截至 2026 年 6 月 30 日总资产 225.06 亿元、归母净资产 8.16 亿元[43]。2025 年全年，据上汽 2025 年年度报告，智己营业总收入 154.11 亿元、归母净利润 -35.98 亿元[11]。

海外业务

2025 年是智己出海元年：据智己官方社区回顾，全年进入 16 个市场、覆盖五大洲，IM6（LS6 海外版）2025 年 3 月登陆泰国，IM5 与 IM6 10 月登陆新加坡，IM5 成为首个获新加坡《海峡时报》2025 年度车型的中国车型[22]。海外销量未单独披露。

智己汽车并无单一民营创始人：它由上汽集团以「一号工程」（L 项目）孵化，由上汽集团董事长陈虹挂帅、总裁王晓秋任总指挥；上汽高管蒋峻、刘涛分别任 L 项目执行组组长与副组长，王晓秋任创始董事长[23][2]。2020 年 12 月 25 日注册时，董事会由王晓秋任董事长，祖似杰、杨晓东、张江高科董事长刘樱与阿里达摩院院长张建锋任董事[2]。

  • 贾健旭——智己汽车董事长（2024 年 12 月 16 日工商变更接任，同时任上汽集团总裁）。1978 年 2 月出生，研究生毕业、工商管理硕士；在延锋汽车饰件系统工作 20 余年（2018 年起任总经理），曾负责上汽欧洲整车市场开拓；2023 年 2 月任上汽大众总经理，2023 年 12 月任上汽集团副总裁，2024 年 7 月任上汽集团总裁[27][15][26][16]。
  • 蒋峻——智己汽车 CEO、上汽集团副总裁（2023 年 12 月 22 日升任上汽集团副总裁，同时仍任智己 CEO；其就任智己 CEO 的具体起始日期未公开披露）。1970 年 3 月出生，管理学硕士；曾任上海通用市场营销部售后服务总监、上汽汽车制造有限公司副总经理、上汽乘用车营销执行总监等职，2020 年起任 L 项目执行组组长[25][15]。
  • 刘涛——智己汽车联席 CEO（就任日期未公开披露）。1997 年毕业于吉林工业大学并同年加入上汽，参与上汽与韩国双龙合资、英国长桥收购等项目；2010 年主导开发 inkaNet 车联网系统，曾任上汽乘用车产品规划总监，2017 年调任集团总部，2020 年任 L 项目组副组长；2026 年 8 月起其工作重心转向海外[1][45][43]。
  • 徐崟——智己汽车副总经理（2026 年 8 月出任，分管国内营销）[43]。

据公司信息公开页面，董事会成员包括刘樱、祖似杰、张建锋、蒋峻，董事长为贾健旭；登记经营范围涵盖新能源汽车整车及零部件的研发、制造与销售[1]。

股权与治理

智己汽车为非上市主体。注册时上汽持股 54%、张江高科与阿里巴巴各持股 18%，其余 10% 分为 ESOP（5.1%）与 CSOP（4.9%）平台；上汽与张江高科最初均通过上海元界智能科技股权投资基金（有限合伙）间接持股，其中上汽认缴 53.99 亿元（占基金 74.986%）、张江高科认缴 18 亿元（占 25%）[2][18]。上汽 2025 年半年报显示，截至 2025 年 6 月 30 日上汽直接持股 7.19%、间接持股 52.34%，合计 59.53%[12]。2025 年 12 月 23 日上汽公告（临 2025-056）拟将经元界基金持有的智己 53.99 亿元注册资本转为直接持有，并称对智己持股比例不变[18]。上汽 2025 年年度报告资本公积附注披露，少数股东增资后，上汽享有智己净资产的比例由 50.93% 降至 50.27%，根据更新后的公司章程，上汽仍对其实施控制[11]。

竞争格局

智己在国内 20 万–40 万元高端新能源市场参与竞争。在 20 万–30 万元级大五座增程 SUV 市场，媒体报道将理想 L6 视为新一代 LS6 在空间与续航上对标的基准车型；在大尺寸旗舰 SUV 细分市场，报道则将 LS9 与问界 M9、领克 900、腾势 N9、极氪 9X 等「9 系」SUV 并列表述[35][32]。其公开强调的差异化是灵蜥数字底盘、恒星超级增程，以及将旗舰技术下放至主流价位的路线[40]。

我们的判断。智己的竞争基础是上汽的制造与供应链体系以及技术密度较高的产品线，但其年销规模（2025 年约 8.1 万辆）仍远低于头部对手，且增长高度依赖 LS6 单一车型[10][21]。品牌定价也随时间从 L7 时代的 40 万元级逐步下探至 20 万元级，有媒体指出这对「高端」定位构成压力[29]。集团内部新品牌「尚界」（上汽与华为合作）定位主流市场，媒体提示其可能与智己形成内部定位上的竞争[33]。

未来展望

公司公开规划。智己于 2026 年 8 月 21 日发布「NEXT·2028」战略，以 NEO 三电架构、全线控底盘、IM Claw 智能体为三大技术支柱，并称将在后续车型全系标配；该战略首款落地车型新一代 L6 已于 2026 年 8 月上市，全新一代 LS6 于 2026 年 9 月 10 日开启预售、预售价 20.99 万元起[40][41][42]。媒体报道智己 2026 年定下全年 11 万–13 万辆的交付目标；公司还表示 2026 年将持续加速海外布局（2025 年已进入 16 个市场）[43][22]。

我们的判断。智己能否延续 2025–2026 年的势头，取决于两个前提：其一，LS8/LS9 与「NEXT·2028」车型能否拓宽目前仍集中于 LS6 的产品线；其二，能否把 2025 年 12 月的首次全成本口径单月盈利，转化为规模仍远小于头部对手前提下的持续盈利[21][43]。全线控底盘与半固态电池是其公开强调的技术差异化点，但在 20 万–30 万元这一价格竞争激烈的区间，其对盈利的贡献仍有待观察[29]。

风险

  • 产品结构集中。智己销量高度依赖 LS6 单一车型（其为首款累计交付破 10 万辆的车型），销量风险集中于单一产品线[21]。
  • 价格竞争与定位。品牌定价从 L7 上市时的 40 万元级逐步下探至 20 万元级，所处市场竞争激烈，对利润率与「高端」定位构成压力[29]。
  • 渠道与经销商风险。2026 年 7 月媒体披露，昆明、珠海部分智己经销商因资金链问题，部分购车者一度无法取得车辆合格证；智己表示正陆续恢复合格证发放与交付，并称全国渠道运营稳健[38]。
  • 规模差距。智己月度销量多次低于 1 万辆，而头部高端新能源品牌月度销量稳定在 1 万辆以上，规模经济受限[39]。
  • 持续亏损。智己 2025 年归母净利润 -35.98 亿元、2026 年上半年 -13.24 亿元，尚未实现盈利[11][43]。
  • 技术迭代与合规。智己正将全线控底盘与高阶辅助驾驶硬件推向全系，相对于其现有规模，技术与合规执行风险上升[40]。
  • 海外与贸易壁垒。智己正拓展欧洲等市场；其母公司上汽披露海外业务受欧盟反补贴调查影响，这亦可能波及智己的欧洲拓展[11]。
