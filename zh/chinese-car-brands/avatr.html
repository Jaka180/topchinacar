Overview

Avatr is a premium intelligent-electric-vehicle brand under Changan Automobile, co-created by Changan, Huawei and CATL. Its predecessor was Changan NIO New Energy Vehicle Technology, a 50:50 venture set up by Changan and NIO in July 2018; after NIO gradually exited, the company was renamed Avatr Technology (Chongqing) in May 2021 and brought in CATL, forming the 'CHN' (Changan, Huawei, CATL) collaboration model [1][9]. Changan holds 40.99% as the largest shareholder (and treats Avatr as an equity-accounted associate), CATL holds 9.17%, and Huawei is a technology partner but not a shareholder [5][9]. Avatr positions itself in the RMB 200,000–700,000 premium band, spanning SUVs and sedans with both pure-electric and extended-range powertrains [4].

Avatr has scaled quickly but remains loss-making. Deliveries rose from 20,021 in 2023 to 61,588 in 2024 and 122,702 in 2025, while revenue grew from RMB 5.645 billion to RMB 25.631 billion over the same period; the brand still recorded a RMB 3.489 billion loss in 2025 [14]. In 2025 it ranked eighth by volume in China's RMB 200,000+ premium NEV market [14]. In 2026 deliveries fell sharply (27,619 in H1, -51.3% year on year) [11]. Avatr filed for a Hong Kong IPO on 2025-11-27 and refiled on 2026-06-30, and holds a 10% stake in Huawei's Yinwang [15]. It is not yet listed; parent-company context is covered in the Changan deep dive.

Avatr runs an asset-light model with no self-built plant: it relies on Changan's manufacturing system and focuses on product definition, design, core technology, brand and sales, employing 3,978 staff (55.0% in R&D) and holding 1,559 patents at end-2025 [20][19]. It has completed four financing rounds totalling about RMB 19.067 billion and filed twice for a Hong Kong listing [19][5].

Current operations

Positioning and model line-up

As of the research cut-off (2026-10-29), the official Avatr China website lists the Avatr 11 (SUV), 12 (sedan), 07 (SUV), 06 (sedan), 06T (shooting brake) and 07L (five-seat SUV), plus the 011/012 limited editions and the T09 concept [4]. The prospectus groups its production line-up into the 06, 07, 11 and 12 series, covering the premium and luxury segments at RMB 200,000–700,000 [1]. Avatr is, per media citing the prospectus, the first emerging NEV brand to complete a dual (pure-electric and extended-range) powertrain layout across its entire model range [19].

Business structure

Vehicle sales are the dominant revenue source. Per the prospectus, NEV sales were RMB 5.542 / 14.417 / 23.902 billion in 2023–2025 (98.2% / 94.9% / 93.3% of revenue), while parts and other revenue grew from RMB 103 million to RMB 1.729 billion (6.7% in 2025) [14]. By model, in 2025 the Avatr 07 delivered 57,338 units, the 06 36,528, the 12 20,963 and the 11 7,873 [14]. In 2025 Avatr ranked eighth by volume in China's RMB 200,000+ premium NEV market; the 06 ranked fourth among RMB 200,000+ mid-size NEV sedans, the 07 fourth in its mid-size SUV band, and the 12 second among RMB 300,000+ large luxury NEV sedans [14]. In H1 2025 revenue reached RMB 12.208 billion, up 98.5% year on year [9]. Avatr has not separately disclosed interim financial statements for H1 2026; the latest disclosed operating data as of the cut-off date are H1 2026 deliveries of 27,619 units (-51.3% year on year).

Technology and platforms

Avatr's technology stack is built on the CHN platform co-developed with Changan, Huawei and CATL. The 11 and 12 originated from Huawei's HI (Huawei Inside) mode with Huawei intelligent driving and the HarmonyOS cockpit; newer models carry Huawei Qiankun ADS 5 and the HarmonySpace cockpit [10][17]. Since the Avatr 07 (2024) the brand added an in-house Kunlun (昆仑) extended-range powertrain, and models use the Taihang (太行) intelligent chassis and the AEEA platform architecture [15]. In November 2025 the Huawei cooperation was upgraded from HI to the 'HI PLUS' joint co-creation model [10]. As of end-2025, 55.0% of employees were in R&D and the company held 1,559 patents [19].

Yinwang investment and Huawei collaboration

In August 2024 Avatr announced it would acquire 10% of Huawei's Yinwang (Shenzhen Yinwang Intelligent Technology), the vehicle that carries Huawei's intelligent-vehicle-solutions business, for RMB 11.5 billion; the deal was completed in February 2025, making Avatr Yinwang's second-largest shareholder [15][14]. Yinwang reported 2025 revenue of RMB 45.018 billion (+72.1%), and Avatr recognised RMB 182 million of share-of-associate profit from the stake in 2025 [15]. The cooperation was upgraded to the 'HI PLUS' joint co-creation model in November 2025, with plans to co-launch 17 products with Huawei by 2030 [10].

Flagship product and monthly rhythm

Launched 8 August 2026, the 07L is a mid-large pure-electric SUV (4,910 mm long, 2,990 mm wheelbase) standard with Huawei Qiankun ADS 5 and a 896-line lidar among 27 sensing units, plus an 89.33 kWh CATL 5C battery supporting 800V charging; the top three-motor version reaches 0–100 km/h in 3.7 seconds [17]. Monthly sales peaked at about 14,100 units in November 2025, then declined into 2026 — H1 2026 averaged under 5,000 units a month, with 7,459 delivered in June 2026 [18][11][10].

Asset-light model and supply chain

Avatr runs an asset-light model: it has no self-built vehicle plant and relies on Changan's manufacturing system, while CATL supplies batteries and Huawei (via Yinwang) supplies intelligent-driving and cockpit technology [20]. Its distribution network expanded from none in 2023 to 259 dealers at end-2024 and 384 at end-2025; distribution revenue rose from 1.1% of total in 2023 to 49.3% in 2025, with 607 stores across 216 cities at end-2025 [20].

Overseas business

Overseas revenue was RMB 223 million in 2024 (1.5% of total) and RMB 1.398 billion in 2025 (5.5%), with an average overseas selling price above RMB 300,000 [15]. Avatr was in 38 countries/regions with 80+ sales points at end-2025, expanding to 43 countries/regions and 95 points by end-May 2026; overseas sales rose 33.4% year on year in January–May 2026 and the overseas business had turned profitable [15]. The company plans to formally enter Europe in 2026 [14].

Founders and leadership

Origin

Avatr has no private founder. It began as Changan NIO, a 50:50 venture between Changan Automobile and NIO set up in July 2018, and was relaunched in 2021 under the Changan, Huawei and CATL partnership [9].

Current management

  • Chen Zhuo (陈卓) — chairman, promoted from president on 30 September 2026. He joined Changan Automobile in 2006; on 14 February 2023 he was appointed senior executive vice president of Avatr, and on 26 December 2023 president. On 15 June 2026 Changan's board appointed him a vice president of Changan Automobile. As chairman he oversees strategy, shareholder coordination, capitalisation and globalisation [13].
  • Yong Jun (雍军) — president, appointed 30 September 2026 (the appointment was still in a publicity period when announced). A Changan-system executive with more than 20 years in the auto industry, he previously led Avatr's R&D and oversaw development and launch of the 11 and 12 [13].

Previous chairmen

Tan Benhong was the first chairman and CEO (2021–December 2023), leading the renaming from Changan NIO and the launch of the Avatr 11. Zhu Huarong, chairman of China Changan Automobile Group, concurrently chaired Avatr from December 2023 to September 2025. Wang Hui was the third chairman (September 2025–September 2026) before returning to Changan as an executive vice president overseeing overseas manufacturing, global KD projects and supply chain [13].

Ownership and governance

Per the prospectus and Changan's announcement, Changan holds 40.99% as the largest shareholder and CATL 9.17%; Huawei is not a shareholder [5]. Changan's financial statements treat Avatr as an equity-accounted associate (not consolidated) [2]. Avatr has completed four financing rounds totalling about RMB 19.067 billion: a RMB 3 billion round in November 2023 (valuation RMB 14.085 billion) and a Series C of over RMB 11 billion in December 2024, in which Changan added RMB 4.551 billion and kept its 40.99% stake [19]. Avatr also holds 10% of Huawei's Yinwang, acquired for RMB 11.5 billion [15]. No actual controller is separately disclosed for Avatr; only the shareholding and voting structure above is stated.

Competition

Our judgment: competitive field. By its RMB 200,000–700,000 premium SUV/sedan line-up, Avatr competes mainly with domestic premium intelligent NEVs such as AITO (Seres/Huawei), Li Auto, Zeekr, NIO and Voyah, plus Xiaomi and Tesla in the sedan segment. This is an analytical selection, not a sales ranking [12][9]. Per CNR, the 07L is pitched in stores against AITO by offering 'M6 pricing with M7 features' [12].

Our judgment: strengths. Avatr benefits from the CHN alliance — Changan's manufacturing and channels, CATL's batteries and Huawei's intelligent-driving stack — giving it a faster product rollout than many start-ups [10]. Its gross margin improved from -3.0% in 2023 to 9.4% in 2025, and overseas vehicles carry a price premium (above RMB 300,000 average) [14][15].

Our judgment: weaknesses. Volume is far below rivals: CNR notes that in the RMB 200,000-class pure-electric SUV segment, the Zhijie R7 and Zeekr 7X outperformed the Avatr 07 [12]. As Huawei's ecosystem expands across many brands, Avatr's Huawei differentiation is diluted; it also faces direct 'same-source' competition from the Huawei 'five worlds' (AITO, Zhijie and others) and 'three realms' brands [18]. The 2026 sales decline and the shift toward the RMB 200,000 segment blur its premium positioning [12].

Our judgment: profitability gap. CNR cites industry data that in 2025 Li Auto and XPeng had gross margins above 18%, NIO 13.6%, Voyah 20.9% and Seres near 30%, versus Avatr's 9.4% [12]. The three shareholders also have different goals — Changan seeks premium-brand scale, CATL battery deployment, and Huawei penetration of its intelligent-driving solution — which can raise coordination costs when volume is under pressure [12].

Our judgment: scale gap. People's Daily (China Economic Weekly) reported in December 2025 that Avatr sold about 14,100 units in November 2025, below the same-month volumes of Leapmotor (70,300), NIO (36,300), XPeng (36,700) and Li Auto (33,200); it also noted that within Huawei's ecosystem the 'five worlds' (AITO, Zhijie and others) and 'three realms' (Yijing, Qijing, Huajing) brands all adopt Huawei solutions, creating same-source competition for Avatr [18].

Outlook

Company targets. Avatr's 'Strategy 2.0' (announced September 2025) targets 400,000 global sales and RMB 100 billion annual revenue by 2027, 800,000 global sales by 2030, and 1.5 million by 2035 [11]. On products, Avatr plans to co-launch 17 models with Huawei by 2030 and to add a flagship six-seat SUV in 2026 [10]. On overseas expansion, it aims to enter 110+ countries/regions by 2030 with overseas sales above 40% of the total, and to formally enter Europe in 2026 [11]. The prospectus states the IPO proceeds will mainly fund product and platform development, brand building, sales-network expansion and working capital [11]. These are disclosed plans and targets, not completed results.

Our judgment. The gap between current scale and the 2027 target is large: H1 2026 deliveries were 27,619 units (a monthly average below 5,000), and even the 2025 peak of 122,702 units was well short of the roughly 400,000 targeted for 2027 [11]. The prospectus also warns the company may still record a net loss for the full year 2026 [11]. Whether the targets are met depends on the 07L and the upcoming six-seat flagship regaining volume, and on overseas expansion converting into sales — all unproven as of the cut-off date [12]. This is our analysis, not a forecast of financial results.

Risks

  • Persistent losses. Avatr lost RMB 3.693 / 4.018 / 3.489 billion in 2023–2025 (about RMB 11.2 billion cumulative), and the prospectus warns full-year 2026 may still record a net loss [14][11].
  • Sales decline. H1 2026 deliveries fell 51.3% year on year to 27,619 units; June 2026 deliveries were 7,459 [11][10].
  • Supply-chain and manufacturing dependence. Manufacturing relies on Changan and key technology on Huawei/Yinwang and CATL, concentrating operational dependence [20].
  • Weakening Huawei differentiation. Huawei's intelligent-driving solutions are now deployed across many brands, diluting Avatr's once-exclusive edge [18].
  • Financial position. Cash and equivalents fell from RMB 19.323 billion (end-2024) to RMB 9.687 billion (end-2025) after the RMB 11.5 billion Yinwang stake purchase, with the current ratio down from 1.3 to 0.6 [14].
  • Overseas and price competition. Overseas expansion faces tariffs and market-entry costs, while domestic price competition has pulled the brand toward the crowded RMB 200,000 segment [11][12].

概览

阿维塔（Avatr）是长安汽车旗下、由长安、华为、宁德时代三方共同打造的高端智能电动汽车品牌。其前身为长安汽车与蔚来于 2018 年 7 月按 50:50 合资设立的长安蔚来新能源汽车科技有限公司；蔚来逐步退出后，公司于 2021 年 5 月更名为阿维塔科技（重庆），并引入宁德时代，形成「CHN」（长安、华为、宁德时代）协同模式 [1][9]。长安汽车持股 40.99% 为第一大股东（并按权益法将阿维塔列为联营企业），宁德时代持股 9.17%，华为为技术合作伙伴而非股东 [5][9]。品牌定位 20 万–70 万元高端区间，车型覆盖 SUV 与轿车、纯电与增程 [4]。

阿维塔规模增长快但持续亏损：交付量由 2023 年的 20,021 辆增至 2024 年的 61,588 辆、2025 年的 122,702 辆，同期营收由 56.45 亿元增至 256.31 亿元，但 2025 年仍亏损 34.89 亿元 [14]。2025 年在 20 万元以上中国新豪华新能源乘用车市场按销量排名第八 [14]。2026 年交付大幅回落（上半年 27,619 辆，同比 -51.3%）[11]。阿维塔于 2025-11-27 首次递表港交所、2026-06-30 二次递表，并持有华为引望 10% 股权 [15]。品牌未单独上市，母公司情况见「长安汽车」深度研究。

阿维塔采用轻资产运营模式，不设自有整车工厂，依托长安汽车制造体系，聚焦产品定义、设计、核心技术、品牌与销售，截至 2025 年末拥有员工 3,978 人、研发人员占比 55.0%，累计专利 1,559 项 [20][19]。公司已完成四轮融资累计约 190.67 亿元，并于 2025 年 11 月、2026 年 6 月两度递表港交所 [19][5]。

现状

定位与产品矩阵

截至研究截止日（2026-10-29），阿维塔中国官网在售车型包括阿维塔 11（SUV）、12（轿车）、07（SUV）、06（轿车）、06T（猎装/旅行）与 07L（大五座 SUV），另有 011/012 限量联名版与 T09 概念版 [4]。招股书将主力量产系列概括为 06、07、11、12，覆盖 20 万–70 万元的高端与豪华细分市场 [1]。据媒体转述招股书，阿维塔是首个完成全部车型纯电与增程双动力布局的新兴新能源品牌 [19]。

业务结构

整车销售是绝对收入主力。据招股书，2023–2025 年新能源乘用车销售收入分别为 55.42 亿、144.17 亿、239.02 亿元（占总收入 98.2%、94.9%、93.3%），零部件及其他收入由 1.03 亿元增至 17.29 亿元（2025 年占 6.7%）[14]。分车型看，2025 年阿维塔 07 交付 57,338 辆、06 交付 36,528 辆、12 交付 20,963 辆、11 交付 7,873 辆 [14]。2025 年阿维塔在 20 万元以上中国新豪华新能源乘用车市场按销量排名第八；阿维塔 06 在 20 万元以上中型新能源轿车中排第四，07 在同价位中型 SUV 中排第四，12 在 30 万元以上大型轿车中排第二 [14]。2025 年上半年营收 122.08 亿元、同比增长 98.5% [9]。阿维塔未单独披露 2026 年上半年的中期财务报表，截至研究截止日已公布的最近经营数据为 2026 年上半年交付 27,619 辆（同比 -51.3%）。

技术与平台

阿维塔技术体系建立在与长安、华为、宁德时代共同开发的 CHN 平台之上。阿维塔 11、12 早期源自华为 HI（Huawei Inside）模式，搭载华为智驾与鸿蒙座舱；新车型普遍搭载华为乾崑 ADS 5 与鸿蒙座舱 HarmonySpace [10][17]。自阿维塔 07（2024）起加入自研昆仑增程动力，并采用太行智控底盘与 AEEA 平台架构 [15]。2025 年 11 月，与华为合作由 HI 模式升级为「HI PLUS」联合共创 [10]。截至 2025 年末，研发人员占员工总数 55.0%，累计专利 1,559 项 [19]。

引望投资与华为合作

2024 年 8 月，阿维塔宣布拟以 115 亿元收购华为引望（深圳引望智能技术，华为智能汽车解决方案业务的载体）10% 股权；2025 年 2 月完成交割，成为引望第二大股东 [15][14]。据披露，引望 2025 年收入 450.18 亿元、同比增长 72.1%，阿维塔 2025 年由此分占联营公司利润 1.82 亿元 [15]。2025 年 11 月，双方合作升级为「HI PLUS」联合共创模式，计划到 2030 年与华为联合推出 17 款产品 [10]。

旗舰产品与销量节奏

2026 年 8 月 8 日上市的 07L 为中大型纯电 SUV（车长 4910mm、轴距 2990mm），全系标配华为乾崑 ADS 5 智驾、含 896 线激光雷达在内的 27 个高感知传感器，以及 89.33kWh 宁德时代 5C 神行超充电池、支持 800V 高压快充，顶配三电机版零百加速 3.7 秒 [17]。月销量于 2025 年 11 月达约 1.41 万辆的年内高点，随后进入 2026 年后回落——上半年月均不足 5,000 辆，6 月交付 7,459 辆 [18][11][10]。

轻资产模式与供应链

阿维塔采用轻资产模式：未自建整车工厂，依托长安汽车制造体系，宁德时代供应电池，华为（经引望）提供智驾与座舱技术 [20]。其经销商网络由 2023 年的零起步，发展至 2024 年底 259 家、2025 年底 384 家；分销收入占总收入比重由 2023 年的 1.1% 升至 2025 年的 49.3%，2025 年底在国内设 607 家门店、覆盖 216 座城市 [20]。

海外业务

海外收入由 2024 年的 2.23 亿元（占 1.5%）增至 2025 年的 13.98 亿元（占 5.5%），海外平均售价超 30 万元 [15]。截至 2025 年底进入 38 个国家和地区、布局 80 多个销售网点，截至 2026 年 5 月底拓展至 43 个国家和地区、95 个分销点；2026 年 1–5 月海外销量同比增长 33.4%，海外业务已实现盈利 [15]。公司计划 2026 年正式进入欧洲市场 [14]。

创始人与管理层

创立背景

阿维塔没有民营意义上的单一创始人。其前身是长安汽车与蔚来于 2018 年 7 月按 50:50 合资设立的长安蔚来，2021 年在长安、华为、宁德时代三方合作下重新出发 [9]。

现任管理层

  • 陈卓——董事长，2026 年 9 月 30 日由总裁升任。其 2006 年入职长安汽车，2023 年 2 月 14 日调任阿维塔高级执行副总裁，2023 年 12 月 26 日升任总裁；2026 年 6 月 15 日被长安汽车董事会聘任为长安汽车副总裁。接任董事长后负责战略规划、股东协同、资本化与全球化顶层布局 [13]。
  • 雍军——总裁，2026 年 9 月 30 日获任（任命发布时仍在公示期）。出身长安体系，拥有 20 余年汽车行业经验，此前负责阿维塔研发，主导阿维塔 11、12 的开发与量产上市 [13]。

历任董事长

谭本宏为首任董事长兼 CEO（2021 年至 2023 年 12 月），主导由长安蔚来更名并落地首款车型阿维塔 11。中国长安汽车集团董事长朱华荣于 2023 年 12 月至 2025 年 9 月兼任阿维塔董事长。王辉为第三任董事长（2025 年 9 月至 2026 年 9 月），后调回长安集团任执行副总裁，统筹海外制造、全球 KD 项目与供应链 [13]。

股权与治理

据招股书及长安汽车公告，长安汽车持股 40.99% 为第一大股东，宁德时代持股 9.17%，华为未持股 [5]。长安汽车财报将阿维塔作为按权益法核算的联营企业列示（未并表）[2]。阿维塔已完成四轮融资、累计约 190.67 亿元：2023 年 11 月完成 30 亿元融资（估值 140.85 亿元），2024 年 12 月完成超 110 亿元 C 轮融资（长安增资 45.51 亿元、持股保持 40.99%）[19]。此外，阿维塔以 115 亿元持有华为引望 10% 股权 [15]。阿维塔未单独披露实际控制人，此处仅陈述上述持股与治理结构。

竞争格局

我们的判断：竞争范围。按阿维塔 20 万–70 万元的高端 SUV/轿车产品线，其主要对手包括问界（赛力斯/华为）、理想、极氪、蔚来、岚图等国内高端智能新能源车型，轿车端还有小米、特斯拉。此为分析所选的竞争范围，不是销量排名 [12][9]。据央广网报道，阿维塔 07L 终端销售以「问界 M6 的价格、M7 的配置」对标问界 [12]。

我们的判断：优势。阿维塔受益于 CHN 联盟——长安的制造与渠道、宁德时代的电池、华为的智驾方案，使其产品推出节奏快于多数新势力 [10]。毛利率由 2023 年的 -3.0% 提升至 2025 年的 9.4%，海外车型平均售价超 30 万元、具备明显溢价 [14][15]。

我们的判断：短板。销量规模远低于对手：央广网指出，在 20 万级纯电 SUV 赛道，同期上市的智界 R7、极氪 7X 市场表现均优于阿维塔 07 [12]。随着华为汽车生态扩容至众多品牌，阿维塔的华为技术专属优势被稀释，并面临问界、智界等「五界」与「三境」品牌的同源竞争 [18]。2026 年销量下滑与向 20 万元级车型下探，使高端定位趋于模糊 [12]。

我们的判断：盈利差距。央广网援引行业数据称，2025 年理想、小鹏毛利率均超 18%，蔚来 13.6%，岚图 20.9%，赛力斯接近 30%，而阿维塔为 9.4%，仍处相对低位 [12]。三方股东诉求也存在差异——长安追求高端品牌规模化、宁德时代看重电池装车规模、华为侧重智驾方案渗透率，销量承压时协同成本会显现 [12]。

我们的判断：规模差距。人民日报（中国经济周刊）2025 年 12 月报道指出，2025 年 11 月阿维塔销量约 1.41 万辆，低于同期的零跑（7.03 万辆）、蔚来（3.63 万辆）、小鹏（3.67 万辆）、理想（3.32 万辆）；并提示在华为生态内，问界、智界等「五界」与奕境、启境、华境等「三境」品牌均采用华为方案，阿维塔面临同源竞争 [18]。

未来展望

公司公开目标。阿维塔「战略 2.0」（2025 年 9 月发布）提出：2027 年全球销量 40 万辆、年收入千亿元；2030 年全球销量 80 万辆；2035 年挑战 150 万辆 [11]。产品方面，计划到 2030 年与华为联合推出 17 款产品，并于 2026 年推出旗舰大六座 SUV [10]。海外方面，目标到 2030 年进入 110 多个国家和地区、海外销量占比超 40%，并计划 2026 年正式进入欧洲 [11]。据招股书，本次 IPO 募集资金将主要用于产品开发与平台技术开发、品牌建设、销售服务网络拓展及补充营运资金 [11]。以上为披露的计划与目标，不是已实现的业绩。

我们的判断。当前规模与 2027 年目标差距较大：2026 年上半年交付仅 27,619 辆（月均不足 5,000 辆），即便 2025 年 122,702 辆的峰值也远低于 2027 年约 40 万辆的目标 [11]。招股书亦提示，2026 年完整财年仍可能录得净亏损 [11]。目标能否达成，取决于 07L 与即将推出的旗舰大六座 SUV 能否重新放量、海外扩张能否转化为实际销量——截至研究截止日这些均尚未得到验证 [12]。以上为我们的分析，不构成对财务结果的预测。

风险

  • 持续亏损。阿维塔 2023–2025 年分别亏损 36.93 亿、40.18 亿、34.89 亿元（三年累计约 112 亿元），招股书并提示 2026 年完整财年仍可能录得净亏损 [14][11]。
  • 销量下滑。2026 年上半年交付同比下滑 51.3% 至 27,619 辆；2026 年 6 月交付 7,459 辆 [11][10]。
  • 供应链与制造依赖。制造依托长安汽车，核心技术依赖华为/引望与宁德时代，经营依赖集中 [20]。
  • 华为差异化弱化。华为智驾方案已落地众多品牌，阿维塔曾经的专属技术优势被稀释 [18]。
  • 财务状况。受 115 亿元收购引望影响，现金及现金等价物由 2024 年末的 193.23 亿元降至 2025 年末的 96.87 亿元，流动比率由 1.3 降至 0.6 [14]。
  • 海外与价格竞争。海外扩张面临关税与市场进入成本，国内价格战则使品牌下探至竞争激烈的 20 万元级区间 [11][12]。
