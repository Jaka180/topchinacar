Overview

Arcfox (Jihu, 极狐) is the new-energy-vehicle brand of BAIC Bluepark New Energy Technology Co., Ltd. (600733.SH), the listed new-energy arm of the state-owned Beijing Automotive Group (BAIC). The brand was launched on 3 August 2016 at Sanlitun, Beijing by BAIC BJEV and is operated by Beijing Bluepark Arcfox Automobile Technology Co., Ltd., a wholly-owned subsidiary of BAIC Bluepark [10] [2]. Arcfox was originally positioned as a premium electric brand built with Magna manufacturing and Huawei HI intelligent driving; its first production models, the Alpha T (2020) and Alpha S (2021), were priced in the RMB 240,000-430,000 range [11].

After weak early sales, Arcfox moved down-market from 2023-2024, launching the Alpha T5 and Alpha S5 in the RMB 100,000-150,000 mainstream band, and its volume then expanded sharply: roughly 43,000 units in 2021-2023 combined turned into 81,017 in 2024 (+169.91%) and 163,000 in 2025 (+101%), making it the top own-brand NEV line of BAIC Group [13] [1]. In May 2026 the brand reorganized into three series — Alpha (technology), Beta (value) and Wendao (premium) — spanning roughly RMB 60,000 to 300,000 [17]. In the first nine months of 2026 Arcfox sold 135,475 vehicles (+54.28% YoY) [19].

Arcfox is the volume mainstay of BAIC Bluepark, which runs a two-brand strategy alongside Stelato (享界), the premium Huawei 'smart selection' brand. Arcfox still operates at a loss at the group level: its parent BAIC Bluepark reported an attributable net loss of RMB 4.563 billion for 2025, though the loss is narrowing [2]. On the brand level, Arcfox and Stelato form BAIC Bluepark's dual-brand matrix, with Arcfox driving volume and Stelato driving brand premiumisation [22].

Current operations

Model line-up

As of the research cut-off (2026-10-29), Arcfox is organized into three series. The Alpha series (technology flagship) includes the Alpha T and Alpha S, the Alpha T5 mid-size SUV, the Alpha S5 coupe-sedan, the Alpha S6/T6 and the Alpha T7 (launched 2026-09-16). The Beta series (value/volume) includes the Beta T1 (A0-class, from RMB 62,800) and the Beta S3 (from RMB 59,800, with a CATL battery-swap version). The Wendao series (premium flagship) currently consists of the Wendao V9 large EREV MPV [4] [17]. The Kaola family MPV and its lower-priced Kaola S variant remain in the line-up as scenario-based family vehicles [14]. By price, the brand now spans roughly RMB 59,800 (Beta S3 swap version) to RMB 269,800 (Wendao V9), a repositioning from its earlier RMB 240,000-plus premium focus [25].

By dimensions, the Alpha T is 4,788 mm long with a 2,915 mm wheelbase and the Alpha S is 4,930 mm with a 2,915 mm wheelbase, both mid-size or above; the Alpha T5 (2,845 mm wheelbase) and Alpha S5 (2,900 mm) emphasise 800V fast charging and class-above space; the Kaola is a compact MPV (4,500 mm long, 2,820 mm wheelbase) [5] [14] [12]. It was only after the Alpha T5 (December 2023) and Alpha S5 (June 2024) launches that Arcfox entered the 10,000-units-a-month club [13].

Technology and platforms

Arcfox's technical assets combine several external partners. In manufacturing, the Zhenjiang BAIC Bluepark Magna plant uses Magna's MAFACT production system and Magna's complete-vehicle engineering capability [6]. In intelligent driving, Arcfox was the first partner under Huawei's HI (Huawei Inside) model — the Alpha S HI edition was the world's first production car with Huawei's full-stack HI solution; in December 2025 the Alpha S entered the MIIT's first batch of L3 conditional autonomous-driving market-access approvals, and Arcfox was among the first to obtain a dedicated L3 licence plate [11] [16]. The Alpha T5 and S5 brought 800V fast charging under RMB 200,000, and the S5 uses 5C super-charging; the BE21 platform was reported as China's first new-energy platform to be exported as technology [12] [27]. The Wendao V9 uses the Taiyue platform with Huawei's Snowy Owl EREV system and an integrated intelligent-driving system [18].

Plants and capacity

Arcfox models are produced at the BAIC Bluepark Magna plant in Zhenjiang, Jiangsu, a joint venture registered on 2019-12-26 with registered capital of RMB 1 billion, owned 51% by a BAIC New Energy-affiliated entity and 49% by Magna [31]. Phase one has annual capacity of 150,000 vehicles, with reserved land for a second phase of 300,000 [31]. The 2019 framework agreement had envisaged capacity of up to 180,000 vehicles per year [6].

Domestic position and business mix

Arcfox is the largest own-brand NEV line of BAIC Group. BAIC Bluepark's total 2025 sales were 209,576 vehicles (+84.06%), of which Arcfox accounted for 163,000 (+101%) and Stelato for about 35,800 [33] [23]. At the parent level, whole-vehicle revenue was RMB 26.008 billion in 2025 (+102.43%), or 93.08% of operating revenue; the parent's 2025 gross margin was 0.43% and its whole-vehicle gross margin was -2.35%, while H1 2026 gross margin improved to 4.21% [33] [32]. These parent-level figures are stated here for reference; Arcfox-specific profitability is not disclosed [2].

Overseas business

Arcfox began overseas expansion in 2023, signing cooperation agreements with Japan's Turing on high-level intelligent driving and with UAE's Bin Omeir Holding Group for the UAE and Saudi Arabia markets, with an initial Bin Omeir order of 600 vehicles [7]. By end-2024 the brand had signed nine overseas markets including Costa Rica [35]. Media reported that monthly overseas sales first exceeded 1,000 units in July 2026 [21].

Founders and leadership

Ownership and governance

Arcfox is a brand rather than a separately listed company; it is operated by Beijing Bluepark Arcfox Automobile Technology Co., Ltd., a wholly-owned subsidiary of BAIC Bluepark (600733.SH), and has no separate actual controller beyond its parent's control chain. BAIC Bluepark is a listed subsidiary of the state-owned BAIC Group, whose actual controller is the Beijing Municipal SASAC [2] [1]. The manufacturing entity, BAIC Bluepark Magna Automotive Co., Ltd., is 51% held by a BAIC New Energy-affiliated entity and 49% by Magna [31].

Current key management

  • Liu Guanqiao (刘观桥) — chairman of BAIC Bluepark, the executive who currently oversees Arcfox. A Tsinghua University graduate in business administration, he held sales-management roles at South East Motor, Shanghai Volkswagen and Beijing Hyundai from 2002 and later headed BAIC Group's operations-management department. He became BJEV deputy general manager and BAIC Bluepark deputy manager in July 2024 (leading marketing), was appointed BAIC Bluepark general manager on 18 March 2025, and was elected BAIC Bluepark chairman by the board on 20 March 2026 [29] [30] [34].
  • Song Jun (宋军) — general manager (经理) of BAIC Bluepark, appointed by the board on 20 March 2026; he was previously employee director, executive deputy general manager and chief financial officer of BAIC Bluepark [29] [34].
  • Wang Sha (王莎) — deputy general manager and chief financial officer of BAIC Bluepark, appointed on 23 April 2026 [3].

Recent senior-management changes

Zhang Guofu (张国富) was appointed BAIC Bluepark chairman on 18 March 2025 and left in March 2026 to become chairman of BAIC Motor (北京汽车股份有限公司, 01958.HK) while serving as a BAIC Group deputy general manager [34] [23]. Dai Kangwei (代康伟), a Beijing Institute of Technology PhD in mechanical engineering, was BJEV general manager from April 2021, BAIC Bluepark manager from June 2021, and BAIC Bluepark chairman from July 2024 until stepping down in March 2025 [30] [24]. Fan Jingtao (樊京涛) became BJEV executive deputy general manager in October 2021 and has long led Arcfox brand marketing; media describe him as Arcfox president from 2022 [15]. The brand has seen frequent leadership turnover since launch: the first ARCFOX BU president was Yu Liguo (于立国) at the time of the Alpha T's 2020 launch, and Wang Qiufeng (王秋凤), then BJEV deputy general manager and Arcfox president, departed in November 2022 [11].

Competition

Our judgment: competitive scope. In Arcfox's core RMB 60,000-150,000 mainstream band, its main rivals include BYD (Dolphin, Yuan Plus, Seal), Wuling, Geely Galaxy, Leapmotor and XPeng's MONA; in the premium MPV segment the Wendao V9 competes with the Denza D9, Voyah Dream and Trumpchi M8 [18]. This is an analytical selection, not a sales ranking.

Our judgment: strengths. Three reported facts underpin Arcfox's position: it grew fastest among Chinese pure-EV lines in 2024 (+169.91%) and reached 163,000 units in 2025, giving it real scale [13]; it carries differentiated technical assets — Magna manufacturing, 800V/5C fast charging, Huawei HI history and a first-batch L3 licence [11] [16]; and it is backed by BAIC Group, whose chairman in 2024 called Arcfox the group's 'No.1 project' for self-owned business [13].

Our judgment: weaknesses. Arcfox's volume is concentrated in low-priced models: media reported that in September 2026 the Beta T1 and Beta S3 (from RMB 59,800-62,800) were the only models above 5,000 monthly units, while higher-priced models sold far less [25]. Its historical premium models (Alpha T/S at RMB 240,000-plus) sold weakly — roughly 2,900 vehicles in 2021 — forcing a down-market pivot [13]. It also shares resources with the Stelato brand inside BAIC Bluepark, and its parent remains loss-making, which limits marketing and R&D headroom [21]. Media analysis also notes that running 'Stelato + Arcfox' in parallel under constrained capital, technology, capacity and channel resources carries a risk of diluting core resources [22].

Outlook

Company plans. At a May 2026 results briefing, BAIC Bluepark chairman Liu Guanqiao set a 2026 two-brand sales target of up to 420,000 vehicles, with Arcfox targeting 300,000-320,000 and Stelato 100,000-120,000 [22]. On overseas, he set a 2026 Arcfox overseas sales target of over 10,000 units (about 5% of Arcfox volume) and over 120 overseas outlets, with plans for 6 models / 12 versions for export by 2027 and an overseas share of over 20% in the long run [28]. The group has also stated a BAIC Bluepark '1 million vehicles by 2030' ambition [28]. On product, the company plans to launch Arcfox T5 and S5 battery-swap versions in 2026 and accelerate CATL swap-station build-out [18]. These are disclosed targets, not completed results.

Our judgment. Arcfox's trajectory depends on three premises: sustaining the Beta/Alpha volume ramp, converting the Wendao series into higher-margin sales without a price war, and turning scale into full-year profit at the parent. The main alternative scenario is that the brand remains stuck in the low-priced segment, keeping per-vehicle margins thin and full-year profitability out of reach; the H1 2026 result (Arcfox delivered 80,581 units, +65.88%, while the parent still lost RMB 1.938 billion) illustrates the gap between volume and profit [3] [20]. This is our analysis, not a forecast of financial results.

Risks

  • Persistent losses at the parent. BAIC Bluepark reported an attributable net loss of RMB 4.563 billion in 2025 and RMB 1.938 billion in H1 2026; Arcfox's own contribution to profit is not separately disclosed [2] [3].
  • Brand down-trading. Volume is concentrated in entry-level models from RMB 59,800, which media argue erodes the brand's premium identity built around Huawei HI and Magna [25].
  • Single-brand concentration. Arcfox accounted for about 82.9% of BAIC BJEV's first-nine-months 2026 volume, concentrating the parent's growth in one brand [19].
  • Small overseas scale. Overseas sales only passed 1,000 units in a single month in July 2026, against a full-year target of over 10,000, leaving the export ramp dependent on the Gulf, Southeast Asia and other early markets [21].
  • Frequent management turnover. BAIC Bluepark saw multiple chairman and general-manager changes over 2024-2026 (Dai Kangwei, Zhang Guofu, then Liu Guanqiao), adding execution uncertainty during the brand's expansion [24] [23].
  • Intense price competition. Arcfox's mainstream band overlaps BYD and other scale players, compressing pricing and gross margin [12].
  • Low gross margin. BAIC Bluepark's H1 2026 gross margin was only 4.21%, so per-vehicle margin headroom is thin and scale has not yet translated into profit [3].

概览

极狐（ARCFOX）是北汽蓝谷新能源科技股份有限公司（600733.SH）旗下的新能源汽车品牌，北汽蓝谷为国有北汽集团的新能源上市主体。品牌于 2016 年 8 月 3 日由北汽新能源在北京三里屯发布，运营主体为北汽蓝谷全资子公司北京蓝谷极狐汽车科技有限公司 [10] [2]。极狐最初定位高端纯电，依托麦格纳制造与华为 HI 智能驾驶，首批量产车型阿尔法 T（2020 年）、阿尔法 S（2021 年）定价在 24 万-43 万元区间 [11]。

早期销量低迷后，极狐自 2023-2024 年起下沉，推出 10 万-15 万元主流价位的阿尔法 T5、阿尔法 S5，销量随之放量：2021-2023 三年合计约 4.3 万辆，2024 年达 81,017 辆（同比 +169.91%），2025 年达 16.3 万辆（同比 +101%），成为北汽集团自主新能源销量主力 [13] [1]。2026 年 5 月，极狐将产品线重组为阿尔法（技术）、贝塔（普惠）、问道（旗舰）三大系列，覆盖约 6 万-30 万元区间 [17]。2026 年前 9 个月极狐销量 135,475 辆（同比 +54.28%）[19]。

极狐是北汽蓝谷的销量支柱。北汽蓝谷实行极狐与高端华为「智选车」品牌享界并行的双品牌战略，极狐承担走量、享界承担品牌向上。极狐目前仍处亏损：母公司北汽蓝谷 2025 年归母净亏损 45.63 亿元，但亏损持续收窄 [2]。品牌层面，极狐与享界构成北汽蓝谷的双品牌矩阵，2026 年极狐进一步拆分为阿尔法、贝塔、问道三大系列，覆盖从入门到旗舰的价格带 [22] [9]。

现状

车型谱系

截至研究截止日（2026-10-29），极狐分为三大系列：阿尔法系列（技术旗舰）包括阿尔法 T、阿尔法 S、中型 SUV 阿尔法 T5、轿跑轿车阿尔法 S5、阿尔法 S6/T6，以及 2026-09-16 上市的阿尔法 T7；贝塔系列（普惠走量）包括 A0 级贝塔 T1（6.28 万元起）与贝塔 S3（5.98 万元起，含宁德时代换电版）；问道系列（高端旗舰）目前为大型增程 MPV 问道 V9 [4] [17]。家庭 MPV 考拉及定价更低的考拉 S 仍作为场景化家用车型在售 [14]。就价格而言，极狐已从早年的 24 万元以上高端定位，扩展到约 5.98 万元（贝塔 S3 换电版）至 26.98 万元（问道 V9）的区间 [25]。

从车型尺寸看，阿尔法 T 车长 4788mm、轴距 2915mm，阿尔法 S 车长 4930mm、轴距 2915mm，均定位中型及以上；阿尔法 T5 轴距 2845mm、阿尔法 S5 轴距 2900mm，主打 800V 超充与越级空间；考拉车长 4500mm、轴距 2820mm，为紧凑型 MPV [5] [14] [12]。上市节奏上，2023 年 12 月阿尔法 T5、2024 年 6 月阿尔法 S5 相继上市后，极狐才真正跨入月销万辆区间 [13]。

技术与平台

极狐的技术能力由多家外部伙伴共同构成。制造上，镇江北汽蓝谷麦格纳基地采用麦格纳 MAFACT 生产体系，依托麦格纳的整车工程能力 [6]。智能驾驶上，极狐是华为 HI（Huawei Inside）模式的首个合作伙伴——阿尔法 S HI 版是全球首款搭载华为全栈 HI 方案的量产车；2025 年 12 月阿尔法 S 入选工信部首批 L3 级有条件自动驾驶准入名单，极狐率先获得 L3 专用正式号牌 [11] [16]。阿尔法 T5、S5 率先将 800V 超充带入 20 万元以内，S5 采用 5C 超充；BE21 平台被报道为中国首个实现技术出口的新能源平台 [12] [27]。问道 V9 采用泰岳平台，搭载华为雪鸮增程系统与元境全场景智驾 [18]。

工厂与产能

极狐车型产自江苏镇江的北汽蓝谷麦格纳基地，该公司于 2019-12-26 注册，注册资金 10 亿元，由北汽新能源关联方持股 51%、麦格纳持股 49% [31]。一期年产 15 万辆，预留二期年产 30 万辆用地 [31]。2019 年的框架协议曾规划年产能最高 18 万辆 [6]。

国内市场地位与业务结构

极狐是北汽集团自主新能源销量的主力。北汽蓝谷 2025 年总销量 209,576 辆（同比 +84.06%），其中极狐 16.3 万辆（+101%）、享界约 3.58 万辆 [33] [23]。母公司层面，2025 年整车收入 260.08 亿元（+102.43%），占营业收入的 93.08%；2025 年毛利率 0.43%、整车毛利率 -2.35%，2026 年上半年毛利率改善至 4.21% [33] [32]。上述为母公司口径，极狐自身盈利未单独披露 [2]。

海外业务

极狐自 2023 年起启动出海，与日本 TURING 签署高阶智能驾驶合作意向书，并与阿联酋本奥米尔集团签约开拓阿联酋、沙特市场，本奥米尔确定首批采购 600 辆 [7]。截至 2024 年底已完成哥斯达黎加等 9 个市场的签约 [35]。媒体报道，极狐单月海外销量于 2026 年 7 月才首次突破 1000 辆 [21]。

极狐是品牌而非单独上市公司，运营主体为北汽蓝谷（600733.SH）全资子公司北京蓝谷极狐汽车科技有限公司，除母公司控制链外无单独认定的实际控制人。北汽蓝谷为国有北汽集团旗下上市公司，北汽集团的实际控制人为北京市国资委 [2] [1]。制造主体北汽蓝谷麦格纳汽车有限公司由北汽新能源关联方持股 51%、麦格纳持股 49% [31]。

现任核心管理层

  • 刘观桥——北汽蓝谷董事长，现为极狐业务的实际操盘人。清华大学工商管理专业毕业，2002 年起先后在东南汽车、上海大众、北京现代从事销售管理，后任北汽集团经营与管理部部长。2024 年 7 月任北汽新能源副总经理、北汽蓝谷副经理（主管营销），2025 年 3 月 18 日升任北汽蓝谷经理（总经理），2026 年 3 月 20 日经董事会选举接任北汽蓝谷董事长 [29] [30] [34]。
  • 宋军——北汽蓝谷经理（总经理），2026 年 3 月 20 日经董事会聘任；此前任北汽蓝谷职工董事、常务副经理、财务总监 [29] [34]。
  • 王莎——北汽蓝谷副经理、财务总监，2026 年 4 月 23 日聘任 [3]。

近期高管变动

张国富于 2025 年 3 月 18 日任北汽蓝谷董事长，2026 年 3 月离任，出任北汽股份（北京汽车股份有限公司，01958.HK）董事长，同时担任北汽集团副总经理 [34] [23]。代康伟为北京理工大学机械工程博士，2021 年 4 月起任北汽新能源总经理、2021 年 6 月起任北汽蓝谷经理，2024 年 7 月升任北汽蓝谷董事长，2025 年 3 月卸任 [30] [24]。樊京涛 2021 年 10 月任北汽新能源常务副总经理，长期负责极狐品牌营销，媒体称其自 2022 年起担任极狐总裁 [15]。极狐品牌自创立以来管理层更替频繁：早期（2020 年阿尔法 T 上市时）ARCFOX BU 总裁为于立国；2022 年 11 月时任北汽新能源副总经理、极狐汽车总裁王秋凤离职 [11]。

竞争格局

我们的判断：竞争范围。在极狐主攻的 6 万-15 万元主流价格带，主要对手包括比亚迪（海豚、元 PLUS、海豹）、五菱、吉利银河、零跑与小鹏 MONA；在高端 MPV 领域，问道 V9 对标腾势 D9、岚图梦想家、传祺 M8 [18]。此为分析所选的竞争范围，不是销量排名。

我们的判断：优势。三点已披露事实支撑极狐的行业位置：2024 年增速（+169.91%）居国内纯电品牌前列、2025 年达 16.3 万辆，具备真实规模 [13]；拥有差异化的技术资产——麦格纳制造、800V/5C 超充、华为 HI 合作历史与首批 L3 号牌 [11] [16]；且背靠北汽集团，集团董事长 2024 年称极狐为北汽自主事业「一号工程」[13]。

我们的判断：短板。极狐销量集中于低价车型：媒体统计 2026 年 9 月仅贝塔 T1、贝塔 S3（5.98 万-6.28 万元起）两款月销超 5000 台，更高价位车型销量远低 [25]。早年 24 万元以上的高端车型（阿尔法 T/S）销量低迷——2021 年约 0.29 万辆——迫使品牌下沉 [13]。同时，极狐在母公司内部与享界共享资源，且母公司仍处亏损，营销与研发投入空间受限 [21]。此外，媒体分析认为，「享界+极狐」双品牌在资金、技术、产能与渠道资源有限的情况下并行投入，存在核心资源被分摊稀释的隐忧 [22]。

未来展望

公司公开规划。在 2026 年 5 月业绩说明会上，北汽蓝谷董事长刘观桥提出 2026 年双品牌销量目标最高 42 万辆，其中极狐 30 万-32 万辆、享界 10 万-12 万辆 [22]。海外方面，其设定 2026 年极狐海外销量过万辆（约占极狐总销量 5%）、建成 120 家以上海外网点，计划 2027 年推出 6 款/12 个版型出海，长期海外销量占比目标 20% 以上 [28]。集团层面还提出北汽蓝谷「2030 年百万辆」的愿景 [28]。产品端，公司计划 2026 年推出极狐 T5 换电版、S5 换电版等车型，并加快与宁德时代的换电站建设 [18]。以上为披露的目标与规划，不是已实现的业绩。

我们的判断。极狐的走势依赖三个前提：贝塔/阿尔法系列持续放量、问道系列在避免价格战的前提下转化为更高毛利、母公司将规模转为全年盈利。另一主要情形是品牌长期停留在低价区间，单车毛利微薄、全年盈利难以达成；2026 年上半年极狐交付 80,581 辆（同比 +65.88%），而母公司仍亏损 19.38 亿元，正体现了销量与利润之间的落差 [3] [20]。以上为我们的分析，不构成对财务结果的预测。

风险

  • 母公司持续亏损。北汽蓝谷 2025 年归母净亏损 45.63 亿元、2026 年上半年 19.38 亿元；极狐自身的利润贡献未单独披露 [2] [3]。
  • 品牌下探。销量集中于 5.98 万元起的入门车型，媒体认为这削弱了极狐依托华为 HI 与麦格纳建立的高端形象 [25]。
  • 单一品牌集中。2026 年前 9 个月极狐约占北汽新能源销量的 82.9%，母公司增长高度集中于单一品牌 [19]。
  • 海外规模尚小。极狐单月海外销量 2026 年 7 月才首次突破 1000 辆，全年目标过万辆，出海爬坡依赖海湾、东南亚等早期市场 [21]。
  • 管理层频繁更替。北汽蓝谷 2024-2026 年间历经代康伟、张国富、刘观桥多次董事长/总经理更替，品牌扩张期存在执行不确定性 [24] [23]。
  • 价格竞争激烈。极狐主攻的主流价格带与比亚迪等规模玩家重叠，压缩定价与毛利空间 [12]。
  • 毛利率仍处低位。北汽蓝谷 2026 年上半年综合毛利率仅 4.21%，单车毛利空间有限，规模效应尚未转化为盈利 [3]。
