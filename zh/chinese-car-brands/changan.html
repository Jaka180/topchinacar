Overview

Chongqing Changan Automobile Co., Ltd. (重庆长安汽车股份有限公司) is a Chongqing-headquartered, state-owned automaker controlled by a central SOE. Its predecessor was the Shanghai Foreign Gun Bureau founded in 1862, and the modern joint-stock company was incorporated in 1996, with B-shares listing in November 1996 and A-shares in June 1997 on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange ([6]). It is one of China's "big four" state automotive groups and is listed on the SZSE with A-shares (000625) and B-shares (200625) ([1]). Its business spans whole-vehicle R&D, manufacturing and sales, plus engine R&D and production ([10]).

In 2025 Changan sold 2.913 million vehicles, including 2.468 million under its own brands, 1.11 million NEVs (+51.1%, about 38.1% of total sales) and 637,000 overseas units (+18.9%, about 21.9% of total sales), and reported revenue of RMB164.0 billion with attributable net profit of RMB4.08 billion ([1]). 2025 sales hit a nine-year high and marked a sixth consecutive year of year-on-year growth ([5]). It runs the Changan, Deepal, Avatr and Qiyuan own brands plus the Changan Ford and Changan Mazda joint ventures, and is expanding local production in Thailand and Brazil ([14]). In October 2026 the company changed its top leadership, with Zhu Huarong stepping down as chairman, Zhao Fei taking over and Ye Pei becoming president ([8]).

Current operations

Business structure

In 2025, sales of goods contributed RMB155.74 billion (94.96% of revenue) and services and other income RMB8.26 billion (5.04%) ([1]). Own-brand and JV businesses run side by side: the Changan brand includes the Qiyuan and Kaicene series; Deepal is a controlled subsidiary, Avatr an equity-accounted associate, and Changan Ford and Changan Mazda joint ventures. The JV segment recorded a second consecutive year of growth in 2025 ([1]). In H1 2026 revenue was RMB65.63 billion (−9.71% year on year), split between RMB43.69 billion in China (−27.68%) and RMB21.94 billion overseas (+78.77%) ([26]).

Latest sales and results

The H1 2026 half-year report discloses 1.119 million vehicles sold (−17.44%), of which 414,000 were NEVs (−8.3%) and 455,000 were overseas exports (+51.9%); H1 2026 net profit attributable to the parent was RMB817 million (−64.32%) ([26]). In the first nine months of 2026 cumulative sales were 1,692,666 units (−18.07%), including 709,256 NEVs (−2.07%) and 94,195 overseas units in September alone; the company disclosed aggregate revenue of about RMB174.38 billion corresponding to the cumulative all-scope sales ([27]).

Technology and platforms

Changan's technology stack centres on the BlueCore powertrains, the new BlueCore ultra-hybrid system, the in-house "Jinzhongzhao" battery (advertised as zero-overheat, with solid-state batteries targeted for gradual mass production in 2027), the Tianshu intelligent-driving system, and Deepal's extended-range and battery-electric platforms; Avatr models use Huawei's Qiankun intelligent driving and Harmony cockpit ([16]). Changan has obtained L3 and L4 autonomous-driving test licences, and its self-developed Tianshu model passed the Cyberspace Administration's generative-AI service filing ([33]); the Tianshu pilot-assist system has logged more than 1.2 billion km of assisted driving ([15]). In hybrid and e-drive, its WE20TG-AA dedicated hybrid engine (co-developed with Dongan Power) has produced its first prototype, the Taihang distributed e-drive is in mass production, and the Jinzhongzhao battery won a 2025 National Science and Technology Progress Award ([33]). During the 14th Five-Year Plan period Changan launched 24 NEV models and 16 energy-saving fuel models ([16]). At end-2025 it held 12,683 valid patents, of which 4,879 were invention patents ([23]).

R&D

At end-2025 Changan reported 12,417 R&D staff (21.3% of employees), including 8,440 with bachelor's, 3,695 with master's and 144 with doctoral degrees. FY2025 R&D input was RMB12.576 billion, or 7.67% of revenue; this includes capitalized development expenditure ([1], pp. 20–21). Separately, the group's website disclosed a global R&D team of 24,000 on 21 April 2026 ([42]).

Plants and capacity

Domestic production is anchored by the Chongqing Yuzui complex, among other bases ([14]). The first overseas NEV plant, in Rayong, Thailand, started production on 16 May 2025 with the right-hand-drive Deepal S05; it has welding, painting, final-assembly, engine and battery-assembly shops with about 90% automation at core quality-control stations, phase-one annual capacity of 100,000 units (up to 200,000 at maximum), and had produced over 20,000 vehicles by June 2026 ([14]). The Brazil plant in Anápolis, Goiás, started production on 26 March 2026 with the locally made UNI-T; with total investment of USD1.52 billion and planned annual capacity of 90,000 units, it equips all models with BlueCore dual-fuel (petrol/ethanol) engines ([14], [16]).

Overseas business

Overseas sales reached 637,000 units in 2025 (+18.9%, 21.9% of total) ([1]). For FY2025, gross margin by geography was 19.49% overseas and 14.54% in China ([1], p. 17). Its overseas sales network expanded from 63 countries/regions in 2020 to 118, with on-sale models growing from 17 fuel cars in 2020 to 41 (25 fuel, 16 NEV) ([16]). In March 2026 monthly overseas sales exceeded 100,000 for the first time, at 103,900 units, up 60% month on month ([41]). In December 2025 Changan produced its 30-millionth China-brand vehicle, the first Chinese automaker to reach that milestone ([16]).

Founders and leadership

Origin

Changan has no individual founder in the modern sense. Its history begins with the Shanghai Foreign Gun Bureau, set up in 1862 under the Westernization Movement initiated by Li Hongzhang, and the current company is a state-owned enterprise restructured and listed in the 1990s ([6]).

Current management

  • Zhao Fei — chairman and legal representative, elected on 16 October 2026; also party secretary of the listed company and deputy party secretary, director and general manager of China Changan Automobile Group. Born in 1974, from Liaoning, with a master's degree and a senior-engineer qualification. His prior roles included Changan assistant president, head of strategic planning, vice president and executive vice president; party secretary and executive vice president of Changan Ford; and president and chairman of the former China Changan group (now Chenzhi Group). He was appointed Changan president on 12 December 2025 and ceased holding that post upon becoming chairman ([40], [35]).
  • Ye Pei — president, appointed on 16 October 2026. Born in 1975, from Shandong, with a master's degree and an engineer qualification. Previous positions included general manager of Nanjing Changan, Changan assistant president, head of strategic planning, head of the sedan sales division, vice president and head of passenger-car marketing, and Senior Executive Vice President ([40]).
  • Yang Dayong — Senior Executive Vice President, appointed on 16 October 2026; previously Executive Vice President ([40]).
  • Deng Chenghao — Executive Vice President, appointed on 16 October 2026; also party secretary and chairman of Deepal ([40]).
  • Zhu Huarong — party secretary and chairman of China Changan Automobile Group. He resigned as chairman, director and legal representative of the listed company on 16 October 2026 ([40]).

Shareholding and governance

As of 30 June 2026, Chenzhi Auto Technology Group held 1,783,090,143 shares (17.99%), China Changan Automobile Group 1,410,747,155 shares (14.23%), Southern Industrial Asset Management 456,253,257 shares (4.60%), and Zhonghui Futong Investment 285,405,718 shares (2.88%). The half-year report identifies indirect controlling shareholder China Changan Automobile Group, its wholly owned subsidiary Chenzhi Group, and Chenzhi's wholly owned subsidiary Zhonghui Futong as parties acting in concert ([26], p. 30). The State Council SASAC is the actual controller ([1]).

Competition

The 2025 annual report lists Changan, Deepal and Avatr as the three main brands, with Gravity, Qiyuan and Kaicene as series under Changan. Products include the CS, Eado and UNI lines, alongside NEVs from Qiyuan, Deepal, Avatr and Kaicene ([1]).

Our judgment: competitive scope and strengths. This product mix spans mainstream family cars, technology-oriented NEVs, intelligent luxury vehicles and commercial vehicles. BYD, Geely, Chery, Great Wall Motor, SAIC, Seres (AITO) and Xiaomi provide comparisons across different segments. Maintaining both fuel and NEV products can diversify sales fluctuations associated with one powertrain route. Overseas business is another source of growth: in H1 2026 overseas revenue rose 78.77% year on year and overseas gross margin was 20.13%. FY2025 overseas gross margin was 19.49%; the two reporting periods should be assessed separately ([26], p. 11; [1], p. 17).

FY2025 profit attributable to the parent was RMB4.07522 billion, down 44.34% year on year; attributable profit excluding non-recurring items was RMB2.79502 billion. Non-recurring items totalled RMB1.2801979 billion, including RMB559.4894 million of government grants recognized in profit or loss and classified as non-recurring. Under the report's definition, that grant category excludes policy-compliant grants closely related to normal operations with a continuing effect on earnings ([1], pp. 6–7). In 2024, associate Avatr reported a net loss of RMB4.02 billion and controlled subsidiary Deepal a net loss of RMB1.57 billion. Avatr is accounted for using the equity method, while Deepal is consolidated ([2], pp. 193–195; [1], definitions and financial-statement notes).

Our judgment: transition and profitability constraints. NEV sales of 709,256 units accounted for approximately 41.9% of total sales of 1,692,666 in the first nine months of 2026, calculated from the disclosed volumes ([27]). The remainder is still substantial, requiring allocation of resources across established and NEV products. FY2025 non-recurring items represented about 31.4% of attributable profit, calculated from the annual-report figures above, so recurring profitability also warrants attention. Avatr's and Deepal's historical losses indicate a need to assess profitability alongside volume growth, but their 2024 losses do not establish their profitability as of the research date. Their consolidation scope and earnings-recognition methods also differ ([1], [2]).

Outlook

Company targets. For 2026 Changan targets 3.3 million total sales, 1.4 million NEVs and 750,000 overseas units ([21]). After the October 2026 management reshuffle the company's stated medium-term goal is total sales of 5 million units by 2030, of which 2 million overseas, and 6 million units by 2035, to rank among the world's top ten automakers ([30]). Earlier guidance had put the 2030 overseas target at 1.5 million units ([23]). The 2025 annual report, disclosed on 11 April 2026, sets a 2026 capital expenditure plan of RMB14.47 billion: RMB4.47 billion of fixed-asset investment and RMB10.0 billion of long-term equity investment, including funds. Fixed-asset priorities include R&D testing capabilities, NEV products and capacity, digitalization and overseas business ([1], p. 28). New president Ye Pei set a 2027 sprint target of more than 3 million global sales, to be delivered through overseas growth, domestic quality, platform efficiency, AI-empowered systems and risk control ([30]).

Our judgment. Achieving the 2026 target depends on improvement in both overseas business and domestic sales: in the first nine months of 2026 cumulative sales were 1,692,666 units, down 18.07% year on year, or 51.3% of the 3.3 million target, so hitting the target requires a very strong fourth quarter ([27]). The overseas path is the more credible engine, with H1 2026 overseas revenue up 78.77% ([26]). An alternative scenario is slower growth caused by overseas trade barriers, exchange rates or demand fluctuations, which the annual report identifies as overseas-market risks. Commercializing technology projects such as solid-state batteries also requires continued investment and validation ([1], pp. 20, 28). On milestones, media reported that in September 2026 Qiyuan's cumulative sales passed one million units and Deepal rolled out its one-millionth vehicle, while September NEV sales reached 119,800 units (+15.64%) ([34], [27]). Associate Avatr first filed for a Hong Kong IPO on 27 November 2025 and updated its prospectus on 30 June 2026 ([29]). The company is also integrating the back offices of Avatr and Deepal — keeping brand front-ends separate while consolidating manufacturing, R&D platforms, core technology and procurement ([30]). The above is our analysis and is not a share-price forecast.

Risks

  • Earnings changes. FY2025 gross margin was 15.54%, and attributable net profit fell 44.34% year on year ([1]).
  • Lower China-region revenue and total sales. H1 2026 China-region revenue fell 27.68% year on year ([26]); total sales fell 18.07% year on year in the first nine months ([27]).
  • Historical NEV business losses. In 2024 associate Avatr reported a net loss of RMB4.02 billion and controlled subsidiary Deepal a net loss of RMB1.57 billion ([2]).
  • Company-disclosed overseas risks. The 2025 annual report identifies tariffs and technical trade barriers, regional conflicts, overseas demand fluctuations and exchange-rate volatility as risks ([1]).
  • Operating cash flow. H1 2026 net operating cash flow was −RMB11.413 billion, which the company attributed mainly to lower collections of sales proceeds falling due ([26]).
  • Non-recurring earnings components. FY2025 non-recurring items totalled RMB1.2801979 billion, including RMB559.4894 million of government grants recognized in profit or loss and classified as non-recurring; this category excludes policy-compliant grants closely related to normal operations with a continuing effect on earnings ([1]).

概览

重庆长安汽车股份有限公司是一家总部位于重庆、由央企控股的国有整车企业，前身可追溯至 1862 年创办的上海洋炮局；现代股份公司于 1996 年设立，1996 年 11 月 B 股、1997 年 6 月 A 股先后在深圳证券交易所上市（[6]）。公司是中国汽车「四大集团」之一，股票为深交所 A 股（000625）与 B 股（200625）（[1]）。业务覆盖整车研发、制造、销售以及发动机的研发、生产（[10]）。

2025 年长安汽车销售汽车 291.3 万辆，其中自主品牌 246.82 万辆、新能源 111.0 万辆（+51.1%，占公司总销量约 38.1%）、海外 63.7 万辆（+18.9%，占公司总销量约 21.9%）；实现营业收入 1640.00 亿元、归母净利润 40.75 亿元（[1]）。2025 年全年销量创近九年来新高、连续六年同比正增长（[5]）。公司经营长安、深蓝、阿维塔、启源等自主品牌及长安福特、长安马自达合资企业，并在泰国、巴西推进本地化生产（[14]）。2026 年 10 月公司完成换帅：朱华荣卸任董事长，赵非接任、叶沛任总裁（[8]）。

现状

业务结构

2025 年销售商品收入 1557.39 亿元，占营业收入 94.96%；提供劳务及其他收入 82.61 亿元，占 5.04%（[1]）。自主与合资并行：长安品牌下设启源、凯程等序列，深蓝为控股子公司，阿维塔为按权益法核算的联营企业，长安福特及长安马自达为合营企业；合资板块 2025 年实现连续两年正增长（[1]）。2026 年上半年营收 656.34 亿元（同比 −9.71%），其中中国区 436.92 亿元（−27.68%）、境外 219.42 亿元（+78.77%）（[26]）。

最新销量与业绩

2026 年半年报披露，上半年实现销量 111.9 万辆（−17.44%），其中新能源 41.4 万辆（−8.3%）、海外出口 45.5 万辆（+51.9%）；上半年归母净利润 8.17 亿元（−64.32%）（[26]）。2026 年前三季度累计销量 169.27 万辆（−18.07%），其中新能源 70.93 万辆（−2.07%），9 月单月海外销量 9.42 万辆；公司披露前三季度累计销量全口径对应的汇总收入约 1743.79 亿元（[27]）。

技术与平台

技术体系以蓝鲸动力、新蓝鲸超擎混动、自研「金钟罩」电池（宣传零过热，全固态电池计划 2027 年逐步量产）、天枢智能驾驶系统，以及深蓝的增程/纯电平台为核心；阿维塔车型采用华为乾崑智驾与鸿蒙座舱（[16]）。公司已获 L3、L4 级自动驾驶测试牌照，全栈自研的天枢大模型通过国家网信办生成式人工智能服务备案（[33]）；天枢领航辅助驾驶系统累计安全护航超 12 亿公里（[15]）。混动与电驱方面，公司与东安动力联合开发的 WE20TG-AA 混动专用发动机首台样机已下线，太行分布式电驱技术进入量产，金钟罩电池获 2025 年度国家科技进步二等奖（[33]）。「十四五」期间公司累计推出 24 款新能源车型、16 款节能燃油车型（[16]）。截至 2025 年末公司拥有有效专利 12683 件，其中发明专利 4879 件（[23]）。

研发

截至 2025 年末，公司研发人员 12,417 人，占员工 21.3%，其中本科 8,440 人、硕士 3,695 人、博士 144 人。2025 财年研发投入 125.76 亿元，占营业收入 7.67%，包含资本化开发支出（[1]，第 20–21 页）。另据集团官网 2026 年 4 月 21 日披露，集团全球研发团队为 2.4 万人（[42]）。

工厂与产能

国内生产以重庆鱼嘴基地等整车基地为依托（[14]）。首个海外新能源整车基地泰国罗勇工厂于 2025 年 5 月 16 日投产，首款车型为深蓝 S05 右舵版，设焊接、涂装、总装、发动机及电池装配五大车间、核心质量管控工位自动化率约 90%，一期年产能 10 万辆（最大可提升至 20 万辆），截至 2026 年 6 月产量已突破 2 万辆（[14]）。巴西戈亚斯州阿纳波利斯工厂于 2026 年 3 月 26 日投产，首款本土制造车型为 UNI-T，总投资 15.2 亿美元、规划年产能 9 万辆，全系搭载可兼容汽油与乙醇的蓝鲸双燃料发动机（[14]、[16]）。

海外业务

2025 年海外销量 63.7 万辆（+18.9%，占公司总销量 21.9%）（[1]）。2025 全年按地区披露的境外毛利率为 19.49%，中国地区为 14.54%（[1]，第 17 页）。海外销售网络从 2020 年的 63 个国家和地区扩展至 118 个，在售车型从 2020 年的 17 款燃油车增至 41 款（燃油 25 款、新能源 16 款）（[16]）。2026 年 3 月海外单月销量首次突破 10 万辆，达 10.39 万辆，环比增长 60%（[41]）。2025 年 12 月长安第 3000 万辆中国品牌汽车下线，成为率先达成该数字的中国车企（[16]）。

竞争格局

2025 年年报列示长安、深蓝、阿维塔三大品牌，其中长安品牌下设引力、启源、凯程三大序列；产品包括 CS、逸动、UNI 系列，以及启源、深蓝、阿维塔及凯程的新能源车型（[1]）。

我们的判断：竞争范围与优势。从上述产品组合看，长安覆盖主流家用、科技新能源、智能豪华及商用车等细分市场；可将比亚迪、吉利、奇瑞、长城、上汽，以及赛力斯（问界）、小米作为不同细分市场的比较对象。燃油与新能源产品并行有助于分散单一路线的销量波动。海外业务也是一个增长支点：2026 年上半年境外收入同比增长 78.77%，同期境外毛利率为 20.13%；2025 全年境外毛利率为 19.49%，两个期间应分别比较（[26]，第 11 页；[1]，第 17 页）。

2025 年归母净利润 40.7522 亿元，同比下降 44.34%；扣非归母净利润 27.9502 亿元。非经常性损益合计 12.801979 亿元，其中列入非经常性损益的计入当期损益政府补助为 5.594894 亿元，该项按年报定义排除了与正常经营密切相关、符合政策且持续影响损益的政府补助（[1]，第 6–7 页）。2024 年，联营企业阿维塔净亏损 40.18 亿元，控股子公司深蓝净亏损 15.72 亿元；阿维塔按权益法核算，深蓝纳入合并范围（[2]，第 193–195 页；[1]，释义及财务报表附注）。

我们的判断：转型与盈利约束。2026 年前三季度新能源销量 709,256 辆，占总销量 1,692,666 辆的约 41.9%（按披露销量计算）（[27]）。其余销量仍占较大比重，转型需要兼顾原有产品和新能源产品的资源配置。2025 年非经常性损益约占归母净利润 31.4%（按上述年报数值计算），评估持续盈利能力还需观察扣非利润。阿维塔和深蓝的历史亏损说明规模增长需要与盈利改善同时考察，但 2024 年亏损不能代表其截至研究日的盈利状态；两者的合并范围与损益确认方法也不同（[1]、[2]）。

未来展望

公司公开目标。2026 年长安汽车目标销量 330 万辆，其中新能源 140 万辆、海外 75 万辆（[21]）。2026 年 10 月管理层调整后，公司公开口径为：2030 年整车目标 500 万辆，其中海外 200 万辆；2035 年销量 600 万辆、跻身全球车企前十（[30]）。此前的口径是 2030 年海外 150 万辆（[23]）。2025 年年报（2026 年 4 月 11 日披露）列示的 2026 年资本开支计划为 144.7 亿元，其中固定资产投资 44.7 亿元、长期股权投资（含基金）100.0 亿元；固定资产投资方向包括研发试验能力、新能源产品及产能、数字化和海外业务（[1]，第 28 页）。新任总裁叶沛提出 2027 年全球销量 300 万辆以上的冲刺目标，并称将抓好海外增量、国内提质、平台高效、体系 AI 化、防控风险五方面工作（[30]）。

我们的判断。2026 年目标能否达成，取决于海外业务与国内销量的共同改善：2026 年前三季度累计销量 169.27 万辆、同比下滑 18.07%，为 330 万辆目标的 51.3%，要达成目标需要四季度大幅放量（[27]）。海外是更可信的引擎——2026 年上半年境外收入同比增长 78.77%（[26]）。另一种可能情形是海外贸易壁垒、汇率或需求波动拖慢增长；年报将这些因素列为海外市场风险。固态电池等技术项目的商业化也需要持续投入和验证（[1]，第 20、28 页）。里程碑方面，媒体报道 2026 年 9 月启源累计销量突破 100 万辆、深蓝迎来第 100 万辆整车下线，9 月新能源销量 11.98 万辆、同比增长 15.64%（[34]、[27]）。联营企业阿维塔已于 2025 年 11 月 27 日首次递表港交所、2026 年 6 月 30 日更新招股书（[29]）。公司还正推进阿维塔与深蓝的后台整合——品牌前端保持区隔，后台的制造、研发平台、核心技术及采购资源开展内部整合（[30]）。以上为我们的判断，不构成股价预测。

风险

  • 盈利变化。2025 年毛利率 15.54%，归母净利润同比下降 44.34%（[1]）。
  • 国内收入与总销量下降。2026 年上半年中国地区收入同比下降 27.68%（[26]）；前三季度总销量同比下降 18.07%（[27]）。
  • 新能源业务历史亏损。2024 年联营企业阿维塔净亏损 40.18 亿元，控股子公司深蓝净亏损 15.72 亿元（[2]）。
  • 公司披露的海外风险。2025 年年报列示关税及技术性贸易壁垒、区域冲突、海外需求波动和汇率波动等风险（[1]）。
  • 经营现金流。2026 年上半年经营活动现金流量净额为 −114.13 亿元，公司称主要因销售到期收款减少（[26]）。
  • 非经常性损益构成。2025 年非经常性损益合计 12.801979 亿元，其中列入非经常性损益的计入当期损益政府补助为 5.594894 亿元；该类别排除了与正常经营密切相关、符合政策且持续影响损益的政府补助（[1]）。
