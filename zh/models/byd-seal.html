Seal is BYD's global mid-size electric sedan and the brand's most direct answer to the Tesla Model 3. It matters because BYD uses Seal to prove that its Blade Battery, CTB body integration and Ocean-series design can compete in mainstream export markets, not only in China. Specifications vary sharply between entry rear-drive cars and the all-wheel-drive Performance version, so range, acceleration and price should be read as a trim range rather than one combined configuration.海豹是比亚迪面向全球市场的中型纯电轿车，也是比亚迪对标 Tesla Model 3 最直接的车型。它的重要性在于，比亚迪用海豹证明刀片电池、CTB 电池车身一体化和海洋网设计语言不仅能在中国市场成立，也能进入主流出口市场。入门后驱版与四驱性能版的续航、加速和价格差异很大，因此本页参数按配置区间理解，而不是单一车型配置。
