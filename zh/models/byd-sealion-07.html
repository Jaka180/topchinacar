Sealion 07 is BYD's Ocean-series coupe SUV for the Model Y class. It sits above Atto 3 in size and positioning, and is important because it shows BYD moving from price-led export growth into more design- and performance-led segments. The fastest acceleration figure and the entry price belong to different trims, so this page presents the main China-market specification band.海狮 07 是比亚迪海洋网面向 Model Y 级别的轿跑 SUV，尺寸和定位高于 Atto 3。它的重要性在于，比亚迪正在从价格驱动的出口增长，进入更强调设计、性能与中高端定位的细分市场。最快零百加速与入门价格来自不同配置，因此本页以中国市场主要配置区间展示。

Performance acceleration belongs to AWD trims; entry pricing is lower-output RWD.高性能加速对应四驱配置；入门价为较低功率后驱配置。
