Chinese Cars in Saudi Arabia沙特阿拉伯：中国汽车市场观察

The Gulf's largest market

Saudi Arabia is the largest car market in the Gulf and a priority target for Chinese brands. In H1 2026 the market fell about 23.5% to roughly 313,000 new vehicles (down 26.9% to about 399,000 over January–August), with Toyota still dominant at about 28.8% share (up from 26.7% a year earlier). A same-basis H1 total for Chinese brands is not available. In the H1 2026 national light-vehicle ranking, Haval (up 48%, #8) and Jetour (up 22.6%, #10) were the only Chinese brands in the top ten — both gainers in a falling market — while BYD placed #18 with about 1% share (roughly 3,100 units).

Models and imports

Best-sellers skew to SUVs and sedans: the Changan CS35/CS75 and Eado, MG RX5/ZS/5, Geely Coolray, Emgrand and Okavango, Jetour T1/T2 and Dashing, Haval H6 and Jolion, and BYD's Atto 3, Song and Han. Imports carry a 5% customs duty plus 15% VAT — an effective cost of roughly 20% of value — and vehicles must be manufactured within five years of import, with SASO conformity certification handled through the FASAH platform.

Local production and Vision 2030

Manufacturing is a national ambition under Vision 2030, anchored by the King Salman Automotive Cluster in King Abdullah Economic City (KAEC). The confirmed plants are largely non-Chinese: Lucid (PIF-backed, kit assembly near Jeddah with full manufacturing targeted for end-2026), Hyundai (KAEC, around 50,000 units a year) and Saudi's own Ceer EV brand — a PIF–Foxconn joint venture whose KAEC complex now schedules regular production for the first quarter of 2027, with first deliveries no earlier than March 2027. BYD has held high-level talks with Saudi officials on local production (January 2026) but as of October 2026 has not announced a firm investment. In the battery chain, CATL opened the Middle East's first NING SERVICE aftermarket centre in Riyadh in January 2026, but no Saudi cell-gigafactory investment has been confirmed. EV penetration is still low but is a state priority, backed by the PIF through Lucid, Ceer and the EVIQ charging network.

海湾最大市场

沙特是海湾最大的汽车市场，也是中国品牌的优先目标。2026 上半年市场同比下降约 23.5% 至约 31.3 万辆（1–8 月累计约 39.9 万辆、同比 -26.9%），丰田仍以约 28.8% 份额主导（上年同期 26.7%）。与上述全国轻型车榜同一口径的中国品牌上半年合计，暂无数据。在 2026 上半年全国轻型车榜上，哈弗（第 8，+48%）与捷途（第 10，+22.6%）是唯二进入前十的中国品牌——在整体下滑的市场中逆势增长——比亚迪则位列第 18、份额约 1%（约 3,100 辆）。

车型与进口

畅销车型以 SUV 和轿车为主：长安 CS35/CS75 与 Eado、MG RX5/ZS/5、吉利 Coolray/Emgrand/Okavango、捷途 T1/T2 与 Dashing、哈弗 H6 与 Jolion，以及比亚迪 Atto 3、Song、Han。进口车需缴 5% 关税加 15% 增值税——综合成本约为车值的 20%——且车辆须在进口年份前五年内生产，SASO 合规认证通过 FASAH 平台办理。

本地生产与 2030 愿景

制造是 2030 愿景下的国家雄心，以阿卜杜拉国王经济城（KAEC）"萨勒曼国王汽车集群"为核心载体。已确认的工厂多为非中国企业：Lucid（PIF 支持，吉达附近组件组装，完整制造目标为 2026 年底）、现代（KAEC，年产约 5 万辆）以及沙特自有的 Ceer 电动品牌——PIF 与富士康的合资公司，其 KAEC 工厂的常规生产计划为 2027 年一季度，首批交付不早于 2027 年 3 月。比亚迪已与沙特官员就本地生产举行高层会谈（2026 年 1 月），但截至 2026 年 10 月尚未公布正式投资。电池环节，宁德时代（CATL）于 2026 年 1 月在利雅得启用中东首座 NING SERVICE 售后中心，但尚未确认在沙特的电芯超级工厂投资。电动车渗透率仍低，但属国家优先事项，由 PIF 通过 Lucid、Ceer 及 EVIQ 充电网络支持。
