Mexico: the tariff turning point

Latin America adopted Chinese brands early and is now a localisation frontier. Mexico is the largest market and the region's biggest inflection point. Under US pressure, Mexico raised the duty on cars from non-FTA countries — mainly China — to 50% from January 2026. Imports fell sharply, but Chinese brands kept growing on stockpiled inventory: H1 2026 sales reached about 137,500 units (up 27.7%) for a 17% share. BYD shelved its planned Mexican assembly plant in response to the tariff.

The open Pacific markets

Chile has the region's highest Chinese penetration: Chinese brands captured about 35% of the market in early 2026, with MG leading and BYD prominent in EVs. Colombia's market grew strongly in 2025, with BYD entering the top ten and taking more than half of EV and PHEV sales. Peru is being reshaped by the Chinese-built Port of Chancay, which is halving shipping times and turning the country into a regional distribution hub, with BYD driving an EV and hybrid surge.

Argentina and the EV opening

Argentina opened a duty-free import quota for electrified vehicles — up to 50,000 units a year exempt from the 35% extra-zone tariff for vehicles with an FOB value up to $16,000, running five years. Imports of electrified vehicles surged (up 165% in H1 2026), and Chinese brands took about 10.7% of the market in June 2026, with BYD reaching #8 overall — its best result — and Chery, BAIC, Haval, MG and Changan all posting triple-digit growth. Across the region, most Chinese volume is still ICE and hybrid SUVs, but BYD leads EVs in several markets. The dividing line is policy: Mexico's 50% wall stands in contrast to the open, low-tariff markets of Chile, Peru and Colombia and Argentina's EV quotas.

墨西哥：关税转折点

拉美很早就接纳了中国品牌，如今正成为本地化前线。墨西哥是最大市场，也是区域最大的转折点。在美国压力下，墨西哥自 2026 年 1 月起将非自贸协定国家（主要是中国）整车关税上调至 50%。进口量骤降，但中国品牌靠库存继续增长：2026 上半年销量约 13.75 万辆（+27.7%）、份额 17%。比亚迪据此搁置了原定的墨西哥组装厂计划。

开放的太平洋沿岸市场

智利的中国品牌渗透率为区域最高：2026 年初中国品牌约占市场 35%，MG 领跑，比亚迪在电动车中表现突出。哥伦比亚市场 2025 年强劲增长，比亚迪进入前十并拿下电动与插混销量的过半份额。秘鲁因中国承建的钱凯港（Chancay）而重塑——航运时间缩短一半，使该国成为区域分销枢纽，比亚迪带动电动与混动车激增。

阿根廷与电动化开口

阿根廷出台电动化车辆免税进口配额——离岸价不超 1.6 万美元的车型每年最多 5 万辆免征 35% 区域外关税，为期五年。电动化车辆进口激增（2026 上半年 +165%），2026 年 6 月中国品牌约占市场 10.7%，比亚迪升至整体第 8（历史最佳），奇瑞、北汽、哈弗、MG、长安均为三位数增长。整个区域，中国销量主体仍是燃油与混动 SUV，但比亚迪在多个市场领跑电动车。分水岭在于政策：墨西哥 50% 的高墙，与智利、秘鲁、哥伦比亚的开放低关税市场及阿根廷的电动车配额形成鲜明对比。
