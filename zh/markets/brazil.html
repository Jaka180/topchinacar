Chinese Cars in Brazil巴西：中国汽车市场观察

Fourth in the year-to-date ranking

Brazil is Latin America's biggest market and the region's manufacturing prize. In July 2026 Chinese brands took a record 22.8% of the passenger market (about 60,500 units), and BYD's rise has been rapid: roughly 112,900 units in 2025, with 37,637 in Q1 2026 alone (up 73.7%, making it the country's fifth-largest brand). In April 2026 BYD became the first Chinese automaker to top Brazil's overall monthly retail brand chart. Through September 2026, Fiat was still first with 421,999 vehicles (19.6% share) and BYD was fourth with 169,135 (7.8%) (AutoData, from Fenabrave). BYD also leads the EV segment with well over half of it, and its Dolphin Mini became Brazil's #1 retail model in February 2026. GWM and Caoa Chery are the other major players.

Local production comes online

Local production is now the main event. BYD's Camaçari complex in Bahia — a roughly $1 billion conversion of a former Ford plant — officially inaugurated its first phase in October 2025 and produced its 100,000th vehicle in July 2026, at 150,000 units a year of capacity, planned to double. Locally built models include the Dolphin Mini and the Song Pro DM-i flex hybrid. GWM's Iracemápolis plant in São Paulo state runs at about 50,000 units a year and builds the Haval H6 hybrid. New entrants are following: Geely began producing the EX5 EM-i at Renault's São José dos Pinhais plant (first locally built unit rolled off in September 2026), MG announced SKD production at Pecém in Ceará, and GAC plans to begin construction in Q4 2026.

The tariff clock

Local production is a direct response to a rising tariff schedule. On 1 July 2026 the import duty on BEVs, PHEVs and hybrids reached the 35% Mercosur cap — completing a phase-in begun in January 2024 (BEVs went 10% → 18% → 25% → 35%). CKD kits stay at 14% until January 2027 (then 35%), while SKD kits already face 35%; a temporary window lets EV CKD/SKD kits enter duty-free in H2 2026 up to a $463 million quota. Chinese brands front-loaded imports ahead of the deadline — China exported over 180,000 vehicles to Brazil in January–July 2026 (+105.4%), with China-origin vehicles accounting for 52.4% of Brazilian imports — and electrified vehicles reached a record 23.5% of Brazilian sales in July 2026.

年初至今排第四

巴西是拉美最大市场，也是区域制造高地。2026 年 7 月中国品牌以约 22.8% 的乘用车份额（约 6.05 万辆）创下新高，而比亚迪增长迅速：2025 年约 11.29 万辆，仅 2026 年一季度就售出 37,637 辆（同比 +73.7%，成为巴西第五大品牌）。2026 年 4 月，比亚迪成为首个登顶巴西整体月度零售品牌榜的中国车企。到 2026 年 9 月累计，菲亚特仍是第 1（421,999 辆，份额 19.6%），比亚迪是第 4（169,135 辆，份额 7.8%）（AutoData，依据 Fenabrave 上牌）。比亚迪还以过半份额领跑电动车市场，其 Dolphin Mini 于 2026 年 2 月成为巴西零售第一车型。长城与 Caoa 奇瑞是另外两大玩家。

本地生产投产

本地生产已成为主战场。比亚迪位于巴伊亚州的 Camaçari 基地——由前福特工厂改造，投资约 10 亿美元——第一期于 2025 年 10 月正式揭幕，2026 年 7 月下线第 10 万辆车，年产能 15 万辆并计划翻倍，本地生产车型包括 Dolphin Mini 与 Song Pro DM-i flex 混动。长城圣保罗州 Iracemápolis 工厂年产能约 5 万辆，生产哈弗 H6 混动。新进入者紧随其后：吉利已在雷诺 São José dos Pinhais 工厂投产 EX5 EM-i（首台本地车于 2026 年 9 月下线），MG 宣布在塞阿拉州 Pecém 进行 SKD 组装，广汽计划 2026 年四季度开工建设。

关税倒计时

本地生产是对关税上行时间表的直接回应。2026 年 7 月 1 日，纯电、插混与混动整车进口关税达到南方共同市场 35% 上限——完成 2024 年 1 月启动的分阶段加征（纯电 10% → 18% → 25% → 35%）。CKD 散件维持 14% 至 2027 年 1 月（此后 35%），SKD 散件已为 35%；另设临时窗口，2026 下半年电动车 CKD/SKD 套件在 4.63 亿美元配额内免税进口。中国品牌抢在节点前集中抢运——2026 年 1–7 月中国对巴西出口整车逾 18 万辆（+105.4%），来自中国的车辆占巴西进口量的 52.4%——电动化车型 2026 年 7 月占巴西销量 23.5%，创历史新高。
