Chinese Cars in Europe欧洲：中国汽车市场观察

Europe is the highest-stakes market for Chinese automakers: big EV demand, strong incumbent brands, and anti-subsidy tariffs that have pushed BYD, Chery, Leapmotor and others toward local production in Hungary, Spain and beyond.欧洲是中国车企风险与回报最高的市场：电动车需求大、本土品牌强势，反补贴关税正把比亚迪、奇瑞、零跑等推向匈牙利、西班牙等地的本地化生产。

Scale and momentum

Europe remains the highest-stakes market for Chinese automakers. In full-year 2025, Chinese brands sold roughly 811,000 vehicles across Europe — up about 99% year on year — for close to a 6.1% share, according to JATO Dynamics. Momentum accelerated through 2026: over the first half of 2026, Chinese brands held about 9.5% of combined EU, UK and EFTA registrations, up from 0.5% in 2021 (Dataforce), with the June monthly figure hitting a record 10.9%. BYD and Chery each reached about 2.4% share and MG about 2.5%. UBS estimated in July 2026 that Chinese brands' monthly share of the European market had reached roughly 11%, up from about 4% a year earlier. By Q3 2026, Dataforce put Chinese brands at 12.7% of European registrations — ahead of Japanese brands (11.9%) for the first time — with September reaching a record 14.5%, up about 3 percentage points from August (Bloomberg via Dataforce).

Who is winning

BYD overtook MG (SAIC) to become the volume leader: across January–September 2026, BYD registered 283,997 vehicles in the EU, UK and EFTA, ahead of SAIC at 272,251 and Chery at 260,650 (ACEA via Electrive). In the first four months of 2026, ACEA figures show BYD's EU registrations more than doubled (+152.9%) to over 71,800, Chery grew 267.1% to over 48,300, and Leapmotor (via its Stellantis joint venture) soared 558.8% to over 28,700, while SAIC/MG added 10.4% to over 77,000. BYD registered roughly 173,000 vehicles across Europe in H1 2026 (including Denza), according to Dataforce. Key models include the MG ZS, MG3 and MG4; BYD's Dolphin Surf, Atto 3 and Seal; the Omoda 5 and Jaecoo 7; and the Leapmotor T03 and C10.

Tariffs and localisation

Trade policy is the defining variable. Definitive EU anti-subsidy duties — in force since October 2024 — add 17.0% for BYD, 18.8% for Geely and 35.3% for SAIC on top of the standard 10% duty (up to about 45% at the top), with 20.7% for other cooperating exporters and 7.8% for Tesla. A new route opened in 2026: in January the Commission published guidance on "price undertakings" (minimum import prices), and in February the CUPRA Tavascan, built by Volkswagen Anhui, became the first Chinese-built model to escape the duty via an accepted undertaking. The response is localisation: BYD's Szeged plant in Hungary began trial production in January 2026 and is due to start full production in Q4 2026 (150,000 units a year initially, scaling toward 300,000), while its planned Turkey plant in Manisa is on hold — construction has not begun and no production timeline has been set; Leapmotor builds the T03 at Stellantis's Tychy plant in Poland and began producing the B10 at Zaragoza in Spain in October 2026; and Chery is restarting the former Nissan plant in Barcelona under the Ebro brand. On 9 October 2026 the European Commission and Beijing agreed to limit imports of Chinese hybrids and plug-in hybrids to 350,000 vehicles a year, rising by about 6% a year, for four years; the measure can take effect as early as early December 2026.

Sources: JATO Dynamics, ACEA, Dataforce, European Commission, UBS, Reuters, company releases.

规模与势头

欧洲仍是中国车企风险与回报最高的市场。据 JATO Dynamics，2025 全年中国品牌在欧洲销量约 81 万辆，同比增长约 99%，份额接近 6.1%。这一势头在 2026 年加速：2026 上半年，中国品牌在欧盟、英国与 EFTA 合计上牌中的份额约 9.5%，而 2021 年仅 0.5%（Dataforce），6 月单月一度创下 10.9% 的纪录。比亚迪与奇瑞份额各约 2.4%，MG 约 2.5%。瑞银 2026 年 7 月估计，中国品牌在欧洲的单月份额已达约 11%，而一年前约 4%。到 2026 年第三季度，Dataforce 数据显示中国品牌占欧洲注册量 12.7%——首次超过日系品牌（11.9%）——9 月更创下 14.5% 的纪录，较 8 月提升约 3 个百分点（彭博社引 Dataforce）。

谁在领跑

比亚迪已在 2026 年超越 MG（上汽），成为销量冠军：2026 年 1–9 月比亚迪在欧盟、英国与 EFTA 注册 283,997 辆，领先上汽（272,251 辆）与奇瑞（260,650 辆）（ACEA，经 Electrive）。ACEA 数据显示，2026 年前四个月比亚迪在欧盟上牌量同比增长 152.9% 至逾 7.18 万辆，奇瑞增长 267.1% 至逾 4.83 万辆，零跑（经 Stellantis 合资）暴涨 558.8% 至逾 2.87 万辆，而上汽/MG 增长 10.4% 至逾 7.7 万辆。比亚迪（含腾势）2026 上半年在欧洲注册约 17.3 万辆（Dataforce）。主力车型包括 MG ZS/MG3/MG4；比亚迪 Dolphin Surf、Atto 3、Seal；Omoda 5 与 Jaecoo 7；以及零跑 T03、C10。

关税与本地化

贸易政策是决定性变量。欧盟自 2024 年 10 月起征收的最终反补贴税在 10% 基准关税之上，比亚迪 +17.0%、吉利 +18.8%、上汽 +35.3%，其他配合调查企业 +20.7%、特斯拉 +7.8%，最高综合税率约 45%。2026 年出现新路径：1 月欧盟发布"价格承诺"（最低进口价）指南，2 月大众安徽生产的 CUPRA Tavascan 成为首个以价格承诺豁免关税的中国制造车型。车企的应对仍是本地化：比亚迪匈牙利 Szeged 工厂已于 2026 年 1 月开始试制，计划 2026 年四季度量产（初期年产能 15 万辆，逐步向 30 万辆扩展），土耳其 Manisa 工厂则已搁置——尚未动工、亦无投产时间表；零跑 T03 在 Stellantis 波兰 Tychy 工厂生产，B10 已于 2026 年 10 月在西班牙 Zaragoza 工厂投产；奇瑞以 Ebro 品牌重启巴塞罗那前日产工厂。2026 年 10 月 9 日，欧盟委员会与中方达成一致，把中国混动和插混的进口限制在每年 35 万辆，此后每年约增 6%，为期四年；该措施最早可于 2026 年 12 月初生效。

来源：JATO Dynamics、ACEA、Dataforce、欧盟委员会、瑞银、路透社、企业公告。
