Chinese Cars in Middle East中东：中国汽车市场观察

The Gulf has become one of the fastest-adopting regions for Chinese cars — ICE SUVs first, EVs increasingly.海湾地区已成为中国汽车渗透最快的区域之一——先是燃油 SUV，如今电动车加速跟进。

The Gulf has become one of the fastest-adopting regions for Chinese cars — ICE SUVs first, EVs increasingly. Chery, GWM, BYD and MG all run growing dealer networks, and Dubai doubles as a re-export hub for the wider region.海湾地区已成为中国汽车渗透最快的区域之一——先是燃油 SUV，如今电动车加速跟进。奇瑞、长城、比亚迪、MG 的经销网络都在扩张，迪拜还是面向更大区域的转口枢纽。

Updated October 2026更新于 2026-10

A fast-opening region

The Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) has moved from the margins to the centre of China's export map. Chinese brands' combined GCC share rose from around 2% in 2019 to an estimated 15% by 2025, according to Roland Berger, and the advance continued in 2026: even as Middle East new-vehicle sales fell by more than a fifth in H1 2026, Chinese brands lifted deliveries from about 118,400 to 129,500 units, taking their regional share from 14.1% to 19.8% (Middle East Auto Insight). Low tariffs are a big reason: the GCC applies a common external duty of roughly 5% on vehicles, with no anti-dumping levy — a stark contrast to the EU and US walls. The treatment of EVs in the UAE into 2026 is mixed across sources: the Federal Tax Authority's 2026 budget keeps the 5% duty for passenger vehicles including EVs, while some import guides still cite a 0% EV rate.

Brands and models

The strongest players are MG, Geely, Changan, Haval and Chery on the ICE side, with Jetour climbing into the UAE's top brands on the strength of the T2 SUV and delivering almost 36,000 vehicles across the region in H1 2026 — and BYD leading on EVs. Typical best-sellers are the Jetour T1 and T2, Changan CS55/CS75, Haval H6 and Jolion, MG RX5 and ZS, Geely Coolray and Emgrand, and the BYD Atto 3, Song, Seal and Han. Dubai's Jebel Ali free zone acts as a regional distribution and re-export hub — roughly three-quarters of vehicles imported to the UAE are re-exported onward — though most volume is still imported fully built rather than assembled locally.

ICE today, EV and local assembly tomorrow

The region remains overwhelmingly an ICE and SUV market — cheap fuel, long distances and extreme heat all favour combustion — but EV sales are climbing fast off a low base, and China dominates the Gulf's EV import mix. Governments are pulling rather than pushing: Saudi Vision 2030 and UAE EV targets back charging infrastructure and incentives instead of punitive tariffs. The manufacturing story is moving beyond the Gulf: BYD's Pakistan plant (a roughly $150 million joint venture with Mega Motor/Hub Power near Karachi) is due to start assembly in H2 2026 at 25,000 units a year, BYD has entered Egypt, and Chery, Jetour and Changan are assembling locally in Pakistan, while BYD's Saudi plant remains at the feasibility-study stage.

Regional shares are estimates — the GCC has no single unified registration body (sources: Roland Berger, Middle East Auto Insight, UAE Federal Tax Authority, company releases).

一个快速开放的区域

海湾合作委员会（GCC）已从中国出口版图的边缘走到中心。据罗兰贝格，中国品牌在 GCC 的合计份额从 2019 年约 2% 升至 2025 年约 15%，2026 年继续上行：即便中东新车销量在 2026 上半年下滑逾五分之一，中国品牌交付量仍从约 11.84 万辆增至 12.95 万辆，区域份额由 14.1% 升至 19.8%（Middle East Auto Insight）。低关税是重要原因：GCC 对整车统一征收约 5% 的对外关税、且无反倾销税——与欧美的高墙形成鲜明对比。电动车在阿联酋进入 2026 年的待遇各来源不一：联邦税务局 2026 年预算维持乘用车（含电动车）5% 关税，而部分进口指南仍称电动车为 0%。

品牌与车型

燃油车阵营中最强的是 MG、吉利、长安、哈弗与奇瑞；捷途（Jetour）凭借 T2 SUV 跻身阿联酋畅销品牌之列，2026 上半年在整个区域交付近 3.6 万辆；比亚迪则领跑电动车。典型畅销车型有捷途 T1/T2、长安 CS55/CS75、哈弗 H6 与 Jolion、MG RX5 与 ZS、吉利 Coolray 与 Emgrand，以及比亚迪 Atto 3、Song、Seal、Han。迪拜杰贝阿里自由区扮演区域分销与转口枢纽——进口至阿联酋的车辆约四分之三再出口至他国——但目前多数销量仍为整车进口，而非本地组装。

今天是燃油，明天是电动与本地组装

该区域仍以燃油 SUV 为绝对主体——低油价、长距离、极端高温都偏向内燃机——但电动车正从低基数快速攀升，中国主导海湾的电动车进口结构。当地政府以"拉"代"推"：沙特 2030 愿景与阿联酋电动化目标以充电基础设施和激励为主，而非惩罚性关税。制造布局正从海湾向外延伸：比亚迪巴基斯坦工厂（与 Mega Motor/Hub Power 合资，约 1.5 亿美元，位于卡拉奇附近）预计 2026 下半年投产、年产能 2.5 万辆，比亚迪已进入埃及，奇瑞、捷途、长安在巴基斯坦本地组装，而比亚迪沙特工厂仍处可行性研究阶段。

区域份额为估算值——GCC 没有统一的上牌统计机构（来源：罗兰贝格、Middle East Auto Insight、阿联酋联邦税务局、企业公告）。
