Chery奇瑞

Chery Automobile is an export-led automaker founded in Wuhu in 1997 and listed in Hong Kong (9973) in 2025. Its portfolio includes Chery, Exeed, Jetour, Omoda, Jaecoo and iCar. Chery Holding ceased to hold its shares directly after the January 2025 restructuring.奇瑞汽车于 1997 年在芜湖成立，2025 年于香港上市（9973），以出口见长，品牌体系涵盖奇瑞、星途、捷途、Omoda、Jaecoo 与 iCar。2025 年 1 月重组后，奇瑞控股已不再直接持有奇瑞汽车股份。

Changan长安

Changan Automobile is a Shenzhen-listed automaker under China Changan Automobile Group, which was established in July 2025. Its industrial heritage dates to 1862. Its portfolio includes Changan, Deepal and Nevo/Qiyuan, alongside its investment in Avatr and the Changan Ford and Changan Mazda joint ventures.长安汽车是深圳上市车企，隶属 2025 年 7 月成立的中国长安汽车集团，工业历史追溯至 1862 年。旗下有长安、深蓝、启源，并投资阿维塔，另有长安福特、长安马自达等合资企业。
