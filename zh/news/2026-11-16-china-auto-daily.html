BYD Nears Hungarian Mass Production as LEPAS Starts Thai L6 Assembly比亚迪匈牙利工厂临近大批量生产，LEPAS启动泰国L6组装

BYD signals progress toward Hungarian mass production, LEPAS begins Thai L6 assembly, and Changan plans a mid-2027 Thai NEVO Q06 launch as Hongqi prepares its Venice debut.比亚迪高管称匈牙利工厂即将大批量生产，LEPAS披露泰国L6组装已开始；长安计划2027年中在泰推出NEVO Q06，红旗准备威尼斯首秀。

By TopChinaCar Editorial作者：TopChinaCar 编辑部 · Published November 16, 2026发布于 2026-11-16 · Compiled from the primary sources cited below基于文末所列一手信源编写

China's overseas automotive expansion is being shaped by work across factories, transport networks and local product launches. A BYD executive says the company's Hungarian plant is nearing mass production, Chery's LEPAS says Thai assembly of its electric L6 has begun, and Changan has outlined a Thai launch and manufacturing plan for NEVO Q06. Hongqi, meanwhile, is preparing an EHS4 debut in Venice. Together, these announcements offer several distinct measures of progress, from assembly already under way to milestones that remain company plans.

Manufacturing progress needs careful reading

BYD executive vice president He Zhiqi said in a post that the Hungarian plant was approaching mass production and stressed advance logistics preparations. He cited European port congestion, strikes and container backlogs. The report gave no specific production start date. The useful signal is therefore that preparations are advancing; a precise operating milestone still requires a further announcement. From an editorial perspective, his emphasis on logistics also makes the preparation outside the factory relevant to assessing its progress. [IT Home]

In Thailand, LEPAS reported a different stage of execution: local assembly of the electric L6 has already begun. The company expects its first batch to reach the market and customers in time for new excise arrangements for fully imported EVs on January 1, 2027. It is reviewing costs and business plans while developing dealer, aftersales and spare-parts services. That disclosure connects manufacturing with the support needed to serve customers, although the first-batch delivery remains an expectation. [Autolifethailand]

Product introductions connect with local operations

Changan Thailand told Autolifethailand that NEVO Q06 would first be shown locally at Motor Expo 2026, with a formal Thai launch planned for mid-2027. Production at the Rayong factory is also planned during 2027. These are prospective milestones, giving readers a sequence to follow as the model moves from an exhibition appearance toward sales and local manufacturing. [Autolifethailand]

Hongqi's next step is a European product debut. Its company announcement says the electric EHS4 SUV will appear in Venice, Italy, on November 16, 2026, before European dealers, media and industry guests. Hongqi says it was developed around European customer needs and claims up to 470 km of range, without identifying a test cycle or local pricing. The debut should therefore be assessed as a product-introduction milestone, while its commercial positioning awaits further information. [Hongqi / PR Newswire]

Shipping records show another part of the delivery chain

Separate vessel records compiled by AutoNewGen for November 15 place BYD Jinan at Wilhelmshaven in Germany. Changzhou was returning from Europe toward Singapore, while Hefei was returning from the UAE toward the same destination. These observations describe ships at different stages of their voyages. They do not disclose the vehicle cargo volumes carried on those sailings, so they cannot establish the size of a delivery batch. [AutoNewGen]

Read alongside the Hungarian factory update, the shipping report draws attention to the coordination between production preparations and transport. Our analytical reading is that overseas execution needs to be judged across those connected activities. A vessel position, an assembly announcement and a product debut each provide useful evidence, but each answers a different operational question.

What to Watch

The next useful evidence will be a specific production milestone for BYD's Hungarian plant and actual first-batch deliveries of the Thai-assembled LEPAS L6. Changan's Motor Expo appearance, planned Thai launch and Rayong production offer further checkpoints. For Hongqi, local pricing and fuller product information would help clarify the EHS4's European proposition. Across these stories, the distinction between completed actions and company expectations remains essential to assessing progress.

中国汽车的海外布局，正在通过工厂、运输网络和当地产品导入继续推进。比亚迪高管称匈牙利工厂即将进入大批量生产，奇瑞旗下LEPAS披露已开始在泰国组装纯电L6，长安则明确NEVO Q06在泰上市和本地生产的计划；红旗也准备在威尼斯举行EHS4首秀。这些信息分别对应已经开始的组装、正在推进的生产准备和未来产品安排，观察其进度，需要把不同阶段分清。

制造进展，需要看清所处阶段

比亚迪执行副总裁何志奇发文称，匈牙利工厂即将进入大批量生产，并强调需要提前做好物流准备。他提到欧洲港口拥堵、罢工和货柜积压等挑战，报道没有披露此次量产的具体启动日期。因此，这条消息能够确认的是准备工作正在推进，明确的运营节点仍需等待进一步公告。从编辑观察的角度看，他对物流的强调，也使工厂之外的准备成为判断项目进度的重要线索。[IT之家]

LEPAS在泰国披露的是另一阶段的执行进展：纯电L6已经开始本地组装。公司预计，首批车辆将赶在2027年1月1日新的整车进口电动车消费税安排实施时上市交付；同时，公司正在评估成本和业务计划，并推进经销商、售后及备件服务建设。这则信息把制造与客户服务准备联系起来，但首批交付仍是预期，后续需要实际进展来验证。[Autolifethailand]

产品导入与当地经营相衔接

长安泰国向Autolifethailand确认，NEVO Q06将先在2026年泰国Motor Expo首次展出，计划2027年中正式在泰上市，并计划2027年在罗勇工厂生产。这些节点仍是未来安排，为读者提供了可以持续跟踪的顺序：先观察当地展出，再核对正式销售与本地生产的推进情况。[Autolifethailand]

红旗下一步的重点则是欧洲产品首秀。企业公告称，纯电SUV EHS4将于2026年11月16日在意大利威尼斯亮相，面向欧洲经销商、媒体和行业嘉宾；红旗称该车围绕欧洲消费者需求开发，续航最高470公里，但未注明测试工况，也未公布当地价格。因此，此次活动更适合作为产品导入节点来观察，其具体商业定位还需要后续信息帮助判断。[红旗 / PR Newswire]

航运记录展示交付链的另一个环节

AutoNewGen汇总的11月15日船位记录显示，比亚迪济南号停靠德国威廉港，常州号由欧洲返航、合肥号由阿联酋返航，均驶向新加坡。这些观察展示了船舶所处的不同航次阶段，但没有披露相关航次的车辆装载量，因此无法据此判断某批车辆的交付规模。[AutoNewGen]

把航运记录与匈牙利工厂的消息放在一起看，值得关注的是生产准备和运输安排之间的配合。我们的分析判断是，海外经营的执行情况需要沿着这些相互关联的活动来观察。船位、组装和首秀各自提供有用信息，也各自回答不同的经营问题，阅读时应保留这些信息的边界。

值得关注

接下来，能够进一步说明进展的证据包括比亚迪匈牙利工厂明确的生产节点，以及泰国组装LEPAS L6首批车辆的实际交付。长安的Motor Expo展出、计划中的泰国上市和罗勇生产，也提供了后续核对点；红旗EHS4的当地价格和更完整产品信息，则有助于理解其欧洲市场定位。贯穿这些消息的观察原则，是把已经完成的行动与企业仍待兑现的预期分别看清。
