China Auto Daily: Modification Rules Enter Consultation as Nissan and Audi Update Plans中国汽车市场日报：改装办法征求意见，日产与奥迪公布产品进展

Modification rules enter public consultation

The Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, or MIIT, published draft vehicle modification rules on November 13. Its First Department of Equipment Industry prepared the document with relevant departments, and comments are due by December 12, 2026. This is a consultation rather than an effective regulation. For businesses assessing the announcement, the immediate development is an opportunity to respond to a proposed framework; the notice does not establish that final rules have taken effect. [MIIT]

Nissan and Audi announce distinct product steps

Nissan used its November 13 NISSAN DAY event in Zhuhai to debut the NV200 PHEV and announce that it would bring NISMO to China. The NV200 PHEV presentation was a global unveiling, so it should not be read as a completed commercial launch. The event also included NX7 pre-sales, but that development was covered by an earlier edition and is excluded from today's new items. The additional news concerns a different vehicle and the NISMO brand announcement. [Nissan China]

Separately, Momenta said the Audi E7X would receive panoramic driver assistance powered by its R7 world model through an OTA update planned for December. The announcement was made at Shanghai's November 13 AUDI DAY and described on Momenta's website the following day. The subject is the SAIC Audi business, which should be distinguished from other Audi operations in China. This remains a scheduled software update, with no completed rollout established by today's item. [Momenta]

Existing vehicle families gain new variants

Arcfox introduced the Beta S3 535 Ultra+ lidar battery-swapping variant and the battery-swappable Beta T1. Both use CATL's Chocolate swapping network. The incremental product news is the availability of these specific versions, rather than a repetition of previously announced cooperation or station plans. That distinction keeps the vehicle launch separate from the development of the supporting infrastructure. [CarNewsChina]

Aistaland, developed jointly by GAC and Huawei Qiankun, added GT7 Ultra Comfort versions with extended-range and battery-electric powertrains, with the variants arriving at stores. Lynk & Co also launched its model-year 2027 03 family in Chengdu, comprising four regular versions and three 03+ versions. These are updates within existing vehicle families. Today's coverage treats the additions as product developments and does not turn temporary purchase incentives into separate pricing-strategy stories. [Xinhua] [Sina Auto]

Board changes remain subject to approval

Foton disclosed that its board had nominated Xiao Xiao and Li Yongqian as director candidates. Shareholders still need to approve the proposal. Wang Xuequan and Zhu Liguang will leave their board roles after new directors are elected and will continue relevant duties until then. The filing therefore describes a governance transition with an approval condition, rather than an already completed replacement. [Foton filing]

What to Watch

The explicit next steps in today's items are MIIT's consultation deadline, the planned Audi software rollout and shareholder consideration of Foton's board proposal. Each remains separate from a completed outcome. The vehicle announcements establish changes to product offerings or stated plans; today's items do not provide a basis for concluding that these changes have already increased domestic sales or market share.

汽车改装办法进入公开征求意见阶段

工业和信息化部装备工业一司11月13日发布《汽车改装管理办法（征求意见稿）》，文件由其会同相关部门司局组织起草，意见反馈截止2026年12月12日。此次监管进展是提出拟议框架并公开听取意见，尚不构成已经生效的规定。对关注这项文件的企业而言，当前可确认的是征求意见程序已经启动；通知本身不能作为最终办法已实施的依据。[工信部]

日产和奥迪公布不同阶段的产品进展

日产在11月13日珠海NISSAN DAY展示NV200 PHEV，并宣布将NISMO品牌引入中国。NV200 PHEV此次为全球首秀，需要与正式商业上市区分。同场启动的NX7预售已经由此前日报报道，因此本期不再重复计算。本次新增新闻关注的是另一款车型的亮相，以及NISMO品牌引入中国的宣布，不能将同场旧条目重新包装为新增事件。[日产中国]

另一项产品进展来自上汽奥迪业务。Momenta披露，11月13日上海AUDI DAY宣布奥迪E7X将采用其R7世界模型赋能的全景辅助驾驶，计划12月通过OTA推送；相关官网文章发表于次日。这里的经营主体是上汽奥迪，不能与奥迪在华其他业务混为一谈。就本期可确认的信息而言，这仍是已公布的车辆软件更新计划，尚不能写成用户已经全部收到更新。[Momenta]

现有车型家族继续扩充版本

极狐推出贝塔S3 535 Ultra+激光换电版与贝塔T1换电版，两款新版本均接入宁德时代巧克力换电网络。本期新增事实是具体车型版本上市，而不是重复此前公布的合作关系或站点规划。整车版本的推出与配套补能网络的发展属于不同环节，报道时需分别确认进度，不能把既有基础设施规划当作当天新建成的网络。[CarNewsChina]

由广汽与华为乾崑共同打造的启境，新增GT7 Ultra舒享版增程和纯电车型，新版本已陆续到店。领克则在成都推出2027款03家族，普通版提供四款车型，03+提供三款车型。这些进展均是现有车型家族的产品更新。本期据此记录车型供给的变化，不把配套限时购车权益另行写成官方价格体系的战略调整，也不把新增版本表述为整款车型首次进入市场。[新华网] [新浪汽车]

董事调整仍需履行批准程序

福田汽车披露，董事会提名萧枭、李永前为董事候选人，相关议案尚须提交股东会批准。王学权、朱励光的董事离任，将在股东会选出新任董事后生效，此前继续履行相关职责。因此，这份公告所确认的是附有批准条件的公司治理调整，不能提前表述为董事替换已经全部完成。[福田汽车公告]
