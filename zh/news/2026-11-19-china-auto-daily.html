Chery’s Jetour Eyes Brazilian Production as Momenta Starts Munich Robotaxi Pilot奇瑞捷途据报敲定巴西生产地点，Momenta启动慕尼黑试乘

Reuters reports a Brazilian production deal for Jetour. Momenta begins supervised rides in Munich, and Pony.ai reports more than 200,000 km of operations in Zagreb.路透社报道奇瑞旗下捷途达成巴西本地生产协议。Momenta在慕尼黑启动有人监督的试乘，小马智行披露萨格勒布累计运营超过20万公里。

By TopChinaCar Editorial作者：TopChinaCar 编辑部 · Published November 19, 2026发布于 2026-11-19 · Compiled from the primary sources cited below基于文末所列一手信源编写

Chery’s Jetour now has a reported site for local production in Brazil, while Momenta’s Munich robotaxi pilot has reached the point where selected passengers can take a ride. Developments across Southeast Asia trace another part of the same story through battery sourcing, dealership openings and software for owners. Together they offer several ways to see what overseas expansion means in practice, from preparation at a factory to the everyday experience of a passenger or car owner.

A Site for Brazilian Production

Reuters cited two Rio de Janeiro state government sources as saying Chery’s Jetour had reached an agreement with the state to produce vehicles locally. The sources identified Jaguar Land Rover’s Itatiaia plant as the production site, with terms still unclear. The named factory gives Jetour’s Brazilian production plans a concrete location to follow. The allocation of responsibilities, the choice of vehicles and the production schedule would show how quickly the reported agreement could result in locally built cars. These are the operational details that matter for the Brazilian market. [Reuters]

European Robotaxi Trials and Operations

Momenta and Uber are offering free rides to selected riders who have registered in advance, initially serving parts of northern Munich around the Olympic Park with designated stops. The pilot operates under test permits issued to Momenta by Germany’s Federal Motor Transport Authority, with a trained safety operator aboard each ride. Designated stops and phased access give the pilot clear boundaries. For passengers, the immediate questions are where they can board and how they can secure access. For the operators, the defined area provides an opportunity to observe how bookings, pickups and passenger trips work under the test permits. [Momenta]

SAIC’s second-generation IM LS6 entered supervised autonomous passenger trials in Munich. The vehicle uses Momenta technology and carries selected riders within a designated area with an onboard safety operator. From the automaker’s perspective, the pilot gives IM Motors a specific passenger-facing deployment abroad. Identifying the model connects the technology supplier’s pilot to the vehicle that riders actually enter and helps readers track both sides of the rollout. [CarNewsChina]

Pony.ai said in its quarterly release that its Zagreb robotaxi service logged more than 200,000 km and completed several thousand passenger trips from its April 8 launch through October 31. It also said road tests were underway in Manchester and deployment work was continuing in London. The Zagreb distance and passenger figures cover the same reporting period, so they can be read together. The two UK cities are at different stages. Road testing in Manchester and work on deployment in London offer separate milestones to follow. [Pony.ai]

Batteries, Dealerships and Software in Southeast Asia

GAC Indonesia’s product planning team told detikOto it was testing battery suppliers and that an existing collaboration was supporting assembly at its Cikampek plant. Aion plans to localize work in phases, starting with battery packaging, while retaining battery technology from China. The plan is about how much of the production process GAC’s Indonesian operation can take on. Once supplier testing is complete, the choice of supplier and the steps assigned to the local production line will provide more tangible evidence of how far the effort has progressed. [detikOto] Changan opened a dealership in San Fernando, Pampanga, the first since its Philippine operation became a wholly owned subsidiary of Changan Auto. The new outlet provides vehicle sales and aftersales service. The opening gives the change in Changan’s Philippine operating structure a physical point of contact. Sales and aftersales at the same location give drivers somewhere to go both before they buy a vehicle and after they take it home. [CarGuide.PH]

BYD and Sime Motors opened a new 3S centre in Ipoh, in Malaysia’s Perak state, operated by authorized dealer Goh Brothers. It brings vehicle sales, servicing and spare parts together at one location. Ipoh brings purchases, servicing and parts together at one site. The service centre gives existing BYD owners a practical reason to pay attention to the expansion, alongside the additional place for prospective customers to see and buy a vehicle. [Paul Tan’s Automotive News] Zeekr introduced an over-the-air update in Malaysia that adds Apple Digital Key 3.0 and remote straight-line parking. It also brings a one-touch driving setup and a lower regenerative braking setting. Zeekr’s changes arrive after the vehicle sale. Unlocking, parking and configuring a drive are routine tasks, so the update gives local owners a visible measure of how the brand supports cars already in their hands. [Paul Tan’s Automotive News]

What to Watch

Watch for Jetour to detail what could be built at Itatiaia and when. In Munich, the next developments to track are which riders will be offered access and where the pilot can operate. Each would give a clearer indication of how the respective local operations are taking shape.

奇瑞旗下捷途在巴西的本地生产计划有了具体工厂指向，Momenta也在慕尼黑把自动驾驶试点推进到获选乘客可以试乘的阶段。东南亚的消息则集中在电池供应商、门店和车机更新，显示出海业务已延伸到从生产到用车的多个环节。

巴西生产走向具体选址

路透社引述两名里约热内卢州政府消息人士称，奇瑞旗下捷途已与州政府达成在当地生产汽车的协议。消息人士称生产地点为捷豹路虎位于伊塔蒂亚亚的工厂，协议条款尚不清楚。 巴西生产计划有了可以追踪的具体地点。接下来值得看参与各方怎样分工、哪些车型会在当地生产，以及何时能从协议走到生产线。对当地市场来说，这些细节也决定了新产能何时可能转化为实际供应。 [Reuters]

欧洲Robotaxi试乘与运营

Momenta与Uber向预先登记并获选的乘客提供免费试乘，初期覆盖奥林匹克公园附近等慕尼黑北部区域，并在指定站点上下车。试点依据德国联邦机动车管理局发给Momenta的测试许可开展，每程均有受训安全员随车。 指定站点和分批邀请给试乘划定了清晰边界。对乘客来说，最直接的问题是哪里能上车、怎样才能获邀。对运营方来说，这次试点提供了在限定区域观察接单、上下车与载客流程的机会。 [Momenta]

上汽旗下智己的第二代LS6在慕尼黑投入自动驾驶试乘。该车型搭载Momenta技术，在限定区域内为获选乘客提供有安全员随车的试乘。 从整车角度看，智己也有了一个具体的海外载客场景。这条消息把技术供应商的试点与乘客实际坐进的车型联系起来，让同一次试乘的整车和软件两方面都更容易追踪。 [CarNewsChina]

小马智行在三季度公告中称，萨格勒布Robotaxi服务自4月8日启动至10月31日累计运营超过20万公里，完成数千次载客行程。公司同时披露，英国曼彻斯特已开展道路测试，并继续推进伦敦部署。 萨格勒布的里程和载客行程使用同一统计区间，可以放在一起看。英国两座城市则各有进度，读者可以分别跟踪曼彻斯特道路测试以及伦敦部署的后续安排。 [Pony.ai]

东南亚的电池、门店与车机服务

广汽印尼产品规划团队向detikOto表示，公司正在测试电池供应商，已有合作参与芝坎佩工厂的组装流程。埃安计划从电池组封装等环节逐步提高本地化程度，电池技术仍由中国方面提供。 这项计划关系到广汽印尼工厂能够承担多少生产工序。供应商测试之后，最终选择哪家企业、哪些步骤能够落到当地生产线上，是判断本地化深度的具体依据。 [detikOto] 长安在菲律宾邦板牙省圣费尔南多开设经销店，是其当地业务改由长安全资子公司运营后开设的首家经销店。新网点提供销售及售后服务。 新门店给长安在菲律宾的渠道调整提供了一个可见的落点。把销售和售后一起落到这个网点，也使当地车主和准备买车的消费者都有了清楚的联系地点。 [CarGuide.PH]

比亚迪与Sime Motors在马来西亚霹雳州怡保开设新的3S中心，由授权经销商Goh Brothers运营。中心将车辆销售、维修保养和零部件服务集中在同一网点。 怡保中心把买车、养车和零部件服务集中到同一处。对已经在使用比亚迪车型的当地车主来说，能多一个办理售后的地点，比单纯知道品牌新增了展厅更有实际意义。 [Paul Tan’s Automotive News] 极氪在马来西亚推出车机OTA更新，加入Apple Digital Key 3.0和遥控直线泊车。更新还带来一键驾驶设置和较低的能量回收挡位。 极氪的动作发生在车辆交付之后。开锁、泊车和驾驶设置都是用户日常会遇到的操作，这些更新也给了当地车主一个观察品牌如何持续支持已交付车辆的窗口。 [Paul Tan’s Automotive News]
