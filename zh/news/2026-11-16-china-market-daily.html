China's latest verified automotive updates cover energy infrastructure and a new pickup variant. NIO has extended its swap network to Heihe, while Zhengzhou Nissan has added the Z9 Yuexiang plug-in hybrid pickup. AI-generated.

Today's Updates

NIO announced the opening on November 15. The fifth-generation station serves NIO, ONVO and firefly, with thermal management and equipment preheating for severe cold. Its China network reached 4,262 swap stations, including 102 in the northeast. [CnEVPost]

Zhengzhou Nissan launched the Z9 Yuexiang on November 14 at a starting price of RMB 149,900. The pickup has 240 kW of combined system output and 110 km of NEDC-rated electric range, plus scheduled charging and external power supply functions. The launch is attributed to Zhengzhou Nissan as the operating entity. [Autohome]

What to Watch

The operational details to follow are the cold-weather swap service and the new pickup's market rollout. These updates establish infrastructure availability and product specifications; they provide no evidence of subsequent demand.

今日动态

蔚来11月15日宣布黑河第五代换电站正式投运，可服务蔚来、乐道和萤火虫三个品牌，采用站内温控及设备预热应对严寒环境。截至当日，公司全国换电站达到4,262座，其中东北地区102座。[CnEVPost]

郑州日产11月14日推出Z9悦享版，起售价14.99万元。该插混皮卡系统综合功率240千瓦，NEDC工况纯电续航110公里，并配备预约充电和对外放电功能。此项上市动态归属于经营主体郑州日产。[汽车之家]
