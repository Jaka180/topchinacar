Changan Swings to Loss and GAC's Deficit Widens as Q3 Reports Expose Profit Squeeze in China长安三季度转亏、广汽亏损扩大，三季报折射国内车市盈利压力

Changan posted a Q3 net loss of RMB 758 million, GAC's quarterly loss widened to RMB 2.52 billion and Great Wall's profit fell 40.75%, while Japanese automakers' China output dropped 32% in April–September.长安汽车三季度归母净亏损7.58亿元，广汽单季亏损扩大至25.2亿元，长城单季净利润同比下降40.75%；日系车企4–9月在华产量同比下降32%。

AI-generated summary based on today's China Auto Market Daily items. A wave of third-quarter reports released on October 30 showed how hard it has become for Chinese automakers to make money. Changan Automobile swung to a quarterly loss, GAC Group's loss widened, Great Wall Motor's profit fell by about two-fifths, and Seres returned to profit for the quarter while still earning far less than a year earlier. Separate data showed Japanese automakers' joint-venture output in China at its lowest level for the April–September period since 2008. In the supply chain, Xiaomi agreed to invest in Sunwoda's EV battery unit, and Li Auto said its i6 had reached 200,000 units of cumulative production.

Earnings: losses and shrinking margins

Changan Automobile, the Chongqing-based state-owned group, reported Q3 revenue of RMB 36.25 billion, down 14.17% year on year, and a Q3 net loss attributable to shareholders of RMB 758 million, compared with a profit of RMB 764 million a year earlier. Its nine-month net profit attributable fell 98.06% to RMB 59.28 million. The company attributed the decline to lower sales volume, higher raw material prices and larger foreign exchange losses [Jiemian].

GAC Group, the Guangzhou-based parent of GAC Toyota and GAC Honda, grew Q3 revenue 17.91% to RMB 28.423 billion, but its net loss attributable widened 42.09% to RMB 2.52 billion from RMB 1.774 billion a year earlier. Its nine-month net loss reached RMB 6.987 billion. GAC cited fierce domestic competition, launch spending at its subsidiary Qijing Auto, pressure on its joint-venture brands, foreign exchange losses and higher inventory impairment [Jiemian].

Great Wall Motor's Q3 revenue fell 5.68% to RMB 57.767 billion and its net profit attributable dropped 40.75% to RMB 1.361 billion; nine-month profit was down 55.69% at RMB 3.826 billion. Of the 920,300 vehicles it sold in the first nine months, 476,000 were overseas sales, up 42.43%. The report did not separate domestic sales, so readers should not treat the total as a China figure. Inventory rose to RMB 40.171 billion at the end of September from RMB 26.148 billion at the end of 2025 [21st Century Business Herald].

Seres, the Chongqing manufacturer that builds AITO-brand vehicles in cooperation with Huawei, reported Q3 revenue down 41.66% at RMB 28.08 billion. Net profit attributable was RMB 1.42 billion, a return to profit from the previous quarter but down 40.12% year on year, and it posted a net loss of RMB 1.365 billion excluding non-recurring items. Its January–September NEV sales fell 15.79% to 256,500 units [The Paper].

Chery Automobile, which reports in Hong Kong, posted nine-month revenue of RMB 241.234 billion, up 12.29%, with gross margin up 2.46 percentage points to 16.17% and profit attributable to owners of RMB 13.963 billion; the report did not give a year-on-year comparison for that profit or split domestic and export business [Jiemian]. JAC Motors narrowed its nine-month net loss to about RMB 1.147 billion from about RMB 1.434 billion, on revenue up 11.33% to about RMB 34.372 billion [NBD via 21jingji]. Smaller Jinbei Automobile reported Q3 net profit down 73.46% at RMB 20.56 million [Jiemian].

Japanese joint ventures keep shrinking

Japanese brands build cars in China through joint ventures with local state groups such as FAW, GAC and Dongfeng, and these ventures are separate operating companies from their Chinese parents. Gasgoo, citing a foreign media report, said Japanese automakers' combined China output fell 32% in April–September 2026. Toyota produced 663,079 vehicles, down 18%; Honda 131,716, down 60%; and Nissan 163,192, down 49%. Figures for individual joint ventures were not disclosed [Gasgoo].

On the sales side, Toyota's China sales fell 25% to 684,903 vehicles in April–September, the lowest for the period since 2017, according to Toyota data reported by Gasgoo. May saw the steepest decline at 31.7%, and September sales were down 19.7%. Sales for GAC Toyota and FAW Toyota were not broken out [Gasgoo].

Supply chain and capacity

Xiaomi's wholly owned Hanxing Venture Capital plans to invest RMB 1.7 billion for a 5.34% stake in Sunwoda EVB, the EV battery unit of Shenzhen-listed Sunwoda, according to a filing released on October 29. Sunwoda's stake would fall from 24.06% to 22.78% and it would stop consolidating the unit; the deal still needs Sunwoda shareholder approval. Li Auto announced a RMB 2.65 billion investment in the same unit in September [CnEVPost].

Li Auto said cumulative production of the i6 electric SUV reached 200,000 units on October 30, about 13 months after launch. The company expects i6 capacity to reach 20,000 units in November and rise further in December, with a current wait time of 7–8 weeks [CnEVPost].

In chips, XHEART, which co-designs processors with driver-assistance supplier Momenta, launched its 700-TOPS X7 Ultra on October 29. It said the X7 series has shipped more than 200,000 chips over 8 months of mass production and is used in 10 production vehicle models; these are company claims [21st Century Business Herald].

What to Watch

Sunwoda shareholders still need to approve Xiaomi's investment in Sunwoda EVB. Li Auto has set a 20,000-unit November capacity target for the i6. Several of today's earnings reports, including Great Wall's, mix domestic and overseas volumes, so domestic performance should be read only where companies disclose it separately.

三季报：亏损与利润收窄

长安汽车第三季度营业收入362.5亿元，同比下降14.17%；归母净亏损7.58亿元，上年同期为盈利7.64亿元。前三季度归母净利润5927.53万元，同比下降98.06%。公司将业绩下滑归因于销量下降、原材料涨价和汇兑损失增加[界面新闻]。

广汽集团是广汽丰田、广汽本田的中方母公司。其第三季度营业收入284.23亿元，同比增长17.91%，但归母净亏损25.2亿元，上年同期亏损17.74亿元，亏损同比扩大42.09%；前三季度归母净亏损69.87亿元。公司提及国内竞争激烈、子公司启境汽车新车上市的销售投入、合资品牌承压、汇兑损失和存货减值增加等因素[界面新闻]。

长城汽车第三季度营业总收入577.67亿元，同比下降5.68%；归母净利润13.61亿元，同比下降40.75%；前三季度归母净利润38.26亿元，同比下降55.69%。前三季度总销量92.03万辆，其中海外销量47.60万辆，同比增长42.43%；报道未单列国内销量，因此总销量不能当作国内销量。截至9月末，公司存货为401.71亿元，2025年末为261.48亿元[21世纪经济报道]。

与华为合作生产问界品牌车型的赛力斯，第三季度营业收入280.8亿元，同比下降41.66%；归母净利润14.2亿元，环比转为盈利，但同比下降40.12%；扣非后净亏损13.65亿元。1–9月新能源汽车销量25.65万辆，同比下降15.79%[澎湃新闻]。

在港股上市的奇瑞汽车前三季度总收入2412.34亿元，同比增长12.29%；毛利率16.17%，同比提高2.46个百分点；公司拥有人应占利润139.63亿元。报道未披露该利润的同比变化，也未区分国内与出口业务[界面新闻]。江淮汽车前三季度营收约343.72亿元，同比增长11.33%，归母净亏损约11.47亿元，上年同期亏损约14.34亿元[每日经济新闻/21财经]。规模较小的金杯汽车第三季度归母净利润2056.14万元，同比下降73.46%[界面新闻]。

日系合资继续收缩

日系品牌在华主要通过与一汽、广汽、东风等国有集团组建的合资企业生产，这些合资公司是独立经营主体，不等同于中方母公司。盖世汽车援引外媒数据报道，日系车企2026年4–9月在华产量合计同比下降32%，为2008年以来同期最低。其中丰田663,079辆，同比下降18%；本田131,716辆，同比下降60%；日产163,192辆，同比下降49%。报道未披露各合资公司的分项数据[盖世汽车]。

销量方面，据丰田公布的数据，丰田4–9月在华销量684,903辆，同比下降25%，为2017年以来同期最低。5月降幅最大，同比下降31.7%；9月同比下降19.7%。报道未单列广汽丰田和一汽丰田的销量[盖世汽车]。

供应链与产能

据欣旺达10月29日披露的公告，小米全资子公司瀚星创业投资拟出资17亿元认购欣旺达动力5.34%股权。增资及董事会改组完成后，欣旺达的持股将由24.06%降至22.78%，并不再将欣旺达动力并表；该协议仍需欣旺达股东大会批准。理想汽车此前已于9月宣布以26.5亿元入股欣旺达动力[CnEVPost]。

理想汽车表示，i6纯电SUV累计产量已于10月30日达到20万辆，距上市约13个月。公司预计i6在11月产能达到2万辆，12月进一步提升；目前等车周期为7–8周[CnEVPost]。

芯片方面，与智驾供应商Momenta联合设计芯片的新芯航途于10月29日发布700TOPS的X7 Ultra。公司称X7系列已量产8个月，累计出货超20万颗，搭载于10款量产车型；以上为企业自述[21世纪经济报道]。

值得关注

小米入股欣旺达动力仍需欣旺达股东大会批准；理想i6设定了11月2万辆的产能目标。今日多份财报（包括长城汽车）将国内与海外销量合并披露，判断国内经营表现时，应只采用公司单独披露的国内数据。
