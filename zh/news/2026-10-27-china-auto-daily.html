XPeng Ships the Mona L05 to 65 Markets as GAC and Changan Plan to Take Driver Assistance Abroad in 2027小鹏 MONA L05 开启全球发运，广汽、长安计划 2027 年把智能辅助驾驶带到海外

XPeng began global shipments of the Mona L05, targeting 65 countries and regions, Chery's Luxeed plans six NEV models abroad, GAC and Changan aim to take driver assistance overseas in 2027, and BYD passed 150,000 deliveries in Australia.小鹏 MONA L05 在国内上市前开启全球发运，计划销往 65 个国家和地区；奇瑞旗下智界将在海外推出 6 款新能源车型；广汽、长安都把 2027 年定为智能辅助驾驶出海的关键一年；比亚迪在澳大利亚累计交付突破 15 万辆。

By TopChinaCar Editorial作者：TopChinaCar 编辑部 · Published October 27, 2026发布于 2026-10-27 · Compiled from the primary sources cited below基于文末所列一手信源编写

Chinese automakers used to launch a car at home, wait a year or two, and then think about export markets. Today's news shows how far that sequence has been compressed. XPeng has started shipping the Mona L05 around the world before its China launch, Chery's Luxeed is developing four SUVs that will not be sold in China at all, and GAC and Changan are already setting dates for taking their driver-assistance software abroad. Meanwhile, the sales data from Australia and smaller European markets shows Chinese models moving from niche to the top of the charts.

XPeng: Global First, China Second

XPeng said on Oct 26 that the Mona L05 has begun global shipments ahead of its China launch, with plans to sell it in 65 countries and regions. It did not disclose the size of the first shipment or delivery timing by market. Left- and right-hand-drive versions were developed in parallel, and XPeng put 660 vehicles through extreme-condition testing in 25 countries and regions, including cabin-noise tests in Germany, high-altitude powertrain tests in Mexico and reliability tests at 50°C in the UAE and -25°C in Norway. The L05 sits above the Mona L03, launched in July, and its China launch event is set for Oct 29. [CnEVPost]

The timing fits XPeng's broader overseas push. Its overseas sales passed 10,000 for the first time in September 2026, reaching 10,055 (+100.94% YoY), and Jan–Sep overseas sales of 59,479 made up 20.92% of total deliveries. At the Paris Motor Show, He Xiaopeng set a target of 1 million global sales a year by 2030, half of them in Europe.

XPeng is also building out the ecosystem around its cars. In Mexico, which it entered in spring 2026 with the G6 and G9, it is working with Electronity, a charging network run by Drivewatt México, to install more than 100 charging points: about 30 fast chargers by end-2026 and the remaining 70 by end-2027. XPeng owners get a 10% charging discount at participating stations for one year, and the station map will be added to XPeng's digital platforms. [electrive]

Luxeed Builds Cars for Markets Outside China

Chery's premium brand Luxeed, developed with Huawei, has set out plans to launch six NEV models abroad: the LLounge large MPV (V9 in China), the Stormax sporty SUV (RX in China) and four crossover SUVs. The LLounge and Stormax go first, with Australia among the first markets. The four SUVs, tentatively named 3, 5, 7 and 9, will not be sold in China and are being developed for overseas markets, with more details due next year. [CarNewsChina]

That is a notable step for a Chinese brand: rather than adapting domestic models, Luxeed is designing a product line around export demand. It fits Chery's overall scale abroad. The group exported 207,814 vehicles in September 2026 and 1,551,178 in Jan–Sep (+65.6% YoY), and says about 48% of its exports are built overseas this year.

Driver Assistance Is the Next Export

GAC International general manager Wei Haigang said 2027 will be the first year of Chinese driver assistance going fully global, with GAC starting in Europe and Southeast Asia. GAC says it has already deployed cloud infrastructure worldwide, has begun rolling out L2 driver assistance overseas, and is working with multinational suppliers such as Bosch and Valeo, as well as local partners, to adapt to each market's rules. [Yicai]

Changan is on a similar timeline. It plans to roll out its Tianshu highway NCA (navigation-assisted driving) overseas first in 2027, followed by city NCA, and will build local computing centers to close the data loop. [Yicai] The local computing point matters: driver-assistance systems need local road data to train and validate, and data rules differ from market to market, so Chinese automakers cannot simply ship their domestic stacks abroad.

From Niche to Top of the Charts

In Australia, BYD has delivered its 150,000th car, a Seal 6 Touring wagon, four years after entering the market and about six months after reaching 100,000. BYD is now Australia's second best-selling car brand, with more than 120 dealers nationwide, and expects over 150 sales and service dealers by end-2026 as it expands into regional towns across several states. [The Driven]

Smaller European markets tell the same story. In Serbia, BYD's Seal U sold 150 units in September 2026 for a 4.6% share, the first time a Chinese model has topped the Serbian monthly chart, while Chery's Tiggo 4 sold 107 units to rank 4th, its first-ever Serbian monthly top-10 finish. [BestSellingCarsBlog] In Moldova, Geely's EX5 sold 123 units for an 8.2% share, topping the model ranking for a sixth straight month, with the Coolray and Monjaro also in the top 10. [BestSellingCarsBlog] In Albania, SAIC's MG 5 ranked 2nd with 76 units and GAC's Aion i60 ranked 4th with 70 units. [BestSellingCarsBlog]

The volumes are small, but the pattern is clear: in markets where Chinese brands compete on price and equipment without heavy tariffs, they are now taking the top spots.

What to Watch

  • Mona L05 launch on Oct 29: China pricing and any detail on first overseas delivery dates will show how quickly the global-first approach turns into sales.
  • Driver assistance in 2027: GAC and Changan have set the year; the next question is which markets approve these systems first, and how local data and computing requirements shape the rollout.

过去，中国车企通常先在国内上市，过一两年再考虑出口。今天的几条新闻说明，这个顺序正在被大幅压缩：小鹏 MONA L05 在国内上市之前就开启了全球发运；奇瑞旗下智界正在开发 4 款完全不在国内销售的 SUV；广汽、长安已经给智能辅助驾驶出海定下了时间表。与此同时，澳大利亚和欧洲小市场的销量数据显示，中国车型正从小众走向榜首。

小鹏：先全球，后国内

小鹏 10 月 26 日宣布，MONA L05 在国内上市前已开启全球发运，计划销往 65 个国家和地区，首批发运规模和各市场交付时间未披露。小鹏称左舵、右舵版本同步开发，并投入 660 辆车在 25 个国家和地区做极端环境测试，包括德国车内噪声、墨西哥高原动力以及阿联酋 50°C、挪威 -25°C 可靠性测试。L05 定位在 7 月上市的 MONA L03 之上，国内上市发布会定于 10 月 29 日。[CnEVPost]

这与小鹏整体的出海节奏一致。2026 年 9 月小鹏海外销量 10,055 辆（同比 +100.94%），首次突破 1 万辆；1–9 月累计 59,479 辆，占总交付的 20.92%。何小鹏在巴黎车展期间表示，目标 2030 年全球年销量 100 万辆、其中一半来自欧洲。

小鹏也在补齐车以外的配套。在 2026 年春季以 G6、G9 进入的墨西哥，小鹏与 Drivewatt México 运营的充电网络 Electronity 合作，计划安装 100 多个充电桩：2026 年底前约 30 个快充桩，其余 70 个在 2027 年底前建成。小鹏车主在合作站点充电可享一年 10% 折扣，充电站地图将接入小鹏数字平台。[electrive]

智界：专为海外开发车型

奇瑞与华为合作的高端品牌智界公布全球计划，将在海外推出 6 款新能源车型：大型 MPV LLounge（国内 V9）、运动 SUV Stormax（国内 RX）以及 4 款跨界 SUV。首批出海的是 LLounge 和 Stormax，澳大利亚是首批市场之一；暂定名为 3、5、7、9 的 4 款 SUV 不在国内销售，专为海外市场开发，更多细节明年公布。[CarNewsChina]

对中国品牌来说，这是值得注意的一步：不再只是把国内车型改一改拿去卖，而是围绕海外需求规划产品线。这也符合奇瑞整体的海外体量：2026 年 9 月出口 207,814 辆，1–9 月累计 1,551,178 辆（同比 +65.6%），今年约 48% 的出口车辆在海外生产。

智能辅助驾驶成为下一个出口品

据第一财经报道，广汽国际总经理卫海岗表示，2027 年将是中国智能辅助驾驶全面出海的元年，广汽将率先在欧洲、东南亚推进。广汽称已在全球提前部署云基础设施，L2 级组合驾驶辅助已开始在海外落地，并与博世、法雷奥等跨国零部件企业以及海外本地合作商合作，适配各地法规。[第一财经]

长安的时间表相近：计划 2027 年在海外率先落地「天枢领航」高速 NCA，城市 NCA 随后跟进，并将在当地建立算力中心，以实现数据闭环。[第一财经]本地算力这一点很关键：辅助驾驶系统需要用当地道路数据训练和验证，而各市场的数据规则不同，中国车企无法把国内方案原样搬到海外。

从小众到榜首

在澳大利亚，比亚迪进入市场 4 年交付了第 150,000 辆车（一辆 Seal 6 Touring 旅行车），距宣布 10 万辆约半年。比亚迪目前是澳大利亚销量第二的汽车品牌，在全国有 120 多家经销商，预计 2026 年底超过 150 家销售和服务网点，并将扩展到多个州的地区城镇。[The Driven]

欧洲小市场也呈现同样的趋势。2026 年 9 月，比亚迪 Seal U 在塞尔维亚售出 150 辆，市场份额 4.6%，位列车型销量第一，这是中国车型首次登顶塞尔维亚月度榜单；奇瑞瑞虎 4 售出 107 辆、排名第 4，首次进入塞尔维亚月度前十。[BestSellingCarsBlog]在摩尔多瓦，吉利 EX5 售出 123 辆、份额 8.2%，连续第 6 个月位居车型销量第一，缤越和星越 L 也进入前十。[BestSellingCarsBlog]在阿尔巴尼亚，上汽名爵 MG 5 以 76 辆排名第 2，广汽埃安 i60 以 70 辆排名第 4。[BestSellingCarsBlog]

这些市场的绝对量不大，但规律很清楚：在中国品牌可以凭价格和配置竞争、又没有高额关税的市场，它们已经开始占据榜首。
