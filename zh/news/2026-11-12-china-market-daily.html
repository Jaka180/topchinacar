China's Domestic Car Sales Slide as CAAM Sees 2026 Passenger Demand Below 19 Million中汽协：10月汽车销量降9.2%，预计今年乘用车国内销量低于1900万辆

CAAM says October vehicle sales fell 9.2% to 3.016 million and expects 2026 domestic passenger-car sales below 19 million, while CPCA data show early-November retail down 21%. Nissan China fell 67.1%; Volkswagen brand set an NEV record.中汽协数据显示10月汽车销量301.6万辆、同比下降9.2%，并预计今年乘用车国内销量低于1900万辆；乘联会11月1–8日乘用车零售同比降21%。日产中国10月销量降67.1%，大众品牌在华新能源交付创纪录。

China's domestic car market is still contracting even as electrified vehicles take a larger share of what is sold. New data from the China Association of Automobile Manufacturers (CAAM), the industry body whose figures cover wholesale shipments including exports, show October vehicle sales of 3.016 million, down 9.2% year on year. The association now expects domestic passenger-car sales in 2026 to come in below 19 million. Early-November retail figures from the China Passenger Car Association (CPCA) point the same way, while company releases show how unevenly the pressure falls across joint ventures, start-ups and suppliers.

Market data: home demand keeps weakening

According to CAAM, China's vehicle sales totalled 26.286 million in January–October, down 5.1%. Within that, domestic passenger-car sales were 14.528 million, down 25.2%, and the year-on-year decline in domestic passenger-car sales has exceeded 20% for seven straight months. CAAM deputy secretary-general Chen Shihua said 2026 domestic passenger-car sales will be below 19 million, while describing this as "not a trend". At the start of the year, CAAM had forecast total vehicle sales of about 34.75 million for 2026 [National Business Daily].

International readers should note that CAAM's headline number mixes domestic and export volumes, so it cannot be read as a measure of Chinese consumer demand on its own. The domestic passenger-car figure is the cleaner indicator of home demand, and it is the one falling fastest.

CPCA's weekly retail data, which track sales to consumers rather than shipments to dealers, show passenger-car retail sales of 293,000 in November 1–8, down 21% year on year. NEV retail sales were 219,000, down 7%, giving NEVs a retail penetration rate of 74.9%. Wholesale volume in the same period was 333,000, down 20% [CnEVPost]. Put simply, NEVs are declining more slowly than the overall market, which is why their share keeps rising.

Joint ventures: a sharp split

The divergence among foreign-brand joint ventures was stark. Nissan (China) Investment Co. reported October China sales of 22,295, down 67.1% year on year. Dongfeng Nissan, the joint venture with Dongfeng Motor that sells the Nissan, Venucia and Infiniti brands, posted retail sales of 18,435, and Zhengzhou Nissan sold 3,860 light commercial vehicles [36Kr].

Volkswagen brand, by contrast, said its China NEV deliveries exceeded 28,000 in October, up 346.9% and a monthly record for the brand. The ID. AURA T6 accounted for more than 12,000 and the ID. ERA 5S for 7,100. The brand plans 13 NEV models in China this year, 11 of which have launched, and aims to rank among China's top 10 NEV sellers in 2027 [Gasgoo].

Other joint ventures are pushing China-specific products. Shu Ruize, CEO of the Audi–SAIC strategic cooperation, said the AUDI brand will launch 3 new models within three years, with China as the core market and no exports to Europe; the Audi Innovation Technology Center in Shanghai plans 4 ADP 2.0-based models from 2028 [Gasgoo]. Lincoln China president Jia Mingdi said the Changan Ford-built Aviator EX8 plug-in hybrid will appear at the Guangzhou Auto Show and launch in the first half of 2027 [ITHome]. DSAT, a technology company funded by six parties including Dongfeng Motor and Stellantis, signed a strategic agreement with Volcano Engine covering smart cockpits, software and SoC chip planning; its first 4 planned NEV models will roll out from 2027 [Gasgoo].

Chinese brands: deliveries, infrastructure and new models

NIO founder William Li said cumulative deliveries of the ES9 flagship SUV reached 40,000 on November 11. The latest 10,000 took 49 days, compared with 46 days for the previous batch, and October ES9 deliveries were 6,714, down 12.87% from September [CnEVPost]. Separately, NIO Power signed an agreement with Poly Property to build charging and battery-swap infrastructure at residential, commercial, office and industrial projects; station numbers and investment were not disclosed. NIO had 4,252 swap stations in China as of November 11 [CnEVPost].

Geely's Lynk & Co unveiled the seven-seat 900L plug-in hybrid SUV, 5,280 mm long, with WLTC electric ranges of 245 km and 320 km and a launch due before the end of 2026 [CnEVPost]. Leapmotor will launch an updated Lafa 5 with an 800V platform at the Guangzhou Auto Show, which opens on November 27; the model delivered 9,486 units in October [CnEVPost]. Huawei's Richard Yu said the Stelato V8, the first MPV from HIMA's Stelato brand, will launch on November 20 after pre-sales at RMB 329,800 to 449,800 [Gasgoo].

Supply chain: lidar growth and a quality lapse

Lidar maker Hesai Group reported unaudited third-quarter net revenues of RMB 1.0683 billion, up 34.3%. Total lidar shipments were 729,673 units, up 65.3%, including 638,055 ADAS units. Net income fell 68.5% to RMB 80.8 million because the year-earlier quarter included one-time income, and the company guided fourth-quarter revenue of RMB 1.1 billion to RMB 1.15 billion [Hesai Group].

BYD apologized after front and rear rims were fitted in the wrong positions on some Da Tang vehicles with 20-inch rims. It traced the error to a coding mistake by supplier Hubei Boyu Industrial, offered free inspections and rim swaps, and said testing showed no effect on driving safety; the number of affected vehicles was not disclosed [CnEVPost].

CPCA's next weekly release will show whether November retail sales keep falling at the pace of the first eight days. The Guangzhou Auto Show opening on November 27 is the next date for several of the models mentioned above, including the updated Lafa 5 and the Aviator EX8, while the Stelato V8 launch on November 20 will reveal its final pricing.

中国国内车市仍在收缩，但新能源车在销量中的占比继续上升。中国汽车工业协会（中汽协）最新数据显示，10月全国汽车销量301.6万辆，同比下降9.2%（该口径含出口）；中汽协预计2026年乘用车国内销量将低于1900万辆。乘联会11月初的零售数据也显示需求偏弱，而合资品牌、新势力和零部件企业的最新披露则呈现出明显分化。

市场数据：国内需求继续走弱

中汽协数据显示，2026年1–10月全国汽车累计销量2628.6万辆，同比下降5.1%；其中乘用车国内销量1452.8万辆，同比下降25.2%，乘用车国内销量同比降幅已连续7个月超过20%。中汽协副秘书长陈士华表示，2026年乘用车国内销量会低于1900万辆，但“这不是趋势”。年初中汽协曾预计全年汽车销量约3475万辆。[每日经济新闻]

需要说明的是，中汽协的总销量是含出口的批发口径，不能直接当作国内消费需求；乘用车国内销量更能反映本土市场，而这一指标下滑最快。

乘联会周度初步数据显示，11月1–8日全国乘用车零售29.3万辆，同比下降21%；新能源乘用车零售21.9万辆，同比下降7%，零售渗透率为74.9%。同期乘用车批发33.3万辆，同比下降20%。[CnEVPost] 新能源零售降幅小于整体市场，因此渗透率继续走高。

合资企业：冷热不均

合资品牌之间差距明显。日产（中国）投资有限公司公布，10月日产汽车中国区销量22,295辆，同比下降67.1%；其中东风日产（含日产、启辰、英菲尼迪品牌）终端销量18,435辆，郑州日产轻型商用车销量3,860辆。[36氪]

大众汽车品牌则公布，10月在华新能源交付超过2.8万辆，同比增长346.9%，创品牌单月纪录；其中ID. AURA T6交付超1.2万辆，ID. ERA 5S交付7,100辆。大众品牌今年在华规划13款新能源车型、已推出11款，目标是2027年进入中国新能源销量前十。[盖世汽车]

其他合资企业也在加码面向中国市场的产品。奥迪与上汽战略合作首席执行官舒睿泽表示，AUDI品牌将在三年内推出3款新车，产品以中国为核心市场、不出口欧洲；位于上海的奥迪创新技术中心计划自2028年起推出4款基于ADP 2.0平台的新车。[盖世汽车] 林肯中国总裁贾鸣镝表示，由长安福特生产的插混车型飞行家EX8将在广州车展亮相，2027年上半年上市。[IT之家] 东风汽车与Stellantis等六方出资成立的神龙汽车科技（武汉）有限公司与火山引擎签署全面战略合作，涵盖智能座舱、应用软件和SoC芯片规划；其首期规划的4款新能源车型将于2027年起陆续投放。[盖世汽车]

自主品牌：交付、补能与新车

蔚来创始人李斌宣布，旗舰SUV ES9累计交付于11月11日达到40,000辆；最近1万辆用时49天，上一个1万辆用时46天。ES9 10月交付6,714辆，环比下降12.87%。[CnEVPost] 另外，蔚来能源与保利物业签约，在住宅、商业、写字楼和产业园区项目共建充换电设施，规划站点数量和投资额未披露；截至11月11日，蔚来在中国共有4,252座换电站。[CnEVPost]

吉利旗下领克发布七座插混SUV 900L，车长5,280毫米，WLTC纯电续航245公里和320公里，计划2026年底前上市。[CnEVPost] 零跑新款Lafa5将在11月27日开幕的广州车展上市，升级800V平台；Lafa5 10月交付9,486辆。[CnEVPost] 华为余承东宣布，鸿蒙智行享界品牌首款MPV享界V8将于11月20日上市，此前预售价为32.98万至44.98万元。[盖世汽车]

供应链：激光雷达增长与质量问题

激光雷达企业禾赛科技公布第三季度未经审计业绩：净营收10.683亿元，同比增长34.3%；激光雷达总出货729,673台，同比增长65.3%，其中ADAS激光雷达638,055台。净利润8,080万元，同比下降68.5%，主要因上年同期含一次性收益；公司预计第四季度营收11亿至11.5亿元。[禾赛科技公告]

比亚迪就部分20英寸轮辋版大唐前后轮辋装反致歉，称原因是供应商湖北博宇实业的编码录入错误；比亚迪提供免费检查和更换轮辋，并称经测试不影响行车安全，受影响车辆数量未披露。[CnEVPost]
