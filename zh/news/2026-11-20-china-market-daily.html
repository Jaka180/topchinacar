CPCA Sees a 70.6% New-Energy Retail Share as Leapmotor Builds Its Two Millionth Vehicle乘联分会预计新能源零售占比达70.6%，零跑第200万辆整车下线

China’s passenger-car association projects 1.15 million new-energy retail sales in November. Leapmotor reached two million vehicles built, while BYD and Chery launched new SUVs.乘联分会预计中国11月新能源乘用车零售约115万辆。零跑累计下线200万辆，比亚迪和奇瑞推出新款SUV。

By TopChinaCar Editorial作者：TopChinaCar 编辑部 · Published November 20, 2026发布于 2026-11-20 · Compiled from the primary sources cited below基于文末所列一手信源编写

China’s passenger-car market is expected to record a 70.6% new-energy retail share in November even as overall retail volume eases from October. On the manufacturing side, Leapmotor reached two million vehicles built, while Tesla’s chief executive signaled that the company intends to keep raising output in China. BYD and Chery added SUVs at different price points, and Dongfeng Nissan’s NX7 moved a step closer to its scheduled launch.

November’s new-energy retail forecast

The China Passenger Car Association, or CPCA, projected on November 19 that China’s November retail sales would reach about 1.63 million vehicles under its narrow passenger-car definition, down 1.1% from October. It forecast about 1.15 million new-energy passenger-car sales at retail, giving the category a 70.6% share of that monthly total. The forecast describes domestic passenger-car retail and covers the full month. The association projects a high share for new-energy vehicles alongside a modest month-on-month decline in the overall market. [CnEVPost]

Chinese production and component manufacturing

Leapmotor announced on November 19 that its two millionth vehicle had rolled off the production line, with its 50,000th D19 also coming off the line that day. The company said it took about five months to move from 1.5 million to two million vehicles built. Those milestones measure cumulative output. In Shenzhen on the same day, Tesla chief executive Elon Musk told the head of the China Council for the Promotion of International Trade that Tesla would keep raising output in China. The scale of the increase and its timetable were undisclosed. [IT Home] [CnEVPost]

Two chassis suppliers disclosed separate manufacturing developments. Bethel said its first motor project had entered production and that the motor would supply its WCBS brake-by-wire system, expanding the range of components Bethel makes in-house for that system. ASIMCO I-tech expects to ship more than 100,000 air supply units for air suspension in 2026. Its separately nominated central air supply system, rated at 40 liters per minute, is expected to enter mass production in 2027. [Gasgoo] [Gasgoo]

New models and features reach the domestic market

BYD’s Fang Cheng Bao launched the Tai 9, a large SUV that uses its DMS hybrid platform and serves as the flagship of the Tai series. It offered three trims priced from 279,900 to 329,900 yuan. Chery’s iCAR launched the V25, an extended-range SUV offered in two- and four-wheel-drive versions. Its four trims carry official list prices of 129,800 to 159,800 yuan. Both models launched on November 19 and occupy distinct price bands. [Autohome] [Autohome]

Li Auto added Audio Vivid spatial audio to its cars. The feature can place and dynamically render individual sound objects, and the in-car versions of NetEase Cloud Music, Qishui Music and Ximalaya already offer content in the format. [IT Home]

What to Watch

November 27 is the scheduled launch date for two models with pre-sales already underway. Yulon Nissan’s investor presentation gave that date for the China launch of the NX7 from Dongfeng Nissan, Nissan’s joint venture with Dongfeng. Leapmotor also scheduled the formal launch of its updated Lafa5 for that day at the Guangzhou Auto Show. The updated car uses an 800-volt platform and began pre-sales at 105,800 yuan on November 19. [Yulon Nissan Motor] [IT Home]

乘联分会发布11月零售预测

中国汽车流通协会乘用车市场信息联席分会11月19日估计，11月国内狭义乘用车零售约163万辆，环比下降1.1%。其中，新能源乘用车零售预计约115万辆，占该口径下全月零售的70.6%。这组预测覆盖11月全月，统计范围为国内乘用车零售。按乘联分会的预测，新能源车型将占当月零售的七成左右，同时整体零售规模较上月略有收缩。[CnEVPost]

整车生产与底盘零部件进展

零跑汽车11月19日宣布，第200万辆整车下线，同日也完成第5万辆D19的下线。零跑称，从累计生产150万辆到200万辆用时约5个月。这两个里程碑采用累计生产口径。同一天，特斯拉首席执行官埃隆·马斯克在深圳会见中国贸促会会长任鸿斌时表示，特斯拉将继续提高在华产量。增产规模和实施时间未披露。[IT之家] [CnEVPost]

两家底盘供应商分别披露了制造进展。伯特利首个电机项目已投产，产品将配套其WCBS线控制动系统，电机由此纳入该系统的自制部件。亚新科智能汽车预计，2026年其空气悬架供气单元出货量将超过10万套。亚新科另一款每分钟40升的中央气源系统已经获得客户定点，预计于2027年进入量产。[盖世汽车] [盖世汽车]

新车型与车载功能进入国内市场

比亚迪旗下方程豹推出钛9。这款钛系列旗舰大型SUV采用DMS混动平台，三款配置售价为27.99万至32.99万元。奇瑞旗下iCAR推出采用增程动力的V25，提供两驱与四驱版本，四款配置的官方指导价为12.98万至15.98万元。两款SUV均于11月19日在中国上市，覆盖不同价格区间。[汽车之家] [汽车之家]

理想汽车上线车载菁彩声Audio Vivid功能，可对声音对象进行空间定位与动态渲染。网易云音乐、汽水音乐和喜马拉雅已在理想车机端上线该格式的内容。[IT之家]
