China Passenger-Car Retail Falls 25% in Early November as Fujian Benz Starts VLE Production11月上半月中国乘用车零售同比降25%，福建奔驰VLE进入量产

Passenger-car retail fell to 611,000 vehicles in November 1–15. Fujian Benz started VLE production, and BAIC Motor proposed selling assets tied to 112 battery-swap stations.11月1日至15日，全国乘用车零售61.1万辆，同比下降25%。福建奔驰在福州启动VLE量产，北京汽车拟出售涉及112座换电站的相关资产。

By TopChinaCar Editorial作者：TopChinaCar 编辑部 · Published November 19, 2026发布于 2026-11-19 · Compiled from the primary sources cited below基于文末所列一手信源编写

China's passenger-car retail sales fell 25% from a year earlier in the first half of November, even as new energy models accounted for nearly three-quarters of vehicles sold through retail channels. Fujian Benz moved its VLE into series production in Fuzhou, while BAIC Motor disclosed a proposed sale of battery-swap assets. Two automated-driving updates tracked different stages of deployment, from a wider robotaxi service area in Guangzhou to intercity highway testing for a passenger vehicle.

New energy models account for 73.2% of passenger-car retail

Weekly figures released by the China Passenger Car Association on November 18 put nationwide passenger-car retail at 611,000 vehicles for November 1–15, down 25% year on year. Retail sales of new energy passenger cars fell 13% to 447,000 vehicles, accounting for 73.2% of the passenger-car market over the same period. New energy models recorded a smaller decline than the overall market in the same reporting window. [CnEVPost]

The share measures how much of the reported retail market came from new energy models. The volume and its year-on-year change measure how many vehicles were sold and how that figure compares with the same days a year earlier. The figures cover the first 15 days, and a full-month reading will show whether the gap between those two rates of decline persisted through November.

Fujian Benz starts production as BAIC Motor proposes an asset sale

Fujian Benz, the local Mercedes-Benz manufacturing joint venture, held a production kickoff in Minhou, Fuzhou, on November 16. The VLE is the first model to reach series production under its new energy business vehicle project. Investment exceeds RMB 4 billion and covers work on facilities including a body shop, a sorting workshop and a battery warehouse. The same project will also support the VLS product line. The production milestone in Fuzhou applies to the VLE, which had completed production preparations. The VLS remains part of the project's subsequent rollout. [Fuzhou Evening News]

BAIC Motor's November 17 filing concerns a separate operation. Its wholly owned subsidiary BAIC Powertrain proposes offering a package of assets associated with 112 battery-swap stations through public bidding at the China Beijing Equity Exchange. The package covers inventory, fixed assets, construction in progress and intangible assets. The listing floor will be based on an appraisal of approximately RMB 277.9 million. The buyer and final price were still undetermined in the disclosure. [BAIC Motor, HKEX filing]

Robotaxi coverage expands and intercity testing begins

Pony.ai said in its November 18 quarterly release that its downtown Guangzhou and Nansha robotaxi operating areas now form a contiguous service area of more than 1,000 square kilometers. Global robotaxi services revenue rose 165.4% year on year to RMB 119.1 million in the third quarter. The filing gives an operating footprint for Guangzhou and a revenue figure for the global business, without a separate figure for China. [Pony.ai]

STELATO said on November 17 that the V8 had entered intercity Level 3 highway tests, with operation at speeds of up to 120 km/h in the test setting. The announced milestone concerns testing across cities. The timing of customer access was undisclosed. [IT Home]

China outlines plans to recycle vehicles and batteries together

At a November 17 briefing held by the State Council Information Office, the Ministry of Commerce said new recycling guidance calls for scrapping, collecting and dismantling new energy vehicles together with their traction batteries. The ministry described two pieces of follow-up work: establishing a digital ID system for traction batteries and revising the rules for processing used traction batteries. [State Council Information Office briefing]

What to Watch

The full-month retail release will provide a broader view of demand and the new energy share after the midmonth reading. For the proposed swap-asset transfer, the next details to watch are the outcome of public bidding, the identity of the buyer and the final transaction price.

新能源乘用车占同期零售的73.2%

乘联分会11月18日发布的周度数据显示，11月1日至15日，全国乘用车零售61.1万辆，较去年同期下降25%。其中，新能源乘用车零售44.7万辆，同比下降13%，占同期乘用车零售的73.2%。在同一个统计窗口内，新能源乘用车的零售降幅小于乘用车整体降幅。 [CnEVPost]

市场份额反映新能源车型在同期乘用车零售中的比重，零售量及其同比变化则分别反映售出车辆数及其与去年同期的差异。这组数据覆盖11月前半个月。观察全月数据时，可以再看新能源与整体乘用车的同比降幅差距是否延续，以及新能源车型在全月零售中占多大比重。

福建奔驰启动量产，北京汽车拟转让换电资产

梅赛德斯-奔驰在当地的制造合资企业福建奔驰，于11月16日在福州闽侯举行新能源商务车项目生产启动仪式。VLE是该项目首款进入量产的车型。项目总投资超过40亿元，涉及焊装车间、分拣车间及电池仓库等设施。按项目安排，后续还将落地VLS产品序列。

这项进展对应的是福州生产端的节点：VLE已完成生产准备并转入量产，而VLS仍属于后续安排。两个序列共用这一新能源商务车项目，当前披露的量产进度指向VLE。 [福州晚报]

北京汽车11月17日披露的是另一项业务。其全资子公司北汽动力总成拟通过北京产权交易所公开挂牌，转让与112座换电站相关的资产组合。资产范围包括存货、固定资产、在建工程和无形资产。挂牌底价以约2.779亿元的资产评估值为依据，披露时买方和最终成交价尚未确定。 [北京汽车港交所公告]

无人出租车扩大服务范围，乘用车启动跨城测试

小马智行11月18日发布三季度业绩，并披露广州中心城区与南沙的Robotaxi运营区已连通，形成超过1000平方公里的连片服务范围。三季度全球Robotaxi服务收入为1.191亿元，同比增长165.4%。广州数据衡量当地运营覆盖范围，收入数字采用全球业务口径，公司未单列中国收入。 [小马智行]

享界11月17日表示，V8已经启动L3级跨城高速道路测试，测试场景覆盖最高时速120公里的高速路段。这次披露的节点是由单城测试拓展至跨城高速路测。面向消费者的功能开通时间未披露。 [IT之家]

商务部详解整车与动力电池一体化回收安排

商务部在11月17日的国新办发布会上介绍，再生资源回收指导意见提出推动新能源汽车整车与动力电池一体化报废、回收和拆解。发布会还明确了两项后续工作，分别是建立动力电池数字身份证管理制度，以及修订废旧动力电池综合利用规范条件。 [国新办发布会]

值得关注

下一次全月零售数据可用来观察11月需求以及新能源车型份额在月中读数之后的变化。北汽动力总成的换电资产转让则可关注公开竞价结果，包括能否确定买方，以及最终成交价。
