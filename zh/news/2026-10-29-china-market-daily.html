China Car Retail Falls 26% in Late October as Industry Profit Drops 23%; Li Auto, BYD Refresh Key Models10月下旬乘用车零售同比降26%，行业前三季度利润降23%；理想、比亚迪更新主力车型

CPCA data show passenger car retail at 1.145 million units on Oct 1-25, down 26% year on year, while auto industry profit fell 23% in January-September. Li Auto and BYD launched updated volume models.乘联分会数据显示，10月1–25日全国乘用车零售114.5万辆，同比下降26%；崔东树整理的数据显示前三季度汽车行业利润同比下降23%。理想、比亚迪、红旗等同日推出新车。

By TopChinaCar Editorial作者：TopChinaCar 编辑部 · Published October 29, 2026发布于 2026-10-29 · Compiled from the primary sources cited below基于文末所列一手信源编写

AI-generated briefing. China's domestic car market remained weak through most of October. Preliminary figures from the China Passenger Car Association (CPCA) put retail sales at 1.145 million units on Oct 1-25, down 26% year on year, and separate data compiled by CPCA Secretary-General Cui Dongshu show auto industry profit fell 23% in the first nine months. Against that backdrop, automakers kept launching updated models, and third-quarter results from suppliers began to arrive.

Demand: Retail Still Well Below Last Year

The CPCA, which tracks passenger vehicles only, said retail sales on Oct 1-25 were flat month on month but down 26% from the same period a year earlier. Manufacturer wholesale volume, which measures shipments from factories to dealers and includes exports, was 1.384 million units, down 22%. New energy vehicles (NEVs, covering battery-electric and plug-in hybrid cars) held up better: NEV retail was 753,000 units, down 13% year on year and 1% month on month, for a retail penetration of 65.7%. NEV wholesale rose 3% to 1.008 million units. [CnEVPost]

These are partial-month, preliminary figures; the CPCA usually publishes full-month data in early November.

Profitability: Margins Thin to 3.3%

Data published on Oct 27 by Cui Dongshu show China's auto industry revenue rose 2.6% to 8,043.5 billion yuan in January-September 2026, while profit fell 23% to 267.5 billion yuan, a margin of 3.3%. September alone was weaker: profit of 14.2 billion yuan was down 68%, and the 1.4% margin was below the 1.8% monthly low of December 2025. The article attributes the squeeze mainly to higher memory-chip and lithium carbonate prices and weak consumer demand. [Gasgoo]

Supplier results reflected the pressure in different ways. HASCO (Huayu Automotive Systems), the parts maker controlled by SAIC Motor, said third-quarter revenue fell 2.28% to 45.529 billion yuan and net profit attributable to shareholders fell 6.42% to 1.687 billion yuan. [Jiemian] Battery maker Gotion High-Tech grew third-quarter revenue 74.62% to 17.662 billion yuan but posted a net loss of 537 million yuan; fair-value changes and disposal losses on financial assets totalled 891 million yuan, and the company cited fair-value changes in equity holdings and exchange-rate movements. [Yicai]

Products: Updates Rather Than Price Cuts

Li Auto launched the 2026 i6 on Oct 28 at 249,800 yuan, unchanged from the 2025 model, with a 239,800-yuan limited-time price for orders by Nov 30. The battery grows from 87.3 kWh to 96 kWh, giving the rear-wheel-drive version 780 km of CLTC range, and driver assistance moves from Nvidia's Thor-U to Li Auto's in-house Mach M100 chip, rated at 1,280 TOPS. [CnEVPost]

BYD put the third-generation Qin Plus DM-i plug-in hybrid sedan on sale on Oct 27 in three versions, with guide prices of 79,900-100,900 yuan and limited-time prices from 76,900 yuan. The wheelbase grows to 2,800 mm and CLTC electric range reaches up to 220 km. [CnEVPost]

State-owned FAW Group had two launches at opposite ends of the market. Its Hongqi brand began selling the G919, a four-motor luxury off-road SUV with a 2.0T range extender and an 800V silicon-carbide platform, at 349,800-429,800 yuan. [Gasgoo] FAW Bestune launched a five-door version of its Pony mini EV at 44,900-54,900 yuan, with 222-328 km of CLTC range. [CarNewsChina]

Great Wall Motor's Wey brand released images of the second-generation Lanshan, a full-size plug-in hybrid SUV with a combined 512 kW output, due in China by year-end; pricing was not disclosed. [CarNewsChina]

Supply Chain: Batteries and Electronics

CATL told investors on Oct 27 that it expects small-batch production of all-solid-state batteries in 2027 and larger-scale mass production around 2030. According to Gasgoo, its sulfide cells have reached 20Ah sample trial production, with an energy-density target of about 500 Wh/kg. [Gasgoo]

United Automotive Electronic Systems (UAES) said its new zone controller platform has entered mass production. By its own account, the product line covered more than 60 models from over ten customers as of end-August, and it expects to supply over 3 million units in 2026. [Gasgoo]

What to Watch

  • The CPCA's full-month October figures, due in early November, will show whether the late-month pattern holds.
  • More third-quarter reports from listed automakers and suppliers are due in the coming days.

AI 生成简报。10月国内车市整体仍然偏弱。乘联分会初步数据显示，10月1–25日全国乘用车零售114.5万辆，同比下降26%；乘联分会秘书长崔东树整理的数据显示，前三季度汽车行业利润同比下降23%。在这一背景下，车企继续推出改款和新车，零部件企业的三季报也陆续发布。

需求：零售仍明显低于去年同期

乘联分会只统计乘用车。其数据显示，10月1–25日全国乘用车零售与上月同期持平，但同比下降26%；厂商批发（指厂家发往经销商的数量，含出口）138.4万辆，同比下降22%。新能源乘用车表现相对较好：零售75.3万辆，同比下降13%、环比下降1%，零售渗透率65.7%；新能源批发100.8万辆，同比增长3%。[CnEVPost]

以上为月内初步数据，乘联分会通常在11月初发布10月全月数据。

盈利：行业利润率降至3.3%

崔东树10月27日发布的数据显示，2026年1–9月汽车行业营业收入80435亿元，同比增长2.6%；利润2675亿元，同比下降23%，利润率3.3%。9月单月更弱：利润142亿元，同比下降68%，利润率1.4%，低于2025年12月1.8%的月度低点。文章将利润承压主要归因于存储芯片和碳酸锂涨价以及消费需求偏弱。[盖世汽车]

零部件企业的三季报呈现不同情况。上汽集团旗下的华域汽车公告，第三季度营业收入455.29亿元，同比下降2.28%；归母净利润16.87亿元，同比下降6.42%。[界面新闻]电池企业国轩高科第三季度营收176.62亿元，同比增长74.62%，但归母净利润亏损5.37亿元；当季持有金融资产的公允价值变动及处置损益为-8.91亿元，公司称利润下滑主要受持有股票资产公允价值变动和汇率变动影响。[第一财经]

产品：以配置升级为主

理想汽车10月28日上市2026款i6，零售价249,800元，与2025款持平；11月30日前购车可享239,800元限时价。电池容量由87.3 kWh增至96 kWh，后驱版CLTC续航780公里；辅助驾驶芯片由英伟达Thor-U换为自研马赫M100，算力1,280 TOPS。[CnEVPost]

比亚迪10月27日上市第三代秦PLUS DM-i插混轿车，共3个版本，指导价79,900–100,900元，限时价76,900元起。新车轴距增至2,800毫米，CLTC纯电续航最高220 km。[CnEVPost]

一汽集团在高低两端各有新车。红旗G919四电机豪华越野SUV上市，采用2.0T增程动力和800V碳化硅平台，官方指导价34.98万–42.98万元。[盖世汽车]一汽奔腾上市小马五门版微型纯电车，售价44,900–54,900元，CLTC续航222–328公里。[CarNewsChina]

长城汽车旗下魏牌发布第二代蓝山全尺寸插混SUV官图，系统综合功率512 kW，计划年底前进入国内市场，价格未披露。[CarNewsChina]

供应链：电池与电子

宁德时代10月27日在互动易平台回复投资者称，预计2027年实现全固态电池小批量生产，2030年前后规模化量产。据盖世汽车报道，其硫化物电芯已进入20Ah样品试制阶段，能量密度目标约500 Wh/kg。[盖世汽车]

联合电子宣布区域控制器新平台进入批量生产阶段。据企业自述，截至2026年8月底该类产品已覆盖十余家客户的60多个车型，预计2026年全年供货超300万。[盖世汽车]

  • 未来几天将有更多上市车企和零部件企业发布三季报。
