Geely's Overseas Sales Jump 169% as GAC Starts Battery Production in Thailand吉利前三季度海外销量增长169%，广汽泰国电池工厂投产

Geely sold 797,670 cars outside China in Jan–Sep, up 169%, while GAC's battery arm Inpow rolled out its first Thai-built pack. Chery's Tiggo 2 got zero stars from Latin NCAP, and Chinese brands kept expanding in Australia.吉利前三季度海外销量 797,670 辆、同比增长 169%；广汽旗下因湃电池泰国工厂首个电池包下线。奇瑞瑞虎2在拉美 NCAP 获零星评级，中国品牌继续在澳大利亚扩张。

By TopChinaCar Editorial作者：TopChinaCar 编辑部 · Published October 30, 2026发布于 2026-10-30 · Compiled from the primary sources cited below基于文末所列一手信源编写

Geely's third-quarter results put a hard number on how far Chinese automakers now depend on markets outside China: the group sold 797,670 vehicles abroad in the first nine months of 2026, up 169% year on year. On the same day, GAC moved part of its battery supply chain to Thailand, a sign that localisation is spreading from vehicle assembly to core components. Meanwhile, a zero-star crash rating for a Chery model in Latin America and a rush of activity in Australia show how much more than volume is now at stake.

Geely: Overseas Is Now the Growth Engine

Geely Auto said on Oct 29 that its Jan–Sep 2026 overseas sales rose 169% to 797,670, topping 100,000 a month for four straight months from June to September. Electrified NEVs made up 61% of nine-month exports, and Lynk & Co's overseas sales grew 151%. Q3 revenue was RMB 98.96 billion, up 10%. [GlobeNewswire]

The mix matters as much as the volume. With NEVs now the majority of its exports, Geely is less exposed to markets that still favour combustion cars. It is targeting 1 million overseas sales in 2026 and plans to take the Emgrand EM-i, Zhanjian 700 and Lynk & Co 07 GT abroad, which means it must keep up its recent monthly pace through the fourth quarter.

GAC: From Car Plant to Battery Plant in Thailand

Inpow, GAC Group's battery manufacturing arm, officially began production at its Thailand plant on Oct 29, rolling out its first locally built battery pack; capacity and investment were not disclosed. Inpow had built 300,000 packs worldwide by Oct 15, supplying Aion models including the i60, N60 and UT. [CarNewsChina]

GAC's Thai vehicle plant has capacity of up to 50,000 a year, and 34,898 GAC cars were registered in Thailand from its 2023 entry through August 2026. Putting battery production next to vehicle assembly deepens GAC's local content in Thailand, which helps on cost and with local industrial policy.

Chery: A Safety Warning in Latin America, a Network Push in Australia

Latin NCAP said on Oct 28 that Chery's Tiggo 2, including the Tiggo 2 Pro, Pro Max and Mexico's Chirey Tiggo 2, scored zero stars under its 2026 protocols, with 0% for both adult and child occupant protection, 49% for pedestrian protection and 45% for safety assist. The structure and footwell were unstable in the frontal test, which also flagged a fire risk. The Tiggo 2 is an entry-level compact SUV for emerging markets in Southeast Asia, Latin America and the Middle East. [CarNewsChina] As safety standards rise in emerging markets, entry-level models are where Chinese exporters are most exposed.

In Australia, Chery's Lepas brand expects to open 23 dealers in November 2026, Chery's third standalone network there after Chery and Omoda Jaecoo. Lepas debuted with the L6 EV and has tentatively confirmed at least 8 more models for Australia before 2028, and five more Chery-owned brands (Exlantix, Freelander, iCaur, Jetour and Luxeed) are due in 2027. [CarExpert]

Australia: Launches, Service Networks and a Security Question

GWM's new-generation Haval H7 has reached its 130 Australian dealers with all-wheel drive across the range, in hybrid and plug-in hybrid forms priced from A$46,990 to A$53,990 drive-away. The PHEV has 137 km of WLTP electric range. [CarExpert]

BYD's Denza will add 17 authorised service points in regional Australia, all at BYD dealerships, after delivering 3,339 cars from early 2026 to the end of September. BYD has more than 120 retail locations in Australia and plans to reach 146 by December. [CarExpert] The competition is moving from showrooms into after-sales coverage outside the big cities.

On policy, Defence Minister Richard Marles said on Oct 28 that Australia has not banned Chinese cars from military sites and that the US has not asked it to, even though the US barred BYD vehicles from Kadena Air Base in Okinawa this month. [CarExpert]

Southeast Asia: MG Bets on Local Assembly in Malaysia

SAIC Motor Malaysia launched the locally assembled (CKD) MG ZS on Oct 29 in 1.5 Turbo and Hybrid+ forms, priced at RM103,900 and RM109,900 on the road excluding insurance. The Hybrid+ delivers a combined 215 PS and 465 Nm. [paultan.org]

What to Watch

  • Whether Geely's monthly overseas sales stay above 100,000 through the fourth quarter as it chases its 1 million target.
  • How Chery responds to the Latin NCAP result, and whether more governments follow the US in restricting Chinese cars at sensitive sites.

吉利汽车的三季报用一个数字说明了中国车企对海外市场的依赖程度：2026 年前三季度海外销量 797,670 辆，同比增长 169%。同一天，广汽把电池产能搬到了泰国，本地化正从整车组装延伸到核心零部件。与此同时，奇瑞一款车型在拉美碰撞测试中获零星评级，中国品牌在澳大利亚密集扩张，出海的比拼已不只是销量。

吉利：海外成为增长主引擎

吉利汽车 10 月 29 日公布 2026 年三季度业绩：前三季度海外销量同比增长 169% 至 797,670 辆，6–9 月连续 4 个月单月海外销量超过 10 万辆。电气化新能源车型占前三季度出口的 61%，领克海外销量同比增长 151%。三季度营收 989.6 亿元，同比增长 10%。[GlobeNewswire]

结构和规模同样重要。新能源已占吉利出口的多数，这让它更少依赖仍以燃油车为主的市场。公司提出 2026 年海外销量 100 万辆的目标，并计划把帝豪 EM-i、战舰 700 和领克 07 GT 推向海外，这意味着第四季度需要继续保持近几个月的高位。

广汽：在泰国从整车厂走到电池厂

广汽集团电池产业化平台因湃电池（Inpow）泰国工厂于 10 月 29 日正式投产，首个本地生产的动力电池包下线，产能和投资额未披露。截至 10 月 15 日，因湃电池全球累计生产电池包 30 万个，产品已用于埃安 i60、N60、UT 等车型。[CarNewsChina]

广汽泰国整车工厂年产能最高 5 万辆，自 2023 年进入泰国至 2026 年 8 月累计上牌 34,898 辆。电池生产与整车组装就近配套，提高了广汽在泰国的本地化程度，有助于控制成本、满足当地产业政策。

奇瑞：拉美安全警示与澳洲渠道扩张

拉美新车评价规程（Latin NCAP）10 月 28 日公布结果，奇瑞瑞虎2（含 Tiggo 2 Pro、Pro Max 及墨西哥市场的 Chirey Tiggo 2）按 2026 年新规获零星评级：成人乘员保护和儿童乘员保护得分均为 0%，行人保护 49%，安全辅助 45%；正面碰撞中车身结构和脚部空间不稳定，并发现起火风险。瑞虎2是面向东南亚、拉美和中东等新兴市场的入门级紧凑型 SUV。[CarNewsChina] 新兴市场的安全标准正在提高，入门车型是中国出口车企最容易暴露短板的地方。

在澳大利亚，奇瑞旗下品牌 Lepas 预计 2026 年 11 月开设 23 家经销店，这是奇瑞在澳继奇瑞、欧萌达·杰酷之后的第三套独立渠道。Lepas 以纯电 L6 登陆澳洲，并初步确认 2028 年前在澳推出至少 8 款新车型；星纪元、Freelander、iCaur、捷途和智界五个奇瑞系品牌也计划 2027 年进入澳大利亚。[CarExpert]

澳大利亚：新车、售后网络与安全议题

长城汽车新一代哈弗 H7 已进入其在澳大利亚的 130 家经销店，全系改为四驱，提供混动和插混两种动力，驾乘价 46,990 至 53,990 澳元，插混版 WLTP 纯电续航 137 公里。[CarExpert]

比亚迪旗下腾势将在澳大利亚地区城市新增 17 个授权服务点，全部设在比亚迪经销店内；2026 年初至 9 月底腾势在澳交付 3,339 辆。比亚迪在澳零售网点超过 120 家，计划 12 月达到 146 家。[CarExpert] 竞争正从大城市展厅延伸到地区城市的售后覆盖。

政策方面，澳大利亚国防部长马尔斯 10 月 28 日表示，澳方尚未禁止中国汽车进入军事设施，美方也未提出类似要求；而美军本月已禁止比亚迪车辆进入日本冲绳嘉手纳空军基地。[CarExpert]

东南亚：MG 在马来西亚押注本地组装

上汽马来西亚 10 月 29 日推出本地 CKD 组装的新款 MG ZS，提供 1.5T 和 Hybrid+ 两种动力，售价分别为 103,900 令吉和 109,900 令吉（上路价，不含保险）；Hybrid+ 系统综合输出 215 马力、465 牛·米。[paultan.org]
