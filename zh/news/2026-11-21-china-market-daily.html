Leapmotor Revenue Rises as STELATO V8 Deliveries Begin and China Adds Chargers零跑三季度营收增长，享界V8开启交付，中国充电枪总量上升

Leapmotor’s quarterly revenue rose 63.0%, STELATO began China deliveries of the V8, and national monitoring data put October’s EV charging connector count at 25.391 million.零跑三季度营收同比增长63.0%，享界V8在中国上市并开始交付。国家能源局数据显示，10月底全国电动汽车充电枪达2539.1万个。

By TopChinaCar Editorial作者：TopChinaCar 编辑部 · Published November 21, 2026发布于 2026-11-21 · Compiled from the primary sources cited below基于文末所列一手信源编写

Leapmotor reported higher revenue alongside a lower gross margin for the third quarter, while its sales network in China reached 1,161 stores. In the domestic vehicle market, the STELATO V8 went on sale and began deliveries, and the SAIC-GM joint venture announced the first Buick model to use its new plug-in hybrid system. A separate release from China’s National Energy Administration put the number of EV charging connectors at 25.391 million at the end of October.

Leapmotor’s sales network and quarterly results

In its unaudited release on November 20, Leapmotor reported group revenue of RMB 31.7 billion, up 63.0% from a year earlier, and a gross margin of 10.9%, down from 14.5% a year earlier. The figures describe group performance. The disclosure also provides a measure of the company’s sales presence within China. At the end of September, the company had 1,161 sales stores across 306 Chinese cities. [Leapmotor filing on HKEX]

The company reported 310,052 global deliveries in the quarter and separately disclosed 63,278 exports. The two vehicle figures use different reporting scopes. The global delivery number covers the company’s operations across markets, while the store count describes its domestic distribution network at a specific point in time. [Leapmotor filing on HKEX]

New models and trim choices for China

The STELATO V8 entered the market on November 20 with seven variants priced from RMB 319,800 to RMB 429,800. The family-oriented multipurpose vehicle is available in battery-electric and range-extended versions, both within the same model line. It uses Huawei’s Qiankun ADS 5.0 driver assistance system, and customer deliveries began at launch. [Autohome]

SAIC-GM, the joint venture, announced its Zhenlong Plug-in Hybrid MAX system on the same day. It pairs a 2.0-liter turbocharged engine developed for hybrid use with an 800-volt architecture. The first vehicle planned to use the system is Buick’s Electra Encasa multipurpose vehicle, which is due to appear publicly at the Guangzhou Auto Show on November 27. [CNS One News]

Other changes were at trim level. GAC Trumpchi expanded its plug-in hybrid Yue 7 lineup with a two-wheel-drive version equipped with lidar at a list price of RMB 195,800. [Autohome] Changan Nevo added the Q06 602 Yuexiang Plus at RMB 139,900, the previous entry trim’s list price. It adds features such as ventilated and heated front seats, with an upgrade available to customers who had already ordered that entry trim. [BitAuto] Shangjie’s Z7T wagon gained two Xingye package variants emphasizing exterior styling, wheels and interior changes, priced at RMB 274,800 and RMB 319,800. [IT Home]

Public and private charging

China’s National Energy Administration, the government agency that released the national monitoring figures, reported 25.391 million charging connectors at the end of October, up 36.2% year on year. The total included 5.283 million public connectors, up 16.5%, and 20.108 million private connectors, up 42.5%. These figures count connectors and show the public and private parts of the installed total. [National Energy Administration]

What to Watch

The November 27 Guangzhou Auto Show appearance is the next announced milestone for Buick’s Electra Encasa and the first planned vehicle application of SAIC-GM’s new plug-in hybrid system. The STELATO V8 has already reached the delivery stage. The two announcements describe different points in the rollout of new vehicles for the Chinese market. [CNS One News] [Autohome]

零跑的国内销售网络和三季度业绩

零跑汽车11月20日公布未经审核的三季度业绩。集团营收为317亿元，同比增长63.0%，毛利率由上年同期的14.5%降至10.9%。这两项指标采用集团口径。公告同时单列了国内销售渠道数据，截至9月底，零跑在中国有1,161家销售网点，覆盖306座城市。 [零跑汽车港交所公告]

公司披露三季度全球交付310,052辆，并单独公布了63,278辆的出口量。全球交付和出口采用各自的统计口径。全球交付量反映企业在各市场的交付规模，销售网点数量则是特定时点的国内渠道数据。 [零跑汽车港交所公告]

国内新车上市与配置调整

享界V8于11月20日上市，共推出七款车型，售价31.98万至42.98万元。这款面向家庭市场的中大型多用途车，在同一个车系内提供纯电和增程两种动力选择，搭载华为乾崑ADS 5.0辅助驾驶系统。享界在上市当天启动了面向客户的交付。 [汽车之家]

合资车企上汽通用同日发布真龙插混MAX系统。系统采用2.0升涡轮增压混动专用发动机和800伏高压架构。别克至境世家多用途车是计划首搭这套系统的车型，上汽通用计划于11月27日在广州车展向公众展示该车。 [中讯社]

另有几款已经在售的车型新增配置。广汽传祺为插电混动SUV越7增加两驱激光雷达版，厂商指导价为19.58万元。 [汽车之家] 长安启源Q06推出602越享+型，13.99万元的售价与此前入门车型一致，增加前排座椅通风和加热等配置，已经预订原入门车型的客户也可升级。 [易车原创报道] 尚界Z7T猎装车新增两款星野套装版，分别定价27.48万元和31.98万元，主要调整外观、轮毂和内饰。 [IT之家]

公共和私人充电设施

国家能源局发布的全国监测数据显示，截至2026年10月底，电动汽车充电枪总数为2539.1万个，同比增长36.2%。其中，公共充电枪为528.3万个，同比增长16.5%，私人充电枪为2010.8万个，同比增长42.5%。这组数据以充电枪为单位，分别列出了公共和私人设施在总量中的构成。 [国家能源局]
