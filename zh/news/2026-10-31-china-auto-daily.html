Changan Hits Record Overseas Quarter as Currency Losses Squeeze Chinese Automakers' Q3 Profits长安海外销量创单季纪录，汇兑损失挤压中国车企三季度利润

Changan sold a record 270,000 cars abroad in Q3 (+64.33%), and SAIC's Jan–Sep overseas sales hit 1.166 million, but FX losses and a delayed overseas subsidy cut into profits at Changan, SAIC and GWM.长安三季度海外销量27万辆（同比+64.33%）创单季纪录，上汽前三季度海外销量116.6万辆，但汇兑损失和海外补贴延期拖累了长安、上汽和长城的利润。

By TopChinaCar Editorial作者：TopChinaCar 编辑部 · Published October 31, 2026发布于 2026-10-31 · Compiled from the primary sources cited below基于文末所列一手信源编写

Third-quarter earnings season has exposed a widening gap at China's carmakers between how fast they sell abroad and how much they keep. Changan, SAIC and Great Wall Motor (GWM) all reported strong overseas growth, but each pointed to overseas-linked headwinds, from currency losses to a delayed tax subsidy, as a drag on profit. Meanwhile, in Indonesia, three Chinese brands broke into the top ten in September.

Changan: a record quarter abroad, at a cost

Changan's Q3 report shows overseas sales of 270,000 in the quarter, up 64.33% and a quarterly record. The company has been investing heavily to get there, putting about 5.46 billion yuan of strategic resources into overseas markets in the first three quarters [Jiemian].

The bill is showing up in its accounts. FX losses came to about 560 million yuan in Q3, and while Jan–Sep revenue was 101.884 billion yuan, net profit attributable to shareholders fell 98.06% to just 59.2753 million yuan [Jiemian]. As overseas volumes grow, currency management and the payback on that investment will shape Changan's earnings.

SAIC and GWM: overseas carries volume, but not profit

SAIC's overseas sales reached 1.166 million in Jan–Sep, up 52.4% and already above its 2025 full-year total. Yet its Q3 net profit attributable to shareholders fell 76.7% to 486 million yuan, and its nine-month profit fell 30.4% to 5.638 billion yuan. SAIC blamed the drop mainly on the capital markets, citing fair-value changes on financial assets and FX losses in its overseas business; financial expenses were 4.052 billion yuan, against -1.142 billion a year earlier [The Paper].

GWM tells a similar story. Its Jan–Sep overseas sales rose 42.43% to 476,000, the main prop for its volumes, and revenue grew 4.09%, but net profit fell 55.69%. The company said a delay in collecting an overseas tax subsidy and currency swings weighed on profit. In Q3 alone, revenue was 57.767 billion yuan (-5.68%) and net profit was 1.361 billion yuan (-40.75%) [21st Century Business Herald].

Taken together, the three reports suggest that for Chinese automakers with a growing share of sales abroad, exchange rates and overseas policy terms have moved from footnotes to real drivers of profit.

Indonesia: Chinese brands move into the top ten

September data from Gasgoo show Chinese brands making strong gains in Indonesia's light-vehicle market. BYD sold 6,031 cars, up 454.3%, making it the best-selling Chinese brand and No. 3 overall; its Atto 1 sold 4,840 units, making it the No. 2 model [Gasgoo].

Chery's Omoda & Jaecoo sold 3,586 cars (+770.4%) and Geely sold 2,273 (+1,347.8%), both entering the top ten brands. Three Chinese automakers in the top ten of a market long dominated by Japanese brands marks a shift from isolated wins to an established presence. Small, affordable electric cars such as the Atto 1 are a big part of that shift, putting pressure on the combustion-engine models that Japanese brands have long relied on in Indonesia.

What to Watch

  • Whether Chinese automakers step up currency hedging and local production as overseas revenue grows, to protect margins from FX swings.
  • Whether GWM collects its delayed overseas tax subsidy, and how Changan's overseas investment translates into profit in the coming quarters.

三季报密集披露，中国车企海外销量与利润之间的反差越来越明显。长安、上汽和长城都交出了强劲的海外增长，但各自也都把汇兑损失、海外补贴延期等与海外相关的因素列为利润承压的原因。与此同时，在印尼市场，三家中国品牌在9月同时进入前十。

长安：海外创单季纪录，代价不小

长安汽车三季报显示，三季度海外销量27万辆，同比增长64.33%，刷新单季纪录。为了实现这一增长，长安前三季度在海外战略资源上累计投入约54.6亿元。[界面新闻]

海外扩张的成本也体现在财报上：三季度公司汇兑损失约5.6亿元；前三季度营收1018.84亿元，归母净利润仅5927.53万元，同比下降98.06%。[界面新闻] 随着海外体量持续扩大，汇率管理能力和海外投入的回报节奏，将直接决定长安未来几个季度的利润表现。

上汽与长城：海外撑起销量，却没能撑起利润

上汽集团前三季度海外销售116.6万辆，同比增长52.4%，已超过2025年全年海外销量。但第三季度归母净利润4.86亿元，同比下降76.7%；前三季度归母净利润56.38亿元，同比下降30.4%。公司称利润下降主要受资本市场影响，以及金融资产公允价值变动和海外业务汇兑损失所致；前三季度财务费用为40.52亿元，上年同期为-11.42亿元。[澎湃新闻]

长城汽车的情况类似。1–9月海外销量47.60万辆，同比增长42.43%，是稳住销量的主要支撑；同期营收增长4.09%，归母净利润却同比下滑55.69%。公司称，海外税收政策补贴收益收回延期以及汇率波动共同压制了利润。单看三季度，营收577.67亿元，同比下降5.68%；归母净利润13.61亿元，同比下降40.75%。[21世纪经济报道]

三份财报放在一起看，对于海外销量占比越来越高的中国车企来说，汇率和海外政策条件已经从财报脚注变成了影响利润的关键变量。

印尼：中国品牌集体进入前十

据盖世汽车数据，9月印尼轻型车市场中，中国品牌表现突出。比亚迪销量6,031辆，同比增长454.3%，是销量最高的中国品牌，全市场排名第三；其中Atto 1售出4,840辆，位列车型榜第二。[盖世汽车]

奇瑞旗下欧萌达与杰酷9月销量3,586辆，同比增长770.4%；吉利销量2,273辆，同比增长1,347.8%，二者均进入品牌前十。在长期由日系品牌主导的印尼市场，三家中国车企同时进入前十，意味着中国品牌已经从零星突破走向成规模存在。

值得关注

  • 随着海外收入占比提升，中国车企是否会加大汇率对冲和本地化生产，以减少汇率波动对利润的冲击。
  • 长城延期的海外税收补贴能否收回，以及长安的海外投入在未来几个季度能否转化为利润。
