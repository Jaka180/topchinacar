Leapmotor Takes the A10 to Thailand and Malaysia as Chery Weighs a London Home for Jaecoo零跑A10登陆泰国和马来西亚，奇瑞考虑把捷酷总部设在伦敦

Leapmotor launched the A10 (B03X) in Thailand and will CKD-assemble it in Malaysia, after exporting 27,454 cars in September. Chery is considering London as Jaecoo's headquarters after the Jaecoo 7 topped UK sales.零跑A10（海外名B03X）在泰国上市，并将在马来西亚CKD组装，其9月出口27,454辆；捷酷7登顶英国9月新车销量后，奇瑞正考虑把捷酷品牌总部设在伦敦。

By TopChinaCar Editorial作者：TopChinaCar 编辑部 · Published November 1, 2026发布于 2026-11-01 · Compiled from the primary sources cited below基于文末所列一手信源编写

A quiet weekend window still sent a clear signal about where Chinese automakers are heading: deeper local roots. Leapmotor launched its A10 small electric SUV, sold abroad as the B03X, in Thailand on Oct 30 and showed it in Malaysia a day later ahead of local assembly. In the UK, Chery is considering London as the headquarters for its Jaecoo brand, while BYD is preparing a coach built specifically for British operators. In Australia, Geely's Zeekr opened orders for an updated 7X.

Leapmotor builds out Southeast Asia

Leapmotor unveiled the A10 in Bangkok on Oct 30, initially selling it through 19 showrooms. It has not yet given local pricing or a delivery date. The launch came with a broader plan: an updated C10 for Thailand this year, 30 sales and service outlets by year-end, and a local spare-parts warehouse and technical service centre [CnEVPost]. Building after-sales capacity at the same time as the launch suggests Leapmotor is planning for the long term in Thailand rather than testing the water.

The export numbers back that up. Leapmotor shipped 27,454 cars abroad in September, up 298.58% year on year and 50.39% from August, bringing January–September exports to 159,572, up 322.46%. Its overseas business is run by Leapmotor International, a joint venture in which Stellantis holds 51%.

A day after the Bangkok launch, the B03X made its Malaysian debut at ACE 2026 as a right-hand-drive car. It will be CKD-assembled in Gurun, Kedah, with launch expected in the first half of 2027. Its front motor makes 197 PS and 200 Nm, and DC fast charging peaks at 133 kW [paultan.org]. The C10 is already assembled in Malaysia, so the B03X would be the second locally built Leapmotor model in the country.

Chery eyes London for Jaecoo

Chery International president Zhang Guibing told Australian media in Wuhu that London is being considered as Jaecoo's headquarters, and that Chery wants to put some brand headquarters outside China. He gave no timetable [CarExpert].

The UK case is strong. The Jaecoo 7, sold there as the J7, was Britain's best-selling new car in September with 10,814 registrations, and Omoda and Jaecoo together registered 22,298 cars that month. Chery also plans to open an R&D centre at UTAC Millbrook later this year, focused on chassis and driver-assistance tuning. A London headquarters would put sales, engineering and brand management for Jaecoo in the same market.

BYD tailors a coach for Britain

BYD's UK commercial vehicle managing director Frank Thorpe said a battery-electric coach developed specifically for the UK is expected by the end of 2026. It is not derived from an existing BYD coach, and its battery capacity has not been disclosed; it is expected to use the same 2×150 kW powertrain as BYD's city and intercity buses [electrive]. BYD has delivered or has orders for more than 1,000 electric buses in the UK, so the coach extends an established business into a new segment with a product designed for local needs.

Zeekr adds power, not price, in Australia

Orders for the updated Zeekr 7X opened in Australia on Nov 1, with first deliveries expected in December. Every version gains 25 kW: the RWD and Long Range RWD now make 335 kW and the Performance AWD 500 kW. Prices are unchanged, starting at A$57,900 before on-road costs [CarExpert]. Geely's premium brand is choosing product updates over price cuts to stay competitive.

What to Watch

Watch for Leapmotor's Thai pricing and delivery start for the A10, and for any decision on a Jaecoo headquarters in London. October export figures from Chinese automakers, due in the coming days, will show whether September's surge in shipments carried into the fourth quarter.

本期窗口正好赶上周末，海外新闻不多，但方向很清楚：中国车企正在海外扎得更深。零跑小型纯电SUV A10（海外名B03X）10月30日在泰国上市，第二天又在马来西亚亮相，将在当地组装。在英国，奇瑞正考虑把捷酷品牌总部设在伦敦，比亚迪则在准备一款专为英国开发的长途大巴。在澳大利亚，吉利旗下极氪的7X改款开放订购。

零跑：深耕东南亚

零跑10月30日在曼谷发布A10，首批在19家展厅销售，暂未公布当地售价和交付时间。与新车一起公布的还有一揽子计划：年内在泰国推出改款C10，年底前把销售服务网点扩到30家，并建设本地备件仓库和技术服务中心[CnEVPost]。上市和售后体系同步搭建，说明零跑在泰国打的是长期战，而不是试水。

出口数据也支撑这种投入。零跑9月出口27,454辆，同比增长298.58%，环比增长50.39%；1–9月累计出口159,572辆，同比增长322.46%。零跑的海外业务由它与Stellantis的合资公司零跑国际负责，Stellantis持股51%。

曼谷发布会第二天，B03X在马来西亚ACE 2026车展首次亮相，展车为右舵版。这款车将在吉打州居林CKD组装，预计2027年上半年上市；前置电机最大功率197 PS、峰值扭矩200 Nm，直流快充最高133 kW[paultan.org]。C10已在马来西亚组装，B03X将成为零跑在当地组装的第二款车型。

奇瑞：捷酷总部或落户伦敦

奇瑞国际总裁张贵兵在芜湖对澳大利亚媒体表示，正考虑把捷酷品牌总部设在伦敦，并希望把部分品牌总部放到中国以外，但没有公布时间表[CarExpert]。

英国有足够的市场基础。捷酷7（英国名J7）9月在英国注册10,814辆，是当月英国最畅销的新车；欧萌达和捷酷9月在英国合计注册22,298辆。奇瑞还计划年内在英国UTAC Millbrook开设研发中心，侧重底盘和辅助驾驶调校。如果总部落户伦敦，捷酷在英国就会同时拥有销售、研发和品牌管理。

比亚迪：为英国定制长途大巴

比亚迪英国商用车总经理Frank Thorpe称，一款专为英国市场开发的纯电长途大巴预计2026年底前推出，并非现有长途车型的衍生版，电池容量暂未公布；该车预计沿用比亚迪城市和城际巴士的2×150 kW动力系统[electrive]。比亚迪在英国已交付或获订的纯电巴士超过1,000辆，这款长途大巴是把已有的公交业务延伸到新的细分市场，而且是按当地需求专门开发的产品。

极氪：在澳加功率不加价

极氪7X改款11月1日在澳大利亚开放订购，首批交付预计在12月。各版本功率均提升25 kW：后驱版和长续航后驱版为335 kW，高性能四驱版为500 kW。售价维持不变，后驱版起价57,900澳元（不含上路费用）[CarExpert]。吉利旗下的这个高端品牌选择用产品迭代而不是降价来保持竞争力。

值得关注

接下来值得关注零跑A10在泰国的定价和交付时间，以及捷酷伦敦总部的最终决定。未来几天中国车企将陆续公布10月出口数据，届时可以看到9月的出口高增长能否延续到第四季度。
