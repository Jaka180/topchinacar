GWM Picks Bulgaria for First European Plant as SAIC's Overseas Sales Pass 1.16 Million长城汽车欧洲首个生产基地落户保加利亚，上汽前三季度海外销量突破116万辆

GWM will build the Ora 5 in Lovech, Bulgaria from Q1 2027 with planned capacity of 50,000 a year, while SAIC's Jan–Sep overseas sales hit 1.166 million and MG topped 280,000 in Europe.长城汽车将在保加利亚洛维奇生产欧拉5，首车预计2027年一季度下线，规划年产能5万辆；上汽1–9月海外销量116.6万辆，MG欧洲销量超28万辆。

By TopChinaCar Editorial作者：TopChinaCar 编辑部 · Published October 29, 2026发布于 2026-10-29 · Compiled from the primary sources cited below基于文末所列一手信源编写

Chinese automakers are moving from shipping cars to Europe to building them there. Great Wall Motor (GWM) has named Bulgaria as the home of its first European production base, while SAIC reported that its overseas sales for the first nine months of 2026 have already surpassed its full-year 2025 total. Smaller moves by XPeng, GAC's Aion and WeRide filled in the picture of an industry steadily widening its footprint abroad.

GWM Commits to Making Cars in Europe

GWM will build cars in Lovech, Bulgaria, working with local partner Bulgarian Automotive Industry (BAI). Line upgrades are due by the end of 2026, the first vehicle is expected in Q1 2027, and planned capacity is 50,000 vehicles a year, starting with the Ora 5. [CnEVPost]

The approach is deliberately incremental. Production starts with SKD assembly, with some parts localized from day one, and the site will supply GWM's sales networks in Italy, Spain and the Netherlands. GWM also plans at least 10 new models and variants in Europe over the next two years. Starting small lets GWM test demand and its supply chain before committing to heavier investment.

Its pickup business continues to anchor the company in emerging markets. GWM Pickup said its Jan–Sep overseas sales rose 29.5% to 59,651, and its Poer range leads Chinese brands in Brazil, Chile and South Africa. [Gasgoo]

SAIC and MG Keep Growing in Europe

SAIC's latest operating data show Jan–Sep overseas sales of 1.166 million, up 52.4% and already above its full-year 2025 overseas total. MG sold more than 280,000 cars in Europe in the first three quarters, up 23%, and set a brand record of 18,000 in the UK in September. Commercial vehicles added momentum, with overseas sales reaching a record 18,395 in September, up 120%. [Gasgoo]

MG is the longest-established Chinese brand in Europe, and its continued double-digit growth shows how brand recognition and a mature dealer network can keep volumes rising.

Building Networks: XPeng and Aion

XPeng said on Oct 27 that it has signed dealer agreements in Nigeria, Oman, Kuwait and Albania, covering the Gulf, West Africa and the Western Balkans. It also launched in Morocco, Tunisia, Bahrain and Mauritius this year, and its sales network now spans 83 countries and regions. [Gasgoo]

In the UK, GAC's Aion has appointed Shelbourne Motors as its sole retail partner in Northern Ireland. Sales start in Q1 2027 from showrooms in Belfast, Newry and Portadown, led by the UT supermini priced from £28,950. Aion's UK business is a 2026 joint venture between GAC and Jameel Motors. [Irish News]

Transitions and Infrastructure

Geely's Polestar has reportedly ended Polestar 2 production at the Luqiao plant after more than six years, with media putting output at just over 200,000; Polestar has not officially announced it. Norway and Sweden no longer accept new configured orders, and Norway, the model's biggest market, took more than 16,000. Polestar says an all-new successor is expected in early 2027. [electrive]

On the autonomous-driving side, WeRide launched a global insurance partnership program with Allianz Insurance Singapore and Ping An P&C as inaugural partners, covering its deployments in China, Southeast Asia and the Middle East as well as upcoming launches in Europe. [GlobeNewswire] Meanwhile, BYD's headcount has passed 1.02 million, including about 60,000 overseas employees, according to Gasgoo. [Gasgoo]

The backdrop remains a strong export market and a soft home market. CAAM data show China's vehicle exports reached 1.053 million in September, up 61.4%, and 8.205 million for Jan–Sep, up 65.8%. CPCA's preliminary figures show passenger car retail sales of 1.145 million for Oct 1–25, down 26% year on year.

What to Watch

  • How fast GWM moves from SKD assembly in Bulgaria toward deeper localization, and whether it adds more European sites.
  • Whether MG can keep up double-digit growth in Europe through the final quarter as more Chinese brands compete for the same buyers.

中国车企在欧洲的打法，正在从“把车运过去”转向“在当地造车”。长城汽车宣布欧洲首个生产基地落户保加利亚，上汽则公布前三季度海外销量已经超过去年全年。小鹏、广汽埃安和文远知行也在各自的方向上继续扩大海外布局。

长城汽车：落子保加利亚

长城汽车将与当地的保加利亚汽车工业公司（BAI）合作，在洛维奇建设生产基地。产线改造计划在2026年底完成，首车预计2027年一季度下线，规划年产能5万辆，首款车型为欧拉5。[CnEVPost]

长城选择循序渐进：初期以SKD组装起步，部分零部件从一开始就本地化，基地将对接长城在意大利、西班牙和荷兰的销售网络。长城还计划未来两年在欧洲推出至少10款新车型及衍生车型。先以较小规模起步，可以在加大投入之前检验市场需求和供应链。

皮卡业务则继续支撑长城在新兴市场的表现。长城皮卡1–9月海外销量59,651辆，同比增长29.5%，炮系列在巴西、智利和南非均位居中国品牌第一。[盖世汽车]

上汽与MG：欧洲继续增长

上汽集团最新经营数据显示，1–9月海外销量116.6万辆，同比增长52.4%，已超过2025年全年海外销量。MG前三季度欧洲销量超过28万辆，同比增长23%，9月英国单月销量1.8万辆，创品牌纪录。商用车9月海外销量18,395辆，同比增长120%，创历史新高。[盖世汽车]

MG是在欧洲根基最深的中国品牌，持续的两位数增长说明，品牌认知和成熟的经销网络能够持续带动销量。

铺设渠道：小鹏与埃安

小鹏汽车10月27日宣布，已与尼日利亚、阿曼、科威特和阿尔巴尼亚的当地伙伴签署经销协议，覆盖海湾、西非和西巴尔干地区。小鹏今年还在摩洛哥、突尼斯、巴林和毛里求斯推出品牌，全球销售网络已覆盖83个国家和地区。[盖世汽车]

在英国，广汽埃安指定Shelbourne Motors为北爱尔兰唯一零售伙伴，2027年一季度起在贝尔法斯特、纽里和波塔当的展厅开售，首款车型为UT小型车，起售价28,950英镑。埃安英国业务是广汽与Jameel Motors于2026年成立的合资公司。[Irish News]

产品更替与基础设施

据报道，吉利旗下极星2在路桥工厂的生产在持续六年多后结束，媒体报道累计产量略超20万辆，极星尚未正式宣布。挪威和瑞典已不再接受新配置订单，挪威作为该车最重要的市场，累计销量超过1.6万辆。极星称全新的下一代车型预计2027年初推出。[electrive]

自动驾驶方面，文远知行推出全球保险合作伙伴计划，安联保险新加坡和中国平安财险为首批合作伙伴，覆盖其在中国、东南亚和中东的现有部署以及即将在欧洲推出的业务。[GlobeNewswire] 另据盖世汽车报道，比亚迪员工总数已突破102万人，其中海外员工约6万人。[盖世汽车]

大背景依然是出口强、内需弱。中汽协数据显示，9月汽车出口105.3万辆，同比增长61.4%，1–9月累计出口820.5万辆，同比增长65.8%；乘联会初步数据显示，10月1–25日全国乘用车零售114.5万辆，同比下降26%。

值得关注

  • 长城在保加利亚从SKD组装走向更深度本地化的速度，以及是否会增加更多欧洲生产基地。
  • 在更多中国品牌争夺同一批欧洲消费者的情况下，MG能否在四季度保持两位数增长。
