Changan Opens Its Technology Unit to Investors

Changan Automobile, the Shenzhen-listed carmaker controlled by state-owned China Changan Automobile Group, said in an announcement released with its third-quarter report on the evening of October 30 that it plans to pre-list wholly owned subsidiary Chongqing Changan Technology Co., Ltd. on a property rights exchange. The aim is to bring in investors through a capital increase. [Yicai]

The terms are still open. The counterparty, the final amount of the capital increase and the equity ratio have not been determined, and the company stressed that the pre-listing is an information disclosure only and does not constitute a transaction. In China, state-linked companies often use a property rights exchange (产权交易所) to test investor interest and set a price before bringing in new shareholders. The pre-listing only starts that process.

The announcement came alongside weak quarterly numbers. Changan's third-quarter revenue was 36.249 billion yuan, down 14.17% year on year. The article does not say what the new capital will be used for, so readers should not infer a specific technology roadmap from the filing alone.

BAIC's Listed Units: BluePark Narrows Its Loss, Foton's Profit Falls

BAIC Group has several listed subsidiaries, and two of them reported on October 30. BAIC BluePark, which runs the Arcfox electric brand and builds the Stelato range under Huawei's HIMA alliance, reported third-quarter revenue of 6.761 billion yuan, up 15.23% year on year, while its net loss attributable to shareholders narrowed 17.5%. Revenue for January–September was 18.358 billion yuan, up 19.33%, and sales for the period were 163,376 units, up 46.52%. [Capital Week]

One caveat on the volume figure: the report does not split domestic and export sales, so the 163,376 units should not be read as a domestic-only number. BluePark is still loss-making. The narrower loss shows the gap shrinking, not a return to profit.

Foton Motor, BAIC's commercial-vehicle maker for trucks, vans and light commercial vehicles, reported third-quarter revenue of 15.262 billion yuan, up 1.22% year on year, while net profit attributable to shareholders fell 28.11% to 241 million yuan. [21st Century Business Herald] The report does not split domestic and export business either. Both companies share a parent, but they serve different markets: BluePark sells passenger electric vehicles and Foton sells commercial vehicles. Their results should not be combined into a single BAIC trend.

GAC Responds on "Rushed Cars" as Testing Rules Are Under Consultation

In a reply on its investor interaction platform on October 30, GAC Group said it has not so far been required to carry out any recall over "rushed cars", a term critics use for models developed with insufficient testing. The company said its validation system covers 12 major vehicle test categories and more than 1,500 sub-items. As an example, it said 685 physical vehicles were used in road testing for the Trumpchi Yue 7. [Auto.ifeng]

The context is regulatory. On October 10, the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (MIIT) and three other departments released a draft notice for public comment on strengthening the management of R&D testing and validation for vehicle products. The notice remains a draft, and China has no official classification of "rushed cars". GAC's statement is a company disclosure, not a regulatory finding.

What to Watch

October delivery and production releases from carmakers, followed by preliminary CPCA passenger-car data, will show how domestic demand ended the month. For Changan, the next milestone is a formal listing of the Changan Technology capital increase with named investors and terms. On regulation, watch whether MIIT and its partner departments finalize the testing-and-validation notice, and in what form.

长安汽车为长安科技挂牌引资

长安汽车（深交所上市，控股股东为国资背景的中国长安汽车集团）10月30日晚随三季报发布公告，拟将全资子公司重庆长安科技有限责任公司在产权交易所预挂牌，以增资扩股方式引入投资者。[第一财经]

具体条件尚未确定：交易对方、最终增资金额及股权比例均未确定，公司强调预挂牌仅为信息披露，不构成交易。在国内，国资背景企业引入新股东前，常先通过产权交易所预挂牌了解投资者意向并形成定价，此次预挂牌只是这一流程的起点。

公告与疲弱的季度业绩同时发布：长安汽车三季度营收362.49亿元，同比下降14.17%。报道未说明增资所得资金的具体用途，因此不宜仅凭这份公告推断其技术路线安排。

北汽旗下上市公司：北汽蓝谷亏损收窄，北汽福田利润下滑

北汽集团旗下有多家上市公司，其中两家在10月30日披露三季报。运营极狐品牌、并在华为鸿蒙智行体系下生产享界车型的北汽蓝谷，第三季度营收67.61亿元，同比增长15.23%，归母净亏损同比收窄17.5%。前三季度营收183.58亿元，同比增长19.33%；前三季度销量163,376辆，同比增长46.52%。[证券市场周刊]

需要说明的是，报道未区分国内与出口销量，163,376辆不应被视为纯国内销量。北汽蓝谷仍处于亏损状态，亏损收窄说明缺口在缩小，并不意味着已经扭亏。

北汽集团旗下的商用车企业北汽福田（产品涵盖卡车、轻型商用车等）第三季度营业收入152.62亿元，同比增长1.22%；归母净利润2.41亿元，同比下降28.11%。[21世纪经济报道] 该报道同样没有拆分国内与出口业务。两家公司虽同属北汽集团，但面向的市场不同：北汽蓝谷以新能源乘用车为主，北汽福田以商用车为主，两者的业绩不宜合并解读为北汽集团的单一走势。

“速成车”讨论与测试验证新规征求意见

广汽集团10月30日在投资者互动平台表示，截至目前，公司不存在因“速成车”问题被要求实施召回的情形。“速成车”是外界对研发验证不充分车型的说法。公司称已建立覆盖12个整车验证大项、1500余项验证子项的验证体系，并以传祺越7为例，称投入685台实车开展道路试验。[网通社]

这一表态有其监管背景。10月10日，工信部会同另外三个部门就加强汽车产品研发测试验证管理的通知公开征求意见。该通知目前仍是征求意见稿，国家层面也没有对“速成车”的官方分类。广汽的回应属于企业自述，并非监管部门的认定结论。

值得关注

接下来，各车企将陆续发布10月交付和产销数据，乘联分会也将公布乘用车初步数据，届时可以看到国内需求在10月的收官情况。长安汽车方面，下一步要看长安科技增资项目是否正式挂牌，以及投资者名单和交易条件。监管方面，可关注工信部等部门何时、以何种形式出台测试验证通知的正式稿。
