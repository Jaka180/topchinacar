GWM Takes Charge of UK Car Sales as BYD Opens Flash Charging in Malaysia长城将在英国自营乘用车业务，比亚迪在马来西亚启用闪充

GWM will run UK passenger-car sales through its own company. BYD opened Flash Charging in Malaysia and launched an updated Denza D9 at RM345,800.长城将在英国设立自营乘用车销售公司。比亚迪在马来西亚启用闪充，并同步推出售价为345,800林吉特的新版腾势D9。

By TopChinaCar Editorial作者：TopChinaCar 编辑部 · Published November 20, 2026发布于 2026-11-20 · Compiled from the primary sources cited below基于文末所列一手信源编写

Great Wall Motor plans to take direct control of its passenger-car business in Britain, while BYD has launched Flash Charging alongside an updated Denza D9 in Malaysia. The two announcements show how different parts of a carmaker’s local operation can shape the customer experience. GWM is changing who manages its relationship with British retailers. BYD is giving Malaysian buyers places to use one of the features it is selling with the new vehicle.

GWM takes charge of UK passenger cars

GWM will establish a UK sales company to manage its passenger-car business. International Motors, its existing distributor, will continue to handle utility vehicles, including the POER300 pickup. The split leaves the two sides with clearly defined responsibilities and gives GWM a local team through which to manage its passenger-car range and work with retailers. [Motor Trader]

Motor Trader cited SMMT figures showing that GWM registered 163 cars in the first ten months of 2026, compared with 439 in the same period a year earlier, a decline of 63%. That gives readers a baseline for following the change. The decisions to watch are which passenger vehicles GWM brings to Britain and how it makes them available through its retail network. The new structure puts those decisions under the carmaker’s own UK operation. [Motor Trader]

BYD pairs a new Denza with places to charge

BYD has switched on Flash Charging at Denza showrooms in Balakong, Glenmarie and Old Klang Road in the Klang Valley. The first locations initially serve compatible Denza models. BYD Malaysia is aiming for 12 operational Flash Charging points by the end of 2026 and 130 by the end of 2027. For drivers, the location of those points will matter as much as the overall count. A site on a regular route changes where a driver can realistically plan to charge. [SoyaCincau]

Denza launched the updated D9 Prestige AWD at RM345,800. The electric MPV uses a second-generation Blade battery, offers a WLTP range of 560 km and supports Flash Charging. Launching the vehicle and the first charging sites together lets buyers ask where they can try the feature as they compare the D9 with other vehicles. The showroom locations also give Denza staff a practical way to explain charging to people considering the MPV. [Paul Tan]

Chery’s Indonesia plans and the UK awards

In Indonesia, Chery’s product planning manager said the company wanted the J6T CSH to compete with established sellers in its segment. KatadataOTO reported that the previously unveiled range-extended SUV will come in rear- and all-wheel-drive versions. The local price is still pending. Buyers now have a clearer idea of the configurations they can compare once that price is available. [KatadataOTO]

In the UK, Carwow selected Volvo’s EX60 as its 2027 Car of the Year and Outstanding EV of the Year. It also named BYD Brand of the Year and gave Flash Charging its Tech Trailblazer Award. Those editorial awards offer a view of what the platform’s reviewers valued. For Flash Charging, the Malaysian rollout gives a separate view of how customers can use the technology. [Carwow] [Carwow]

What to Watch

Watch for GWM’s first decisions on its British passenger-car range and retail network under direct management. In Malaysia, track where BYD opens its next Flash Charging points and how far the network reaches beyond the initial showrooms. Those details will make both announcements easier to judge from a customer’s perspective.

长城计划直接管理英国乘用车业务，比亚迪则在马来西亚同步推出闪充和新版腾势 D9。这两件事都涉及车主在当地买车、用车时会接触到的环节。长城重新安排自己与英国零售伙伴的分工，比亚迪则为购买新车的马来西亚消费者提供了可以使用闪充的地点。

长城接手英国乘用车销售

长城将设立英国销售公司，直接管理乘用车业务。原分销商 International Motors 会继续负责包括 POER300 皮卡在内的多用途车辆。这一分工明确了双方各自负责的车型，长城也可以通过自己的当地团队管理乘用车产品阵容，与零售伙伴协作。 [Motor Trader]

Motor Trader 援引英国汽车制造商和贸易商协会数据称，长城今年前十个月在英国上牌 163 辆，上年同期为 439 辆，同比下降 63%。这一数字为观察调整后的表现提供了起点。接下来值得跟进的是长城将哪些乘用车引入英国，以及消费者可以通过哪些渠道买到这些车型。随着英国销售公司成立，这些决定将由长城自己的当地业务团队负责。 [Motor Trader]

比亚迪在马来西亚同步推出新车和闪充

比亚迪在巴生谷的 Balakong、Glenmarie 和 Old Klang Road 腾势展厅启用了闪充，首批站点率先服务兼容的腾势车型。比亚迪马来西亚计划到 2026 年底运营 12 个闪充点，并在 2027 年底增至 130 个。对于车主，站点的地理分布和总数一样值得关注。充电点能否接入日常路线，会影响车主是否方便使用。 [SoyaCincau]

腾势在马来西亚推出新版 D9 Prestige AWD，售价为 345,800 林吉特。该纯电 MPV 配备第二代刀片电池，WLTP 续航为 560 公里，并支持比亚迪闪充。车辆和首批站点一起推出，消费者比较车型时也可以了解在哪里体验闪充。展厅既是看车的地方，也让销售人员可以向有意购买这款 MPV 的消费者解释当地充电条件。 [Paul Tan]

奇瑞印尼配置与英国年度评选

在印尼，奇瑞当地产品规划负责人表示，希望 J6T CSH 与同级畅销车型竞争。KatadataOTO 报道称，这款此前已在当地亮相的增程 SUV 将提供后驱和四驱两种配置。当地售价仍待公布。等正式定价公布后，消费者就能结合两种驱动配置，比较它们的价格和选择空间。 [KatadataOTO]

在英国，Carwow 将 2027 年度车型奖和年度杰出电动车奖都颁给沃尔沃 EX60，同时将年度品牌奖颁给比亚迪，并授予比亚迪闪充技术先锋奖。作为平台编辑评选，这些结果反映了评审团队对车型和技术的看法。马来西亚已经启用的站点则提供了另一个观察角度，让读者看到兼容车型的车主如何在当地用上这项技术。 [Carwow] [Carwow]

值得关注

关注长城直接管理乘用车业务后，在英国车型及零售网络上会做出哪些安排。在马来西亚，接下来可以跟踪比亚迪在哪里开设新的闪充点，以及网络能否走出首批展厅、覆盖更多车主的日常路线。这些进展会让两项变化在当地消费者眼中更具体。
