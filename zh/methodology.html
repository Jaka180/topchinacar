Who we are, and who this is for我们是谁、给谁看

TopChinaCar is an industry information site for international readers who follow China's auto industry. We write about the companies, the people, the sales and the overseas business, using the public record.TopChinaCar 是面向关注中国汽车产业的国际读者的行业信息站。我们根据公开记录，写中国车企、相关人事、销量和海外业务。

The collection stage watches 143 sources. The current list is on the sources page. The rules for what we will and will not claim are in the editorial policy.采集这一步看着 143 个信源。当前清单在信源池。什么能写、什么不能写，写在编辑方针里。

Business flow业务流程

A piece moves through six stages. The words on the arrows are the action between stages. After a correction is accepted, it is written back to the related pages.一条内容经过六个环节。箭头上写的是这一步在做什么。更正采纳之后，会写回相关页面。

How we use AIAI 使用说明

Daily briefings, organization readings and contested readings are written by AI. Those pages are labeled AI-generated. People-intel items are extracted from announcements and keep a link to the original text. Sales rankings are calculated by a program from public figures, and the page names the data and the basis.日报、组织解读和争议解读由 AI 写成，页面上标明「AI 生成」。人事情报从公告抽取，并附上原文链接。销量排名由程序根据公开数字计算，页面写明数据和口径。

AI can be wrong. Please check names, titles, dates and figures against the original source. Corrections are welcome.AI 会出错。人名、职务、日期和数字请以原文为准。欢迎来更正。

Corrections更正政策

To ask for a correction, open the tip form for a correction. Say what is wrong, what the public record says, and include the source link. We reply within 48 hours, the same commitment as the editorial policy.申请更正请打开更正表单。写清哪里错了、公开记录怎么说，并附上出处链接。我们按编辑方针，在 48 小时内回复。

Once a correction is confirmed, it is applied on every related page, so the same fact is not left stale in a second place. The page keeps a note that it was corrected.核对成立后，更正会同步到所有相关页面，避免同一事实一处改了、另一处还是旧的。页面上会留下更正说明。

Editorial principles编辑原则

Public sources only只写有公开出处的事实

A factual sentence needs a public source a reader can open. We do not fill a gap with a made-up figure.事实句要有读者打得开的公开出处。空档不用编出来的数字去填。

No rumors, factions or private lives不写传闻、派系、私生活

We do not publish rumors, factional claims or details of private life.不刊登传闻，不写派系，不写私生活。

Inference sits apart from fact推断和事实分开写

A conclusion we draw is labeled as inference, separate from the sourced facts. A contested reading stays unpublished until the editor confirms it.我们自己做出的判断会标明是推断，和有出处的事实分开。争议解读在站长确认前不发布。
